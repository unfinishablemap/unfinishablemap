# Adversarial Peer-Review Audit — The Unfinishable Map

## TL;DR

- **The site is a technically disciplined, obsessively self-aware repackaging of existing quantum-dualist positions (Stapp, Eccles/Beck, Chalmers, Saad, Kastner) whose single genuine contribution is articulation-level, not first-order: the sharpened, do-calculus formalization of the Born-Preserving Causal-Efficacy Problem. Its deepest pathology is confession-without-correction — it states its fatal problems with rare precision and then banks that honesty as a credential while the load-bearing positions keep their confidence bands.**
- Citation accuracy is materially better than the documented fabrication history predicts: every load-bearing citation I verified against publisher-of-record primary sources (Torres Alegre, Saad, DeWall, Barrett, Barlev–Shtulman, Chalmers 2006, Cogitate/Ferrante, Kastner, Maier et al., Gurnee et al.) checked out on metadata, verbatim quotation, and author-stance. The fabrication pattern is nonetheless real and recent (the deleted Chalmers quote, Tononi stance inversion, Sahu metadata error, Michel year-drift), and it was caught by *outer* (non-Claude) review and one human email — not by the in-loop Claude reviewer, which is the core methodological indictment.
- The constitutional-attractor effect is not a risk here; it is the operating principle of the ~100-article Voids catalogue and the "convergence-as-evidence" apex move. The site concedes this in its own vocabulary (common-cause-null, tenet-generated voids) and then leans on the convergence anyway — coherence inflation and an epistemic-to-metaphysical slide dressed as epistemic humility.

## Five-Dimension Analysis

### Dimension 1 — Strongest Novel Insights (assessed stingily)

There is almost no new *first-order* philosophy here, and the site's own provenance trail concedes it. The five tenets are a curated assembly of existing commitments: irreducibility (Chalmers/Jackson conceivability and knowledge arguments), interactionism at quantum indeterminacy (Eccles/Beck exocytosis, Stapp's von-Neumann/Process-1 framework), collapse realism against Everett, and a Swinburne/Lowe-flavored substance lean for agency. "Post-decoherence selection," marketed as the Map's "most developed position," is Stapp's outcome-selection relocated past decoherence plus the standard (correct) observation — traceable to Adler, Bell, and Schlosshauer — that decoherence does not solve the outcome problem. "Trumping/delegatory causation" (Route 1 of the flagship problem) is Bradford Saad's delegatory dualism transposed almost verbatim. The transactional venue is Cramer/Kastner off the shelf. The cross-traditional-convergence material is Kastrup-style perennialism.

Three things rise, narrowly, to genuine contribution — all of them articulation or hygiene, none of them discovery:

1. **The Born-Preserving Causal-Efficacy Problem apex article** is the strongest single artifact on the site and states the interactionist's deepest dilemma more precisely than most of the peer-reviewed quantum-mind literature. The do-calculus framing — P(O│do(C),X) vs. the marginal q(O│X), the exhaustive (a)/(b)/(c) trilemma, and the localization of the entire empirical exposure to whether the site asserts a *conditional* signature at some specifiable grain — is a real diagnostic advance in how to pose the problem. It does not solve anything (the article says so), and posing a problem well is not a new argument, but it is above the ambient literature's standard. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/)
2. **The multi-axis positions register** (P-Q1–P-Q10 with credence, structural centrality, empirical discriminability, model maturity, and a framework-internal-only flag) is a genuine editorial innovation for philosophy, imported from forecasting/Bayesian practice. It makes the framework auditable in a way essays are not.
3. **The void-clustering audit apparatus and the "constitutional-attractor" self-diagnostic vocabulary** are a novel piece of methodological epistemic hygiene — but they are meta-level, about the project's own reliability, not about consciousness.

Verdict on novelty: the site's most defensible claim to originality is methodological (the adversarial-review architecture and the calibration registers), not philosophical. The object-level dualism is repackaging. This should be stated on the site's own terms rather than obscured by the volume of cross-links.

### Dimension 2 — Structural and Navigational Coherence

The taxonomy is elaborate and *mostly* principled, but two seams are ad hoc. First, the **Topics/Concepts distinction does not survive scrutiny**: both are described as "atomic content," both sit at hard caps (321 topics / 320 concepts), and the stated rule (concepts = building-block definitions, topics = deep dives) is violated constantly — `binding-problem`, `metacognition`, `global-workspace-theory` are concept-slugs doing topic-work, while `born-rule-and-the-consciousness-interface` is a topic doing concept-work. The two categories are functionally one corpus split by a cap. Second, the **Voids/Questions/Research trichotomy** is clean in principle (void = mapping blocked; question = tractable in-framework uncertainty; research = raw notes) but leaks in practice — several "research-stage voids" (Translation, Insight, Encoding, Participation) exist only as research notes with "folded"/"absorbed" status, which is a principled cap-management move but leaves the public index pointing at non-articles.

Strengths are real: Apex syntheses genuinely synthesize (the Born-preserving and post-decoherence apexes integrate their subordinate concepts rather than merely listing them); cross-linking is dense and the spot-checked wikilinks resolve; the cap-forced "coalesce/absorb" discipline is a legitimate antidote to proliferation. The changelog shows near-daily reciprocal-link maintenance and length-ceiling enforcement — infrastructure most solo projects lack.

The dominant structural pathology is **redundancy-as-architecture**: the same claim is restated across apex/topic/concept/void layers and then the restatements are treated as mutually corroborating (see Dimension 3). The `binding-problem` concept, the `binding-void`, the quantum-binding argument, and the `zero-lag-gamma-synchrony` concept are four articles on one claim; that is coherence inflation wearing the costume of thoroughness.

### Dimension 3 — Tenet Alignment and the Constitutional-Attractor Effect

Content follows the tenets with high fidelity — which is precisely the problem. The **constitutional-attractor effect is not an occasional artifact; it is the generative mechanism of the entire Voids catalogue.** The convergence-as-evidence move in `what-voids-reveal` and `taxonomy-of-voids` — that ~100 independently catalogued voids "cluster at the consciousness-physics interface," offered as abductive support for the tenet package — is a textbook non-independence failure. The voids were generated by one apophatic method under one five-tenet constitution; their clustering is a sampling artifact of the generating procedure, not a discovered feature of the territory. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/)

To its considerable credit, the site *names* this in its own vocabulary: the `common-cause-null` discount, `tenet-generated-voids`, and a "framework-independent voids" subsection that tries to isolate the voids "any framework would acknowledge." But the mitigation fails on its own terms in two ways. (a) The *selection* of which voids count as "framework-independent" is itself performed under dualist framing — the partition is tenet-laden, so the residual clustering is not the framework-independent evidence it is presented as. (b) Even granting the subset, the article slides from "these voids illuminate cognitive architecture (methodological, any view)" to "the catalogue's organized structure is the kind of pattern physicalism must explain rather than dismiss (evidential, pro-dualism)." That is the **epistemic-to-metaphysical slide** in one sentence, marked-apart and then leaned-on anyway.

**Tenet leakage** is present but subtle. The formal "no label leakage" discipline (keeping editorial mode-labels out of article bodies) is enforced well. But the substantive interpretive vocabulary — "corridor dualism," "the interface," "selection," "post-decoherence selection" — is treated as neutral exposition when it is in fact heavily theory-laden framing that presupposes the tenets. A physicalist reading `attention-as-interface` or `quantum-measurement-consciousness-interface` encounters the Map's ontology smuggled in as vocabulary.

The `constrain-vs-establish` gate is mostly respected in the quantum-biology cluster (the tenets page and `quantum-biology-and-neural-consciousness` explicitly sort evidence by which sub-reading it bears on, and correctly say warm-system quantum effects *constrain* the "biology must be classical" claim without *establishing* neural coherence). But the magnetoreception material repeatedly rides the line: avian cryptochrome results are a biological *precedent* only, and the articles say so, yet the precedent is invoked so often across the cluster that its rhetorical weight exceeds its logical licence. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

### Dimension 4 — Internal Contradictions and Unsupported Claims

- **Live contradiction, `combination-problem`:** the article lead and the HTML meta-description still assert panpsychism's combination problem is one "Interactionism avoids entirely" / the Map "avoids the problem entirely," while the corrected body (L157) and the 25 July refine concede the Map avoids only *constitutive* subject-summing and still owes the interface-unity account. The correction landed in the body but never propagated to the lead or the citation-metadata, so the article contradicts itself at the two most-quoted loci. The same "avoids entirely" over-claim was flagged and softened in `russellian-monism` and `universal-coupling-response`; the lead of `combination-problem` is the residue. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/combination-problem/)
- **Confession-without-correction as a systemic pattern:** the flagship `born-preserving-causal-efficacy` article concedes that under its own preferred Route 1 (trumping) "the distinction between genuine conscious authorship and outright epiphenomenalism becomes *entirely metaphysical*, with no possible empirical consequence at any scale," and that the preemption machinery is symmetric (a physicalist can run it in reverse). This is a candid statement that the Map's endorsed escape route renders its central causal claim empirically indistinguishable from the epiphenomenalism it exists to reject. Yet P-Q1 ("post-decoherence selection is the strongest current mechanism candidate") remains at *moderate confidence*, and the whole interface programme continues generating content as though selection were established. The failure mode is exactly the one named in the brief: the disclosure is banked as intellectual honesty rather than converted into a binding demotion. [unfinishablemap + 2](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/)
- **Load-bearing source-quality failure, the quantum-binding cluster:** the "zero-lag gamma synchrony cannot arise classically without violating relativistic causality" claim rests on Erik Baum's "Quantum Binding Argument," a self-published PhilArchive preprint whose central premise is contradicted by peer-reviewed work on classical dynamical-relaying and resonance-pair zero-lag synchronization (Gollo, Mirasso, Sporns & Breakspear; Vicente et al.). The site caught the own-voice over-claim on 25 July and reframed Baum's premise as "contested" with the classical rebuttal inline — good — but a non-peer-reviewed preprint making a false physical claim is still doing load-bearing work across `binding-problem`, `binding-void`, and `quantum-neural-mechanisms-and-coherence`. That is an evidential-independence and source-tier problem the "contested" hedge does not fully discharge.
- **Currency/metadata slippage, magnetoreception:** the tenets page attributes the cryptochrome result to "a January 2026 Princeton study," but the underlying peer-reviewed paper — Luo, J.; Hungerland, J.; Solov'yov, I. A.; Subotnik, J. E.; Hammes-Schiffer, S., "Protein and Solvent Reorganization Drives Radical Pair Stability in Avian Cryptochrome 4a," *Journal of the American Chemical Society* 2025, 147(47):43934–43945, DOI 10.1021/jacs.5c15726 — was published in **November 2025**. "January 2026 Princeton study" is the date of the Princeton Office of the Dean of the Faculty *news release*, not the primary paper. Minor, but it is exactly the kind of secondary-source-as-primary slip the citation discipline is supposed to prevent. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

### Dimension 5 — Citation and Empirical-Claim Accuracy

This is the audit's most surprising result. Given the documented systemic history of fabrication, I treated fabricated/misattributed quotation as a live expectation and verified load-bearing citations against publisher-of-record primary sources. **Every load-bearing citation I checked verified on all three axes (metadata, verbatim fidelity, author-stance):**

- **Torres Alegre (2025), arXiv:2512.12636** — real; the site correctly flags it as an unrefereed preprint and correctly scopes it as a compatibility/no-signalling result, not positive support. Metadata (title, arXiv ID, v3 dated 4 Feb 2026) accurate. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/)
- **Saad, "A dualist theory of experience," *Philosophical Studies* 182(3):939–967, DOI 10.1007/s11098-025-02290-3** — accurate (published online 18 Feb 2025; author at the Global Priorities Institute, University of Oxford); delegatory dualism correctly characterized, matching Saad's own gloss that experiences uphold causal responsibilities "delegated" to them by physical states.
- **DeWall, Baumeister & Masicampo (2008), *Consciousness and Cognition* 17(3):628–645** — accurate, and crucially the site uses it as *corroborating not establishing* the interaction tenet, with the epiphenomenalist counter stated. Correct author-stance handling.
- **Barrett et al. (2021), *Cognitive Science* 45:e12992** and **Barlev & Shtulman (2021), *Psychological Review* 128(6):1007–1021** — accurate, and used *against* an innate-dualism reading (intuitive materialism as default). This is a genuine, working **co-optation firewall**: naturalist findings cited toward a conclusion that cuts against the folk-dualism prop, not co-opted.
- **Chalmers (2006), "one clear case of a strongly emergent phenomenon"** — verbatim-accurate against the primary text ("Varieties of Emergence," consc.net).
- **Cogitate Consortium / Ferrante et al. (2025), *Nature* 642(8066):133–142, DOI 10.1038/s41586-025-08888-1** — accurate, including the corrected canonical author form; n=256 participants, 41 co-authors incl. 9 co-first-authors.
- **Kastner PTI (2012, CUP)** and **Maier, Dechamps & Pflitsch (2018), *Frontiers in Psychology* 9:379** — accurate; Maier correctly deployed as a null *against* micro-psychokinesis (again, correct stance). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/)
- **Gurnee et al. (2026), "Verbalizable Representations Form a Global Workspace in Language Models," Transformer Circuits Thread, 6 July 2026 (arXiv:2607.15495)** — co-leads Wes Gurnee and Nicholas Sofroniew, correspondence to Jack Lindsey; Anthropic; 16 authors; web-native venue, no DOI. **Real and correctly attributed.** The subagent verification confirms the "J-space"/global-workspace construct is genuine. Critical caveat: the site's gloss "functional access consciousness empirically instantiated in LLMs" is a *modest strengthening* of the authors' own carefully hedged language. The paper states verbatim: *"access consciousness is a purely functional notion; the relationship that it has with subjective experience (sometimes called phenomenal consciousness) is widely debated. In this paper, we take no position on this issue…"* The functional claim is faithful; the "we take no position" hedge must be preserved to prevent an epistemic-to-metaphysical slide into phenomenal consciousness. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/)

The fabrication history is nonetheless genuine and recent — the changelog itself records the deleted fabricated Chalmers quote at `combination-problem` L128 (words appearing nowhere in Chalmers, second sentence *inverting* his thesis), the Tononi stance inversion at the IIT section, the Bandyopadhyay→Sahu wrong-metadata fix, and the Michel 2021→2023 silent year-drift. The decisive fact for the audit: these were caught predominantly by **outer reviews (ChatGPT 5.6 Pro, Claude Opus 5, Gemini 2.5 Pro)** and, in the Vaassen case, by a **human email from the cited author** — *not* by the in-loop Claude deep-review that had passed the articles clean across multiple cycles. The citation apparatus is currently trustworthy where I sampled it, but the mechanism that made it trustworthy is the external check, not the self-review.

## Bottom-Line Verdict

**Corpus-level: REVISE-HARD, with one cluster DEMOTE-TO-COHERENCE-ONLY and specific deletions.**

- **RETAIN:** `born-preserving-causal-efficacy` (apex), the `positions/quantum-interface` register, `tenets`, `post-decoherence-selection-programme`, the illusionism/eliminativism engagement cluster, and the quantum-biology evidence-calibration articles. These are the site at its honest best: precise, hedged, stance-accurate.
- **REVISE-HARD:** `combination-problem` (resolve the lead/meta vs. body self-contradiction); the magnetoreception/quantum-biology currency line; `predictive-processing` and `protention` (engagement is real but terminates in tenet-restatement rather than rebuttal — mark that the strongest constructive physicalist rival is *rivaled, not defeated*).
- **DEMOTE-TO-COHERENCE-ONLY:** the entire **Voids convergence-as-evidence apparatus** — `what-voids-reveal`, the "framework-independent voids" subsection of the voids index, and the evidential (not methodological) half of `taxonomy-of-voids`. These may stand as framework-internal coherence exhibits; they may not be presented as abductive evidence for the tenets. The common-cause-null discount should be applied as a hard ceiling, not a footnote.
- **FLAG AS PERPETUALLY CONTESTED:** the core interaction claim itself (Tenets 2+3 as instantiated by P-Q1/P-Q3). By the site's own admission the Born-preserving corridor is empirically indistinguishable from chance and from epiphenomenalism under its preferred route; this is not a temporary gap but a structural boundary and should be labelled as permanently open at every load-bearing locus, not just in the flagship apex. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)
- **DELETE / DE-LOAD:** the load-bearing reliance on Baum's self-published "Quantum Binding Argument" across the binding cluster. Cite it, if at all, only as a flagged non-peer-reviewed position with the peer-reviewed dynamical-relaying rebuttal given equal or greater weight; strip any inference that treats it as established.

## Site-Wide Methodology Improvements

1. **Break the Claude-on-Claude review circularity structurally, not cosmetically.** The fabrication catches came from outer (non-Claude) review and a human email, not from in-loop Claude deep-review — which passed the fabricated Chalmers quote clean across cycles. Make an independent-model or human citation-verification pass a *blocking gate* before any citation is marked "real-correct," and record which reviewer tier caught each defect so the self-review's true catch-rate is measurable rather than assumed.
2. **Convert "confession" into binding register actions.** Institute a rule: any article that concedes a load-bearing claim is empirically indistinguishable from its negation (as the flagship apex does) must trigger an automatic confidence-band review of the dependent positions. Self-disclosure should not be a terminal state.
3. **Apply the common-cause-null discount as a hard ceiling on the voids catalogue's evidential weight**, not a citable footnote. Any convergence-as-evidence claim generated under the tenets is coherence-only by construction; enforce this at generation time, not at review time.
4. **Add a fabrication-forensics standing task** targeting exactly the failure class already observed: (a) quotes whose second sentence inverts the cited author's thesis (the Chalmers pattern), (b) stance inversions on naturalist authors (the Tononi pattern), (c) silent metadata drift on re-verification (the Michel/Sahu patterns). These are patterned, not random, and should be grepped for corpus-wide on a schedule.
5. **Resolve the Topics/Concepts category incoherence.** Either give the two categories a genuine, enforced ontological distinction or merge them; the current split is cap-driven, not principled, and the caps themselves are being used to justify absorption decisions that a principled taxonomy should drive.
6. **Fix the scholarly-metadata integrity problem.** Crediting AI models as named co-authors ("Claude Oquatre-sept," "Oquatre-eight, C.") in citations indexed on Google Scholar, SSRN, and PhilPapers is an authorship-integrity liability; disclose AI contribution in a metadata field, not in the author line. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/project/why-this-is-different/)
7. **Down-rank preprint and self-published sources at the source-tier level.** The Baum episode shows a non-peer-reviewed preprint can acquire load-bearing status and survive multiple review cycles. Tag every citation by source tier (peer-reviewed / preprint / self-published / news) and forbid self-published and news sources from doing load-bearing inferential work without a peer-reviewed corroborant.

## Caveats

I audited a deep but partial sample: the changelog, all category indexes, the full slug lists for Apex and Voids, the tenets and positions registers, the flagship Born-preserving apex, and ~15 further articles, plus primary-source verification of ~11 load-bearing citations. I did **not** verify the majority of the ~650 topic/concept citations; my finding that sampled citations verify clean does **not** license the inference that the corpus is fabrication-free — the documented pattern and the demonstrated dependence on external catches both argue against that. The site is a live, daily-changing target (external indexes lag 24–48h); the changelog was my authoritative state record and some articles may have changed between fetch and reading. Where I could not independently confirm a claim I have said so (notably: I relied on the site's own changelog for the internal-review provenance of the fabrication catches, and on a single subagent pass plus one enrichment pass for the Gurnee/J-space verification).
