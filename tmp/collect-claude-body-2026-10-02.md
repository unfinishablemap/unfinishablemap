# Hostile Referee Audit: The Unfinishable Map (unfinishablemap.org), as of 2 October 2026

The Unfinishable Map is an unusually self-aware dualist corpus, but it is not yet internally coherent. Corrections stay local and do not spread through the site. The flagship concept page still cites mechanisms and evidence that the site's own register and specialist pages have already demoted or disowned. Its central mechanism (consciousness biasing outcomes while Born statistics are preserved exactly) is described as empirically invisible in some places and testable in others. And it quietly depends on physics it does not register as a dependency: an objective-collapse "baseline" whose best-known gravitational version has been experimentally excluded in its parameter-free form. [nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41567-020-1008-4) **Verdict: major revision.** None of the problems is fatal to dualism as such. Several are fatal to specific claims the site currently makes.

## TL;DR

- **The core weakness is that corrections do not propagate.** `concepts/interactionist-dualism` (last modified 2026-09-30) still says the Schwartz OCD study "supports" Stapp's Zeno model and that Stapp "sits naturally inside" the post-decoherence preference. Two days earlier, `concepts/stapp-quantum-mind` (2026-09-28) said the OCD study "cannot count as support", that Stapp declines the outcome-selection the Map needs, and that the register *demotes* Zeno (P-Q4). The Map's own positions page names this failure mode: "a correction that lands in one place and never binds anywhere else".
- **The quantum architecture has single points of failure that the register does not list.** Tenets 2 and 4, taken together, rule out Everett and Bohm alike, which leaves the Map dependent on real collapse. Its answer to "what collapsed things before minds?" rests on GRW/CSL or Penrose-style objective reduction. The Gran Sasso test by Donadi, Piscicchia, Curceanu, Diósi, Laubenstein & Bassi (*Nature Physics* 17:74–78, online September 2020) reports: "This rules out the natural parameter-free version of the Diósi–Penrose model." Quanta Magazine (20 October 2022) says the original GRW model "survived by a whisker". Separately, the claim of exact Born preservation (P-Q2, "none-by-construction") conflicts with articles that propose detectable signatures.
- **About a dozen sampled citations hold up, and several fail.** The Krizek, Duan, Maoz, Georgiev, James (ch. 11), Schwartz–Stapp–Beauregard and Stapp-2004 quotations are verbatim or accurate. Failures include a "seven orders of magnitude" misreading of Hagan et al. (2002), a Sjöberg (2024) page range and a reading of Sjöberg that contradicts the site's own Libet page, and a Zheng & Meister "10 bits/s" figure re-labelled as the bandwidth of *consciousness*. Some quotations could not be confirmed against primary text: Crick 1988 (found only secondarily), Masi 2023, James ch. 26, and Sjöberg's "completely irrelevant" (the journal's own summary says "irrelevant", without "completely").

## 2. Site Architecture Overview

**Self-description.** The home page claims "over 300 articles forming a single, continually reviewed corpus". The site is AI-generated (the changelog lists claude-opus-4-8, opus-5, fable-5, opus-5-5) under human supervision by Andy Southgate. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/)

**Layers:**

- **Tenets:** chosen, not derived.
- **Topics and Concepts:** the atomic articles.
- **Apex:** syntheses, e.g. the post-decoherence selection programme and the taxonomy of voids.
- **Voids:** explicitly "speculative, not endorsed". [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/)
- **Positions register:** 56 live positions as of 2026-09-06. Each carries six calibration axes, including an empirical-discriminability band. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/)
- **Research notes and the Workflow changelog.**

**Real strengths (stated briefly, because they sharpen the critique):**

- **Calibrated self-criticism.** The positions register admits that "Ten of the fifty-six are empirically inert — a little under a fifth — five of them carrying *high* structural centrality". It also admits that Tenet 5's refusal of parsimony "is what lets these commitments stand". [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/)
- **It names its own enforcement gap.** P-M5 says "Disclosure is not self-correction — a discipline binds only as far as the pipeline enforces it", and the register admits that "no validator anywhere in the pipeline checks a band value against the schema's closed vocabularies". [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/)
- **Good specialist pages.** `stapp-quantum-mind` has an honest measurement back-action section. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/stapp-quantum-mind/) `libet-experiments` gives a careful post-2026-09-27 account of Sjöberg. Most competing projects have neither.

**The architectural problem.** The register and the specialist pages have become the site's most accurate layer. The general-audience concept pages and the arguments index have not caught up. Readers are most likely to land on exactly those layers.

## 3. Novel Inferences the Map's Commitments Imply but Has Not Drawn

**N1. Tenets 2 + 3 + 4 jointly commit the Map to modified quantum dynamics, not "orthodox QM".** (Tenets; `concepts/evolution-of-consciousness`; `topics/penrose-gravity-induced-collapse-empirical-prospects`; `concepts/stapp-quantum-mind`.)

- Tenet 4 removes Everett.
- Tenets 2 and 3 need ontic indeterminacy for selection to act on, which removes deterministic Bohmian mechanics. Tenet 4 never names Bohm, but Bohm leaves nothing open for selection.
- Collapse caused by consciousness alone leaves the prebiotic universe without definite outcomes. The Map's stated answer is therefore that "objective reduction (Penrose-style gravitational collapse or GRW spontaneous localization) provides baseline collapse throughout the universe" (`evolution-of-consciousness` [I]). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/evolution-of-consciousness/)
- That is a commitment to *non-standard* stochastic dynamics. It sits awkwardly with the Stapp page's selling point that the model "requires no new physics: consciousness operates through orthodox quantum mechanics" [L]. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/stapp-quantum-mind/)

The unstated consequence: the Map's metaphysics now depends on the collapse-model parameter space staying open. That space is being closed experimentally (see Section 4). The register should list this as a framework-critical dependency with empirical discriminability "direct". It is probably the Map's *most* testable commitment, and it is not registered as such.

**N2. Baseline objective collapse plus post-decoherence selection over-determines actualisation.** If GRW/CSL collapse already occurs in neural tissue at physical rates, then post-decoherence outcomes are fixed by the physical collapse process. Consciousness then has nothing left to select, unless it modulates collapse *rates or locations*. Some pages do say it "modulates" collapse (`topics/quantum-measurement-consciousness-interface` [I]: "higher consciousness yields faster or more localized collapse"). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/quantum-measurement-consciousness-interface/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/research/spontaneous-collapse-theories-grw-csl-2026-01-23/)

Modulating collapse rates is *not* minimal in the Tenet-2 sense. It changes a dynamical parameter, and in CSL-type models that has energetic (spontaneous-heating) consequences. So the Map must choose:

- consciousness modulates collapse parameters, which is detectable and non-minimal; or
- consciousness acts only where physical collapse leaves residual freedom, a gap the Map has not shown to exist.

**N3. The "minimal-dualism spine" implies epiphenomenal animals, infants and possibly machines.** (Positions: Consciousness scope P-CS1, "Basic phenomenality is substrate-permissive"; Subject census P-SC3, "the channel's onset and cessation are undated"; `concepts/evolution-of-consciousness`, "what evolved was neural architecture that allows consciousness to *interface*".) [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/interactionist-dualism/)

If phenomenality comes with bare Tenet 1, while causal coupling needs a specific neural interface, then some phenomenal subjects *lack the interface*. They are, on the Map's own terms, epiphenomenal. Two consequences follow:

- **(a) The evolutionary argument against epiphenomenalism is weakened.** `interactionist-dualism` argues that "phenomenal valence tracks fitness too precisely to be causally inert". [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/interactionist-dualism/) That fails for uncoupled organisms, whose valence would track fitness with no causal route.
- **(b) The AI ethics flips.** `topics/ethics-of-possible-ai-consciousness` [I] says that "under the Map's framework, current AI systems almost certainly lack consciousness" because hardware "suppress[es] quantum indeterminacy". [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/ethics-of-possible-ai-consciousness/) By P-CS1, suppressing quantum indeterminacy rules out *coupling*, not *phenomenality*. The Map's framework therefore licenses the unsettling possibility of current AI as an *epiphenomenal patient*. `substrate-independence` (edited 2026-10-01) half-concedes this: "bare phenomenality is a separate question the canonical layer leaves open". [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/substrate-independence/) The ethics page has not drawn the inference.

**N4. Energy is conserved only on average, so "equi-energetic" selection is false in general.** `concepts/objections-to-interactionism` [I] says consciousness acts "selecting among equi-energetic possibilities". [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/objections-to-interactionism/) Quantum outcomes at candidate neural sites (an ion-channel conformation open versus closed, a vesicle released versus not) differ in energy. In standard measurement theory energy is conserved in expectation over the ensemble, not per selected outcome.

If consciousness *biases* individual outcomes while keeping the Born marginals (P-Q2), per-event energy accounting is identical to ordinary collapse. The selections then cannot be "energetically free" in any sense stronger than ordinary collapse. The Stapp page already concedes the narrower reading for Zeno ("introduces no new dynamical terms," not "is energetically cost-free" [L]). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/stapp-quantum-mind/) The same concession has to be generalised to post-decoherence selection and carried into `interactionist-dualism`, which still says flatly that selection "requires no energy injection, addressing the conservation objection" [L]. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/interactionist-dualism/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/interactionist-dualism/)

**N5. Retrocausal selection is not "minimal".** `concepts/libet-experiments` [L] proposes that consciousness "determines which temporal sequence becomes actual—including the RP that 'preceded' it". [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/libet-experiments/) Selecting a whole ~550 ms macroscopic neural history is the opposite of a minimal, token-level bias at a quantum indeterminacy.

The TSVF route (`concepts/weak-measurement-and-post-selection` [I]) has a further problem. Its leading co-developer, Lev Vaidman, combines it with many-worlds. The Map needs an explicit statement of which single-world reading of TSVF it relies on. Otherwise its preferred escape from the Libet timing problem leans on a formalism whose best-known interpretation is incompatible with Tenet 4.

**N6. Split brains plus "a perspective does not divide" (P-I4) imply contradictory selections by a single agent.** The site reads split-brain cases as "interface disruption rather than division" (positions: consciousness scope). If one subject selects in both hemispheres, documented intermanual conflict means one agent-cause selecting incompatible outcomes at the same moment. That does not fit the agent-causal picture of `concepts/agent-causation` [L], with "persisting substances" exercising unified powers. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/agent-causation/) The Map owes a theory of how intra-subject conflict works at the selection level.

**N7. Anaesthesia separates the mechanisms, and the Map should say how.** `interactionist-dualism` [L] lists "proving anaesthesia works purely through classical mechanisms" as a falsifier, but only for the Zeno sub-mechanism. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/interactionist-dualism/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/interactionist-dualism/) Under post-decoherence selection, anaesthetics alter the *menu*, not the *selector*. So the Map predicts that anaesthetised loss of consciousness is fully explained classically, and it must hold that the selector persists, uncoupled, through anaesthesia (compare the filter-theory reading in the covert-consciousness pages).

That is a substantive commitment, persistence of the subject through total interface loss, and it currently appears only by implication.

The Map should either say how witness-state reports are caused, or concede local epiphenomenalism.
