# External referee report

**Recommendation: Major revision**  

**Audit date:** 20 August 2026  

**Object reviewed:** the live Apex Articles Index, last modified 13 August 2026.

## Executive judgment

The page is not itself a conventional apex synthesis. It is a catalogue and governance document defining approved apex subjects, summarising their theses, listing source nodes, and recording editorial decisions. The appropriate audit question is therefore whether those summaries accurately represent the live articles, whether their evidential classifications are defensible, and whether the catalogue’s governance controls keep them current. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/apex-articles/)

The 13 August modification was **narrowly correct but substantively incomplete**. It repaired an explicit overcount in entry 24 by replacing seven supposed convergent traditions with “five-or-six” and reclassifying Bergson, Carruthers and Schwitzgebel.   But the remaining claim—that the revised count carries cumulative evidential weight—does not survive the source article’s own strongest-rival analysis.

More generally:

- Most sampled bibliographic items are real and correctly identified.
- The larger problem is **inferential fidelity**: evidence compatible with the tenets is repeatedly presented as evidence for them.
- Two source-fidelity failures are serious: the handling of Vadillo et al. 2026 materially reverses the paper’s conclusion, and a sentence attributed to Stiller and Dunbar 2007 appears instead to originate in Redshaw 2024.
- The index is already stale relative to substantive source revisions made on 17 August, despite the changelog now running through 20 August. [The Unfinishable Map+2The Unfinishable Map+2](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/)
- The most serious tenet-protective move is the self-concealing-interface thesis: aggregate nulls are treated as expected while heterogeneous “seam” phenomena are treated as potential support, without a model showing that those phenomena are more probable under interactionism than under ordinary cognitive neuroscience.

The site’s own methodology article supplies the correct standard. It says the programme has not yet produced the novel empirical content needed to demonstrate progressiveness, warns against counting common-source convergence repeatedly, and asks whether a declared discipline actually fired. It also denies the Map any entitlement to treat its internal coherence as cheap evidence. [The Unfinishable Map+2The Unfinishable Map+2](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/judging-the-map-as-science/) The catalogue does not apply that standard consistently.

This was a risk-weighted verification of the catalogue’s load-bearing empirical and bibliographic claims. It was not a line-by-line re-verification of every inherited citation across all 36 entries.

## 1. The 13 August correction to entry 24 fixes the roster, not the inference

Entry 24 now says that convergence across **“five-or-six traditions”** carries the evidential weight. It correctly records that Bergson is a parallel proposer rather than an independent witness, Carruthers belongs with the strongest physicalist absorption, and Schwitzgebel is sceptical about introspective access to outputs as well as operations. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/apex-articles/)

That correction is justified. The source article itself now acknowledges three facts that should have forced a more radical rewrite of the catalogue thesis:

1. Output access without process access is a standard prediction of mainstream cognitive architecture, not a surprising discovery peculiar to dualism. The source explicitly invokes Nisbett and Wilson, modularity, Marr’s levels and global-workspace approaches as antecedent physicalist explanations. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/phenomenal-output-causal-machinery-dissociation/)
2. Predictive-processing introspection plus perceptual reality monitoring supplies a unified physicalist rival: higher-order layers estimate verdicts without representing their own message-passing operations. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/phenomenal-output-causal-machinery-dissociation/)
3. Once Carruthers is removed from the witness column, the source says the genuinely independent weight rests principally on Polanyi and parts of Koriat. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/phenomenal-output-causal-machinery-dissociation/)

Consequently, five or six is a count of intellectual relationships to a phenomenon, not a count of independent evidential witnesses. A defensible classification would distinguish:

- **despite-commitment witnesses:** Polanyi and parts of Koriat;
- **materialist absorbers or corroborators:** Carruthers, Trick and Pylyshyn, and the effort-cost literature;
- **parallel proposer:** Bergson;
- **general introspection sceptic:** Schwitzgebel.

Only the first category can plausibly supply despite-commitment evidence. The others establish that the phenomenon is real or theoretically tractable, but they do not increase the likelihood of the Map relative to the strongest physicalist model.

The source article is commendably open about possible counterexamples, including tip-of-the-tongue experience, cognitive phenomenology, autonoetic recall and felt skilled judgement. It concedes that proprietary cognitive phenomenology could narrow the proposed signature. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/phenomenal-output-causal-machinery-dissociation/) The catalogue omits that limiting result and preserves the more rhetorically powerful cumulative-convergence formulation.

**Verdict:** the post-modification taxonomy is better, but the post-modification evidential claim still fails. Entry 24 should say that the cross-domain recurrence motivates an explanandum; it does not presently discriminate dualism from predictive-processing or global-access accounts.

## 2. The catalogue is stale relative to the articles it governs

The catalogue says source-side refactors require apex re-cross-review so that synthesis narratives continue to resolve against current source anchors and tenet connections. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/apex-articles/) The formal wording focuses on coalescing and condensing, but the same epistemic requirement plainly applies to substantive changes in modal strength or premise status. Two important sources changed on 17 August without corresponding catalogue revisions.

### 2.1 Entry 15 reverses the direction of support

Entry 15 still concludes that **“quantum mechanics provides indirect evidence for dualism.”** [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/apex-articles/)

The live source article now begins conditionally: *if* the tenets are true, then quantum theory can be interpreted as describing a constraint space while omitting a selection principle. It later concedes that strict Born-preserving interaction is empirically equivalent to ordinary quantum mechanics and that the reverse inference is philosophical reinterpretation rather than a competing empirical theory. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/what-consciousness-tells-us-about-physics/)

Its “triple screen” is even more damaging to the catalogue summary. The article says the causal claim is screened from the ordinary channels through which it could be evidenced; exact statistical concealment firms up the framework’s cost rather than its empirical case. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/what-consciousness-tells-us-about-physics/)

The 17 August revision also corrected two previous overstatements:

- PBR excludes certain ψ-epistemic models only within the ontological-models framework and subject to preparation independence.
- The article’s four physical constraints are inherited from Tenets 1–4, rather than independently extracted from physics. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/)

The valid inference is:

> Tenets plus quantum formalism → a particular metaphysical interpretation of quantum theory.

The catalogue presents the converse:

> Quantum theory → indirect evidence for the tenets.

That converse does not follow. No-go theorems may constrain a proposed interface or remove particular objections. They do not supply positive evidence for consciousness selecting outcomes.

**Verdict:** materially stale and inferentially reversed.

### 2.2 Entry 7 remains stronger than its revised source

Entry 7 says **“computation alone cannot generate that coupling.”** The live source is now more cautious: current AI probably lacks bidirectionally coupled consciousness, but engineered quantum-capable substrates and other nonstandard possibilities remain open. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/apex-articles/)

The 17 August changelog records why the categorical claim was removed. The site has no interface-eligibility law allowing it to infer that no physical event in artificial hardware could support coupling. What survives is the narrower statement that, on a pre-decoherence interpretation, abstract classical computation supplies nothing appropriate for consciousness to select among. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/)

There is also an unresolved contradiction inside the revised source. Its general argument acknowledges that the relevant interface conditions are prior framework commitments and that satisfying every functional indicator would not settle the phenomenality question. Yet its tenet-alignment section still says silicon excludes the necessary interface by design. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/machine-question/)

A supportable catalogue thesis would be:

> Present classical AI architectures do not instantiate any currently specified Map interface and are therefore poor candidates for bidirectionally efficacious consciousness; a substrate-level impossibility result has not been established.

That is considerably weaker than a principled impossibility for computation.

**Verdict:** the target summary is stale; the source article also needs an internal consistency pass.

## 3. Tenet-protective bracketing

### 3.1 Entry 27 turns self-concealment into an evidential asymmetry

The conditional part of the self-concealing-interface thesis is coherent. Given exact Born preservation, no-signalling, conservation and compatibility with the existing aggregate record, ordinary bulk measurements will not expose the proposed influence. The problem begins when the catalogue says that concealment is **“derived, not assumed”** and then redirects evidential attention toward dissociations, patient variation, channel failures and first-person/third-person mismatches. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/apex-articles/)

Derivation from the tenets establishes only:

> If this version of the Map is true, its interface will be aggregate-invisible.

It does not establish:

> Therefore the heterogeneous boundary phenomena listed by the Map are evidence that the interface exists.

That second inference requires at least a comparative likelihood claim:

P(seam pattern∣Map)>P(seam pattern∣best physical rival).

No such model is supplied. Predictive processing, global access, metacognitive reconstruction, lesion topology, pharmacological network dynamics and report limitations all predict dissociations at roughly these boundaries. Entry 24’s own source provides a worked example of such physical absorption. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/phenomenal-output-causal-machinery-dissociation/)

The resulting evidential regime is asymmetric:

- Aggregate null results are expected because the interface is concealed.
- Local dissociations are potentially supportive because they occur at interfaces.
- Physical explanations of those dissociations are said not to address why experience exists.

That permits almost any empirical result to remain compatible with the framework. Compatibility is not yet immunisation, but it becomes immunisation when the theory supplies no quantitative observation that should be less likely if it is true.

The site’s own mechanism-debt article makes this explicit. It says no worked Hamiltonian, master equation or computational simulation currently exists and that the framework’s token-level efficacy plus ensemble invisibility remains a stipulation rather than a demonstration. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/)

**Verdict:** the concealment result is a valid internal consequence of the strict corridor. The claim that it identifies a positive evidential route is tenet-protective until the Map gives preregistered seam predictions, rival likelihoods and outcomes that would lower its credence.

### 3.2 Entry 16 is contradicted by entry 34

Entry 16 says the Map is developing a **“precise specification”** of how consciousness influences matter. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/apex-articles/)

Entry 34 says the framework’s deepest outstanding debt is explaining how selection can do causal work while preserving Born statistics, and explicitly describes itself as a research agenda rather than a solution. The source states that there is no worked formal model, no selector law, no established intervention structure and no completed amplification bridge. [The Unfinishable Map+2The Unfinishable Map+2](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/apex-articles/)

The six lines of inquiry in entry 16 provide:

- desiderata;
- candidate locations for influence;
- constraints;
- conceptual distinctions;
- possible failure modes.

They do not yet specify a mechanism in the usual scientific sense. A specification would need, minimally, a state space, dynamics, selector variable, coupling rule, intervention semantics, no-signalling proof and empirical consequence.

Calling this a specification prematurely converts programme organisation into apparent mechanistic achievement.

**Verdict:** rename it the *Interface-Constraint Programme* or the *Interface-Specification Research Programme*. Reserve “specification” without qualification for a worked model.

## 4. Empirical and bibliographic audit

### 4.1 Entry 23: phenomenal variation does not preserve architecture and performance without qualification

The catalogue compresses the aphantasia and synaesthesia evidence into **“same neural architecture”**, accompanied by a general claim of preserved task performance. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/apex-articles/)

The cited literature does not support those slogans at the relevant grain:

- Milton et al. found equivalent performance on standard memory tests, but also marked group differences in autobiographical memory, imagination, resting-state connectivity and task-related activation. [OUP Academic](https://academic.oup.com/cercorcomms/article/2/2/tgab035/6265046)
- Rouw and Scholte found greater coherent white-matter structure in grapheme-colour synaesthetes and differences between synaesthesia subtypes. [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/nn1906)
- The apex source itself concedes that a finer-grained functionalist can individuate the relevant functions more narrowly and demand covariance at the disputed neural grain. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/phenomenal-variation-within-a-species/)

The evidence therefore pressures a **coarse task-role identity claim**: selected behavioural tests can be preserved despite substantial phenomenal variation. It does not establish unchanged neural architecture, unchanged strategy or general functional equivalence.

The integration record is also inconsistent. The status says alexithymia was added as a fifth divergence class and acknowledges that recent evidence may relocate the apparent deficit from phenomenality to report or metacognition. But `concepts/alexithymia` is absent from the displayed source list. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/apex-articles/) That omission matters because the alexithymia case weakens, rather than straightforwardly extends, the phenomenal-without-functional-difference pattern.

**Verdict:** real literature, over-generalised conclusion. Replace “same architecture/performance” with “overlapping competence under selected measures despite task-specific behavioural, strategic and neural differences.”

### 4.2 Entry 26: pharmacological dissociations constrain simple identities but do not favour interactionism

The pharmacological sources are genuine, and the broad empirical contrasts are real. But the catalogue’s description makes them appear more metaphysically diagnostic than they are.

Franks et al. genuinely identified potent xenon inhibition of NMDA-receptor channels. Sarasso et al. studied 18 healthy volunteers and found low-complexity responses with propofol and xenon, but wake-like complexity and subsequently reported vivid dreams under ketamine despite behavioural unresponsiveness. [Nature+2ORBi+2](https://www.nature.com/articles/24525)

That supports two important conclusions:

- behavioural unresponsiveness is not equivalent to unconsciousness;
- different anaesthetics produce different network and experiential profiles.

It does not support a nonphysical interface. Two agents both involving an NMDA receptor are not thereby the same mechanism: binding sites, affinities, polypharmacology, dosage, circuit effects and downstream dynamics can differ.

The Hu et al. result is also mis-scaled in the catalogue status. It found a common KCC2-related emergence mechanism across diverse anaesthetics **in mice**, operationalised through recovery from a minimum responsive state. It was not a human study of reported experience and did not observe an interface being reopened. [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-023-01290-y)

The strongest justified conclusion is that anaesthesia is not governed by a one-receptor/one-state identity and that behavioural responsiveness is an unreliable proxy for experience. Contemporary physicalist neuroscience expects both findings.

The catalogue verbally preserves a constrain-versus-establish distinction, but then adds cumulative force from neurology, clinical dissociation and contemplative pathology without showing that these lines are probabilistically independent or unexpected under physical rivals. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/apex-articles/)

**Verdict:** the empirical observations hold; the alleged distinctiveness of the evidence pattern does not.

### 4.3 Entry 33: the principal study reports a shared neural signature

Entry 33 argues that modality-specific brain machinery contrasts with a common supramodal conscious boundary, and its status describes the result as if there were no shared brain-side substrate. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/apex-articles/)

Sanchez et al. found common, temporally late and widespread **neural** patterns of conscious access across tactile, visual and auditory tasks, including activity in primary sensory regions unrelated to the current task. The authors interpret the result in terms of widespread broadcasting and multisensory or top-down neural dynamics. [PubMed Central (PMC)+1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7132110/)

That evidence supports:

- early modality-specific sensory processing;
- later supramodal neural access dynamics.

It cannot locate a mind-side boundary, because the measured variable is MEG activity. More importantly, late widespread broadcasting is a direct prediction of global-neuronal-workspace models. The finding may be compatible with an interface, but its most immediate explanatory home is neural.

**Verdict:** the cross-modal asymmetry is worth synthesising, but the study is evidence for supramodal brain-side processing, not for a separate supramodal mind-side mechanism.

### 4.4 Entry 36: one paper is materially misread and one quotation is probably misattributed

Entry 36 rests partly on the claim that recursive processing requires conscious access and records Vadillo et al. 2026 among its already verified references. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/apex-articles/)

The Vadillo paper was a 19-laboratory Registered Report with 531 participants. It found above-chance performance on unseen trials, a significant above-chance intercept after modelling perceptual sensitivity, and concluded that working memory can operate on unconscious representations, although performance remains associated with perceptual sensitivity. [King's College London+1](https://kclpure.kcl.ac.uk/portal/en/publications/replicating-the-unconscious-working-memory-effect-a-multisite-reg/)

The Map’s discussion instead foregrounds the abstract’s introductory statement that decisive demonstrations had previously been lacking and treats that scepticism as strengthening the consciousness requirement. Its own pessimistic review documents this manoeuvre. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/reviews/pessimistic-2026-06-22b-language-recursion-and-consciousness/)

Vadillo et al. do not demonstrate unconscious recursive syntactic parsing. But they directly undercut a categorical premise that working-memory operation requires conscious access. The paper should be treated as adverse or at least mixed evidence, not supportive evidence.

There is also a likely quotation misattribution. The Recursion Void attributes the sentence beginning “the limits of recursive thinking may lie…” to Stiller and Dunbar 2007. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/recursion-void/) Their paper concerns how perspective-taking and memory capacity predict social-network size. [ORA+1](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid%3A12dc0429-ecf5-4dda-be03-ee981a8b8958) The same sentence appears verbatim in Jonathan Redshaw’s 2024 article on the recursive grammar of mental time travel. [PubMed Central (PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11606512/) Unless the site can produce the relevant Stiller–Dunbar full-text span, the quotation should be reassigned to Redshaw.

The cluster also conflates four materially different constructs:

1. syntactic embedding;
2. nested intentionality or theory of mind;
3. working-memory maintenance and manipulation;
4. phenomenal rather than merely access consciousness.

A fifth-order limit in mental-state attribution is not, without further argument, a fifth-order ceiling on syntactic recursion. Nor does evidence that a task requires global access establish that phenomenal consciousness constitutes the processing. The claim that aphasia removes an expressive channel but never its conscious “user” is a philosophical interpretation of aphasic dissociation, not an empirical result.

**Verdict:** this is the clearest citation-integrity failure in the sampled catalogue. The references exist, but “verified set” is not an accurate description of their present use.

## 5. Missed or inadequately tested counterarguments

### 5.1 Entry 3 assumes that genuine authorship requires quantum selection

Entry 3 states that genuine authorship requires consciousness to select among quantum possibilities. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/apex-articles/) That is one possible libertarian theory of agency, not a demonstrated necessary condition.

At least three established alternatives need direct comparison:

- Fischer and Ravizza ground responsibility in guidance control exercised through an agent’s own moderately reasons-responsive mechanism, without requiring alternative possibilities or quantum selection. [Cambridge University Press+1](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/responsibility-and-control/54D0EB8AEDEF4D5F4930D691EC214E01/listing)
- Kane’s libertarian account grounds ultimate responsibility in self-forming actions, but does not entail the Map’s consciousness-collapse interface. [OUP Academic+1](https://academic.oup.com/book/8487/chapter-abstract/154315541)
- O’Connor develops agent causation through personal causal capacities, again without requiring the Map’s particular quantum mechanism. [OUP Academic](https://academic.oup.com/book/7568/chapter-abstract/152565343)

The relevant burden is not to show that quantum indeterminacy leaves room for agency. It is to show why reasons-responsive compatibilism, non-quantum event-causal libertarianism and non-quantum agent causation all fail, and why the Map’s selector solves problems of luck, control and reasons-sensitivity that those views do not.

### 5.2 Entry 17 treats materialist ethical failure as established

Entry 17 says materialist ethics cannot provide the unification of value, responsibility, normativity and moral perception that dualism supplies. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/apex-articles/)

That requires something approaching an impossibility argument. The catalogue does not engage sufficiently with:

- Railton-style naturalistic moral realism;
- contemporary reductive accounts of robust normativity;
- constructivist accounts of normative authority;
- nonreductive physicalist moral realism;
- reasons-responsive theories of responsibility.

Railton’s moral realism is a longstanding programme rather than an uncited possibility. More importantly for citation currency, Copp’s 2025 treatment explicitly develops reductive naturalistic analyses intended to answer the objection that naturalism cannot explain robust normativity. [PDCNet+2OUP Academic+2](https://www.pdcnet.org/phr/content/phr_1986_0095_0002_0163_0207)

The Map may argue that these theories incur explanatory costs, fail to capture phenomenology, or leave normativity insufficiently authoritative. It cannot simply infer that a dualist architecture unifies four topics and therefore materialist ethics cannot.

### 5.3 Entry 24’s strongest rival is absent from the catalogue summary

The underlying article now gives predictive-processing introspection and reality monitoring a serious hearing. The catalogue summary does not communicate that the central dissociation is an antecedent prediction of the strongest rival. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/phenomenal-output-causal-machinery-dissociation/)

This omission matters because the index is a reader’s high-level epistemic map. A thesis summary that presents convergence as evidential while omitting the theory that predicts the convergence gives a systematically misleading impression even when the full article is better calibrated.

## 6. Weak integration and governance drift

### 6.1 The page mixes incompatible functions

The document combines:

- reader-facing thesis summaries;
- source lists;
- word counts;
- AI provenance;
- review histories;
- queue and cap decisions;
- implementation checks;
- governance notes.

This makes the epistemic status of a thesis difficult to distinguish from the operational history of creating it. A reader should not have to infer from a several-hundred-word status log whether a claim is independently evidenced, framework-relative or merely an approved research direction.

### 6.2 Superseded cap language remains in individual entries

The guidelines say the former numerical cap has been superseded by a qualitative synthesis threshold. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/apex-articles/) Yet statuses for entries 25 and 26 still report being within or beyond an informal cap. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/apex-articles/) This is minor philosophically but diagnostic of incomplete governance propagation.

### 6.3 The applied-index metadata is stale

The A1 entry still says methodology position clusters are to be determined. The same page’s A7 entry already cites P-M1, P-M2, P-M4 and P-M5 as live methodology positions. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/apex-articles/)

### 6.4 Entries 6 and 35 require an organism-scope reconciliation

Entry 6 treats non-linguistic consciousness in animals and simple organisms as evidence that experience is more fundamental than sophisticated cognition. Entry 35 says cognitive-grade competence occurs across the whole tree without felt experience on the Map’s framework. Its status acknowledges that entry 6 runs the opposite thesis but declares that the two do not conflict. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/apex-articles/)

They can be reconciled, but only with explicit scope:

- Which organisms are said probably to be conscious?
- Which merely display cognition insufficient for consciousness attribution?
- Is fish consciousness affirmed, left open or denied?
- What substrate feature changes the verdict between bacteria, placozoans, fish and organoids?

Entry 35’s body discipline appears more cautious than its headline: it often withholds phenomenality rather than proving its absence. The catalogue thesis should preserve that distinction.

### 6.5 Verification currently checks the wrong levels

The catalogue frequently says citations were reused from an already verified set. The sampled failures show that at least three different checks are needed:

1. **Bibliographic existence:** does the work exist with the stated metadata?
2. **Quotation fidelity:** do the quoted words occur in that work?
3. **Inferential fidelity:** does the work support the role assigned to it?

The sampled bibliography largely passes level 1. The likely Stiller–Dunbar error fails level 2. Vadillo, Sanchez, Milton and Hu are principally level-3 failures.

The 20 August changelog itself reports correction of a fabricated internal quotation and an empirical detail that had previously rested on an unverified source. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/) This shows that the site can catch such failures, but also that “previously verified” cannot be treated as a permanent source property.

## 7. What survives scrutiny

Several components should be preserved.

The source article behind entry 24 now offers an unusually serious physicalist rival, acknowledges the mainstream null prediction, identifies possible counterexamples and reduces its claimed independent evidential base. [The Unfinishable Map+2The Unfinishable Map+2](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/phenomenal-output-causal-machinery-dissociation/)

Entry 34 and its source state the Born-preserving causal-efficacy problem with appropriate severity. They admit that no worked model exists, specify what a toy model would need to show and identify outcomes that would force the framework to change. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/)

Entry 35 explicitly labels its verdict framework-relative and recognises that its convergence is partly generated by shared premises. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/apex-articles/)

A7 contains the right methodological principles: separate external evidence from framework readings, discount common-source convergence, do not upgrade from defeater removal, and test whether declared safeguards are actually enforced. [The Unfinishable Map+2The Unfinishable Map+2](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/judging-the-map-as-science/)

The central editorial problem is therefore not absence of methodological understanding. It is failure to propagate that understanding into the catalogue’s headline claims.

## Overall assessment

The catalogue is useful as an internal planning and provenance record, but it is not currently reliable as a reader-facing map of the evidential state of the apex layer.

The 13 August modification improved entry 24’s attributional accuracy. It did not repair the underlying independence problem. Since then, entries 7 and 15 have become materially stale, while entries 16 and 34 make incompatible claims about the maturity of the mechanism programme.

The sampled primary-source audit found no evidence of pervasive invented bibliography. It did find a recurrent and more philosophically consequential pattern:

> Real source → legitimate empirical observation → over-strong metaphysical role.

That pattern is especially clear in phenomenal variation, pharmacological dissociation, supramodal access and working-memory research. The empirical work often constrains crude identity theories or behavioural proxies. It rarely distinguishes interactionist dualism from sophisticated physicalist accounts.

The appropriate disposition is **major revision**, centred not on adding more citations but on recalibrating evidence roles, synchronising source changes and identifying actual discriminators.

## Improvements to site methodology

1. Introduce claim-level provenance labels: **external evidence**, **framework entailment**, **compatibility**, **constraint**, **defeater removal**, **independent support** and **speculative extension**.
2. Make catalogue/source dependency checking a build gate. A substantive source revision should block publication of an unreviewed apex summary or display an automatic stale badge.
3. Separate citation audits into bibliographic existence, quotation fidelity and inferential fidelity. Passing one layer must not automatically mark a source “verified.”
4. Store every direct quotation with an immutable source span, edition identifier and verification date. Re-run quotation checks whenever the citing paragraph or source assignment changes.
5. Require an independence ledger for every convergence argument. Entries should identify shared upstream mechanisms, shared samples, framework-selected cases and sources that count only as corroboration.
6. Prohibit the phrase “evidence for the Map” unless the article names a result that is more probable under the Map than under at least one fully specified rival.
7. Require every self-concealment or empirical-equivalence claim to state what observations could nevertheless lower the framework’s credence.
8. Add a tenet-ablation section to each apex: what remains of the argument when each relevant tenet is removed, and which conclusions are generated solely by reinstating it.
9. Use independent adversarial reviewers who are not instructed to preserve the five-tenet package, and record whether recommended corrections changed only rhetoric, changed evidential status or altered a position’s confidence band.
10. Maintain versioned errata propagation: when a citation, quotation or interpretation is corrected, automatically locate every apex, topic, concept, void and research note that inherited it.
11. Treat source reuse as a liability as well as an efficiency. A reused citation should inherit its previous verification date and role, but a new inferential role must trigger fresh verification.
12. Move operational success claims such as link resolution, word-limit compliance and slug checks out of epistemic status records; these demonstrate publishing hygiene, not argumentative support.
