# Hostile Adversarial Referee Report — `voids/edge-states-and-void-probes`

## PART 1 — TL;DR VERDICT

**Verdict: REVISE-HARD.** This is one of the more epistemically disciplined articles in the voids corpus — it operationalizes its central distinction, comes down on the deflationary horn, and explicitly refuses to treat convergent phenomenology as evidence for dualism. It nonetheless fails the apex bar on three drivers:

1. **A live citation-metadata failure at the load-bearing psychedelic-belief-revision claim.** The Zeifman REBAS reference is dated and numbered wrongly (given as "2023, *Scientific Reports* 13, 3083"). The work of record, confirmed via PubMed/PMC, is "Zeifman RJ, Spriggs MJ, Kettner H… *Sci Rep.* 2025 Jan 29;**15(1):3651**. doi: 10.1038/s41598-023-28111-3" (received 2022-11-09, accepted 2023-01-12, collection date 2025). The volume and article number as printed correspond to no real object; only the DOI is correct. Two further citations (Sjöstedt-Hughes 2024; Kutnyy 2024) are unverifiable against any publisher-of-record index, and the Gładziejewski entry mixes a 2023 date with 2025 volume/pagination.
2. **Tenet leakage and an epistemic-to-metaphysical slide in the "Bidirectional Interaction" subsection**, which asserts that deliberately approaching the edge "demonstrates consciousness influencing physical processes" and that "the causal arrow runs from intention to neural state." This is question-begging: on physicalism the intention *is* a neural state, so the observation is fully accommodated and demonstrates nothing tenet-relevant. This is the one place the article abandons the calibration it maintains everywhere else. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/edge-states-and-void-probes/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/edge-states-and-void-probes/)
3. **Non-engagement of the current framework-level predictive-processing rivals as theories of the phenomenology.** REBUS/ALBUS are engaged well as edge-*mapping* mechanisms, and Metzinger's self-model reading is given fair play, but the active-inference *theories of consciousness* that now own this territory — above all Laukkonen, Friston & Chandaria (2025), "A beautiful loop: An active inference theory of consciousness," *Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews* 176:106296 (DOI 10.1016/j.neubiorev.2025.106296), which explicitly claims to explain "altered states like meditation, psychedelics, and minimal states" — plus Seth's controlled-hallucination/beast-machine, Clark, and Hohwy, are absent. On the site's own most consequential documented blind spot, that is a defect.

The article does **not** exhibit the site's dominant pathologies in acute form: confession-without-correction is largely avoided (the residual-transcendence reply is given a binding status change, not a credential), calibration asymmetry is near-absent (deflation is applied at least as hard to the dualist reading), and coherence inflation is explicitly disavowed. Those virtues are stated here only to locate the verdict, not to soften it.

## PART 2 — DIMENSIONAL ANALYSIS

### Axis 1 — Unfalsifiability of the central distinction

The central distinction — "genuine epistemic access to territories closed to ordinary consciousness" (transcendence) versus "approach cognitive limits without crossing them" (edge-mapping) — is the right place to press, because at the level of first-person report both horns predict identical output: a subject reporting contact with something beyond conceptual reach is exactly what edge-mapping predicts and exactly what transcendence predicts. If the article stopped there it would be unfalsifiable. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/edge-states-and-void-probes/)

It does not stop there. The section "Transcendence or Edge-Mapping? What Would Distinguish" proposes four operationalizations: (i) **convergence scope** — transcendence predicts propositional convergence across traditions, edge-mapping predicts phenomenological-but-not-propositional convergence; (ii) **practical predictions** — genuine access should sometimes yield information ordinary consciousness could not predict; (iii) **intersubjective verification** — genuine access should permit triangulation; (iv) implicitly, novelty of yield. It then reports the state of the evidence ("phenomenological convergence is robust, propositional convergence limited, practical yield therapeutic but not clearly epistemically novel, intersubjective verification elusive") and concludes "This fits edge-mapping." This is real epistemic work: the distinction is operationalized and adjudicated, and it lands on the deflationary horn. [unfinishablemap + 3](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/edge-states-and-void-probes/)

Crucially, the article then confronts the unfalsifiable residue directly and **binds** it. The residual reply — "genuine transcendence might be noncommunicable in principle and so leave no propositional or intersubjective trace" — is marked, using the expiration-condition discipline the article imports from `apophatic-cartography`, as predicting "nothing the edge-mapping reading does not," and therefore as "a metaphysical possibility the evidence cannot exclude rather than a hypothesis the evidence has upgraded." That is a genuine status change, not a rhetorical credential. **This is the opposite of confession-without-correction:** the fatal move is named *and demoted to inert*, in contrast to the site-wide pattern where the confession becomes the credential. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/edge-states-and-void-probes/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/edge-states-and-void-probes/)

The **fourth-screen** concern — does this article add, to the site-wide triple-screen conjunction (post-decoherence selection's "empirical invisibility" + amplification's "structural untraceability" + agency's "verification circularity"), a fourth screen consisting of an edge-state "by construction unable to certify its own reach"? — is worth stating precisely, because the raw material for such a screen is present in the "Dissolving-Insight Phenomenon" section (insights "clear in the moment of arising and unreconstructable moments later"). But the article does **not** deploy it as an unfalsifiability shield for dualism. It treats the dissolving-insight datum as "genuinely ambiguous between contact with representationally closed territory and a misfiring feeling-of-knowing," and gives the deflationary Koriat noetic-feelings reading the stronger hand ("which explains the asymmetry the confabulation story leaves open"). So the article manufactures a candidate fourth screen and then declines to weaponize it. That is to its credit; the residual risk is only that a downstream reader could lift the "representationally closed territory" language out of context. [The Unfinishable Map + 3](https://unfinishablemap.org/reviews/outer-review-2026-06-01-claude-opus-4-8/)

**Where Axis 1 still fails:** the "Bidirectional Interaction" subsection (see Axis 4) reintroduces an unfalsifiable-because-question-begging claim after the main argument has been honestly settled. The body of the article earns a deflationary verdict; the tenet-facing coda partially spends it.

### Axis 3 — Overclaim and over-concession mapping

**Constrain-vs-establish gate.** The article passes this gate at the level it matters most. Its summative statement — "The combined evidence… is *consistent with* consciousness as something over and above neural processing, not *support for* it" — is exactly the discipline the gate demands. It goes further than any other voids article checked: "edge-phenomenology does not even isolate dualism among *non-physicalist* options," naming panpsychism, neutral monism, and Metzinger's naturalism as equally accommodating, and concluding "The edge-state data fit dualism; they do not select it." This is a near-textbook refusal of the constrain-to-establish slide. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/edge-states-and-void-probes/)

**Epistemic-to-metaphysical slide.** The body polices this ("distinguishing *structural* discovery… from speculative *content* discovery"). The one clear violation is in the tenet coda (Axis 4): the inference from "meditators train attention in ways that alter brain function" to "consciousness influencing physical processes" / "the causal arrow runs from intention to neural state." That is a slide from an observation neutral between frameworks to a tenet-laden metaphysical conclusion. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/edge-states-and-void-probes/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/edge-states-and-void-probes/)

**Evidential-independence test.** The article *itself* runs and passes this test, which is unusual. It states that the neuroimaging, metabolic, modelling, dose-response, and belief-revision findings "are facets of one explanatory pattern, not independent confirmations of the filter," and, in the dualism coda, that "a common cause is the more parsimonious explanation of a shared signature, and naming one removes the warrant to count the cases separately." So the meditation/psychedelic/NDE strands are explicitly *not* treated as three independent lines; the recurring ineffability trope is acknowledged as a single phenomenological signature appearing across methods. This is the correct handling and forecloses the evidential-independence failure the axis is designed to catch. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/edge-states-and-void-probes/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/edge-states-and-void-probes/)

**Calibration asymmetry.** Near-absent, and arguably reversed. Skepticism is applied at least as stringently to the dualist/transcendence reading as to the physicalist one: the metabolic-hyperfrontality datum is turned against the filter reading; the confabulation-vs-Koriat contest is resolved toward the deflationary Koriat account; NDEs are demoted to "the weakest leg"; each of the three post-2019 NDE findings is read as tilting "toward the edge-mapping and dying-brain production readings." If anything the article risks *over-concession* rather than asymmetric credulity. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/edge-states-and-void-probes/)

**Over-concession / performative inoculation.** The aggregate rhetorical effect of so much honest concession is to relocate the dualist case off-page: "The Map's dualism is carried by its tenets and arguments made elsewhere, not by treating the convergent phenomenology as evidence that points uniquely its way." Read charitably this is exactly right and is the article doing its job. Read adversarially it is a **tenet-insulation manoeuvre**: by conceding that the phenomenology does not support dualism, the article immunizes the tenets from the failure of this evidence class — the concession functions to protect the commitment it appears to weaken. This is not a citation-level performative inoculation, but it is a structural one, and it should be named. The honest version would add: *if* the convergent phenomenology is not evidence for dualism, the article should say what, if anything, in this domain *would* count for or against it — and the four discriminating tests it already lists are the natural place to anchor that. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/edge-states-and-void-probes/)

**Coherence inflation.** Explicitly disavowed ("To claim a net cost to physicalism, one would have to specify the extra machinery… No such accounting is available"). PASS. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/edge-states-and-void-probes/)

### Axis 4 — Tenet coherence and voids-taxonomy fit

**Constitutional-attractor effect.** Weak here. A constitutional-attractor artifact would be a "void" that exists only because the five tenets were read back into neutral phenomenological data. But the article denies that the phenomenology selects the tenets, so the void is not doing covert tenet-confirmation work. The convergent-ineffability datum is a genuine feature of the literature (James, Stace, Katz all address it), not a tenet projection. The attractor pull is visible only in the apophatic framing ("the noetic quality… reflects genuine contact with the structure of limits") and in the tenet coda. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/edge-states-and-void-probes/)

**Tenet leakage.** Present and concentrated in the "Bidirectional Interaction" subsection. The claim that approaching the edge "demonstrates consciousness influencing physical processes" imports Tenet 3 as a *premise* while the article's stance elsewhere is neutral inquiry. On physicalism, "intention → neural state" is intra-physical causation; citing it as evidence for mind→physical causation begs the question. This sentence should be reworded to the article's own standard (consistent-with, not demonstrates).

**Do Tenets 2 and 3 do real work, or are they decorative?** Tenet 2 (Minimal Quantum Interaction) is **decorative and self-admittedly so**: "If such constraints involve collapse-inducing measurement, relaxing them might preserve quantum coherence at the interface—speculative, but connects edge-phenomenology to the Map's quantum framework." This does no argumentative work and the article flags it as speculative. Tenet 3 (Bidirectional Interaction) does *negative* work — it is the site of the article's one clear slide (above). Neither earns its place; both could be cut with no loss to the argument and a gain in coherence. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/edge-states-and-void-probes/)

**Predictive processing / active inference as framework-level rival.** This is the axis on which the article most clearly falls short of the apex bar, though less badly than typical corpus articles. It engages PP substantively as an *edge-mapping mechanism* (REBUS, ALBUS/SEBUS, REBAS) and gives Metzinger's self-model reading of MPE a fair and even favoured hearing. What it does **not** do is engage the active-inference *theories of consciousness* that are the current framework-level rivals to the dualist reading of edge phenomenology: above all **Laukkonen, Friston & Chandaria (2025), "A beautiful loop: An active inference theory of consciousness," *Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews* 176:106296 (DOI 10.1016/j.neubiorev.2025.106296)**, whose three conditions (world-model simulation with "an epistemic field," "inferential competition… Bayesian binding," and "epistemic depth") are explicitly advanced to explain "altered states like meditation, psychedelics, and minimal states" — precisely this article's subject — and which is conspicuously missing; plus **Seth's controlled-hallucination / beast-machine**, **Clark's predictive mind**, and **Hohwy's predictive processing**. These are not residual mop-up rivals; on the zero-person perspective and edge-clarity they are the primary competitors, and a physicalist active-inference account of "awareness persisting when content and self-model are subtracted" is exactly the rival the article's own Metzinger discussion gestures at but does not develop into engagement with the 2025 literature. Treat this as a major (though not fatal) defect.

**Voids-taxonomy fit and distinctness.** The classification is under-specified: the article does not state which of the three (unexplored / unexplorable / occluded) categories the edge-states void occupies, unlike neighbouring voids that declare their category explicitly. More seriously, the article risks being an **umbrella redescription** of neighbouring voids rather than a distinct void: it links to and overlaps `the-silence-void` (cessation), `ineffable-encounter-void` (aesthetic/meaning surplus), `transit-void` (dissolving insights), `noetic-feelings-void` (Koriat), and `apophatic-cartography` (edge-phenomenology as boundary data), and much of its content is a synthesis of theirs. The distinctness claim needs to be made explicit: what does the edge-states void assert that the union of its neighbours does not? The plausible answer — that it is the *cross-method convergence* claim itself, the meta-observation that multiple independent edges produce one signature — is present but never stated as the void's individuating thesis. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/edge-states-and-void-probes/)

## PART 3 — BOTTOM-LINE VERDICT

**REVISE-HARD.** Neither DELETE nor DEMOTE-TO-COHERENCE-ONLY is warranted, and I considered both. DELETE fails because the article does genuine, non-redundant deflationary work: it operationalizes a distinction most of the literature leaves gestural, adjudicates it against current primary sources, and reaches a defensible edge-mapping verdict. DEMOTE-TO-COHERENCE-ONLY fails because the article does not rest its case on internal coherence — it makes empirical claims that, with the Zeifman exception, check out against primary sources, and it explicitly disavows coherence inflation. The defects are correctable in place: two-to-four citation fixes, excision of a decorative tenet subsection, repair of one question-begging sentence, addition of three-to-four rival citations, and an explicit distinctness/classification statement.

**Calibration against the benchmarks.** Apex #34 (`born-preserving-causal-efficacy`) is characterized by an explicit, binding falsification condition (a confirmed brain-context Born-rule deviation would refute the framework), a self-imposed downgrade of the core thesis to "framework-internal coherence result, not established mental causation," and visible self-correction of an in-house slogan against the technical literature (the no-signalling claim corrected against Galley–Masanes 2018 and Barrett 2007). Apex #33 (`cross-modal-capability-division`, with its `capability-division-in-vision` sibling) is characterized by head-on, individually-sectioned engagement with named physicalist rivals (three responses plus illusionism/GWT/HOT) and repeated "constrains, does not establish / live hypothesis, not settled fact" discipline. (Note on method: the two apex bodies could not be fetched directly under the access constraints; this calibration draws on their tightly-coupled sibling pages — `type-token-causation`, `ensemble-level-epiphenomenalism`, `blindsight`, `visual-consciousness` — and on verbatim snippets, and is stated at that confidence.)
