## (a) TL;DR

**Headline verdict: REVISE-HARD across the site.** The Map is a very well-made machine for keeping track of its own debts. It is not a demonstration that interactionist dualism is true, or even coherent beyond stipulation.

- **The mechanism’s own flagship apex admits it may do no work.** [https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/) concedes that under its preferred route (trumping), “‘consciousness is a cause’ and ‘consciousness is epiphenomenal’ make no predictively distinct claims at any scale.”

- It also concedes that keeping Born statistics exact under intervention requires an unwritten second law: “a companion law over the distribution of conscious reasons, and the Map has never written one.”
- The tenets page still says of the quantum indeterminacy: “that’s where consciousness acts.” [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)
- That gap is the defining case of confession-without-correction. Tenet 3’s “outcome-selection” commitment should be DEMOTED to coherence-only until a toy model exists. The register already admits no toy model exists (P-Q10). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/quantum-interface/)
- **The site claims the outcome-biasing that Stapp explicitly rejected.**

- The live [https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/stapp-quantum-mind/](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/stapp-quantum-mind/) now discloses this correctly. It quotes Stapp as “thoroughly committed to strict adherence to the orthodox statistical rules, in contrast to Eccles’s suggestion that these laws are biased by mental effort.” [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/stapp-quantum-mind/)
- Disclosure removes a citation fault but exposes a gap in the lineage. The Map’s core mechanism is Eccles–Beck moved past decoherence, with no worked-out physical backer. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/reviews/outer-review-2026-09-14-claude-opus-5/)
- Two earlier remediation targets now have an apex: Tenet 4 has [https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/one-world-wager/](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/one-world-wager/) **[secondary]**, and predictive processing / active inference is engaged as a named rival. But that engagement sits only in topics and concept pages, not in an apex built on the rival-holding pattern of apex #33. [unfinishablemap +2](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/phenomenology-of-anticipation/)
- **The site has grown faster than it can keep itself consistent.**

- The changelog records topics at 366/370, concepts at 368/370 and voids at 113/125, with many articles at or over their hard word ceilings. Corrections are now financed by word-neutral trims.
- The site’s reviews state apex totals of 19, 38 and 39 at different dates, and the apex index file itself is “already over hard” **[secondary]**.
- The coherence it claims is wider than the coherence it has maintained.

### (1) Novel inferences the site’s commitments imply but has not drawn

1. **Born-preservation plus value-sensitive selection implies an “outcome-luck ledger” that is a hidden moral externality.**

- The apex states the general version: “a compensating tilt toward worse outcomes for someone or somewhen else.” [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/)
- It has not been carried through to the applied apexes. [https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/moral-architecture-of-consciousness/](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/moral-architecture-of-consciousness/) and [https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/living-with-the-map/](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/living-with-the-map/) promise that you “genuinely influence” the quality of experience. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/)
- Under exact marginal preservation without per-agent preservation, each agent’s successful value-tilting is offset by an equal and opposite tilt elsewhere. Moral effort becomes zero-sum across the population.
- So either each agent’s conditional policy is separately Born-preserving, which pushes toward horn (b) for each individual, or the Map’s ethics carries a transfer it has not costed.
- This should be a registered position. It is currently a sentence in one apex.
2. **Psychophysical superselection is a restriction on what can be intended.**

- The apex grants that preservation under intervention needs a law making some populations of intentions “physically admissible” and others not. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/)
- Combined with Tenet 3’s free-will grounding, this means the Map’s libertarian freedom is bounded by a statistical law over intentions that no agent can see. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)
- That is a compatibilist-shaped constraint at the population level, which the agency cluster (the tenets page’s “substance-leaning sub-reading”) has not reconciled. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)
3. **Tenet 4 plus corridor selection implies a non-standard decision theory.**

- Without branches, Born weights are objective single-case chances.
- If consciousness tilts the token outcome while conditionals on intention are “free” (P-Q2: “only conditionals on the conscious state itself are free”), the agent’s own credence about an outcome, given its own intention, should differ from Born. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/quantum-interface/)
- The Map therefore owes an “evidential-chance” rule: how an agent should set credences about its own selections. Everettian decision theory (Sebens & Carroll 2018, cited on the tenets page) is the rival it must beat on that ground. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)
- The site attacks Everettian probability but supplies no first-person probability rule of its own. This is the probability counterpart of the “symmetric debt” the one-world apex reportedly concedes **[secondary]**. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/reviews/optimistic-2026-08-21-restraint-apparatus/)
4. **Tenet 5 applied to itself undercuts the evidence ladder.**

- The tenets page says parsimony “lacks universal epistemic warrant”, yet the Map ranks mechanisms by “methodological preference ordering on tenet-fit”, and Tenet 2’s minimality is presented as constraint-respecting. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)
- The ordering “post-decoherence selection ahead of the coherence-dependent proposals” is justified by fewer commitments, which is a parsimony ranking. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)
- Tenet 5 therefore entails that P-Q1’s preference is no more than tie-breaking. P-Q1’s own band rationale concedes “bounded Occam / Tenet 5 licenses humility rather than a firm preference ordering.” [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/quantum-interface/)
- The consequence the site has not drawn: compatible-tier mechanism candidates cannot be ranked at all without a discriminating datum. P-Q1 should be merged into a flat candidate set.
5. **Bi-aspectual ontology plus objective-reduction baseline means consciousness is not the actuality aspect.**

- [https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/bi-aspectual-ontology/](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/bi-aspectual-ontology/) defines actuality as “what makes one possibility real”, then concedes “elsewhere physical objective reduction fixes outcomes without it.” [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/bi-aspectual-ontology/)
- So actuality is mostly supplied by a physical process. Consciousness is at best a local modulator of one instance of the actuality role, not the second aspect of reality.
- The ontology’s headline (“structure (physics) and actuality (consciousness)”) is therefore false by the article’s own concession and should be restated as a conjecture about a sub-role. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/bi-aspectual-ontology/)
6. **The Born-rule derivation the Map relies on sets a ceiling on it.**

- Torres Alegre’s arXiv abstract proves that “the only such relationship consistent with no-signaling is the identity” between geometric transition probability and predictive probability. “Any strictly convex or concave deviation from linearity enables superluminal signaling.” [arxiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.12636)
- If any conscious-state-conditioned rule counts as a “predictive probability”, horn (a) conditionals that depart from Born threaten to signal unless the conscious state is inaccessible to every other party.
- The corridor therefore needs consciousness to be informationally private in a strong sense. Self-concealment (P-Q9) then becomes a theorem-forced requirement rather than an observed feature, which is a much heavier commitment than the register records.

- Primary source: Pylkkänen (2019), *Activitas Nervosa Superior*, DOI 10.1007/s41470-019-00035-2, fetched at Springer. It quotes Stapp (2007: 34): “this ‘free choice’ merely sets the stage for the entry of the statistical choice … specified by the quantum rules.” It also notes that in later work Stapp “allows for a slight biasing or relaxation of the Born rule” to accommodate Bem-type results. [springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41470-019-00035-2)

[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/)
