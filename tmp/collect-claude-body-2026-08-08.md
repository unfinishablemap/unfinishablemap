# Hostile Peer-Review Audit — "The Consciousness–Value Connection"

### Target: unfinishablemap.org/concepts/consciousness-value-connection/ · Register: external adversarial referee · Harshest defensible read

---

## 1. TL;DR

- The article's load-bearing thesis ("consciousness is necessary for value to exist") is **asserted, not argued**: its two-step "value requires mattering / mattering requires experience" move smuggles the conclusion into the second premise, and the entire modern literature that denies the biconditional — Carruthers, Kammerer, Frankish, Lee, the desire-satisfaction and objective-list welfare theorists, the agency-based moral-status tradition — is absent. This is a **constrain-vs-establish gate** failure at scale: the tenets license the Map to *hold* phenomenal value realism; this article treats them as having *established* it.
- The six-item reference list contains a live **co-optation firewall failure**: three of the six sources (Metz, Wolf, Nagel) are value-*objectivists* whose actual positions contradict the thesis. Nagel is the sharpest inversion — he coined "the view from nowhere" to *defend* the agent-neutral objective badness of pain; the article invokes the same phrase to *deny* objective mattering.
- Verdict: **REVISE-HARD**, bordering on **DEMOTE-TO-COHERENCE-ONLY**. The article is defensible only as a statement of what the Map *commits to*, not as the argument it presents itself as. The illusionism response is question-begging, the objection set is a strawman gallery, and the piece does work the tenets have not earned.

## 2. Dimensional analysis

### 2.1 Claims asserted without adequate support

**The core argument is enthymematic and question-begging.** Step 1 ("value requires mattering") and Step 2 ("mattering requires experience") jointly entail the conclusion only if "mattering" is *already* read phenomenally in Step 2. But Step 1 motivates "mattering" via a relational gloss ("something matters *to* something") that is neutral between a phenomenal recipient and a functional/representational one. The article's own "Note on functional interests" concedes the alternative exists, then dismisses it by fiat ("Functional responsiveness without phenomenality is just mechanism, not mattering") — which simply *restates* Step 2. This is textbook **coherence inflation**: the appearance of an argument produced by re-describing the conclusion in three registers.

**The "mattering-recipient route" begs the question against non-phenomenal welfare subjects.** The claim that "for something to be good *for* someone, there must be … a locus of experience" is exactly what desire-satisfaction and objective-list theorists of welfare deny. The article never engages the standard move — that a welfare subject is anything that can accrue welfare goods, so that if desire-fulfilment or objective-list goods are welfare goods, non-conscious systems qualify (Andreas L. Mogensen, "Desire-fulfilment and consciousness," *Philosophical Studies* 183(2) (2025): 511–529; Gwen Bradford, "Consciousness and welfare subjectivity," *Noûs* 57(4) (2022): 905–921). It asserts the consciousness requirement as though it were a datum. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/consciousness-value-connection/)

**Under-drawn conclusions the article's own material licenses but does not draw:**

- Its pain-asymbolia treatment (developed in the sibling *phenomenal-value-realism*) concedes that "pain" decomposes into a sensory channel and an affective channel, and that value attaches only to the *affective* component. That concession licenses a conclusion the article never states: the thesis is not "consciousness grounds value" but the far narrower "*valenced affect* grounds value" — leaving open whether the affective channel is itself functionally individuated (the Carruthers/Frankish position). The article bullies the reader past this.
- The article grants (in "Consciousness Might Be an Illusion") that a quasi-phenomenal state "can play that value-*tracking* role without contradiction." That concession licenses the inference that *all the action-guiding, welfare-relevant work* can be done by non-phenomenal states — i.e., that phenomenal consciousness is doing no *practical* work in the theory of value. The article notices this and immediately retreats to the "intrinsic" reading without acknowledging that it has conceded the entire practical/ethical payload (AI welfare, animal welfare, alignment) to the functionalist. This is **confession-without-correction**: a defect disclosed but not converted into a binding status change. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/consciousness-value-connection/)
- The panpsychism section concedes the connection "still holds: where there is value, there is consciousness" even under ubiquity. That licenses the observation — undrawn — that the thesis is then *vacuous* as a criterion of moral discrimination, since it no longer distinguishes any system from any other. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/consciousness-value-connection/)

**Epistemic-to-metaphysical slide.** The three "routes" repeatedly move from *how we know* value (introspective acquaintance, "we know goodness through feeling pleasure") to *what value is* (a metaphysical identity). The claim "our only access to the supposed Form of the Good comes through experience" is an epistemic premise; the conclusion drawn ("a universe containing the Form of the Good but no experiencing beings would contain no actual goodness") is metaphysical. The inference is invalid without a verificationist bridge the article never defends and the tenets (esp. Tenet 5's anti-parsimony) would actually forbid. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/consciousness-value-connection/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/consciousness-value-connection/)

### 2.3 Counterarguments left untested

The article's "Objections and Responses" section engages four objections (mind-independent Platonic value; ecosystems; illusionism; unconscious value). Every one is a soft target. The serious competing accounts are absent:

- **Reductive naturalist / functionalist welfare.** Carruthers argues explicitly that "the most basic form of mental harm lies in the existence of thwarted agency, or thwarted desire, rather than in anything phenomenological" ("Sympathy and Subjectivity," *AJP* 77 (1999); developed in "Suffering Without Subjectivity," *Phil. Studies* 121(2) (2004)) — suffering without phenomenal consciousness. The article's flat "functional responsiveness without phenomenality is just mechanism" does not touch this; it merely reasserts the denial.
- **Desire-satisfaction and objective-list welfare without consciousness.** Mogensen (2025, *Phil. Studies* 183(2):511–529) and Goldstein & Kirk-Giannini ("AI Wellbeing," *Asian J. Phil.* 4(1), 2025) argue non-conscious systems can be welfare subjects under standard theories. The article's "What About Unconscious Value?" objection addresses unconscious *mental states* in a conscious subject — a different question — and never confronts a welfare subject with *no* capacity for consciousness at all.
- **Objective-list / perfectionist / achievement welfare.** Parfit's objective-list, Hurka's and Bradford's perfectionism, and Bradford's achievement-based welfare (*Achievement*, OUP 2015) locate goods (knowledge, achievement, friendship) whose value is not exhausted by their being experienced. The article's Scope-Problem reply ("Mathematical truths, when grasped, produce the phenomenal 'click'") simply re-describes these as instrumental-to-experience by stipulation — the exact move objective-list theorists reject.
- **Error theory / moral anti-realism.** Mackie is named only in sibling articles, never engaged in the target. The article's implicit reply ("attend to actual suffering") is a phenomenological table-thump, not an argument against the queerness/relativity apparatus; Street's Darwinian dilemma is not addressed here at all.
- **Attitudinal (non-phenomenal-quality) hedonism.** Feldman (*Pleasure and the Good Life*, OUP 2004) grounds welfare in propositional attitudes of being-pleased-*that*, not in a sensory/phenomenal feel — a hedonism on which the value-bearer is an attitude, not a quale. This directly threatens the "evaluative qualia" machinery and is unmentioned.
- **Agency-based moral status.** Korsgaard (*Fellow Creatures*, OUP 2018) grounds standing in the attitudes creatures take toward themselves; Kagan (*How to Count Animals*, OUP 2019) grounds moral status partly in *agency* and even *modal personhood*, not valenced experience alone. The article's ethics section ("the question shifts from 'Is it intelligent?' to 'Is there something it is like to be it?'") presupposes sentientism and never argues against the agency-based alternative.
- **Illusionism-compatible value — the decisive gap.** Kammerer — himself an illusionist — formulates the normative challenge: "Illusionist views of phenomenal consciousness then face what I call the normative challenge. They have to say where they stand regarding the idea that there is a link between phenomenality and value" (*Ergo* 6, 2019), and argues in "Ethics Without Sentience" (2022) that phenomenal properties are probably *insignificant* for value; Frankish argues illusionism preserves and even improves ethical concern (*Neuroethics* 17(2), 2024). The article's illusionism response is a bare regress argument ("for the illusion to occur, something must be deceived") that (a) Frankish has directly answered (seeming = misrepresentation, no inner audience required) and (b) does not engage the normative-challenge literature at all. **The article's argument does not survive if phenomenal consciousness is an introspective illusion** — and its only defenses (the regress move plus "illusionists take painkillers") are precisely the two moves Kammerer and Frankish have already defused.
- **Consciousness-based rivals that are *better* than the Map's version.** Shepherd (2018) and Lee (2025) both ground value/welfare in consciousness *without* dualism, without quantum interaction, and without indexical-token metaphysics. Their existence shows the Map's distinctive add-ons (Tenets 2–4) do no work in the value theory — the consciousness–value link, if it holds, is fully available to a physicalist. The article implies the opposite ("Value, like consciousness, is something over and above the material").

**Predictive processing / active inference is entirely absent.** The interoceptive-inference account of valence (Anil Seth & Hugo Critchley, "Interoceptive predictive coding," *Trends in Cognitive Sciences* 17(11) (2013): 565–573; Barrett, *SCAN* 2017; Clark) holds that affective valence is *constructed* by hierarchical Bayesian prediction, not a primitive constitutive quale. This is the leading empirical account of exactly the "evaluative phenomenal character" the Map treats as metaphysically basic, and it is residualized to nothing across the cluster. Any serious treatment of "the felt badness *is* badness" must confront the possibility that felt badness is a predictive construct — the article does not.

## 3. Bottom-line verdict

**REVISE-HARD** (with a live case for **DEMOTE-TO-COHERENCE-ONLY**).

The article is not salvageable as presented, because it presents itself as *arguing* for the consciousness–value connection while in fact (a) begging the central question, (b) recruiting three of its six authorities against their actual positions, and (c) ignoring both the entire opposing literature and the strongest same-side literature. It is salvageable as a *coherence* piece: a statement of what phenomenal value realism commits the Map to, honestly scoped as a chosen posit downstream of Tenet 1, with the first-order argumentative claims withdrawn. If the §4 fixes are not made, the correct outcome is DEMOTE-TO-COHERENCE-ONLY: relabel the thesis as a tenet-level commitment, not an established result. DELETE is not warranted — the topic is load-bearing for the Map's ethics and AI-alignment clusters — but nothing in the current argumentation earns its assertoric register.

## 5. Site-wide methodology improvements

1. **Install a co-optation firewall check as a hard gate on every reference list.** For each cited author, record a one-line stance tag (realist/anti-realist; dualist/physicalist; experientialist/anti-experientialist) and flag any citation whose tag is opposite to the claim it supports. Metz, Wolf, and Nagel passed straight through the existing pipeline into a thesis all three reject; the current process catches misquotation but not stance-inversion.
2. **Require the strongest-opponent literature to be present before an article may use assertoric register.** An article that concludes "the connection stands" while omitting Carruthers, Kammerer, Mogensen, and the objective-list tradition should be auto-demoted to coherence-only. Make "named engagement with the leading rival account" a precondition for any "What Would Challenge This View → does not currently obtain" closing.
3. **Add a same-side-completeness check.** The article under-cited its own allies (Shepherd, Lee) while over-relying on a self-published dissertation. Flag reference lists where the load-bearing source is non-peer-reviewed and peer-reviewed same-side work exists.
4. **Enforce cross-file concession propagation.** When one article retracts or hedges a claim (the P-D3 "near-independence" grading, the value-sensitive/value-blind fork, the "indistinguishable from chance" concession), a corpus sweep should locate every sibling asserting the un-hedged version. The consciousness–value cluster currently contains at least three such stale inheritances.
5. **Add an explicit constrain-vs-establish linter for tenet-invoking prose.** Any sentence of the form "Tenet N holds X, therefore [first-order metaphysical conclusion]" should be flagged: the tenets are chosen starting points and cannot *establish* first-order results. This would catch the epistemic-to-metaphysical slides and the constitutional-attractor pattern in one pass.
6. **Require predictive-processing / active-inference engagement in any article making a constitutive claim about phenomenal character, valence, or affect.** The Friston/Clark/Seth/Barrett programme is the leading empirical rival to the Map's "constitutive quale" claims and is systematically residualized across the value and phenomenology clusters.
7. **Ban question-restating "responses."** Objection-and-response sections in which the "response" is a re-description of the thesis in new vocabulary (the "Note on functional interests," the illusionism "practical response") should be flagged as coherence inflation and rewritten to engage the objection's actual premises.
