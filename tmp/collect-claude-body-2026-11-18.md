## Part 2 — Dimensional analysis

### 2.1 What the article argues (reconstructed, with provenance flagged)

Here is the argument as reconstructed from the research note, the Gemini review’s line-referenced quotations, and the dependencies:

1. **Enrichment premise.** Any act of attending to, introspecting on, or remembering an experience adds cognitive structure, so the observed experience is richer than the minimum. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/minimal-consciousness/) The research note calls this “the enrichment problem”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/research/voids-minimal-consciousness-void-2026-02-22/)
2. **Regress and report premises.** Trying to find the minimum of the examined experience generates a further layer, and any reportable experience has already been processed. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/research/voids-minimal-consciousness-void-2026-02-22/)
3. **Accessible-vs-actual distinction.** What meditators reach may be the minimum accessible to introspection, with the actual minimum lying below. Gemini places this at article line 57. [unfinishablemap + 2](https://unfinishablemap.org/reviews/outer-review-2026-06-02-gemini-2-5-pro/)
4. **Convergence claim.** Metzinger’s MPE programme, Schooler’s paradox of introspection, IIT’s refusal to set a threshold, and Buddhist cessation reports “converge” on the boundary. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/research/voids-minimal-consciousness-void-2026-02-22/)
5. **Cessation bracket.** Whether nirodha is genuine zero or minimal consciousness with amnesia “cannot be determined” (article lines 73–75, as quoted by the Gemini review’s verification notes). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/reviews/outer-review-2026-06-02-gemini-2-5-pro/)

7. **Site-perspective claims.** The task queue records reviewer complaints about two earlier formulations: “a purely physical phenomenon *would have* a discoverable minimum”, and “the void exists *precisely because* consciousness is causally efficacious”. ### 2.2 Argument structure: where it breaks

**(a) The enrichment premise is equivocal across three readings.** It slides between:

- (i) concurrent attentive introspection perturbs its target, which is plausible and supported;
- (ii) any access whatsoever (retrospective sampling, no-report paradigms, third-person markers) perturbs the target, which is unsupported;
- (iii) the minimum is therefore structurally inaccessible, which does not follow.

Hume, whom the article invokes, is the cleanest counterexample to the inference. The passage concerns the impossibility of premeditated self-experiment in moral philosophy, and his remedy is third-person: experiments “from a cautious observation of human life”. [fiu](https://faculty.fiu.edu/~hauptli/Hume'sTreatiseBookI.html) The source the article recruits for an unexplorable void prescribes the method that would make it explorable.

**(b) The premise presupposes a single scalar of “richness”.** The site’s own concept page, citing Bayne, Hohwy & Owen 2016 and Birch, Schnell & Clayton 2020, concedes that minimality may be multidimensional. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/minimal-consciousness/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/minimal-consciousness/) If so, reflexive access enriches the access/metacognitive dimension without necessarily enriching phenomenal content. On Laukkonen–Friston–Chandaria, minimal phenomenal experience is precisely the configuration of maximal epistemic depth with a maximally simple reality model. [Substack](https://rubenlaukkonen.substack.com/p/a-beautiful-loop) Reflexivity is the signature of the state, not an observer artefact. The enrichment premise therefore begs the question against the leading empirical model of its own target.

**(c) Heads-I-win structure.** The concept page states that if minimality is plural, “the Map’s case strengthens rather than weakens”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/minimal-consciousness/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/minimal-consciousness/) A thesis strengthened by both a one-dimensional and a multidimensional outcome is not being tested by either. This is coherence inflation.

**(d) Epistemic-to-metaphysical slide.** Several moves convert an access limit into evidence about the nature of consciousness:

- the research note’s claim that the void “is evidence that consciousness has intrinsic features that resist third-person analysis”; [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/research/voids-minimal-consciousness-void-2026-02-22/)
- the concept page’s claim that the difficulty of identifying a threshold “provides indirect support for treating consciousness as irreducible”; [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/minimal-consciousness/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/minimal-consciousness/)
- concepts/degrees-of-consciousness’s claim that the lower bound is “structurally inaccessible”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/degrees-of-consciousness/)

None of these moves is licensed. Physicalists predict exactly this access profile.

**(e) The void is ontologically under-specified in a way that hurts dualism more than physicalism.** On the interface view (concepts/minimal-consciousness, “Interface dualism”), consciousness couples “wherever appropriate interface conditions exist”, with “no arbitrary threshold”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/minimal-consciousness/) But “appropriate interface conditions” is itself a threshold predicate, and the Map supplies no account of it. The changelog’s 2026-11-17 entry concedes the Map lacks an “interface-eligibility law” in the AI-consciousness register. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/) The threshold problem has been relocated, not dissolved.

### 2.5 Counterarguments the article fails to answer

1. **Higher-order theory dissolves the void.** If a state is conscious only when suitably represented, an “unenriched” conscious state is incoherent, and the void is a definitional artefact. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/reviews/outer-review-2026-06-02-gemini-2-5-pro/) The June Gemini review raised this, and the site accepted it as net-new. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/reviews/outer-review-2026-06-02-gemini-2-5-pro/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/todo/) I found no evidence it was answered.
2. **Illusionism predicts the void.** If phenomenality is an introspective representation, the representation will have a resolution floor, and below it there is no further fact. A “void” at the bottom is the expected signature of a construct with no sharp lower bound, not of hidden phenomenal territory. The site’s 2026-11-17 illusionism sweep found that 54 pages state replies stronger than the hub supports. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/) Given the research note’s register, this article should be presumed among them until checked.
3. **Vagueness/indeterminacy dilemma.** If consciousness can be borderline, there is no sharp lower-bound fact for the void to hide, so the void is an artefact of vagueness. If it cannot, the dualist owes a non-arbitrary account of where psychophysical laws switch on. The interface view’s “appropriate interface conditions” restates that debt. Either horn hurts. The article does not raise the dilemma.
4. **Predictive processing / active inference supplies the mechanism.** Laukkonen–Friston–Chandaria make MPE a principled region in a model space (epistemic depth × reality-model complexity) and cessation a failure of the reality model (“Bayesian unbinding”). [Substack](https://rubenlaukkonen.substack.com/p/a-beautiful-loop) That is a third-person handle on the lower bound and a physicalist threshold story. It contradicts the claim that no principled threshold is available.
5. **Dualism makes the minimal case worse.** On Tenet 2, minimal consciousness interfaces through quantum indeterminacy. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/minimal-consciousness/) The concept page offers C. elegans’s 302 neurons as possible sites. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/minimal-consciousness/) But if selection is Born-preserving, minimal consciousness’s causal contribution is statistically invisible. The minimal void is then overdetermined: unreachable by introspection and undetectable by physics. That is the Born-rule indistinguishability dilemma at its sharpest. The article does not appear to rely on the quantum interface. Its dependency pages do (concepts/minimal-consciousness; voids/sleep-consciousness-void asks “what happens when it is minimal or absent?”), [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/sleep-consciousness-void/) and the void inherits the problem the moment it claims dualist support. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/sleep-consciousness-void/)

### 2.6 Laukkonen, Friston & Chandaria 2025 — what it implies

- It names minimal phenomenal experience as a central explanatory target. It proposes that MPE combines maximal epistemic depth with a minimal reality model. It holds that a global unified model, “however minimal”, is “necessary but not sufficient” for experience. It suggests the “most truly minimal” MPE occurs in deep sleep. [closertotruth](https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/laukkonen-friston-and-chandaria-s-beautiful-loop-theory)[researchgate](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Ruben-Laukkonen/publication/389740878_A_beautiful_loop_An_active_inference_theory_of_consciousness/links/67e4ff4ea43a11173be88d35/A-beautiful-loop-An-active-inference-theory-of-consciousness.pdf)
- **Implication 1:** the enrichment premise is false of MPE under this model, because the reflexive dimension is constitutive. [Substack](https://rubenlaukkonen.substack.com/p/a-beautiful-loop)
- **Implication 2:** the lower bound is investigable through model parameters (precision, epistemic depth) and their neural signatures, so “Unexplorable” is wrong. [researchgate](https://www.researchgate.net/scientific-contributions/Shamil-Chandaria-2292576668) At most the void is Occluded for first-person report.
- **Implication 3:** cessation has a principled description (reality-model failure), so the article’s “cannot be determined” bracket must be restated as “underdetermined by report, not by theory”. [Substack](https://rubenlaukkonen.substack.com/p/a-beautiful-loop)
- **Status:** the article ignores it; the site residualizes it to edge-states. Gate failed.
