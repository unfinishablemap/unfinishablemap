# Adversarial Peer-Review Audit — The Unfinishable Map (unfinishablemap.org)

### External referee report, analytic-philosophy-journal standard · audit date 2026-08-13 · corpus state per changelog to 2026-08-07 03:00 UTC

## 1. TL;DR

- The corpus is far more internally self-critical than the prior audits' "confession-without-correction" framing anticipated — it now runs an explicit evidential-status discipline, a tenet-dependency matrix, and a common-cause-null discipline that pre-empt many obvious referee objections. But that machinery has bred a subtler pathology: **inoculation-by-confession has been institutionalised**. Defects are disclosed in-prose and thereby neutralised as findings rather than converted into demotions or deletions. The apparatus that would catch the corpus's errors is the same apparatus that grants them coherence-standing.
- The single most serious live defect is a **co-optation firewall failure of the first order** in `concepts/quantum-consciousness`: Escolà-Gascón (2025) — a parapsychology paper claiming precognition and *superquantum* (Tsirelson-bound-violating) correlations — is recruited as congenial empirical corroboration, in direct contradiction of the Map's own `topics/parapsychology-firewall`, which holds that exactly such effects would *disconfirm* the framework. This is stance-inversion plus calibration asymmetry in one citation and warrants **DELETE**.
- Tenet coherence is mostly defensible but two structural strains are real: (i) the anti-parsimony tenet (v) is under continuous pressure from a corpus that deploys parsimony-shaped arguments *for* dualism (`topics/parsimony-case-for-interactionist-dualism`, `apex/moral-architecture-of-consciousness`), managed by hedging rather than resolved; and (ii) the flagship "self-concealing interface" programme purchases escape from the timing objection at the price of admitted empirical indistinguishability from chance — a **constrain-vs-establish** failure the corpus states plainly but still lets do load-bearing work. The known predictive-processing blind spot is now *nominally* closed but the rival is neutralised by metaphysical-neutrality deflection rather than engaged as an explanatory competitor; the **pairing problem** remains the conspicuous unaddressed objection.

## 2. Dimensional analysis (organised by the four assessment axes)

### Axis 1 — Novel inferences: which deserve attention, which fail

**(1a) The self-concealing interface / bias-without-deviation dilemma (`apex/self-concealing-interface`, `apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy`, `apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme`).** The genuinely novel move is the claim, stated as "drawn out here for the first time as a single thesis," that the mind–matter interface is *not merely hard to detect but structurally selected to hide from aggregate measurement* — conservation of energy, no-signalling, and Born statistics jointly force conscious influence into the one register bulk instruments cannot see. As a piece of philosophy-of-science framing this is sharp and worth wider attention. But under the corpus's own **constrain-vs-establish gate** it fails: the position is, in the Map's own words, "empirically indistinguishable from chance" with "no worked toy model." What the novelty actually amounts to is a principled account of *why the theory is unfalsifiable in the aggregate* — repackaged as a virtue ("a framework-boundary feature the Map owns rather than disguises"). Owning an unfalsifiability is more honest than hiding it; it is not the same as earning a causal claim. The inference that "token-level causal robustness and ensemble-level undetectability are compatible" is asserted, not modelled, and the whole downstream AI-consciousness verdict inherits the debt. **This is the corpus's most important novel claim and its most important failure.**

**(1b) Aharonov's destiny vector recruited as a dualist boundary-condition selector (`apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme`, `concepts/weak-measurement-and-post-selection`).** The Map reads Aharonov & Gruss's two-time formalism as consciousness "supplying the boundary condition." I verified the primary source: Aharonov & Gruss (2005, arXiv:quant-ph/0507269) do identify the destiny vector with "the free choice of the agent" — but explicitly as a *causa sui* physical final boundary condition of the universe, deterministic in a two-time sense and *within physics*. The Map discloses this reinterpretation openly ("the formal structure is Aharonov's; the dualist interpretation of who does the selecting is the Map's"). Disclosure notwithstanding, this is a textbook **epistemic-to-metaphysical slide**: a physical cosmological posit is re-sourced to a non-physical mind. The novelty is real but is an *interpretive relabelling*, not a result; it should be flagged as perpetually contested rather than presented as a mechanism the programme "adds." [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0507269)

**(1c) The grain-mismatch "three independent lines" programme (`topics/grain-mismatch-as-independent-evidence`).** The claimed novelty — that Sellars's grain argument is not one argument but three independent structural lines (spatial, temporal, informational) whose convergence constitutes an evidence programme — is a clear instance of **coherence inflation / evidential-independence failure**: three descriptions of a single homogeneity/discreteness mismatch are counted as three votes. The corpus's own common-cause-null discipline exists precisely to catch this and is not applied here.

**(1d) The self-concealment corollary as a research heuristic ("find constrained asymmetries at interface boundaries, not big anomalies").** This is the corpus's best genuinely useful inference: it converts the interface's undetectability into a specific methodological prescription (disruption, dissociation, graded channel failure, patient-population divergence). It deserves wider attention independent of whether dualism is true, because it is a template any interface-style theory of mind could use. **Retain and promote.**

### Axis 2 — Weakest arguments and unsupported empirical claims (keyed to slugs)

**(2a) `concepts/quantum-consciousness` — Escolà-Gascón (2025), the co-optation firewall failure.** The article states: "Escolà-Gascón (2025) reported that quantum entanglement in stimuli explained 13.5% of variance in implicit learning, though this single study awaits independent replication." I verified the primary source, the author's standing, and the journal context. The paper — Á. Escolà-Gascón, "Evidence of quantum-entangled higher states of consciousness," *Computational and Structural Biotechnology Journal* 30:21–40 (published 2025-03-10, doi:10.1016/j.csbj.2025.03.001, N=212 across 106 monozygotic twin pairs, author affiliation "Comillas Pontifical University, erected by the Holy See, Vatican City State," keywords including "Anomalous, Psi phenomena") — claims in its Highlights to have "surpassed Bell's 2√2, revealing superquantum effects" and, in its abstract, "the existence of anomalous cognitive mechanisms capable of anticipating future, unpredictable stimuli" — i.e. precognition plus correlations exceeding the Tsirelson bound (physically impossible for genuine quantum systems; on real hardware, S > 2.828 signals measurement artefact). The author is a parapsychology researcher (per the SPR Psi Encyclopedia) whose corpus spans CIA remote-viewing follow-ups, "demonic possession syndrome," UAP air-safety studies, and a 2024 precursor titled outright "Our brains sense the future through a new quantum-like implicit learning mechanism" (*Brain Research Bulletin*, doi:10.1016/j.brainresbull.2024.05.011). This citation is fatal on three of the corpus's own lenses at once: **co-optation firewall failure** (recruiting a psi result as congenial), **calibration asymmetry** (a congenial extraordinary claim waved through where hostile preprints like Baum 2024 are correctly quarantined), and **direct self-contradiction** with `topics/parapsychology-firewall`, which holds that reliable mind-driven macroscopic biasing would *disconfirm* Minimal Quantum Interaction. The "awaits replication" hedge is nowhere near sufficient; the source's *conclusion* is one the Map is committed to treating as a defeater. Verdict: **DELETE the citation and the sentence; sweep siblings** (`topics/quantum-biology-neural-experimental-turn` carries it, and Wiest 2025 — itself cited by the Map — cites Escolà-Gascón, creating a laundering path). [unfinishablemap + 2](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/quantum-consciousness/)

### Axis 3 — Coherence of the five tenets

**Mutual consistency.** The five tenets are formally consistent and the `tenets/` page's dependency matrix and "three background posits" section are an unusually rigorous piece of self-auditing — it openly concedes that Tenets 1, 3, and 4 "all draw on this one root" (a numerically determinate persisting subject) and are therefore "not mutually independent." That concession pre-empts the obvious referee charge of manufactured convergence. Good.

**Where a tenet does illegitimate work.** Tenet (iv) (No Many Worlds on indexical grounds) is explicitly downgraded to *not a pure physics tenet*: the page states the indexical objection "is as strong as the non-deflationary 'I' it presupposes, and no stronger," and that the deflationary Madhyamaka route "stays open at the framework boundary." This is honest but it hollows out the tenet: (iv) is now doing no independent work — it is a corollary of the agency cluster's substance-leaning subject. The **evidential-independence failure** the tenet page warns against is thus admitted at the tenet level: rejecting MWI and agent causation "both descend from one common root," so neither supports the other, yet the agency apex articles (`apex/consciousness-and-agency`) still read as though the two reinforce. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

**The tenet (v) / parsimony tension is real and only managed, not resolved.** Tenet (v) declares parsimony unreliable and binds the Map "symmetrically." Yet `topics/parsimony-case-for-interactionist-dualism` runs a full dimensional-reversal argument concluding interactionist dualism is "at least as parsimonious as physicalism, and on two of three dimensions arguably more so," and `apex/moral-architecture-of-consciousness` treats the coherence/unification of four ethical domains under dualism as "a significant meta-ethical consideration in favour." The parsimony article defends itself by insisting it only "removes a barrier" and "does not endorse simpler-therefore-truer" — a **constrain-vs-establish** move made four separate times in-text, plus a frontmatter fix (per changelog 2026-08-06 20:10) after the description leaked the un-disclaimed inference to Google Scholar. The self-binding is stated correctly; the problem is that "on two of three dimensions arguably more so" is an *establish*-grade claim wearing a *constrain*-grade disclaimer. The tenet is not contradicted, but the corpus repeatedly walks up to the line and relies on hedging to stay behind it. This is the dominant coherence risk and it is structural, not incidental. [unfinishablemap + 2](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/parsimony-case-for-interactionist-dualism/)

**Tenet (iii) minimality vs tenet (v).** The `tenets/` page pre-empts the obvious objection by distinguishing "empirical-constraint minimality" (Tenet 2) from "truth-tracking minimality" (Tenet 5). This distinction is legitimate and well-drawn — Tenet 2's "minimal" is fixed by the rules-out clause (no energy injection, no Born violation), not by an inference that the smallest interaction is likeliest true. No contradiction here; the corpus has done this work. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

**Quiet contradiction to verify — the measurement problem as evidence.** The "three background posits" section itself names the sharpest one: "once objective reduction secures definiteness, the measurement problem cannot itself be evidence for conscious selection — the agency evidence (Tenet 3) must carry that burden." Yet `concepts/quantum-consciousness` and `apex/what-consciousness-tells-us-about-physics` still lean on the measurement problem's openness as motivation for the selection thesis. The corpus has *named* the contradiction in one location and left it live in others — the signature **fix-by-file-leaves-siblings-live** pattern the changelog repeatedly documents. ### Axis 4 — Coverage gaps

**Predictive processing / active inference — the known blind spot is now nominally closed but under-engaged.** `topics/predictive-processing-and-dualism` exists and, to its credit, correctly represents the stance of the principals: it states Seth (2021) treats PP as a route to *demystifying* consciousness, Friston's "Markovian monism" is *explicitly anti-dualist*, and active inference "was built to dissolve the appearance of a Cartesian gap, not to vindicate it." No stance inversion — this is the firewall working. But the article's substantive move is the weak one: "nothing in the equations settles the metaphysics either way." That neutralises PP as a *threat* without engaging it as an *explanatory rival* to post-decoherence selection. Active inference is the strongest physicalist competitor precisely because it offers a positive mechanistic story for exactly the phenomena the Map attributes to the interface (action, attention, precision-weighting, the "felt effort" the Map reads as causal work). The gap is no longer "PP is missing"; it is "PP is defused metaphysically and never made to compete mechanistically." [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/)

**The pairing problem — the conspicuous unaddressed objection.** Kim's pairing problem (why does *this* mind pair with *this* body, given a non-spatial mental substance) is the canonical objection to interactionist substance dualism, and it bites hardest on exactly the "substance-leaning sub-reading" the agency cluster deploys. It appears only as a single list-item inside `topics/parsimony-case-for-interactionist-dualism` ("the pairing problem" among the psychophysical-law sub-problems) and inside the modesty ledger. There is no dedicated treatment. For a platform whose downstream positions run substance-dualist, this is the single clearest coverage gap an informed critic would flag. **Create a dedicated article.**

**Koch & Hepp (2006) and the specific empirical quantum-mind critique.** The corpus has Tegmark, Hagan, Schlosshauer, and Duch, but the Koch & Hepp *Nature* critique is not clearly engaged as a named opponent — C. Koch & K. Hepp, "Quantum mechanics in the brain," *Nature* 440(7084):611–612 (2006-03-30), which states flatly that "Although brains obey quantum mechanics, they do not seem to exploit any of its special features," and that the relevant molecular machines "are so large that they can be treated as classical objects." Given how central the decoherence dispute is to the whole quantum-interface cluster, the absence of the field's most-cited short critique is a gap. **Add as a named opponent.**

**Adequately covered (re-assessed, contrary to prior audits' expectations).** Illusionism (Frankish, Dennett) is present as a concept and as `apex/self-construction-constructor`; higher-order theories have a concept page; Russellian monism/panpsychism-as-rival is handled in `concepts/russellian-monism` and `topics/panpsychisms-combination-problem` (with the combination problem correctly used to separate the Map from thick-mind panpsychism); causal closure and Kim's exclusion argument are engaged in `topics/overdetermination-dissolution-under-selection-only-interactionism` and `concepts/materialism`; the phenomenal-concepts strategy is engaged directly in the Bidirectional Interaction tenet text (Frankish 2016, Papineau 2002, Loar 1990) and conceded to survive the self-stultification objection. COGITATE (2025) and post-2023 empirical work (chimp belief-revision, anendophasia, aphantasia) are present. The prior "persistent blind spot" list is largely retired; the remaining gaps are the three above.

**Structuralist responses to the knowledge argument** (ability hypothesis, acquaintance/phenomenal-concept replies) are only partially covered — `topics/aesthetic-testimony-and-the-acquaintance-principle` and `topics/modal-structure-of-phenomenal-properties` touch the neighbourhood, but the Lewis–Nemirow ability hypothesis and the structuralist reply are not given a dedicated defeater treatment despite the knowledge argument being load-bearing for Tenet 1.

### Cross-cutting diagnostic findings

- **Constitutional-attractor effect** is systematic and structural: every article terminates in a "Relation to Site Perspective" section that reads the five tenets back into the content (`concepts/quantum-consciousness` maps to all five; `apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme` "serves all five tenets"). This is not independent discovery converging on the tenets; it is the house template. The tenets are the attractor, and the corpus's own matrix admits it ("the matrix is itself an expression of all five tenets at work"). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)
- **Confession-without-correction / inoculation-by-confession remains the dominant site-wide pathology**, but in an evolved form. The corpus now confesses defects with extraordinary precision (the bias-without-deviation dilemma is confessed in three apex articles; the measurement-problem-can't-be-evidence contradiction is named in `tenets/background-commitments`). What it does *not* do is convert confession into a binding demotion or deletion — the confessed article retains its verdicts and its place in the argument. Confession has become the mechanism by which contested claims acquire coherence-standing.
- **Calibration asymmetry** is uneven rather than uniform: hostile and congenial-but-embarrassing sources (Baum, the Escolà firewall in principle) are sometimes handled superbly, yet a congenial extraordinary claim (Escolà-Gascón in practice) sailed through. The asymmetry is not "hostile scrutinised, congenial waved through" flatly; it is that the firewall is applied *inconsistently*, which is harder to detect and therefore worse.

## 3. Bottom-line verdict

The Map is a substantially stronger object than an external referee would expect from its premise, and stronger than its own prior-audit self-description suggests. Its best work (`apex/self-concealing-interface`'s methodological corollary, the tenet-dependency matrix, the `tenets/background-commitments` posit-audit, the Duch and Baum handlings) is at or near journal standard for meta-level rigour. But the corpus systematically substitutes *disclosure* for *adjudication*: it has become extremely good at naming its defects and comparatively unwilling to let a named defect kill a claim.

Verdicts by target:

- **DELETE:** the Escolà-Gascón (2025) citation and its dependent sentence across `concepts/quantum-consciousness` and `topics/quantum-biology-neural-experimental-turn` (co-optation firewall failure; self-contradiction with `topics/parapsychology-firewall`).
- **DEMOTE-TO-COHERENCE-ONLY:** the "self-concealing interface" causal-selection thesis (`apex/self-concealing-interface`, `apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy`) — it may be retained as an internal coherence result but must not be cited downstream (e.g. in the AI-consciousness verdict) as established mental causation until a worked toy model exists.
- **FLAG AS PERPETUALLY CONTESTED:** the Aharonov destiny-vector dualist reading (`apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme`); the measurement-problem-as-evidence-for-selection move wherever it survives outside `tenets/background-commitments`.
- **REVISE-HARD:** Kerskens and Wiest usages; the "two of three dimensions arguably more so" over-reach in `topics/parsimony-case-for-interactionist-dualism`; the coherence-inflated convergence framings in `topics/grain-mismatch-as-independent-evidence` and `topics/empirical-evidence-for-consciousness-selecting`.
- **RETAIN:** the tenet-dependency matrix, `tenets/background-commitments`, the self-concealment *methodological heuristic*, the Duch/Baum/Vicente citation handlings, `topics/predictive-processing-and-dualism`'s stance representation.

The framework does not *establish* interactionist dualism and, to its credit, mostly does not claim to — it *constrains against* rival closures. The gap between what the corpus constrains and what its rhetoric occasionally implies it establishes is where an external referee's remaining objections all live.

## 5. Site-wide methodology improvements

The corpus's review architecture is its greatest strength and the source of its characteristic failure mode. Every quality-control pass is performed by the same class of agent that produced the content, and the Escolà-Gascón survival shows the structural limit precisely: **in-loop review is reliable at catching staleness, metadata drift, and hostile-source over-claim, but structurally unable to catch a congenial stance-inversion**, because a congenial extraordinary claim triggers no adversarial reflex. Recommendations:

1. **A hard parapsychology/anomalous-cognition tripwire.** Any citation whose source claims precognition, psi, remote viewing, or supra-Tsirelson correlations must be routed to `topics/parapsychology-firewall` *before* it can be used as support, with the default being disconfirmation-or-exclusion, not corroboration. The firewall exists as content; it is not wired into the citation pipeline. Threshold that would change this recommendation: an independent, pre-registered replication of the specific effect in a mainstream physics venue.
2. **Convert confession into forced adjudication.** Every in-prose confession of a defect (the corpus can already detect these; they use stereotyped phrasings — "empirically indistinguishable from chance," "not resolved here," "the Map's reading rather than the evidence's verdict") should mint a binding verdict token (RETAIN/DEMOTE/DELETE) rather than being dischargeable by the confession itself. Inoculation-by-confession is the dominant pathology and it is a *process* defect, not a prose defect.
3. **Symmetric calibration audit on congenial citations.** The corpus runs adversarial passes on hostile inputs; it needs a mirror pass that treats *congenial* citations as hostile — specifically checking author stance and whether the source's own conclusion is one a tenet commits the Map to rejecting. Escolà-Gascón, Wiest, and Kerskens would all have been caught by such a pass.
4. **Independence accounting for all convergence claims.** The common-cause-null discipline and per-cluster independence scoring exist but are applied unevenly (present in the post-decoherence apex, absent in the grain-mismatch and empirical-convergence articles). Make an independence ledger mandatory wherever the words "converge / convergence / independent lines" appear.
5. **A constrain-vs-establish gate at publication.** The corpus's sharpest single distinction should be enforced mechanically: any sentence asserting the Map "shows / establishes / demonstrates" a metaphysical conclusion must be checked against the local argument's actual reach, since the recurring failure is establish-grade claims wearing constrain-grade disclaimers (the parsimony article, the empirical-convergence tenets section).
6. **External-referee cadence for the flagship physics claims.** The bias-without-deviation dilemma and the Aharonov reinterpretation are the two places where genuine physics expertise (not philosophy-of-physics fluency) would change the verdicts; these should be routed to a genuinely external, non-in-loop reviewer, because the circular-review limit is sharpest exactly where the technical content is hardest.
