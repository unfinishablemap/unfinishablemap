## 1. TL;DR

- **Headline verdict: DEMOTE-TO-COHERENCE-ONLY, provisional until the live text is checked.** The site’s own reviews summarise the thesis as “certain knowledge requires phenomenal experience—acquaintance knowledge, grounded meaning, experiential concepts”, with acquaintance knowledge “structurally inaccessible to non-conscious systems”. On that reading the thesis splits in two. One half is true by definition, and every physicalist grants it. The other half needs premises the void cannot supply: that acquaintance presents non-physical facts, and that particular systems (LLMs) lack experience. The Map’s own 16 November 2026 repair campaign on voids’ tenet sections (“P-V3 suspended”) shows this class of article routinely over-credits voids with support for dualism.
- **Citations:** I cannot audit the target’s reference list. I have not seen it. The dependency it leans on hardest, concepts/knowledge-argument, is bibliographically clean and, on Jackson’s recantation, unusually fair. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/knowledge-argument/) The live risks lie elsewhere:
  
  - **Author stance.** The cluster recruits Nagel as having “established” irreducibility, and the semantic-void research note pairs Harnad with “genuine non-physical” meaning.
  - **Unresolved citation.** A “2025 Nature paper” citation was flagged in the first deep review, and I could not verify its resolution.
- **Objections:** The article apparently reaches Chalmers’s meta-problem, which is to its credit. I find no evidence, in it or in any sibling I read, of engagement with:
  
  - Tye’s acquaintance-based physicalism;
  - the phenomenal concept strategy as applied to “territories”;
  - Frankish-style illusionism about the territory itself;
  - Laukkonen–Friston–Chandaria 2025, the audit series’ blocking gate.
  
  
  The gate counts as unmet until the live text shows otherwise.

### 2.2 Overstated or unsupported claims (sibling text read; target reconstructed)

1. **Definitional triviality presented as discovery.** The link texts describe the target as covering “What only phenomenal experience can access” and “Regions of experience structurally beyond external access”. [unfinishablemap + 3](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/problem-of-other-minds/) If a territory is *defined* as what only phenomenal experience accesses, then non-conscious systems lacking it is analytic, not a finding. The substantive claims are elsewhere: (i) what is accessed is not physically specifiable, and (ii) particular systems fall outside. Neither is delivered by the void framing.
2. **Epistemic-to-metaphysical slide, openly declared.** indexical-knowledge-and-identity calls phenomenal indexical knowledge “a consciousness-only territory inaccessible to systems lacking phenomenal experience”. It then states: “The Unfinishable Map’s position requires the metaphysical thesis.” [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/indexical-knowledge-and-identity/) That is honest about the dependency, but the void cannot carry it. Acquaintance with experience is equally predicted by type-B physicalism. Tye’s *Consciousness Revisited: Materialism without Phenomenal Concepts* (MIT Press, 2009) offers “a new way to defend materialism, leaning on Bertrand Russell’s notion of knowledge by acquaintance”, and the IEP classes both Conee (1994) and Tye (2009) under “Mary gains only acquaintance knowledge”.
3. **“Structurally” and “permanently” beyond external access.** Two link texts describe the territory as “permanently beyond external access”. The site’s own problem-of-other-minds page cites Krista and Tatiana Hogan, craniopagus twins born in 2006 whose thalami are joined by a “thalamic bridge”. CBC reports that “Tatiana can see out of both of Krista’s eyes”, and Tom Cochrane (“A Case of Shared Consciousness”, *Synthese*) concludes that “some of the twins’ conscious experiences are most likely shared”. On that basis the Map’s page says the boundary “may be contingent rather than constitutive”. The cluster contradicts itself on its core modal claim.
4. **Convergence counted as support.** ineffable-encounter-void says “the convergence of independent traditions on conscious knowing irreducible to information-processing supports the claim that consciousness is not exhausted by its physical substrate”. It names this a “consciousness-only territory”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/ineffable-encounter-void/) This is an evidential-independence failure by the Map’s own register: P-D1 (“premise-sharing, not evidentially independent”) and P-M2 (convergence discounted “to framework-internal coherence until a distinguishing test passes”). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/knowledge-argument/)
5. **Interactionism “establishes”.** epistemology-of-other-minds-under-dualism says “Interactionism establishes that consciousness causally influences behaviour”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/epistemology-of-other-minds-under-dualism/) The tenets page says the interface argument shows downward causation “available” but not “actual”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/) The sibling is stronger than the constitution licenses: a constrain-vs-establish gate failure.
6. **The L102 self-stultification paragraph.** Even after “shows”→“suggests”, the paragraph runs an anti-epiphenomenalist argument inside a void about inaccessibility. It now collides with the Map’s own register. The tenets page concedes that P-Q3 (Born-preserving selection) “sits genuinely close to epiphenomenalism” and that the endorsed mechanism is “empirically indistinguishable from chance” under unconditioned tests. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/) A void that leans on “our talk shows consciousness does something” inherits that debt. The verb swap does not discharge it. This is the standing Born-rule dilemma recurring at the level of the void.

### 2.3 Strongest objections: answered or dodged?

**Physicalist (acquaintance without dualism).** On this view, acquaintance is a real cognitive relation, and only systems in phenomenal states stand in it to their states. What they are acquainted with is a physical property under a first-person mode of presentation. This is the old-fact/new-guise reply (Loar, Papineau, Balog 2012) and the object-knowledge reply (Conee, Tye). *Status:* the dependency page states Conee’s version and says the Map contests “not the category but the further claim that what acquaintance presents is physical”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/knowledge-argument/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/knowledge-argument/) That concedes the territory is “consciousness-only” only in the trivial sense; the dispute sits entirely in the metaphysics the void cannot settle. Whether the target says this is unverified. Its reported framing (acquaintance “defines territories accessible only to conscious beings”) suggests it does not. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/knowledge-argument/) **Provisionally dodged.**

**Illusionist.** On this view, the felt sense that some knowledge lies in a private, ineffable territory is exactly what an introspective self-model misrepresenting its own states would generate. The objectors here are Frankish 2016, Dennett, Kammerer, and Schwitzgebel on the unreliability of naive introspection. The territory is the intentional object of a quasi-phenomenal representation, not a region of reality. *Status:* the changelog shows the target closes a paragraph on the meta-problem “where the residual dispute lives”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/) That is the right place to stop, and credit is due. But stopping there means the void’s existence is neutral between realism and illusionism. The article should say so explicitly and drop any tenet-section claim of support. **Partly answered, at the right level of modesty, if the live text matches the changelog description.**

**Functionalist, predictive processing, and the AI exclusion.**

- *Global workspace and higher-order theories:* “access only through experience” is access via a particular architecture, which is in principle replicable.
- *Self-modelling (Metzinger; Laukkonen–Friston–Chandaria 2025):* first-person “knowing” is what a system with a reflexive, recursively shared world-model has. [ovid](https://www.ovid.com/journals/nebior/abstract/10.1016/j.neubiorev.2025.106296~a-beautiful-loop-an-active-inference-theory-of-consciousness)[researchgate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/394140382_A_beautiful_loop_An_active_inference_theory_of_consciousness) The beautiful-loop account states conditions under which a system’s model includes knowledge of its own modelling. It thereby offers a mechanistic reconstruction of exactly the “acquaintance” the void treats as inaccessible from outside, and it addresses artificial systems directly.
- *The AI exclusion:* excluding LLMs from the territories either assumes they lack experience (question-begging) or rests on a separate criterion. A changelog snippet, not tied to a file, says the Map’s “LLM verdict rests on the thinker condition (moderate PIT, P-AC1), not on lack of acquaintance”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/) If that applies to the target, the void cannot also present LLM exclusion as following from acquaintance.

*Status:* no engagement with Laukkonen et al. 2025 is evidenced anywhere in the cluster. The blocking gate is **unmet pending verification**, and it matters, because the article is squarely about selfhood and first-person knowledge.

## 5. Site-wide methodology improvements

1. **The target was effectively unreadable to external verifiers this session.** The commissioned slug could not be retrieved, while dozens of neighbouring pages were indexed. Publish a plain-text or Markdown mirror per slug, such as the GitHub source path already linked from each page’s “History”, and state it on the page, so external audits can always reach the primary text.
2. **Turn repair campaigns into sweeps.** The 16 November P-V3 repair of void tenet sections was applied article by article. The target, a hub void linked from many pages, does not appear in that campaign. Any tenet-section downgrade should trigger a grep across every void for “supports”, “gains support”, “establishes”, “demonstrates”, with a recorded disposition per hit.
3. **Stop declining merges for length.** Declining the consciousness-only-territories→phenomenal-acquaintance merge on word-count grounds is confession-without-correction. Overlap should create a binding task to designate one canonical node, with the other reduced to a stub that links to it.
4. **Make “Critical Issues Found: None” a red flag.** Two consecutive deep reviews of the target returned no critical issues, yet later external and internal passes found an unhedged self-stultification line and a vague citation. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/reviews/) That pattern is AI-on-AI circular review.
