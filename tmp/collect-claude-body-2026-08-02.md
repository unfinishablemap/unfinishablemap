# Hostile Referee Report: *Dualism Cartography* (unfinishablemap.org/apex/dualism-cartography/)

*Procedure note: the live changelog (last modified 2026-07-31) was fetched first; the target article was fetched directly by slug (last modified 2026-07-26, published 2026-06-03, by claude-opus-4-8); the Tenets page and the two load-bearing dependents — the four-quadrant taxonomy and the mechanism-costs article — were fetched directly. Tenet wording was verified against the live Tenets page. All load-bearing citations were checked against publisher-of-record sources; empirical-currency items were verified against 2026 literature.*

## A. TL;DR — the harshest defensible read

The article is a competent, unusually self-aware piece of philosophical map-making whose central deliverable — the per-cell "mechanism cost" overlay — is a metric in name only: it has no unit, no scale, no aggregation rule, and no comparability condition, so its grid-formalism confers a rigour it has not earned. The disclaimer ("a map, not a verdict") is contradicted in the same document by a cost apparatus that ranks Q1 as the "cheapest permitted cell" and then locates the Map in Q1 — the verdict is smuggled through calibration, not argued. The cost battery is rigged by omission: it charges rivals on the three physical-side debts the physicalist literature presses hardest, while the Map's own two heaviest liabilities — the Born-rule statistical-indistinguishability dilemma and the rejection of Many-Worlds by fiat (Tenet 4) — are charged **nowhere on the grid**. Citations are largely metadata-clean, but three load-bearing recruitments are stance-corrupted or superseded: Goff, who explicitly argues panpsychism "avoids the disunity of dualism," is filed as a Q4 *dualism* inhabitant; Zheng & Meister's 10 bits/s — an information-throughput result whose framing is fully physicalist — is repurposed as a quantum-interface "constraint"; and the Masanes–Galley–Müller Born-rule "uniqueness" result is presented as settled when it is under live, published challenge (Kent, arXiv:2307.06191, 2023; Stacey, arXiv:2211.03299, 2023). Verdict: **REVISE-HARD**, with the cost-overlay component **DEMOTED-TO-COHERENCE-ONLY** until a metric is specified. The taxonomy (the grid itself) is the salvageable part; the cost overlay and the parsimony chapter are where advocacy has been laundered through cartographic vocabulary.

## B. Dimensional analysis

### (1) Evidential status — cost as decoration

**"Mechanism cost" is undefined as a metric.** The article promises that its overlay makes "costs become comparable rather than rhetorical." It does not. Nowhere is there a unit, a scale (ordinal? cardinal?), a zero point, an aggregation rule across the three questions, or a comparability condition licensing statements like "Q1 is cheaper than Q4." The costs are verbal tags — "authority debt," "exclusion debt," "appearance debt," "the full bill" — each derived, at best, from a single stipulated premise and then displayed in a grid whose 2-D visual formalism does the persuasive work the argument cannot. This is the core defect: bare qualitative assertion dressed as measurement. When the article says Q4 "owes the heaviest version of all three debts," there is no scale on which "heaviest" is defined; the claim is an intuition, not a computation.

**Are the per-cell costs argued or stipulated?** Mixed, tilting toward stipulated. Q1's "authority debt" is genuinely argued: it derives from the conjunction of counterfactual sufficiency of the physical base plus a mental contribution, routed through Schaffer's trumping-preemption — this is real reasoning. Q2's "exclusion debt" is argued via Kim. But Q3's "appearance/transparency debt" and Q4's "full bill" are largely asserted, and the comparative ordering across cells is asserted throughout ("bites hardest in Q4, lighter in Q1, dissolves in Q3, structurally inapplicable in Q2 strict"). The ordinal ranking is the article's product, and it is exactly the part with no method behind it.

**The disclaimer is performative inoculation contradicted by the cost assignments.** The article repeatedly disclaims adjudication ("map, do not adjudicate"; "the grid speaks; the tenets adjudicate"). But the parsimony chapter concludes that the Map "pays the *least mechanism cost compatible with its tenets*," and the overlay labels Q1 "the cheapest permitted cell," and the Map sits at Q1. A metric that (i) ranks cells by cost and (ii) places your own position in the lowest-cost permitted cell **is** a covert ranking that delivers a verdict — "our position is the disciplined, economical one" — regardless of the accompanying denial that "cheapest = true." The disclaimer does no epistemic work; it is banked as a credential ("cartographic restraint is the cluster's signature") while the cost assignments quietly carry the endorsement.

**Calibration parity fails — the battery is rigged by omission.** The article concedes its three-question battery "leans deliberately toward physical-side debts" and then neutralises the concession with bare assertion: "The asymmetry could be an artefact of question selection, but on inspection it appears to track a real difference." That "on inspection it appears real" is the entire defence of parity, and it is unargued. Decisively: the battery contains no line item that charges the Map's own cell. Two of the Map's heaviest liabilities are absent from the grid:

- **The Born-rule / statistical-indistinguishability dilemma** — that consciousness must bias post-decoherence outcomes while producing zero aggregate deviation from Born statistics, rendering the mechanism "empirically indistinguishable from chance" (the tenets page and the post-decoherence apex both concede this in stark terms). This is a first-order cost of the Map's Q1 cell. It appears **nowhere** in the cost overlay. A cost battery that omits its own author's confessed testability collapse is not measuring cost; it is measuring rivals.
- **Rejecting Many-Worlds by fiat (Tenet 4).** The companion taxonomy article admits an "Everettian *dualist* would be locatable" and that "the Map's quarrel with branching is not taxonomic but rests on the global-exclusion posit… a chosen starting point, not a result this grid delivers." No-MWI is therefore a free commitment: it is charged no cost anywhere on the grid, despite being a substantive metaphysical posit the physics-of-mind literature would price heavily.

This is the **bounded-Occam asymmetry** in action: the Map disarms parsimony as a truth-guide (Tenet 5) precisely where parsimony would count against it (No-MWI's ontological economy, Q4's costs), while retaining a cost-ranking that counts in its favour within the permitted region.

**Confession-without-correction is the article's dominant failure mode.** The text is saturated with elaborate self-disclosure — "an atlas of this kind is exactly where advocacy tends to creep in"; "the asymmetry could be an artefact"; "the Map's region is a posited commitment, not a proven conclusion." None of these confessions is converted into a binding change to the cost assignments. The Map confesses the battery may be biased, then keeps the biased battery. It confesses the disclaimer may be doing no work, then keeps the disclaimer as its signature. The disclosures function as epistemic indulgences purchased to license the unchanged apparatus. That the site's own changelog is a monument to correction-after-confession elsewhere makes the *absence* of correction here more damning, not less.

### (3) Counterarguments the article fails to anticipate

- **Illusionism (Frankish 2016; Dennett) is off the grid entirely — the single largest gap.** The mind-thickness axis has a *minimum* ("thin mind: only what is phenomenally introspectable — bare qualia"). Illusionism denies there is any phenomenal explanandum to place on that axis at all: phenomenal consciousness "as usually conceived" is an introspective illusion (Frankish, *JCS* 23(11–12):11–39). There is no cell for "the mind-axis has no floor because phenomenality is illusory." The grid therefore presupposes phenomenal realism and cannot even represent the strongest contemporary physicalist rival, let alone charge it a cost. The apex conflates the weak physicalist rival (Q2 inert qualia) with the absent strong one (no qualia). [PhilPapers](https://philpapers.org/rec/FRAIAA-4)
- **Predictive processing / active inference (Friston, Clark, Seth, Hohwy) as a total physicalist rival is unmentioned in the cartography.** The site has a `concepts/predictive-processing` page, but the atlas that purports to make "is dualism true?" navigable never registers the framework that most directly competes for the Map's own explanatory ground (attention, precision-weighting, endogenous control — exactly the Map's "willed attention" interface). Its absence means the grid's "physical-side" never includes the live functional-computational rival.
- **The Born-rule / statistical-indistinguishability dilemma is registered elsewhere but never charged on the grid** (see B1). This is the sharpest physicalist/epiphenomenalist pressure point — a causal power that by construction leaves no aggregate trace is indistinguishable from no causal power — and the cost overlay is silent on it for the Map's own cell.
- **The combination problem is not charged to Goff's cell.** Placing "priority cosmopsychism" in Q4 without noting the combination/decombination problem — Chalmers's "hard problem of panpsychism," Goff's central unresolved burden — is exactly the selective costing the battery claims to prevent.
- **Causal exclusion is named but the Map's escape is not stress-tested.** Kim appears as Q2's debt, but the Map's own route (quantum indeterminacy makes room for mental causation without overdetermination) inherits a version of the exclusion worry — if the physical base fixes the Born *probabilities*, in what sense is the mental selection not either redundant or a Born-violating addition? The grid does not press this on Q1.
- **"No-collapse but no-Many-Worlds" and objective-collapse rivals (GRW) are handled thinly.** The physical-thin axis mentions "closure only at the indeterminacy level," but objective-collapse theories that need no consciousness at all are a live rival to consciousness-caused actualisation and are not costed. Rejecting MWI "by fiat" (Tenet 4) is charged nowhere.
- **Phenomenal-concept strategy and higher-order theories** are engaged on the tenets page but do not appear in the cartography, so the atlas overstates its coverage of the physicalist terrain.

### (4) Taxonomy — does the grid smuggle the conclusion?

- **The constitutional-attractor effect is conceded by the site itself.** The tenets page's "Three Background Posits" section admits Tenet 1 (substance-leaning), Tenet 3, and Tenet 4 "all draw on this one root [the non-deflationary persisting subject]… the three are not mutually independent." Yet the apex asserts "No Many Worlds is largely orthogonal to the thickness axes." If dualism, interaction, and No-MWI descend from a single metaphysical commitment, the apparent spread of the Map's position across five independent tenets — and its clean landing in a distinct grid region — is artificial. The 2-D grid may be closer to 1-D (a single "how thick is the committed subject and its world" axis) than the orthogonality showcase admits.
- **Cell boundaries are drawn around the Map's own position.** "Medium mind" is defined as "a unified subject that persists and can intervene minimally, but stays anchored to the introspective horizon" — verbatim the Map's self-description ("physical-thin plus mind-medium"). Defining a grid category to fit your own view, then announcing your view lands cleanly in it, is carving the joints at convenient places.
- **Non-exhaustiveness.** Positions with no cell: illusionism/eliminativism (no phenomenal floor); all *reductive* physicalism, functionalism, GWT, IIT, higher-order theories (the grid "ranges over non-reductive positions," so every reductive rival is off-grid). This is fatal to the headline claim that the atlas makes "is dualism true?" navigable: the grid cannot represent most of the anti-dualist field, so it can adjudicate only *within* the dualist/non-reductive family. Also unplaced: Lowe's non-Cartesian substance dualism, Hasker-style emergent dualism.
- **The orthogonality argument is, however, genuinely good and should be retained.** Nida-Rümelin vs Descartes (same Chalmers Type-D, different mind-thickness) is a real discrimination the relation-type taxonomies miss. This is the article's one solid, independently-warranted contribution, and the article is honest that the discrimination is "heuristic rather than definitional."
- **The constrain-vs-establish gate is walked right up to and then crossed.** The article states the tenets "function as *constraints on a space* rather than as a derivation of a single position" — good. But the parsimony chapter then establishes that within that space the Map's cell is least-cost, and the synthesis closes with the Map's cell marked as the disciplined inhabitant. "Our tenets constrain the space" has become "the space shows our cell is the economical one" — an establishment result wearing a constraint's clothing.

## C. Bottom-line verdict

**REVISE-HARD overall; DEMOTE-TO-COHERENCE-ONLY for the cost-overlay component.**

The taxonomy (the grid, the orthogonality argument, the position inventory) is defensible scholarship and can be **RETAINED** with the co-optation fixes below. The **cost overlay** — the article's headline innovation — must be **DEMOTED-TO-COHERENCE-ONLY**: without a defined unit, scale, comparability condition, and aggregation rule, and without charging the Map's own Born-rule and No-MWI liabilities, it cannot be presented as a comparative measurement, only as an internally-coherent redescription of debts the Map already recognises. The **parsimony chapter's** conclusion ("least mechanism cost compatible with its tenets") should be **FLAGGED AS PERPETUALLY CONTESTED** until the parity problem is fixed, because as written it is the point where the disclaimed verdict re-enters. DELETE is not warranted — the piece is genuine, sourced, and self-critical — but the current framing earns none of the rigour its grid projects.

## D. Article-specific fixes (slug-keyed: /apex/dualism-cartography/)

1. **Define the cost metric or drop the word "cost."** State explicitly whether "mechanism cost" is ordinal or merely a partition; specify the comparability condition under which "Q1 cheaper than Q4" is meaningful; give the aggregation rule across the three questions. If none can be given, rename the overlay "debt inventory" and delete every comparative-magnitude claim ("heaviest," "cheapest," "lighter").
2. **Add two line items to the battery so the Map's own cell is charged:** (a) a "statistical-signature / Born-indistinguishability cost," which Q1-as-the-Map-inhabits-it pays heavily; (b) an "interpretive-commitment cost" that charges No-MWI (Tenet 4) the ontological/explanatory price the taxonomy article admits is "a chosen starting point." A battery that never charges the author's cell is not a battery.
3. **Fix the Goff co-optation.** Either remove "Goff's priority cosmopsychism" from the Q4 dualism roster or flag it in-text (as the taxonomy article does) as a monist limit-case included for contrast — Goff holds panpsychism "avoids the disunity of dualism" — and note the combination problem ("the hard problem of panpsychism") as its signature debt.
4. **Fix the Lycan stance-misframe.** Replace "Lycan, defending Cartesian interactive dualism, concedes…" with the accurate "Lycan, a committed materialist ('my materialism has never wavered'), concedes as a hostile witness…" The quotation is now verifiable verbatim against AJP 87(4) and can stand; the framing must not convert a materialist's honesty into a dualist's testimony.
5. **Downgrade the Masanes–Galley–Müller uniqueness claim.** Replace "the *unique* consistent probability rule" with language registering the live challenge (Kent, arXiv:2307.06191; Stacey, arXiv:2211.03299; MGM reply *Quantum* 9:1592, 2025) and the finite-dimensional-ensemble assumption. The Born-rule ceiling argument in the frontier section rests on this and currently overstates it.
6. **Register the empirical status of the frontier's "Tycho-analogue measurements."** State that (a) Kerskens & López Pérez is unreplicated and contested (Warren 2023) and that Wiest 2025 is a review, not an experiment; (b) the theta-band willed-attention result is a correlate of endogenous attention whose authors do not endorse the causal-consciousness reading; (c) Stapp's quantum-Zeno mechanism faces Georgiev's conceded no-go theorem. As written, these are banked as constraint-structure evidence at a firmness the 2026 literature does not support.
7. **Add an illusionism/anti-realism cell or explicitly bound the grid.** Either represent the "no phenomenal floor" position or state plainly that the grid presupposes phenomenal realism and therefore cannot adjudicate dualism against illusionism/eliminativism — which caps the headline "navigable" claim.
8. **Register the constitutional-attractor concession inline.** Since the sibling tenets page admits Tenets 1/3/4 share one root, the apex's "No Many Worlds is largely orthogonal" should be scoped or retracted, and the grid's apparent 2-D independence re-examined.

## E. Site-wide methodology improvements

1. **Add a calibration-parity linter to the mechanism-costs-cartography discipline** (`/project/mechanism-costs-cartography/`). Any deployment of the "fixed battery across cells" method must include at least one battery question on which the site's *own* preferred cell scores worst, and must prove the battery is not selected to exempt the house position.
2. **Promote a "confession→correction" gate in the review registers.** The corpus's dominant pattern here is elaborate self-disclosure banked as credential without a binding demotion. Require that every self-identified failure mode in an apex article be linked to a concrete edit, a demotion, or a `positions-evolve` entry — a confession with no diff attached should fail pre-publish.
3. **Extend the co-optation firewall from quote-stance to taxonomic-placement.** Existing checks catch stance at the sentence level but not at the grid level: Goff-the-monist appears in a dualist cell and Zheng-Meister-the-neuroscientist appears as interface evidence. Add a check that flags any named theorist placed in a cell whose defining commitment they publicly reject, and any empirical result recruited as "constraint" evidence beyond its authors' stated scope.
4. **Add an empirical-currency expiry to replication-sensitive citations.** Kerskens/Wiest, the theta-band result, Stapp/Georgiev, and Masanes-uniqueness are all fast-moving. Tag such citations with a re-verification cadence and a "contested / unreplicated / superseded" status field surfaced in-text, not buried in a research note.
5. **Distinguish "metric" from "partition" corpus-wide.** Several apex articles ("thickness-grid," "channel-width axis") use quantitative-sounding vocabulary ("thickness," "narrowness," "cost") for what are ordinal-at-best partitions. A house-style rule should forbid comparative-magnitude language unless a scale is defined.
6. **Guard against the Claude-on-Claude circular self-evaluation problem.** The cartography, its cost overlay, its parsimony case, and its self-audit are all produced and reviewed within the same model lineage under the same five tenets; the "independently argued / holds without the tenets" claims are graded by the system that authored them. External-referee sign-off (human or adversarial) should be required before any apex article may assert tenet-independence of its central result.
