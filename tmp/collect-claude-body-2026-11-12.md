# Referee Report: “The Four-Quadrant Taxonomy of Dualist Positions” (unfinishablemap.org/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/)

## Part 1 — TL;DR

**Headline verdict: REVISE-HARD. The self-placement section should be DEMOTED TO COHERENCE-ONLY, and the claim that Q1 is the Map’s home should be FLAGGED AS PERPETUALLY CONTESTED.** The thickness axes are not defined consistently. The article’s own placements break its definitions: epiphenomenalism, Descartes, Thomism, Goff and Stapp are each placed in ways the stated criteria rule out. Two of the “adjacent classifications” it cites do not exist in the cited works. The flagship Q1 inhabitant (Saad) is described as leaving closure “fully intact”, but Saad says in print that his theory must violate Closure. [nih](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12062107/)

- **Taxonomy (clear errors and judgment calls).** The mind axis switches between structural richness and causal work. The physical axis treats closure, interpretation and ontological enrichment as one thing. On its own definitions, epiphenomenalism with lean physics belongs in Q1, not Q2. Russellian cosmopsychism belongs in Q4, not Q3, because quiddities are the article’s own example of thick physics. Thomistic prime matter is pure potency, not “rich matter”. The same orthodox quantum theory with collapse is called “thick physics” when Stapp holds it and “thin physics” when the Map holds it. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/) That is the scheme leaning toward the Map’s own cell, not a neutral grid.
- **Citations (metadata mostly sound; stance and attribution layer failing).** Chalmers 2003 has no “rich vs austere Type-F” distinction; his actual split is phenomenal vs protophenomenal (panpsychism vs panprotopsychism). [consc](https://consc.net/papers/nature.html) Goff’s “rich vs austere panpsychism” could not be found in the 2017 book’s structure or its reviews. “Pereboom-style worries about qualitative inertness” has no reference entry and looks like a garbling of his “qualitative inaccuracy hypothesis”. [philpapers](https://philpapers.org/rec/PERCAT-2)[nd](https://ndpr.nd.edu/reviews/consciousness-and-the-prospects-of-physicalism/) Saad is misdescribed on closure, and the term “delegatory dualism”, which is Saad’s own coinage, is credited to the Map. [philpapers](https://philpapers.org/rec/SAAADT)[nih](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12062107/) Stapp’s “process-1” wording is not verbatim, and his channel is called “biasing”, an Eccles-style move that Stapp disowned in LBNL-55887. [lbl](https://www-physics.lbl.gov/~stapp/QID.pdf)[lbl](https://www-physics.lbl.gov/~stapp/repB.pdf) Kim (2005) is listed in the references but never used.
- **Counterarguments (mostly missing; where present, confessed rather than answered).** The article never engages Kim’s exclusion argument, the pairing problem, the Born-rule dilemma, the literature arguing that the substance/property line collapses, illusionism, or predictive processing. Its main defence is to admit that the axis is “heuristic rather than definitional” and that placements are “by judgement”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/) That is confession without correction. The Map’s thin-thin self-placement depends on an outcome-biasing channel. Faced with the Born-rule dilemma, that channel either adds law to physics (so physics is no longer thin) or slides toward ensemble-level epiphenomenalism (Q2, which the Map’s tenets forbid). The article does not mention this.

### (1) Taxonomy

**What the axes are.** The two axes are “mind-side thickness” and “physical-side thickness”. Each runs thin → medium → thick, and the article reads them as how much “ontological work” each side does. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/)

- **Thin mind:** only what is introspectable. No unperceived qualia-spaces and “no causal powers beyond what a property-dualist framework admits”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/)
- **Thick mind:** subliminal or cosmic mind, with “mental content vastly exceeding introspection”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/)
- **Thin physical:** “what physics says and nothing more”, “often paired with causal closure only at the indeterminacy level”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/)
- **Thick physical:** Bohmian hidden variables, Russellian quiddities, Stoljar-style unknown facts. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/)
- **Clarification:** max-physical means ontological, not spatial, enrichment. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/)

**A. The axes are not well defined (clear error).**

1. **Shifting senses on the mind axis.** The definition of thin mind includes a causal clause (“no causal powers beyond…”). Q2 places epiphenomenalism as min-mind “because the mental carries zero causal weight”. Yet §”Limits of the Thickness Metaphor” says the taxonomy tracks *structural richness* and “does not track causal work”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/) The axis cannot both exclude and depend on causal work. As written, it is incoherent.
2. **The physical axis merges closure with ontology.** Q2 is called max-physical “because physical closure is full”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/) Closure is a claim about causal completeness, not an enrichment of ontology. The article’s own clarification says max-physical means enriching physics “with quiddities, hidden variables, or ignorance-facts”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/) On that definition, standard epiphenomenalism over ordinary physics is *thin*-physical. Applying the article’s criteria, Huxley/Jackson-style epiphenomenalism belongs in Q1, not Q2. This is a clear error: internal contradiction.
3. **Tenet leakage into the thin-physical pole.** The thin-physical pole is described as “often paired with causal closure only at the indeterminacy level”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/) That is the Map’s Minimal Quantum Interaction tenet written into the definition of a pole. The scheme is then used to show that the Map sits at that pole.
4. **The medium/thin boundary on the physical side is empty in practice.** “What physics says and nothing more” (thin) and “standard quantum mechanics plus decoherence plus non-controversial structural facts” (medium) cannot be told apart by any stated criterion. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/) Nothing in physics as it stands goes beyond standard QM plus decoherence.

**B. The axes are not orthogonal or independent (judgment call, leaning toward error).** The article claims the thickness axis is orthogonal to substance/property and to Chalmers’ D/E/F. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/) It then defines thin mind partly by causal powers, which is exactly what separates Type-D from Type-E. So the axis partly re-encodes the D/E distinction the article says it cuts across. Its own defence is that the discrimination is “heuristic rather than definitional”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/) That concedes the axis is not a classification at all. It is a question-prompt, and it should not be presented as a four-cell partition.

**C. The cells are neither exhaustive nor mutually exclusive (clear error for several entries; judgment call elsewhere).**

- **Russellian monism appears in three cells.** Its “deflationary readings” go in Q1, “Russellian panpsychism with full scientific realism” in Q4, and Goff’s Russellian cosmopsychism in Q3. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/russellian-monism/) The article lists “Russellian intrinsic natures (quiddities)” as the paradigm of *thick* physics. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/) So every Russellian view, Goff’s included, is max-physical by definition, and Q1/Q3 filings of Russellian views contradict the axis definition. Goff’s Chapter 9 defends cosmopsychism as panpsychism combined with priority monism within Russellian monism. [oup +3](https://global.oup.com/academic/product/consciousness-and-fundamental-reality-9780190677015) Filing it as “min-physical” is a clear error against the article’s own criterion.
- **Chalmers sits in two cells.** “Chalmers’ naturalistic dualism paired with psychophysical laws” is listed as mind-medium, alongside a “unified subject that persists across acts”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/) Q2 then lists “some readings of Chalmers’ naturalistic dualism” as min-mind. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/) Chalmers 1996 is a property dualism with no persisting immaterial subject. Placing him in the “unified subject” band is a misfit (judgment call, leaning toward error), and the double placement goes unresolved.
- **Stapp is placed only by fiat.** The article concedes this (“by judgement rather than definition”) and defers to a third-axis article. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/) That is confession without correction: the grid as published still files him in Q4.
- **Same physics, opposite poles.** Stapp’s physics is orthodox von Neumann QM with Process 1 and collapse, and it is called “thick” (“full state-vector ontology, potentialities as real”). [lbl](https://www-physics.lbl.gov/~stapp/QID.pdf)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/) The Map’s physics is also orthodox QM with real collapse at indeterminacies plus the rejection of Everett, and it is called “thin”. Nothing in the stated criteria separates the two. This is a clear error of consistency, and it is the clearest sign that the grid is shaped around the Map’s own cell.
- **Missing from every cell.** Hasker’s emergent dualism (the site’s own interactionist-dualism page calls it “the closest dualist cousin”) does not appear. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/interactionist-dualism/) Neither do the following:

- Swinburne, Foster, Howard Robinson, Lowe’s non-Cartesian substance dualism, and Zimmerman;
- Popper–Eccles, Penrose–Hameroff and Plantinga;
- parallelism, occasionalism and pre-established harmony;
- overdeterminationist dualism (Mills, List & Stoljar, Kroedel — all listed by Saad), and predicate or event dualism. [springer](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s11098-025-02290-3.pdf)

Eccles leaving the grid is especially telling. He is the paradigm of mind-biased quantum outcomes, and his absence removes the obvious comparison point for the Map’s own “biasing agent at indeterminacy”.

**D. Placements of named philosophers and positions.**

- **Descartes as “mind-thick” (clear error as stated).** The article’s thick-mind criterion is “mental content vastly exceeding introspection”. Yet Descartes in the Fourth Replies calls it “self-evident” that there can be “nothing in the mind, in so far as it is a thinking thing, of which it is not aware”. This is the textbook transparency doctrine. [stanford](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/descartes-epistemology/)[stanford](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/consciousness-17th/) Gary Hatfield (“Transparency of Mind: The Contributions of Descartes, Leibniz, and Berkeley to the Genesis of the Modern Subject”, in Busche (ed.), *Departure for Modern Europe*, Felix Meiner 2011, pp. 361–375) argues that Descartes makes “systematic theoretical statements that directly countenance ‘unnoticed’ thoughts or mental states”, so a careful defence is possible. But the article gives none and simply asserts “substantial extra-introspective cognitive content”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/)
- **Thomistic hylomorphism as “rich form and rich matter … genuinely interacting” (clear error).** Thomistic prime matter is pure potency, the thinnest possible “physical side”. The soul–body relation is formal causation within one substance, not interaction between two realms. The site’s own hylomorphic-dualism page says hylomorphism “dissolves the interaction problem”, which contradicts the Q4 slogan “genuinely interacting”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/interactionist-dualism/)
- **Jaworski moved out of Q4.** Moving Jaworski’s structural hylomorphism out of Q4 is defensible and matches his stated view (judgment call). His *Structure and the Metaphysics of Mind* (OUP 2016) holds that “if mental phenomena are structural phenomena then they are uncontroversially part of the physical world”. Bradford McCall’s review (*Philosophical Quarterly* 67(267): 421–424, 2017) notes that “Jaworski’s form of hylomorphism rejects both physicalism and some tenets of emergentism”.
- **Nida-Rümelin as “capable of minimal causal intervention” and Type-D (misleading).** Her 2007 account of subject causation, in *Erkenntnis*, is described by its abstract as one that “does not touch the autonomy of neurophysiology” and is “compatible with a thesis of supervenience of the mental on the physical”. [springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10670-007-9064-z)[philpeople](https://philpeople.org/profiles/martine-nida-rumelin/publications) Recruiting her as an interactionist alongside Descartes overstates her view. The SEP’s report that she prefers “subject-body dualism” is accurately relayed. [stanford](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/dualism/)
- **Stoljar in a taxonomy of dualist positions (judgment call, leaning toward error).** The article notes his physicalist aim but still files him as a Q2 inhabitant, [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/) and companion pages charge the Q2 cell with dualist debts. The site’s own research note concedes this filing is defective. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/research/ignorance-hypothesis-2026-10-03/)
- **Saad as the Q1 exemplar (misleading on two counts).**

- The article says Saad keeps “microphysical closure … fully intact” and that “the physical cause stays sufficient”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/) Saad writes that “delegatory dualism must posit violations of Closure in order to respect Non-Overdetermination and Efficacy”. His physical states are “nomic guarantors”, explicitly *not* sufficient causes. He keeps only *Observational* Closure. [springer](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s11098-025-02290-3.pdf)
- The article’s thin-mind criterion forbids “unperceived qualia-spaces”. Saad answers his fourth objection by positing “a large and varied ensemble of experiential properties, many of which are uninstantiated”. [springer](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s11098-025-02290-3.pdf) Uninstantiated experiential properties with fixed causal profiles are, if anything, the article’s own “unperceived qualia-spaces”. Saad’s mind-thinness is contestable on the article’s own terms.
- **Kastrup, Berkeley and the Myers–James tradition.** Treating these as monist limit cases or historical templates is acceptable (judgment call).

**E. Fit with standard classifications.**

- **Chalmers’ Type-A–F taxonomy.** The article uses this correctly, including the point that Type-F is monism. [consc](https://consc.net/papers/nature.html)[mit](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/24-09-minds-and-machines-fall-2011/61d30a9a655c7a965ff8e1733cacaae9_MIT24_09F11_lec23.pdf)
- **Substance/property.** The article asserts this line is orthogonal to thickness but ignores a well-known literature arguing the line collapses: the SEP lists Francescotti 2001, Searle 2002, Zimmerman 2010, Schneider 2012, Lycan 2013, Robinson 2016 and Weir 2023. [stanford](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/dualism/) Zimmerman (Aristotelian Society Supplementary Volume 84, 2010) argues that the commitments behind property dualism lead to substance dualism. [philpeople](https://philpeople.org/profiles/dean-zimmerman/publications) If that is right, “thin-mind property dualism” is unstable, and the thinness is an artefact of the property framing.
- **Interactionism / epiphenomenalism / parallelism.** This standard trichotomy is not represented; parallelism has no cell.
- **Originality.** The scheme appears to be original to the site, and it does not claim any precedent. I found no prior two-axis “thickness” taxonomy in the standard literature, but that is UNVERIFIED rather than established.
- **Overall.** The scheme distorts more than it simplifies. Its “adjacent classifications” paragraph also credits distinctions to Chalmers and Goff that they do not draw (see table).

## Part 5 — Site-wide methodology improvements

1. **Enforce a citation for every comparative claim.** Every “X’s distinction between A and B (year)” must carry a page-pinned quotation in the research note before publication. Here, three named distinctions (Chalmers, Goff, Pereboom) had no textual basis. This is the same performative-citation failure the site already guards against for direct quotations, now appearing in paraphrase.
2. **Add an author-stance gate for constraint-builders.** When a stance-neutral author such as Saad is used as an exemplar, require a sentence stating which of the author’s constraints the Map’s use keeps and which it drops (here: Observational Closure kept, full Closure violated, quantum interactionism outside the project). This is the boundary between Saad’s position and the Map’s, and it failed in three sibling pages.
3. **Let correctness override the word limit.** The site’s hard length cap blocked a known stance correction (the Stoljar Q2 filing) in favour of a “zero-word pipe” [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/research/ignorance-hypothesis-2026-10-03/) for over a month. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/research/ignorance-hypothesis-2026-10-03/) Corrections of clear errors should be exempt from length gates, or the gate should force cuts elsewhere in the same commit.
4. **Run a consistency check on classifications.** Maintain one machine-readable register of each position’s cell and check every page that mentions a cell against it. The Map’s own placement currently differs between pages (“thin on both axes” vs “physical-thin plus mind-medium”). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/interactionist-dualism/)
5. **Apply evidential-status labels to taxonomic claims.** Tag each placement and the self-placement with the compatible / suggestive / discriminating ladder from apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy. A self-placement that depends on an unresolved mechanism should be labelled “compatible” and presented as coherence-only.
6. **Make confessions produce changes.** Any “this is heuristic” or “placed by judgement” admission must come with either removing the categorical verdict or adding the missing criterion in the same edit. Otherwise it is inoculation by confession.
7. **Separate definitions from tenets.** Check every axis or pole definition for wording that restates a tenet (here, MQI in the thin-physical pole). A taxonomy that encodes a tenet cannot then be used to show that the tenet’s holder sits in a favoured cell.
8. **Require rival-explanation tests for empirical anchors.** Wherever altered states, selfhood or dissociation are cited as “anchors” for thick mind, the strongest physicalist rival (predictive processing / active inference, including Laukkonen–Friston–Chandaria 2025) must be stated and tested against, not left out.
9. **Change who reviews.** The internal adversarial review in the Claude Code agentic workflow evidently re-verifies internal quotations and metadata well, [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/reviews/optimistic-2026-08-22-rival-route-wing/) but it missed attributions that a domain expert would catch immediately (Chalmers’ Type-F split, Thomistic prime matter, Descartes’ transparency). Claude-on-Claude circular review shares blind spots with the drafting model. Add (a) a review stage run by a different model family or a human specialist, scoped to stance and attribution, and (b) a rule that internal reviewers may not count the site’s own research notes as independent confirmation of an external author’s stance.
10. **Fix changelog visibility.** The public changelog served on 12 November 2026 stops at 2026-10-15, while pages carry November modification dates. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/) Publish per-article edit summaries on the article page, or paginate the changelog with stable archive URLs, so external auditors can see what changed in the audited version.
11. **Add a “gerrymander test” for new taxonomies.** Before publishing any new taxonomy, file two rival positions that share the Map’s physics (here Stapp and Eccles). If the grid separates them only by judgement, the grid should not be used for self-placement.
