## A. TL;DR

- **Headline verdict: REVISE-HARD, thesis DEMOTE-TO-COHERENCE-ONLY.** The inference from entanglement to phenomenal unity rests on a structural-isomorphism premise the article never states and never defends. The article's own Leibniz concession defeats that premise. Under interactionist dualism the premise is also explanatorily idle: if unity belongs to the non-physical subject, physical non-separability adds nothing. The site's own binding-void page says unity is "what consciousness adds."
- **Physics and tenet consistency fail at the load-bearing joint.** The lead states that entanglement is "genuine ontological holism where the whole is prior to the parts" as fact. That is Schaffer-style priority monism, not physics, and the article's later confession does not correct it. The BP2 mechanism needs coherent neural entanglement, which is the pre-decoherence path the tenets demote to "live fallback." The decoherence section escapes by retreating to post-decoherence selection, and that retreat abandons the holistic state the thesis needs. The Bidirectional paragraph asserts "downward causal efficacy" in defiance of the tenets page's explicit instruction to carry the P-Q3 epiphenomenalism debt.
- **Citations: metadata is mostly sound; stance and descriptive fidelity are not.** Neven et al. 2024 is co-opted. It is "firmly rooted in Everett's 'many worlds' formulation" and places experience at superposition formation, not collapse, and the phrase "only true binding agent" is not in the paper. Khan et al. 2024 is misdescribed: it used a within-subject pre/post design (N = 8), not controls, and was published in August, not September. Warren 2023 is understated. The Schlosshauer "mixture" wording is misattributed. Laukkonen, Friston & Chandaria 2025, which offers "Bayesian binding" and an account of minimal states, is ignored, so the article fails the standing gate. Tye, Bayne 2010, Lockwood, Marshall, Chalmers & McQueen and the philosophy-of-physics holism literature (Teller, Healey, Esfeld, Maudlin) are all absent.

## B. Dimensional Analysis

### (1) Physics accuracy

**Holism is overstated in the load-bearing places.** The lead asserts: "Quantum entanglement provides something structurally different: genuine ontological holism where the whole is prior to the parts." The meta-description repeats it: "holism where wholes precede parts." [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/quantum-holism-and-phenomenal-unity/) What the formalism delivers is narrower. A pure entangled state of a composite system is not a product state, so the joint state is not fixed by the subsystems' reduced states. That is non-separability of states, or in Teller's terms ("Relational Holism and Quantum Mechanics," *BJPS* 37(1):71–81, 1986) relational holism: a relation that "does not supervene upon the non-relational properties of its relata," which quantum mechanics exhibits "on at least some interpretations." It is not priority of the whole.

"The whole is prior to the parts" is Schaffer's priority monism. Schaffer's own entanglement argument generalises to the cosmos as the only fundamental whole, which is precisely the boundary problem the article never faces. Esfeld's moderate ontological holism keeps the parts. Subsystems persist, with well-defined reduced density matrices; what fails is supervenience of the whole's properties on theirs. Healey's and Maudlin's treatments likewise separate non-separability from any claim that the parts are unreal.

To its credit, the body's "Why Quantum Entanglement Matches Unity" section concedes that "statistical, relational, and Everettian interpretations accept the same non-separability without granting metaphysical oneness, so the step from formalism to 'one thing' is an interpretive commitment, not a datum of physics." [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/quantum-holism-and-phenomenal-unity/) That confession is accurate. It is not propagated: the lead, the meta-description, "Classical physics provides only external relations," and "genuinely one thing rather than many coordinated things" all still carry the uncorrected claim. The sibling concepts/unity-of-consciousness goes further, saying "entanglement produces systems that were never fully separate." [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/unity-of-consciousness/) That is false as stated, since entangled subsystems are separately definable.

**Entanglement is conflated with neural coherence.** The decoherence section treats coherence lifetimes as if they were lifetimes of the relevant entanglement. Decoherence does not destroy entanglement. It is entanglement: the system becomes entangled with its environment. So after decoherence, the non-separable whole is brain plus environment, and it is not bounded by the skull or by the subject. The article's picture needs entanglement restricted to the neural degrees of freedom that carry the bound contents. That requires coherence isolated from the environment, which is exactly what Tegmark disputes. The article never distinguishes "entanglement exists" (trivially true, and ubiquitous) from "entanglement confined to the subject's content-bearing substrate persists" (the contested claim).

**Monogamy and the boundary problem are not mentioned.** Entanglement is ubiquitous and monogamous. Maximal entanglement between two subsystems excludes entanglement with a third. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monogamy_of_entanglement) The pattern of entanglement therefore does not track the boundaries of a single mind. Something else has to fix which non-separable whole counts as a subject. IIT at least has an exclusion postulate for this job. The quantum-holism proposal has nothing comparable, and the article's IIT critique ignores this comparative disadvantage.

**Baum's argument is mis-diagnosed at the physics level.** The article rightly cites Vicente et al. 2008 against Baum's classical-impossibility premise. It still frames the dilemma as "either violate relativity or invoke quantum non-locality," as though quantum non-locality were an available resource for phase-locking distant oscillators. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/quantum-holism-and-phenomenal-unity/) It is not. The no-signalling theorem means Bell correlations cannot coordinate firing times. Neven et al. themselves stress that "entanglement cannot be employed to achieve faster-than-light communication." [escholarship](https://escholarship.org/content/qt85q225bk/qt85q225bk.pdf) Baum's argument fails on both horns, not one.

**Decoherence figures are accurate.** The subagent verified at arXiv that Tegmark's 10⁻¹³–10⁻²⁰ s and Hagan et al.'s 10⁻⁵–10⁻⁴ s match the sources. [arxiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0005025)[arxiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9907009) The 8–9 orders of magnitude gap is correct against Tegmark's microtubule figure. The article omits Hagan et al.'s further speculative 10⁻²–10⁻¹ s under actin-gel ordering. [arxiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0005025) The sibling topics/quantum-biology-neural-experimental-turn says Hagan "extended estimates by seven orders of magnitude," [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/quantum-biology-neural-experimental-turn/) which conflicts with the target's "eight to nine."

### (2) Inference validity and the dualism tension

Reconstructed, the core argument runs:

1. Phenomenal unity is intrinsic singularity, not a coordinated plurality.
2. *(Suppressed)* Phenomenal unity requires a physical correlate with matching mereological structure (the isomorphism or structural-mirroring premise).
3. Classical physics offers only external relations among separate parts.
4. Entanglement offers non-separable wholes.
5. Therefore entanglement is the physical correlate of phenomenal unity.

Premise 2 is never stated, let alone argued. It is also not Chalmers's principle of structural coherence, which maps phenomenal structure onto the structure of awareness or information, not onto mereological non-separability. Without premise 2, premise 4 is irrelevant to premise 1.

The article then concedes, under "The Remaining Gap," that "The Leibniz argument, at full strength, denies that *any* physical structure—entanglement included—can *constitute* phenomenal unity." It retreats to "analogical and locational, not constitutive." [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/quantum-holism-and-phenomenal-unity/) An analogy is not an explanation. A mere resemblance cannot "address the structure-matching problem that defeats all classical approaches," as the lead still claims. Resemblance alone generates no such problem; only premise 2 does. Once premise 2 is given up, the classical failure the article diagnoses is not a failure at all. That is the inference's fatal equivocation.

**The dualism dilemma is not faced.**

- *Horn 1.* If unity is a feature of the non-physical subject, as the tenets page implies when it lists "the unity of consciousness" among the positive arguments for dualism, then physical holism is idle. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/) The subject is unified whatever the physics. The site's own voids/binding-void says: "unity is not what computation does but what consciousness adds." [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/binding-void/) The cross-modal-capability-division apex, as described by the interoception research note (not fetched directly), assigns "phenomenal unity of a perceived object" to the supramodal *mind* side. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/research/interoceptive-consciousness-and-the-interface-2026-06-20/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/research/interoceptive-consciousness-and-the-interface-2026-06-20/)
- *Horn 2.* If unity needs physical non-separability, the mind's unity is inherited from the physics. That cannibalises the unity argument for dualism: unity becomes a physically explicable feature, not evidence of irreducibility.

The article sits on both horns at once. "Classical Mechanisms Remain Necessary" states "Both are necessary; neither is sufficient alone." "Relation to Site Perspective" states "The unity of experience reflects the unity of the underlying quantum state." [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/quantum-holism-and-phenomenal-unity/)

**The "co-occurrence" fallback is a dual-aspect slide.** "The same event that is physically described as entangled state collapse is phenomenologically described as unified experience." [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/quantum-holism-and-phenomenal-unity/) That is dual-aspect or Russellian-monist identity language, the Lockwood and Goff register, not interactionist dualism. Interactionism needs two relata and a causal interface, not one event under two descriptions. The sentence smuggles in the identity premise the Leibniz paragraph has just disavowed.

**Comparison classes are missing.**

- *Lockwood* (Mind, Brain and the Quantum, 1989) is the canonical quantum-holism-for-unity account, and it is Everettian (many-minds).
- *Marshall* ("Consciousness and Bose-Einstein condensates," *New Ideas in Psychology* 7(1):73–83, 1989) argued "from the unity and complexity of states of consciousness that no classical physical system could play the role." He is the historical originator of the entanglement-binding argument, cited as such even by pro-quantum literature. [arxiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1809.03490)
- *Neven et al.* are Everettian. [escholarship](https://escholarship.org/content/qt85q225bk/qt85q225bk.pdf)
- *Chalmers & McQueen* ("Consciousness and the collapse of the wave function," in Gao (ed.), *Consciousness and Quantum Mechanics*, OUP 2022) combine "integrated information theory... with... continuous spontaneous localization," while professing "considerable sympathy... especially with many-worlds interpretations." They meet the very integration-versus-non-separability question, including a Q-shape test the site knows about (topics/consciousness-collapse-versus-decoherence-experiments cites it).
- *Hameroff–Penrose Orch OR* is the only collapse-realist entanglement account the article engages, and it is a gravitational-OR physicalist-panprotopsychist view, not dualism.
- *Stapp's* orthodox-QM dualism does not use holism for unity at all.

The article therefore places itself in a genre whose best-developed members either reject Tenet 4 or reject dualism, and it does not say so.
