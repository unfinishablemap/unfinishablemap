## 2. DIMENSIONAL ANALYSIS

### 2.1 The core inference — staged dissolution vs. unconfronted deflationary rivals (Constrain-vs-Establish gate)

The article's central inference: the staggered disengagement of attention, imagery-control, agency, and reality-monitoring "suggests the interface comprises semi-independent channels rather than a single coupling mechanism," mapping onto the three coupling-modes (basis / timing / probability control).

Credit where due: the article confronts the **prefrontal disinhibition** account and states plainly that "the filter model and the disinhibition account make overlapping predictions... which limits how much the phenomenological data alone can adjudicate between them," and that "the Map favours the filter interpretation because it coheres with the broader case." That is a genuine constrain-vs-establish concession.

It does not go far enough, and the omission is non-random — the *weaker* rival is confronted while the two *stronger* ones are absent:

**(a) Predictive processing / active inference (a single-mechanism defeater).** A single deflationary mechanism — graded precision-attenuation over one hierarchical generative model — predicts the very staggered timelines the article treats as evidence for distinct channels. Under active inference: sleep onset withdraws precision from sensory prediction-errors first (attentional release/drift); top-down priors then dominate (autonomous imagery); NREM's low-cholinergic state reduces the precision of likelihood mappings *between* hierarchical levels, taking the "temporally deep" level offline (agency/self-model loosening); and reality-monitoring failure is the collapse of the internal/external discriminator that precision-weighting normally maintains. One functional architecture, four staggered signatures, **no dualist commitment and no distinct channels**.

This is not an exotic outside rival — the Map's own `concepts/consciousness-selecting-neural-patterns` cites **Sandved-Smith, Hesp, Mattout, Friston, Lutz & Ramstead, "Towards a computational phenomenology of mental action: modelling meta-awareness and attentional control with deep parametric active inference," *Neuroscience of Consciousness* 2021(1), niab018 (26 Aug 2021)** — which casts "mental action as policy selection over higher-level cognitive states" and models "meta-awareness states that modulate the expected confidence (precision) in the mapping between observations and hidden cognitive states" — and explicitly calls active inference "the strongest physicalist rival for this section's account, confronted here as such." The hypnagogia article confronts a weaker rival (disinhibition) while ignoring the one the corpus elsewhere concedes is strongest. That is a **calibration asymmetry between siblings** and a failure to hold the rival the Map's own standards require. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/consciousness-selecting-neural-patterns/)

**(b) Global neuronal workspace fragmentation.** Progressive loss of long-range integration/ignition capacity predicts that functions dependent on different hub subnetworks disengage at different rates as workspace broadcast breaks down at sleep onset — again one mechanism yielding staggered loss, no channels, no dualism. Per **Mashour, Roelfsema, Changeux & Dehaene, "Conscious Processing and the Global Neuronal Workspace Hypothesis," *Neuron* 105(5), 776–798 (4 March 2020)**, the GNW "proposes that, in the conscious state, a non-linear network ignition associated with recurrent processing amplifies and sustains a neural representation, allowing the corresponding information to be globally accessed by local processors"; the graded withdrawal of that ignition capacity is a single-architecture account of staggered dissolution.

Because a single deflationary architecture predicts the same staggered timelines, the phenomenological ordering **non-discriminatingly fits both readings**. Under the site's own constrain-vs-establish gate, the evidence at most *constrains* physicalism (rules out a naive binary switch — which no serious physicalist theory holds) and does not *establish* a multi-channel dualist interface. "Against Binary Models" knocks down a strawman; "For Multi-Channel Interface" then presents the graded data as positive support — the exact gate failure the site names.

### 2.2 Sequencing claims against the current literature — are the four timelines really separable?

The explanandum — "attention typically first, then voluntary imagery, then agency, then reality-monitoring" — is **not** an established empirical sequencing result, and the literature does not robustly support four separable timelines:

- **Hori's 9-stage EEG staging** (correctly cited as the granular standard) correlates *imagery type and voluntary controllability* with EEG substage: content becomes "increasingly hallucinatory and unamenable to voluntary control as EEG patterns shift from an alpha EEG to a spindling EEG." Per **Hayashi, Katoh & Hori, "Hypnagogic Imagery and EEG Activity," *Perceptual and Motor Skills* 88(2), 676–678 (April 1999)**, 7 subjects reported imagery each minute across Hori substages, and "the content of the hypnagogic imagery changed as a function of the hypnagogic EEG stages." Hori-system data show gradedness — e.g., the sensation of "thinking" declining progressively from ~50.5% in stages H1–H2 to ~2.5% by H9 — and Nielsen concluded "kinaesthetic experiences occur earlier than visual experiences over the course of sleep-onset, consistent with Hori et al. (1994)." This supports **continuous gradedness indexed to one EEG progression**, not a clean four-channel dissociation with separable onsets.
- **Nielsen's covert-REM model** and the microdream literature establish that sleep stages are "fluid, dissociable clusters of processes, with overlaps and admixtures" — which cuts *both* ways: it supports gradedness while undermining any claim of a robust, ordered four-step sequence. [Academia.edu](https://www.academia.edu/8309176/The_Covert_REM_Sleep_Processes_Model_of_Dreaming_and_Protoconsciousness_2014_)
- **Reality-monitoring/metacognition** dissociates — **Filevich, Dresler, Brick & Kühn, "Metacognitive Mechanisms Underlying Lucid Dreaming," *Journal of Neuroscience* 35(3), 1082–1088 (21 Jan 2015)** found "greater gray matter volume in the frontopolar cortex (BA9/10)" in high- vs low-lucidity participants, and Voss et al. (2013) link lucidity to secondary-consciousness metacognition — but that is a *different state* (REM lucidity), not a hypnagogic reality-monitoring onset reliably following agency loss.
- **Variability is high**, and the ordering is inferred from **retrospective report gathered as reality-monitoring was already degrading**. The article concedes this ("read as suggestive rather than established"). Good — but "For Multi-Channel Interface" and the MQI section then treat the sequence as a fixed architecture ("traces the interface from its most active components... to its most passive"). No cited study measures the four components' onsets with effect sizes, sample-level timing, or replication; the ordering traces to Mavromatis's phenomenological catalogue and Thompson's philosophical framing. The sequence is a **narrative**, and the multi-channel inference is built on it.

### 2.4 CO-OPTATION FIREWALL FAILURE (Thompson) — the load-bearing defect

The article recruits Thompson (2014) for four propositions:

1. hypnagogia "progressively dissolves ego structure — the sense of being a distinct agent";
2. "the self that acts withdraws before the self that witnesses";
3. advanced practitioners "report a state where phenomenal awareness persists while voluntary attention, agency, and reality-monitoring have all withdrawn," which "demonstrate that consciousness can operate through only the receiving channel of the interface — experiencing without acting";
4. this "supports the view that consciousness is not identical to any particular brain configuration."

Proposition 1 is faithful to Thompson's ego-dissolution account (his verbatim: "this structure dissolves in the hypnagogic state"). Proposition 2 is a paraphrase, not verifiable verbatim text. **Propositions 3 and 4 invert his position.** Thompson co-founded enactivism (*The Embodied Mind*, Varela/Thompson/Rosch); in *Waking, Dreaming, Being* and the companion paper "Dreamless Sleep, the Embodied Mind, and Consciousness" (*Open MIND* 37(T), Metzinger & Windt eds., 2015, doi:10.15502/9783958570351), he holds the self is "an experiential process, not a thing or an entity" that "enacts" rather than refers to a substance, and he states flatly there is "no evidence for the kind of dualism... according to which a mental continuum can exist apart from physical nature." He explicitly designs his handling of OBE/NDE/witness material to *temper* "the claim that consciousness does not depend upon the brain." He is neither dualist nor reductive materialist — a non-dualist naturalist who denies exactly the brain-independent, substance-like consciousness the Map's "receiving channel of the interface" requires. [Science and Nonduality + 3](https://scienceandnonduality.com/article/waking-dreaming-being/)

Recruiting Thompson's witness-consciousness phenomenology as evidence that "consciousness is not identical to any particular brain configuration" and can "operate through only the receiving channel of the interface" is the textbook **co-optation firewall failure**: a stance-rejecting author's descriptive phenomenology bent toward the metaphysical conclusion his own work repudiates. It is not peripheral — Thompson is the *sole* cited authority for the "Contemplative Approaches" section and the witness-consciousness plank, which the article's own "What Would Challenge This View" section calls "the strongest evidence for consciousness persisting while the interface partially disengages."

Compounding this is an **evidential-independence failure**: "Tibetan dream yoga and Hindu yoga nidra," "advanced practitioners report," and "Thompson (2014, ch. 6–7)" are presented as converging streams. They are not independent — Thompson is the conduit for the contemplative reports, and the traditions are his sources, not separate confirmations.

### 2.5 Tenet leakage, constitutional-attractor effect, epistemic-to-metaphysical slide

**Tenet leakage is pervasive and begins with the title** ("Interface Modulation"). The supposedly neutral exposition is saturated with framework-internal vocabulary — "interface," "channel," "coupling," "loosening," "receiving channel," "transmitting channel," "basis control / timing control / probability reweighting." "Attentional Release" does not describe attention withdrawing; it describes "the first component of the interface to disengage," immediately routed through coupling-modes. There is no interface-neutral layer from which the interface reading is then argued; the phenomenology is pre-loaded with the conclusion.

This is the **constitutional-attractor effect** in clean form: the multi-channel reading is not independently discovered from the data and then matched to the tenets — it is drawn *from* the coupling-modes tenet structure (basis/timing/probability) and projected onto the phenomenology, then presented as "mapping the architecture of consciousness–brain coupling with unusual clarity." The article concedes the mapping is speculative ("could map onto," "may correspond to," "if probability control operates at all") while retaining the architectural conclusion.

**Epistemic-to-metaphysical slide:** the honest core claim is epistemic — reports of graded dissolution and an asymmetry between vivid reception and near-zero voluntary output. This is inflated into a metaphysical claim about graded *coupling* through distinct *channels*. The article half-catches itself ("reduced voluntary output at sleep onset has a standard physiological explanation — motor gating plus disinhibited imagery generation — that does not need distinct channels") — then proceeds to the two-channel reading anyway. The **"No Many Worlds" section is the sharpest slide**: "the felt difference between moments of agency and moments of passivity supports collapse interpretations where selection occurs or fails, rather than branching interpretations." A phenomenal contrast between feeling-in-control and feeling-passive has no evidential bearing on whether uninstantiated branches exist; an epistemic phenomenological datum is inflated into an interpretation-of-QM verdict.

### 2.7 Over-servicing Tenet 2/3 and the Born-rule multiplication problem (the structural finding)

The site's working hypothesis is "corridor dualism (Born-rule-preserving)... posited to average to exactly |⟨φ|ψ⟩|² over the ensemble." With *one* selection channel, the corridor constraint is discharged once. If the interface is genuinely **three semi-independent channels** (basis/timing/probability) each disengaging on its own timeline, then each is an independent locus at which token-level efficacy must be reconciled with type-level Born invariance — and the "probability control" channel the article speculates "may be the most robust, persisting into sleep" is *by name* the one that most directly threatens Born statistics. The graded/multi-channel reading, if accepted, therefore **worsens** the framework's deepest problem, and the article presents it as confirmation while noticing none of this.
