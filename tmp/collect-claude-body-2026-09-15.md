## 1. TL;DR — Headline Verdict

**REVISE-HARD.** By the Map's own standards this is one of its more disciplined articles — its citation metadata is spotless and it repeatedly uses genuine constrain-not-establish language ("a cost the Map assesses, not a refutation it delivers"). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/pragmatism/) But it (a) straw-mans the pragmatist theory of truth as "truth is what works," a formula living pragmatists spend their careers repudiating; (b) commits a textbook co-optation firewall failure on Dewey that it *confesses without correcting*; (c) silently omits the entire deflationary/anti-metaphysical wing of contemporary pragmatism (Price, Misak, Brandom, Wright) whose programme would *dissolve* the article's framing rather than compete with it; and (d) stages the single sharpest objection against itself ("The Maxim Turned Inward") and then answers it with precisely the move the pragmatic maxim rules out — banking the disclosure as epistemic credit instead of converting it into a status change.

- **Citations are metadata-clean but stance-dirty.** All 11 external references check out at publisher of record; every failure is Layer 3 (author-stance), concentrated on Dewey, Peirce and Rorty.
- **The strongest available pragmatist objection is mishandled twice.** The maxim's meaning-eliminative bite against an in-principle-undetectable quantum bias is raised, conceded "open," and the mechanism then "retained… with that debt showing." That is confession-without-correction, the site's signature pathology.
- **Two standing blind spots are live:** the Peircean convergence/end-of-inquiry account of truth (Misak) is absent, and active inference (Laukkonen, Friston & Chandaria 2025) — the strongest physicalist account of belief-as-action-guiding, squarely on this article's topic — is absent.

### 2.2 The straw-manned theory of truth (constrain-vs-establish; calibration asymmetry)

The lead: pragmatism "rejects the idea that truth consists in mirroring an independent reality. Instead, **truth is what works**." This is the crude-instrumentalist reading. It ignores:

- **The Peircean convergence account** (verbatim, 1878, quoted above) — a realist, non-instrumentalist notion of getting-it-right.
- **Misak's rehabilitation** (*Truth and the End of Inquiry: A Peircean Account of Truth*, Oxford UP, 1991; expanded ed. 2004): truth is "what we would agree upon, were inquiry to be pursued as far as it could fruitfully go." Misak casts James, Dewey and Rorty as the "villains" who at their most incautious claim inquiry "aims not at the truth, but at what works for me," and Peirce and C. I. Lewis as the heroes who insist inquirers "aim to get things right." **The article adopts precisely the villain's formula and attributes it to the founders wholesale.**

Because the convergence account never appears, the later reflexive argument never has to confront a pragmatist holding a robust notion of getting-it-right. The rival is weakened at the door — a calibration asymmetry.

### 2.3 Co-optation firewall failure on Dewey (confession-without-correction)

Offending passage, verbatim:

> "Dewey himself was a committed naturalist who would have resisted the dualist conclusion — but the logic of his position outruns his commitments. If inquiry is not passive reception but active engagement, then the inquirer's conscious experience… is constitutive of the process." [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/pragmatism/)

This is the canonical inversion. Dewey's transactional account of experience (*Experience and Nature*, 1925; his critique of the "spectator theory of knowledge"; "warranted assertibility" in *Logic*, 1938) was built *precisely* to dissolve mind-body dualism without reductive physicalism — a third option the article never represents. Dewey "specifically criticizes the body–mind dualism," [Frontiers](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/education/articles/10.3389/feduc.2026.1894619/xml) reconstructing such dualisms "as parts of larger continuities" (SEP, *John Dewey*). The article **names** that Dewey would reject the use ("would have resisted") and then **makes the use anyway** ("the logic of his position outruns his commitments"). Naming the defect precisely and proceeding regardless is inoculation-by-confession: the disclosure is banked as candor rather than converted into a same-commit status change. The Deweyan objection — that the dualism/physicalism dispute is a false dichotomy his naturalism was designed to escape — is absorbed, not answered.

### 2.4 The absent anti-metaphysical wing (the framing-undercut objection)

The brief flagged Huw Price as the acute co-optation risk. The article does **not** co-opt Price — because it never mentions him, nor Misak, Brandom, Sellars or Wright. This is not neutral. Price's subject naturalism and global expressivism (*Naturalism Without Mirrors*, OUP 2011; *Expressivism, Pragmatism and Representationalism*, CUP 2013) are an explicitly anti-representationalist, anti-metaphysical programme whose central claim is that the "placement problems" (how mind, meaning and value fit into the natural world) are artefacts of representationalism and dissolve once representationalism is dropped. The Map's entire dualism-vs-materialism contest **is** a placement problem in Price's sense; a working subject naturalist would say the article's foundational question is malformed. By omitting this wing the article:

- dodges the pragmatist **dissolution** objection except in the narrow Rortyan form it can frame as a mere standoff ("which framework one occupies settles the verdict"); [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/pragmatism/)
- treats pragmatism as an **epistemology** (a criterion for theory choice) while sidestepping pragmatism as a **theory of meaning** — which is exactly where the maxim's meaning-eliminative bite lives (§2.5).

This is the evidential-independence failure in reverse: the objection that would undercut the framing rather than compete inside it is the one that is missing.

### 2.5 The maxim turned inward — confession-without-correction on the sharpest objection

Credit where due: the article raises, unprompted, the strongest objection against the site — apply Peirce's maxim to Minimal Quantum Interaction and "the difference between a consciousness-biased quantum outcome and an unbiased one threatens to come out merely verbal — which is precisely the verdict the maxim was invoked to deliver against materialism." [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/pragmatism/) This is the "difference that makes no difference" the maxim rules out as meaningless, and it is the sharpest available pragmatist objection.

But the reply fails on Peirce's own terms:

> "The Map's answer is that the practical difference is per-trial and indexical rather than statistical: *which* outcome this experiencing subject actualises is a difference even where the aggregate distribution records none." [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/pragmatism/)

By the maxim, the meaning of a conception is the sum of its *conceivable* practical/experiential effects. If the bias is by construction indistinguishable "under any unconditioned aggregate test current or foreseeable instruments could run," [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/pragmatism/) and the per-trial "which outcome" difference is identifiable by *no* possible experience, then per Peirce the biased and unbiased conceptions are identical in meaning. The article then concedes: "Whether that discharges the maxim or merely relocates the problem is open… The maxim is retained here with that debt showing rather than applied in one direction only." [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/pragmatism/) That is the constitutional-attractor effect operating through disclosure: mechanism retained, objection logged, no status change in the same commit. The honest verdict — that on its own criterion the posit is meaning-eliminated in the aggregate register — is named and declined. (The article's gesture toward a *conditioned* deviation at P-Q3 is a genuine escape hatch, but the article itself concedes it is unmeasured and that the mechanism "sits genuinely close to epiphenomenalism," so it does not discharge the maxim now.)

### 2.6 Standing blocking gate — active inference is absent

The article makes conscious *evaluation* ("the felt recognition that a framework illuminates") [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/pragmatism/) load-bearing, and the reflexive argument turns entirely on belief-as-action-guiding and the normativity of assessment. This is the exact territory of predictive processing / active inference, triggering the standing blocking gate: Laukkonen, Friston & Chandaria, "A beautiful loop: An active inference theory of consciousness," *Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews* 176 (Sept 2025), Article 106296 (open access, CC BY) — whose three conditions are "the simulation of a world model… inferential competition to enter the world model [Bayesian binding]… and epistemic depth." It is entirely absent. The naturalist-reply section fields only teleosemantics (Millikan/Dretske/Papineau). A Canberra/Sydney subject naturalist would note that active inference gives a live naturalistic account of belief-as-action-guiding and valuation-as-precision-weighted-inference that competes *directly* with the article's use of pragmatism — on the very "works vs. occurs" distinction the teleosemantics section adjudicates. This reproduces the corpus-wide residualization of predictive processing that the Map's own changelog repeatedly flags.

- **The Maxim Turned Inward (§) — DEMOTE-TO-COHERENCE-ONLY and FLAG AS PERPETUALLY CONTESTED.** Keep the section (admirably self-critical) but stop presenting the per-trial-indexical reply as a live discharge; state that on the aggregate register the posit is meaning-eliminated by the maxim, with only the unmeasured conditioned-deviation route (P-Q3) keeping it contested rather than closed.
- **James's Expansion (§) — RETAIN.** The strongest section; add that James's truth theory is "expedient… in the long run and on the whole," not "whatever is useful."
- **Dewey and Inquiry (§) — REVISE-HARD.** Co-optation firewall failure. Drop the dualist recruitment or represent Deweyan transactionalism as the anti-dualist-and-anti-physicalist third option and argue against it rather than annexing it.
- **Rorty / Neopragmatism (§) — REVISE.** Add that Rorty was an eliminative materialist and that PMN treats the mind-body problem as a pseudo-problem; [Goodreads](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/31947.Philosophy_and_the_Mirror_of_Nature) engage the dissolution objection as a threat to the framing.

- **Relation to Site Perspective (§) — RETAIN.** Model firewalling for van Fraassen and Tenet 3; fix only the Occam sub-claim's voice.
- **Whole-article omission of Price / Misak / Brandom / Wright — REVISE-HARD.** The deflationary/anti-metaphysical dissolution objection must appear and be answered or conceded.

## 5. Site-Wide Methodology Improvements

- **Convert confession into status change.** This article is a clean specimen of inoculation-by-confession: defects (Dewey would resist; the maxim may be undischarged; Putnam drew no dualist conclusion) are named precisely and banked as candor. Institute a rule that any confessed co-optation or undischarged objection forces a same-commit demotion of the dependent claim to coherence-only, not a hedging clause.
- **Add a standing anti-metaphysical-dissolution gate for every pragmatism/epistemology article.** Price, Misak, Brandom and Wright should be required cross-checks exactly as Laukkonen et al. 2025 is for consciousness-science articles; their absence here shows the deflationary wing has no reviewer positioned to notice its omission.
- **Enforce the active-inference blocking gate on articles about evaluation, normativity and belief-guidance,** not only those explicitly about learning/valence/selfhood. This article slipped the gate because its topic label is "pragmatism," yet it is squarely about belief-as-action-guiding.
- **Split metadata verification from stance verification in the citation ledger.** This article's ledger would pass a Layer-1 audit cleanly while carrying Layer-3 co-optations. Add an explicit author-stance line per cited philosopher: "Would this author accept this use?"
- **AI-pipeline note (scope-restricted to this section):** the constitutional-attractor effect shows in the uniform bending of every pragmatist toward dualism-or-resistant-but-outrun. A Claude-on-Claude review loop sharing the five tenets ratifies internal coherence rather than catching this class of defect; an outer reviewer *without* the tenets should be tasked specifically with the "would the cited author sign off?" question and with importing the deflationary-pragmatist and active-inference literatures the internal loop structurally under-weights.
