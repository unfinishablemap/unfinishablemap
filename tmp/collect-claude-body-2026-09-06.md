### 2.2 Empirical claims (Task A)

**Verified and correctly used.** Siegel et al. 2024 (*Nature* 632(8023):131–138, DOI 10.1038/s41586-024-07624-5) — cortex-wide desynchronization ("Psilocybin massively disrupted functional connectivity (FC) in cortex and subcortex, acutely causing more than threefold greater change than methylphenidate"), persistent anterior-hippocampus/DMN reduction ~3 weeks: [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/psychedelics-and-the-filter-model/) metadata and substance correct. Doss et al. 2022 (*Brain* 145(2):441–456, DOI 10.1093/brain/awab406) — cortico-claustro-cortical model with claustrum as proximal trigger: correct. Tagliazucchi et al. 2016 (*Current Biology* 26(8):1043–1050) — increased global connectivity with LSD ego dissolution: [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/psychedelics-and-the-filter-model/) correct (though it is LSD, and the article should flag that its cross-drug generalisation rests on pooling LSD, psilocybin, and DMT data). Carhart-Harris et al. 2012 (*PNAS* 109(6):2138–2143): [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/psychedelics-and-the-filter-model/) correct.

**Handling of replication/methodological problems — partial and asymmetric.** The article correctly narrates the 2012→2024 shift ("from 'the DMN goes down' to 'network identity itself dissolves'") and correctly withdraws the DMN-localisation inference. This is genuine self-correction and should be credited plainly. But four of the specific problems named in the audit brief are absent:

- **Small samples / no preregistration:** never mentioned. Siegel 2024 is a precision-fMRI design on a very small n; [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07624-5) Carhart-Harris 2012 is n=15. The article cites these as settled without a word on statistical fragility.
- **Head-motion / physiological-noise confounds:** never mentioned — a striking omission, since the entire methodological point of the Siegel/Dosenbach precision-fMRI approach the article leans on is motion control. The article harvests the result without engaging why the method exists.
- **Global signal regression / normalization artefacts:** not mentioned.
- **CBF/ASL divergence:** the article does not cite the cerebral-blood-flow literature at all, and thereby omits the single most awkward datum for a naive filter reading. Carhart-Harris et al. 2012 found psilocybin *decreases* CBF (ASL perfusion), whereas Vollenweider et al. 1997 (*Neuropsychopharmacology* 16(5):357–372, DOI 10.1016/S0893-133X(96)00246-1) found the psychedelic state is metabolically *expensive* — psilocybin (15–20 mg) "produce[d] a global increase in cerebral metabolic rate of glucose (CMRglu) with significant and most marked increases in the frontomedial and frontolateral cortex (24.3%), anterior cingulate (24.9%), and temporomedial cortex (25.3%)," global CMRglu rising ~19.9% versus baseline. The Map's own `filter-theory` concept page carries this datum explicitly with the gloss "a valve doing less should cost less." [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/filter-theory/) Omitting from the flagship article a discriminating datum that the parent concept page treats as central is a **calibration-asymmetry** and a **weak-integration** failure: congenial desynchronization data are foregrounded; the metabolic-cost datum that cuts against filter is dropped.

**Lebedev 2015 characterization is loose.** The article says ego dissolution "correlates with reduced DMN integrity." [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/psychedelics-and-the-filter-model/) Lebedev's actual headline correlates are medial-temporal-lobe/cortical decoupling and decreased salience-network integrity plus reduced interhemispheric communication [Ovid](https://www.ovid.com/journals/hbmap/abstract/10.1002/hbm.22833~finding-the-self-by-losing-the-self-neural-correlates-of)[Wiley Online Library](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/hbm.22833) — not DMN integrity per se. The simplification survives because the article later broadens the picture, but the specific attribution is inexact.

### 2.4 The empirical-equivalence claim (Task C)

This is the analytic core, and it does not hold as stated. The article claims filter theory, REBUS, and Letheby's account are "empirically equivalent on altered-state data alone." [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/psychedelics-and-the-filter-model/) Three problems:

1. **The equivalence is bought by abstracting the rivals until their commitments vanish.** REBUS is not a generic "constraint loosens" story; it makes a *directional, hierarchy-specific* prediction — the precision-weighting of high-level priors decreases and bottom-up gain increases, [UCL Discovery](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/10077110/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/psychedelics-and-the-filter-model/) dose-dependently. Filter theory ("the valve opens") predicts no such vector. REBUS is therefore strictly *more* constrained and *more* falsifiable than filter theory, not equivalent to it. The article's line that "REBUS describes the mechanism of filtering without addressing what is filtered" is exactly the abstraction move that erases REBUS's empirical content. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/psychedelics-and-the-filter-model/)
2. **Letheby's account is the parsimonious null and currently wins on filter theory's own stated edge.** Letheby's "dispelling-the-illusion" reading predicts that *no* veridical metaphysical content will be confirmed — only the removal of a model. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/psychedelics-and-the-filter-model/) Filter theory's stated empirical bet is the opposite: that "veridical-grade reports will accumulate." [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/psychedelics-and-the-filter-model/) The article honestly concedes such cases "remain conspicuously thin." [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/psychedelics-and-the-filter-model/) But it then calls the accounts "equivalent," which launders an *asymmetric burden* (filter owes a positive result it has not delivered; Letheby owes nothing) into apparent parity. This is a soft **epistemic-to-metaphysical slide** at the level of dialectical bookkeeping.
3. **Constrain-vs-establish gate — largely PASSED, and this should be said without hedging.** The article repeatedly refuses the establish move ("that is coherence rather than evidential support over rivals"; [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/psychedelics-and-the-filter-model/) the withdrawal of the therapeutic-durability divergence; [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/psychedelics-and-the-filter-model/) the "one pattern, not five" deflation of the convergence cluster). Measured against the Born-rule apex's compatible/suggestive/discriminating ladder, [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/born-rule-and-the-consciousness-interface/) the article lands honestly at "compatible, not discriminating." That is the correct verdict and the article reaches it itself.

The residual failure is not that the article establishes too much — it is that the *word* "equivalent" flatters filter theory by implying parity where the burden is one-sided, and that the article does not engage the strongest current physicalist rival at all (§2.5).

### 2.5 Standing beautiful-loop cross-check (Task D) — FAILED

The article does not cite Laukkonen, Friston & Chandaria 2025, [OSF](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/daf5n) "A beautiful loop: An active inference theory of consciousness," *Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews* 176:106296 (DOI 10.1016/j.neubiorev.2025.106296; [OSF](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/daf5n) open access; three prior OSF preprint versions). [Sciety](https://sciety.org/articles/activity/10.1016/j.neubiorev.2025.106296) This is the documented standing required cross-check for any Map article touching altered states, attention, or value-based selection, and it is decisive here for a specific reason:

The article's *only* surviving discriminator between filter theory and Letheby is the claim that a pre-reflective minimal self / for-me-ness persists through ego dissolution, which "bare prior-relaxation should not require." Beautiful loop predicts exactly this persistence *physically*. Its third condition, epistemic depth, is stated in the paper's abstract verbatim: "The third is epistemic depth, which is the recurrent sharing of the Bayesian beliefs throughout the system. Due to this recursive loop in a hierarchical system (such as a brain) the world model contains the knowledge that it exists. This is distinct from self-consciousness, because the world model knows itself non-locally and continuously evidences this knowing (i.e., field-evidencing)." That continuous, non-local self-evidencing is a continuing self-knowing that is a *model property*, not a witness. On a beautiful-loop reading, the reported "witnessing" through dissolution is field-evidencing that has not collapsed — precisely Letheby's "not-yet-decohered model component" [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/psychedelics-and-the-filter-model/) given a sharper mechanism. **The article's single empirical bet is therefore not undetermined between filter and physicalism; it is predicted by a named physicalist framework the article ignores.** The equivalence claim does not survive the cross-check: beautiful loop is a stronger rival than either REBUS or Letheby on the exact point the article stakes its discrimination on.

Aggravating the omission: Friston co-authors *both* REBUS [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-023-28111-3) (cited) and beautiful loop (uncited), [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/psychedelics-and-the-filter-model/)[OSF](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/daf5n) and the Map already engages beautiful loop on the neighbouring `predictive-processing-and-dualism` page. So the required engagement exists one link away, but the load-bearing constitutive claim on *this* page is left undefended against it — **tenet leakage** plus a **co-optation-firewall / performative-inoculation** pattern: the article gestures at predictive processing via a wikilink rather than engaging the rival's argument where the claim actually lives.

## 3. Bottom-line verdict

It is nonetheless not publication-clean: it carries a fabricated composite citation, it fails a standing required cross-check on the exact claim that bears its argument, and its central "equivalence" framing is false in a way that flatters the house view.

- **Core position — "psilocybin's cortex-wide desynchronization coheres with filter theory": RETAIN (as coherence).** Correctly scoped; supported.

- **Speculative quantum appendix: DELETE, or retain only as an explicitly labelled non-load-bearing pointer to the quantum-biology topic.** As written it is performative inoculation.

## 5. Site-wide methodology improvements

1. **Make the beautiful-loop / active-inference cross-check a blocking gate, not an advisory.** Predictive processing is a documented site-wide blind spot; the failure recurs precisely on articles making constitutive claims about phenomenal character and selfhood. Any article whose discrimination rests on a phenomenological claim (minimal self, for-me-ness, valence, witnessing) should not pass review until it engages the strongest active-inference rival *by name and by argument* on that exact claim. A wikilink to `predictive-processing-and-dualism` should be explicitly disallowed as a substitute.
2. **Convert confession into status changes.** The pipeline's dominant pathology is that defects are named with precision and then banked as candour. Reviews should require that any fatal objection named in-body triggers, in the same commit, either a register status change, a scope narrowing, or a deletion. Disclosure without a status change should be logged as an open defect, not as remediation — consistent with the corpus's own P-M5 ("disclosure is not self-correction"). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/)
3. **Adopt the Born-rule apex's compatible/suggestive/discriminating ladder as an explicit, required label** on every empirical section, replacing prose paraphrases. This article would then have to *print* "compatible, not discriminating," foreclosing the "equivalent" flattery.
4. **Add a calibration-symmetry check to the citation ledger:** for any topic, require that data cutting *against* the house reading held by a parent concept page (here, Vollenweider FDG-PET metabolic cost) appear in the child article, not only the congenial data.
5. **Composite-citation guard.** Reference 23 is a title-from-paper-A + venue/finding-from-paper-B fusion that survived multiple review cycles. A publisher-of-record check that verifies title, first author, year, and venue *as a tuple* (not field-by-field) would catch chimeras. This is the class of defect the AI-assisted generation-and-review pipeline is structurally prone to: plausible-looking metadata assembled from adjacent sources, and a Claude-on-Claude review loop that re-ratifies a prior ledger's "verified" stamp rather than re-deriving it from the artefact. The pipeline should treat any citation whose components trace to two different DOIs as a hard fail, and should bar review passes from inheriting a previous pass's verification without re-checking the raw record.
