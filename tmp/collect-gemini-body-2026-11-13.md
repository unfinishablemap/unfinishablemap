# Pre-Publication Audit Report: UnfinishableMap.org and the Empirical Deficits of Non-Reductive Architectures

## 1. Introduction and Epistemic Scope of Audit

The present report constitutes a severe, exhaustive pre-publication audit of the philosophical and empirical scaffolding that underpins the digital repository and theoretical framework hosted at *UnfinishableMap.org*. The site attempts an incredibly ambitious, yet fundamentally flawed, project: constructing a comprehensive, evolving theoretical architecture to defend a robust interactionist-dualist framework of consciousness. Its central metaphysical thesis posits that consciousness operates as a non-physical selector, intervening at points of quantum indeterminacy within the neural substrate, and interfacing with classical brain dynamics through strictly low-bandwidth causal channels. The site presents itself as an exhaustive synthesis of contemporary philosophy of mind, cognitive science, and neurobiology, explicitly aiming to bridge the explanatory gap by formalizing the mechanisms of mental causation.

However, a granular audit of its foundational claims reveals systemic epistemic vulnerabilities that render the project unfit for serious academic consideration in its current state. The remit of this audit is strictly confined to evaluating the empirical accuracy of the site's claims against the 2020–2026 scientific literature, identifying its reliance on retracted, challenged, or conceptually obsolete experimental paradigms, exposing the internal contradictions between its primary and secondary articles, and cataloging its failure to engage with robust, untested counterarguments.

In assessing the site for a top-tier philosophy of mind venue, the standard of review demands that whenever a metaphysical architecture (such as interactionist dualism) rests its weight on specific empirical phenomena, the characterization of those phenomena must be beyond reproach. The overarching finding of this audit is that the site engages in persistent epistemic gerrymandering. It forces empirical data to fit its a priori dualist commitments, relies heavily on anachronistic paradigms—such as mid-twentieth-century interpretations of split-brain patients and classical Libet-style readings of the readiness potential—while systematically ignoring or marginalizing the 2020s literature that explicitly dismantles these models. Furthermore, the site suffers from severe internal contradictions, frequently making categorical claims in one high-level synthesis article to support a dualist distinction, only to concede the empirical falsification of those same claims in obscured, secondary articles.

The subsequent sections of this report dismantle the site's metaphysical architecture by isolating specific domains where its empirical claims, logical inferences, and counterargument coverage collapse under the weight of contemporary scientific and philosophical consensus.

## 2. The Metaphysics of the Filter Theory and the Psychedelic Mischaracterization

In the article `filter-theory`, the site attempts to resurrect and formalize the James-Bergson-Huxley hypothesis, which posits that the brain acts as a "reducing valve" or interface that constrains an independently existing consciousness, rather than generating it. To provide empirical weight to this metaphysical stance, the article relies heavily on early-2010s neuroimaging data of psychedelic states. Specifically, it cites Carhart-Harris's 2012 fMRI findings and the 2019 REBUS (Relaxed Beliefs Under Psychedelics) model. The article argues that because psychedelics appear to decrease Default Mode Network (DMN) activity while simultaneously expanding the subjective richness of conscious experience, the brain must be filtering a broader, pre-existing consciousness that is phenomenologically "let in" when physical neural constraints are relaxed.

This framing is empirically obsolete, theoretically specious, and borders on willful ignorance when evaluated against the high-precision functional neuroimaging literature of the 2020s. The site's argument hinges on the premise that psychedelics merely "relax" a biological constraint, essentially opening the aperture of a dualist transmission valve. However, the article fails entirely to engage with the landmark 2024 study by Siegel et al., published in *Nature*, which utilized precision functional MRI mapping to track the brains of healthy adults before, during, and after psilocybin administration.

Siegel et al. (2024) unequivocally demonstrate that psilocybin does not simply "relax" a filter; rather, it profoundly and persistently *desynchronizes* the human brain, fundamentally disrupting functional connectivity across both cortical and subcortical regions. The most severe desynchronization occurs between the hippocampus and the DMN, a network crucial for the construction of self, time, and space. Under the drug's influence, the brain is not receiving a higher bandwidth of external consciousness; it is suffering an acute, temporary disintegration of the semantic and autobiographical neural networks that generate the coherent, predictive self-model.

The `filter-theory` article asserts that on materialist production models, "disrupted neural organization should produce disrupted consciousness," framing the subjectively "expanded" psychedelic state as an insoluble paradox for physicalism. This constitutes a profound category error regarding the phenomenological nature of the psychedelic state. The dissolution of ego, the distortion of time, and the vivid, chaotic sensory imagery reported under psilocybin are the exact phenomenological correlates one would predict from a severely desynchronized generative model. The "expansion" of consciousness reported by users is essentially a phenomenological descriptor of boundary-loss, which maps perfectly onto the neural desynchronization of the networks responsible for boundary-maintenance.

By omitting the 2024 precision functional MRI data, the site artificially preserves the "reducing valve" metaphor. A dualist transmission model requires the brain to act as a coherent receiver or interface. If the receiver is aggressively desynchronized and functionally shattered, a transmission model should predict a loss of signal fidelity, resulting in unconsciousness, static, or a diminution of experiential bandwidth. Instead, the system hallucinates wildly and generates profound, albeit disorganized, subjective significance. This is a phenomenon easily explained by a generative, predictive-processing brain that, suddenly stripped of its prior constraints and error-correction mechanisms, is left to free-wheel.

The site's failure to test its filter hypothesis against the network-desynchronization paradigm of Siegel et al. renders the `filter-theory` article empirically inadequate. To highlight the severity of this oversight, we can compare the predictive success of the site's preferred model against the contemporary generative model based on the 2024 data.

| Phenomenological Datum under Psychedelics | Filter/Transmission Theory Prediction (Site's Framework) | Generative Desynchronization Model (Siegel et al., 2024) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Loss of Ego/Self-Boundary** | A pre-existing cosmic consciousness is "let in," overwhelming the individual self. | Desynchronization of the DMN and hippocampus dissolves the autobiographical predictive self-model. |
| **Increased Vividness/Hallucination** | Reduced "valve" resistance allows more external phenomenal properties to bypass the brain. | Top-down predictive priors are disrupted, leading to unchecked bottom-up sensory prediction errors (hallucinations). |
| **Neural Substrate Behavior** | The receiver (brain) does "less work," simply opening the interface. | The brain exhibits profound, measurable network desynchronization, failing to maintain coherent resting-state functional connectivity. |

The site attempts to dismiss functionalist explanations by claiming they lack "independent criteria" for predicting when disruption leads to enhancement versus degradation. Yet, the 2024 precision mapping provides exactly this independent criterion: the specific uncoupling of the hippocampal-DMN axis directly correlates with subjective reports of ego dissolution. The site's `filter-theory` article is thus sustained only by an intentional omission of the decade's most significant neuroimaging breakthrough in psychedelic science.

## 3. Epistemic Gerrymandering in Unconscious Working Memory

A critical pillar of the site's argument for the causal efficacy of consciousness rests on establishing rigid, impermeable functional boundaries between conscious and unconscious processing. In the primary synthesis article `conscious-vs-unconscious-processing`, the site issues categorical decrees: it claims that working memory—the durable maintenance of information for ongoing reasoning—"requires consciousness," that subliminal priming effects "decay within 500ms," and that without conscious access, information fails to enter a global broadcast network and fades rapidly. This strict functional division is weaponized to argue that consciousness enables "qualitatively different engagement with information," thereby justifying an interactionist-dualist framework where consciousness serves as an essential, non-physical executive required for sustained cognition.

This assertion is not merely empirically false according to the 2020–2026 literature; it is explicitly and fatally contradicted by the site's own secondary articles. In the article `working-memory`, the site concedes that "maintenance can be unconscious," referencing the 2022 Bayesian meta-analysis by Gambarota et al., which confirms the existence of unconscious visual working memory. The site also mentions a 2026 multisite Registered Report by Franco-Martínez et al. (a 19-laboratory replication involving 531 participants) that successfully replicated unconscious working memory effects, demonstrating above-chance working memory performance for stimuli reported as unseen.

The internal contradiction here destroys the site's structural integrity. The primary article (`conscious-vs-unconscious-processing`) requires the 500ms decay limit to be absolute in order to prove that consciousness is *necessary* for durable information maintenance. Without this necessity, the argument that consciousness possesses unique causal properties apart from unconscious neural processing evaporates. Yet, the secondary article (`working-memory`) admits that information can be held online and maintained for seconds without awareness, but attempts to salvage the dualist argument by shifting the goalposts, claiming that it is only the *manipulation* of information that requires consciousness.

Even this revised "maintenance vs. manipulation" gradient is severely challenged by recent literature. Studies by Pedale et al. (2021–2023) mapping prefrontal activation during working-memory tasks with unconscious stimuli demonstrate robust cognitive control operations that link unconscious stimuli to goal representations. While Pedale et al. note that unconscious stimuli do not trigger flexible attentional shifts to the *same degree* as conscious ones, the evidence decisively proves that the frontoparietal networks traditionally associated with the "global workspace" are actively maintaining and processing information outside of phenomenal awareness.

The site's methodology in handling these facts constitutes a severe breach of academic rigor. It places the bold, falsified claims in its high-level synthesis articles (e.g., `conscious-vs-unconscious-processing`) to establish its metaphysical tenets, while burying the empirical retractions in granular, localized articles (e.g., `working-memory`). A philosophical framework that relies on the functional necessity of consciousness for durable information maintenance cannot survive the empirical reality of unconscious visual working memory, nor can it survive its own internal admittance of this data.

To illustrate this structural failing, consider the irreconcilable claims published across the site's own architecture:

| Site Article | Core Claim Regarding Memory and Awareness | Alignment with 2020-2026 Empirical Literature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `conscious-vs-unconscious-processing` | "Working memory... requires consciousness. Subliminal priming effects decay within 500ms." | **Falsified**. Contradicts massive multisite Registered Reports (Franco-Martínez et al., 2026) proving unconscious retention beyond seconds. |
| `working-memory` | Acknowledges maintenance can be unconscious based on Gambarota et al. (2022) but claims "manipulation requires consciousness." | **Contradicts internal framework**. Invalidates the core argument of `conscious-vs-unconscious-processing` while retreating to an unsupported secondary goalpost. |

The site attempts to gerrymander the definition of "mental effort" and "interface friction" to align with whatever cognitive task currently resists unconscious priming. This is a classic "god of the gaps" retreat. If consciousness is merely redefined as whatever currently represents the limit of unconscious processing, it is not a distinct metaphysical selector, but a placeholder for scientific ignorance. The text is thus invalidated as a serious scientific philosophy.

## 4. The Split-Brain Anachronism and the Conflation of Conscious Unity

In its taxonomy of phenomenal experience, the article `types-of-consciousness` attempts to map the structural dimensions of consciousness. Under the dimension of "Unity," the site asserts that "split-brain cases demonstrate that unity can fracture," citing this as definitive evidence that the experiential field can be divided into "multiple independent experiential streams". The site uses this presumed fracturing as foundational support for building out speculative topologies of AI consciousness, such as Birch's "Shoggoth Hypothesis," relying entirely on the assumption that a structural, anatomical division in the neural substrate equates directly to a metaphysical division in phenomenal subjectivity.

This reliance on the classical twentieth-century interpretation of the split-brain syndrome represents a catastrophic failure of literature review and philosophical analysis. The canonical view, propagated by Sperry and Gazzaniga in the 1960s and 70s, posited that severing the corpus callosum creates two independent conscious systems—two distinct minds—within a single cranium. However, the period between 2017 and 2023 saw a total paradigm shift in the neurocognitive understanding of callosotomy patients, driven heavily by the extensive, rigorous empirical work of Pinto, De Haan, and their colleagues.

In comprehensive behavioral and neuroimaging studies of split-brain patients, Pinto and De Haan (2017, 2020, 2023) demonstrated that while visual *perception* is indeed divided—stimuli cannot be consciously compared across visual half-fields—*consciousness itself remains unified*. Their research unequivocally proved that split-brain patients do not exhibit a response type by visual field interaction; they can respond to stimuli in the left visual field with the right hand or verbally, indicating that the conscious agent orchestrating the response is singular, even if the sensory processing is fundamentally uncoupled. De Haan and Pinto explicitly conclude that "severing the cortical connections between hemispheres splits visual perception, but does not create two independent conscious perceivers within one brain".

By clinging to the debunked model of fractured subjectivity in split-brain patients, the site's `types-of-consciousness` and `ai-consciousness-typology` articles construct their taxonomy on a foundation of retracted clinical lore. The contemporary consensus—that uncoupled modular perception feeds into an undivided conscious agency despite severe structural severing—poses a profound philosophical challenge to both standard physicalist integration theories (like Integrated Information Theory, IIT) and the site's own dualist interface models.

If a severed brain maintains a unified consciousness, the mechanisms of the site's dualist "interface" cannot be simplistically mapped onto gross anatomical connectivity. The site entirely ignores this 2020s literature, presumably because addressing the fact that physical brain-splitting does *not* split the conscious subject would require a total rewrite of its assumptions regarding spatial character, unity, and interface topology. The site repeatedly conflates access disunity (the inability of the left hemisphere to verbally report what the right hemisphere sees) with phenomenal disunity (the existence of two distinct phenomenal subjects). This conflation is amateurish for a philosophy of mind journal. The result is a taxonomy of consciousness that operates in an empirical vacuum, completely isolated from the last decade of neurological research.

## 5. Mental Causation, the Readiness Potential, and the Illusion of Conscious Selection

The site's most aggressive metaphysical claim is that consciousness acts as a genuine causal "selector" at the quantum level. In the articles `asymmetric-bandwidth-consciousness`, `conscious-vs-unconscious-processing`, and `valence-and-conscious-selection`, the site formalizes this through a control-theoretic lens: consciousness is posited as a low-bandwidth controller (estimated at ~10 to 20 bits/second) that "gates, stabilises, vetoes, and steers neural dynamics". To ground this empirically and argue that consciousness operates upon pre-computed unconscious options, the site references Benjamin Libet's classical experiments on the Readiness Potential (RP), framing the RP as an unconscious neural initiation that consciousness subsequently acts upon by selecting or vetoing.

This framework relies on a fundamentally obsolete understanding of what the Readiness Potential actually represents. The site's reliance on the "unconscious initiation vs. conscious veto" dichotomy ignores the transformative 2021 consensus paper by Schurger, Hu, Pak, and Roskies published in *Trends in Cognitive Sciences*. Schurger et al. (2021) systematically dismantle the classical interpretation of the RP. Through advanced computational modeling and electroencephalography (EEG) analysis, they demonstrate that the RP is *not* a specific preparatory signal indicating that the brain has "decided" or "prepared" to move.

Instead, the RP is best understood through a stochastic accumulator model (drift-diffusion). In tasks requiring spontaneous, self-paced movement without external cues (such as the Libet task), ongoing spontaneous fluctuations in neural activity are integrated over time. The movement occurs mechanically when these noisy fluctuations happen to cross a pre-defined motor threshold. The "Readiness Potential" observed in EEG is simply a statistical artifact—the inevitable backward-averaged shadow of these spontaneous fluctuations leading up to the threshold crossing.

The implications of Schurger's 2021 paper are devastating for the site's dualist architecture, specifically its claims in `valence-and-conscious-selection`. The site argues that the brain presents "improper-mixture components" (pre-computed neural options) and that conscious valence denominates them so that consciousness can select the outcome. But if the RP reflects mere stochastic noise accumulating toward a boundary, there are no discrete, pre-computed "options" waiting for a non-physical executive to select them. The brain is not offering a menu to a quantum selector; it is executing a continuous dynamical system driven by stochastic drift.

By failing to engage with the accumulator model of the RP, the site builds its "consciousness as selector" mechanism on a profound misinterpretation of electrophysiological noise. The site claims that its "Minimal Quantum Interaction" tenet proposes that consciousness biases outcomes without injecting energy. However, if the timing and initiation of action are driven by the baseline ebb and flow of neural excitability (as mathematically modeled by Schurger), the supposed "veto" or "selection" window—the very locus of the site's interactionist dualism—disappears entirely.

| View of the Readiness Potential | Ontological Implication for Mental Causation | Compatibility with Site's Framework |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Classical Libet View** (Site's Premise) | Unconscious neural preparation begins hundreds of milliseconds before the conscious "urge," leaving a window for a conscious "veto." | **High**. Allows the site to posit a dualist selector that gates pre-computed neural plans. |
| **Stochastic Accumulator / Drift-Diffusion Model** (Schurger et al., 2021) | The RP is a backward-averaged statistical artifact of spontaneous neural noise crossing a threshold. There is no discrete unconscious "decision" preceding movement. | **Zero**. Destroys the concept of the brain presenting discrete "options" for a low-bandwidth conscious controller to select. |

The site's failure to test its central causal framework against the 2021 accumulator consensus renders its claims of mental causation empirically bankrupt. It assumes a neurobiological reality that has been mathematically and experimentally falsified.

## 6. Evolutionary Dualism and the Omission of Pathological Complexity

To justify the sudden insertion of non-physical consciousness into the physical world, the site must gerrymander evolutionary biology. In the article `evolution-of-consciousness`, the site presents three broad possibilities for the emergence of consciousness: panpsychist continuity, gradual emergence, and threshold emergence. It heavily favors threshold emergence, specifically citing Ginsburg and Jablonka's Unlimited Associative Learning (UAL) framework, which places the transition in the Cambrian explosion. The site then leverages this sudden threshold to argue in `evolution-under-dualism` and `evolutionary-case-for-mental-causation` that "evolution of consciousness forces choices about the nature of mind." It claims that physicalist biology cannot explain why this particular organization produces experience, and thus concludes that dualism is necessary because neural systems with the right architecture act as literal "interfaces" for a non-physical consciousness.

This framing is a highly selective reading of evolutionary biology that deliberately marginalizes the robust, continuous Darwinian frameworks that have gained overwhelming prominence in the 2020s. A prime example is the work of Walter Veit (2023) in *A Philosophy for the Science of Animal Consciousness*. Veit provides a rigorously developed evolutionary bottom-up approach, entirely independent of dualist leaps or sudden, unexplainable "interface" thresholds. Veit argues that consciousness evolved gradually as a multidimensional evaluative system to handle "pathological complexity"—the complex action-selection problems faced by early motile organisms navigating immense trade-offs in their ecological environment.

In Veit’s framework, subjective experience (phenomenological complexity) is not a non-physical passenger; it is the very mechanism of action-selection in early nervous systems. There is no need to posit a non-physical "selector" interfacing with quantum indeterminacies; the biological evaluative system *is* the phenomenal system, evolving to maximize fitness by allowing organisms to weigh competing motivations in real-time.

The site’s articles completely ignore this Darwinian synthesis. Instead of engaging with contemporary cognitive ethology which treats consciousness as an evolved, multidimensional life-history trait, the site forces a false dichotomy: either consciousness does nothing (epiphenomenalism) or interactionist dualism is true. The site argues that because human consciousness "appears to have been elaborated, not merely preserved," it must exert causal influence, and physicalism cannot explain this elaboration. But Veit (2023) and the modern bio-evaluative consensus demonstrate exactly how physical, biological complexity drives the elaboration of sentience without violating physical closure. By tying its evolutionary arguments to rigid thresholds and ignoring the 2023 literature on pathological complexity and gradualist ecological approaches, the site reveals a teleological agenda. It does not look to evolution to explain consciousness; it uses a caricatured, discontinuous version of evolution to create artificial "gaps" into which it can insert its pre-determined dualist interface.

## 7. Quantum Substrates, Decoherence Wishcasting, and Meta-Theoretical Marginalization

Finally, the site’s reliance on quantum mechanics to ground its interactionist dualism represents a severe over-extrapolation of preliminary biophysics, crossing the line from speculative philosophy into quantum mysticism. In the articles `quantum-consciousness`, `stapp-quantum-mind`, and `consciousness-in-smeared-quantum-states`, the site reviews mechanisms like Orchestrated Objective Reduction (Orch OR) and Henry Stapp's Quantum Zeno Effect. The site acknowledges the Tegmark decoherence objection—that the brain is too warm and wet to sustain quantum coherence on the timescale of neural firing (~300ms)—but attempts to wave it away by pointing to recent 2024–2025 research on "superradiance" in tryptophan networks and lithium isotope effects.

This constitutes biological wishcasting. The site quotes studies on optical/electronic quantum effects (operating at frequencies of ~101510^{15}1015 Hz) and attempts to use them to validate mechanical, conformational-mode coherence on the timescale of human decision-making. This is a profound misapplication of physical scales. While quantum biology has indeed demonstrated functional quantum effects in photosynthesis and avian magnetoreception (e.g., radical pair mechanisms in cryptochromes), these effects are highly localized, specialized, and operate on nanosecond or microsecond timescales.

The site’s `quantum-consciousness` article requires macroscopic quantum states to persist long enough for a non-physical consciousness to "veto" or "select" among neural action templates. There is zero empirical evidence in the 2020s literature supporting macroscopic, brain-wide quantum entanglement persisting for the hundreds of milliseconds required for cognitive selection. The site’s attempt to stitch together Stapp’s Zeno effect with Orch OR tubulin dynamics using 2024 optical fluorescence data is a speculative leap that fails basic biophysical feasibility constraints.

Furthermore, this reliance on fringe physics highlights a broader meta-theoretical failure. As noted by Seth and Bayne (2022) in their comprehensive review of theories of consciousness (ToCs) for *Nature Reviews Neuroscience*, the contemporary science of consciousness is driven by frameworks like Global Workspace Theory, Integrated Information Theory, Higher-Order Theories, and Predictive Processing. Seth and Bayne point out that theories relying on exotic physics (like Orch OR) remain highly marginalized precisely because they attempt to solve one mystery (consciousness) by invoking another (quantum collapse), without yielding tractable, testable neuroscientific predictions.

The site selectively cherry-picks concepts from mainstream ToCs—such as borrowing the "bottleneck" concept from Global Workspace Theory to justify its "low-bandwidth interface"—while ignoring the fact that GWT is a thoroughly physicalist, cognitive-level theory that explicitly rejects the site's dualist premises. By treating quantum mind hypotheses as equally viable competitors to mainstream neurocognitive theories, the site distorts the current epistemic landscape of consciousness research. It constructs an architecture that is philosophically pretentious but empirically vacant.

## 8. Conclusion: Identified Weaknesses Justifying Rejection

The architecture of *UnfinishableMap.org* is fundamentally compromised. It operates as a metaphysical house of cards, built upon a foundation of cherry-picked, outdated, or explicitly retracted empirical claims. The following five weaknesses represent fatal epistemic vulnerabilities that justify the immediate rejection of this framework from serious academic consideration:

1. **Mischaracterization of Psychedelic Neurodynamics:** The site (`filter-theory`) relies on outdated models of relaxed constraints to argue the brain is a "reducing valve," completely ignoring precision functional mapping that proves psychedelics cause profound, generative structural desynchronization of the hippocampus and default mode network, negating the need for a dualist transmission model.

- *Omitted Source:* Siegel, J. S., et al. (2024). "Psilocybin desynchronizes the human brain." *Nature*, 632(8023), 131-138.
2. **Internal Contradiction Regarding Unconscious Processing:** The site claims in `conscious-vs-unconscious-processing` that durable information maintenance strictly requires consciousness, while quietly admitting in `working-memory` that unconscious visual working memory exists, directly contradicting its own functional taxonomy and destroying its argument for the unique causal necessity of consciousness.

- *Contradicted Source:* Gambarota, F., et al. (2022). "Unconscious Visual Working Memory: A critical review and Bayesian meta-analysis." *Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews*, 136, 104618.
3. **Reliance on the Myth of Split-Brain Fracturing:** The site (`types-of-consciousness` and `ai-consciousness-typology`) grounds its taxonomy of conscious unity on the assumption that split-brain patients experience a fracturing of consciousness, directly contravening the modern empirical consensus that uncoupled perception does not sever conscious agency.

- *Omitted/Mishandled Source:* Pinto, Y., et al. (2023). "Visual integration across fixation: Automatic processes are split but conscious processes remain unified in the split-brain." *Frontiers in Human Neuroscience*, 17, 1278025.
4. **Obsolete Framing of Action Initiation (Readiness Potential):** The site (`asymmetric-bandwidth-consciousness` and `valence-and-conscious-selection`) frames consciousness as a selector acting on pre-computed neural options (the Readiness Potential), ignoring the consensus that the RP is a statistical artifact of stochastic drift-diffusion, not a discrete preparatory signal.

- *Omitted Source:* Schurger, A., et al. (2021). "What Is the Readiness Potential?" *Trends in Cognitive Sciences*, 25(7), 558-570.
5. **False Dichotomies in Evolutionary Biology:** The site (`evolution-of-consciousness` and `evolutionary-case-for-mental-causation`) relies on artificial biological thresholds to justify dualist intervention, failing to engage with contemporary Darwinian bottom-up models that fully explain the continuous evolution of consciousness via biological evaluative systems without invoking non-physical properties.

- *Omitted Source:* Veit, W. (2023). *A Philosophy for the Science of Animal Consciousness*. Routledge.

**Verdict on Academic-Submission Readiness:**
Due to systemic empirical inaccuracies, egregious internal contradictions, and a pervasive failure to engage with the definitive 2020–2026 literature across multiple subfields of cognitive neuroscience, this framework is entirely unready for academic submission and warrants outright rejection.
