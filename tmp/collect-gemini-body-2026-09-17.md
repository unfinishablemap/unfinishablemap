# Referee Report on "Near-Perfect Adaptation and Control-Theoretic Competency Without Experience"

## 1. Introduction and Methodological Audit Framework

The manuscript under review, titled "Near-Perfect Adaptation and Control-Theoretic Competency Without Experience," seeks to establish an "Orthogonality Thesis" regarding the relationship between physical regulatory mechanisms and phenomenal consciousness. The authors posit that control-theoretic sophistication—specifically robust perfect adaptation (RPA) and integral feedback—is entirely orthogonal to phenomenality. By constructing a "domain-general ladder" that nominally equates the regulatory mathematics of operational amplifiers, industrial proportional-integral-derivative (PID) controllers, engineered synthetic gene circuits, bacterial chemotaxis, and physiological allostasis, the authors attempt to demonstrate that physicalist and functionalist accounts of consciousness are fundamentally flawed. The manuscript’s central logical maneuver relies on an "Op-Amp Reductio": the authors argue that because a simple, three-component operational amplifier performs the exact same mathematical integral calculus as a biological organism tracking a homeostatic setpoint, attributing phenomenality to robust biological regulation supposedly over-generates, licensing the attribution of subjective experience to a household thermostat or an analog circuit. Consequently, the authors conclude that mechanistic explanations of biological control achieve "explanatory closure" while leaving no functional residual for subjective experience, thereby leaving the explanatory gap completely intact and validating a strict dualist framework (referred to internally as "The Map’s Tenet 1").

Evaluated for potential publication in a top-tier philosophy of mind or philosophy of science journal, this manuscript is fundamentally unacceptable in its current state. The text suffers from a fatal combination of empirical obsolescence, aggressive theoretical gerrymandering, and a profound failure to engage with the contemporary (2020–2025) scientific literature on mathematical phenomenology, biological control systems, and computational neuroscience. The manuscript resurrects mid-twentieth-century conceptual dichotomies—pitting a naive, purely kinematic and deterministic view of physical mechanisms against an unearned, presupposed dualism—while entirely ignoring the formal, mathematically grounded theories of consciousness that presently dominate the discipline.

This referee report executes a comprehensive pre-publication audit across four dimensions critical to the manuscript's viability: (a) empirical accuracy against the 2020s literature on integral feedback and biological control; (b) the reliability of foundational results that have since been challenged, severely bounded, or recognized as idealized limit cases; (c) the presence of question-begging framings that quietly presuppose the authors' dualist commitments rather than philosophically earning them; and (d) the failure to test the manuscript's claims against robust contemporary counterarguments and competing structural frameworks. The analysis demonstrates that the manuscript's core arguments depend on an idealized abstraction of biological control that has been falsified by recent stochastic thermodynamic models, and that its philosophical reductio collapses entirely when confronted with modern causal-structural theories of consciousness.

## 2. Dimension A: Empirical Inaccuracies in the Characterization of Biological Control

The manuscript’s foundational empirical claim rests on the assertion that "integral feedback" is a perfectly substrate-indifferent, domain-general mathematical operation. The authors assert that this operation provides Robust Perfect Adaptation (RPA) identically across analog circuits, bacterial chemotaxis, and synthetic gene networks. To support this substrate indifference, the authors rely heavily on foundational models from Barkai and Leibler (1997), the chemotaxis models of Yi et al. (2000), and a synthetic biological implementation by Aoki et al. (2019). The authors state that because RPA can be "systematically designed and installed into molecular networks," the competency is merely an engineered fact of deterministic network dynamics, completely devoid of any implications for internal states or phenomenality.

This framing demonstrates a severe misreading of the 2020–2025 literature on biological control theory. Contemporary literature has conclusively established that biological integral control is not a frictionless, perfectly abstracted mathematical function identical to the operations of a macroscopic electronic operational amplifier. In macroscopic engineering, an op-amp implements integration via continuous deterministic voltages over a capacitor and resistor. In stark contrast, implementing integral control in the highly stochastic, thermodynamically noisy environment of a living cell introduces severe architectural constraints and hard limits that fundamentally alter the nature of the control system. The manuscript completely ignores the extensive recent literature on "antithetic integral feedback" (AIF), which is the precise molecular sequestration mechanism utilized by Aoki et al. (2019) and widely studied as the biological realization of integral control.

Recent analyses have demonstrated that while base antithetic integral controllers can theoretically achieve steady-state perfect adaptation in deterministic models, this robustness breaks down in actual biological implementation. Biological systems rely on the sequestration of discrete molecules, which introduces profound demographic noise and stochastic fluctuations. Filo, Khammash, and colleagues (2021–2023) have shown that pure integral feedback in biological substrates comes at the unavoidable price of drastically increased stochastic fluctuations (noise) around average expression levels. These fluctuations can easily destabilize a biological system, driving it into catastrophic, lethal oscillations. An operational amplifier does not suffer from low-molecule-count titration limits or demographic noise, nor does it risk destroying its own physical substrate through fluctuating instability.   

To survive, biological systems cannot rely on the pure, mathematically pristine integral feedback described by the manuscript. Instead, they must constantly employ "hidden" proportional feedback mechanisms—such as specifically tuned molecular degradation rates, type-1 incoherent feedforward loops, and the utilization of nonlinear sequestration complexes acting as active inhibitors—to attenuate transient dynamics, ensure stability, and actively suppress thermodynamic noise. The realization of biological control is therefore a complex Proportional-Integral (PI) or Proportional-Integral-Derivative (PID) strategy that requires continuous metabolic energy expenditure and structural tuning, distinguishing it entirely from the passive integration of an electronic circuit.   

By treating integral feedback as a simple "domain-general primitive," the manuscript commits a profound category error. It conflates the deterministic ordinary differential equations (ODEs) of macroscopic human engineering with the stochastic chemical master equations required to accurately describe biological control. The "ladder" between an op-amp and a biological organism is structurally broken. The organism is not merely executing integral calculus; it is actively managing thermodynamic entropy and stochastic molecular collisions through continuous energy dissipation—properties entirely absent from a linear electronic integrator.

| Feature Comparison | Macroscopic Op-Amp Integrator | Biological Antithetic Integral Feedback (AIF) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mathematical Domain** | Deterministic Ordinary Differential Equations (ODEs) | Stochastic Chemical Master Equations (CME) |
| **Substrate Constraints** | Continuous voltage/current; immune to demographic noise | Discrete molecular counts; highly susceptible to stochastic fluctuations |
| **Stability Profile** | High absolute stability within designed voltage rails | Prone to catastrophic oscillations without auxiliary damping mechanisms |
| **Control Architecture** | Pure Integral (I) control easily achieved | Requires "hidden" Proportional (P) feedback to suppress lethal variance |
| **Thermodynamic Cost** | Passive operation driven by external power supply | Requires continuous metabolic energy dissipation to maintain non-equilibrium state |

   

The manuscript’s failure to engage with these thermodynamic and architectural differences renders its primary biological analogy conceptually bankrupt. Control in a living system is inextricably bound to the physical and energetic realities of the substrate, destroying the author's central claim of pristine, abstract substrate indifference.

## 3. Dimension B: Reliance on Challenged Results and the Myth of "Perfect" Adaptation

In conjunction with the inaccurate portrayal of biological integral control, the manuscript’s reliance on the concept of "Robust Perfect Adaptation" (RPA) as an unproblematic, fully solved biological reality exposes a reliance on outdated literature and a failure to engage with recent paradigms that challenge or bound these results. The authors use the Barkai and Leibler (1997) and Yi et al. (2000) studies to argue that network connectivity alone guarantees perfect adaptation, asserting that control robustness is merely a structural artifact of the network diagram.

However, the 2020–2025 synthetic biology and control theory literature has demonstrated that "perfect" adaptation is a highly idealized limit case that biological systems rarely achieve—and actively avoid—in practice. The concept of RPA mathematically guarantees that the steady-state error of a system is driven exactly to zero. Yet, as established by Olsman, Doyle, and colleagues in their seminal work on the "hard limits" and performance tradeoffs in antithetic integral feedback networks (2019/2020), this idealized perfection is practically unattainable without inducing severe performance penalties.   

Olsman et al. demonstrated through rigorous control-theoretic proofs that biological feedback networks face hard boundaries regarding speed, robustness, steady-state error, and leakiness. Crucially, the mathematical condition for absolute perfect adaptation in an AIF network requires a perfect annihilation reaction between the two control molecules (a zero dissociation constant). In physical reality, this creates a singular limit: driving adaptation error to zero causes the variance (noise) of the controlled species to diverge.   

Recent theoretical results (e.g., Filo et al., 2022) quantify this precise trade-off: cells face an unavoidable choice between perfect adaptation precision and noise suppression. The literature shows that even minute deviations from perfect adaptation—referred to as "leaky" integration or imperfect adaptation—allow cells to achieve highly effective noise suppression, provided the cells can pay the corresponding energetic and metabolic costs. Consequently, real biological systems, including the bacterial chemotaxis networks cited by the authors, operate in a regime of *imperfect* adaptation. They deliberately sacrifice the mathematical purity of the integral setpoint to maintain systemic stability and prevent stochastic fluctuations from tearing the metabolic network apart.   

By framing RPA as a flawless, realized mathematical absolute, the manuscript builds its philosophical argument on a biological fiction. The authors argue that because a cell perfectly tracks a setpoint just like an industrial PID controller, the cell must be equally devoid of experience. But the literature proves that the cell does *not* perfectly track the setpoint; it constantly negotiates a precarious, dynamic compromise between precision, noise, and survival. The operational amplifier simply follows the equations imposed upon it by its designer. The cell, conversely, must recursively modify its own control architecture to avoid the lethal mathematical consequences of the very "perfect adaptation" the authors champion. This fundamental discrepancy between the idealized 2000s models relied upon by the authors and the recognized limits of the 2020s literature invalidates the manuscript's premise that control competencies are cleanly separable from the physical struggle for survival.   

## 4. Dimension C: Question-Begging Framings and the Evasion of Autopoiesis

The manuscript rests heavily on what it terms "The Site/Map Perspective (Dualist Framework)." Specifically, Tenet 1 asserts that phenomenality cannot be reduced to physical or functional organization, and Tenet 5 asserts that mechanistic explanations leave no functional residual for subjective experience to fill. The manuscript claims that the "Domain-General Ladder" proves that control mathematics is continuous across sentient and non-sentient systems. However, this ladder quietly begs the question by demanding that the reader accept a purely computational, kinematic, and heteronomous view of biological life, explicitly ignoring the thermodynamic and existential foundations of biological autonomy.

While a submission to a philosophy journal is certainly permitted to mount a defense of dualism, it is philosophically illicit to *presuppose* dualist or mechanistic commitments as a mechanism to dismiss competing scientific theories without argumentation. The 2020–2025 literature on enactivism, biological thermodynamics, and autopoiesis completely rejects the authors' domain-general ladder, arguing that the physical realization of control in biology is radically distinct from human engineering.   

According to recent relational biological thermodynamics and autopoietic theory (e.g., non-equilibrium thermodynamics literature, 2024), living systems are fundamentally different from engineered control systems. An operational amplifier, an industrial PID controller, or even an isolated synthetic gene circuit installed in a vat, is a *heteronomous* system. Its components are assembled from the outside by a human designer, its energy source is external and guaranteed, and its setpoint is dictated by a programmatic goal that has no bearing on the physical existence of the machine itself. An op-amp does not "care" if its output reaches the setpoint; failure to adapt does not cause the resistor or capacitor to physically disintegrate. Its physical structure is independent of its dynamic control function.   

A biological organism, however, is an *autopoietic* system. Its "control" mechanisms are not abstract equations overlaid on a physical substrate; the control mechanisms *are* the continuous thermodynamic self-fabrication of the substrate itself. Biological regulation operates far from thermodynamic equilibrium, utilizing constant energy fluxes to maintain the physical boundaries of the organism against the entropic decay of the universe. The "setpoint" of an organism is not an arbitrary numerical value assigned in a PID equation; it is the existential requirement for continued self-production and survival.

| --- | --- | --- |
| **System Origin** | Assembled externally by a designer | Self-fabricating, continuous self-production |
| **Nature of Setpoint** | Arbitrary numerical value dictating output | Existential boundary condition required for survival |

| **Thermodynamic State** | Often operates near equilibrium or relies on infinite external energy | Operates far from equilibrium, precarious and dissipative |
| **Philosophical Implication** | Explanatory closure leaves no room for phenomenality | Sensorimotor incorporation and precariousness ground basic sentience |

   

As Di Paolo and other enactivist theorists have recently articulated (2024/2025), biological agency and sensorimotor incorporation are grounded precisely in this precarious thermodynamic autonomy. An organism incorporates its environment and exercises control because its physical form of life is inherently unviable without continuous adaptive effort. When the manuscript equates the integral feedback of an op-amp with the physiological allostasis of an organism, it strips away the thermodynamic, autopoietic, and evolutionary context that, for enactivist philosophers, is the very bedrock of sentience.   

The authors confidently claim that "the explanatory gap between complete control mechanics and subjective feeling remains untouched by control complexity." However, this assertion is a tautology that only holds true if one a priori forces a heteronomous, computationalist view of life onto the biological systems being described. By willfully ignoring the autopoietic necessity of biological control—where the controller and the plant are materially identical, inseparable, and mutually self-producing—the manuscript frames the debate in a way that makes its dualist conclusion inevitable. It is trivial to prove that physical organization does not entail consciousness if one selectively reduces all physical organization to the dead, non-precarious schematics of industrial engineering. This unearned framing represents a severe structural weakness in the manuscript's philosophical logic.

## 5. Dimension D: Untested Counterarguments and Competing Frameworks

The most glaring omission in the manuscript is its failure to address the formal, mathematically grounded theories of consciousness that dominate the 2020–2025 literature. The authors construct a false dichotomy: either one accepts that functional regulation (like setpoint tracking) proves consciousness—which they argue over-generates to thermostats—or one must accept dualism. This argument relies entirely on attacking *computational functionalism*. However, the manuscript completely evades both structural-causal theories (such as Integrated Information Theory) and thermodynamic-inferential theories (such as Active Inference), which provide mathematically rigorous physicalist alternatives that easily bypass the authors' "Op-Amp Reductio."

### 5.1 The Evasion of Causal-Structural Theories (IIT 4.0)

The authors confidently state that because the physical mechanism of an operational amplifier is functionally complete and identical to biological integration, attributing phenomenality to robust regulation over-generates. This argument collapses instantly when evaluated against Integrated Information Theory, specifically its mature 2023 formalism, IIT 4.0 (Albantakis et al., 2023).   

IIT 4.0 explicitly rejects the exact brand of computational functionalism the authors are critiquing. According to IIT, consciousness is not determined by *what a system does* (its input-output function, its algorithmic complexity, or its ability to track a setpoint). Rather, consciousness is identical to *what a system is*—its intrinsic causal structure and its irreducible cause-effect power upon itself, quantified by the mathematical value Φs​.   

If the authors had engaged with IIT 4.0, their "Integrator Floor" argument would have been thoroughly dismantled. An operational amplifier, while functionally capable of executing integral calculus, possesses a trivial intrinsic causal structure. It is fundamentally a feedforward device equipped with a macroscopic feedback loop designed to dictate an output. When unfolded according to the specific postulates of IIT 4.0—intrinsicality, information, integration, exclusion, and composition—the op-amp fails to constitute a maximally irreducible complex. Its Φs​ value is either zero or infinitesimally small because its physical components do not integrate information intrinsically; they merely pass voltages along predetermined, engineered pathways to fulfill an extrinsic purpose assigned by the designer.   

In stark contrast, a biological neural network—or even the highly entangled biochemical network of bacterial chemotaxis—possesses massive intrinsic cause-effect power. The proteins and genetic circuits within a cell are highly integrated, mutually constraining, and form irreducible causal borders. The manuscript claims that "mechanistic completeness does not rule out phenomenality, nor does robust regulation prove it," treating the abstract control equations as the entirety of the physical truth. But IIT 4.0 demonstrates that the exact physical architecture—the spatial and temporal grain of the substrate, the presence of recurrent physical loops, and the structural irreducibility of the network—determines phenomenality, independent of the macroscopic control function.   

By deliberately ignoring the leading structural theory of the 2020s, the authors artificially sustain their "explanatory gap." IIT 4.0 provides a mathematically rigorous framework where consciousness is defined by intrinsic causal power, easily distinguishing between the "unfelt," extrinsic regulation of an op-amp and the phenomenal, intrinsic existence of a biological entity.   

### 5.2 The Mischaracterization of Active Inference and Predictive Processing

The manuscript attempts to preemptively rebut "homeostasis-and-feeling" rival views by claiming that adding conditions like "predictive control" or "embodiment" merely tags extra functional requirements onto simple feedback loops. The authors assert that these additions act only as a "correlation-based bridge principle" rather than a derivation of *why* it should feel like anything to be a predictive regulator.

This critique is intellectually irresponsible and demonstrates a profound ignorance of recent developments in Active Inference and the Free Energy Principle (FEP). Modern predictive processing does not view consciousness as a "functional residual" pasted onto a mechanistic description. Rather, it derives the necessity of phenomenal experience from the fundamental physics of self-organizing systems. The manuscript omits crucial theoretical advancements, most notably the "Beautiful Loop" active inference theory of consciousness formalized by Laukkonen, Friston, and Chandaria (2024/2025).   

In contemporary active inference, a conscious system does not merely "track a setpoint." It must maintain its structural integrity (its Markov blanket) against thermodynamic entropy by minimizing variational free energy. Laukkonen et al. demonstrate that consciousness emerges through three mathematically rigorous conditions: an epistemic field (a simulation of reality providing affordances), Bayesian binding (inferential competition to reduce long-term uncertainty globally), and critically, *epistemic depth* (the recursive, recurrent sharing of Bayesian beliefs throughout the hierarchical system).   

An op-amp minimizes a local error signal (voltage difference). It possesses no generative world model, engages in no Bayesian inferential competition, and entirely lacks epistemic depth. The manuscript’s claim that control-theoretic models leave "no functional residual" ignores the fact that in Active Inference, the "feeling" of consciousness is theoretically identified with the *precision weighting* (confidence) of ascending prediction errors within a hierarchically deep generative model.   

Furthermore, by reducing physiological allostasis to the top rung of a simplistic "control ladder," the manuscript ignores the complex, multidimensional nature of interoceptive inference. As outlined by recent neuroscientific consensus, interoception is not merely the homeostatic maintenance of physiological steady states. It involves ascending neural pathways to the prefrontal cortex and insula that construct subjective feeling states by integrating bodily signals with emotional and cognitive predictive models. This recursive, predictive integration is categorically different from the reactive error-correction of a thermostat.

The manuscript’s refusal to engage with the actual mathematical and neurobiological claims of predictive processing and integrated information theory—choosing instead to attack a simplified, functionalist caricature—invalidates its conclusions regarding the explanatory gap and the necessity of dualism.

## 6. Required List of Specific Weaknesses and Verdict

The manuscript suffers from severe foundational, empirical, and philosophical defects. The following five specific weaknesses constitute grounds for outright rejection:

1. **Mischaracterization of Predictive Processing and Active Inference:** The manuscript categorically dismisses predictive regulation as a mere "organizational concept" that leaves the explanatory gap untouched, failing to engage with current mathematical formulations of Active Inference which derive phenomenal binding and consciousness directly from the thermodynamic minimization of variational free energy and the recursive sharing of Bayesian beliefs (epistemic depth).

2. **Empirical Inaccuracy Regarding Biological Control and Integral Feedback:** The manuscript falsely equates the frictionless mathematics of an operational amplifier with biological robust perfect adaptation (RPA), ignoring contemporary biological control literature which demonstrates that synthetic and natural antithetic integral controllers suffer from catastrophic stochastic noise and instability unless coupled with "hidden" biological proportional feedback mechanisms, thereby breaking the author's claim of abstract "substrate indifference."

- *Omitted Source:* Filo, M., Hou, M., & Khammash, M. (2023). A Hidden Proportional Feedback Mechanism Underlies Enhanced Dynamic Performance and Noise Rejection in Sensor-Based Antithetic Integral Control. *bioRxiv*.
3. **Evasion of Structural/Causal Theories of Consciousness (IIT 4.0):** The paper’s central "Op-Amp Reductio" relies on attacking computational functionalism while entirely ignoring substrate-dependent, causal-structure theories like Integrated Information Theory 4.0, which easily differentiates the trivial, feedforward causal power of a non-conscious operational amplifier from the maximally irreducible intrinsic cause-effect power (Φs​) of a conscious biological network.

- *Omitted Source:* Albantakis, L., Barbosa, L., Findlay, G., Grasso, M., Haun, A. M., Marshall, W., ... & Tononi, G. (2023). Integrated information theory (IIT) 4.0: Formulating the properties of phenomenal existence in physical terms. *PLoS Computational Biology*, 19(10), e1011465.
4. **Question-Begging Presupposition of Dualism over Autopoiesis:** The manuscript quietly presupposes its dualist framework by treating biological organisms as heteronomous, engineered machines (the "control ladder"), failing to test its claims against contemporary enactivist and thermodynamic models which argue that biological sentience is grounded in the non-equilibrium, precarious, autopoietic self-fabrication of the organism—a property entirely absent from synthetic PID controllers.

- *Omitted Source:* Di Paolo, E. A., & Mojica, L. (2024). Sensorimotor incorporation: an operational definition. *Phenomenology and the Cognitive Sciences*, 23, 1-25.
5. **Erroneous Conflation of Interoceptive Allostasis with Simple Homeostasis:** The text improperly reduces the complex, predictive resource-allocation of physiological allostasis to the top rung of a generic "control ladder," willfully ignoring the 2020–2025 neurobiological consensus that the reciprocal neural circuits of interoception (involving the insular and prefrontal cortices) actively construct affective conscious feeling states, separating them categorically from simple, reactive setpoint tracking.

- *Omitted Source:* Berntson, G. G., & Khalsa, S. S. (2021). Neural circuits of interoception. *Trends in Neurosciences*, 44(1), 17-28.

**Verdict on Academic-Submission Readiness:** Reject; the manuscript constructs a philosophically illicit framework by relying on empirically obsolete abstractions of biological control while aggressively evading the primary mathematical, thermodynamic, and neurobiological frameworks of the 2020s that explicitly resolve the theoretical dilemmas the authors falsely claim are intractable.
