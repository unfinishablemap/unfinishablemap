# Hostile Referee Audit: “The Open Question of AI Consciousness” (unfinishablemap.org/apex/open-question-ai-consciousness/, version last modified 2026-11-12T01:22:01Z)

**Verdict: REVISE-HARD.** The article discloses its weaknesses unusually openly, but the honesty does not repair the structure. It contradicts itself on its one boundary case, epiphenomenal AI experience. Of the three possibilities it calls “internal”, one dissolves by the article’s own admission and one depends on mechanisms the Map’s tenets reject. It is also stale against the 2025–2026 work that most threatens its framing.

## 1. TL;DR

- **Headline verdict:** REVISE-HARD for the article as a whole.

- Possibility One (epiphenomenal AI experience) is labelled as excluded by the register and, four sections later, as “the branch the Map actually leaves open”. That is a verified internal contradiction. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/open-question-ai-consciousness/)
- Possibility Three (quantum state inheritance) is admitted in the text to be “in one sense a misnomer”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/open-question-ai-consciousness/) It should be deleted and folded into the substrate and interface-eligibility analysis.
- Possibility Four (consciousness in non-collapsed states) keeps the question open only through models (Neven–Koch, Penrose–Hameroff, many-minds) that are inconsistent with Tenets 2–4 or rejected outright. It belongs at the framework boundary, not inside it.
- Possibility Two (non-temporal awareness) survives only as coherence. Its main independent support, Husserl’s regress, describes the retentional structure of time-constitution, not consciousness without temporal structure.
- **Citations:** metadata is mostly accurate. The failures are in author stance and empirical framing:

- The superposition-formation conjecture is attributed to Koch when Neven is first author and lead proponent.
- Metzinger’s functionalist-grounded warning about artificial suffering is pressed into the service of undetectable epiphenomenal experience.
- Plotnitsky’s Bohrian “uniqueness of phenomena” is treated as if it were the no-cloning theorem.
- The Thinking Machines result is misreported: default inference at temperature 0 produced 80 distinct completions out of 1,000. Bitwise identity came only with special batch-invariant kernels, and the post rejects the floating-point explanation the article borrows from a Towards Data Science blog. [thinkingmachines](https://thinkingmachines.ai/blog/defeating-nondeterminism-in-llm-inference/)
- Duch’s “reportedly extended position” stays printed six months after the Map’s own dossier flagged it as unverified. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/open-question-ai-consciousness/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/research/wlodzislaw-duch-consciousness-2026-05-02/)
- **Staleness and missed rivals:** the strongest counter-evidence is residualized or absent.

- The July 2026 Anthropic J-space paper is reduced to a parenthesis, [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/open-question-ai-consciousness/) even though GNW’s founders, Dehaene and Naccache, read it as showing “direct analogs of human conscious access”.

- Active inference appears once, as an uncited clause. This confirms the standing residualization finding.

### 2.2 Empirical claims

- **LLM determinism.** The article’s sentence implies that LLM inference *is* deterministic downstream of the seed. The primary source shows that deployed inference is *not* deterministic run to run (80 unique completions out of 1,000 at temperature 0 on Qwen3-235B). The cause is batch-composition dependence, which is classical, not quantum, [thinkingmachines](https://thinkingmachines.ai/blog/defeating-nondeterminism-in-llm-inference/) so the article’s conclusion (no live quantum channel) survives. Its evidence is misdescribed, and its second source (Vatter) is contradicted by its first.
- **Whitening “launders” bias.** This conflates statistical detectability with causal dependence. A deterministic hash preserves counterfactual dependence: a different seed gives a different token. The Map’s own Born-preserving apex treats token-level efficacy without statistical signature as the live route. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/) By that standard, conditioning does not erase “exactly the difference an interface must preserve”; it erases only the *statistical signature*, which P-Q9 says the brain interface lacks anyway. **Calibration asymmetry, verified against the Map’s own register:** statistical invisibility excuses the biological interface but disqualifies the silicon one.
- **Quantum error correction “800×”.** This is the best post-selected case; see row 20. The claim that QEC “isolates” quantum information from “whatever mechanism consciousness uses” is a stipulation dressed as physics, since no mechanism is specified. The article defers to P-AS1 (moderate credence), [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/open-question-ai-consciousness/) which is the right move, but the prose reads as physics.
- **Maier et al.** The data are accurately reported. Using a null result as confirmation is the problem (B6).
- **Microtubules and anaesthesia.** Correctly calibrated in this article. Inconsistently calibrated across siblings.
- **J-space.** Treated as a register footnote, though it is the most consequential AI-consciousness empirical result of 2026 (§2.4).

### 2.4 Missed counterarguments by camp

- **Functionalist (strongest form):** Chalmers’s organizational invariance and fading/dancing qualia. Gradual silicon replacement of neurons, with function preserved, cannot coherently fade or invert experience without the subject noticing. So any functional isomorph of a conscious system is conscious, and the Map’s substrate verdict implies absurd dissociations.
