### 2.2 Evolutionary and biological claims against current literature

1. **“The human-great ape intelligence gap—approximately tenfold despite only 1-2% genetic difference”.** [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/evolution-of-consciousness/) This is unsourced, and “tenfold intelligence” has no operational meaning in comparative cognition. The genetic figure is also selective. The Chimpanzee Sequencing and Analysis Consortium (*Nature*, 2005) put genome-wide nucleotide divergence at 1.23%, while lineage-specific indels cover about 3% of each genome, roughly two and a half times the substitution difference. **Delete or source.**
2. **“They fail precisely where consciousness appears required.”** Current comparative work does not support a clean dissociation. The page’s own sibling, consciousness-and-cognitive-distinctiveness, cites great-ape false-belief anticipation (Krupenye et al. 2016; Buttelmann et al. 2017). It also concedes the comparative gap “does not discriminate between the rival readings”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/consciousness-and-cognitive-distinctiveness/) The two pages contradict each other.
3. **Phylogenetic distribution.** “cephalopod consciousness evolved independently of vertebrate consciousness” is asserted as fact. It presupposes cephalopod consciousness, which the New York Declaration grades only as “a realistic possibility”, and the dichotomy (a)/(b) omits (c): absence in one lineage. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/evolution-of-consciousness/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/evolution-of-consciousness/) The page should engage the markers-based literature, including:

- Birch, Schnell & Clayton 2020 on dimensions;
- the 2021 LSE review for Defra by Birch, Burn, Schnell, Browning & Crump, which drew on over 300 studies, recommended that all cephalopods and decapods be regarded as sentient, and informed the Animal Welfare (Sentience) Act 2022;
- Barron & Klein 2016 on insects;
- Chittka’s bee play and nociceptive-modulation work;
- the unresolved Sneddon–Key fish-pain dispute.

Birch is cited but not used.
4. **Threshold models misdescribed.** IIT is listed under “threshold emergence”, but Φ is graded. UAL is rendered as an all-or-nothing switch, which G&J do not hold. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/evolution-of-consciousness/)
5. **Unconscious-processing evidence.** The page’s first falsifier asks for a demonstration that “all behavioural flexibility can be explained by unconscious processing”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/evolution-of-consciousness/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/evolution-of-consciousness/) That is not the live empirical question. ### 2.3 Omitted or understated physicalist, epiphenomenalist and illusionist replies

The page’s anti-epiphenomenalist core reads: “if consciousness evolved through selection, it must have had effects for selection to act on.” [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/evolution-of-consciousness/) Against this, the strongest replies are:

1. **Identity/functionalist reply (absent).** If phenomenal states are, or are realised by, neural-functional states, they were selected because their realisers were efficacious. James’s argument then tells against epiphenomenalism, not for dualism. The page’s “Dualist answer” and “Materialist answer” both predict the same distribution of consciousness, so the evolutionary data cannot choose between them. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/evolution-of-consciousness/) This is evidential-independence failure.
2. **By-product/spandrel reply (residualised to one clause).** Gould & Lewontin’s spandrels [mindmatters](https://mindmatters.ai/2020/02/did-consciousness-evolve-a-darwinist-responds/) and Jackson’s 1982 polar-bear-coat analogy are absent: the heavy coat is selected and its weight comes along. So is Robinson’s sustained defence of epiphenomenalism against the evolutionary argument (his SEP entry and his work on evolution and epiphenomenalism). The site has a co-optimization page, and the dependency concedes in one sentence that “an epiphenomenalist co-optimizer explains valence-fitness tracking too”. But the body still says the evolutionary case shows epiphenomenalism “untenable”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/evolution-of-consciousness/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/evolution-of-consciousness/) That is confession-without-correction inside a single page.
3. **Psychophysical-harmony literature (absent).** Mørch’s “evolutionary argument for phenomenal powers”, Goff’s harmony problem, Cutter & Crummett’s theistic harmony argument, Saad, and Pautz on normative harmony are the current state of the James argument. Their shared upshot is that the harmony datum is a problem for *non-physicalist* views too, including interactionism with brute psychophysical laws. The page treats harmony as a one-way weapon.
4. **Kim’s exclusion, inherited (not addressed here).** Interactionism at quantum indeterminacies escapes exclusion only by denying completeness. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/arguments-for-mental-causation/) That, in turn, triggers the Born dilemma (2.4).
5. **Illusionist adaptive accounts (absent).** The page asks “why couldn’t genuine consciousness provide those advantages directly?” [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/evolution-of-consciousness/) That question begs the issue. Humphrey’s evolved “magic show” of sentience and Graziano’s attention schema theory each supply an explicit adaptive function for the *representation* of phenomenality: attention control, social modelling and self-valuing. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/research/illusionism-consciousness-2026-01-14/) Neither is cited.
6. **Meta-problem and evolutionary debunking (absent here).** Chalmers’s meta-problem shows that phenomenal judgments have a functional explanation available to every side. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/metaproblem-of-consciousness-under-dualism/) An evolutionary debunker can then explain the “too-systematic tracking” intuition without phenomenal efficacy.
7. **Predictive processing / active inference (absent, so the gate fails).** “Rather than needing explicit reward signals, conscious organisms are *motivated* by felt quality” is precisely the claim that active-inference accounts of valence answer functionally. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/evolution-of-consciousness/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/evolution-of-consciousness/) On those accounts valence is tied to precision-weighted prediction-error dynamics and expected free energy. Laukkonen, Friston & Chandaria’s “A beautiful loop: An active inference theory of consciousness” (*Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews* 176:106296, 2025) offers an evolutionary-functional story for learning, valence and selfhood with no non-physical relatum. The page neither engages nor residualises it. It is simply absent.
8. **The decisive general point (absent).** Selection acts only on physical behavioural and functional differences. [Substack](https://generousking.substack.com/p/why-consciousness-could-not-have-cd4)[nih](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6117425/) Any physicalist view with the same functional profile predicts the same selective history. The evolutionary argument is therefore at most *compatible* with interactionism on the evidence ladder, never *discriminating*.

### 2.4 Overclaims, internal inconsistencies, confidence outrunning evidence

- **Born-rule/selectability dilemma (central, unresolved).** The page now adds the proviso that selection favours consciousness “provided that effect shifts fitness-relevant frequencies”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/evolution-of-consciousness/) Take the first horn: consciousness shifts outcome frequencies in fitness-relevant contexts. Then it produces aggregate deviation from Born statistics in exactly those contexts, which contradicts the Born-preserving apex’s no-deviation commitment and makes the view empirically exposed. Take the second horn: it does not shift them. Then, per the site’s own selectability result, selection has nothing to act on, and the evolutionary case for mental causation *through the selector* collapses. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/) The page names the proviso but picks neither horn. Meanwhile it retains “the strongest positive case for why consciousness was selected” and “untenable”. This is confession-without-correction and coherence inflation together.
- **Zombie inconsistency.** The page says: “The Map’s answer: there are no philosophical zombies … the neural processes associated with consciousness *constitutively involve* consciousness.” [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/evolution-of-consciousness/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/evolution-of-consciousness/) This sits badly with the site’s arguments index, which says “Chalmers’ zombie argument shows consciousness is not entailed by physical facts”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/) It is also near-identity language that undercuts substance dualism. Worse, under Born-preserving selection a consciousness-free physical duplicate *would* be statistically indistinguishable, which the metaproblem page concedes (“make no predictively distinct claims at any scale”). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/metaproblem-of-consciousness-under-dualism/)
- **“Generate” vs “interface”.** The page quotes the Cambridge Declaration’s substrates that “generate consciousness” approvingly, then asserts “Brains don’t generate consciousness; they channel it”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/evolution-of-consciousness/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/evolution-of-consciousness/) The empirical declaration is being used in a framing it contradicts.
- **“Dualist advantage” overclaim.** “The interface view dissolves the strong emergence problem” and the page “handles these questions better than materialist alternatives”. In fact dualism relocates the puzzle into brute psychophysical coupling laws (“lawful relationships”), and the page offers no account of why coupling tracks the architectures it does. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/evolution-of-consciousness/) That is calibration asymmetry: “Strong emergence looks like magic” when the materialist posits brute bridge facts, yet brute coupling facts are free for the dualist.
- **Fox et al. non sequitur.** “you cannot get better at observing a causally inert process.” [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/evolution-of-consciousness/) On epiphenomenalism, introspective *judgments* are physically caused, and training changes the physical processes producing them. Accuracy was measured against a physical criterion. [plos](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article) The study cannot bear on epiphenomenalism. **Delete the inference.**
- **Illusionism equivocation.** “That something, whatever we call it, is what the Map means by consciousness.” [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/evolution-of-consciousness/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/evolution-of-consciousness/) If the Map’s “consciousness” is whatever functional organisation necessitates, the Dualism tenet has been traded away for a verbal win.
- **Falsifier #2 already arguably triggered, but not acknowledged.** “If artificial systems achieved adaptive behaviour through purely classical computation … this would challenge the claim.” [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/evolution-of-consciousness/) Contemporary classical AI systems show substantial flexible behaviour, and the site’s AI pages hold they lack an interface. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/open-question-ai-consciousness/) The page should say whether that defeater has fired and why not. Falsifier #1 is unfalsifiable as worded (“all behavioural flexibility”). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/evolution-of-consciousness/)
- **Sibling drift (snippet-level, live state unverified).** Indexed copies show interactionist-dualism saying “This resolves the materialist puzzle … selection acts on it directly”. The arguments index says epiphenomenal consciousness means “natural selection cannot explain its existence”, which is false, since by-product explanation explains existence. The metaproblem page says consciousness provides “information that purely physical mechanisms lack”, in tension with Born preservation. [unfinishablemap + 2](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/metaproblem-of-consciousness-under-dualism/) The changelog shows the bidirectional-interaction “positive support” line was already repaired, so some snippets may be stale. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/)
- **Credit where due.** The page openly concedes the Everettian branch-local adaptationist story (“a posit the Map adopts, not a result the evolutionary case derives”). It withdraws photosynthesis, flags the working-memory comparison as contested, and concedes that Tallis’s regress does not refute illusionism. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/evolution-of-consciousness/) These are genuine corrections.
