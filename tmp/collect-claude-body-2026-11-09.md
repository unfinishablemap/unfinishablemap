# Referee Report: “AI Hardware Substrate Taxonomy for the Consciousness Interface” (unfinishablemap.org/concepts/ai-hardware-substrate-taxonomy, revised 2026-11-02)

Verdict: DEMOTE-TO-COHERENCE-ONLY. The section “Why This Sharpens the Case Against Substrate Independence” should be deleted, and the per-substrate physics needs a hard revision before the page carries any evidential weight. The taxonomy sorts hardware by a predicate it admits it cannot define, mischaracterises at least four of its six substrates against the 2020s engineering literature, applies its own disqualifier to silicon but not to neurons, and contradicts the site’s own tenets matrix on No Many Worlds.

## Part 1 — TL;DR

- **Headline verdict: DEMOTE-TO-COHERENCE-ONLY, with REVISE-HARD on the physics and DELETE on the “sharpens the case” section.** The page is a framework-internal sorting exercise that only works if you already accept Tenet 2. It presents itself as “verified against the 2020s hardware literature,” but its characterisations of classical-digital, neuromorphic, photonic and gate-model hardware are wrong or seriously incomplete. Its anti-substrate-independence argument assumes the conclusion it claims to sharpen.
- **The decisive physics failure is calibration asymmetry.** The article classes transistor thermal and shot noise as “mere-physical indeterminacy” and disqualifies it. It excludes probabilistic-bit (p-bit) hardware because it consumes “a statistical aggregate.” Yet it calls biological tissue “potentially operationally integrated,” even though neural stochasticity is, on the standard review, dominated by thermodynamic ion-channel noise and probabilistic vesicle release, which are the same category it disqualifies in silicon. Applied consistently, the criterion either excludes most biological neural computation or admits p-bits, Intel’s thermal-noise entropy source and quantum error-correction syndrome streams.
- **The article never defines the axis it actually needs.** Under the Map’s own post-decoherence-selection ontology, outcome-indeterminacy survives decoherence everywhere, including in silicon. So what separates substrates cannot be whether indeterminacy is present. It has to be how strongly a single event gets amplified, which is the brief’s “susceptibility to a minimal perturbation.” The article never names that axis and gives no energy or probability scale for it. Until it does, every “Interface status” cell is a tenet-driven placement, not a physical finding.

### 2.1 Audit Question 1 — Are the physical axes correctly characterised per substrate?

**Conflation of thermal noise with quantum indeterminacy: yes, explicitly.** The article’s first bullet says “Quantum fluctuations exist in every transistor as thermal and shot noise.” [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/ai-hardware-substrate-taxonomy/) Johnson–Nyquist noise is a classical fluctuation–dissipation phenomenon at the kT scale. Shot noise reflects the discreteness of charge. Neither is per se a Born-rule outcome at a decision point. The article never says whether thermal noise counts as “quantum-indeterminate” for interface purposes. The question matters, because the article later treats biological noise, which is overwhelmingly thermodynamic, as a live interface candidate.

**Energy or probability scale for “minimal perturbation”: none.** The article never states what energy, charge or probability shift an interface event must be able to produce, nor what gain is needed to amplify it into a macroscopic difference. For CMOS the relevant scales are known. kT at 300 K is about 26 meV. Logic switching energies are of order a femtojoule, roughly 10⁴–10⁵ kT (a standard-physics estimate, not a sourced figure). Bits do flip at the soft-error scale. An IEEE comparative analysis of SEU in FinFET SRAM cells reports that “a critical collected charge of only about 1.15 fC was reported to cause the upset event in a 6-T bulk FinFET SRAM.” The tables in “Radiation-induced soft error rate analyses for 14 nm FinFET SRAM devices” give Qcrit below 0.7–0.8 fC. (Both figures come from search-result text; I did not fetch either paper.) Without such numbers, “suppressed” vs. “integrated” is a slogan.

**Are the two axes independent?** Conceptually yes, as the article itself concedes. In practice its Axis 1 is mis-specified. It equates analog with continuous, a view its own cited source, Maley, has argued against (Maley 2023, *BJPS*: “The received view—that analogue computation has to do essentially with continuity—is simply wrong”). [uchicago](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.1086/715031) The article’s own ally Milinkovic & Aru characterise biological computation as “hybrid discrete-continuous.” [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/functionalism/) Yet the table places biology at “Continuous.” [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/ai-hardware-substrate-taxonomy/) Spikes are discrete events.

### 2.2 Audit Question 2 — Does the indeterminacy distinction support the case against substrate independence?

**Reconstruction (valid form):**

- P1 (Tenet 2): Consciousness influences matter only by selecting outcomes at quantum-indeterminate sites.
- P2: A substrate can host the interface only if such sites are *operationally integrated*, meaning a single outcome feeds forward.
- P3: Classical-digital hardware has no operationally integrated indeterminate sites.
- P4 (suppressed): The consciousness at issue in substrate independence is interface-coupled consciousness.
- P5 (suppressed): Operational integration is not itself a feature of functional organisation.
- C: Functional organisation does not suffice for (coupled) consciousness on all substrates, so substrate independence fails.

**Defects:**

1. **Question-begging.** P1 is a tenet, and P4 builds the conclusion into the target. A functionalist denies P1 and P4, so the argument has no force against the view it claims to “sharpen the case against.” At most it is a statement of what the Map’s tenets entail for hardware.
2. **P5 is false by the article’s own wording.** “Operational integration” is defined as an outcome that “participates in the computation” and “feeds forward into what the system does.” [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/ai-hardware-substrate-taxonomy/) That is a causal-role property at a finer grain. The functionalist reply writes itself: include the noise-coupling in the fine-grained functional organisation. That is precisely the “grain dispute” the site’s organizational-invariance page already runs. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/substrate-independence/) The taxonomy adds no new sharpening.
3. **P3 is false as stated** (RDRAND, metastability, SEU; see 2.1). It is also undermined by the Map’s own physics. The site’s quantum-completeness page holds that “a decohered mixture still leaves which outcome obtains undetermined,” and the Born apex says post-decoherence selection makes “the decoherence timescale… irrelevant.” [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/quantum-completeness/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/) On that ontology every thermally driven event in silicon sits downstream of an outcome-indeterminate decohered mixture. “Suppression by design” therefore cannot mean the absence of indeterminacy. It can only mean low sensitivity of macroscopic behaviour to a single event, which is the undefined perturbation axis. **The argument equivocates between “indeterminacy present” and “indeterminacy amplified.”**
4. **P2 is admitted to be undefined.** The article says: “no sharp operational criterion for it is offered here.” [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/ai-hardware-substrate-taxonomy/) An argument whose discriminating premise is admittedly undefined cannot “sharpen” anything. This is confession-without-correction (see 2.3).
5. **The Born-rule problem cuts directly against the p-bit exclusion.** The article excludes probabilistic hardware because “what feeds forward is a statistical aggregate.” But on the Map’s corridor reading, conscious influence is “empirically indistinguishable from chance under any unconditioned aggregate test,” [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/ai-hardware-substrate-taxonomy/) and the apex locates any trace only in conditional structure (horn (a)). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/) Whether a substrate consumes aggregates or single outcomes is therefore not the relevant variable. What matters is whether a conditioned bias at one event can propagate, which again means amplification. The article’s stated reason for the p-bit verdict is incoherent with the Map’s own mechanism.
6. **Consistency with biology.** Neural computation is evolved for robustness: population codes, redundancy, averaging across many release sites. Its known noise is thermal (Faisal et al. 2008). Apply the article’s p-bit test to vesicle release and biology fails too. Apply its CMOS test (thermal noise is “mere-physical”) to channel noise and biology fails again. The article rescues biology only through a conditional on a “biological-hosting hypothesis the Map has not established,” whose leading instance it “actually disprefers.” That is special pleading, despite the article’s assertion that “There is no special pleading for carbon here.” [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/ai-hardware-substrate-taxonomy/)
7. **Constrain-vs-establish gate: failed in the headings, passed in the hedges.** The body hedges (“category placements rather than settled verdicts”). But the section titles (“Why This Sharpens the Case,” “the taxonomically decisive case”) [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/ai-hardware-substrate-taxonomy/) and the substrate-independence page’s description of the taxonomy (axes “that decide which could host the interface”) present it as establishing a result. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/substrate-independence/)
8. **Tenet leakage and the constitutional-attractor effect.** “Classical-digital systems are causally closed in exactly the way the tenet’s interface is not” reads Tenet 3 into hardware rather than deriving anything from it. The No Many Worlds paragraph claims the substrate question “requires a determinate answer, which branching ontologies deny.” [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/ai-hardware-substrate-taxonomy/) The site’s own substrate-independence page (2026-11-05) says the opposite: the tenet “is not invoked by the substrate question (tenets matrix, machine-consciousness rows): whether a system is conscious is determinate branch-relatively even under many-worlds, so the tenet bears on this page as coherence only.” [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/substrate-independence/) **That is a direct contradiction with the site’s tenets matrix.** Coherence inflation follows: five tenets are each shown to “bear on” the taxonomy, which looks like convergent support but is one premise (Tenet 2) restated five ways.
9. **Epistemic-to-metaphysical slide.** “A substrate that offers no quantum-indeterminate site… offers nothing for the interface to act on” [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/ai-hardware-substrate-taxonomy/) moves from “we have no model of coupling to X” to “X cannot be coupled.” The site’s own P-AC1 (“no independent evidence”) and its universal-coupling position (“adequacy suffices for coupling”) make the metaphysical version unavailable.
