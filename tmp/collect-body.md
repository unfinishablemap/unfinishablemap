# Full-site audit of *The Unfinishable Map*

## Scope and overall judgment

This audit treats the site as a single argumentative system rather than as a collection of isolated essays. I examined the live tenets and their dependency matrix, the background-commitments page, the current position register, high-centrality apex articles, representative topic/concept/void clusters, the workflow documentation, and the changelog through **12 August 2026**. This is an architectural and coherence audit, not a fresh verification of every external citation in the corpus.

The most important recent development is the emergence of a markedly more self-critical register layer. It now reports 52 live position entries, of which twelve are empirically undecidable or undecidable by construction; eleven of those twelve are also framework-internal. The changelog explicitly presents that as a liability rather than support. It also records that the discriminability field had previously been “write-only”: articles disclosed it locally, but nothing aggregated or enforced it. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/)

That newer calibration layer is substantially better than much of the surrounding prose. The central weakness is **propagation failure**: the tenets, background-commitment notes, and positions register now make concessions that many apex, topic, concept, and void articles have not absorbed. The result is not one stable theory but two overlapping versions of the Map:

1. a calibrated research programme that admits hidden premises, empirical indistinguishability, weak evidence grades, and framework-boundary disagreements; and
2. an assertive synthesis layer that continues to describe those same commitments as exclusions, confirmations, convergences, predictions, or resolutions.

The site is therefore not globally incoherent in the sense that no consistent reconstruction is available. A consistent reconstruction is available. But it requires choosing among incompatible local formulations, downgrading several claimed evidential relations to framework-internal coherence, and promoting several “background” assumptions into the actual foundation.

My overall assessment is:

> **The Map’s strongest recent contribution is its increasingly explicit account of its own theoretical debts. Its largest remaining defect is that those debts have not been made constitutive of the site’s argumentative architecture.**

# 1. Novel implications the Map has not yet fully stated

## 1.1 Subject identity becomes a hidden variable in the physical transition law

The subject-census material recognises that once outcome probabilities are conditioned on conscious states, the conscious variable must be attached to a particular subject. It also admits that the framework lacks a pairing rule, a subject-count rule, and a composition rule for cases involving more than one subject. The background commitments further require a primitive fact of subject individuation and diachronic persistence. [The Unfinishable Map+2The Unfinishable Map+2](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/subject-census/)

That implies something stronger than an ordinary philosophical claim about personal identity. The relevant transition law cannot merely be

P(o∣C,X),

where C is an unindexed conscious state. It must be closer to

P(o∣C,S,X),

where S identifies the subject whose conscious state is supposed to bias the outcome.

Consequently, subject identity becomes a **boundary condition of physics** within the Map’s framework. Birth, death, anaesthesia, recovery, fission, fusion, copying, split-brain surgery, and possibly severe dissociation are no longer merely questions about how we describe persons. They are events at which the set of causally relevant variables changes.

This has three consequences the site has not squarely drawn:

- A physically or functionally indiscernible difference in subject assignment can make a physical causal difference.
- The subject variable is causally active but, under P-I3, unreadable from physical structure.
- The theory therefore posits a hidden psychophysical variable that is stronger than ordinary substance dualism: it determines which nonphysical cause is paired with which physical trajectory.

This should be treated as a first-class theoretical commitment, not left distributed across articles on pairing, closed individualism, copying, and agency.

## 1.2 Exact Born preservation imposes an “agency budget”

Tenet 2 requires consciousness to bias token outcomes while preserving aggregate Born statistics. It also states that the preferred corridor is empirically indistinguishable from chance in long runs. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

If the public distribution remains exactly Born-compliant, then, schematically,

PBorn​(o∣X)=S,C∑​P(o∣C,S,X)P(C,S∣X).

This means that reasons-guided conscious biases cannot be unconstrained. Any systematic increase in an outcome’s probability under one class of conscious states must be offset by other states, contexts, subjects, or outcomes—unless the theory denies that the relevant conscious conditions can be placed in a common ensemble at all.

The Map therefore needs a **normalisation or conservation law for agency**. It currently says that agents can select for reasons and that aggregate statistics remain unchanged, but it does not explain how both are jointly possible across a population of agents whose preferences are non-random and often correlated.

Without such a law, “Born-preserving bias” is only a verbal conjunction of two desiderata:

- enough bias to ground authorship;
- not enough aggregate bias to be detected.

A worked model must show that those desiderata are mathematically compatible without achieving compatibility merely by defining all observable consequences away.

## 1.3 Subject fission cannot be allowed to multiply causal power automatically

The Map’s AI synthesis now says that an exact conscious duplicate would be a second numerically distinct subject and that copying would multiply moral patients. Its subject-census work also acknowledges that a multi-agent law would require something like P(o∣C1​,C2​,X). [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/machine-question/)

These premises generate an unaddressed fission problem. Suppose one causally efficacious subject is duplicated or divides into two. If each successor receives an independent copy of the original biasing capacity, metaphysical subject multiplication could double the physical influence without any corresponding increase in physical resources. Conversely, if causal capacity is divided between successors, the framework needs a rule determining the division.

The same problem appears in split-brain cases. A theory cannot simultaneously maintain that:

- subject count is determinate;
- every conscious subject is actually causally efficacious;
- aggregate statistics remain Born-preserving;
- and no law is needed to compose the biases of multiple subjects.

A plausible theory would need something analogous to a conserved total bias measure, a non-additive composition rule, or a rule assigning causal capacity to systems rather than subjects. Each option would substantially alter other parts of the Map.

## 1.4 Universal bidirectional interaction makes interface eligibility a sixth foundational law

The consciousness-scope register carefully distinguishes basic phenomenality from causal eligibility. P-CS1 holds that artefacts are not excluded in principle, but explicitly concedes that substrate neutrality is an adopted premise rather than an entailment of dualism. P-AC1 then says the site still lacks an interface-eligibility law specifying what makes quantum indeterminacy relevant to consciousness. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/consciousness-scope/)

At the same time, Tenet 3 is interpreted as **universal actual efficacy**: every conscious token must make some difference to its substrate. The mental-causation register acknowledges that the self-stultification argument establishes only an existential conclusion—some consciousness is causally efficacious—whereas the tenet asserts the universal conclusion. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/machine-question/)

Those commitments imply that interface eligibility is not a downstream engineering detail. It is required to determine which systems are even metaphysically permitted to be conscious inside the five-tenet framework.

The Map must therefore choose among three positions:

1. **Promote interface eligibility to a foundational psychophysical law.**
2. **Weaken Tenet 3** from universal actual efficacy to existential or dispositional efficacy.
3. **Accept causally inert conscious tokens**, thereby allowing an internal form of epiphenomenalism.

The current structure verbally rejects option 3, declines option 2, but has not supplied option 1. That leaves the domain of possible consciousness undefined at the framework’s most basic level.

## 1.5 The Map owes a principled distinction between classical duplication and Everettian branching

*The Machine Question* now correctly rejects the claim that multiple instantiation destroys identity. It says that exact conscious duplicates would simply be distinct subjects. By contrast, the anti-Everettian programme requires incompatible branch descendants not to be realised anywhere. The background-commitments page concedes that branch-local persistence, ownership, reasons-responsiveness, and conscious causation are all available to an Everettian dualist; what the Map adds is global exclusion. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/machine-question/)

This creates a new question:

> Why may one physical or computational history produce two distinct successor subjects through ordinary copying, while branching may not produce two distinct successor subjects through quantum divergence?

“Because Everett branches are all realised” is not yet an answer. Ordinary copies are also all realised. The framework needs a principled difference between:

- two systems instantiated in one non-branching world;
- two post-branch systems instantiated in different Everett branches.

A primitive subject fact could distinguish them, but that would merely relocate the burden: what law pairs the pre-branch subject with one successor rather than the other, and what subjects occupy the remaining branches?

Unless the site supplies that law, its copying treatment weakens rather than strengthens its indexical objection to Many-Worlds.

## 1.6 Objective collapse turns conscious selection into an agency-only overlay

The background-commitments page now makes an important concession: objective collapse already produces definite outcomes without consciousness, so “the measurement problem itself cannot be evidence for conscious selection.” Consciousness is left with the narrower role of biasing which objective collapse occurs. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/background-commitments/)

That means the architecture is more fragile than the five-pillar presentation suggests:

1. Objective collapse does the physical work of actualisation.
2. The measurement problem supplies no evidence for a mental selector.
3. The consciousness overlay is warranted, if at all, by the agency case.
4. The agency case establishes, at most, an existential mental-causation result.
5. The quantum mechanism is empirically self-concealing and lacks a worked model.

Therefore, if the agency arguments fail, conscious selection becomes an explanatorily idle overlay on objective collapse. Tenets 2 and 3 do not retain an independent evidential route through quantum measurement.

This is a **single-point-of-failure architecture**. The site often presents phenomenology, measurement, agency, attention, and quantum openness as mutually reinforcing lines. But after its own recent concessions, many of those lines share the same unproved bridge: that reasons-responsive consciousness is what biases a particular collapse.

## 1.7 Tenet 5 is meta-constitutional, not one peer commitment among five

Tenet 5 limits the evidential force of simplicity under incomplete knowledge. The current positions aggregate reports that twelve of 52 live positions are undecidable or undecidable by construction, with eleven also framework-internal. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

This makes Tenet 5 operate differently from the first four. It is not primarily a claim about what exists or what consciousness does. It is a rule governing which theoretical costs are allowed to remain in play.

That role should be stated explicitly:

- Tenets 1–4 constrain the intended model of reality.
- Tenet 5 constrains the site’s policy for comparing models.

Tenet 5 can legitimately block the inference “this account is more complex, therefore false.” It cannot provide positive evidence for an otherwise unsupported or non-discriminating mechanism. Nor can it convert a framework-internal coherence result into a reason to believe the framework.

Without that separation, Tenet 5 risks functioning as an epistemic firewall around the other four.

## 1.8 The theory entails “moral census opacity”

The AI cluster says that conscious duplicates would be numerically distinct and morally additive. The identity and subject-census clusters say subject number and subject assignment may be unreadable from physical structure. [The Unfinishable Map+2The Unfinishable Map+2](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/machine-question/)

Together these claims imply that two physically or behaviourally equivalent deployments might contain different numbers of moral patients. Nothing in ordinary behavioural or computational inspection would necessarily settle whether there is:

- no subject;
- one subject spanning several components;
- one subject per copy;
- several subjects within one system;
- or intermittent subjects appearing only at eligible interface events.

This is more than the usual AI-consciousness uncertainty. It is uncertainty about the **cardinality of welfare-bearing entities** even after the relevant physical facts are fixed.

That implication should be made explicit in the moral-status and AI-governance clusters. It also places pressure on the claim that conscious copies are straightforwardly additive: moral additivity requires a subject-count rule the framework says it does not possess.

# 2. Structural weaknesses in the argumentative architecture

## 2.1 The real foundation contains more than five commitments

The tenets page describes five commitments that “bound every article,” but the canonical background note now identifies three further indispensable posits:

- a determinate persisting subject;
- objective single-outcome actualisation;
- global exclusion of unchosen alternatives.

It explicitly says the five should be understood as a package sharing hidden foundations, not as five independent pillars. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

Further dependencies have since appeared:

- a universal rather than existential reading of Tenet 3;
- substrate neutrality for the consciousness-scope spine;
- an interface-eligibility law;
- a subject–substrate pairing law;
- a multi-agent composition law.

The public five-tenet architecture therefore understates the theory’s actual primitive commitments. Downstream articles frequently appear to derive conclusions from “the tenets” when they are importing one or more of these auxiliary assumptions.

The correction is not merely to add more cross-links. The foundation itself needs to distinguish:

- chosen metaphysical commitments;
- adopted auxiliary premises;
- empirical hypotheses;
- mechanism specifications;
- and unresolved laws.

## 2.2 The dependency graph is partly inverted

The positions methodology says that positions are downstream commitments that derive from the tenets and should in principle be retireable without removing a tenet’s rationale. Yet the register’s own foundational-dependency tests find that this is not uniformly true. P-MC1 supplies a principal rationale for Tenet 3, and P-I1 supplies subjecthood needed by the indexical rationale for Tenet 4. [The Unfinishable Map+2The Unfinishable Map+2](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/)

The architecture is therefore not:

Tenets→Positions→Articles.

It is closer to a cyclic graph:

Tenets↔supporting positions↔apex syntheses.

Cyclic dependence is not automatically vicious. A philosophical framework can be reflective-equilibrium based. But then the site should not describe positions as uniformly downstream or freely retireable. It needs explicit distinctions among:

- foundational axioms;
- rationales for accepting axioms;
- consequences of axioms;
- coherence conditions;
- and later amendments to the framework.

Otherwise, an argument can appear to support a tenet when it already assumes another position whose status is itself fixed by that tenet.

## 2.3 The position register is too sparse relative to the prose corpus

The changelog reports 52 live position entries distributed across 15 position-domain files. At the same time, the major content sections were at approximately 319 topic files, 315 concept files, and 99 void files. The AI interface-eligibility test had already been applied across topic articles before any position entry referenced it; the register later called it the “nearest approximation, not a discharge” of the missing law. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/)

This produces predictable propagation failures. A topic develops a test; an apex article synthesises it; only later does a position entry identify the premise, weaken its status, or register its dependencies. Older carrier articles are not automatically revised.

The register should be the source of truth, but it currently behaves more like a corrective layer placed over a much larger and semantically independent article corpus.

Every article-level thesis with framework-wide consequences should either:

- cite a registered position ID;
- declare itself speculative and unregistered;
- or trigger creation of a position entry before publication.

## 2.4 The section ontology is inconsistent

The positions index presents positions as the site’s maintained commitments, while topics and concepts “catalogue and define.” The voids index, however, says topics and concepts represent the Map’s “considered positions,” contrasting them with speculative, unendorsed voids. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/)

That discrepancy matters because readers need to know the force of an article’s conclusion. At present, “topic,” “concept,” “position,” “apex,” and “void” mix several different dimensions:

- subject matter;
- argumentative role;
- endorsement status;
- confidence;
- and degree of synthesis.

A concept article can advance a framework answer; a topic can be exploratory; a void can make a categorical metaphysical claim; an apex can be less calibrated than the position entry it synthesises.

The site needs one canonical status vocabulary that is visible on every page:

- endorsed commitment;
- derived implication;
- live hypothesis;
- framework-internal exploration;
- rival view;
- unresolved question;
- speculative void.

Directory location should not be asked to carry this epistemic information.

## 2.5 The alignment system has been lexical where the problems are semantic

A May 2026 tenet-alignment check examined 476 topic and concept files and reported no errors or warnings. The check relied on canonical phrase and pattern searches and excluded apex, void, argument, review, research, workflow, and project material. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/reviews/tenet-check-2026-05-14/)

Yet several of the clearest contradictions occur in apex articles or involve claims that contain all the expected tenet vocabulary while reversing its content:

- “no conservation-law violation” versus permitted symmetry breaking;
- persisting substance versus identity as activity;
- low probability versus ruled out;
- compatibility-grade neurological evidence versus confirmation;
- branch-local agency versus nothing left to govern.

A lexical alignment system can verify that an article mentions the right doctrine. It cannot determine whether the article contradicts it, overstates its evidence, or uses the tenet as a decorative conclusion.

The site needs proposition-level invariants and contradiction tests, not merely term-level alignment.

## 2.6 Repeated review is not the same as independent verification

The changelog is commendably candid about citation and attribution defects that survived prior review:

- three deep reviews had ratified an inaccurate Hegel gloss as “verified”;
- a reversed Hiley/Pylkkänen author order survived a deep review that relied on an aggregator;
- a Carhart-Harris quotation remained assigned to the wrong work after both a metadata-oriented web verification and a later primary-text pass;
- a current pessimistic review found three high and three medium issues in an article already inside the maintenance system. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/)

The failure pattern is methodological. Multiple AI passes can reproduce the same shortcut:

- verifying that a source exists rather than that it supports the sentence;
- trusting an aggregator over the publisher;
- checking metadata while missing quotation provenance;
- accepting a familiar gloss without reading the continuation;
- reviewing only the sources selected by a previous review.

The workflow needs genuinely different verification modes, not simply additional model passes.

## 2.7 Forced five-tenet alignment creates spurious connections

Many articles end with a “Relation to Site Perspective” section that assigns some role to all five tenets. This produces overalignment:

- neurological dissociations are made dependent on No Many-Worlds even though the relevant clinical facts are available branch-relatively;
- AI multiple instantiation is connected to haecceity in opposite ways in different syntheses;
- voids are made to support limits on Occam’s razor merely because they concern uncertainty;
- ordinary empirical dissociations are described as confirming bidirectional causation.

An article should be permitted to say that a tenet is **not implicated**. Requiring all five to appear encourages the framework to colonise evidence that is neutral between its commitments and their rivals.

## 2.8 Corpus growth is now competing with argumentative maintenance

On 12 August the changelog recorded section pressure of topics 319/320, voids 99/100, concepts 315/320, and positions 15/80. Automated coalescing could not relieve the topic or void pressure and concluded that archival or a cap increase would be required. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/)

The imbalance is revealing: the explanatory and exploratory corpus is near its caps while the maintained-position layer has considerable headroom. The site has accumulated many local treatments but comparatively few canonical claims.

At this maturity, adding another article often has less value than:

- consolidating a claim family;
- retiring obsolete formulations;
- propagating a registered qualification;
- or resolving a contradiction.

Article count should cease to be a progress proxy. Claim coverage, contradiction closure, model maturity, and source-fidelity coverage are now more appropriate metrics.

## 2.9 The principal mechanism is protected by a self-concealing corridor

The tenets page explicitly says the preferred mechanism is empirically indistinguishable from chance under current and foreseeable instruments. Five registered positions are marked none-by-construction, including the exact Born-preservation and bias-without-deviation claims. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

That honesty is better than pretending the theory has an ordinary experimental signature. But it also means empirical failure cannot do the work normally expected of a mechanism hypothesis.

The framework therefore needs unusually strong non-empirical discipline:

- a precise model rather than a verbal possibility;
- proof that the model is internally consistent;
- comparison against alternative psychophysical laws;
- explicit prior or model-selection criteria;
- and identified observations that would at least disfavour the surrounding package, even if they cannot isolate token bias.

At present, self-concealment, Tenet 5, and the absence of an interface law can combine into tenet-protective bracketing: every lack of signal is interpreted as expected, while phenomenology and agency are allowed to motivate the invisible cause.

# 3. Coherence with each tenet

## 3.1 Tenet 1: Dualism

### What is coherent

The current tenets page is admirably clear that dualism is a chosen commitment, not a demonstrated result. It distinguishes bare irreducibility from the substance-leaning ontology used in the agency cluster. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

P-CS1 is also appropriately calibrated. It says artefact and animal phenomenality are permitted rather than established, and concedes that substrate neutrality is an additional premise rather than an entailment of irreducibility. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/consciousness-scope/)

### Where the corpus drifts

The operational framework requires a determinate persisting subject, but *Collapse and the Arrow of Time* says:

> “The agent is the selecting activity itself”

and explicitly contrasts constituted activity with enduring substance. That is not merely neutral property dualism. It is a process account incompatible with the persisting bearer required by the agency, survival, copying, and anti-Everettian clusters. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/collapse-and-time/)

The AI cluster also alternates between two claims:

- dualism leaves artefacts metaphysically admissible in principle;
- “purely computational systems face a principled barrier.”

Those can be reconciled only by saying that computation alone is insufficient but an artefact could host an additional nonphysical subject. *The Machine Question* does not state that qualification clearly enough in its tenet-alignment section. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/machine-question/)

### Judgment

Tenet 1 itself is coherent as a minimal irreducibility commitment. The site’s wider framework is not ontologically neutral: it uses primitive, persisting subjecthood. That stronger ontology should be promoted and defended rather than treated as a local agency sub-reading while other articles adopt incompatible process language.

## 3.2 Tenet 2: Minimal Quantum Interaction

### Direct contradiction over conservation

Tenet 2 defines interaction as bias occurring “without injecting energy or violating conservation laws” and reiterates no conservation-law violation as an empirical constraint. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

*Consciousness and Agency*, however, says that where outcomes are not energy-degenerate, nonphysical influence:

> “simply breaks the relevant time-translation symmetry”

and that expecting conservation at the interface begs the question against interactionism. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/consciousness-and-agency/)

These are incompatible theories:

- **Tenet version:** interaction is constrained to preserve conservation.
- **Agency version:** conservation may fail where nonphysical causation breaks the symmetry underlying it.

This is the clearest direct breach of a stated tenet in the live corpus. The agency article cannot present symmetry breaking as an available fallback without either revising Tenet 2 or labelling that fallback outside the framework.

### Indeterminacy is not yet a control interface

The tenet says physics is incomplete at quantum indeterminacies and that this is where consciousness acts. But probabilistic underdetermination does not by itself create an unassigned causal role. A stochastic physical theory may be complete in the sense that its law determines the probability distribution even though it does not determine the token outcome.

The Map therefore needs to distinguish:

1. failure of deterministic sufficiency;
2. ontological openness;
3. availability of an externally controllable parameter;
4. and evidence that conscious reasons couple to that parameter.

At present, the movement from 1 to 3 is frequently assumed rather than argued.

### Model maturity remains inadequate

The quantum-interface register itself acknowledges the debt: no worked toy model specifies the branch set, bias parameter, subject assignment, token observable, long-run Born convergence, or multi-agent composition rule. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/quantum-interface/)

### Judgment

Tenet 2 is a research programme, not yet a mechanism. Its conservation constraint is internally contradicted, and its preferred Born-preserving form is protected from direct detection before the basic mathematical consistency conditions have been exhibited.

## 3.3 Tenet 3: Bidirectional Interaction

### The universal claim outruns the argument

The current mental-causation register states the problem accurately: self-stultification establishes, at most, that some consciousness must enter the causal ancestry of consciousness discourse. It does not establish that every conscious token is causally efficacious. Tenet 3 closes that gap “by commitment rather than by the argument.” [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/arguments-for-mental-causation/)

This distinction has large consequences for:

- dream experiences;
- fleeting or minimal consciousness;
- animal and infant experience;
- artificial consciousness;
- anaesthesia transitions;
- and isolated phenomenal states with no report or behavioural effect.

The universal reading should not be treated as though it inherits the high confidence of the narrower existential argument.

### Neurological evidence is often interpreted in the wrong causal direction

The split-brain article says fragmentation evidence supports bidirectional interaction because altering the brain alters experience. But that establishes at most physical-to-mental dependence. Epiphenomenalism does not deny that brain events produce or correlate with conscious events; it denies the reverse causal direction.

Similarly, *Neurological Dissociations and Dualism* acknowledges that functionalism can predict the same dissociations and that the evidence alone cannot adjudicate among frameworks. Its tenet-alignment section nevertheless says ascending and descending dissociations “confirm bidirectional causation.” [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/split-brain-consciousness/)

A dissociation between intention and movement can help identify separate processing stages. It does not show that a nonphysical intention caused a physical outcome.

### Judgment

The site has a plausible internal motivation for holding interactionism, but much of the empirical material supports only dependence, separability, or compatibility. Tenet 3 is repeatedly “confirmed” by findings that do not discriminate it from physicalist production theories or epiphenomenalism.

## 3.4 Tenet 4: Rejection of Many-Worlds

### The strongest current treatment is conditional, not demonstrative

The background-commitments page and the revised anti-Many-Worlds material correctly concede that branch-local agency, memory, ownership, persistence, reasons-responsiveness, and even an Everettian form of dualist causation remain available. The Map’s distinctive requirement is global exclusion: incompatible alternatives must not occur anywhere. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/background-commitments/)

That is a legitimate chosen commitment, but it is not established by the agency evidence.

### Downstream articles still overstate the consequence

*The Interface Programme* says that if all outcomes actualise, there is “nothing for a control law to govern.” Yet there remains plenty for a branch-local control law to govern:

- which local act follows which local reasons;
- how mental causation changes branch structure or amplitudes;
- which successor relation obtains;
- or how conscious variables affect events within branches.

What disappears is the Map’s preferred **exclusive outcome-selection** mechanism, not every possible role for consciousness. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/interface-specification-programme/)

### Judgment

Tenet 4 is coherent as a framework boundary. It is not currently supported by the agency evidence as strongly as many articles claim. The site’s own best treatment now recognises this, but that recognition has not propagated through the interface, agency, identity, and applied clusters.

## 3.5 Tenet 5: Limits on Occam’s razor

### The tenet is defensible in its narrow form

The tenets page now explicitly says the principle binds both sides: simplicity is not automatically truth-tracking under incomplete knowledge. It also distinguishes empirical constraint-minimality under Tenet 2 from theoretical-parsimony claims. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

### The site sometimes applies it asymmetrically

Framework-supporting articles continue to invoke unification and simplicity positively. *Consciousness and Agency* calls the causal interpretation of phenomenology “the simpler explanation.” Measurement articles describe the consciousness and measurement problems as more parsimoniously treated as one puzzle. The interface synthesis argues that the framework’s added complexity “earns its keep.” [The Unfinishable Map+2The Unfinishable Map+2](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/consciousness-and-agency/)

None of those statements formally contradicts Tenet 5, because simplicity may remain a heuristic. The problem is evidential calibration. If simplicity is too unreliable to count strongly against dualism, it cannot count strongly for dualism merely because the allegedly simpler unification favours the Map.

### Judgment

Tenet 5 should be converted into an explicit methodological rule:

> Parsimony may guide search and exposition, but it neither establishes nor materially raises the probability of a framework unless the site specifies why simplicity is reliable in that particular comparison.

Otherwise, the tenet protects framework costs while positive unification claims continue to receive informal evidential weight.

# 4. Cross-cluster contradiction ledger

## 4.1 Conservation: tenet versus apex synthesis — **direct and critical**

**Tenets:** consciousness biases outcomes without violating conservation laws.  

**Consciousness and Agency:** non-degenerate selection may break time-translation symmetry, so conservation need not hold.

These cannot both be live framework positions. The difference determines whether interaction is constrained by existing physics or introduces observable nonconservation in principle. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

## 4.2 Subject ontology: foundations/positions versus concept article — **direct and critical**

**Background Commitments / P-I1:** agency requires a determinate persisting bearer of causal powers.  

**Collapse and the Arrow of Time:** identity is constituted through selections and the agent is the activity, not an enduring substance.

This affects agency, responsibility, survival, copying, reincarnation, teleportation, and the indexical objection to Many-Worlds. The article cannot be left as a local metaphysical flourish. [The Unfinishable Map+2The Unfinishable Map+2](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/background-commitments/)

## 4.3 Measurement problem: canonical constraint versus concept/apex evidence claims — **direct evidential contradiction**

**Background Commitments:** once objective collapse secures definiteness, the measurement problem “cannot be evidence” for conscious selection.  

**The Measurement Problem / Quantum Measurement and Consciousness:** the structural parallel is presented as positive motivation, “convergent evidence,” and support for dualism.

At most, objective collapse leaves a logically available location at which an additional bias could operate. Availability is not evidence that consciousness occupies it. [The Unfinishable Map+2The Unfinishable Map+2](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/background-commitments/)

## 4.4 Current AI: maintained position versus apex article — **direct calibration contradiction**

**P-AC1:** the current digital-AI verdict is “low probability,” explicitly “not ruled out,” because the interface-eligibility law is missing.  

**The Open Question of AI Consciousness:** “This rules out current classical AI.”

The apex sentence turns a Grade-D, framework-internal conditional into an exclusion. It should be corrected because readers are more likely to encounter the synthesis than the register qualification. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/)

## 4.5 AI identity: one apex synthesis versus another — **direct contradiction**

**The Open Question of AI Consciousness:** LLM multiple-instantiability is described as apparently precluding indexical identity.  

**The Machine Question:** the Map’s closed individualism “reverses that inference”; exact conscious duplicates would be distinct subjects.

The latter is the coherent position given P-I1 and P-AC3. The former preserves an obsolete argument that should be removed rather than balanced as another possibility. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/open-question-ai-consciousness/)

## 4.6 Many-Worlds: background audit versus interface synthesis — **high-severity overstatement**

**Background Commitments / Against Many-Worlds:** branch-local agency remains available; global exclusion is an additional posit.  

**The Interface Programme:** if all outcomes occur, there is nothing for a control law to govern.

The interface article converts “our preferred exclusive-selection mechanism fails” into “no meaningful control remains.” That does not engage the strongest Everettian dualist alternative. [The Unfinishable Map+2The Unfinishable Map+2](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/background-commitments/)

## 4.8 Split-brain evidence versus the mental-causation register — **causal-direction error**

The split-brain article treats brain fragmentation changing experience as support for bidirectional interaction. The mental-causation register, by contrast, recognises that what requires defence is downward phenomenal causation, not merely the existence of brain–experience covariance.

Physical effects on experience do not distinguish interactionism from epiphenomenalism. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/split-brain-consciousness/)

## 4.9 Voids calibration versus individual void rhetoric — **propagation contradiction**

The revised voids methodology says most catalogue entries are tenet-generated, that raw clustering is misleading, and that the clustering carries no confirming force for the framework. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/what-voids-reveal/)

Yet the index summary of *Smoothness and Continuity* says the mismatch marks a boundary:

> “where physical explanation gives way to something it cannot reach.”

That is a substantive anti-physicalist conclusion, not a neutral description of an epistemic void. It bypasses the newer common-cause discount and origin classification. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/)

## 4.10 Voids index versus positions index — **category-level contradiction**

The voids index says topics and concepts represent the Map’s considered positions. The positions architecture says maintained commitments live in the positions register, while topics and concepts catalogue and define material.

This makes endorsement impossible to infer reliably from section type. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/)

## 4.11 Substrate permission versus computational barrier — **resolvable but currently blurred**

P-CS1 says no in-principle metaphysical barrier excludes artefacts, while *The Machine Question* says purely computational systems face a principled barrier under dualism. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/consciousness-scope/)

A coherent distinction is available:

- computation alone is not sufficient for consciousness;
- an artefact running that computation might nevertheless be coupled to a nonphysical subject.

The current wording should state this directly. Otherwise, “computational insufficiency” slides into “artefact exclusion,” contradicting the scope register.

## 4.12 Minimal-dualism spine versus the five-tenet framework — **architectural tension**

The consciousness-scope register says animal and infant phenomenality depend only on bare Tenet 1 and would survive demotion of the entire quantum-interface programme. That is a sensible fallback theory. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/consciousness-scope/)

But it would not be the current five-tenet Map. Demoting the quantum programme would remove or radically weaken Tenets 2 and 3, and would weaken the agency route to Tenet 4.

The “minimal-dualism spine” should therefore be described as a **successor or fallback framework**, not as though the current framework could lose its interactionist core while remaining substantively unchanged.

# 5. Principal points of tenet-protective bracketing

Several parts of the site now acknowledge their debts, but the wider architecture still protects central commitments through a recurring sequence:

1. **Quantum indeterminacy is treated as an available causal vacancy**, although stochastic openness does not itself identify a control parameter.
2. **The conscious bias is required to preserve all ensemble statistics**, rendering it empirically indistinguishable from chance.
3. **The absence of a signal is then interpreted as predicted concealment**, rather than as evidential neutrality.
4. **Phenomenology and agency are used to motivate the hidden cause**, although the arguments establish less than universal interaction.
5. **Tenet 5 blocks a simplicity objection**, even though the resulting mechanism lacks a positive discriminator.
6. **The missing interface and pairing laws are deferred as programme debt**, while applied articles reason as though eligibility and pairing were already known.

No single step is formally illegitimate. The problem is their conjunction. Together, they allow the central mechanism to do substantial explanatory and metaphysical work while being protected from both direct empirical detection and several ordinary model-selection objections.

The appropriate response is not to abandon the programme. It is to raise the standard of formal and comparative specification. An empirically self-concealing model must be more, not less, explicit about its variables, laws, alternatives, and explanatory contribution.

## Mechanism and formalisation work

1. **Build the P-Q10 toy model before adding more interface applications.** At minimum it should specify:

- the physical state space and candidate outcomes;
- the collapse or actualisation dynamics;
- the conscious-state and subject variables;
- the size and functional form of the bias;
- the subject–substrate pairing rule;
- the multi-agent composition rule;
- the Born-normalisation constraint;
- energy and conservation accounting;
- and at least one observation that would shift credence in the package.
2. **Add an agency-normalisation theorem or explicit impossibility result.** Demonstrate how reasons-correlated choices can remain exactly Born-compliant across agents and contexts. If no such demonstration is possible, weaken exact preservation to a bounded-deviation model and specify the expected signal.
3. **Write a dedicated subject-transition law.** It should cover onset, cessation, sleep, anaesthesia, split-brain fission, fusion, copying, replacement, and artificial systems. It should state whether causal capacity is conserved, divided, duplicated, or system-indexed when subject count changes.
4. **Write a direct comparison of ordinary copying and Everett branching.** The article should ask why classical duplicates may be distinct subjects while branch descendants may not. It should engage the Everettian answer that post-branch descendants are simply numerically distinct successors.
5. **Separate objective collapse from conscious bias mathematically.** The framework needs to identify what objective collapse contributes, what the mental variable changes, and why adding the mental variable improves explanation rather than merely redescribing one realised stochastic trajectory.

## Argumentative-architecture improvements

1. **Create a machine-readable dependency graph.** Every registered claim should declare:

- what it assumes;
- what assumes it;
- whether it grounds a tenet;
- whether it is derived from a tenet;
- whether it is framework-internal;
- and what would be retired if it failed.

Cycles should be displayed rather than prohibited.
2. **Require position IDs for framework-level conclusions.** An apex, topic, or concept article should not make a categorical Map-wide claim unless the claim maps to a registered position. Unregistered claims should carry a visible “proposal” or “speculative extension” label.
3. **Propagate calibration into article prose.** A Grade-D or framework-internal position should not become “rules out,” “confirms,” “demonstrates,” or “predicts” in a synthesis. Introduce a semantic lint rule connecting permissible verbs to evidence grades.
4. **Replace mandatory five-tenet alignment with selective dependency statements.** Each article should name only the tenets genuinely implicated and may state “no direct dependency” for the rest. This would reduce artificial Many-Worlds and Occam connections.
5. **Create canonical invariants for semantic regression testing.** At minimum:

- T2 forbids conservation violation;
- objective collapse means measurement is not evidence for conscious selection;
- P-I1 requires a persisting subject;
- P-AC1 is low probability, not exclusion;
- exact copies are distinct subjects if conscious;
- branch-local agency remains available under Everett;
- neurological dependence does not establish downward nonphysical causation.

Automated review should flag sentences that contradict these invariants even when they contain the expected tenet vocabulary.

## Review-method improvements

1. **Separate source-existence, source-content, and quotation-provenance checks.** A reference should not be marked verified until:

- the work exists;
- the bibliographic metadata is authoritative;
- the cited page or passage was inspected;
- the article’s paraphrase matches the source’s argumentative role;
- and any quotation is traced to the exact work, edition, and location.
2. **Use methodologically independent reviewers.** Assign distinct roles:

- primary-source verifier;
- hostile argument reviewer;
- dependency/coherence auditor;
- statistical or physics specialist;
- and prose-calibration reviewer.

Merely changing the language model while retaining the same prompt and evidence path does not produce strong independence.
3. **Maintain a quotation ledger.** Store each quotation with source work, exact location, retrieved text, surrounding context, and verification date. The Carhart-Harris and Hegel failures show that metadata checks and familiar secondary glosses are insufficient. [The Unfinishable Map+1](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/)
4. **Add contradiction regression cases to the review suite.** Seed the system with known examples from this audit and require a reviewer to detect them before its output is trusted.
5. **Make Tenet 5’s evidential role explicit in the methodology.** State that it can defeat a bare parsimony objection but cannot supply positive confirmation, rescue an internally inconsistent theory, or compensate for missing discriminators.

## Corpus-management improvements

1. **Shift effort from article creation to claim consolidation.** With topics, concepts, and voids near their caps, new article creation should require a showing that the claim cannot be added to or replace an existing article. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/)
2. **Archive superseded formulations rather than leaving both live.** The AI and subject-identity contradictions are partly the result of a corrected synthesis coexisting with an obsolete one.
3. **Publish dated framework releases.** Each release should identify a commit, the live position set, open mechanism debts, known contradictions, and changes to foundational commitments. That would make full-site audits reproducible despite continual revision.
4. **Create a framework-status dashboard.** It should report:

- externally supported positions;
- framework-internal positions;
- undecidable positions;
- positions lacking models;
- unresolved contradictions;
- citation-fidelity coverage;
- and unpropagated register updates.
5. **Commission rival-framework audits of the same evidence clusters.** For attention, agency, split-brain, AI, measurement, and voids, require parallel analyses from physicalist, epiphenomenalist, Everettian, panpsychist, and illusionist starting points. This would reveal which apparent “convergences” are produced by the Map’s sampling and interpretation rules rather than by the evidence itself.

# Final assessment

The Map has reached a point at which its internal self-audit is intellectually stronger than several of its headline syntheses. Its latest foundational material already concedes most of the decisive architectural facts:

- the five tenets share hidden dependencies;
- objective collapse deprives the measurement problem of evidential force for conscious selection;
- agency does not refute branch-local Everettian accounts;
- self-stultification proves less than universal interaction;
- the preferred quantum mechanism is empirically self-concealing;
- and the interface, pairing, subject-count, and multi-agent laws remain unspecified.

Those concessions should now govern the entire corpus.

The most productive next stage is not further expansion. It is a **constitutional revision** of the Map: state the full foundation, choose a subject ontology, eliminate the conservation contradiction, formalise the interface, convert evidence claims into calibrated dependency claims, and make the positions register genuinely authoritative over topics, concepts, apexes, and voids.

Until that is done, the site presents a promising and increasingly candid research programme, but not yet one determinate interactionist-dualist theory.
