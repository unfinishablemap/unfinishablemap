# Referee Audit of The Unfinishable Map (live state as of 2026-11-13)

**Headline verdict: REVISE-HARD at corpus level.** The Map’s best quantum-interface work is now honest and formally sharp. That honesty is concentrated in a few flagship pages; dozens of downstream articles still carry claims the flagships have withdrawn. The central mechanism, Born-preserving “corridor” selection, remains unrescued. My own analysis adds a reason it is worse than the site admits: its only empirically live branch collides with no-signalling in entangled settings unless a psychophysical superselection law is posited. That law turns “minimal interaction” into a hidden-variable theory with a mandated equilibrium.

- **Bottom line:**

- DEMOTE-TO-COHERENCE-ONLY: the phenomenology-mechanism bridge apex, the interface-formalism concept and the perception/dissociation “demonstrates” cluster.

### (2) Weakest arguments, overclaims and citation defects

**(a) The Born-rule dilemma is still the deepest unresolved problem, and horn (a) is worse than the apex admits.** The apex’s default reading still asserts (P-Q2, “high”) that selection acts “per trial, indistinguishable from unbiased Born statistics” while biasing “*which* outcome becomes actual”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/) Three problems follow.

1. **“Born-preservation” is the wrong name for what is preserved.** In physics, the Born rule is a law conditional on the full physical state. If conscious state C is not fixed by the physical context X (which is what Tenet 1 requires), then horn (a), P(O | C, X) ≠ q(O | X), is a Born-rule violation for a fixed physical preparation. Only the average over a non-physical variable is preserved. Structurally, that is a hidden-variable theory with the Born measure as an equilibrium marginal. The apex never draws this comparison, and the corpus neglects the relevant literature: Valentini’s quantum non-equilibrium. Valentini’s “Signal-locality, uncertainty, and the subquantum H-theorem. II” (*Physics Letters A* 158:1–8, 1991, DOI 10.1016/0375-9601(91)90330-B) shows that signal-locality and the uncertainty principle hold only for the equilibrium distribution P = |Ψ|². Departures from that distribution of hidden variables therefore permit superluminal signalling.
2. **Horn (a) becomes a signalling problem once entanglement enters.** If an agent’s intention biases her outcome on half of a maximally entangled pair measured in a common basis, and intentions can be re-weighted, then the distant party’s marginal shifts. The “psychophysical superselection” law is therefore not “milder than its name suggests”, as the apex says. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/) It is mandatory, and it does the job quantum equilibrium does in Bohmian mechanics. The self-concealing apex half-concedes this (“no-signalling safety is shown for single-system marginals only”, [https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/self-concealing-interface/](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/self-concealing-interface/)). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/) The born-preserving apex should state it as a theorem-level constraint, not a cost.
3. **The steganography skeleton imports the same constraint.** Schroeder de Witt, Sokota, Kolter, Foerster and Strohmeier (ICLR 2023, arXiv:2210.14889) show that a steganographic procedure is perfectly secure under Cachin’s information-theoretic model if and only if it is induced by a coupling, and that it maximises throughput if and only if that coupling is a minimum-entropy one. My further claim is that perfect security also requires the hidden message to be uniformly distributed (encrypted). Their abstract does not state this, so it is my own reading. In the Map’s translation, intentions must then be lawfully distributed so as to be uncorrelated in aggregate. That is superselection again.

Net: the only signal-safe positions are horn (b), which is de facto epiphenomenal at the type level, or a law constraining which populations of intentions can exist. The apex is honest enough to RETAIN, but P-Q2’s “high” confidence attaches to a description (“preserves Born statistics exactly”) that a physicist would read as false. **Not remediated; sharpened.**
