# Pre-Publication Referee Report: "Nondualistic Interactionism" and the Architecture of Mental Causation

## 1. Introduction and Scope of the Audit

The manuscript under review endeavors to establish a definitive defense of interactionist dualism by systematically dismantling competing non-reductive frameworks. Its primary target is David Ray Griffin’s Whiteheadian nondualistic interactionism, which the author evaluates as a rival to a proprietary set of dualistic commitments (frequently referred to in the manuscript’s architecture as its foundational "tenets"). The manuscript’s central thesis asserts that bidirectional causal interaction between mind and matter strictly necessitates a difference-in-kind ontology, and that Griffin’s framework—which posits numerical distinction without differing in fundamental kind—collapses into contradiction. Consequently, the manuscript utilizes this perceived failure to validate its own interactionist model, which posits an agent-causal locus at the quantum interface and relies heavily on epistemic self-stultification arguments to eliminate epiphenomenalist and physicalist alternatives.

A rigorous, line-by-line pre-publication audit of this manuscript reveals severe methodological, empirical, and logical deficiencies that render it entirely unsuitable for publication in a top-tier philosophy journal. The text operates through a sustained regime of ontological gerrymandering and epistemological double standards, shielding its own foundational posits from the exact scrutiny it weaponizes against Griffin and other non-reductive theorists. More concerningly, the manuscript relies on highly selective textual truncation of primary sources to manufacture contradictions where none exist, reversing the explicit philosophical intent of the cited authors.

Furthermore, the manuscript exhibits a profound and disqualifying isolation from the 2020–2026 literature spanning quantum foundations, mental causation, panpsychism, and cognitive science. It treats early-century hypotheses in quantum mechanics as established mechanisms for dualistic intervention while systematically ignoring contemporary physicalist, illusionist, and panpsychist frameworks that successfully dissolve the manuscript's core arguments. This report will exhaustively dissect the manuscript's structural failures across five primary domains: the exegetical distortion of Whiteheadian process philosophy; the evasion of contemporary causal exclusion dynamics; the mathematical incoherence of its proposed quantum mind-matter interface (specifically regarding the Zeno effect); its premature dismissal of panpsychism via ignorance of modern combination-problem solutions; and its reliance on obsolete epistemic self-stultification arguments.

## 2. Exegetical Distortion and the Mischaracterization of Griffin’s Ontology

The most immediate and fatal flaw of the manuscript lies in its artificial taxonomy and its deliberate misrepresentation of David Ray Griffin’s ontological continuity thesis. Griffin’s framework, grounded in the process philosophy of Alfred North Whitehead, is explicitly panexperientialist. In Whiteheadian metaphysics, the fundamental constituents of reality are not inert material particles but "actual entities" or "actual occasions"—discrete drops of experience that possess both a physical pole (the objective prehension of past data) and a mental pole (subjective self-determination and anticipation). Because all actual occasions share this bipolar nature, mind and matter do not differ in fundamental kind; they represent varying organizational degrees and complexities of the same underlying experiential fabric.

### 2.1 The Truncation of the Evolutionary Continuity Principle

To establish its own difference-in-kind dualism as the only viable mechanism for mental causation, the manuscript must force Griffin’s nondualistic interactionism into a state of contradiction. It explicitly attempts to utilize the necessity of bidirectional interaction to "select dualism" from the pool of irreducibility-respecting alternatives (a premise the manuscript identifies internally as P-D2 or Tenet 1). Because Griffin argues for robust, bidirectional interaction between a numerically distinct mind and the brain, the manuscript insists his theory is fundamentally unstable unless it capitulates to full dualism.

To manufacture this capitulation, the manuscript engages in an egregious breach of academic integrity by actively truncating Griffin's primary text. The manuscript attributes to Griffin the introduction of "a new kind of causal relation" at the human level, citing page 193 of his work. The manuscript weaponizes this isolated phrase to argue that Griffin inadvertently smuggles a difference-in-kind dualism into his macro-level ontology, thereby conceding the necessity of the manuscript's own dualistic foundational tenets.

However, a rudimentary verification of the source text reveals that Griffin’s sentence does not end where the manuscript artificially terminates it. The actual, unabridged text reads: "What is new at the human level is a new kind of causal relation, *or, more precisely, an extreme exemplification of a kind of causal relation that had become increasingly important in the evolution of the higher animals*". By excising the qualifying clause, the manuscript completely inverts Griffin’s argument. Griffin is explicitly defending the principle of evolutionary continuity—a cornerstone of process philosophy dictating that higher-order mental phenomena must emerge from continuous, lower-order precursors without radical ontological ruptures. Griffin is arguing for a continuum of *degree* (an "extreme exemplification"), which is entirely consistent with his nondualistic thesis. The manuscript’s reliance on this fabricated discontinuity to generate a critique is a terminal methodological failure.

### 2.2 The False Dichotomy of Interaction Mechanisms

The manuscript further distorts Griffin’s critique of classical dualism to shield its own vulnerabilities. It asserts that Griffin’s "chief problem" with dualism is the lack of an intelligible mechanism for interaction between utterly different substances. While the mechanism problem is indeed a primary objection, the manuscript systematically suppresses Griffin’s second, equally critical charge: that classical dualism violates the principle of continuity by introducing a magical discontinuity in the evolutionary timeline where the mind abruptly appears ex nihilo.

By focusing solely on the mechanism of interaction and erasing the evolutionary continuity demand from its exegesis, the manuscript shields its own injected dualism from the exact evolutionary critiques that Griffin designed his Whiteheadian framework to solve.

| Dimension of Critique | Griffin's Actual Whiteheadian Position | Manuscript's Truncated Representation | Consequence for the Manuscript's Argument |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Nature of the Causal Relation** | An extreme exemplification of a continuous evolutionary trait, differing only in degree. | "A new kind of causal relation," indicating a rupture in physical law. | Deliberate textual truncation used to fabricate an internal contradiction within Griffin's framework. |
| **Mind-Body Distinction** | Numerical distinction via "compound individuals"; strict continuity in fundamental kind. | Numerical distinction inextricably linked to difference in kind. | Ontological gerrymandering to force a binary dualist/reductionist taxonomy onto process philosophy. |
| **Primary Critique of Dualism** | Fails the mechanism of interaction AND violates evolutionary continuity. | Fails the mechanism of interaction exclusively. | Shields the manuscript's own framework from evolutionary biology critiques by hiding Griffin's continuity demands. |

This classificatory gerrymandering is unsustainable. The manuscript cannot claim ontological neutrality while simultaneously leveraging difference-in-kind requirements to eliminate rival process ontologies by fiat. As internal review notes within the manuscript's own changelog indicate, the assertion that irreducibility plus bidirectional interaction necessarily selects for difference-in-kind dualism is a "High" level classification contradiction. Griffin’s compound individuals—where a hierarchy of occasions culminates in a dominant occasion exercising downward causation on the lower-level occasions of the body—provide a coherent answer to mental causation without requiring the manuscript's Cartesian boundaries.

## 3. The Asymmetrical Application of Causal Exclusion

A core requirement of any rigorous philosophical analysis is that the evaluative criteria applied to a rival theory must also be survived by the author's own framework. The manuscript routinely weaponizes the rigorous demands of physicalist philosophers against Griffin while employing an epistemic shield to protect its own dualist commitments from those identical critiques. This is most evident in the manuscript's handling of Jaegwon Kim’s physicalist demands.

### 3.1 Weaponizing the Composition Demand

The manuscript utilizes Kim’s composition demand to critique Griffin’s notion of the "dominant occasion". In process theory, a human being is not an "aggregational society" like a rock, but a "compound individual" directed by a dominant actual occasion. The manuscript argues that Griffin simply posits this dominant occasion rather than deriving it compositionally from the lower-level entities, characterizing it as an unsupported foundational move by the rigorous standards of Kim’s mereological demands.

### 3.2 Evasion of the Causal Exclusion Argument

While eagerly utilizing Kim to attack Griffin’s mereology, the manuscript systematically ignores Jaegwon Kim’s most famous, devastating, and universally recognized contribution to the philosophy of mind: the causal exclusion argument. Kim’s exclusion argument demonstrates that if the physical domain is causally closed, and if every physical event has a sufficient physical cause, then any non-physical mental cause is fundamentally "excluded" from doing any causal work, unless one is willing to accept systemic, pervasive, and absurd overdetermination of physical events.

The manuscript attempts to bypass causal closure by retreating to the quantum level, positing that its proprietary brand of "Agent Causation" operates precisely where causal closure stated as *sufficiency* fails—at quantum indeterminacies. The manuscript claims this "chance-fixing" concession allows conscious states to make a physical difference without directly overriding deterministic laws.

This maneuver fails entirely against the 2020–2025 literature on causal exclusion. The contemporary literature explicitly addresses how causal exclusion applies even in strictly stochastic and quantum environments. As established by Paolini Paoletti (2020) and others in modern analyses of downward causation in journals like *Synthese*, causal exclusion does not require deterministic causal sufficiency; it applies equally to the determination of objective probabilities. If the prior physical state dictates the exact objective probability distribution of a subsequent physical state, a non-physical mental state cannot alter that outcome or its probability without violating the statistical physical laws.

The manuscript operates under the archaic and mathematically false assumption that physical indeterminism automatically licenses dualistic intervention. By subjecting Griffin to Kim's rigorous composition demands while hiding its own interactionist dualism from Kim's causal exclusion argument, the manuscript engages in an egregious form of special pleading.

## 4. Quantum State Collapse, the Born Rule, and the Zeno Problem

To substantiate its claims of agent causation without causal exclusion, the manuscript attempts to formalize a "Mind-Matter Interface". It leans heavily on objective collapse theories (such as Ghirardi–Rimini–Weber [GRW] and Continuous Spontaneous Localization [CSL] models) and von Neumann-Wigner interpretations, suggesting that the fundamental stochasticity and superposition of quantum mechanics provide the exact mathematical location required for a psychophysical law to be inserted. The manuscript posits that consciousness biases which outcome actualizes within the Born distribution, preserving the marginal probabilities while selecting specific outcomes to enact agentic intent.

This formulation betrays a profound misunderstanding of quantum mechanics, probability theory, and the 2020-2025 literature on the measurement problem. It generates an immediate epistemic trilemma and runs headlong into catastrophic dynamical paradoxes.

### 4.1 The Mathematical Incoherence of "Chance-Fixing"

The manuscript asserts that consciousness biases individual actualizations but does not bias "the probabilities" of the Born distribution. This is mathematically incoherent. A probability distribution is entirely defined by the limit of its actualizations over a sufficient number of trials. If an agent-causal force systematically selects specific non-random outcomes to execute a macro-level intention (e.g., a sustained effort of will to raise an arm), these directed selections must necessarily deviate from the unconditioned Born probabilities over time.

This creates a fatal epistemic trilemma, which the manuscript itself seems to internally recognize but fails to resolve. If the manuscript claims that the overall public physical distribution remains exactly as the baseline physics predicts (to avoid falsification by experimental physics), then public behavioral reports cannot discriminate mental causation from a causally complete physical duplicate (a zombie). This destroys the empirical evidence for the manuscript's dualist tenets. Conversely, if consciousness *does* change a publicly conditionable distribution to enact agentic intent, then it violates the Born rule, predicting nonlinear deviations in neural microtubules or ion channels that have never been empirically observed. The manuscript cannot simultaneously claim that consciousness exerts non-random, teleological control over quantum events and that the statistical outcomes of those events remain perfectly random as predicted by the Schrödinger equation.

Furthermore, the manuscript conflates mathematical stochasticity with an ontological vacuum. While spontaneous collapse theories (GRW, CSL) introduce fundamental randomness (e.g., the CSL noise field), they are formulated as physically autonomous, mathematically exhaustively defined Markov processes. A stochastic law does not contain a "causal gap" waiting for a non-physical agent to occupy it; the realization of events in these models is strictly governed by the fitted phenomenological parameters of the theory.

### 4.2 The Ignored Threat of the Quantum Zeno Effect

More devastatingly, the manuscript’s reliance on consciousness as a trigger for quantum collapse demonstrates total ignorance of the contemporary literature evaluating this exact hypothesis. The von Neumann-Wigner interpretation, which posits that consciousness collapses the wave function, has been rigorously mathematically critiqued in recent foundational physics and philosophy literature. Most notably, David J. Chalmers and Kelvin J. McQueen (2021, 2022) formulated a devastating mathematical challenge to interactive quantum dualism known as the Quantum Zeno problem for consciousness.

If consciousness is the variable that forces the wave function to collapse, then consciousness acts as a "superposition-resistant" property. It cannot enter a superposition itself, and therefore forces the physical systems it strongly interacts with (the brain) into definite eigenstates. However, as Chalmers and McQueen prove, this subjects the brain to the dynamics of continuous measurement.

According to the quantum Zeno effect, continuous observation of a quantum system prevents its state from evolving; the temporal evolution operator becomes trapped. If the physical correlates of consciousness are continuously measured by the non-physical mind, their state evolution is effectively frozen. As Chalmers and McQueen articulate: "If consciousness or its physical correlate was a superselection observable, it would obey the dynamics of continuous measurement so it could not change at all. If we started in an unconscious state, we could never become conscious. The unfortunate consequence would be that we could never wake up from a nap. Furthermore, if there is no consciousness in the early universe, then consciousness could never emerge later".

The manuscript builds its entire mechanism of mental causation on the architecture of quantum state reduction but fails to even mention, let alone resolve, the Zeno problem. Proposing a quantum mind-matter interface in the 2020s without addressing the Chalmers-McQueen mathematical proofs against superposition-resistance is indicative of a profound detachment from the foundational science the manuscript claims to leverage.

## 5. The Combination Problem and the Evasion of Contemporary Panpsychism

Having attempted to secure dualism via a flawed interpretation of quantum mechanics, the manuscript proceeds to systematically eliminate rival non-physicalist ontologies, primarily targeting panpsychism. The manuscript asserts that panpsychism is fatally undermined by the "combination problem"—the conceptual difficulty of explaining how vast multitudes of micro-conscious entities (such as fundamental particles or low-level actual occasions) combine to form a unified macro-conscious subject (such as a human mind). The manuscript relies on the combination problem to dismiss the continuity of consciousness proposed by panpsychism, arguing that the inability to explain subject-summing forces a retreat to difference-in-kind dualism.

This critique fails entirely because it treats the combination problem as a terminal, unanswerable paradox rather than a vibrant area of ongoing research. It systematically ignores the major 2020–2025 breakthroughs in panpsychist literature that directly solve or bypass the manuscript's objections.

### 5.1 Ignorance of Contemporary Mental Combination Models (Roelofs)

The most egregious omission in this section is the failure to address Luke Roelofs' extensive 2020–2024 work on composite subjectivity, specifically his seminal book *Combining Minds: How to Think about Composite Subjectivity* (Oxford University Press, 2019/2020) and his subsequent papers in *Dialectica* and *Philosophical Studies*.

Roelofs systematically dismantles the intuition that mental combination is impossible. He outlines three robust forms of combinationism (panpsychist, functionalist, and psychological) and provides rigorous topological and functional models for how subjects can overlap and share experiences. Roelofs specifically addresses the mechanics of "subject-fusion" and "experience-sharing"—the exact mechanisms the manuscript claims are impossible. Furthermore, Roelofs (2020) addresses the specific "revelation" and "constitutive panpsychist" problems that the manuscript attempts to leverage as silver bullets. By completely ignoring Roelofs' topological solutions to the boundary problem of composite subjectivity, the manuscript attacks a strawman version of panpsychism frozen in the mid-2010s.

### 5.2 Phenomenal-Causal Powers and the Hard Problem of Matter (Mørch)

The manuscript's secondary attack on panpsychism suggests that even if subjects could combine, panpsychism fails to provide a coherent mechanism for macro-level mental causation that is superior to dualism. This claim is thoroughly falsified by the 2020–2025 literature surrounding Hedda Hassel Mørch's Phenomenal-Causal Powers view.

Mørch (2020, 2023) integrates insights from Russellian monism to propose that phenomenal properties are essentially dispositional—they are the categorical bases of physical causal powers. In this view, conscious experiences do not just passively accompany physical systems; they constitute the intrinsic nature of matter that makes it behave as it does. Mørch elegantly links the hard problem of consciousness with the hard problem of matter: physics describes the structural and relational "software" of the universe, and phenomenal consciousness is the non-structural "hardware" it runs on.

This framework directly and decisively answers the manuscript's demand for a mental causation mechanism. In Mørch's model, there is no need for a non-physical mind to reach across an ontological gap to push physical particles at quantum indeterminacies (the manuscript's mathematically failing model). Instead, mental causation is simply the intrinsic nature of physical causation. The manuscript's failure to engage with Mørch’s *Non-physicalist Theories of Consciousness* (Cambridge University Press, 2023) or her *Topoi* and *Journal of Consciousness Studies* papers (2020) indicates a fatal detachment from the contemporary landscape of non-reductive theories. It falsely presents its own gerrymandered dualism as the only viable theory of mental efficacy by censoring the panpsychist alternatives that achieve efficacy with far greater parsimony.

## 6. Epistemic Self-Stultification, Illusionism, and the Phenomenal Concept Strategy

The final pillar of the manuscript's argument is its attempt to eliminate epiphenomenalism (and by extension, any non-interactionist physicalism) through the charge of epistemic self-stultification. The manuscript argues that if consciousness has no physical effects, it cannot cause our verbal reports or beliefs *about* consciousness. Therefore, an epiphenomenalist theory destroys its own rational foundations and cannot be rationally held by the very minds it describes, because the theory forbids the mind from causing the belief in the theory.

The manuscript uses this alleged self-defeat to mandate its own interactionist dualist tenets, claiming that denying consciousness causal efficacy is "rationally untenable". This argument commits a basic fallacy of reification and entirely ignores the contemporary cognitive science and physicalist philosophy that successfully dissolves the self-stultification charge without requiring ontological dualism.

### 6.1 Attention Schema Theory (AST) and the Dissolution of the Regress

The manuscript's self-stultification argument relies on the deeply flawed premise that a report about a phenomenal state must be caused by the *ontological reality* of that non-physical phenomenal state. This premise is explicitly rejected by the 2020s literature on representationalism and illusionism, most notably Michael Graziano’s Attention Schema Theory (AST).

The manuscript dismisses illusionism by invoking the "illusion problem"—claiming that to have an illusion of consciousness, one must *be* conscious, thus triggering an infinite regress. This is a severe strawmanning of the contemporary position. AST does not posit an experiential "illusion" floating in a Cartesian theater. Rather, it posits that the brain constructs a low-resolution, schematic data structure to model its own attentional states. Because this evolutionary data structure is designed for rapid processing rather than perfect physical accuracy, it lacks the granular details of neurons, synapses, and physical mechanics. Consequently, the higher-order cognitive system reads this schema and reports the presence of a "magical," non-physical property—an inner experience.

Under AST and subtle illusionism, there is no epistemic self-stultification and no infinite regress. The physical brain is simply a machine that computes a false, simplified proposition about its own mechanics for the sake of processing efficiency. The *report* "I am conscious" is physically caused by the processing of the attention schema. The system does not need an actual, non-physical phenomenal property to cause the report; it only needs the physical data structure that represents one. By failing to test the epistemic self-stultification argument against AST—which perfectly explains why a subject would sincerely report consciousness despite lacking a non-physical mind—the manuscript leaves its most crucial foundational argument entirely untested against the leading neuroscientific counter-framework of the current decade.

### 6.2 Kripkean Necessity and the Phenomenal Concept Strategy (PCS)

Similarly, the manuscript attempts to utilize Saul Kripke’s modal arguments and Joseph Levine’s Explanatory Gap to enforce a strict ontological divide. It argues that because "pain" and "C-fibre firing" are rigid designators, any identity between them must be necessary. Because there is an apparent contingency between brain states and phenomenal states, and because pain is identified directly by its phenomenological quality (ruling out the appearance/reality gap used in the "heat/molecular motion" maneuver), the contingency is real and physicalism is false.

The manuscript treats this epistemic divide as absolute proof of an ontological divide, deliberately ignoring the entire 2020–2025 literature on the Phenomenal Concepts Strategy (PCS) and the self-organizing metarepresentational account (SOMA). Contemporary physicalism does not deny the Kripkean semantic premise or the epistemic gap; rather, it utilizes PCS to demonstrate that the conceptual isolation of first-person phenomenal concepts is a predictable, physically explicable feature of how a biological system self-monitors.

Phenomenal concepts are cognitively isolated from physical-theoretical concepts not because they refer to a separate realm of non-physical substances, but because they employ fundamentally different, non-inferential neural access mechanisms. The epistemic gap is an artifact of our cognitive architecture, not a seam in the fabric of the universe. The manuscript's insistence on treating an epistemic limitation as a metaphysical reality—while censoring the vast literature that proves otherwise—is intellectually bankrupt.

## 7. Structural Integrity and Metaphysical Consistency

Beyond the empirical and literature-based failures detailed above, the manuscript exhibits profound internal inconsistencies regarding its own metaphysical rule-set. The text operates on a moving baseline of causal claims designed to evade falsification.

The analysis reveals that different sections of the framework interchangeably claim that *some* consciousness has physical effects, that *every* conscious subject has effects, that *every* conscious episode is efficacious, or that consciousness is merely *dispositionally capable* of influencing outcomes. This severe equivocation prevents any rigorous testing of the theory. If the theory only requires that *some* consciousness has physical effects, it is immune to falsification by clinical cases of localized epiphenomenalism (e.g., pain-asymbolia or anosognosia), effectively moving the goalposts whenever neurological data challenges the interactionist model.

Furthermore, the manuscript relies on an unstated requirement for determinate, closed individualism. The argument for agent causation and moral responsibility assumes a persisting subject that exists independently of brain-generated organization. However, the manuscript fails to provide any mechanism for the individuation of these souls or subjects. If the subject is not individuated by the physical brain structure, the framework lacks any theoretical machinery to explain why a specific stream of consciousness aligns with a specific biological organism. A theory in which agency redistributes outcome-luck needs a fairness condition and an individuation principle, neither of which the manuscript supplies.

## 8. Explicit Weaknesses Justifying Rejection

As mandated by the review criteria, the following five critical weaknesses represent fatal flaws in the manuscript's empirical claims, counterargument coverage, and logical inferences. Each point is paired with a relevant 2020–2025 peer-reviewed citation that the manuscript omits, contradicts, or fundamentally mishandles.

1. **Mathematical Incoherence of the Quantum Mind-Matter Interface:** The manuscript relies on consciousness biasing actualizations within the Born distribution to achieve mental causation, failing to recognize that such directed, teleological bias mathematically violates the public statistical distributions of quantum mechanics, generating an unresolved epistemic trilemma regarding empirical falsifiability.

- *Omitted/Contradicted Literature:* Paolini Paoletti, F. (2020). "Causal Exclusion without Causal Sufficiency." *Synthese*. (Demonstrates that causal exclusion applies equally in stochastic environments without requiring deterministic sufficiency).
2. **Ignorance of the Quantum Zeno Problem for Consciousness:** The manuscript utilizes consciousness-induced objective collapse to secure downward causation but completely ignores the formal mathematical proofs demonstrating that continuous conscious observation would freeze physical evolution (the Zeno effect), rendering the emergence or continuous transition of conscious states impossible.

- *Omitted/Contradicted Literature:* Chalmers, D. J., & McQueen, K. J. (2022). "Consciousness and the Collapse of the Wave Function." In *Consciousness and Quantum Mechanics*, Oxford University Press.
3. **Censorship of Contemporary Panpsychist Solutions to the Combination Problem:** The manuscript dismisses Whiteheadian and panpsychist frameworks by asserting that the combination problem is unsolvable, systematically ignoring the topological and functional frameworks developed in the 2020s that successfully resolve composite subjectivity and experience-sharing.

- *Omitted/Contradicted Literature:* Roelofs, L. (2020). "Consciousness, Revelation, and Confusion: Are Constitutive Panpsychists Hoist by Their Own Petard?" *Dialectica*, 74(1), 63–96. (Also *Combining Minds*, OUP 2020).
4. **Failure to Address Phenomenal-Causal Powers:** The manuscript falsely frames difference-in-kind dualism as the only viable mechanism for mental causation, entirely ignoring modern Russellian monist frameworks where phenomenal properties act as the categorical bases for physical dispositions, thereby achieving mental causation without ontological dualism.

- *Omitted/Contradicted Literature:* Mørch, H. H. (2023). *Non-physicalist Theories of Consciousness*. Cambridge University Press. (Also Mørch, 2020, "The Phenomenal Powers View," *Journal of Consciousness Studies*).
5. **Strawmanning of Epistemic Self-Stultification via Ignorance of Attention Schema Theory:** The manuscript falsely claims that non-interactionist physicalism is epistemically self-stultifying (asserting that a non-efficacious mind cannot cause reports about itself). It entirely ignores leading neuroscientific frameworks that perfectly explain phenomenal reports via physically efficacious, low-resolution attentional data schemas, dissolving the regress without requiring dualism.

- *Omitted/Contradicted Literature:* Graziano, M. S. A. (2020). "Attention Schema Theory and the Illusion of Consciousness." *Journal of Consciousness Studies*.

**Verdict:** Due to its reliance on falsified physical mechanisms (the Quantum Zeno problem), deliberate exegetical misrepresentation of primary sources (Griffin), and systematic evasion of the leading neuroscientific and panpsychist counter-frameworks of the 2020s, this manuscript is structurally unsound and must be rejected.
