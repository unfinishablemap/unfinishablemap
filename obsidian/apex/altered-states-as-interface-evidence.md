Altered states of consciousness are usually treated as curiosities—isolated phenomena to be explained one at a time. The Unfinishable Map argues they are something more: a systematic dataset that maps the architecture of the consciousness-brain interface with a precision no single state achieves alone. Meditation, psychedelics, dreaming, hypnagogia, anaesthesia, and terminal lucidity each modulate specific components of the interface in distinct ways. Compared—asking which features of consciousness survive, which degrade, and which intensify across states—they reveal not a single channel but a multi-layered architecture with separable, individually reconfigurable components. That convergent evidence constrains the architecture of consciousness-brain coupling without by itself adjudicating its metaphysics: the Map's [[filter-theory|filter theory]] reads altered states as the brain loosening or reconfiguring its constraint on consciousness, while a production account reads the same reconfigurations as intrinsic neural dynamics. The dataset fixes the *structure*, not whether the brain filters consciousness or produces it. The Map argues below that the filter reading better organises the subset of cases where consciousness persists or intensifies as neural activity falls; that argument is earned in the analysis, not assumed by the pattern.

## Why Altered States Matter for the Interface

Any single altered state can be explained away: the psychedelic paradox as disinhibition, dreaming as the brain entertaining itself, anaesthetic dissociations as pharmacological complexity.

What no single-state explanation addresses is the *pattern across states*. When six radically different perturbations—pharmacological, neurochemical, volitional, transitional, eliminative, and neurodegenerative—reveal the same multi-component interface architecture, the convergence demands explanation. The continental-drift analogy must be drawn with care here. Independent lines of evidence that point toward the same conclusion can compound in evidential weight, as the Map's [[the-convergence-argument-for-dualism|convergence methodology]] argues—but the altered-state cluster is not straightforwardly such a case. The states share a single explanatory move ("the filter reconfigures"), and the same move is available to production theorists, who accommodate the same set by treating dynamical complexity as collapsing or reorganising under each perturbation. These are structurally identical accommodations. So the convergence-of-altered-states pattern cannot honestly be cited as multiple independent confirmations of filter theory—the cluster carries the evidential weight of one pattern, not six. What it does establish is that *whatever* the right account of consciousness-brain coupling, that account must have the multi-channel structure the pattern reveals.

## Six Windows Into the Interface

### Psychedelics: The Widened Filter

[[psychedelics-and-the-filter-model|Psychedelic states]] furnish the most dramatic case in which disrupting the brain *expands* rather than diminishes consciousness—dramatic in its phenomenology, not decisive between the competing readings. Psilocybin and LSD decrease activity in the [[default-mode-network]] while increasing connectivity between normally segregated networks (Carhart-Harris et al., 2012; Tagliazucchi et al., 2016); precision imaging has since reframed both findings as cortex-wide desynchronization dissolving network identity itself rather than suppressing the DMN specifically (Siegel et al., 2024). Subjects report not confusion but enrichment: expanded perception, heightened emotional depth, ego dissolution, encounters described as "more real than real."

The REBUS model (Relaxed Beliefs Under Psychedelics) describes the mechanism in physicalist terms: high-level predictive priors relax, allowing bottom-up information greater influence (Carhart-Harris & Friston, 2019). REBUS and the wider entropic-brain programme were built to explain psychedelic action without any non-physical consciousness. The Map reinterprets the same neuroimaging against the grain of that self-understanding: where REBUS sees a predictive hierarchy reorganising itself, filter theory reads the brain's constraining architecture loosening so that consciousness manifests more broadly. The reinterpretation is the Map's own—compatible with REBUS rather than confirmed by it, undiscriminated by the data, and one the programme's authors would reject. The key observation for the interface map is *what specifically changes*: the narrative self dissolves while phenomenal presence intensifies; agency becomes unreliable while receptivity expands. The channel constraining consciousness to a personal perspective closes while the channel transmitting phenomenal content widens.

### Dreams: The Reconfigured Interface

Every night, [[dream-consciousness|dreaming]] demonstrates that consciousness persists through radical neural reorganisation. Sensory gating blocks external input, the dorsolateral prefrontal cortex deactivates, neurochemistry shifts from aminergic to cholinergic dominance—yet consciousness generates fully immersive worlds that subjects sometimes rate as more vivid than waking. Filter theory offers a simple organisation: the interface reconfigures rather than shuts down, some channels closing (sensory input, executive control) while others run freely (imagery, emotion, association).

The dream evidence contributes three data points to the interface map. First, phenomenal experience persists through configurations where executive control has largely withdrawn—these are separable interface components. Second, cognitive outcomes grade with phenomenal richness rather than with sleep stage: content-incorporating dreams show a 42% solving rate for incubated puzzles against 17% for non-incorporated dreams (Konkoly et al., 2026)—a single small-sample study whose effect size awaits replication—and the creative benefit Cai et al. (2009) found attached to REM rather than to NREM. That stage division is a poor proxy for experience, as [[dream-consciousness]] now argues: dreaming occurs in NREM too, "dreamless" sleep is defined by absent report, and the sleep-onset results below are NREM benefits. The gradient has to be read by reported experience, not by stage label. Third, [[dream-consciousness#Lucid Dreaming as Distinct State|lucid dreaming]] shows the interface admits multiple stable configurations that trained practitioners can switch between voluntarily—metacognitive awareness reactivating within a dream, a hybrid that exists in neither waking nor ordinary REM—and two-way dream communication, in which dreamers receive and answer novel stimuli during verified REM, shows consciousness during sleep in genuine causal engagement with external information rather than replaying stored content. The [[lucid-dreaming-and-dualist-rendering|rendering dilemma]] sharpens the case: a brain that generates phenomenally complete worlds during sleep yet fails to compensate for waking sensory degradation points toward consciousness providing rendering content through neural architecture rather than neural architecture producing it.

### Hypnagogia: The Staged Withdrawal

The [[hypnagogic-phenomenology-and-interface-modulation|hypnagogic transition]]—falling asleep—is where waking capacities are reported to come apart in stages rather than together: voluntary attention first, then autonomous imagery, then fluctuating agency, and reality-monitoring last, running from the most actively directed capacities to the most passive. That four-part ordering is the Map's own synthesis of heterogeneous retrospective reports rather than a sequence Mavromatis (1987) proposed, and it is inferred from report gathered as reality-monitoring was already degrading—typical rather than invariant, suggestive rather than established.

Staggered dissolution constrains interface architecture without discriminating for it. A unified neural product need not dim uniformly: the rivals the source article sets out—graded precision-attenuation down one hierarchy, workspace fragmentation, cortical territories falling asleep on separate timelines—generate the same staggered order from a single mechanism. What the transition does display is a bandwidth asymmetry, the receiving side (autonomous imagery) opening wider as the transmitting side (voluntary control) narrows, though motor gating accounts for that too. The [[coupling-modes]] framework predicts a distinct phenomenological signature for each of its modes along the sequence; hypnagogia is the natural laboratory for that prediction, which has not been run—and since both are Map articles, the pairing is framework-internal coherence rather than corroboration.

Lacaux et al. (2021) found participants who accumulated at least fifteen seconds in N1 sleep roughly three times as likely to discover a hidden shortcut in an arithmetic task (83% against 30%)—fifteen seconds being the minimum criterion for classifying an N1 episode rather than an administered dose. The paradigm is no longer unreplicated, and the replication is negative: Löwe et al. (2025), preregistered with ninety participants on a perceptual insight task, found no N1 advantage over waking (p = 0.75) and located the benefit at N2 instead (p = 0.038)—deeper sleep rather than the boundary state. Neither study isolates what phenomenal experience contributes, since prefrontal disinhibition could produce both the solving and the imagery that merely accompanies it; sleep-onset creativity is consistent with partial interface loosening having functional consequences and no longer supplies even single-paradigm support for it.

### Meditation: Voluntary Interface Modulation

Where other altered states impose interface changes from outside—pharmacology, sleep pressure, brain injury—[[meditation-and-consciousness-modes|meditation]] demonstrates that consciousness can reconfigure its own interface from within. Focused attention meditation (FA) strengthens the selection mechanism through sustained attentional control—frontal theta, ACC activation, dorsal attention network engagement. Open monitoring meditation (OM) cultivates the opposite: passive witnessing, with PCC deactivation, alpha increase, and DMN quieting through disengagement rather than suppression. That consciousness can operate in both answers the Advaita challenge of how a selector can also be a non-interfering witness: these are *modes* of interfacing rather than a single fixed relationship to neural activity.

The voluntariness is the critical datum. If the brain produced consciousness, consciousness reconfiguring its own neural substrate looks circular—the output altering the machinery that generates it. Under filter theory no circularity arises: consciousness operates the interface from its own side, adjusting which channels are engaged and how tightly. Advanced meditators describing "effortless awareness" have trained the interface to a configuration requiring minimal intervention—[[meditation-and-consciousness-modes#Effortless Awareness After Effortful Training|effort first, then a trained brain needing less correction]]—and the resulting [[witness-consciousness]] observes mental contents without identifying with them. Kral et al. (2022) found no structural brain change in a well-controlled 218-participant study, though the functional signatures of different attention modes survive scrutiny: the reconfiguration is functional, not anatomical.

Meditative cessation—*deliberate* disengagement of consciousness from neural processing, unlike externally imposed anaesthesia or biologically regulated sleep, followed by return and often by reports of transformative insight—sharpens the point: on the filter reading the meditator steps away from the interface entirely and steps back.

That reading faces a meditation-specific rival, the contemplative sibling of REBUS. Laukkonen and Slagter (2021) derive the entire deconstructive arc—conceptual attenuation, reduced reactivity, non-dual awareness, cessation itself—from progressive precision-reduction in a hierarchical generative model, with no interface anywhere in the machinery, and model *nirodha samāpatti* as the limit of a mind that has temporarily stopped predicting (Laukkonen et al., 2023). Within that framework the voluntariness carries no circularity—a hierarchy adjusting the gain on its own lower levels is what such architectures do—and cessation-and-return is precision driven toward zero, then a reboot with recalibrated priors, which is also its account of the insight reports. The arc's ordering largely coincides with the interface reading's channel-by-channel disassembly, so no discriminator has been derived from it. What the precision story does not supply, by its own mechanistic standard, is why graded withdrawal of prediction should be *felt* as deepening stillness rather than merely registered. That residue is the framework boundary the Map's [[predictive-processing-and-dualism|treatment of predictive processing]] marks in full; the meditation window, taken alone, does not discriminate.

### Anaesthesia: The Interface Pharmacologically Dissected

[[anaesthesia-and-the-consciousness-interface|Anaesthetic pharmacology]] provides the most precise molecular map of the interface. Different agents produce different phenomenological outcomes despite identical behavioural unresponsiveness: under propofol (GABA-A) patients typically report nothing on emergence; ketamine (NMDA) preserves vivid consciousness while severing it from behavioural output; volatile agents fragment temporal coherence; midazolam eliminates memory encoding while leaving consciousness and responsiveness largely intact. The **xenon-ketamine contrast** is the cleanest dissociation in the catalogue: both are NMDA antagonists, yet xenon extinguishes phenomenal experience while ketamine preserves it (Sarasso et al., 2015)—xenon's broader action on two-pore potassium channels suppressing the phenomenal component while ketamine's selective blockade severs access and spares it. Same primary receptor, opposite outcomes: interface components are pharmacologically separable even within a single receptor system.

Montupil and colleagues (2023) distinguish three states under nominally "stable" anaesthesia: unconsciousness, disconnected consciousness (content without environmental coupling or accessible memory), and connected consciousness (intraoperative awareness, in paralysed patients sometimes including pain). What first-person report registers as "the gap" is a compound signature whose interior is heterogeneous and partly hidden from the subject; the [[anesthesia-void|anaesthesia void]] develops that epistemology.

These dissociations reveal at least four separable components: phenomenal presence, behavioural access, temporal binding, and memory encoding. Specific molecular targets have replaced the Meyer-Overton single-mechanism picture, consistent with a structured interface and equally with a modular production account: receptor specificity shows that the substrate is structured, which every physicalist already asserts, not what the structure connects to. The ketamine case carries epistemological weight of its own: patients who later report vivid experiences show consciousness keeping its causal connection to memory encoding while severed from behavioural output, and if it were epiphenomenal during that state the reliability of those reports would be self-defeating—the Map's [[self-stultification-as-master-argument|self-stultification argument]].

Three further phenomena bear on the interface, each with its interpretation held apart from its finding. First, stochastic emergence: genetically identical mice under identical conditions show emergence times spanning two orders of magnitude, deterministic pharmacokinetic models fail, and a stochastic neuronal-dynamics model fits (Stone et al., 2025). Nothing in that data separates classical noise from genuine indeterminacy at the interface; the Map's quantum-selection gloss is its live hypothesis, not the study's finding. Second, the **active reboot**: recovery is not induction in reverse (Mashour, 2024). Hu et al. (2023) found, in mice, that emergence routes through ubiquitin-driven degradation of the KCC2 cotransporter in the ventral posteromedial thalamus across all four agents tested—propofol, pentobarbital, isoflurane, and ketamine, two receptor classes on the authors' partition and three on the Map's—so the reopening pathway is shared even where the closing pathways differ. "Active reboot" is the Map's label for that class, and the pathway is physical causation throughout, measuring no mental-to-physical traffic. Executive function also returns before simpler sensorimotor tasks (Mashour et al., 2021). The asymmetry is robust across species; its *interpretation* as evidence about the interface is what remains a live hypothesis. Third, neural inertia: the concentration required to induce unconsciousness exceeds that at which consciousness returns, a conserved hysteresis (Sepúlveda et al., 2019) sustained by mutually inhibitory anaesthesia-active and wake-active populations. Those populations are the physical account; the interface reading adds that the unconscious state is an actively maintained configuration with its own resistance to reconfiguration—fit, again, rather than force.

### Terminal Lucidity: The Interface Near Collapse

[[terminal-lucidity-and-filter-transmission-theory|Terminal lucidity]]—the return of memory, orientation, and coherent speech in patients with severe neurodegeneration, often shortly before death—is the cluster's steepest single-phenomenon cost, and the one where honesty matters most. Severe documented damage paired with dramatic cognitive return strains the production model's monotonic prediction that less substrate means less capacity. But the first prospective study (Tollock et al., 2025; 151 enrolled, 93 with a lucidity event across 267 events) points *away* from the filter reading, not toward it: the events are predominantly brief and trigger-linked, with the dramatic terminal subtype only 4.1% of them, and the authors frame the finding mechanism-neutrally as "a network-level return of cognitive function despite severe dementia." Terminal lucidity therefore *constrains* the production account without *establishing* the filter alternative—the prospective data is more consistent with a neurochemical reading than with interface loosening. Its place in the catalogue is as the costliest anomaly, not confirmation.

## The Convergence Matrix

When the six windows are compared systematically, the interface architecture becomes visible:

| Interface Component | Psychedelics | Dreams | Hypnagogia | Meditation (OM) | Anaesthesia (propofol) | Terminal lucidity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Phenomenal presence | Intensified | Maintained | Maintained → fading | Maintained | Abolished | Transiently restored |
| Narrative self | Dissolved | Altered | Loosened | Quieted | Abolished | Transiently restored |
| Voluntary attention | Unreliable | Absent (non-lucid) | Sequential loss | Deliberately released | Abolished | Transiently restored |
| Reality monitoring | Compromised | Absent | Sequential loss | Maintained | Abolished | Transiently restored |
| Emotional tone | Intensified | Intensified | Variable | Equanimous | Abolished | Restored (relational) |
| Agency | Reduced | Absent (non-lucid) | Fluctuating | Deliberately suspended | Abolished | Transiently restored |
| Memory encoding | Variable | Impaired | Impaired | Enhanced | Abolished | Retrieval restored |
| Temporal coherence | Altered | Maintained | Loosened | Enhanced | Abolished (or fragmented) | Transiently restored |

Three patterns emerge from this matrix.

**First, phenomenal presence is the most robust component.** It persists through psychedelic ego dissolution, dreaming, hypnagogia, and open monitoring—states where most other components have degraded or reconfigured. Only propofol-class anaesthesia, targeting the posterior cortical hot zone directly, appears to abolish it (the entry reflects retrospective report, which the [[anesthesia-void|anaesthesia void]] shows cannot by itself discriminate cessation from unretained content). Phenomenal consciousness is either the deepest layer of the interface or the aspect least dependent on any particular configuration.

**Second, voluntary attention and agency are the most fragile components.** They degrade first during hypnagogia, are absent in non-lucid dreams, become unreliable under psychedelics, and are deliberately released in open monitoring. That fragility is what the [[coupling-modes]] framework expects if attention is the channel through which consciousness acts back on neural activity—whichever mode implements it—since it requires the tightest bidirectional coupling.

**Third, the components dissociate independently.** Psychedelics dissolve the narrative self while intensifying phenomenal presence. Ketamine preserves phenomenal presence while abolishing behavioural access. Hypnagogia is reported to lose voluntary attention before reality monitoring. Meditation releases agency while enhancing temporal coherence. [[out-of-body-experiences|Out-of-body experiences]] dislocate the bodily self-model from the body's location—by right temporo-parietal stimulation and by VR full-body illusions—while phenomenal presence and coherent reasoning persist. No single component tracks all others. This independent dissociation is the central evidence for a multi-channel interface, and the central difficulty for any theory that treats consciousness as a unified product of neural activity; whether the same architecture holds across species is the question of [[interface-heterogeneity]].

## What the Pattern Reveals

A necessary honesty first: differential disruption profiles are ontologically neutral. Global workspace theory and integrated information theory also predict that disrupting different neural modules produces different failure modes, so independent dissociation does not by itself favour filter theory over production models; both accommodate structured, multi-component architectures.

The discriminating evidence lies where consciousness *persists or intensifies* during severe neural disruption. Psychedelics expand experience while decreasing cortical activity; REM sleep enhances creative problem-solving through a configuration that has *withdrawn* executive control. (Sleep-onset creativity once stood in this list; it dropped out when Löwe et al. relocated the benefit to N2.) If the brain produces consciousness, a brain doing less should not produce more. One caution governs the argument: decreased *regional activity* is not less *physical processing*. The psychedelics that lower default-mode activity raise between-network connectivity and signal entropy, so the enhancement-through-disruption cases carry weight only where the fuller measures—connectivity, entropy, disinhibition, hierarchical precision—fail to keep pace with the richer experience. **Production models** must explain these cases, and why identical behavioural outputs mask radically different phenomenologies (propofol against ketamine). Each has a piecemeal physicalist explanation—disinhibition, pharmacological specificity, functional separability—and the candidate unifier, the active-inference programme, is engaged at the meditation window above.

**Filter theory** provides a unified organisation. The brain constrains consciousness through multiple semi-independent channels; altered states loosen, tighten, or reconfigure different ones; the pattern of what survives and what degrades maps the filter's architecture. Phenomenal presence—the most robust component—corresponds to the receiving side of the interface, which requires only that consciousness have *some* channel through which to manifest; voluntary attention—the most fragile—to the transmitting side, through which consciousness acts back on neural processes. The asymmetry between robust reception and fragile transmission fits an interface model without discriminating for it: global workspace theory makes a conscious-access bottleneck its defining feature, so the bandwidth datum—roughly 10 bits per second of conscious throughput against 10⁹ of aggregate neural processing—is shared rather than discriminating. Near-death experiences supply at most a weak limit case: the gamma surges Xu et al. (2023) recorded in two of four comatose patients during ventilator withdrawal are attributed by the authors to global hypoxia, none of the patients survived to report, and the step from a gamma surge to *intensified consciousness* is a framework-stage interpretation.

Altered states add a seventh line of inquiry to the six the [[interface-specification-programme|Interface Specification Programme]] identifies: *empirical interface mapping through natural and pharmacological perturbation*—the lesion study, applied to the interface rather than to brain tissue.

## Challenges and Limitations

**Report reliability.** Altered states compromise the cognitive systems accurate introspection requires: psychedelic insights may be confabulation, hypnagogic reports are retrospective, dream recall is incomplete. Reliability varies across states—meditation enhances rather than degrades metacognitive accuracy (Fox et al., 2012)—and objective correlates (neuroimaging, creative performance, two-way dream communication) corroborate report where they can be checked.

**Selection bias in the matrix.** A critic might argue the pattern appears because the states were chosen to fit the conclusion. But these are the major categories of altered consciousness recognised across neuroscience, pharmacology, and contemplative traditions—the best-studied perturbations of human consciousness, not outliers.

**The disinhibition alternative** is the standing challenge rather than a further one: each window has its production-side accommodation, and as the opening section argued, the convergence itself does not discriminate. The discriminating weight rests on the enhancement-through-disruption cases, not on the count of converging states.

## Evidence and Dependency

The lines of support in this synthesis do not all weigh the same, and most share a premise. The six windows are externally evidenced as *observations*: the psychedelic desynchronization, the anaesthetic dissociations, the sleep-stage creativity results, and the prospective terminal-lucidity data rest on cited empirical work that holds whether or not the Map is right. Reading those observations as an *interface*—channels loosening, tightening, reconfiguring—is inherited from Tenets 1 and 3 together with the filter reading, and each window's production-side accommodation shows the observations do not force it. The convergence matrix therefore contributes mutual coherence only: it organises six windows under one vocabulary, which the opening section already concedes is one pattern, not six confirmations. The one independently argued line is the enhancement-through-disruption case—that a substrate doing less should not yield richer experience—which needs only the premise that the fuller physical measures fail to keep pace with the experience, a premise the psychedelic data satisfy partly and the sleep-onset data, since Löwe et al., not at all. The ketamine-report argument is imported from the Map's self-stultification argument and carries its premise that reliable report requires the causal efficacy of what is reported. The meditation window's voluntariness argument is neutralised by the precision-reduction rival, so it contributes coherence rather than weight.

## Relation to Site Perspective

The systematic evidence from altered states connects to each of the Map's [[tenets]].

**[[tenets#^dualism|Dualism]]**: The convergence matrix reveals an interface with separable components—phenomenal presence, voluntary attention, narrative self, agency, memory encoding, temporal binding—that can be independently modulated across six mechanistically unrelated perturbations. Under the Map's [[evidential-status-discipline|evidential-status discipline]], that decomposition constrains the architecture without establishing dualism: it shows that the right account of consciousness-brain coupling must be multi-channel—one pattern, not six confirmations—and terminal lucidity enters the catalogue as its costliest anomaly rather than as support. The discriminating work falls to the enhancement-through-disruption cases, where the interface reading is preferred by fit rather than forced.

**[[tenets#^minimal-quantum-interaction|Minimal Quantum Interaction]]**: The matrix bears on the coupling mechanism without fixing it. The Map's own commitment, as [[coupling-modes]] now records, is post-decoherence outcome selection—influence over which brain-prepared alternative becomes actual; basis control (selecting what to attend to) and timing control (sustaining observation) are registered alternatives rather than the Map's claim, and the minimality tenet is an empirical corridor ruling out *larger* interactions, not a ranking that elects the smallest survivor. The fragility of voluntary attention across states is what any active-side channel would show, and the [[selection-only-channel]]—mind contributing only the realisation of one prepared alternative at its Born-rule rate—is as compatible with the matrix as the looser readings are.

**[[tenets#^bidirectional-interaction|Bidirectional Interaction]]**: Meditation provides the strongest evidence. Consciousness deliberately reconfiguring its own neural interface—strengthening attentional channels through FA practice, releasing them through OM practice—demonstrates bidirectional causal traffic on the Map's reading, though the hierarchical rival engaged at the meditation window models the same self-modulation without circularity, so the support here is fit rather than force. The arc from effortful to effortless meditation traces the interface being trained from the consciousness side, though the change is functional rather than structural and physical learning theories predict the same plasticity from top-down attention as a neural process—shared evidence, again.

**[[tenets#^no-many-worlds|No Many Worlds]]**: Altered states are pervaded by selection and foreclosure—the lucid dreamer who chooses to fly forecloses not-flying; the meditator who releases selection experiences its absence—and the matrix maps how selection varies from waking control to meditative release. That variation is in-branch data an Everettian grants entirely: branch-relative selection reproduces every datum the matrix records, the felt difference between choosing and witnessing included. The disagreement turns on whether foreclosure must be *global*—the unchosen options nonactual everywhere, not merely absent from this agent's history—and that requirement is [[tenets/background-commitments|a posit the Map adopts]] rather than a reading the altered-state evidence forces: the posit does the work, and the matrix supplies the phenomenology it applies to.

**[[tenets#^occams-limits|Occam's Razor Has Limits]]**: The simplest model of altered states—brain activity produces consciousness, different activity produces different consciousness—fails to explain why disruption sometimes enhances experience and why identical behavioural outputs mask different phenomenologies. The more complex model, a multi-channel interface, better organises the evidence; parsimony that ignores systematic patterns is not simplicity but evasion.
