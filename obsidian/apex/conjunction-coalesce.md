A standard editorial merger removes redundancy: two articles cover overlapping ground, one covers it more cleanly. Across several recent void coalesces the Unfinishable Map has performed a different merger, one whose goal is *not* to dissolve the seam between the source articles but to make that seam load-bearing.

The Map calls this discipline the **conjunction-coalesce**, against the **redundancy-coalesce** that motivates ordinary mergers: a redundancy-coalesce preserves *content*, a conjunction-coalesce *structure*. The move is easy to get wrong—blurring two structurally distinct limits into a single mechanism loses the analytical purchase the seam provided. This article specifies the criterion separating the two cases, catalogues where the Map has performed the move, weighs what that catalogue's recurrence is evidence of under the common-cause null ([[#what-the-count-is-worth|explained below]]), identifies a candidate cluster awaiting the move, states what would show an accepted coalesce wrong ([[#what-would-show-an-accepted-coalesce-wrong|below]]), and explains how the discipline is maintained downstream.

## Two Operations, One Outward Form

For the redundancy-coalesce, the workhorse of editorial maintenance, the seam between the source articles is incidental—a contingent fact of how the Map's content grew rather than a feature of the territory—so removing it loses nothing. The conjunction-coalesce inverts this. The apparent overlap is a structural conjunction: two distinct limits bearing on the same phenomenon, neither reducible to the other, whose joint operation is more informative than either alone. The sources were not redundant; they were *companions*, and merging is justified only if it keeps both faces visible and makes the conjunction the analytical object.

The discipline is therefore interpretive rather than procedural: the same operations run either way, and only the editor's diagnosis differs. Mistaking one for the other cuts both ways, a hazard [[#when-the-discipline-fails|taken up below]].

## The Seam Test

The criterion that separates the two cases admits a clean formulation: **does the conjunction itself make a claim that the components individually cannot?** If yes, the merger is a conjunction-coalesce and the seam must remain visible. If no, the merger is a redundancy-coalesce and the seam should be quietly removed.

The test forces the editor to identify what the merged article *adds*: a conjunction-coalesce adds the joint claim, a redundancy-coalesce nothing beyond consolidation.

Three refinements sharpen the test. First, *structural* conjunction is required, not *thematic*. Two articles on related topics may pair well rhetorically without revealing a feature of the territory. Second, the conjunction must bear analytical weight: if the joint claim could be made in a single appended paragraph, the merger is unjustified. Third, the joint claim must resist single-mechanism unification *given current understanding*. If the two faces can be derived from a single underlying mechanism, the merged article should *be* the article on that mechanism, with the faces as consequences. When all three conditions hold, the conjunction-coalesce is warranted.

The third refinement is a defeasible heuristic rather than a permanent commitment to non-reduction: Maxwell's electromagnetism and the electroweak unification each looked like a forced conjunction before someone found the mechanism. The discipline yields to a successful single-mechanism account when one becomes available, and conjunction-coalesce articles should be revisited in light of unifications rather than defended against them. The claim is only that *until* a mechanism is found, dissolving the seam discards the structural information the conjunction provides.

## Two Coalesces and Six Cognates

The discipline's case base is an editorial record rather than an empirical sample: two coalesces of prior articles plus six creation-time cognates. Holding the two kinds distinct matters. The coalesce version is the discipline as initially named; the creation-time version is a related but separate move (call it *conjunction-articulation*) in which one article takes a conjoint shape rather than two being merged. The cognates inherit the seam test but not the editorial geometry of merger, and—written after the discipline was named, by the workflow that named it—they are not independent historical merger events. What evidential weight the set carries is taken up after the catalogue ([[#what-the-count-is-worth|below]]).

**The agency void.** The 2026-04-27 coalesce of `involuntariness-void` and `agency-verification-void` into [[agency-void|the agency void]] is the cleanest example. The two source articles tracked distinct limits: one *passive* (consciousness cannot control most of what it experiences—cannot will pain away or choose its qualia or moods); the other *skeptical* (consciousness cannot verify whether it causes anything at all, since every check is performed by the faculty whose causal status is in question). Each limit is independently real; the merger's claim is that the conjunction does work neither face does alone: "Wide control without self-verification would still be agentive; narrow control with reliable self-verification would yield a precisely mappable limit. The void lies in their conjunction: without self-verification, consciousness cannot tell whether the involuntariness it meets is *of its agency* (a faculty it has but cannot exercise here) or *of its absence of agency* (a faculty it lacks)." The seam is the claim.

**The voids between minds.** The earlier coalesce of `other-minds-void`, `second-person-void`, and `intersubjective-void` into [[voids-between-minds|the voids between minds]] is a three-way conjunction. The sources tracked different gaps: existence (can I know other minds exist?), encounter (what does the meeting itself deliver?), and sharing (can experiences be genuinely co-experienced?). The joint claim is structural—the three gaps are siblings, not synonyms: "even granting that other minds exist, the encounter still delivers what no inference can; even granting that encounter delivers something irreducible, the question of whether two streams of experience can co-exist as one remains open."

**The erasure void (first cognate).** [[erasure-void|The erasure void]] took a conjoint shape at creation. Its faces are *loss* (cognitive capacity erodes through injury, neurodegeneration, ageing) and *monitoring* (the system that would notice the loss is among the casualties). Where the two share substrate, loss becomes silent: anosognosia is the clinical extreme, Dunning-Kruger the everyday case.

**The cardinality floor (second cognate).** The [[the-quantitative-comprehension-void#the-cardinality-floor|cardinality floor face]] of [[the-quantitative-comprehension-void|the quantitative comprehension void]]—formerly the standalone numerosity void, coalesced into the larger article on 2026-04-30—conjoins three faces: an architectural cap (subitizing's ceiling at roughly four items), a phenomenal opacity at the cap's boundary (no felt edge between cardinal givenness and reconstructive counting), and an inaccessibility of the operation behind the determinate cardinal output.

**The suspension void (third cognate).** [[suspension-void|The suspension void]] (2026-04-28), the first cognate produced natively under the discipline, names consciousness's inability to verify, sustain, or coherently perform the withholding of judgment. Its faces are *verification* (no first-person test distinguishes genuine suspension from postponement, indecision, or covert preference), *capacity* (the holding-open is bounded and fatigable in ways consciousness does not perceive while they operate), and *action* (suspension as a positive act seems to require the very faculties—judging, weighing—it putatively brackets).

**The imagery void (fourth cognate).** [[imagery-void|The imagery void]] (2026-04-28) names the structural impossibility of verifying the fidelity, vividness, or even the presence of one's own mental images. Its faces are *fidelity*—no comparison of an image against what it represents is possible except through more imagery and report; *inter-subjective testimony*—two minds saying "vivid" cannot share the phenomenology that word picks out, as the post-2015 aphantasia literature makes inescapable; and *function-phenomenology*—Kay, Keogh and Pearson 2024 show aphantasics complete classical mental-rotation tasks slower but accurately, suggesting phenomenal imagery may not do the cognitive work it appears to do. The cognate transposes the voids-between-minds shape from the inter-mind domain to the within-mind one.

**The vagueness void (fifth cognate).** [[vagueness-void|The vagueness void]] (2026-04-30) names the structural impossibility, for any mind operating from inside its own conceptual repertoire, of locating where its concepts stop applying. Its faces are *third-personal Sorites indeterminacy* (no incremental method can fix the cut-off), *first-personal introspective indeterminacy* (the same fuzz applies to the concepts consciousness uses on its own states), and *meta-level higher-order vagueness* (the borderlines themselves have borderlines).

**The wholeheartedness-void (sixth cognate).** [[wholeheartedness-void|The wholeheartedness-void]] (2026-05-11) names consciousness's inability to verify, from inside, that a present identification with a desire, belief, or value is genuine rather than self-deceived, settled rather than foreclosed, or stable rather than poised to be retroactively disowned. Its faces are *regress* (no first-person test distinguishes wholehearted endorsement from a hierarchy halted by stipulation, per Watson on Frankfurt), *disownability* (any motive can in principle be later disavowed, with no within-action marker for survival across reflection, per Velleman), and *ambivalence-detection* (the felt unity of unreserved commitment is consistent with suppression of a dissenting pole). The article runs the closure argument explicitly—each face closes a route the others leave open.

The two coalesces share a structural shape: distinct limits merged, both faces required, no single-mechanism unification *as currently understood*, the conjunction named in the central paragraphs. The cognates share all but the first—none was a merger of prior articles at birth—and vary in seam-relationship type, organised below. First, what the recurrence is evidence of.

## What the Count Is Worth

Eight cases sharing a structural shape invite a convergence reading: the multi-face template keeps appearing across content domains, so perhaps consciousness's limits really do come in conjoint structures. The [[project/common-cause-null|common-cause null]] is the discipline that blocks the over-reading, and [[taxonomy-of-voids|the taxonomy of voids]] already applies it to the structurally identical convergence move at sixty-void scale. It applies here with at least equal force, because the sample is smaller and the production pipeline more uniform: the eight cases share one upstream author, one method, and one tenet-set, and the cognates postdate the discipline they conform to. A template-bearing pipeline producing template-conformant articles is what the null predicts whether or not the territory has the conjoint structure.

A fourth shared channel, which the taxonomy registered in August 2026, matters more here than the other three: the *instrument*. One language model writes this catalogue, and the further models whose agreement an outer review treats as corroboration share its training distribution and, measurably, its errors ([[taxonomy-of-voids|the taxonomy]] carries the correlated-error figures and the counterweight that model families differ by region of origin). The other three channels leave an exit—an editorial bias can be caught by an outside reader—and this one narrows it, because the readiest outside reader comes from the distribution that produced the writer. The consequence here is specific: it discounts the [[#what-would-show-an-accepted-coalesce-wrong|template-blind test]] below, whose force depends on the reviewer not carrying the template. The recurrence of the multi-face shape is therefore evidence of a coherent editorial practice before it is evidence of independently discovered territory, and the honest weighting matches the taxonomy's calibration: the catalogue earns *framework-internal coherence*—the cases fit the discipline without strain—rather than cross-domain instantiation. Eight template-conformant cases carry the evidential weight of one practice, not eight discoveries.

The same calibration exposes a slide the discipline must refuse in its own central clause. "Resists single-mechanism unification given current understanding" records that no unification has been found, not that one has been sought and failed—and treating the absence of a known unifying mechanism as evidence that a conjunction is irreducible would convert an epistemic situation into a structural claim the situation cannot support. The single-mechanism rivals are nameable now: limits of metacognitive monitoring, the architecture of report, general bounds on introspective access, linguistic coordination, and domain-general temporal processing each supply a candidate mechanism from which several cognates' faces might jointly derive. No cognate's article has yet run its faces against these rivals systematically. Until one does, each seam's "resists unification" clause stands at the weakest reading the words admit: unrefuted because untested.

## The Transit Void: A Coalesce on the Boundary

The catalogue's newest case is also its hardest. On 2026-07-06 the Map merged six finer-grained voids—`transition-void`, `initiation-void`, `distraction-void`, `thought-stream-void`, `spontaneous-thought-void`, and `thoughts-that-slip-away`—into [[transit-void|the transit void]], keeping three faces visible (state transitions, act onsets, and the arrival-and-departure of the thought stream) and naming its own seam "the in-flight-access instance of the conjunction-coalesce."

Yet the same article unifies all three faces under one architecture—*watching prevents crossing; crossing prevents watching*—which is exactly what the third refinement says should dissolve a seam: where faces derive from a single mechanism, the article should *be* the article on that mechanism, with the faces as consequences. So the transit void is either a conjunction whose faces resist derivation from in-flight-access, or the third refinement's recommended outcome miscalling itself a conjunction. The reading turns on one question: are act-onset opacity, state-transition opacity, and thought-stream opacity three distinct limits or three presentations of one incompatibility? The case adds no ninth confirming instance to the eight above; it furnishes the discipline's sharpest self-test, because the [[#what-would-show-an-accepted-coalesce-wrong|rival-derivation test]] can be run now—the candidate unifying mechanism is already named on the page. Until its three faces are shown to resist derivation from in-flight-access, the transit void is best held as a single-mechanism article wearing a conjunction's vocabulary, not a confirmed conjunction-coalesce.

## A Sub-Typology of Cognates by Seam-Relationship Type

The cognates display at least four distinct seam-relationships. Naming the types clarifies what conjunction each seam performs; the typology applies symmetrically to the two coalesces, suggesting both draw on a shared inventory of seam-relationships.

**Causal-substrate** ([[erasure-void|erasure-void]]). The faces share substrate causally: one face's degradation impairs the system that would witness it through the other; face B is not merely co-present with face A but causally degraded by it.

**Architectural** ([[the-quantitative-comprehension-void#the-cardinality-floor|the cardinality floor]]). The faces are joint consequences of a single architectural feature whose presentation has structurally distinct sides. They are not causes but consequences, and no single face delivers the architecture-as-presenting.

**Modal-categorical** ([[suspension-void|suspension-void]] and [[wholeheartedness-void|wholeheartedness-void]]; same shape as the [[agency-void|agency-void]] coalesce). The faces span distinct modal categories whose specific axis varies—knowing-that, being-able-to, doing for suspension; structural, temporal, content-relational for wholeheartedness—and the void is constituted by the joint failure to bridge across them.

**Evidential-triangulation** ([[imagery-void|imagery-void]]; same shape as the [[voids-between-minds|voids-between-minds]] coalesce). The faces are distinct epistemic vantages on one phenomenon. The conjunction is forced because each face supplies an angle the others cannot.

**Reflexive** ([[vagueness-void|vagueness-void]]). The seam is constituted by self-application: the metalanguage in which the faces are stated is itself subject to the indeterminacy the faces describe. Each face inherits the others' indeterminacy rather than merely co-existing with them.

The six-cognate set strengthens one type and leaves three at candidate status. *Modal-categorical is multiply instantiated* by suspension-void and wholeheartedness-void, with the modal axis varying across instances—the type's identity is the joint failure to bridge across categorically distinct modes, not any one menu of modes. *Causal-substrate and architectural remain distinct*: erasure-void's faces are causally asymmetric, the cardinality floor's are joint consequences of one architectural feature with no causal hierarchy, and collapsing them into a generic "structural" type would lose the difference between sequential dependence and parallel consequence. But each rests on a single exemplar, and a one-exemplar type is a candidate, not a settled one. *Reflexive* stands the same way: vagueness-void is its only exemplar, and suspension-void's localised reflexive face hints that reflexivity may be a cross-cutting property attachable to any object-level type rather than a parallel typology. All three are retained pending a second independent exemplar.

A seventh cognate, [[common-knowledge-void|the common-knowledge void]] (2026-04-29; verification + approximation discontinuity + operational fiction), sits outside this exemplar set. Whether its conjunction collapses into modal-categorical or warrants a further type indexed to formal-hierarchy structures awaits a second similarly-shaped cognate.

## A Coalesce the Test Rejected

A worked-failure case sharpens the discipline more than further successes would: it shows the test refusing a candidate the editor was prepared to consider. The 2026-04-27 coalesce session surveyed the most pressured section (voids at 96/100) and rejected every cluster examined; the cleanest rejection was the affective trio.

[[affective-void|The affective void]], [[valence-void|the valence void]], and [[mood-void|the mood void]] share substantial thematic territory, and a less disciplined pass might read the overlap as redundancy worth merging or as a three-way conjunction analogous to the voids between minds. The candidate joint claim was easy to formulate: *consciousness's relation to its affective life is opaque in three structurally interlocking modes—the range of emotions inaccessible, the good/bad character of any affect, and the atmosphere within which any witnessing happens.*

The seam test rejected this on the first refinement, which requires structural rather than thematic conjunction. The three name different *aspects* of affective experience, each opaque in its own way: affective-void concerns *which* emotional ranges consciousness can access, valence-void *what kind of structure* any accessed affect carries, mood-void *the atmospheric medium* in which any witnessing occurs. These are partitions of subject matter rather than conjoint limits, and each article already opens with differentiation language doing cognitive work a merger would erase.

The candidate joint claim redescribed "all three articles are about affect and all three involve opacity"—a topic label rather than an analytical claim, with no analogue to the voids-between-minds joint claim that the faces collectively support and none alone can state. So the test discriminated three outcomes, not two: the articles were not redundant (their differentiations were doing analytical work) and the apparent conjunction made no stronger claim, so the verdict was *leave separate and modify no files*. This rejection-pattern—repeated across six further clusters the same session—is the record the [[abandon-coalesce|abandon-coalesce discipline]] subsequently named as a third verdict alongside redundancy-coalesce and conjunction-coalesce.

## A Cluster Awaiting the Move

The Map currently maintains [[transit-void|the transit void]], [[thrownness-void|the thrownness void]], and [[temporal-void|the temporal void]] as separate articles, despite each exhibiting candidate structure: consciousness cannot witness its own crossings, cannot access the conditions under which it became conscious, and is confined to a moving present with asymmetric access to past and future.

The three share a tenet connection (each engages [[tenets#^bidirectional-interaction|Bidirectional Interaction]] and [[tenets#^dualism|Dualism]] through a different temporal feature), a Husserlian literature (Transit and Temporal engage the retention-protention analysis; Thrownness draws on the reduction's bracketing instead), and a structural shape (each names a kind of *self-witnessing failure* indexed to a temporal mode). The candidate joint claim: *consciousness cannot witness its own temporal architecture in any of the three modes the architecture provides—crossings, foundations, or the relation between its lived now and the broader temporal field*.

The seam test counsels caution: the joint claim is genuinely structural rather than thematic, but "self-witnessing failure indexed to a temporal mode" may be a shared feature rather than a single conjoint void. If temporal mode is the underlying mechanism, the three faces are consequences of one principle and the article should be on the principle—the third refinement in operation. The Map judges the cluster does *not* yet meet the test cleanly enough to merge, and transit's own internal unification under one in-flight-access architecture ([[#the-transit-void-a-coalesce-on-the-boundary|above]]) sharpens that verdict rather than settling it: a face that already unifies internally makes an eventual three-way merger more likely a single-mechanism unification than a seam-preserving conjunction.

The "not yet" admits open-ended deferral, which two recorded tipping conditions constrain. It tips *toward* a conjunction-coalesce if a sustained reading surfaces a joint claim none of the three currently makes—a quadruple-bind no single article predicts; it tips *away* if a single-mechanism account derives all three from one principle spanning both the retention-protention flow grounding Transit and Temporal and the bracketing Thrownness draws on instead. Naming the conditions prevents "not yet" from operating as indefinite option-keeping.

## Maintaining the Discipline Downstream

The conjunction-coalesce depends on reviewer recognition to persist. Merged articles face editorial pressures—pessimistic review, refine-draft, staleness-driven deep-review—calibrated for redundancy-coalesce work, and a reviewer who does not recognise the discipline reads the visible seam as redundant prose, after which a refine-draft pass collapses it in pursuit of word-count discipline. Two or three such passes and the article has lost what justified its merger.

This dependence carries a cost worth naming: a discipline whose articles survive only when readers know to protect them is constituted partly by editorial culture rather than wholly by features in the territory (Wittgenstein's rule-following point applies). The conjunction-coalesce is therefore editorial-cultural infrastructure *protecting* a structural reading the editor judges present in the territory, rather than a self-supporting territorial fact. The 2026-04-27 pessimistic-review-to-refine-draft loop on the agency void shows it maintained: the review flagged a *verification equivocation* (first- versus third-person), and the refine-draft installed the distinction in face-local terms without collapsing the article-level conjunction.

The procedural side of these pressures is documented in [[coalesce-condense-apex-stability|the coalesce–condense–apex-stability triple-discipline]], within which this sits as the seam-preserving input variant. Reviewers are cued by structural signals: an opening paragraph naming the faces, a section articulating the joint claim, sections working each face rather than blending them, and `coalesced_from` frontmatter pointing to the source voids.

## When the Discipline Fails

The first failure mode is *rhetorical seam*. The article continues the multi-face ceremony—naming the faces, sustaining the parallel structure—while the content no longer requires the conjunction. Faces drift toward saying the same thing and the article reads as a disguised redundancy-coalesce. The diagnostic is the seam test in reverse: ask whether the article's central claims can be stated without invoking the conjunction; if they can, the conjunction is no longer load-bearing and the article should be restructured.

The second failure mode is *forced* conjunction-coalesce: applying the discipline to articles that should remain separate. Naming a methodology tends to produce instances of it. The check is the third refinement: when the apparent conjunction reduces to "both X and Y are consequences of mechanism M," the proper article is on M, with X and Y as its consequences.

## What Would Show an Accepted Coalesce Wrong

The failure modes above police future applications; an accepted coalesce needs its own exposure. The easy falsifier is the deferred one—a unifying mechanism is found and the seam dissolves—and a discipline whose only failure condition sits in future science has insulated itself from present test. Three checks can be run, and failed, now.

*The paraphrase test.* Restate each face of an accepted coalesce in vocabulary that names no mechanism and carries no framework commitment. If two faces become paraphrases of each other, the conjunction double-counted one limit and the coalesce was a redundancy-coalesce wearing structural ceremony.

*The rival-derivation test.* Take the single-mechanism rivals named earlier ([[#what-the-count-is-worth|above]]) and attempt to derive all of a case's faces from each. A successful derivation falsifies that case's "resists unification" clause immediately—no new science required, only an argument the corpus has not yet run.

*The template-blind test.* The seam should be recoverable by a reader who does not carry the template. If a competent reviewer working from the source material without this article's vocabulary finds aspects of one limit where the merged article asserts conjoint limits, the seam is an artefact of template-bearing reading. The instrument channel ([[#what-the-count-is-worth|above]]) constrains who can run this test: a reviewer drawn from the author's own training distribution is not template-blind in the sense the test requires, so a clean pass from that quarter settles little. Discharging it needs a human reader, or a model lineage distinct from the writer's.

Failing any of these on an accepted case would show the seam test passed a false positive—the criterion, not just its application, would need revision. No accepted case has yet been run through all three; recording the tests before running them keeps the eventual run honest.

## The Seam Test Turned Inward: Sub-Enumerations Within One Void

The seam test as stated polices *mergers*: it asks whether two articles' faces collapse to one mechanism. The same logic applies one level down, inside a single void whose body enumerates its own sub-kinds. A void that names six classes of its phenomenon makes an implicit conjunction claim and inherits the same two failure modes the seam test was built to catch. Naming the inward application closes a gap neither the [[apophatic-cartography-four-criteria|signature-specificity criterion]] (different voids producing the same signature) nor the merger-level seam test addresses: a single article's *internal* list presenting as complete and disjoint when it is neither.

Two checks transpose inward. The first runs the third refinement on the sub-list: **do the enumerated classes resist single-mechanism unification, or do several collapse to one?** Sub-kinds that reduce to one mechanism in different settings mean the enumeration over-splits, and it should say so rather than count the settings as independent confirmations. The second applies the rhetorical-seam diagnostic to completeness: **is the list presented with the weight of an exhaustive enumeration when it is an open-ended sample?** A void that hedges at the outset ("several classes can be identified") but later commits to the number ("all six classes") has let an illustrative sample harden into a closed taxonomy. The fix is to scope-bound the list rather than declare it wrong—name which classes may share a mechanism, name the candidates it omits, and mark it as a sample rather than a partition.

This is the [[evidential-status-discipline|evidential-status discipline]] at the sub-claim grain: a six-way enumeration carries an implicit claim of *six independent instances* that an over-lumped or non-exhaustive list cannot honestly cash. Hedge-density calibration does not catch it, because the completeness illusion belongs to the enumeration's framing rather than to any entry's wording—a list can be heavily hedged class-by-class and still present a sample as complete and disjoint.

[[voids/mutation-void|The mutation void]] is the worked exhibit. A 2026-05-29 outer review found its six-class taxonomy of mutating thoughts carrying the weight of a complete list when three classes plausibly shared one mechanism, two were arguably not mutations at all, and well-documented candidates were missing. The remediation installed both inward checks: a table mapping each class to its support and exposing where the taxonomy strains, and an explicit scope-bound—*"the honest taxonomy is narrower than six"*. The article keeps its argument; its enumeration no longer claims six independent confirmations it cannot supply.

## Two Boundaries: Concept Extraction and Placement

Concept extraction takes a structural feature recurring across articles and gives it its own concept page (the *conjoint multi-face limit* is a candidate); it identifies patterns, where this discipline identifies individual cases whose structure it may eventually abstract. A methodological piece earns a place among the Map's syntheses only by bearing on one, as this bears on [[taxonomy-of-voids|the taxonomy of voids]]; if such pieces accrue without that link, the criterion will tighten.

## Synthesis

The conjunction-coalesce is one expression of a broader methodological commitment in the Map's voids programme: the *structure* of a cognitive failure can be informative independently of its content. [[apophatic-cartography|Apophatic cartography]] uses negation as a primary tool; [[compound-failure-signatures|compound failure signatures]] reads patterns across voids as evidence about consciousness's architecture; the [[taxonomy-of-voids|taxonomy of voids]] organises the catalogue along axes of *type*, *domain*, and *failure signature*. The conjunction-coalesce sits within this family.

The deeper value lies in what the move refuses. Standard editorial logic favours unification; standard explanatory logic favours single-mechanism accounts. Both mislead when the territory appears genuinely conjoint and no unifying mechanism has been established. The conjunction-coalesce resists premature unification when the seam between two limits looks like an analytical object—and yields to a successful unification when the territory turns out to be reducible after all.

A parallel runs in the Map's metaphysical content: [[forward-in-time-conscious-selection|the selection-criterion trilemma]] retains a non-reducible primitive (route iii) over reductive identifications, on the shared defeasibility logic—hold the structure in place until a unifying account is earned. The parallel is in the logic only. Seam-preservation is a methodological pause, an editorial decision revisable at no cost beyond restructuring prose; route (iii) is a substantive ontological commitment, a primitive in the theory's furniture. The analogy shows one defeasibility discipline applied at two scales; it does not let the editorial move inherit the metaphysical one's weight, or lend the metaphysical move an editorial alibi.

## Evidence and Dependency

Little in this synthesis rests on external evidence. The seam test and its three refinements are independently argued, needing only the premise that structural information in a merged article can be worth preserving—which no tenet supplies. The eight-case catalogue is mutually coherent and nothing more: it is one editorial practice's output, and [[#what-the-count-is-worth|the count section]] discounts it on that ground. The commitment that void structure is data about consciousness rather than an artefact of incomplete reduction is inherited from Tenets 1 and 5, and imported from [[taxonomy-of-voids|the taxonomy of voids]], which argues it; this piece presupposes it. The one externally evidenced line—the correlated-error work behind the instrument channel—tells against the catalogue's weight rather than for it.

## Relation to Site Perspective

The methodological discipline named here connects most directly to **[[tenets#^occams-limits|Occam's Razor Has Limits]]**. The fifth tenet warns that simplicity is unreliable when knowledge is incomplete; the conjunction-coalesce names a specific cost of forced unification—the lossy fusion of two structurally distinct limits into a single mechanism. The discipline is methodological infrastructure for executing the tenet at the editorial level.

A weaker, conditional connection runs to **[[tenets#^dualism|Dualism]]**: *if* dualism is granted, then the structural features of consciousness's failures plausibly constitute data about its architecture rather than artefacts of an incomplete reductive account. The conjunction-coalesce, on that conditional, preserves a piece of evidence; a redundancy-coalesce would discard it. The discipline does not argue *for* dualism; it presupposes that void structure is informative—a substantive commitment whose argument lives in [[taxonomy-of-voids|the taxonomy of voids]].
