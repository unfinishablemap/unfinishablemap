A theory that says consciousness influences the physical world owes an account of why nobody has caught it doing so. The Unfinishable Map's answer is sharper than the usual appeal to difficulty: the interface is *structurally selected to hide from unconditioned aggregate measurement*, not merely hard to detect. The constraints the Map accepts—conservation of energy, the no-signalling theorem, the Born statistics, the integrity of the empirical record—leave the proposed conscious influence one register, where bulk physical instruments cannot see it: *if* a strict-corridor model satisfying all of them jointly exists, it is ensemble-invisible. Whether one exists is open. No joint multi-agent law and no energy-degenerate alternative set has been specified, and no-signalling safety is shown for single-system marginals only; joint and Lorentz-covariant safety remain debts ([[positions/quantum-interface#^p-q10|P-Q10]]; [[multi-agent-born-preservation-problem]]). The corollary is that the proper evidential route is not "find a big quantum anomaly" but "find constrained asymmetries at interface boundaries": disruption, dissociation, graded channel failure, patient-population divergence, and first-person/third-person mismatch.

This is a framework-internal claim about *architecture*, not an empirical result. It says what kind of thing the interface must be if the tenets hold, and where evidence about it could in principle appear. Naming the self-concealing architecture is a coherence achievement at the [[evidential-status-discipline|framework-internal-weight register]], and reading off where the seams are is a research-programme reorientation—neither is positive evidence that consciousness selects. The synthesis draws together what [[tenets|the tenets]], [[brain-internal-born-rule-testing|brain-internal Born-rule testing]], [[forward-in-time-conscious-selection|forward-in-time selection]], [[apex/attention-as-causal-bridge|attention as the causal bridge]], [[apex/introspection-architecture-void-cluster|the introspection-architecture cluster]], [[memory-channel-interface-evidence|the memory hierarchy]], and [[apex/taxonomy-of-voids|the voids catalogue]] each already imply, and names the architecture they converge on.

## The Concealment Is Built Into the Tenets, Not Added to Them

The self-concealing property falls out of the tenets' corridor reading rather than being bolted on when detection failed, and tracing the derivation is what separates an honest architectural claim from an unfalsifiable dodge.

[[tenets#^minimal-quantum-interaction|Minimal Quantum Interaction]] commits to the *smallest* deviation from standard quantum mechanics that could permit mind-matter causation. The Map's strongest reading of that minimum is the strict corridor: consciousness selects which outcome becomes actual from the improper reduced-state mixture left after [[decoherence|decoherence]] completes, choosing among Born-equiprobable alternatives without altering the ensemble distribution. The tenets page states the cost plainly: this "leaves no statistical signature in long runs," so that "under any *unconditioned aggregate* test the mechanism is *empirically indistinguishable from chance*—by construction, not by any sensitivity limit." The aggregate-statistics routes that constrained earlier interactionist proposals therefore cannot probe it; a deviation *conditioned* on intention, task or subject still could. The very move that escapes the timing objection—acting after decoherence, on the improper mixture rather than on sustained superpositions—is the move that empties the aggregate signature. A selection that respected energy conservation but *shifted* the Born distribution would carry a signature in principle. The Rules-out clause forbids any shift detectable at current precision, and the corridor forbids a shift altogether; the minimum-outside-the-corridor fallback the Map keeps open is therefore limited to shifts below current precision, which escape today's instruments by their size, not by construction.

[[brain-internal-born-rule-testing|Brain-internal Born-rule testing]] makes the consequence precise: the strict corridor predicts *no* Born-statistical signature, by construction. A null result from a within-brain test of Born statistics cannot Popper-refute the strict corridor, because the corridor never promised a statistical fingerprint; null results can retire competing mechanisms or remove the motivation for the search, but they leave the strict reading where they found it. This is the cleanest instance of the general pattern. The no-signalling theorem closes the door on the interface ever being used to transmit information faster than light or between isolated systems; conservation laws close the door on its being caught injecting energy; the Born rule closes the door on its perturbing the statistics. Each closed door is a constraint the Map *accepts as true*, and the conjunction, if jointly satisfiable, is what does the concealing.

The architecture, then, is stronger than "the interface happens to be elusive": on the corridor reading the tenets favour, the standard battery of aggregate physical measurements is constitutively unable to register the interface. There concealment is entailed, not assumed; on the outside-corridor fallback it is a matter of current precision.

The concealment also has a formal statement. [[agency-budget|The agency budget]] maps an exactly distribution-preserving selector onto a perfectly secure channel in the information-theoretic sense, and perfect security is *equivalent to* zero third-person statistical evidence in the marginal it preserves. The result cuts both ways: under the corridor constraint, concealment is a theorem rather than a by-product to excuse, with a calculable ceiling on what such a selector can carry; and by the same equivalence no test of that marginal can ever witness the channel. The theorems are published; the mapping onto mental causation is the Map's own construction and carries the coherence-only citation grade.

## A Constraint, Not a Defeater—and Not a Vindication

Naming a self-concealing architecture invites two opposite errors, and the discipline of this synthesis is to refuse both.

The first error is the sceptic's: "a cause that cannot be measured in aggregate is no cause at all; you have defined yourself into unfalsifiability." This overstates. The strict corridor is undetectable by *aggregate Born-statistics tests* specifically; it is not undetectable in every register. The reorientation below shows where evidence *can* appear—at seams—and several of those predictions are operational enough to fail. A framework that says "look here, not there, and here is what would count against me at the seams" is constrained, not vacuous. What it forfeits is the easy falsifier: no clean ensemble-level anomaly can settle the matter, because the corridor predicted none from the start.

The second error is the believer's: "the interface is hidden exactly as a real-but-minimal interface would be, so its hiddenness confirms it." This is the slippage [[evidential-status-discipline|the evidential-status discipline]] exists to catch. That the evidence is absent *where the architecture predicts absence* is compatibility, not support. A framework earns support over its rivals only by naming a discriminator the rivals do not also predict—and "no aggregate signature" is predicted equally by the hypothesis that there is no interface at all. The hiddenness is therefore evidentially inert between the Map and eliminativism about the interface. The honest reading is the one the tenets page already takes: without seam-level discriminating evidence, "the tenet remains an interpretive proposal rather than a tested commitment."

The compatibility reading carries an operational rule, which keeps the discipline from sliding back into the believer's error under empirical pressure. An aggregate null result—a within-brain Born-statistics test that finds nothing, a micro-psychokinesis experiment that returns chance—counts as *neither* confirmation *nor* refutation of the strict corridor, because the corridor predicted the null from the start and its rivals predict it too. The single condition under which a null does evidential work, in either direction, is that a *quantitative prediction was preregistered*: a specific effect, at a specified conditioning grain, with a stated magnitude or bound, fixed before the data were seen. Absent that, the null is inert—it cannot be banked by the believer as "exactly the hiddenness the interface predicts," and it cannot be spent by the sceptic as "one more failed detection," because neither side staked anything falsifiable on the outcome. Hiddenness is compatibility; compatibility becomes evidence only through a bet placed in advance, at a seam, with a number attached.

The thesis therefore sits at the framework-internal architecture-naming tier: it tells the Map what shape its commitments would force the interface into and where the evidential programme should point, without moving the needle on whether the commitments are true. Left unstated, a self-concealment thesis drifts into "you can't disprove it, therefore it's plausible"; stated with its tier marked, it names the victories the Map may not claim.

## The Architecture's Five Consequences

If the interface conceals itself from bulk measurement, several features of the Map that have read as separate puzzles turn out to be consequences of one architecture, each a place where the corpus already moves toward seam-evidence.

**Strict-corridor invisibility** is the consequence at the level of physics, treated above. The interface leaves the ensemble distribution untouched; aggregate Born-statistics tests are blind to it by construction. This is the pivotal case, because it forecloses the obvious detection route and forces every other consequence to carry the evidential burden.

**Attention bandwidth** is the consequence at the level of throughput. If consciousness acts through [[apex/attention-as-causal-bridge|a narrow selection bridge]], its contribution enters where conscious behavioural throughput runs at roughly ten bits per second, against neural and sensory rates orders of magnitude higher. The figure measures what conscious agents *do*. Reading it as the interface's capacity needs the further premise that the reporting and influencing channels are one, which [[bandwidth-of-consciousness|the bandwidth data]] neither supply nor refute, and the tenet's minimality is fixed by the empirical record, not by a bandwidth. What the narrowness contributes is dilution: a channel this slow makes any conscious contribution easy to lose in the brain's classical traffic. Global-workspace accounts predict the same bottleneck, so this face fits the architecture without testing it.

**Introspection opacity** is the consequence at the level of self-report. [[apex/introspection-architecture-void-cluster|The introspection-architecture cluster]] establishes that consciousness receives reconstructed endpoints, not the operations that produce them, across five distinct registers—storytelling, source-tagging, gating, fabrication, reality-monitoring. The cluster draws the implication the self-concealing thesis needs: "if the interface leaves no phenomenal markers, the absence of a felt tag for mind-side causation is *expected*. A markerless interface is, from the subject's side, observationally indistinguishable from no interface." The confabulation engine retroactively supplies reasons for every action regardless of its true cause, which means the subject's own introspective report is the *last* place a conscious causal contribution would show up. Self-concealment extends from the third-person instrument to the first-person witness.

**Memory-channel vulnerability** is the consequence at the level of graded breakdown. [[memory-channel-interface-evidence|The memory-channel interface-evidence article]] reads a conjectured degradation order—autonoetic most vulnerable, noetic intermediate, anoetic most preserved, recurring with exceptions across anaesthesia, sleep, hemispherectomy, terminal lucidity, and dissociation—not as a decisive proof but as "the *shape* of evidence a multi-channel interface would predict, and a shape production theories seem to struggle to produce without ad-hoc fitting." This is the architecture showing itself precisely where the self-concealing thesis says it should: in the *order* in which channels fail when the interface is partially blocked, which no measurement of the intact ensemble shows. Recovery adds a second face, developed below: *which way the system is moving* when channels fail.

**The voids catalogue** is the consequence at the level of cognitive cartography. [[apex/taxonomy-of-voids|The taxonomy of voids]] finds over a hundred cognitive limits, roughly twenty of them framework-independent, clustering at the consciousness-matter interface, with the self-knowledge and introspective families densest. The catalogue's own audit already states the seam-level prediction the self-concealing thesis generalises: "if the tenet-package is correct, *introspective-channel disruption*, *attention disruption*, *memory degradation*, and *source-monitoring failure* should cluster together under conditions that disrupt the interface." Read this way, the voids map where a self-concealing interface would leave its only legible marks—at the edges where cognition fails, not in the middle where it runs smoothly. The taxonomy grants that production accounts can accommodate the co-occurrence, which discriminates only in its matched-direction detail.

The five are one architecture under five descriptions. A minimal post-decoherence selector, acting through a narrow bandwidth, reporting itself only through a reconstructive introspective channel, degrading channel-by-channel under partial blockade, and leaving its densest legible traces at cognitive seams—this is a single thing seen from physics, throughput, self-report, breakdown, and cartography. The unifying claim is that all five are faces of *concealment by construction*.

## Re-Orienting the Falsification Programme Around Seams

If the architecture is right about itself, the falsification programme has been looking in the wrong place whenever it looked for bulk physical traces. The [[falsification-roadmap-for-the-interface-model|falsification roadmap]] already distinguishes corridor, outside-corridor, and trumping readings of Tenet 2, and already notes that the strict corridor predicts no Born signature; the self-concealing thesis turns that scattered caution into a positive editorial instruction. The evidence that could ever bear on the interface lives at five kinds of seam.

**Disruption.** Perturb the interface and watch how function breaks, not whether the bulk signal shifts. [[apex/altered-states-as-interface-evidence|Altered states]]—anaesthesia, psychedelics, dreaming, hypnagogia—modulate specific interface components in predictable ways; the pattern of which features survive, degrade, or intensify is the datum, and it is a seam datum because it is visible only under perturbation.

**Dissociation.** [[apex/phenomenal-output-causal-machinery-dissociation|The phenomenal-output / causal-machinery dissociation]] shows phenomenal access reaching outputs but not the operations producing them, across perception, cognition, agency, and memory. A dissociation is a seam by definition: it is the gap between two things that the smooth case presents as one. The self-concealing interface predicts that the gap is where the architecture is legible.

**Graded channel failure.** The memory hierarchy is the worked exhibit: ordered degradation under partial blockade, a seam datum because no single measurement of intact memory reveals it—only the sequence of failures does. Its sharper face is that recovery does *not* simply retrace degradation. [[memory-channel-interface-evidence|Recovery-order asymmetry]] makes the *direction* of interface change part of the datum: the autonoetic channel returns last under rising substrate function (anaesthesia emergence) and is reported, in retrospective case narratives, to return first under falling substrate function (terminal lucidity), a divergence a substrate-symmetric production account does not predict from a single direction-blind vulnerability profile, though one willing to add direction-specific mechanisms per case can absorb it. The signature *constrains* production rather than *establishing* the filter reading. It belongs to a wider [[direction-of-interface-change|signature family]]—hysteresis of state transition, recovery-order reversal across mechanisms, abrupt dissociative reconnection versus gradual onset, direction-decoupled emergence timing—that no substrate-state-at-a-time description captures. Its members share one upstream architecture, so the [[common-cause-null|common-cause null]] credits them as one view, not four. The seam is sharp enough to have a design. A reversible perturbation read on both its up-ramp and its down-ramp—the [[targeted-lesion-discriminating-tests-between-production-and-filter-readings-of-the-memory-hierarchy|substrate-state]] and [[direction-dependent-discriminating-test-design|direction-axis]] designs—tests for equilibrated non-mirrored orderings, which static-threshold production forbids, and so could, if run, deliver more than the constraining force the convergence currently earns.

**Patient-population divergence.** [[apex/pharmacological-dissociation-as-evidence|Pharmacological dissociation]] and the neurological and clinical dissociation literatures supply contrasts—same target / opposite outcome, different targets / convergent outcome, same behaviour / different mechanism / different phenomenology—that a normal population averages away. The divergence between populations whose introspective architecture *differs* carries weight precisely because it cannot be running through shared neurotypical machinery, which is the structural defence the [[common-cause-null|common-cause null]] demands.

**First-person/third-person mismatch.** [[forward-in-time-conscious-selection|Forward-in-time selection]] names the cleanest instance: the asymmetry between the third-person measurability of a commitment-relevant signal and the first-person opacity of the closing-itself is "what an interface doing causal work at a primitive seam predicts: the cause cannot be observed from inside the system whose cause it is." Where the inside report and the outside instrument disagree, the disagreement is the data.

The reorientation sharpens falsifiability rather than softening it. Each seam admits genuine failure conditions. The memory hierarchy would be undercut by a population in which the degradation order reverses without an independent confounder; the recovery-order asymmetry by a controlled mirror-symmetric recovery result, or by a single substrate-state mechanism that derives both the rising-trajectory and falling-trajectory orderings without per-case additions; the dissociation cluster by clean single-mechanism derivation of all its faces; the patient-population divergences by demonstration that the contrasts run entirely through shared upstream architecture. What the programme forfeits is the fantasy of a single decisive ensemble anomaly. What it gains is a structured set of places where the architecture has committed itself to predictions it could lose, and in the up-ramp/down-ramp design one that could move a seam from constraining to discriminating.

A caution belongs here. Seam evidence is almost always *constraining* rather than *establishing*: a dissociation removes the simplest identity reading without by itself installing the interactionist one, because production accounts can usually accommodate any single seam with an extra mechanism or a parameter set after the fact. The force is cumulative and bounded. And because the seams share one upstream architecture, the [[common-cause-null|common-cause null]] applies in full: counting the five seam classes as five independent confirmations would inflate the weight, since if the Map is right they are five views of one thing. The honest accounting credits the convergence at the framework-internal-weight register and reserves tier-elevation for seams that supply a discriminator the rivals do not equally predict.

## The Testability Register: Conditional at the Seam, Not Aggregate

A tension runs through the corpus that this thesis forces into the open. The Map calls Minimal Quantum Interaction its most empirically tractable tenet, a claim [[falsification-roadmap-for-the-interface-model|the falsification roadmap]] scopes to the tenet's mechanism-committed readings, yet the architecture named here is aggregate-invisible by construction. The two claims belong to different registers. At the aggregate register the strict corridor forecloses testing, and the null-results rule above acknowledges the foreclosure. At the *conditional* register the tenet keeps empirical content: whether specifiable conditioning—on intention, task, subject, population, or the direction of substrate change—separates the outcome distribution from the unconditioned Born measure. For the corridor reading, testability means testability at the conditional/seam register; this synthesis commits to the conditional reading of testability, the only one the strict corridor leaves standing.

This is the same fork [[apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy|the Born-preserving causal-efficacy problem]] states formally as a trilemma. Its horn (a)—the conditionals differ for some specifiable context, so in-principle signatures exist, conditioned on intentions, tasks, or subjects—is exactly the register the seam programme occupies. The seam tests below are instances of horn (a): they are *conditional residual-structure* tests, not generic Born-frequency tests. None has yet been run on the brain-internal corridor: the preregistered intention-to-RNG micro-psychokinesis nulls target an external RNG, which [[brain-interface-boundary|brain-locality]] places outside the interface, so they were predicted either way. Committing to the conditional register names where the Map's empirical exposure actually sits, rather than retreating from testability, and concedes that horn (b), the conditionals never differing at any grain, is precisely the collapse into epiphenomenalism the whole programme stands exposed to. The seam predictions are the Map's wager that the world is on horn (a).

## Three Preregisterable Seam-Level Predictions

The reorientation has so far named seam *classes*; the discipline it owes is a few *specified* predictions, concrete enough to preregister so that a null could do the evidential work the rule above requires. Each of the three below, drawn from a seam the corpus already gestures at, states an effect direction, a measurement protocol, an expected magnitude or bound, the comparator a production account offers, and a likelihood-ratio reading. The recurring finding of external review is that P(E | Map) can be high while the programme rarely establishes P(E | Map) > P(E | best rival), because the seam patterns the Map cites are mostly also expected under production-based neuroscience. Each prediction therefore makes its likelihood ratio, and the exact condition under which it exceeds one, explicit rather than assumed.

**Prediction 1 — Direction-dependent memory-channel ordering under a reversible perturbation.** *Direction* (the Map's advance stake, not an entailment: the filter reading forbids no ordering): under a reversible anaesthetic ramp, the autonoetic channel, relative to the others, crosses its threshold at a higher substrate-function level on the falling-substrate leg than on the rising-substrate (emergence) leg. *Protocol:* within-subject induction and emergence ramps of a single agent (propofol), with autonoetic / noetic / anoetic recall probed along both legs; the datum is the sign of the direction × channel interaction, not any single threshold. *Magnitude / bound:* a nonzero interaction persisting in the [[direction-dependent-discriminating-test-design#what-each-reading-predicts-and-forbids|quasi-static, state-matched limit]], at measured, equilibrated substrate function. *Physicalist comparator:* the static-threshold production model predicts zero there and only there, since a direction-blind substrate whose channels lag unequally, a candidate for the single-mechanism failure condition above, gives a nonzero interaction against administered dose. *Likelihood ratio:* a single nonzero interaction is weakly discriminating—a production account can add a direction-specific mechanism for the one case—so the ratio exceeds one only where the *same sign recurs across mechanistically unrelated agents* (propofol, xenon, ketamine) in that limit, without per-case fitting; at finite rates, channel-intrinsic lags could repeat it under every agent. Even then it rules out only static-threshold and lag rivals, since multistable and emergence-circuit accounts survive it; a single-agent result leaves the ratio near one, by the [[common-cause-null|common-cause null]]'s own logic.

**Prediction 2 — Intention-conditioned residual structure surviving physical screening-off.** *Direction:* in a within-brain stochastic-gating paradigm—a near-random neural or perceptual event gating a behavioural choice—the outcome carries residual mutual information with a preregistered intention covariate that the unconditioned distribution does not show: I(outcome ; intention | measured physical state) > 0 while the marginal stays Born-exact. *Protocol:* preregister the intention operationalisation (a pre-cued goal fixed before each trial) and the physical-state covariate set; test whether conditioning on intention separates the outcome distribution *after* the measured physical state has been conditioned on. *Magnitude / bound:* the filter reading predicts a small conditional dependence bounded below current no-signalling and micro-psychokinesis sensitivity—present in the conditional, absent in the marginal; the production reading predicts zero conditional dependence once the physical state is included, because the physical state *screens off* the mental on any account that holds the physical to be causally complete. *Physicalist comparator:* screening-off is not an ad-hoc production manoeuvre but a firm commitment of causal completeness—which is what makes this the sharpest of the three, since the Map here presses a standard the physicalist already endorses. *Likelihood ratio:* a residual that survives one physical-state measurement is attributable by the production theorist to unmeasured physical variables, so P(E | Map) exceeds P(E | production) only in the limit where the residual *does not shrink* as physical-state measurement is made progressively more complete. A residual invariant under rising measurement completeness is what no screening-off account predicts; a one-shot residual leaves the ratio near one.

**Prediction 3 — Same-target, opposite-outcome pharmacological dissociation across introspective-architecture-divergent populations.** *Direction:* a single well-characterised molecular target, driven identically, yields opposite phenomenal-report outcomes in two populations distinguished by an independent introspective-architecture marker, and the divergence does not reduce to a measured downstream circuit difference. *Protocol:* preregister the target, the two populations, the architecture marker, and a mediation model; test whether a population × outcome interaction survives adjustment for the measured downstream neural state. *Magnitude / bound:* the filter reading predicts a residual population × outcome interaction *not* mediated by measured circuit state; the production reading predicts full mediation—same target, different circuit state, different output, with no residue once the circuit state is adjusted for. *Physicalist comparator:* full downstream mediation is the production account's structural defence, and it is exactly what the [[common-cause-null|common-cause null]] demands the Map rule out before it counts a population divergence as its own. *Likelihood ratio:* as with Prediction 2, P(E | Map) > P(E | production) only where the unmediated residual *survives increasingly complete mediation analysis*. A residual that shrinks toward zero as circuit-state measurement improves confirms production; only a residual that resists progressively complete mediation lifts the ratio above one.

The three share a structure. Each is built so that a *single* run is weakly discriminating at best—production can absorb any one seam with an extra parameter—and each turns discriminating only under a *convergence condition* the common-cause null does not grant for free: cross-mechanism invariance in the quasi-static limit for Prediction 1, invariance under rising measurement completeness for Predictions 2 and 3. The Map does not obtain P(E | Map) > P(E | best rival) from any single seam, nor by *counting* seams, since the seams share one architecture; it obtains it only where a seam's signature proves invariant under exactly the manipulation a production account expects to dissolve it—for lag, a slower ramp, not another agent. Preregistering these three, bounds and comparators fixed in advance, would let their nulls count and keep a positive result from being read past the ratio it has earned.

## What the Architecture Does Not Settle

The self-concealing interface is an account of *where* and *how invisibly* consciousness acts. It is silent on *why one outcome rather than another*. [[forward-in-time-conscious-selection|The selection-criterion trilemma]] is the gap it leaves open: if consciousness selects for internal reasons the dualism collapses into functionalism; if it selects randomly it contributes nothing; if it selects by a non-reducible phenomenal preference, an unanalysed primitive sits at the centre. The Map's route is the third, and [[selection-criterion-problem|the selection-criterion problem]] develops what that primitive owes. The two are complementary open structures—one names the architecture's invisibility, the other the primitive doing the selecting inside it—and neither dissolves the other; the architectural picture should not be mistaken for a mechanism of valuation it does not supply.

Nor does the thesis settle the deeper metaphysics. A markerless, statistics-neutral interface is what dualism would predict and what eliminativism about the interface predicts too; the case for the Map's commitments is made elsewhere, on grounds the self-concealing thesis neither strengthens nor weakens.

## The Practical Ontology the Architecture Names

The self-concealing interface is also a portrait of the kind of mind the Map is committed to: neither a Cartesian controller with transparent access to its own operations, nor a panpsychist ground present everywhere in the physical, nor an idle epiphenomenal witness, but a subject with bounded access, acting through a narrow selection channel prepared by brain architecture, whose causal contributions are, on the corridor reading, invisible to aggregate physical measurement and unmarked in first-person report. The interface hides from the instrument and from the self alike. Naming that practical ontology lets the Map's live position be distinguished from the broader dualisms it is often confused with.

## Evidence and Dependency

The architecture is inherited from Tenet 2's corridor reading and the empirical constraints the tenets page accepts, and holds only if a strict-corridor model satisfying them jointly exists, which [[positions/quantum-interface#^p-q10|P-Q10]] records as unbuilt. The equivalence between perfect concealment and zero third-person evidence rests on published steganography theorems; its mapping onto mental causation is the Map's own and carries the coherence-only grade. Three of the five faces are inherited from other syntheses—[[apex/attention-as-causal-bridge|attention]], [[apex/introspection-architecture-void-cluster|introspection]] and [[apex/taxonomy-of-voids|the voids catalogue]]—so their agreement here repeats support already credited there.
