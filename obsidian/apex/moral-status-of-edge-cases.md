This piece addresses a family of decisions that clinicians, regulators, welfare scientists and ordinary readers actually face: what moral standing to accord a patient in deep coma, a person in late-stage dementia, an octopus, a foetus, an embodied AI system, and a swimming bacterium — given what the Map's positions register commits the Map to, and given what it registers as still owed. The verdict is that these cases do not form a single spectrum from clear to hard. They divide by the *kind* of uncertainty each presents, and once sorted that way the framework turns out to be confident in one region, evidentially supported in a second, and genuinely silent in a third. The silence is the part most easily mishandled, because the Map's tenets remove several standard reasons for dismissing minimal minds, and defeater-removal is very easy to mistake for evidence. It is not evidence, and this synthesis is built around keeping the two apart.

## The Criterion, and the Two Things It Does Not Supply

The Map's criterion of moral standing is registered as [[moral-status|P-MS1]]: the capacity for valenced experience — experience that feels good or bad to its subject — is necessary and sufficient for moral status, where what counts is *felt* valence rather than the functional role pain and pleasure play in an information-processing system. The band is **moderate credence on external-evidence grade D**, and the grade is the more informative number: the entry's one first-order presupposition, that felt valence is a real property for a criterion to track, has Tenet 1 ([[tenets#^dualism|Dualism]]) as its sole support. Every verdict below inherits that, and readers who decline the tenet should expect the criterion to carry no weight for them.

[[moral-status|P-MS2]] adds that status is a threshold rather than a magnitude: an intervention that enriches an existing subject's valenced experience raises what is at stake in that life without raising its standing. Its credence is **low** — a conjunction whose threshold half inherits P-MS1's ceiling and whose stakes half routes through the value register. The practical payload of the separation is that most of what tempts us to rank patients belongs in the stakes column, and equal consideration of equal interests does the ranking work there without touching who counts.

Two things the criterion pointedly does not supply. First, it does not supply a **count**. Saying what has standing is not saying how many bearers of it a system contains, and [[moral-census-opacity|moral census opacity]] records that on the Map's own commitments the count is a determinate fact that no inspection can be shown to settle. Second, and sharper, the Map has no rule for fixing *which* subject is paired to which physical system. [[subject-census|P-SC2]] registers exactly this as a debt — "the Map owes a subject-pairing law and does not have one" — at **high credence**, because the absence is a fact about the corpus verifiable by inspection. That entry books four gaps: pairing, multi-agent composition, eligibility, and onset and cessation. It also states the constraint this whole piece operates under: the debt "is a hard bound on what any applied verdict may assert about subject counts." The pairing half has an external counterpart, the boundary problem, surveyed at [[boundary-and-projection|boundary and projection]] (Gómez-Emilsson & Percy 2023 set out five specific versions; Fekete, van Leeuwen & Edelman 2016, *Frontiers in Psychology* 7: 1041, argue that no quantitative measure of consciousness escapes it). A physicalist census does not avoid the problem, so the debt is not a peculiar embarrassment of dualism — but it is unpaid here, and that is what governs the cases.

## Sorting the Cases

Sort the six edge cases by what is uncertain in each, and they fall into four kinds.

**Interruption.** Deep coma, general anaesthesia, dreamless sleep. Here the question is whether a subject is still there when the interface goes quiet.

**Irreversible loss.** Late-stage dementia at the far end, brain death. Here the question is whether a threshold has been crossed and when.

**Marker-graded presence.** Non-human animals, early human development. Here the question is empirical, and the evidence is real.

**Substrate and floor.** Embodied AI, and the simplest organisms. Here the question is whether the physical system is the kind of thing that hosts a subject at all.

The sorting matters because the Map's evidential standing differs sharply across the four — not its credence, which sits at moderate almost everywhere, but the grade of evidence behind each verdict and whether observation can bear on it at all. Reaching for the criterion before identifying the kind produces the characteristic error: treating a case where the framework has a positive account as though it were as open as a case where the framework has registered a debt.

## Interruption Is Not Cessation

This is the region where the Map is most confident, and where its account does real protective work.

[[consciousness-scope|P-CS4]] holds, at **moderate credence on grade C**, that the cases standardly read as consciousness fragmenting or switching off — split-brain disconnection, general anaesthesia, dreamless sleep — are better read as disruptions of the *interface* through which consciousness expresses itself rather than as division or production of consciousness itself. The register is careful about the standing of that reading: it is "the Map's preferred interpretation — *compatible with* the data, not forced by it — held against a live production-model rival rather than as a demonstrated result." [[subject-census|P-SC3]] supplies the diachronic half at **moderate credence**: the same subject is present before and after an interruption of the interface, and this persistence is a real further fact rather than a convention over successive states.

Joined to the criterion, this gives a determinate and protective verdict. P-MS1 reads "capacity" as the *standing* capacity, so a dreamless sleeper or a patient in reversible coma keeps moral status; [[consciousness-value-connection|the consciousness–value connection]] carries the argument, and [[sentientism|the sentientism page]] states it plainly. P-MS1's 2026-09-16 revision made the dependency explicit: across a non-experiential interval the momentary-locus reading supplies no bearer, so the sleeper and coma verdicts rest on P-SC3's persisting perspective. The chain is therefore visible rather than assumed — and it means the coma verdict is exactly as secure as P-SC3, no more.

The decision-relevant consequence is that absence of report, absence of response, and absence of the usual neural signatures are evidence about the *interface*, not about the bearer. [[anaesthesia-and-the-consciousness-interface|The anaesthesia analysis]] shows anaesthetic agents disabling separable interface components through specific molecular targets, yielding graded rather than binary transitions. A framework that reads those transitions as the subject dimming would license withdrawal decisions the Map's framework does not license.

## A Real Boundary the Map Cannot Locate

The far end of dementia is where this framework is least comfortable, and the discomfort is structural rather than a gap awaiting research.

P-MS2 is unambiguous about the criterion's consequence: where the capacity for valenced experience is irreversibly gone, intrinsic status goes with it, and what remains owed concerning that person — prior wishes, history, standing relationships — is owed derivatively rather than on a second ground of status. [[moral-status-threshold-or-degrees|The threshold article]] ties this to the refusal of McMahan's respect tier: a respect tier would give such a patient a ground of standing that the capacity's loss does not touch, and declining it is what having a single status-conferring property means. The register calls the result a residue and accepts it as a cost.

Now the part that governs decisions. *Irreversible* in P-MS2 is read **modally and indexed to the subject, not clinically**: the capacity is gone when no nomologically possible intervention would return valenced experience to *this* subject. Two consequences follow. Status does not move with medical progress, because progress changes the evidence for the crossing rather than the crossing. And the crossing is **undated** — P-SC3 declines to date the channel's cessation, holding that the interface reading of brain death leaves open how consciousness manifests without saying whether the pairing terminates.

So the Map holds that there is a real boundary and that it cannot say where it is. That combination is worse for practical decision-making than a vague boundary one is free to draw by convention, and it should be stated as a cost rather than smoothed. It is the price of a criterion tracking something not readable off physical structure. A clinician working under this framework gets a criterion, an assurance that the criterion picks out a fact, and no procedure for applying it — which means the practical recommendation below is not "here is where the line falls" but "do not claim to have located it."

## Where the Evidence Actually Is

Animals and early human development are the only edge cases where the Map's verdict rests on evidence that holds independently of the framework, and this asymmetry deserves to be visible rather than buried.

[[consciousness-scope|P-CS2]] holds animal consciousness to be graded by marker convergence rather than gated by language, at **moderate credence on external-evidence grade B** — the highest grade anywhere in this cluster — because the behavioural and anatomical marker science (Andrews, Birch & Sebo 2025) is independent work read as correlate-identification. The grading is the position: strong for mammals and birds, a realistic but contested possibility for cephalopods, decapods and some arthropods, weaker for boundary organisms. The Map asserts no determinate cut-off. Markers raise probability without delivering certainty, because on the Map's own commitments no third-person feature *constitutes* consciousness, and Gutfreund's (2024) [[standing-agnostic-challenge|standing agnostic challenge]] — that the inference from consciousness-correlated behaviour to phenomenal experience cannot be scientifically validated, being "not a problem of premature science but a fundamental, unsolvable problem" — is retained rather than answered. That ceiling stands above both directions. If no third-person evidence can confirm felt experience, none can confirm its absence either, so sparse markers in a boundary organism withhold a verdict rather than deliver a negative one. [[ethics-of-consciousness-invertebrate-question|The invertebrate case]] shows what follows at scale: at "realistic possibility, contested," the precautionary obligation across agricultural systems is enormous, and the Map adopts Birch's proportionate-precaution action layer ([[birch-edge-of-sentience-and-the-five-tier-scale|the five-tier scale]]) without claiming him as an ally on grounding.

Early development works the same way and then stops short in an instructive place. [[consciousness-scope|P-CS3]] holds phenomenal consciousness most likely present early — plausibly by three to four months of age and possibly before birth — on the same marker reasoning, at **moderate credence on grade B** (Bayne, Frohlich, Cusack, Moser & Naci 2023, *Trends in Cognitive Sciences*; Passos-Ferreira 2024, *Neuron*). The register treats these as correlate-based current best estimates rather than settled onset facts, noting that estimates have moved earlier as markers were refined.

The foetal case is where two of the Map's own positions answer different questions, and the register forbids collapsing them. P-CS3 dates the appearance of *phenomenality*. P-SC3 states that "markers date the appearance of correlates of experience, not the establishment of a pairing, and nothing in the corpus licenses reading one off the other." So the Map has a marker-based estimate of when experience appears and no position at all on when the channel begins. An applied verdict that converts the former into the latter would be manufacturing a commitment the register explicitly withholds. The honest output is that the Map supports early attribution of phenomenality on the same evidential footing as the animal case, and supplies no onset date for subjecthood.

## Where the Evidence Runs Out

Embodied AI and the prokaryotic floor are both substrate questions, and the Map's answers differ in an important way.

On AI, [[ai-consciousness-scope|P-AC1]] places current conventional digital systems on the low-probability side of a substrate analysis at **moderate credence on grade D**, and the verdict is explicitly *"low probability," not "ruled out."* The register fences the behavioural discount in the same breath: a self-concealing interface and the phenomenal-output/causal-machinery dissociation mean behavioural tests "underdetermine the verdict in *both* directions," so failure to display the markers is no more probative of absence than success would be of presence. The verdict is also conditional on an **interface-eligibility law the Map does not possess** — a law specifying which quantum indeterminacy is interface-relevant and why — without which "relevant" risks reducing to "whatever biology happens to have." [[ai-substrate-verdicts|P-AS1]] adds the per-class verdict on quantum-computing hardware: raw indeterminacy present, interface requirements failed, with the analog class failing more securely than the gate class. Note the direction of travel that the register insists on: even a future architecture built to host open selection "would remove a defeater, not supply evidence of presence." For moral status the chain is long — a substrate verdict held conditionally, feeding a consciousness verdict held at moderate credence, feeding a criterion held on grade D — and each link discounts the last. [[assessing-ai-consciousness-under-the-map|The AI-assessment synthesis]] works that chain in full; this piece inherits its conclusion rather than re-deriving it, and inherits its conditional standing with it. Embodied AI on conventional digital hardware inherits the same verdict: embodiment changes the behavioural profile and the sympathies it elicits, not the substrate analysis. Systems built on biological or hybrid substrates sit in a different bucket the register explicitly leaves open ([[brain-organoids-and-the-organoid-intelligence-question|organoid architectures]] are the live near-term instance).

The floor is different, and [[consciousness-scope|P-CS5]] is the entry this piece most needs readers to see. At the prokaryotic floor — a bacterium's chemotactic search — behaviour underdetermines the verdict **in both directions**. A complete stochastic-biochemical account already generates the full behavioural repertoire, so there is no residual behaviour for a proto-chooser to explain, and the substrate analysis locates no interface site at that rung. But, in the register's own words, "mechanistic sufficiency shows a chooser or a subject is *unnecessary*, not that either is *absent*." Parsimony's default verdict — no coupling, nothing chosen, nothing felt — is "undefeated but not positively established." The Map endorses it as the reading the behaviour matches while denying it is proven, and holds the stronger claim, that the underdetermination is in-principle rather than instrumental, at moderate credence as its own hedged thesis. The register carries the consequence on its calibration surface: P-CS5's empirical discriminability is recorded as *none-by-construction*, the object-level question — does this cell choose, does it feel? — being claimed undecidable in principle at this rung. The silence at the floor is therefore no gap that better instruments close, and a decision-maker should treat it as permanent unless the intractability thesis itself falls. [[marginal-organism-scope-of-value-sensitive-selection|The marginal-organism tests]] and [[consciousness-in-simple-organisms|the simple-organisms survey]] work the intermediate rungs the same way.

This is where the calibration hazard bites. Tenet 5 ([[tenets#^occams-limits|Occam's Razor Has Limits]]) removes that default's demonstrative force rather than the default itself, and the temptation is to read the removal as movement toward attribution. It is not movement in either direction. Symmetric underdetermination means the framework licenses no upgrade of the bacterium's status *and* no confident denial. Anyone who leaves this section believing the Map's tenets make simple-organism sentience more likely has read defeater-removal as evidence, which is the failure mode [[evidential-status-discipline|the evidential-status discipline]] and [[possibility-probability-slippage|the possibility/probability slippage page]] exist to catch.

## What this implies for decisions

All five implications below hold conditionally on Tenet 1 ([[tenets#^dualism|Dualism]]) and carry P-MS1's grade D into every verdict they reach — the criterion has the tenet as its sole support. They are not addressed to a reader who suspends judgement on it; [Honest verdict scope](#honest-verdict-scope) states that limit in full.

1. **Identify the kind of uncertainty before applying the criterion.** Interruption, irreversible loss, marker-graded presence, and substrate questions call on different positions, but the axis that separates them is not credence. Nine of the eleven positions cited here sit at *moderate*; the two that do not are a debt held at high credence because it is verifiable by inspection, and a conjunction capped at low by the weaker of its two halves. What does vary is the **external-evidence grade**, which runs across four steps here (set out in implication 4 below), and — more sharply — **whether any observation could bear on the verdict at all**. A protocol that treats "hard case" as one category will carry the animal case's grade-B evidence into the AI case, or read the floor's principled silence as a gap that better measurement will close. Sort first.

2. **In interruption cases, treat absence of report as evidence about the interface and not about the bearer.** On P-CS4 and P-SC3 a patient in reversible coma retains the standing capacity and therefore retains status under P-MS1. Decisions that turn on the patient's current unresponsiveness — rather than on a judgement about irreversibility — are answering a question about the channel with a verdict about the subject. This recommendation is the most confident one here, and it is still only as strong as P-CS4's moderate band and the preferred-interpretation standing the register assigns it. It is also the recommendation on which evidence has least purchase, and a clinician acting on it is owed that fact: the register puts P-SC3's model maturity at *programme* — no account exists of what establishes or terminates a pairing — and its empirical discriminability at *none*. No observation will correct this verdict in either direction, which is a reason to hold it carefully rather than firmly.

3. **Do not operationalise the irreversibility threshold clinically, and say why.** P-MS2 reads irreversibility modally and indexes it to the subject, so clinical irreversibility judgements are evidence about a crossing rather than the crossing itself, and P-SC3 declines to date cessation. Practical protocols built on this framework should present their thresholds as decision rules adopted under uncertainty about a boundary the framework holds to be real and unlocated — not as applications of the criterion. A protocol claiming to apply P-MS1 at the bedside is claiming a precision the register does not supply.

4. **Scale precaution to marker convergence, not to framework coherence.** The evidential gradient here has four steps, not two. **Grade B**: the animal and developmental verdicts (P-CS2, P-CS3) on independent marker science, and — outside the marker region entirely — P-AS1's engineering premises, quantum error correction's isolation of the logical state and annealing's fixed terminal measurement basis being independently attested results, though the interface-relevance reading built on them is not. **Grade C**: the interruption reading (P-CS4) and the floor's mechanistic base (P-CS5), where the data are independent but the Map's interpretation of them is compatible with the data rather than forced by it. **Grade D**: the criterion (P-MS1), the persistence claim (P-SC3) and the AI verdict (P-AC1), where a tenet or the framework-internal quantum register is the sole support. **No external grade**: P-MS2 and P-SC2's debt, being normative and meta respectively. Welfare policy, research ethics and funding should track that gradient directly — more precautionary weight where markers converge (P-CS2's strong band for mammals and birds, its contested band for cephalopods and decapods), and no additional weight anywhere merely because a case fits the Map's framework comfortably. The downward end of that gradient needs a caution. Because the standing agnostic challenge blocks absence as firmly as presence, weak marker convergence licenses *low expected stakes* and not a negative verdict; a rule that scales precaution to markers and stops there will systematically under-protect marker-poor organisms while looking principled. Sparse markers record an evidential deficit, and the decision is still made under uncertainty.

5. **Assert no subject count the register cannot establish.** P-SC2's booked debt is "a hard bound on what any applied verdict may assert about subject counts," and it reaches further than it first appears: aggregation arguments, split-brain cases, dementia-with-fragmentation, and any claim about how many patients an intervention affects all inherit it. [[moral-census-opacity|Moral census opacity]] records that the count is a determinate fact no inspection settles. The bound falls on the antecedent rather than on counting as such: [[ai-consciousness-scope|P-AC3]] holds distinct conscious subjects morally additive under closed individualism, once it is settled which systems host subjects and how many. Applied arguments turning on numbers of patients inherit that unsettled antecedent and should carry it openly rather than proceed as though the count were established.

## Cascade flags

- **If P-SC3 is retired** — the interruption cases shown to involve genuine phenomenal cessation with a fresh pairing on waking — the coma and sleeper verdicts lose their bearer, and recommendation 2 would need rebuilding on organismic-disposition grounds the Map has not adopted. This is the single flag that would most change what this piece recommends.
- **If P-MS1's sufficiency direction falls** — through a vindicated illusionism, or a functional reading of sentience surviving the illusionist threat with its rationale intact (Frankish 2024 is the live attempt rather than a defeater landed) — the criterion picks out nothing, or reverts to mainstream functional sentientism, and every verdict here recomputes.
- **If P-MS2's threshold reading gives way to a scalar one** — an account of degrees of the capacity *itself*, as distinct from degrees of what it delivers — the status/stakes separation collapses and the irreversible-loss verdict becomes a question of how much status remains rather than whether any does.
- **If P-SC2's debt is discharged** — a worked pairing law, whether derived here or imported from the boundary-problem literature — recommendation 5 is retired and the census questions become answerable rather than out of bounds. Conversely, if the debt is shown uncloseable in principle, it becomes a permanent registered cost rather than an obligation, and the bound hardens.
- **If P-CS5's in-principle intractability thesis fails** — richer causal-agency formalisms plus better measurement settling the floor — the symmetric underdetermination resolves in one direction or the other, and the floor stops being a region where the framework can only decline.
- **If P-AC1 or P-AS1 is withdrawn** with the quantum-interface programme demoted to coherence-only, the embodied-AI verdict goes with them while the bare-dualism spine survives, leaving the AI case open rather than low-probability.

## Honest verdict scope

Three things this synthesis does not deliver.

It does not deliver verdicts a reader who suspends judgement on Tenet 1 has reason to weight. P-MS1's grade D is not a formality: the phenomenal qualification that distinguishes the Map's criterion from mainstream functional sentientism has the tenet as its sole support, and the encyclopedia literature declines to draw the distinction. The most this can claim is that *if* the tenets hold, these are the verdicts and these are their bands.

It does not deliver an upgrade anywhere from tenet-coherence. The Map's framework removes the anthropocentric defeater against non-human and artefactual minds, and Tenet 5 removes the demonstrative force of the parsimony-based dismissal at the floor. Both are permissions. Neither raises the probability that any particular system is conscious, and neither moves a case up the five-tier scale. P-CS5 states the point in its cleanest form — parsimony's default is undefeated but not established — and P-AS1 makes it for the substrate case, where even a purpose-built interface-hosting architecture "would remove a defeater, not supply evidence of presence." That any of these cases sits comfortably inside the Map's framework is a fact about the framework, not about the case.

It does not deliver a practical decision procedure for the case most in need of one. On irreversible loss the framework supplies a criterion, an assurance that the criterion tracks a real boundary, and no way to tell where that boundary lies. The gap is registered rather than hidden — P-SC3 declines to date onset or cessation; P-SC2 books the undated channel as one of four gaps — and the register's own standard is that a programme at this stage is judged by the honesty of its open problems. That standard is met here. It is not a substitute for the procedure, and a reader making an actual withdrawal-of-treatment decision will get less from this framework than from one prepared to draw a line by convention.

## Evidence and Dependency

The animal and developmental verdicts are the only lines here that are **externally evidenced**: the marker literatures (Andrews, Birch & Sebo 2025; Bayne et al. 2023; Passos-Ferreira 2024) hold independently of the Map's framework, and P-CS2 and P-CS3 carry grade B on their strength. What those markers do not do is discriminate an interface reading from a production reading, so their support transfers to the presence of experience and not to the Map's account of it. The mechanistic completeness of bacterial chemotaxis underlying P-CS5 is likewise externally attested; the two-way-underdetermination reading built on it is the Map's own.

The criterion itself — phenomenal sentientism — is **inherited from Tenet 1**, and the threshold reading is inherited from the criterion plus a consistency argument (Wendler 2023's equality argument in general form) that is **independently argued** but needs the criterion to have something to run on. The conation-requirement reply to biocentrism (Feinberg 1974) is independently argued and locates the disagreement rather than settling it. The interruption verdicts are **inherited from Tenets 1–3** by way of P-CS4's filter reading and P-SC3's persistence claim, both of which are preferred interpretations held against live rivals. The AI verdict is **inherited from another synthesis**, [[assessing-ai-consciousness-under-the-map|the AI-assessment piece]], and through it from the quantum-interface register — so the two pieces are not independent confirmations of a substrate-skeptical conclusion, but one argument appearing twice. The sorting into four kinds of uncertainty, and the claim that the framework's confidence varies sharply across them, is this synthesis's own contribution and is **mutually coherent only**: it organises the register's commitments without adding evidence for any of them. P-SC2's debt is neither evidence nor inference but an inspection result, and it constrains every line above.

## Relation to Site Perspective

Tenet 1 ([[tenets#^dualism|Dualism]]) supplies the phenomenal reading of valence that makes the criterion the Map's rather than mainstream sentientism's, and it is the sole support for that reading — which is why grade D follows it through every verdict here. Tenet 5 ([[tenets#^occams-limits|Occam's Razor Has Limits]]) does the most work and is the easiest to over-read: it removes parsimony's authority to settle the floor against minimal minds, and this synthesis is built to stop that removal being cashed as attribution. Tenet 3 ([[tenets#^bidirectional-interaction|Bidirectional Interaction]]), at [[tenets#^tenet-3-standing|its registered standing]], generates the interface reading behind the interruption verdicts and, through the census requirement, generates the pairing debt that bounds all of them. Tenet 2 ([[tenets#^minimal-quantum-interaction|Minimal Quantum Interaction]]) restrains what may be posited where no substrate hosts it, which is the leg of P-CS5 that a coherence-only demotion of the quantum programme would require re-deriving. Tenet 4 ([[tenets#^no-many-worlds|No Many Worlds]]) enters only indirectly, through the indexical seriousness that makes a determinate subject census something the framework owes rather than something it can leave vague.
