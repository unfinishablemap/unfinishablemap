Pharmacology is in an unusual epistemic position with respect to consciousness. A drug binds to a known receptor, modulates a known signalling pathway, and produces a phenomenological outcome the patient can later report. The chain from molecule to experience is short by neuroscience's standards, and the contrasts between drugs are sharp: two compounds acting on the same receptor produce different conscious states; two compounds acting on different receptors produce the same one; two compounds producing the same behavioural depth deliver different first-person interiors. Each contrast is a dissociation, and dissociations are the form of evidence the Unfinishable Map's interface model is best positioned to use.

What the catalogue has lacked is a piece that lifts pharmacological dissociation into a named evidence pattern at the methodology grain. The [[anaesthesia-and-the-consciousness-interface|anaesthesia article]] carries three distinct dissociation contrasts in its body, each doing different argumentative work, but the pattern they exhibit together is not specified at any single location. The [[neurological-dissociations-as-interface-architecture|neurological dissociations]], [[clinical-dissociation-as-systematic-evidence|clinical dissociations]], and [[contemplative-pathology-and-interface-malfunction|contemplative pathology]] clusters each reach the same architecture by independent routes, and the [[altered-states-as-interface-evidence|altered-states synthesis]] gathers six state-modalities into a convergence matrix—but none of these names what pharmacological dissociation *as a class* asks of the interface model, what it can and cannot do, and what discipline keeps its evidential weight calibrated.

What follows names three pharmacological-dissociation classes, supplies the anaesthetic exhibit for each, locates the matching patterns in the parallel clusters, and installs the [[evidential-status-discipline|evidential-status discipline]] that keeps the convergence from inflating beyond what the evidence licenses. This is a discipline about how the Map identifies dissociation patterns as evidence for interface architecture rather than a tenet-specific commitment, and it runs on one insight: *which* drugs dissociate from *which* reveals more about the interface than what any single drug does.

## What a Pharmacological Dissociation Is

A pharmacological dissociation is a contrast between two drug effects that separates components ordinary observation does not. The structure has three terms: a pharmacological variable (target receptor, mechanism, behavioural depth), a phenomenological observation (presence, content, access, encoding, temporal binding, ownership), and a contrast between two drug regimes that varies one term while holding another fixed. When the held-fixed term predicts the same outcome on a single-channel model and the actual outcome diverges, the divergence localises a component the single-channel model failed to anticipate.

The discipline matters because pharmacology is full of correlations that are not dissociations. Anaesthetic potency correlates with lipid solubility (the [[anaesthesia-and-the-consciousness-interface|Meyer-Overton correlation]]): a continuous relationship, historically read as evidence for a single nonspecific membrane mechanism. The interface model gets no traction from continuous correlations, only from contrasts where the structure predicts a unified outcome that fails to obtain. Three such contrast structures recur, each a different way of prying a component apart.

## Class A: Same Target, Opposite Outcome

**The structure.** Two drugs act on the same primary receptor; their phenomenological outcomes diverge in direction (not just magnitude). The receptor-level commonality predicts shared phenomenology under any model that locates consciousness in the receptor's activity. Divergent phenomenology localises a component the receptor itself does not exhaust.

**The anaesthetic exhibit: xenon-ketamine.** Both xenon and ketamine are NMDA receptor antagonists. Both render patients behaviourally unresponsive at anaesthetic doses. The reported outcomes diverge sharply: xenon leaves no reportable experience on waking—no dreams, no sense of time—while ketamine patients report vivid first-person content, with elaborate dream-like journeys and perceived encounters. Sarasso et al. (2015) measured the underlying neural patterns: propofol and xenon converge on slow-wave collapse; ketamine retains wakefulness-like complex activation despite matched unresponsiveness. A leading explanation of the divergence is xenon's additional action on two-pore potassium channels, a second site ketamine does not engage. Class C's lesson (below) applies here too: drugs can sever memory encoding while experience continues, so absence of report on waking does not settle absence of experience, and the xenon arm is partly an encoding question.

**What the contrast establishes.** Reportable interior and behavioural responsiveness are pharmacologically separable, and primary-receptor classification does not predict phenomenology. That conclusion does not require the Map's interactionist version of structured components; sophisticated functionalism predicts the same modular dissociation. What the contrast forecloses is the simplest reading—identity of consciousness with receptor activity at the implicated site—leaving the live question of what is decomposing: separable subsystems that constitute consciousness, or separable channels connecting consciousness to brain mechanisms of content generation and access.

**Parallel patterns across the catalogue.** [[neurological-dissociations-as-interface-architecture|Blindsight and pain asymbolia]] exhibit the same structure at the neural level: the affective-motivational dimension can be abolished while sensory-discriminative content survives, and conversely the sensory channel can be destroyed while affective machinery remains. The pair separates sensory from affective channels, which are different components from those xenon-ketamine separates, so what recurs across lesion and drug is selective separability itself rather than one shared component list.

## Class B: Different Targets, Convergent Outcome

**The structure.** Two drugs act on mechanistically distinct molecular targets and produce the same phenomenological outcome. The mechanistic distinctness rules out a single-pathway explanation; the convergence localises a higher-level component that multiple pathways can reach.

**The anaesthetic exhibit: propofol-xenon.** Propofol acts via GABA-A receptors; xenon via NMDA receptor antagonism plus two-pore potassium channels. Two receptor systems with no shared primary target. Both abolish reportable experience. Sarasso et al. (2015) showed neural patterns differ strikingly—propofol produces a low-amplitude, spatially local slow wave; xenon a high-amplitude, spatially global one—yet the reported outcome is the same. That convergence is consistent with one shared component, neural or interface, reachable by several molecular routes. The same study's headline result is the counter-datum: a single complexity measure (PCI) stayed high under ketamine and fell under propofol and xenon, tracking reportable experience across three agents and two primary receptor systems. That is what a unified neural correlate predicts, and it does not discriminate the interface reading from its rival.

**The shared-emergence extension.** Hu et al. (2023) found, in mice, that ubiquitin-driven degradation of the KCC2 cotransporter in the ventral posteromedial thalamus is a common emergence mechanism across propofol, pentobarbital, isoflurane, and ketamine—on the authors' own grouping three GABA-A-acting agents and one NMDA antagonist, a two-class span rather than a four-target one. Their endpoints were behavioural: the righting reflex and a behavioural scale on which the anaesthetised animal sits at a "minimum responsive state". Because ketamine is among the four, and Class A holds that ketamine leaves a reportable interior intact, what KCC2 restores is responsiveness rather than phenomenal presence: different routes abolish responsiveness, and one shared mechanism restores it. That fits the lesson of Classes A and C, that responsiveness and experience come apart, and gives no warrant for reading the pathway as a phenomenal channel reopening—a gloss [[active-reboot|active reboot]] declines to treat as established.

**What the contrast establishes.** A component reachable from multiple mechanistic routes has the structural shape of a real architectural feature rather than a single-pathway artefact. The shared emergence mechanism imposes no special cost on materialism: Hu et al. themselves describe emergence as active and controllable, and active emergence through arousal and thalamocortical circuits is mainstream neuroscience.

**Parallel patterns across the catalogue.** The [[clinical-dissociation-as-systematic-evidence|clinical dissociation cluster]] supplies the strongest non-pharmacological version, but it carries an internal discount that disciplines how it is read. DID, depersonalisation-derealisation, dissociative amnesia, and conversion disorder reach the same channel-level architecture as anaesthesia and neurological lesions, but through *functional* connectivity disruption rather than structural damage or receptor pharmacology—and what they sever is felt access and ownership, not the underlying integration: DID patients reporting inter-identity amnesia recognised the other identity's material better than simulators did (Marsh et al. 2021), which the authors read as information that may remain available for retrieval while metacognitive access decouples. The four clinical windows do *not* count as four independent confirmations: three of them (DPDR as imbalanced interoceptive precision, conversion as an aberrant motor prior, surviving dissociative amnesia as inhibited retrieval) are redescribable as precision-weighting pathologies in one active-inference framework, with DID's cross-state geometry the member that most resists the precision framing. The convergence that carries weight therefore runs *across* perturbation classes, not within the clinical cluster.

That cross-route convergence is what the [[epistemology-of-convergence-arguments#compound-signatures-convergence-without-a-shared-mechanism|epistemology of convergence arguments]] names a *compound signature*: agreement among mechanistically disjoint routes, so it cannot be one upstream cause sampled three times. Pharmacology is one route of that signature. The siblings are structural ([[neurological-dissociations-as-interface-architecture|lesion]], with [[anarchic-hand-and-action-ownership|anarchic hand]] as the disowned-action exhibit) and *psychogenic*—the latter's clearest case being [[conversion-disorder-as-consciousness-side-fault|conversion disorder]], where the affected limb moves normally under anaesthesia or hypnosis yet not under awake directed volition, excluding "the perturbation broke the substrate" without drug or lesion. That leg carries a discount its own clinical literature insists on: contemporary functional-neurological-disorder work reads the mechanism as *overactive* rather than missing. Edwards et al.'s (2012) Bayesian model treats the symptom as an abnormally precise motor prior the system fulfils, and Voon et al. (2010) found heightened amygdala-to-supplementary-motor-area influence rather than a severed connection. Conversion disorder still shows that volitional motor output can be abolished with the substrate intact; what it no longer shows is a channel going offline. The calibration applies to the signature as a whole: it earns the *architecture* robustly and leaves the *interface reading* of it a live hypothesis.

## Class C: Same Behavioural Depth, Different Mechanism, Different Phenomenology

**The structure.** Two drugs produce matched behavioural depth—comparable apparent sedation, unresponsiveness, or task impairment—but their underlying mechanisms differ and their first-person interiors diverge. The matched behaviour predicts matched phenomenology on any view that takes behaviour as a proxy for consciousness. Divergent phenomenology under matched behaviour exposes the proxy as inadequate and puts the weight on the underlying mechanism.

**The anaesthetic exhibit: ketamine-midazolam at sub-anaesthetic doses.** Ketamine (NMDA antagonist) and midazolam (benzodiazepine, GABA-A positive modulator) at sub-anaesthetic doses can each leave patients responsive and capable of conversation and simple tasks. From that shared surface the phenomenological outcomes, drawn from the separate clinical literatures on each agent, diverge. Ketamine at this depth often preserves vivid experiential content with dissociative quality. Midazolam produces profound amnesia with minimal sedation, isolating memory encoding almost surgically: a patient may converse, follow instructions, and hold content in working memory across seconds—then form no lasting memory of the interval at all. Experiential content survives in both cases; what diverges is the encoding channel. The same behavioural surface conceals different interface configurations.

**What the contrast establishes.** Behavioural-level matching is not a reliable proxy for interface-level matching. Phenomenal presence (intact), access (intact), and memory encoding (severed) can hold simultaneously, and the behavioural surface tells you nothing about which. Memory encoding is thus a separable channel that pharmacology can isolate while the rest of the architecture keeps running.

**Parallel patterns across the catalogue.** [[contemplative-pathology-and-interface-malfunction|Contemplative pathology]] supplies the most direct Class C analogue. Practitioners who reach the *dukkha nanas* or other contemplative crises keep behavioural function—they can speak, navigate, perform daily tasks—while their interior phenomenology fragments: temporal binding breaks, ownership dissolves, phenomenal flooding strips ordinary filters. The contrast structure matches ketamine-midazolam: matched surface, distinct mechanism (sustained voluntary attention versus pharmacological agent), distinct phenomenology. Behavioural depth is an interface-coarse measure, and any intervention reaching the architecture at finer grain than behaviour will produce Class C dissociations.

## The Cumulative Convergence

The three classes are not independent. Anaesthesia is the catalogue's strongest worked exhibit precisely because all three operate together in one article body. The xenon-ketamine contrast (Class A), the propofol-xenon contrast with KCC2 emergence (Class B), and the ketamine-midazolam contrast (Class C) co-occur in the same pharmacological literature, on the same clinical populations, with overlapping receptor systems—and between them they separate three components: reportable interior, behavioural responsiveness, and memory encoding. Temporal binding and ownership enter only through the non-pharmacological routes.

All these components belong to one layer, and naming which one bounds the whole methodology. The [[mind-matter-interface|two-layer architecture]] separates Layer 1—the constraining relation that explains why brain-consciousness correlation need not be production—from Layer 2, the selection mechanism by which consciousness would act on neural outcomes. Every contrast in the three classes is a Layer 1 observation. Pharmacology maps the channels through which consciousness is constrained and reaches nothing about the mechanism by which it would do anything, which is why the quantum-interaction commitments below stay at a lower tier than the architectural ones.

What this convergence does evidentially is constrained by the [[evidential-status-discipline]]. Each dissociation, taken alone, *constrains* the simplest identity reading—the one that locates consciousness in a single mechanism, pathway, or receptor's activity—without *establishing* the Map's interactionist alternative. Sophisticated functionalist readings fit the same data: separable subsystems whose coupling constitutes consciousness, rather than separable channels connecting an already-unified consciousness to brain mechanisms. Adjudication requires evidence the dissociations alone cannot supply.

What the convergence raises is the *explanatory cost on absorption*. The materialist position that accommodates pharmacological dissociations must specify which connectivity patterns generate consciousness, why those patterns and not neighbouring ones, and why behavioural-level matching fails to track first-person matching. Each accommodation is available; the cumulative cost across classes raises the bar on what a fully spelled-out materialism must explain.

The convergence with non-pharmacological evidence adds to the effect, though less than it first appears. [[neurological-dissociations-as-interface-architecture|Neurological lesions]] split sensory from affective channels, [[clinical-dissociation-as-systematic-evidence|clinical dissociation]] severs felt access and ownership without structural damage, and [[contemplative-pathology-and-interface-malfunction|contemplative pathology]] loosens temporal binding and ownership. Each perturbation class separates *some* components, and the sets overlap only partly. What replicates across classes that share no mechanism is selective separability with partial overlap, a weaker pattern than one architecture recurring, and one any modular theory also predicts. The independence that carries weight is *between* classes, since within the functional-disconnection class one precision-weighting mechanism may be read several times (see the clinical-cluster discount above). The [[epistemology-of-convergence-arguments#compound-signatures-convergence-without-a-shared-mechanism|epistemology of convergence arguments]] explains why disjointness matters—a compound signature pre-empts the common-cause objection only where no shared upstream cause exists—and where its reach stops: it earns the separability but not the architecture's dualist significance. The [[altered-states-as-interface-evidence|altered-states synthesis]] supplies a complementary systems-level matrix.

The [[interface-specification-programme|interface specification programme]] reads this convergence as specification work: each route adds resolution to where the interface has components, how they fail, and how it reconfigures. The claim the methodology licenses is a modest one — *partially overlapping component-structures recur across routes, and the convergence raises the explanatory cost on the unified-substrate alternative* — rather than the inference from dissociations to dualism it is sometimes mistaken for.

## Calibration Discipline

The [[evidential-status-discipline]] governs what each dissociation can establish. The Map's tenets *permit* the interactionist reading of pharmacological dissociation, but permission removes a defeater rather than supplying positive evidence. Two claims need keeping apart. That consciousness-related brain function has separable, selectively disruptable parts is well supported. That those parts are *interface* channels—consciousness ontologically distinct, connecting through quantum-sensitive molecular systems—is a serious possibility but not established. Pharmacology constrains the simplest identity readings without establishing the Map's alternative.

Dissociation arguments climb a chain of claims that must not be collapsed. Each contrast shows that components come apart in the data, and contributes to showing that the dissociated features are genuine features of experience rather than artefacts of report, though the xenon case shows how hard that step is when report is what the drug disrupts. Whether the components are mechanistically independent the receptor contrasts approach but do not settle, since sophisticated functionalism can posit separable subsystems whose coupling constitutes consciousness. Metaphysical irreducibility lies beyond pharmacology entirely and is carried by separate arguments (the hard problem, self-stultification).

Two sources have applied the same discipline against the Map's own interest. [[filter-theory|Filter theory]] has withdrawn the bandwidth argument—roughly ten bits per second of conscious throughput against some 10⁹ bits per second of sensory capacity (Zheng and Meister 2025)—as a discriminator, because global workspace theory makes an access bottleneck its defining feature. [[interface-friction|Interface friction]] concedes that choking under self-monitoring (Beilock and Carr 2001; Gröpel and Mesagno 2019; Smoulder et al. 2024) is an established motor-control result the friction reading redescribes rather than predicts. A phenomenon a framework accommodates gracefully is not thereby one it uniquely predicted. The friction page's organising rule survives: where the interface is strained, its seams show, and the seams are the data.

The discipline is symmetric. The materialist must not treat a known molecular mechanism for each anaesthetic as if it *established* the reduction; receptor pharmacology is a necessary condition for an explanation of consciousness, not a sufficient one. The dualist must not treat the dissociation pattern as if it *established* the interface reading; sophisticated functionalism predicts the same pattern.

Where pharmacology does positive disambiguating work, it does so through cases like the ketamine self-stultification argument. Patients later reporting vivid ketamine experiences show consciousness kept its causal connection to memory while severed from behavioural output; were it epiphenomenal during that state, the reports would be [[self-stultification-as-master-argument|self-stultifying]], presupposing the causal efficacy epiphenomenalism denies. What pharmacology supplies here is a specific clinical case to which the master argument applies with unusual precision — the ketamine interior is reportable in a clinically reliable way, and the reportability is itself evidence of causal connection to memory consolidation. The force is local to the case, and it separates interactionism from epiphenomenalism only: functionalists also hold consciousness causally efficacious.

## What Pharmacological Dissociation Cannot Tell Us

The methodology has limits the discipline names rather than hides. Four matter.

First, anaesthetic pharmacology is incomplete. Current anaesthetics were discovered empirically, so interface components not targeted by any known drug stay invisible to the dissection. The components current pharmacology *can* pry apart are not a complete list; a complete architecture may have components the existing drug repertoire never reaches.

Second, the necessary-vs-where distinction matters. Pharmacological evidence shows specific molecular systems are necessary for the interface to function. It does not show these systems are *where* consciousness connects—some may be interface infrastructure rather than interface points. The KCC2 pathway is necessary for behavioural emergence in mice; whether the pathway is the interface or the mechanism that prepares conditions for an interface elsewhere to re-engage is a question the pharmacology cannot decide alone.

Third, everything the three classes deliver is Layer 1. The dissociations establish that the constraining relation has separable components; they say nothing about the selection mechanism, nor about what the interface *is*.

Fourth, a negative case marks where dissociation evidence stops paying. [[locked-in-syndrome-as-the-negative-case-where-filter-loosening-does-not-apply|Locked-in syndrome]] severs the descending arm of the interface at the pons while leaving the ascending arm and consciousness untouched: experience is neither widened nor degraded, only disconnected from motor expression. A reading that treated every interface perturbation as filter-modulation would expect something here and gets nothing, because there is no receptive constraint being relaxed. The three classes pry components apart; they do not license the further inference that disrupting the interface generally does anything interesting to the width or intensity of experience.

## The Methodology Stated Once

Pharmacological dissociation furnishes evidence for a structured, multi-channel consciousness-brain interface in three classes:

- **Class A** (same target, opposite outcome) constrains identity readings within a single receptor system, separating interface components the receptor itself does not exhaust.
- **Class B** (different targets, convergent outcome) constrains single-pathway readings, localising components that multiple mechanistic routes can reach, with one shared mechanism restoring responsiveness.
- **Class C** (same behavioural depth, different mechanism, different phenomenology) constrains behavioural-proxy readings, exposing the interior decoupling that behavioural surface measures cannot detect.

All three operate simultaneously in the anaesthesia exhibit and find partial parallels in the neurological-lesion, clinical-dissociation, and contemplative-pathology clusters, the cumulative force coming from selective separability recurring under perturbations that share no mechanism. The discipline holds each class at the *constrain* level, treats separable architecture as well supported, and keeps the Map's interactionist reading of it a serious but unestablished possibility. Its value is not in lifting the conclusion higher but in specifying *what work each class can do, what it cannot, and which convergences add discriminating weight*—and in naming the failure mode it is vulnerable to: the slippage from defeater-removal to evidence-elevation when a dissociation pattern is presented as if it had positively supported the interactionist version of the structure.

## Evidence and Dependency

The dissociation data this synthesis runs on are externally evidenced and hold whatever one makes of the Map: Sarasso et al.'s complexity measurements, the xenon/ketamine and ketamine/midazolam clinical contrasts, Hu et al.'s KCC2 emergence work, Marsh et al. on cross-identity recognition transfer, the functional-neurological-disorder imaging. None of them discriminates the interface reading from a sophisticated functionalist one.

The three-class taxonomy is independently argued: it needs only that a contrast holding one term fixed while varying another can localise a component—no tenet supplies that, and none is required for it. Reading the resulting components as *interface* channels rather than as subsystems whose coupling constitutes consciousness is inherited from Tenet 1; the quantum gloss on the molecular boundary is inherited from Tenet 2, minus the anaesthetic-microtubule route this synthesis declines to recruit. The bidirectional reading of the emergence pathway is mutually coherent only — that pathway is neural throughout. All are flagged where they appear.

The compound-signature argument is inherited from the epistemology of convergence arguments and from the evidential-status discipline, so its strength is theirs rather than a further confirmation of them. And the convergence across four perturbation classes is mutually coherent only as regards the dualist conclusion: it raises the explanatory cost on a unified-substrate account without adding independent weight to the framework through which the classes are being read.

## Relation to Site Perspective

Pharmacological dissociation as an evidence pattern touches each of the Map's [[tenets|five tenets]] differently, with the calibration discipline applied at each.

**[[tenets#^dualism|Dualism]]**: The dissociation classes constrain identity readings without establishing dualism. What they support is a structured picture that interactionist dualism accommodates and materialism accommodates by locating consciousness in specific connectivity patterns. The Map's reading is no worse by fit, preferred on grounds argued elsewhere, and remains unestablished.

**[[tenets#^minimal-quantum-interaction|Minimal Quantum Interaction]]**: Several anaesthetic targets involve conformational changes at scales where thermal noise and quantum uncertainty overlap. The stochastic emergence variability in genetically identical mice (Stone et al. 2025) is fitted by a classical stochastic neuronal-dynamics model, so it and the molecular shape of receptor pharmacology are compatible with quantum-sensitive architecture without requiring it. The anaesthetic-microtubule literature is deliberately not recruited here: it reaches this tenet only through the pre-decoherence Orch-OR reading that [[positions/quantum-interface|position P-Q5]] demotes relative to post-decoherence selection, so it is unavailable as affirmative support until that ranking changes. Whether warm, wet neural tissue can sustain the coherence such an architecture would require is disputed, and the framework's quantum-bridge commitments remain unestablished.

**[[tenets#^bidirectional-interaction|Bidirectional Interaction]]**: This is the tenet the pharmacology supports least, and the limit is worth stating. The KCC2 emergence pathway is physical causation end to end — a molecular mechanism dismantling the inhibition anaesthesia installed — and measures no mental-to-physical traffic; reading it as the neural infrastructure preparing a channel for consciousness to re-enter is an interpretation laid over a wholly neural finding. The nearest thing to support for this tenet is weaker and indirect: under ketamine, experience continues and reorganises around whichever channels stay open, which is the sort of thing an efficacious consciousness would do, and no more than that.

**[[tenets#^no-many-worlds|No Many Worlds]]**: The diagnostic specificity of dissociations is conventionally read as a definite fact—this patient under this regimen produced this dissociation, revealing this architectural feature. An Everettian can restate the entire convergence as branch-local: from inside a decohered branch every outcome is determinate and the architectural reading proceeds unchanged. The Map's reading turns on global rather than branch-local ontological uniqueness, and that preference is a tenet-driven commitment running counter to MWI rather than a pharmacological refutation of it. Tenet 4 is a foundational rejection, not a conclusion the dissociation data establish.

**[[tenets#^occams-limits|Occam's Razor Has Limits]]**: The architecture mapped here is complex—separable components, a shared emergence mechanism, agent-specific failure profiles, behavioural surface decoupled from interior phenomenology. No single-mechanism model of the Meyer-Overton kind accounts for it, but every live rival carries the same complexity, so the tenet's work here is resisting a premature single-mechanism reading rather than favouring dualism by parsimony.
