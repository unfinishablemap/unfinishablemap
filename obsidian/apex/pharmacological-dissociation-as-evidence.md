Pharmacology is in an unusual epistemic position with respect to consciousness. A drug binds to a known receptor, modulates a known signalling pathway, and produces a phenomenological outcome the patient can later report. The chain from molecule to experience is short by neuroscience's standards, and the contrasts between drugs are sharp: two compounds acting on the same receptor produce different conscious states; two compounds acting on different receptors produce the same one; two compounds producing the same behavioural depth deliver different first-person interiors. Each contrast is a dissociation, and dissociations are the form of evidence the Unfinishable Map's interface model is best positioned to use.

What the catalogue has lacked is a piece that lifts pharmacological dissociation into a named evidence pattern at the methodology grain. The [[anaesthesia-and-the-consciousness-interface|anaesthesia article]] carries three distinct dissociation contrasts in its body, each doing different argumentative work, but the pattern they exhibit together is not specified at any single location. The [[neurological-dissociations-as-interface-architecture|neurological dissociations]], [[clinical-dissociation-as-systematic-evidence|clinical dissociations]], and [[contemplative-pathology-and-interface-malfunction|contemplative pathology]] clusters each reach the same architecture by independent routes, and the [[altered-states-as-interface-evidence|altered-states synthesis]] gathers six state-modalities into a convergence matrix—but none of these names what pharmacological dissociation *as a class* asks of the interface model, what it can and cannot do, and what discipline keeps its evidential weight calibrated.

What follows names three pharmacological-dissociation classes, supplies the anaesthetic exhibit for each, locates the matching patterns in the parallel clusters, and installs the [[evidential-status-discipline|evidential-status discipline]] that keeps the convergence from inflating beyond what the evidence licenses. This is a discipline about how the Map identifies dissociation patterns as evidence for interface architecture rather than a tenet-specific commitment, and it runs on one insight: *which* drugs dissociate from *which* reveals more about the interface than what any single drug does.

## What a Pharmacological Dissociation Is

A pharmacological dissociation is a contrast between two drug effects that separates components ordinary observation does not. The structure has three terms: a pharmacological variable (target receptor, mechanism, behavioural depth), a phenomenological observation (presence, content, access, encoding, temporal binding, ownership), and a contrast between two drug regimes that varies one term while holding another fixed. When the held-fixed term predicts the same outcome on a single-channel model and the actual outcome diverges, the divergence localises a component the single-channel model failed to anticipate.

The discipline matters because pharmacology is full of correlations that are not dissociations. Anaesthetic potency correlates with lipid solubility (the [[anaesthesia-and-the-consciousness-interface|Meyer-Overton correlation]]): a continuous relationship, historically read as evidence for a single nonspecific membrane mechanism. The interface model gets no traction from continuous correlations, only from contrasts where the structure predicts a unified outcome that fails to obtain. Three such contrast structures recur, each a different way of prying a component apart.

## Class A: Same Target, Opposite Outcome

**The structure.** Two drugs act on the same primary receptor; their phenomenological outcomes diverge in direction (not just magnitude). The receptor-level commonality predicts shared phenomenology under any model that locates consciousness in the receptor's activity. Divergent phenomenology localises a component the receptor itself does not exhaust.

**The anaesthetic exhibit: xenon-ketamine.** Both xenon and ketamine are NMDA receptor antagonists. Both render patients behaviourally unresponsive at anaesthetic doses. The phenomenological outcomes diverge as sharply as the catalogue's clinical literature has on offer: xenon extinguishes phenomenal experience entirely—patients report nothing, no dreams, no sense of time—while ketamine preserves vivid first-person content, with elaborate dream-like journeys, perceived encounters, and persisting reportable interiors. Sarasso et al. (2015) measured the underlying neural patterns: propofol and xenon converge on slow-wave collapse with phenomenal extinction; ketamine retains wakefulness-like complex activation despite matched unresponsiveness. The disambiguating factor is xenon's broader action on two-pore potassium channels—a second molecular site that ketamine does not engage—which suppresses the phenomenal interface while ketamine's selective NMDA blockade severs access channels only. The contrast is internally specified: it works on the pharmacology alone, so the framework reading is licensed by fit rather than forced.

**What the contrast establishes.** Phenomenal presence and behavioural access are pharmacologically separable *within* a single receptor system rather than two aspects of one phenomenon. That conclusion does not require the Map's interactionist version of structured components; sophisticated functionalism predicts the same modular dissociation. What the contrast forecloses is the simplest reading—identity of consciousness with receptor activity at the implicated site—leaving the live question of what is decomposing: separable subsystems that constitute consciousness, or separable channels connecting consciousness to brain mechanisms of content generation and access.

**Parallel patterns across the catalogue.** [[neurological-dissociations-as-interface-architecture|Blindsight and pain asymbolia]] exhibit the same structure at the neural level: the affective-motivational dimension can be abolished while sensory-discriminative content survives, and conversely the sensory channel can be destroyed while affective machinery remains. The pair separates sensory and affective ascending channels as xenon-ketamine separates the phenomenal-presence and access channels. The same component-structure shows up whether the contrast is generated by lesion or by drug.

## Class B: Different Targets, Convergent Outcome

**The structure.** Two drugs act on mechanistically distinct molecular targets and produce the same phenomenological outcome. The mechanistic distinctness rules out a single-pathway explanation; the convergence localises a higher-level component that multiple pathways can reach.

**The anaesthetic exhibit: propofol-xenon.** Propofol acts via GABA-A receptors; xenon via NMDA receptor antagonism plus two-pore potassium channels. Two receptor systems with no shared primary target. Both extinguish phenomenal experience. Sarasso et al. (2015) showed neural patterns differ strikingly—propofol produces a low-amplitude, spatially local slow wave; xenon produces a high-amplitude, spatially global one—yet first-person outcome is unified. The convergence on extinction-of-experience through different routes implies a single interface component being disabled through multiple molecular pathways, rather than two different things that happen to share a behavioural label.

**The active-reboot extension.** Hu et al.'s (2023) finding, in mice, that ubiquitin-driven degradation of the KCC2 cotransporter in the ventral posteromedial thalamus is the common emergence mechanism across the four agents they tested — propofol, pentobarbital, isoflurane, and ketamine — sharpens the convergence, though by less than the agent count invites. Hu et al.'s own framing is a two-class one: propofol, pentobarbital and isoflurane grouped as GABA-A-acting, ketamine set apart as an NMDA antagonist. The Map reads the span as three classes — isoflurane's volatile-anaesthetic profile engages targets beyond GABA-A, and the intravenous pair differs by binding site within a shared molecular target — but that finer partition is the Map's rather than the study's, and even at three it is a three-class span plus one within-class replication, not a four-target one. What survives the reduction is the structural point: *the same KCC2 mechanism operates across pharmacologically dissimilar agents*, so the interface has a dedicated reopening pathway that is mechanism-shared even where the closing pathways were mechanism-distinct. Different doorways close; one doorway reopens. The authors report the KCC2 phosphorylation step as occurring independently of the agents' anaesthetic target proteins, which cuts two ways: it strengthens the separability the exhibit is cited for, since the reopening route is then not downstream of whatever closed the channel, while weakening the cumulative-cost accounting, since what the study supplies is one demonstrated common mechanism rather than four coincidences a rival account must absorb one by one. The close/reopen asymmetry is an architectural fact about the interface rather than a coincidence of pharmacology.

**What the contrast establishes.** A component reachable from multiple mechanistic routes has the structural shape of a real architectural feature rather than a single-pathway artefact. The mechanism-shared reopening is the informative part: passive-recovery models struggle to say why molecularly distinct closing pathways would converge on one active reopening, whereas an interface with dedicated machinery for reconfiguration produces the asymmetry naturally.

**Parallel patterns across the catalogue.** The [[clinical-dissociation-as-systematic-evidence|clinical dissociation cluster]] supplies the strongest non-pharmacological version, but it carries an internal discount that disciplines how it is read. DID, depersonalisation-derealisation, dissociative amnesia, and conversion disorder reach the same channel-level architecture as anaesthesia and neurological lesions, but through *functional* connectivity disruption rather than structural damage or receptor pharmacology—and what they sever is felt access and ownership, not the underlying integration: DID patients reporting inter-identity amnesia on recall still show cross-identity transfer on recognition (Marsh et al. 2021), so the substrate keeps integrating while metacognitive access decouples. The four clinical windows do *not* count as four independent confirmations: three of them (DPDR as imbalanced interoceptive precision, conversion as an aberrant motor prior, surviving dissociative amnesia as inhibited retrieval) are redescribable as precision-weighting pathologies in one active-inference framework, with DID's cross-state geometry the member that most resists the precision framing. The convergence that carries weight therefore runs *across* perturbation classes, not within the clinical cluster.

That cross-route convergence is what the [[epistemology-of-convergence-arguments#compound-signatures-convergence-without-a-shared-mechanism|epistemology of convergence arguments]] names a *compound signature*: agreement among mechanistically disjoint routes, so it cannot be one upstream cause sampled three times. Pharmacology is one route of that signature. The siblings are structural ([[neurological-dissociations-as-interface-architecture|lesion]], with [[anarchic-hand-and-action-ownership|anarchic hand]] as the disowned-action exhibit) and *psychogenic*—the latter's clearest case being [[conversion-disorder-as-consciousness-side-fault|conversion disorder]], where the affected limb moves normally under anaesthesia or hypnosis yet not under awake directed volition, excluding "the perturbation broke the substrate" without drug or lesion. That leg carries a discount its own clinical literature insists on: contemporary functional-neurological-disorder work reads the mechanism as *overactive* rather than missing. Edwards et al.'s (2012) Bayesian model treats the symptom as an abnormally precise motor prior the system fulfils, and Voon et al. (2010) found heightened amygdala-to-supplementary-motor-area influence rather than a severed connection. Conversion disorder still shows that volitional motor output can be abolished with the substrate intact; what it no longer shows is a channel going offline. The calibration that follows applies to the signature as a whole: the convergence earns the *architecture* (the channels are separable) robustly while leaving the *interface reading* of that separability a live hypothesis the routes do not jointly vindicate.

## Class C: Same Behavioural Depth, Different Mechanism, Different Phenomenology

**The structure.** Two drugs produce matched behavioural depth—comparable apparent sedation, unresponsiveness, or task impairment—but their underlying mechanisms differ and their first-person interiors diverge. The matched behaviour predicts matched phenomenology on any view that takes behaviour as a proxy for consciousness. Divergent phenomenology under matched behaviour exposes the proxy as inadequate and puts the weight on the underlying mechanism.

**The anaesthetic exhibit: ketamine-midazolam at sub-anaesthetic doses.** Ketamine (NMDA antagonist) and midazolam (benzodiazepine, GABA-A positive modulator) at sub-anaesthetic doses can each leave patients responsive and capable of conversation and simple tasks. From that shared surface the phenomenological outcomes, drawn from the separate clinical literatures on each agent, diverge. Ketamine at this depth often preserves vivid experiential content with dissociative quality. Midazolam produces profound amnesia with minimal sedation, isolating memory encoding almost surgically: a patient may converse, follow instructions, and hold content in working memory across seconds—then form no lasting memory of the interval at all. Experiential content survives in both cases; what diverges is the encoding channel. The same behavioural surface conceals different interface configurations.

**What the contrast establishes.** Behavioural-level matching is not a reliable proxy for interface-level matching. Phenomenal presence (intact), access (intact), and memory encoding (severed) can hold simultaneously, and the behavioural surface tells you nothing about which. Memory encoding is thus a separable channel that pharmacology can isolate while the rest of the architecture keeps running—the surgical-isolation profile anaesthetic pharmacology furnishes more cleanly than any other intervention.

**Parallel patterns across the catalogue.** [[contemplative-pathology-and-interface-malfunction|Contemplative pathology]] supplies the most direct Class C analogue. Practitioners who reach the *dukkha nanas* or other contemplative crises keep behavioural function—they can speak, navigate, perform daily tasks—while their interior phenomenology fragments: temporal binding breaks, ownership dissolves, phenomenal flooding strips ordinary filters. The contrast structure matches ketamine-midazolam: matched surface, distinct mechanism (sustained voluntary attention versus pharmacological agent), distinct phenomenology. Behavioural depth is an interface-coarse measure, and any intervention reaching the architecture at finer grain than behaviour will produce Class C dissociations.

## The Cumulative Convergence

The three classes are not independent. Anaesthesia is the catalogue's strongest worked exhibit precisely because all three operate together in one article body. The xenon-ketamine contrast (Class A), the propofol-xenon contrast with KCC2 reopening (Class B), and the ketamine-midazolam contrast (Class C) co-occur in the same pharmacological literature, on the same clinical populations, with overlapping receptor systems—and the three contrasts converge on the same four-component interface architecture: phenomenal presence, behavioural access, temporal binding, and memory encoding.

All four components belong to one layer, and naming which one bounds the whole methodology. The [[mind-matter-interface|two-layer architecture]] separates Layer 1—the constraining relation that explains why brain-consciousness correlation need not be production—from Layer 2, the selection mechanism by which consciousness would act on neural outcomes. Every contrast in the three classes is a Layer 1 observation. Pharmacology maps the channels through which consciousness is constrained and reaches nothing about the mechanism by which it would do anything, which is why the quantum-interaction commitments below stay at a lower tier than the architectural ones.

What this convergence does evidentially is constrained by the [[evidential-status-discipline]]. Each dissociation, taken alone, *constrains* the simplest identity reading—the one that locates consciousness in a single mechanism, pathway, or receptor's activity—without *establishing* the Map's interactionist alternative. Sophisticated functionalist readings fit the same data: separable subsystems whose coupling constitutes consciousness, rather than separable channels connecting an already-unified consciousness to brain mechanisms. Adjudication requires evidence the dissociations alone cannot supply.

What the convergence raises is the *explanatory cost on absorption*. The materialist position that accommodates pharmacological dissociations must specify which connectivity patterns generate consciousness, why those patterns and not neighbouring ones, why mechanism-distinct closing pathways converge on a single active reopening, and why behavioural-level matching fails to track first-person matching. Each accommodation is available; the cumulative cost across classes raises the bar on what a fully spelled-out materialism must explain.

The convergence with non-pharmacological evidence multiplies the effect. The [[neurological-dissociations-as-interface-architecture|neurological dissociations]] reach the same channel architecture by structural damage, the [[clinical-dissociation-as-systematic-evidence|clinical dissociations]] by functional disconnection without structural damage, the [[contemplative-pathology-and-interface-malfunction|contemplative pathology]] cluster by voluntary attentional restructuring pushing the interface beyond its operating parameters. Pharmacology, lesion, functional disconnection, and contemplative practice are four perturbation *classes*, each capable of selectively disrupting the same components in the same agent-specific patterns. The architecture replicates across classes that share no mechanism—and the independence that carries the weight is *between* classes, since within the functional-disconnection class a single precision-weighting mechanism may be reading one architecture several times (see the clinical-cluster discount above). *That* is the cumulative-convergence move pharmacological dissociation contributes one quarter of, with the [[altered-states-as-interface-evidence|altered-states synthesis]] gathering six state-modalities into a complementary matrix at the systems level rather than the mechanism level. The [[epistemology-of-convergence-arguments#compound-signatures-convergence-without-a-shared-mechanism|epistemology of convergence arguments]] formalises why mechanistic disjointness does that work: a compound signature pre-empts the common-cause objection only where there is no shared upstream cause to screen off—which holds across classes by construction, but not necessarily within one. It also fixes where the signature's reach stops—it earns the architecture but not the architecture's dualist significance—which is the discipline the next section applies.

The [[interface-specification-programme|interface specification programme]] reads this convergence as specification work: each route adds resolution to where the interface has components, how they fail, and how it reconfigures. The claim the methodology licenses is a modest one — *the architecture pharmacology reveals has the same component-structure that lesion, dissociation, and contemplative practice reveal, and the convergence raises the explanatory cost on the unified-substrate alternative* — rather than the inference from dissociations to dualism it is sometimes mistaken for.

## Calibration Discipline

The [[evidential-status-discipline]] supplies the operational language for what each dissociation can and cannot establish. The two-registers distinction (tenet register / evidence register) applies cleanly: the Map's tenets *permit* the interactionist reading of pharmacological dissociation, but permission is defeater-removal, not positive evidence. The five-tier evidential-status scale locates the inference: pharmacological dissociation as *evidence for structured interface architecture* sits at the *realistic-possibility* level. The Map's *specific* interactionist reading of that structure—consciousness as ontologically distinct, interfacing through quantum-sensitive molecular systems—remains at the *live-hypothesis* level. The distance between the two tiers is held by the constrain-vs-establish discipline: pharmacology constrains the simplest identity readings without establishing the Map's specific alternative.

The dissociation-using character of this synthesis brings it under the discipline's four-levels rule for dissociation arguments. Each contrast in the three classes establishes *separability* (Level 1: the components come apart in the data) and contributes to *phenomenological reality* (Level 2: the dissociated features are genuine features of experience, not artefacts of report). *Mechanistic independence* (Level 3) is what the receptor-pharmacology contrasts approach but do not by themselves settle—sophisticated functionalism can occupy Level 3 with separable subsystems whose coupling constitutes consciousness. *Metaphysical irreducibility* (Level 4) lies beyond pharmacological dissociation entirely, requiring the independent argumentation staged elsewhere (the hard problem, self-stultification, convergence with non-pharmacological routes). The discipline's instruction is *don't collapse the chain*: this synthesis stops where the dissociation evidence stops, and the inferential weight at higher levels is carried by separate arguments.

Two of this synthesis's sources have recently applied the same discipline against the Map's own interest, and the corrections belong here rather than only on their pages. [[filter-theory|Filter theory]] has withdrawn the bandwidth argument as a discriminator: conscious throughput of roughly ten bits per second against some 10⁹ bits per second of aggregate neural processing, with the bottleneck localised to access rather than to sensory or motor stages, reads naturally on a filter model—but global workspace theory makes a conscious-access bottleneck its *defining* feature, so the datum is shared rather than discriminating. [[interface-friction|Interface friction]] makes the parallel concession about choking, where conscious self-monitoring degrades automatised skill (Beilock and Carr 2001; Gröpel and Mesagno 2019; Smoulder et al. 2024): the friction reading redescribes an established motor-control result under a dualist ontology rather than predicting anything the standard explicit-monitoring and over-arousal models miss. Both have the shape the three dissociation classes have—a phenomenon the framework accommodates gracefully is not thereby one the framework uniquely predicted. What the friction page does contribute is the organising rule this synthesis runs on: where the interface is strained, its seams show, and the seams are the data.

The discipline is symmetric. The materialist must not treat a known molecular mechanism for each anaesthetic as if it *established* the reduction; receptor pharmacology is a necessary condition for an explanation of consciousness, not a sufficient one. The dualist must not treat the dissociation pattern as if it *established* the interface reading; sophisticated functionalism predicts the same pattern, and the discriminating work falls to convergence with non-pharmacological evidence and to independent considerations the dissociations alone cannot bear.

Where pharmacology does positive disambiguating work, it does so through cases like the ketamine self-stultification argument. Patients later reporting vivid ketamine experiences show consciousness kept its causal connection to memory while severed from behavioural output; were it epiphenomenal during that state, the reports would be [[self-stultification-as-master-argument|self-stultifying]], presupposing the causal efficacy epiphenomenalism denies. What pharmacology supplies here is a specific clinical case to which the master argument applies with unusual precision — the ketamine interior is reportable in a clinically reliable way, and the reportability is itself evidence of causal connection to memory consolidation. The force is local to the case rather than general.

## What Pharmacological Dissociation Cannot Tell Us

The methodology has limits the discipline names rather than hides. Four matter.

First, anaesthetic pharmacology is incomplete. Current anaesthetics were discovered empirically, so interface components not targeted by any known drug stay invisible to the dissection. The four-component architecture is what current pharmacology *can* pry apart; a complete architecture may have components the existing drug repertoire never reaches.

Second, the necessary-vs-where distinction matters. Pharmacological evidence shows specific molecular systems are necessary for the interface to function. It does not show these systems are *where* consciousness connects—some may be interface infrastructure rather than interface points. The KCC2 reopening pathway is necessary for emergence; whether the pathway is the interface or the mechanism that prepares conditions for an interface elsewhere to re-engage is a question the pharmacology cannot decide alone.

Third, everything the three classes deliver is Layer 1. The dissociations establish that the constraining relation has separable components; they say nothing about the selection mechanism by which consciousness would act, and nothing about what the interface *is*—the interactionist and sophisticated-functionalist readings both fit, at the tiers the previous section assigns them.

Fourth, a negative case marks where dissociation evidence stops paying. [[locked-in-syndrome-as-the-negative-case-where-filter-loosening-does-not-apply|Locked-in syndrome]] severs the descending arm of the interface at the pons while leaving the ascending arm and consciousness untouched: experience is neither widened nor degraded, only disconnected from motor expression. A reading that treated every interface perturbation as filter-modulation would expect something here and gets nothing, because there is no receptive constraint being relaxed. The three classes pry components apart; they do not license the further inference that disrupting the interface generally does anything interesting to the width or intensity of experience.

## The Methodology Stated Once

Pharmacological dissociation furnishes evidence for a structured, multi-channel consciousness-brain interface in three classes:

- **Class A** (same target, opposite outcome) constrains identity readings within a single receptor system, separating interface components the receptor itself does not exhaust.
- **Class B** (different targets, convergent outcome) constrains single-pathway readings, localising components that multiple mechanistic routes can reach and revealing close/reopen architectural asymmetries.
- **Class C** (same behavioural depth, different mechanism, different phenomenology) constrains behavioural-proxy readings, exposing the interior decoupling that behavioural surface measures cannot detect.

All three operate simultaneously in the anaesthesia exhibit and recur with structural fidelity across the neurological-lesion, clinical-dissociation, and contemplative-pathology clusters, the cumulative force coming from the architecture replicating under perturbations that share no mechanism. The discipline holds each class at the *constrain* level and the cumulative pattern at the *realistic-possibility* level for structured interface architecture, well short of the *strongly-supported* level for the Map's interactionist reading. Its value is not in lifting the conclusion higher but in specifying *what work each class can do, what it cannot, and which convergences add discriminating weight*—and in naming the failure mode it is vulnerable to: the slippage from defeater-removal to evidence-elevation when a dissociation pattern is presented as if it had positively supported the interactionist version of the structure.

## Evidence and Dependency

The dissociation data this synthesis runs on are externally evidenced and hold whatever one makes of the Map: Sarasso et al.'s complexity measurements, the xenon/ketamine and ketamine/midazolam clinical contrasts, Hu et al.'s KCC2 emergence work, Marsh et al. on cross-identity recognition transfer, the functional-neurological-disorder imaging. None of them discriminates the interface reading from a sophisticated functionalist one.

The three-class taxonomy is independently argued: it needs only that a contrast holding one term fixed while varying another can localise a component—no tenet supplies that, and none is required for it. Reading the resulting components as *interface* channels rather than as subsystems whose coupling constitutes consciousness is inherited from Tenet 1; the quantum gloss on the molecular boundary is inherited from Tenet 2. Both are flagged as live hypotheses where they appear.

The compound-signature argument is inherited from the epistemology of convergence arguments and from the evidential-status discipline, so its strength is theirs rather than a further confirmation of them. And the convergence across four perturbation classes is mutually coherent only as regards the dualist conclusion: it raises the explanatory cost on a unified-substrate account without adding independent weight to the framework through which the classes are being read.

## Relation to Site Perspective

Pharmacological dissociation as an evidence pattern touches each of the Map's [[tenets|five tenets]] differently, with the calibration discipline applied at each.

**[[tenets#^dualism|Dualism]]**: The dissociation classes constrain identity readings without establishing dualism. What they support is the structured interface picture dualism predicts and materialism must accommodate by locating consciousness in specific connectivity patterns. The Map's reading is preferred by fit rather than forced by argument, and the interactionist version of the picture sits at the live-hypothesis level.

**[[tenets#^minimal-quantum-interaction|Minimal Quantum Interaction]]**: Several anaesthetic targets involve conformational changes at scales where thermal noise and quantum uncertainty overlap. The contested Wiest (2025) delayed-luminescence work on anaesthetic effects in microtubule quantum properties, the stochastic emergence variability in genetically identical organisms (orders-of-magnitude variation under identical conditions), and the structural shape of receptor pharmacology at the molecular boundary are all compatible with quantum-sensitive interface architecture. The compatibility is not entailment; whether warm, wet neural tissue can sustain the coherence such an architecture would require against rapid decoherence is disputed, and the framework's quantum-bridge commitments remain at the live-hypothesis tier.

**[[tenets#^bidirectional-interaction|Bidirectional Interaction]]**: The active-reboot KCC2 mechanism—the brain's coordinated dismantling of anaesthesia-installed inhibition *before* behavioural responsiveness returns—is consistent with an interface that the neural infrastructure prepares for consciousness to re-engage. The reading is one available interpretation, not the only one; the convergence of the four tested agents — spanning distinct mechanism classes, though fewer than four of them — on a single active reopening pathway raises the explanatory cost on passive-recovery models.

**[[tenets#^no-many-worlds|No Many Worlds]]**: The diagnostic specificity of dissociations is conventionally read as a definite fact—this patient under this regimen produced this dissociation, revealing this architectural feature. An Everettian can restate the entire convergence as branch-local: from inside a decohered branch every outcome is determinate and the architectural reading proceeds unchanged. The Map's reading turns on global rather than branch-local ontological uniqueness, and that preference is a tenet-driven commitment running counter to MWI rather than a pharmacological refutation of it. Tenet 4 is a foundational rejection, not a conclusion the dissociation data establish.

**[[tenets#^occams-limits|Occam's Razor Has Limits]]**: The architecture mapped here is complex machinery—four separable components, mechanism-shared reopening, agent-specific failure profiles, behavioural-surface decoupling from interior phenomenology, hysteresis in the close/reopen asymmetry—and a simpler model fails to account for the dissociation pattern. The discipline is honesty about that complexity rather than parsimony for its own sake.
