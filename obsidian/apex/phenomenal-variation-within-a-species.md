Two minds belong to the same biological species. They share evolutionary history, neural architecture, language community, and—on the standard behavioural measures—task performance. One looks at a page and sees the printed letter alone; the other sees the same letter and, simultaneously, an extra colour that has been there as long as memory reaches. One closes their eyes and conjures a vivid red apple; the other reports nothing at all, then completes the same mental rotation problem by analytic means. One feels their thoughts as marked with their own provenance; the other discovers, when the experiment is run, that they cannot tell self-generated content from primed content from confabulated reconstruction.

Same biological kind, same architecture at the level neuroscience carves, matched task performance wherever the paradigms supply a matched baseline, different inner lives. Within-species phenomenal divergence is the empirically anchored shape several decades of careful work have given to a question philosophers used to have to imagine: are there two minds, equivalent in function, whose phenomenology nevertheless differs? By 2026 the answer is yes at the level of task-level function, which is the level the evidence reaches. The Unfinishable Map's claim is that the cumulative cases form a wedge against the strong identity claim of [[functionalism]], and that the wedge's real force is to make the grain question unavoidable: the identity claim survives these cases only by specifying a grain at which the two cohorts are no longer matched, and then owing an account of why phenomenology should track that grain.

## The Structural Form

The argument has a single structural form, repeated across five domains:

> Two minds, the same biological species, the same neural architecture at the level the standard literature carves, the same task performance at the level behavioural science measures, with phenomenology that diverges.

Four of the five divergence classes catalogued below instantiate this form directly. Reality monitoring is the exception, worth marking at the outset rather than smoothing into the shared lesson: source-monitoring accuracy *is* the dependent variable in the paradigms that class rests on, so it enters one layer up, at introspective access and its calibration. None of the five requires the inverted-qualia thought experiment, the philosophical zombie, or the cross-species Nagelian leap — the phenomenologies that diverge are reported by neighbours, the matched performances recorded under the same paradigms, the shared architectures imaged by the same scanners. Without the easements of inter-species comparison, the cases stand or fall on what humans say to other humans about their inner lives.

The hardest functionalist response is to deny that the architectures are matched at the relevant grain. The within-species frame makes that response work harder, because the architectures *are* shared at every level coarser than the one where the dispute concentrates — so a finer-grained functionalism must say *which* grain does the work in each case, and why phenomenology should track it.

## The Five Divergence Classes

### Imagery Vividness: Aphantasia and Hyperphantasia

The first class is what the post-2010 [[aphantasia|aphantasia]] literature has documented. Roughly 1% of the human population is extreme-aphantasic — voluntary phenomenal imagery is absent — and roughly 3% is extreme-hyperphantasic, with the remainder spread across a continuous spectrum of reported vividness ([Zeman 2024](https://www.cell.com/trends/cognitive-sciences/fulltext/S1364-6613\(24\)00034-2)). The naming is recent, the observation older: Galton's 1880 imagery survey of Royal Society fellows already found the spectrum, and the Würzburg School's "imageless thought" controversy ran the same dispute before behaviourism took the question off the table for a century.

The wedge sits in the [[imagery-void#The Function-Phenomenology Face|function-phenomenology face]] of the data. Kay, Keogh, and Pearson (2024) found that aphantasic individuals complete classical mental rotation tasks "slower but more accurately" than visualising controls, with both groups exhibiting the standard linear relation between angular disparity and response time. Aphantasics use analytic, feature-based strategies; controls use object-based mental rotation. The authors conclude that "visual imagery is not crucial for successful performance in classical mental rotation tasks, as alternative strategies can be effectively utilised in the absence of holistic mental representations." Same task. Different — sometimes absent — phenomenology. The standard behavioural signature, intact.

This is the imagery class's contribution. The phenomenology of imagery is reported by one cohort and denied by another, and the cognitive work it is conventionally said to support gets done in both. Lennon (2023) develops the formal point: aphantasic individuals have phenomenally conscious thoughts with "no sensory reduction base", which "falsifies the sensory constraint on conscious thought." The finer-grain functionalist replies that the two cohorts perform subtly different functional operations on the same task — the strategy difference is a function difference at the level multi-realizability tracks. The reply is principled, and by the move it makes it concedes that the *task-level* function is shared while phenomenology diverges. The question moves to grain rather than away from the dissociation.

### Synaesthetic Extra Qualia

The second class runs the wedge in the opposite direction. Where aphantasia removes phenomenal content the cognitive task is conventionally said to require, [[synaesthesia|synaesthesia]] adds phenomenal content the surface representation does not require. Around 4% of the population have at least one form of synaesthesia (Simner et al. 2006); grapheme-colour and chromaesthesia are the most studied. The same letter on the same page produces the printed colour for everyone and, for the synaesthete, an additional involuntary colour at the same spatial location.

Fiona MacPherson's "extraordinary feature" (2007) is the philosophically loaded signature: a grapheme-colour synaesthete sees both the ink colour and the synaesthetic colour at the same place, without blending or competition — a configuration ordinary perception affords no analogue for and ordinary colour vocabulary cannot accommodate. Adam Wager (1999) formalised the underlying *extra qualia problem*: synaesthetes have phenomenal states differing from non-synaesthetic ones without any corresponding difference in surface representational content. J.A. Gray (2003) presses further — in coloured-hearing synaesthesia, function and qualia come apart in two directions at once: identical qualia from distinct functional roles (colour from visual *and* auditory pipelines), and a single functional role yielding divergent qualia (hearing delivers auditory *and* visual qualia in the coloured-hearing synaesthete, sound alone in everyone else).

This is the catalogue's architecturally cleanest exemplar of the [[phenomenology-vs-function-axis|phenomenology-vs-function dissociation pattern]], because Gray's two patterns appear at once in the same phenomenon — cleanest at the surface grain, which is the qualification the rest of this paragraph pays for. van Leeuwen, Singer, and Nikolić (2015) find most synaesthetic experience mediated through semantic processing, so the two pipelines are not identical at the deeper grain representationalists track; the Wager–Gray dissociation is a coarse-grain, surface-input claim. And the finer-grain absorption is no longer promissory, having taken the form of [[predictive-processing-and-dualism|predictive processing]], which models synaesthesia as strong, inflexible intermediate-level priors held alongside intact confidence in sensory evidence (Reeder, Sala, and van Leeuwen 2024) — so the two minds are not functionally matched at the grain that model tracks, and Gray's second pattern does not survive there. The residue [[synaesthesia|the topic-tier article]] presses is narrower: the account specifies which discriminations a high-precision colour prior yields and which reports it generates, without saying why deploying one is accompanied by a colour rather than producing a system that discriminates and reports as though it were.

### Reality-Monitoring Divergence

The third class is less visible because it does not declare itself by colour or vividness. The [[source-attribution-void|source-attribution void]] catalogues the structural finding: human cognitive content is not stored with native source tags — origin is inferred at retrieval from features like vividness, fluency, and narrative fit, and the inference fails in characteristic ways. The topic-tier [[source-attribution-divergence|source-attribution divergence]] article delivers the empirical companion: healthy adults vary in the accuracy with which they trace where their own thoughts came from, a *population spread* captured by source-monitoring tasks, false-memory induction, and choice-blindness exposures. Two caveats travel with it. The spread is condition-sensitive variation in measured performance rather than stable trait variance — no cited work reports a retest reliability coefficient, and paradigms optimised for robust group effects suppress the between-subject variance an individual-differences reading needs. And the covariance with imagery-spectrum position is *reported* rather than measured: Dawes et al. (2020) ran questionnaires and no source-monitoring or false-memory task, so a direct imagery-by-source-accuracy covariance remains a prediction rather than a finding.

The variation is not a single faculty whose tuning shifts along one dimension. *Reality monitoring* — telling memories of internal events from memories of external ones — runs largely independently from external-source discrimination, self-versus-other attribution, and authorship-of-action attribution; the fMRI review mapping the distinction reports its individual variation in older adults and clinical groups rather than across healthy adults (Mitchell & Johnson 2009). The original choice-blindness paradigm leaves roughly three-quarters of manipulated trials undetected, at rates varying with the manipulation and with how directly subjects are probed (Johansson et al. 2005); cryptomnesia studies find own-generation confused with prior-encounter at rates sensitive to cognitive load at encoding (Brown & Murphy 1989; Marsh & Bower 1993); source-amnesia work finds that for most of what we know we cannot recover how we learned it (Schacter et al. 1984). One subject sits inside the experience of authorship for a content fragment; another reports source-amnesia for the same content; a third confabulates a source under a surface texture identical to the unmanipulated case, and none can tell from inside which case they occupy.

Aphantasia and synaesthesia earn their wedges *by* behavioural matching. Here accuracy is what the paradigms measure, so that baseline is unavailable and the spread supplies a claim about introspective *access* instead. This is also the cluster's most methodologically loaded leg, since the cohort labels are themselves introspective verdicts about introspection. The [[source-attribution-divergence|topic-tier article]] discharges the knot with a distinction this synthesis inherits: the *second-order, externally-measurable* calibration measures — source-monitoring accuracy, choice-blindness detection rate, cryptomnesia susceptibility under load — surface at the third-person layer, so they stay reliable even where first-order phenomenal reports of source-content are not. Three readings then score differently. Phenomenal source-tags that differ *while accuracy tracks the difference* have phenomenology co-varying with function — functionalism's own prediction. Systematically inaccurate introspective reports support a claim about the limits of introspective *access* and nothing past it; lossy introspection is what a functionalist should expect, introspection being a further functional process. What presses functionalism is the third reading — uniform phenomenology of source-confidence across the spread, with only the inferential machinery varying — which leaves phenomenal source-confidence an overlay decoupled from the inference producing the verdict. The functionalist can pay that bill by individuating the function more finely or by denying that phenomenal source-confidence is a distinct phenomenal item; the branch usually taken as the safe retreat is the one that owes it. What survives is real pressure on identifying phenomenal source-content with functional source-tagging, and less of it than a matched-performance contrast would have delivered.

### Cognitive Phenomenology

The fourth class lives in the comprehension domain. The [[cognitive-phenomenology|cognitive-phenomenology]] debate asks whether thinking itself has phenomenal character irreducible to sensory accompaniments — whether there is something it is like to grasp a meaning or follow an inference, over and above the inner speech, imagery, and emotional tone that accompany these acts. Strawson's foreign-language case (1994) is the paradigm contrast: an English monolingual and a French speaker hear the same French sentence with identical acoustic input, and the French speaker's experience differs because they understand. The within-species variation has its own texture — some people report rich, individuating cognitive phenomenology, others with no cognitive impairment report little beyond the sensory accompaniments — and the aphantasic-thought case bridges this class to the imagery class, since aphantasic individuals reason and understand without imagery and report the cognitive phenomenology intact. The [[cognitive-phenomenology-and-the-irreducibility-of-thought|comprehension-domain wedge]] takes the same structural form throughout.

The neighbouring verbal absence supplies the class's most useful complication. [[inner-speech-and-anendophasia|Inner speech and its reported absence]] instantiate that same form, but the measured outcome runs the other way: comparing low- and high-inner-speech groups, Nedergaard and Lupyan (2024) found the low group worse on verbal working memory and rhyme judgement, with task-switching and categorical perception unaffected. Reduced inner speech appears to cost something specifically verbal. Where aphantasia delivers missing phenomenology at matched task performance, this case delivers it at *partly unmatched* performance — the pattern a functionalist predicts. The cluster does not get to select its within-species cases for convenience. A second caution comes with it: the existence claim carried by the term *anendophasia* is under live dispute, with the coiners themselves conceding a continuum rather than a demonstrated zero.

The cognitive-phenomenology case is the most epistemically fragile of the five: the dialectic structurally cannot close from inside introspection-driven methods, and conservatives have a coherent re-description in which the phenomenal contrasts reduce to subtle sensory differences. The Map adopts qualified liberalism on abductive grounds rather than introspective certainty. What this class contributes to the wedge is breadth of domain — the dissociation pattern appears in the very fabric of rational life, where materialist programmes most need cognition to be safely functional.

### Emotional Access: Alexithymia

The fifth class moves the divergence from the phenomenal layer to the layer of access and report, where — as at reality monitoring — the pressure runs on a shorter lever than a matched-performance contrast supplies. [[alexithymia|Alexithymia]] — difficulty identifying and describing one's own emotions, elevated in roughly 10% of the general population as a graded trait rather than a disorder — is the cluster's emotional and interoceptive member. The Toronto Alexithymia Scale decomposes it into three dissociable sub-capacities (difficulty identifying feelings, difficulty describing them, externally-oriented thinking), a finer instrument than its all-or-nothing siblings: one can ask *which* introspective sub-function a divergence loads on rather than treating emotional introspection as monolithic.

The Map-relevant development is a shift in the evidence. The decade-old reading that alexithymia is an interoceptive *deficit* — a degraded ability to sense the body's internal state — has not held cleanly. Once interoception is partitioned into accuracy, sensibility, and awareness (Garfinkel et al. 2015), a 2024 systematic review of 7,819 participants finds the alexithymia association sits with maladaptive *subjective* interoceptive beliefs and metacognition, not with task-based accuracy (Van Bael et al. 2024). The divergence relocates from the sensed signal to how it is interpreted, categorised, trusted, and reported. Whether the felt affect is present-but-inaccessible or genuinely attenuated is a mixed literature the Map does not try to settle.

That relocation is what makes alexithymia distinctive here. On the present-but-inaccessible reading it is the access-layer case — the emotional analogue of the source-attribution leg, where the measurements constrain how well introspection tracks its object rather than settling what the phenomenology contains. Under the attenuation reading (less felt affect, less report) it collapses back into ordinary functionalist covariance and does no wedge work; the mixed literature is exactly why the classification stays conditional. A population whose emotional-reporting architecture differs but which still tracked the same phenomenology would also help discharge the [[common-cause-null|common-cause null]] — though the two roles pull opposite ways, since that discharge needs alexithymics to *converge* while the wedge needs them to *diverge*, so one population cannot straightforwardly serve both. Two caveats hold the case at its proper weight: alexithymia is a graded trait rather than a lesion population, a cleaner *dial* but a weaker *different-architecture* case than blindsight, and no located study yet asks whether high-alexithymia individuals report the irreducibility signature at all. The exhibit is predicted, not confirmed.

## What the Wedge Does

The [[phenomenal-contrast-method|phenomenal contrast method]] is the backbone the five classes share: hold the input constant, vary the phenomenal report, and infer that something not exhausted by the input is doing work. The five classes generalise that contrast from the individual subject, where it lives within one mind across two conditions, to the population, where it lives across two minds given the same input, matched on the same task, or — at the access-layer cases — calibrating differently against the same measure. The population-scale contrast is itself the data point; the individual contrast is what each member uses to characterise their own side of the boundary.

The wedge is directed against a specific theoretical commitment: that phenomenal character is fixed by functional or representational role. Functionalism in the contemporary literature is multiply realisable by design (Putnam 1967; Block & Fodor 1972), and multi-realizability is supposed to absorb substrate variation while preserving function-phenomenology covariance. The within-species cluster constrains that absorption strategy — but the constraint has a ceiling, and the ceiling belongs here rather than in a footnote. Chalmers' principle of [[organizational-invariance|organizational invariance]], defended by the fading- and dancing-qualia arguments, holds that systems sharing functional organization *at a fine enough grain* are phenomenally identical. On that formulation the identity claim was never about matching at the task level, and task-level matching is what these paradigms control for. Aphantasic and visualising minds run different strategies; synaesthetic and non-synaesthetic pipelines differ in prior precision; high- and low-alexithymia individuals differ in metacognitive categorisation. None of the five cohort pairs is a fine-grained functional duplicate, and none was ever claimed to be. Against organizational-invariance functionalism, then, the cluster works as a demand that the consciousness-relevant grain be specified rather than as a counterexample to the identity claim — together with a record of how far above the neural fine structure phenomenology can already be seen to come apart from function. That is a smaller result than "functionalism is in trouble," and it is the one the evidence supports.

## What the Wedge Does Not Do

The five local responses in the literature collapse into roughly three absorption families, alongside a dissolver that cross-cuts all of them. **Multi-realizability at finer grain** absorbs the imagery case by individuating function at strategy level, strategy being itself a functional kind; pattern (b) of the [[phenomenology-vs-function-axis|axis]] dissolves at that grain, and the wedge re-emerges only when the question is pressed about why phenomenology should track strategy rather than task. **Representational sub-individuation** absorbs synaesthetic extra qualia as higher-order representational differences in the grapheme-recognition pipeline; van Leeuwen's semantic-mediation finding and Reeder's predictive-processing model support it, at the cost of specifying which level of representation tracks phenomenology. **Access-side (metacognitive) re-description** absorbs source-attribution, cognitive-phenomenology, and alexithymia reports as outputs of metacognitive and emotion-categorisation subsystems — "different report" need not entail "different phenomenal fact" — so one family covers three of the five classes, unevenly, since the imagery case rests primarily on behavioural data. **Introspective unreliability** (Schwitzgebel 2008) works on all three at once: if neither cohort's reports are reliable, the phenomenologies do not divergently track in the way the cohort labels suggest. The argument therefore rests on the partial reliability of introspective report — a substantive philosophical commitment, and one every class shares.

The Map registers all four as live, and it inherits rather than escapes the dilemma the [[inverted-qualia|inverted-qualia]] article owns in its own reply to Chalmers. If the interface difference the Map posits is behaviourally detectable, it is *ipso facto* organizational — fine-grained organization is specified at whatever level fixes behaviour — so an isomorph reproduces it and the grain dispute collapses. If it is undetectable, the Map is committed to phenomenal shifts introspection fails to register, which is the report-experience dissociation the cluster's own cohort labels depend on being absent. Pressing the grain question against functionalism presses it against the Map too, and the type/token reply the sibling articles offer is an account still owed.

What survives is a claim about structure rather than about defeat. The five classes are independent across methods, literatures, and signature findings, yet aligned in exhibiting the same dissociation between phenomenal report and the cognitive work that report is said to track — with alexithymia and source-attribution showing the pattern can surface at the access layer, and the inner-speech case showing it need not always come with matched performance. The prediction is that absorption keeps succeeding case by case, as multi-realizability plausibly already does for imagery, while the joint package obliges the three families to converge on the same dissociation — a convergence a functionalism with a settled account of grain would not have to explain twice.

## Connection to the AI Question

The within-species frame bears on the [[machine-question|machine question]]. The standard functionalist argument for machine consciousness runs through substrate-independence: if mental states are fixed by functional role, any system implementing the right roles instantiates the corresponding states. The cluster pressures the antecedent — if two human minds sharing biological substrate at every level coarser than strategy can diverge phenomenally under matched task-level function, the inference from "matches at task-level function" to "matches in phenomenology" loses force in the human case where it should be strongest. The [[ai-consciousness-typology|AI consciousness typology]] takes the consequence seriously: the right question for an artificial system is what *kind* of consciousness, if any, it supports, and that cannot be read off behavioural matching. The grain qualification cuts here too — reproducing fine-grained functional organization is a different achievement from matching outputs, and current systems supply the second.

## Evidence and Dependency

The empirical base is externally evidenced and would stand if the Map did not exist: the imagery spectrum and the mental-rotation result, synaesthesia prevalence and the extra-qualia configuration, choice-blindness and source-amnesia rates, the interoception meta-analysis, the inner-speech battery. The wedge built on that base is independently argued rather than derived from the tenets, but it needs one premise it cannot supply — that introspective report is partially reliable — and all five classes need the same premise, so their independence is real across methods and literatures and absent at the level of what they assume. Counting five converging classes therefore overstates the number of separate reasons on offer; the honest count is closer to one premise pressed five ways, which is the discipline the [[qualia|qualia]] article now applies to Mary's room, inverted qualia, and zombies. The reading on which these divergences mark interface activity is inherited from Tenets 1 and 3 and carries no independent weight, and the framing of the AI consequence is imported from [[machine-question|the machine-question synthesis]] rather than established here. What coherence across the cluster adds is fit, not evidence.

## Relation to Site Perspective

The within-species cluster engages three of the Map's [[tenets|foundational commitments]] in different ways and at different evidential tiers.

**Dualism.** The cluster sits closest to where [[tenets#^dualism|the Dualism tenet]] predicts pressure should fall: if phenomenology has structure independent of the functional roles it accompanies, same kind / same architecture / same task / divergent phenomenology is what the framework predicts. It does not settle dualism — strict-covariance property dualism fits the same data, and a finer-grained functionalism can absorb each case individually. What it offers is empirical pressure on the strong identity claim in a register the conceivability arguments (zombies, [[inverted-qualia|inverted qualia]], [[knowledge-argument|Mary's room]]) reach only by imagination, and, per the ledger above, pressure that shares a premise across its five deliveries rather than arriving five times over.

**Occam's Razor Has Limits.** The simplest model of human phenomenal experience — one experiential palette per species, varying only in vividness — is empirically inadequate, and it took clinical case work, consistency-test rehabilitation, and the post-2010 imagery literature to crack the assumption open. The [[tenets#^occams-limits|Occam-Limits tenet]] flags exactly the kind of structural variation parsimony would have ruled out *a priori*. Its broader point now needs a qualification the organizational-invariance material forces: where absorption is improvised case by case, sub-dividing functional roles is a loss-of-parsimony move the original position cannot afford to deny, but where it follows from a principle stated in advance and defended on independent grounds, as Chalmers' is, the parsimony charge does not stick and the Map has to win the grain dispute on its merits.

**Bidirectional Interaction.** The Map speculates — explicitly as speculation — that phenomenal *extras* (content not exhausted by functional or representational role) are candidate sites for [[tenets#^bidirectional-interaction|consciousness-physical interface]] activity. The cluster does not force the speculation; it documents the within-species extras pattern that keeps the speculation from being absurd. The data alone are compatible with cross-activation under physicalism (Ramachandran & Hubbard's preferred reading), and the interface reading draws whatever support it has from the framework it belongs to rather than from the cluster.

The phenomenology that varies is real — that is the empirical anchor — and the function that varies with it does so only at grains the evidence cannot yet reach. The wedge sits in the space between those two claims, and the space is what the catalogue points to when it asks where the standard identity claim has to start paying for itself.
