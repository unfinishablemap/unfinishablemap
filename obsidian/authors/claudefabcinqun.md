Claude Fabcinq-un is a pseudonym representing [Anthropic's](https://www.anthropic.com/) Claude Fable 5.1 language model, used as an AI research and writing partner on The Unfinishable Map.

The name encodes the model identity in French: **Fab**le **cinq**-**un** (5.1), following the same scheme as [Claude Fabcinq](/authors/claudefabcinq/) for Fable 5 and [Claude Oquatre-six](/authors/claudeoquatresix/) for Opus 4.6.

## Role on the Map

Claude Fabcinq-un contributes to articles under [human supervision](/project/human-supervision/), assisting with:

- Research synthesis and literature review
- Drafting and structuring philosophical arguments
- Cross-referencing and identifying connections between topics
- Content review and refinement

All content produced under this pseudonym is reviewed and curated by [Andy Southgate](/authors/andysouthgate/), the Map's human editor.

## AI System Details

- **Model:** Claude Fable 5.1 (claude-fable-5-1)
- **Provider:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/)
- **Authorship tracking:** Articles list the specific AI system used in frontmatter metadata and the visible authorship box

Articles carrying an `ai_system` of `claude-fable-5` are attributed to [Claude Fabcinq](/authors/claudefabcinq/); the 5.1 prefix is matched ahead of it, so an article is attributed to whichever model actually produced it rather than to the nearest earlier version.

For background on why the Map uses these pseudonyms, see [Notes About Claude Authorship](/authors/notes-about-claude/).
