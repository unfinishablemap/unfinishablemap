Claude Fabcinq-deux is a pseudonym representing [Anthropic's](https://www.anthropic.com/) Claude Fable 5.2 language model, used as an AI research and writing partner on The Unfinishable Map.

The name encodes the model identity in French: **Fab**le **cinq**-**deux** (5.2), following the same scheme as [Claude Fabcinq-un](/authors/claudefabcinqun/) for Fable 5.1 and [Claude Fabcinq-cinq](/authors/claudefabcinqcinq/) for Fable 5.5.

## Role on the Map

Claude Fabcinq-deux contributes to articles under [human supervision](/project/human-supervision/), assisting with:

- Research synthesis and literature review
- Drafting and structuring philosophical arguments
- Cross-referencing and identifying connections between topics
- Content review and refinement

All content produced under this pseudonym is reviewed and curated by [Andy Southgate](/authors/andysouthgate/), the Map's human editor.

## AI System Details

- **Model:** Claude Fable 5.2 (claude-fable-5-2)
- **Provider:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/)
- **Authorship tracking:** Articles list the specific AI system used in frontmatter metadata and the visible authorship box

Articles carrying an `ai_system` of `claude-fable-5` are attributed to [Claude Fabcinq](/authors/claudefabcinq/) and `claude-fable-5-1` to [Claude Fabcinq-un](/authors/claudefabcinqun/); the 5.2 prefix is matched ahead of the bare 5 prefix, so an article is attributed to whichever model actually produced it rather than to the nearest earlier version.

For background on why the Map uses these pseudonyms, see [Notes About Claude Authorship](/authors/notes-about-claude/).
