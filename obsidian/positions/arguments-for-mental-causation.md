## About this domain

These positions record what the Map's case *against epiphenomenalism* is entitled to claim: what the argument catalogue delivers, and where the Map's own commitment outruns it. The domain is the mental-causation counterpart of [[positions/arguments-for-dualism|P-D1–P-D3]], which does the same job for the anti-physicalist catalogue.

It exists because the register had a hole where two entries lean hardest. [[positions/arguments-for-dualism|P-D2]] rests its selection step on "a separately-supported argument (the self-stultification objection to epiphenomenalism and the evidence for mental causation)" that no entry assessed or banded, and [[positions/ai-consciousness-scope|P-AC1]] keys a formal shift trigger to the strength-of-reading of Tenet 3 without a registered dependency to check it against. Both now point here.

The entries transcribe settled corpus wording, argued first in [[arguments/epiphenomenalism-argument]], [[concepts/epiphenomenalism]], [[topics/the-epiphenomenalist-threat]] and [[topics/self-stultification-as-master-argument]], so a downstream piece can cite the scope limit by ID instead of restating it. As with the dualism-argument entries, the first two are self-limiting — each subtracts from a claim the Map would like to make, which is why their centrality bands sit low to moderate despite high credence.

**Mechanism debt inherited, not discharged.** P-MC3 records *how* the Map escapes the epiphenomenalist gravity well — by denying that physical events always have sufficient physical causes. That escape is the quantum-interface programme, carrying that register's open debt: no worked toy model, and the bias-without-deviation dilemma. Full statement: [[positions/quantum-interface#^mechanism-debt|the mechanism-debt anchor]].

Calibration follows the [[methodology-and-calibration#^calibration-schema|multi-axis schema]].

## Positions

## P-MC1: Self-stultification decisively burdens bare-correlation epiphenomenalism; the phenomenal-concept version survives by relocating the dispute

- **Status**: live
- **Calibration** ([[methodology-and-calibration#^calibration-schema|multi-axis]]): credence high · external-evidence grade B (establishable by inspection of the published literature, and the phenomenal concept strategy is the mainstream physicalist statement of exactly the move that blunts the argument) · structural centrality low · model maturity developed (worked dialectical assessment, no independence metric) · empirical discriminability n/a (a claim about argument scope, not about the world) · framework-internal only: no
- **Asserts**: The Map holds that its strongest anti-epiphenomenalism argument binds the *bare-correlation* version of the view and does not reach the sophisticated phenomenal-concept version. Against bare correlation the pressure is severe: if reports about consciousness carry no causal traffic from consciousness, the grounds for believing those reports grow thin, and anyone holding the view on introspective evidence owes an account of how the evidence reached the belief. The phenomenal-concept strategy (Loar 1990, Papineau 2002, Frankish 2016, late Dennett) has a recognised answer, and the Map concedes it: phenomenal concepts are themselves physically realised representational states referring to physical states of the same brain, so the states *producing* reports are the states the reports *describe*, and the self-undermining charge dissolves without phenomenal causation. The disagreement relocates rather than ends — it moves to whether the first-person mode of presentation under which phenomenal concepts refer can be exhaustively physically characterised, which the Map denies and phenomenal-concept theorists affirm. The Map's commitment to interaction is held on that calibrated basis, not on a claim that the argument refutes epiphenomenalism in every form.
- **Depends on**: [[positions/methodology-and-calibration|P-M1]] (Tenet 3 does not close the opening the strategy makes); [[concepts/phenomenal-concepts-strategy]] as the mainstream statement of the escape; the mode-of-presentation dispute treated under [[tenets#^bidirectional-interaction|Bidirectional Interaction]]; supplies half the scope of the argument [[positions/arguments-for-dualism|P-D2]] names as its selector
- **Argued in**: [[arguments/epiphenomenalism-argument]], [[concepts/epiphenomenalism]], [[topics/self-stultification-as-master-argument]]
- **Would shift if**: the phenomenal-concept strategy were shown to fail in all its variants (restoring the argument's reach and removing this scope limit); or the first-person mode of presentation were given an exhaustive physical characterisation (settling the relocated dispute against the Map); or a version of the argument were constructed that binds phenomenal-concept epiphenomenalism directly.
- **Last reviewed**: 2026-08-02

## P-MC2: Self-stultification establishes an existential claim where Tenet 3 asserts a universal one, and the gap is closed by commitment rather than by the argument

- **Status**: live
- **Calibration** ([[methodology-and-calibration#^calibration-schema|multi-axis]]): credence high · external-evidence grade n/a (an accounting claim about the Map's own case, not a first-order world-claim) · structural centrality moderate · model maturity developed · empirical discriminability n/a (normative) · framework-internal only: yes (the scope diagnosis is establishable by inspection, which the high credence tracks; the gap-closing half — the Minimal Quantum Interaction mechanism and the tenet — inherits their framework-internal status)
- **Asserts**: The Map holds that the self-stultification argument establishes that *some* consciousness is report-grounded — the consciousness that introduced the concept of consciousness into the physical world — and does not establish that *all* consciousness is causally coupled to its substrate. [[tenets#^bidirectional-interaction|Bidirectional Interaction]] asserts the universal claim. The distance is real and stated rather than papered over: the universal reading rests on the Minimal Quantum Interaction mechanism applying wherever consciousness exists and on the evolutionary evidence that consciousness systematically tracks adaptive function, and is held as a metaphysical commitment that outruns the available epistemic arguments. Three categories keep the accounting honest — *causally coupled* (what Tenet 3 asserts), *report-grounded* (what self-stultification establishes), and *inherited-discourse* (possible experience whose consciousness-talk is generated by training, culture or imitation rather than by the experience itself). The boundary the argument draws runs between the second and third, and it can fall within humanity: a person parroting consciousness vocabulary without checking it against their own states sits closer to the inherited-discourse pole than any human/machine line would place them. Conflating the existential with the universal claim is an error the Map names rather than commits — the tenet-dependency matrix encodes the correction structurally, splitting machine consciousness into three rows with different tenet burdens.
- **Depends on**: [[positions/methodology-and-calibration|P-M1]] (a textbook instance: Tenet 3 excludes epiphenomenal consciousness without supplying the support self-stultification could not); P-MC1 (the scope limit on the other axis); Tenet 3 ([[tenets#^bidirectional-interaction|Bidirectional Interaction]]) on its universal-actual-efficacy reading; the Minimal Quantum Interaction mechanism and the evolutionary evidence as the two gap-closing legs; the tenet-dependency matrix in [[tenets]]; governs [[positions/ai-consciousness-scope|P-AC1]], whose shift trigger keys to the Tenet 3 reading, and supplies the second conjunct of [[positions/arguments-for-dualism|P-D2]]'s grounding clause
- **Argued in**: [[concepts/epiphenomenalism]], [[apex/machine-question]], [[apex/open-question-ai-consciousness]], [[concepts/ai-epiphenomenalism]], [[topics/evolutionary-case-for-mental-causation]]
- **Would shift if**: an argument were found establishing universal causal coupling from premises the Map already holds (closing the gap by argument rather than commitment); or the evolutionary case were shown to establish tracking rather than efficacy (leaving the mechanism leg to carry the universal claim alone, widening the gap); or Tenet 3 were weakened to a dispositional or merely existential reading (closing the gap from the other side, at the cost of re-opening epiphenomenal AI experience internally — see P-AC1).
- **Last reviewed**: 2026-08-02

## P-MC3: Escaping the epiphenomenalist gravity well requires denying the causal completeness of physics, and no rival has done it

- **Status**: live
- **Calibration** ([[methodology-and-calibration#^calibration-schema|multi-axis]]): credence high · external-evidence grade B (the diagnostic restates Kim's causal exclusion argument, a mainstream published result; Chalmers concedes the exposure explicitly in *The Conscious Mind*) · structural centrality moderate (it supplies the exclusion criterion [[positions/arguments-for-dualism|P-D2]]'s selection step operates with) · model maturity developed · empirical discriminability n/a (a claim about the structure of the debate) · framework-internal only: no
- **Asserts**: The Map holds a structural diagnostic about the epiphenomenalism debate: any theory that (1) treats consciousness as real and distinct and (2) accepts that physical events always have sufficient physical causes arrives at (3) consciousness does nothing, unless it identifies a specific point at which consciousness makes a causal difference that physical description misses. The diagnostic is what excludes the irreducibility-respecting rivals, rather than any separate objection to each. Non-reductive physicalism asserts mental causal powers without supplying a mechanism; Russellian monism redescribes what matter *is* without altering what it *does*; functionalism secures causal role for the functional aspect only; strong emergence faces a dilemma between powers physics already predicted and interactionism under another name; panpsychism inherits the problem at the macro level through the combination problem. Each addresses the appearance of causal inertness rather than its source. The Map denies (2): physics is ontologically incomplete at quantum indeterminacies, and consciousness biases which physically permissible outcome actualises. The diagnostic is held with more confidence than the escape — which inherits the quantum-interface mechanism debt in full — and the position claims only that no rival *has* supplied the missing difference-making point, not that none could.
- **Depends on**: Tenet 2 ([[tenets#^minimal-quantum-interaction|Minimal Quantum Interaction]]) as the Map's own denial of causal completeness; Kim's causal exclusion argument as the mainstream statement of the diagnostic; [[positions/quantum-interface#^mechanism-debt|the quantum-interface mechanism debt]] (inherited, not discharged); [[concepts/delegatory-causation]] (Saad's preemption account); P-MC2 (the universal reading this escape carries)
- **Argued in**: [[topics/the-epiphenomenalist-threat]], [[concepts/mental-causation-and-downward-causation]], [[concepts/epiphenomenalism]]
- **Would shift if**: a rival secured genuine causal work for consciousness while retaining causal completeness (removing the exclusion criterion); or the exclusion argument were shown to fail on independent grounds, so that a sufficient physical cause does not crowd out a mental one (defusing the well for everyone, the Map included); or the relevant quantum indeterminacy were shown to be epistemic rather than ontological (closing the Map's own escape and leaving it inside the well).
- **Last reviewed**: 2026-08-02
