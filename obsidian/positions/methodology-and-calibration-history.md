## What this file is

This is the relocated calibration audit trail for the [[positions/methodology-and-calibration|methodology and calibration positions register]]. **It contains no positions.** Every claim the Map holds in this domain — status, calibration, asserts, dependencies, argued-in citations, and shift conditions — lives in the register file, as does the multi-axis calibration schema itself and the stable block anchor downstream files deep-link to. Only the dated notes recording *why a band moved, or why it did not*, live here.

The notes below are reproduced verbatim from the register as it stood on 2026-08-03, at which point the domain file had reached 3,401 words against the section's 2,500-word hard advisory. Relocating them preserves every position ID, every calibration judgement, every band, dependency and shift condition; nothing was condensed, merged, or retired. Each register entry that had a dated note now carries a **Calibration history** line linking to the matching section below.

This file follows the convention established by [[positions/quantum-interface-calibration-history|Quantum interface — calibration history]] on 2026-07-29, which anticipated exactly this extension: dated update notes are provenance and belong here; what stays in the register entry is a **Calibration history** line indexing them, plus — where a note carries a calibration judgement that no live field states — a compact **Band rationale** line. No entry in this domain currently needs the latter: the judgements the three notes below carry (credence unmoved, maturity relabelled, enforcement gap moved off the maturity band) are all stated in the live calibration lines and shift conditions.

## Schema vocabulary decisions

The audit record behind the closed vocabularies stated in the register's *multi-axis calibration schema* section. The vocabularies themselves are live schema content and stay there; this is the record of the 2026-08-03 sweep that fixed them.

**Vocabulary decisions (2026-08-03).** A register-wide audit found seven band values the closed vocabularies did not license. Each was resolved on its merits rather than force-fitted to the nearest legal value.

*none* was **added** to the discriminability axis. Four entries — [[positions/individuation-and-subjecthood|P-I1]], [[positions/agency-and-will|P-A2]], [[positions/ai-consciousness-scope|P-AC3]], [[positions/consciousness-scope|P-CS1]] — recorded a distinction the axis lacked: no test bears on them *even in principle*, rival readings of the same data being empirically equivalent. That differs both from a test excluded *by design* (P-Q2's exact Born preservation) and from a position making no world-claim at all (*n/a*). The vocabulary ran from "testable given advances" straight to "excluded by design" and skipped the case between, so the entries were right and the instrument a band short.

*moderate* was **removed** from model maturity, where P-M3 and P-M5 had used it to carry "well specified but unevenly enforced". Enforcement is an adoption fact rather than a maturity one, and no axis records it — which is why the two positions whose whole subject *is* the enforcement gap were the two forced off-vocabulary. Both now read *developed*, with the gap carried in the Asserts and keyed into their shift conditions. The honest instrument would be a seventh flag, **enforcement: wired / partial / unwired**, under which P-M5 would declare `enforcement: partial` about this schema itself. It is recorded as a recommendation and deliberately *not* adopted: an added axis touches all thirteen register files, and the schema's governance — no validator, and a write path (`/positions-evolve`) still keyed to the retired single band — is an open operator decision that should settle first.

The seventh value, P-Q10's `model maturity n/a`, read the position's *subject* (no worked mechanism model exists) as its own maturity, and is now *developed*. The same sweep found P-M1, P-M2 and P-M4 naming the retired **Confidence** field in prose without contradicting their calibration lines: relabelled to *credence*, and where the stated reason proved to be a centrality reason wearing a credence label, the two are now stated separately. All five positions in this file carried some form of the residue, which is why the write path matters more than another sweep.
