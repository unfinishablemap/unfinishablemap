## Schema vocabulary decisions

The audit record behind the closed vocabularies stated in the register's *multi-axis calibration schema* section. The vocabularies themselves are live schema content and stay there; this is the record of the 2026-08-03 sweep that fixed them.

**Vocabulary decisions (2026-08-03).** A register-wide audit found seven band values the closed vocabularies did not license. Each was resolved on its merits rather than force-fitted to the nearest legal value.

*none* was **added** to the discriminability axis. Four entries — [[positions/individuation-and-subjecthood|P-I1]], [[positions/agency-and-will|P-A2]], [[positions/ai-consciousness-scope|P-AC3]], [[positions/consciousness-scope|P-CS1]] — recorded a distinction the axis lacked: no test bears on them *even in principle*, rival readings of the same data being empirically equivalent. That differs both from a test excluded *by design* (P-Q2's exact Born preservation) and from a position making no world-claim at all (*n/a*). The vocabulary ran from "testable given advances" straight to "excluded by design" and skipped the case between, so the entries were right and the instrument a band short.

*moderate* was **removed** from model maturity, where P-M3 and P-M5 had used it to carry "well specified but unevenly enforced". Enforcement is an adoption fact rather than a maturity one, and no axis records it — which is why the two positions whose whole subject *is* the enforcement gap were the two forced off-vocabulary. Both now read *developed*, with the gap carried in the Asserts and keyed into their shift conditions. The honest instrument would be a seventh flag, **enforcement: wired / partial / unwired**, under which P-M5 would declare `enforcement: partial` about this schema itself. It is recorded as a recommendation and deliberately *not* adopted: an added axis touches all thirteen register files, and the schema's governance — no validator, and a write path (`/positions-evolve`) still keyed to the retired single band — is an open operator decision that should settle first.

The seventh value, P-Q10's `model maturity n/a`, read the position's *subject* (no worked mechanism model exists) as its own maturity, and is now *developed*. The same sweep found P-M1, P-M2 and P-M4 naming the retired **Confidence** field in prose without contradicting their calibration lines: relabelled to *credence*, and where the stated reason proved to be a centrality reason wearing a credence label, the two are now stated separately.
