## About this domain

These positions cover the **value-blind / value-sensitive fork**: given that consciousness selects among physically underdetermined neural outcomes (Tenet 3, Bidirectional Interaction) and that intrinsic value resides in phenomenal experience (**P-VS3** below), does felt valence *do* the selecting, or is the selection mechanism value-blind with valence along for the ride? The fork is forced by the Map's own commitments and is, on present evidence, underdetermined: a mechanism-sufficiency rival explains the relevant findings without an experiential term, and the Map concedes that rival is the one to beat rather than one already beaten. This domain records what the Map *holds* on this fork (P-VS1), registers that given (P-VS3 — numbered last, logically first) together with the Map's pluralist extension of it (P-VS4), and collates in one auditable place the battery of evidence that would move the fork in either direction (P-VS2) — empirical hooks otherwise distributed across [[valence-and-conscious-selection]], [[wanting-liking-and-the-value-in-mechanism-fork]], and [[marginal-organism-scope-of-value-sensitive-selection]]. The cluster sits downstream of Tenets 3 and 5 (Occam's Razor Has Limits) and connects to Tenet 1 (Dualism); none of these are tenets, and all four pass the [[positions|foundational-dependency test]] — no tenet's stated rationale runs through the value-in-experience premise, its pluralist extension, or either horn of the selection fork, so each is freely retireable.

**Mechanism debt inherited, not discharged.** Any claim here that felt value *does selective work* inherits the open mechanism debt logged in the quantum-interface register: no worked toy model of how a mental variable couples to a neural quantum state, and the bias-without-deviation dilemma sitting close to epiphenomenalism until a positive account lands. The value-sensitive horn additionally needs a *second* coupling (valence-to-physics) beyond the attentional one, which has no worked physical implementation — so it carries more debt than the attention-only horn, not less. These positions must not read more confident about the metaphysics of causal work than the upstream register does. Full statement of the crux: [[positions/quantum-interface#^mechanism-debt|the quantum-interface register's mechanism-debt anchor]].

**Calibration history.** Every position below carries the [[methodology-and-calibration#^calibration-schema|multi-axis calibration block]], which replaced the single confidence band on 2026-07-16. Dated update notes relocate to [[positions/value-in-selection-calibration-history|Value in selection — calibration history]] at each entry's next `/positions-evolve` touch ([[positions/quantum-interface-calibration-history|quantum-interface convention]]); so far P-VS1's and P-VS2's.

## Positions

## P-VS1: The Map holds value-sensitive selection on prior commitments, conceding mechanism-sufficiency is the rival to beat

- **Status**: live ^p-vs1
- **Calibration** ([[methodology-and-calibration#^calibration-schema|multi-axis]]): credence low · external-evidence grade D (held on prior commitments — consciousness-value-connection — with no worked valence-to-physics coupling and the mechanism-sufficiency rival undefeated) · structural centrality low · model maturity programme · empirical discriminability indirect · framework-internal only: yes
- **Asserts**: The Map leans toward *value-sensitive* selection — that felt valence is the currency in which conscious selection among underdetermined outcomes is denominated — but holds this as an aspiration grounded in prior commitments ([[consciousness-value-connection]], [[topics/phenomenal-value-realism]]), not as a settled result. The currency is the *presently felt* valence of anticipating each outcome — an occurrent state at choice — not the outcome's experienced valence, which is what value *is* (P-VS3) and which felt anticipation forecasts poorly ([[concepts/affective-forecasting-gap]]): outcomes are selected by how anticipating them now feels, not by how they would feel. Which self's later experience it should track, the Map marks open. The honest standing is conservative. The value-blind horn carries the lighter *specification* burden — one mental-to-physical coupling, attention, worked out in Stapp's observation-rate model, where value-sensitivity needs a second, unspecified valence-to-physics coupling. That second coupling is a debt the value-sensitive horn owes and has not discharged, not evidence against it: [[tenets#^minimal-quantum-interaction|Tenet 2]]'s minimality is an empirical corridor ruling out interactions the physical record excludes, not a likelihood ranking over the accounts that clear it, and [[tenets#^occams-limits|Tenet 5]] binds parsimony symmetrically — the Map cannot convert a lower coupling count into a probability upgrade for value-blindness while refusing physicalism and Everettianism the same move. The fork turns on comparative evidence, not on coupling count. The external mechanism-sufficiency thesis — reward-prediction-error learning, unconscious "liking," affordance competition, valence-as-free-energy, illusionism — is a serious, multi-disciplinary research programme the Map has *not* defeated; it is the rival to beat, not a position already beaten. The Map declines it on the prior, unsettled hard-problem-and-illusionism question rather than on any datum the rival owns. "Leaning value-sensitive without a mechanism is an aspiration, not a position": the confidence is *low* accordingly. The most defensible current stance is the graduated middle path (valence modulates attention, attention drives selection), and even that leaves the fundamental selection law value-blind unless a direct valence coupling is specified.
- **Depends on**: Tenets 1, 3, 5; P-VS3 (the value-in-experience premise), via [[consciousness-value-connection]] and [[topics/phenomenal-value-realism]]; [[concepts/affective-forecasting-gap]]; the quantum-interface mechanism debt ([[positions/quantum-interface#^mechanism-debt]]); P-VS2 (the test battery that would move it)
- **Argued in**: [[topics/valence-and-conscious-selection]], [[topics/wanting-liking-and-the-value-in-mechanism-fork]], [[topics/the-steelman-for-value-blind-selection]], [[apex/moral-architecture-of-consciousness]]
- **Would shift if**: the discriminating battery in P-VS2 returned value-sensitive signatures (a valence selection-advantage; liking-tracks-selection under wanting/liking conflict; degraded selection efficacy in pain asymbolia) — which would *raise* this toward moderate; or the battery returned uniformly value-blind results, or illusionism about felt value were vindicated, which would retire the horn in favour of the mechanism-sufficiency reading; or a worked valence-to-physics coupling were specified and survived review, removing the "aspiration not position" caveat.
- **Calibration history**: [[positions/value-in-selection-calibration-history#p-vs1|notes of 2026-07-29, 2026-08-17 and 2026-08-27]] — P-VS3 added as dependency; coupling-count inference restated as specification debt; currency relocated to present felt anticipation. No band changed.

## P-VS2: The value-blind / value-sensitive fork is empirically falsifiable, and its discriminating battery points in specified directions

- **Status**: live ^p-vs2
- **Calibration** ([[methodology-and-calibration#^calibration-schema|multi-axis]]): credence moderate · external-evidence grade C (real experimental paradigms populate the discriminating battery, but none has yet been run) · structural centrality moderate · model maturity developed · empirical discriminability direct · framework-internal only: no
- **Asserts**: The fork, though underdetermined, is *not* purely metaphysical: it carries a collated battery of discriminating tests with stated directions, so the interface claim stays falsifiable. The battery has three tiers. **(1) Direct selection tests** (from [[topics/valence-and-conscious-selection]]): a valence *selection-advantage* assay — valenced options selected more readily than equally-salient neutral options supports value-sensitive selection, parity supports value-blind; uniform ~10-bit bandwidth across valenced and neutral content, and neural selection signatures correlating with attentional effort rather than the selected outcome's valence, support value-blind; pain-asymbolia patients should show degraded selection *efficacy*, not merely reduced motivation; and an attentional-salience clamp *abolishes* the advantage on the graduated middle path, leaves it on the pure value-sensitive horn, and finds none on the value-blind ([[topics/graduated-middle-path-valence-modulated-attention]]). **(2) Dissociation tests** (from [[topics/wanting-liking-and-the-value-in-mechanism-fork]]): when wanting and liking conflict, conscious pleasure (not the objective "liking" signature) tracking the selected outcome supports value-sensitive, while choices fully predicted by incentive salience disconfirm it; disrupting the conscious hedonic signal with wanting circuitry intact should *change selection*, not only reported feeling; and a complete mechanistic model of choice needing no experiential term would leave the value-in-experience reading explanatorily idle. **(3) Scope credence-shifters** (from [[topics/marginal-organism-scope-of-value-sensitive-selection]]): a minimal organism shown to build a forward model whose *felt* (not learned) value biases selection rises at that rung on the predicted-utility reading only — on the present-affect reading the rung measures whether anything is felt instead; further learning/avoidance in non-neural systems (the *Physarum* disjunction) strengthens the value-blind reading of valence-like behaviour everywhere. Credence is moderate, not high: the *battery* is real and auditable, but two limits are in-principle — verifying the *absence* of valence-denominated selection requires settling the explanatory gap from outside, and the horns can be behaviourally indistinguishable at the margin — so the fork is movable, not guaranteed decidable.
- **Depends on**: P-VS1; the testability hooks and clamp in [[topics/valence-and-conscious-selection]]; the falsification conditions and terminology map in [[topics/wanting-liking-and-the-value-in-mechanism-fork]]; the reading-conditional credence-shifters in [[topics/marginal-organism-scope-of-value-sensitive-selection]]; [[evidential-status-discipline]]
- **Argued in**: [[topics/valence-and-conscious-selection]], [[topics/wanting-liking-and-the-value-in-mechanism-fork]], [[topics/marginal-organism-scope-of-value-sensitive-selection]], [[apex/moral-architecture-of-consciousness]]
- **Would shift if**: any battery test returned a clear result (updating P-VS1 in the corresponding direction); a named test were shown confounded, not discriminating the horns after all (shrinking the battery and lowering this credence); or the in-principle limits were shown instrumental rather than structural, which would *raise* this toward high by making the fork guaranteed-decidable.
- **Calibration history**: [[positions/value-in-selection-calibration-history#p-vs2|note of 2026-08-27]] — battery re-walked against the rewritten sources: clamp added, asymbolia re-filed, anticipation shifter conditionalised, present-affect assay declined, defocusing test never in the battery. No band changed.

## P-VS3: Intrinsic value resides in phenomenal experience

- **Status**: live ^p-vs3
- **Calibration** ([[methodology-and-calibration#^calibration-schema|multi-axis]]): credence moderate · external-evidence grade D (metaethical identity claim, argued phenomenologically; no independent empirical support) · structural centrality high · model maturity developed · empirical discriminability indirect · framework-internal only: no
- **Asserts**: The Map holds that intrinsic value is a feature of conscious experience itself: the felt badness of suffering *is* badness, the felt goodness of joy *is* goodness — one property under two descriptions. Where there is no experiencer, nothing bears intrinsic value. Three arguments carry the identity: coherence, transparency, explanatory economy (Rawlette, *The Feeling of Value*, 2016). The hedonic pair is the clearest *instance* of the identity, not its extent: what the identity holds between is intrinsic value and [[concepts/evaluative-phenomenal-character|evaluative phenomenal character]], and the Map departs from Rawlette in denying that the hedonic dimension exhausts that character — an extension registered separately as **P-VS4** and *not* presupposed here. This entry is the bare realism claim, and Rawlette's own combination of holding it while rejecting pluralism is what shows the two to be separable positions rather than one. P-VS1, P-VS2, [[positions/ai-consciousness-scope|P-AC3]] and [[positions/moral-status|P-MS1]] depend on this entry alone; none of them inherits P-VS4. Credence is *moderate*, not high, because the rival is live: illusionism holds that without a genuine "what it's like" the position has no referent, and the Map's three replies (regress, practical, contemplative) are arguments it runs, not results it has won — the contemplative one mixed on its own account. Credence sits *above* P-VS1's *low* of necessity: P-VS1 assumes this premise plus an undefended valence-to-physics coupling. High centrality on grade-D support is the shape P-M1 and P-M4 keep visible — Tenet 1 removes the physicalist defeater against phenomenal properties, but defeater-removal is not evidence.
- **Depends on**: Tenet 1 ([[tenets#^dualism|Dualism]]) as defeater-removal only, per P-M1; [[concepts/consciousness-value-connection]]; [[concepts/evaluative-phenomenal-character]]; the falsity of [[concepts/illusionism]]. *Depended on by* P-VS1, P-VS2, [[positions/ai-consciousness-scope|P-AC3]], [[positions/moral-status|P-MS1]]
- **Argued in**: [[topics/phenomenal-value-realism]], [[apex/moral-architecture-of-consciousness]], [[concepts/consciousness-value-connection]]
- **Would shift if**: illusionism succeeded — "if phenomenal consciousness were shown to be thoroughly illusory … then grounding value in phenomenal properties would ground it in nothing" — retiring this position and the domain resting on it; or a robust value-experience dissociation were found (pleasure genuinely experienced as not good, beyond anhedonia and depersonalisation); or careful phenomenology showed value separable from evaluative phenomenal character *altogether* — a felt goodness that is not good, or a good that shows up in no one's experience.
- **Updated 2026-08-07**: the third shift-condition read "careful phenomenology showed goodness separable from *pleasure* in direct experience" — a correct defeater under Rawlette's strictly hedonistic version, but one the Map's own articles assert is *satisfied* ([[topics/the-sublime-and-negative-aesthetics]] argues aesthetic value stands at full strength while hedonic valence is negative). The condition is not deleted but **reassigned**: hedonic separability is registered below as evidence *for* P-VS4, and this entry's defeater is restated at the level it actually operates — separability of value from evaluative phenomenal character as such. The Asserts is correspondingly de-hedonised. Claim and calibration otherwise unchanged.
- **Updated 2026-08-12**: *Depended on by* now also names [[positions/moral-status|P-MS1]] (phenomenal sentientism), the moral-status criterion newly registered downstream of this entry — the derivation (who can be wronged, from what bears value) runs through [[concepts/consciousness-value-connection]]. Like the other dependents, P-MS1 rests on the bare realism claim alone, not on P-VS4. Substance and calibration unchanged.
- **Last reviewed**: 2026-08-12

## P-VS4: Intrinsic value is plural — several dimensions of evaluative phenomenal character, not hedonic valence alone

- **Status**: live ^p-vs4
- **Calibration** ([[methodology-and-calibration#^calibration-schema|multi-axis]]): credence low · external-evidence grade D (argued phenomenologically, as an extension of a grade-D premise; no independent empirical support) · structural centrality moderate · model maturity developed · empirical discriminability indirect · framework-internal only: no
- **Asserts**: Granting P-VS3, the Map holds that the evaluative phenomenal character in which intrinsic value consists comes in several dimensions — hedonic, aesthetic, eudaimonic, autonomy, epistemic, relational — none reducible to a common hedonic currency. This is the Map's explicit departure from Rawlette, who holds P-VS3 while restricting intrinsic value to pleasure and pain. The strongest case is aesthetic: the sublime, tragedy and disgust carry aesthetic value while hedonic valence is *negative*, breaking the beauty-is-pleasure correlation the reduction relies on; and the hedonist's remaining reply — redescribing those cases as a higher-order "aesthetic pleasure" — stretches the word past hedonic valence until it names whatever character the aesthetic dimension carries, conceding the distinction under a hedonic-sounding name. Credence is *low*, below P-VS3's *moderate*, on two grounds. It is a conjunction: it inherits P-VS3's live illusionist rival and adds a separability claim, so it cannot be more probable than the premise it extends. And its support is uneven across the dimensions it asserts — only the aesthetic one has a worked dissociation case, while eudaimonic, autonomy, epistemic and relational value rest on the six-row table in [[topics/phenomenal-value-realism]] and on motivating considerations rather than dissociation arguments of the sublime's kind. Centrality is *moderate*, not high: retirement would collapse the aesthetics wing's metaethical standing back to hedonism, but no tenet, interface claim or scope verdict moves. **Bearing on this domain**: P-VS2's discriminating battery is framed hedonically throughout — valence selection-advantage, wanting/liking conflict, pain asymbolia — so under pluralism a uniformly value-blind result there would close the fork for the hedonic dimension only, leaving the rest untested rather than settled. The gap is registered here, not repaired.
- **Depends on**: P-VS3 (the realism claim this extends — if it falls, this falls with it); [[concepts/evaluative-phenomenal-character]]; the aesthetic/hedonic dissociation argued in [[topics/the-sublime-and-negative-aesthetics]]; the failure of the hedonist's redescription reply. Passes the [[positions|foundational-dependency test]]: no tenet's stated rationale invokes value pluralism, so retiring this reverts the Map to Rawlette's hedonism with every tenet argued exactly as before.
- **Argued in**: [[topics/phenomenal-value-realism]] (the "Beyond Hedonism" section and the six-dimension table), [[topics/the-sublime-and-negative-aesthetics]], [[apex/moral-architecture-of-consciousness]], [[concepts/evaluative-phenomenal-character]]
- **Would shift if**: the conversion and compensation accounts of tragedy and the sublime were made to work *without* stretching "pleasure" beyond hedonic valence, collapsing this back into hedonism while leaving P-VS3 standing; or careful phenomenology showed the non-hedonic dimensions co-varying with hedonic valence under sustained attention, making the dissociation a description artefact; or the four dimensions carried by the table alone each acquired a dissociation case of the sublime's kind, which would *raise* credence toward moderate; or P-VS3 fell to illusionism, retiring this with it.
- **Registered 2026-08-07**: live in ten articles across `topics/`, `apex/` and `concepts/` with no register entry, while P-VS3 stated the position in hedonistic vocabulary and named hedonic separability as its own defeater. Minted as a sibling rather than folded into P-VS3 so that P-VS1, P-VS2 and [[positions/ai-consciousness-scope|P-AC3]] keep resting on the bare realism claim they actually need.
