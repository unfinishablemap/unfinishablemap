## About this domain

These positions cover what the Map currently holds about the mechanism by which consciousness influences quantum-indeterminate outcomes in neural systems: which mechanism class is preferred, how the Born rule is treated, the scope and minimality constraints on the interaction, and where the programme remains incomplete. The cluster sits downstream of Tenets 2 (Minimal Quantum Interaction) and 3 (Bidirectional Interaction); none are themselves tenets, and each elaborates the *mechanism* of those tenets rather than supplying either one's rationale, so all ten pass the foundational-dependency test.

**Scope limit — no spatial localization.** These positions individuate the interface *dynamically*, by Born-rule treatment and by which token outcome becomes actual at a quantum indeterminacy, and not *spatially*: nothing here licenses conscious selection acting at particular neuroanatomical sites. Downstream arguments turning on which sites an intervention bypasses or preserves therefore import a premise the register does not carry. This is a gap rather than a denial — a localization claim would have to be argued and graded as a new position, and none is currently registered.

**Calibration schema.** Every position below carries the [[methodology-and-calibration#^calibration-schema|multi-axis calibration block]] (credence · external-evidence grade · structural centrality · model maturity · empirical discriminability · framework-internal only), which works hardest in this domain — P-Q2 and P-Q7 rate high on centrality where P-Q6 rates high on evidence, a difference the old single band hid. [[positions/methodology-and-calibration]] works that contrast through.

**Length and the split question.** This is the longest domain file in the register, over the section's hard threshold even after the 2026-07-29 relocation of its audit trail to [[positions/quantum-interface-calibration-history|Quantum interface — calibration history]]. Splitting has been declined twice, on 2026-06-08 and 2026-07-29, on three grounds: the ten positions form one densely-interlocked dependency graph crossing any candidate sub-boundary (P-Q9 on P-Q2/P-Q7 *and* P-Q5/P-Q6; P-Q10 over five others; P-Q4 and P-Q5 on P-Q1); both halves would still sit over soft; and a split would break addresses a dozen files deep-link, including `#^mechanism-debt` and the P-Q2/P-Q7/P-Q10 headings named by [[positions/methodology-and-calibration]]. Held whole for cohesion and address stability, not because nothing could move.

**Mechanism-debt convention.** P-Q3 (the bias-without-deviation dilemma) and P-Q10 (no worked toy model) together constitute the Map's *mechanism debt*. Any downstream article that asserts consciousness "does causal work" and builds a practical or normative conclusion on it inherits this debt rather than discharging it, and should not read more confident than the register does upstream. The criterion is that *use*, not membership of a domain list: agency, motor selection, value-sensitive selection, placebo and functional neurological disorder are where it has bitten so far, not the extent of what it binds. An outer-review convergence (2026-08-13, all three reviewers; the Claude review's bottom-line verdict was demote-to-coherence-only on the self-concealing-interface causal-selection thesis) tightened that comparative discipline into an explicit **citation grade**: the causal-selection thesis — that token-level selection is genuinely causal while exactly Born-preserving, and self-concealing in the aggregate because of it — is citable downstream as a *framework-internal coherence result only*, never as established mental causation, until the toy-model desiderata in [[apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy]] are met. No band moved: the cluster already sits at that grade (P-Q1 low-edge moderate, framework-internal; P-Q9 credence moderate, grade D). What changed is that the concession now binds the citation verdict rather than accompanying it — the review's charge was precisely that a disclosed defect was being neutralised as a finding while the thesis kept doing load-bearing downstream work. ## Positions

## P-Q1: Post-decoherence selection is the strongest current mechanism candidate

- **Status**: live
- **Calibration**: credence moderate (low edge; conditional on both the horn-(a) wager and the P-Q10 toy-model debt being payable — see *Band rationale* below) · external-evidence grade D · structural centrality moderate · model maturity programme · empirical discriminability indirect · framework-internal only: yes
- **Asserts**: Among the live mechanism proposals for how consciousness might influence quantum outcomes in neural systems, the post-decoherence selection family is currently the Map's preferred candidate. It is preferred because it sidesteps the warm-wet decoherence-timescale objection that Tegmark and others have pressed against pre-decoherence proposals (Stapp-Zeno, Orch-OR): selection acts on already-decohered branch-outcomes rather than on coherent neural superpositions. Here "post-decoherence" names the *timing of selection relative to environmental decoherence*, not a claim that branch-outcomes are already singular before selection acts: decoherence yields an improper mixture rather than realised outcomes with one already picked out, and under Tenet 4 (No Many Worlds) the selection *is* the effective reduction to a single actual branch, not a choice among already-actual ones. The advantage is confined to the *decoherence-timescale* axis and does not address the bias-without-deviation dilemma — *what* is selected once outcomes are Born-fixed, and *how* without deviating from those weights — which moving after decoherence relocates rather than resolves, and which P-Q3 tracks as its own live challenge. The preference is held as a working stance rather than a settled result; no worked toy model of the mechanism currently exists (see P-Q10).
- **Depends on**: Tenets 2 and 3; [[concepts/post-decoherence-selection]]; the decoherence-timescale argument; P-Q3 (inherits its open dilemma); the born-preserving conditional-signature trilemma ([[apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy]])
- **Argued in**: [[apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme]], [[topics/forward-in-time-conscious-selection]], [[topics/comparing-quantum-consciousness-mechanisms]]
- **Would shift if**: a working coherence-time calculation supporting pre-decoherence proposals in neural tissue lands; or post-decoherence selection turns out to require a mechanism (e.g., a retrocausal or final-boundary structure) that itself faces fatal physical objections; or a fourth mechanism class emerges that handles the decoherence problem with fewer commitments. **Falls to *low* credence** if the born-preserving conditional signature — horn (a): P(O | do(C), X) ≠ q(O | X) for some specifiable context X — proves unspecifiable or unrealizable in principle and horn (b) obtains: if no grain of conditioning ever separates a selected from an unselected outcome, "selection" collapses to an epiphenomenal redescription of chance and the mechanism preference loses its content ([[apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy]]). **Also falls to *low* credence** if the Map commits *exclusively* to Route 1 (trumping) and forecloses Route 2 (minimum-outside-corridor): a sole-Route-1 framework realizes horn (b) in practice, since the flagship itself concedes that under corridor-plus-trumping the causal and the epiphenomenal readings make no predictively distinct claims at any scale, leaving the distinction entirely metaphysical. The mechanism preference would then rank routes none of which is even in-principle interface-discriminating, losing the horn-(a) support the low-edge moderate rests on. The moderate band is therefore held partly *because* the Map keeps Route 2 open.
- **Subordination scope (2026-06-10, 2026-06-14)**: where the Map expresses a *how*-of-selection preference it favours forward-in-time post-decoherence selection, so time-symmetric / retrocausal readings are subordinate fallbacks rather than competing primary claims — both the weakly-held *atemporal-selection* fallback carried by [[positions/agency-and-will]] P-A3 (Libet timing) and the transactional-interpretation / two-state-vector-formalism (TI/TSVF) route in [[topics/qm-interpretations-beyond-many-worlds]] (high conceptual resonance, low mechanism specificity).
- **Band rationale (2026-07-16, 2026-07-27)**: two convergent outer reviews pressed a vacuity charge. It is answered *in principle* — horn (a) is specifiable ([[apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy]], "Where a Selection Could Show, If Anywhere") — so the credence was not cratered to *low*. But the honest residue forces a notch down within *moderate*: the signature is currently **unmeasured**, horn (b) remains live, P-Q10 stands, and bounded Occam / Tenet 5 licenses humility rather than a firm preference ordering. The schema's credence axis has no sub-moderate band, so that notch is carried by the low-edge conditionalization rather than a relabel. The 2026-07-27 trumping concession does not *by itself* force a further drop: its empirical silence is already priced into the low-edge pin, and this entry ranks mechanisms on the *decoherence-timescale* axis, so the efficacy-indistinguishability concession bears primarily on P-Q3, with its downstream reach already capped by the mechanism-debt convention.
- **Calibration history**: [[positions/quantum-interface-calibration-history#p-q1|notes of 2026-06-05, 2026-07-16, 2026-07-25 and 2026-07-27]] — the full narrative behind the band rationale above, plus two clarifications absorbed into the Asserts paragraph. No note changed a band.

## P-Q2: The default reading of MQI preserves Born statistics exactly

- **Status**: live
- **Calibration**: credence high · external-evidence grade D · structural centrality high · model maturity developed · empirical discriminability none-by-construction · framework-internal only: yes
- **Asserts**: In the Map's default reading of Minimal Quantum Interaction, conscious selection acts within a corridor whose outputs are per-trial indistinguishable from unbiased Born statistics. The interaction biases *which* outcome becomes actual within a given trial, but the aggregate distribution over many trials matches what standard quantum mechanics predicts. This is structural to the default reading, not an empirical claim about neural systems.
- **Depends on**: Tenet 2; [[concepts/causal-consistency-constraint]]; the no-signalling argument (Torres Alegre 2025, arXiv:2512.12636 — an arXiv preprint, not yet peer-reviewed; a compatibility result, not positive support)
- **Argued in**: [[topics/born-rule-and-the-consciousness-interface]], [[concepts/causal-consistency-constraint]], [[apex/phenomenology-mechanism-bridge]]
- **Would shift if**: an alternative MQI reading is shown to be both physically permissible and required for genuine agency (the "minimum-outside-the-corridor" branch the Map currently leaves open as a fall-back); or the corridor reading is shown to be incompatible with the kind of *selection* the Bidirectional Interaction tenet requires.
- **Calibration history**: [[positions/quantum-interface-calibration-history#p-q2|notes of 2026-07-16 and 2026-07-25]] — the multi-axis migration and the surfacing of Torres Alegre 2025 as an unrefereed compatibility result. Neither changed the claim.

## P-Q3: The bias-without-deviation dilemma is the strongest live challenge to the default mechanism

- **Status**: live
- **Calibration**: credence high (that the dilemma is genuine and unresolved) · external-evidence grade D · structural centrality high · model maturity programme · empirical discriminability indirect (horn (a) of [[apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy]] gives this question in-principle signatures conditioned on intentions, tasks, subjects or context, and one coarse-grain conditional test has already run — the preregistered intention-to-RNG nulls of Maier et al. 2018; the finer grains no instruction reproduces stay untested) · framework-internal only: yes
- **Asserts**: The default MQI reading faces a real philosophical-of-physics challenge: to be genuine *selection* the influence must do something the unbiased Born distribution does not, but to be *minimal and undetectable* it must not deviate from Born statistics. If the aggregate statistics are exactly preserved (P-Q2) and the selected outcome is observer-indistinguishable from an unselected one, the position sits genuinely close to epiphenomenalism — a framework-boundary pressure the Map marks honestly rather than treats as dissolved. The Map currently treats this as the strongest challenge to answer, not as a refutation, and locates a *candidate* resolution in the per-trial-vs-ensemble distinction and the indexical character of which outcome an experiencing subject actualises; whether that move discharges the epiphenomenalism worry or merely relocates it is the open question. The challenge is acknowledged as under-confronted in the current corpus and is on the active task list. ^mechanism-debt
- **Depends on**: P-Q2; Stapp–Bourget formulation; Chalmers and McQueen (2022), arXiv:2105.02314 — offered by its authors as "a research program here worth exploring" rather than a result, and bearing on the dilemma's formulation rather than on the Map's dualism: they hold that the consciousness-collapse thesis "need not lead to dualism" and is "compatible with materialist views on which consciousness is a complex physical property", and where they do develop a dualist reading it is *property* dualism, with consciousness "a fundamental property akin to spacetime, mass, and charge", rather than the substance reading this cluster leans toward
- **Argued in**: [[topics/born-rule-and-the-consciousness-interface]] (lightly); [[concepts/ensemble-level-epiphenomenalism]] gives the sharpest route-by-route statement of this corridor-channel-vs-idleness question (trumping and minimum-outside-the-corridor routes, each rated honestly) without resolving it, and flags a layering tension: the corridor's own per-trial selection is itself a difference-making act, continuous with the minimum-outside-corridor route but discontinuous with trumping's difference-making-free authority, so reading it through the trumping lens stacks two structurally different channels rather than one. A deeper positive engagement is pending in the outer-review task queue
- **Would shift if**: the per-trial-vs-ensemble move is shown to collapse on closer analysis (e.g., the indexical reading turns out to require something stronger than the corridor permits); or a positive account of how the bias makes a phenomenological difference without making a statistical one is worked out and survives review.
- **Calibration history**: [[positions/quantum-interface-calibration-history#p-q3|notes of 2026-06-16 through 2026-07-28]]. Two bear on the shift conditions above: the 2026-07-27 trumping concession in [[apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy]] **reinforces** this position rather than shifting it, the credence here being credence *that the dilemma is real and open*, so the propagation was carried by P-Q1's demotion trigger instead; and [[concepts/inverted-qualia]]'s type/token distinction is registered (2026-07-28) as a **candidate** account of the kind the second shift condition asks for, explicitly not as satisfaction of it. The debt is not discharged; no band changed.
- **Updated 2026-08-24**: empirical discriminability `none-by-construction` → `indirect`. That band belongs to P-Q2, whose subject is the *unconditioned* Born marginal and whose rating is untouched and still correct. P-Q3's subject is different — whether there is genuine selection rather than metaphysical relabelling — and [[apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy]] locates that question's exposure in *conditional* structure, which Born preservation leaves free ([[concepts/selection-only-channel]] shows marginal preservation is compatible with maximal conditional dependence). A `none-by-construction` rating could not stand while the corpus keeps conditionally different distributions live. No other band moved: the credence here is credence *that the dilemma is real and open*, and re-rating its testability does not settle it.

## P-Q5: Orch-OR is currently demoted relative to post-decoherence selection

- **Status**: live
- **Calibration**: credence moderate (the 2026-07-31 re-basing is held at *constant* credence — a 2026-08-13 review reverted that pass's one-band raise as defeater-removal inflation; see history) · external-evidence grade C · structural centrality low · model maturity programme · empirical discriminability indirect · framework-internal only: no
- **Asserts**: Penrose-Hameroff Orchestrated Objective Reduction is ranked below post-decoherence selection and is not retired. The demotion rests on the biophysical coherence record — Tegmark (2000), and Reimers, McKemmish and colleagues (2009, 2014) finding the revised Hagan-Hameroff-Tuszyński (2002) parameters either unphysical or descriptive of regimes microtubules do not occupy in living tissue — and *not* on the underground radiation bounds, which test a Poissonian, Markovian Diósi-Penrose completion Orch-OR never specified. That leaves Orch-OR untested on the radiation channel rather than constrained, and untested *because* underspecified, which is not a point in its favour. Derakhshani et al.'s (2022) "highly implausible in all the cases analyzed" verdict is likewise conditional on a DP pairing its own authors say further-modified collapse models could reopen.
- **Depends on**: P-Q1, plus the coherence-feasibility record cited above. **Not P-Q6** — the radiation bounds do not reach this position
- **Argued in**: [[topics/penrose-gravity-induced-collapse-empirical-prospects]], [[topics/comparing-quantum-consciousness-mechanisms]], [[apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme]]
- **Would shift if**: a microtubule coherence-time model lands and survives review, which would re-elevate the family; or Orch-OR is supplied with the collapse dynamics it currently lacks, at which point the radiation bounds would bear on it directly; or Orch-OR develops a specific neural signature that post-decoherence selection does not predict. Retiring it would take the coherence question closed negatively, not merely the DP pairing constrained.

## P-Q6: The parameter-free Diósi-Penrose model has been empirically falsified

- **Status**: live
- **Calibration**: credence high · external-evidence grade A · structural centrality low · model maturity formalised (settled experimental result) · empirical discriminability direct · framework-internal only: no
- **Asserts**: The 2021 Donadi et al. underground-germanium result (Nature Physics 17, 74–78) rules out the Diósi-Penrose model in the version whose regularisation length R₀ is fixed by physics rather than fitted. Some regularisation is mandatory (a point-like density makes the self-energy integral diverge), so what the null search excludes is *Penrose's own prescribed value*, the nuclear wave-function spread in the material (0.05 × 10⁻¹⁰ m for cooled germanium), not a limiting idealisation. Its bound of R₀ > 0.54 × 10⁻¹⁰ m at 95% confidence sits an order of magnitude above that prescription. The Map accepts the result. It falsifies the physics-level model and does not reach Orch-OR, which never specified the collapse dynamics tested here (see P-Q5).
- **Depends on**: empirical results, not tenets — Donadi et al. (2021); Majorana's 2023 erratum, `10.1103/PhysRevLett.130.239902` (bound corrected to R_DP > 2.54 × 10⁻¹⁰ m; the arXiv default still serves the superseded figure); Figurato et al. (2024), `10.1088/1367-2630/ad8c77`
- **Argued in**: [[topics/penrose-gravity-induced-collapse-empirical-prospects]], [[topics/comparing-quantum-consciousness-mechanisms]], [[topics/falsification-roadmap-for-the-interface-model]]
- **Would shift if**: the Donadi result is shown to have a methodological flaw or is overturned by a contrary experiment; or a parameter-free DP variant survives the radiation bounds. **Extension rather than shift**: Figurato et al. (2024) cap the free-R₀ successor at R₀ ≲ 10⁶ Å, beyond which collapse no longer delivers macroscopic classicality. Should that ceiling meet the radiation floor, this position would widen to the whole DP family.
- **Calibration history**: [[positions/quantum-interface-calibration-history#p-q6|notes of 2026-07-16 and 2026-07-31]] — the multi-axis migration, and the precision pass naming Penrose's own value as what was excluded.
- **Last reviewed**: 2026-07-31

## P-Q8: Warm-quantum-biology results are a precedent for the interface, not a licence for it

- **Status**: live
- **Calibration**: credence high (that these results are precedent, not licence) · external-evidence grade B (narrowed 2026-07-30 to one pillar: cryptochrome radical-pair magnetoreception is the surviving established warm-biology quantum result; photosynthesis no longer supports *long-lived electronic* coherence — see the shift-condition note below — and both bear only as precedent for the neural case) · structural centrality low · model maturity developed · empirical discriminability indirect · framework-internal only: no
- **Asserts**: Quantum effects in warm, wet biological systems establish that nature does sometimes use quantum mechanisms at scales and temperatures relevant to biology. The strongest surviving case is cryptochrome radical-pair avian magnetoreception. Photosynthetic energy transfer, long cited alongside it, no longer supports the claim in its *long-lived electronic coherence* form: Duan et al. (2017), *PNAS* 114(32):8493–8498, measured electronic coherence in light-harvesting complexes dephasing within roughly 60 femtoseconds and found no hint of a biofunctional role, and the long-lived oscillations once read as electronic are now attributed to vibrational coherence. Short-timescale vibrational/vibronic coupling is a live and different question. The Map treats what survives as a *precedent* (the regime is not categorically impossible) rather than a *licence* for the neural case (no coherence-time calculation supports the neural cases at present). Citations of warm-quantum-biology that read as support rather than precedent are calibration drift and are corrected when caught.
- **Depends on**: empirical results (cryptochrome radical-pair magnetoreception; photosynthesis, now only for short-timescale vibrational coherence); calibration discipline
- **Argued in**: [[tenets]] (calibrated treatment), [[topics/quantum-biology-and-neural-consciousness]]; an active task tracks an over-reading of this in [[concepts/interactionist-dualism]]
- **Would shift if**: a positive coherence-time calculation for the neural case is published and survives review; or the warm-biology results are themselves shown to admit fully classical explanations. **The second condition is partially engaged as of 2026-07-30**: Duan et al. 2017 returns photosynthetic energy transfer to an incoherent-transport account, so one of this position's two pillars has met it. Cryptochrome stands, so the precedent-not-licence claim survives in weakened form and the position is not retired; whether one surviving pillar still warrants credence *high* and grade **B** is referred to a `positions-evolve` band review.
- **Last reviewed**: 2026-07-30

## P-Q9: The interface is self-concealing by construction

- **Status**: live
- **Calibration**: credence moderate · external-evidence grade D · structural centrality moderate · model maturity programme · empirical discriminability indirect (aggregate channel silent by construction, but mechanism-level and psychophysical tests still bite) · framework-internal only: yes
- **Asserts**: Given the Map's tenets — minimal interaction, Born-statistics preservation, no detectable signal in aggregate — the interface is structurally selected to leave only indirect traces. This is a framework-internal architectural claim, not an empirical result, and does not raise the probability that the tenets are correct. Its value is that it predicts the pattern of evidence the framework should and should not produce: the absence of a direct neural quantum-interface signature is predicted, so it is not disconfirming; the presence of one would be surprising. Self-concealment is local to the *aggregate-statistics channel*, which remains silent by construction, and is not a global unfalsifiability shield. The residue the framework still risks is positive, named, and runs on two channels. At the *mechanism* level: a coherence-time result, a microtubule-signature result, or a Diósi-Penrose-style test can disconfirm specific positions in this register — the Donadi falsification of parameter-free Diósi-Penrose (P-Q6) is a live constraint that did bite — and the falsification roadmap names further exposure ([[topics/falsification-roadmap-for-the-interface-model]]). At the *psychophysical* level: because phenomenal character is held to be causally efficacious, any actual instance of qualia-inversion should produce subtle behavioural differences — in aesthetic preferences, emotional valences, reaction-time asymmetries, or the fine structure of introspective reports — which MQI's bandwidth-limited outbound channel predicts will show up in finer-grained measurements rather than in coarse discrimination tasks. That prediction is the Map's own empirical commitment and could falsify it, so self-concealment does not extend to it.
- **Depends on**: Tenets 2 and 3; P-Q2; P-Q7; P-Q5; P-Q6; P-Q3 and P-Q10 (the selection being concealed is posited-and-coherent, not established — the mechanism-debt citation grade above governs how this entry may be cited downstream)
