## About this domain

These positions record what the Map holds about **consciousness in thought** — whether thinking, understanding and reasoning have phenomenal character of their own, what that character does, and what the first-person texture of intellectual life is entitled to claim as evidence. The intellectual-life wing states its organising commitment in three articles at three grains — the hub [[topics/phenomenology-of-intellectual-life]] "holds a Phenomenal Constitution Thesis", [[concepts/phenomenal-constitution-thesis]] adopts it as "an abductive bet", and the spokes read first-person texture as constraining rival accounts without establishing anything — and until 2026-09-14 registered it nowhere: no position file mentioned cognitive phenomenology or PCT. The first entry below records the commitment once, so the three readings are visibly one position at three grains rather than three confidence levels, and so the spokes have a registered standard to cite.

The domain is kept separate from [[positions/arguments-for-dualism|Arguments for dualism]], where the entry was first proposed as P-D4, for a reason of kind rather than tidiness. That domain's entries are counting rules *about* the anti-physicalist catalogue and are self-limiting by design — its intro commits every one of them to being a restraint whose retirement would strengthen Tenet 1's case. P-TU1 is a first-order commitment whose retirement would weaken the wing that rests on it, so it would have falsified that intro; and the file stood forty-three words under its hard length threshold. The premise-sharing that domain records still applies here and is registered as a dependency, not restated.

**The foundational-dependency test.** P-TU1 passes in the forward direction and is freely retireable. [[tenets#^dualism|Tenet 1]]'s stated rationale is the explanatory gap for sensory consciousness, the judgement that materialism has not closed it, and the positive argument catalogue; none of those rests on thought having proprietary phenomenal character. Retiring P-TU1 narrows the gap's claimed domain back to sensation and leaves the tenet's rationale intact. What retirement would cost is downstream: the Chinese Room verdict, the symbol-grounding diagnosis and the Map's scepticism about LLM understanding are conditional on the constitutive reading, and every article that uses them says so.

Calibration follows the [[methodology-and-calibration#^calibration-schema|multi-axis schema]] (credence · external-evidence grade · structural centrality · model maturity · empirical discriminability · framework-internal only).

## Positions

## P-TU1: Thought has proprietary, individuative phenomenal character in the ordinary range; the Map adopts the constitutive reading abductively, and first-person texture constrains rival accounts without establishing irreducibility or causal efficacy

- **Status**: live ^p-tu1
- **Calibration** ([[methodology-and-calibration#^calibration-schema|multi-axis]]): credence moderate (at the upper end for qualified liberalism — proprietary and individuative cognitive phenomenology in ordinary cognition — and lower for the constitutive horn, which the corpus holds as an abductive bet against a live deflationary opposition and a live weak-liberal one) · external-evidence grade C (the dissociation base is real and independently established — aphantasics completing mental rotation analytically in Kay, Keogh & Pearson 2024, conscious thought with no sensory reduction base in Lennon 2023, attenuated ipseity in Sass & Parnas 2003 — but its *phenomenal* interpretation is contested where its architectural reality is not, and none of it bears on the constitution claim directly) · structural centrality moderate (Tenet 1's rationale does not rest on it; the intellectual-life wing, the Chinese Room verdict, the symbol-grounding diagnosis and the Map's LLM-understanding scepticism are all conditional on it) · model maturity developed (worked abductive case, no formal model) · empirical discriminability indirect (dissociation cases, neuroimaging of comprehension and contemplative report bear on the liberal claim; a clean zero case would bear on it directly) · framework-internal only: no (independent evidence bears on liberalism; the content-determinacy application of the constitutive reading leans partly on the Map's phenomenal-intentionality commitments and is flagged conditional wherever it is used)
- **Asserts**: The Map holds *qualified liberalism* about cognitive phenomenology: within ordinary cognition — the range in which tip-of-the-tongue states, feeling-of-knowing, expert perception and mathematical insight are reported — thinking has proprietary phenomenal character not exhausted by accompanying imagery, inner speech or affect, and that character individuates thoughts with different contents. The claim concerns *kind*, not intensity: the phenomenology admits of degrees and is dismantlable under contemplative discipline, so the conservative needs a zero, not a gradient. On that base the Map adopts the constitutive horn — the [[concepts/phenomenal-constitution-thesis|Phenomenal Constitution Thesis]], meaning constituted by phenomenal character rather than accompanied by it — and adopts it *abductively*, as the one account that unifies content determinacy, the understanding/processing distinction and the dissociation data where rivals handle them piecemeal, not as a demonstrated result, and unification is loveliness, so the [[inference-to-the-best-explanation-against-dualism|IBE consistency statement]] blunts this abduction exactly as far as the physicalist's; every downstream application (Chinese Room, symbol grounding, LLM-understanding scepticism) is conditional on it. What the first-person texture of intellectual life earns is calibrated below that commitment: phenomenal contrast, tip-of-the-tongue, aphantasia and insight *constrain* rival accounts by removing the simplest identity claim — that cognitive phenomenology just is sensory accompaniment — and they do not *establish* irreducibility, since a conservative redescription of each case remains available, nor the causal efficacy of the phenomenology, since the felt effort of reasoning cannot certify its own causal standing ([[positions/agency-and-will|P-A4]] governs that). The hub's "holds a Phenomenal Constitution Thesis" and the spokes' "constrains without establishing" are therefore one position read at two grains: the constitutive reading is what the Map holds; constraint-not-establishment is what the evidence delivers for it.
- **Depends on**: Tenet 1 ([[tenets#^dualism|Dualism]]) read as irreducibility, which the constitutive reading extends from sensation to thought; the [[concepts/phenomenal-contrast-method|phenomenal-contrast method]] with Fürst 2017's limit on it recorded — introspective judgements about phenomenology are regimented by the view initially held, the dialectical face of the three-faced irresolvability seam in [[concepts/cognitive-phenomenology]]; the Würzburg–Titchener precedent as the honesty anchor, a dispute of this structural shape having been abandoned rather than settled; [[positions/arguments-for-dualism|P-D1]] (the argument from cognitive phenomenology shares the phenomenal-realist premise with the rest of the catalogue, so it widens the gap's domain to thought without adding an independent confirmation); [[positions/agency-and-will|P-A4]] (the verification limit that caps what felt intellectual effort can certify); [[positions/arguments-for-mental-causation|P-MC1]] (the phenomenal-concept strategy's relocation of the dispute to the mode of presentation applies to cognitive phenomenal concepts as much as to sensory ones, which is why the constitutive reading's bearing on Tenet 3 is suggestive rather than decisive); [[positions/methodology-and-calibration|P-M2]] (the converging lines — tip-of-the-tongue, dissociation, contemplative report, insight, expert perception — share an introspective source and are discounted accordingly)
- **Argued in**: [[concepts/cognitive-phenomenology]], [[concepts/phenomenal-constitution-thesis]], [[topics/cognitive-phenomenology-and-the-irreducibility-of-thought]], [[topics/phenomenology-of-intellectual-life]]; the constraint-not-establishment reading applied spoke by spoke in [[topics/consciousness-and-the-phenomenology-of-translation]] and [[topics/phenomenology-of-intellectual-courage]]; the dissociation base catalogued in [[voids/imagery-void]] and [[concepts/phenomenology-vs-function-axis]]; the same constitutive structure, applied to the memory kind with content held fixed, is registered at P-MA1
- **Would shift if**: a clean zero case were found — genuine understanding, with reliable transfer and error-detection, and no reportable phenomenal character to the grasping — which would count against liberalism directly rather than by redescription (the existing partial dissociations — hyperlexia, savant computation, agrammatic aphasia — separate verbal output from understanding, not phenomenology from competence); or a physicalist account explained *why* neural states carry the specific character of felt necessity, closing the gap for intellectual phenomenology in particular, which would dislodge the constitutive reading short of a general solution to the hard problem; or the phenomenal-contrast cases were shown to reduce entirely to subtle sensory or evaluative differences, the deflationary reply made good, which would retire the constraint claim and the liberal one with it; or an induced-satiation paradigm paired trial-level reports of the meaning-character itself, form-level strangeness not counting, with a transfer-grade competence measure on the same items and found competence intact at reported zero, supplying that zero case on demand where existing studies test only shallow competence (Tian & Huber 2010's speeded-matching accuracy, Moulin et al. 2021's later recognition, both intact); competence falling with the report would favour the constitutive reading only weakly, a common cause being available. In the other direction, an independent marker of phenomenality that let the dissociation base be read non-interpretively would raise the external-evidence grade; nothing first-personal can, which is the Würzburg lesson.
