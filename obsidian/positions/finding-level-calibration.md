## About this domain

These positions record what the Map commits to about calibration at the grain of a *single finding* — how one result's claims are separated before either is cited, and at what discount each is then allowed to bear on the tenets. The domain is the finding-grain sibling of [[methodology-and-calibration|Methodology and calibration]], which registers the Map's conduct commitments at the claim, framework, and pipeline registers: the tenet-register / evidence-register separation (P-M1), the standing convergence discount (P-M2), the citation-verification and weight-class standard (P-M3), the framework-stage calibration (P-M4), and the disclosure-versus-enforcement gap (P-M5). Those operate on a claim, a convergence, a source, or the framework as a whole. These operate on one finding at a time, upstream of tier assignment — which is why they are registered apart rather than appended there.

The positions here are *normative*: commitments about the Map's own citation conduct, not first-order claims about consciousness or matter. Their empirical axes are therefore legitimately *n/a* under the [[methodology-and-calibration#^calibration-schema|multi-axis calibration schema]]. Neither these nor the methodology positions are tenets; each is a chosen discipline that could be revised or retired without disturbing the five foundational commitments. The domain's root is [[tenets#^occams-limits|Tenet 5]] read self-bindingly — the discipline binds the Map's *own* readings of its evidence, not only its critics' use of parsimony against it.

## Positions

## P-F1: A finding's architecture-tier and significance-tier claims are cited at their own discounts

- **Status**: live
- **Calibration** ([[methodology-and-calibration#^calibration-schema|multi-axis]]): credence high · external-evidence grade n/a (normative) · structural centrality high (it governs how the catalogue's largest empirical base — the three-route clinical dissociation convergence — is permitted to bear on dualism; three live positions are instances of it) · model maturity developed · empirical discriminability n/a (normative) · framework-internal only: no
- **Asserts**: The Map commits to splitting any finding it cites into two claims before either is discounted: the *architecture tier* — the structural fact the finding reports (an asymmetry, a dissociation, a separable feature, a dedicated mechanism) — and the *significance tier* — what the Map reads that structure as evidence for. The two do not inherit the same framework-dependence discount. An architecture-tier claim that survives restatement across rival vocabularies (functionalist, identity-theoretic, illusionist, predictive-processing, dualist) takes the smaller discount, because its reality is not hostage to one framework's machinery; the significance tier is stated in the Map's vocabulary and presupposes the Map's reading, so it takes the larger one. Citing "the finding" without naming which tier does the inferential work, or letting the architecture tier's robustness cover the significance tier, is the coherence-inflation pattern at the finding level and is flagged in review. The discipline's *own bounds are part of what is registered here*, not trimmings on it. It delivers **no quantitative discount factor**: "smaller" and "larger" are qualitative with reasoning attached, and a number would be exactly the systematic-looking figure masking a judgement-laden split that the countermeasures guard against. It delivers **no tier-graduation**: splitting a finding assigns neither claim a five-tier label, which remains the single-claim discipline's work downstream. And it **does not hold that the significance-reading is false** — the larger discount records *whose framework supplies the reading*, not that the reading is mistaken; a reader may grant both tiers, and what is forbidden is granting the second *because* the first was robust. The discount applies **differentially, not universally**: its whole content is the *gap* between the tiers, so it does work only where the architecture tier is genuinely more cross-vocabulary-robust than the significance tier, and where a finding's architectural and significance claims are equally framework-shaped it has nothing to separate and should not be invoked. Without that scope condition it becomes a universal solvent that would discount every interpretive claim the Map makes to the point of saying nothing. Credence is *high* because the discipline is held against the Map's own most-cited evidence and its most tempting inference, and because it is the rule the interface-architecture case must survive rather than a rule applied to rivals.

Instantiated by [[positions/quantum-interface|P-Q8]] (warm quantum biology as precedent, not licence), [[positions/consciousness-scope|P-CS4]] (the fragmentation cases as preferred interpretation, compatible-with rather than forced-by the data), and [[positions/voids-as-evidence|P-V2]] at the void grain, where the larger discount reaches the strict-prohibition limit case under the [[project/voids-circularity-discount|voids-circularity discount]].

- **Would shift if**: the cross-vocabulary-translatability test were shown to be unprincipled, or framework-relative in a way the asymptotic adversarial-audit response cannot absorb — the discipline's own stated honest limitation, and the criterion that fixes where the line between the tiers falls; or a principled *quantitative* discount factor were derived, which would change the discipline's kind from a direction-and-separation rule into a magnitude rule and require this entry to be rewritten rather than retired; or a class of findings were identified whose architectural and significance claims are systematically equally framework-shaped, which would narrow the scope condition rather than overturn the position; or the split were shown to be an artefact of the Map's own individuation of findings, with no stable architecture tier surviving across rival vocabularies, which would retire it.
