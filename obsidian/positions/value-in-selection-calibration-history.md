## What this file is

This is the relocated calibration audit trail for the [[positions/value-in-selection|value in selection positions register]]. **It contains no positions.** Every claim the Map holds in this domain — status, calibration, asserts, dependencies, argued-in citations, and shift conditions — lives in the register file. Only the dated notes recording *why a band moved, or why it did not*, live here.

It follows the convention established by [[positions/quantum-interface-calibration-history|Quantum interface — calibration history]] on 2026-07-29 and extended to [[positions/methodology-and-calibration-history|methodology and calibration]] on 2026-08-03: dated update notes are provenance and belong here; what stays in the register entry is a **Calibration history** line indexing them, plus — where a note carries a calibration judgement that no live field states — a compact **Band rationale** line. Unlike those two files, this one was opened before the domain's audit trail had outgrown its live content: the domain file measured 2,480 words against the section's 2,500-word hard advisory when P-VS2's first update note fell due, and 20 words of headroom would not hold it. Notes are relocated at the next `/positions-evolve` touch of each entry; the domain-level migration note and all four entries' notes are now here. P-VS3's two notes were relocated on 2026-09-05 under the domain's documented length relief rather than at a touch of the entry itself — the register had crossed its 2,500-word hard advisory and the relocation is what paid for P-VS2's two new scope qualifications; P-VS3's bands, Asserts and shift conditions were not read for that pass and are unchanged by it. No entry currently needs a Band rationale line. On 2026-09-14 P-VS2's notes, about half this file's bulk, were split out to [[positions/value-in-selection-calibration-history-p-vs2|a companion of their own]]; the split is by entry so each register line keeps one target.
