The [[memory-channel-interface-evidence|recovery-order-asymmetry article]] and the [[direction-of-interface-change|direction-of-interface-change concept]] both name, as outstanding future work, a discriminator the existing catalogue has not designed: a focal perturbation producing a channel ordering one reading forbids and the other permits, sited specifically on the *direction-of-travel* axis. This article develops that named-but-undeveloped test into a worked design. It is a **proposed experiment, not a result.** Nothing here asserts the discriminator has been run, and nothing here raises the probability that the [[filter-theory|filter reading]] is correct; the [[evidential-status-discipline|evidential-status discipline]] requires that the design be held at the design tier. Until the experiment is performed its discriminating verdict is genuinely underdetermined by the evidence: the design constrains *which* outcomes would count for and against each reading, but does not presuppose which way they would resolve. What the design contributes is a structure: a reversible focal perturbation applied separately in the *closing* and the *reopening* phase of a consciousness transition, with concurrent multi-channel probing, configured so that the substrate-symmetric production reading, in its static form, makes an **advance-stated prediction it can lose** about the order in which a high-cost channel goes dark and comes back. The discriminator is one-sided and narrow. That static production model predicts mirror symmetry once the system is equilibrated and forbids its failure, while the filter reading permits a dissociation without predicting one and forbids no ordering, the mirror-symmetric one included. A direction-blind substrate whose channels respond with unequal lags can produce the same non-mirror order under a finite-speed ramp, so the design must control for lag before a reversal tells against anything. The strongest production rivals, multistable state dynamics and the documented emergence circuitry of the anaesthesia literature, survive every outcome the design can produce (see [[#what-a-result-would-and-would-not-settle|what a result would settle]], below).

This is the companion to the [[targeted-lesion-discriminating-tests-between-production-and-filter-readings-of-the-memory-hierarchy|targeted-lesion discriminating-tests article]], and the two should not be conflated. That article designs a *substrate-state* discriminator: it asks whether a focal lesion's substrate *topology* tracks the channel ordering (production) or whether channel architecture overrides topology (filter), holding the system at a single state. The present article designs a *direction-dependent* discriminator: it asks whether the channel ordering is *the same in both directions of travel* (the simplest production prediction, at equilibrium) or *direction-dependent* (an outcome the filter reading permits but does not require). The substrate-state article cannot deliver this test, because a lesion is a fixed insult with no reopening phase to compare against its closing phase.

## What Each Reading Predicts and Forbids

The discriminating power of any test is exactly the size of the gap between what the rival readings *predict* and what they *forbid*. The direction axis opens such a gap on one side only, and only at equilibrium: the static form of the substrate-symmetric production reading forbids the equilibrated channel ordering from carrying a memory of the direction of travel, while the filter reading leaves that open.

The **substrate-symmetric production reading** — the simplest production account, on which one substrate both generates and recovers each channel — reads the degradation ordering off a single, direction-blind regional-vulnerability profile. On the Map's conjectured ordering the autonoetic channel is most vulnerable and the procedural channel least, which this reading puts down to how much cortical reconstruction work each depends on. Crucially, on this reading the *same* profile is read in both directions: the channel that fails first under deepening perturbation is the channel that recovers last under lifting perturbation, because the profile that orders failure is the profile that orders recovery. Read statically, with each channel's state fixed by the current perturbation level alone, this reading **predicts symmetry** of the channel ordering between closing and reopening, and **forbids** a clean reversal in which a high-cost channel that failed first comes back first.

**The forbidding holds only at equilibrium.** A law that runs the same way in both directions does not give time-reversed trajectories in a dissipative system, and a brain under a ramped perturbation is one. The minimum rival any design must beat is a lag model: each channel's internal disruption *qᵢ* relaxes toward the applied perturbation *p* with its own time constant (τᵢ dqᵢ/dt = p − qᵢ), and the channel fails whenever *qᵢ* exceeds a fixed threshold θᵢ. The model is direction-blind, with one threshold per channel, yet at ramp rate *r* a channel fails near the setting θᵢ + rτᵢ going up and recovers near θᵢ − rτᵢ coming down. With channel A at θ = 0.40, τ = 0.10 and channel B at θ = 0.45, τ = 0.20, ramped at r = 1, A fails first (0.50 against 0.65) and, as the perturbation falls, also recovers first (0.30 against 0.25): the clean reversal the static reading forbids. Matched ramp rates equalise *r*, not τᵢ, so they leave the effect in place; slowing the ramp shrinks the offsets and, below a critical rate, removes the reversal. What forces a mirror order is narrower than "one substrate, one profile": every channel must be a threshold read-out of one shared, ordered quantity, so that the channel lost first is regained last however that quantity moves. Static thresholds meet this condition. Unequal lags do not, since each channel tracks its own lagged quantity, and neither do multistable dynamics in which channels read different latent states.

The **filter reading** treats the channel ordering as a property of interface architecture rather than of a direction-blind substrate profile. If channels differ in bandwidth and reconstruction cost, *which* components block or reopen, and in *what order*, may depend on the direction and the cause of the change: partial blockade closes high-cost channels first, while a different perturbation — or the *lifting* of a perturbation through a dedicated reopening pathway ([[active-reboot|active reboot]]) — could reopen a high-cost channel out of degradation order. This reading **permits** the closing ordering and the reopening ordering to dissociate, and is **not committed** to their symmetry; it does not predict a dissociation, and it forbids no ordering. The reopening pathway is doing real work here, and its own mechanism is undischarged: [[active-reboot|active reboot]] is a *posited* route, not a specified one, and the [[mechanism-costs-dualism-thickness-quadrants|mechanism-costs cartography]] is where the dualist-thickness cost of positing it is tracked — invoking the pathway is not free, and a result that depended on it would inherit that cost.

The gap is real only if the production reading genuinely forbids what the filter reading permits. It does for the static, equilibrated form of the substrate-symmetric reading, and for nothing richer. That qualifier is why this is a constrain-not-establish test. Richer production accounts reach asymmetry through ordinary dynamics or documented machinery: unequal channel lags (above); multistable dynamics, in which a system leaving through a different attractor basin from the one it entered need not retrace its path; and emergence-specific circuitry, which is documented rather than posited. Genetic ablation of orexinergic neurons in mice "delays emergence from anesthesia, without changing anesthetic induction" (Kelz et al. 2008), and the literature reviewed under [[active-reboot]] treats exit from anaesthesia as more than the mirror image of entry. Invoking independently evidenced machinery is not a per-case addition a production account pays for, and since [[active-reboot|active reboot]] is the Map's interface reading of the same findings, the findings themselves favour neither reading. The test therefore discriminates the filter reading from the *simplest* production rival only. It raises the cost of production accounts that need new machinery to absorb a reversal, not the cost of production as such. This is the same calibration the [[memory-channel-interface-evidence|source article]] holds, transposed from observed clinical asymmetry to a controlled, advance-registered experiment.

## The Design: A Reversible Perturbation Applied in Both Directions

The experiment has six ingredients. The first three are shared with the substrate-state design; the last three are what make it direction-dependent.

**A reversible, focal, repeatable perturbation.** Unlike a lesion, the perturbation must be switchable so that one subject can be probed in both the closing and the reopening phase. Transcranial focused ultrasound is a candidate modality whose ramp-ability is unvalidated. Verhagen et al. (2019) showed that forty seconds of repetitive ultrasound to a medial frontal target in macaques changed that region's activation and connectivity profile for up to two hours, reversibly and without tissue damage; in humans, theta-burst ultrasound to the posterior cingulate lowered GABA, with changes that "last at least 50 mins" and may show "state- or location-dependency" (Yaakub et al. 2023). The modality has also reached human subcortical targets in disorders-of-consciousness pilot work (Cain et al. 2021) and the posterior cingulate in a pilot whose [[brain-stimulation-and-the-witness-mode#ultrasound|active–sham contrast was null]]. Its reversibility is what supplies the *reopening* phase, but these are *offline* after-effects of a brief sonication: a reopening phase would run in tissue still carrying the closing-phase effect, so the design needs either a carry-over term or closing and reopening phases in separate sessions, at the cost of between-session variability.

**A high-cost target channel.** The perturbation should preferentially load the autonoetic channel — which, on the design's working assumption, swings most in recovery order and so carries the largest predicted direction effect — by targeting a region the autobiographical-retrieval literature implicates in re-experiencing while sparing the regions that route noetic recognition. Its high cost is likewise a working assumption: the [[memory-channel-interface-evidence|source article]] holds the autonoetic-first ordering as "a hypothesis with exceptions, not an established cross-condition finding" and ranks channel cost partly by that same ordering, so no result here can confirm the cost ranking without circularity. The targeting further posits a spatial dissociation between re-experiencing and recognition substrates; the assumed selectivity is itself a precondition the experiment must check, not a result it can presume. So is target engagement: one ultrasound protocol's motor inhibition "was driven by peripheral auditory stimulation, not direct neuromodulation" (Kop et al. 2024), so the effect on the target must be shown against a sound-matched active sham, with exposure reported to the ITRUSST consensus standard, in situ estimates included (Martin et al. 2024).

**Concurrent multi-channel probing.** Autonoetic, noetic, and anoetic channels must be measured separately and simultaneously under the perturbation (remember-know judgements, source-memory accuracy, repetition priming, motor-sequence retention), so that the *ordering* among channels can be read at each moment rather than inferred across sessions. These probes "were not designed for tight cross-channel comparison under focal perturbation", as the [[targeted-lesion-discriminating-tests-between-production-and-filter-readings-of-the-memory-hierarchy|substrate-state design]] concedes, and they differ in response format, chance level, reliability and decision criterion. Without a measurement model (fitted psychometric curves, matched formats where possible, reliability estimates), the order read off them is an order of behavioural thresholds, not of latent channels. Which probe indexes which channel is itself contested: a state-trace analysis of 37 studies found "little or no evidence of bidimensionality" in the remember-know task (Dunn 2008), and source memory, often read as recollective, engages "familiarity-like, not recollection-like" neural patterns (Kim 2026). Probing in both phases also trains the participant through retrieval practice, growing familiarity and shifts in strategy, so the second phase measures someone the first phase changed. That is a further physical route to a direction effect, and a sham run through the same probe schedule must estimate it.

**Phase-matched ramping — the direction manipulation.** The perturbation is ramped *up* (closing phase) and, in a separate matched block, ramped *down* (reopening phase), at matched rates, with the multi-channel ordering recorded throughout each ramp. The pre-registered comparison is between the order in which channels degrade during the up-ramp and the order in which they recover during the down-ramp. The static production reading predicts these two orderings are reverses of each other; the filter reading permits them to dissociate. Matched rates alone do not equalise the channels' own response times (the lag rival, explained above), hence the next ingredient.

**Rate and dwell controls — the lag rival.** The ramp rate is varied across blocks: a reversal that shrinks and then vanishes as the ramp slows is lag. The perturbation is also held at each level until the channels settle, with equilibration verified independently of the channel read-outs (for example, by a concurrent neural index that has stopped drifting), and orderings are compared at matched *measured* neural state rather than matched administered intensity, since the same setting going up and coming down need not mean the same tissue state. The discriminating read-out is a reversal that persists in this quasi-static, state-matched limit. Surviving both controls excludes simple lag, but not multistability, whose escape times from an attractor basin can outlast any practical dwell, or emergence-specific circuitry, which acts on the way out and not on the way in.

**A hysteresis read-out.** Because the same perturbation is applied in both directions, the design also recovers the *concentration-analogue* hysteresis the anaesthesia literature reports — the loss-of-function and recovery-of-function thresholds for each channel. A static substrate-symmetric reading predicts a single threshold per channel, crossed at the same setting in both directions; a direction-dependent architecture permits the closing and reopening thresholds to separate, and permits them to separate *by different amounts for different channels*. That single threshold belongs to the channel's internal state: indexed to the administered setting under a finite-speed ramp, even the lag model separates each channel's two thresholds, by 2rτᵢ, an amount that differs between channels. Differential, channel-specific hysteresis rules out lag, as well as the static reading, only if it survives the equilibrated, state-matched comparison.

## Why the Anaesthesia Hysteresis Literature Both Motivates and Cautions

The design is motivated by, and disciplined against, the existing neural-inertia literature. Sepúlveda, Tapia, and Monsalves (2019) characterise *neural inertia* as an active resistance to state change: the effect-site anaesthetic concentration at loss of consciousness is usually higher than at recovery, so the two thresholds do not coincide. That whole-organism hysteresis is the macroscopic version of the per-channel hysteresis the design proposes to resolve.

The caution is sharper and must be stated where the design is, because it bounds what a positive result could claim. Proekt and Hudson (2018) argue that whole-organism neural inertia need not imply distinct induction and emergence machinery at all: a stochastic state-switching model reproduces the hysteresis without direction-specific machinery. A two-well bistable landscape driven by noise produces a hysteresis that *collapses over time* as the system relaxes toward a common dose-response curve — but the authors find that two states are not enough to capture the full signature (the negative correlation between the emergence threshold shift and the shallower Hill slope is not explained by a two-well potential), and extend the model to a ten-state Markov process (three awake, seven unconscious) to reproduce the left shift and shallower slope of emergence. On their account, induction and emergence can converge on the same underlying stochastic mechanism while still presenting an apparent asymmetry over realistic timescales. The design must therefore not treat any observed threshold separation as automatically favouring a direction-dependent architecture: a stochastic multistate production model predicts hysteresis — and even an *increasing variability* of recovery — without any direction-specific channel routing. That multistate rival is more formidable than a bare bistable one, not less, which sharpens rather than softens the caution. Human imaging adds a direction asymmetry in the physical dynamics themselves: under slowly titrated propofol, the dynamics changed gradually en route to unconsciousness, but regaining consciousness brought "an abrupt restoration of cortical but not subcortical temporal autocorrelation" and an abrupt boost in subcorticocortical connectivity, and "Pharmacokinetic effects could not account for the difference" (Huang et al. 2021). That finding strengthens the caution, not the filter reading. This is why the *discriminating* read-out is not hysteresis-presence but the **cross-channel ordering** under direction reversal — whether the channels recover in a *different order* than they fail. Proekt and Hudson's paper is silent on that question: its model has awake and unconscious states but no memory channels, so it neither predicts nor forbids a change in channel order. Extended to channels, a multistate transition graph can produce rank changes when different channels read different latent states, because the path out need not pass through the states the path in did. What prevents rank changes is nesting — every state that supports the high-cost channel also supports the lower-cost ones — which makes the channels threshold read-outs of one ordered depth variable (the condition explained above). The ordering test bites only on production models whose channels are nested in this way, and a richer production model can drop the restriction without strain. Conflating "hysteresis exists" with "the interface reading is favoured" would be exactly the inflation the [[evidential-status-discipline|evidential-status discipline]] exists to catch.

## What a Result Would and Would Not Settle

A clean **reversal** — a high-cost channel that fails first during the up-ramp but recovers first during the down-ramp, persisting in the quasi-static limit at matched measured neural state, net of practice effects, replicated within subject and across the focal target — would remove the static-threshold production model and the simple lag model, not substrate-symmetric production as a whole. Production accounts built on multistable dynamics or emergence-specific circuitry survive it, paying nothing extra where that machinery has independent evidence. What such an account still owes is a channel-level mapping — why its emergence route restores the high-cost channel first — which is the same specification debt the filter reading's reopening pathway carries (explained above); the per-case cost the [[mechanism-costs-dualism-thickness-quadrants|mechanism-costs cartography]] tracks falls only on machinery introduced to absorb this result. The reversal would *not* establish the filter reading, because production accounts of these kinds absorb it; the result discriminates against the *simplest* rivals, and any advantage it gives the filter reading stays comparative.

A clean **symmetry** — channels recovering in exact reverse of their degradation order, with single per-channel thresholds at equilibrium — would be the more consequential outcome for the Map, because it is the signature the direction-dependence family reports observationally failing to appear under controlled test. It would not falsify the filter reading, which forbids no ordering, but it would cost that reading this line of support. The [[direction-of-interface-change|direction-of-interface-change]] family is held at framework-internal weight precisely so that this outcome counts: a controlled symmetry result would withdraw the family's accommodation-grade support and leave the substrate-symmetric account unchallenged on this axis. Designing the test so that *both* outcomes are informative is what keeps it a falsification-programme contribution rather than a confirmation-seeking exercise.

There is also a null this design must respect. The four members of the direction-of-change family may share one upstream architecture, so even a positive reversal here is **not** an independent fourth or fifth confirmation to be tallied against the others — it is a controlled probe of the *same* thing the [[common-cause-null|common-cause null]] warns against over-counting. What a controlled reversal would add over the observational cases (anaesthesia emergence, terminal lucidity, dissociation) is not multiplicity but *quality*: a prospective, within-subject, advance-registered measurement that could replace retrospective and selection-biased case reports. The upgrade is in evidential grade on one axis, not in the number of axes.

## Relation to Site Perspective

The design engages two tenets at the methodology layer, framed honestly as a proposed falsification handle rather than as support the experiment has delivered.

**[[tenets#^dualism|Dualism]]** (Tenet 1) is engaged because the design exposes one of the Map's evidence families to test, though not the interface reading itself, which forbids no ordering: a controlled symmetry result would count against the direction-dependence the Map reads as evidence for a multi-channel interface, and the design is built so that this outcome is registered in advance rather than absorbed. The evidence is not shielded from the experiment; the symmetry branch is a genuine route to disconfirming one of the Map's evidence types, and no branch is a route to disconfirming the tenet.

**[[tenets#^bidirectional-interaction|Bidirectional Interaction]]** (Tenet 3) is engaged through a working assumption of the design, not a prediction of the filter reading: that any direction effect will be largest on the autonoetic channel, the channel usually described as involving the most active subject-participation. "Subject-participation", like channel cost, has no measure independent of the channel ordering it is invoked to explain, so the assumption carries no evidential weight of its own. If consciousness only received from the brain, the simplest expectation is that substrate readiness alone fixes the recovery order, so the equilibrated up-ramp and down-ramp orderings would coincide. That expectation is defeasible in the same way the static production reading is: multistable dynamics or documented emergence machinery could separate the orderings without any contribution from the subject. A direction-dependent ordering, were it found, would therefore at most weakly favour a reading on which the autonoetic channel is high-engagement in both directions; the favouring is constraining, not establishing, and is reserved for a result the design has not produced.
