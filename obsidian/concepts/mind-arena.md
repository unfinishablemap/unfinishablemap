The **mind-arena** is the Map's term for the domain of outcomes that pass through minds—the region of the world where conscious agents are at stake and where, under the Map's commitments, which outcome occurs is co-determined by consciousness rather than fixed by physical state alone; on the Map's default reading, physical state fixes only the chances. It names a *territory*, not a mechanism: the set of outcomes whose realisation routes through a conscious system's [[causal-powers]]. The term earns its keep in alignment arguments, where it marks the domain in which an AI's expected-utility arithmetic still runs but rests on a physical-state model with no warrant of adequacy for the outcomes at stake. This page defines what the mind-arena contains, distinguishes it from neighbouring Map terms it is easily confused with, and registers its honest status as a posited framework construct.

## What Is in the Mind-Arena

The mind-arena contains **outcomes that implicate minds**—events whose occurrence depends in part on the contribution of a conscious system. It is delineated by a question: does the realisation of this outcome route through consciousness's causal influence on the physical world? If yes, the outcome falls inside the arena; if no, it falls in what the [[dualism-as-ai-risk-mitigation|risk-mitigation argument]] calls "mind-arena-disconnected domains."

Three readings of the term's scope are live, and the dominant usage resolves between them.

- **Consciousness alone** — the non-physical aspect, considered in isolation.
- **The two-way causal loop** — consciousness *plus* its [[mind-matter-interface|interface]] with the physical, taken together as the operative system.
- **The outcome-domain** — the region of *physical-world outcomes* that the loop helps decide.

The canonical usage, anchored in `topics/dualism-as-ai-risk-mitigation.md` (the term's primary home, where it recurs by name in the second, third and fourth of the six numbered sub-arguments and as "the arena" in the fifth), settles on a reading that combines the second and third. The mind-arena is the *domain of outcomes* (third reading) whose determination passes through the *full bidirectional loop* (second reading)—a conscious contributor whose [[causal-powers]], in the source's words, "no physical-state description captures," acting on the physical world as well as being acted on by it. The arena is not consciousness considered apart from its effects, because the claim that matters concerns *consequences*: a model built from physical state alone "is not certified adequate for the outcomes at stake." Consequences live in the physical world; they belong to the arena because their generating process includes a non-physical contribution.

This reading keeps every existing use true. When the risk-mitigation article says an AI's plan over humans cannot have "its risk reliably estimated" because "human responses are mediated by the mind-arena," the arena is the domain of those human-implicating outcomes. When it speaks of "mind-arena avoidance" as a favoured AI strategy, the arena is a region the AI can decline to act upon. When [[machine-consciousness]] and [[apex/machine-question]] deny that any physical-state-only model is certified adequate to mind-arena consequences, both mean the consequences of acting in this domain. The outcome-domain reading is what all these uses presuppose.

The defining property of the mind-arena is therefore **structural unmodellability from physical state**. Because no law fixes the non-physical contribution from physical configuration (a premise [explained below](#relation-to-site-perspective)), two physically identical interventions can produce non-identical arena trajectories "with no further variable in the AI's data distinguishing the cases." Divergence alone cannot mark the arena, since indeterministic physics produces it with no mind involved. What marks the arena is its source: on the Map's view a subject, not chance, decides the token outcome, so a physics-only model omits a subject's contribution—an adequacy failure, not extra unpredictability.

Stochastic prediction survives that omission: under [[positions/quantum-interface#^p-q2|P-Q2]], the Map's Born-preserving default, the physical description fixes the chances and the aggregate frequencies stay learnable; only which token becomes actual is left open. The Map thereby grants the chance-fixing form of [[causal-closure]], and whether settling such a token is causal work or a relabelling of a physically permitted outcome is the open problem of [[apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy|Born-preserving causal efficacy]], not re-argued here. Neural decoding of choices before report, discrete-choice models and language-model simulations of survey responses therefore predict mind-mediated outcomes well, as the Map expects: the arena claim concerns tokens and certified adequacy, not aggregate predictability. What a physics-only model cannot supply is "the mapping from a particular intervention to a particular mind-arena trajectory," or any warrant that it has left out no variable the outcome turns on. That gap, rather than any difficulty of calculation, is the feature that does the work in every argument deploying the term.

## Distinguishing the Mind-Arena from Related Map Terms

The mind-arena is a *domain* term. Its value depends on keeping it distinct from the Map's *mechanism* and *formalism* terms, which it is easily collapsed into.

**Versus [[mental-causation-and-downward-causation|mental causation]].** Mental causation is the *thesis and mechanism*: that mental states cause physical effects, via selection at quantum indeterminacies where physics is incomplete. The mind-arena is the *domain in which that mechanism operates*. Mental causation answers "how does mind affect matter?"; the mind-arena answers "which outcomes are thereby co-determined?" The AI in the risk-mitigation argument may grasp the *mechanism* of mental causation; what it cannot model from its data is the *domain* of outcomes that mechanism shapes. One is a causal relation; the other is the territory that relation leaves unmodellable from physical state.

**Versus the mind-matter interface / consciousness-physics interface.** The [[mind-matter-interface|interface]] and the [[consciousness-physics-interface-formalism|interface formalism]] concern the *locus and mathematical form* of the coupling between consciousness and physics—the state spaces, the coupling map C: E × P → P, and the constraints any such coupling must satisfy. The interface is *where and how* mind and matter meet. The mind-arena is *the downstream region of outcomes* that this meeting affects. The interface is a thin junction; the arena is the wide field of consequences flowing from it. One could fully specify the interface formalism and the mind-arena would still be unmodellable from physical state, because the formalism fixes the coupling's *form* without making the non-physical contribution recoverable from that state.

**Versus "the mental" / "the subjective."** These name the *phenomenal* category—experience, qualia, first-person character. The mind-arena is not a synonym for these: it is defined extensionally by *outcomes*, not intensionally by what-it-is-like. A purely epiphenomenal mind would have a rich subjective domain but an *empty* mind-arena, because nothing in the physical-outcome space would route through it. The arena exists only because the Map endorses [[bidirectional-interaction]]: it is the *causal footprint* of the mental, not the mental itself.

The short form: **mental causation is the mechanism, the interface is the junction, the subjective is the phenomenal category, and the mind-arena is the domain of outcomes co-determined by all three.**

## Relation to Site Perspective

The mind-arena is a construct that exists *only under the Map's tenets*. It is downstream of two tenets and of one further premise they do not supply.

Under **Tenet 1 (Dualism)**, consciousness is irreducible—so there is a non-physical contributor that no purely physical description includes. Under **Tenet 3 (Bidirectional Interaction)**, that contributor genuinely acts on the physical world—so the contribution shows up in *outcomes*, not merely in inner experience. The mind-arena is the conjunction of these: the domain of physical outcomes that a non-physical, causally efficacious consciousness co-determines. Remove Tenet 1 and there is no non-physical contributor to omit; remove Tenet 3 and the mental leaves no outcome-footprint to demarcate. The term is therefore a compact name for *the region where Tenets 1 and 3 jointly bite*.

The two tenets fix that region but not its defining property. Tenet 1 is [[tenets#^dualism|neutral]] between substance and property dualism, and on Chalmers's (1996) [[substance-property-dualism|naturalistic dualism]] phenomenal properties are irreducible yet lawfully tied to physical ones. Give them causal work under Tenet 3 and their contribution is lawfully fixed by physical state, so a learner fitting outcomes to physical data could in principle absorb that contribution. Unmodellability needs a further premise: no psychophysical law fixes the token selection from physical state. The Map takes that premise from its agency cluster, where a persisting subject decides among open outcomes; that subject enters with [[tenets/background-commitments|Posit One]], not Tenet 1 ([[where-the-substance-commitment-enters]]). Irreducibility leaves the question open, so a property dualist who accepts both tenets and denies the premise is not refuted here; at this point the arena rests on a declared commitment rather than a derived result.

Its operative property—unmodellability from physical state—draws on the Map's account of [[causal-powers]], on which consciousness, if it selects among quantum outcomes, has causal powers "that are not simply sums of physical powers." Under [[tenets#^minimal-quantum-interaction|Tenet 2 (Minimal Quantum Interaction)]] the property needs no specific channel width, only that no law fixes the mind-arena's contribution from physical state; as the risk-mitigation article puts it, "even a vanishingly narrow channel doing genuine work is a variable the physical model omits." That settles sufficiency and says nothing about size: any non-zero channel leaves a physics-only model incomplete, but how much the gap matters for prediction, control or [[instrumental-convergence|AI safety]] depends on channel strength and amplification, which the Map does not claim to know.

## Honest Tenet-Alignment and Evidential Status

The mind-arena is a **posited framework construct, not an established entity**. As a definitional term whose content is fixed by the tenets, it is not itself a claim placed on the Map's [[evidential-status-discipline|five-tier evidential scale]]; its credence tracks the tenets it presupposes rather than any independent evidence. Its existence is conditional on the Map's tenets and that further premise being true; a physicalist owes no such domain, because for the physicalist a complete physical-state model omits no variable an outcome depends on, so the arena collapses to the empty set. That rejection targets the defining property and leaves the routing intact: a realisation or nonreductive physicalist grants that beliefs and intentions mediate these outcomes as physically realised causes, so the delineating question above picks out a non-empty region on that view too, and the dispute is over an omitted non-physical variable. The term should never be deployed as if its referent were a discovered region of the world. It is a *consequence* of Dualism-plus-Bidirectional-Interaction on that premise, useful precisely for marking where those commitments make a difference.

Two honest caveats. First, the boundary of the arena is not sharply operationalised: which concrete outcomes "pass through minds" is itself contestable, and the risk-mitigation argument concedes that coercion can handle some mind-implicating effects by "routing through physical bottlenecks" that *are* modellable—so the arena's edge is fuzzier than a clean partition suggests. Second, the whole construct shares the empirical exposure of Tenet 2: if mind-matter influence were shown empirically impossible, the mind-arena would have no non-trivial extension. Its sharper exposure is a conditional residual: outcome frequencies departing from the Born measure once conditioned on intention, task or subject, so far untested at every grain ([[apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy#where-a-selection-could-show-if-anywhere-the-conditional-signature|horn (a) of the conditional-signature trilemma]]). The page names no grain for that test, inheriting the apex's open choice of ensemble; if no grain ever shows a residual, the arena survives only as authorship without a distributional footprint. Short of any refutation, the construct also inherits the Map's [[positions/quantum-interface#^mechanism-debt|mechanism debt]]: a non-empty arena presupposes consciousness doing genuine causal work, and the register cites the causal-selection thesis only as a framework-internal coherence result, so the arena's extension is posited and coherent rather than demonstrated. The Map holds the term as *infrastructure for its own arguments*, not as a claim that any neutral observer must accept.
