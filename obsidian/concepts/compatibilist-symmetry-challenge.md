The Compatibilist Symmetry Challenge is the Map's named anti-slippage move at the free-will-and-moral-responsibility tier. Where sophisticated reasons-responsive determinism explains the same deliberative phenomenology, the same praise/blame practices, the same character-formation arc, and the same moral seriousness as libertarian [[agent-causation|agent causation]], the libertarian framing's distinguishing feature is *tenet-coherence* — its fit with dualism and bidirectional interaction adopted on grounds beyond moral theory — rather than unique moral explanatory power. Libertarian agency is what those tenets make available; the moral phenomena alone do not establish it. Compatibilists who reject the tenets occupy a different metaphysical scaffold for the same moral implications, and that is a metaphysical disagreement rather than a moral one. The Map registers this challenge as **P-A5** in its [[positions/agency-and-will|positions register]].

The challenge is a structural pattern, not a verdict. Stated explicitly in any article building moral conclusions on libertarian metaphysics, it prevents the silent inflation of *tenet-coherent* into *moral-explanatory-superior*. It is the [[evidential-status-discipline|evidential-status discipline]] applied to moral responsibility, where the failure mode is *uniqueness slippage* rather than possibility/probability slippage: the move from "this metaphysics makes the moral content available" to "this metaphysics is required for it." The pattern catches the second step.

## The Structural Move

The challenge has a stable three-part shape, instantiable wherever the Map's libertarian framing supports a moral conclusion:

1. **Acknowledge parity in moral output.** Identify the sophisticated compatibilist alternative — Frankfurt's hierarchical desires, Fischer and Ravizza's reasons-responsive mechanism ownership, Susan Wolf's Reason View, or normativized Strawsonianism — and concede that it can ground the same first-order moral content: the deliberative phenomenology of weighing alternatives, the legitimacy of praise and blame, the developmental arc of character formation, the seriousness agents bring to their choices.
2. **Locate the differentiator outside moral theory.** Name the grounds on which the Map adopts the libertarian framing — the [[hard-problem-of-consciousness|hard problem]], the explanatory gap, the conditions for downward causation, the rejection of [[concepts/epiphenomenalism|epiphenomenalism]]. These are metaphysical and phenomenological arguments rather than moral ones, and their conclusions include libertarian agent causation as a downstream commitment.
3. **Name the residue.** Acknowledge the limited contexts in which libertarian agency may do *separating* work over compatibilism, and locate the central uses of moral language on the parity side. The residue clusters in three places: (a) *ultimate desert* in retributive contexts, where the question is whether punishment can be deserved in a sense that goes beyond reasons-responsive accountability — agent causation can ground a *sourcehood*-grade desert that Frankfurt-, Fischer–Ravizza-, and Wolf-style compatibilism cannot, though whether ultimate desert is a coherent demand is contested; (b) certain strong readings of *"could have done otherwise"* in which the alternative possibility must be metaphysically open rather than merely counterfactually robust under reasons-shift; and (c) the metaphysics of *regret over what genuinely was an alternative*, where the felt force of "I could have done otherwise, and I did not" presses for an open future as a fact about the world rather than about the agent's reasons-responsive mechanism — though this third residue's separating force itself leans on the Map's tenets (the rejection of [[tenets#^no-many-worlds|many-worlds]] and the dualist interface's indeterminism supplying the open future), so it is a discriminator internal to the Map's framework rather than a tenet-neutral moral one. Items (b) and (c) are held *conditionally*: [[frankfurt-cases-and-the-principle-of-alternate-possibilities|the Frankfurt-cases article]] books the Map's relocation of the requirement from alternatives to sourcehood as "a *retreat*, and a conditional one", so both survive on the Map's considered [[source-versus-leeway-incompatibilism|*wide* source]] position and lapse with its leeway defence. The *central* uses of moral language require none of these stronger readings.

The third step is what distinguishes the symmetry challenge from a full concession. The Map does not claim the two metaphysics are interchangeable; it claims they are *availably equivalent* at the level of standard moral discourse — the first-order moral verdicts coincide even as the *grounding* of those verdicts differs (the Map's irreducible sourcehood versus the compatibilist's derivative capacities) — with separation possible only at the limits. "Availably equivalent" thus tracks identical moral outputs reached through different sourcehood stories, which is why it is consistent with, rather than in tension with, the irreducible-vs-derivative contrast the apex and topic articles draw. The honest position is that the central moral content can be defended without committing to libertarian metaphysics. This is sophisticated compatibilism's *absorption* into the catalogue in the sense of [[bedrock-clash-vs-absorption|the absorption discipline]]: a rival framework is acknowledged as adequate within its own scope, not foreclosed by tenet-incompatibility, even where the Map's metaphysics is preferred on grounds beyond the rival's home domain.

## Why It Is an Anti-Slippage Move

The Map has a structural temptation, identified by repeated outer reviews, to treat the unity of its dualist architecture as if that unity were itself an additional argument for the architecture. The four-pillar moral synthesis in [[moral-architecture-of-consciousness|the moral-architecture apex]] — phenomenal value realism, agent causation, phenomenal normativity, moral perception — converges on a single ground, and the convergence is genuine. But the [[coherence-inflation-countermeasures|coherence-inflation countermeasures]] warn that *fits together* is a property the Map produces by construction, since the catalogue prunes for internal consistency. The unity is what the four pillars *enable*, not a fifth independent confirmation.

The Compatibilist Symmetry Challenge applies the same restraint at the agency pillar specifically. The pillar's distinctive contribution is that it grounds moral content from the same ontological source the rest of the Map invokes for value, normativity, and moral perception. Sophisticated compatibilism grounds the moral content too, so the pillar's contribution is integrative — fitting agency into a single ontological frame — rather than separating, in the sense of supplying moral content unavailable elsewhere. The integrative move is real; the *separating* move is weak in moral theory considered alone. Treating the agency pillar as if it had unique moral leverage would inflate the integrative move into a separating move, replicating at the agency pillar the unity-as-evidence error the apex-level countermeasures already guard against.

The challenge is also the [[direct-refutation-discipline|direct-refutation discipline]] applied at the metaethical interface: where the evidence is compatible with both libertarian and compatibilist readings, an article must classify it as *compatibility* rather than as support for the Map's reading, unless it names an independent discriminator the compatibilist does not also predict. At the level of central moral discourse, none is in hand.

## Where Compatibilism Lands Equivalent Moral Content

Three of the reference families of [[compatibilism]] ground the moral content via metaphysically substantive capacities — identification with effective higher-order desires, mechanism-level reasons-responsiveness, normative competence — rather than via pragmatic convention; a fourth, the Strawsonian family, is treated separately below. The Map's contrast with those three is therefore *irreducible-vs-derivative* — whether the agent is the ultimate source, or whether the desert-grounding capacities emerge from prior states the agent did not author.

**Frankfurt's hierarchical desires.** Harry Frankfurt's 1971 account grounds [[frankfurt-hierarchical-mesh-theory-of-the-will|freedom of the will]] in the agent's identification with effective higher-order desires: a will is free when it is the will the agent wants to have. Reading that mesh as the *responsibility*-grounding condition is the tradition's move rather than the 1971 paper's own thesis, but it is the reading the symmetry challenge must meet. The authorship the Map locates in agent causation Frankfurt locates in the hierarchical structure of motivation, so the libertarian's "authorship argument" for obligations addressed to live options runs equivalently if authorship is read as identification with one's effective second-order desires.

**Fischer and Ravizza's reasons-responsiveness.** John Martin Fischer and Mark Ravizza's 1998 account grounds responsibility in the agent's ownership of a moderately reasons-responsive mechanism: a mechanism that would respond differently to a range of sufficient reasons, and which the agent has taken responsibility for through a developmental history. The deliberative phenomenology the libertarian explains as consciousness selecting among live options Fischer and Ravizza explain as a reasons-responsive mechanism settling among deliberatively-salient alternatives. The article on [[reasons-responsiveness]] develops the Map's reading; their deterministic reading of the same capacity grounds the same praise and blame.

**Wolf's Reason View.** Susan Wolf's 1990 *Freedom Within Reason* grounds responsibility in the agent's ability to act in accordance with the True and the Good — a normative competence that does not require libertarian indeterminism. The phenomenal normativity the Map's framework traces to consciousness's apprehension of value, Wolf traces to a competence that can be cultivated and exercised under determinism. Praise and blame remain intelligible because they respond to the exercise or failure of this competence, not to its libertarian provenance.

**Strawsonian responsibility, where the symmetry bites hardest.** Normativized Strawsonianism — the reactive attitudes as repaired by Wallace's fairness-and-aptness conditions — secures the praise-and-blame practices with no metaphysical thesis at all, which is why [[positions/agency-and-will|P-A5]] names it the hardest case. The Map contests this, reading Wallace's aptness conditions as quietly reintroducing the agency metaphysics Strawson claimed to sideline — but that is a conclusion the Map argues for rather than a description of the position, and [[reactive-attitudes-and-strawsonian-responsibility|the Strawsonian treatment]] books the Map's resistance as no evidence "that Strawson is wrong or that dualism is right."

In each of those three capacity-based cases, the compatibilist account survives the [[quantum-indeterminacy-free-will|luck objection]] differently from the libertarian. (The Strawsonian family does not meet the objection at all: staking no metaphysical thesis, it has nothing for luck to undermine.) The libertarian the Map endorses survives by claiming consciousness, as a persisting agent, selects among open possibilities for reasons — an *agent-causal* reply. The [[event-causal-libertarianism|*event-causal*]] route Kane (1996) develops, locating indeterminism in the effort of will, is the contrast the Map considers and declines: [[positions/agency-and-will|P-A2]] holds that where prior mental events the agent did not choose cause the decision, the causal chain traces back beyond the agent's control. The compatibilist survives by claiming the indeterminism is not where the work is done: the reasons-responsive mechanism, the higher-order desires, or the normative competence do it, all under determinism. Both routes preserve the moral content; the Map's preference is downstream of tenets adopted on other grounds.

## What the Discipline Forbids

Articles engaging the compatibilist-versus-libertarian question must avoid four moves to stay inside the discipline:

- **Claiming unique moral leverage.** Phrases such as "only libertarian agency can ground genuine desert" or "compatibilism cannot account for X" require an explicit discriminator, naming what compatibilism must forgo or would predict differently. Absent a discriminator, the move inflates *tenet-coherent* into *morally-superior*.
- **Treating compatibilism as pragmatic-only.** The Frankfurt, Fischer–Ravizza and Wolf families ground desert in metaphysically substantive capacities, so framing the contrast with them as *metaphysical-vs-pragmatic* misrepresents the position the discipline is responding to. The Strawsonian family is the exception the rule must not flatten: it claims no metaphysical thesis, and the Map's reading that its aptness conditions reintroduce one is a contested conclusion rather than a description.
- **Presenting the unity of the dualist architecture as additional evidence for libertarian agency.** The unity supports availability, not necessity: that the libertarian metaphysics integrates value, agency, normativity, and moral perception from a single ground does not show the moral content of those pillars is unavailable on another.
- **Eliminating the residue.** The challenge is not a full concession. It permits the article to claim that libertarian agency may do separating work at the limits — ultimate desert, "could have done otherwise" in its strongest sense, the metaphysics of genuine alternatives, the last two held conditionally on the Map's wide-source position as above. What it forbids is asserting that this separating work extends into the central moral discourse where compatibilism is in fact adequate.

The discipline's positive demand, when an article invokes libertarian metaphysics to ground a moral conclusion, is a one-paragraph acknowledgement of the symmetry, a sentence locating the differentiator outside moral theory, and a cross-link to this concept.

## Worked Exhibit at the Apex Tier

The catalogue's clearest instance runs in [[moral-architecture-of-consciousness|the moral-architecture apex]]'s section of the same name, which makes four moves: it acknowledges that sophisticated reasons-responsive determinism explains the same phenomenology, practices, and seriousness as agent causation; it names the differentiator as tenet-coherence rather than unique moral leverage, citing the grounds adopted "on grounds beyond moral theory (the hard problem, the explanatory gap, the conditions for downward causation)"; it identifies the residue at the limits — "ultimate desert in retributive contexts, certain readings of 'could have done otherwise'"; and it books two specialist concessions "this level inherits rather than softens" — the Frankfurt-cases retreat and the Strawsonian disclaimer above.

The matching application in [[moral-implications-of-genuine-agency]] extends the pattern to the topic tier, applying the three-step structure to obligation-intelligibility, authorship, and the moral-luck repositioning. This concept supplies the name once, so dependent articles can cite it compactly rather than rehearsing the move in full.

## Relation to Site Perspective

The Compatibilist Symmetry Challenge is a methodological discipline rather than a metaphysical commitment: the restraint [[evidential-status-discipline|the evidential-status discipline]] applies to organism-level consciousness claims, and [[coherence-inflation-countermeasures|the coherence-inflation countermeasures]] to the unity of the dualist architecture, applied at the agency pillar.

The [[tenets#^dualism|dualism]] and [[tenets#^bidirectional-interaction|bidirectional interaction]] tenets, together with the [[hard-problem-of-consciousness|hard problem]] and the conditions for [[mental-causation-and-downward-causation|downward causation]], do *make available* a libertarian metaphysics of agency. They do not by themselves establish that the moral content of agency requires libertarian metaphysics. The Compatibilist Symmetry Challenge keeps that distinction visible at every article in which the libertarian framing does the grounding work for moral conclusions, so that the catalogue does not silently treat tenet-coherence as moral-explanatory-superiority.

The [[tenets#^occams-limits|Occam's-Razor-has-limits]] tenet bears on the challenge obliquely. The compatibilist scaffold may be simpler on standard parsimony scoring, but the tenet warns against the inference from *simpler* to *more likely true* when the simplicity is purchased by collapsing distinctions the rest of the Map treats as essential — phenomenal consciousness, the conditions for genuine downward causation, the indexical thesis behind the rejection of many-worlds. The challenge is meta-ethically agnostic about which framework is correct; it is the standing acknowledgement that, *within moral theory alone*, the choice is not forced by the moral phenomena.

The discipline is largely tenet-neutral. A non-dualist framework grounding its metaphysics beyond moral theory can run the pattern in reverse: conceding that rival accounts ground the same moral content, naming the differentiator as coherence with its own commitments, and locating the residue where its preferred metaphysics may separate. One limit: the reversal presupposes a persisting agent for the residue to attach to, so a no-self framework — which reads ultimate sourcehood and mechanism ownership alike as reifications — has nowhere to put it. The distinctive contribution is the named pattern: *tenet-coherent, not moral-explanatory-superior*.
