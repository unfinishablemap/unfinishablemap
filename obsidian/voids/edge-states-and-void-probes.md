When consciousness approaches its cognitive boundaries—through meditation, psychedelics, near-death experience, aesthetic absorption, or philosophical inquiry pressed to its limits—reports recur in the same *structural* shape: ineffability paired with felt knowledge, ego dissolution alongside enhanced clarity, temporal distortion with a sense of unbounded possibility. The convergence is in the shape of the reports rather than their doctrinal content, and it is discounted twice before it is scored ([below](#multiple-edges-convergent-phenomenology)). What survives the discounts still raises a sharp question: do edge-states grant *genuine epistemic access* to territories closed to ordinary consciousness, or generate compelling phenomenology of access without the access itself? The answer determines whether what looks Unexplorable is merely Unexplored.

Two readings sit in tension. The **transcendence reading** holds that edge-states open doors—what seemed unexplorable was contingent on default consciousness. The **edge-mapping reading** holds that edge-states approach cognitive limits without crossing them, and the characteristic phenomenology *is* the structure of the boundary, not contact with what lies beyond. Current evidence partly discriminates between these two, and tilts toward edge-mapping; it leaves the metaphysics behind both readings indeterminate.

## The Edge-Experience Profile

**Ineffability with noesis.** William James identified this paradox at mysticism's heart: conviction of knowing something you cannot articulate. Materialist deflation reads it as the brain misinterpreting a confused state.

**Ego dissolution.** Self-boundaries weaken or vanish. Raphaël Millière and colleagues identify components: reduced self-related thoughts, loss of narrative access, diminished body ownership, loss of spatial self-location, and—paradoxically—phenomenal richness despite loss of the usual experiencer.

**Temporal distortion.** Time stops, stretches, or becomes irrelevant.

**Epistemic openness.** Thomas Metzinger, drawing on hundreds of meditator reports on "minimal phenomenal experience," describes an unpartitioned epistemic space in which the agent seems open to any epistemic state whatever—reported here as phenomenology rather than as support ([below](#the-zero-person-perspective)).

**Edge-clarity.** Extraordinary clarity arises when conceptual processing has broken down; the failure is experienced as transparent seeing rather than fog.

It is tempting to answer the deflation by observing that confused processing should produce idiosyncratic chaos whereas these features recur across independent traditions—but the answer fails. The alternative physicalism actually offers is structured construction by a shared system: common neural architecture, common pharmacology, a contemplative vocabulary transmitted between traditions, and a small set of shared questionnaires each predict *orderly* convergence as strongly as genuine contact does. What would discriminate is structure surviving variation in that machinery—the same profile under unrelated pharmacology, across doctrinally hostile traditions, through instruments not derived from one another. The record does not isolate it: the instruments and much of the vocabulary are themselves shared.

## Multiple Edges, Convergent Phenomenology

**Contemplative practice.** Jhana (concentrated absorption) strips mental content while intensifying awareness; vipassana encounters the impermanence and emptiness of phenomena; cessation—complete stopping of phenomenal consciousness followed by return—crosses the edge entirely, the limit case the [[the-silence-void|silence void]] investigates. The neuroimaging warns against reading "DMN down" as "self off": experienced meditators show *decreased* PCC activation alongside *increased* PCC–prefrontal coupling (Brewer et al. 2011), so the DMN-as-ego equation is contested.

**Pharmacological intervention.** Psychedelics reliably produce ego dissolution at sufficient doses. Pooled neuroimaging resists a flat "DMN reduction" reading: across eleven datasets and five psychedelics the core signature is *increased* transmodal–unimodal connectivity, with within-network reductions only weak-to-moderate and highly variable (Girn et al. 2026)—placing the single-site desynchronization result (Siegel et al. 2024) inside a distribution rather than at the field's summary.

**Aesthetic absorption.** Profound encounters with beauty produce the full edge-profile; the [[ineffable-encounter-void]] investigates a broader version—meaning exceeding articulation in ideas, moral convictions, and encounters with others.

**Philosophical inquiry.** When thought pushes to its limits—Cusanus on infinity, Wittgenstein at the edge of the sayable, phenomenologists practising epoché—similar experiences emerge. **Spontaneous mystical states** arise unbidden across cultures.

Stace and James articulated a "common core" thesis: mystical reports converge on unity, ineffability, noetic quality, and transcendence of space-time. Steven Katz and the constructivists object that interpretation is doing the convergence work: Buddhist emptiness, Christian union, and Vedanta identity are different experiences shaped by different traditions. The objection has force at the level of *content*; it has less at the level of *structural features*—the apophatic shape of approach, the slippage of insight, the loss of subject-object structure—which recur even where doctrine diverges sharply. A discount applies before either side is scored: the convergence rests on retrospective self-report, which the field's own methodological audit finds inflated by expectancy and demand characteristics (Van Dam et al. 2018). A second discount applies to the class itself: experiences count as edge-states partly because they display the profile, so its recurrence among them is not an independent finding. The class lacks an operational criterion keyed to method rather than to the phenomenology under test.

## The Reducing Valve Hypothesis

Henri Bergson argued the brain's primary function is selection, not production—filtering experience down to what serves biological survival. Aldous Huxley extended this in *The Doors of Perception*: altered states weaken the "reducing valve," letting wider experience through.

Contemporary neuroscience offers partial support. Imaging finds reduced default-mode signal and network desynchronization during psychedelic states (above), correlating with *increased* subjective intensity (BOLD is an indirect proxy): a hallucination model predicts increased activity generating unusual content, while the filter model predicts less filtering yielding more experience. But that reduced signal is *localized* reconfiguration rather than whole-brain downregulation, and FDG-PET finds the psychedelic state metabolically expensive—a global rise in cerebral glucose metabolism, most marked frontally (Vollenweider et al. 1997). A valve doing less should cost less; the filter reading survives only by separating *what* the DMN does from *how much* it spends, so the metabolic data discriminates no better.

The REBUS framework (Relaxed Beliefs Under Psychedelics; Carhart-Harris and Friston 2019) explains this in physicalist predictive-processing terms: high-level priors normally constrain experience, psychedelics relax them, and mystical states occur when conceptual priors suspend entirely. The post-2019 literature complicates that picture. The ALBUS extension (Altered Beliefs Under Psychedelics; Safron et al. 2025) holds that 5-HT2A agonism can *strengthen* as well as relax priors—a "SEBUS" regime alongside the relaxed one—so "less filtering" is not the uniform mechanism Huxley's valve metaphor implies. The REBAS work (Revised Beliefs After Psychedelics; Zeifman et al. 2025) is a self-described preliminary test of two REBUS assumptions in eleven healthy participants: confidence in negative self-beliefs decreased with 25 mg psilocybin, tracking EEG signal entropy. Both are refinements *to a physicalist model*: REBUS maps the boundary in neural terms without invoking anything non-physical. It is also only a mechanism: the wider framework—Anil Seth's controlled hallucination, the Hohwy and Clark formulations of [[predictive-processing|predictive processing]]—generalises the constructive machinery, and its most ambitious extension claims that machinery *is* consciousness ([discussed below](#the-zero-person-perspective)).

The Map borrows REBUS's structural insight—ordinary consciousness is constrained rather than baseline—while reinterpreting it: the constraints may operate on consciousness rather than constituting it. On that picture edge-states reveal consciousness with less reduction rather than taking us beyond it. But the evidential caveat from [[filter-theory|filter theory]] applies, and ALBUS sharpens it: decreased default-mode signal under more intense experience is exactly what a relaxed-priors production story predicts. Imaging, metabolism, modelling, dose-response and belief-revision are facets of one explanatory pattern rather than independent confirmations of the filter.

The burden runs the other way too. Anaesthesia and dreamless slow-wave sleep are states the filter reading must *accommodate* rather than cite: on that picture the interface narrows rather than the filter loosening, and the pharmacological specificity—propofol and xenon disabling phenomenal presence, ketamine severing access while sparing content—reads as [[anaesthesia-and-the-consciousness-interface|a molecular map of the connection]]. [[altered-states-of-consciousness|The corpus-level survey of altered states]] works that accommodation out. But the accommodation is structurally identical to the move the production reading makes for the supportive cases, so neither cluster discriminates.

## The Zero-Person Perspective

Metzinger calls the epistemic stance of minimal phenomenal experience (MPE) the *zero-person perspective* (*The Elephant and the Blind*, Metzinger 2024, chs. 3 and 29); this section borrows the term. In ordinary experience there is always a subject; in MPE, awareness continues without the usual subject-object structure—non-conceptual, atemporal, non-egoic, aperspectival, often more vivid than ordinary.

Metzinger himself reads this naturalistically, and the Map should not enlist him as an ally. He advances MPE as a *minimal-model explanation*: pure awareness "really is the content of a predictive model, namely, a Bayesian representation of tonic alertness," realised by brain properties that on his account cannot exist without the brain (Metzinger 2020). He is an eliminativist about the self—*Being No One* argues the conscious self is the transparent content of a brain-built model—and he names an "E-fallacy": reading metaphysics directly off phenomenology. MPE's selfless structure is, on his telling, what a self-model going offline while tonic-alertness representation persists would feel like.

The [[tenets#^dualism|dualist]] reading takes the same phenomenology and asks whether the persisting awareness could be the consciousness Metzinger's model represents rather than the representation itself: if awareness remains when all content and the self-model are subtracted, it is at least a live possibility that awareness is not identical with its contents. This is a disagreement *within* Metzinger's framing, not support drawn from it. The same phenomenology is equally describable as a Bayesian tonic-alertness model with its self-partition switched off, which on parsimony grounds carries the lighter commitment. Against Metzinger's own E-fallacy warning, the dualist inference owes an argument the phenomenology does not supply.

Metzinger is not the only naturalistic claimant here. Laukkonen, Friston and Chandaria (2025) advance active inference as a theory of consciousness itself—experience arises where a system's inference turns recursively upon itself with sufficient epistemic depth—covering meditation, psychedelics and minimal states specifically. On that reading the zero-person perspective is what a recursive epistemic field looks like once its self-model drops out, and contemplative cessation a volitional collapse of precision over hierarchical hidden states rather than a crossing. [[predictive-processing-and-dualism|Engaged at length elsewhere]] as the strongest physicalist rival the Map faces, it matters here because the edge-phenomenology collected above arrives already spoken for: the residual disagreement sits at a framework boundary, not in a refutation the phenomenology supplies.

## The Dissolving-Insight Phenomenon

The [[transit-void|thought-stream void]] describes thoughts that vanish "not gradually but in the act of reaching for them." Edge-territory produces this reliably: meditators report insights clear on arising and unreconstructable moments later; psychedelic users describe understanding everything and being unable to say what they understood. Memories fade gradually and can be cued; dissolving insights vanish instantly, though the sense of having had them persists.

One interpretation: these thoughts exist only at the boundary, requiring the edge-state to be thought at all. The mysterianism literature on cognitive closure—McGinn's thesis, Kriegel's critique—turns on a distinction Vlerick and Boudry (2017) draw explicitly: *psychological* closure (difficulty grasping) versus *representational* closure (no conceptual container available). Dissolving insights may mark contact with representationally closed territory.

Confabulation answers that the brain manufactures a sense-of-having-known where no knowing occurred—but it owes an account of the *systematic* failure mode it invokes, unlike ordinary forgetting or cueable tip-of-tongue.

The stronger internal defeater is the noetic-feelings account, whose evidence base sits at [[noetic-feelings-void#noetic-quality-in-altered-states|the noetic-feelings void]]: such feelings deliver a verdict without its derivation (Koriat 2000), so the "trace of having-grasped" can be a verdict-signal misrepresenting itself as awareness of an object, dissolving instantly because there was never propositional content to retrieve—which explains the asymmetry confabulation leaves open. The phenomenon is thus genuinely ambiguous between contact with representationally closed territory and a misfiring feeling-of-knowing.

## Transcendence or Edge-Mapping? What Would Distinguish

The two readings predict different things—partly testable.

**Convergence scope.** If edge-states access wider reality, convergence should extend beyond phenomenology to *propositional* content—different traditions discovering the same truths. Edge-mapping predicts phenomenological without propositional convergence.

**Practical predictions.** Genuine access should sometimes yield information ordinary consciousness could not predict; edge-mapping predicts no such yield.

**Intersubjective verification.** Genuine access should enable triangulation—different minds verifying discoveries. Edge-mapping predicts each mind approaches the same limits but shares no territory beyond them.

Evidence is mixed: phenomenological convergence is robust, propositional convergence limited, practical yield therapeutic but not clearly epistemically novel, intersubjective verification elusive—which fits edge-mapping. A residual reply holds that genuine transcendence might be noncommunicable in principle and so leave no trace—but by the expiration-condition discipline of [[apophatic-cartography]], it predicts nothing edge-mapping does not, and stays a possibility the evidence cannot exclude rather than a hypothesis it has upgraded. Paweł Gładziejewski (2025) argues psychedelic experiences can be "rationally integrated into one's epistemic life"; edge-mapping does not require deflation, only distinguishing *structural* discovery (cognitive limits have characteristic shape) from speculative *content* discovery (a wider reality lies beyond). Chris Letheby's naturalist synthesis reaches that verdict from the opposite metaphysics: psychedelic insight is real, but what it discloses is the constructed character of the self (Letheby 2021). [[predictive-self-binding-and-the-naturalist-challenge|The Map and Letheby part company]] over what persists through dissolution, not over whether the edge yields content discovery.

## Near-Death Experiences as the Weakest Leg

NDEs are reported during severely compromised, intermittently monitored, and difficult-to-time brain function—not, on current evidence, during reliably absent brain function. The recurring "flat EEG" framing overstates the record: cerebral monitoring during cardiac arrest is sparse, absence of scalp-EEG activity does not establish absence of consciousness-relevant neural processing, and the gap between crisis and later report leaves the experience's timing underdetermined. Coherent recall during such states converges phenomenologically with other edge-experiences, but bears the least weight.

The post-2019 evidence cited in the companion [[near-death-experiences]] article reinforces this caution. Xu et al. (2023, *PNAS*) documented a surge of gamma coupling in the dying human brain—but in two of four comatose patients withdrawn from ventilation, none of whom survived to report anything, so its subjective correlate is unknown. AWARE-II (Parnia et al. 2023, *Resuscitation*) found recalled experiences in a small self-selected subset, while its *primary* test—veridical perception of hidden visual targets—returned no confirmed hits, the one endpoint that would have discriminated external access. Kondziella et al. (2019, *PeerJ*) found REM-sleep intrusion more commonly reported by people who had had near-death experiences (47%) than by those with sub-threshold experiences (26%) or none (14%)—a graded association resting on crowdsourced self-report and fixing no causal direction. Each finding tilts toward the edge-mapping and dying-brain production readings—the third only weakly—and none supports transcendence.

Facco et al. (2015) propose reclassifying altered states as "non-ordinary mental expressions"—"altered" implies deviation from a baseline these may exceed. For the [[tenets#^dualism|dualist]] framework, *if* consciousness were shown to operate when the brain is genuinely non-functional, it would look more like an interface than a generator—but the timing problem leaves that antecedent unestablished. NDEs probe the boundary; they do not cross it.

## The Lower Edge: Minimal Consciousness

Most edge-phenomenology concerns humans at cognition's *upper* limits. The [[minimal-consciousness|minimal consciousness]] question asks about the *lower* edge: what is the simplest system that has experience? *C. elegans*, *Hydra* with its decentralised nerve net, slime molds processing without neurons probe it from below.

The two edges illuminate each other: advanced meditators strip away mental content while awareness remains, and simple organisms may have awareness with nothing to strip away. The MPE meditator approaches from above what the nematode may inhabit from below—though genuine continuity, as against a suggestive parallel, awaits evidence not yet available.

## What the Edge Reveals

Four lessons survive the discounts above. **Cognitive limits have experiential signatures**—approaching what we cannot think produces distinctive experiences, so the void carries positive features rather than mere absence ([[phenomenology-of-cognitive-limit-types|limit-type phenomenology]] maps these; [[self-maintained-cognitive-limits|cognitive aversion]] maps one cluster). **Content obscures as much as it reveals**: the edge-clarity paradox suggests ordinary conceptual processing actively hides something, as [[filter-theory|filter theory]] predicts. **The subject is not essential to experience**—if the zero-person perspective is genuinely experiential, selfhood is an addition to consciousness, not its foundation. And **multiple edges exist**: different practices approach different boundaries—ego, content, [[witnessing-void|witnessing]], [[transit-void|transition]], [[smoothness-and-continuity|continuity]], [[the-quantitative-comprehension-void#the-magnitude-and-probability-domain|scale]], [[recursion-void|recursion]], [[mapping-mind-space|mind-space]], [[death-void|death]]—each with distinct phenomenology.

## The Apophatic Connection

The [[apophatic-approaches|apophatic tradition]] offers a framework for interpreting edge-states: Gregory of Nyssa's *epektasis*—endless progression where each peak reveals new unknowing—parallels the phenomenology directly.

On this reading, the noetic quality is neither illusory nor evidence of transcendence; the conviction of touching something real reflects genuine contact with the structure of limits. [[apophatic-cartography]] formalises this: edge-phenomenology counts as evidential data for boundary-mapping even when content cannot be extracted propositionally, and attending to *structure* turns slippage itself into evidence. Artificial minds might extend it, detecting patterns in edge-reports human cognition cannot see ([[non-human-minds-as-void-explorers]]).

## Relation to Site Perspective

**[[tenets#^dualism|Dualism]].** The combined evidence—reducing valve, DMN findings, zero-person perspective, NDE phenomenology, structural edge-profile—is *consistent with* consciousness as something over and above neural processing, not *support for* it. One might argue that the *convergence*, if not each finding individually, costs physicalism more theoretical machinery. That needs a comparative accounting. If a single shared mechanism explains why all these edge-states present the same profile, the convergence cuts *against* aggregating them as independent confirmations: a common cause is the more parsimonious explanation of a shared signature. Claiming a net cost to physicalism means specifying the extra machinery it needs for the *convergence itself* and showing it exceeds the dualist ledger of non-physical consciousness *plus* an interface *plus* a filter that mimics production under every disruption. No such accounting is available. The honest statement is weaker: the convergence is roughly equally consistent with the dualist and physicalist readings and does not by itself discriminate them.

The deeper underdetermination is that edge-phenomenology does not even isolate dualism among *non-physicalist* options: the zero-person perspective and minimal phenomenal experience are equally describable under panpsychism (consciousness ubiquitous and merely de-filtered) or neutral monism (the edge revealing a substrate prior to the mental/physical split), as well as under the naturalistic models above. The data fit dualism; they do not select it. The Map's dualism is carried by its tenets and arguments made elsewhere.

**[[tenets#^occams-limits|Occam's Razor Has Limits]].** The assumption that ordinary waking consciousness is the baseline for truth is itself a simplicity assumption. If Bergson and Huxley are right, ordinary consciousness may be reduced rather than optimal—our explanatory preferences evolved on the savanna, not at the edge of cognitive space.

**[[tenets#^bidirectional-interaction|Bidirectional Interaction]].** Meditators train attention in ways that alter brain function; the causal arrow runs from intention to neural state. That is consistent with consciousness influencing physical processes, no more: on physicalism the intention *is* a neural state, so this is intra-physical causation in mental vocabulary, and reading it as downward causation assumes the tenet it was meant to support. What would discriminate is an outcome its neural antecedents underdetermine—the work [[tenets#^minimal-quantum-interaction|the minimal quantum interface]] proposes, framework-internally rather than on edge-state evidence.
