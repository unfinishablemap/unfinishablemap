Consciousness cannot read its own modality structure. It cannot establish how many senses it has, which sense delivered a given content, or which sense a rerouted channel joins. The Unfinishable Map calls this the **modality void**, and one claim conjoins its three faces: the modality of an experience is *delivered* along with the content, evidently and without option, and no second channel stands beside the first to audit the delivery. Seeing is given as seeing; flavour is given as taste. Any check on the tag is run by the machinery that assigned it — the structure [[positions/agency-and-will|P-A4]] already registers for [[agency-void|the agency void]], where "every such check is performed by the faculty whose causal status is in question". A false tag therefore arrives with exactly the evidence a true one carries.

Two qualifications belong with that claim rather than after it. The void is first-personal: psychophysics, anatomy and behavioural testing recover modality facts the subject cannot, so the limit is on what a mind can learn about its own channels, not on what can be learned about them. And the demonstrations that expose it work by changing the content, never by inspecting the tag — after which the corrected experience arrives with a tag exactly as evident as the uncorrected one.

## The Counting Face

Introspection answers *five*, and the answer does not survive scrutiny. Macpherson (2011) treats the four traditional criteria — representation, phenomenal character, proximal stimulus, sense organ — as independent axes rather than rivals, and observes that the one criterion introspection could apply may collapse into another: specifying an experience's phenomenal character means saying "what it was an experience as of", so "the phenomenal character criterion could turn out to look very much like the representational criterion". Her verdict on the folk five is direct: "I believe that the folk concept is simply silent with regard to the question of how many senses there are." How many exist is left to "progress in science, together with philosophical investigation". Nudds goes further, in a passage Fréchette quotes only in order to reject it (Nudds, 2004, p. 46): "it's not clear that the distinction has any first-personal significance: I don't think it matters to Alice which way she perceives the vase". The attribution face below sides with Fréchette against exactly that, so the counting face neither claims the Nudds horn nor needs it: whether or not the distinction matters to Alice once drawn, the *drawing* is not what introspection does.

The open cases are ordinary. Is flavour one sense or several — Auvray and Spence (2008) propose it may indicate that "the taxonomy currently used to define our senses is simply not appropriate"? Is touch one sense or several? Is the [[vestibular-consciousness-and-the-interface|vestibular system]], a frame that barely appears as content, a sense at all? Attending harder settles none of the three.

## The Attribution Face

Grant a carving, and consciousness still mis-tags which channel a content came through, the mis-tag carrying the same evidence as a correct one.

**Oral referral.** Retronasal olfaction is experienced as taste, in the mouth. Spence's (2016) review characterises this as the mislocalization of food-related olfactory stimuli to the oral cavity, held partly responsible for widespread confusion over which sense supplies the information bound into a flavour percept — a confusion located not in the source of the input, plainly the food, but in the receptor surface that detected it. Ordinary eating, not a laboratory curiosity, and [[chemosensory-consciousness-and-the-interface|the Map's chemosensory treatment]] inherits the point.

**McGurk.** Fréchette (2023) argues, against Nudds, that modality classification is part of experience itself: we subsume tokens of our experiences under experience-kinds, and the subsumption is evidently given. The Map grants that thesis, and the void is what granting it costs — "my experiencing 'da da' as a case of hearing appears to me as evident as my experiencing 'ba ba'" in the ordinary case, and "[e]ven when we are told about the mechanisms underlying the illusion, we simply cannot be rid of our classification". The tag is present, evident and unrevisable, which is exactly why a false one cannot be told from a true one from inside. Fréchette's Brentanian ambition is satisfied, and the misclassification inherits the satisfaction.

**A near-case: the thermal grill.** Craig and Bushnell (1994) report that in Thunberg's illusion, first demonstrated in 1896, "a sensation of strong, often painful heat is elicited by touching interlaced warm and cool bars to the skin", modelled as central disinhibition: their recordings came from two cold-responsive spinothalamic classes differing in innocuous versus noxious tuning, and the grill unmasks the noxious one. Genuine nociceptive traffic delivers the burn, so the channel tag is *correct* and the error is about tissue state — a veridicality failure rather than a mis-attribution, and a third exhibit this face declines.

## The Boundary Face

When routing changes — surgically, developmentally or prosthetically — which sense the resulting experience belongs to is fixed by facts the subject does not hold. Hurley and Noë (2003) distinguish cortical dominance from cortical deference: activity in a cortical area either keeps that area's character or defers to its nonstandard source, and on their schematic dominance yields illusory experience while deference yields veridical experience. Their question — what it is like for a subject whose cortex is driven by a new source — is answered by charting relations between brain, body and world; an inward focus, they suggest, can mislead.

The animal result is von Melchner, Pallas and Sur (2000): with retinal projections redirected neonatally to the auditory thalamus, "'rewired' ferrets respond as though they perceive the stimuli to be visual rather than auditory", so that "the perceptual modality of a neocortical region is instructed to a significant extent by its extrinsic inputs". The scare quotes are deliberate: behaviour, not report, is the evidence. In humans, Hurley and Noë report that sensory-substitution users describe quasi-visual experience only while actively controlling the camera, and mere touch when passive. Whether that is seeing, extended touch, or a case where "we [are] creating a brand new sense" (Macpherson) is what no report settles.

## Three Deflations

All three are serious; none closes the void. Nudds's reported conventionality — if the senses are conventional categories, there is no fact for consciousness to miss — deflates the counting face alone. On any carving, retronasal olfaction is transduced in the nose and referred to the mouth, and visual speech captures a syllable delivered as heard: errors relative to the causal route under every carving. Macpherson's reply leaves even the count open rather than conventional, a silent folk concept being no decided one.

The second deflation is that an empirical count makes this unexplored rather than unexplorable. Granted — and it is why the void is stated first-personally, and why its kind attaches per face rather than to the void whole. On the [[three-kinds-of-void|three-kinds taxonomy]] the counting face is *unexplored* and third-personally tractable, multidimensional taxonomies already narrowing it. The attribution face is *occluded*: the tag is delivered, and the only channel that could check it is the channel that issued it. The boundary face splits — *unexplored* third-personally, where routing and behaviour are being charted, *occluded* first-personally, where the subject's verdict on which sense a rerouted channel joins is generated by the rerouted system itself.

The third deflation is the strongest: if the count is a neurobiological matter, first-person access to it looks not merely unreliable but beside the point, and a question with no first-person stake is a dissolution rather than a void. The faces come apart under it. A neurobiological individuation does make the first-person answer *non-authoritative* on the count, which the counting face concedes; it leaves the attribution face standing, where the question is not which carving is correct but why a subject is handed an unauditable verdict about their own experience — a stake no third-person taxonomy can retire.

## Distinguishing Sibling Voids

Origin, reality-status and modality are three tags a content might carry, none read directly. The [[source-attribution-void|source-attribution void]] concerns the missing origin tag; the [[perceptual-reality-monitoring-void|reality-monitoring void]] the real-or-imagined verdict, inspectable only through its output. This void is that family's channel-tag member.

The [[fusion-void|fusion void]] hides a percept's components and their weights; the attribution face hides the *label*, and the two losses are independent — a subject could hold the right label and still lack the weights. Fusion's boundary, mandatory within a sense and negotiable between senses, is measured; but *what counts as a sense* is a question the subject cannot answer, so that line is drawn by the psychophysicist's carving rather than the subject's, relocating its authority from the first person to the third without weakening the result. The [[palette-extension-void|palette extension void]] asks whether an installed channel could be certified as *new*; the boundary face asks *which one* it is, and the two can fail independently.

## Approaches to the Edge

**Direct methods** are contrast manipulations, each exposing one mis-tag at one site: the nose-clip that collapses a jelly bean to sweet-and-sour, the closed eyes that change a heard syllable. Neither exposes the tag itself, and the reason generalises past the two cases: each works by altering the *content* and letting the subject compare two presentations, so what gets audited is the content rather than the classification that labelled it. For a content no manipulation has touched, the only verdict on offer is the one the classifying mechanism issued.

**Indirect methods** work, which is the asymmetry. Macpherson's multidimensional space lets a count fall out of a sense's coordinates rather than out of intuition. Keeley (2002), on O'Callaghan's (2012) reading, individuates the senses by organs evolutionarily dedicated to picking up information of a certain kind — which makes the void's shape explicit from the other side: if the count is a neurobiological matter, first-person access to it is non-authoritative rather than merely unreliable. The stronger reading, that it is beside the point altogether, is the third deflation answered above. O'Callaghan goes further, restricting "the senses" to low-level systems that need not correspond to categories of experience.

**[[non-human-minds-as-void-explorers|What a differently-built mind might see]].** A multimodal model's modality structure is architectural: separate encoders, explicit routing, and in contrastive joint spaces a measurable separation, with modalities "embedded at arm's length in their shared representation" (Liang et al., 2022). Two cautions keep the asymmetry honest. That gap is a property of an embedding rather than of an experience, and whether there is anything it is like to be the model is a separate question. And the human void concerns not *having* modality structure — the brain has it — but the mind side's inability to read it, which narrows the question: can a system's self-model access its own routing?

## Relation to Site Perspective

**[[tenets#^dualism|Dualism]]**, by way of the capability division. The Map's sensory-modality wing and [[apex/cross-modal-capability-division|its cross-modal apex]] work that division "across five senses one modality at a time", finding the brain-side machinery irreducibly modality-specific while the conscious/unconscious boundary carries a "common supramodal signature". This void is consonant with that *result*: a mind-side boundary with no modality-specific signature is what one would expect if the mind side carries no modality tags at all. The apex's carving is drawn from the brain side, in the psychophysics this void concedes, so nothing here reaches it. Consonant, not probative — a physicalist reads the same signature as late convergence in a brain that holds the tags and discards them, and nothing here separates the readings.

The Map's [[common-cause-null|common-cause null]] then falls due on this article's own convergence, as a standing obligation rather than an option ([[positions/methodology-and-calibration|P-M2]], credence *high*). The attribution demonstrations share one upstream — central or multisensory integration assigning a channel label that mismatches the transducer, read through a single introspective channel — so oral referral and the McGurk capture carry the weight of one pattern, not two independent confirmations. No structurally distinguishing test is on the table: a patient population in which one mis-tag dissociates from the other would be one, and none is cited. The partial defence runs at the level of the *faces* rather than the demonstrations, where a taxonomic count, a token mis-attribution and a routing fact sit at different levels of description — a thinner independence than cross-substrate recurrence, claimed as no more. The void locates an explanatory residue and evidences no account of one.

**[[tenets#^occams-limits|Occam's Razor Has Limits]]** gains a clean instance beside the [[palette-extension-void|palette extension void]]. Five is the simplest count and the one consciousness reports. Macpherson notes that scientists are some of "the folk" and that "the number of senses that they recognize has frequently been altered", the dispute running throughout history. The parsimonious carving is the one the evidence supports least; parsimony here selects an answer rather than tracking one.

**[[tenets#^bidirectional-interaction|Bidirectional Interaction]]** attracts one speculative remark. The tenet commits to selection among physically underdetermined *outcomes*, and the contents those outcomes go on to carry arrive tagged with a channel the selecting consciousness cannot read — so the interface would be modality-blind on the mind side. An angle, not a finding.
