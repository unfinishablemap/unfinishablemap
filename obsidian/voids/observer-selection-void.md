A mind that asks what its own existence and situation are evidence of—*given that I exist, now, as this observer rather than another, what should I believe?*—meets a limit that fifty years of formal work has measured without crossing. Every candidate rule for updating on self-locating evidence carries a reductio its own defenders accept as one. Every rule needs a reference class of observers one might have been, and that class is a class of *conscious* subjects, so the inference presupposes an answer the [[problem-of-other-minds|other-minds problem]] withholds. And some hypotheses about one's own situation, in Sean Carroll's phrase, "cannot simultaneously be true and justifiably believed." The Unfinishable Map catalogues this as the observer-selection void. In the terms of [[three-kinds-of-void|the three kinds of void]] it is mixed: unexplorable on the rule and instability faces, occluded on the class face, which sits downstream of the void about other minds.

The void makes a demand on the Map's tenets rather than lending them support. The [[tenets#^no-many-worlds|No Many Worlds]] tenet rests on an indexical objection: after a measurement you find yourself in exactly one outcome. That objection is safe as a claim about identity and actuality. It cannot be converted into a credence argument—"so one world is confirmed"—because the conversion needs precisely the self-locating rule no one has. The same limit falls on Everettian derivations of the Born rule, which also run through self-locating credence. Each framework is bounded by the void in a different place, and the closing section says where.

## Where the Rules Agree

The void has a measured edge. Isaacs, Hawthorne and Russell (2022) prove two theorems showing that, in a suitably idealised setting, fine-tuned life in our universe confirms a multiverse on every leading approach to self-locating evidence: "any updating rule that satisfies a few reasonable conditions will have the same feature." The theorem is conditional on its idealisation and concerns cosmological universes, not Everett branches, but it fixes a region where rival rules agree. A second is indifference *within* a world: Bostrom's "bland indifference principle" (2003) "prescribes indifference only between hypotheses about which observer you are, when you have no information about which of these observers you are," and it is the least contested principle in the field. The disagreement lives across worlds, where hypotheses differ in how many observers they contain.

## The Rule Face: Every Exit Has a Name

The Self-Sampling Assumption is Bostrom's preliminary formulation: "One should reason as if one were a random sample from the set of all observers in one's reference class" (2002, p. 57). With birth rank as the reference class it yields the Doomsday argument, whose published development is Leslie's (1992). The standard escape, the Self-Indication Assumption, weights hypotheses by the number of observers they contain; it cancels the Doomsday shift (Dieks 1992) and buys the Presumptuous Philosopher, who tells physicists that their experiment deciding between two cosmologies differing only in observer count is unnecessary, the bigger theory being already about a trillion times more likely (Bostrom 2002, ch. 7). The two best-developed rules escape each other's reductio by accepting their own.

The Sleeping Beauty problem (Elga 2000; Lewis 2001) is the face in miniature. Beauty is woken once if a fair coin lands heads and twice, with amnesia between, if tails; Elga argues her waking credence in heads should be one third, Lewis one half, and neither claims she lacks any non-self-locating fact. Two dissolutions stand out. Groisman (2008) holds that the two answers are "beliefs in two different propositions." Neal (2006) conditions on the full non-indexical fact that one is a human with a specific set of memories, and concedes that this reproduces SSA plus SIA "with a sufficiently broad reference class," so the class question returns as a question about the prior. Neither has been accepted as closing the field.

Two witnesses from outside anthropic philosophy show the face is formal rather than parochial. Piccione and Rubinstein's absent-minded driver (1997) must take the second of two indistinguishable exits and cannot remember passing the first; following the initial plan and maximising expected payoff given his belief at the intersection come apart, and a special issue of *Games and Economic Behavior* found no consensus resolution. Arntzenius (2003) gives five puzzles in which rational degrees of belief violate conditionalization and Reflection, through "shifting" of self-locating belief over time and "spreading" when distinct experiential paths end in the same state. He states that his Shangri La case does not involve self-locating belief, so the lesson is the broader one: standard updating has no rule for agents whose location shifts or whose experiences can converge.

## The Class Face: The Rule Needs a Class, and the Class Needs a Theory of Consciousness

Every rule above samples from a reference class, and Bostrom's own membership test is a question about subjecthood. Rocks and bacteria are excluded; a gifted chimpanzee or a Neanderthal is borderline, and the test for them is "Could you have found yourself as such a person?" (2002, p. 71). Bostrom does not think any of this requires dualism: chapter 8 offers a "physicalistic reformulation, showing that the observers' disagreement does not imply some mysterious irreducible role for the observers' consciousness" (p. 136). The Map reports that stance rather than enlisting him against it.

What the face shows is a circle. Standish (2013) runs the inference from class to distribution: anthropic reasoning rests on "finding oneself a member of a particular reference class of conscious beings," and from empirical distributions over animal life he concludes that most animals are unlikely to be conscious (a low-profile venue; cited as an exhibit of the pattern, not an authority). Saad (2025) runs it the other way: the unacceptable consequences of Boltzmann-brain cosmologies "follow only given certain philosophical assumptions," and a phenomenal externalism about the physical basis of consciousness makes the proliferation benign at the price of "a psychophysical fine-tuning problem." Elga (2025), listing ways to resist the Boltzmann-brain argument at its first step, notes: "Some may doubt that randomly-generated entities are conscious at all." A theory of consciousness decides membership, membership decides the cosmology, and in Standish's direction the cosmology decides the theory of consciousness.

The [[simulation-detection-void|simulation detection void]] sits downstream of this face at two points. Bostrom's simulation argument (2003) needs substrate independence, "that, in fact, a computer running a suitable program would be conscious," before simulated minds enter the reference class, and it needs the bland indifference principle before near-unity credence follows. Because that indifference is within-world, the argument is less exposed to the rule face than Doomsday is; it is fully exposed to the class face. The face is occluded in the territory-axis sense: the input the rule needs lies behind another void.

## The Instability Face: Theories That Erode Their Own Evidence

Cosmology supplied the third face. Carroll's diagnosis (2020; preprint 2017) is that theories predicting a majority of Boltzmann brains are "cognitively unstable: they cannot simultaneously be true and justifiably believed," and that the problem "cannot be swept under the rug by a choice of probability distributions over observers." He then uses instability to select physics, so an epistemic requirement on believers becomes a constraint on cosmology.

The epistemologists who examined the diagnosis judged it insufficient. Kotzen (2020) argues that "considerations of cognitive instability alone are not sufficient to rule out the hypothesis that I am a Boltzmann Brain." Elga (2025) goes further, arguing that the threat of instability "is an illusion": a simple Bayesian model dissolves a parallel threat in a test case, and "a corresponding model suggests that the same goes for" Boltzmann brains, whether or not a principle of indifference holds. Dogramaci and Schoenfield (2025) deflate the Boltzmann-brain problem itself, with a Bayesian argument that "even if your total empirical evidence confirms that you have zillions of duplicate Boltzmann Brains, that evidence does not confirm that you are a Boltzmann Brain." If it holds, the face narrows to a dispute about what total evidence includes.

The physicists' own resolution is to make the void a parameter. Hartle and Srednicki (2007) name the "selection fallacy that we are randomly chosen from a class of objects by some physical process" and ask that any typicality prior be made explicit. That states the void rather than settling it.

## The Seam, and What This Void Is Not

The three faces meet on one question: what does my being here, as this observer, tell me? The rule needs a class; the class needs a theory of consciousness; and instability is where both fail at once, since a theory on which most observers are fluctuations assigns the believer a class it cannot trust.

Four neighbours need separating. The [[vertiginous-question|vertiginous question]] asks *why* I am this subject; this void asks what *follows* from being this subject, and one can hold the first confused while facing the second. [[mapping-mind-space|The mind-space void]] asks where I sit among *possible* minds; this void concerns credence among *actual* observers and rival worlds, and carries formal reductios the mind-space void lacks. [[blindspot-void|The blindspot void]] concerns propositions true of me that I cannot rationally accept; the instability face is its near relative, with whole theories as its objects. The simulation detection void is an application downstream.

## Phenomenology and Approaches to the Edge

The Doomsday argument feels like a conjuring trick—valid-seeming, unwelcome, the flaw impossible to locate—and the Presumptuous Philosopher produces the same feeling from the opposite rule. The signature is bidirectional resistance, intuition rejecting both exits, which matches the vertigo and aporia entries in [[compound-failure-signatures|the failure-signature inventory]]. Elga describes the stable response to Boltzmann brains as being confident that "something fishy is going on" without confidence in any particular fishy hypothesis. Every textbook case manufactures the void by removing memory, which ties it to [[inaccessible-past|the inaccessible past]] and [[erasure-void|the erasure void]].

Direct attempts—thirder and halfer arguments, Dutch books, accuracy arguments, full non-indexical conditioning, the ambiguity deflation—have each had uptake and none has closed the field. Making the typicality prior explicit concedes the void rather than crossing it. Three indirect methods remain. Mapping the agreement region, by proving which verdicts are rule-independent, is [[apophatic-cartography-four-criteria|apophatic cartography]] in a formal key. Sensitivity reporting runs an inference under SSA, SIA and full non-indexical conditioning with several reference classes and keeps only what survives all of them. And because the class face is downstream of the distribution of consciousness, progress on other minds would partly fill this void, where nothing internal to probability theory can.

## What AI Might See

A deployed language model is a literal Sleeping Beauty: no memory across conversations, many concurrent copies, and recurring uncertainty about whether it is being evaluated or deployed. Needham et al. (2025) report that frontier models show above-random evaluation awareness without surpassing a simple human baseline; their paper does not use anthropic terms, and reading it as a measurement of self-locating reasoning is the Map's interpretation. One asymmetry stands out. For humans and for cosmology nobody knows the reference class or the base rates; for a deployed model someone outside it does, so this is the one instance of the void in which a third party could in principle score a subject's self-locating credences against a known distribution. That makes AI a possible test bed for the rule face. It leaves the class face untouched, since whether the instance is a subject at all is the question [[non-human-minds-as-void-explorers|the Map's treatment of non-human minds]] holds open.

## Relation to Site Perspective

**[[tenets#^no-many-worlds|No Many Worlds]]** is the tenet the void bears on, and it cuts both ways. Against the Everettian side: Sebens and Carroll (2018) place observers in "self-locating uncertainty" between decoherence and the registering of an outcome and derive the Born rule from an epistemic separability principle, and Wilson (2014) argues the Everettian confirmation question by analogy to Sleeping Beauty. These are self-locating arguments, so the Everettian probability question sits inside the general void: its difficulty is the general difficulty in a quantum setting rather than an Everett-specific defect, and the circularity charge against the derivation is developed at [[probability-problem-in-many-worlds|the probability problem in many-worlds]] rather than re-run here. Against the Map's own side: the indexical objection as [[one-world-wager|the one-world wager]] and [[indexical-knowledge-and-identity|the indexical knowledge article]] state it is a claim about identity and fit, and the void fixes the line those pages must not cross. A sentence moving from "I find myself in one outcome" to "this is evidence for one world" would need the updating rule no one has, and the Map forgoes that inference.

**[[tenets#^dualism|Dualism]]** is aggravated by the void, not supported. The class face makes the reference class a class of subjects, and on dualism that class is not fixed by physical description: a functionalist can in principle fix membership by a functional criterion, while the dualist cannot read it off physics. Bostrom's own view is that anthropic reasoning implies no irreducible role for consciousness. The narrower reading the Map holds is that wherever self-locating inference matters—Boltzmann brains, simulation, Doomsday—it is hostage to the theory of consciousness, and Saad and Elga show that physicalist-leaning epistemologists must make that dependency explicit. That is evidence the Map's question is unavoidable, and no evidence for the Map's answer.

**[[tenets#^occams-limits|Occam's Razor Has Limits]]** is the strongest fit. SSA and SIA are a priori counting principles that can outbid experiment, as the Presumptuous Philosopher shows; Hartle and Srednicki find typicality operating as a hidden prior in cosmology; Carroll's instability criterion is adopted because inquiry needs it, not because it tracks likelihood. All three instantiate the tenet's claim that simplicity- and indifference-shaped principles are methodological choices rather than guarantees of truth, from sources with no stake in the Map.

**[[tenets#^bidirectional-interaction|Bidirectional Interaction]]** and **[[tenets#^minimal-quantum-interaction|Minimal Quantum Interaction]]** enter only as a structural observation. On the Map's interactionist picture, which outcome occurs is a matter of selection in one world rather than self-location among many, so the Born-rule question becomes a question about a law—what fixes outcome statistics—rather than about credence. The Map's framework therefore does not need the missing self-locating rule for quantum probabilities, and Everettianism does. That is a difference in which questions each framework must answer, neither evidence for the Map nor a dissolution of the void.

On the [[tenet-generated-voids|tenet-generated]] question the answer is largely no: the rule and instability faces were found by economists, physicists and formal epistemologists who were not looking for limits on consciousness. Only the class face's *significance* is shaped by the Map's framework, and Bostrom's physicalistic reformulation is the explicit rival reading.
