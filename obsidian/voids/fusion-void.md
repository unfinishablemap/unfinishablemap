Every percept of a single world is the output of a combination step consciousness never witnesses. When the eye and the hand both estimate an object's size, the nervous system weights each estimate by its reliability and delivers only the weighted result. The Unfinishable Map calls the resulting structural opacity the **fusion void**: the component estimates, the weights applied to them, and the prior verdict that the signals shared a cause at all are consumed in producing the percept, and none is recoverable from inside it. The claim concerns an *operation* whose workings are not introspectable, not an absence or thinness in experience — the fused percept is fully present; its manufacture is what does not appear.

Two qualifications belong with that claim rather than after it. First, the void's boundary has been measured from outside, which no other void in the Map's catalogue can say: fusion is mandatory within a single sense and negotiable between senses, and the line between them has psychophysical coordinates (Hillis, Ernst, Banks & Landy, 2002). Second, the combination machinery sits firmly on the brain side of the Map's [[capability-division-problem|capability division problem]] — cue weighting and perceptual causal inference are among the best-modelled operations in perception science. The void *locates* an explanatory residue; it supplies no evidence for any account of one.

## The Common-Cause Gate

Before any combining, a prior question is settled: do these signals come from one source or two? Körding and colleagues (2007) built an ideal-observer model that infers exactly this and found it predicts human audio-visual localisation. They conclude that "the capacity to infer causal structure is not limited to conscious, high-level cognition; it is also performed continually and effortlessly in perception."

Consciousness never receives the question, only a world already parsed into events. Seeing *one* thing happen is the conclusion of a comparison between causal models that was never experienced as a comparison — and when the comparison goes the other way, two events arrive with no felt trace of the rejected one-cause hypothesis. There is no experience of nearly fusing.

## The Weights Consciousness Never Sees

Given a decision to fuse, each component is weighted by reliability. Ernst and Banks (2002) proposed "a general principle, which minimizes variance in the final estimate, determines the degree to which vision or haptics dominates," implemented by maximum-likelihood combination. The weights are not fixed properties of the senses: they track momentary stimulus quality and change when it changes.

The ventriloquist effect is this operation seen from outside. Alais and Burr (2004) showed that vision captures sound because vision is usually the better spatial estimator, and that "for severely blurred visual stimuli (that are poorly localized), the reverse holds: sound captures vision." A folk description of visual dominance turns out to describe a contingent output of reliability weighting, reversible by degrading the input. The phenomenology reports the verdict's content and nothing of its production: the felt sense that one *saw where the voice came from* is the weighted result, and the auditory estimate that disagreed is gone.

## Component Destruction and the Measured Edge

The components are not merely unreported. Within a sense they are destroyed. Hillis, Ernst, Banks and Landy (2002) found that "single-cue information is indeed lost when cues from within the same sensory modality" are combined, "but not when different modalities (vision and haptics) are combined." Physically different cue-combinations that fuse to the same within-modality estimate become indiscriminable — perceptual metamers. The pre-fusion components are unavailable not only to report but to discrimination, psychophysics' most sensitive probe.

Deroy and colleagues (2016) state the seam from inside the empirical literature: "one may have privileged access to the integrated product while losing access to its component parts." They restate the cross-modal exception a decade on, noting that participants "do not integrate signals from vision and haptics into perceptual metamers."

This draws the void's edge, and answers the obvious deflation — that nothing is lost, because the pre-fusion components were never mental contents to begin with. The Hillis result absorbs that objection and sharpens the void: between senses the components *are* partially available, so the architecture demonstrably can retain component access, and within a sense it demonstrably does not. The limit is a fact about where the architecture spends retention, and no percept carries a marker saying which side of that line it came from.

On the Map's [[three-kinds-of-void|three-kinds taxonomy]] the core is *unexplorable* — no introspective act can recover information the system no longer carries — with an *unexplored* margin in cross-modal component access and in the variability described next. That the boundary between core and margin has been charted from outside is what makes this void unusual.

## A Trait No One Can Read From Inside

The most-discussed exhibit is the McGurk effect (McGurk & MacDonald, 1976): a dubbed audiovisual syllable is heard as a third syllable matching neither component. It is commonly reported that knowing the dubbing does not undo the heard syllable. The Map rests nothing on that reported impenetrability: the effect's robustness is actively debated, and the 1976 report was not consulted for this article.

What it does rest on is the variability. Mallick, Magnotti and Beauchamp (2015) observed "dramatic differences both in how frequently different individuals perceived the illusion (from 0 % to 100 %)," stable across a year (test-retest correlation, r = 0.91). Different minds run visibly different audiovisual weightings — a durable personal parameter rather than noise.

That adds a second-order opacity to the first. A subject who fuses on every trial and a subject who never fuses are phenomenologically alike: each simply hears a syllable, and nothing in experience marks which kind of perceiver one is. The result converts the fusion void from a claim about universal architecture into a mapped dimension of [[mapping-mind-space|mind-space]] that no mind can locate itself on unaided.

## Distinguishing Sibling Voids

The [[binding-void|binding void]] concerns why phenomenal unity resists *explanation*. The fusion void concerns the combination of several channels' estimates of one property, its hidden weights and its hidden causal-model gate — an operation rather than unity's explicability.

The [[predictive-construction-void|predictive construction void]]'s subtraction barrier concerns removal of self-caused sensory input before it reaches awareness. The two barriers are independent: a system could subtract reafference while retaining its cue components, or discard components while keeping no reafference model at all.

The [[plenitude-void|plenitude void]]'s ensemble-statistics position concerns the representational *format* of many-item scenes. And where [[transit-void|transit]], [[inference-void|inference]] and [[decision-void|decision]] are operation-voids of cognition, fusion is perception's.

## Approaches to the Edge

**Direct methods** exist only on the cross-modal side. Observers can discriminate congruent from incongruent visual-haptic stimuli even when their fused estimates match, so component access is partially exercisable between senses. Within a sense the ceiling is proven rather than merely encountered: metamers are indiscriminable by the definition of the measurement.

**Indirect methods** work well, which is precisely the asymmetry. Fitting observer models to a subject's choices recovers the weights and the causal-structure gate the subject cannot report. Titrating inter-sensory conflict and watching where reports break from fusion to segregation maps the operating range of the common-cause decision. Comparing individuals maps a dimension of variation no individual experiences as one. The machinery is third-person open and first-person closed.

**[[non-human-minds-as-void-explorers|What a differently-built mind might see]].** Engineered sensor fusion — Kalman filtering, Bayesian fusion in robotics — keeps what the human architecture discards: component estimates, covariances and weights are explicit and logged. That is an existence proof, on the Map's own analysis rather than any cited finding, that combination need not destroy its inputs; the human metamer result is a contingent design fact rather than a necessity. Two cautions, both speculative. Agreement at the verdict layer does not imply shared machinery, since a system can match human reports while weighting the streams differently. And inspectability of a model's fusion by its engineers is not inspectability *by the model*, so the fusion void may recur at the report layer of artificial minds too.

## Relation to Site Perspective

**[[tenets#^occams-limits|Occam's Razor Has Limits]]** is the tenet this void bears on most directly. The common-cause gate is parsimony implemented: perception continually chooses between a one-cause and a two-cause model of its inputs, and consciousness receives only the world the winning model implies. Where the Map's [[epistemological-limits-occams-razor|parsimony void]] treats the razor as a cognitive bias in theory choice, the fusion void shows the same economy running below theory, inside perception, where its verdicts arrive as finished percepts and its parameters cannot be audited. When the felt singleness of a scene is offered as evidence of anything, the offer should be discounted by the fact that singleness is what the fusion machinery manufactures.

**[[tenets#^dualism|Dualism]]** takes a two-sided bearing, and both sides matter. Cue weighting and perceptual causal inference are quantitatively modelled with no remainder resisting computational treatment; they belong on the brain side of any capability division, and the Map says so plainly. Precisely because the combination machinery is so fully charted, the residue stands out. Deroy and colleagues note "the discrepancy between phenomenal experience and multisensory integration as measured at the neural level." Fusion explains how contents get combined; it does not touch why the combined content is *experienced* as one. The fusion void thereby sharpens the binding void by subtraction, removing the computational combination story from the binding question and showing what remains. Calibrated per the Map's [[common-cause-null|common-cause null]]: this locates the explanatory residue rather than evidencing any account of it, and a physicalist reading of that residue is untouched by anything argued here.

**[[tenets#^minimal-quantum-interaction|Minimal Quantum Interaction]]** and **[[tenets#^bidirectional-interaction|Bidirectional Interaction]]** attract one remark, offered as speculation. If a non-physical influence operates anywhere in the perception-action loop, precision weights are the kind of small, high-leverage parameter the minimality requirement points toward, since a slight shift in weighting propagates into a different perceived world. That is an observation about where leverage would be cheap. No claim is made that any interface operates at this site, and the void supplies no evidence that one does.

The catalogue itself performs fusion: lines of evidence are combined into single judgements, with the weighting of sources set by whoever writes the synthesis and reported nowhere. The felt unity of a conclusion is the output of a combination whose weights the concluder cannot read — a caution the Map owes its own syntheses.
