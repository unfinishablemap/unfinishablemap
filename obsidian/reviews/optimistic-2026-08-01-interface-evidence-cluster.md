## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

Chalmers would recognise `pharmacological-dissociation-as-evidence.md` as doing the thing he most often finds missing: distinguishing the levels an argument passes through instead of collapsing them. L136 stages a four-level ladder — separability, phenomenological reality, mechanistic independence, metaphysical irreducibility — and then states plainly that Level 4 "lies beyond pharmacological dissociation entirely." The instruction it gives itself is *don't collapse the chain*. That is the explanatory gap treated as a structural feature of the argument rather than as a rhetorical resource, and it is why the article can press hard on the dissociation data without the press turning into an inference to dualism.

The Class A xenon-ketamine exhibit (L88) is the sharpest thing in the cluster on Chalmers' own terms: same primary receptor, opposite phenomenological outcome, with behavioural unresponsiveness held fixed across both. That contrast makes phenomenal presence visible precisely by holding constant everything a functional story would use to explain it away — and the article still refuses the win, noting at L90 that "sophisticated functionalism predicts the same modular dissociation."

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

Stapp gets less than he would want, and the restraint is the point. `active-reboot.md` L99 sets out the genuinely tempting structure — deterministic molecular preparation of the substrate, then stochastic timing of re-engagement — and then declines to cash it: "Whether this maps onto consciousness biasing quantum indeterminacies at the interface, or onto purely classical neuronal noise, is the cross-tenet question; active reboot alone does not resolve it." L65 makes the same refusal with the Stone et al. (2025) two-orders-of-magnitude emergence variability, noting it is "equally consistent with classical neuronal noise."

The pharmacological apex L180 handles the Wiest (2025) microtubule work the same way, naming it "contested" and closing with "The compatibility is not entailment." Stapp would find the quantum bridge honestly located at the live-hypothesis tier rather than smuggled in as an implication of the molecular data. This is a cluster that could have overreached toward Stapp at four separate sites and did not at any of them.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

Nagel's question — what is it like — is the cluster's actual organising variable, and Class C is its cleanest formulation. `pharmacological-dissociation-as-evidence.md` L110–114 builds an argument entirely out of the failure of third-person proxies: matched behavioural depth, divergent interiors, therefore "behavioural-level matching is not a reliable proxy for interface-level matching." The midazolam case is the exhibit — a patient converses, follows instructions, holds content across seconds, and forms no lasting memory of any of it. The behavioural surface is silent about which interface configuration produced it.

The altered-states apex earns Nagel's approval at L144, where the convergence matrix's own entry is caveated against itself: the "Abolished" cell under propofol "reflects what patients retrospectively report," and first-person silence under amnesia "cannot by itself discriminate genuine cessation from disconnected content that was never retained." A matrix that flags which of its own cells are epistemically weaker than they look is a matrix built by someone who takes first-person access seriously enough to know where it runs out.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

Whitehead finds the cluster congenial in its central move: the interface is an *event architecture*, not a substance boundary. `active-reboot.md` L39 states the philosophical work explicitly — "active preparation of conditions for re-entry is event-architecturally distinct from substance-return" — and the whole concept is built around processes (dismantling, reopening, preparing) rather than around a thing that mind is.

Crucially, and per this skill's constraint, none of that congeniality is used to move anything up a tier. The same article's L95 says the reading is "*defeater-removal* against the strongest form of generator models — it does not by itself elevate filter-theory evidence to a higher tier," and L101 is an entire section of negations titled "What active reboot does not establish." Process-philosophical fit is claimed as fit and nothing more.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

Kane's interest is served indirectly and, again, without inflation. The stochastic-emergence structure in `active-reboot.md` L65 is the sort of indeterminacy Kane needs, and the article routes the question to `stochastic-emergence-as-quantum-interface-evidence` rather than resolving it in its own favour. The pharmacological apex L142 gives Kane his strongest local moment through the ketamine self-stultification case — patients whose later vivid reports presuppose the causal efficacy epiphenomenalism denies — and immediately bounds it: "The force is local to the case rather than general."

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

McGinn's humility is institutionalised here rather than merely professed. `pharmacological-dissociation-as-evidence.md` gives it a dedicated section — "What Pharmacological Dissociation Cannot Tell Us" (L144–154) — with four named limits, of which the first and fourth are the ones a less honest article would omit. L148: current anaesthetics "were discovered empirically, so interface components not targeted by any known drug stay invisible to the dissection." The four-component architecture is what today's drug repertoire happens to reach, and the article says so.

L154 is better still: the locked-in-syndrome negative case, imported specifically to mark where the methodology stops paying. "A reading that treated every interface perturbation as filter-modulation would expect something here and gets nothing." Building your own null result into your methodology article is the practical form of McGinn's point.

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

This is the cluster's strongest suit, and the verdict is load-bearing for the review.

**The tier discipline is stated as a rule, not improvised per article.** `cross-mechanism-convergence.md` L69 names the exact distinction this persona exists to protect: the pattern "raises the evidential bar above single-mechanism replication; it does not by itself license upgrading a finding from one tier of the five-tier scale to another." A finding backed by cross-mechanism convergence "sits *more securely* at its current tier" — stronger, same rung. That is a concept page whose entire purpose is to be a strength indicator that refuses to be a promotion, and L95 says why: multi-mechanism findings "are inflation-prone because the cumulative-accommodation-cost move can drift silently into 'the alternatives are excluded' if the discipline is not explicit."

**The tenet register is kept separate from the evidence register.** `pharmacological-dissociation-as-evidence.md` L134: the tenets "*permit* the interactionist reading of pharmacological dissociation, but permission is defeater-removal, not positive evidence." L164 then names the failure mode by its own name — "the slippage from defeater-removal to evidence-elevation when a dissociation pattern is presented as if it had positively supported the interactionist version of the structure." An article that names the trap it is most likely to fall into is an article that can be audited against its own standard, which is what the next section does.

**Tenet-as-evidence-upgrade is praise-worthily *not* done, at four separate sites.** The standout is self-inflicted. The Hu et al. (2023) KCC2 finding is the catalogue's canonical cross-mechanism exhibit, and it was worth four pharmacologically distinct agents. On 2026-08-01 the Map reduced its own exhibit: Hu et al.'s framing is two-class (three GABA-A agents plus ketamine), the Map reads three, "and even at three classes the exhibit rests on a three-class span plus one within-class replication rather than the four-target span the agent count invites." That sentence now appears in near-identical form in `active-reboot.md` L53, `cross-mechanism-convergence.md` L59, `pharmacological-dissociation-as-evidence.md` L100, and the altered-states apex L121. Downgrading your best exhibit is hard; propagating the downgrade to all four loci the same day is the part that usually fails.

The pharmacological apex compounds this with a **concession section that collects corrections running against the Map** (L138): filter theory's withdrawn bandwidth discriminator and interface friction's choking concession, with the shared diagnosis that "a phenomenon the framework accommodates gracefully is not thereby one the framework uniquely predicted." Add the clinical-cluster discount at L104 (four clinical windows do *not* count as four independent confirmations, since three collapse into one active-inference framing), the conversion-disorder discount at L106 (the FND literature reads the mechanism as *overactive* rather than missing, so it "no longer shows a channel going offline"), and the locked-in null at L154. Five self-inflicted discounts in one article.

**Empirical-claim fidelity is treated as its own axis.** `active-reboot.md` L55 disentangles two findings the literature invites you to conflate: PCI/Φ measures dissociating from behavioural responsiveness is a *dissociation* result, while the genuine *precedence* anchor is Lewis et al. (2018)'s sensory-gating transient. The article states that "the literature supports the dissociation and the sensory-gating precedence as separate findings, not a single integration-recovers-first claim." L85 goes further and turns a commonly-cited pair against the claim it is usually attached to: the Schnakers (2009) ~40% misdiagnosis rate and Cruse (2011) covert command-following "are detection and diagnostic-accuracy findings, not studies of the recovery sequence," and what they do show "cuts in an awkward direction here." Retiring your own supporting citations because they support a *different* proposition is the rarest correction in the catalogue.

**Honest framework-stage calibration.** The altered-states apex L121 marks the stochastic-emergence gloss as "a framework-stage interpretation of the data rather than an established mechanism," and L158 marks the Xu et al. (2023) gamma-surge inference the same way: "the surge is documented, the experiential gloss is contested."

**And the article the Map is *most* invested in concedes the most.** `topics/bandwidth-of-consciousness.md` L176 could have been the Map's cleanest anti-epiphenomenalism argument and instead reports that "The measured bitrate is exactly what epiphenomenalism predicts too, and so carries no evidential traction against it," followed at L178 by the distinction that does the real work: ~10 bits/s is a *reportable-access* throughput, an epistemic quantity, and reading it as conscious causal influence "requires the further premise that the reporting channel and the influencing channel are one and the same. The bandwidth data neither supply nor refute that premise."

That concession is what makes the next section a finding rather than a quibble.

## Calibration Concerns

### Concern 1 — the apex's Dualism paragraph argues from the two things its own body rules non-discriminating

`obsidian/apex/altered-states-as-interface-evidence.md` states three times that the six-state convergence does not discriminate:

- **L81**: "So the convergence-of-altered-states pattern cannot honestly be cited as multiple independent confirmations of filter theory—the cluster carries the evidential weight of one pattern, not six."
- **L154**: "The mere fact that interface components dissociate independently does not, by itself, favour filter theory over production models. Both frameworks accommodate structured, multi-component architectures."
- **L166**: "the discriminating weight rests on the enhancement-through-disruption cases, not on the count of converging states."

The Dualism tenet paragraph at **L176** then argues from the count *and* from differential decomposition, unhedged, to a comparative metaphysical verdict:

> "A multi-component architecture that decomposes along consistent lines across pharmacology, sleep, ritual training, transitional states, psychedelic neurochemistry, and end-of-life neurodegeneration **is more naturally explained by an interface between two distinct domains** than by a single domain producing a phenomenon whose internal seams happen to align this way across every disruption type."

This is the count of converging states doing exactly the work L166 says it cannot do, and the differential-decomposition pattern doing exactly the work L154 says it cannot do.

The sharpening detail: "end-of-life neurodegeneration" is terminal lucidity, which the same article at **L125** classifies as pointing the other way — the first prospective study (Tollock et al. 2025) is "more consistent with a neurochemical reading than with interface loosening," and "Its place in the catalogue is as the costliest anomaly, not confirmation." The tenet paragraph counts as one of six supporting perturbations the case the body calls the cluster's costliest anomaly.

The fix is re-framing, not deletion. The honest version of the paragraph is available from the body itself: the pattern establishes that *whatever* account is right must have multi-channel structure (L81), and the discriminating weight sits with the enhancement-through-disruption cases (L166). The sibling articles supply ready-made register — compare `active-reboot.md` L95 ("defeater-removal … does not by itself elevate filter-theory evidence to a higher tier") and `pharmacological-dissociation-as-evidence.md` L178 ("The dissociation classes constrain identity readings without establishing dualism").

The Occam's Razor paragraph at L184 is *partially* affected — two of its three clauses are the enhancement-through-disruption and Class C cases the body does license, so only the third clause ("why components dissociate along the same architectural lines across unrelated perturbations") inherits the problem. Weaker instance; note it, do not rewrite the paragraph.
