## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

- **Internal contradiction, Relation to Site Perspective (L93)**: "self-stultification
  *establishes* the causal efficacy dualism requires" sat in the paragraph that says the
  retorsive instance is "arguably" a framework-boundary engagement and "not a premise-neutral
  defeat". It also contradicted the L65 and L73 installs and tenets ^tenet-3-epiphenomenalism
  ("deepest difficulty … rather than its refutation"). **Resolved**: "establishes" →
  "argues for".
- **Calibration, retorsion sentence (L73)**: the sentence still asserted in the Map's own voice
  that the eliminativist's and epiphenomenalist's acts "presuppose the efficacious consciousness
  their thesis deflates". Dennett's reply is that the acts presuppose efficacious *cognition*,
  which he grants, and not efficacious *phenomenal* consciousness. **Resolved**: added "on the
  argument's reading" and named the epiphenomenalist's strongest reply. That reply is the
  [[phenomenal-concepts-strategy|phenomenal-concept strategy]], which denies the reading rather
  than the performance. This mirrors master-argument L131/L133. One refinement on the queued
  wording: the strategy is now called "the epiphenomenalist's strongest reply" (the hub's L75
  phrase), because it is not the eliminativist's escape and the sentence names both.
- **Calibration, Stroud reply and datum guardrail (L83–L89)**: the Stroud challenge is posed
  about *efficacy* ("merely *believe* that consciousness is efficacious"). The reply secures the
  phenomenal *datum*, which is a different claim. The reply's closing sentence ("does not merely
  show that we must *believe* in efficacious consciousness") implied that the reflexive argument
  delivers efficacy, while the next guardrail only fenced *irreducibility* off from the datum. A
  reviewer who accepts the tenets would still flag this, because tenets
  ^tenet-3-epiphenomenalism makes efficacy turn on a contested epistemic premise. **Resolved**:
  (a) the Stroud reply now ends "starts from consciousness as given, not from a belief we merely
  cannot shed"; (b) guardrail 3 now says the datum secures neither irreducibility nor causal
  efficacy, and adds that "an epiphenomenalist can grant it while denying the efficacy, which is
  why the closure instance carries its proviso about knowledge". This is the same calibration
  family as the 10-08 sweep. The sweep did not reach it because it searched for refutation verbs,
  and this overclaim was carried by sentence structure, not by a verb.
- **Internal tension, second paragraph (L33)**: "Pragmatism is judged by its own 'what works'
  test and fails it" was flat, while the pragmatism section (L61) says the collapse follows
  "only given the Map's own commitment". **Resolved**: "and, granted a premise the Map
  supplies, fails it".
- **Overclaim, tenet paragraph (L93)**: "closure's circularity and Cutter's monism recoil …
  shows rivals unable to stop short of dualism on their own standards". The closure dilemma
  shows that the anti-dualist argument fails. It does not drive the closure theorist to dualism,
  and the same paragraph says it only "clears the ground". **Resolved**: "shows each rival
  failing its own standard: the closure argument cannot exclude dualism, and the monist cannot
  stop short of it."
- **Unverifiable quotation (L48)**: Castagnoli was quoted as saying that the ancient variety shows
  someone "cannot coherently or consistently maintain" a thesis. The phrase is not in the CUP
  blurb, the BMCR 2011.08.21 review or the Uckelman review. Google Books search-within on
  `X3ajOmFles0C` does not find it as a contiguous phrase. The control queries worked: "Democritus"
  had 8 hits and "entails its contradictory" was found contiguous on p. 118. The only source is
  the Map's own 2026-07-07 research note, whose "Quote" line gives no page. The substance is
  correct: Uckelman says self-refutations "are merely indefensible; they need not be false". The
  quotation marks were not. **Resolved**: the article now quotes Castagnoli's verbatim "indefensible
  in debate" (p. 28, found by search-within: "indefensible in debate; does this mean that what it
  expresses … has been proved not to be the case? I suggest that the answer is 'no'"), and the
  maintainability gloss is paraphrased without quotation marks.

### Counterarguments Considered

- **Eliminativist (Churchland)**: assertion need not presuppose belief. This is now covered by
  "on the argument's reading". It stays bedrock at this page's altitude, as in the 08-12 stability
  notes.
- **Physicalist (Dennett)**: the performance presupposes efficacious cognition, not phenomenal
  efficacy. **Addressed** by the L73 hedge and the guardrail-3 efficacy clause.
- **Quantum Skeptic (Tegmark) / Many-Worlds (Deutsch)**: the article makes no quantum or
  branching claims. Nothing to engage.
- **Empiricist (Popper)**: maintainability claims are not falsifiable. The article already uses
  the dialectical (maintainability) reading as its weaker, safer claim, so no change.
- **Madhyamaka (Nagarjuna)**: the "datum" is itself constructed. This is bedrock. Turning the
  method on the Map is covered by the judging-the-map-as-science Further Reading entry.
