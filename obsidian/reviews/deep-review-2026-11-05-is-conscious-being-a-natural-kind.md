## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

1. **Kendler, Zachar & Craver (2011): a quotation that is not in the source, plus an unverifiable rationale.** L46 quoted MPC as "offers the most promising answer to the question of what kinds of things psychiatric disorders are". The abstract, read raw from Crossref/OpenAlex, says instead: "The MPC view is the best current answer to 'What kinds of things are psychiatric disorders?'" The full text is paywalled; Cambridge, Ovid and Semantic Scholar were all checked. The sentence also gave a reason for the rename ("homeostatic" wrongly implied physiological equilibrium) and a caution ("too early to force psychiatric categories into the mold"). Neither appears in the abstract, and neither could be checked against the body. The 2026-07-15 ledger said the rationale was "confirmed against the paper's abstract". That was wrong: the word "homeostatic" does not occur in the abstract. **Fix**: the sentence now uses only verbatim abstract text: a model "first proposed for biological species"; kinds defined "not in terms of essences but in terms of complex, mutually reinforcing networks of causal mechanisms"; "the best current answer". The word "current" keeps the provisional tone.
2. **Antony (2006): an unverifiable quoted term, and a claim from the 2008 paper credited to 2006.** L64 had the 2006 paper "name the 'intuition of sharpness'" for CONSCIOUS STATE *and* CONSCIOUS CREATURE. The 2006 abstract concerns CONSCIOUS STATE only; it "gives reasons for taking seriously" that the concept is sharp. The CONSCIOUS STATE/CONSCIOUS CREATURE pairing is the title of Antony's 2008 *Erkenntnis* paper, and Schwitzgebel (2023, §9) cites "Antony (2006, 2008)" separately. The phrase "intuition of sharpness" turns up nowhere checkable: not in the 2006 abstract, not in Schwitzgebel's ~12,000-word discussion of Antony, and not in web search. The phrase's only Map source is the research note. **Fix**: the sentence now says the 2006 paper gives reasons to take seriously that CONSCIOUS STATE is sharp, and that "his 2008 sequel extends the claim to CONSCIOUS CREATURE". Antony 2008 was added to References after a Crossref check. The page's own label, "the sharpness intuition", stays as Map wording with no quotation marks.
3. **Antony's conclusion lost its qualifier.** L78 said "identity theory, functionalism, *and* dualism are all false". Antony's conditional covers "common versions" only, those that "identify, realize, or correlate" consciousness with complex properties. That wording appears in the abstract and in the siblings `phenomenal-sorites-problem` L67 and `coupling-engagement-condition` L56. **Fix**: "common versions of identity theory, functionalism, *and* dualism are false". The next sentence already restricts the scope to complex properties, so the paragraph is now consistent with itself.
4. **Schwitzgebel's naturalism premise was missing (a sibling repair).** L66 said Schwitzgebel "defends" borderline cases without the premise those cases depend on. The published abstract reads: "Assuming mainstream naturalism about consciousness, we ought to reject the first three options". The siblings `coupling-engagement-condition` L66 and `architectural-adequacy-at-the-built-edge` L120 carry the premise; this page was the last one without it. **Fix**: "argues, assuming mainstream naturalism about consciousness, for genuine gray-zone cases". The baldness/colour analogy was checked against the author's manuscript: "It's like the property of being bald, or green, or extraverted".
5. **L72 still grounded presence in efficacy (left over from the 2026-09-06 fix).** The 2026-09-06 review moved L78's on/off truth-maker from the interface threshold to bare coupling-engagement, and its stability note forbids grounding *presence* in an *influence* construct. L72 still read "a determinate fact about whether the non-physical difference is being made". "Making a difference" is efficacy, and on the Map's picture a subject can be present below the interface threshold without making a selective difference. That left L72 contradicting L78. **Fix**: "whether a non-physical subject is coupled to it".
6. **"Entailed by the tenets" was a calibration error.** L76 said "The sharp boundary is entailed by the tenets", and L72 drew sharpness from Tenet 1 alone. The Map's own canonical statement, [[tenets/background-commitments]] Posit One, puts the determinate subject among posits that "no tenet states outright". [[coupling-engagement-condition]] L98 says dualism makes a non-graded ground "available"; it does not say dualism entails one. A reviewer who accepts every tenet would still flag the sentence, because the five stated tenets do not get to sharpness by themselves. **Fix**: L72 adds "the determinate subject the tenets presuppose without stating" as the premise that turns an essence-like kind into a *sharp* one. L76 now reads "*if* the Map's dualism and its determinate subject, *then* a sharp joint follows. The sharp boundary is drawn from those commitments". This moves the calibration downward, so it honours the 2026-07-15 and 2026-09-06 stability notes against moving it toward entailment.
7. **The double-edge reply did not state the condition it depends on.** [[coupling-engagement-condition]] says three things. The Map "does not claim to have *shown* that engagement is non-graded" (L34). The grounding-versus-triggering problem is "an open metaphysical question" (L78). And the Tenet-4 defence rests "on a posit the Map has argued for but not secured, and the conditional should be stated wherever the defence is used" (L98). This page uses the same construct for the same escape and stated none of that. **Fix**: "That is how interactionist dualism proposes to avoid…", followed by a sentence saying the Map posits non-gradedness rather than showing it, and that a graded trigger could bring the sorites back. The closing line now reads "…only if the posit holds — the burden is the Map's to carry, not a free consequence."
