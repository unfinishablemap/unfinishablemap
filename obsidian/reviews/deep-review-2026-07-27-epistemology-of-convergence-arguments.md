## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

**1. Citation-framing error — Salmon mis-framed as strengthening a test he bounded. CORRECTED.**

The article read: *"Reichenbach's common-cause principle (1956), **sharpened by Salmon**, holds that a correlation demands either a direct causal link or a common cause that screens it off."*

Web-verified at the publisher of record (SEP, `plato.stanford.edu/entries/wesley-salmon/`; self-contamination guard clear — unfinishablemap.org did not appear in the verifying source). Salmon's actual contribution runs the other way: after van Fraassen's criticism he **identified limits** to screening-off and introduced **interactive forks** — cases where a genuine common cause *fails* to screen its effects off from each other (p(A∧B|C) > p(A|C)·p(B|C); Compton scattering, the billiard-ball case).

This is the [[citation-framing-accuracy-lens]] defect class: the citation is real, the metadata is correct (Princeton UP, 1984, confirmed), and the attributed conclusion is mis-framed. It mattered here more than usual because the mis-framing **flatters the article's own load-bearing test**: the article uses screening-off as its discriminator between genuine triangulation and one architecture read N times, and Salmon's actual result is that surviving the screening-off test is *weaker* evidence of independence than the clean form of the test suggests. Suppressing that bound made the Map's discipline look sharper than the literature licenses.

Corrected in place: Salmon now "developed the principle and also bounded it," with the interactive-fork limit stated and its consequence for the article's own test made explicit. The References annotation was updated from the bare "[Common-cause explanation.]" to name conjunctive/interactive forks and the screening-off failure cases.

**2. Philosophical misattribution — Yogācāra lumped with Madhyamaka as dissolving the witness. CORRECTED.**

The article read: *"the metaphysical interpretations diverge informatively (**Madhyamaka/Yogācāra dissolution of the witness**, Vedānta's Ātman-as-Brahman, theistic union)."*

This is backwards for Yogācāra. Verified: *svasaṃvedana* (reflexive self-awareness) is **defended** by the Yogācāra epistemologists — Dignāga in the *Pramāṇasamuccaya*, Dharmakīrti, Śāntarakṣita — and **attacked** by Prasaṅgika Madhyamaka, Candrakīrti and Śāntideva, on the ground that inherently reflexive consciousness would be self-validating and so not empty of *svabhāva*. Yogācāra is the school that *retains* a reflexive witness; grouping it with Madhyamaka under "dissolution of the witness" collapses the sharpest intra-Buddhist disagreement on precisely that question.

Two independent tells that this was AI-introduced rather than sourced: the sibling [[comparative-phenomenology-of-meditative-traditions]] the sentence paraphrases **never mentions Yogācāra** (it discusses Madhyamaka only, at its line 173), and the pairing is doctrinally reversed. Corrected to "Madhyamaka's dissolution of the witness against Yogācāra's reflexive *svasaṃvedana*" — which converts the error into a sharper instance of the very point the sentence makes, since the Madhyamaka/Yogācāra split *is* informative divergence of interpretation over a shared phenomenological finding.

**3. Self-application failure — the compound-signature exhibit over-claimed immunity from the audit. CORRECTED.**

The article claimed of its clearest worked exhibit (the three-route clinical compound signature): *"there is arguably **no candidate common cause** to screen off, because the routes are mechanistically disjoint."*

This fails the article's own standard, and fails it in exactly the shape the Map settled today in the voids demotion. Mechanistic disjointness of the *perturbations* (lesion / pharmacology / psychogenic dissociation) closes the **shared-machinery** channel only. It does nothing about a common cause of a different kind: the three routes are held to converge on a *single channel taxonomy* (ascending content, self-model, descending motor, bilateral unity, attentional gating) that the investigator supplies. A shared individuating scheme produces agreement by construction whether or not the conclusion is true — which is the whole content of the voids demotion, where the framework individuating the voids is the framework their clustering was read as supporting.

Corrected: "no candidate common cause" narrowed to "no candidate *mechanism*"; the bound stated explicitly ("Disjointness closes only the mechanism channel"); and the distinction named as shared definitions versus shared machinery, with the Map's own voids catalogue cited as the worked case where the definitional channel defeats the evidential reading. The Bovens–Hartmann sentence was moved ahead of the new bound so the qualification lands after the claim it qualifies rather than contradicting it.

This edit is the one place the voids demotion touches the article, and it enters as a *supporting exhibit for the discipline*, not as a correction of a misstatement about voids.
