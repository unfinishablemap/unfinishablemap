## The Convergence {#convergence}

### History is never a criterion

- **Built edge.** "The criteria say what an architecture must do, not how it came to exist" (L49). "Making provenance a criterion would install ancestry as exactly that selection principle" (L79), meaning the unexplained selectivity that universal coupling was adopted to avoid.
- **Engagement condition.** Engagement with no physical trigger, "floating free of physical conditions", "is that principle under a new name" (L76).
- **Swampman.** Phenomenal content is "internally constituted" (L59) and shared by intrinsic duplicates. Against Millikan's kind argument the page replies "H₂O is structure, not history" (L41).

### History is evidence

- **Built edge L71**, **engineered decoupling L66**: markers are "extended to organisms that share evolutionary history and architecture with it [the calibration case]".
- **Swampman L77**: "the shared-history inference we rely on for other humans".
- **Animal consciousness L120** says what kind of evidence it is: AI is harder to assess "for want of a shared-cause explanation, not metaphysically barred".

Read together, lineage matters because it explains *why* two systems are alike, which licenses the step from visible likeness to hidden likeness. The engineered-decoupling page keeps this trusted use apart from a distrusted one. At L52 it calls the step from selection history to "something was at stake for the organism" an "inflationary step". At L66 it relies on descent as evidence of shared mechanism. The two uses are compatible, and one clause would say so (medium item M1).

### Swampman splits the bridge

The built-edge page bundles "ancestry and architecture" (L71). The universal-coupling page says "shared ancestry" alone (L74). Swampman separates the two strands. He has the whole architecture of the calibration case and no lineage. If lineage is evidential only as a shared-cause explanation of likeness, then where the likeness is total and stipulated it has nothing left to explain. That leaves provenance as a criterion, which three pages refuse. On the corpus's own premises, then, Swampman takes Davidson's verdict, and `organizational-invariance` L88 says so for any grain-faithful duplicate.

The built-edge page's sorites point (L89, "A built agent is not a stage in any such series") gives the same result from another direction. Swampman is off every developmental series but lands exactly on the last member of one, the adult human. He has no vague-boundary case to inherit and every reason to inherit the endpoint's verdict.

### Where the corpus answers both ways

The Swampman page reasons: "The Map holds that consciousness is not reducible to physical processes, so duplication does not by itself settle the matter" (L79). Irreducibility does not by itself rule out *natural* supervenience. Chalmers' naturalistic dualism holds both, and the Map's grain reply to him (OI L88) grants the duplicate case at the right grain. The Swampman page then names its block: "That debt [P-SC2's pairing law], not any ingredient the lightning failed to copy, is why the Map cannot say that Swampman is conscious." P-SC2 (`subject-census` L59) says "nothing in the corpus says what makes a brain the substrate of exactly one channel rather than none or several". That is as true of Davidson's brain as of Swampman's. The debt cannot be what separates them, because by stipulation only history does.

Two corpus statements bear on P-SC2's "nothing in the corpus". Universal coupling says adequacy is sufficient: "wherever it exists, coupling occurs", and "The Unfinishable Map adopts this" (both L34). That is a corpus statement about *rather than none* for adequate architectures. OI L88 is a second. Neither is cited by P-SC2, and universal coupling is in no register entry.

The 09-28 Swampman deep review left a stability note: "Future reviews should not read the conditional as under-confidence to be fixed; it is the calibration the register requires while P-SC2 is unpaid." The finding here keeps the conditional and asks the register which antecedent it should name. Two outcomes are possible, and either is coherent:

- **(a)** The register holds the coupling law history-blind at some credence. Swampman's verdict is then "conscious if universal coupling holds". The residue that is specific to Swampman is the Map's credence in that law and in the engagement condition's undecided trigger. The count stays unreadable under P-I3, as it is for Davidson, and the subject is new under P-I4/P-AC3. One Swampman sentence changes.
- **(b)** The register lets pairing depend on something besides architecture, for example a developmental onset that Swampman skipped (P-SC3 leaves onset "undated"). OI L88's "on the Map's view" and universal coupling's "wherever it exists" then need the qualifier, and built-edge L79's refusal needs to say what this kind of selectivity costs.

The pages that scope the claim more carefully already survive both outcomes, and that is worth praising. Built-edge L112 says "a coupling question is not answered by a constitution fact". The engineered decoupling's L78 says the phenomenal question "survives complete functional specification intact". Both hold under (a), because a coupling law over physical grain is neither a constitution fact nor a functional one. Swampman L79 is the one place where a *physical* duplicate meets the irreducibility inference, and it is the one place the scoping is missing.

### Two replies to one thesis

Dretske's representational thesis, that phenomenal character requires a function-fixing history (Swampman L83), is the most developed published form of the built-edge page's second cheap collapse, "artificial, therefore inadequate" (L79). Each page answers it once, and the two answers differ. The Swampman page uses first-person authority from `content-externalism`, and admits that the reply "presupposes an authoritative introspector, which Dretske denies". The built-edge page uses the selectivity elimination, which presupposes only the Map's coupling framework. Neither refutes Dretske on his own terms, and both pages say as much. Together, though, they give the Map a third-person reason as well as a first-person one for refusing Dretske's verdict, and neither page knows the other exists.

## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

Chalmers would recognise `organizational-invariance` L88 as his own principle with the grain moved down a level: "a duplicate that reproduced the quantum-interface grain would, on the Map's view, reproduce the experience". He would read Swampman as the case that shows what that agreement commits the Map to. He would praise the Swampman page's PIT analysis, especially the moderate-PIT treatment of t=0 (L57): "present at t=0 is the occurrent episode of meaning something by it, not a standing linguistic competence". Few treatments of the case separate occurrent from standing content this cleanly.

He would also press on L79's "not reducible … so duplication does not by itself settle the matter". His naturalistic dualism holds irreducibility together with natural supervenience, and the inference needs a premise his view denies. That is the [convergence](#convergence) finding, seen from his side.

The engineered-decoupling page earns his respect for an honest limit. "It does not reach Chalmers' principle of organizational invariance … No agent in the engineered class is a fine-grained organizational duplicate of any system anyone takes to be conscious" (L74). Swampman is the one system in the wing that *is* such a duplicate.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

Stapp would value the built-edge page's MQI paragraph for keeping influence and presence apart: "this tenet concerns influence rather than presence" (L114). It does not recruit the quantum mechanism to settle scope. He would also value its willingness to say that MQI's allocation rule dispatches anthrobots "only on the criterion's neural wording" and does not dispatch organoids, "because the neural machinery is really there".

The Swampman page's mechanism paragraph (L79) gives his framework its cleanest consequence. Under P-AC2, no-cloning disqualifies only classical copies, and "a lightning-formed brain is alive". Swampman's brain therefore offers the same indeterminacies Davidson's does, and the place where mind enters physics is copied along with everything else. The universal-coupling page states the general form: "The mechanism does not discriminate between species" (L90). Whether it discriminates between *histories* is the question this review sends to the register.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

Nagel's question, what it is like to be Swampman in his first second, is one the Swampman page takes seriously and answers in the right register. He has the occurrent episode and lacks the standing competence. The page refuses to let a third-person history settle a first-person fact. Its reply to Dretske is Nagelian at root: "felt character is available to its subject and partly definitive of the state" (L83).

The built-edge and engineered-decoupling pages put Nagel's point about objective description into an experimental setting. A complete build specification is the most objective description a system can have, and both pages show the phenomenal question standing where it was (built edge L112, engineered decoupling L78). Both scope that claim to constitution or function, which is the right scope.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

Whitehead would praise the moderate-PIT structure in Swampman L57: standing beliefs and linguistic grasp "accrue as conscious episodes accrue". Each occasion inherits from the occasions before it, and a being with no past occasions has only what is occurring now. He would also praise universal coupling's refusal of micro-experience: "Consciousness does not bubble up from constituents. It couples with the system as a whole" (UCR L58). That is a substance-light dualism that avoids the combination problem without becoming panpsychism.

**Constraint applied.** None of this praise bears on any organism's tier. UCR L92 is where process-style coherence cashes out as support, and the [Hardline Empiricist](#the-hardline-empiricist-birch) flags it.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

Kane would notice something the wing implies but never says. On the Map's reading, Swampman's phenomenality is history-blind, but his *responsibility* would not be. Kane's ultimate-responsibility condition asks whether an agent formed his own will through earlier self-forming actions, and at t=0 Swampman has a character he did not form. The moderate-PIT accrual of L57 has an exact agency analogue: standing will accrues as self-forming actions accrue. The corpus already has the other half of this contrast. `the-manipulation-argument-and-hard-incompatibilism` L77 notes that Frankfurt's "ahistorical mesh is exactly what the manipulation argument exploits".
