## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

- **Result-direction error, Marsh et al. 2021 (§DID L76, table row, lead).** The page said Marsh found "only a trend toward loss on free recall" and that patients recognised more than simulators, and filed Marsh with the transfer studies. The full text (PMC7880432, General Discussion) says the reverse of the framing. DID participants "scored significantly lower on recognition sensitivity" for the other identity's material. Their recall "trended towards a profile of reported amnesia". Simulators produced the same profile. The authors say it is "not possible to determine" true from feigned amnesia, and the only leakage is that patients recognised more than simulators. Donath et al. 2025 (#2) names Marsh 2021 as a paradigm "where simulation is easy" and excludes it. **Fix**: Marsh is now described as an amnesia-like profile with some leakage relative to simulators. The table's DID performance cell said "Intact on recall, recognition and indirect tests". It now reads "Transferred on feigning-resistant tests; amnesia-like, with some leakage, on easily feigned ones". The lead's "replicated finding ... over intact first and second levels" became "better-supported finding ... over transfer at the first two" on "feigning-resistant tests", in line with L91's own "better-supported reading". The §autonoetic slogan "objectively transferred" became "on feigning-resistant tests, transferred". All of these lower the tier.
