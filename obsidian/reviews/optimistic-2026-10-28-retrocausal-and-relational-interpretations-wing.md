## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

The RQM page says the one thing a property dualist most wants said about an interpretation that drops the observer: "Its silence on phenomenology is exclusion, not neutrality" (L137). It then refuses the cheap version of the point — it does *not* claim RQM is refuted — and frames the dispute as "honest framework-boundary disagreement — neither refutes the other from inside" (L87). That is the hard problem used as a locating instrument rather than a cudgel.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

Three pages give Stapp's programme what it has always lacked in its own literature: an explicit accounting of *which* interpretation's machinery a selection story inherits and at what cost. The TI page's reason for valuing the handshake is exactly the right one — "any selection story built on TI **inherits the Born rule for free** rather than having to bolt it on" (L43) — and the Bohm page's reason for the active-information channel is the conservation profile, "a way for something to steer a physical process *without supplying energy to it*" (L43). The Bohm page then does what a Stapp partisan rarely does: it says Bohmian determinism "would therefore *undercut* the Map's selection mechanism, not support it" (L69) and prices both escape seams. This persona also notes that the wing honours the house rule that convergence counts as one observation: no page sums TI, TSVF and Bohm into "three independent lines of support"; the atemporal page calls them "three interpretive frameworks" that "draw on the same time-symmetric equations" (L59), which is the single upstream fact read three ways.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

The RQM page's Whitehead section separates two denials of the view from nowhere — relativising facts to observers versus *achieving* definiteness through becoming — and uses the difference to say what RQM leaves out ("something must still account for why each occasion achieves the definiteness it does", L115). The atemporal page connects selection to "the phenomenology of decision" (L106) without claiming the phenomenology *confirms* the physics; it says the experiences "match atemporal constraint satisfaction better than sequential causation", a comparative fit claim and nothing more.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

Bohm is Whitehead's natural ally and the page reads him seriously — the holomovement, soma-significance, "separateness is a secondary appearance abstracted from a prior wholeness" (L49). The atemporal page's §Philosophical Precedents places concrescence beside Plotinus and Aquinas as a lineage for causation outside sequence. Per this persona's constraint, none of that is used to move any claim up the evidential scale: the Bohm page's verdict is that the Map "catalogue[s] Bohm's apparatus as a borrowable channel and an instructive rival, not to host it as its own mechanism" (L74). Whitehead is on the co-optation roster; the RQM page states the Map "does not endorse Whitehead's panexperientialism" (L117), which is the honest one-liner; the atemporal page's Whitehead paragraph does not state his anti-dualism, but it enlists him only for the structural claim that constitution need not be sequential and points to `process-philosophy` for the rest, so I do not count it as a firewall breach (see Ideas for Later for the cheap tightening).

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

The atemporal page's dissolution of Libet — "a cause that isn't located in time cannot be 'too late'" (L45) — is stated, and then immediately bounded by the section that denies it is a mechanism. The positions register already holds this correctly: `positions/agency-and-will` P-A3 marks "the atemporal-selection add-on" as "held weakly" and subordinate to the forward-in-time preference in P-Q1 (L76–78, reconciled 2026-06-10). The page and the register agree; nothing to repair.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

The weak-measurement page is the wing's exemplar of saying how far an analogy reaches. Its §The Analogy and Its Limits names three breakpoints — projective filtering, the ensemble requirement, and the ontological addition TSVF's creators do not endorse — and then says "The Map treats these disanalogies as the central open problems of the proposal, not as minor caveats" (L94). The atemporal page closes its own loop: the concept is "the *where* ... and the *what* ..., but the *how* of non-physical participation remains an open question" (L139).

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

This persona's verdict is the one the wing earns most clearly.

- **The firewall page cuts both ways on purpose.** L77 refuses to grant the Map's own mechanism "a free pass while denying presentiment one on scale alone", and L103 extends the same rule to the physics: "a confirmed phenomenon is not a confirmed interpretation ... What the firewall buys is comparative, not absolute". The 07-18 ledger confirms the parapsychology statistics were re-verified at the publishers of record (Mossbridge CI, Galak N and d, the Wagenmakers "weak to nonexistent" quote at PubMed), so the restraint sits on checked numbers.
- **Interpretation-dependence is owned as a falsification handle, not hidden as a caveat.** The weak-measurement page says the proposal "stands or falls with how this contested interpretive question resolves" (L120) and that the Born-statistics identity constraint "can fail the theory but cannot establish it" (L122) — the asymmetry stated the right way round.
- **The source-role firewall holds across the wing.** Every borrowed author is held at arm's length by name: Cramer "assigns no role to consciousness" (TI L23); "Kastner herself does not invoke consciousness as the transaction selector; the Map constructs this as original synthesis" (atemporal L67); "Neither Wharton nor Price invoke consciousness in their models" (atemporal L79 — Price is on the pragmatist roster, and the page cites only his physics-vs-metaphysics distinction, which is the usable part); "neither Aharonov nor other TSVF proponents propose consciousness as the source of post-selection" (weak L104); Hiley–Pylkkänen's framing "is designed to *avoid* dualist interaction" (Bohm L59). Nobody is conscripted.
- **No epiphenomenalism is conceded anywhere.** The RQM page describes RQM as making consciousness "epiphenomenal by default" and treats that as RQM's cost, not a concession (L145).
- **Tenet 5 is applied to the Map's own reasoning only.** The firewall page's Tenet 5 paragraph (L107) uses it to forbid the Map two inferences — dismissing retrocausality because presentiment is dubious, and affirming it because presentiment seems supported — rather than to tax a rival's simplicity.
- **One calibration concern** (below): the "all-or-nothing" paragraph in atemporal-causation reads a Bell-scenario result as strengthening a phenomenological framing, which is a register crossing even though the citation itself is verified.

The two personas converge on the same passages (Bohm L69–74, weak L92–94); by the skill's own test, the wing has resolved the Whitehead/Birch tension honestly.
