### Medium Issues Found

- **"Same subject, same concentration, opposite sign"** overstated Holland & Ferner's design (paired and parallel-group studies were pooled). Resolution: dropped "Same subject"; the point survives as same-concentration, opposite-sign.
- **Closing generalisation "the signal inverts where the condition transforms the gauge"** stretched the concept over cases the section itself presents as plateau (Van Dongen), resolution limit (Hobbs) and deletion (Cryer/Baxter). Only the alcohol case is a clean inversion; the opening sentence already said "decouple from, or run opposite to". Resolution: "Three further signatures, none a clean inversion, fill out the limb" and "the signal inverts, plateaus or drops out where the condition transforms the gauge". This keeps the acute-state limb honest about which of the article's three failure modes (§vs-unreliability) each case instantiates, and avoids a latent tension with §cluster's contrast between absence-of-signal opacity (inventory blindness) and present-but-inverted-signal opacity.
