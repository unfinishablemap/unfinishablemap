## Critiques by Philosopher

### The Eliminative Materialist

"Your instrument evidence is the folk construct reflected back at you. Zaman et al. (2024) factor-analysed self-ratings of items written around 'reliving' and 'mental time travel', and recovered factors called re-experiencing and mental time travel. Look at what else the paper found. The remember/know contrast, the field's classic operationalisation, was *not* a core marker. The factor structure predicted the richness of autobiographical texts but *not* recall of the experimentally encoded video. So the operationalisations diverge, and that is De Brigard's construct-validity charge in partial form."

The register books Zaman only as "recovering a stable latent structure for autonoesis" (L48). It also books nothing from the signal-detection side. ### The Hard-Nosed Physicalist

"Scope the 'kind' honestly and see what remains. Your parent page concedes that autonoesis is not necessary for episodic memory. So the 'kind' must be remembering-as versus knowing-that, individuated by the mode itself. 'The mode constitutes the kind' is then close to definitional. "Your access reading also posits an ungraded quale standing behind graded reports, which is a fact about experience that outruns what anyone reports. Reinstatement probes rescue it from heterophenomenological emptiness only if the probe set is fixed before the data arrive." (Issue 3.)

## Counterarguments to Address

### The contingentist's dilemma
- **Current content says**: Autonoesis is constitutive of the kind of memory one has, held against De Brigard's construct-validity challenge.
- **A critic would argue**: "Pick a kind. If it is episodic memory, your parent has conceded my necessity limb. If it is remembering-as, your claim is that two modes are discrete, and 'constitutive versus accompaniment' adds nothing."
- **Suggested response**: Accept the second horn openly (item 1). The discreteness claim is substantive and falsifiable, which is the register's own point at L50. What the register can still resist is reclassifying re-experiencing as non-phenomenal, but that rests on hard-problem realism, not on P-MA1.

### The strength model
- **Current content says**: What separates remembering from knowing is "a mode of consciousness and not the information carried" (L49).
- **A critic would argue**: Wixted & Mickes (2010) read remember/know as criterion placement on one strength signal, and Zaman et al. (2024) found remember/know failing to load as a core marker.
- **Suggested response**: Book the strength model in the Calibration line beside the Gentry & Buckner countermodel. The manner/content support then rests on SDAM and the flashbulb dissociation, not on remember/know, which is how `anoetic` L106 already states it.

### Selection in cue-driven retrieval
- **Current content says**: The odour-cue result is evidence for the access reading (L52).
- **A critic would argue**: A free-cue paradigm samples whichever memory the cue evokes. The memories odour reaches may be those that kept their episodic character because they escaped rehearsal. That is selection among memories, not recovery within one.
- **Suggested response**: Item 2's rewording keeps the result as evidence while marking what was measured. The register's own standard ("within a single memory") applies here too.
