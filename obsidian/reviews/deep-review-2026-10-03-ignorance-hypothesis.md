## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

1. **Logical problem misstated: a dropped conditional changed its structure (L45).** The ch. 2 abstract gives the triad as "there are experiential truths; *if there are experiential truths*, every truth is entailed by, or supervenes on, some non-experiential truth; and *if there are experiential truths*, not every truth is …". Without the conditionals, theses 2 and 3 flatly contradict each other, and the "three theses that cannot all be true" become a contradictory pair. **Fixed**: "There are experiential truths; if there are, every truth is entailed by, or supervenes on, some non-experiential truth; and if there are, not every truth is (chapter 2)."
2. **The categorical feature was attributed to the McGinn contrast (L50).** Papineau assigns the two extras to two different views. Permanence is McGinn's ("this ignorance is chronic"). The categorical claim is Russell's: "Russell's neutral monism adds a positive account of the content of our ignorance … the categorical grounds of physical properties". The article said both features separate the view from McGinn's mysterianism. **Fixed**: "…which chapter 5 compares with it, and from Russell's neutral monism. The ignorance need be neither permanent, as McGinn holds, nor about categorical properties, as Russell held." Also "Asking for less than mysterianism" → "Asking for less than either".
3. **Chalmers 2018: verbatim predicate, spliced subject (L90).** The article said Chalmers "holds that the problem intuitions 'concern the phenomenal rather than the physical'". The source (PDF pp. 32–33) first concedes: "I agree that a structural conception of the physical is partly responsible for problem intuitions involving a gap between physical processes and consciousness. That said, there are many problem intuitions that do not take this form. For example, the key intuition … is that explaining functions does not explain consciousness. These intuitions concern the phenomenal [page break] rather than the physical". The article had dropped the concession and generalised the subject. **Fixed**: "Chalmers (2018) grants that a structural conception of the physical is partly responsible for some [[meta-problem-of-consciousness|problem intuitions]], but holds that others, including the key intuition that explaining functions does not explain consciousness, 'concern the phenomenal rather than the physical'". McClelland's reply now reads "problem intuitions are hybrid", since "they" had lost its antecedent.
4. **Tenet 5 parity stated above the strength tenets.md supports (lead L39, L102, L116).** This is a calibration error. Tenets L141 is modal throughout: "our conceptual tools *may be* fundamentally inadequate", "We *may* not", "The apparent simplicity of physicalism *could* reflect ignorance". The article said the tenet "obliges it to grant … that we are ignorant of something relevant", called the premise "incompleteness of knowledge" and "E2 in substance", said the Map "asserts the premise", and said the Map "grants E2 as a live hypothesis". "Live hypothesis" is a five-tier label assigned here on tenet-register grounds, which is P-M1 applied at the reviewer's desk, in the opponent's favour. A tenet-accepting reviewer would flag every one of these. **Fixed**:
   - Lead: "obliges it to grant that half the view may be true: that we are ignorant of something relevant."
   - L102: "That premise, that knowledge relevant to the mind–body problem may be incomplete, is E2 held as a possibility." "asserts the premise" → "presses the possibility". "grants E2 as a live hypothesis" → "treats E2 as an open possibility, not a finding".
   - L116: "the Map grants E2" → "the Map leaves E2 open".
   - The lead's "It contests the other half: that the missing truths are non-experiential and would close the gap" is now coherent with E2 being open. The Map contests the conjunction, not the bare possibility.
5. **The filing paragraphs contradicted each other (L56, L58).** "None of the texts checked files the 2006 hypothesis by letter" was followed by Stoljar 2020a filing it by letter ("type‐C Materialism"). "Type-C on every account checked" also overclaimed, because Chalmers's texts and McClelland give no letter for the 2006 view. **Fixed**: "none of *his* texts checked" and "The 2006 hypothesis is type-C by Chalmers's definition and by Stoljar's own filing." The next sentence, "Chalmers's own definition lets…", became "That definition lets…" to remove the repetition.
