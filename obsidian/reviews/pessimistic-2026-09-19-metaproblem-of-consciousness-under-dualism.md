### 1. The article declares bedrock over a space its own cited source canvasses — Chalmers 2020 contains an *interactionist* reply to the coincidence argument

- **File**: `obsidian/topics/metaproblem-of-consciousness-under-dualism.md`

- **Locus C — L78**, verbatim: *"The two interactionist assets—causal contribution and acquaintance—therefore do not compose into a reply to the reformulated argument"*
- **Severity**: High
- **Problem**: The three Chalmers quotations at L72, L74 and L76 all come from the same two paragraphs of `consc.net/papers/debunking.pdf` (the "Regarding premise 1 / premise 2" passage, ~p. 5–6). The article reads that passage correctly and then stops. Chalmers's paper is a *reply to commentators*, and its later section — headed **"Realizationism as a response to debunking"** — does exactly what the article says has not been done. Verified verbatim at the primary (NFKC-normalised, de-hyphenated):

  > "Hedda Hassel Mørch and Bradford Saad discuss what I called realizationism, a realist strategy which resists the arguments by holding that consciousness realizes the processes responsible for our judgments … **Mørch and Saad both offer versions of realizationism that resist the coincidence debunking argument.**"

  And on Saad specifically:

  > "**Saad proposes another very interesting version of realizationism that takes an interactionist rather than a panpsychist form.** Where Mørch's approach centers on phenomenal powers, **Saad's centers on a proposal about a fundamental psychophysical law by which consciousness affects matter.** Saad's idea is roughly that Pautz's normative harmony thesis has the status of a psychophysical law."

  So the argument space the article calls bedrock contains at least two named, published, Chalmers-evaluated moves, and the one that matches the Map's own position — interactionist dualism with a psychophysical law by which consciousness affects matter — is Saad's.

  **This is an over-concession, not an overclaim**, and it is the mirror of the defect the calibration passes have been hunting. The tell is the closure vocabulary: "therefore do not compose", "has not yet made either", "closer to bedrock". Per [[over-concession-gets-ratified-not-merely-missed]], this class survives review precisely because it reads as admirable humility.

- **Do not over-correct**: Chalmers does *not* endorse Saad. He immediately raises objections ("Obviously there are many questions. For a start, conscious states often don't cause the states that they rationalize…"). The repair is **not** "the Map has an answer". It is that the article should distinguish *"the Map has not made this argument"* (true, keep it) from *"there is no argument in this space and we are at bedrock"* (false, remove it).
- **Recommendation**: In the *What the Reply Does Not Reach* section, replace the bedrock sentence with a short naming of the two published realizationist replies to the coincidence argument (Mørch's phenomenal-powers version; Saad's interactionist psychophysical-law version), state that the Map has not adopted or developed either, and note Chalmers's own reservations about Saad's. That converts an unearned closure into an accurate map of open work. Verified metadata, lifted from Chalmers's own reference list: **Saad, B. (2019). A Teleological Strategy for Solving the Meta-Problem of Consciousness. *Journal of Consciousness Studies* 26(9–10), 205–216.** and **Pautz, A. (2020). Consciousness and coincidence: Comments on Chalmers. *Journal of Consciousness Studies*.** and **Mørch, H.H. (2020). - **Confirms and re-scopes two open tasks** — see *Interaction with Open Tasks* below. This is one repair, not three.

### 2. "The challenge is symmetric" is false, and the section that says it was stranded by today's concession

- **File**: `obsidian/topics/metaproblem-of-consciousness-under-dualism.md`
- **Locus — L120**, verbatim: *"But the challenge is symmetric. If problem intuitions are culturally contingent, they cannot serve as evidence *for* illusionism either. The debunking argument loses its target—there is nothing robust to debunk."*
- **Severity**: High
- **Problem**: Two distinct errors, and the second is created by today's edits.

  (a) **The symmetry does not hold.** Díaz's result is not neutral between the positions; it is mild evidence *for* the debunker. Verified at the primary (OpenAlex abstract, DOI `10.48350/156357`): *"The results of four studies will show that (1) problem intuitions are not widespread, and (2) when they arise, they do so because of factors that are unrelated to the nature of consciousness. This suggests that consciousness is, after all, not so problematic."* The illusionist's burden is to give a structural explanation of why we produce problem intuitions. Díaz makes that burden **lighter** — fewer people produce them, and mundane factors predict who does. The Map's realizationism, by contrast, says consciousness is among the causes of problem reports; but everyone is conscious and only a minority produce the reports, so realizationism has no differential purchase on the actual distribution.

  (b) **"The debunking argument loses its target" is a non-sequitur, and it directly contradicts the section two above it.** The debunker's target is the justification of phenomenal realism held by the people who do have the intuitions; rarity does not remove that target. More seriously: the *What the Reply Does Not Reach* section (installed today) concedes that Chalmers's live argument turns on **a near-complete structural explanation of the intuitions that could obtain without consciousness**. Díaz's finding — that the intuitions are predicted by factors unrelated to consciousness — is *evidence for exactly that premise*. The article therefore concedes premise 1 at L76 and then, at L120, claims the empirical work that supports premise 1 costs the debunker his target.

  `git log -S` confirms the mechanism: *"But the challenge is symmetric"*, *"the prevalence of an intuition tells us about cognitive and cultural factors"* and the whole *Empirical Complication* section trace to the **original create commit `3926e0c8a0`** and have never been touched by any of today's five passes. This is [[sweep-fixes-the-disclaimer-and-strands-its-dependents]] running in reverse — the new concession stranded an old section that depends on the concession being false.

- **Preserve**: the *rest* of the section is sound and should survive. *"the universality of problem intuitions is irrelevant to their accuracy"* and the heliocentrism parallel are a good reply, and they are the reply the section should rest on.
- **Recommendation**: Cut the three-sentence symmetry move. Replace with an honest statement: Díaz's result is a point for the structural explanation's near-completeness (cross-reference the coincidence section rather than repeating it), and the Map's reply is the accuracy-vs-prevalence point, which does not require symmetry to work. Roughly length-neutral.

Either drop item 1 or reframe it as a claim about the *content* of the explanatory debt rather than the *duration* of the dispute. Removing it also buys ~20 words against a 405-word ceiling.
