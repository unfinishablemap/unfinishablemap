## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

1. **Tenet 3 paragraph inferred an outbound claim from inbound data.** The "Bidirectional Interaction" paragraph read: "Set and setting profoundly influence psychedelic outcomes... This suggests consciousness actively shapes its own psychedelic experience rather than passively receiving whatever altered brain chemistry delivers." Set-and-setting variance is inbound-leg evidence — intention, expectation and environment are among the priors that determine what a loosened filter admits — and REBUS, the article's own named rival, *predicts* that sensitivity directly (relaxed high-level priors make outcomes more context-dependent, not less). The inference from that variance to consciousness acting *on* the brain is unearned, and it is precisely the error the filter-vs-interface distinction exists to catch. This is a calibration error inside the Map's own framework (a tenet-accepting reviewer would still flag it), not a bedrock disagreement. **Resolution**: paragraph rewritten to state that the psychedelic evidence gives Tenet 3 nothing, to locate the set-and-setting datum on the inbound leg where REBUS accounts for it, and to point Tenet 3's actual support at [[agency-void]], [[self-stultification]], and the tenets page's own record that downward causation is shown [[tenets#^tenet-3-standing|available rather than actual]].

2. **MQI tenet paragraph mislocated the quantum mechanism's leg.** It said a microtubule-quantum-coherence mechanism "would, if substantiated, supply a candidate physical substrate for how the *filter* operates." MQI specifies the *outbound* leg; a substrate for conscious participation in collapse bears there first, and its bearing on inbound filtering is a further step. **Resolution**: corrected in place; the sentence now names the outbound leg and marks the inbound extension as a separate claim the Map's filter case does not need.

3. **No leg assignment anywhere in 62 uses of filter vocabulary.** A reader could not tell which half of the coupling any body of evidence in the article supported. **Resolution**: new front-loaded section "Which Leg of the Coupling the Evidence Touches" (placed immediately after the lede, before "The Neuroimaging Paradox"), assigning each body of evidence by leg — see below. The concept's argument is cross-referenced, not imported.

### Leg assignment applied (the substance of this pass)

- **Neuroimaging** (desynchronization, alpha collapse, glutamate valence) and reported expansion of experience — **inbound**: what changes is the width and content of experience while the subject can still speak.
- **Huxley's valve, REBUS's relaxed priors, Letheby's opaque self-model** — three rival readings of the same **inbound** machinery.
- **Selective-access bet** (phenomenological / psychological / veridical) — **inbound**.
- **Report channel** — **outbound but only instrumentally**: the 5-HT2A introspection objection asks whether a drug-altered channel faithfully carries inbound content, not whether the content exists. This sharpens an objection the article already engaged.
- **Convergence cluster** — **mixes legs**: psilocybin, contemplative practice, sensory deprivation are inbound changes; terminal lucidity is a both-legs-return case. This is an independent reason for the article's existing "one pattern, not five" corollary.
- **Anaesthesia reply** — labelled in place: propofol/xenon phenomenal-presence suppression is an *inbound* narrowing, ketamine's severed access with spared phenomenal content is an *outbound* failure (matches the concept's reading of the same case).
- **Quantum appendix / MQI** — **outbound** (see critical issue 2).
- **Net claim recorded**: nothing in this article is evidence about the outbound leg. Psychedelics' distinctive contribution is controlled dosing plus a working outbound leg that makes the inbound change reportable — reportability, not evidence of outcome-selection.
