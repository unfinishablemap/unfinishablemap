## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

1. **Source/Map conflation in the framing vocabulary.** The article said "The Jourdain Hypothesis rests on a distinction between two kinds of metacognition" and gave a procedural/declarative table. The full text of Gruber et al. (2015, PMC4319388) contains neither "procedural" nor "declarative" (0 hits each). The paper states the hypothesis as re-representation versus metarepresentation (Whiten's terminology, after Karmiloff-Smith), and its footnote 1 says the authors "focussed on the representational nature of apes' knowledge, suggesting that metarepresentation is the more appropriate term" than metacognition. Seven prior deep reviews praised the table without checking whose vocabulary it was. Resolution: the section now opens by stating the paper's own terms and marks the two-kinds-of-metacognition vocabulary and table as the Map's gloss.
2. **Empirical claim against the record: "Empathic concern ... appears limited" in great apes.** Uncited. Romero, Castellanos and de Waal (2010) report an exceptionally large dataset of chimpanzee consolation and read it as support for the empathy hypothesis ("possible expression of sympathetic concern"). Resolution: the paragraph now reports consolation with that citation, says the third level is not simply absent, and leaves open whether the consoler represents the other's state as a state. "The gradient tracks the Jourdain transition" became "The gradient is the Map's ordering of the Jourdain transition". Reference added. "Unconscious" was dropped from the contagion bullet (the sibling page `topics/consciousness-and-social-understanding` already notes de Waal does not claim contagion occurs without consciousness).
3. **Unlabelled necessity claims.** (a) "Metarepresentation requires holding a mental state and a representation of that state in unified awareness" stated the Map's contested premise flatly, while the rest of the article keeps it conditional. Now prefixed "On the Map's reading" (label: Map reconstruction). (b) "Cumulative culture ... requires metarepresentation" outran the source, whose abstract says metarepresentational differences "may thus explain" limits in cumulative behaviour. Now "requires metarepresentation on the hypothesis", with the paper's own modal quoted (label: framework-conditional implication). (c) "The gap tracks consciousness-dependent capacities, just as the Bidirectional Interaction tenet predicts" asserted the conclusion unconditionally one sentence after "This isn't conclusive"; removed.
4. **Source/Map conflation in Relation to Site Perspective.** "The Jourdain Hypothesis provides a specific mechanism: consciousness enables the transition" attributed a consciousness claim to a hypothesis whose source paper has 0 occurrences of "conscious" or "phenomenal". Now "The Map's reading of the Jourdain Hypothesis proposes a specific route".
