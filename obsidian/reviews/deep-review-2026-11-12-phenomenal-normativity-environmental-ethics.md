## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

1. **Independence overclaim contradicting P-D3 and the sibling article.** The ecocentrism reply said the four traditions "sharing no common textual lineage ... all converge" and called the consciousness-centred view "a cross-culturally convergent finding". [[positions/arguments-for-dualism|P-D3]], cited one paragraph later, grades the evidence as near-independence, and [[topics/evaluative-qualia-phenomenal-normativity-across-traditions]] states that the Buddhist and Indian strands share a milieu ("Abhinavagupta writes with Buddhist epistemology in view") and deflates the tally to at most three near-independent lineages. The 2026-07-31 refine softened the next paragraph and left this one. **Resolution**: the sentence now says the claim "recurs well outside the West"; the traditions "work within incompatible metaphysical frameworks, though not in isolation (the Buddhist and Indian strands share a milieu)". The same edit removes an em-dash "not X—it is Y" construct.

2. **Tenet 2 misstated.** "The Map holds that consciousness interfaces with the physical world at the smallest possible scale." The tenet is "The smallest possible non-physical influence on physical outcomes", exercised at the quantum level; minimality is of the influence, not of the scale. **Resolution**: restated in the tenet's words. The following sentence asserted that "the distribution of consciousness across species depends in part on" quantum-capable architectures; the tenet says nothing about which architectures those are, so this is now "where consciousness can act depends in part on ... which the tenet does not specify".

3. **Tenet 4 rationale misstated.** The parenthetical said "the Map holds that genuine moral stakes require a single actualised outcome". The tenet as it stands rests on the indexical objection and records global exclusion of unchosen alternatives as a posit "asserted rather than derived". No Map page argues from ethics to single actuality. **Resolution**: "that only one outcome is actual is a commitment the Map holds, resting on the indexical objection, and not something it derives from ethics". This is a tenet-wording alignment, and does not reopen the MWI disagreement the 07-28 stability note closed.

### Medium Issues Found

4. **Coiner credit: the beneficence bridge.** The article gave the bridge ("whatever can be benefited or harmed must count") to Attfield alone. Attfield 1987 p. 51–52: "The case for proposition (B) has been well presented by Kenneth Goodpaster. Anything which can be benefited or harmed must be regarded as having moral standing, granted the centrality of beneficence within morality". **Resolution**: "crediting the case for it to Kenneth Goodpaster" added. Goodpaster's own paper was not fetched; the credit is stated as Attfield's, which is what was verified. The following "Both assume" became "Taylor and Attfield both assume" so the added name does not capture the pronoun.

5. **Lee 2025 scope.** The article had Lee arguing that "the theory of welfare subjects ... is logically independent of the theory of welfare goods". Lee's claim is narrower: "the phenomenal theory is logically independent of both experientialism and anti-experientialism", and he grants "systematic connections" between the two theories. **Resolution**: "his theory of welfare subjects ... is logically independent of experientialist and anti-experientialist theories of welfare goods".

6. **Residue of the 07-28 fabricated quotation.** "Indian *rasa* theory ... locates aesthetic value exclusively in evaluative consciousness" kept the word "exclusively" from the de-quoted teaching-unit sentence ("consists exclusively in a kind of knowledge or consciousness"). **Resolution**: "locates aesthetic relish in the spectator's awareness", matching the wording the 07-28 pass installed in the Aesthetic Dimension section.
