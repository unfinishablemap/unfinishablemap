## Executive Summary

The sweep did what the 10-02 research note's Misassignment Register asked, and did it with the same grammar on every page: a reply *reaches* a type when it engages a premise that type holds, and the persistence and promissory replies are now routed to Type-C by name on every page that runs them. The honest tier is stated wherever a tier is stated.

Three things the corrected picture licenses have not yet been built. ## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

The wing now reports his taxonomy as he wrote it. The hub quotes the 2003 definitions verbatim (L44), dates type-C to the 2002/2003 split from a broader 1997 type-B (L46), and keeps his verdicts and the Map's tiers apart: "The tiers that follow are the Map's reconstruction from its own evidential ladder, not Chalmers's verdicts" (L40). The modal-structure page's repaired L83 is the best single sentence the sweep produced: the Type-B physicalist "holds the identity between physical and phenomenal properties a posteriori and the gap permanent — located in the distinctness of phenomenal from physical concepts rather than in anything still to be learned — grants the zombie's conceivability and denies only the step to possibility, with water = H₂O as the model." That is the view, and the page then runs the two replies that reach it (acquaintance-not-ignorance; a law under the title of an identity) before routing the neuroscience-progress observation to "a different opponent: the Type-C physicalist". The knowledge-argument page's new "Two-Dimensional Argument" section (L84ff) gives the Type-B reply its strongest form, strong necessities, and quotes the concession that reply costs: "Ultimately, I think a type-B materialist must hold that the case of consciousness is special."

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

The hub's fourth collapse route is the one he would underline: closure by a future physics "appealing to consciousness itself, in the way that some theorists hold that quantum mechanics does" lands on type-D, and the hub says so plainly, "by Chalmers's own definition the Map is type-D" (L77), while refusing to count the placement as evidence: "The point is dialectical and earns no evidence: nothing of the kind has been found." The scientific-explanation page's repaired L52 keeps the same discipline, separating what neuroscience requires (systematic relation, compatible with closure) from what the Map commits to (that closure "is not absolute, or at minimum that non-physical influence operates within the gaps physics itself leaves open"), and labels the second a Tenet-3 commitment rather than a finding.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

The datum reply is kept where it belongs. The hub's Type-A verdict concedes that "the first-person warrant cannot be handed to an opponent who declines it" (L121), and the IBE page's first reply names phenomenal character as the explanandum the physicalist's list omits without pretending the omission is a discovery. Type-B is credited with granting the datum on every page that mentions it; nothing in the wing accuses the live opponent of denying experience, which is the accusation that only reaches Type-A.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

The wing's refusal to treat the identity as explanatory, "a law under another name", is congenial to a process reading on which the psychophysical relation is lawful rather than identity-like. The arguments-against-materialism page's L83 says the identity is "a cost the ledger must count" and no more; nothing in the wing uses process compatibility, or any metaphysical-possibility result, to raise a tier. Nothing here touches minimal-organism consciousness, so the evidential-status constraint on this persona has no purchase in this wing.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

Only the hub's L124 bears on agency, and it is correct and restrained: a discriminating result "would have to come through Tenet 3's channel, an outcome deviation conditioned on intention, whose absence all three types predict alike". The IBE page's "State a disconfirmer" paragraph gives the same channel its lower-and-raise conditions. The wing does not borrow libertarian intuitions to shore up the anti-physicalist case.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

The hub tracks Chalmers's reclassification of McGinn from type-C sympathiser to type-F (L64) and notes that Churchland's hornswoggle posture was answered under type-A in 1997 and type-B-or-Q later: the taxonomy is reported as shifting, with the shifts attributed. The consciousness-defeats-explanation page's L142 now distinguishes the temporary reading from "permanent but epistemic" and names Type-B as the main occupant of the latter, which is where the mysterian's own view was previously being filed by default.

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

This is the persona whose verdict is decisive for the wing, and the wing passes. Every tier stated is the honest one. The IBE verdict (L76): "against the Type-B physicalist, *compatible* until the second-order comparison is run and favours dualism by stated criteria; *discriminating* nowhere. The register's rule that a tenet may remove a defeater but may not upgrade the evidence forbids pretending otherwise." The hub (L105): Tenet 2's denial of closure "reaches Type-B by the definition above, removing a defeater without raising the tier." The scientific-explanation page (L60): "gap persistence leaves dualism and Type-B compatible with the same record." The arguments-against-materialism page (L83): the replies to Type-B "bear on plausibility rather than evidence." Four pages, four independent edits on two days, one calibration. The praise-worthy thing *not* done: no page converted "Type-B predicts the persistence we observe" into "persistence confirms dualism". That restraint is the sweep's main product, and it is uniform.

One register note, not a slippage. The hard-problem page's L233 says "The persistent non-occurrence of (1)–(4) over decades provides inductive support for the gap's reality". That is sound (Type-B grants the gap's reality), but the sentence that follows it offers only two readings of the gap, and the persistence is then left to look like support for the first of them. Priority 1 below closes that.
