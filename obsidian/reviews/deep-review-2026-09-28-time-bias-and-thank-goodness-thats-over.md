## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found
- **Wrong first name**: "John Maclaurin" — the co-author of the 2002 *Ratio* paper is **James** Maclaurin (Crossref). Corrected.
- **Hare 2007 parity overstated / thinly sourced**: "On his treatment the two biases stand or fall together" compressed Hare's actual structure, and "Hare engages the question inside a broadly physicalist ontology" is not supported by the 2007 paper. Rewritten to Hare's argument as read in the author's PDF: reconciling either bias with the overall good needs a metaphysics that makes the favoured item special; four-dimensionalism supplies none; monadic tense does this for time, egocentric presentism for the self. Stance now reads "does not offer it as support for dualism".
- **Calibration (possibility/probability slippage, mild)**: the Relation section said the Map's growing-block metaphysics "gives future-bias an intelligible object" without registering Hare's own point that, short of a *shrinking* block, monadic-tense metaphysics does not explain why past pains matter less. On a growing block the past is what exists. Added the caution to the Hare section and a qualifier to the normative paragraph ("that object is not a ground"). A reviewer who accepts the tenets would still flag the omission, so this counted as critical.
