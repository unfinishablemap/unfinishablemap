## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

`palette-extension-void` is the knowledge argument run in a laboratory, and the article knows it without saying so. Mary's room has become a squirrel monkey's retina with a third opsin added by viral vector, and the result is exactly Jackson's: discrimination arrives, and the qualitative question does not move. The page's finest sentence is the one that refuses the easy win — *"Instrument precision and phenomenal thinness are not in tension here; together they are the void's signature"* (L57). Chalmers would note that this is the explanatory gap stated as a *measurement property*: every improvement in the colorimetric instrument sharpens the coordinate and leaves the quality where it was.

`phenomenal-quality-void` earns his other kind of approval. Its asymmetry argument (L108) — reality-feeling can fail, but "there is no state of consciousness in which givenness itself fails," so the givenness dimension has no contrast condition — is a hard-problem argument in the catalogue's own idiom, and the page draws the honest consequence: this makes givenness "harder to detect than any other void," which is a claim about *evidence*, not a claim about *what is true*.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

Stapp would go straight to `fusion-void` L94 and find his mechanism offered to him and then withdrawn. *"If a non-physical influence operates anywhere in the perception-action loop, precision weights are the kind of small, high-leverage parameter the minimality requirement points toward, since a slight shift in weighting propagates into a different perceived world."* That is the Zeno-attention story's natural home in perception science — precision is what attention *is* in the predictive idiom — and the article gives it one sentence, labels it "offered as speculation," and closes: *"No claim is made that any interface operates at this site, and the void supplies no evidence that one does."* He would want more and would concede the discipline is right; the 09-04 cross-review recorded the same verdict.

`predictive-construction-void` L127–129 is the page he would argue with longest, because it says his own kind of proposal is at risk of being unfalsifiable and names the cure: *"the tenet remains on probation until third-person work gives it something to fail against."* The article lists what such work would look like — anomalous decoherence signatures, deviations from measurement statistics, activity/report dissociations — and says none currently exists. That is the Map policing its own channel from inside a voids page.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

The wing's Nagel moment is a quotation the palette page fetched from a colour scientist. C. L. Hardin, on the tetrachromat cDa29, via Newall 2025: *"Nobody asked the woman about the quality of her experience."* The rigour produced the silence — forced-choice psychophysics was designed to exclude exactly the datum Nagel says is the datum. The article's gloss, *"the rigour produced the silence,"* is the right one, and the page then does what Nagel asks: it treats the five subjects' four colour words as *evidence about the report channel* rather than as evidence about the colour.

`fusion-void` supplies the negative form. *"There is no experience of nearly fusing"* (L46) is a first-person fact that only the ideal-observer model makes visible: the common-cause comparison was run and consciousness received the verdict. And `acquaintance-void`'s "empathy ceiling" — the sensation of "running out of imaginative material" (L108) — is the phenomenology of the bat argument reported from the inside.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

Whitehead would recognise `fusion-void`'s closing paragraph as his own kind of reflexivity: *"The catalogue itself performs fusion: lines of evidence are combined into single judgements, with the weighting of sources set by whoever writes the synthesis and reported nowhere"* (L96). The relational structure of the percept is made a model of the relational structure of the inquiry. He would also approve `synesthetic-void`'s handling of Lacalli's experience-space — a high-dimensional relational geometry, explicitly non-dualist in its author's hands, used as "an interpretive overlay, not a measurement" (L62).

**Where the constraint binds:** Whitehead-friendliness must not cash out as an upgrade of `synesthetic-void`'s filter reading of neonatal pruning, and the page does not let it — L84: *"the Map's filter-theory reading is preferred for its consilience with the broader tenet commitments rather than because the evidence forces it, and this section can bear no more weight than that underdetermination allows."* That is the "tenet-coherent, not evidence-elevating" pattern in its own words. The two older pages that fail this test are treated under Birch.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

Kane gets less from this wing than from most, and would say so: it is a wing about what the agent *receives*, not what the agent does. Two things would hold him. `predictive-construction-void`'s subtraction barrier (L59–61) — self-caused input is deducted "in proportion to what they did, by an operation they cannot inspect" — is a finding about agency's phenomenology with a live empirical dispute attached (Thomas et al. 2022 vs Job & Kilteni 2023), and the article reports the dispute rather than picking the side the Map would prefer. And `acquaintance-void` L146 makes the anti-epiphenomenalist point cleanly: if the exclamation on seeing a new colour is caused by the acquaintance, acquaintance does work in the world, and the void's limits are "genuine constraints on what minds can do."

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

`binding-void` is the catalogue's most McGinn-shaped page, and its best paragraph is one it wrote *against* its earlier self. L55: three analyses (Dainton, Bayne–Chalmers, Tye) are circular "by the Map's lights," but "only Dainton holds that decomposition stops here"; the three are joined by a documented citation lineage, so the pattern is "genealogical recurrence rather than independent arrival" and the independence condition convergence arguments require is unmet. McGinn would call that intellectual honesty about closure rather than closure as a slogan.

`palette-extension-void` L81 gives him the wing's rarest admission: what a differently-built mind might see — *"here, unusually for this catalogue, not much."* Give a model a new channel and it will describe it fluently in the vocabulary it has, "because fluency is what the architecture produces regardless." A void the catalogue's usual probe cannot reach is a void the catalogue is willing to leave.

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

This persona's verdict is load-bearing for the wing, because the wing's subject is precisely the gap between a measurement and a phenomenal claim.

**What is praise-worthily not done.** `palette-extension-void` had the structural opportunity to say olo *is* a new colour — the authors' own word is "novel," the press said "never seen before," and the Map's tenets remove the parsimony-based defeater against phenomenal novelty. The page declines at every level: the deflationary reading "is not refuted below" (L39); Barbur's gloss is "the simpler hypothesis … held by a competent specialist for good reasons, not by a strawman" (L87); a physicalist "accommodates this by denying there is a further fact to find, and that denial is untouched by anything argued here" (L89). And it names the move it is not making: parsimony here is "*selecting* an answer where the evidence cannot vote" — which is a claim about Tenet 5's scope, not a claim that the novelty reading wins. `fusion-void` does the same in the other direction: it concedes cue combination is "quantitatively modelled with no computational remainder" and belongs on the brain side of the capability division, then holds that assignment "open to dispute rather than settled" because the owning page says so. Both pages end their Dualism paragraphs with the common-cause null's own sentence. On the five-tier scale nothing in either page moves above *live hypothesis*, and nothing is asked to.

**The taxonomy is used as a calibration instrument, not a label.** `palette` files its induction face as *unexplored and shrinking* and its verification face as a *candidate* unexplorable — the word "candidate" is doing real work — and `three-kinds-of-void` gained the diachronic form of face-spanning on the same day. `fusion` files its core as unexplorable and its margin as unexplored and says which experiment drew the line.

**Where the Process Philosopher's praise would have cashed out as a tier-upgrade, and the page refused** — `synesthetic-void` L84, quoted above, and L76's two readings of the training-fade ("ordinary skill atrophy" vs "architectural constraints reasserting"), where "the data alone do not force it."
