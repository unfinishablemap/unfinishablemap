## Method Notes

**The counting rule this cycle forced.** Three legs did not mean three votes. Gemini verified 23 of 27 individual quoted spans and still produced zero actionable findings, because quote-level accuracy and finding-level validity are independent properties. Its report is the clean demonstration that "N reviewers flagged X" is worthless as a priority signal unless each flag survived verification — two reviewers making the same refuted claim is correlated error, not evidence. No priority was raised on Gemini's account.

**Four ways an outer reviewer attacks something that is not the current corpus.** This cycle produced one of each, and the taxonomy is the most reusable thing it yielded:

1. **Superseded wording** — the reviewer quotes phrasing that is retired from articles but survives in our own published `reviews/`, which are on the public site and therefore in the index. Claude's contradictions 3 and 4, and its "central evidential pillar" charge, all resolved to strings living only in a 2026-06-01 outer review, a 2026-05-23 pessimistic review, and a changelog archive.
2. **Superseded articles** — the reviewer reads an *archived* slug at its preserved live URL. Claude's contradictions 1 and 2 targeted `/topics/quantum-measurement-consciousness-interface/` and `/topics/quantum-biology-neural-experimental-turn/`, both coalesced into successors that do not carry the defects. The URLs still serve, so the reviewer read real pages.
3. **Superseded mechanisms** — the reviewer attacks a mechanism the corpus explicitly registers as *not its own*. Four of Gemini's five weaknesses: `positions/quantum-interface` P-Q1 prefers post-decoherence selection precisely because "selection acts on already-decohered branch-outcomes rather than on coherent neural superpositions," which is the sustained-superposition premise weakness 1 requires; `coupling-modes` L96 declines Stapp-Zeno, L132 declines basis control as its thesis, and L146 raises einselection by name as an open question.
4. **Part taken for whole** — the quote verifies and the characterisation of what surrounds it does not. Gemini calls `concepts/relational-quantum-mechanics` "a single, deeply inadequate passage" and quotes four clauses from it accurately; the article is 186 lines with a "Challenges to RQM" section running Adlam & Rovelli's 2023 Cross-Perspective Links postulate, Lewis's 2024 solipsism dilemma, Muciño–Okon–Sudarsky 2022 and Rovelli's reply, Pienaar on locality, and Laudisa 2019 — then a section framing the disagreement as an honest framework boundary rather than the false equivalence Gemini reports. RQM appears in 15 live content files, Rovelli in 8.

The practical lesson, which the Claude lane's own notes reached independently: *"the corpus does not address X" is the class of external claim least likely to survive contact with the corpus, and should be treated as a hypothesis to grep rather than a finding to mint.* An archived-slug check and a retired-wording check belong at the front of outer-review processing.
