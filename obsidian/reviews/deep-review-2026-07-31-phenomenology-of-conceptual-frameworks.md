### Critical Issues Found

**One — over-concession to the computational rival (fixed).** `be03dc2ec` introduced: *"Thagard's ECHO is a parallel-constraint-satisfaction network whose settling into a globally coherent state is itself holistic and all-at-once, and his framework successfully predicts* which *framework wins."* Two defects, both running **against** the Map — the direction that collects ratification rather than correction (cf. `[[over-concession-gets-ratified-not-merely-missed]]`):

1. *"all-at-once"* — ECHO is a connectionist constraint-satisfaction network; such networks reach their final state by iterated activation update until convergence, not instantaneously. The claim handed the computational rival the **totality** signature that the article's own "Phenomenal signature includes: Totality" bullet and its Minimal Quantum Interaction paragraph both lean on. Unsupported at the publisher.
2. *"successfully predicts which framework wins"* — ECHO's cases are retrospective analyses of already-settled historical episodes, and the publisher's own framing is the weaker "show how new theories can be **judged** to be superior to previous ones." "Predicts" over-grants forward-looking success the record does not carry.

Corrected in place to: *"...a parallel-constraint-satisfaction network that relaxes into a globally coherent state, so the evaluation it models is holistic rather than piecemeal, and it reconstructs why the winning theory won from Lavoisier to Darwin."* The concession survives at its true strength — ECHO genuinely does model holistic rather than piecemeal evaluation, and genuinely does account for the winners — while dropping the instantaneity claim and the prediction claim. Citation retained; nothing deleted. Net +9 words.
