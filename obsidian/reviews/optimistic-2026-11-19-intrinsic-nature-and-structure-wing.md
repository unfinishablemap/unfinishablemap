## Executive Summary

This wing is the Map's account of what experience reveals and what structural description leaves out. It also covers the rivals that deny any remainder: Revelation, Kripke's modal argument, quiddities and the contingency thesis, phenomenal structuralism, ontic structural realism, the disclosure reading of measurement, and the disguised-dualism diagnostic. Its calibration is some of the best in the corpus. Five of the seven pages state outright that a rival's defeater, a removed obstacle, or a framework fit is not evidence for the Map. The quiddity page names that rule as "Non-inheritance of a defeater is a structural property of the theory's architecture, not a point of confirmation". Three pages still overstate. ## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

Chalmers would find his own dialectic handled with unusual care. He would value `ontic-structural-realism` most. It quotes his concession that "There are respectable structuralist or dispositionalist views of physics on which physics involves just structure or dispositions all the way down", and then draws the lesson against itself: "The Map should not be more confident against a position than the philosopher whose argument it borrows." The Kripke page's handling of the phenomenal-concepts reply would please him too. It sets out the reply in Loar's own terms (the "semantic premise", 1997 p. 599). It names the step where the argument's force rests, the step from "no contingent mode of presentation" to "no illusion of contingency", and sends readers to the master-argument dilemma instead of claiming to have won there. The disguised-dualism diagnostic applies his Type-B dilemma narrowly. The "pure two-concepts view" escapes "cleanly", and the diagnostic bites only where a recovered phenomenal feature does explanatory work.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

`physics-as-disclosure` is the wing's Stapp page. Its picture of the measurement problem as the point "where structure meets something structure cannot capture" is Process 1 in ontological dress. Stapp would also value its candour about the record: Barrett is "no advocate", and when asked what disclosure predicts that its rivals do not, "the answer at present is: none". But Stapp's view is the one the Map has moved away from. The tenets now have physical objective reduction fix outcomes everywhere, with consciousness biasing them only at neural interfaces. The page has not caught up (Calibration Concern 1). A Stapp-sympathetic reader would want the page to say plainly what the disclosure reading claims once definiteness is a physical matter.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

Nagel would praise `revelation-thesis` for refusing a cheap first-person argument. It concedes that "The argument from revelation is therefore not available to the Map as support for its first tenet". It then states the first-person datum the Map does keep: experience reveals "*something*". He would also recognise the anti-structuralist case in `structuralism-about-phenomenal-character`, the move from individuation to constitution. The page states it fairly ("Similarity data alone cannot settle the matter, since an intrinsicalist can accept whatever similarity structure the structuralist measures") and credits Levine 1995 for it. The two-senses-of-transparency section sorts out a terminological trap that would otherwise corrupt twenty pages: "Metzinger's transparent self-model conceals its own construction; Goff's transparent concept conceals nothing."

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

Whitehead would welcome the wing's refusal of bare structure. "Galileo relocates the residue into the sentient body; ontic structural realism abolishes it." He would also welcome the disclosure reading's two aspects, structure and actuality, which avoid a crude two-substance picture. His sympathies lie with Russellian monism and with experience all the way down. `quiddity-epiphenomenalism` treats that programme as a serious rival. It does not use the contingency thesis to score a point against it: "The contingency thesis tells us where one bridge is weak, not that any other bridge will bear weight." **Constraint check:** no page in this wing uses process-philosophical compatibility to raise anything on the evidential scale. The one candidate for slippage concerns the Map's own dualism (the quiddity Relation section, Watch Item A), not an organism or system.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

Kane's interest is the causal efficacy of the phenomenal. On the delegatory account the quiddity page describes, "the *specific* experience — this pain, this decision — is what takes over the causal work". The page refuses to let that count as proof: "The cost is relocated, not eliminated", and the ensemble-level epiphenomenalism objection is answered only "framework-internal[ly]". `revelation-thesis` adds a point Kane would find sharp. If strong Revelation were true, the selection power the Map posits would be "no part of the state's essence", which is Goff's epiphenomenalism pressure turned on the Map's own mechanism. So denying strong Revelation is part of what keeps agency efficacious on the Map's terms.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

McGinn would find the wing's best moment in Kripke's footnote 77, which the page quotes in full instead of trimming: some identity-theorist arguments "strike me as highly compelling arguments which I am at present unable to answer convincingly", and the mind-body problem is "wide open and extremely confusing". The page then explains why it cites Kripke only for the negative result: "Kripke's argument is anti-materialist without being pro-dualist, and he is on record declining the inference from the one to the other." `structuralism` closes with matching restraint: "The critics' arguments, recent and largely unanswered in print, are unrefuted rather than confirmed."

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

Birch's verdict on this wing would mostly be praise, and his strongest praise is for declined upgrades:

- `quiddity-epiphenomenalism`: Russellian monism's failure "is not evidence for the Map", and non-inheritance of a defeater is "not a point of confirmation". This is the template sentence for the whole tenet-coherent-not-evidence-elevating pattern.
- `physics-as-disclosure` L78: decoherence's failure to solve measurement "removes a possible defeater — one every rival below survives equally", because "the absence of a principled barrier is not the presence of support" (P-M1).
- `ontic-structural-realism` L67: losing the remainder argument "costs a defeater-removal rather than a premise".
- `revelation-thesis` L80: the interface power "is consistent with the Map's epistemology only because the Map holds that what experience reveals is partial, and that holding is a framework commitment, not an independent finding." The page saw the chance to turn Liu's explanation of the intuition of dualism into evidence and declined it: "a dualist who grants that the intuition is explained by the intuitiveness of a thesis they themselves deny in its strong form cannot cite the intuition as evidence."
- `kripke` L65 applies the convergence discount from P-D1, matching its 2026-11-10 cluster re-map. Conceivability and the explanatory gap share this argument's cluster, "so they corroborate one another more than they multiply evidence".

Birch's flags are the three Calibration Concerns below. Each one is a closing or framing sentence that claims more than its own page or the framework it cites. None is an upgrade on the evidential scale for a boundary organism; the wing contains none.

**Where the two personas meet.** On `quiddity` and `ontic-structural-realism`, Whitehead's praise (taking the rival programme seriously) and Birch's praise (declining to profit from its troubles) land on the same passages. ## Calibration Concerns

### Concern 1: physics-as-disclosure still makes consciousness the outcome-*maker*

Since 2026-07-16 the framework has said that physical objective reduction secures definiteness and consciousness only biases outcomes at neural interfaces:

- `tenets/background-commitments` L42: "Once objective collapse already secures definiteness, the measurement problem itself cannot be evidence for conscious selection."
- `tenets/background-commitments` L60: "The Map escapes redundancy only by narrowing consciousness's role to outcome-*biasing* rather than outcome-*making*"
- `concepts/bi-aspectual-ontology` L38: "The Map holds that consciousness supplies this actuality where a neural interface exists; elsewhere physical objective reduction fixes outcomes with no mind involved."

`physics-as-disclosure` (created 2026-03-15) predates that accounting. The 2026-10-30 propagation refine (`c70a9528b1`) fixed only its L46 gloss. These sentences still assume the older picture:

- L34: "actuality belongs to a different aspect of reality: [[bi-aspectual-ontology|consciousness]]". L64: "a different *kind* of thing — actuality, consciousness, the first-person dimension that no third-person description captures". Both equate the actuality aspect with consciousness.
- L76: decoherence "stops where the question of actuality begins". L86 (the disclosure prediction): "No proposal resolves the actuality question on purely physical terms without eliminating the question itself." By the Map's own account, objective reduction *does* settle which outcome becomes actual everywhere outside neural interfaces.
- L84 assigns "The search for a physical collapse mechanism (GRW, CSL, gravitational collapse)" to the rival *deficiency* reading, though the Map's prebiotic resolution posits exactly such a mechanism.
- L100 says the fall of objective-collapse theories "would redistribute credence across every surviving Born-exact reading". That is true, but it leaves out that their fall would also remove the leading candidates for the Map's own baseline collapse.

`concepts/spontaneous-collapse-theories` L170 already gives the reconciling reading: "GRW/CSL handle the *structural* dimension of collapse (baseline rates, mass-density coupling, prebiotic outcomes) while preserving the disclosed boundary where consciousness operates." The disclosure page should take up that reading and scope its claim to match. The fault is internal inconsistency, not an upgrade: the page's likelihood statement and P-M1/P-M2 bookings are sound. But a reader of this page alone would take the Map to hold an outcome-making view its tenets gave up. **Task minted** (refine-draft, P3).

### Concern 3: revelation-thesis disowns an acquaintance premise that ontic-structural-realism says the Map holds and uses

Applying the revelation page's own definitions first: weak Revelation holds that "experience reveals the existence of a quality and part of its character", and the page says "The Map's existing commitments presuppose weak Revelation and nothing stronger." Yet L76 says: "The Russellian hinge stated on the [[intrinsic-nature]] page, that we know what pain is like by feeling it, is marked there as the programme's most contested premise, and the Map does not rest on it." The gloss it gives is a weak-strength claim. What `intrinsic-nature` L71 marks as contested is that introspection delivers a *quiddity*: "The hinge is also the programme's [[revelation-thesis|most contested premise]]: that introspection delivers a *quiddity* rather than itself a structural or representational state".

The siblings do rest on the asymmetry. `structuralism` L66: the Map denies epistemic structuralism about experience "because it holds that acquaintance gives one non-structural item directly". `ontic-structural-realism` L89: "The Map holds that consciousness is the one intrinsic nature known from the inside rather than by structural inference — the hinge of [[intrinsic-nature|intrinsic nature]]." OSR L105 counts acquaintance among "both claims doing the work — the acquaintance and Newman asymmetries — face standing replies". OSR's wording goes one step further than the Map's ontology allows. Its phrase "the one intrinsic nature" is the Russellian identification (`intrinsic-nature` L77: "Russellian monism is the theory that builds on it: physics describes structure, consciousness is the one intrinsic nature we know"), and `bi-aspectual-ontology` L94 rejects it: "Consciousness is the missing ontology, not the missing intrinsic nature of the ontology physics already describes."

So one page says the Map does not rest on a premise, and another says the Map holds it and uses it as one of its two main grounds. The consistent grade, implied by all three pages but stated by none, is this: the Map holds the acquaintance asymmetry at weak strength and does not rest on its quiddity reading. Revelation was written 2026-10-01 and OSR on 2026-09-04. The OSR deep review of 10-20 checked neither against the other. **Task minted** (refine-draft, P3, with OSR as secondary at a net-≤0 budget).
