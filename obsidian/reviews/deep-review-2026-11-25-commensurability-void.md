## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found (all fixed)

1. **Fabricated Nagel quotation.** "organized along dimensions we cannot even conceive" stood in quotation marks as Nagel's. The word "dimensions" does not occur in the paper. Seed: research note L51, a key-point paraphrase, promoted into quote marks. Three prior reviews called it an "appropriately qualified paraphrase" and the 2026-06-24 ledger passed it as "correctly attributed". Removed; replaced by three verbatim spans.
2. **Nagel's inference reversed around a verbatim quote.** The "continuum" sentence is verbatim but comes from footnote 8, which concedes that "it may be easier than I suppose to transcend inter-species barriers" and that "a lesser degree of partial understanding may still be available". The page used it to launch "the continuum fractures". Seed: research note L49 ("may collapse entirely" is the note's addition). The footnote's direction is now stated and the fracture is labelled the Map's conjecture.
3. **Nagel's "deeper point concerns commensurability" stated as Nagel's.** Nagel says "I am not raising that epistemological problem" and frames the point as needing to take up the bat's point of view; "commensurability" is the Map's gloss. Also omitted: the paper's closing proposal of an objective phenomenology and its remark that structural features may be objectively describable, both of which run against unsolvability. Now included.
4. **Díaz-Boils: hedge dropped from a quotation.** The quoted span is verbatim but the source sentence begins "in light of our phenomenological intuitions, it might be right that"; the page introduced it with "As they put it" as a flat conclusion. The 2026-06-24 repair restored "quantitatively speaking" and left the leading hedge off. The sentence also exists only in arXiv v1; v2 removed it. Quoted now with its hedge and marked v1-only.
5. **Díaz-Boils: model-relative result stated as a fact about experience.** Source: "assuming phenomenal comparison among experiences yields only a partial comparison"; restrictions "imposed by our means of modelling and mathematical choices"; a modeller "only needs to add extra structure" such as metrics. Page: "reach a striking conclusion: phenomenal comparison does not produce a total order" and "transforms the commensurability question from a practical difficulty into a formal constraint". Seed: research note L40 ("demonstrates formally"), L111, L117. Now conditional on the model.
6. **Díaz-Boils: order-incomparability merged with untranslatability.** The page inferred "some minds inhabit experiential regions with no mapping onto others ... because no such mapping exists", and the lead said the work "formalises this intuition" of no shared framework. The model places all experiences of all species in one poset; incomparable pairs are unranked inside a shared framework, and the paper goes on to define mappings between configurations. The source therefore presupposes what the page said it denies. The page now separates three senses (unranked, no common measure, no shared framework), cites the value literature's split usage of "incommensurable" via SEP, and labels the step from the first to the third as the Map's conjecture. This is a calibration error in the skill's sense: a tenet-accepting reader would still flag "unsolvable" as overstated.
7. **Díaz-Boils on evolution: "may" upgraded to "has".** Source v1: "evolution may encompass diverse modes of experiencing"; page: "Evolution has generated diverse modes of experiencing that do not form a linear scale". "linear scale" has 0 hits in either version. Replaced with the v2 abstract's own wording, quoted.
8. **Browning: invented content.** "Standard Welfare Unit approaches ... rely on assumptions about hedonic capacity that themselves require the commensurability they aim to establish" has no basis in the paper (0 hits for "Standard Welfare Unit", "SWU", "hedonic capacity"). Seed: research note L83. The 2026-06-24 review certified this exact content as "faithful" while fixing the author name. Removed; replaced by Browning's actual point about neural proxies, quoted.
9. **Browning: epistemic diagnosis used for a structural claim.** Browning diagnoses underdetermination, holds that comparisons proceed on similarity assumptions, and expects the biology of sentience to strengthen them. The page ran her diagnosis directly after "structurally impossible" as if it supported that. Her stance is now stated and the void's question marked as one she does not raise.
10. **Wittgenstein §272 presented as his observation; the argument's direction omitted.** "As Wittgenstein observed" introduced a remark that states the private-experience picture under examination; §293 has the private object "cancel out". On the usual reading the argument dissolves the very private exemplars the void compares. The page also stated the community reading ("shared patterns of use within a community") as the argument's content, which SEP §4.1 records as disputed (Malcolm for, Baker and Hacker against), and dropped "in principle" from the summary of the conclusion. All three corrected; the disagreement with Wittgenstein is marked as sitting at the dualism tenet.
11. **IIT misdescribed.** The page said IIT offers Φ and then objected that Φ measures quantity, not quality, as if the objection were news to IIT. IIT 4.0 itself assigns quality to the Φ-structure and quantity to Φ, and its Φ-structures all live in one mathematical space, which is a stronger challenge to the void than the scalar. Introduced by the 2026-03-19 review's repair (the "Missing IIT engagement" fix), with no citation. Rewritten with the source quoted and cited; the residual disagreement (IIT identifies experience with structure) is stated as such.
