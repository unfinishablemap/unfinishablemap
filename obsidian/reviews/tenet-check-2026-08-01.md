**P1. `obsidian/concepts/mind-brain-separation.md` L118** — the worst of the family, and a double defect. Zero boundary markers in the file.
> "The Map's rejection of many-worlds preserves the phenomenological datum: choosing feels like determining which possibility becomes real because it *is* determining which possibility becomes real."

Two problems in one sentence. The *"because it **is**"* clause is flatly circular — the phenomenology is said to be veridical because the thing it is phenomenology of is real, which is the conclusion. And treating the felt difference between selecting and watching as a **datum** the tenet preserves is the move `tenets.md` L92 forbids in terms: Tenet 3 is held *"not as a directly introspectible datum"*, and `agency-void#The Verification Circularity` gives the structural reason. Fix: delete the "because it *is*" clause; recast the phenomenology as what the framework must *account for*, not what it establishes.
