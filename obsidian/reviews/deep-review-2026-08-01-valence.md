## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

- **Tenet 5 violation — parsimony used *for* the Map's own framework** (§"Valence Does Causal Work"). The functionalist rebuttal closed on: *"The most parsimonious answer is that the felt quality is a distinct property doing distinct causal work—precisely what the dualist claims."* [[tenets#^occams-limits|Tenet 5]] states explicitly that "This tenet binds the Map's use of parsimony, not only its critics'… The discipline is symmetric: parsimony cannot decide for or against a framework when the relevant knowledge is incomplete." The article made parsimony the *decisive* move in favour of dualism — precisely the inference Tenet 5 disarms. This is a calibration error, not a bedrock disagreement: it passes the diagnostic test, since a reviewer who fully accepts the Map's tenets would still flag it (indeed *because* they accept Tenet 5).

  **Resolution**: rewrote the closing move as a specification demand internal to functionalism, and named the self-binding explicitly. Functionalism individuates mental states by functional role, so owing an account of what individuates "the function of felt badness" from intact nociception is a demand the position generates against itself. The passage now ends: *"The Map takes the second horn on the strength of the missing specification, not on grounds of simplicity: by [[tenets#^occams-limits|Occam's Razor Has Limits]] the Map binds parsimony arguments made *for* its framework exactly as it binds those aimed against it."* The replacement is both Tenet-5 compliant and argumentatively stronger than what it replaced.

  **Family note (checked, not swept)**: the `most parsimonious` construction appears elsewhere in the corpus, but the sampled instances already carry the discipline — [[universal-coupling-response]] ("parsimony is not the primary reason to adopt it"), [[forward-in-time-conscious-selection]] ("The framing reduces the parsimony cost; it does not erase it"), [[cross-traditional-convergence-on-consciousness-irreducibility]] (verdict explicitly calibrated against a common-cause null). No corpus-wide sweep task minted; this was a local defect, not a family one.

The debate is live and the evaluativist has published replies; "decisive" asserts closure the literature has not reached. **Resolved** → "the strongest evidence".

- **One-directional-dissociation claim left the strongest counter-case unengaged.** "Valence without representation is harder to identify" passed over objectless moods — free-floating anxiety, undirected elation — which are the standard candidate for exactly that direction. **Resolved**: the counter-case is now stated, the evaluativist's reply (moods represent the world at large under an evaluative aspect) is given, and the passage is honestly downgraded to "contested rather than settling it" while preserving the asymmetry argument that actually survives.

The freshly-installed `[[sentientism]]` link was glossed as "the capacity for negatively valenced experience is necessary for moral consideration," but [[sentientism]] defines the position as *valenced* experience being necessary **and sufficient**. Per [[navigation-surfaces-carry-unreviewed-claims]], the label must not misdescribe its target. **Resolved** → "on which the capacity for valenced experience is what qualifies a being for moral consideration."
