## The Commissioned Read: Gennaro 2020 and Gerrans 2024

The wing review's "Ideas for Later" (L184) said the page holds its neutrality verdict "provisionally" against two unread physicalist treatments of how a model represents its own non-existence, and that Gennaro 2020 is open access so "the next deep review of the page should do it." Done.

**Gennaro (2020), read in full** (HTML galley at the journal, https://philosophymindscience.org/index.php/phimisci/article/view/8957/11942; DOI 10.33735/phimisci.2020.i.41, *Philosophy and the Mind Sciences* 1(I), 1–20, Crossref-confirmed). What it says:

- Frames Cotard as an apparent threat to higher-order-thought (HOT) theory, since HOT theory "requires a HOT about one's own mental state to accompany every conscious state" and the Cotard patient's conscious belief denies the subject. Argues HOT theory "has nothing to fear from it".
- Decomposes the case into "S1: a conscious feeling or experience of unfamiliarity with myself (or body or mental states)" and "S2: a conscious belief or thought that I am dead or I do not exist", and holds that "no matter how or when S2 is generated with respect to S1, HOT theory is not threatened and is indeed consistent with both S1 and S2."
- Concludes "some kind of minimal 'I-as-subject' seems to remain even in the most extreme cases of Cotard syndrome", and that Cotard patients "are indeed capable of having some 'I-thoughts' about their bodies and mental states" — "I do not suppose that Cotard syndrome is characterized by a total lack of self-consciousness."
- Adopts Guillot's three-way split: "the experience of prototypical Cotard patients exhibits for-me-ness, but neither me-ness nor mineness."
- Via Radovic, notes "I am dead" may mean "my body is no longer alive or functioning" or "my conscious existence has ceased", and that this ambiguity "could also partly explain the apparent contradiction in some statements made by Cotard patients."
- Cites Billon 2016 and Young's two-stage interactionist account; does not cite Davies & Coltheart; does not discuss dualism or physicalism by name (HOT theory as Gennaro defends it is a reductive representational theory; the article now says "defending a reductive higher-order-thought theory" and nothing stronger).

**What was done with the verdict**: kept, and the hedge upgraded from "unread" to a stated engagement. Gennaro's paper is the physicalist camp claiming the residual position in so many words — the "minimal I-as-subject" that the Map reads as the surviving subject is, on his account, the unconscious I-concept in a HOT about S1. That is exactly the move L65 attributed to "the physicalist self-model theorist" sight unseen, so the read confirms the neutrality verdict rather than disturbing it. Two sentences installed: the engagement at L65 (verbatim S1/S2 and "minimal I-as-subject" quotes, "the residual position, claimed for the model") and a caveat at L63 that the immortality/existence-denial inconsistency datum may be partly verbal (Gennaro's ambiguity reading), which weakens one of the page's two "constructed report" arguments and is recorded as such.
