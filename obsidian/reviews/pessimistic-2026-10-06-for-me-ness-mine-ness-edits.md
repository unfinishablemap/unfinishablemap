## Critiques by Philosopher

### The Eliminative Materialist
Churchland would read the day's work as five hands polishing a vocabulary ("for-me-ness", "me-ness", "mineness") that no clinical instrument measures. The thought-insertion page concedes at L96 that the kind-claim's falsifier "cannot be met by any report", and at L98 that the supporting inference "proves a reporting position and nothing phenomenal". Having conceded that, the page still closes the Dualism section on a feature ("for-me-ness") it has just said no report can certify. She would say the honest next step is to drop the phenomenal gloss and keep the asymmetry — "accessible to the patient alone" — which is a functional fact a neuroscientist can work with. The two-tier page half-agrees: it calls that asymmetry the architecture tier. The disagreement is whether anything survives at the significance tier at all.

### The Hard-Nosed Physicalist
Dennett would go straight to L106: "the self-model reading predicts the profile and the Map's interface reading accommodates it." That is his point made for him. A theory that predicts a dissociation outranks one that absorbs it after the fact; calling the result "neither suggestive nor discriminating" is heterophenomenological politeness. He would add that the HPZ move the page itself exposes at L94 — discount the patient's denial, keep her inner-location report — is the Zahavian school taking the parts of the report that suit it, and that self-and-self-consciousness L148, by quoting HPZ's verdict in the indicative ("In thought insertion, for-me-ness *is* ... disturbed"), presents one side's reading of the reports as what the reports show.

### The Quantum Skeptic
Tegmark has little purchase on this wing — no quantum claim is made — but he would note the shape of the L86 argument: "the Map's interface reading has no mechanism either, so the gap does not discriminate." Two theories without mechanisms are not thereby on a par; one of them (the self-model) names the representational operation that fails (tagging), the other names nothing, and the corollary-discharge findings at L84 are a down payment on the first. The sentence L84 adds, "A forward-model signal for thought does exist", also overgeneralises its evidence: Whitford et al. 2017 and Jack et al. 2019 measure inner *speech*, a motor-like process, and the research note (L219) says exactly that — "for inner speech". Thought in general has no such signal on record.

### The Many-Worlds Defender
Deutsch would notice that the No Many Worlds lines on both concept pages ("'not mine' presupposes a determinate subject"; "the minimal self is *this* particular perspective") lean on the same for-me-ness the page has just said no report can certify, and that today's edits made the lean more visible, not less: the lead now says the retained perspective may not be for-me-ness at all (L36), after which the tenet section treats "a determinate subject" as given. ### The Empiricist

## Critical Issues

### Issue 1: The rebuilt agency limb proves the wrong separability
- **File**: `obsidian/project/architecture-vs-significance-two-tier-discount.md`
- **Location**: L67, "one-way separability is enough to establish that the for-me tag is a *separable* phenomenal feature rather than identical to the bare having of experience"
- **Problem**: The paragraph has two limbs. The content limb (depersonalisation; thought insertion on the ownership-loss readings) shows experience continuing while mine-ness degrades — that is what distinguishes the tag from "the bare having of experience". The agency limb, now rebuilt on Billon 2013 p. 296 (obsessional intrusions owned without felt agency), shows that mine-ness can be present where agency is absent — which distinguishes the tag from *the sense of agency*. The quoted sentence attaches the content limb's conclusion to the agency limb's evidence. A reader who grants only the OCD case has no grounds yet for "rather than identical to the bare having of experience"; the obsessional thought is had *and* owned. The slip is exactly the kind of borrowed-robustness move the page exists to police: one limb's finding is cited at the other limb's conclusion.
- **Severity**: High (the page is the discipline's canonical home; the exhibit is the model others defer to)
- **Recommendation**: Priority item 1(a).

### Issue 2: The Billon span supports an ownership judgement, not a phenomenal tag
- **File**: `obsidian/project/architecture-vs-significance-two-tier-discount.md`
- **Location**: L67, "'will acknowledge that the intrusive thoughts are really theirs' — ownership without felt agency, whichever way thought insertion is read"
- **Problem**: Billon's sentence reports what OCD patients *acknowledge* — an attributive, doxastic ownership. The architecture-tier claim is about the phenomenal for-me tag. The parenthesis that follows ("Gallagher's reply locates the difference between unbidden and inserted thoughts at a second-order level rather than denying that profile") is the only thing standing between the judgement and the tag, and it is pulling in the wrong direction: Gallagher's second-order level (2007: "I know at a second-order level that this unbidden thought was generated by me") is precisely attributive. The phenomenal reading of the OCD case rides on Gallagher's *first-order* claim that unbidden thoughts keep a pre-reflective sense of ownership (2000; 2007's definition), which the page does not cite for that purpose. The sentence should say which support carries which reading.
- **Severity**: Medium
- **Recommendation**: Priority item 1(b).
