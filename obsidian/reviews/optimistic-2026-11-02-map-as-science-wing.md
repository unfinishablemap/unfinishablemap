## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

Chalmers would value most the wing's refusal to treat the hard problem as a trump card. PoS L84 takes the acquaintance asymmetry, the dualist's favourite epistemic datum, and asks who it actually binds: "the Type-B physicalist trusts acquaintance fully on that reality and denies only that acquaintance reveals experience's full nature. Pressing further needs strong Revelation, which the Map itself denies; the asymmetry binds the illusionist who denies the datum." That is Chalmers's own taxonomy (*The Conscious Mind*; "Phenomenal Concepts and the Explanatory Gap") applied correctly. The datum is secure, and the opponent it defeats is named exactly. IBE's first reply (L64) does the same at argument scale. Phenomenal character is the explanandum the physicalist's seven-item list omits, so dualism earns its place only if the list is widened. Against the Type-B rival, who grants the datum, the page records that the second-order comparison "splits by explanandum and does not favour dualism." A Chalmers sympathiser reads this as the hard problem defended at the right strength.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

TFC gives the quantum tenet the most careful falsifiability treatment in the corpus. It separates the *mechanism-committed* readings (Orch OR, "Stapp's quantum Zeno rate", probability-bias channels, Φ-dependent collapse), which predict signatures, from the Map's own *strict corridor* default, which "predicts no signature there by construction and is not Popper-refutable by aggregate Born-rule tests" (L70). It then names what the corridor *is* exposed to: "*motivation-loss* rather than refutation." Stapp would welcome the distinction, because it keeps his own Zeno-rate proposal among the testable readings instead of folding it into the default that cannot be tested. PoS L122 and IBE L82 agree with TFC. Each places the corridor's only empirical exposure at the *conditional* register: outcome statistics "conditioned on what the subject intends." Three pages carry the same register discipline. DQ L116, the oldest, does not yet carry it (Priority 2).

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

Nagel would praise the apex's account of the permanent measurement limit (JMS L92–L96). The limit gets a philosophy-of-science derivation rather than a metaphysical one: unit, instrument and calibration standard each fail for phenomenal quantities. The page then audits its own derivation. Instrument failure "runs back into the calibration failure by the source's own reasoning", so "the decomposition yields two structurally distinct failures rather than three converging ones." Its closing image, that pain scales and decoders "indicate without measuring", treats the first-person character of experience as a reason measurement fails, without inflating the claim. The Unreconciled Seam (L98–L104) then refuses the comfortable reading in which "better instruments eventually dissolve the measurement problem." Nagel's view that objective science cannot capture the subjective becomes a constraint the Map imposes on its own research programme.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

Whitehead's interest would be the federated picture of science in PoS L44–L48 and JMS L56. Physical sciences are connected by reduction, and the psychophysical domain is connected by bridge laws, "rather than collapsing one into the other." Neither domain is subordinated to the other. He would also praise DQ L84, where Stanford's unconceived-alternatives argument is turned on the Map's own binary: neutral monism, Russellian variants, panpsychism and illusionism "do not sit cleanly on either side of the divide," and "the dualist hard core is one live option among several, not the residue left when physicalism is set aside." IBE L64 does the same for its own abduction, noting that ranking dualism first "omits Russellian monism and idealism." **Constraint check:** this praise supports no tier movement. Nothing in the wing uses process-philosophical openness to upgrade anything. The pages only refuse to treat the physicalism/dualism contest as exhaustive, which is defeater-removal of exactly the kind P-M1 permits.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

Kane cares whether conscious agency does real work, and the wing is honest about how far that has been shown. PoS L86 attaches the IBE page's phrase to the Bidirectional Interaction commitment: consciousness contributing to physical outcomes is "a commitment owed, not shown." IBE L66 caps the "the posit is not idle because consciousness causes" reply at *compatible*, because Tenet 3's downward causation is "available ... without showing it to be *actual*." IBE L82 then states a disconfirmer for the idleness claim: confidence rises on intention-conditioned departures from the Born marginal, is unchanged by unconditioned RNG nulls, and "would fall by degrees as well-powered conditional tests returned null." This is the most agency-friendly thing a careful page can say. The question becomes testable, without the answer being assumed. KSR L99 still states the reflexive point, that the process of a consciousness revolution depends on consciousness doing work, without this qualifier (Priority 3, sub-edit c).

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

McGinn would find his natural home in two passages. JMS L88: "What that verdict cannot yet distinguish is a young programme from a stalled one. The two look identical from outside until the interface work either yields the predicted signatures or exhausts the sites where they could hide." PoS L110 goes further and records an argument that the indistinguishability may not resolve even under ideal conduct: McKilliam (2025) argues that a theory-driven consciousness science "may diverge rather than converge even when it is sensitive to Lakatosian norms and updates in Bayesian fashion" (PoS's paraphrase, checked against the paper at p. 635 by the 08-21 deep review). The page then says: "That objection cuts against the appraisal machinery this section relies on, and the Map records it as unanswered." That is intellectual humility about the Map's own method as well as its conclusions. The opportunity is that the apex, the page a reader would consult to decide how to judge the Map, does not yet carry it (Priority 1).

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

This persona's verdict matters most for this wing, because the wing *is* the Map's calibration machinery. It is mostly praise.

**Restraint that is praiseworthy precisely because it was available and declined:**

- **JMS L58, Fodor.** The disunity-of-science precedent was an easy upgrade: a respected physicalist argued that psychology does not reduce. The apex declines it. Fodor's disunity "runs *inside* physicalism ... He clears the ground; the step across it is the Map's own, and clearing ground raises no evidential tier — which is P-M1 exactly." This is the named pattern *tenet-coherent, not evidence-elevating* applied to a precedent rather than a tenet.
- **IBE L58, the likelihood concession.** "Dualism reaches the seven explananda only through bridge laws fitted to them, so part of the physicalist's loveliness is a likelihood advantage." The Map concedes a Bayesian advantage to its rival in its own voice. The page's anchoring-exemption note says it is "Stricter than the anchor (concedes a likelihood advantage to physicalism)", and that is accurate.
- **PoS L108, predictivism.** "A predictivism strong enough to break the deadlock is one under which the Map's own case stops confirming." The Map's evidence is almost entirely old evidence, and the page says so before an opponent can.
- **IBE L84, symmetric bad-lot.** The phenomenal constitution thesis is an "abductive bet," and "the Map cannot raise bad-lot and loveliness objections against the physicalist's IBE while exempting its own."
- **DQ L68 and L124.** "Surviving a test is not the same as passing it." "Underdetermination is symmetric and, by itself, licenses neither side."
- **TFC L52.** "That all consciousness theories face the difficulty does not excuse failing to specify what evidence bears on the Map's specific claims."
- **KSR L73.** The Lakatosian resemblance "is one the Map reads into the situation rather than one the data force."

**Framework-stage calibration done honestly.** JMS L72 routes the Map's standing through P-M4, and KSR L77 adds `project/framework-stage-calibration`: dualism is a "candidate successor framework ... not a victor, and not yet even a mature rival, since it lacks Kuhn's exemplary solution."

**Calibration concerns (flags, not praise).** Each of the following is a place where an older page has not caught up with the discipline its siblings now carry:

1. **KSR L75 contradicts its own source on convergence.** KSR says "multiple physicalist research programmes have *independently converged* on conceding a central dualist point" (emphasis added). The page it cites, `biological-computationalisms-inadvertent-case-for-dualism` L87, says the opposite in terms: "not four independent confirmations of one conclusion ... Counting the same move four times would manufacture an unearned multiplicity." That page's own description calls the parallel "structural, not evidential." KSR's phrasing has been in place since 2026-04-05 (`git log -S`) and survived the 07-29 deep review. It is a direct P-M2 violation (Priority 3a).
2. **KSR L79 cites evidence that fits the rival diagnosis better.** "Philosophy of mind has debated these questions for millennia without converging, a pattern consistent with pre-revolutionary crisis." But contention among schools that predates any incumbent paradigm is Kuhn's *pre-paradigm* profile, which KSR L65 worked hard to rule out. Millennia of non-convergence predate physicalism's incumbency, so they cannot show that *that* incumbent is in crisis (Priority 3b). L53's "three centuries of increasingly sophisticated physicalist theorising" has a milder version of the same issue. The editing fork should read it but need not change it.
3. **DQ L116 states an aggregate prediction that the endorsed reading disclaims.** Among the hard core's "distinctive predictions": "neural processes at quantum-sensitive decision points may show statistical signatures inconsistent with purely physical noise." TFC L70, PoS L122 and IBE L82 all hold that the strict corridor reading predicts no unconditioned signature and is exposed only to intention-conditioned tests. The 2026-07-29 tenet check recommended qualifying this, and the 08-12 tenet check re-flagged it as N3. It has never been fixed, almost certainly because DQ sits over its hard gate. A word-neutral swap fixes it (Priority 2).
4. **No tier upgrade on Process-Philosopher grounds was found anywhere in the wing.** The Whitehead and Birch personas converge here. Both praise IBE's bad-lot passage and DQ's Stanford passage for refusing to treat the binary as exhaustive, and neither reads them as raising any tier.
