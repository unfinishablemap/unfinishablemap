## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)
The lead refuses the easy dissolution twice: it insists the improper mixture is not a menu (L55) and that Many-Worlds' denial of outcomes is rejected rather than ignored (L66). Locating consciousness at *measurement-outcome selection* and not at framework selection (L57–64) is the kind of precision the hard-problem literature rarely gets from interactionists. `consciousness-in-smeared-quantum-states` L46 adds the right guard: "Determinacy alone does not establish that role, which rests on the separate case for mental causation."

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)
The wing treats Stapp with unusual fidelity: `stapp-quantum-mind` L64 quotes his own refusal of outcome selection ("nature's choice between 'Yes' and 'No'"), `post-decoherence-selection` L72 says plainly that Stapp "stops short of the outcome-selection this article locates in the gap", and `forward-in-time` L121 describes a *relocated* Process 1 rather than pretending the original fits. Stapp would not agree with the demotion (P-Q4) but would recognise his position in the Map's description of it.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)
`smeared-states` keeps first-person determinacy as a datum and then asks what it constrains, rather than reading the constraint off the physics. The 10-03 ordering preserves the asymmetry Nagel cares about: consciousness is the author of *which* outcome, never a parameter in the dynamics.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)
`evolution-of-consciousness` L155 maps the mechanism onto attention-mediated prehension without committing the Map to Whiteheadian metaphysics. Praise stops there: the paragraph's "consciousness modulating quantum collapse at neural sites" is the retired verb (zero-word rider below), and nothing in the Whitehead resonance is or should be used to move any organism up the evidential-status scale. No tier-upgrade pressure was found on this wing.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)
Token preemption inside the Born corridor is exactly the "ultimate source" structure Kane wants: the agent authors the outcome without touching the physics' probability law. The wing is honest that this authorship is, on the data, indistinguishable from chance (`prebiotic-collapse` L132; PDS L116) — Kane's own "plural voluntary control" problem in quantum dress.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)
"No combined law has been supplied; the debt is open" (prebiotic L126) and "a selection that leaves no ensemble signature has not been shown to be causal work rather than invisible authorship" (PDS L116) are model statements of what the Map does not know.
