## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

- **L67, openness as evidence, in a banned construction.** The paragraph read "Deliberative vertigo is not a dysfunction. The Map proposes it is consciousness registering something real: the genuine openness of the situation." This is the "not X. It is Y." form, and it treats felt openness as registering real openness without marking the step. Under the concession that is possibility/probability slippage: a reviewer who accepts the tenets would still flag it. **Resolved.** The paragraph now opens with the epistemic reading, which is true on any view ("the felt groundlessness tracks an actual absence of sufficient ground"). The metaphysical reading is labelled as the Map's interpretation and linked to the trilemma's concession. The paragraph says the feeling "may be veridical without deciding which situation obtains". Epiphenomenalism is not conceded.
- **L129, "Commitment selects", listed as evidence.** The intro at L113 said the phenomenology "provides evidence for" the Map's commitments, and the third claim argued from felt resolution to selection. Felt resolution would accompany a deterministic settlement too. **Resolved.** L113 now says "bears on". L129 gains: "The phenomenology fits this third claim without supplying evidence for it: a deterministic settlement, or a subpersonal random one, could feel just as resolved." The old "not one of randomness—it is of *resolution*" construction is rephrased. My first draft said that felt resolution "tells against randomising". That repeats the same slide against Horn 2, so I cut it before finishing.
- **L117, Dreyfus's stance.** The page listed "expert intuition acquired through domain experience (Dreyfus)" among frameworks that "all provide computational frameworks". Dreyfus's position is the reverse: expert performance is intuitive and not calculative, and calculative rationality does not exhaust intelligence. This is the *Mind over Machine* line, reported in Gobet 2018 and Dreyfus & Rousse 2018 (*Journal of Expertise*); the wording came from a search summary and was not grep-verified against raw text, so it is paraphrased here. The 06-09 ledger certified only the L127 attribution, so the stance leg was never run on L117. **Resolved.** Dreyfus's account is now "anti-calculative" and "explains expert intuition as skill acquired through experience". He is not enlisted for dualism.
- **L139, epistemic-to-physical slide.** The paragraph went from "evidence and algorithm underdetermine the outcome" straight to "otherwise indeterminate neural processes". That is phenomenology-to-metaphysics at the tenet level. "Contribute genuine novelty" also sat uneasily with L129's selection-only reading ("brings the choice, not new content"). **Resolved.** The underdetermination is now called epistemic. Physical indeterminacy is "an empirical question the felt uncertainty cannot settle", and the interface claim is conditional ("Where they are … would let"). "Genuine novelty" is removed.
