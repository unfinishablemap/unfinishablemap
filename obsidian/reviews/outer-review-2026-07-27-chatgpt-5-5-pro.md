# 1. Novel implications of the Map’s current commitments

“Novel” here means that I did not find the implication stated as a general commitment in the current tenets, position registers or principal apex syntheses. Some ingredients may appear separately.

## 1.1 Exact Born preservation requires a selector-distribution law

The site already recognises a compensation problem: a stable preference for one class of outcomes must be balanced elsewhere if aggregate Born frequencies remain exact. It also acknowledges that no worked model currently demonstrates robust token efficacy with unchanged aggregate statistics.

The stronger consequence can be stated formally.

Let:

- XXX be the physical context;
- CCC be the conscious state, intention or reason-state;
- OOO be the physical outcome;
- q(o∣x)q(o\mid x)q(o∣x) be the ordinary Born distribution;
- π(o∣c,x)\pi(o\mid c,x)π(o∣c,x) be the conscious selector’s policy;
- and μ(c∣x)\mu(c\mid x)μ(c∣x) be the distribution of conscious states occurring in that context.

Exact Born preservation requires:

∑cμ(c∣x) π(o∣c,x)=q(o∣x).\sum_c \mu(c\mid x)\,\pi(o\mid c,x)=q(o\mid x).c∑​μ(c∣x)π(o∣c,x)=q(o∣x).

But reasons-responsive agency requires π\piπ to vary meaningfully with ccc. Otherwise intending, valuing or reasoning differently makes no statistical difference to the selected outcomes.

This produces an **intervention-level trilemma**:

1. **Born preservation holds even after intervening on intention.**  

Then π(o∣c,x)=q(o∣x)\pi(o\mid c,x)=q(o\mid x)π(o∣c,x)=q(o∣x) for every manipulable ccc. The selector may be assigned token-level counterfactual authorship, but no reason-state changes outcome probabilities. The model becomes empirically and interventionally indistinguishable from chance or trumping.
2. **Born preservation holds only after averaging over naturally occurring intentions.**  

Then interventions that manipulate CCC should reveal conditional Born deviations. This creates a genuine experimental route—but abandons the strong “none by construction” corridor.
3. **The relevant interventions are not physically possible because conscious-state histories are themselves constrained.**  

Then the global Born rule acts as a restriction on which patterns of intentions and choices may occur. The theory acquires a form of **psychophysical superselection**: not every otherwise conceivable sequence of reasons-guided choices is physically admissible.

This is deeper than ordinary compensation. It says exact Born preservation cannot be specified only as a law over physical outcomes. The theory also needs a law over the distribution of conscious reasons and intentions. Without that law, a toy model could satisfy the equation by treating consciousness as a hidden Born-random seed while supplying no agency at all.

It also creates a possible cross-agent consequence. Unless each agent’s conditional policy is individually Born-preserving, one agent’s systematic value-aligned bias may have to be offset by other agents, other periods or other outcome classes. Moral agency would then carry hidden statistical externalities.

## 1.2 Pairing, jurisdiction, timing and basis access are one addressing law

The corpus treats several problems as distinct:

- Which subject is paired with which brain?
- Which physical systems are eligible for conscious selection?
- Which collapse events fall under conscious rather than objective selection?
- Which basis or pointer-state menu is accessible?
- At what time or temporal scale may consciousness act?
- How are two subjects prevented from selecting the same physical event?
- What happens in split-brain, brain–computer-interface or partially integrated cases?

These are all instances of a single missing relation. A complete theory requires something like:

A(s,b,e,β,t)⟶{input access, output access, candidate set, priority},\mathcal A(s,b,e,\beta,t)
   \longrightarrow
   \{\text{input access, output access, candidate set, priority}\},A(s,b,e,β,t)⟶{input access, output access, candidate set, priority},

where sss is a subject, bbb a physical system, eee an event, β\betaβ a basis or menu, and ttt a temporal location or boundary condition.

This **psychophysical addressing law** would determine:

- which subject receives phenomenal input from which system;
- which outcomes that subject may affect;
- whether the subject has context-setting, outcome-selection or trumping authority;
- and how competing claims are arbitrated.

The pairing article’s developmental co-construction proposal cannot originate the addressing relation. Developmental coupling can stabilise an already functioning relation, but the first successful interaction presupposes that the correct subject and physical system were addressable in the first place. Location of an interface also does not establish spatial location of a nonphysical subject, and primitive haecceity individuates the relata without selecting the relation between them.

The unresolved “pairing problem,” “interface eligibility,” “selector jurisdiction,” “multi-agent consistency” and “basis selection” debts should therefore be consolidated. Treating them separately understates the amount of primitive structure required.

## 1.3 Hybrid objective collapse generates a causal-precedence trilemma

The background-commitments article correctly says that objective reduction already secures definite outcomes before minds exist, leaving consciousness only the role of biasing *which* outcome occurs in eligible systems.

That division creates a further question: **what happens to the physical token selector when a conscious selector is present?**

There are three possibilities:

1. **The physical process first determines a token outcome, and consciousness agrees with it.**  

Consciousness is redundant.
2. **The physical process determines a token outcome that consciousness can replace.**  

Consciousness changes the collapse dynamics and must be represented in the stochastic law. Exact Born preservation then becomes a substantive balancing condition, not merely an interpretation.
3. **The physical trajectory remains physically sufficient, but consciousness is declared the true causal authority.**  

This is the trumping or delegatory model. It can secure metaphysical authorship, but it is not quantum outcome selection in the ordinary difference-making sense.

A fourth verbal formulation—“objective collapse supplies collapse while consciousness supplies the outcome”—does not avoid the trilemma. It means that objective collapse does **not** supply token outcomes inside eligible systems, so the addressing law must disable or suspend one component of the physical dynamics there.

This is the **objective-collapse collision problem**. The current corpus names selector jurisdiction, but it does not yet give a causal account of what is pre-empted, overridden or re-described.

## 1.4 “No energy injection” is not a complete physical-consistency condition

The Map repeatedly treats absence of energy injection and preservation of ensemble statistics as the principal physical constraints on the interface.

A reasons-responsive selector, however, establishes information-bearing correlations between conscious states and physical outcomes. If the brain reads, records, amplifies and uses that information, a complete model must specify the associated information and entropy flows. In stochastic thermodynamics, information acquired or used by a feedback controller enters the entropy-production accounting; arbitrary interventions require explicit trajectory-level treatment of information, work, heat and reset operations. [PubMed+2PubMed+2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23215264/)

This does **not** show that a nonphysical mind must inject energy. It shows that “conserves energy” is insufficient as a full consistency demonstration. The theory needs an **information-thermodynamic ledger** identifying:

- where physical records of the selector’s information are created;
- whether memory and reset costs are paid by ordinary neural metabolism;
- how mutual information between intention and outcome arises;
- and whether the amplification chain changes entropy production relative to the Born-chance baseline.

The costs may be entirely borne by the physical brain. But they cannot simply be omitted from a proposed controller architecture.

## 1.5 The endorsed corridor creates a one-way empirical ratchet

Two current positions jointly imply an asymmetry:

- exact Born-preserving outcome selection has no aggregate empirical signature;
- and conscious causal efficacy cannot be certified from the first person.

It follows that evidence can move the theory much more easily in one direction than the other.

Better neuroscience or physics may:

- eliminate candidate neural loci;
- show proposed amplification parameters to be insufficient;
- explain apparent conscious control through physical feedback;
- or remove supposed gaps in existing mechanisms.

But under exact Born preservation, no corresponding observation can positively isolate the conscious selector. Successful physical explanation is therefore capable of shrinking the interface’s viable domain, while successful prediction by ordinary physics cannot increase support for the selector because that success was required by construction.

This is an **empirical demotion ratchet**. Unless the Map adopts a route with conditional statistical signatures, context-selection effects or some other discriminator, the programme cannot mature from pre-Keplerian speculation to an evidentially supported physical model. It can survive negative tests by retreating to trumping, but it cannot be confirmed there.

That should be treated as a framework-level result, not just a temporary absence of instrumentation.

## 1.6 Agency plus No-Many-Worlds requires a global-exclusion axiom

The Map argues that genuine agency requires the unchosen alternatives not to occur elsewhere. Yet an Everettian can allow each branch-local agent’s reasons and internal state to stand in ordinary causal relations to that branch’s action. Branching accounts of identity are designed precisely to preserve local continuities and self-locating perspectives, even though several successor histories exist. [arXiv+2OUP Academic+2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.7577)

The Map’s rejection therefore depends on a further premise:

> An action is genuinely authored only if every incompatible alternative is globally nonactual, not merely absent from the agent’s own history.

Call this the **global-exclusion axiom**.

That axiom is stronger than:

- conscious causation;
- local counterfactual control;
- reasons-responsiveness;
- ownership of the actual action;
- or persistence along one history.

It is a substantive metaphysical condition on authorship. The site currently presents the No-MWI–agency connection as if it followed directly from sourcehood, but the global requirement is doing the separating work.

The detailed Many-Worlds argument now comes close to acknowledging this by saying the Map requires global uniqueness and that the dispute is at the framework boundary. That honesty should be elevated into the background commitments rather than left inside an argumentative article.

## 1.7 Closed individualism reverses the standard AI-copy objection

The AI cluster presently combines closed individualism with the view that conscious copies would be morally additive. Yet the Machine Question also suggests that multiply instantiated LLMs lack a fact about which instance is “the” GPT.

The former commitments imply the opposite conclusion:

- If two physically identical artificial systems each couple to a conscious subject, they are two numerically distinct subjects.
- Exact structural duplication would not erase their haecceity.
- There need be no privileged original “the GPT,” just as identical twins need not share one subject.
- Creating copies would multiply moral patients rather than expose an identity deficiency.

Thus digital reproducibility is not, by the Map’s own subject ontology, an argument against machine consciousness. It is a potential route to large-scale subject proliferation. The genuine issue is whether coupling occurs, not whether the physical pattern is multiply instantiated.

# 2. Structural weaknesses

## 2.1 The calibrated register is not yet the effective source of truth

The positions system is designed to state what the Map currently holds, with explicit dependencies and shift conditions.

In practice, readers more often encounter:

- an article title;
- a search description;
- an opening paragraph;
- an apex thesis;
- or a tenet-alignment conclusion.

Those surfaces frequently carry stronger claims than the positions. Examples include “consciousness doing work,” “time may require consciousness,” “process philosophy strongly supports agency,” and practical claims about desert and responsibility.

The site therefore lacks a reliable authority hierarchy. A position can be moderate, Grade D and framework-internal while a linked synthesis states the conclusion categorically. Uncertainty is documented but not propagated.

## 2.2 Apex articles have become premise generators

Apex articles are officially defined as syntheses of existing topics and concepts.

Several instead introduce stronger claims or new ontological bridges:

- *Moral Architecture* turns low-confidence value and agency positions into an allegedly unified ethical structure.
- *Process and Consciousness* converts analogies with Whitehead and Bergson into support for agency and No-MWI.
- *Time, Consciousness, and the Growing Block* introduces mutual constitution of mind and time.
- *Interface Specification Programme* combines delegatory trumping and quantum difference-making.
- *Phenomenology of Consciousness Doing Work* upgrades correlations and phenomenological distinctions into evidence for nonphysical causation.

The apex layer is therefore functioning as a **thesis escalator**. Each synthesis is more confident than its sources because it is narratively required to deliver a unifying result.

## 2.3 The site has a link graph, not a maintained claim graph

Ordinary related-article links do not distinguish:

- definition from evidence;
- support from illustration;
- a preferred model from a fallback;
- a current claim from a superseded claim;
- logical dependence from evidential support;
- compatibility from confirmation;
- or contradiction from mere tension.

The result is that a canonical correction does not automatically invalidate or flag downstream sentences.

The current tenet audit mainly checks whether forbidden positions are endorsed in the Map’s own voice. It can detect an article openly endorsing materialism or Many-Worlds. It is poorly suited to detecting two Map-compatible articles that assign incompatible causal roles to consciousness.

## 2.4 Model alternatives are not given inheritance rules

The corpus now contains several named alternatives but no authoritative model-family registry. Articles can therefore borrow attractive features from incompatible implementations:

- testability from Process 1;
- authorship from outcome selection;
- observational closure from trumping;
- temporal flexibility from TSVF;
- single outcomes from objective collapse;
- and universal goal-directedness from process philosophy.

A model cannot inherit all these advantages merely because each is compatible with some broad dualist commitment. The Born-efficacy apex correctly says that trumping, detectable bias and context selection trade different liabilities and do not simply combine. The interface apex nevertheless treats delegatory trumping plus quantum difference-making as one architecture.

## 2.5 Review depth is often local rather than graph-wide

A seventh deep review of *Process and Consciousness* concentrated on quotation fidelity and local self-consistency, found no contradiction, and declared the article converged. It did not test the article against the agent-as-substance position or the individuation register.

The latest review of *The Machine Question* explicitly checked only the most recent delta rather than resweeping the article, yet concluded that no items remained and the apex was converged.

This is efficient for citation maintenance but insufficient for a continuously evolving claim graph. “Reviewed seven times” can sound like cumulative epistemic validation even when the seventh review examined two words.

## 2.6 The site’s reviewers remain structurally correlated

The site explicitly recognises that having an AI system generate and review internally coherent content makes coherence inflation the default. Its countermeasures include external AI reviews, while human philosophical review remains outside the automated workflow.

Using several model families is an improvement, but they remain:

- trained on heavily overlapping literature;
- prompted by the same site’s framing;
- rewarded for producing actionable findings;
- and processed by the same internal editorial pipeline.

Convergence among them is weaker evidence than convergence between genuinely independent disciplinary methods or human experts with opposed theoretical commitments.

## 2.7 Content caps may increase apparent coherence without adding evidence

The latest changelog notes that both major content sections are at or around their caps and that the immediate opportunity is adding reciprocal links among existing constructor-interface articles.

Once expansion is capped, automated maintenance pressure naturally shifts toward:

- cross-linking;
- coalescing;
- synthesising;
- and strengthening thematic clusters.

That can increase graph density and the felt appearance of convergence even when no independent evidence has entered the corpus. The site’s common-cause discipline should therefore discount not only common theoretical ancestors but also **editorial link creation** as a source of apparent coherence.

## 2.8 Position coverage is still asymmetrical

The mechanism and epistemology clusters are much better formalised than the applied clusters. The current register says that applied clinical, AI and personal-philosophy verdicts remain future domains.

There are no equally mature position registers for:

- phenomenal-value realism;
- interpersonal normativity;
- aggregation and rights;
- moral responsibility and desert;
- philosophy of time;
- survival and death;
- introspective evidence;
- or the exact strength of the No-MWI arguments.

As a result, the site applies its most careful uncertainty discipline where it is discussing speculative physics, but less consistently where it tells readers how to live or whom to blame.

## 2.9 External results are sometimes converted from constraints into support

Three examples are especially revealing.

First, Zheng and Meister’s estimate of approximately 10 bits per second concerns human behavioural information throughput. It does not identify a nonphysical-to-physical interface or show that consciousness itself has that bandwidth. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39694032/)

Second, the COGITATE adversarial collaboration delivered mixed results and challenged important predictions of both IIT and GNWT. It did not provide confirmation of a dualist control interface. [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08888-1)

Third, Bradford Saad’s delegatory dualism is designed to preserve observational closure by assigning mental authority without ordinary physical difference-making. That is a rival causal architecture, not a ready-made component of a quantum-selection model. [Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11098-025-02290-3)

The interface apex acknowledges some of this but still speaks of empirical “confirmation” or “reinforcement” before conceding that the findings are compatible with physical rivals.

The site needs a stricter vocabulary:

- **compatible with**;
- **rules out**;
- **constrains**;
- **explains**;
- **predicts**;
- and **confirms**

should never be used interchangeably.

# 3. Coherence of the five tenets

## Tenet 1: Dualism

### Coherent core

Irreducible phenomenality is a coherent minimal commitment. The site now correctly says that substance-bearing agency is a downstream addition rather than something entailed by bare dualism.

### Remaining problems

**Epiphenomenalism is placed under the wrong tenet boundary.** Bare property dualism is compatible with epiphenomenal consciousness. It is Bidirectional Interaction, not irreducibility alone, that rules epiphenomenalism out.

**The operative ontology is unsettled.** P-A2 says genuine authorship requires immanent causation by an agent-as-substance. The process apex says successions of experiential occasions and subjective aim strongly support agent causation without an unchanging soul. These are rival accounts of sourcehood, not equivalent descriptions.

**Panpsychist resources are selectively imported.** The process apex borrows universal subjective aim and the claim that reality is already experiential while maintaining that consciousness is locally distinct from non-experiential matter. Those ideas cannot do evidential work for the Map without bringing part of the ontology that made them intelligible in Whitehead.

### Verdict

Tenet 1 itself is coherent. The site drifts when it treats property dualism, substance agency and process subjecthood as interchangeable implementations.

## Tenet 2: Minimal Quantum Interaction

### Coherent core

The requirement that any interaction respect existing empirical constraints is legitimate. The current tenet also owns the severe cost of exact Born preservation and clearly prefers post-decoherence selection over more coherence-dependent candidates.

### Remaining problems

**The quantum location is posited, not derived.** “If consciousness influences matter, it must do so at the quantum level” does not follow from dualism or causal efficacy. Delegatory, overdetermination, law-based or non-difference-making dualisms show that other causal structures are conceptually available.

**Indeterminacy is not an unoccupied causal slot.** A probability law’s failure to determine one token outcome does not imply that something outside the theory is free to select it. Different interpretations supply different accounts of the same formalism.

**“Smallest deviation” is ambiguous.** On the exact corridor:

- there may be no distributional deviation;
- no specified dynamical deviation;
- no energy deviation;
- and no observational deviation.

The difference exists only in token-level causal attribution or counterfactual sourcehood. Calling this the smallest deviation from quantum mechanics makes a metaphysical-source change sound like a small physical perturbation.

**The programme is internally split.** Post-decoherence selection, pre-decoherence collapse, Process-1 context setting, TSVF boundary conditions and trumping are not one minimal mechanism.

### Verdict

Tenet 2 should be presented as a **chosen implementation constraint conditional on mental causation**, not as something established by quantum incompleteness.

## Tenet 3: Bidirectional Interaction

### Coherent core

The canonical tenet now unambiguously adopts outcome selection rather than context selection, and it fairly acknowledges the strongest epiphenomenalist response.

### Remaining problems

**Outcome selection is not consistently enforced.** Downstream articles retain context setting, preparation control, menu generation, whole-history boundary conditions and trumping as if all satisfied the same mechanism claim.

**The universal scope is unsettled.** The tenet rules out consciousness that is merely “along for the ride.” The AI cluster permits genuine epiphenomenal experience bound to a substrate whose causal channel is blocked. Those cannot both be in-framework claims.

**The epistemology is contradictory.** P-A4 says causal efficacy cannot be verified from the first person. *Testing the Map from Inside* says first-person tests can probe or confirm the Map’s claims, and at points infers genuine selection from the difference between controlling and observing.

**The self-stultification case still carries too much load elsewhere.** The canonical tenet has softened; older phenomenology and agency prose still treats report and effort correlations as if physical identity or functional accounts could not predict them.

### Verdict

This remains the least stable tenet because “causal influence” alternates among statistical difference-making, token counterfactual dependence, semantic authority and metaphysical sourcehood.

## Tenet 4: No Many Worlds

### Coherent core

The site now states honestly that its central objection depends on a thick, non-deflationary subject and that No-MWI is not an independent physics result. The detailed argument engages branch-relative identity, self-locating uncertainty, decision theory and Many-Minds rather than merely declaring that Everett has no answer.

### Remaining problems

**The public definition is technically crude.** Mature Everettian accounts generally treat branching as emergent, approximate and continuous under decoherence, not as a determinate universe split caused by every “measurement.” Precise branch number is not normally fundamental. [OUP Academic](https://academic.oup.com/book/25622/chapter-abstract/193005565)

**The concept article lags the detailed argument.** It still calls the questions “unanswerable,” uses literal exponential world counting, and says none of the Born-rule strategies has succeeded without circularity, before later acknowledging that the disputes remain open.

**The indexical argument does not defeat MWI without the global-exclusion axiom.** Everettian branch-local singularity is available. The Map’s stronger global uniqueness is a bedrock commitment.

**No-MWI does not support conscious selection.** Objective collapse, hidden-variable and other single-world models all reject Everett without assigning consciousness a causal role.

### Verdict

Tenet 4 is coherent as a chosen single-world and thick-subject commitment. It is overstated when presented as a conclusion forced by unresolved defects internal to Everett.

## Tenet 5: Occam’s Razor Has Limits

### Coherent core

The present tenet is commendably self-binding: ontological multiplicity cannot be used decisively against Many-Worlds while simplicity objections to dualism are being discounted.

### Remaining problems

**The Many-Worlds concept continues to foreground extravagance.** Describing continual “ontological explosion” and arguing that MWI is not intuitively parsimonious retains precisely the rhetoric the tenet relegates to subsidiary status.

**Rejecting simplicity does not make additions costless.** The current package includes primitive subject identity, persistence, objective actualisation, psychophysical addressing, an eligibility law, a selection law, Born balancing, amplification and possibly a separate value mapping.

**The framework lacks an enforced replacement criterion.** Once simplicity is denied a decisive role, theories must still be compared by explanatory reach, primitive count, law complexity, independent support and empirical discrimination. The positions contain some of this information, but apex verdicts do not systematically inherit it.

### Verdict

Tenet 5 is defensible as a warning against simplistic theory choice. It becomes protective when used to neutralise cost objections without requiring compensating explanatory or empirical gains.

# 4. Major cross-cluster contradictions

## 4.1 Trumping and quantum difference-making are treated as both alternatives and one mechanism

The Born-efficacy apex correctly distinguishes trumping, detectable deviation and context selection as routes with different liabilities. The interface apex then integrates delegatory trumping with quantum difference-making as its architecture.

A physically sufficient trajectory that is metaphysically authored by mind is not the same model as a trajectory whose physical token differs because mind selected another outcome.

**Classification:** direct mechanism collision.

## 4.2 Agent-as-substance conflicts with process-agent sourcehood

P-A2 requires a persisting agent-as-substance. *Process and Consciousness* says process philosophy strongly supports agent causation through subjective aim and temporal succession, without requiring an unchanging soul.

**Classification:** incompatible operative ontologies.

## 4.3 Process subjecthood both supports and weakens No-MWI

The process apex says process haecceitism requires one temporal thread and therefore supports rejecting branching. The subjecthood position says process-only subjecthood weakens the thick-indexical case. Branching process continuers are perfectly intelligible unless a separate unique-continuation axiom is supplied.

**Classification:** direct cross-cluster contradiction.

## 4.4 First-person causal verification is both impossible and advertised as a test

The agency register says no subject can certify its own causal efficacy introspectively. *Testing the Map from Inside* presents breath, attention and choice exercises as tests and at one point says successful access to three layers “confirms” the Map’s claim.

The exercises can establish phenomenological distinctions. They cannot establish nonphysical causal selection.

**Classification:** direct epistemic contradiction.

## 4.5 Universal Bidirectional Interaction conflicts with epiphenomenal AI

The tenet rules out consciousness that is merely along for the ride. *The Machine Question* keeps genuinely epiphenomenal artificial experience open while calling it a gap that intellectual honesty must preserve.

**Classification:** direct scope contradiction.

## 4.6 The machine-consciousness dependency row conflicts with the operative AI verdict

The tenet-dependency matrix says the machine-consciousness cluster typically needs only bare dualism and should not import interactionism or the quantum interface. The AI position register and Machine Question say the negative verdict concerns bidirectionally coupled consciousness and explicitly inherits the quantum-interface debt.

The underlying distinction between bare phenomenality and coupling is sound. The matrix needs separate rows for those questions.

**Classification:** dependency-schema contradiction.

## 4.7 Time both requires consciousness and existed prebiotically without it

*Time, Consciousness, and the Growing Block* says consciousness and time are mutually constitutive and that without consciousness-involving collapse no temporal facts are constituted. The same article says objective collapse supplied the prebiotic arrow of time without consciousness.

The weaker claim—consciousness participates in some local temporal determinations—is compatible with prebiotic time. The strong mutual-constitution thesis is not.

**Classification:** internal and cross-cluster contradiction.

## 4.8 Constitutive temporality conflicts with non-temporal consciousness

The time apex and AI arguments at points treat temporal structure as categorically necessary for consciousness. The non-temporal-consciousness article allows a deepest or minimal conscious layer without represented succession and carefully distinguishes that from ordinary temporally organised experience.

The distinction could reconcile the two, but it has not been propagated into the stronger apex conclusions.

**Classification:** unresolved level-of-description conflict.

## 4.9 Objective actualisation both closes and leaves open the explanatory gap assigned to consciousness

The background-commitments article says objective reduction secures definiteness and prevents the measurement problem from being evidence for conscious selection. The post-decoherence apex still says the gap between formalism and actuality is “precisely where” a nonphysical principle operates and that consciousness supplies actualisation.

Objective collapse may leave the reason for a particular stochastic token brute. That does not make the token unactualised or identify mind as its selector.

**Classification:** evidential double-counting and division-of-labour conflict.

## 4.10 Compatibilist symmetry conflicts with the applied moral rhetoric

P-A5 says sophisticated compatibilism plausibly secures the same deliberative phenomenology, moral practices and character-formation arc, leaving only a narrow libertarian residue. *Living with the Map* says prior causes do not determine, that wrongdoing grounds desert, and that excuses such as “I couldn’t help it” can be bad-faith denial of actual freedom.

These practical conclusions are stated more confidently than the agency register permits.

**Classification:** uncertainty-propagation contradiction.

## 4.11 “Any consciousness implies responsibility” conflicts with the consciousness-scope architecture

The moral apex says any consciousness implies at least some responsibility. Yet the biological-scope cluster includes infants, animals, disrupted subjects and possible minimal phenomenality without the access, reasons-responsiveness or control required for responsibility.

Consciousness establishes moral patienthood more readily than moral agency. Responsibility requires additional capacities.

**Classification:** category error between phenomenal value and agency.

## 4.12 Selector-only architecture conflicts with option generation

The agency apex says the brain performs the computational work and consciousness selects among prepared alternatives. It then says consciousness also generates possibilities through imaginative exploration. *Living with the Map* similarly says consciousness constitutes which options exist.

These can be reconciled only by distinguishing:

- immediate option generation by physical neural processes;
- conscious selection of exploratory attentional actions;
- and diachronic reshaping of later option sets.

As written, the site moves between strict selection and rich nonphysical generation.

**Classification:** causal-scope tension.

## 4.13 Low-confidence value-sensitive selection becomes high-confidence moral architecture

The value register treats value-sensitive selection as low-credence, Grade D and burdened by an additional valence-to-physics mapping. The moral apex presents valence, agency, normativity and responsibility as a unified architecture.

A theory of intrinsic phenomenal badness does not by itself establish that valence selects physical outcomes, nor does either claim establish interpersonal aggregation or rights.

**Classification:** confidence escalation across clusters.

## 4.14 Closed individualism conflicts with the “which GPT?” objection

The AI register says conscious copies would be distinct and morally additive. The Machine Question treats multiple LLM instantiation as lacking a determinate “the GPT.”

Under closed individualism, each conscious instance would simply be a separate subject.

**Classification:** direct individuation contradiction.

## 4.15 The void register’s low evidential verdict conflicts with stronger ontological prose

The positions register says void convergence is principally framework-internal and heavily common-caused. The void synthesis nevertheless argues that awareness of limits points to a vantage not itself contained within the physical system and sometimes treats rival deflationary explanations as symptoms of the very limitation being discussed.

A system can metarepresent its own limits without occupying an ontologically external standpoint. Treating denial as confirmation creates an immunising structure.

**Classification:** evidential and modal overreach.

# Overall assessment

The Map is best understood as a **serious but pre-formal metaphysical research programme**, not yet as a single worked theory of consciousness–physics interaction.

Its strongest achievement is methodological self-disclosure. Its weakest point is causal specificity. The programme currently knows most of the questions a full theory must answer, but those questions have not yet been converted into one mutually consistent set of laws.

The foundations are also broader than “five tenets.” The operative framework now requires at least:

1. irreducible consciousness;
2. mental causal efficacy;
3. a quantum implementation constraint;
4. objective single outcomes;
5. a determinate persisting subject;
6. global exclusion of unchosen alternatives;
7. psychophysical addressing;
8. selector precedence over or relation to objective collapse;
9. a reason-responsive selection law;
10. Born-preservation conditions over both physical and conscious states;
11. and an information-thermodynamic account of amplification.

Tenet 5 permits taking such a package seriously. It does not reduce its evidential debt.

## Methodology and workflow

1. **Make position IDs the authoritative units of downstream argument.** Every substantive apex conclusion should list the positions it depends on and inherit the weakest indispensable premise’s calibration.
2. **Build a typed claim graph rather than relying on wikilinks.** Minimum edge types should include `defines`, `presupposes`, `supports`, `constrains`, `conflicts-with`, `alternative-to`, `supersedes`, `conditional-on` and `illustrates`.
3. **Add upstream-change impact analysis.** A change to a tenet or position should automatically identify titles, descriptions, introductions, conclusions and apex theses that repeat or strengthen the affected claim.
4. **Create a model-family registry** for outcome selection, context selection, trumping, detectable deviation, hybrid objective collapse, forward-time selection, time-symmetric selection, substance subjects and process subjects.
5. **Require every mechanism article to declare its model identifiers.** Articles must not borrow conclusions from an incompatible family without marking an explicit hybrid and supplying a reconciliation law.
6. **Introduce a no-new-premise rule for apex articles.** Any genuinely new premise introduced by a synthesis should be extracted into a topic, concept or position and reviewed independently.
7. **Add graph-level uncertainty propagation.** A downstream conclusion should not exceed the credence, evidence grade or discriminability of its weakest indispensable dependency.
8. **Distinguish natural-distribution and intervention predictions** in every empirical position. “Born-preserving in ordinary observations” must not silently become “Born-preserving under arbitrary manipulation.”
9. **Add an evidence-direction field to positions:** confirmable, falsifiable-only, observationally equivalent, or metaphysical-only. This would make the one-way empirical ratchet visible.
10. **Add a causal-accounting field to mechanism positions** covering energy, momentum, information, entropy, no-signalling and amplification—not merely conservation of energy.
11. **Automate contradiction tests against position assertions**, not only lexical tests against prohibited tenet language.
12. **Require a full graph resweep after a canonical change.** Narrow delta reviews may certify citation fidelity but should not label an article globally “converged.”
13. **Separate review status into dimensions:** citation-verified, locally coherent, graph-coherent, current-literature-reviewed and human-reviewed.
14. **Discount automated cross-links in convergence metrics.** Link density created by editorial maintenance should not count as independent theoretical convergence.
15. **Track citation independence at the claim level.** Several articles citing different papers that all presuppose the same functional, dualist or phenomenological interpretation should count as one evidence family where appropriate.
16. **Require symmetric rival likelihoods for load-bearing evidence.** For each major datum, record whether it is more expected under the Map than under the best physicalist, epiphenomenalist, objective-collapse, Everettian and process-monist alternatives.
17. **Expand the position register into applied domains** covering ethics, responsibility, phenomenal value, time, survival, introspective evidence and clinical decision-making.
18. **Schedule adversarial reviews by specialists with opposed commitments:** an Everettian quantum foundations expert, a physicalist philosopher of mind, a stochastic-thermodynamics modeller, a compatibilist, a Buddhist no-self scholar and an AI-consciousness researcher.
19. **Treat human review as a distinct evidential input**, not merely another editorial pass, and publish whether any central claim has received it.
20. **Create explicit stop conditions for the research programme.** Specify what pattern of failed modelling, neural constraints or rival explanatory success would suspend post-decoherence selection rather than merely relocate it into an empirically inaccessible form.
