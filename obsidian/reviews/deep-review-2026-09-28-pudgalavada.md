## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

1. **Map position overstated on rebirth (calibration).** "How Far the Parallel Holds" counted "continuous across lives" as "the diachronic job the Map's subject does", and "Mostly misses" had the Map answer "same" to the *Kathāvatthu*'s transmigration trilemma. The Map takes no position on rebirth (`tenets/background-commitments` L32 names agency, No-Many-Worlds, personal-identity, copying and survival puzzles; none is rebirth). Both loci now scope the answer to persistence within a life and say the Map takes no position on rebirth. A tenet-accepting reviewer would have flagged this, so it is a calibration error, not bedrock.
2. **Internal slip on the agent definition.** "Direct hits" said Vasubandhu's definition of the agent as "the principal cause" is one "the Map can accept, since it claims the subject is a real cause among the action's causes". A real cause among many is not thereby the principal cause, and Tenet 2 makes the subject's physical influence minimal. Rewritten: the Map can meet the definition only if the subject's selection is what settles which open alternative becomes actual, and whether that makes it the principal cause is unsettled.
3. **"Silent on the subject's origin" was false.** `concepts/emergent-dualism` holds Hasker's view as "a live cousin" tested against the dependence question. Now: the Map "holds only a candidate, Hasker's [[emergent-dualism]], as a cousin rather than a commitment".
4. **Source-fidelity: "fifth category of knowables".** Priestley (IEP) says "a fifth category of existence, the 'inexpressible'"; "knowables" is not in the IEP entry or in Pruden's ch. 9. Corrected to the source's wording.
5. **Reference metadata (real-wrong-metadata, two entries).** See ledger: Carpenter 2017 volume subtitle and editors; Carpenter 2015 page range and DOI added.
