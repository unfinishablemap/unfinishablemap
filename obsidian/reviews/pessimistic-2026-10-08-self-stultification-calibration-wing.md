## Counterarguments to Address

### "Reports lose their causal grounding"
- **Current content says** (dualism L154, delegatory L132–134): without phenomenal causation introspective reports are ungrounded.
- **A critic would argue**: Chalmers 1996 p. 198 (per SEP §2.4) — experiences are "partially constitutive of beliefs about experiences", so justification accrues by acquaintance, not causation; Yetter-Chappell 2022 (hub L175–181) makes the same move for a non-physical subject. The grounding lost is *causal* grounding, which is only a loss if knowledge of experience needs it.
