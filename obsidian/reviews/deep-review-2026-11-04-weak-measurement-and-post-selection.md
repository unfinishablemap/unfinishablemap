## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

- **False originality claim (L104), already corrected on the apex but never propagated here.** The page read: "This is the Map's original synthesis—neither Aharonov nor other TSVF proponents propose consciousness as the source of post-selection." On 2026-06-08 (`d26c074828`) the same claim was withdrawn from [[post-decoherence-selection-programme]] after an outer review showed that Aharonov & Gruss (2005) link the destiny vector to "the free choice of the agent" and apply it to cerebral quantum measurements. The 2026-07-13 review then listed this sentence under "Map's-Own-Synthesis Phrases — NOT touched (critical guard honored)", which approved it as written. **Verified at arXiv full text** (quant-ph/0507269v1): "consider the case that the extra degree of freedom of an agent-system, the destiny vector, corresponds to what we refer to as the 'free choice of the agent'. This determines the outcomes of (cerebral) quantum measurements". The destiny vector is defined in the paper as the wave function "evolving from the final boundary condition backwards in time". **Resolution**: the page now credits the agency link to Aharonov & Gruss and keeps their conditional register ("consider the case in which"). The Map's claim is narrowed to the dualist reading, in which a non-physical consciousness, not the Universe's physical final boundary condition, supplies the boundary. The universal claim "no TSVF proponent" became "Aharonov and Gruss do not make that move", which can be checked. L92 "adds an ontological claim that TSVF's creators do not endorse" became "adds a *dualist* ontological claim", because Aharonov does endorse the agency link itself. Reference added.

- **Internal contradiction and calibration slippage in Bidirectional Interaction (L134).** The page said "Post-selection is precisely such influence: the choice of final state shapes what is physically real at intermediate times, as weak value experiments demonstrate." This contradicted L84–86 ("Post-selection does not *cause* the final state—it selects data after the fact … a method of conditional data analysis") and L76 ("the experiments do not adjudicate between [the realist and statistical readings]"). It presented the contested realist reading as an experimental result. A reviewer who accepts the tenets would still flag it. **Resolution**: "On the realist reading of TSVF, a post-selection boundary is such influence … the experiments themselves show only that weak values depend on the final state chosen, which the statistical reading treats as conditioning." "runs backward" became "would run backward".

- **Factually wrong single-world claim (L136).** The page said "Post-selection presupposes that exactly one outcome becomes actual … the framework is inherently single-world. Many-worlds eliminates the need for post-selection by actualizing every branch." TSVF's own developers contradict this. **Verified at arXiv full text** (Aharonov & Vaidman, updated review, quant-ph/0105101v2 §7): "The TSVF fits very well into the many-worlds interpretation (MWI) … the preferred interpretation of quantum theory of one of us (LV)". There, post-selection splits the world and "in each of these worlds the weak value will be that corresponding to the particular post-selection". Aharonov instead holds that "the backward evolving quantum state … exists! … This state corresponds to particular outcomes of all measurements in the future." The sibling [[time-symmetric-selection-mechanism]] L84 has stated since 2026-07-30 that "Vaidman, its principal developer, is himself an Everettian", so this page contradicted it. **Resolution**: the page now says the *proposal* is single-world by construction and the *formalism* is not. It reports the founders' split and states that the proposal needs Aharonov's reading, so the tenet chooses among readings of the formalism rather than following from it. This matches the "conditional consistency, not support" form the corpus uses in its No Many Worlds paragraphs (e.g. [[episodic-memory]] L178). The 2026-03-29 stability note ("MWI defenders will always find the single-world framing unsatisfying") is untouched. The tenet stands. What was corrected is a factual claim about the physics offered in its support, which §2.4 step 9 keeps open to review. Engagement remains **Mode Three**, and the boundary is now marked more honestly.

- **TSVF credited to the wrong paper (L46).** The page said "the two-state vector formalism developed by Aharonov, Bergmann, and Lebowitz (1964)". The ABL abstract (OpenAlex, publisher text) argues that measurement's time-asymmetry "is actually related to the manner in which statistical ensembles are constructed", and notes as a by-product that the later state may be assigned "with as much justification" as the earlier one. The two-vector description comes from Aharonov & Vaidman (1990, *PRA* 41, 11–20): "Two wave functions, the first preselected by the initial measurement and the second postselected by the final measurement, describe quantum systems at a single time." **Resolution**: the page now uses the corpus's corrected form from [[time-symmetric-selection-mechanism]] L72: formulated by Aharonov and Vaidman (1990), building on ABL's (1964) time-symmetric treatment of measurement. Reference added.

### Medium Issues Found

- **Wrong paper credited for realising AAV, and an unsupported claim of metrological advantage (L70).** The page said "Aharonov, Albert, and Vaidman's original 1988 proposal has been realised in optical systems, where anomalous weak values produce measurable beam deflections far exceeding what standard measurement allows (Hosten & Kwiat, 2008)." Ritchie et al. (1991) is the realisation. Its abstract reads "We present the first realization of a measurement of a 'weak value' … translates the centroid of the beam by a distance far larger than the birefringence-induced separation". Hosten & Kwiat (2008) is a later metrology application ("enhance the original displacement by nearly four orders of magnitude, attaining sensitivity to displacements of approximately 1 angstrom"). "Far exceeding what standard measurement allows" is unsupported, and whether weak-value amplification beats standard estimation is itself disputed. **Resolution**: the sentence now credits Ritchie for the realisation and gives Hosten & Kwiat's figures as they report them.
- **A calculation described as a measurement (L72).** The page said "Weak measurements on pre- and post-selected ensembles revealed…" of Aharonov et al. (2002), which is a gedanken analysis. Its abstract: "an exact numerical coincidence between a) a pair of classically contradictory assertions … and b) the results of weak measurements". **Resolution**: "Their calculated weak values coincide exactly with the paradox's classically contradictory assertions while remaining mutually consistent." This keeps Lundeen & Steinberg (2009) as the experimental confirmation, and their abstract supports that ("the resulting weak probabilities verify all of these statements but, at the same time, resolve the paradox").
- **Overstatement of Lundeen et al. (2011) (L74).** The page said "a result previously thought impossible since wavefunctions are not observables". The paper claims something narrower (arXiv:1112.3575 abstract): "directly measuring the transverse spatial wavefunction of a single photon, a task not previously realized by any method", against a background in which "scientists determine the wavefunction through tomographic methods". **Resolution**: the sentence is restated in the paper's own terms.
- **Leftover "retained" wording (L132).** "The post-selection condition determines which evolution is retained" was flagged by the 2026-03-29 pessimistic review (L115) and never fixed. It also implies the same "discard the alternatives" picture that the 2026-08-03 pass removed from L136. **Resolution**: "constrains which history becomes actual" (0 words).
