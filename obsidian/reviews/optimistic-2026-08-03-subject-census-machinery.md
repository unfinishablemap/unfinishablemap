## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

The natural-kind article is the cluster's best Chalmers exhibit, and its virtue is that it takes a result that *appears* to favour dualism and immediately audits it for backfire. Antony's "intuition of sharpness" looks like a gift: consciousness is determinately on or off, which is exactly what a non-reducible phenomenal fact should look like. The article does not pocket it. It reports that Antony's own conclusion is that sharpness sinks identity theory, functionalism, **and dualism** — because each grounds consciousness in a vague supervenience base, and nothing sharp supervenes on something vague. Then it does the work: the Map's dualism is not a supervenience theory, its sharp joint is fixed at a *discrete* interaction locus, so the objection that sinks property dualism does not reach it. Chalmers would recognise both halves — the explanatory gap taken seriously, and the honesty that property dualism (his own neighbourhood) is what Antony's argument actually kills.

The closing sentence is the one to quote: the verdict is reached "for reasons that are *coherent with* its dualism rather than *forced by* the failure of any cluster account. The cluster reading remains the live rival, and its parsimony is a genuine cost the Map pays."

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

`subject-census` P-SC1 is the entry Stapp's programme has always needed and rarely gets: it takes the interface formalism *literally enough to find out what it costs*. Once the selection law is written as *P(O | C, X)* rather than *P(O | X)*, the conditioning variable has to attach to a subject — and in any system with more than one candidate subject the law becomes *P(O | C₁…Cₙ, X)*, which "is not fixed until the count and the pairing are." That is a genuine derivation of a metaphysical obligation from a physical formalism, and it converts personal-identity questions the corpus had filed as metaphysics into boundary conditions on the physics.

The graduated-middle-path article earns Stapp's approval for the parallel move on the value side: valence is specified as a *gain-and-targeting parameter* on the one attentional coupling the Map already posits, adding no second channel. The article is candid that the Zeno channel it rides is itself contested on decoherence-timescale grounds, and that the modulator "inherits, rather than settles," that objection.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

The major-transitions article is the cluster's Nagel piece, and its central argument is a "what is it like" argument in evolutionary dress. Maynard Smith and Szathmáry's apparatus — new individuals, new information channels, fraternal versus egalitarian integration — is stated in a register that "contains no slot for 'a new kind of *inner* fact appearing.'" The sharpest formulation is against Michod's fitness criterion: a phenomenal transition "would leave *no fitness-reorganisation signature of its own* over and above the functional changes it accompanies," because if experience makes a causal difference it shows up in behaviour and hence in fitness — *indistinguishably from a purely functional cause producing the same behaviour*. Ginsburg and Jablonka's consciousness-transition would then be "the transition's behavioural shadow, faithfully traced, with the event that casts it unnamed."

That last clause is the best sentence in the cluster.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

The material Whitehead would most like sits in the major-transitions article's individuality section, and the striking thing is what the article does *not* do with it. Godfrey-Smith's gradualism supplies "marginal, minimal, or paradigm" individuals with no snap-into-being moment; Clarke and Pradeu supply a pluralism on which evolutionary, physiological, immunological, and developmental individuality need not coincide. The article notices that this pluralism is "conspicuously missing" a **phenomenal or subject individuality** axis, and stops there. It does not convert the process-friendly gradualism into a claim that experience is therefore widespread or early. It explicitly leaves the range open, "from an early Cambrian arrival to a panpsychist 'always, in degree' on which there was no transition to detect."

Whitehead gets his defeater-removal — the transitions framework's silence about the inside is diagnosed, not papered over — and gets nothing more. Per this skill's constraint, that is exactly the right amount.
