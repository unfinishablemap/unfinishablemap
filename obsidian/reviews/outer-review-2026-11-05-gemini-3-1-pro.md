# Referee Report: Metaphysical and Empirical Viability of "The Unfinishable Map"

The philosophical manuscript presented across the domain unfinishablemap.org constitutes an expansive and highly systematic attempt to resurrect interactionist-dualism by anchoring it to contemporary quantum mechanics, cognitive neuroscience, and causal modeling. The site’s governing ambition is to demonstrate that a non-reductive account of consciousness—one in which subjective experience exerts a genuine, productive, and irreducible causal influence upon the physical brain—can survive both the conceptual rigors of analytic philosophy and the empirical constraints of 2020s neuroscience. Specifically, the manuscript attempts to operationalize this interaction through two foundational pillars: "Minimal Quantum Interaction," which posits that consciousness exploits the ontological gaps in objective wave-function collapse to steer neural outcomes without violating Born rule statistics, and "Bidirectional Interaction," which insists that this quantum steering generates macroscopic cognitive effects that are functionally distinct from unconscious processing.

However, a forensic audit of the manuscript’s empirical claims, literature integration, and logical inferences reveals that the project is structurally compromised. The manuscript systematically insulates its core metaphysical commitments from the very scientific literature it purports to synthesize. This insulation is achieved through a pattern of severe empirical misrepresentations, the selective bracketing of fatal counter-evidence, the misappropriation of formal causal frameworks, and profound category errors regarding neuroscientific taxonomy. Rather than engaging the physicalist and neuroscientific consensus on its actual terms, the site gerrymanders the empirical record, utilizing outdated physical models when convenient and weaponizing methodological nuances against physicalist rivals while excusing identical vulnerabilities within its own dualist architecture.

The ensuing critique dissects the manuscript across five critical domains of failure. It examines the site's reliance on a falsified variant of quantum gravity phenomenology; its fatal misunderstanding of the limitations of non-reductive interventionist causal models; its internal contradictions regarding the taxonomy of conscious versus unconscious processing (specifically concerning the blindsight literature); its epistemologically hostile framing of adversarial collaborations in structural neuroscience; and its scientifically illiterate conflation of motor output bottlenecks with the causal bandwidth of phenomenal experience.

## The Falsification of Gravity-Induced Collapse and the Ad Hoc Boundary

To secure a mechanism for interactionist-dualism that does not explicitly violate the causal closure of the physical domain, the manuscript requires a physical ontology wherein quantum superpositions collapse objectively, independently of human observation, but with sufficient stochastic openness to allow consciousness to exert a token-level selection pressure. The manuscript locates this mechanism in the objective collapse theories, strongly favoring the gravity-induced wave function collapse model originally proposed by Roger Penrose and Lajos Diósi. At the URL [[penrose-gravity-induced-collapse-empirical-prospects]], the manuscript argues that the incompatibility between quantum superposition and general relativity provides a "natural quantum-classical boundary with nothing fitted to the data". The site explicitly contrasts the Diósi-Penrose (DP) model with competing spontaneous collapse models like GRW and CSL, criticizing the latter for introducing "phenomenological parameters—a collapse rate and a localisation width—chosen to match observation rather than derived from deeper physics". The DP model, the site argues, is vastly superior for dualist interaction because "gravity itself determines when superpositions become untenable", calculating the decay time of a superposed state simply as $\tau \approx \hbar / E_{\Delta}$, where $E_{\Delta}$ is the gravitational self-energy of the difference between the two mass distributions.

This argument is the linchpin of the site’s physical ontology, yet it relies upon a deliberate suppression of the most devastating empirical finding against the DP model in contemporary physics. In 2020 and 2021, a team led by Sandro Donadi published the results of a dedicated underground experiment at the Gran Sasso laboratory, designed specifically to test the parameter-free version of the gravity-related wave function collapse model. The theoretical mechanics of the DP model dictate that the spontaneous collapse of the wave function—driven by the gravitational self-energy of the difference between superposed mass distributions—must be accompanied by a specific signature of momentum diffusion. For charged particles, this continuous spatial localization and momentum diffusion inevitably results in the spontaneous emission of radiation, specifically in the X-ray spectrum.

The Gran Sasso experiment measured the radiation emission rate of germanium crystals to establish a lower bound on the effective size of the mass density of nuclei. The results were unequivocal and highly publicized within the physics community: the expected spontaneous X-ray emission predicted by the parameter-free DP model was entirely absent. Donadi et al. (2021) concluded that their findings set a bound three orders of magnitude larger than previous constraints, explicitly ruling out the natural parameter-free version of the Diósi-Penrose model.

The manuscript’s handling of this fatal falsification is academically unacceptable. Within isolated research notes (e.g., [[consciousness-measurement-problem-2026-01-16]]), the site briefly concedes that the model "has been experimentally constrained (Donadi et al. 2021)". However, the primary theoretical pages intended to establish the site's metaphysical architecture proceed as if the natural parameter-free boundary remains a scientifically viable foundation for dualist interaction.

The philosophical implications of this omission destroy the manuscript's architectural justification. To save the DP model from the Gran Sasso null results and the associated ultraviolet (UV) divergence problem, physicists are forced to introduce a finite smearing length (often denoted $r_C$) or a coarse-grained mass density, radically altering the spatial spectrum of the noise field. Once this ad hoc smearing parameter is introduced, the DP model loses the exact theoretical virtue the manuscript praises it for: it is no longer a natural boundary derived purely from the incompatibility of general relativity and quantum mechanics, but rather just another phenomenological model with parameters "fitted to the data" to avoid predicting enormous and unobserved spontaneous heating.

By obscuring the fact that the Diósi-Penrose model now requires the exact same ad hoc parameter tuning that the site decries in GRW and CSL models, the manuscript presents a sanitized, obsolete version of quantum gravity phenomenology. The core claim that gravity provides a boundary "with nothing fitted to the data" is demonstrably false against the 2020s literature. The site’s reliance on this falsified purity to anchor its dualist physics—claiming that "gravity itself determines when superpositions become untenable"—is empirically bankrupt and logically insupportable.

## Causal Exclusion, Interventionism, and the Epiphenomenalist Trap

Having established a highly questionable physical boundary at the quantum level, the manuscript attempts to scale this interaction up to macroscopic cognitive behavior. To defend mental causation against Jaegwon Kim’s canonical Causal Exclusion Argument, the site deploys recent work in interventionist causal modeling. Specifically, at the URL [[interventionist-and-counterfactual-dualism]], the manuscript relies extensively on Bram Vaassen’s 2024 paper in the *Australasian Journal of Philosophy*, "Mental Causation for Standard Dualists".

Kim’s exclusion argument posits that if the physical domain is causally closed, every physical event (including bodily behavior) has a sufficient physical cause. Therefore, any distinct mental cause would systematically overdetermine the behavior, which is absurd. The manuscript argues that an interventionist account, operating in the difference-making tradition of James Woodward, provides an escape route that vindicates mental causation within a standard dualist ontology. Under this framework, mental states qualify as genuine causes because they serve as "difference-making variables" or "points of control" for behavior, regardless of whatever energy the physical supervenience base transfers. The manuscript confidently asserts that since "difference-making is not energy transfer," the causal closure of the physical domain—read strictly as a claim about energy or forces—is left untouched. This ostensibly allows both the non-physical experience and its physical base to operate as genuine co-causes without generating problematic overdetermination.

This argument constitutes a profound and fatal misappropriation of the interventionist literature, achieved by aggressively ignoring the devastating mathematical critiques leveled against non-reductive interventionism by Michael Baumgartner (2020). Baumgartner has meticulously demonstrated that standard interventionism, contrary to the hopes of non-reductive physicalists and standard dualists, inevitably yields macro-to-micro epiphenomenalism.

The core of Woodward’s manipulability theory requires a strict condition for assessing causal efficacy: to establish a variable $X$ (the mental state) as a cause of $Y$ (the behavior), one must be able to theoretically intervene on $X$ while holding all other causes of $Y$ fixed. However, if a mental property $M$ is metaphysically necessitated by, or supervenes upon, a physical base $P$ (which itself is a cause of $Y$), it is logically and physically impossible to intervene on $M$ while holding $P$ fixed. A change in the supervening mental state logically requires a change in the physical subvenient base. Because the interventionist holding-fixed condition cannot be satisfied, mental properties fail the test for causal efficacy entirely, rendering them epiphenomenal.

The site attempts to bypass this mathematical blockade by uncritically adopting Vaassen’s proposed solution—a highly controversial modification to the interventionist axioms (specifically "Intervention IV") that explicitly exempts variables standing in a metaphysical necessitation relation from the holding-fixed requirement. What the manuscript fails to acknowledge, and what severely undermines its entire interactionist project, is that this ad-hoc exemption strips causation of its productive power, leaving the dualist entirely exposed to the "oomph" objection.

By redefining causation as mere counterfactual dependence stripped of productive necessitation, the site achieves a purely semantic victory that contradicts its own central tenets. The manuscript claims, under its "Bidirectional Interaction" and "Minimal Quantum Interaction" tenets, that consciousness actively *steers* physical systems, "settling which outcome is actual" at quantum indeterminacies. This is an assertion of robust, productive power—a claim that consciousness is doing actual ontological work to collapse the wave function in a specific direction.

Yet, the site simultaneously leans on an interventionist framework designed specifically to decouple causation from physical productivity and energy transfer. The manuscript cannot logically maintain that consciousness exerts a robust, reality-defining selection pressure on quantum states while simultaneously defending its causal efficacy via a lightweight, dependence-based interventionist model that explicitly denies the necessity of physical "oomph". If mental causation is reduced to a counterfactual abstraction where the mind makes a "difference" only because the causal modeling rules have been mathematically gerrymandered to ignore the absolute sufficiency of the physical base, then the site's interactionist dualism is hollowed out. This theoretical incoherence is masked by selective citation and a steadfast refusal to test the proposed causal mechanisms against Baumgartner’s rigorous exclusionary proofs.

## The Blindsight Contradiction and the Evaporation of the Functionality Gap

To argue that consciousness is not merely an epiphenomenal byproduct of neural computation, the manuscript attempts to establish a rigid "Functionality Gap" between conscious and unconscious processing. At the URL [[conscious-vs-unconscious-processing]], the site asserts that unconscious processing follows strictly "fixed stimulus-response mappings" and is fundamentally incapable of durable information maintenance, novel combinations, and spontaneous intentional action.

The primary empirical anchor for this sweeping taxonomy is blindsight—the neurological phenomenon whereby patients with primary visual cortex (V1) damage demonstrate above-chance visual discrimination (e.g., detecting motion or orientation) while purportedly lacking all phenomenal visual experience in their blind field. The site presents blindsight as a clean double-dissociation, stating definitively that in the absence of consciousness, information "guides automatic responses through fixed pathways but cannot be deployed voluntarily," and that blindsight reveals "consciousness isn't a passive observer of processing that would occur anyway".

The fatal flaw in this argument is that the site’s own pages contain the very empirical evidence that destroys the "Functionality Gap," yet the manuscript deliberately fails to synthesize or resolve the contradiction.

At the URL [[blindsight]], the text briefly acknowledges Ian Phillips's (2021) seminal paper in *Psychological Review*, which fundamentally dismantles the orthodox interpretation of blindsight. Phillips utilizes Signal Detection Theory (SDT) to demonstrate that blindsight is not unconscious vision, but rather qualitatively degraded conscious vision combined with a highly conservative response bias. Because their primary visual cortex is damaged, blindsight patients receive extremely weak, noisy, and degraded visual signals. These degraded phenomenal experiences fall below the patients' subjective reporting threshold, leading them to claim they see nothing, even though a conscious, albeit severely blurry, signal is present.

If Phillips (2021) is correct—and the 2020s psychophysical literature increasingly supports the degraded-consciousness interpretation utilizing SDT models—then blindsight ceases to be an example of robust unconscious processing. The philosophical and empirical consequences of this for the manuscript are catastrophic. If the reason blindsight patients cannot engage in spontaneous intentional action, durable working memory maintenance, or flexible reasoning regarding stimuli in their blind field is simply that their visual signal is massively degraded and noisy, then their cognitive deficits provide absolutely zero evidence that *unconscious processing inherently lacks these capabilities*.

| Manuscript's Foundational Claim | The SDT / Phillips (2021) Falsification |
| --- | --- |
| Blindsight patients cannot use visual information for spontaneous intentional action because the information is strictly *unconscious*. | Blindsight patients cannot use the information for complex reasoning because the signal is consciously *degraded* to the point of near-uselessness, not because it lacks phenomenal character. |
| Blindsight serves as the ultimate control condition proving unconscious processing relies entirely on "fixed pathways". | If blindsight is actually degraded conscious vision, the manuscript lacks any valid empirical control group to define the absolute limits of unconscious computation. |
| The "Functionality Gap" proves consciousness is causally necessary for novel combinations. | The gap merely proves that high-fidelity, high-signal-to-noise neural processing is necessary for novel combinations. |

The manuscript attempts to have it both ways through academic compartmentalization. In its dedicated blindsight page, it treats Phillips's thesis as a respectable alternative interpretation to bracket the debate. But in its core architectural arguments regarding the causal necessity of consciousness (`conscious-vs-unconscious-processing`), it reverts entirely to the orthodox, 1970s-era Weiskrantz interpretation of blindsight as a pure "zombie" process devoid of qualia. A rigorous academic manuscript cannot build a definitive, load-bearing taxonomy of unconscious limitations upon an empirical phenomenon that the current literature heavily suggests is actually conscious. By isolating the Phillips (2021) challenge and refusing to allow it to revise the "Functionality Gap" thesis, the site engages in blatant empirical cherry-picking to protect its dualist commitments.

## Epistemological Gerrymandering in Adversarial Neuroscience Collaborations

The manuscript devotes significant rhetorical energy to attacking physicalist structuralism, particularly targeting Integrated Information Theory (IIT) and Global Neuronal Workspace Theory (GNWT). At the URL [[the-unfolding-argument-against-causal-structure-theories-of-consciousness]], the site leverages the Unfolding Argument (Doerig et al., 2019) to argue that theories identifying consciousness with internal causal structure independently of outward function are mathematically vulnerable. To bolster this narrative of physicalist crisis, the site points to the recent adversarial collaborations in consciousness science, specifically focusing on the COGITATE Consortium's highly anticipated 2025 publication in *Nature*.

The site frames the COGITATE experiment as a profound empirical disappointment that "confirmed the pre-paradigm state rather than resolving it," implying that physicalist theories are stagnant and generating an explanatory void that only interactionist-dualism can fill. The manuscript argues that GNWT explicitly failed its predictions regarding prefrontal cortex (PFC) ignition, particularly at the offset of a visual stimulus, and uses this purported failure to suggest the theory is crumbling under rigorous experimental scrutiny.

This framing is highly deceptive and relies on the deliberate omission of critical 2025 literature that contextualizes the COGITATE findings. The site completely ignores the published rebuttals and methodological clarifications from the GNWT theorists themselves. As detailed extensively by Naccache et al. (2025) in *Neuroscience of Consciousness*, the absence of an offset ignition in the COGITATE data does not constitute a failure of GNWT predictions because of the specific experimental design utilized by the consortium.

The COGITATE experiment deliberately employed a "no report" condition, presenting participants with *task-irrelevant* non-target stimuli for long and variable durations (500, 1000, or 1500 ms) in order to prevent post-perceptual processing artifacts. GNWT, as formulated by Dehaene and colleagues since 1998, only predicts a second wave of PFC ignition at stimulus offset *if the participant is consciously attending to that offset event*. Because the participants had no task-driven motivation to attend to the disappearance of the long-duration, task-irrelevant stimuli, systematic awareness of the offset was highly unlikely. Consequently, the absence of prefrontal offset ignition is exactly what the GNWT model would predict under these specific boundary conditions.

The site’s handling of this literature reveals a deeply hostile and asymmetric review standard. When the dualist Diósi-Penrose model is explicitly falsified by Donadi et al. (2021) at Gran Sasso, the site retains the gravitational boundary as a core tenet, quietly accepting the ad hoc introduction of smearing parameters in isolated research notes. But when physicalist theories like GNWT yield complex, methodologically nuanced results in an adversarial collaboration, the site immediately declares them bankrupt, stripping away all methodological context regarding task-relevance and attention. The manuscript weaponizes the COGITATE findings to clear the field for its interactionist metaphysics, but it does so by pretending that the physicalist response to the data—which robustly defends the integrity of the GNWT architecture against the specific "no report" paradigm used—does not exist.

## Category Errors: Conflating Phenomenal Bandwidth with Motor Bottlenecks

Perhaps the most glaring neuroscientific illiteracy in the entire manuscript is found at the URL [[bandwidth-of-consciousness]]. In an attempt to quantify the "interface-friction" between the non-physical mind and the physical brain, the site cites a 2025 paper by Zheng & Meister. The site states that "overall cognitive throughput sits near ~10 bits/second despite sensory input arriving orders of magnitude faster," and explicitly claims, "The Map glosses this slow inner rate as a candidate measure of consciousness's causal bandwidth". The manuscript proceeds to link this ~10 bits/s limit to working memory capacity limitations, arguing it reflects a fundamental constraint on the interaction between consciousness and matter.

This constitutes a catastrophic category error that unravels the manuscript's internal logic. Zheng & Meister (2025) did not measure the "causal bandwidth of consciousness," nor did they measure the richness of phenomenal experience or the capacity of internal cognitive manipulation. They explicitly measured the information rate of *overt behavioral report*—specifically physiological output tasks like speech, typing, and motor tracking. The ~10 bits/second limit is a well-documented physiological bottleneck of the mammalian motor and speech output systems, constrained by muscle latency and descending corticospinal tract bandwidth, not a metric of phenomenal richness.

To claim that because a human being can only type or speak at ~10 bits per second, their internal conscious experience is restricted to a ~10 bits/second "causal interface" is to completely misunderstand the difference between cognitive processing and motor execution. The visual field alone contains massive amounts of structural information that is phenomenally present simultaneously. Furthermore, the site's own architectural reliance on Endel Tulving's taxonomy of consciousness highlights this absurdity. The manuscript explicitly endorses Tulving's concept of *autonoetic consciousness*—the capacity for mental time travel, allowing a subject to vividly re-experience past episodic memories with rich phenomenal grounding.

It is mathematically and phenomenologically impossible to compress the rich, immersive, multi-modal experience of autonoetic mental time travel into a 10 bits/second bottleneck. To reduce the holistic unity of autonoetic and phenomenal consciousness to a metric derived from the biomechanics of finger-tapping and vocal cord modulation is scientifically absurd.

The site attempts to dress this up as an "interpretive move belonging to the Map's coupling law research", but one cannot merely "interpret" a measurement of muscle-bound motor output as a measurement of quantum-mind coupling. This error fundamentally infects the site’s working memory arguments. The text claims that passive maintenance operates without effort while active manipulation is effortful because manipulation involves this 10 bits/s "interface-friction". Yet, the literature on predictive processing and active inference—which the site explicitly marginalizes—routinely accounts for the phenomenology of effort through precision-weighting and metabolic constraints on network switching (e.g., transitioning between the Default Mode Network and Executive Control Network) without ever needing to posit a dualist quantum bottleneck. By anchoring its metaphysical architecture to a profound misreading of Zheng & Meister (2025), the site demonstrates a fundamental inability to accurately parse the scientific literature it claims to transcend, severely compromising its credibility.
