## Executive Summary

This is the Map's most epistemically disciplined wing. The cluster has invented, in its own prose, every restraint it needs: an exposition-before-appropriation firewall, an axis-by-axis independence audit, a common-cause-null discount that it applies *against* its own case, and a source-politics audit that names its own attribution asymmetry. The Hardline Empiricist persona finds more to praise here than anywhere else in the catalogue, because the cluster repeatedly performs the restraint that matters most — it declines to convert a tradition's agreement with the Map into evidence for the Map.

The one calibration concern is not that the discipline is missing but that **it is written and not propagated**, and it fails in a single predictable place: the *Relation to Site Perspective* sections. Article bodies mark their appropriations and decline their upgrades; the tenet sections then revert to the support register — the failure the Map's own tenets page already names as *alignment-line inheritance*. Seven verified loci fall into two families: independence claims the Map's own common-cause audit has already discounted (including one same-article contradiction six lines wide and one live meta description), and Tenet 4 recruiting soteriology as metaphysical indexicality in two different traditions' articles while a third has the correcting sentence written. Every fix is a clause-level swap, and in all but one case the corrected wording already exists elsewhere in the wing.

## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

The hub does what almost no comparative survey does: it refuses to let the hard problem be a Western parochialism *and* refuses to claim universality for it. The Chinese section is the proof. Rather than quietly omitting the tradition that does not generate the gap, the hub names it as "a genuine challenge" and draws the consequence explicitly (`the-hard-problem-in-non-western-philosophy` L100):

> "one cannot count traditions that share the framework as independent evidence while dismissing traditions that reject the framework as insufficiently attentive."

Chalmers would recognise this as the honest form of his own position: the gap is real, and its reality is not established by counting heads.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

Stapp would appreciate the restraint in the Minimal Quantum Interaction paragraphs, which is where comparative work usually overreaches. The hub declines the easy anachronism (L152): Sāṃkhya's reflection model "shares a thematic concern" with the Map, but "the underlying structures differ substantially (pre-scientific metaphor versus quantum measurement)." The African article makes the same refusal sharper (`african-philosophy-of-consciousness` L59): the structural parallel with interactionist dualism "is a comparison rather than a connection — the Akan framework predates quantum mechanics and is independent of it."

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

The Indigenous Australian article contains the best sentence in the wing on what a tradition can and cannot be made to say (`indigenous-australian-philosophy-of-consciousness` L79):

> "That is not agreement with the Map's answer; it is a tradition that declines the Map's question."

Nagel's point was that the subjective standpoint is not eliminable by objective description. This article extends it: a *tradition's* standpoint is not eliminable by comparative redescription either. The article then acts on it, refusing the co-optation in terms (L77): "Reading Aboriginal thought as a vindication of the Map's dualism would be exactly the co-optation the Map guards against."

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

Whitehead has genuine material here — Buddhist momentariness as process metaphysics predating him by millennia, Mulla Sadra's processual soul, Nahua *teotl*, the Bantu force ontology in which force is not an attribute of being but what being is. The cluster presents all of it without the crude-dualism reading Whitehead objected to.

Critically, and to the cluster's credit, **this praise is nowhere cashed out as a tier-upgrade**. The African article makes the refusal explicit where it would have been easiest to slide (L75): the Bantu force ontology "echoes panpsychist intuitions," but a rank ordering "supplies no mechanism by which lower-level experiences compose into a higher-level one," and an ontology positing no micro-subjects "is then not the panpsychism the comparison invokes." The resonance is named and then declined as support. That is the honest pattern.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

Nyāya is handled with real care in both the hub and the convergence article: a non-material self exercising causal control through sense organs, articulated as agent causation independently of the Western tradition. Kane would note approvingly that the cluster does not pretend this settles anything — `cross-cultural-convergence-on-mental-causation` L93 concedes immediately that "Buddhist traditions complicate this picture — *anattā* denies an enduring agent while preserving moral responsibility through different metaphysics."

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

Al-Ghazālī's cognitive-closure parallel is drawn precisely and without inflation (hub L76): human cognition "has structural limits it cannot transcend from within." The cluster's deepest mysterian move, though, is methodological — `cross-cultural-convergence-on-mental-causation` L83:

> "Contact between traditions can be researched; the shared channel cannot be researched away by any amount of historical work."

This is a genuine epistemological insight, cleanly stated: one class of confound is empirically tractable and one is not, and no amount of historical scholarship converts the second into the first. It deserves to be quoted back at the rest of the catalogue.

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

**This persona's verdict is the review's centrepiece, and it is overwhelmingly positive.** The cluster performs tenet-as-evidence-upgrade restraint at a level found nowhere else in the Map.

**What is praise-worthily *not* done.** The African article had every structural opportunity to collect a non-Western vote for dualism and explicitly declined, four separate times:

- On Yoruba *ori* (L65): "What *ori* implies about consciousness is a further step, and one the Map takes rather than finds."
- On the Akan interface reading (L59): "a Map reconstruction rather than Gyekye's position."
- On ancestors (L79): a status "that can be forfeited by behaving badly is a normative position within a community, not a report on what consciousness is."
- On the whole convergence (L93): "What the convergence supplies is context ... not additional confirmation."

Most impressive is that the article gives its strongest counter-case a full paragraph rather than a footnote. Wiredu's quasi-physicalist reading of *okra* and *sunsum* — which, if right, removes the Akan material from the dualist column entirely — is set out at length (L83) and the conclusion drawn against interest: what the material yields is "a standing dispute between dualist and naturalist reconstructions, not a non-Western vote for dualism that the Map can simply collect."

And then the governing principle, at L97:

> "deciding it by which answer suits the tenets would resolve a live scholarly dispute by convenience."

That sentence is the Hardline Empiricist's own discipline, stated better than this persona would state it.

**The five-tier scale is honoured in substance.** Per `project/evidential-status-discipline.md` L71 the tier labels are editor-internal and deliberately not exposed in article prose, so their absence is compliance rather than defect. The natural-language equivalents are doing the work throughout: "candidate lineage, not an established one," "near-independence," "unsettled," "does not establish dualism specifically."

**The independence audit is exemplary.** `cross-cultural-convergence-on-mental-causation` grades axis by axis instead of asserting independence wholesale (L77–L83). The Indo-Greek analysis is real historical reasoning: Alexander reached Bactria in the 320s BCE, *after* Plato's death, so the self-moving-soul argument cannot have been shaped by that channel — but the later Indian systematisation does overlap it, hence "near-independence" rather than independence. The West African strand is held open in both directions, and the article volunteers a coarse-graining objection against its own structure (L81): joining Akan and Ubuntu into one lineage "would be a convenience of the article's structure, not a finding about their history."

**The common-cause null is applied against the Map's own case.** P-M2's standing rule — a convergence counts as one observation read N times until a distinguishing test passes — is quoted and then conceded (L83): "This article does not claim to have passed such a test." The article also separates what the discount does and does not remove (L85): the epiphenomenalism reply "survives this discount, because it does not depend on the count."

**Two dedicated anti-inflation sections exist in finished form.** Both convergence articles carry a *Methodology, Data, and Framework Commitment Are Not Three Independent Routes* section establishing that the Map's own framework is the interpretive lens, not a third confirmation. `cross-traditional-convergence-on-consciousness-irreducibility` L154 states the conclusion flatly: "It does not establish dualism specifically."

**The hostile-tradition case is handled best of all.** `buddhism-and-dualism` is the wing's model, because Buddhist no-self is prima facie hostile to the Map's commitments and the article never buys compatibility cheaply. It marks its own thesis as one reading among several (L42). It quotes a sibling's anti-conscription rule *against itself* on Yogācāra (L83: "the Map must not conscript it as evidence for its own position"). It declines a self-serving *gzhan stong* reading and gives the primary-text citation for why (L117, *Mūlamadhyamakakārikā* ch. 3). It names a cheap compatibility it refuses (L129: restricted dualism "buys compatibility cheaply and cannot pay for Tenets 2 and 3"). And it concedes a tenet outright rather than repairing it (L158): "**Not compatible**—and the Map marks the disagreement as bedrock rather than repairing it."

Its convergence sentence is the one the rest of the wing should inherit (L70):

> "This is suggestive support, not independent corroboration: as convergent phenomenological evidence is honest about, all human introspection shares one instrument, so the circle is narrowed rather than broken."

And its principled answer to the obvious charge of selective appropriation — why the Map may drop the permanent self without dropping phenomenal experience — is the strongest paragraph in the wing (L119): "phenomenal evidence has a different epistemic standing than theoretical posits about what underlies it."

**Where the Process Philosopher and the Hardline Empiricist converge.** The Chinese counter-example is repurposed as a natural experiment (`cross-traditional-convergence-on-consciousness-irreducibility` L135): traditions sharing the human-introspection architecture but starting from different conceptual frameworks produce different outputs, so the convergence is "conceptually-conditional rather than architecturally-determined." This is an original epistemological move that earns weight *by* taking the disconfirming case seriously. Both personas can praise it without conflict — which, per this skill's own test, is the signature of an article that has resolved the tension honestly.

**Where they conflict — see the calibration concern below.**

## Content Strengths

### `chinese-philosophy-of-mind.md` — the firewall template

- **Strongest point**: a two-part scope discipline — a lede pledge (L29) that the article "expounds that tradition on its own terms first, and only then—clearly separated—reads it through the Map's framework," enforced by a head-of-section marker at *Relation to Site Perspective* (L87): "This section is the Map's own interpretation and should not be read back into the Chinese sources above."
- **Notable quote**: "The Chinese case does not sit outside philosophy; it sits outside the basin." (L83)
- **Why it works**: the article narrows its own thesis to the defensible version rather than the flattering one (L61): "So a flat claim that Chinese thought never set spirit against body would be false." It then explains precisely why the Fan Zhen *Shenmie lun* controversy still is not the hard problem — the dispute was about *persistence*, not constitution, and "nobody asked why a bodily process should be accompanied by anything it is like at all." The qualification that most threatens the thesis is carried by the most recent specialist source in the wing (Wong 2025, *Dao* 24(1)).

### `indigenous-australian-philosophy-of-consciousness.md` — the source-politics audit

- **Strongest point**: it audits its own attribution asymmetry (L69) — the strongest "theory of consciousness" readings come from non-Indigenous comparative philosophers (Mathews, Rose), while Indigenous-authored material, Graham's above all, "is framed as ethics, Law, and relationality rather than as a thesis about consciousness." It even flags the *JCS* special issue it cites as "itself a Western academic framing, however sympathetically intended."
- **Why it works**: the underdetermination discipline is applied against the article's own Tenet 1 claim rather than around it (L75) — the ethical-relational reading "advances no thesis about how consciousness is distributed and is therefore compatible with physicalism, so the convergence with Tenet 1 holds only for the metaphysical readings, not for every reading surveyed here." It also declines a tenet where none applies (L77): Minimal Quantum Interaction "has no counterpart here and would be foreign to the tradition's frame."

### `african-philosophy-of-consciousness.md` — concession run to completion

- **Strongest point**: the Wiredu counter-case (L83), given a full paragraph and allowed to win the section.
- **Notable quote**: "deciding it by which answer suits the tenets would resolve a live scholarly dispute by convenience." (L97)
- **Why it works**: today's refine converted the article from a survey that collected traditions into one that grades them. The recurring "the Map takes rather than finds" construction marks every appropriation in place, so a reader can see exactly where exposition ends. Note also that its reference list now carries genuine specialist sources (Gyekye, Wiredu, Abimbola, Hountondji, Majeed 2013, Ikuenobe & Etieyibo 2020).

### `buddhism-and-dualism.md` — the hostile-tradition model

- **Strongest point**: it concedes Tenet 4 outright (L158) rather than manufacturing compatibility, and books the consequence in the positions register (P-I1/P-I2) — the only article in the Buddhist sub-cluster that cites `positions/` at all.
- **Notable quote**: "What licenses dropping the permanent self does not equally license dropping phenomenal experience, because phenomenal evidence has a different epistemic standing than theoretical posits about what underlies it." (L119)
- **Why it works**: it anticipates the charge of selective appropriation and answers it with a *principled* asymmetry rather than an ad hoc one, drawing the parallel to the Map's split verdict on eliminative materialism. It also marks which of its results survive on the epistemic reading only (L68, L152) — a discipline no other article in the wing performs.

### `buddhist-perspectives-on-meaning.md` — the best self-objection in the wing

- **Strongest point**: the *upādāna* challenge (L137) turns a Buddhist doctrine against the Map's **method** rather than its conclusions — the harder and more interesting objection. The Map's grounding project is read as "clinging dressed as metaphysics," and the reply at L139 correctly refuses to declare victory: "a framework-boundary disagreement, honestly noted, not a point either side refutes on the other's terms."
- **Notable quote**: "a foundation laid for a self that is only a conventional designation may be load-bearing for nothing." (L137)
- **Why it works**: writing the strongest available attack on your own methodology, and then declining to win, is rare. Note that this sits in the same article as Family B locus 7 — the body argues better than the tenet section.

### `cross-cultural-phenomenology-of-agency.md` — the functional/phenomenal firewall

- **Strongest point**: it limits its own evidence base up front (L59): "The Map's argument rests on the phenomenal invariants specifically; functional ones are expected on any account and carry no special weight for irreducibility."
- **Why it works**: the empirical move at L85 is the wing's most original — decades of contrary contemplative training treated as a natural experiment on whether the phenomenology is confabulated. "A Buddhist practitioner who has spent decades training the conviction that no self authors action still reports a qualitative difference between acting and being acted upon," with the functional confound explicitly separated ("efference copy still operates in Buddhist brains").

### `cross-cultural-convergence-on-mental-causation.md` — the calibration engine

- **Strongest point**: the axis-by-axis independence audit plus the *Coherence with Interactionist Dualism Is Not an Independent Route* section, which enumerates the rival framings compatible with the same data (property dualism, agent-causation libertarianism, Nyāya enduring-self dualism, certain Buddhist analyses) and concedes the convergence "does not discriminate among these without the further mechanism argument."
- **Why it works**: this is the only article in the wing carrying the full register apparatus — 7 `positions/` and 5 `project/` references (P-D3, P-M2, `common-cause-null`, `evidential-status-discipline`). It is the model the rest of the wing should be measured against.

## Calibration Concern (flagged, not praised)

Per this skill's design, where the Process Philosopher's praise would cash out as a tier-upgrade and the Hardline Empiricist objects, the report flags rather than praises, and generates a `refine-draft` rather than an expansion opportunity.

**The unifying diagnosis: the wing's expository prose received the discipline retrofit; its "Relation to Site Perspective" sections did not.** Every locus below sits in a tenet-alignment section. The bodies of these articles mark appropriations, name rival readings, and decline upgrades — and then the tenet section reverts to the support register. The Map's own tenets page already names this failure mode (`tenets/tenets.md` L175, *alignment-line inheritance*):

> "The alignment line records which tenets an article must not contradict; it does not license drawing on them as support, and a subsection covering a not-invoked cell should mark itself as coherence commentary."

Two families, both verified.

### Family A — independence assertions the Map's own audit has already discounted

The Map ran a focused audit — `topics/neoplatonist-common-cause-weight-for-cross-tradition-introspection-architecture-parallels` — which grades the Neoplatonist transmission channel for early Sufism at **moderate-to-substantial** (Evagrian *logismoi* prefigure *khawāṭir* by ~400 years; al-Kindī's Arabic Plotinus is contemporaneous with Muḥāsibī in mid-9th-century Baghdad), while clearing Advaita at *weak-to-negligible* and Stoicism at *weak*. Commissioning research that discounts your own convergence evidence, and publishing the verdict, is exemplary. The verdict then failed to reach the surfaces that assert the discounted independence.

Verified loci (a **sample**, not the population — the refiner must re-grep):

1. **`concepts/islamic-sufi-philosophy-of-consciousness.md` L37 vs L43 — a same-article contradiction six lines wide.** L37 (lede) asserts "Islamic philosophy's independent arrival at consciousness irreducibility through entirely different methods than Western analytic philosophy contributes to the broader cross-traditional convergence pattern." L43 carries the audit that grades exactly that strand *moderate-to-substantial*. The article contains its own correction and has not applied it. **Sharpest locus; this file has no open task.**
2. **`topics/the-hard-problem-in-non-western-philosophy.md` `description:`** — "Indian, Islamic, African, and Japanese traditions independently found consciousness irreducible." A nav surface, live in `hugo/content/` and syndicated to search and social cards, asserting flat independence and naming the Islamic strand specifically. The sibling `cross-traditional-convergence-on-consciousness-irreducibility` L136 pre-emptively names this error: "reading the Islamic (Ibn Sīnā) row as flatly independent overstates that strand's isolation."
3. **Hub L150** — "The cross-cultural convergence on irreducibility is the strongest support" for Dualism, against sibling L154: "It does not establish dualism specifically."
4. **Hub L158** — the Occam's paragraph counts depth of introspective investigation as weight, the move P-M2 discounts as one observation read N times.
5. **`islamic-sufi` L101** — "The tradition's strongest support for dualism," with no rival framing named that the same material also coheres with.

**Root cause, measured:** the hub carries **0** `positions/` and **0** `project/` references, despite being the wing's entry point and its most-linked article. It cites `cross-traditional-convergence` at L126 for the supportive *attractor* framing while leaving that sibling's *Engaging the Common-Cause Null* section unimported — a selective import of the pro-convergence half. `islamic-sufi` likewise carries 0 register references and predates the retrofit (`ai_system: claude-opus-4-6`, created 2026-03-08).

### Family B — Tenet 4 recruits soteriology as metaphysical indexicality

The same error appears in two tradition articles, on the same tenet, and the correcting sentence is already written in a third.

6. **`concepts/islamic-sufi-philosophy-of-consciousness.md` L107** — "Islamic philosophy's emphasis on the unity of the individual soul—its particular trajectory through the nafs stages, its unique relationship to God—is **fundamentally incompatible with observer-proliferation**." An unhedged modal claim about an interpretation of quantum mechanics, sourced from a soteriology of the soul.
7. **`topics/buddhist-perspectives-on-meaning.md` L163** — "The indexical significance the Map defends—why am I *this* consciousness?—**finds support** in Buddhism's focus on individual liberation through individual effort."

Locus 7 is flatly contradicted by its own sibling. `concepts/buddhism-and-dualism.md` L158 reaches the opposite verdict on the same tenet and the same tradition — "**Not compatible**—and the Map marks the disagreement as bedrock rather than repairing it" — and states the correction in one clause: "Buddhist urgency about *this* life is soteriological, not metaphysical indexicality." The tenets page independently records the Madhyamaka no-self analysis as "a genuine bedrock disagreement, not an in-framework defect the Map can refute." So `buddhist-perspectives-on-meaning` recruits as support the tradition the Map's own tenets page names as its bedrock opponent on that exact tenet.

Locus 6 is the same shape in Islamic dress: *nafs* stages and a singular destiny before God are soteriological commitments, and converting them into a verdict on Everettian branching is precisely the move L158 forbids.
