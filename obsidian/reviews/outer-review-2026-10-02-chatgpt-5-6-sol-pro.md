# Full-site external review of *The Unfinishable Map*

**Review snapshot:** live repository and public site as available on **2 October 2026**, with the changelog checked through its latest entry at **1 October 2026, 23:54 UTC**. This is a full audit of the site’s argument architecture, dependency structure and representative article layer. It is not a line-by-line certification of every citation across the several-hundred-page corpus. 

**Overall judgement: major revision.**

The Unfinishable Map has become considerably more rigorous than its headline thesis might suggest. Its best recent work openly records hidden premises, separates evidence from structural centrality, discounts common-cause “convergence,” concedes that no worked interface model exists, and withdraws arguments that recent external reviews showed to be invalid. Those are real methodological achievements.

The central problem is now different from the one an older review would have identified. The site’s **control plane**—the tenets commentary, positions registers, methodology pages and recent apex qualifications—is increasingly honest. But that honesty has not propagated reliably into the **content plane**: article descriptions, introductions, older concepts, evidential grades and cross-cluster syntheses often continue to make claims that the control plane has already weakened or abandoned.

The result is best characterised as a **sophisticated conditional research programme**, not yet as an empirically supported theory of mind–matter interaction. It shows that a certain family of dualist positions can be made more coherent than critics sometimes assume. It has not shown that the family is more probable than physicalist, neutral-monist, epiphenomenalist or non-interactionist alternatives.

The deepest choice now facing the project is this:

> Either consciousness makes a conditionally identifiable difference to physical outcomes, in which case the Map needs a risky psychophysical model; or consciousness is “causally authoritative” while every physical distribution remains exactly what it would otherwise have been, in which case the view is a metaphysical interpretation of physically complete data rather than an empirically supported mechanism.

At present, different clusters rely on different horns.

## 1. What the current site gets right

### 1.1 It now names much of its hidden architecture

The recently added background-commitments page acknowledges that the five stated tenets are not independent. They jointly depend on at least three additional posits:

1. a numerically determinate, persisting subject;
2. objective single-outcome actualisation;
3. global non-occurrence of unchosen alternatives.

It also correctly recognises that objective collapse removes the measurement problem as independent evidence for conscious selection, and that the rejection of many-worlds on agency grounds requires global exclusion rather than merely branch-local authorship. 

That is a substantial improvement. It prevents three formerly tempting but invalid inferences:

- definite outcomes therefore require consciousness;
- authorship therefore requires alternatives not to occur anywhere;
- several tenets mutually confirm one another merely because they are mutually congenial.

### 1.2 Its methodology register is unusually self-critical

The methodology-and-calibration register correctly separates:

- credence;
- external evidence;
- structural centrality;
- model maturity;
- empirical discriminability;
- framework-internal status.

It also explicitly warns that internal consistency only removes a defeater and does not constitute positive evidence, that multiple articles may be repetitions of one underlying datum, and that the site still lacks an enforcement mechanism ensuring that low-evidence upstream commitments constrain downstream prose. 

The last admission is particularly accurate. It describes the dominant failure found in this audit.

### 1.3 It has genuinely retracted or narrowed bad arguments

The latest changelog does not merely add qualifications around the edges. It withdraws several substantive overclaims:

- covert command-following no longer counts as a refutation of functionalism or neural identity;
- clinical misdiagnosis is separated from cognitive motor dissociation;
- Cruse 2011 is no longer treated as clean evidence after the Goldfine reanalysis;
- “probable consciousness” replaces stronger certainty language;
- filter-theory and IIT implications have been narrowed;
- behaviourally undetectable function is no longer confused with absence of function.

These corrections track the primary literature more faithfully. Bodien and colleagues found cognitive motor dissociation in 60 of 241 participants who showed no observable command response—25% of that subgroup—but this is evidence that bedside behaviour can miss internally implemented command-following, not evidence against functional organisation. [New England Journal of Medicine+1](https://www.nejm.org/doi/abs/10.1056/NEJMoa2400645)

### 1.4 Its strongest physics pages now state the real debt

The quantum-interface register assigns the preferred post-decoherence mechanism an external-evidence grade of D, concedes that exact Born preservation can render it indistinguishable from no mental influence, and records that there is no Hamiltonian, master equation, transition rule or simulation showing how intention couples to a neural quantum state. 

The Born-preserving apex goes further. It distinguishes unconditional marginals from intention-conditioned distributions and recognises that arbitrary reweighting of intentions destroys any conveniently balanced marginal unless either:

- the conditionals do not differ after all; or
- an additional law constrains which intention distributions are physically possible.

That is exactly the right formal pressure to identify. 

### 1.5 The voids catalogue has become better calibrated

The current voids methodology no longer treats a large number of named “voids” as dozens of independent discoveries. It recognises that many are framework-relative redescriptions of a smaller number of underlying epistemic limitations, and that convergence within a tenet-generated catalogue is principally evidence of internal coherence. [[positions/voids-as-evidence]]

That correction should now be imposed on the apex layer more consistently.

# 2. Novel implications the site’s commitments generate

The following are not merely restatements of objections the Map already records. They arise by combining commitments that are currently discussed in different clusters.

## 2.1 Self-stultification and trumping cannot both do the advertised evidential work

The epiphenomenalism cluster argues that conscious experience must help cause accurate reports about experience. Otherwise, a report such as “I am in pain” would have been generated entirely by physical states independently of the pain’s phenomenal character, leaving its accuracy unexplained. The site now qualifies this argument appropriately against phenomenal-concept and inherited-discourse replies, but it continues to use report-grounding as a major motivation for bidirectional interaction. 

The trumping-preemption cluster proposes something different. On that account, the physical base retains a complete default causal profile: it would have generated the same effect by itself. Consciousness becomes the “authoritative” cause through a psychophysical priority law, while the physical trajectory and observable output remain unchanged. 

These strategies cannot simply be added together.

Suppose physical state $B$ would produce report $R$ whether or not phenomenal state $E$ exercised authority. Trumping may make $E$ the metaphysically authorised cause of $R$, but it does not make the **content of $R$** counterfactually depend on the content of $E$. The self-stultification objection was meant to require precisely that content-sensitive dependence.

The Map therefore faces a fork:

- **Content-sensitive interaction:** varying phenomenal content changes report probabilities or report content. This gives self-stultification real force but creates an empirically exposed channel.
- **Authority-only trumping:** experience is designated the cause while the same report would emerge from the physical base. This may answer a metaphysical exclusion problem, but self-stultification ceases to be evidence for it.

The present corpus frequently draws evidential motivation from the first route while paying the empirical cost with the second.

### Required correction

Add a joint article, preferably an apex, titled something like **“Report Grounding versus Authority-Only Causation.”** It should ask whether the relevant claim is:

$$P(R\mid do(E),B)\neq P(R\mid B)$$

or merely that $E$ is the authoritative cause of an unchanged $R$. Only the first vindicates the report-grounding argument.

## 2.2 Subject individuation becomes part of the physical transition law

The subjecthood register holds that subjects are determinately individuated even when no publicly accessible physical criterion tells us which subject is present. The agency cluster then says that the intentions of that subject select physical outcomes.  

Taken together, these imply that a complete psychophysical transition law cannot merely be:

$$P(O\mid I,B,X)$$

where $I$ is an intention, $B$ a brain state and $X$ the physical context. It must be something like:

$$P(O\mid I,S,B,X)$$

where $S$ identifies the subject whose intention carries causal authority.

This matters in precisely the cases discussed elsewhere on the site:

- split brains;
- fission;
- copying;
- temporary loss of integration;
- overlapping or nested subjects;
- AI instances and restored checkpoints;
- two subjects acting on entangled or shared physical degrees of freedom.

If different assignments of $S$ produce different physical outcomes, subject identity has empirically relevant consequences and cannot remain wholly outside physical inquiry. If no possible outcome depends on which $S$ is present, then primitive subject identity is causally idle and cannot carry the agency and many-worlds work assigned to it.

This is a new version of the pairing problem. The current agent-causation article treats identifying candidate neural structures as if that largely locates the subject. It does not. A physical interface location tells us **where** influence occurs, not **which non-physical subject** owns it. 

### Required correction

The interface programme needs a **subject-assignment law** with at least:

- a domain of subjects;
- a relation between subjects and interface-bearing systems;
- conditions for one-to-one, one-to-many and many-to-one coupling;
- persistence conditions through fission and integration loss;
- a conflict rule when more than one subject has access to one candidate set.

Until then, the subjecthood cluster is not downstream metaphysics. It is an omitted state variable in the proposed dynamics.

---

## 2.3 Exact public indistinguishability gives a Bayes factor of one

The Map increasingly says that the preferred corridor preserves ordinary physical statistics exactly and may be indistinguishable from no conscious influence under every unconditioned test. The trumping route goes further and places causal efficacy outside all distributional change.  

The Bayesian consequence should be stated explicitly.

For any dataset $D$, if:

$$P(D\mid M)=P(D\mid N)$$

where $M$ is the Map’s interface hypothesis and $N$ is a no-mental-influence physical model, then:

$$BF_{M,N}=\frac{P(D\mid M)}{P(D\mid N)}=1.$$

No quantity of such data raises the odds of $M$ over $N$.

This means that the following cannot accumulate into empirical support merely through repetition:

- normal neural correlates;
- successful psychotherapy;
- attentional control;
- absence of gross Born-rule deviations;
- quantum effects elsewhere in biology;
- clinical dissociations compatible with both views;
- the persistence of an explanatory gap;
- a null result that the corridor was designed to predict.

They may establish compatibility, remove an objection or motivate inquiry. They do not increase the likelihood ratio unless the Map shows that the rival would have made the observation less likely.

This implication is already latent in the methodology register’s warning that consistency is not evidence, but the positions register does not yet apply it consistently. In particular, the external-evidence grade C assigned to agent-causal libertarianism is too generous when its cited observations are not more probable under substance-agent causation than under compatibilist or physicalist control models.  

### Required correction

Every evidence entry should include a **comparative likelihood field**:

| Field | Required question |
| --- | --- |
| Observation | What exactly was observed? |
| Target proposition | Which precise claim is being graded? |
| Primary rival | What is the strongest live alternative? |
| $P(D\mid H)$ | Why is the datum expected under the Map? |
| $P(D\mid R)$ | Why is it less expected under the rival? |
| Evidential role | Support, compatibility, constraint or defeater-removal? |

Without a comparative entry, the datum should default to **compatibility**, not support.

## 2.4 Trumping makes the quantum tenet dispensable

The agency register says the case for conscious authorship is logically separable from the particular quantum mechanism and should survive if post-decoherence selection fails. 

Trumping reinforces that separation. Its physical base is already sufficient; consciousness adds causal authority without changing the trajectory. Nothing in that structure requires quantum indeterminacy. Indeed, trumping is attractive precisely because it can preserve complete observable physical sufficiency. 

The site therefore cannot treat quantum selection and trumping as mutually supporting layers of one mechanism. They answer the exclusion problem in opposing ways:

- **Quantum-gap strategy:** the physical cause is not outcome-sufficient, leaving a genuine alternative for consciousness to select.
- **Trumping strategy:** the physical cause remains sufficient, but consciousness is given priority or authority over the same effect.

If trumping succeeds, Tenet 2 is unnecessary to secure Tenet 3. If Tenet 2 is constitutive of the Map, trumping is at most a rival fallback—not part of the mechanism’s cumulative support.

This adds weight to a broader architectural conclusion: **Minimal Quantum Interaction should be a high-centrality research position, not a foundational tenet.** It is substantially more specific, less evidenced and more replaceable than the core ontological and causal commitments.

---

## 2.5 Strict selection creates a semantic report bottleneck

The selection-only article now correctly says that all candidate content must be physically pre-generated. Consciousness cannot introduce a quale, report or semantic distinction that the candidate space does not already encode. 

That has a stronger consequence than the article currently draws.

If the physical system pre-generates a set of reports $R_1,\ldots,R_n$, it has already encoded the semantic alternatives. To explain reliable first-person reporting, conscious state $E_i$ must systematically select the report whose content matches $E_i$. Merely selecting *some* candidate is insufficient.

The relevant quantity is not only channel capacity $\log_2 N$. It is content-sensitive coupling, for example:

$$I(E;R\mid B)$$

or an interventionist equivalent:

$$P(R_i\mid do(E_i),B)>P(R_i\mid do(E_j),B).$$

Without such a semantic matching law, the physical system supplies both the vocabulary and the disposition to utter the correct report, while consciousness contributes no explained relation between phenomenal content and report content.

This is another point at which the strict selection model must either become empirically exposed or surrender the self-stultification argument.

## 2.6 Bidirectional Interaction currently has three incompatible quantifiers

The general epiphenomenalism article says Bidirectional Interaction is a universal metaphysical claim: wherever consciousness occurs, it is causally coupled. It also concedes that the report-grounding argument establishes much less—at most, that some consciousness is report-grounded. 

The AI-epiphenomenalism article then permits a conscious artificial subject whose experience has no influence over the system’s outputs because the architecture lacks a suitable interface. It attempts to reconcile this with Tenet 3 by redescribing causal efficacy as an intrinsic capacity that may be blocked. 

That is not the same tenet. The corpus alternates among:

1. **Actual universal efficacy:** every conscious state exercises some physical influence.
2. **Dispositional universal efficacy:** consciousness has a causal capacity, although an unsuitable substrate may completely prevent its manifestation.
3. **Biologically scoped efficacy:** human or biologically integrated consciousness acts causally, while other possible consciousness may not.

AI epiphenomenalism contradicts the first, is compatible with the second, and is undecided under the third.

### Required correction

Tenet 3 must choose one quantifier. The choice has consequences:

- Under **actual universal efficacy**, an actually conscious but causally inert AI is impossible.
- Under **dispositional efficacy**, self-stultification no longer supports the full tenet, and the site must admit genuinely epiphenomenal token consciousness.
- Under **biological scope**, the claim is not a general thesis about consciousness and cannot automatically govern artificial, alien or minimal subjects.

The present wording allows the site to invoke universality when attacking epiphenomenalism and mere disposition when accommodating AI.

# 3. Structural weaknesses in the argument architecture

## 3.1 The “five tenets” are not five items of one logical kind

They mix at least five levels:

| Tenet | Logical type |
| --- | --- |
| Dualism | Ontological commitment |
| Minimal quantum interaction | Specific mechanism family |
| Bidirectional interaction | Causal-role commitment |
| No many-worlds | Interpretation/world-structure commitment |
| Limits to Occam’s razor | Methodological rule |

This heterogeneous list encourages circular support. A mechanism is protected as a starting point, then articles written under that mechanism are counted as evidence that the starting point unifies the corpus.

A cleaner constitution would be:

**Layer A — Core commitments:** irreducible consciousness; at least some mental causation.

**Layer B — Background ontology:** determinate subjects; single outcomes; any global-exclusion thesis.

**Layer C — Research hypotheses:** post-decoherence selection, Born corridors, agent causation, particular interface sites.

**Layer D — Methodological constraints:** evidential symmetry, parsimony limits, common-cause discounting, update rules.

This would allow the site to abandon a failed quantum programme without pretending that its fundamental view of consciousness had collapsed.

## 3.2 The site has a mechanism-shaped hole rather than a partial mechanism

The current programme lacks not merely parameter values but the relation that would make the theory dynamical:

- what variables on the phenomenal side enter the law;
- what physical candidate set they act on;
- how the relevant basis is fixed;
- how a subject is paired with one physical system;
- whether intention changes transition probabilities;
- how exact Born marginals are retained;
- how multiple selectors compose;
- how objective collapse and conscious selection are ordered;
- how Lorentz-compatible joint outcomes are secured.

The multi-agent article now acknowledges the joint-correlation and nested-observer portions of this debt and calls for a two-agent Bell-pair toy model. 

That is good, but it means “mechanism” should presently be reserved for externally developed proposals such as GRW, CSL or specified quantum models. The Map has a **mechanism programme** and a set of desiderata, not yet a mechanism.

## 3.3 The programme converts concealment into a design requirement

The preferred model is often praised for being minimal because it preserves observed physics. But there are three very different achievements that can be hidden under “minimal”:

1. satisfying existing experimental bounds;
2. making a small but testable conditional difference;
3. making no possible observational difference by construction.

Only the first two are empirical virtues. The third is survival through observational equivalence.

The site now partly recognises this in the Born-preserving apex. The problem is that other articles still describe exact invisibility as though it were both a virtue and evidence of fit. It is a cost to empirical confirmation, even if it is a coherent metaphysical choice.

## 3.4 The evidence grades attach to motivating phenomena rather than target propositions

Consider the agent-causal position. Willed attention recruits additional frontal and frontoparietal activity; psychotherapy can alter neural pathways; deliberation has a phenomenology of effort; reasoning appears norm-sensitive. These are genuine phenomena.

But the target proposition is much stronger:

> a numerically persisting non-physical substance directly originates choices through irreducible causal powers.

The cited observations do not discriminate that proposition from:

- event-causal libertarianism;
- reasons-responsive compatibilism;
- hierarchical control;
- dynamical-systems accounts;
- interventionist higher-level causation;
- physicalist metacognitive control.

Rajan and colleagues identify greater frontal theta and bidirectional frontoparietal communication during willed rather than instructed attention, explaining the difference through decision and conflict processes. They do not identify an extra-physical causal input. [OUP Academic+1](https://academic.oup.com/cercor/article-abstract/29/7/2832/5041999)

The site’s own prose often says “consistent with rather than establishing.” The external-evidence grade should follow the exact proposition and therefore remain D unless a discriminating argument is supplied.

## 3.5 Route proliferation risks immunisation

Across the corpus, mental causation may be:

- post-decoherence outcome selection;
- probability bias;
- Born-preserving token selection;
- basis or context selection;
- quantum-Zeno stabilisation;
- Orch-OR-linked activity;
- atemporal or retrocausal selection;
- trumping preemption;
- lawful co-causation;
- interventionist higher-level causation.

The interface programme now admits these are often mutually incompatible implementation routes and should not be evidence-stacked. Yet its description and inherited framing still advertise “six converging lines.” 

A programme with many alternatives is not automatically weak. The danger arises when:

- success of any route is credited to the overall framework;
- failure of one route is treated as irrelevant because another remains;
- no probability distribution or retirement rule exists over the routes.

That structure can make the umbrella hypothesis resistant to evidence without making any member more probable.

## 3.6 The site’s corrective process is reactive rather than propagating

The changelog documents repeated cases in which flagship or register pages were corrected but dependent concepts retained the invalid inference. An August audit described this as the third consecutive pass in which flagships were right and the concept cluster beneath them was not. The latest October corrections repeat the pattern for cognitive motor dissociation, functionalism, clinical consciousness and identity claims. 

The project therefore has a source-of-record policy but not a functioning **reverse-dependency update system**.

At this scale, prose reminders are insufficient. A changed upstream claim needs to generate an invalidation queue for:

- article bodies;
- summaries;
- descriptions;
- apex theses;
- position grades;
- “Relation to Site Perspective” sections;
- image captions and search snippets;
- related-article annotations.

## 3.7 Model diversity is being mistaken for epistemic independence

The site admirably discloses extensive AI assistance and conducts repeated reviews with different models. This is useful for error discovery. It is not equivalent to independent evidence or independent expert judgement.

Models share:

- training corpora;
- common philosophical summaries;
- the site’s own prompt framing;
- a tendency to optimise local coherence;
- access to the same already-selected source set.

Agreement among models is therefore closer to robustness under related editorial processes than to independent confirmation. The common-cause methodology applied to voids should be applied to reviewers as well.

## 3.8 The homepage still misdescribes what “starting from dualism” accomplishes

Starting from dualism does not simply “keep the question open.” It keeps a neglected dualist research programme open, which is a defensible editorial purpose, but it also conditions the corpus on physicalism being false. Articles produced inside that condition cannot later be counted as neutral evidence that physicalism is false. The distinction between **exploration under a premise** and **support for the premise** should be visible at the site entrance, not only in methodology pages. [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/)

# 4. Tenet-by-tenet coherence audit

## Tenet 1: Dualism

### Coherent core

The minimal claim that phenomenal consciousness is not exhaustively captured by present third-person physical description is consistently represented and philosophically defensible as a starting point.

### Strain

The tenet advertises neutrality between substance and property dualism, while the operating framework requires:

- a persisting subject;
- primitive numerical identity;
- an owner of causal powers;
- determinate cross-temporal survival;
- non-deflationary mine-ness.

The site now admits that this substance-leaning enters through the agency and subjecthood layers rather than following from bare dualism. 

The remaining problem is public architecture: a reader can accept irreducible phenomenal properties while rejecting the persisting substance-agent on which the free-will and many-worlds arguments rely. Those downstream conclusions should not appear to inherit the authority of Tenet 1.

A second overreach appears in the consciousness-scope register, where substrate permissiveness or possible universality is treated as following fairly directly from dualism. Irreducibility does not entail substrate independence. It leaves substrate conditions underdetermined. [[consciousness-scope|positions/consciousness-scope]]

### Verdict

**Internally coherent only after splitting bare irreducibility from thick subject ontology.** The current “neutral tenet, substance-operating framework” is a disclosed tension rather than a direct contradiction.

---

## Tenet 2: Minimal Quantum Interaction

### Coherent core

Quantum indeterminacy offers one place to investigate models that avoid naïve energy injection. Treating post-decoherence selection, Zeno effects and objective-collapse interfaces as research programmes is legitimate.

### Strain

The tenet is far more specific than the evidence warrants. Its preferred mechanism has:

- no worked dynamical law;
- no established neural implementation;
- no subject-pairing rule;
- no joint multi-agent rule;
- no demonstrated conditional signature;
- an admitted risk of empirical equivalence with no influence.

The physical precedents invoked do not close this gap. Tegmark estimated extremely rapid neural decoherence, while Hagan and colleagues obtained longer times under substantially different and contested modelling assumptions. That disagreement shows model sensitivity, not evidence for a mental interface. [PubMed+1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11088215/)

Likewise, radical-pair work on cryptochrome supports a plausible quantum-biological mechanism in magnetoreception, but recent Cry4a work is computational and mechanistic support rather than direct demonstration of consciousness-relevant coherence. [ACS Publications+1](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/abs/10.1021/jacs.5c15726)

The Map’s own P-Q8 correctly withdrew the older suggestion that long-lived functional electronic coherence in photosynthesis provides a strong precedent: spectroscopy on the FMO complex reports electronic decoherence on roughly femtosecond scales and does not establish the claimed long-lived functional effect. [PubMed+1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28743751/)

### Verdict

**Too specific and too replaceable to remain a tenet.** It should be reclassified as the programme’s leading mechanism hypothesis.

---

## Tenet 3: Bidirectional Interaction

### Coherent core

The site can coherently take at least some mental causation as a foundational commitment. The argument from report grounding and rational thought provides a serious philosophical motivation for rejecting bare-correlation epiphenomenalism.

### Strain

The evidence does not establish the current universal formulation.

The site itself concedes:

- self-stultification establishes at most that some report-grounded consciousness is causal;
- inherited discourse can produce consciousness-talk without local phenomenal grounding;
- phenomenal-concept accounts remain live;
- AI consciousness, if present, might be causally blocked;
- exact Born preservation may erase every statistical trace.

The tenet also oscillates between outcome selection and weaker causal notions such as authority, report grounding, attention, neural plasticity and higher-level control. These are not interchangeable.

### Verdict

**Coherent as a restricted commitment; overextended as a universal law.** It needs an explicit quantifier and a distinction between actual, dispositional and report-grounding efficacy.

---

## Tenet 4: Rejection of Many-Worlds

### Coherent core

A single-world ontology is a legitimate starting point. The Map is entitled to explore its implications and to object to accounts that it regards as failing to preserve an adequate notion of one persisting subject.

### Strain

The rejection is not primarily a result of physics or agency evidence. It rests on two additional philosophical commitments:

- a thick, non-deflationary subject;
- global exclusion of incompatible alternatives.

The background-commitments page now recognises that branch-local authorship, reasons-responsiveness, memory and persistence are available to Everettian agents. What the Map demands is that alternatives not occur anywhere, and that demand is asserted rather than derived from sourcehood. 

The same difficulty appears in the subjecthood register. It admits that closed individualism cannot be retired without removing much of the tenet’s rationale, even though the positions architecture says ordinary positions should be retireable without disturbing the tenets. 

Rejecting many-worlds also does not answer “why this outcome?” A single stochastic outcome and an Everettian branch-relative outcome face different but related explanatory questions. The site now sometimes acknowledges this, but older arguments still present global uniqueness as if it were supplied by agency.

### Verdict

**Coherent as a chosen single-world boundary condition, not established by the other tenets.** Either determinate subjecthood and global exclusion must be promoted into the foundations or Tenet 4 must be explicitly conditional on them.

---

## Tenet 5: Limits to Occam’s Razor

### Coherent core

The site is correct that parsimony is defeasible, that entity-counting is not a mechanical truth procedure, and that theoretical virtues must be weighed against explanatory adequacy.

### Strain

A limit on parsimony cannot become a general waiver for:

- unarticulated laws;
- redundant causes;
- observationally equivalent additions;
- mechanism proliferation;
- failure to assign probabilities across alternative routes.

Parsimony should not by itself defeat dualism, but the Map still owes comparative costs. “The world may be more complex than expected” does not tell us which additional ontology to accept.

The risk is asymmetry: physicalist simplicity is treated as question-begging, while the simplicity of “one selector actualises one outcome” is sometimes used rhetorically in favour of the Map despite the further subject, pairing, psychophysical and collapse laws that the phrase suppresses.

### Verdict

**Substantively sound when self-binding; vulnerable when used as a shield.** It should be operationalised as a symmetric model-comparison rule rather than a tenet supporting dualism.

# 6. Citation and quotation verification

## 6.1 Clinical consciousness: recent corrections are justified

**Bodien et al. 2024.** The widely reported figure is 60 of 241 behaviourally command-unresponsive participants, or 25%, showing cognitive motor dissociation through fMRI or EEG. The finding supports under-detection by bedside behavioural assessment. It does not establish that every positive signal is certain phenomenal consciousness, nor does it refute functionalism, because the phenomenon is detected through preserved functional command processing. [New England Journal of Medicine+1](https://www.nejm.org/doi/abs/10.1056/NEJMoa2400645)

**Claassen et al. 2019.** Sixteen of 104 acutely unresponsive ICU patients showed EEG evidence of command-related activation. The authors treated interpretation and generalisation cautiously. The site’s latest narrowing from a broad filter-theory claim to a specific clinical detection result is warranted. [New England Journal of Medicine+1](https://www.nejm.org/doi/abs/10.1056/NEJMoa1812757)

**Schnakers et al. 2009.** The older misdiagnosis result involving 18 of 44 patients is real, but it concerns diagnostic classification using standardised behavioural assessment, not the prevalence of covert command-following as later operationalised. The changelog’s separation of those denominators is correct. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19622138/)

**Cruse/Goldfine.** I confirmed the existence of the original study, the Goldfine reanalysis and the subsequent exchange. I did not retrieve enough complete primary text to certify every numerical or signal-processing detail independently. The site’s decision not to use Cruse 2011 as uncomplicated positive evidence is appropriately conservative. [PubMed+2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23351802/)

## 6.2 Volition and readiness potentials: challenge to simple Libet readings, not support for dualism

Schurger and colleagues showed that a stochastic accumulator model can generate the readiness-potential pattern when trials are aligned to threshold-crossing movements. This weakens the claim that the readiness potential straightforwardly records a settled unconscious decision long before awareness. It does not show that a non-physical agent intervenes. [Chapman University Digital Commons+1](https://digitalcommons.chapman.edu/psychology_articles/175/)

Rajan and colleagues’ willed-attention findings identify additional frontal theta and frontoparietal interactions relative to instructed attention. Nadra and Mangun likewise investigate neural predictors and networks associated with internally selected attention. These results distinguish forms of control; they do not discriminate agent-causal dualism from neural decision and metacognitive-control models. [OUP Academic+1](https://academic.oup.com/cercor/article-abstract/29/7/2832/5041999)

## 6.3 COGITATE: negative pressure on two theories, not positive evidence for dualism

The 2025 adversarial collaboration tested preregistered predictions associated with IIT and global neuronal workspace theory in 256 participants. Several distinctive predictions of both theories were challenged. That is a significant result about those formulations. It is not a finding that consciousness is non-physical, causally selects quantum outcomes, or lies outside neural identity. [Nature+1](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08888-1)

Any site-wide synthesis should classify COGITATE as:

- evidence against particular predictions;
- evidence for theory revision or pluralism;
- not evidence for the Map’s mechanism without a likelihood argument.

## 6.4 Quantum biology: precedent is repeatedly stronger in rhetoric than in evidence

Tegmark’s neural-decoherence calculation and the Hagan–Hameroff–Tuszynski response are both real. They depend on different physical assumptions, and neither constitutes empirical confirmation that consciousness exploits the proposed degrees of freedom. [PubMed+1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11088215/)

The FMO literature does not support the older claim of long-lived functional electronic coherence at biologically relevant temperatures in the strong form sometimes used by quantum-consciousness proponents. The site’s quantum register has corrected this; all downstream mentions should follow. [PubMed+1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28743751/)

Recent Cry4a research gives sophisticated theoretical and computational support to radical-pair candidates for avian magnetoreception. It should be described as a plausible quantum-biological mechanism under active investigation, not as demonstrated evidence that bird navigation—or still less consciousness—uses a protected quantum channel of the required kind. [ACS Publications+1](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/abs/10.1021/jacs.5c15726)

Donadi and colleagues’ result constrains the parameter-free Diósi–Penrose collapse proposal through spontaneous-radiation limits. It does not directly falsify Orch OR as a whole. The current register’s distinction is accurate. [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41567-020-1008-4)

## 6.5 Philosophical and formal mechanism sources

Bradford Saad’s delegatory-dualism paper exists as a peer-reviewed philosophical proposal and broadly supports the site’s description of an experience preempting part of a physical state’s default causal profile. It demonstrates a coherent metaphysical model under its assumptions; it is not empirical evidence that psychophysical delegation occurs or that it is quantum-mediated. [DOI+1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11098-025-02290-3)

The Torres Alegre Born-rule derivation exists as an arXiv preprint in a generalised-probabilistic framework. Its result is conditional on substantial assumptions, including the relevant purification and steering structure. It is a formal constraint within a class of theories, not observed evidence of a consciousness–physics interface. The current site mostly says this correctly, but several downstream formulations still move too quickly from “a theorem makes Born preservation natural under assumptions” to “the proposed interface natively satisfies relativistic causality.” [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.12636)

Bong and colleagues’ local-friendliness theorem is real and important. Current implementations do not place a phenomenally conscious friend into coherent superposition, so its application to the Map remains conditional on future controllability and on the consciousness status of the “friend.” [DOI+1](https://doi.org/10.1038/S41567-020-0990-X)

## 6.6 AI introspection

The cited 2026 work on emergent introspective awareness investigates whether language models can report or use experimentally injected internal representations. # 8. Methodological improvements

## 8.1 Build a claim-level dependency graph

Every load-bearing claim should have a stable identifier, for example:

- T3-ACTUAL-UNIVERSAL
- Q-BORN-MARGINAL
- Q-CONDITIONAL-SIGNATURE
- SUBJECT-DETERMINATE
- SUBJECT-PHYSICALLY-UNREADABLE
- MWI-GLOBAL-EXCLUSION
- AI-INTERFACE-INELIGIBLE

Articles would declare which claims they:

- assume;
- support;
- constrain;
- contradict;
- merely illustrate.

Changing one claim should automatically identify every dependent paragraph.

## 8.2 Add continuous-integration checks for prose

The repository should fail a content check when:

- a description says “establishes” but the position says “compatible”;
- an article cites a retired evidence claim;
- an apex says “independent convergence” while its sources share one upstream premise;
- a D-grade mechanism supports an unqualified applied verdict;
- a page says “no-signalling by construction” while the joint-level law remains open;
- a summary is older than a substantive body correction;
- a position marked retireable is declared necessary for a tenet elsewhere.

This is not beyond automation. Much of the relevant structure is already present in frontmatter.

## 8.3 Separate four evidential relations

Use explicit labels throughout:

- **supports** — raises relative likelihood;
- **compatible with** — does not refute;
- **constrains** — rules out versions or parameters;
- **motivates** — makes the question worth investigating.

Most current empirical material belongs to the last three categories.

## 8.4 Grade propositions, not articles or clusters

A study may strongly support command-following while providing no evidence for phenomenal consciousness; support willed control while providing no evidence for substance causation; constrain a collapse parameter while providing no evidence for Orch OR.

Each evidence grade should therefore attach to one proposition at one inferential distance.

## 8.5 Impose a centrality quarantine

A claim that is simultaneously:

- structurally central;
- externally grade D;
- empirically non-discriminating;
- model-immature;

should not generate strong downstream factual or moral conclusions without a visible inherited warning.

This would directly constrain current claims about:

- AI moral status;
- survival through copying;
- mechanisms of meditation;
- filter theory;
- free will;
- quantum biological interfaces.

## 8.6 Require route probabilities and retirement criteria

For each mechanism route, record:

- current credence;
- unique predictions;
- shared predictions;
- incompatibilities with other routes;
- what would lower its credence;
- what would retire it;
- whether failure would affect a tenet or only a position.

Without this, mechanism pluralism becomes cost-free.

## 8.7 Stop corpus expansion until integration catches up

The site does not presently need more speculative concepts. It needs a temporary expansion freeze and an integration pass focused on:

- title/body alignment;
- evidential inheritance;
- collapse ordering;
- T3 scope;
- subject assignment;
- joint Born preservation;
- AI interface eligibility;
- route exclusivity.

The changelog shows that adding reviews without enforcing propagation produces recurring drift.

## 8.8 Introduce genuinely independent specialist review

Model-based adversarial reviews are useful, but central pages should periodically receive named or anonymised review from specialists in:

- foundations of quantum mechanics;
- philosophy of causation;
- philosophy of personal identity;
- disorders of consciousness;
- computational neuroscience;
- AI interpretability;
- Buddhist philosophy where invoked.

The specialist should review the exact proposition and source chain, not merely prose quality.

## 8.9 Treat toy models as a publication gate

Before further claims of a “mechanism,” require at least:

1. one single-agent conditional-probability model;
2. one two-agent entangled model;
3. one objective-collapse-plus-conscious-selection ordering model;
4. one subject-assignment model for fission or split-brain cases;
5. explicit simulations showing what is and is not empirically identifiable.

A failed toy model would be more informative than dozens of additional verbal compatibility articles.

# Final assessment

The Unfinishable Map’s most impressive feature is not that it has established interactionist dualism. It has not. Its most impressive feature is that it has begun constructing an unusually visible audit trail of how a highly speculative metaphysical programme can discover and expose its own debts.

Its strongest current conclusions are negative and methodological:

- correlation does not by itself settle identity;
- bedside behaviour can under-detect preserved internal processing;
- decoherence alone does not select a unique outcome under every interpretation;
- some physicalist and dualist accounts are observationally underdetermined;
- agency phenomenology does not settle libertarianism;
- quantum-biological precedent is not a consciousness mechanism;
- multiple apparently convergent arguments may share one premise;
- exact Born preservation threatens empirical idleness;
- the preferred interface has no worked model.

Those are worthwhile results.

The site’s positive theory remains underdetermined. Its five-tenet presentation hides a larger package of subject, world and causal assumptions. Its empirical material is mostly compatible with the framework rather than differential evidence for it. Its preferred quantum route is externally grade D and formally unfinished. Its alternative trumping route secures metaphysical authority only by surrendering the difference-making relation on which its strongest anti-epiphenomenalist argument relies. Its AI cluster exposes an unresolved equivocation over whether causal efficacy is actual or merely dispositional. Its post-decoherence and objective-collapse clusters still double-book the act of actualisation.

Accordingly, the right next stage is not further breadth. It is **constitutional consolidation and formal exposure**:

1. reclassify the foundations;
2. resolve the Tenet 3 quantifier;
3. choose a canonical collapse ordering;
4. distinguish quantum-gap from trumping causation;
5. make primitive subject identity enter—or cease to enter—the causal law;
6. attach evidence grades to exact propositions;
7. enforce dependency propagation automatically;
8. produce the first worked toy models.

Until those steps are taken, the Map should present itself as:

> **a rigorously developed conditional case for one family of interactionist-dualist possibilities, accompanied by an explicit research programme for making that family discriminating**

rather than as a cumulative evidential case that dualist quantum interaction is probably how consciousness works.
