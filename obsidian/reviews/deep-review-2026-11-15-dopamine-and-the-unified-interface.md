- Chakroun et al. 2023 (*Nat Commun* 14:5369) — metadata real-correct (8 authors match). Claim: **partly overstated**. The full text (PMC10477234) reports threshold (α) reductions under *both* L-dopa and haloperidol, and the value coefficient of the drift rate is not credibly changed (L-dopa v_coeff −.0104, 95% HDI [−1.057, .834]). Corrected to "without credibly changing how strongly value drives the drift rate; responses trended faster and *less* accurate".
