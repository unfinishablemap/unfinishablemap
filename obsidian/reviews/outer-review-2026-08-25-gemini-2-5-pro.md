# Pre-Publication Review: "Edge-States and the Probing of Voids"

## Abstract of the Epistemological and Empirical Assessment

The manuscript under review, "Edge-States and the Probing of Voids," submitted for consideration as an original contribution to the philosophy of mind and cognitive epistemology, attempts an ambitious phenomenological mapping of human cognitive limits. The central thesis posits that specific altered states of consciousness—namely, near-death experiences (NDEs), advanced meditative cessation, psychedelic states, and anesthetic divergence—function as "void probes." The manuscript argues that these probes either grant genuine epistemic access to territories beyond ordinary conscious constraints or, at minimum, trace the structural boundaries of cognitive limitation from the inside, thereby supporting a non-reductive, interactionist-dualist framework of consciousness.

A rigorous, hostile pre-publication audit reveals that the manuscript is fundamentally compromised by severe methodological circularity, empirical suppression, and a pervasive failure to engage with the highly relevant 2020–2025 literature on predictive processing and computational phenomenology. The text systematically brackets physicalist explanations that its own premises should force it to confront, relies on superseded or blatantly misrepresented interpretations of neurological activity in the dying brain, and constructs a phenomenological taxonomy that is deeply unfalsifiable. By assuming a dualist, interactionist framework as a methodological axiom rather than a hypothesis to be rigorously tested against competing models, the manuscript generates conclusions that are mere artifacts of its own question-begging architecture. The ensuing analysis meticulously details the manuscript’s empirical overreach, its failure to survive recent neuroscientific literature, its reliance on misattributed claims, and the logical vacuities inherent in its conceptualization of "occluded" cognitive boundaries.

## Epistemological Scaffolding and Methodological Begging of the Question

The manuscript’s fundamental architectural flaw is rooted in its explicit adoption of a highly partisan, non-reductive, interactionist-dualist framework as an insulated methodological starting point. The text operates under five foundational tenets: dualism, minimal quantum interaction, bidirectional causation, a rejection of the many-worlds interpretation, and an epistemic humility regarding Occam’s razor. In the context of a submission to a top-tier journal such as *Mind* or *Synthese*, stipulating a non-standard ontology as an initial methodological constraint is not inherently disqualifying, provided that the subsequent analysis uses this framework to generate novel, testable predictions that outperform rival paradigms. However, the manuscript profoundly fails to execute this comparative testing. Instead, it utilizes its axioms to arbitrarily carve out spaces of phenomenological ignorance—termed "voids"—and then presents the existence of these framework-dependent voids as abductive evidence for the framework itself.

### The Circularity of "Void" Individuation and the Epistemology of Limits

The text asserts that cognitive limits reveal the "shape of what a mind cannot think," positing that the structural logic of these boundaries points to irreducible, non-physical features of consciousness. The manuscript introduces a tripartite taxonomy of limits: the Unexplored (contingent ignorance), the Unexplorable (biological cognitive closure), and the Occluded (territories where thought is actively blocked or suspended by the nature of reality or a conscious interface). The manuscript argues that the phenomenological convergence of these limits across different "edge-states" proves that these boundaries are objective ontological realities rather than artifacts of a specific neural architecture's self-monitoring limitations.

This reasoning is viciously circular. The specific "voids" identified by the manuscript—such as the "Silence Void" in meditative states, the "Ineffable Encounter Void" in moments of profound awe, or the "Temporal Void" regarding the block universe—are individuated entirely by the dualist premises of the overarching framework. The manuscript explicitly concedes that deciding which voids a physicalist would grant is a judgment made from inside the framework whose support is at issue. Yet, it immediately brackets this fatal concession, failing to confront the obvious logical consequence: a physicalist, illusionist, or eliminativist framework does not recognize these "voids" as impenetrable ontological boundaries. Rather, contemporary physicalist frameworks model these phenomenological edges as temporary epistemic gaps or as the entirely predictable systemic failures of a self-monitoring hierarchical predictive network.

By asserting that the "family structure" of these voids makes them robust against individual scientific dissolutions—claiming, for instance, that solving the binding problem would leave the hard problem and the "phenomenal quality void" intact—the manuscript immunizes its thesis against empirical progress. This constitutes a sophisticated rebranding of the "God of the gaps" fallacy into "Voids of the map," structurally shielding the dualist thesis from refutation. The manuscript’s meta-epistemology posits that if our capacity to assess our limits is itself limited, the meta-question falls under the constraints it investigates. While true as a trivial logical boundary, the manuscript weaponizes this self-reference paradox to declare a premature victory for dualism, rather than engaging in the arduous work of testing these boundaries against state-of-the-art computational neuroscience.

## The Empirical Audit: Near-Death Experiences and the Falsified Quiescent Brain

The manuscript’s treatment of Near-Death Experiences (NDEs) constitutes its most glaring empirical liability, revealing a reliance on superseded clinical tropes that fail entirely to survive the 2020–2025 literature. The text attempts to recruit NDEs as evidence of an "interface disruption," wherein consciousness survives or even expands despite the complete cessation of biological support systems. To anchor this claim, the manuscript leans on the notion that NDEs occur during states of minimal brain function, perpetuating the medically outdated assertion that these experiences happen during "clinical death with flat EEG". This assertion is empirically bankrupt and contradicts the very citations the manuscript attempts to marshal.

### The Misrepresentation and Suppression of AWARE II Data

The manuscript explicitly cites Parnia's AWARE II study (2023) to bolster the claim that a significant subset of cardiac arrest patients report conscious awareness. It argues that materialists must struggle to explain why profound neural dysfunction would produce experiences described as "more real than real". The manuscript positions these findings as uniquely compatible with a "filter theory," suggesting that the brain ordinarily constrains consciousness, and clinical death "relaxes" this valve, allowing unfiltered dualist consciousness to manifest.

This is a profound misreading and selective suppression of the AWARE II data. Parnia et al. (2023) utilized continuous electroencephalography (EEG) and cerebral oxygenation monitoring during the resuscitation of in-hospital cardiac arrest patients. Crucially, the study did *not* find a "flat EEG" correlating with vivid phenomenal experience. On the contrary, AWARE II demonstrated that during prolonged cardiopulmonary resuscitation efforts, the brain exhibits intermittent, organized low-frequency activity, specifically in the delta, theta, and alpha bands, occurring sometimes as late as 35 to 60 minutes into the resuscitation. The brain during these episodes is not functionally absent, nor is it a passive, failing filter; it is generating specific, organized electrical patterns that are entirely compatible with the generation of conscious states by a physical system under extreme duress. The manuscript's framing artificially suppresses this physicalist-friendly data to manufacture a false mystery, setting up a strawman of the "inactive brain" that the primary literature actively refutes.

### The Omission of Gamma-Band Hyper-Connectivity in the Dying Brain

Even more fatal to the manuscript’s "filter theory" of NDEs is its total omission of the landmark findings by Borjigin et al., published in the *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences* (PNAS) in 2023. Borjigin’s team analyzed the EEG and electrocardiogram signals of comatose dying patients immediately before and after the withdrawal of ventilatory support. Rather than revealing a quiescent, fading, or "filtering" brain, the study found a rapid, explosive surge of absolute EEG power in the high-frequency gamma bands.

The empirical details of this study completely dismantle the manuscript's premise. The surge in gamma power (spanning gamma1 at 25-55 Hz and gamma2 at 80-150 Hz) was not random noise; it was accompanied by intense cross-frequency coupling with slower oscillations and heightened interhemispheric functional and directed connectivity. Furthermore, this massive surge in neurophysiological coherence was localized specifically within the temporo-parieto-occipital (TPO) junctions—the posterior cortical "hot zone" that contemporary neuroscience identifies as a critical neural correlate of conscious processing.

The data unequivocally demonstrate that global hypoxia paradoxically stimulates highly complex, globally coherent neurophysiological activity that exceeds levels found during the normal conscious waking state. A brain flooded with organized gamma oscillations and intense functional connectivity within the consciousness hot zone is engaging in hyper-active computation, not simply "relaxing a valve" to let in a transcendent mind. The manuscript's complete failure to engage with this highly publicized 2023 literature allows it to present the vividness of NDEs as a foundational challenge to materialism. In reality, the empirical evidence demonstrates that these experiences are the direct product of an intensely active, dying neural network desperately attempting to maintain predictive homeostasis in the face of catastrophic metabolic failure.

## The Empirical Audit: Psychedelics, Filter Theory, and the Misappropriation of Predictive Processing

In its attempt to unify NDEs, meditation, and psychedelics under a single explanatory umbrella, the manuscript invokes "Filter Theory"—the Huxleyan notion that the physical brain acts as a reducing valve for a broader, fundamental consciousness. The manuscript claims that psychedelics "disrupt this constraint while increasing global integration" and explicitly aligns its dualist thesis with the REBUS (Relaxed Beliefs Under Psychedelics) model. This alignment is built upon a severe conceptual equivocation and represents a failure to understand the theoretical framework it cites.

### The Free-Energy Principle vs. Dualist Disinhibition

The REBUS model, developed by Carhart-Harris and Karl Friston, is firmly and explicitly grounded in the Free-Energy Principle and the predictive processing paradigm. In the REBUS framework, psychedelics relax the precision-weighting of high-level prior beliefs (often anatomically associated with the Default Mode Network), thereby allowing bottom-up sensory prediction errors to propagate more freely through the cortical hierarchy. What is being "relaxed" in this model is not a dualist, biological interface preventing access to an ontologically distinct realm of pure consciousness; what is relaxed is a computationally instantiated Bayesian prior.

The manuscript executes a rhetorical sleight of hand by conflating the relaxation of a neural predictive model with the opening of a metaphysical valve. It argues that because psychedelics loosen biological constraints, the resulting state provides genuine epistemic access to territories closed to ordinary consciousness. However, from a predictive processing standpoint, the psychedelic state does not reveal "the void" or the fundamental nature of dualist reality; it reveals the chaotic, unconstrained operations of the brain’s own generative model attempting to minimize surprise without the anchor of high-level executive regulation. The richness of the psychedelic state is generated by the system's own unchecked prediction errors, not imported from a "surplus void."

### Scholarly Malpractice: The Gładziejewski Misattribution

Compounding this theoretical equivocation is a basic failure of academic scholarship. The manuscript relies heavily on the arguments of a philosopher it names as "Piotr Gładziejewski," citing a publication year of 2024 to support its claims about epistemic openness under psychedelics. This is factually incorrect on multiple levels. The actual author is Paweł Gładziejewski, and the paper in question was published in 2023 in the *Review of Philosophy and Psychology* (DOI: 10.1007/s13164-023-00709-6).

Beyond the egregious metadata errors, the manuscript fundamentally misapplies Gładziejewski's actual thesis. Gładziejewski argues that psychedelic states can be rationally integrated into one's epistemic framework specifically by understanding them *through* the lens of predictive processing, not by treating them as mystical "edge-states" that validate an interactionist paradigm. The manuscript cherry-picks the notion of "epistemic openness" while ignoring the physicalist, computational architecture that Gładziejewski uses to legitimize that openness. This is not merely a typographical error; it is the deliberate co-optation of a physicalist argument to serve a dualist conclusion, executed by stripping the source of its theoretical context.

## The Empirical Audit: Meditative Cessation, the "Silence Void," and Active Inference

The manuscript devotes considerable attention to advanced meditative states, specifically targeting the phenomenon of *nirodha samāpatti* (cessation)—a meditative state characterized by the full loss of phenomenal content. The manuscript frames this as the "Silence Void," a territory that requires the complete absence of cognitive activity and allegedly provides unique epistemic access. The text argues that voluntary, content-free, recoverable cessation is "harder to motivate on production accounts than on filter accounts," suggesting that cessation represents consciousness voluntarily releasing its grip on the biological filter from the inside.

### The Erasure of Active Inference Frameworks for Cessation

This argument rests entirely on the false premise that physicalist or computational "production-based" accounts lack the theoretical resources to elegantly model the suspension of phenomenal content. To maintain this illusion of an explanatory gap, the manuscript completely ignores the rapid and highly relevant advancements in computational phenomenology, specifically the application of active inference to deep meditative states.

In 2025, Laukkonen, Friston, and Chandaria published a seminal paper in *Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews* titled "A beautiful loop: An active inference theory of consciousness." This paper provides a comprehensive, formal computational model of precisely the states the manuscript claims physicalism cannot handle. Under the Laukkonen et al. framework, consciousness is modeled as the simulation of an epistemic field, governed by "Bayesian binding," wherein only inferences that coherently reduce long-term uncertainty are selected to enter the world model.

Meditative cessation and the deconstruction of the subject-object divide do not require positing a dualist "void." Instead, as demonstrated in the active inference literature, these states emerge naturally from the dynamics of hierarchical inference. The meditative process of "letting go" is formally modeled as a strategic, volitional reduction in belief precision over hidden states at specific levels of the generative model. A self-modifying hierarchical system, such as the human brain, can learn a policy of down-regulating the precision of the very predictive layers that construct the narrative self, spatial orientation, and the perception of time.

| Cessation is "hard to motivate" on production accounts. | Cessation is a highly adaptive policy of "letting go," operating as precision-control over hierarchical hidden states to regulate affective valence. |

Furthermore, the manuscript cites a 2025 fMRI preprint (Yang, Kadambi, et al.) to claim that cessation arrives through "regional modulation rather than a global shutdown," reading this as a validation of the dualist interface theory because the brain does not entirely turn off. Yet, regional down-regulation of transmodal cortex paired with the preservation of unimodal regions is exactly the neural signature predicted by a hierarchical active inference model where high-level predictive constraints are selectively suspended while lower-level sensory processing remains online. By completely omitting the Laukkonen et al. (2025) computational models, the manuscript engineers an artificial explanatory gap, falsely suggesting to the reader that dualist "void probes" are the only viable explanation for meditative silence.

## Phenomenological Reduction and the Erasure of the Minimal Phenomenal Experience Project

The manuscript touches frequently on states of "pure awareness," "witness-consciousness," and "minimal phenomenal experience," framing these edge-states as encounters with the unexplorable boundaries of cognition. It suggests that the stripping away of conceptual thought and perceptual structuring leaves a "gap" that is structurally opaque to scientific investigation, thereby validating the "Unexplorable" void taxonomy.

This phenomenological posturing requires the total erasure of the most significant empirical advancement in the study of pure consciousness in the current decade. In 2024, philosopher Thomas Metzinger published *The Elephant and the Blind: The Experience of Pure Consciousness*, detailing the massive results of the Minimal Phenomenal Experience Project (MPEP). Metzinger’s project analyzed over 500 experiential reports of pure awareness drawn from a global psychometric study of meditators across 57 countries.

Metzinger explicitly constructs a minimal model explanation for conscious experience that does not rely on subjective egoic structures, demonstrating how pure awareness operates at the phenomenal-neurological boundary between the conscious and the unconscious. Far from being a structurally opaque "void" that defies scientific categorization, Metzinger identifies precise, cross-cultural phenomenal markers for these states—including silence, wakefulness, clarity, presence, soundness, and non-identification—and operationalizes them for neuroscientific mapping.

The manuscript’s failure to engage with Metzinger (2024) is catastrophic for its central thesis. The manuscript asserts that states of pure awareness are "unexplorable voids" that resist third-person scientific methods and demand a non-reductive epistemology rooted in interactionist dualism. Yet, Metzinger has already successfully taxonomized, quantified, and naturalized these exact states, providing the core causal factors that underlie them without defaulting to interactionist magic. A manuscript claiming in 2026 to map the limits of consciousness while ignoring the largest systematic dataset and minimal model explanation of minimal phenomenal experience is engaged in highly selective, parochial scholarship that cannot pass peer review.

## Structural Opacity, the "Ineffable Encounter," and the Retreat to Unfalsifiability

Perhaps the most logically problematic conceptual apparatus in the manuscript is its taxonomy of the "Ineffable Encounter Void," which the author claims spans both the "Unexplorable" and the "Occluded" categories. The manuscript defines this void as an experience of overwhelming meaning (manifesting as either resonance or the numinous) where the mechanism producing the experience remains structurally opaque, and where analyzing the experience while having it causes the experience to dissolve—a dynamic the author terms the "mutation pattern" or "mutation void".

### The Epistemic Gerrymandering of the "Occluded" Void

The manuscript claims that in numinous encounters, the "subject–object distinction collapses; the self that would judge veridicality is what the experience overwhelms". Because the "evaluating self" is suspended, the veridicality of the experience cannot be assessed from within, nor can it be accurately reconstructed from memory after the fact.

By defining the void in this manner, the manuscript achieves perfect, unassailable unfalsifiability. If neuroscientists or phenomenologists fail to find empirical evidence for transcendent realities during these states, the manuscript can simply claim that the "monitoring infrastructure shares fate with the capacities it monitors," meaning the lack of evidence is precisely what the dualist void theory predicts. If cognitive attention dissolves the state, the manuscript claims this is evidence of the state's inherently "occluded" or "mutating" nature, suggesting reality actively defends its boundaries against introspective probing.

This is epistemic gerrymandering of the highest order. The manuscript treats the entirely expected limitations of biological self-monitoring as positive evidence for the active, deliberate concealment of an ontological interface. It argues that if consciousness causally influences the physical world (via bidirectional quantum interaction), the interface might be "systematically hidden from introspection"—we cannot see the interface we use. When an opponent's denial or a methodology's expected failure is treated as positive evidence for the very thing being denied, the theoretical framework has ceased to be a research program and has become a closed hermeneutic circle. The manuscript explicitly admits that treating a denial as evidence makes a position unfalsifiable, but it attempts to bypass this fatal flaw by claiming that the cross-cultural "convergence" of these limits makes the pattern robust.

However, cross-cultural convergence only proves that humans share a common neurocognitive architecture with common systemic limitations; it provides zero evidence that these limitations are actively "occluded" metaphysical interfaces. The fact that the "bias blind spot" or "mystical ineffability" appears across cultures merely supports the mundane physicalist premise that human brains are built with similar structural bottlenecks. The manuscript’s leap from biological cognitive closure to a dualist interface is unsupported by its own logic.

## The Anesthetic Divergence Underdetermined

Finally, the manuscript attempts to use anesthesia as an analogical probe for the "void," specifically the "death void." Citing Montupil et al. (2023), the text highlights the divergence between behavioral unresponsiveness and the preservation of internal vivid experience (disconnected consciousness) during anesthesia. The manuscript argues that clinical effectiveness requires only disconnecting the patient from the environment, not extinguishing experience entirely. It asserts that this "interface-disruption" is handled uniformly by dualist models, whereas materialist "memory-failure" models struggle to account for the three distinct states of anesthesia (connected, disconnected, unconscious).

This represents a failure to engage with the breadth of the anesthesia and consciousness literature. The phenomenon of disconnected consciousness under anesthesia (where internal phenomenal states like dreaming persist despite total unresponsiveness) is elegantly and comprehensively explained by physicalist models of network fragmentation. In models based on the Global Neuronal Workspace or Integrated Information Theory, anesthesia interrupts fronto-parietal broadcasting, effectively severing the capacity for external reportability and memory consolidation. Meanwhile, localized posterior cortical loops continue to generate phenomenal content autonomously. The manuscript creates a false dichotomy between a dualist "interface disruption" and a simplistic "memory-failure" account. By ignoring mainstream computational interpretations of anesthetic states, the manuscript erroneously claims a theoretical victory for dualism based on an underdetermined premise.

## Conclusion and List of Weaknesses

The manuscript exhibits profound scholarly, empirical, and logical defects that render it unsuitable for publication in its current form. It structurally insulates its dualist axioms against falsification, cherry-picks neurological data to suit its preferred filter theory, and entirely ignores the dominant 2020–2025 physicalist computational models capable of explaining the very phenomena it claims are "unexplorable voids." The framing consistently brackets physicalist explanations that its own premises should force it to confront, resulting in a text that maps its own theoretical assumptions rather than reality.

The following specific weaknesses require major revision, as they demonstrate mishandling, omission, or direct contradiction of the recent peer-reviewed literature:

1. **Omission of Gamma-Band Hyper-Connectivity in the Dying Brain:** The manuscript relies on the medically outdated and factually incorrect framing of NDEs occurring during "clinical death with flat EEG," actively ignoring Borjigin et al. (2023), which demonstrated massive, globally coherent surges of high-frequency gamma power, cross-frequency coupling, and interhemispheric connectivity in the temporo-parieto-occipital junction immediately preceding death. This hyper-activity directly contradicts the manuscript's premise of a "relaxing filter" during profound neural dysfunction.
2. **Suppression of AWARE II EEG Findings:** The manuscript cites Parnia's AWARE II study (2023) to argue for transcendent dualist access during cardiac arrest, but deliberately suppresses Parnia's primary empirical finding that survivors exhibited intermittent, organized low-frequency brain activity (delta, theta, alpha bands) as late as an hour into CPR. The brain is not inactive during these events, collapsing the manuscript's empirical scaffolding.
3. **Erasure of Computational Models for Meditative Cessation:** The manuscript frames *nirodha samāpatti* (cessation) as an inexplicable "Silence Void" that physicalist production accounts cannot model, completely omitting Laukkonen, Friston, and Chandaria (2025). Their "Beautiful Loop" active inference theory successfully and formally models meditative deconstruction and cessation as the volitional reduction of belief precision over hierarchical hidden states, providing exactly the robust physicalist mechanism the manuscript claims does not exist.
4. **Ignorance of the Minimal Phenomenal Experience Project:** The manuscript treats "pure awareness" and witness-consciousness as territories permanently opaque to scientific mapping, ignoring Metzinger's (2024) *The Elephant and the Blind*. Metzinger provides a rigorous, 500-subject psychometric operationalization and minimal model explanation for pure consciousness, directly falsifying the manuscript's claim that such states represent an unexplorable methodological void.
5. **Misattribution and Misapplication of Predictive Processing:** The manuscript misattributes a crucial citation to "Piotr Gładziejewski (2024)" when the author is Paweł Gładziejewski (2023). Furthermore, the manuscript misappropriates the REBUS model and Gładziejewski’s arguments regarding epistemic openness under psychedelics, ignoring that these authors use the physicalist Free-Energy Principle and predictive processing architectures to explain the integration of edge-states, rather than positing the dualist interactionist interface the manuscript attempts to graft onto their work.

Verdict: The manuscript requires outright rejection, as its foundational claims are empirically falsified by the very 2020-2025 literature it suppresses, and its epistemic architecture relies on vicious circularity rather than rigorous philosophical argumentation.
