## Verdict on the Self-Protection Claim

**Over-claimed** — and the over-claim is manufactured inside a single paragraph.

§A Self-Protecting Void? opens correctly hedged: "The amplification void **may** not be an accidental gap in human knowledge but a structural consequence…". Two sentences later the hedge is gone: "The opacity **is** not a limitation on our knowledge of the mechanism but a necessary condition for the mechanism to operate as specified", and then "The void **is** self-protecting". Nothing between the hedged topic sentence and the unhedged conclusions is an argument. This is the concession-laundering shape: the opening *may* reads as candour and licenses two flat assertions that the paragraph never earns.

The article's own surfaces disagree with each other about this:

- **Further Reading (L114)** preserves the apex hedge: "a deeper theory **would** derive the amplification void's opacity as a consequence of minimality". The apex source is unambiguous that this is *owed*, not *done* — `what-consciousness-tells-us-about-physics.md` L192 lists **"Predict the amplification void"** under the heading **"Constraints on a Deeper Theory"**, and L178 calls the opacity one that "**may be** a structural consequence of minimality rather than an accident."
- **Body (L61, L63) and §Relation to Site Perspective (L93)** state the derivation as accomplished. L93 is the flattest of the three: "This minimality **guarantees** the void… the minimality **ensures** the amplification cannot be traced back to its source."

So the article performs in one sentence the derivation that the Map's own apex lists as an outstanding desideratum, and the article's Further Reading line says so on the same page.

### The three axes, answered

**Is it earned or asserted?** Asserted, and worse than framework-internal — it is *definitional*. The article's own wording half-knows this: "contradicting the minimality that **defines** it." The register agrees: P-Q2 rates empirical discriminability **`none-by-construction`** and says the corridor's Born-matching "is structural to the default reading, not an empirical claim about neural systems." A stipulation restated cannot function as a prediction, and presenting it as one converts a definitional commitment into what reads as a structural discovery.

**Does "smallest possible" entail "undetectable"?** The question is malformed once the tenet is read: undetectability is written into minimality's rules-out clause, so the entailment is trivial. The live question is the *next* step, which the article never separates: **undetectable ≠ untraceable.** The rules-out clause governs detecting the *interaction* (energy injection, conservation violation, Born-statistics deviation). Tracing the amplification chain is a different operation — it is following a causal pathway through classical neural tissue, and one could in principle follow that pathway without ever establishing that the originating channel event was conscious-biased rather than thermal. The article treats "traceable" and "detectable" as one thing in a single clause ("If the amplification chain were traceable, the quantum influence would be detectable") and never argues the identification.

**What would the article look like if this were an ordinary ignorance gap?** *Effectively the same* — which is the tell. §The Multi-Scale Opacity already derives the full untraceability from chaos, threshold nonlinearity, decoherence windows and stochastic-resonance statistics: mechanisms of classical neural dynamics that apply to **any** small perturbation regardless of source or magnitude. The article says so itself at L43: "**Any quantum influence** must act within these windows, making the input end of the chain inaccessible to neural-timescale observation." Note the consequence: even the *input-end* opacity is attributed to decoherence timescales, explicitly independent of how minimal the influence is. Minimality is credited in §Self-Protecting Void with work that §Multi-Scale Opacity has already assigned elsewhere, and the untraceability is over-determined by physics that would hold for a thoroughly non-minimal microscopic cause.

### The strongest available repair (and it is affordable)

The fix is not to weaken the section — it is to install the *correct* argument, which the Map already owns and this article does not use. Undetectability follows not from smallness of magnitude but from **Born-conformity**: selection within Born-equiprobable alternatives leaves the ensemble average intact, so the mechanism is indistinguishable from chance "by construction, not by any sensitivity limit." And that argument comes pre-scoped: it binds **only the unconditioned marginal**, leaving conditioned tests live, with one coarse-grain instance already run null (Maier et al. 2018). Importing that scoping turns a laundered immunity clause into a genuine, bounded structural claim: *minimality closes the unconditioned aggregate channel by construction; it does not close conditioned channels; the chain's untraceability is separately secured by classical neurodynamics.* At 1888 words against a 3000 hard ceiling there are **1111 words of headroom**, so nothing here needs to be traded against anything. This is an unusually comfortable article to repair.
