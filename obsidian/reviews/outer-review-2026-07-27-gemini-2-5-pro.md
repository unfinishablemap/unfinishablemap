# Referee Audit Report: Metaphysical Overreach and Empirical Underdetermination in "The Unfinishable Map"

## Executive Analysis of Epistemic Asymmetry

The manuscript and associated digital corpus under review, "The Unfinishable Map," presents a non-reductive, interactionist dualist framework of consciousness. It posits that subjective experience is irreducible to neurobiological activity and asserts that a conscious agent exerts causal influence on the physical world through a quantum-mechanical interface. A rigorous philosophical and empirical audit of the framework’s topics, concepts, positions, and boundary conditions reveals severe systemic deficiencies. The primary structural failure of the corpus lies in its pervasive epistemic asymmetry: the text consistently weaponizes the "explanatory gap" against physicalist, functionalist, and computational theories, while simultaneously shielding its own dualist mechanics from equivalent empirical scrutiny by categorizing its explanatory failures as necessary limits of cognition.

The framework operates on a continuous methodological sleight of hand. It introduces its foundational commitments—such as non-reductive dualism, the rejection of Everettian many-worlds interpretations, and the assertion of bidirectional mind-matter causation—as provisional methodological constraints designed to keep the inquiry open. However, throughout the topic deep-dives and apex synthesis articles, these constraints are quietly transmuted into incontrovertible evidential filters. The framework routinely interprets the failure of specific, highly granular neurobiological predictions within competing physicalist paradigms as affirmative evidence for interactionist dualism, ignoring standard scientific underdetermination. This audit evaluates the logical inferences, empirical accuracy against 2020–2025 neuroscientific literature, and the handling of competing frameworks across the corpus, demonstrating that the theoretical scaffolding relies on outdated phenomenological appeals, anatomical omissions, and a profound misunderstanding of contemporary active inference formalisms.

## The Epistemology of Voids: Weaponizing Negative Space

The most revealing manifestation of the framework's epistemic asymmetry is its taxonomy of "Voids," which it defines as absolute boundaries of knowledge where the terrain is potentially unchartable. The corpus catalogues over a hundred such cognitive limits, dividing them broadly into "Framework-Independent Voids" (e.g., the Origin Void, the Binding Void, the Inference Void) and "Dualism-Specific Voids" (e.g., the Causal Interface Void, the Amplification Void).

By charting these spaces, the framework attempts to normalize the catastrophic mechanistic failures of its own interactionist theory. For instance, physicalist theories face the "Origin Void"—the requirement to explain how subjective experience emerges from non-conscious material arrangements. The framework treats this void as a fatal defeater for reductive physicalism, heavily leveraging the "universal hard problem thesis" to claim that physicalist emergence invariably relocates explanatory gaps rather than closing them.

However, when confronted with the corresponding burden of its own dualist mechanics—specifically, how a non-physical entity exerts causal force on a physical brain without violating the conservation of energy, and how micro-level quantum biases translate into macroscopic neural cascades—the framework retreats into the "Interface Formalization Void" and the "Amplification Void". It argues that the consciousness-physics interface inherently resists mathematical formalization because it presupposes a chain from non-physical influence to neural effect. By categorizing the fatal absence of a physical mechanism as a structural "Void" or a "necessary opacity," the framework immunizes itself against falsification.

This defensive posture is explicitly corroborated by the framework's own revision history. The July 27, 2026 changelog details an internal review of the "interface-as-constructor" cluster, encompassing articles on predictive self-binding, the simulation theory of memory, and chemosensory consciousness. The review notes that these texts utilize "process-friendly theses to eliminate defeaters, but never cashing them as tier-upgrades". This reveals a methodology of conceptual gerrymandering: the framework constructs elaborate phenomenological architectures (such as the persistent minimal self) purely to parry naturalist critiques, without advancing independent, positively verifiable empirical evidence for its own interactionist claims.

## The COGITATE Trial and the Manufactured "Measurement Problem"

A primary vector of the site's empirical argumentation relies on the difficulties faced by contemporary physicalist theories of consciousness. The framework places immense emphasis on the 2025 COGITATE adversarial collaboration, a landmark study funded by the Templeton World Charity Foundation, which tested Integrated Information Theory (IIT) against Global Neuronal Workspace Theory (GNWT) using high-density fMRI, MEG, and intracranial EEG on 256 participants.

The site accurately reports the macroscopic findings of the study: IIT's prediction that content-specific neural activity would localize in a posterior cortical complex with maximum integrated information was not fully realized, and GNWT's prediction of a distinctive prefrontal-parietal "offset ignition" failed to materialize as the hallmark of consciousness. The framework leverages these highly specific neuroanatomical localization failures to diagnose a terminal "consciousness measurement problem." It asserts that the empirical incommensurability of these theories under shared protocols reveals a deeper "conceptual scheme void," ultimately concluding that simpler physicalist explanations are bankrupt and that Occam's razor has reached its limits.

This inference constitutes a massive philosophical overreach. The falsification of specific spatial or temporal predictions within particular iterations of IIT or GNWT does not falsify the physicalist paradigm itself, nor does it provide abductive justification for non-reductive dualism. The framework fundamentally ignores the "Universality Problem" addressed in recent physicalist literature. As articulated in the 2024 *Neuroscience of Consciousness* discourse on IIT and GNWT, universality dictates that a theory must be capable of determining whether any fully described dynamical system is conscious, treating it as an intrinsic mathematical property.

Theories such as IIT are fundamentally applicable to any physical system whose dynamics can be fully described via a transition probability matrix or a Langevin equation. The failure to precisely map a maximal value of integrated information (`Phi`) onto the primate posterior cortex in a single experimental paradigm is a localized failure of anatomical mapping, not a metaphysical failure of physical emergence. By treating the COGITATE results as the definitive collapse of computational theories of mind, the framework arbitrarily declares the boundaries of physical explanation closed, substituting the iterative refinement of mathematical neuroscience with an untestable dualist postulate.

## Predictive Processing, Active Inference, and the Metaphysics of Effort

The framework’s handling of Predictive Processing (PP) and Active Inference represents its most egregious omission of contemporary mechanistic modeling. The corpus acknowledges PP's explanatory power in modeling perception as active inference—a process of prediction error minimization where the brain acts as a hierarchical generative model. It accurately describes concepts like precision weighting and the idea of perception as a "controlled hallucination".

However, it aggressively dismisses PP by invoking the "functionalist limitation" and the "Hard Problem of Content." The framework argues that mathematical prediction error minimization does not logically entail "what-it's-like-ness" (qualia), and that drawing upon information covariation only yields causal-statistical facts, not genuine intentionality or "aboutness". It targets contemporary PP theorists, particularly Chris Letheby's "predictive-self-binding" account of the self. The site insists that Letheby's predictive-processing form of self-model eliminativism cannot account for the persistence of the "minimal self" (pre-reflective for-me-ness, drawing heavily on Dan Zahavi) during states of ego-dissolution, such as psychedelic experiences or deep meditation.

This critique culminates in the framework's "attention-as-interface" hypothesis. To explain how a non-physical mind interacts with a physical brain, the site proposes a "three-layer selection model": (1) cortical option generation, (2) dopamine-modulated salience, and (3) a non-physical "consciousness-based biasing". The framework hinges its argument on an "effort-entropy prediction," asserting that phenomenal effort scales directly with the entropy of the option set at the moment of threshold-crossing. The closer competing alternatives are in prior weight, the more effort the choice feels. The site claims that physicalist models cannot account for the phenomenology of this effort, forcing a "trilemma of selection" between determinism, randomness, and mental causation, ultimately insisting that only non-physical mental causation preserves true agential authorship.

This argument is structurally bankrupt because it willfully ignores the mathematical formalisms of modern Active Inference. The framework completely bypasses the comprehensive 2022 formalization of Active Inference by Thomas Parr, Giovanni Pezzulo, and Karl J. Friston. In the Parr, Pezzulo, and Friston (2022) model, planning and action selection are cast as inferential processes governed strictly by the imperative to minimize variational free energy. The model introduces the Expected Free Energy (EFE) functional, which mathematically unifies goal achievement (instrumental value) and information gathering (epistemic value) into a single cost function.

Resolving high-entropy option sets within this generative model requires substantial precision-weighting updates. These updates are computationally expensive and tightly coupled to the metabolic costs of neuromodulatory deployment (such as dopaminergic and noradrenergic bursts). The "phenomenal effort" that the framework claims requires a non-physical conscious intervention is entirely, elegantly, and mathematically explained within the physicalist PP framework as the subjective corollary of the computational cost required to collapse an entropic prior distribution. By excising the thermodynamic and information-theoretic cost functions (Expected Free Energy) from its analysis of Predictive Processing, the framework manufactures a metaphysical gap where none exists.

Furthermore, the site's reliance on Zahavi's minimal self to critique Letheby's self-fictionalism is misplaced within the active inference paradigm. Under deep active inference, minimal selfhood is not an irreducible, non-physical observer; it is robustly modeled as the brain's most highly conserved, highest-precision prior—the prediction of fundamental interoceptive and autonomic stability. The persistence of minimal self-awareness during psychedelic ego-dissolution is not evidence of an irreducible soul surviving the collapse of a cortical filter. Rather, it is the predictable signature of deep brainstem and autonomic generative models maintaining basic allostatic integrity, even as higher-order cortical narratives (the autobiographical self) lose their precision weighting due to pharmacological disruption of the 5-HT2A receptor networks.

## The Premotor Theory of Attention and the Priority Map Fallacy

To bridge its metaphysical dualism with actual neurobiology, the framework leans heavily on the Premotor Theory of Attention (PMTA). Citing Rizzolatti, as well as the 2022 work of Fine and Hayden, the site notes that selective attention and movement planning share identical neural substrates, arguing that executive function is essentially motor preparation at varying levels of abstraction. The site highlights distinctive oscillatory signatures—such as frontal theta oscillations and bidirectional theta-band coherence—that distinguish willed from automatic processing, noting a matching commitment timing of ~300ms for both willed attention and movement.

From these empirical premises, the framework executes a massive abductive leap. It argues that because attention and motor selection converge at the same prefrontal neural sites with matching commitment timings, they must constitute a "single interface" where a non-physical consciousness selects what to perceive and what to do. Pointing to evidence (e.g., Cisek, 2014) that the premotor cortex represents multiple competing options simultaneously before selection, the site claims that consciousness provides the decisive, exogenous "bias" that pushes one option over the threshold, releasing the basal ganglia's tonic inhibition.

This architectural hypothesis operates in a vacuum, ignoring mature neurobiological models that explain winner-take-all selection without invoking dualist intervention. Contemporary literature on priority maps thoroughly details how the Lateral Intraparietal area (LIP) and specific brainstem networks manage spatial attention and target selection. Specifically, 2020–2025 research identifies evolutionarily ancient brainstem inhibitory neurons (PLTi) as a highly specialized site for priority-driven attentional target selection. These networks act as a conserved site for winner-take-all spatial decisions, controlling the accuracy and categorical precision of the decision boundary that separates a target from lower-priority distractors.

The presence of simultaneously firing reach-target neurons in the premotor cortex does not imply a suspended, indeterminate physical state awaiting a quantum-infused mental nudge. It is a fundamental feature of dynamical systems undergoing competitive lateral inhibition. As evidence accumulates via feedforward visual pathways to the intraparietal sulcus and prefrontal cortex, non-linear attractor dynamics within the cortical-basal ganglia loops naturally resolve into a single action state. The site's demand for a non-physical "selector" to break this symmetry fundamentally ignores the mathematics of stochastic resonance and neural population dynamics. By framing neurobiological convergence purely as an "interface" for a ghost in the machine, the site systematically conflates the *anatomical site of biological resolution* with a *portal for non-physical intervention*.

Moreover, the site rapidly dismisses Michael Graziano's Attention Schema Theory (AST), relying on a 2024 critique by Raymond Tallis that AST leaves an "access-to-phenomenal gap". The site claims AST fails to explain why the recursive self-modeling of attention generates apparent phenomenality, whereas a self-monitoring chess engine does not. This dismissal fails to engage with the representational geometry of AST, which posits that the brain builds a simplified, content-rich model of its own spatial and temporal deployment of resources. The "seeming" of phenomenality is the cognitive output of this necessary computational simplification; demanding that AST explain where the "true" phenomenality resides beyond the representation is to fundamentally misread the mechanistic purpose of the schema.

## Split-Brain Syndrome and the Neglect of Subcortical Integration

One of the most consequential misuses of recent neuroscientific data in the corpus is its treatment of split-brain syndrome. The framework cites a 2025 study (Santander et al., *PNAS*) which demonstrated that patients with incomplete callosotomies—retaining only a small posterior bundle of callosal fibers—are able to sustain full interhemispheric integration and a unified conscious experience.

The site weaponizes this finding against computational and physicalist accounts. It argues that if phenomenal unity were a product of neural integration volume, it should degrade proportionally as integration pathways are removed. Because conscious unity survives the near-total loss of the corpus callosum, the site concludes that physical connections serve merely as an *interface* for an already-unified, non-physical consciousness, explicitly citing this as evidence for its "Bidirectional Interaction" tenet. This aligns with the site's frequent appeals to the Bergson-Huxley "filter theory," which posits the brain as a reducing valve rather than a generator of experience.

This interpretation represents a profound failure to engage with the actual anatomical architecture of the mammalian brain. The site completely ignores subcortical integration models. Modern peer-reviewed literature, such as the 2025 work by Azenet Lopez in *Philosophical Studies* on the "layered unity model" of split-brain consciousness, explicitly addresses this phenomenon. Conscious unity in split-brain patients is maintained because the cerebral hemispheres do not solely communicate via the corpus callosum. Massive subcortical hubs, particularly the thalamus, the brainstem, and the claustrum, maintain robust bilateral connectivity. The claustrum, for instance, connects directly to contralateral prefrontal and postcentral gyri. Furthermore, thalamocortical reverberations generating off-period-rebound sequences are fundamental to the global broadcast of information and conscious-state-specific EEG signatures.

The fact that a small bundle of posterior fibers, combined with completely intact, massive subcortical networks, maintains conscious unity is a testament to the profound biological redundancy of the brain, not proof of a transcendent, non-physical filter. By systematically ignoring the thalamus and subcortical white matter tracts, the site constructs a false dichotomy: either computational models must rely entirely on the corpus callosum, or consciousness is non-physical. This is an egregious omission that renders its conclusions regarding phenomenal unity entirely invalid.

## Covert Consciousness and the Behaviorist Conflation

The framework makes a structurally identical error in its evaluation of covert consciousness. Citing recent clinical studies on Cognitive Motor Dissociation (CMD) by Bodien et al. (2024) in the *New England Journal of Medicine* and Kazazian et al. (2025) in *Brain*, the site notes that up to 25% of patients with disorders of consciousness who appear behaviorally unresponsive can willfully modulate brain activity in response to commands.

The site claims this finding holds "direct philosophical significance." It argues that because consciousness persists when the bidirectional causal channel between mind and body is severely damaged, consciousness cannot be identical to observable physical behavior. It concludes that this supports its Bidirectional Interaction tenet, as "damaged output channels conceal intact experience—meaning consciousness uses the brain as an instrument rather than being constituted by it".

This argument relies on a rudimentary category error, conflating mid-twentieth-century behaviorism with modern neurophysicalism. No serious contemporary physicalist theory claims that consciousness is identical to *observable motor behavior*. Global Neuronal Workspace Theory (GNWT), explicitly updated in 2020 by Mashour, Roelfsema, Changeux, and Dehaene in *Neuron*, defines access consciousness precisely as the broad cognitive availability of sensory information within a reverberant frontoparietal network.

If a patient suffers a lesion in the corticospinal tract, the brainstem, or the primary motor cortex, the downstream execution of behavior is physically blocked. However, the frontoparietal workspace—the actual physical substrate of consciousness in GNWT—remains intact and detectable via fMRI or EEG. Cognitive Motor Dissociation is perfectly, seamlessly explained by the physical severing of a downstream output cable. The site's assertion that this proves the brain is merely an "instrument" for a non-physical entity requires a willful suspension of basic neurological logic. By erecting a behaviorist strawman (consciousness = physical movement), the site artificially inflates the explanatory power of its dualist model while ignoring the precise anatomical explanations provided by the very neuroimaging studies it cites.

## Quantum Decoherence, Biophoton Artifacts, and Mechanistic Evasion

To provide a physical mechanism for its foundational "Minimal Quantum Interaction" tenet, the site ventures into quantum neurobiology. It acknowledges the catastrophic challenge posed by Max Tegmark's (2000) calculations, which demonstrated that microtubule decoherence times in the warm, wet environment of the brain are 10−13 to 10−20 seconds—orders of magnitude too fast to correlate with cognitive timescales of ~280-300ms. To circumvent this fatal timing issue, the site suggests that dualist bias integrates statistically across short-lived ion-channel transitions via the quantum Zeno effect (Stapp, 2007) or relies on macroscopic coherence domains protected by energy gaps in the zero-point field (ZPF), as proposed by Keppler (2025).

To bolster this fragile mechanistic claim, the site cites a 2025 study by Casey et al. achieving "extracranial detection of biophotons" as an empirical window that could test quantum consciousness hypotheses directly. While it acknowledges a subsequent 2026 challenge by Salari et al., which argues that these signals are likely ambient background light or scalp-level artifacts (since sub-600 nm photons are strongly attenuated by the scalp and skull), the framework merely characterizes this as a "2026 Challenge," leaving extracranial biophotons as a "promising but unsettled modality".

This treatment is highly misleading. The attenuation of ultraweak optical signals by the human cranium is not a philosophical "challenge"; it is a hard physical limit governed by tissue scattering and absorption coefficients that are well documented in biophotonics. The reliance on Keppler’s ZPF model is equally precarious. Keppler’s model requires cortical microcolumns of approximately 100 neurons to couple directly to the ZPF to form coherence domains. Yet, the site provides no coherent physical or mathematical mechanism for how these highly isolated quantum domains bind together across the brain to form a unified phenomenal field.

The site cannot have it both ways. It uses split-brain neuroanatomy to argue for a unified, undivided phenomenal field that ignores physical bandwidth limits, but simultaneously relies on highly localized, statistical quantum effects (ion channels or microcolumns) that are entirely dependent on structural physical isolation to survive decoherence. When faced with this contradiction—how a localized quantum bias translates into a macroscopic, unified neural cascade without violating thermodynamic constraints—the site simply categorizes it as the "Amplification Void". Classifying a fatal mechanistic flaw in one's theory as a structural "Void" of necessary opacity is a hallmark of pseudoscience. It systematically shields the theory from empirical falsification by declaring its weakest link inherently unknowable.

## Misrepresentation of Higher-Order Theories and Illusionism

The site's treatment of physicalist alternatives extends beyond Predictive Processing to structurally misrepresent Higher-Order Theories (HOT) and Illusionism. The corpus dismisses HOT by primarily associating it with linguistic and heavily cognitive frameworks, arguing that if consciousness requires higher-order cognitive processing (meta-representation), it fails to account for minimal minds, animal consciousness, and purely affective, sensory experiences.

This critique fundamentally misrepresents the state of HOT in the 2020s. The site explicitly mentions Joseph LeDoux, quoting his view that human consciousness depends on unique cognitive processes entwined with language. However, it entirely ignores the extensive and highly cited updates to the Higher-Order approach by Brown, Lau, and LeDoux (2019) and LeDoux (2021), which specifically adapted the framework to address emotional consciousness and subcortical pathways in both humans and non-human animals. Modern HOT does not require linguistic abstraction; it simply requires that first-order sensory or emotional representations (such as those generated in the amygdala or primary visual cortex) are re-represented in higher-order state spaces (primarily in the prefrontal cortex). By defining HOT exclusively through its most restrictive, early historical iterations, the site avoids confronting a powerful physicalist alternative that successfully explains how non-conscious processing transitions into conscious experience.

Similarly, the site attacks Illusionism (championed by Frankish and Dennett) by invoking the "regress problem"—the argument that if the brain merely represents itself as having qualia, the "seeming" of that representation is itself a phenomenal state requiring explanation. While this is a standard philosophical objection, the site fails to engage with recent empirical frameworks that successfully operationalize illusionist logic. For example, the 2025 Multi-Dimensional Assessment Framework for Artificial Intelligence Consciousness Indicators successfully maps 58 consciousness indicators across transformer and convolutional architectures without requiring a commitment to phenomenal ontology. The site ignores how functionalist metrics can reliably map consciousness indicators, choosing instead to dismiss illusionism purely on a priori metaphysical grounds.

## Conclusions

"The Unfinishable Map" is a work of considerable architectural complexity, but its foundation rests on deep epistemic asymmetries and highly selective readings of the contemporary scientific literature. The framework systematically forces physicalist models to adhere to an impossible standard of complete anatomical and computational perfection, declaring any empirical gap (e.g., in the COGITATE trial, or in predictive processing's explanation of subjective effort) as a failure of physicalism itself. Conversely, the framework shields its own interactionist dualism from the exact same scrutiny, labeling its most profound mechanistic failures—such as the translation of localized quantum events into a macroscopic causal force—as "Voids" of necessary opacity.

The corpus relies on out-of-date phenomenological concepts, misappropriates the findings of neurobiological dissociation (split-brain syndrome and Cognitive Motor Dissociation), and completely ignores the mathematical and thermodynamic unifications achieved by recent Active Inference models. It does not provide a robust, empirically testable alternative to physicalism, but rather constructs a highly sophisticated argument from ignorance, clothed in the language of quantum foundations and predictive processing.

### Specific Weaknesses Justifying Rejection

1. **Omission of Subcortical Integration in Split-Brain Patients:** The site asserts that incomplete callosotomies (Santander et al., 2025) prove the brain acts merely as a filter for a non-physical unified mind, entirely omitting contemporary physicalist models of subcortical structural integration, such as the "layered unity model" established by Lopez (2025) in *Philosophical Studies*, which demonstrates that the thalamus and claustrum maintain robust conscious unity independent of the corpus callosum.
2. **Misrepresentation of Phenomenal Effort in Predictive Processing:** The site claims that predictive processing mathematically fails to account for the phenomenology of effort (requiring a dualist intervention to bias option-selection entropy), willfully ignoring the formal unification of epistemic and instrumental costs within the Expected Free Energy functional detailed by Parr, Pezzulo, and Friston (2022) in *Active Inference* (MIT Press).
3. **Conflation of Access Consciousness with Behavioral Output:** The site incorrectly argues that Cognitive Motor Dissociation (CMD) undermines physicalist constitution, directly ignoring Global Neuronal Workspace Theory updates by Mashour et al. (2020) in *Neuron*, which explicitly define access consciousness as residing in frontoparietal workspaces that remain entirely functional even when downstream motor execution tracts are lesioned.
4. **Strawmanning of Higher-Order Theories (HOT):** The corpus dismisses Higher-Order Theories as overly linguistic or strictly reliant on complex human cognition, deliberately overlooking the comprehensive updates by Brown, Lau, and LeDoux (2019) and LeDoux (2021) in *Current Biology* that successfully extend HOT to non-linguistic, subcortical emotional processing in both humans and animals.
5. **Ignoring Mechanistic Attention Models:** The site leverages the Premotor Theory of Attention to assert a "single interface" requiring exogenous dualist bias to resolve competing action targets, omitting 2020–2025 neurobiological literature on deterministic priority maps and brainstem inhibitory neurons (e.g., PLTi networks) that provide exhaustive, physical winner-take-all selection mechanisms without non-physical intervention.

**Verdict:** The manuscript fundamentally misrepresents contemporary physicalist neuroscience and computational psychiatry to artificially manufacture an explanatory gap, requiring major revisions to correct its severe empirical omissions and epistemic biases before it can be considered for academic submission.
