## Critiques by Philosopher

### The Eliminative Materialist (Churchland)
The 2026-06-17 debiasing was real and I credit it — phenomenology and cross-cultural convergence are now explicitly discounted under the intuitive-dualism common-cause null. But look at what survives: P-A1 says the convergence claim now "rests on the genuinely independent subset, the neural willed-vs-instructed data and the argument from reason." That is a *convergence* of two. One of them is an argument, not data. The other is an fMRI/EEG contrast that the register's own 2026-08-20 note says is "consistent with rather than establishing conscious engagement." So the word *convergent* — which is carrying the whole inference to substance-agent authorship now that the trilemma has been conceded non-exhaustive — is doing work over a set of two, one of which the register says does not establish the thesis. Churchland's line writes itself: you debiased honestly, and did not then re-ask whether what remained was still a convergence.

## Critical Issues

### Issue 1: P-A1's anchor accounting does not reconcile with itself, and the one empirical anchor is stated less carefully here than anywhere else in the corpus
- **File**: `obsidian/positions/agency-and-will.md`, P-A1, **L47–L48** (one locus, three sub-defects)
- **Severity**: High — this is the register's spine (`structural centrality high`; P-A2, P-A3, P-A5 hang from it), and all three sub-defects sit in the sentence that states what the case actually rests on.

**(a) Five anchors in, two discounted, two out — clinical neuroplasticity vanishes.** L48 names five: *"the phenomenology of effort and willing, neural data distinguishing willed from instructed action, the argument from reason, clinical neuroplasticity, and cross-cultural convergence on substance-causal accounts of persons"*. It then discounts two: *"The phenomenology and cross-cultural anchors are discounted for the intuitive-dualism common-cause null"*. It then names the survivors: *"so the convergence claim rests on the genuinely independent subset, the neural willed-vs-instructed data and the argument from reason."* Five minus two is three. **Clinical neuroplasticity is neither discounted with a reason nor retained in the subset.** This is an error in the *conservative* direction — the register under-counts its own case — but it is still unaccounted, and it is not a trivial item: `concepts/agent-causation` (P-A1's own `Argued in`) gives it an independent argument of its own, that CBT and pharmacological interventions reach comparable outcomes "through *different neural pathways* … a divergence mental causation predicts and pure redescription cannot easily explain." The 2026-06-17 fix rewrote this sentence to install the discount and dropped the item while rewriting.

**(b) The register names the wrong modality for its own load-bearing anchor.** P-A1 says *"neural data distinguishing willed from instructed **action**"*. The cited study is Rajan et al. 2019, *"Theta oscillations index frontal decision-making and mediate reciprocal frontal-parietal interactions in **willed attention**"*. `concepts/motor-selection` L153 — the anchor article the 2026-08-20 sweep cites — states this correctly and marks the extension as the Map's own inference: *"Willed attention shows greater frontal theta power and bidirectional frontoparietal coherence than instructed attention (Rajan et al. 2019)—a relative increase rather than an all-or-none marker. Motor control shows the same pattern"* — with **no citation attached to the motor sentence**. `concepts/agent-causation` L162 states it most honestly of all: *"Frontal-theta and coherence signatures, with latencies of the same order, recur across attention and motor domains, weighing against single-domain artefacts."* P-A1 is the **least careful statement of this anchor in the corpus**, and it is the one that governs.

**(c) The 2026-08-20 scope-narrowing landed in the position where the evidence is incidental, not the one where it is load-bearing.** P-A2's `Updated 2026-08-20` (L66) re-scoped this exact evidence: *"the effect is a relative frontal-theta increase from ~500 ms post-cue (Rajan et al. 2019), **consistent with rather than establishing conscious engagement**"*, closing "Grounds restated only; no calibration or status change." That is correct for P-A2, where the evidence appears inside a "Bears on" note. It was never asked of P-A1, where after the 2026-06-17 debiasing this is **one of two surviving anchors**. L47's calibration line still cites it flatly — *"external-evidence grade C (neural willed-vs-instructed data and the argument from reason)"* — with no trace of the narrowing.

- **Recommendation** (`positions-evolve`, length-neutral, all three in one edit): in L48, restore clinical neuroplasticity to the independent subset (or state in four words why it is discounted); change *"instructed action"* to *"instructed attention, extended to motor selection"*; and import P-A2's own phrase — *"consistent with rather than establishing"* — into the subset sentence, replacing words rather than adding them. **Whether grade C survives (b)+(c) is a judgement for the register's owner, not for this review**; flag it, do not pre-empt it. Do not restate the argument in `concepts/agent-causation` or `concepts/motor-selection` — both are already correct.
