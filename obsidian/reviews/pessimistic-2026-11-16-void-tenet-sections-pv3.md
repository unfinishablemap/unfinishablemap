P-V1 (L43) supplies the test this review applies to every page: "clustering at the interface does not *discriminate* the Map from those rivals" and "The defensible claim is that the tenets and the void catalogue fit without strain, not that independent cataloguing confirmed antecedent predictions." P-V2 (L55) adds that "a tenet which dissolves an objection to a void's dualist reading removes a *defeater* but does not, by itself, raise the void's independence score".

Applying those definitions before alleging a conflict: P-V3 suspends the *cumulative* step, the one by which the catalogue as a whole would count. It does not forbid a void page from reporting an argument that stands outside the catalogue (the knowledge argument, conceivability). So a page conflicts with the register in two ways only: by saying the void itself supports dualism, or by presenting a datum every rival predicts as if it favoured the Map. A page that says "the Map's ground here is argument X, contested at point Y, and the void adds nothing to it" is consistent with the register. That is the standard used below, and it is why four pages are class (b) and not class (a).

### 2. `voids/disappearance-voids` — class (a)

**Dualism, L162.** "gains support from all three mechanisms" (count 1). Three data are offered and each fails the test.
- Absorption: self-awareness drops while experience intensifies, "suggesting it is not reducible to its own self-monitoring". The page's own L140 cites "DMN suppression during flow and meditation" as "third-person confirmation of a genuine structural feature". A neural account of the dissociation is therefore already on the page.
- Habituation: processing continues while awareness lapses, "supporting the claim that phenomenal consciousness is something over and above functional competence" (count 1). Processing outside awareness is the founding datum of workspace theories. It shows that awareness is unnecessary for that competence, which is a point physicalists and epiphenomenalists cite, and it sits awkwardly beside the next paragraph's claim that consciousness does causal work.
- Anaesthesia: "a dissociation harder to explain as a single neural hierarchy than as dissociable couplings" (count 1). Dissociable couplings are neural couplings. The contrast is between two physical models.

**Bidirectional Interaction, L164.** "demonstrating that consciousness actively selects its own mode of operation" (count 1; tenet check 2026-10-21 quoted it, never minted). Trained entry into a state is a skill on every account; tenet 3 is "not […] a directly introspectible datum". The fatigue sentence ("an order that tracks how much deliberate engagement each requires") says nothing about causation but claims little; leave it.

**Occam's-limits, L166.** "is directly implicated" by a pun: habituation as "cognitive simplification", "simplest is not richest". None of the three examples concerns parsimony in theory choice. Low severity, and the page has no room; listed, not priced.

**Over-concession**: none. **Altered-state symmetry**: the page is not filter-framed and cites anaesthesia itself; the audit does not fire.

### 6. `voids/binding-void` — class (b)

**Dualism, L133.** "gains indirect support" (count 1). The page is the most register-aware of the ten: L131 says the route is apophatic, "the absence of a competing explanation rather than positive evidence", L95 says the circularity diagnosis "is consistent with a genuine boundary without separating a boundary from an unfinished research programme", and L125 cites P-V1 and P-V2. The Dualism paragraph then reverts. Its argument is the unity argument, which the tenets page lists among the positive arguments "each opponent disputes at a different point" (L55). The step is: "if phenomenal binding resists analysis into anything more basic, no account of the physical parts delivers the unity" (count 1). That moves from *unanalysable into simpler experiential relations* (what the survey of Dainton, Bayne, Chalmers and Tye at L95 concerns) to *not deliverable by the physical parts*. The first is a claim about the structure of experience; the second about its realisation. The evidence supports the first and the conclusion needs the second. This is the epistemic/metaphysical pattern the review skill names, at medium severity, since the sentence is conditional.

## Critiques by Philosopher

### The Eliminative Materialist
Churchland would read the ten Dualism paragraphs as one argument made ten times: something about the mind is hard to explain, therefore it is not physical. She would point out that six of the ten data (habituation, absorption, fluency-driven confidence, superadditive interference, sensorimotor grounding, binding by synchrony) come from her own field's literature, and that the pages cite the neuroscience for the phenomenon and then credit dualism with the explanation. `disappearance-voids` L140 and L162 is the sharpest case: DMN suppression confirms the structure, and four sections later the structure supports dualism.

Dennett's target would be `noetic-feelings-void`. The page establishes that felt obviousness is manufactured and unauditable, then exempts the one class of feelings the Map leans on. ### The Quantum Skeptic
Tegmark would note that across ten tenet sections the Minimal Quantum Interaction paragraphs contain no physics. `binding-void` L135 counts acts, `noetic-feelings-void` L181 matches terseness to bandwidth, and `apophatic-cartography` L160 answers a decoherence calculation with a counsel of humility. ### The Many-Worlds Defender
Deutsch would take `apophatic-cartography` L162 and `meta-epistemology-of-limits` L166 together. ### The Empiricist
The Popperian complaint is about asymmetry. `apophatic-cartography` sets out operational expiration conditions for a void (L84) and then, in its tenet section, treats non-expiration as evidence. A method that counts every surviving gap for its hypothesis and explains every closed gap as a lesson learned (L86-88) cannot lose. `meta-epistemology-of-limits` L174 comes close to saying so: "The hard problem remains as puzzling after 30 years […] the situation the suspended position predicts." A suspended position predicts nothing.

### The Buddhist Philosopher
Nagarjuna would press `binding-void`. The page takes the unity of the field as given and asks what binds it; the Madhyamaka answer is that the unity is itself constructed and the search for a binder reifies it. Tenet 4's own text grants this route "stays open at the framework boundary" (L121). The page's NMW paragraph, which speaks of "*this* unified field", would benefit from one clause acknowledging that the unity is a premise.
