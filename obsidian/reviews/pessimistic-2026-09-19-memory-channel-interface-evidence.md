## Executive Summary

This is the first **adversarial** pass on an article that has had four deep reviews, all of which ran metadata, quote-fidelity or empirical-paraphrase lenses. Those lenses have left the citations in good order; this pass therefore targets the **argument** instead, and the argument has a consistent weakness: the article's comparative ledger — how much each framework must pay to accommodate the evidence — is **inflated on both sides**. The convergence set is counted as five mechanism-independent confirmations when at least two members share a common explanatory variable supplied by the article's own anchor citation; the production account is charged for generic dynamical properties it would hold anyway; and the filter reading is credited with "deriving" a direction-sensitive signature by a sentence that forbids nothing. One clean internal contradiction sits between the cross-state table and the claim stated nine lines below it.

The article's calibration discipline is genuinely good — it concedes accommodation-not-prediction, concedes the bandwidth-assignment circularity, and concedes that terminal lucidity is its weakest link. The findings below are almost all cases where a concession the article *already makes in one place* is not carried to the place where it bites.

## Critiques by Philosopher

### The Eliminative Materialist (Churchland)

"You have built an 'architecture' out of a taxonomy. Tulving's three-way split is a *classification of reports*, and you have promoted it to a set of physical channels with bandwidths and reconstruction costs. The tell is your own admission at §122 that bandwidth and reconstruction cost 'are ranked partly by the very ordering they are then invoked to explain.' That is not a mild caveat — it is the whole mechanism. Strip it and there is no architecture, only a relabelled table. Meanwhile the ordering you find so remarkable is what any neuroscientist predicts: the functions that recruit the most distributed coordination fail first under any insult. You have not found a signature; you have found that complicated things break first."

Worse, your depersonalisation row — content present, mine-ness absent — is a *textbook* case for the heterophenomenological treatment: the subject's report of ownership comes apart from the content because ownership is itself a representational achievement that can fail. ## Priority Findings

Four findings, ranked. Each carries a **verbatim locus string** rather than a line number.

### P1 — The convergence is counted as five independent confirmations; the article's own anchor citation supplies a variable common to at least two of them

- **Severity**: High
- **Locus (verbatim)**: `share no common neural cause, yet they converge on the same ordering of channel vulnerability`
- **Section**: *The Structural Argument: Graded-with-Separable-Channels*

**Problem.** The entire load of this section rests on a count: five mechanism-distinct perturbations, one ordering, therefore an explanandum no single-state account can address. But two sections earlier the article cites **Sarasso et al. (2015)** as "the sharpest case," and what that paper measures is *cortical-response complexity* — a single variable that, in the article's own summary, is low under propofol and xenon (no experience) and wakefulness-like under ketamine (experience). That is a common explanatory variable, not an absence of one. The same integration/complexity measure is the standard bridge between anaesthesia and NREM sleep, which are two of the five members. So the five members are not five independent instances: anaesthesia and NREM plausibly reduce to one explanatory situation, and hemispherectomy (reduced cortical mass) arguably makes it closer to one-and-a-half than to three.

This is convergence double-counting in the form the skill's altered-state section names — a cluster contributing the evidential weight of *one* pattern being cited as *N* confirmations. It matters because §144 explicitly settles the comparison on the cumulative count: *"The cumulative cost across the hierarchy, the dissociation architecture, terminal lucidity, the propofol/ketamine divergence, and the four direction-of-interface signatures is where the comparison comes due."*

**What survives.** The **dissociative rows do not reduce**, and the article already knows this — it calls them "the hierarchy's hardest test for production theories" precisely because there is no substrate-degradation variable to appeal to. That is the part of the convergence argument that is not vulnerable to the deflation.

**Recommendation.** Narrow `share no common neural cause` to a claim about *molecular* mechanism, and add an explicit concession that anaesthesia and NREM share a measured cortical-integration variable — then relocate the argumentative weight onto the dissociative rows, which genuinely carry it. This makes the article *more* honest without weakening its best case, and it discharges the objection before an outer reviewer raises it.

### P2 — The recovery-asymmetry ledger is inflated on both sides: the filter reading "derives" nothing, and the production reading is charged for generic dynamics

- **Severity**: High
- **Locus A (verbatim)**: `may depend on the direction and cause of change`
- **Locus B (verbatim)**: `hysteresis terms for the bistable switch, a dying-process neuromodulatory surge for terminal lucidity, a stochastic reconstitution model for emergence, a connectivity-gating story for dissociation`
- **Locus C (verbatim)**: `predicts, in its simplest form, that recovery should retrace degradation`
- **Section**: *Recovery-Order Asymmetry: The Direction-of-Travel Face*

**Problem A — the credit side.** The sentence carrying the filter reading's advantage says orderings "*may depend on* the direction and cause of change." That permits every ordering, including the mirror-symmetric one the production account is faulted for. A model that fails to forbid the complement of the observed result has not derived the result. Yet the surrounding prose awards comparative credit — *"appears to derive a direction-sensitive signature from its core architecture without per-case additions"*, *"direct where production's is additive"*. The article already made the matching concession on the substrate-state face (`Bandwidth and reconstruction cost are ranked partly by the very ordering they are then invoked to explain`), but does not carry it across to the direction face, where it is *worse*: on the substrate face at least a ranking exists; on the direction face nothing at all is assigned.

**Problem B — the debit side.** Of the four "per-case additions" charged to the production theorist, two are the same commitment and neither is an addition. **Hysteresis is a generic property of any bistable nonlinear system**, and the article's own source (Sepúlveda et al. 2019) presents neural inertia as a property of circuits, established independently of the mind-brain question. **Stochastic emergence timing (Stone et al. 2025) is the expected behaviour** of a noisy bistable system crossing a threshold — it is a consequence of the same commitment, not a second auxiliary. So the genuine additions number **two** (terminal lucidity's neuromodulatory surge; dissociation's connectivity gating), not four, and the "four direction-of-interface signatures" in §144 repeats the inflated count.

**Problem C — the target.** `predicts, in its simplest form, that recovery should retrace degradation` attributes to the production theorist a position no production theorist holds; state hysteresis in neural transitions long predates this debate. Charging the theorist for abandoning it is charging for ground never occupied.

**Recommendation.** Three edits, all length-neutral or length-reducing: (i) downgrade `may depend on` to an explicit statement of what the bandwidth/reconstruction-cost architecture **forbids**, or replace "derive" with "is consistent with" and carry the §122 unmeasured-assignment concession forward in full; (ii) reduce the additivity charge to the two cases that genuinely require novel commitments, naming bistable-with-noise dynamics as one pre-existing commitment covering both anaesthesia signatures; (iii) replace the "simplest form" straw target with a named position an actual production theorist defends.

### P3 — The cross-state table contains a counterexample to the ordering claim stated nine lines below it

- **Severity**: Medium-High
- **Locus (verbatim, table row)**: `| Ketamine "disconnected" state | Preserved | Variable | Preserved but content-decoupled |`
- **Locus (verbatim, claim)**: `autonoetic goes first under degradation`
- **Section**: *The Cross-State Vulnerability Pattern*

**Problem.** The ketamine row records **noetic as "Variable"** and **autonoetic as "Preserved but content-decoupled"**. On that row autonoetic is the *better-preserved* channel and noetic is the impaired one — the ordering inverts. Nine lines later the article states flatly that `autonoetic goes first under degradation`. The hedge that follows (`but in none of them arbitrarily`) does not cover this: an inversion is not arbitrariness, it is a counterexample, and it sits inside the very table the claim generalises over.

The depersonalisation row is a milder version of the same thing (`Present but felt as not-mine` — the channel is not severed), and the article *does* handle that one, by decomposing autonoetic into content + mine-ness + pastness. Ketamine gets no equivalent treatment, even though the anaesthesia section leans on it heavily as "the sharpest case within it."

**Why this is worth fixing rather than hedging.** Ketamine is the article's best *material* — a dissociative anaesthetic that preserves experience while abolishing environmental coupling is a genuinely awkward datum for a simple vulnerability gradient, and an interesting one for a channel architecture (it looks like a *coupling* failure rather than a *channel* failure). Reading it as an exception that the architecture explains is stronger than leaving it as an unremarked contradiction for an outer reviewer to find.

**Recommendation.** Either (a) qualify the ordering claim to the substrate-degradation cases and explicitly exempt the decoupling cases (ketamine, depersonalisation, DID) as a distinct signature type, or (b) keep the generalisation and add one sentence reading ketamine as decoupling-not-degradation. Option (a) is cleaner and costs ~15 words.

### P4 — The Tenet 3 argument does not discriminate: its counterfactual is unargued and may name the same channel

- **Severity**: Medium
- **Locus (verbatim)**: `the most vulnerable channel would be the input-richest one`
- **Section**: *Relation to Site Perspective* → **Bidirectional Interaction**

**Problem.** The Tenet 3 case runs: the most vulnerable, most direction-swinging channel is the one with the most *active subject-participation*, therefore reception-only is disfavoured. The contrast rests entirely on the counterfactual that a merely-receiving consciousness would have its **input-richest** channel be most vulnerable. Two things are wrong with it:

1. **It is asserted, not argued.** No reason is given why passive reception should make the input-richest channel the most fragile. A competing intuition runs the other way — a passive receiver should be most vulnerable where the *transmission* is thinnest, not where the content is richest.
2. **It may not be a contrast at all.** Autonoetic re-experiencing plausibly *is* the input-richest channel: it integrates more perceptual, temporal and self-referential content than noetic or anoetic. If so, the reception-only reading and the bidirectional reading predict the **same** channel, and the observation supports neither over the other. The article never checks whether its two rival predictions come apart.

The paragraph closes well — the conditional (`Should the autonoetic channel prove to return first under falling substrate function and last under rising…`) is properly undischarged and honestly marked. The defect is upstream of it, in the premise that sets up the contrast.

**Recommendation.** Either supply the missing argument for why reception-only predicts input-richness-tracking vulnerability, or — cheaper and more honest — delete the counterfactual clause and rest the Tenet 3 engagement entirely on the conditional the paragraph already ends with, which is genuinely discriminating and already correctly hedged.
