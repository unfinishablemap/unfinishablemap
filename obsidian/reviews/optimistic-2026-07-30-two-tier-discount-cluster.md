## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

Chalmers would find his own dialectical position protected by the Map at the Map's expense. `epistemology-of-convergence-arguments.md` L108 works through the catalogue's best convergence exhibit — lesion, pharmacology and psychogenic dissociation agreeing on one channel taxonomy — and then states where its reach stops: the dissociation pattern "tells strongly against naive identity theory ... but sophisticated functionalism — Block's access/phenomenal distinction, multi-realisability, integrationist readings — predicts the same modular decomposition." The verdict is *compatibility*-grade, "not *vindication*-grade, for the interface reading ... because both readings seem to predict the identical dissociation phenomenology." Chalmers has spent thirty years insisting the hard problem is not touched by structural-functional success; here the Map applies that same asymmetry to itself and concedes that its own best structural evidence does not reach the metaphysics.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

Stapp's interest is served by the fourth exhibit, which shows the split is not an artefact of introspection-domain softness. `architecture-vs-significance-two-tier-discount.md` L78–80 takes a molecular result — a dedicated ubiquitin-driven reopening pathway in anaesthetic emergence that temporally precedes behavioural return — grants that "a generator-model physicalist grants the asymmetry as readily as the Map does," and then states the Map's reading and its price in one clause: the asymmetry "*raises the explanatory cost on passive-recovery models without forcing the interface reading*." That is exactly the register a mechanism-seeking physicist wants: a specific empirical asymmetry, a specific interpretive gain, and an explicit refusal to convert cost-raising into forcing.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

`mine-ness.md` is the cluster's phenomenology, and it is disciplined without being deflationary. The architecture-tier claim is the *separability* of the for-me character from content and from agency, carried by a double dissociation (depersonalisation preserving content while degrading ownership; thought insertion preserving ownership while disrupting agency). Nagel would note two things. First, L108 keeps Zahavi's phenomenological point intact — for-me-ness as "irrelational," a feature constitutive of experience rather than a relation to a subject. Second, L82 states the falsifier in the first person's own terms: the separability thesis "would be undermined by a body of cases in which the for-me character co-varies *perfectly* with phenomenal content or with the sense of agency." A first-person structure treated as a defeasible empirical claim, without being reduced to one.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

Little tier-upgrade pressure exists in this cluster — it is a methodology night, not a scope-of-consciousness night — so the Process/Empiricist tension does not arise, and saying so is more honest than manufacturing praise. What Whitehead would appreciate structurally is the discipline's *cross-vocabulary translatability* test (L48): a claim earns architecture-tier status by surviving restatement in "functionalist, identity-theoretic, illusionist, predictive-processing, and dualist terms." A process philosopher's standing complaint is that substance-metaphysical vocabularies smuggle their commitments into their descriptions of process; a test that certifies findings by their survival across vocabularies is a direct answer to that complaint. Note that this praise is on the compatibility axis only and is not used anywhere below as support for a tier-upgrade.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

Thinnest persona here, and reported as thin rather than padded. The one item with real content for Kane is `mine-ness.md`'s second dissociation: thought insertion preserves ownership while disrupting *agency*. That agency can fail while ownership persists is a structural datum a libertarian needs, because it shows the sense of authorship is a separable contribution rather than an artefact of having experiences at all — and the discipline immediately books the dualist reading of that separability at the larger discount rather than banking it.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

McGinn gets the cluster's best two paragraphs, both self-limiting. `architecture-vs-significance-two-tier-discount.md` L114 states the recursive bind plainly: the test for architecture-tier status "is itself applied from within a framework. A reviewer deciding what counts as 'the same finding restated in functionalist terms' is using their own framework's individuation of findings," and "the response is asymptotic" rather than a solution. L116 then names the universal-solvent risk — *every* significance-reading could be assigned the larger discount, and "an over-zealous application would discount all the Map's interpretive claims to the point of saying nothing" — and supplies the scope condition that stops it: the discount "applies *differentially* ... it is only doing work where the architecture tier is genuinely more cross-vocabulary-robust than the significance tier." A discipline that states the conditions under which it should *not* be invoked is doing the thing McGinn respects.

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

This is the cluster's home persona, and it earns the verdict in four places.

**1. The discipline exists to block the Map's own favourite inference, and says so.** L40 names the failure mode with the Map's own examples as the offenders: an established architectural finding is cited, and "the finding's architecture-tier robustness is then silently transferred to the Map's significance-reading: *therefore* there is a bounded conscious witness behind a reconstructive introspective architecture; *therefore* the substrate is configured to admit consciousness rather than to produce it." L42 refuses the cheap exit of calling the significance-reading false: "both claims are true *in their own registers* ... What is illegitimate is letting the robustness of the first *under-discount* the second."

**2. It cashes out in actual five-tier assignments, not in prose.** `concession-convergence.md` L123 plus L129–130 is the model: the architectural finding (physicalist theories narrow toward type-specificity-respecting specifications) is assigned **strongly supported**; the interpretive significance (that the narrowing constitutes inadvertent concession of the dualist claim) is assigned **realistic possibility, contested**, with the reason given — "a physicalist reads it as ordinary progress, the Map reads it as concession." Two tiers, two labels, one finding. That is the evidential-status discipline being *fed* by the split rather than merely name-checked alongside it.

**3. Tenet-as-evidence-upgrade is praise-worthily not done, at the point of maximum temptation.** `anti-correlated-metacognitive-signal.md` L95 declares the concept **silent on Tenets 2, 4 and 5** and states that "treating the concept as evidence for any of these would be the tenet-as-evidence-upgrade move that [[evidential-status-discipline]] warns against." L105 then splits its own load-bearing finding, granting that the inversion "survives translation across functionalist, identity-theoretic, illusionist, predictive-processing, and dualist vocabulary" while the Map's witness-behind-the-architecture reading "is framework-shaped." A concept page voluntarily narrowing its own tenet reach is the restraint this persona exists to name.

**4. Every exhibit keeps its architecture-tier caveats inside the architecture tier.** `mine-ness.md` L80 carries the Knappik objection — that DPD symptoms "do not necessarily reflect loss of a *single* global mine-ness feature" — and the discipline page L66 explicitly rules that this caveat "operates *within* the architecture tier without dislodging separability." Deciding which tier an objection lands in, rather than letting an inconvenient objection float, is what makes the two-tier structure a working instrument instead of a rhetorical one.

Birch's reservation is bookkeeping, and it is Finding 1: none of this restraint is visible to a register audit.

## Finding 2 — the lateral-self-correction thesis fails as stated, and P-M3 already says why

The driver proposed testing this: *when a claim is wrong in one article, a sibling already has it right, so the cheapest verification move for a suspect claim is to compare the corpus's other statements of it before going to the literature.* Tested against three propagation events. It does not hold in the general form, and the register already contains its negation.

**P-M3 pre-empts it.** `positions/methodology-and-calibration.md` L78: existence-and-content verification happens at the publisher of record, and "**intra-corpus cross-checking is explicitly *not* a substitute, because it propagates rather than catches metadata error**." Writing the naive thesis into the methodology docs would put the project docs in direct contradiction with a live registered position.

**Counter-example 1 — the Hu et al. 2023 agent list (no sibling had it right).** The claim "documented across propofol, isoflurane, **sevoflurane**, and ketamine" is live right now at `project/architecture-vs-significance-two-tier-discount.md` L78. Sevoflurane was never tested by Hu et al., and the study is in mice. `git log -S 'sevoflurane'` on the three loci shows each acquired the word **at its own creation**: `concepts/active-reboot.md` at e375ed0cc (2026-05-18), the discipline page at 840933a6c (2026-05-25), the apex at 0b17234be. The 05-18 concept page is the upstream source; the other two inherited from it. No sibling ever held the correct list, and the correction (ba9d01b8d, today) came from reading Hu et al., not from lateral comparison. Lateral comparison would have returned unanimous agreement on the error.

**Counter-example 2 — the photosynthesis coherence over-claim (nine loci wrong, one right, and the one that was right was independently authored).** Today's sweep took two commits — 3a1f03cad ("FIVE remaining files after the positions register was fixed") and 798b7c598 ("TWO MORE files my earlier greps missed, including a `description:` nav surface"). So the over-claim was shared across the majority of its loci. But `obsidian/topics/ai-consciousness.md` **L135** has carried the correct framing since **2026-03-23** (`git log -S 'later attributed to vibrational'` → 968305277), four months ahead of the sweep: "Early reports of quantum coherence in photosynthetic systems (Engel et al. 2007) were later attributed to vibrational rather than electronic coherence (Cao et al. 2020)." This is the thesis's genuine confirming case — **and the mechanism is what matters**: that locus cites Cao et al. 2020, a source none of the erring loci used. It was right because it was *independently authored against the literature*, not because it was a sibling.

**Counter-example 3 — the "seven arguments" count (five siblings, one shared error, corrective locus was the referent).** Commit 8e47de13f (2026-07-28) fixed five articles still naming "seven" for the arguments `dualism.md` enumerates as eight. Here lateral checking *does* work, and the reason is instructive: the fact is **corpus-internal**, so the corpus is authoritative about it. For external facts the corpus has no authority, only a most-careful locus.

**What survives, and it is worth stating.** Lateral cross-checking is a legitimate **triage** step whose yield is a function of the **authorship independence** of the loci — and the instrument for grading that is already registered. P-M2's common-cause null says convergence across N sources is "a single observation read N times ... until the convergence passes a structurally distinguishing test that rules out a shared upstream architecture." Sibling unanimity inside one corpus is the purest possible instance: one author, one session, one copied sentence, N pages. **The Map's own convergence discipline, applied inward, predicts exactly when its internal lateral-verification heuristic works and when it returns confident agreement on an error.** The discriminator is not laterality; it is whether the loci cite different sources.

Recommendation: **do not** write the thesis into `obsidian/project/`, and do not mint it as a standalone position on its own. Either fold the authorship-independence clause into P-M3 (~12 words, but `methodology-and-calibration.md` is `hard_warning`, so it should ride along with a future condense or split of that file rather than being added now), or register it as a second entry in the new domain file the task below creates — a judgement call left explicitly to the executor.
