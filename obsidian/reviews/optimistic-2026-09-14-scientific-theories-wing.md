## Executive Summary

This wing has a signature the corpus should be proud of: **every one of the eleven articles makes the same two-step move, and nine of them make it in the same words.** Step one grants the rival theory its correlates in full — Lamme's four stages, Prinz's attention gate, Metzinger's transparency, Dehaene's ignition. Step two says the theory has *relocated* the explanatory gap rather than closed it, and then, in the wing's best pages, turns the blade round and concedes that the Map relocates the mystery too. The word "relocat-" occurs in nine of the eleven files. The move is disciplined, honest and repeatedly self-aware — and it has **no home page**. [[first-order-representationalism]] and [[attention-as-interface]] both name it "the relocation objection" (the latter aiming it at the Map's own interface hypothesis), 39 live articles deploy it, and no concept page defines it. With the `expand-topic` lane at one open task and `concepts/` at 327 of 360, this is the one place a new page is warranted rather than link-work.

The wing's structural weakness is the mirror of its strength: because each article re-derives the shared move privately, the members barely know each other where it matters. The wing's most formal instrument — the unfolding argument — is unreachable from either of its own targets; its two "transparent-model" theories (AST and SMT) make an identical objection and never mention each other; and the article the wing calls its "survey" surveys three of ten siblings.

One defect crossed the line from structure into content. [[integrated-information-theory]] §"The Controversy" presents the June-2023 Templeton-funded test (250 subjects, six labs, "2 of 3 predictions vs. 0 of 3") and the 2025 COGITATE paper as **two experiments, the second "more rigorous"**. Details in [Finding 1](#finding-1--integrated-information-theory-counts-one-experiment-twice-and-two-deep-reviews-fenced-it).

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

The wing keeps the gap open without pretending the rivals are stupid. [[recurrent-processing-theory]] is the model: "Grant Lamme everything empirical — grant that localised recurrent processing is the reliable, even the *necessary and sufficient*, neural correlate of experience" — and the question survives. Better, it presses the demand *from inside Lamme's commitments*: a theory that "asks report-based approaches to stop helping themselves to unexplained primitives owes the same discipline at its own foundation." [[first-order-representationalism]] notices the structural echo across Shoemaker, Chalmers and Cohen — each "secures the phenomenal-character/content link the same way — by writing the perceiver's response into the represented property" — and then states the cost precisely: "A content specified by reference to feel cannot be the naturalistic base that feel reduces to."

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

Tenet 2 is handled with the right modesty. [[attended-intermediate-representations-theory]] builds its own rebuttal: if attention "fully fixes which representations reach working memory... then consciousness selects only what the mechanism has already selected, a spectator doing no bidirectional work," and the only escape is to locate the interface at "the genuinely open remainder — which of the quantum-indeterminate alternatives is taken up." That is the ensemble-level worry stated against the Map by the Map. [[the-unfolding-argument-against-causal-structure-theories-of-consciousness]] is sharper still: Tenet 2 "cuts against a clean escape: an effect minimal enough could also be sub-detectable... exactly IIT's predicament of a consciousness difference no measurement reaches."

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

[[self-model-theory-of-subjectivity]] catches Metzinger at the one point where his method decides the question by bracketing it: he annexes the contemplative *sākṣin* to minimal phenomenal experience "only 'on a purely phenomenological reading that abstracts away from all metaphysical and epistemological aspects of the term' (2020, p. 11)" — and "whether the witness is an irreducible experiential locus or one more representational layer *is* the metaphysical question, so setting the metaphysics aside cannot be what settles it." [[higher-order-theories]] does the same to Rosenthal's mental-qualities reply: it "answers the rock objection's letter while showing where the gap sits."

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

Two articles carry explicit process-philosophy sections. [[integrated-information-theory]]'s is well-behaved: it marks the real disanalogy ("For Whitehead, integration *occurs within* experience; for IIT, experience *is identical to* integration") and claims only resonance. [[attention-schema-theory]] §Process Philosophy Perspective is where this persona would applaud loudest — "AST inverts the order of explanation, deriving experience from modeling when modeling is composed of experiential moments" — and it is exactly the applause the Hardline Empiricist below declines to join. See the calibration concern there.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

Thin in this wing by design; the free-will cluster lives elsewhere. What is here is right-sized: [[neural-correlates-of-consciousness]] notes NCC "bears on free will: if consciousness genuinely selects among superposed neural states... NCC identifies where such selection occurs — though NCC methodology alone cannot make this distinction." The concession is in the same sentence as the claim.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

[[multiple-drafts-model]] is the wing's most honest page about what the Map cannot show. Having pressed the verificationism objection against Dennett, it turns it: "If access does not fix existence, then no measurement currently discriminates the phenomenal residue the Map retains from its absence, and the Map's warrant for keeping it is avowedly non-empirical." And it records Dennett's on-record answer to "for whom?" rather than pretending he has none: "The narrator premise is therefore not a burden Dennett's own account concedes and then fails to discharge; it is a framework commitment... that he rejects at the source."

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

This persona's verdict is load-bearing for the wing, and it is favourable with one exception.

**Restraint praised.** [[neural-correlates-of-consciousness]] refuses to double-count: the four filter-theory findings "form one evidential pattern... not four independent confirmations of the transmission reading, and should be counted as one," because "the same accommodation move is symmetrically available to the production theorist." Its taxonomy of report-dependence (direct, post-trial, behavioural proxy, no-report, clinical marker, theory-laden) replaces a slogan with an auditable chain. [[higher-order-theories]] reports the Rounis 2010 TMS result *and* its non-replication (Bor et al. 2017), noting that "the same group supplies both the evidence this section leans on and the evidence weakening it." [[recurrent-processing-theory]] warns that the "posterior areas are sufficient" line "comes from the IIT proponents' supplementary interpretation, not from the consortium's own conclusions" — a provenance discipline the IIT page itself lacks (Finding 1). [[global-workspace-theory]] gives Dehaene and Naccache's crypto-dualism charge in their own words and answers it in one clause: "a forecast dissolution is not a result."

**Tenet-as-evidence-upgrade praise-worthily not done.** [[integrated-information-theory]] §Assessment: "Honesty requires acknowledging that the Map's interaction mechanism is no more empirically established than IIT's identity claim." [[the-unfolding-argument-against-causal-structure-theories-of-consciousness]]: "the Map cannot wield 'IIT is unfalsifiable because it is anti-functionalist' as a clean criticism: the same charge would rebound onto the non-behavioural aspects of dualism."

**Calibration concern (the one conflict).** [[attention-schema-theory]] §Process Philosophy Perspective opens "Whitehead's process philosophy illuminates why AST's explanation fails" and heads its next subsection "Why Models Cannot Generate Experience". The argument runs from a panexperientialist premise ("the schema is itself a pattern of actual occasions, each with its own micro-experience") to a verdict against AST. The Map is not panexperientialist — its own IIT page says it "sides with something closer to Whitehead" only in the sense of consciousness being "genuinely distinct from its physical substrate," which is not Whitehead's view either. Process resonance here is doing the work of a refutation. The same slippage appears in miniature at [[integrated-information-theory]] L207, where the Map's collapse-plus-retrocausality account "addresses" IIT's temporal gap and "creates genuine temporal extension" two paragraphs after the page conceded the mechanism is "no more empirically established than IIT's identity claim."
