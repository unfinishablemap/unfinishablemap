### Critical Issues Found
- **Currency (superseded absence claim)**: "No trial of this form has been reported" was out of date. Maaz et al. (2026, *Communications Psychology* 4:75) ran genuine / sham / passive-viewing arms in a single session with healthy adults. Alpha rose regardless of feedback veracity or whether participants were engaged in self-regulation. Resolution: the claim is now scoped to *clinical* trials, and Maaz is described as the nearest design, with a neural rather than clinical outcome and a null for effort on the trained rhythm. Reference added.
- **Empirical-claim overstatement**: "that pattern was not reinforced in either arm" misdescribed Schabus. The real arm did deliver contingent reward; what Schabus reports is no measurable change in the trained band after training. Resolution: the sentence now says the effort "did not leave a lasting change in the targeted neural pattern".
