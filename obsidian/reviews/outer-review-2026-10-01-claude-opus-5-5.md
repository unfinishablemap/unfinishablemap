## Reply

# Hostile Referee Audit: "Neural Correlates of Consciousness" (unfinishablemap.org/concepts/neural-correlates-of-consciousness/, rev. 2026-09-24)

**Verdict: major revision, close to reject.** The article's one defensible thesis is that correlation leaves the metaphysics open. That point is true, but it is trivial, it applies equally to every side, and it does not favour dualism. Everything built on top of it fails audit: a misattributed encyclopedia quotation, a misreported prevalence figure, a definition of "full NCC" that contradicts the article's own cited source, a thin account of COGITATE, four falsifiers that are unreachable or that cannot tell the rival views apart, tenet sections that leak conclusions the article says the data cannot support, and no engagement at all with predictive processing, the strongest physicalist rival.

### 2.2 Empirical claims audit (body)

- **"Damage to V4 causes complete loss of color experience (achromatopsia)."** This is over-stated (reviewer's domain knowledge, not re-verified here). Cerebral achromatopsia typically follows ventral occipitotemporal (lingual/fusiform, "V4/V8 complex") lesions, is often partial (dyschromatopsia) and usually needs bilateral damage for full loss. The article uses this one oversimplified case twice as its showcase for the gap.
- **"No-report paradigms suggest that consciousness-related activity localises to posterior regions."** No source is given (Tsuchiya et al. 2015 is missing). The article ignores that no-report designs are themselves contested: they may over-subtract, and they still calibrate against report, as the article's own later section concedes.
- **Psychedelics "dissolve the Default Mode Network… If consciousness were generated by these neural patterns, disrupting them should diminish experience."** This is a strawman. No production theory identifies the DMN as the generator of consciousness. It is the standard candidate for the *self-model*, so a production view predicts that DMN disruption alters self-experience and does not abolish experience. The site's own filter-theory page concedes that the psychedelic state is "metabolically expensive" (Vollenweider et al. 1997) and that "the metabolic data discriminates no better than the imaging". [[filter-theory]] The NCC article does not carry that concession.
- **"Propofol and ketamine both produce behavioural unresponsiveness but radically different experiences."** This is true, but it is the strongest *production-side* datum on the page and the article does not say so. Sarasso et al. 2015 showed that the perturbational complexity index was low under propofol (0.2–0.3) and xenon (0.05–0.25), with no reports on emergence, and awake-like under ketamine (0.35–0.55), after which subjects "reported long, vivid dreams unrelated to the external environment" (values as summarised in a *Current Biology* dispatch). A *neural* measure predicted the presence of experience independently of behaviour. That is a successful forward prediction from brain dynamics to phenomenal presence. The article mentions the PCI only as a "clinical marker" in the report taxonomy.
- **Covert consciousness: "if awareness persists when its supposed neural basis fails to generate behavioural outputs, consciousness is not identical to those outputs."** This attacks a target nobody holds. No physicalist in the NCC literature identifies consciousness with behavioural output. Covert consciousness is detected *by* preserved cortical activity, which shows dependence on the brain, not independence from it. [neurology](https://www.neurology.org/doi/abs/10.1212/WNL.0000000000210208)

### 2.3 The three readings and the "compatible with all three" thesis

1. **The symmetry point is conceded and then not applied.** The article correctly says "the choice of analogy determines the intuition" and that correlation "alone cannot settle" the matter. It then says "This persistent opacity supports Dualism", "This supports No Many Worlds" and "This supports Occam's Razor Has Limits". [[neural-correlates-of-consciousness]] That is an epistemic-to-metaphysical slide. The appended confession ("The Map adopts the datum reading as a posited stance") is confession-without-correction, because the verb "supports" survives the confession.
2. **Bayesian evidential asymmetry is missing.** "Both physicalism and interactionism predict exactly the correlations NCC research discovers" is false on any likelihood-sensitive reading. Interactionism "predicts" correlations only in the thin sense of being consistent with *some* correlation pattern. It does not predict the posterior/frontal debate, lesion specificity, the anaesthetic PCI split, dose-graded loss, or timing. Production theories generate those predictions and sometimes lose (COGITATE). An interface theory with unconstrained psychophysical laws spreads its likelihood over every pattern and so gains little from any one. Compatibility is a floor, not a tie.
3. **Missing standard counterarguments.** These include: the causal-closure argument (Papineau) and the SEP's statement that interactionism "violates physical closure after all"; [stanford](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/dualism/) Kim's exclusion argument; the interaction and pairing problems; [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interactionism_(philosophy_of_mind)) parsimony as a genuine theoretical virtue rather than something Tenet 5 can switch off; the abductive argument that the NCC programme's progress (decoding, PCI, covert-awareness detection, preregistered theory tests) is itself evidence for the production hypothesis; and the Type-B physicalist / phenomenal-concept-strategy prediction that the gap *will persist* under physicalism. That last omission undercuts the article's claim that "If consciousness were identical to neural activity, we would expect the identity to eventually become intelligible." Only Type-A physicalism is mentioned. [[neural-correlates-of-consciousness]]
4. **Emergence is never developed.** The three-option list names emergence and then drops it. Strong emergence is the closest rival to interaction (it shares the causal-novelty commitments) and needs separating on criteria the article never states.
5. **Filter theory makes no distinct prediction in this article.** The article itself concedes that "the same accommodation move is symmetrically available to the production theorist" and that the cluster "cannot honestly be cited as independent confirmation". [[neural-correlates-of-consciousness]] The filter-theory page adds that filter theory "lacks independent criteria for *which* disruptions should enhance versus degrade". [[filter-theory]] The terminal-lucidity page (index extract) says the Map treats filter theory as valuable "in coherence across diverse phenomena… rather than in point predictions". [concepts/terminal-lucidity](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/terminal-lucidity/) A framework without point predictions cannot have a "filter theory predictions failed" falsifier (Falsifier 3). The site contradicts itself.
