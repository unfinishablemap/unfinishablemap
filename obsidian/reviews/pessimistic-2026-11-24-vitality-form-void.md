## Executive Summary

The page's stance discipline is good: it states the physicalist reading at full strength, grades its own registers, and claims no upgrade for any tenet. Its weaknesses are on the evidence side, and three were confirmed at source. **(1)** The page says the insular signature "appeared when the task asked for it and not otherwise" and builds the *occluded* register on that task-dependence, but its own reference 3 (Di Cesare et al. 2019) scanned people who were told to attend to the action type, found the same sector active, and called the recognition automatic. **(2)** The autism dissociation is quoted from the Introduction of a 2024 paper that is summarising two earlier studies; that paper's own first finding is that autistic children do convey gentle and rude forms. **(3)** The "two lineages that developed separately" claim is undercut by the cited Sheets-Johnstone paper, which cites Stern (1985) and points to her own discussion of it from 1999. ## Critiques by Philosopher

### The Eliminative Materialist
Churchland would say the page has rediscovered a motor-control parameter and called its late measurement a void. Kinematic profile (velocity, force, trajectory) is exactly the kind of thing neuroscience characterises once someone builds the stimulus set; the 2020 review itself describes vitality forms through "trajectory, energy and power". The page concedes most of this in §Whether the Void Is Real, which is to its credit. Her remaining complaint is fair: "the categories that organise psychology ... all sort by content" is a claim about a folk taxonomy, and the page offers no survey of psychology to support it.

### The Hard-Nosed Physicalist
Dennett would press the §Phenomenology passage: "what displaces it is attending to it as a something" is an introspective report about the limits of introspection, offered with no third-person check. The page's own caution (no one has tried continuous sliders or matched movement) is the heterophenomenological experiment he would ask for, and until it is run the "any word installs the wrong category" claim is a prediction. He would also note that the 2019 behavioural task had listeners label sounds as rude, gentle or unclear on request (the accuracy figures were not read for this review), so some thematic access on request is built into the group's own method.

### The Quantum Skeptic
Tegmark has nothing to attack and would say so: the page states that Minimal Quantum Interaction "has no bearing, since vitality forms are macroscopic phenomena". This is correct and should be preserved.

### The Many-Worlds Defender
Deutsch would call the No Many Worlds sentence ("*this* surge in *this* body now") decoration. The page already calls it "at most a thematic link". No issue.

### The Empiricist
The Popperian finds the page better than most voids: it names what would dissolve the occlusion claim (non-category response formats). But the one neural observation it offers as support for occlusion, task-dependence, is contradicted by the group's own later design (Issue 1). ### The Buddhist Philosopher
Nagarjuna would observe that the page treats "the form" as a thing that is "given constantly" and then lost on being named, which reifies a dependent arising: a contour exists only relative to a content and an observer's parsing. He would also note that contemplative traditions train attention to the arising and passing of experience as such, which is a long-standing human practice of thematising the *how*. The page's "rare, trained" dancer case gestures at this without asking whether trained attention falsifies "lost the moment one tries to say what the form was".

## Critical Issues

### Issue 1: The task-dependence claim is contradicted by the page's own reference 3
- **File**: `obsidian/voids/vitality-form-void.md`
- **Location**: L54 "The dimension appeared when the task asked for it and not otherwise, which is the void's own structure reproduced in the scanner: a mind running the default *what* task never meets the *how*."; L84 "occluded is supported by the task-dependence of the neural signature and by the disintegration finding"
- **Problem**: Two faults. (a) A "how vs what" contrast shows the dorso-central insula more active under the *how* task; it does not show the sector silent under the *what* task. (b) Di Cesare, Marchi, Pinardi & Rizzolatti (2019), the page's reference 3, designed the fMRI session to test the opposite: "participants were instructed to pay attention to the action type regardless to the vitality forms. This instruction was used to corroborate, if the insula will be activated, the hypothesis of an automatic recognition of vitality forms." The insula activated, and the Discussion concludes that action sounds conveying vitality forms "automatically determines the activation of two distinct circuits". So on the group's own record a mind attending to the *what* does process the *how*. The page cites this paper two sentences later for modality-generality and does not notice the conflict.
- **Severity**: High. The *occluded* register, one of the "two registers ... secure" at L84, has two supports and this is one of them.
- **Recommendation**: Replace the inference with what the record shows: the form is processed without being asked for and is reported when asked for. That is arguably a better fit for the void than the current claim (a dimension registered everywhere and thematised by no standing category), but it moves the occlusion from the scanner to the conceptual apparatus, and L84 should then rest occlusion on the disintegration finding and categorical displacement alone, with the neural record marked as not supporting it.
- **Propagation**: `obsidian/workflow/highlights.md` L137 (2026-11-10, tweeted) says "the insula lights up only when people are asked how, not what". That is a dated record of a sent tweet; whether to annotate it is the operator's call.

### Issue 2: The autism dissociation is cited to a paper that reports it second-hand, and the paper's own result is omitted
- **File**: `obsidian/voids/vitality-form-void.md`
- **Location**: L58 "The clinical record adds a dissociation: autistic children struggle to identify vitality forms in others' actions "while they do not differ from neurotypical children in discerning the goal of the action" (Di Cesare, Bruschetta et al. 2024)."
- **Problem**: The quoted words are in the Introduction of the 2024 paper and read in full: "At a behavioral level, few studies have explored the potential recognition deficits of VFs in autistic children. Findings suggested that autistic children struggle to identify VFs when observing others' actions 21, 22, while they do not differ from neurotypical children in discerning the goal of the action 22." Reference 22 is Rochat et al. (2013), *Neuropsychologia* 51(10): 1918–1924; reference 21 is Di Cesare et al. (2017). The *what*-intact half therefore rests on one 2013 study (17 participants with ASD, by the 2017 paper's summary). The 2024 paper is a kinematic study of production, and its first stated finding is that autistic children "are able to tune the motor profile of their actions, effectively conveying both gentle and rude VFs". The page is careful about carriers elsewhere ("via Ammaniti & Ferrari") and drops that care here, along with the source's "few studies" and "suggested".
- **Severity**: Medium-High. A fidelity defect of the wrong-work kind, and it feeds the sentence the open P3 is about to soften.
- **Recommendation**: Attribute the dissociation to Rochat et al. (2013) as summarised by the 2024 paper, keep the source's hedge, and add the 2024 paper's own production result so "the *how* altered" is not read as "the *how* absent".

### Issue 3: "Two lineages that developed separately" is not what the cited paper shows
- **File**: `obsidian/voids/vitality-form-void.md`
- **Location**: L70 "two lineages that developed separately converge on a kinetic Gestalt with the same components"; L84 "cross-observer convergence weak to moderate (independent lineages, one laboratory)"
- **Problem**: Sheets-Johnstone (2024) cites Stern (1985), the book that introduced vitality affects, twice, and writes "As discussed elsewhere (Sheets-Johnstone, 1999/2011), Stern's (1985) experimental neonatal evidence of volition ...". She has been working with that book since *The Primacy of Movement* (1999). The page's narrower statement, that the 2024 paper "does not cite Stern for this claim", is true; the inference to separate development is not licensed by it. An OpenAlex full-text query also returns three Sheets-Johnstone works whose indexed text contains "vitality affects" (2005; 2018, doi:10.1007/978-3-319-98657-9_5; 2019, doi:10.1111/misp.12131). "The same components" also overstates: Stern lists five (movement, force, time, space, intention) and Sheets-Johnstone's dynamic has three (space, time, force) plus four qualities.
- **Severity**: Medium. It inflates one of the four criteria grades.
- **Recommendation**: Say the lineages are in contact and describe overlapping components; lower or re-justify the convergence grade.

## Counterarguments to Address

### "The categories that organise psychology all sort by content"
- **Current content says**: perception, emotion, cognition and action all sort by content, so the *how* has nowhere to go.
- **A critic would argue**: psychology has long-standing dimensional and dynamic vocabularies that are not content categories: the arousal or activation axis of dimensional affect models, the activity and potency factors of the semantic differential, prosody and paralanguage research, and Laban's effort qualities in movement analysis. On that view the *how* had several homes and lacked only a unified name and a scan.
- **Suggested response**: this is a lead, not a verified finding; none of these literatures was checked this run. It is worth a research-topic pass before the page's "unexplored" grade is treated as settled, because the page's only evidence for neglect is the measuring group's own framing of its novelty.

### Thematic access
