# Hostile-Referee Adversarial Peer-Review Audit: The Unfinishable Map (unfinishablemap.org)

## Part 1 — TL;DR and Headline Verdict

**Headline verdict: The framework is not undone by sloppy scholarship. Its citation metadata is strong, its author-stance firewalls mostly hold, and it engages its strongest physicalist rival head-on. It is instead undone by three structural facts it discloses in detail but cannot repair: (1) the constitutional five-tenet architecture is unfalsifiable by design, protected by an explicit "foundational-dependency test" that immunises the tenets from evidential retirement; (2) the load-bearing quantum mechanism (corridor / post-decoherence selection) is "empirically indistinguishable from chance by construction," which is arguably not selection at all; and (3) the No-Many-Worlds tenet's headline argument has been narrowed until it defeats only branch-egalitarian Everett and now rests on an undefended rejection of modal realism — while its strongest philosophical ally, Christian List, holds a *many-worlds* theory.**

- **The dominant pathology is confession-without-correction operating at the constitutional layer.** The site runs an extraordinary self-audit apparatus and even hosts a position (P-M5) declaring "disclosure is not self-correction — a discipline binds only as far as the pipeline enforces it." Yet its own deepest defect — the bias-without-deviation dilemma, its own P-Q3, rated "framework-critical" — is banked as disclosed "honest cost" while the mechanism it undermines remains "the Map's working hypothesis." Disclosure is systematised into epistemic credit rather than converted into demotions.
- **The Stapp inversion is confirmed and the Born-rule dilemma is the true reductio.** The site's "corridor dualism" claims outcome-selection — precisely the move Henry Stapp disowned, restricting mental causation to von Neumann Process 1 and leaving outcomes to Born-obeying "nature." The site concedes this openly, converting the inversion from a citation error into a coherence problem it declines to resolve.
- **Verdicts:** Tenet 1 → FLAG AS PERPETUALLY CONTESTED; Tenet 2 (as a truth-apt mechanism claim) → DEMOTE-TO-COHERENCE-ONLY; Tenet 3's "genuine causal work" claims → REVISE-HARD; Tenet 4's indexical argument → DEMOTE-TO-COHERENCE-ONLY against centred-worlds variants; Tenet 5 → RETAIN as written on the tenets page but REVISE-HARD in the Occam's-razor argument article, which deploys parsimony asymmetrically.

## Part 2 — Dimensional Analysis

### 2.1 Novelty assessment (item 1): which insights are genuinely novel versus restatement

The corpus contains almost no novel *philosophy*. Its genuine originality is *architectural and diagnostic* — the tenet-dependency matrix; the six-axis position calibration (credence / external-evidence grade / structural centrality / model maturity / empirical discriminability / framework-internal flag); the "common-cause null"; and the compatible / suggestive / discriminating evidence ladder from the Born-rule apex. These are real contributions to how a partisan corpus can police its own inheritance. As philosophy of mind, every substantive first-order claim is a restatement:

- **Interactionist dualism via quantum indeterminacy** — closest prior work: Eccles & Popper, *The Self and Its Brain* (1977); Beck, F. & Eccles, J.C., "Quantum aspects of brain activity and the role of consciousness," *PNAS* 89(23):11357–11361 (1 Dec 1992), DOI 10.1073/pnas.89.23.11357, which presents "a quantum mechanical model for exocytosis … based on a tunneling process of the trigger mechanism." The Map's "minimal quantum interaction" is Eccles–Beck with the interface relocated post-decoherence.
- **Post-decoherence selection / corridor dualism** — closest prior: Stapp's quantum-Zeno interactionism (*Mind, Matter and Quantum Mechanics*), which the Map explicitly departs from; the actualisation-postulate framing echoes objective-collapse (GRW/CSL) plus a mental selector. The specific "select which branch is realised while the ensemble average stays |⟨φ|ψ⟩|²" claim is the site's own synthesis, but it is a variant of Chalmers & McQueen (2022) Φ-collapse minus the detectable modification.
- **The indexical objection to MWI** — closest prior: Christian List, "The Many-Worlds Theory of Consciousness," *Noûs* 57(2):316–340 (2023), DOI 10.1111/nous.12408; Saunders/Wallace/Deutsch Everettian decision theory; Sebens & Carroll (2018, *BJPS* 69:25). The Map's argument is not new; its honest concession that the argument fails against List's centred-worlds variant is the one place it adds analytic value.
- **Dualism vs Occam** — closest prior: Sober (2015, *Ockham's Razors*); Baker's SEP "Simplicity"; Swinburne on simplicity. Restatement.
- **Hard-problem / knowledge-argument / zombie material** — Chalmers (1996), Jackson (1982), Levine (1983). Pure restatement; the tenets page lists the knowledge argument as a live pro-dualist argument without foregrounding Jackson's own recantation — Jackson, F. (2003), "Mind and Illusion," in A. O'Hear (ed.), *Minds and Persons*, Royal Institute of Philosophy Supplement 53, pp. 251–271, Cambridge University Press — in which Jackson repudiates the anti-physicalist conclusion of his own 1982 argument.

**Verdict on novelty:** near-zero first-order novelty; genuine second-order (methodological/architectural) novelty. A hostile referee should treat the site as a well-engineered synthesis-and-audit engine, not a source of new arguments.

### 2.3 Structural weaknesses (item 2), with offending passages quoted

**(i) The bias-without-deviation dilemma — the deepest vulnerability, confirmed.** The tenets page states the mechanism is "*empirically indistinguishable from chance*—by construction, not by any sensitivity limit." The Born-rule article concedes the Lakatosian charge in terms: "a hard core insulated from *aggregate* test (corridor plus trumping), ringed by falsifiable auxiliaries the programme disavows in advance … is the textbook profile of a *degenerating* programme buying survival by content-reduction." [[topics/born-rule-and-the-consciousness-interface]] The site's own P-Q3 rates this "the strongest live challenge to the default mechanism — confidence: high · no independent evidence · framework-critical." A selection producing no aggregate deviation is — as Chalmers & McQueen (2022) imply by treating detectable collapse-modification as the scientific virtue of such a model — arguably not selection. This is a genuine reductio the site converts into disclosed "cost."

**(ii) Constitutional-attractor + confession-without-correction.** The positions page states the foundational-dependency test: "a position counts as retireable only if removing it leaves every tenet with an intact rationale," [[positions]] concluding "a tenet survives the loss of its argument as a bare posit." [[positions]] This explicitly immunises the five tenets from evidential defeat: arguments may fall, tenets may not. Combined with P-M5 ("Disclosure is not self-correction"), the site has *named* the pathology and continued to exhibit it — textbook inoculation-by-confession.

**(iii) Undischarged mechanism debt.** P-Q10 records "no worked toy model of the mechanism currently exists" (framework-critical). The entire quantum-interface cluster is therefore a promissory note. The apex `post-decoherence-selection-programme` holds the dilemma "open" as a "live hypothesis" indefinitely with no demotion trigger — coherence inflation, where perpetual "openness" substitutes for resolution.

**(iv) The trumping escape hatch is unfalsifiable by construction.** The Born-rule article concedes "trumping is orthogonal to Born tests outright, so retreat *there* buys survival by unfalsifiability." [[topics/born-rule-and-the-consciousness-interface]] Keeping delegatory-causation/trumping-preemption as a live fallback precisely because it cannot be tested is the definition of a degenerating auxiliary.

**(v) Evidential-independence of the void catalogue.** The convergence-as-evidence argument ("dualism best explains why 60+ explanatory failures cluster at the consciousness–matter interface") [[reviews/outer-review-2026-06-01-claude-opus-4-8]] is discounted by the site's own common-cause null (P-V1–P-V3), which notes only ~15 of 100+ voids are framework-independent. ## Part 5 — Site-Wide Methodology Improvements

The disclosure apparatus is genuinely sophisticated, but the pipeline that produces it is a closed loop: content is authored and reviewed by the same Claude model family, and the changelog itself records the failure mode — a fabricated Churchland quotation survived six consecutive reviews, and a review once recorded a quotation as "confirmed character-for-character" when the string did not exist in the source. Convergence damping means that once an article "stops moving, the reviews stop looking." Recommendations, in escalating order, with the thresholds that would change them:

1. **Wire enforcement to disclosure.** P-M5 is correct that an ungated discipline is "a stated intention." [[positions]] Add validators that (a) block publication of any quoted string not grep-verifiable in a retrieved primary source, and (b) fire a mandatory re-review whenever a cited author's stance is physicalist or stance-neutral. *Threshold to relax:* once zero fabricated quotations have cleared review for a sustained period.
2. **Break the Claude-on-Claude loop for high-centrality claims.** The external outer reviews (ChatGPT, Gemini) already surfaced the strongest findings — the triple-screen unfalsifiability conjunction, the List double-edge, the missing predictive-coding sources — yet the same findings recur unfixed. Route every framework-critical P-Q position and the tenets page to external human philosophers of mind and to non-Claude models with authority to force demotions, not merely log them. *Threshold:* until an external reviewer signs off, framework-critical claims cannot be labelled anything above "coherence-only."
3. **Convert the foundational-dependency test from a shield into a trigger.** As written it guarantees tenets survive any evidential loss. Add the symmetric rule: when a framework-critical position (P-Q3, P-Q10) stands unresolved past a defined horizon, the *tenet's* status label — not just the position's — moves to "coherence-only."
4. **Cap perpetual "live hypothesis" labels.** Any claim held open beyond N review cycles without a discharged debt auto-demotes, closing the confession-without-correction channel at its source.
