## Executive Summary

This wing runs, unprompted and in three separate articles, the shared-premise audit that the positions register states as P-D1 — the discipline that converging arguments do not add when they share a premise. The hub audits its own five arguments down to three premises and says so in its opening paragraph; `aesthetic-testimony` and `the-comic` each independently discover that their case and the knowledge argument are two applications of one intuition rather than two routes to one conclusion. Outside the phenomenal-output/causal-machinery apex, this is the catalogue's most explicit self-discount, and it was reached in a domain nobody had scored. The wing also produces one thing the registers do not hold: the finding that the aesthetic *creation* argument is the only line that survives both a closed explanatory gap and a successful holistic reduction. Against this, one locus recruits a biological naturalist as support for an anti-materialist premise — a framing defect that a prior deep review looked at directly and cleared on metadata grounds.

## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

Chalmers would recognise `the-sublime-and-negative-aesthetics` as doing something the standard qualia literature does not: isolating the evaluative dimension from the hedonic one by finding cases where they come apart. The article's own analogy is the right one — "the sublime, tragedy, and disgust break the correlation while preserving the aesthetic value, isolating the aesthetic dimension from the hedonic one the way pain asymbolia isolates the sensory component of pain from its affective component" (L86). Pain asymbolia is a dissociation Chalmers's own tradition leans on; the article finds its aesthetic analogue.

He would also approve the hub's treatment of formal beauty, which refuses the easy win: "Formal beauty's precision actually *worsens* the gap: the relevant structure is fully mapped (Helmholtz, 1863/1954; Plomp & Levelt, 1965), yet the gap remains. More structural detail specifies the gap more precisely rather than closing it" (L118). Choosing the case where reduction *should* be easiest, and showing the gap survives there, is the harder and better argument.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

Stapp gets one paragraph, and it is correctly sized. The hub's Minimal Quantum Interaction section (L156) proposes that beauty's capacity to command sustained attention "is the kind of attentional concentration that models like Stapp's quantum mind theory require" — and then immediately adds "The connection remains speculative, and the phenomenological arguments stand independently." Stapp would want more; the honest thing is that the wing does not have more, and it says so rather than inflating a thematic resonance into mechanism. Compare `phenomenology-of-musical-understanding` L137, which does the same job in the Bidirectional Interaction section by naming the epiphenomenalist's standard reply, conceding it is coherent, and locating the disagreement in explanatory instability rather than incoherence.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

`phenomenology-of-musical-understanding` is the wing's most Nagelian article, and its best passage is the one that takes the felt structure seriously enough to notice something the representationalist cannot place: "When a cello plays a slow, descending phrase in a low register, most listeners experience something that resembles grief. But grief *about what*? There is no propositional object" (L99). Emotion without an intentional object is a genuinely awkward datum for evaluativist accounts, and the article presses it precisely rather than rhetorically.

Nagel would also credit the article's treatment of Bergsonian interpenetration (L73) — "C heard after G in the context of a C major phrase is qualitatively different from C heard after F in the context of an F major phrase" — as an unusually concrete statement of a claim that is normally left gestural.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

Whitehead would find the wing's temporal commitments congenial: `phenomenology-of-musical-understanding` treats the note as constituted by its relations rather than as a substance bearing properties, and the hub's binding section treats the aesthetic gestalt as an event with properties "categorically different from anything in its parts" (L126). This is the process-friendly reading of unity, and it is reached through musical phenomenology rather than asserted.

**The praise stops where the discipline requires.** None of these articles uses process-philosophical congeniality to elevate an evidential claim. `aesthetics-beyond-art` L117 states the boundary explicitly and against its own interest: "the step from 'aesthetic value is observer-dependent' to 'aesthetic value requires *consciousness* in the Map's strong sense' is largely the Map's extension, not an established position in environmental aesthetics… Enlisting them as evidence *for* consciousness-dependence would over-read the field." Berleant, Carroll and Brady foreground the perceiving subject; the article refuses to bank that as support. The Process Philosopher's praise and the Hardline Empiricist's converge here rather than conflicting.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

The hub's creation argument is the wing's contribution Kane would care about most, because it is a *causal* argument rather than an epistemic one. The revision loop is specified concretely: "the artist steps back, perceives that the work falls short, feels specifically *how* it falls short (too heavy, too bright, too rigid), and adjusts. The causal chain runs: phenomenal judgment → motor action → material change" (L134). And it puts a burden on both rivals by name — the epiphenomenalist must explain the systematic correlation across millions of revisions; the identity theorist must explain how a template about a not-yet-existing work can be a neural firing pattern.

The hub's own audit singles this line out as the only one that stands clear: it "survives both a closed gap and a successful holistic reduction" (L108). That is a strong structural claim, honestly qualified in the same sentence by noting that its zombie-artist sharpening "borrows the conceivability intuition back."

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

`aesthetic-testimony-and-the-acquaintance-principle` is the wing's exercise in not knowing, and it is the most impressive article of the six. It is named after a principle it concludes cannot do the job it was recruited for. It reports the hostile consensus in the field's own words — Riggle "records the state of play his paper pushes against: the principle was once well entrenched in aesthetics, but 'today there is a near consensus that it fails'" (L67) — and then draws the consequence rather than deflecting it:

> The most sympathetic recent treatment and the most hostile arrive at the same place — the principle keeps its plausibility only by giving up the epistemic role the Map wants from it. (L81)

McGinn would also credit L73, which refuses to convert an unmoved intuition into evidence: "a reluctance that survives every relocation of its supposed ground is as good a candidate for a phenomenon with no location as for a joint in nature the theories keep missing. Nothing about the invariance itself decides between those readings."

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

This is the wing's strongest showing, and the verdict is load-bearing because three of the six articles had an obvious opportunity to inflate an argument count and declined it.

**The wing runs the shared-premise audit three times, independently, without being asked.** This is the discipline `positions/arguments-for-dualism.md` P-D1 states — "Arguments within a single cluster therefore contribute little more than one strong argument from that cluster" — applied in a domain the register never scored:

1. **The hub**, in its second paragraph, before any argument is made: "The five are not five independent arguments… Aesthetics is the strongest single test case by concentration rather than tally" (L82). The audit at L108 then does the work: feature convergence and aesthetic binding are "one constitutive-holism premise at two grains"; substrate independence and formal precision share "the structure-experience gap, which substrate independence falls back on the moment a structural property *S* is offered. That is the explanatory gap in aesthetic dress, not a new line of evidence."
2. **`aesthetic-testimony`** L79: "Two routes to 'some knowledge is essentially experiential' would be a stronger evidential situation than either alone *if the routes were independent*, and here the independence is only partial… The corroboration is real to the degree the two resist a *common* debunking; it is not automatic from their merely being two, and the Map has not yet identified a debunking that separates them."
3. **`the-comic`** L72: "They are not two independent routes to the same conclusion; they are two applications of one intuition, and an opponent who neutralizes the intuition neutralizes both together."

Three articles, three authors' worth of drafting, one conclusion nobody prompted. Birch's praise-worthy category is *tenet-as-evidence-upgrade that is praise-worthily not done*; this is the count-inflation version of the same restraint, and the wing declines it three times.

**Two further restraints deserve naming.**

`aesthetics-beyond-art` L125 flags a citation that cuts against the Map's own binding argument rather than quietly using the half that helps: "Irvin is **dual-use**, not an ally. Her pervasiveness thesis helps the Map's premise, but the very same paper denies that everyday aesthetic experience requires *unity or closure* — and the unity of aesthetic experience is one of the premises the case in [[aesthetics-and-consciousness]] rests on. Enlisting Irvin for pervasiveness while ignoring that she cuts against the binding argument would be selective citation." Naming your own selective-citation temptation in the article is rare.

`the-comic` supplies an actual discriminator and refuses to predict its result (L76): "if mirth is the reward for a covert-error retraction, its intensity should track the magnitude and the covertness of the commitment retracted, and should be indifferent to features of the joke that leave that retraction unchanged. A felt quality not fixed by the structural facts carries no such prediction. Nobody has run the discriminating experiment, and the Map does not claim its result in advance." The article also states its opponent's best reply and then concedes the reply is good: "The answer is available and it is good" (L66).

## Content Strengths

### aesthetics-and-consciousness.md
- **Strongest point**: The self-audit that deflates its own headline. The article's `description` frontmatter carries it too — "its five arguments rest on three premises, not five independent ones: the case is concentration, not tally" — so the discount is visible to a chatbot that reads only the metadata.
- **Why it works**: It converts a weakness (the five lines are not independent) into the actual thesis (one phenomenon puts all three premises in play at once). The argument survives the audit because the audit was designed into it.

### aesthetic-testimony-and-the-acquaintance-principle.md
- **Strongest point**: Concluding against the principle the article is named for, on the testimony of the principle's *defender*.
- **Why it works**: The pessimism/optimism map is drawn before the Map's reading is stated, and the Relation-to-Site-Perspective section opens by marking the transition explicitly (L77: "The preceding sections report what the philosophers of aesthetics claim. This section states how the Map reads that material"). The reader can see exactly where reporting stops and interpretation begins.

### the-comic-and-humor-as-an-aesthetic-category.md
- **Strongest point**: Stating the naturalising rival at a strength that costs the article its conclusion. The *Inside Jokes* section reconstructs Hurley, Dennett and Adams's mechanism, grants that "**no experiential primitive is posited or needed**" (L62), and then supplies the rival's answer to the article's own best case before conceding "The answer is available and it is good" (L66).
- **Why it works**: The disagreement is then located precisely — "is the retraction event individuated by its functional profile alone, or partly by how it feels?" — and labelled as what it is: "a disagreement at the framework boundary rather than a refutation inside it."

### the-sublime-and-negative-aesthetics.md
- **Strongest point**: The hedonist's last reply is answered by showing the reply is a concession (L88): once "aesthetic pleasure" is stretched to cover terror, grief and revulsion, "it has become a placeholder for whatever evaluative character the aesthetic dimension carries — which is exactly the pluralist's distinct dimension under a hedonic-sounding name."
- **Why it works**: The article does not claim victory from it. The sentence ends "The dispute then is verbal where it is not lost" — conceding that a verbal win is not a win.

### aesthetics-beyond-art.md
- **Strongest point**: Running the strengthening and diluting readings of the *same* datum side by side, from the lead (L39) onward, and declining to resolve them: "This article lays out both subfields and the tensions; it does not claim the tensions resolve in the Map's favour."
- **Why it works**: Pervasiveness is the wing's most tempting piece of evidence and its most dangerous. The article names the danger (a category applying to everything discriminates nothing) as the field's own central worry rather than as a foil.

### phenomenology-of-musical-understanding.md
- **Strongest point**: The predictive-coding section, which grants the rival its full explanatory range — "It accounts for why unfamiliar tonal systems gradually become meaningful… why expert listeners hear differently from novices, why deceptive cadences evoke their specific quality of thwarted expectation" (L89) — and then locates the disagreement narrowly: "The divergence from strong functionalism is not about the mechanics but about whether those mechanics exhaust what requires explanation" (L93).
- **Also**: The Fritz et al. (2009) treatment is properly hedged, naming the confound (Western stimuli; tempo and timbre cues alongside mode) rather than banking a universality claim the study cannot carry.

## Calibration Concerns

**Revonsuo is recruited against his own position — one locus, live on the published site.** `obsidian/topics/aesthetics-and-consciousness.md` L128 reads:

> If [[unity-of-consciousness|phenomenal unity]] in seeing a red apple already poses problems for materialism (Revonsuo, 2006), the unity of experiencing beauty—that same six-domain binding, carrying intrinsic value—poses those problems in amplified form.

Revonsuo is a biological naturalist. The article's own reference list gives the book's subtitle: "Revonsuo, A. (2006). *Inner Presence: Consciousness as a Biological Phenomenon*. MIT Press" (L198). Everywhere else in the corpus the framing is correct — `topics/lucid-dreaming-and-dualist-rendering.md` L48 calls him one of the "[l]eading **physicalist** dream researchers"; `research/lucid-dreaming-mind-side-rendering-2026-03-09.md` L85 records "Revonsuo's evolutionary framing is physicalist"; `research/consciousness-dreams-2026-01-14.md` L132 notes "Revonsuo's naturalism differs from site's dualism"; `topics/quantum-holism-and-phenomenal-unity.md` and `project/cluster-integration-discipline.md` L59 cite him only for the BP1/BP2 *taxonomy*, which is neutral and correct.

The provenance is visible. `archive/topics/binding-and-beauty.md` L119 — one of the four articles coalesced into the hub — makes the claim with **no citation at all**: "If the unity of seeing a red apple already poses problems for materialism, the unity of experiencing beauty… poses those problems in amplified form." The coalesce attached an authority to a previously bare assertion, and attached the wrong kind.

This survived direct inspection. `reviews/deep-review-2026-07-18-unity-of-consciousness.md` L50 lists this exact locus as "**Deliberately NOT changed**" on the ground that it "correctly cite[s] Revonsuo's genuine *2006 book* Inner Presence." The metadata verdict is right; the framing question was not the one being asked. The fix is re-framing, not deletion — the BP2 unity puzzle is real and Revonsuo did articulate it, but as a hard problem *for consciousness science*, not as trouble for materialism.

A `refine-draft` task is queued. This is the one place in the wing where the Process Philosopher's reading and the Hardline Empiricist's diverge, and it resolves into a specific line rather than a general worry.
