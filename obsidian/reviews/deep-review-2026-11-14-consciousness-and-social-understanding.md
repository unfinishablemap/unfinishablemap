## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found (all fixed)

1. **L111 Zahavi 2001 misreads the paper's title thesis.** The old text said "Zahavi (2001) argues that this goes beyond empathy as traditionally conceived — it involves a direct perceptual encounter…". In the paper, "beyond empathy" means that the phenomenologists' analyses of intersubjectivity go past the face-to-face empathic encounter. The abstract of the 2010 republication (*Santalka* 18(1):69-82, via OpenAlex) says intersubjectivity "doesn't merely concern concrete face-to-face encounters between individuals. It is also something that is at play in simple perception, in tool-use…". The old sentence also hung direct perception on "this", which referred to *imaginative* perspective-taking. Direct perception is the view that sets itself against simulation and imaginative projection. The Map's own `concepts/empathy` L79 already reads the paper correctly, so the article contradicted a sibling page. **Fix:** "A more basic layer lies beneath such shifts: following Scheler, Zahavi (2001) describes a direct perceptual encounter with the other as a subject, not mere inference from behavioural cues." This is word-neutral. It keeps the "direct perceptual encounter with the other as a subject" wording that the 2026-03-26b review harmonised, and the layering claim stays in the Map's voice.
2. **L153 Stueber 2006 inverted.** The old text said "genuine empathy goes beyond folk-psychological prediction". Stueber's thesis is that empathy is *central to* folk psychology. The book abstract (OpenAlex W1599669677, raw) reads: "Stueber argues that empathy is epistemically central for our folk-psychological understanding of other agents". **Fix:** "folk-psychological understanding of other agents depends centrally on empathy, including re-enactment of the other's perspective." (+1). The forgiveness argument that follows still stands.
3. **L65 Nagel 1974 over-attributed.** The old text said "what Nagel (1974) established as the irreducible 'what it is like' dimension". Nagel argued for the gap and declined the dualist conclusion. Raw JSTOR text (Bremen course PDF): "It would be a mistake to conclude that physicalism must be false" and "physicalism is a position we cannot understand because we do not at present have any conception of how it might be true". **Fix:** "the 'what it is like' dimension that Nagel (1974) argued objective physical theory leaves out, without concluding that physicalism is false." (+7). The clause gives the author's stance, on the model of `topics/predictive-processing-and-dualism` L48.
4. **L115 "(automatic, unconscious)" was a stranded sibling of the 09-23 de Waal repair.** That repair withdrew "requires no consciousness" at L117 and added that "de Waal himself does not claim contagion occurs without consciousness". The parenthetical two lines above still asserted the stronger claim in the Map's voice. De Waal's abstract says perception "automatically activates shared representations causing a matching emotional state in the observer", and an emotional state is ordinarily felt. **Fix:** "(automatic, without registering whose feeling it is)" (+4). This matches `concepts/empathy` L47 and L117's "no grasp of the other *as* a subject".

### Medium Issues Found (all fixed)

- **L77 source scope (article-compresses-a-distinction-its-research-note-got-right).** "Where phenomenal histories diverge radically … the more common readout is …: perspective-takers stop adjusting…" Epley 2004 and Krueger & Clement 1994 studied ordinary participants. The research note's L222 says so explicitly ("in general populations"), and its L47 adds "Between similar minds the template mostly fits". No cited study measures the readout across radical divergence, so "more common" asserted a frequency nobody measured. **Fix:** "the likelier readout is a completed reading that feels like success, since perspective-takers in general stop adjusting…" (+2). The claim now reads as a prediction from the general stopping rule.
- **L109 Husserl "called this process the 'pairing'".** "This process" is the gradual, conversation-by-conversation deepening. In raw CM §51 (Cairns translation, CSULB course PDF), pairing is "a primal form of that passive synthesis which we designate as 'association'". The ongoing confirmation runs through "new appresentations that proceed in a synthetic[ally harmonious]" sequence. **Fix:** "grounded this process in the 'pairing'" (+1). The term the 06-22 pessimistic review certified is unchanged.
- **L121 Tomasello "provides the developmental evidence" (stance and calibration).** The sentence came right after "…depends on phenomenal subjects". It therefore read as a naturalist's data evidencing the Map's phenomenal-dependence claim, which the article's own L127 disclaims ("not an independent confirmation"). **Fix:** "documents the developmental side" (±0).
