Per this skill's design, the Process Philosopher and the Hardline Empiricist converge on an honestly calibrated article. Across seven of eight they now do. On [[lucid-dreaming-and-dualist-rendering]] they part — **not in the body, which is exemplary, but in the `description:` line alone.**

The description (L3) asserts:

> "Lucid dreaming **reveals that** consciousness renders phenomenal worlds through neural architecture rather than from it—the rendering dilemma, capability signatures, vividness surplus, and degradation asymmetry **converge on mind-side generation**."

The body declines that conclusion **four separate times**:

- L48: "The dilemma itself is neutral between the two readings — it sharpens the question rather than settling it — and the case below argues only that the dualist horn carries less explanatory debt, **not that the evidence forces it**."
- L66: "**None of this decides the production-versus-filter question by itself**; it relocates the explanatory burden, which is the most the phenomenological observation can do."
- L74: "The physicalist has responses, **and they are not weak ones**… they suffice to **keep the productivist horn open**."
- L76: "**neither reading is forced** by the dream phenomenology alone, which is **compatible with both** the production and the filter framings."

Three independent checks confirm the mismatch is real rather than a reading of mine:

1. **The body never makes the description's claim.** A grep for `converge` across the file returns three hits, all at L48/L58 and all about *physicalist researchers converging on a concession* — never about the four signatures converging on mind-side generation. The description's central assertion appears nowhere in the article.

The register names lucid dreaming specifically and declines the description's register.
3. **The wing has already fixed this exact defect twice, on the same ground.** `8efc6199fd` rewrote the anaesthesia description citing P-CS4; `9f514c5090` rewrote the sleep-and-consciousness description this morning. This is the third and last instance of a family the wing has otherwise closed.
