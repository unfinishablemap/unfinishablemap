## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

EUD handles Chalmers's own Vulcan case against the Map's criterion and says plainly which side it takes: "The narrower criterion changes no verdict here; it settles which property the verdicts track." (EUD L109). It then books the cost as defeater 6 (L192), where the necessity half of P-MS1 "would fall with it". A page that cites its named ally for the conclusion it rejects, and lists that conclusion as a live defeater, is engaging him at full strength.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

The wing keeps the mechanism where it belongs. EUD L81: "the quantum proposal is the Map's candidate, not a premise the ethics requires". ECE L92 notes that the register's positions concern token selection, so the bypass argument "imports a locality assumption the Map has not registered". Stapp would want more commitment, but the pages are right that the ethics does not depend on it.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

CNF's distinction between indecision and conflict is the best single phenomenological observation in the wing: "in indecision, the agent lacks sufficient reason; in normative conflict, the agent has *too much* reason directed at incompatible actions" (CNF L117). PML's gestalt description (L61–65) and its three attention predicates (receptive, sustained, ego-diminished; L79) do similar work. These are descriptions a reader can test against their own experience, which is what the method requires.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

PML L103 treats the arc from tension through commitment to remainder as "a unified phenomenal episode", an event-structured reading of moral agency. Nothing in the wing uses process-friendliness to move any organism up the evidential scale. EUD's taxonomy (L111–117) takes its tiers from the declarations, not from the tenets.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

DUR L107 states the luck objection against its own position more sharply than most critics do: "Replay the interrogation and, if the outcome is open, some replays end in endurance and others in a break, with the same reasons and the same felt strain present in every one." It then says what the Map's answer costs (a primitive "whose modal structure is owed rather than supplied"). Kane would recognise both the problem and the honesty.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

NV L52 draws the distinction the whole void turns on: "This distinction matters: unreliability concerns *accuracy*, not *accessibility*." The 07-18 deep review named it as a strength, and it is. NV L102 gives the void a usable phenomenological signature: "not \"they're wrong\" but \"I can't parse what they're claiming.\""

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

This persona's verdict is the one the wing most depends on, and for five pages it is favourable.

- EUD L121 borrows Birch's procedure and declines to enlist him: "so the Map borrows his decision procedure without claiming him as an ally on the grounding question". EUD L113 separates the declaration's tier from the Map's own: "the table's lower insect credence is the Map's own". The New York Declaration wording was fetched today and matches (see ledger).
- DUR L83 names the test that would matter and says it has not been run: "What would discriminate is not the *size* of the unexplained variation but its *structure*". The residual is then called "an interpretation the residual permits, not a result it establishes." (count 2: L83 and the closing L119).
- PML L125 declines to count contemplative reports as evidence: "Skilled cultivation predicts the same course, so these reports are consonant with moral realism without testing it against projection."
- ECE L52 runs the control an empiricist would ask for: "Holding welfare, autonomy, experiential effects, personal continuity, social pressure, and distribution constant, does adding the dualist premise change what one ought to do?" and answers no for five of six conclusions.

Two reservations. CNF has not had this pass (see [Priority List](#priority-list), item 2). And EUD L115 asserts flatly that "Bacteria, plants, and artifacts lack subjects for whom responses constitute experience." The heading above it hedges ("Probably not conscious (framework-dependent)") but the sentence does not; this is a downward certainty, so it is not tier-upgrade slippage, and with 27 words of headroom it is noted only.

No Process-versus-Empiricist conflict was found: no passage in the six uses tenet coherence to raise an evidential tier.
