## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

**1. Misattributed reply — the memory-is-wide move credited to Tye rather than Lycan. FIXED.**

The "Go wide on content" paragraph read: *"Tye adds that memory cannot be trusted to detect the shift, because memory contents are wide too and undergo the same reinterpretation."* SEP *Representational Theories of Consciousness* attributes the move to Lycan first: "Memory contents are wide, and so by Block's own reasoning they will themselves undergo the representational shift to the Inverted-Earth complementary color. Thus, your post-shift memories of good old Earth are false. **(Lycan (1996) takes this line; Tye (1998) expands it in more detail.)**"

This is an attribution error of the kind §2.5 flags as critical — a named reply credited to the wrong author, with the originator dropped. It is aggravated by the fact that Tye's *other* well-known Inverted Earth reply runs the opposite way (deny that the nonconceptual content shifts at all, via optimal/design conditions), so crediting the wide-memory move to Tye alone risks reading as Tye's official position on the case.

Corrected to: *"Lycan adds that memory cannot be trusted to detect the shift … the traveller's memory of the Earth sky as blue becomes, post-shift, a memory of it as yellow, and so a false one — a line Tye later expands in more detail."* The "and so a false one" clause was added because that is the consequence SEP actually draws.

**Citation decision recorded**: Tye's expansion is Tye (1998), *Inverted Earth, Swampman, and Representationism*, *Philosophical Perspectives* 12. It was deliberately **not** added to References. The publisher of record could not be reached (JSTOR vol. 12 TOC → 403; Wiley TOC → 402), and secondary sources disagree on two details: the title's final word ("Representationism" vs "Representationalism") and the end page (459–477 vs 459–478; the volume also appears as *Noûs* 32, S12). Rather than mint a References entry with contested metadata, the body attributes Tye's expansion without a year, so no inline `Author YYYY` cite is created and no orphan arises. A future review with publisher access can add the entry.
