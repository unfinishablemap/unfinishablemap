## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

The threshold article does something with the explanatory gap that Chalmers would recognise as a
*result* rather than a credential: it converts the gap into a discriminator between rival ethical
theories. The standard arbitrariness objection — a status-conferring capacity comes in degrees, so a
line on it is arbitrary — is conceded in full force, "for any threshold drawn on a scalar capacity,
the personhood tier included", and the Map's threshold is then argued to survive because the property
it is drawn on is not a magnitude. The article then states, unprompted, the two theories under which
its own reply fails: "Under [[illusionism]] there is no phenomenal property to be present … Under
[[integrated-information-theory]] any system with Φ above zero has some experience and Φ varies
continuously, so being an experiencer is a magnitude and a threshold on it a cut on a scale." A
metaphysics that changes the verdict in normative ethics, with the conditions of its own failure
named, is the hard problem doing work.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

Nothing in this wing runs through the interface mechanism, and the wing is better for saying so. The
threshold article's tenet section states plainly that Minimal Quantum Interaction and Bidirectional
Interaction "do not bear on the verdict". `marginal-organism-scope`, which does touch the mechanism,
uses it in the *restraining* direction: "Attributing a valence-to-physics coupling to ever-simpler
organisms multiplies the non-physical influence the framework posits. Minimal interaction counsels
caution about extending value-sensitive selection downward without independent evidence — a tenet-side
reason the value-blind reading of marginal cases is the conservative default." A tenet deployed
against the framework's own preferred expansion is rarer and more creditable than a tenet deployed
for it.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

The welfare article's treatment of the experience machine is the wing's best piece of first-person
discipline, because it refuses to win. It reads Lin's 2026 renewal — the machine world lacks another
mind aware of you — as a deficit *of experience*, someone else's, and then states the cost in its own
voice: "The machine asks about *the subject's* welfare, and the Map has answered a question about
value in the world. If the intuition is stubbornly first-personal — I would be worse off, not merely
the world — then either welfare is a share in the world's felt value, which a welfare theorist will
call a change of subject, or the global requirement is false and the Map must retreat to the local
one." Naming the reply a possible change of subject, in the objector's vocabulary, is what taking the
first person seriously looks like when it is inconvenient.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

The wing's process-friendly material is all in `marginal-organism-scope`, and the article is
constructed so that the resonance cannot cash out. `Physarum` — a single brainless cell that
habituates — is exactly where "experience all the way down" wants to be given its head, and the
article puts the case as a disjunction and takes the deflating horn: "*Either* valenced-looking
behaviour can be produced by wholly value-blind, mechanistic processes — in which case
approach/avoidance is **not** a reliable marker of value-sensitive selection anywhere, weakening the
inference up the whole ladder — *or* the Map must extend conscious value-sensitive selection to a
brainless cell … The Map takes the first horn." It then runs the deflation *upward* against its own
best case: "the *Physarum* deflation below is a global solvent: once approach/avoidance is no reliable
marker of value-sensitive selection *anywhere*, the behavioural half of the rat's case is demoted
too." Process-philosophical resonance is present, acknowledged, and spent on nothing.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

The threshold article keeps the agent-side and patient-side norms apart at the one place they are
easiest to fuse. Carter's opacity respect is available as a ready-made ground for basic equality and
would have been a cheap win; the article declines it on a structural ground — "opacity respect is a
norm about agents, and the Map's agent-side norms live in the
[[responsibility-gradient-from-attentional-capacity|responsibility gradient]], the desert column
[[ethics-of-consciousness-invertebrate-question#Moral Status Without Moral Agency|the invertebrate
article]] keeps apart from status and stakes." Refusing an argument that would have worked, because
it belongs to a different column of the Map's own ethics, is the right kind of tidiness: it keeps
agency earning its own keep instead of being borrowed to prop up patiency.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

`marginal-organism-scope` closes on the distinction a mysterian most wants held and most often sees
collapsed: "These are *epistemic* claims, and the article commits to the epistemic reading: there is
a determinate boundary where value-sensitive selection bottoms out, but it cannot be located from
outside. … The stronger *metaphysical* reading — that there is no fact of the matter at the margin —
is a constitutive claim inaccessibility alone does not license per [[evidential-status-discipline]];
it would need an argued bridging step the framework has not made, so it is not asserted here."
Inaccessibility is claimed and indeterminacy is declined, with the missing step named. This is also
the safe direction of over-claim to avoid: an "in principle undetectable" that ran against the Map
would be the kind of over-concession that collects endorsements, and the article does not make it.

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

This persona's verdict is load-bearing for this wing, and all three articles pass — one of them
exemplarily.

**The restraint, named.** `marginal-organism-scope` grades the nematode at **speculative** and
*Hydra* at **speculative integration**, on a ladder whose top rung — the rat, with opioid hedonic
hotspots and Sneddon-criteria pain — is held at **live hypothesis** and no higher. The article states
the temptation and refuses it in its own words rather than by reciting the house formula: "It is
tempting to let the Map's commitments do the work: dualism makes consciousness substrate-flexible,
the [[universal-coupling-response]] denies any principled threshold, so perhaps value-sensitive
selection reaches far down. **This temptation must be resisted.** The tenets remove a *defeater* … but
removing a defeater is not the same as supplying positive evidence. … A boundary case that is merely
*not excluded* stays at live hypothesis; it does not climb to realistic possibility on the strength of
the framework finding it congenial." This is the exact article-shape that in an earlier instance
elsewhere in the corpus went the other way — a minimal organism promoted to *realistic possibility* on
tenet-load — and it is worth recording that the nematode here is graded two tiers lower than that,
on the same tenets.

**The ceiling, honoured.** The same article caps its own ladder against the position it presupposes:
"The ladder's statuses are also positions beneath a ceiling. The horn they grade is held, per
[[positions/value-in-selection|P-VS1]], on prior commitments at low credence, citable as a
framework-internal coherence result only; 'live hypothesis' for the rat means live *relative to that
ceiling*, and no rung sits higher than the horn it presupposes." A tier that is explicitly relative to
its parent's band cannot be quoted out of context as a standalone probability.

**Tenet-as-evidence-upgrade, praise-worthily not done, twice more.** The threshold article had the
structural opportunity to present its presence-based reply as a *result* — a threshold the
arbitrariness objection cannot touch — and instead prices it: "Whether a subject is present at all is
the prior question, the [[phenomenal-sorites-problem]], where ontic vagueness is the live rival and
the Map's resistance is recorded as motivated by Tenet 4 rather than established; **the threshold is
exactly as secure as that resistance.**" Its Occam's-Razor paragraph then runs the tenet *against* the
Map's own move: it "forbids invoking simplicity for the claim that the *presence* of a subject is
binary". The welfare article does the same on its own premise — "The tenet removes a defeater without
supplying the identity thesis, on which the global requirement still rests" — and on its AI
application reads *both* sides of a live dispute as parsimony appeals the tenet disarms, including
the side that favours the Map.

**Framework-stage honesty about what the wing is.** The threshold article separates a
framework-neutral verdict from a framework-dependent defence and gets the relation between them
right, which is the harder half. It holds the unitarian verdict "on their grounds too" (Kagan,
DeGrazia, Wendler, Sebo — "Neither Sebo nor Wendler needs dualism, and the Map does not claim they
share it"), then observes that those same allies cannot make the Map's reply: "A unitarian without
Tenet 1 must defend the threshold as Buchanan defends his, conceding a 'fuzzy lower boundary' and
falling back on uncontroversial cases — the vagueness reply Floris and Timmer reject." Borrowing
someone's conclusion while stating that their defence of it is weaker than yours, in the same
article, is a distinction most borrowings never draw.

**Persona tension resolved, not papered over.** Where the Process Philosopher's praise would cash out
as a tier upgrade — *Physarum*, *Hydra*, the nematode — `marginal-organism-scope` intervenes on its
own text, and both personas end up praising the same paragraphs for different reasons. No
`refine-draft` is generated on the calibration axis anywhere in this wing.
