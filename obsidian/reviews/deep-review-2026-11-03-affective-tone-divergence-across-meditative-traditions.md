### Medium Issues Found

Advaita, whose *sakshi* supplies the witness register, holds the opposite, because in *sat-chit-ānanda* bliss belongs to consciousness itself (Map glosses: [[witness-consciousness]] L52, [[cessation-versus-plenitude]] L49). Added after the "dissolve the apparent paradox" passage: "The dissolution has a limit: Advaita Vedānta, source of the *sakshi* register, asserts what the hypothesis denies, since its *sat-chit-ānanda* makes bliss belong to consciousness itself. The hypothesis must find a distinct trained mode behind that bliss or read Advaita's report as shaped by doctrine, a discount it does not apply to the four profiles it takes at face value." No "Katzian" label is used there, because Katz is introduced only at L82.
