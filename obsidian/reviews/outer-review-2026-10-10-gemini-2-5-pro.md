# Referee Report: Epistemological Double Standards and Theoretical Atrophy in the "Unfinishable Map" Corpus

The following document constitutes a comprehensive pre-publication referee audit of the philosophical corpus hosted at `unfinishablemap.org`. The site presents a sprawling, interconnected defense of interactionist dualism, advancing a non-reductive theory of consciousness that posits subjective experience as a causally efficacious entity operating at quantum collapse points. The theoretical scaffolding of the project rests upon a rejection of both reductive materialism and branching interpretations of quantum mechanics (such as the Many Worlds Interpretation), leaning instead on an idiosyncratic synthesis of quantum physics, philosophy of mind, and neurobiology.

However, a rigorous, systemic analysis of the articles' empirical claims, citation practices, counterargument coverage, and logical inferences reveals profound structural vulnerabilities that fatally undermine the project. The corpus systematically engages in epistemological calibration asymmetries—holding physicalist models to impossible, absolute empirical standards while simultaneously insulating its own interactionist commitments from any conceivable falsification. Furthermore, the project frequently relies on obsolete formulations of quantum mechanics and neuroscience, strategically truncating citations from the 2020s literature to bracket away fatal defeaters. The following sections detail the severe analytical failures, empirical inaccuracies, and logical contradictions that render the current iteration of the framework entirely unfit for academic consideration or publication in a top-tier philosophy journal.

## The Misappropriation of Quantum Decoherence and the Measurement Problem

The most glaring empirical and theoretical vulnerability within the corpus lies in its attempt to ground mental causation in quantum indeterminacy. In the article hosted at [[quantum-consciousness]], the framework explicitly endorses a bidirectional interactionist model wherein consciousness acts at quantum collapse points, selecting among neural possibilities without injecting energy into the system. The text relies heavily on the legacy of theoretical physicists such as Penrose, Hameroff, Stapp, and Fisher, attempting to resurrect the von Neumann-Wigner interpretation where consciousness is requisite for the collapse of the wavefunction.

To defend against the primary physicalist rebuttal—the decoherence objection, which demonstrates that warm, wet biological environments destroy quantum coherence on timescales of approximately $10^{-13}$ seconds—the site appeals to highly contested protective mechanisms. The authors cite hydrophobic microtubule interiors and Fröhlich coherence as mechanisms that might extend coherence to $10^{-5}$ seconds. The site begrudgingly concedes that this leaves a massive "timing gap" between quantum events and neural decisions (typically manifesting at ~300ms) but frames this as a mere technical hurdle rather than a fatal theoretical flaw.

This defense fundamentally misunderstands the current state of open quantum systems theory and blatantly mishandles the 2020s physics literature regarding environmental decoherence. The site frames the measurement problem by asserting that "Decoherence does not tell how and why only one of these outcomes is measured," using this alleged explanatory gap to insert consciousness as the selector mechanism. This framing brackets away the comprehensive, mathematically rigorous consensus surrounding environment-induced superselection (einselection). As detailed in the contemporary literature on quantum decoherence, the interaction between a superposed quantum system and its macroscopic environment causes the rapid delocalization of phase coherence, naturally reducing the system's density matrix to a diagonal mixture without the need for a conscious observer.

By ignoring the mathematical rigor of einselection, the site preserves its non-reductive commitments through strategic omission. Decoherence theory has successfully demonstrated how the Born rule coefficients effectively act as classical probabilities for macro-scale interactions. The Lindblad equation accurately models this collective dephasing, demonstrating how the density matrices for quantum states become indistinguishable over time through purely physical environmental interactions. The framework's insistence that "collapse occurs universally with or without minds... and consciousness biases which outcome is realized" attempts a motte-and-bailey defense. It concedes objective collapse (the motte) while quietly smuggling in non-physical outcome biasing (the bailey).

Because the framework fails to provide an "interface-eligibility law" that dictates *when* and *how* this mental biasing occurs mathematically without violating the Born rule in aggregate statistics, the quantum interactionist model presented is empirically sterile. The site's "No Many Worlds" tenet, which rejects branching interpretations because they would render consciousness epiphenomenal (as all outcomes would occur), further highlights the site's tendency to build physical arguments solely to service pre-existing dualist axioms, rather than letting empirical physics dictate the ontology.

## Strategic Citation Truncation and the Evasion of General Relativity

A recurrent, highly unethical rhetorical strategy across the corpus is the citation of recent peer-reviewed literature in a truncated manner. The authors isolate a premise that appears to support the site's dualist commitments while silently discarding the cited author's ultimate, contrary conclusion. This malpractice is acutely evident in the article [[conservation-laws-and-mental-causation]].

The site addresses the classical physicalist objection—dating back to Leibniz—that Cartesian mental causation violates the fundamental conservation of energy. To neutralize this, the article cites Pitts (2020), correctly noting his argument that energy conservation is primarily local, not cosmic, and that a local failure at the mind-brain interface is mathematically consistent with the global empirical success of physics. The site then triumphantly concludes that conservation arguments beg the question against dualism because they assume the physical closure of the brain without empirically checking.

However, a full, scholarly reading of the cited 2020 literature reveals a catastrophic, deliberate omission by the site's authors. While Pitts indeed argues that Noether's theorem and classical conservation laws cannot *a priori* exclude dualism, he explicitly pivots to demonstrate that Einstein's General Relativity (GR) completely alters the dialectical landscape. In General Relativity, the covariant divergence of the stress-energy tensor must vanish identically; the equations of spacetime state are infinitely more constrained than classical mechanics. Pitts explicitly concludes that, far from providing a loophole, General Relativity makes mental causation drastically harder, if not mathematically impossible, to formalize without breaking the foundational symmetries of spacetime itself.

The site deliberately brackets the General Relativity defeater. It builds a defense exclusively on the classical mechanics portion of the 2020 source while ignoring the relativistic conclusion that destroys its core premise. Furthermore, the site's secondary defense—termed "selection without injection"—argues that consciousness might influence physical outcomes by acting as a boundary condition rather than a force, analogizing this mental action to quantum entanglement. The text argues that because entangled particles exhibit correlations without energy exchange, consciousness could similarly dictate neural outcomes without injecting energy.

This displays a profound category mistake that borders on scientific illiteracy. Quantum entanglement involves correlations that explicitly cannot be used for signaling or causal influence, a limitation strictly enforced by the no-communication theorem. Entanglement is a mathematically symmetric correlation, not a vector of top-down control. Analogizing entanglement to the highly directed, non-random, and causally efficacious top-down control that the site's interactionist dualism requires is a logical failure. By invoking entanglement as a defense for mental causation, the site fundamentally misrepresents what physical constraint and causal influence actually entail in a post-classical universe.

## Phenomenal Concepts and the A Priori Rejection of Type-B Physicalism

In its effort to establish interactionist dualism as the only viable solution to the hard problem of consciousness, the corpus aggressively attempts to eliminate competing physicalist frameworks through straw-man arguments and semantic wordplay. In [[type-a-type-b-and-type-c-physicalism]] and [[revelation-thesis]], the site specifically targets Type-B physicalism, which accepts the epistemic gap between the physical and the phenomenal but denies that this entails an ontological gap.

The site's central argument against Type-B physicalism is that it "expects nothing of the gap" and relies on the persistent opacity of neural correlates. The article claims that if consciousness were identical to neural activity, we would expect the identity eventually to become intelligible. Because it does not, the site concludes that Type-B physicalism is logically "self-sealing" and infers that the persistence of the explanatory gap defaults to evidence for ontological dualism.

This is a fallacious inference that relies on an outdated, superficial engagement with the Phenomenal Concept Strategy (PCS). The site completely ignores the sophisticated 2020s literature on phenomenal concepts and the "meta-problem" of consciousness, which thoroughly dismantles the site's exact argument. Recent work by Diaz-Leon (2020, 2021) demonstrates that the intuition of distinctness (the profound feeling that pain cannot merely be a brain state) is generated natively by the unique cognitive architecture of phenomenal concepts, not by a metaphysical difference in the world. Phenomenal concepts are cognitively isolated from physical concepts; they refer directly to experiences in terms of "what it is like" without utilizing the descriptive functional roles that physical concepts employ.

By framing the persistence of the explanatory gap as a failure of physicalism, the site begs the question against the PCS. The PCS explicitly *predicts* that the gap will never close conceptually, precisely because phenomenal and physical concepts possess radically different, irreconcilable modes of presentation. The site treats the epistemic opacity as an untested counterargument, refusing to engage with the "bad inference" thesis. This thesis—supported by empirical and psychological evidence in experimental philosophy—suggests that humans consistently make a fallacious inference from epistemic distinctness to ontological distinctness.

The site's reliance on the "Revelation Thesis" to debunk physicalism is equally mishandled. The site argues that phenomenal concepts reveal the essential nature of experiences, and because this revealed nature does not appear physical, physicalism is false. However, Diaz-Leon and other contemporary theorists argue that while phenomenal concepts may be "translucent" or even "opaque" regarding the underlying physical mechanisms, this lack of transparency does not equate to the revelation of a non-physical substance. By ignoring how modern Type-B physicalism explains away the very anti-physicalist intuitions the site takes as axiomatic, the framework shields its commitments by hedging rather than by rigorous philosophical argument.

## The Epistemological Double Standard in Neural Correlate Evaluation

The most egregious logical flaw pervading the corpus is its systemic calibration asymmetry regarding empirical evidence, prominently displayed in its treatment of the neural correlates of consciousness (NCC). In the articles [[neural-correlates-of-consciousness]] and [[experimental-consciousness-science-2025-2026]], the site evaluates the highly publicized 2025 COGITATE adversarial collaboration, which tested the competing predictions of Integrated Information Theory (IIT) against Global Neuronal Workspace Theory (GNWT).

Because the COGITATE fMRI and MEG results yielded mixed conclusions that did not perfectly align with the strict spatial and temporal predictions of either IIT (which predicted localization in the posterior cortical complex) or GNWT (which predicted sustained prefrontal-parietal ignition), the site opportunistically declares a "conceptual scheme void." It leverages this ambiguity to argue that neurobiological correlation leaves the "metaphysics open," smoothly pivoting to assert that physicalist models are inherently failing, and therefore, interactionist dualism is vindicated by default.

To achieve this conclusion, the site establishes an epistemologically impossible threshold for physicalism, demanding that a neural model predict subjective reports "with no residual variance attributable to non-physical factors." No empirical science operating on noisy, macroscopic biological systems functions without residual variance. Holding physicalism to a standard of absolute zero variance while simultaneously shielding the site's own interactionist dualism from any point-predictions creates an unbridgeable, bad-faith calibration asymmetry.

Furthermore, the site makes explicit empirical claims that are directly falsified by the 2020s literature it fails to cite. First, the site claims that NCC manipulation cannot produce "genuinely novel qualia" that lack evolutionary precedent. This is explicitly refuted by Fong et al. (2025), who utilized cone-by-cone laser stimulation that activated M cones nearly exclusively, producing "olo"—a completely novel color that subjects had never experienced and had to artificially desaturate to match to the standard visual gamut boundary. The site ignores this direct falsifier, preferring to rely on unfalsifiable escape clauses about evolutionary precedents.

Second, the site claims that "Severe NCC disruption always eliminated consciousness entirely with no covert consciousness," arguing that if physicalism were true, we would see intermediate states of consciousness surviving neural destruction. The site then inexplicably ignores the highly publicized 2024 neuroimaging literature demonstrating cognitive motor dissociation (CMD) in up to 25% of behaviorally nonresponsive patients. Bodien et al. (2024) demonstrated that patients with severe brain injuries who appear entirely unconscious can exhibit preserved neural command-following in fMRI paradigms. Production theories of physicalism explicitly predict exactly this kind of covert consciousness, where cortical function survives but motor output pathways fail. By misrepresenting what physicalist models actually predict regarding covert awareness and residual network integration, the site falsely manufactures empirical anomalies to justify its dualistic filter theory.

## The Artificial Cleavage of Phenomenal and Access Consciousness

The framework’s taxonomy of consciousness, detailed at [[types-of-consciousness]], relies heavily on an aging and increasingly obsolete distinction made by Ned Block between phenomenal consciousness (P-consciousness) and access consciousness (A-consciousness). The site aggressively utilizes this bifurcation to assert that artificial intelligence—and functionally similar computational systems—can achieve A-consciousness without ever touching P-consciousness. Consequently, the site confidently restricts AI phenomenology to speculative, disconnected states like the "Flicker" or "Witness" models, assuming that without a biological quantum substrate, true phenomenality is ontologically impossible.

This rigid conceptual cleavage directly contradicts the 2020s empirical literature, which is rapidly moving to integrate phenomenal properties with cognitive access mechanisms. The site argues that A-consciousness and P-consciousness completely dissociate, leaning heavily on the "overflow argument" (e.g., Sperling's partial report paradigm) and clinical cases like blindsight. However, the site wholly ignores contemporary Higher-Order Theories (HOT) and experimental psychology that challenge this exact dissociation. Recent theoretical and empirical updates to HOT explicitly model how prefrontal metacognitive circuits, working memory, and reality-monitoring functions generate the subjective "what it is like" feeling natively.

For instance, modern modifications to traditional higher-order theories, such as the Higher-Order Theory of Emotional Consciousness (HOTEC) advanced by LeDoux and Fleming (2023), demonstrate that introspectively accessed states depend functionally on higher-order representations (HORs) within the General Network of Cognition (GNC). In these models, the "subjective signal strength" and reality-monitoring functions are not merely accessing a pre-existing, non-physical phenomenal field; they are constitutively *generating* the phenomenology itself. When a higher-order state includes self-referential information, it creates a "self-HOROR" state, which is the exact physical mechanism responsible for the subjective "what it is like" aspect of experience.

By treating P-consciousness as a pristine, irreducible quantum phenomenon that merely "overflows" access, the site actively ignores the competing framework that phenomenal features are constructed by the very top-down attention and metacognitive access mechanisms the site attempts to exclude. The site relies on Tulving's anoetic/noetic/autonoetic hierarchy to claim that AI systems merely "simulate" autonoetic outputs without phenomenal experience, yet it offers no empirical metric to distinguish a "simulation" of autonoetic consciousness from the functional generation of it.

Furthermore, the site's arguments regarding the temporal structure of consciousness (e.g., specious presence) ignore recent evolutionary and sensory ecology arguments regarding the speed of consciousness. As Dainton and Michel note, the sensory horizon of an organism dictates the speed of its conscious integration. Visual consciousness is inherently slow because it relies on top-down recurrent processing, which contradicts the site's assertion that consciousness operates instantaneously at quantum collapse points. The failure to test the site's dualist AI predictions against modern, integrated higher-order representationalism represents a severe, fatal gap in counterargument coverage.

While the site cites Lively et al. This completely misreads the developmental neuroscience consensus, which views synaptic pruning and neural reorganization as continuous, physically reductive processes optimizing functional networks, not as metaphysical ruptures requiring non-physical bridging mechanisms.

Similarly, the site's internal methodology pages ([[reflexive-methodology]]) reveal active attempts to soften physicalist arguments while hardening its own. The site admits to altering text from claiming that self-stultification "establishes" causal efficacy to stating it "argues for" it, yet it simultaneously declares that the causal closure of the physical "cannot exclude dualism." This asymmetry—where physicalism is perpetually portrayed as conceptually deficient while interactionist dualism is shielded from the exact same epistemological burdens—renders the entire project an exercise in confirmation bias rather than genuine philosophical inquiry.
