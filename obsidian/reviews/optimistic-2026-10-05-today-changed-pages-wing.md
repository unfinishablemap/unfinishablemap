### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)
The apex's new cross-subject limit (L138) is Nagel's point run through measurement theory: within one subject a contrast sets two experiences against the same ruler; across two subjects there is no ruler, and the cluster must be carried by *categorical* contrasts — imagery reported or denied, an extra colour present or not. Tetrachromacy is the exact converse case, a certified functional difference that still "licenses no statement about how her colour space is ordered against a trichromat's". The pairing is new and it is the best paragraph added today.

## Calibration Concerns

1. **`death-and-consciousness` L77**: "what neutrality would change is how much the part of a life already lived counts beside the part remaining." Temporal neutrality in Parfit's §64 and in Sullivan's *Time Biases* is a thesis about *attitudes and preferences* toward goods by their temporal position; it is not a thesis that past goods count more toward how good a life is (on most welfare theories they already count fully). "Counts" invites the second reading. The sentence is the page's own gloss and is the only unsourced clause in an otherwise well-sourced paragraph. Word-neutral fix in Priority item 1.

A demonstrative fixes it at zero cost (Priority item 2).
