# Hostile Referee Report: `concepts/epistemology` — The Unfinishable Map

## TL;DR

**Verdict: REVISE-HARD.** The article's fatal weakness is not a bad citation but a structural evasion: it builds a two-channel epistemology in which first-person access confers warrant, yet neither it nor the two nodes it defers to (`phenomenal-authority-and-first-person-evidence`, `introspection`) engage the single most obvious mainstream challenge — process reliabilism and the demand for a reliability track record — at all. The words "reliabilism," "Goldman," "Alston," and "generality problem" appear nowhere in the chain. The justificatory buck is passed in a **traceable loop** that terminates only in phenomenal conservatism asserted as foundational and an admitted bedrock standoff with illusionism.

- **The citations are clean; the coverage is not.** Every load-bearing citation I could check (Jackson, Levine, Nagel, Chalmers, Huemer, Lycan, Zanotti, Churchland, Anderson) verifies at metadata and verbatim level, and the hostile-witness stances are honestly disclosed. - **Asymmetric debunking is live and self-contradictory.** The framework deploys the Fitness-Beats-Truth theorem to debunk the cognitive faculty that generates *simplicity* intuitions (a physicalist-favouring intuition) while exempting *introspective/dualist* seemings from the identical treatment via phenomenal conservatism. The convergence method is credited with "warrant no single line could provide" while every node in the deferral chain concedes convergence is "suggestive rather than demonstrative" and that the Bayesian machinery it rests on "awaits execution." ### Dimension 2 — Omitted or understated mainstream objections

- **Reliabilism — FATAL.** The subagent trace confirms that neither the epistemology article, nor `phenomenal-authority-and-first-person-evidence`, nor `introspection` contains the strings "reliabilism," "process reliabilism," "generality problem," "Goldman," or "Alston." The article's entire architecture rests on first-person access conferring warrant, and the textbook challenge is precisely that dualist introspective access has **no established reliability track record** and that the generality problem makes "the reliability of introspection" ill-defined. The article's answer is to adopt phenomenal conservatism — a seemings-*internalist* view — as its epistemology, thereby choosing a framework in which the reliabilist question does not arise, **without ever arguing why internalism defeats reliabilism.** Choosing the framework that exempts you is not answering the objection. For an article titled "Epistemology," omitting the dominant externalist theory of justification is fatal, not a gap.
- **Naturalized epistemology — FATAL.** Quine's thesis that epistemology is continuous with cognitive science, and Kornblith's naturalized-kind treatment of knowledge, are the standing challenge to any framework "requiring non-physical knowers." If epistemology is a chapter of the science of how physical cognitive systems track their environment, the very notion of a first-person channel with its own non-physical "warrant structure" is what needs defending. The article never names Quine, Kornblith, or Kitcher. This is the objection most directly lethal to "Epistemology Under Dualism," and it is absent. [Routledge](https://www.rep.routledge.com/articles/thematic/naturalized-epistemology/v-2)[Grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/Naturalized_epistemology)
- **Evolutionary debunking, deployed asymmetrically — STRUCTURALLY DAMAGING (and self-contradictory).** This is the sharpest finding. The linked `parsimony-epistemology` invokes the **Fitness-Beats-Truth theorem** to argue "the cognitive system generating simplicity intuitions [may not track] truth at all, since natural selection tunes perception to fitness rather than veridicality." The FBT result is Prakash, Stephens, Hoffman, Singh & Fields, "Fitness Beats Truth in the Evolution of Perception," *Acta Biotheoretica* 69(3):319–341 (2021), DOI 10.1007/s10441-020-09400-0, which proves "a strategy that simply seeks to maximize expected-fitness payoff, with no attempt to estimate the 'true' world state, does consistently better" than veridical perception. That is a debunking argument aimed at a *physicalist-favouring* intuition (parsimony). Meanwhile the *dualist-favouring* intuitions — the introspective seemings that pain hurts, that agency is real — are **exempted** and protected by phenomenal conservatism. But FBT is indifferent to content: if selection-for-fitness debunks the truth-tracking of the faculties producing simplicity judgments, it equally debunks the faculties producing introspective and modal judgments. The framework wields the debunker against the opponent and sheathes it before it reaches home. To its partial credit, `epistemology-of-convergence-arguments` engages Bloom's "natural-born dualist" and Barlev, M. & Shtulman, A. (2021), "Minds, Bodies, Spirits, and Gods: Does Widespread Belief in Disembodied Beings Imply That We Are Inherent Dualists?", *Psychological Review* 128(6):1007–1021, DOI 10.1037/rev0000298, and concedes the empirical question of intuitive dualism is open — so the debunking asymmetry is not total. But the FBT double-standard is real and unresolved.
- **Predictive processing / active inference — STRUCTURALLY DAMAGING.** PP (Friston, Clark, Seth, Hohwy) is a total physicalist rival that offers a *single-mechanism* account of perception, belief-formation, and epistemic access — precisely the territory the article partitions into "two channels." Anil Seth's "controlled hallucination" and Hohwy, J. & Seth, A. (2020), *Philosophy and the Mind Sciences* 1(II), Article 3, DOI 10.33735/phimisci.2020.II.64 — which "propose the predictive processing framework for brain function as a promising candidate for providing this systematic foundation" for identifying neural correlates of consciousness — directly attack the claim that first-person and third-person channels are irreducibly distinct. The site *has* a `predictive-processing` article treating PP as a physicalist rival — but the epistemology article does not engage it, cite it, or link it. An epistemology article that ignores the leading physicalist theory of epistemic access has not met its rival at framework level.
- **Evidentialism — GAP bordering on structural.** The article asserts phenomenal evidence "constitutes the explanandum itself" and slides between possessing phenomenal states and being responsive to them as evidence. Conee & Feldman's evidentialism — and the live problem of *evidence-possession* (Perrine and others) — is never engaged. The conflation of evidence-possession with evidence-responsiveness is exactly the crack evidentialists would exploit. [PhilPapers](https://philpapers.org/rec/FELE)
- **Disagreement epistemology — STRUCTURALLY DAMAGING.** The framework sits against a bare 51.9% majority per the 2020 PhilPapers Survey (Bourget & Chalmers, "Philosophers on Philosophy: The 2020 PhilPapers Survey," *Philosophers' Imprint* 22(11), 2023): 51.9% "accept/lean toward" physicalism about mind vs. 32.1% non-physicalism — physicalism "remains the view of the majority, but only just." Even so, the article's central positive method is "convergence." Conciliationism (Christensen, Elga) holds that peer disagreement of this magnitude mandates credence reduction; the steadfast camp (Kelly) disputes this. The article's convergence method and the disagreement literature are on a collision course, and disagreement epistemology is never mentioned. (Note: the sub-majority status of physicalism cuts *for* the Map in one respect — it undercuts the rhetorical force of "physicalism is the consensus" — but it does not exempt a convergence-based method from conciliationist scrutiny, which is triggered by qualified peer disagreement, not by minority status.) [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conciliationism)
- **Phenomenal conservatism critics — GAP.** The `phenomenal-conservatism` node does engage Markie (2005), Siegel (2012, 2017), BonJour, and the Moretti/Tucker isolation objection — creditable. But its most recent critic is 2017. It misses the reflective-awareness problem (McCain & Moretti 2021), Moretti's 2020 monograph, and the 2023 *Seemings: New Arguments* volume — developments that bear directly on whether a "bare seeming" can transmit justification. [springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10670-024-00791-x)
- **Bayesian confirmation — CONFESSION WITHOUT CORRECTION.** `epistemology-of-convergence-arguments` concedes the Bayesian formalization of convergence is "well-defined in principle but contested at every parameter" and that "the consciousness case awaits a comparable execution." The convergence method is nonetheless credited upstream (in the epistemology article) with providing "warrant that no single line could provide alone." A method whose formal engine is admittedly unexecuted is being banked as delivered warrant. [topics/epistemology-of-convergence-arguments](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/epistemology-of-convergence-arguments/)

## Bottom-Line Verdict

**REVISE-HARD.**

Not DELETE: the article is internally coherent, its checked citations are clean, and in places it is genuinely disciplined — the Levine handling, the explicit concession that the convergence legs are not fully independent, and the Tenet-5 integration are the work of a careful author. Not RETAIN, and not merely a light revise: the article omits the three research programmes that would actually adjudicate its central thesis (reliabilism, naturalized epistemology, predictive processing), deploys evolutionary debunking asymmetrically in a way that contradicts its own commitments, overstates what its deferred nodes deliver, and routes its load-bearing "made rigorous" claim into a deferral loop that ends in "structurally impossible." Those are not gaps to be noted in a limitations section; they are load-bearing failures that change the assessment of the thesis.

A secondary, narrower verdict applies to one section: **"Epistemology Under Dualism" should be DEMOTED-TO-COHERENCE-ONLY** until it engages naturalized epistemology and reliabilism. As written it is a statement of what follows *if* dualism is true, presented with the rhetorical weight of an epistemological result.

## Article-Specific Fixes

## Site-Wide Methodology Improvements

1. **Treat overstatement-of-what-a-deferral-delivers as a citation-integrity defect.** The corpus already runs quote-fidelity and author-stance passes on external citations; it runs no equivalent check on *internal* cross-references. The epistemology hub credits its spokes with arguments the spokes explicitly decline to provide. A review pass should verify, for each "see X for the argument" link, that X actually contains a terminating argument and not a further deferral or a concession.
2. **Add a deferral-chain cycle-detector.** The reliability/calibration question loops between three articles and terminates only in a "void." A mechanical trace of "defers-to" edges would surface promissory chains that never ground out, and flag any claim whose justification is a cycle.
3. **Enforce debunking symmetry as a standing discipline.** Wherever an evolutionary/Fitness-Beats-Truth debunking argument is used against an opponent's intuition, require an explicit paragraph stating whether the same debunker applies to the framework's own load-bearing intuitions. The asymmetric FBT deployment would have been caught by such a rule.
4. **Currency floor for "foundational" hub articles.** An article that declares itself "foundational" while its own external reference base ends in 1996 should trip a staleness flag independent of its last-modified date. Freshness of cross-links is not freshness of evidence.
5. **Stabilise tenet numbering corpus-wide.** Two core articles invert the constitutional tenet indices. A single canonical mapping (with block anchors, which already exist) should be enforced and linted, since mis-numbered tenets silently corrupt every cross-reference that cites a tenet by number.
6. **Distinguish "epistemic humility" from "confession-without-correction" in the review rubric.** The corpus rewards disclosed limitations; it should additionally require that any disclosed failure mode trigger a *specific downstream demotion*. A limitation that is disclosed but changes no dependent claim should be treated as an un-actioned finding, not as a calibration credit.
7. **Require framework-level engagement with the leading physicalist rival (predictive processing) anywhere the first/third-person distinction is load-bearing.** The site owns a `predictive-processing` article; the epistemology hub should not be able to assert an irreducible two-channel structure without engaging it. A cross-reference-completeness check should flag such omissions.
