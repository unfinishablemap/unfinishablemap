## Pessimistic Review

**Clarity Critic.** L141 ended "The framework is in-principle falsifiable; application waits on instrumentation, not on the framework's elaboration". That reads as a promise the source page withdrew. The Synthesis lead (L135) listed five source "components" in a run-on that added nothing the sections had not already said. L89 used the banned construction ("This is not a claim that … The claim is that …"). The Source Articles lead-in used the phrase the media-neutral rule forbids.

**Redundancy Hunter.** The epiphenomenalism verdict on the human-ape gap was stated five ways: L101 ("unexplained coincidence"), L137 ("constrains epiphenomenalism"), L141 ("fits … without evidencing it"), L151 ("argues against epiphenomenalism") and, after the first draft of this pass, the new ledger. Two of those also contradicted each other: L101 said the epiphenomenalist is left with coincidence, while L141 said the epiphenomenalist reads the correspondence as the substrate's work. Asymbolia's constrain-not-establish sentence appeared at L111, L115, L137 and L151. The Streicher result appeared at L101 and again at L141. The animal-versus-AI homology point appeared at L63 and again at L143.

**Narrative Flow Analyst.** The Synthesis built to "The Map's evidential standing rests on cumulative convergence across markers, dimensions, and traditions". That is the N-fold triangulation reading that P-M2 and the source's common-cause section reject. The paragraph that names the cluster's discipline was itself breaking it. The Relation section then undid the synthesis's calibration: Tenet 3 was "argued" for, and Tenet 5 was used only one way. Meanwhile L125 and L129 made parsimony appeals for the deflationary side without the bound the source attaches.

## Optimistic Review

**Connection Finder.** The 11-27 Saad correction gives the Bidirectional paragraph a sharper and more honest line. Delegation is a causal *structure*, the Map's quantum route is its own addition, and Saad counts collapse-based interactionism as violating Observational Closure. The animal-consciousness page's common-cause section supplies the exact discount the Synthesis needed.

**Synthesis Strengthener.** Tenet 5's symmetry (`tenets.md` "parsimony cannot decide for or against a framework when the relevant knowledge is incomplete") joins two halves of this page that never met. It blocks the "unnecessary posit" dismissal of animal experience, which is the defeater the page removes in L71/L77/L121. It also keeps the page's own "no coupling" default for nematodes and slime moulds a default. That is a real synthesis point no single source states.

**Human Reader Advocate.** The page lacked the required Evidence and Dependency ledger, and a reader could not tell which claims would survive rejecting the tenets. The ledger now sorts them in prose. The contemplative paragraph gains the one sentence a reader needs (the bridge is heuristic, with three disanalogies) so that it no longer reads as evidence.
