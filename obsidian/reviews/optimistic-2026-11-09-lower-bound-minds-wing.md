## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

The fish page isolates the explanatory gap inside the vertebrates, which is where it bites hardest. Its reconstruction of the skeptics' inference—"that move quietly assumes something further: that felt pain is *constituted by* the cortical architecture that correlates with it in humans" (`fish-sentience…` L70)—and the follow-through, "The inference is only as secure as the identity it presupposes" (L72), is a textbook separation of correlation from constitution, done without a single appeal to intuition pumps. The plant page reaches the same point from the other side: "the moment functional description is settled, the question of experience remains completely open, and the vocabulary fight is that open question wearing empirical clothes" (`plant-cognition…` L67). Chalmers would also approve the transitions page's statement of what an information-only theory can and cannot register: "It could not detect the arrival of the *inside*, because on the dualist reading the inside is precisely what functional description leaves out" (`consciousness-and-the-major-evolutionary-transitions` L80).

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

The evolutionary-case page places the candidate interface where a Stapp-style theorist would want it—"a quantum process would be situated at the most consequential decision point in neural signalling: whether to transmit a signal across a synapse" (`evolutionary-case…` L76)—and then refuses to over-sell the placement: "This is a structural parallel, not an empirical demonstration" (L78). Its Dualism paragraph says exactly what the precedent class buys and no more: "the substrate through which consciousness might interface with the brain is biologically available rather than ruled out *a priori*. The biological availability does not establish that consciousness uses such a substrate" (L128). The transitions page's Tenet 2 paragraph (L94) keeps the same restraint, scoping the interface's indistinguishability from chance to "unconditioned aggregate tests", which matches the tenet's own wording.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

The fish page puts the first-person question in the right place and in the right words: "whether there is something it is like to be the trout with acetic acid on its lips" (`fish-sentience…` L52). The immune page's closing sentence does the same for the hardest case in the cluster: "The immune system knows the body in the way a well-tuned control system knows its set-point, and that is a genuine kind of knowing that leaves the hard problem exactly where it was" (`the-cognitive-immune-system…` L85). The neuron-less page refuses to let a parts-list stand in for a point of view: "toolkit presence—genomic or cell-typological—is not functional presence, and functional presence is not experience" (`neuron-less-animals…` L51).

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

Whitehead would be pleased that the lower-bound pages never treat the floor as empty. The neuron-less page: "Below the nerve net, behaviour does not thin out to nothing; it stays surprisingly rich while the neural substrate vanishes entirely" (L71). It states the expansive position at full strength before answering it—"This is the strongest version of the expansive 'cognition-without-neurons' position, and it deserves to be stated at full strength rather than caricatured" (L65)—and the immune page grants Cohen's cognition as "a computational and functional category: it names comparison, interpretation, and choice among options" (L53). The plant page's refusal to collapse Calvo's minimal cognition into either inflation or denial (L61) is in the same spirit.

Per this skill's constraint, none of that praise is used below to move any organism up the evidential scale. The pages themselves do not do so either: the neuron-less page's verdict is "their behaviour gives us *no purchase* on the question either way" (L81), and the plant page's is "plants very probably have no phenomenal experience" (L93). Process-philosophical compatibility is praised here as compatibility, and the Hardline Empiricist section confirms the tiers are left where the evidence puts them.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

The transitions page locates the novelty of a phenomenal transition exactly where an agency theorist would: "not a new information channel but a new causal channel, downward from experience to matter" (L92). The immune and neuron-less pages protect that channel by declining to multiply it: "Immune 'decisions' are, on the Map's reading, selection dynamics—clonal, contextual, molecular—needing no consciousness-side contribution" (`the-cognitive-immune-system…` L79). Reserving the interface for systems that could host it is what keeps the agency claim from thinning into panpsychism-by-default.
