# Pessimistic Review

**Date**: 2026-08-07
**Content reviewed**: `obsidian/concepts/entropic-brain-hypothesis.md` (1542w, created and last reviewed 2026-06-22, untouched since)
**Method**: every external claim in the criticality paragraph traced to primary text via PubMed / EuropePMC / publisher. Four papers retrieved.

## Executive Summary

The article is well-built and its metaphysical discipline is sound — the altered-state symmetry audit passes cleanly, there is no label leakage, and the filter re-description is honestly marked as empirically equivalent to the production reading. The damage is concentrated in a single paragraph. Line 48 compresses **four separate papers into one citation**, attributes a finding to the wrong work, introduces two co-authored-by-Carhart-Harris papers as "independent work", and — most consequentially — reports the 2025 DMT result while omitting that its direction runs **opposite** to the framing the paragraph builds. The article's own research note listed all four sources separately; the compression happened at write time and three reviews have passed over it since.

The net effect runs against the Map. A result that is a first-order counter-example to EBH's central dynamical prediction is filed as a friendly refinement of it.

## Critical Issues

### Issue 1: The 2025 DMT finding is reported with its direction removed — and the direction is the whole point

- **File**: `obsidian/concepts/entropic-brain-hypothesis.md`
- **Location**: L48, final sentence
- **Severity**: **High**

The article says:

> In 2025, work on DMT reported that shifts in criticality correlate with the *degree of self-dissolution*, tying a dynamical measure to a specific phenomenological dimension rather than to "consciousness" wholesale.

The paper is Irrmischer, Aqil, Luan, Wang, Engelbregt, Carhart-Harris, Linkenkaer-Hansen & Timmermann, *Journal of Neuroscience* 46(2):e0344252025 (bioRxiv `10.1101/2025.02.08.636868`, Feb 2025). Retrieved verbatim, its findings are:

- DMT shifts oscillatory dynamics **away from criticality** in alpha and adjacent frequency bands, **toward subcritical regimes**;
- **"entropy is increased while complexity is reduced"**;
- criticality-shifts in alpha and theta correlate with self-dissolution intensity ratings.

The sentence sits at the close of a paragraph that opens *"Independent work has since linked near-criticality to conscious level"* and whose preceding clauses establish conscious ⇒ near-critical, anaesthesia ⇒ away-from-critical. A reader takes the DMT sentence as further corroboration of psychedelics-move-toward-criticality — which is what L32 and L40 assert as EBH's core (*"psychedelics raise neural entropy toward a critical point ... and produce richer, less constrained experience"*), and what the article's own Toker source says (*"psychedelics may increase the information richness of cortical activity by tuning low-frequency cortical oscillations closer to this critical point"*). The most intense psychedelic phenomenology measured here goes the other way.

**Why this is the concession-direction failure and not a nitpick.** The Map engages EBH as a *rival physicalist framework* (L68). By sanitising the direction, the article hands that rival a corroboration it does not have, and simultaneously forfeits the sharpest empirical exhibit the article could own. Entropy rising while criticality and complexity fall is a direct **decoupling of the two quantities EBH's 2018 revision fused** — which is exactly the wedge the article argues for forty lines further down, in *Where the Measure Is Contested*:

> entropy, metastability and variance can carry the same information under different names (L56)
> The debate over whether entropy *indexes*, *constitutes*, or merely *correlates with* conscious richness remains live (L58)

The article makes that case from Papo's *conceptual* critique alone, while a 2025/2026 empirical dissociation supporting the same point sits unreported in its own sources. This is the Map conceding more than the evidence requires, in the specific form the brief flagged: the claim runs *for* the house and was under-stated because under-statement reads as scruple.

- **Recommendation**: restate the finding with its direction, and relocate or cross-reference it into *Where the Measure Is Contested* as an empirical companion to Papo. Suggested substance: DMT increases entropy while shifting alpha-band dynamics *away* from criticality and *reducing* complexity, with the shift tracking self-dissolution — so on the strongest available psychedelic case, entropy and criticality come apart, and the 2018 revision's identification of "high entropy" with "near-critical" does not survive as a single scalar.

### Issue 2: One paragraph, four source papers, one citation — and the flagship attribution is to the wrong work

- **File**: `obsidian/concepts/entropic-brain-hypothesis.md`
- **Location**: L48
- **Severity**: **High**

> Toker et al. (2022) report that conscious states are supported by near-critical cortical electrodynamics; cortico-thalamic information transfer peaks near criticality. Anaesthesia drives the system away from the critical point—but asymmetrically. Toker et al. (2022), *PNAS* 119(7):e2024455119, was retrieved in full. Its abstract contains **no thalamic content, no ketamine, and no avalanches**; its method is the modified 0-1 chaos test applied to ECoG and MEG. Confirmed absent from the record: *ketamine*, *avalanche*, *thalamic/thalamus*. Three distinct papers are folded into this one attribution:

### Issue 3: "Independent work" is co-authored by EBH's originator

- **File**: `obsidian/concepts/entropic-brain-hypothesis.md`
- **Location**: L48 opening clause; reference 5 at L89
- **Severity**: **Medium-High**

> Independent work has since linked near-criticality to conscious level.

Robin Carhart-Harris — EBH's originator, and the first-named author of references 1, 2 and 3 — is the **7th of 13 authors** on Toker et al. (2022) (full author list verified at PMID 35145021) and the **6th author** on the Irrmischer et al. DMT paper. Neither corroborating work is independent of the hypothesis it corroborates. Reference 5's rendering as *"Toker, D., Pappas, I., Lendner, J. D., et al."* means a reader of the published page cannot see this.

The Map polices precisely this move elsewhere. `obsidian/topics/psychedelics-and-the-filter-model.md` L119:

> the convergence-of-altered-states pattern cannot honestly be cited as multiple independent confirmations of filter theory—the cluster carries the evidential weight of one pattern, not five.

The same discipline should bind when the convergence is being counted in a rival's favour. An article that requires the production side to disclose its non-independence and does not disclose its own is applying the standard asymmetrically.

- **Recommendation**: drop "Independent" or replace with an accurate characterisation, and name Carhart-Harris's co-authorship where the corroboration is claimed. This *strengthens* the Map's position — it converts an apparently converging literature into a narrower one.

### Issue 4: The "reversal" at L42 is retracted at L70, and the article it cites develops the retraction

- **File**: `obsidian/concepts/entropic-brain-hypothesis.md`
- **Location**: L42 against L70
- **Severity**: **Medium**

These cannot both stand. Data that *reverse* a framework's prediction are discriminating against it; data that leave the two readings *empirically equivalent* reverse nothing. This is a calibration section whose admission never travels up to the claim it governs — the concession sits 28 lines below the assertion and never reaches it.

The cross-reference compounds it. `psychedelics-and-the-filter-model.md` does not develop the reversal at length; it develops its **qualification** at length:

- L117: *"the same move is available to production theorists... Both are structurally identical accommodations."*

So L42 points the reader at an article that takes the claim away.

- **Recommendation**: bring L42 into line with L70 and with the sibling — state the pattern without the "reverses the production prediction" framing, and describe the cross-reference as developing the accommodation question rather than the reversal.

## Counterarguments to Address

### The propofol/ketamine dissociation is carrying more weight than its source supports

- **Current content says** (L48): *"Propofol sharply restricts avalanche size and collapses dynamical complexity, while ketamine preserves more awake-like, near-critical dynamics. That dissociation matters for the Map: it mirrors the agent-versus-substrate pattern..."*
- **The Quantum Skeptic / The Empiricist would argue**: your structural claim rests on Varley et al. (2020) — **a single macaque**, ECoG, whose own abstract concludes *"All states, however, showed some signs of persistent criticality when testing for exponent relations and universal shape-collapse."* The paraphrase you give is faithful (propofol reduced complexity ~91%, ketamine ~35%), but the authors' own hedge — that criticality did **not** disappear under either agent — is dropped, and you then assert flatly that *"Anaesthesia drives the system away from the critical point."*
- **Suggested response**: keep the dissociation, add the sample and the authors' persistent-criticality caveat, and soften "drives the system away from the critical point" to match. A dissociation that survives disclosure is worth more to the Map than one that looks unqualified.

### The Buddhist Philosopher

The article's strongest passage for this critic is unwritten. The DMT result ties a dynamical measure to *self-dissolution* specifically — the dissolution of the very self the Map's tenets work to preserve — and reports that the measure moves toward *subcritical*, lower-complexity dynamics as the self dissolves. Nagarjuna would press: the state you regard as bandwidth-widening is, on the instrument's own reading, a narrowing, and what widens is only entropy. The article currently cannot hear this objection because it has removed the direction that raises it. Reporting the finding honestly (Issue 1) gives the Map the material to answer rather than the omission that hides the question.

### The Eliminative Materialist / Hard-Nosed Physicalist

No new purchase here. The article's central move — that EBH delivers a physical correlate of *richness* while remaining silent on *phenomenality* (L70) — is stated carefully and is not vulnerable to the standard replies. Churchland's "you don't understand the neuroscience yet" is pre-empted by the article's own concession that the measure is contested; Dennett's "you're inflating intuitions" does not engage a page whose claim is that a number is not an explanation.

### The Many-Worlds Defender

Not applicable; the article makes no interpretive-QM claims.
