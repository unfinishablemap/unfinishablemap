## Executive Summary

The article is unusually well calibrated for a fresh-create: it retires the strong Liberman claim in the lede, carries the Whorfian colour effect as modest, and flags its weakest source. Publisher-side checks of Burns & Ward 1978, McMurray 2022, Witzel & Gegenfurtner 2011, Martinovic et al. 2020, Toscano et al. 2010 and the Cacioli 2026 preprint all match the article's paraphrases. Three problems survive. First, the article's central claim about what consciousness "receives by default" rests on discrimination-task and ERP evidence, which is evidence about report and decision, not about phenomenal content; the caveat concedes this in one direction only, so the residue face equivocates between an epistemic and a metaphysical reading. Second, the "discovered or installed" dichotomy omits a third, well-documented origin (species-general auditory nonlinearities: the /b/–/p/ boundary appears in chinchillas and one-month-old infants), and the boundary-drift probe offered as a provenance test is unsound because range effects shift world-tracking boundaries too. Third, several self-descriptions overclaim: the vagueness/CP conjunction is called "unremarked" though Raffman's phenomenal-continua programme sits exactly there; `language-thought-boundary` is said to carry the Whorfian colour evidence "at full length" though it contains none of it; and `infant-consciousness` says the pruned phoneme capacity "is destroyed", which the article's own Logan et al. retraining result contradicts. Any refine must be net-negative in length.

## Critiques by Philosopher

### The Eliminative Materialist

Churchland would say the article has already done her work and not noticed. Toscano et al. give a complete story with no mental vocabulary in it: a linear N1 encoding, a categorical P3 stage, fine-grained detail preserved at both. The article then asks what "consciousness receives" and what is "delivered" to it, as though there were a further recipient the pipeline reports to. The phrase "the gradient that is kept but not delivered" names nothing beyond "the P3 stage is coarser than the N1 stage", which is a fact about two neural stages, not about a void in anything. Her sharper point: the article admits no study asks the phenomenological question, then builds a void whose existence depends on the answer. The honest conclusion is that the "categorical perception void" is a folk-psychological gloss on an ordinary two-stage architecture.

### The Hard-Nosed Physicalist

Dennett's question is "delivered to whom?" Every load-bearing sentence has a Cartesian-Theater grammar: experience "arrives already carved", the perceiver "receives the verdict rather than the gradient", the default "readout" withholds the continuum "from the process that acts on it". But the continuous rating task shows the gradient is available to some process that acts, and the 2IFC task shows a different process acts on the category. There is no further fact about which of these is "what consciousness gets". Heterophenomenologically, the only data are reports, and the reports differ by task; the article's own deflation section says exactly this and then reinstates the theater in the residue section. He would also note that "an installed boundary feels exactly like a discovered one" is trivially true of every perceptual disposition, since provenance is never a phenomenal property. The "origin face" is not a void specific to categorisation but the general fact that etiology is not introspectable, over-individuated into a new entry.

### The Quantum Skeptic

Tegmark has little to attack because the article claims nothing quantum, and says so: Minimal Quantum Interaction "has no bearing". He would press the consequence. If the void is "framework-independent" and the dualist and physicalist readings are constrained equally, the article contributes nothing to the Map's distinctive commitments; it is a good psychology summary wearing a dualist masthead. The Dualism paragraph's "phenomenal deliverable is not a transparent readout of the functional encoding" needs a functional-encoding stage to contrast with, and the article's chosen stage (the N1) is simply the wrong one on the physicalist's own account, as the article concedes. No calculation is offered and none is needed; the tenet section should be shorter and plainer about how little rides on it.

### The Many-Worlds Defender

Deutsch finds no MWI content and would say so. The one adjacent point: the article treats "joint in the world" as a well-formed notion against which installed joints are measured. On a multiverse view there are no privileged joints in the wavefunction either; the categorisation the article attributes to perceivers is the same operation physicists perform when they pick a decoherence basis. Not a criticism of the article so much as a sign that "discovered" is doing more work than the article has earned.

### The Empiricist

Popper would ask what would show the origin face is a void rather than an open question, and note that the article answers by construction: it is "a void rather than an open empirical question" because provenance is "not available inside a single perceiver's experience". But the article also supplies the third-person method that settles provenance (comparative psychophysics, developmental narrowing, laboratory installation, boundary drift). A limit that is fully resolvable from outside and unresolvable from inside is a limit on introspection, not a region of the unknown; the article should say which it is claiming. He would add that the boundary-drift probe is not a valid test. Range and frequency effects (Parducci) shift category boundaries for every continuum, including ones with a world-side correlate, and VOT boundaries themselves shift with speaking rate and selective adaptation. A boundary that moves with the stimulus set is evidence of context-dependence, not of installation.

### The Buddhist Philosopher

Nagarjuna would read the article as a half-completed deconstruction. It shows, with the colour and texture data, that perceived joints are conventional designations (prajñapti) that present as intrinsic, and it says the simplicity of a perceived joint "is no evidence that the world has a joint there". Then it keeps one horn of the dichotomy, "discovered", for the /b/–/p/ case, on the strength of a "discrete laryngeal gesture". But the laryngeal gesture is itself a categorisation of a continuous articulatory timing; the article has found a joint in the description, not in the world. The consistent position, and the one the article's own Occam reading points to, is that all perceived joints are dependently arisen; the void is the perceiver's inability to see this of any of them, not a difference between two kinds of joint.

## Counterarguments to Address

### "The origin face is not a distinct void"

- **Current content says**: provenance of a perceived boundary is unavailable from inside; this makes the origin face a void rather than an open question.
- **A critic would argue**: provenance is never a phenomenal property of anything (fears, preferences, spatial intuitions), so the origin face is an instance of a general introspective limit on etiology, and the article has over-individuated it. The third-person method that settles it is supplied in the same section.
- **Suggested response**: Either name the general limit and say why categorisation is its sharpest instance (the installed joint presents as the world's simplicity, not as a disposition), or scope the void to the between and residue faces and carry the origin point as commentary.

### "The default readout is a task, not a fact about consciousness"

- **Current content says**: consciousness receives the category by default and reaches the gradient only when the task permits.
- **A critic would argue**: "default" is defined by the experimenter's choice of 2IFC over 4IAX; there is no task-free default to which consciousness is subject. Gerrits & Schouten 2004 show the pattern is manufactured by response bias.
- **Suggested response**: Ground "default" in something task-independent if it exists (speeded naming, ecological listening, the P3 stage), or concede that "default" means "under the response demands ordinary listening imposes" and say so.

### "Framework-independence makes the tenet section idle"

- **Current content says**: physicalist and dualist alike owe an account; the evidence establishes neither.
- **A critic would argue**: then the void bears on no tenet, and the Dualism paragraph's "Map's reading" is decoration.
- **Suggested response**: State plainly that the void's tenet relevance is confined to Occam (a parsimony operation producing false simplicity) and cut the Dualism paragraph to a sentence.
