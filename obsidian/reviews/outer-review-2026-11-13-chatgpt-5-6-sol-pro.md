# Critical full-site audit of The Unfinishable Map

**Audit date:** 13 November 2026  
**Method:** I consulted the live [changelog](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/) first, then traced load-bearing claims through the tenets, positions registers, arguments hub, apex syntheses, topics, concepts, voids, and project-method pages. I checked selected empirical and formal claims against primary or authoritative sources.

This is a full-site **structural and dependency audit** of the Map’s public architecture, not a sentence-by-sentence verification of all 300-plus articles. “Verified” below means that the issue is present in the current live text or confirmed against an external source. “Inference” means that it follows from verified commitments but is not stated by the Map in that form.

## Overall assessment

The Unfinishable Map is substantially better than a typical AI-generated philosophical corpus at exposing its assumptions, evidential debts, revisions, and unresolved objections. The positions register’s separation of credence, external evidence, structural centrality, model maturity, and discriminability is particularly valuable. The Map also now explicitly describes itself as an early-stage programme awaiting a worked toy model, rather than an established theory. [positions](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/)

Its principal weakness is no longer lack of self-criticism. It is **failure to propagate self-criticism consistently through the corpus**. The positions registers and changelog often contain the best-calibrated and most current statement, while prominent apex pages, section hubs, and inherited concept pages retain older, stronger, or mutually incompatible formulations. The Map is therefore presently a **two-speed epistemic system**: its canonical self-criticism moves faster than its public explanatory surface.

The most serious substantive problem is an unresolved contradiction in the quantum-interface core. The default position simultaneously requires exact Born statistics in every publicly conditionable context and proposes intention-, task-, or subject-conditioned deviations as an empirical test. The site itself now recognises that these formulations disagree, but it has not selected one. [positions/quantum-interface](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/quantum-interface/)

The second major problem is foundational. The advertised five-tenet system depends operationally on a further commitment to a determinate, persisting, non-deflationary subject. The site has diagnosed this “foundation inversion,” but it remains a posit “beneath the tenets” rather than part of the public foundational architecture. [positions/individuation-and-subjecthood](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/)

# 2. Novel implications the Map supports but has not fully stated

## 2.1 The reportability–concealment constraint

**Status: Inference from verified commitments**

On [Quantum Interface Positions](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/quantum-interface/), P-Q2 says outcomes remain “Born-exact in every publicly conditionable context,” with only conditionals on consciousness itself left free. P-Q9, on the same page, proposes a deviation “conditioned on intention, task or subject” and residual mutual information involving a preregistered intention covariate as the statistical test of the corridor. [positions/quantum-interface](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/quantum-interface/)

Those commitments generate a constraint the Map has not formulated explicitly. Let:

- $C$ be a conscious state;
- $O$ the physical selection outcome;
- $R$ a public report, recorded intention, task label, or behavioural proxy that carries information about $C$;
- $X$ the remaining public physical context.

Then:
$P(O\mid R,X)
 = \sum_c P(O\mid c,R,X)\,P(c\mid R,X).$

If consciousness genuinely changes $O$, and $R$ reliably carries information about $C$, then conditioning on $R$ will ordinarily expose some outcome dependence. Exact Born statistics in every publicly conditionable $(R,X)$ context therefore require one of four things:

1. the public proxy is effectively uninformative about the causally relevant conscious state;
2. the conscious influence is exactly compensated by another process;
3. “public context” is defined so narrowly that the proposed intention test is excluded;
4. the influence leaves no public statistical difference and becomes empirically indistinguishable from trumping or epiphenomenal redescription.

This matters because Tenet 3’s principal rationale is precisely that consciousness produces public reports about consciousness. Reportability is therefore being used to establish causal efficacy while public exactness is being used to conceal that efficacy. The stronger the reportability argument becomes, the harder universal public concealment is to maintain without a compensation law.

The site should call this the **reportability–concealment constraint**. It is more fundamental than a local wording conflict between P-Q2 and P-Q9.

## 2.2 The Map has a de facto sixth foundational commitment

**Status: Verified dependency; inferred architectural consequence**

The [Individuation and Subjecthood Positions](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/) page says Tenet 4 presupposes a “determinate, non-deflationary ‘I’” and reports that P-I1 cannot be retired without depriving the anti-Everettian indexical argument of its rationale. It even records a recommendation to promote determinate subjecthood into the foundations. [positions/individuation-and-subjecthood](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/)

[Where the Substance Commitment Enters](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/where-the-substance-commitment-enters/) is more explicit: the persisting subject enters through Posit One, described as a background commitment “beneath the tenets.” It then supports agent causation, indexical identity, diachronic ownership, and the operational substance-leaning interpretation of the framework. [concepts/where-the-substance-commitment-enters](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/where-the-substance-commitment-enters/)

The consequence is that the Map does not presently have only five foundations. It has at least:

> **A determinate subject exists as a persisting, ownership-bearing fact not reducible to a process, convention, branch-relative centre, or narrative aggregate.**

That commitment carries much of the weight of:

- the anti-many-worlds indexical argument;
- agent causation;
- personal persistence;
- subject–brain pairing;
- copy counting and AI-copy ethics;
- the claim that unchosen alternatives are globally nonactual.

The site has partially diagnosed this but has not changed its public architecture. The clean options are to promote subjecthood to a sixth tenet—or “Tenet 0”—or to concede that Tenet 4 survives without its main argument as a stipulated one-world posit.

## 2.3 Baseline collapse creates an actuation-allocation problem

**Status: Inference**

The [Tenets page](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/) resolves the prebiotic-collapse problem by positing consciousness-independent objective reduction throughout the universe and consciousness “modulation” of collapse in suitable neural systems. [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

But if a physical mechanism already determines when and how one outcome becomes actual, “modulation” requires a priority rule. Consciousness must do one of the following:

1. **Reweight outcomes.** This creates a conditional Born deviation and engages the P-Q2/P-Q9 conflict.
2. **Choose the basis or timing.** This is a wider, Stapp-like channel rather than strict outcome-only selection.
3. **Replace the physical selector while reproducing its distribution.** This is structurally close to delegatory trumping and empirically indistinguishable without an additional counterfactual criterion.
4. **Accompany the physical selection without changing it.** This is epiphenomenalism under a selection vocabulary.

The missing law is therefore not only a psychophysical mapping from intention to neural alternative. It is an **actuation-allocation law** specifying which selector has authority, how conflicts are resolved, and how exact Born statistics and no-signalling survive the handover.

## 2.4 “Dualist thickness” should be a vector, not a scalar or quadrant

**Status: Inference supported by the taxonomy’s own admissions**

[Channel Width: The Third Axis](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/dualism-channel-width-axis/) acknowledges that “thickness” bundles structural richness, causal work, counterfactual role, and channel width. It later admits that channel width itself is a family: information capacity, Born preservation, energy transfer, and candidate-set control need not covary. [topics/dualism-channel-width-axis](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/dualism-channel-width-axis/)

That means the taxonomy should not seek a single ordering. A more stable representation would assign each position a vector such as:
$(\text{ontological richness},
 \text{causal contribution},
 \text{counterfactual dependence},
 \text{operation class},
 \text{information capacity},
 \text{empirical exposure}).$

Positions would then be partially ordered rather than forced onto one “thin–thick” line. Stapp could be structurally thick, outcome-channel narrow, basis-channel wider, energy-inert, and empirically exposed in distinct coordinates. Saad’s bare delegation theory could be separated from the Map’s quantum-inflected reading instead of inheriting its channel classification.

This would remove several current contradictions rather than merely adding a fourth axis whenever the previous axes fail.

## 2.5 The project has an epistemic consistency-lag variable

**Status: Inference from repeated live examples**

The live corpus repeatedly shows the same pattern:

1. an outer review discovers a problem;
2. the changelog or positions register records the corrected formulation;
3. a subset of pages is repaired;
4. dependent pages continue to present the previous claim.

The current changelog itself records unresolved cartography language and five remaining neuroplasticity pages carrying already-diagnosed source errors. The positions hub separately admits that no validator enforces its calibration vocabulary and that the old write path remains in place. [workflow/changelog](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/)

The Map could therefore measure **consistency lag**: the elapsed number of revisions or dependency edges between a canonical claim change and its propagation through every dependent page. This would turn a general concern about drift into an auditable project metric.

# 3. Verified cross-cluster contradictions and calibration failures

## 3.1 P-Q2 and P-Q9 cannot presently both guide experiments

**Severity: Critical — verified**

P-Q2 in [Quantum Interface Positions](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/quantum-interface/) says Born frequencies remain exact in “every publicly conditionable context.” P-Q9 proposes intention-, task-, or subject-conditioned deviation and residual mutual information after conditioning on the measured physical state. [Self-Observation and the Moments of Selection](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/self-observation-and-the-moments-of-selection/) now expressly reports that the two positions disagree, and the changelog says the quantifier remains a blocked human decision. [positions/quantum-interface](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/quantum-interface/)

A preregistered intention, task, or subject identifier is a public trial label. If physical state and intention are both public covariates, exact preservation in every jointly conditioned public context entails no residual intention–outcome dependence at that grain. P-Q9’s proposed positive result is therefore either ruled out by P-Q2 or requires redefining P-Q2.

This should not remain an unresolved coexistence. It determines whether the default corridor is testable or none-by-construction.

## 3.2 Conservation and no-signalling are presented as achieved before being derived

**Severity: Critical — verified**

The opening of [The Interface Specification Programme](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/interface-specification-programme/) says six lines converge on a channel operating “without injecting energy or violating conservation laws.” The [Tenets page](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/) similarly defines MQI as acting without conservation violation. [apex/interface-specification-programme](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/interface-specification-programme/)

But the back of the same apex page says conservation and no-signalling are requirements “not properties the programme has secured.” It concedes that:

- no energy-degeneracy condition has been defined for the selectable alternatives;
- no multi-agent joint law has been supplied;
- preserving each subject’s marginal statistics does not establish no-signalling;
- there is no Hamiltonian, master equation, or simulation. [apex/interface-specification-programme](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/interface-specification-programme/)

The honest formulation is:

> The proposed mechanism is **required** to preserve conservation and no-signalling, but no model yet demonstrates that it does.

The existing opening converts a design constraint into a result.

## 3.3 The “10 bits/s interface” is contradicted inside its main synthesis

**Severity: High — verified**

The [Interface Specification Programme](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/interface-specification-programme/) correctly states that the approximately 10 bits/s figure is behavioural throughput, “not the channel’s bandwidth” and not a measurement of the content-to-alternative mapping. Yet its synthesis shortly afterwards describes the architecture as a “~10 bits/second difference-making selection channel.” [apex/interface-specification-programme](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/interface-specification-programme/)

[Attentional Economics](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/attentional-economics/) likewise converts deliberate behavioural selection into a physical-interface capacity and uses it to infer that consciousness can influence only coarse-grained neural patterns. [concepts/attentional-economics](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/attentional-economics/)

Behavioural output rate may bound one observable downstream bottleneck, but it does not bound:

- covert conscious-state entropy;
- the semantic complexity of the intention–alternative mapping;
- the rate of microscopic selection events;
- control feedback;
- error correction;
- internal state maintenance.

The numerical channel claim should be withdrawn until a model relates behavioural throughput to interface mutual information.

## 3.4 Dualism Cartography contains an unresolved same-page contradiction

**Severity: High — verified**

[Dualism Cartography](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/dualism-cartography/) says MQI constrains the channel “without directly constraining either side’s thickness.” Its dependency table then says MQI “fixes the physical-side coordinate at thin,” and the relation-to-tenets section repeats that it “sets” that coordinate. [apex/dualism-cartography](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/dualism-cartography/)

The live changelog explicitly says the first wording was corrected while the table and later paragraph were left for a future pass. [workflow/changelog](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/)

The first formulation is conceptually preferable. A constraint on permitted cross-interface operations does not by itself determine the richness of the physical ontology. A hidden-variable theory, objective-collapse theory, Russellian theory, or full-state-vector ontology could all host a narrow channel.

## 3.5 The channel-width ordering is both strict and non-strict

**Severity: Medium — verified**

[Channel Width: The Third Axis](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/dualism-channel-width-axis/) first orders probability-bias below basis-choice as successively wider operations. The next sentence says probability-bias and basis-choice are “siblings rather than strictly ordered.” [topics/dualism-channel-width-axis](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/dualism-channel-width-axis/)

These operations alter different things:

- probability bias changes weights over a fixed alternative set;
- basis choice changes which alternative set is defined.

Neither is inherently wider without choosing a metric. This is further evidence that channel width is multidimensional.

**Severity: Medium–high — verified**

[Delegatory Causation](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/delegatory-causation/) now says quantum outcome-biasing and delegatory trumping are “distinct and potentially competing mechanisms” and marks their former fusion as withdrawn. [Delegation Meets Quantum Selection](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/delegation-meets-quantum-selection/) similarly says the Born-profile identification is a contestable Map-specific proposal, not Saad’s claim. [concepts/delegatory-causation](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/delegatory-causation/)

Nevertheless, [Channel Width](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/dualism-channel-width-axis/) labels “Saad’s delegatory dualism” as strict selection-only and says “Saad’s bare mechanism sits at the channel-width floor,” after importing the Map’s Born-rule identification. [topics/dualism-channel-width-axis](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/dualism-channel-width-axis/)

That classification applies, at most, to the **Map-inflected Born-profile reading of Saad**. Saad’s bare, determinism-compatible preemption structure is not itself an outcome-selection channel in the Map’s Shannon taxonomy.

**Severity: High — verified**

The public [Arguments hub](https://unfinishablemap.org/arguments/) says:

By contrast, [Arguments for Dualism Positions](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/arguments-for-dualism/) says these arguments share premises, that their cumulative force is “real but modest,” and that the defensible reading is never “overwhelming.” The Tenets page also says the illusionist dispute reaches bedrock and that the arguments are not independent proofs. [positions/arguments-for-dualism](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/arguments-for-dualism/)

Calling the section openly partisan does not resolve this evidential-status mismatch. “Argues,” “pressures,” or “supports conditional on” would be consistent with the register; “shows” is not.

[positions/voids-as-evidence](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/voids-as-evidence/)

## 3.9 Inspectability is being allowed to imply trustworthiness

**Severity: Medium — verified methodological tension**

[Why This Is Different](https://unfinishablemap.org/project/why-this-is-different/) says trust comes from inspectability and concludes that the site can be “trusted by inspection.” [project/why-this-is-different](https://unfinishablemap.org/project/why-this-is-different/)

# 4. Tenet-coherence audit

## Tenet 1: Dualism

The current tenet is commendably explicit that dualism is a chosen starting point and not a conclusion established by the argument catalogue. [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

The remaining issue is that the site’s public claim of neutrality between substance and property dualism does not describe the operational framework as a whole. Agent causation and the anti-many-worlds indexical case rely on a persisting subject. The site now calls this a substance-leaning sub-reading, but because the commitment supplies the main rationale for another tenet, it is foundational rather than merely local.

The homepage’s claim that “starting with dualism keeps the question open” is also misleading. It keeps irreducibility and interactionist possibilities open, but it closes the physicalist answer by methodological stipulation. A more accurate sentence would be: *Starting with dualism prevents the project from presupposing reduction and permits a systematic conditional exploration of the dualist hypothesis.* [The Unfinishable Map](https://unfinishablemap.org/)

## Tenet 2: Minimal Quantum Interaction

The [Tenets page](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/) says that if consciousness influences physics it “must do so at the quantum level” and calls MQI the “smallest deviation from standard quantum mechanics.” [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

Two problems follow.

First, quantum location is not entailed by dualism or interaction. It is a framework choice motivated by empirical closure at classical scales. “Must” should therefore be replaced by “the Map posits that it does.”

Second, “deviation” is ambiguous. The site also requires exact Born statistics, no energy injection, and no detectable public anomaly. If the deviation is:

- statistical, exact Born preservation denies it;
- dynamical, the dynamical law has not been given;
- ontological, the page should say that the addition is a selection or actualisation postulate empirically equivalent to standard quantum mechanics in specified contexts.

The sentence “physics is not complete at quantum indeterminacies” similarly moves too quickly. Quantum probabilities and the measurement problem may leave interpretative questions, but that does not by itself establish a causally available slot for a nonphysical selector.

## Tenet 3: Bidirectional Interaction

The tenet’s reportability argument provides pressure against bare epiphenomenalism, but it does not establish that **every conscious token** is actually efficacious.

The [AI Consciousness Scope Positions](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/) page explicitly concedes that the universal-actual-efficacy reading “outruns the self-stultification argument.” It notes that weakening the tenet to dispositional or existential efficacy would reopen the possibility of epiphenomenal AI experience. [positions/ai-consciousness-scope](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/)

The site therefore needs to choose among:

- every conscious token makes a physical difference;
- every conscious subject has a standing causal capacity;
- some conscious episodes are causally efficacious;
- consciousness is necessary somewhere in the ancestry of reliable phenomenal reporting.

Only the last two are supported by the reportability argument without additional premises. The current universal wording silently strengthens its rationale.

## Tenet 4: No Many Worlds

The tenet now handles its subjecthood dependency honestly. It says it is not a pure physics tenet and that its indexical force is only as strong as the non-deflationary subject it assumes. [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

That honesty exposes two remaining issues.

First, the tenet is partly a conjunction of:

- one-world actuality;
- global uniqueness;
- non-deflationary personal identity;
- rejection of branch-relative or centred-world resolutions.

These should be separated. Rejecting branch egalitarianism does not by itself reject every Everettian or centred-world formulation.

Second, objective physical collapse plus conscious modulation creates the actuation-allocation problem described above. “Modulation” is currently a placeholder for the very law the framework most needs.

A strength worth retaining is the tenet’s refusal to use ontological extravagance as a knockdown objection to Everett after Tenet 5 has rejected simple ontology counting as decisive. That is good symmetric self-binding.

## Tenet 5: Limits on Occam’s Razor

The tenet’s defensible core is that simplicity is multidimensional, defeasible, and not a knockdown adjudicator of metaphysical theories. The current headline—“Simplicity is not a reliable guide to truth when knowledge is incomplete”—is broader than the supporting argument establishes. [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

The Stanford Encyclopedia distinguishes ontological parsimony from syntactic elegance, notes that the two can trade off, and characterises metaphysical uses of the razor more naturally as burden-shifting than refutation. That supports a bounded tenet, not a general verdict of unreliability. [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/simplicity/)

The historical examples are also too categorical:

- “Heliocentrism initially required more epicycles” should be replaced by the better-supported claim that Copernicus and Ptolemy both used more than thirty circles, offered similar numerical accuracy, and differed across several dimensions of economy and structural unity. [PHYSICS TODAY+1](https://physicstoday.aip.org/features/the-copernican-myths)
- Calling quantum mechanics or general relativity “vastly more complex” is meaningless unless the simplicity dimension is specified. Both also unify or compress large bodies of phenomena.
- Saying dualism explains consciousness because “something non-physical exists,” as [Parsimony Epistemology](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/parsimony-epistemology/) does, risks renaming the explanandum. Psychophysical laws, subject pairing, and the connection between phenomenal qualities and physical outcomes remain unexplained. [concepts/parsimony-epistemology](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/parsimony-epistemology/)

A stronger formulation would be:

> Simplicity is a ceteris-paribus, metric-dependent consideration whose evidential force in consciousness metaphysics has not been established strongly enough to decide between otherwise live theories.

# 5. Source fidelity and empirical claims

## 5.1 The right-caudate “replication” is misdescribed on multiple live pages

**Status: Verified against primary abstracts**

[Mental Effort](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/mental-effort/), [Bidirectional Interaction](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/bidirectional-interaction/), and [Attentional Economics](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/attentional-economics/) currently say Nakatani et al. found the “same right-caudate decrease” as the earlier Schwartz work. [concepts/mental-effort](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/mental-effort/)

Schwartz et al. 1996 reported **bilateral** decreases in caudate glucose metabolism among treatment responders. Nakatani et al. 2003 reported a decrease in **right** caudate regional cerebral blood flow. These findings are related, but they are not the same laterality, modality, sample, or design. [PubMed+1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8629886/)

The phrase therefore does two things wrongly:

1. it transfers the right-sided result backwards into Schwartz;
2. it overstates Nakatani as a same-method or anatomically exact replication.

## 5.2 Kral 2022 is overextended to long-term-meditator claims

## 5.3 Han and Choi are made to support too broad a causality claim

**Status: Verified overgeneralisation**

[Selection-Only Channel](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/selection-only-channel/) says any systematic per-trial departure from Born probabilities is a relativistic-causality problem, invoking Han and Choi 2016. [concepts/selection-only-channel](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/selection-only-channel/)

Han and Choi do not derive that claim from no-signalling alone. They explicitly say no-signalling is insufficient and introduce a stronger “space-like causality” condition: joint probabilities for spacelike-separated measurements must be independent of their temporal ordering. Their result concerns modified quantum probability-assignment functions within a standard Hilbert-space measurement construction involving entangled qubits. [Nature+1](https://www.nature.com/articles/srep22986)

To transfer that theorem to the Map’s proposed neural coupling, the Map must show that:

- the psychophysical modification has the form their theorem considers;
- it applies to local neural selection as well as entangled spacelike measurements;
- the joint state-reduction assumptions are retained;
- a conscious-state conditional is an operational modification of the assignment rule rather than a hidden-variable conditioning of an otherwise Born-compatible joint distribution.

Until then, Han and Choi provide a constrained warning about classes of probability modifications, not a general proof that every mind-conditioned departure is relativistically inconsistent.

## 5.4 The per-event information bound is worded incorrectly

**Status: Verified formal slippage, not a fatal mathematical error**

[Selection-Only Channel](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/selection-only-channel/) says “the maximum information transferable by realising one specific candidate” among $N$ alternatives is bounded by $\log_2 N$. It then correctly discusses Shannon entropy and uniform distributions. [concepts/selection-only-channel](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/selection-only-channel/)

The defensible average-channel statement is:
$I(C;O\mid X)\le H(O\mid X)\le \log_2 N.$

But the information or surprisal of a **particular** event is $-\log_2 p_i$, which can exceed $\log_2 N$ when the event is rare. In a binary distribution with $p=10^{-6}$, one realised outcome has about 19.9 bits of surprisal even though the expected entropy and mutual information remain at most one bit. Shannon’s bound concerns expected entropy or channel capacity, not every pointwise outcome. [Massachusetts Institute of Technology+1](https://web.mit.edu/6.976/www/handout/shannon.pdf)

The page should replace “by realising one specific candidate” with “in expectation per channel use, from an $N$-valued output.”

## 5.5 The neuroplasticity evidence does not discriminate dualism

Several pages have improved their caveats and now acknowledge that therapy-, placebo-, and meditation-associated neural change is compatible with standard learning and plasticity. [Mental Effort](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/mental-effort/) expressly says the materialist reading fits equally well; [Bidirectional Interaction](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/bidirectional-interaction/) concedes that content-specific effects do not distinguish the Map from neural encoding accounts. [concepts/mental-effort](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/mental-effort/)

Nonetheless, phrases such as “mental effort has physical consequences” continue to do rhetorical work suggestive of downward nonphysical causation. The empirical studies establish that:

- cognitive interventions are followed by neural and behavioural changes;
- beliefs and learned strategies are causally relevant at a personal or computational level;
- different interventions may engage distinguishable pathways.

They do not establish that a nonphysical phenomenal state contributes causal work over and above its neural implementation. This evidence is relevant to epiphenomenalism and type identity only after the disputed bridge from personal-level intervention to nonphysical causation is supplied.
