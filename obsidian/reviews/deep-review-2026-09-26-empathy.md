## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found
- **Internal strawman (L107 vs L105)**: the physicalist reading was stated as "one cannot undergo the other's states". Two sentences later the dualist-fit claim contrasted the other "given as a centre of experience I cannot occupy" with "a separate object I happen not to be". That contrast put a "happen not to" into the physicalist's mouth after the article had already credited them with "cannot". Fixed: the passage now grants the physicalist's "cannot" and locates the difference in *what* is impossible (two token systems being one, versus another point of view given *as a subject*). It links [[explanatory-gap]] and keeps "a judgement about fit, not a demonstration".
- **Coiner/populariser (named-idea attribution)**: "*Einfühlung* ... That was Theodor Lipps's term". Robert Vischer introduced the word in aesthetics, and Lipps made it central. The English word was coined by Titchener in 1909, not vaguely "in the early twentieth century". Fixed from the SEP "Empathy" entry. Titchener 1909 was added to References.

### Medium Issues Found
- **Scheler's taxonomy incomplete**: Scheler's four forms of fellow-feeling are community of feeling (*Miteinanderfühlen*), fellow-feeling, contagion and identification. *Nachfühlen* is distinguished from all four. The list omitted *Miteinanderfühlen*, so it has been added with Scheler's own example (parents over a dead child).
- **"the analogy model that Scheler refuted"**: this overstated a result the article itself calls "strong but contested" (L103, with Jacob 2011 keeping it live). Changed to "criticised".
- **Occam paragraph "shows"**: the same overstatement, changed to "argues".
- **No Many Worlds line** "within any branch the empathiser meets a determinate other": it read as conceding branches. Reworded as independence from whether other branches exist.
