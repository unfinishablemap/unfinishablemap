## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

1. **Tenet 1 over-read (calibration: tenet-entailment).** The old text said Tenet 1 "commits the Map to holding that … *whether* an agent is the genuine originator of an action is a genuine question about that irreducible consciousness". That reads agent origination into the Dualism tenet. `where-the-substance-commitment-enters` exists to block exactly that move: "The requirement is the agent-causal claim's, not the tenet's." A reviewer who accepts the tenets would still flag it. **Fixed**: Tenet 1 now delivers only the irreducibility claim and realism about the metaphysics of mind. Origination "enters with the Map's agent-causal commitment, made below the tenets", with a link to `where-the-substance-commitment-enters`.
2. **Tenet 5 misfiled the dissolution as a parsimony judgement.** The paragraph opened "Tenet 5 … sharpens the point at exactly the place Wallace's repair operates", which credited the tenet with the aptness argument. It closed by having Tenet 5 decline "that parsimonious redescription". Neither limb of Strawson's dissolution is a parsimony judgement: limb 1 is inescapability (p. 198) and limb 2 is gains-and-losses rationality (p. 199). This is the same error class as the 10-14 Pereboom misfiling. **Fixed**: the aptness argument is now labelled "The Map's own argument". Tenet 5 "reaches less far" and "answers neither" limb. It bears only on a further inference, from "the practices need no metaphysical foundation" to "there is none", and the paragraph closes: "That constrains the Map's own reasoning and brings no charge against the Strawsonian."

### Medium Issues Found

3. **The reply engaged only limb 1.** The 07-27 pass added Strawson's second limb to the exposition, but the Map's reply ("a practice's inescapability does not settle what is *true*") still answered only the first. **Fixed**: "Granting that the reactive framework is inescapable, and that any choice about it would turn on gains and losses to human life, the Map observes that neither inescapability nor value settles what is *true*." A matching clause was added: "and right by that measure to keep".
4. **"Strawson brackets that assumption" misstated his position.** Strawson does not set libertarian sourcehood aside. He denies the practice needs it. **Fixed**: "Strawson denies that the framework needs any such assumption: his modified optimism does without 'the obscure and panicky metaphysics of libertarianism' (p. 211)." The quote is verbatim from the essay's closing sentence, read on the British Academy scan p. 211. "Modified" is Strawson's own verb ("modify the optimist's position accordingly").
5. **The P-A5 residue was eliminated.** The old text said "earns it no *moral* advantage", unscoped. **Fixed**: the sentence now reads "no *moral* advantage in these central practices", followed by: "The narrow residue the Map's register keeps, chiefly ultimate desert, is contested, and Galen Strawson's Basic Argument presses exactly there." The new sentence links `positions/agency-and-will#^p-a5`.
6. **Two uses of the "This is not X. It is Y." construct.** (a) "It is not answered with a 'no'; it is dissolved." This also sat in tension with the page's own "genuine compatibilist rival", since compatibilism is the "no" answer. It became "his compatibilist 'no' comes by dissolving the question rather than answering it on its own terms." (b) "The dissolution is not a verbal trick or an evasion. It is a serious proposal…" became "Far from a verbal trick or an evasion, the dissolution is a serious proposal…".
7. **A navigation label carried an unhedged claim.** The Further Reading gloss "How agent-causal sourcehood grounds desert" was presented as fact. The Basic Argument page now calls that the claim under pressure. **Fixed**: "The Map's case that agent-causal sourcehood grounds desert". A Further Reading entry for `basic-argument-and-constitutive-luck` was added.
