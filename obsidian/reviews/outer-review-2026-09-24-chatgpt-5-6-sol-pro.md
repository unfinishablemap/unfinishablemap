## Reply

# External referee report: full-site audit of *The Unfinishable Map*

**Recommendation: major revision of the argumentative architecture, while continuing to present the site as an open research programme.**

The Map is no longer merely a large collection of dualist essays. It has acquired a genuine research-programme structure: canonical tenets, a positions register, dependency matrices, evidential grades, mechanism-debt conventions, cost ledgers, adversarial reviews and unusually transparent changelogs. Its strongest methodological pages are often more cautious than its critics would expect.

The central weakness is no longer simple philosophical naïveté. It is **failure of dependency control across a very large, rapidly changing corpus**. The canonical pages now make careful distinctions that many downstream pages ignore. The central quantum-interface proposal is articulated precisely enough to expose several deep unsolved problems, but not precisely enough to constitute a physical or causal model. Meanwhile, the site’s repeated “Relation to Site Perspective” sections are systematically reintroducing claims that the bodies of the same articles have already qualified.

The appropriate verdict is therefore:

> **The Map is a sophisticated and increasingly self-critical framework-stage programme, but it is not yet an integrated positive theory of mind–matter interaction. Its strongest current achievement is a constraint-and-debt architecture, not an empirically supported mechanism.**

## Scope and method

This review concerns the live site as it stood after the changelog entries of **23 September 2026**. I examined the canonical tenets and background commitments, the positions registers, the central interface and Born-preservation apex articles, representative articles across topics, concepts, voids and arguments, and the recent site-wide and delta tenet audits. The most recent full sweep covered 834 live articles; the 23 September delta then read 57 newly edited articles in full and rechecked 14 carried loci. [workflow/changelog](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/)

This is an architecture-level full-site audit, not a fresh verification of every reference in all 834 articles. Citation checking below concentrates on claims that bear directly on the central mechanism and on recent cases where the site’s own review pipeline found source-fidelity failures.

I distinguish:

- **Direct contradiction**: two current pages make claims that cannot both stand as written.
- **Unresolved tension**: the claims might be reconciled, but only by a law or distinction the site does not yet supply.
- **Calibration drift**: a page changes “compatible with” into “supports”, “suggests” into “shows”, or a framework posit into a derived result.

## 1. Overall strengths

### 1.1 The canonical core is substantially better calibrated than the surrounding corpus

The current tenets page explicitly says that the five tenets are chosen starting points rather than proofs. It now distinguishes a cumulative case from independent proofs, treats downward mental causation as available rather than established, acknowledges the epiphenomenalist common-cause reply, confines the anti-Everett argument to a non-deflationary conception of subjecthood, and symmetrically binds the Map’s own use of parsimony. [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

The September full-site sweep also found no article straightforwardly embracing physicalism, illusionism or Many Worlds in the Map’s own voice. The core flagship clusters were mostly sound. The problems are concentrated in local entailment claims, evidential verbs and alignment paragraphs rather than wholesale repudiations of the tenets. [reviews/tenet-check-2026-09-20](https://unfinishablemap.org/reviews/tenet-check-2026-09-20/)

### 1.2 The Map has become unusually candid about its hidden assumptions

The page on the “Three Background Commitments” is an important advance. It concedes that the five stated tenets depend on:

1. a determinate, persisting subject;
2. objective single-outcome actualisation;
3. global exclusion of unchosen alternatives.

It also correctly admits that several tenets share these roots and therefore do not provide independent support for one another. In particular, branch-local persistence, ownership, reasons-responsiveness and conscious causation are all available to an Everettian dualist; the Map’s further demand is that incompatible alternatives be unrealised anywhere. [tenets/background-commitments](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/background-commitments/)

### 1.3 It often states its mechanism debts accurately

The Interface Specification Programme acknowledges that it lacks a psychophysical law, a subject–brain pairing law, a basis-selection rule, a timing rule, a multi-agent composition law and a developmental onset account. It also admits that it has no coupling Hamiltonian, master equation or simulation. That degree of specification of what is missing is valuable. [apex/interface-specification-programme](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/interface-specification-programme/)

The mechanism-cost ledger is also methodologically sound: Tenet 5 may block a simplistic parsimony verdict, but it does not make empirical, coherence or formal costs disappear. [project/mechanism-cost-ledger](https://unfinishablemap.org/project/mechanism-cost-ledger/)

These are genuine strengths. The problem is that these calibrated concessions are not reliably inherited elsewhere.

# 2. Novel implications of the Map’s commitments

I did not find a canonical treatment of the following consequences in their fully developed form. Some ingredients are already present, but the implications below go beyond the current formulations.

## 2.1 The causal-intervention identification trilemma

The Born-preserving apex article defines mental causal efficacy through:

P(O∣do(C),X)=q(O∣X),

where C is a conscious intention and X is the relevant physical context. But its preservation equation is observational:

q(O∣X)=C∑​P(O∣C,X)P(C∣X).

The page therefore moves between **interventional** and **observational** conditionals without supplying assumptions that identify one from the other. [apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/)

That produces a trilemma:

1. **Manipulate a cue or instruction.** A pre-cued goal changes neural and bodily state as well as conscious intention. The intervention is on a physical instruction variable, not on phenomenal C alone.
2. **Condition on a reported conscious state.** A report given after the outcome may itself be affected by the outcome, memory, confidence or action. Conditioning on it risks post-treatment or collider bias.
3. **Treat C as a private phenomenal variable.** Then there is no publicly verifiable intervention that sets C while holding X fixed.

The Self-Concealing Interface proposes pre-cued intentions and asks whether a residual remains after progressively richer physical measurement. That is a useful research direction, but “progressively more complete” has no finite stopping criterion, and a residual can always be assigned to an omitted physical state unless the experiment has an independently defended instrument or exclusion restriction. [apex/self-concealing-interface](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/self-concealing-interface/)

This is more than a sensitivity problem. It is a **causal-identification problem**. Before testing P(O∣do(C),X), the Map needs to explain what could count as an intervention on phenomenal content rather than on its physical causes or reports.

## 2.2 The phenomenal-partition invariance problem

The balancing equation depends on how conscious states are partitioned. One may classify C as:

- intention to choose left versus right;
- strength of intention;
- felt effort;
- valence;
- exact phenomenal content;
- subject-specific or population-level states.

A conditional effect can disappear under one coarse partition and appear under a finer one. The Born article explicitly retreats from coarse intention-to-RNG nulls toward “finer grains” such as spontaneous or value-laden states. [apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/)

But unless there is a principled state space and measure over phenomenal states, “try a finer grain” is indefinitely extensible. The theory needs a consistency requirement analogous to coarse-graining consistency:

> Refining, merging or relabelling phenomenal categories must not arbitrarily change whether the psychophysical law counts as efficacious.

That requires specifying which distinctions among phenomenal states are causally real, which are merely descriptions, and how the selection law transforms under refinement. Without this, the empirical signature depends partly on the investigator’s taxonomy.

## 2.3 Local minimality implies possible global complexity

The programme often describes consciousness as exerting a very small local influence. Yet exact Born preservation, joint quantum correlations, no-signalling, semantic intention-to-outcome mapping, multi-agent conflict resolution and subject–brain pairing may require a law with access to globally distributed information.

The site already concedes the semantic-mapping and multi-agent gaps: preserving each agent’s marginal is not enough to preserve entangled joint distributions, and the single-agent theorem does not provide a many-subject factorisation law. [apex/interface-specification-programme](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/interface-specification-programme/)

The resulting inference is:

> **A mechanism can be minimal in per-event energetic magnitude while being maximal in informational scope, coordination radius or rule complexity.**

Tenet 2 therefore needs a multidimensional definition of minimality. At minimum:

(energy or amplitude, spatial scope, information access, semantic specificity, coordination complexity, parameter count).

Calling an influence “small” on the first axis does not make it minimal overall. Tenet 5 cannot dismiss this as mere parsimony, because failure to define the global coordination law is a coherence and specification cost, precisely the category the mechanism-cost ledger says survives Tenet 5. [project/mechanism-cost-ledger](https://unfinishablemap.org/project/mechanism-cost-ledger/)

## 2.4 The evolutionary dual-inheritance dilemma

The interface programme says that, if conscious selection affects fitness, evolution should optimise interface reliability and bandwidth across species, development, lesions and anaesthesia. [apex/interface-specification-programme](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/interface-specification-programme/)

That generates an unstated inheritance problem. What exactly is inherited?

- **Only neural interface architecture is inherited.** Then ordinary physical evolution explains the improved behavioural control, leaving the extra conscious selector causally underdetermined.
- **The psychophysical mapping itself evolves.** Then a nonphysical or cross-domain law must somehow be inherited across subjects and generations.
- **The mapping is a fixed fundamental law.** Then it must explain highly species-specific, development-sensitive and semantically rich intentions without evolutionary modification.
- **Each subject learns the mapping.** Then learning requires an already functioning interface, creating an onset regress: how is the first successful mind-to-brain mapping acquired?

The Map needs a distinction between evolution of the **physical receiver/controller**, evolution of **subject eligibility**, and evolution or learning of the **psychophysical codebook**. At present these are treated together under “interface optimisation”.

## 2.5 Objective collapse creates a priority race, not merely a completeness tension

The prebiotic-collapse solution says that objective physical reduction already creates definite outcomes throughout the mindless universe, while consciousness modulates outcomes in developed brains. It correctly recognises that an outcome-complete physical collapse law threatens to leave no work for the mental selector. [concepts/prebiotic-collapse](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/prebiotic-collapse/)

The deeper consequence is dynamical. A model needs at least two hazards or transition processes:

- a physical-collapse process;
- a conscious-selection process.

Then it must answer:

- Which acts first?
- Is consciousness allowed to delay physical collapse?
- Does it alter the collapse rate, the outcome probabilities, or only an outcome not yet chosen?
- What defines the interval after decoherence but before objective reduction?
- How is the ordering defined for spacelike-separated or frame-dependent events?

If physical collapse acts first, the selector receives no menu. If mental selection pre-empts or alters it, the objective-collapse dynamics have been modified and may acquire an empirical signature. If the two jointly determine the transition, the site needs a combined stochastic law or master equation.

This is the **collapse-priority problem**. It is sharper than saying merely that “modulation must coexist with objective collapse”.

## 2.6 The subject-census inference can become circular

The subject-census register correctly observes that the formal law needs indexed variables C1​,…,Cn​, an eligibility rule and a pairing rule. It also concedes that none currently exists. [positions/subject-census](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/subject-census/)

But the site commonly infers subjecthood in animals, artificial systems, disorders of consciousness and altered states from behaviour, reports or neural signatures. Those are also the kinds of outcomes the interface is supposed to influence. If observed outcomes determine who enters the subject census, and census membership then conditions the outcome law, the model risks selecting its latent causes by their effects.

The programme therefore needs:

1. eligibility criteria fixed independently of the outcome being tested;
2. sensitivity analyses across rival census models;
3. rules for copies, split brains, fission and collective systems;
4. an account of whether adding more subjects changes the balancing weights.

A particularly sharp consequence concerns copies. If multiple subjects with the same intention enter P(C1​,…,Cn​∣X), either their causal influence accumulates, dilutes, cancels, or is globally renormalised. Each option is theoretically consequential. The current framework does not choose among them.

# 3. Structural weaknesses

## 3.1 The operative constitution is not five commitments

The public presentation remains “five foundational commitments”. In operation, the framework requires at least:

- five stated tenets;
- three background posits;
- a subject-pairing and eligibility law;
- a psychophysical control law;
- a multi-agent composition law.

The background page now admits the first three extra posits, which is excellent, but they remain structurally subordinate to the five-tenet branding even though the agency and anti-Everett arguments cannot operate without them. [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

The Map should be described as having **five headline tenets within a larger foundational dependency graph**, not as five nearly self-standing pillars.

## 3.2 The interface programme is a constraint catalogue, not yet an engineering specification

The Interface Specification Programme repeatedly calls itself engineering-style. It identifies candidate control operations, empirical constraints and possible failure modes, but it also admits that it has no interaction law, Hamiltonian, master equation or simulation. [apex/interface-specification-programme](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/interface-specification-programme/)

That means it presently specifies:

- desired behaviour;
- prohibited outcomes;
- some hypothesised interfaces;
- some qualitative predictions.

It does not yet specify the transformation that maps a phenomenal state and physical state to a probability law over physical outcomes. “Specification” is defensible if clearly qualified as a **requirements specification**. It should not be allowed to imply a worked mechanism.

## 3.3 Several mutually different causal routes remain live

The site distinguishes:

- post-decoherence difference-making selection;
- pre-decoherence proposals such as Zeno or Orch OR;
- Born-preserving corridor models;
- looser Born-deviating models;
- delegatory or trumping causation with no token difference.

The Interface Specification Programme commendably recognises that difference-making and delegatory trumping have incompatible closure profiles. [apex/interface-specification-programme](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/interface-specification-programme/)

However, downstream empirical pages still move between these routes. A null result may constrain one route while leaving another untouched; a Born deviation would refute strict corridor preservation while perhaps supporting a looser interactionist model. Without route labels, the framework can absorb almost any result by moving sideways between mechanisms.

Every mechanism-bearing article needs an explicit field:

> **Mechanism route assumed:** strict corridor / outside-corridor / pre-decoherence / objective-collapse modulation / delegatory-trumping / mechanism-neutral.

## 3.4 Article proliferation is now an integration problem even where merging is inappropriate

The latest coalescence pass was the fifteenth consecutive no-merge decision. The reasons for retaining many pairs are often sound: concept versus topic, mechanism versus comparison, or general AI consciousness versus uploading. But the site is operating near its own section targets, and the repeated no-merge results show that literal merging is no longer the right integration tool. [workflow/changelog](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/)

The problem is less exact duplication than **distributed restatement**:

- the same tenet summaries are rewritten hundreds of times;
- mechanism debts are restated inconsistently;
- rival-theory conclusions drift between pages;
- a correction to a canonical page does not automatically update dependent prose.

The remedy is canonical claim federation and generated dependency summaries, not forced article fusion.

## 3.5 The mandated alignment section is the main coherence leak

The September full sweep found that almost all serious Tenets 1–4 problems lay not in the article’s main argument but in its “Relation to Site Perspective” section. The latest delta found at least 11 new or edited pages overstating Tenet 3 and seven importing Tenet 4 where it was not invoked. [reviews/tenet-check-2026-09-20](https://unfinishablemap.org/reviews/tenet-check-2026-09-20/)

The problem is structural: 807 of 811 articles in the audited set carried a mandated alignment section, even where the local argument did not depend on all five tenets. The site’s own dependency matrix predicts the resulting “alignment-line inheritance”. [reviews/tenet-check-2026-09-20](https://unfinishablemap.org/reviews/tenet-check-2026-09-20/)

The section should not be free-written independently in every article. It should be generated from the dependency matrix or restricted to tenets actually used.

## 3.6 The editorial system documents controls faster than it enforces them

The methodology register explicitly states that disclosure is not self-correction and that an unenforced countermeasure is merely an intention. The latest changelog illustrates the problem: priority defects identified on 20 September remained untouched three days later because the reporting process did not automatically mint work items. [positions/methodology-and-calibration](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/methodology-and-calibration/)

This is the most important operational weakness. The site already knows many of its defects, but knowledge of a defect and repair of a defect are separate pipeline states.

## 3.7 Citation checking remains reactive

The transparency of the recent correction record is a major strength, but the kinds of errors being found are too load-bearing to be left to later deep reviews. One recent metabolic article required correction of eight critical issues, including reversed or false claims and citations that did not support the attributed comparison. The IIT page had treated one COGITATE study as two studies. A social-understanding article attributed unconscious contagion to de Waal when the cited abstract supported only automatic activation. A collapse quotation was assigned to the wrong source and broadened from GRW to GRW and CSL. [workflow/changelog](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/)

These are not merely typographic mistakes. They show that the current source gate is insufficient for empirical claims later used in tenet alignment.

## 3.8 Review coverage is uneven

As of the 23 September changelog, 191 live files had never been named in the preceding 40 optimistic reviews. The newly reviewed intersubjective cluster immediately yielded several familiar errors, including epiphenomenalism overclaims, Everett misdescription and a phenomenally defined prediction wrongly labelled falsifiable. [workflow/changelog](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/)

Review selection should therefore be coverage-driven as well as salience-driven.

## 4.1 Tenet 1: neutrality is narrower than the working ontology

The site says Tenet 1 is neutral between property and substance dualism. Yet agent causation, personal identity, survival, copying and the indexical objection to Everett depend on a numerically determinate subject persisting through time. The site now admits this, but it should therefore stop allowing “Tenet 1” to stand as shorthand for the stronger subject ontology. [tenets/background-commitments](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/background-commitments/)

There are also residual linguistic contradictions. The full audit identified claims that conscious activity “supervenes on” neural processes, that parietal cortex “produces” the experience of intending, and that cortical processing “generates” phenomenal content. These may be informal expressions rather than intended metaphysics, but on this site they cross a deliberately policed boundary. [reviews/tenet-check-2026-09-20](https://unfinishablemap.org/reviews/tenet-check-2026-09-20/)

## 4.2 Tenet 2: the post-decoherence route does not yet buy a usable opportunity for selection

It is correct that decoherence alone does not select one unique outcome in all interpretations. But the further inference—therefore consciousness can select after decoherence—does not follow. Decoherence’s failure to solve the measurement problem leaves an interpretive gap; it does not establish a physical interval, menu or causal variable accessible to a nonphysical selector. Standard reviews describe the relation between decoherence and the measurement problem as interpretation-dependent and controversial. [APS Journals](https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/RevModPhys.76.1267)

Moreover, the Map simultaneously adopts objective reduction to handle prebiotic definiteness. Once that physical process supplies basis, timing and outcome, post-decoherence mental selection must either pre-empt it, modify it or become redundant. The current “modulation” vocabulary names the desired relation without specifying it. [concepts/prebiotic-collapse](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/prebiotic-collapse/)

## 4.3 Tenet 3: most empirical material supports processing, not phenomenal causation

Studies of cognitive load, belief revision, attention, lesions, anaesthesia or neuroplasticity can establish that particular neural and cognitive processes matter. They do not by themselves establish that phenomenal character contributes an additional cause rather than accompanying the causally sufficient neural process.

The tenets page says this correctly: comparative-cognition findings corroborate but do not establish mental causation because the physical common-cause model remains available. Yet at least 11 recently edited articles reverted to “supports”, “proves”, “must be causally efficacious” or “genuine selector”. [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

The same distinction applies to transmission or filter evidence. Evidence that consciousness persists while a neural interface is impaired would bear on production versus transmission—principally Tenet 1. It does not establish the outbound leg of Tenet 3. The register states this partition, while `filter-theory`, terminal-lucidity and several related pages breach it. [reviews/tenet-check-2026-09-20](https://unfinishablemap.org/reviews/tenet-check-2026-09-20/)

## 4.4 Tenet 4: global exclusion is an asserted value condition on agency

The most important current concession is that an Everettian can preserve, inside each branch:

- a single experienced history;
- psychological continuity;
- ownership of the action;
- reasons-responsive deliberation;
- conscious causal relations.

The Map’s conclusion requires the additional judgement that authorship is diminished if incompatible alternatives occur elsewhere. The site has not demonstrated that condition; it adopts it. [tenets/background-commitments](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/background-commitments/)

This is not an incoherent position, but it is a framework boundary. Articles should say:

> “The Map’s global-exclusion conception of authorship rejects this.”

They should not say that Many Worlds makes local agency, unity or ownership impossible.

## 4.5 Tenet 5: the self-binding discipline is stronger than its historical rationale

The strongest version of Tenet 5 is methodological symmetry: complexity must be recorded, but parsimony alone cannot decide between frameworks when the relevant explanatory space is poorly understood.

The weaker portion is the suggestion that historical failures of simplicity make parsimony generally unreliable. Historical counterexamples defeat an inflexible simplicity rule, not all Bayesian or abductive preference for less adjustable theories. The Map should retain “defeasible and non-decisive” and avoid “carries no truth-tracking weight”.

# 5. Cross-cluster contradictions and unresolved tensions

## 5.1 No-signalling: `positions` versus `apex` and `topics` — **direct contradiction**

`P-Q7` assigns high confidence to the claim that the default mechanism does not break Lorentz invariance or no-signalling, on the ground that it preserves Born statistics by construction. [positions/quantum-interface](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/quantum-interface/)

But the Interface Specification Programme says that preserving each subject’s marginal is insufficient and that a joint law over entangled systems, multiple subjects and spacelike-separated selections has not been supplied. The Multi-Agent Born-Preservation article says the same problem is currently unspecified. [apex/interface-specification-programme](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/interface-specification-programme/)

The latter is correct. `P-Q7` must be split into:

- single-system marginal compatibility;
- multi-agent joint no-signalling;
- Lorentz-covariant dynamics.

Only the first is presently secured by stipulation.

## 5.2 “Self-concealing by theorem” versus “requirements not yet shown” — **direct contradiction**

The Self-Concealing Interface says conservation, no-signalling and Born statistics force the influence into an ensemble-invisible register and that concealment follows directly from the tenets. [apex/self-concealing-interface](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/self-concealing-interface/)

The Interface Specification Programme instead says conservation and no-signalling are future requirements: energy-degenerate alternatives have not been defined, and no joint law has been written. [apex/interface-specification-programme](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/interface-specification-programme/)

The self-concealment article may state a conditional:

> Any successful corridor model satisfying these constraints would be hard to detect in unconditioned aggregates.

It cannot yet state that the constraints have been jointly met or that concealment is entailed by a completed architecture.

## 5.3 Tenet 3 standing versus `self-stultification`, `ai-consciousness`, `categorical-surprise` and related pages — **calibration contradiction**

The canonical position says self-stultification is a deep challenge to epiphenomenalism, not a refutation. Yet the self-stultification article says consciousness “must” be causally efficacious; the AI page says the argument “proves” some consciousness does causal work; and several phenomenology pages turn correlations into positive evidence for selection. Some of these articles contradict their own more cautious bodies. [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

## 5.4 Tenet 4’s branch-local concession versus peripheral alignment paragraphs — **calibration contradiction**

The canonical background page says branch-local unity, ownership, persistence and reasons-responsiveness remain available. Nevertheless, articles on AI consciousness, social understanding, constitutive exclusion, timing gaps and interface formalisation attribute these goods directly to rejecting Many Worlds. [tenets/background-commitments](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/background-commitments/)

## 5.5 Filter evidence: `positions` versus `concepts`, `topics` and clinical articles — **direct dependency breach**

The positions register treats transmission-over-production evidence as relevant to irreducibility and the inbound brain-to-consciousness relation, not as evidence for consciousness-to-brain causation. Yet `filter-theory` infers outbound influence from the interface premise, terminal-lucidity invokes enhanced downward causation, and volitional-control attaches classical neuroplastic changes to quantum modulation despite admitting that Hebbian mechanisms explain them. [reviews/tenet-check-2026-09-20](https://unfinishablemap.org/reviews/tenet-check-2026-09-20/)

## 5.6 Artificial consciousness: dependency matrix versus the functionalism argument — **direct contradiction**

# 6. Citation and empirical spot-checks

## 6.1 COGITATE is still misstated in the Interface Specification Programme

The article says the 2025 adversarial collaboration found conscious content in posterior rather than prefrontal regions “either rival predicts”. That is inaccurate. IIT made important posterior-cortex predictions, while GNWT placed more emphasis on prefrontal ignition. The study found information in visual, ventrotemporal **and inferior frontal** cortex, aligned with some predictions of both theories, and challenged particular tenets of each. [apex/interface-specification-programme](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/interface-specification-programme/)

A more accurate formulation would be:

> COGITATE produced mixed results: some findings aligned with predictions of both IIT and GNWT, while absence of sustained posterior synchronisation challenged IIT and limited prefrontal ignition or representation challenged GNWT.

The current sentence conflates the rivals’ predictions and overstates a simple posterior-versus-prefrontal result.

## 6.2 The 10-bit-per-second result is behavioural throughput, not interface bandwidth

Zheng and Meister estimate the information throughput of human behaviour at roughly 10 bits per second and contrast it with vastly higher sensory input. They do not measure a nonphysical mind–brain channel. [The Meister Laboratory](https://meisterlab.caltech.edu/documents/30100/Zheng_2024_The_unbearable_slowness_of_being-_Why_do_we_live_at_10_bitss.pdf)

The Map now acknowledges this caveat in places, which is an improvement. But it still repeatedly calls 10 bits/s a “bandwidth constraint” on the interface and uses it to motivate a complete low-bandwidth control repertoire. At most it is:

- an observed behavioural bottleneck;
- compatible with a low-bandwidth controller;
- not a measurement of the postulated psychophysical channel.

## 6.3 Torres Alegre does not establish the Map’s no-signalling claim

The cited preprint proves, within finite-dimensional generalised probabilistic theories satisfying purification and steering assumptions, that nonlinear transformations of transition probabilities can enable signalling and that the identity relation is uniquely no-signalling under those assumptions. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.12636)

It does not supply:

- a model of consciousness;
- the Map’s conditioned token-selection law;
- a multi-agent composition rule;
- Lorentz-covariant dynamics;
- proof that individually Born-preserving conscious selectors preserve the full joint law.

It is relevant as a constraint on altered probability rules, but `P-Q7` overextends it into confirmation of a mechanism the paper does not analyse.

## 6.4 The Kerskens–López Pérez study should not be described as “initial support” without stronger qualification

The 2022 paper reports unusual MRI signals and argues that the findings *may* indicate nonclassical brain functions or mediated entanglement. The authors themselves repeatedly use tentative language, and the method depends on disputed inferences from the absence of identified classical NMR contrasts. [SETI](https://seti.net/Neuron%20Lab/NeuronReferences/Quantum%20Brain%20Functions%20Kerskens_2022_J._Phys._Commun._6_105001.pdf)

The Map’s current tenet audit correctly flags the unity article for saying the work is “now lending initial support” without naming its unreplicated and tentative status. [reviews/tenet-check-2026-09-23](https://unfinishablemap.org/reviews/tenet-check-2026-09-23/)

It should be classified as:

> a single exploratory protocol generating a controversial nonclassical interpretation, not evidence for consciousness-mediated entanglement until independently replicated and alternative signal sources are excluded.

## 6.5 The treatment of the natural Diósi–Penrose model is now appropriately narrow

The Donadi underground experiment places strong constraints on the parameter-free natural Diósi–Penrose proposal through predicted spontaneous radiation. The current positions register now confines the exclusion claim to the relevant natural parameter choice rather than the entire family. That correction is sound. [Nature+1](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41567-020-1008-4)

## 6.6 Decoherence literature supports an outcome problem, not a conscious selector

The Map is justified in saying that decoherence alone does not, without interpretive additions, establish why one unique outcome is experienced. But this supports only the existence of a measurement-problem residue. It does not support the further hypothesis that consciousness selects the outcome. [APS Journals](https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/RevModPhys.76.1267)

The evidential relation should be recorded as:

- decoherence removes one objection to a post-decoherence proposal;
- it does not raise the probability of a mental selector over objective collapse, Bohmian dynamics, Everettian branching or other interpretations.

## 6.7 Prebiotic definiteness is not neutral empirical evidence for objective collapse

The definite CMB record and definite stellar history are consistent with objective collapse, but they do not discriminate it from branch-relative definiteness in Everettian models or other no-collapse interpretations. The `prebiotic-collapse` page presents definiteness as evidence that collapse occurred before minds; given the site’s rejection of Many Worlds this is an in-framework reading, not an interpretation-neutral empirical inference. [concepts/prebiotic-collapse](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/prebiotic-collapse/)

It should be labelled “what the Map’s one-world framework requires”, not evidence that independently favours objective collapse.
