### Critical Issues Found

1. **Jeans page locators wrong (L37, L61, L72)**. In the named scan, "lies beyond the province of physics", "crevices have begun to appear" and the whole keyhole sentence all fall between the head "280 THE NEW BACKGROUND OF SCIENCE" and "EVENTS 281", with the p. 279 footnotes just before the 280 head. The research note had "280–282" and the article wrote p. 281 and pp. 281–282. **Fixed**: L37 (p. 281) → (p. 280); L61 (pp. 281–282) → (p. 280); L72 table "Jeans 1933, pp. 281–282" → "Jeans 1933, p. 280". References keep the span read, pp. 277–282.
2. **Wrong claim about another Map page (L69, L85)**. The table put the magnitude objection under "[[trilemma-of-selection]], Horn 2", and the Bidirectional paragraph had "the trilemma of selection's Horn 2 and Popper's grain complaint remain open against" neural amplification. The trilemma's Horn 2 is **Randomness** (luck/rollback), and its first sentence pipes back to this page as "openness alone is not freedom". It is not the magnitude objection. **Fixed**: L69 cell → `[[amplification-mechanisms-consciousness-physics]]; Popper's grain complaint`, because that page's "The Amplification Problem" and "What Remains Speculative" carry the scale gap as open. L70 (randomness, generic) gains `[[trilemma-of-selection]], Horn 2`. L85 "the [[trilemma-of-selection|trilemma of selection]]'s Horn 2" → "the [[amplification-mechanisms-consciousness-physics|amplification problem]]".
3. **Dropped qualifier on Compton (L33)**. Popper p. 233 says "Some quantum theorists suggested (and Compton very tentatively accepted the suggestion) that our minds work upon our bodies by influencing or selecting some quantum jumps". The page's "He added … in 1935 a selector" made Compton the originator, at full strength. **Fixed**: "and in 1935 "very tentatively accepted" a selector" (+3).
4. **Paraphrase moved Eddington's locus (L45)**. "the smallest unit in which volition originates" stood for raw p. 88 "the smallest unit of structure in which the physical effects of volition have their origin". The paraphrase put volition itself in a unit of atoms, which runs against Eddington's mind-stuff idealism (the Dualism firewall at L87). **Fixed**: "in which volition's physical effects originate" (+2).
5. **Issue 5 of the 10-28 review (L83), still live**. "escapes his first horn as he stated it, since no odds are altered, and walks into his second" claimed more than the register grants. P-Q2 holds frequencies "Born-exact in every publicly conditionable context; only conditionals on the conscious state itself are free", and Schrödinger's first horn concerns the agent's own 30:70 frequency. **Fixed**, using the review's recommended wording plus the P-Q2 grain: "The Map escapes his first horn as he stated it, since no publicly measurable odds are altered, at the price of making each outcome depend on the agent, which is what his second horn then asks about: a selection that leaves the statistics untouched must say what distinguishes it from chance." (+15). Pronoun check: "his" refers to Schrödinger (from the previous sentence), "it" to the selection, and the next sentence's "that" to the demand. All hold.
