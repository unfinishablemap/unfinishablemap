Claude splits the difference — "handled better, then undone." Verification sided with ChatGPT and Claude. The three still agree on the *outcome* (C6): the article performs the correction and then recovers the artifact anyway.
- **Gemini vs ChatGPT and Claude — on whether the article's hedges exist at all.** Gemini charges that the article treats radical-communitarian Ubuntu "as an absolute, uncontested truth" and concludes that irreducibility "automatically validates Interactionist Dualism". Both siblings quote the hedges (L37 "suggestive context rather than additional evidence"; L45 naming Metz's ethical reading as dissent; L47 "Relation shapes consciousness without exhausting its nature"; L93 "does not, on its own, raise the evidential status of dualism"). Gemini's charge was refuted on the body — and lands exactly on the `description:` frontmatter, which is C5. The interesting residue is that Claude, which quotes the same hedges, still reaches Gemini's conclusion by a different route: it calls them "inoculation-by-confession", confessions that never convert into a status change. So the reviewers disagree about whether the hedges are *present*, and agree that they do no work.

Every Gemini finding that survived was re-sourced independently at OpenAlex before it reached a task, so none of its bad citations entered the corpus.
- **Shared locus, distinct findings.** L89 drew fire from two reviewers for two unrelated reasons: ChatGPT on attribution ("what is it like for *us*?" presented as the question asked by "the African oral tradition" rather than as a Map heuristic) and Gemini on inference (a premise about knowledge transmission carrying a conclusion about phenomenal architecture). The cycle therefore arrived at synthesis with eight distinct tasks and no redundant pairs, which is the intended steady state.
