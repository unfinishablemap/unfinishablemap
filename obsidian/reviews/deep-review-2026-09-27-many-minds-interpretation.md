## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

- **Inverted critique target plus a paraphrase presented as a quote (probability problem, L76).** The article said "Critics from Barrett (1999) to Loewer's own 1996 reassessment judge this [Albert–Loewer's measure over minds] 'puzzling and insufficient'". The raw IEP text shows three problems. (a) Loewer 1996 and Barrett 1999 both criticise *Lockwood's* probability measure, not Albert–Loewer's. (b) "puzzling and insufficient" is the IEP's own summary of Barrett, not a quotation from Barrett or Loewer. (c) Loewer 1996 ("Comment on Lockwood") was cited inline with no References entry. RESOLVED: the passage is rewritten so the critique is aimed at Lockwood's measure. It uses the IEP-verbatim fragments "entirely new notion of probability" (Barrett quoted by the IEP) and "temporal 'length'" / "superpositional 'width'", and adds the point that Albert–Loewer's stochastic minds do supply genuine chances. Loewer 1996 is added to References (BJPS 47(2): 229–232, DOI 10.1093/bjps/47.2.229).
- **Coiner misattribution ("mindless hulks," L52).** The article said Barrett (1999) "called" it the mindless hulks objection. Marchildon 2015 (SHPMP 52) says the problem was "emphasized by Albert (1992, 130)" and "has become known as the mindless-hulk problem". Barrett popularised and developed it. RESOLVED: now reads "emphasized by Albert (1992: 130) and pressed in detail by Barrett (1999)". Albert 1992 is added to References. The page number comes from a secondary source (Marchildon) and has not been checked in the book.
- **Paraphrase presented as a Lockwood quote (L64, L88).** "senseless to ask which, of the many possible minds, are 'yours'" does not appear in the SEP, the IEP, current Wikipedia, or Marchildon's long Lockwood quotations. The only web source is the thoughtexperiments.net blog's own prose. RESOLVED: now reported speech, grounded in Marchildon's primary-backed statement that Lockwood "rejects the transtemporal identity of individual minds". The Parfit alignment is softened from "explicitly aligning" to "parallels". In the Relation section, 'declares "senseless"' becomes "holds there is none of".
- **Spliced, unverifiable Zeh quote plus an unverifiable "selects" (L68, L86).** "the most natural extension of standard psycho-physical parallelism" is absent from Zeh 2000, which was grepped in full. A search finds it only in a secondary paper, and there it reads "...of standard *views on* psycho-physical parallelism", so the article's version was also spliced. The quoted "selects" / "consciousness selects a branch" has zero hits in Zeh 2000. RESOLVED: replaced with strings that are verbatim in Zeh 2000: "leads inevitably to a many-minds interpretation" (abstract), "a (suitably modified) psycho-physical parallelism can be re-established", and "dynamically passive parallelism (or epiphenomenalism)". The last one now supports the acausality point in the Bidirectional section with Zeh's own words. Zeh's coinage "multi-consciousness interpretation" (1981) is added.
- **Orphan inline cites.** Zeh 1970 was cited inline with no reference, and Zeh 1981 is the original of the 2000 reprint. RESOLVED: added Zeh 1970 (Found. Phys. 1: 69–76, Crossref-verified) and noted the reprint status on the Zeh 2000 entry.
