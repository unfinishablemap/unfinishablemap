## Critiques by Philosopher

### The Eliminative Materialist
Churchland would grant the architecture tier and say the entry has already conceded the game: the "mind-side family (object-unity, owned affect, viewpoint-frame)" is, on the entry's own citations, a set of integration products — the viewpoint-frame is temporo-parietal multisensory integration (Blanke 2012, via the apex), owned affect is interoceptive inference (Seth 2013, Barrett & Simmons 2015). A "family of contributions" whose every member has a neural construction story is folk-psychological vocabulary awaiting replacement, and the entry's own grade D says as much. Her sharpest point is against the *architecture* tier: it partitions "brain-side extraction" from "mind-side unity" as though the partition were neuroanatomical, but the supramodal signature is itself late cortical activity. The two "sides" are both brain.

### The Hard-Nosed Physicalist
Dennett's target is the phrase "the transition into awareness" (L49). Sanchez et al. decoded *reported-seen vs reported-unseen* trials; what generalises across senses is a signature of *reportability*. The entry's Would-shift condition — that the decoding might "track report or task demands rather than the transition into awareness" — presupposes there is a transition into awareness distinct from the onset of report, which is exactly what he denies and what the Global Neuronal Workspace denies. On the workspace view, tracking report *is* tracking the transition, so the entry's stated defeater is the rival's description of the same fact. He would add that "the ownership term the broadcast under-describes" (L49) is a user-illusion the broadcast *produces*: availability-to-the-system plus a self-model yields the appearance of availability-to-someone, and the entry names no theory that says so.

### The Quantum Skeptic
Tegmark has little purchase here and the entry earns that: it makes no quantum claim, cites Tenet 2 nowhere, and holds the significance tier at D. His residual objection is about scale and register: a millisecond-scale MEG signature is evidence about cortical dynamics, and an entry that calls it "the operational shape of the brain/mind distinction" is using neuroscience as illustration for a metaphysics it admits the data cannot reach. The honest position is one the entry already states; he would ask why the significance tier is in a positions register at all if it is "probative of nothing".

### The Many-Worlds Defender
Deutsch is not engaged by this entry (no Tenet 4 dependency, no branching claim). One glancing point: the entry's "what one subject contributes" (L49) presupposes a determinate single subject per perceiving system, which the Map's own P-SC2 books as an unpaid debt (no pairing rule) and P-I3 says is not readable off physical structure. The entry does not depend on that debt, but it borrows the phenomenology of "one subject" without citing where the Map owes the account.

### The Empiricist
Popper would credit the architecture tier with genuine Would-shift conditions (replication failure, a shared distance-sense mechanism, non-converging interoception) and then note that one of them is not counterfactual. Cross-modal recruitment of visual cortex in early blindness (Sadato et al. 1996), the metamodal organisation hypothesis (Pascual-Leone & Hamilton 2001), and task-selective, sensory-independent organisation (Amedi and colleagues) are a standing literature on distance senses sharing extraction machinery; the apex's own Sanchez paragraph reports "visual-cortex activity helping predict whether a near-threshold sound was consciously perceived". The entry's "textbook" high credence for the negative half survives this only under a reading it does not state — that modality-specificity is claimed of *transduction and early cortical representation*, not of every stage of extraction. On the significance tier he would say the shift condition ("a form a broadcast account could not redescribe") has no criterion for non-redescribability and the entry concedes the two isolations in hand "are each redescribable"; a condition nothing could meet is a decoration, and the honest label is the D already assigned.

### The Buddhist Philosopher
Nagarjuna would read the vestibular case as the Map making his argument for him: the "spatial frame of the self" is, by the entry's own account, a product of multisensory integration with "nowhere independent to converge from", and its failure modes (depersonalisation, the out-of-body experience) are the self-frame coming apart into its conditions. An owner who is constructed from integrated signals and dissolves when they fail is dependently arisen, not a contributor. The entry's "what one subject contributes" reifies the owner at the exact point where its own evidence shows the owner being assembled.

## Critical Issues

### Issue 1: The positive half is over-generalised from three senses to "across senses"
- **File**: `obsidian/positions/perception-and-the-interface.md`
- **Location**: L49 (Asserts, architecture tier): "across vision, audition, chemosensation, touch and proprioception the brain-side machinery … is modality-specific … while the transition into awareness carries a supramodal signature that decodes across senses"
- **Problem**: The sentence lists five senses and then says the signature "decodes across senses", which a reader takes as the same five. The cited support is a single study (the entry's own calibration note says so) that decoded across tactile, visual and auditory near-threshold trials; the apex is careful — "decodes across vision, hearing, and touch" (apex L126, L48). Chemosensation and proprioception have no cross-decoding evidence anywhere in the wing. The register entry is therefore stronger than the article it registers, which inverts the register's purpose.
- **Severity**: Medium
- **Recommendation**: "…carries a supramodal signature that decodes across vision, hearing and touch (the three senses so far tested)". Roughly +6 words.

### Issue 2: The architecture tier states a report-contrast in phenomenal vocabulary, so its own defeater coincides with the rival's reading
- **File**: `obsidian/positions/perception-and-the-interface.md`
- **Location**: L49 "the transition into awareness carries a supramodal signature"; L52 (Would shift if) "or were shown to track report or task demands rather than the transition into awareness, which would remove the positive half"
- **Problem**: Epistemic/metaphysical equivocation in the sense of the evidential-status discipline. What Sanchez measured is the contrast between trials reported detected and trials reported undetected — an *access/report* marker. Calling it "the transition into awareness" is a phenomenal reading. The entry then lists "tracks report … rather than the transition into awareness" as a condition that would *retire* the positive half — but on the Global Neuronal Workspace reading the entry itself names as the strongest rival, tracking report is what the transition into (access) awareness consists in. So the condition either (a) never fires, because report-tracking is compatible with the tier as the rival reads it, or (b) fires only if "awareness" means phenomenal awareness — which is significance-tier vocabulary that has leaked into the architecture tier and would fail the entry's own cross-vocabulary-translatability test (project doc L49). Either way the architecture tier is not stated in the rival-neutral form the two-tier discount requires.
- **Severity**: High (it is the tier the entry grades B and calls "cross-vocabulary-robust")
- **Recommendation**: State the positive half in access vocabulary — "the contrast between reported and unreported near-threshold stimuli carries a supramodal signature" — and rewrite the Would-shift clause to the confound that would genuinely retire it: the decoding tracking *task demands or motor preparation for report* rather than the seen/unseen contrast itself. Keep "transition into awareness" for the significance tier, where it belongs. Net length roughly neutral.

### Issue 3: The brain-side retirement condition is framed as counterfactual while a live literature already bears on it
- **File**: `obsidian/positions/perception-and-the-interface.md`
- **Location**: L48 "high for the architecture tier, whose negative half is textbook sensory neuroanatomy"; L52 "a distance sense were shown to share its brain-side extraction machinery with another, which would break the negative half of the architecture tier and retire the asymmetry as stated"
- **Problem**: The metamodal-organisation and cross-modal-plasticity literatures (Pascual-Leone & Hamilton 2001; Sadato et al. 1996; task-selective sensory-independent organisation in the Amedi group's work) report distance senses sharing cortical extraction machinery under deprivation and, on the metamodal reading, by default. The apex's own evidence paragraph (L70) reports other-modality primary cortex contributing to the supramodal signature. - **Severity**: Medium
- **Recommendation**: Qualify the negative half to transduction and primary representation, and add one clause to Would-shift naming the metamodal literature as *not* triggering it because the shared machinery is downstream of modality-specific transduction. Roughly +35 words. Any citation added must be verified at publisher of record per P-M3; the three names above are given here from memory as leads, not as verified references.

## Counterarguments to Address

### "Modality-specific brain side" (architecture tier, negative half)
- **Current content says**: the brain-side extraction machinery is modality-specific across the five distance senses, "textbook sensory neuroanatomy".
- **A critic would argue**: transduction is modality-specific; cortical extraction is not, on the metamodal reading, and demonstrably not under deprivation. Sanchez's other-modality-cortex recruitment is the apex's own datum for this.
- **Suggested response**: restrict the negative half to transduction and primary representation and say so; the asymmetry survives in that form and the Would-shift condition becomes real again (Issue 3).

### "Probative of nothing" as an immunity clause
- **Current content says**: the significance tier is "consonant with the interface and probative of nothing", grade D.
- **A critic would argue**: a reading that is by construction probative of nothing cannot be shifted by evidence either — the entry admits the two isolations in hand are redescribable and gives no criterion for a non-redescribable one. This is the over-concession pattern: "probative of nothing" reads as humility but functions as insulation.
- **Suggested response**: name what a non-redescribable isolation would look like (a within-modality ownership dissociation that persists with the self-model intact and the broadcast intact — i.e. one the self-model rival must also fail on), or accept that the significance tier has discriminability *none* rather than *indirect* and say the position is held on argument alone.

### "One subject"
- **Current content says**: the mind-side family is "what one subject contributes".
- **A critic would argue**: the Map registers that it has no pairing rule (P-SC2) and that subject boundaries are not readable off structure (P-I3); "one subject" per perceiving system is assumed, not earned.
- **Suggested response**: one clause in Depends-on acknowledging that the count of contributing subjects is fixed phenomenologically here and owed under P-SC2; no verdict changes.
