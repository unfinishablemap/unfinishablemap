## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

- **Alter & Pereboom (2023) — source-fidelity error (fixed).** The 07-23 pass had the SEP entry "survey the resulting fragmentation — physicalist, Russellian, and dual-aspect variants sharing only the structuralist premise". Checked against the live entry (plato.stanford.edu/entries/russellian-monism/, substantive revision 4 July 2023): the entry's variants are **Russellian physicalism, Russellian panpsychism, Russellian panprotopsychism**; the string "dual-aspect" does not occur in it (0 hits); "Russellian" is not a *variant* of Russellian monism; and structuralism about physics is one of *three* core theses (with realism about quiddities and quidditism about consciousness) — moreover §4.1 is where Alter & Pereboom treat structuralism as the thesis Russellian monists *need not* accept. So the sentence attributed to the source a taxonomy it does not give and inverted the one thesis it singles out. The list appears to have been lifted from the outer reviewer's own gloss ("physicalist, panpsychist, panprotopsychist and dual-aspect approaches") and garbled. Rewritten to: "survey the resulting spread — Russellian physicalism, panpsychism, and panprotopsychism, agreeing that quiddities underlie physical structure and matter to consciousness while disagreeing over what they are, with even the structuralist premise about physics now treated as dispensable". The sentence's point (the "monist" label spans divergent positions) survives intact.
