## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

- **Chalmers-McQueen said to breach Constraint 1 "by design" (L83) and to "modify Born statistics at the ensemble level" (L103). FALSE, introduced today.** The primary text (arXiv:2105.02314, published in Gao ed. 2022) says collapse is "brought about according to the Born rule". In the CSL gambler's-ruin picture, the probability that a branch "wins" "is determined by its initial squared amplitude according to the Born rule". Φ sets *whether and how fast* collapse happens, not *which* outcome. The authors mention, and say they do not find "especially attractive", a stronger variant in which agentive collapses are "not entirely constrained by the Born probabilities". **Fix:** the model still sits outside the corridor, now for the correct reasons. It replaces unitary evolution with stochastic collapse dynamics that carry their own empirical signatures (the authors concede simple versions are falsified by the quantum Zeno effect), and CSL energy non-conservation bears on Constraint 5 (this sentence was kept). Only the non-endorsed variant would breach Constraint 1. The L103 parenthetical was corrected to match. The outside-the-corridor classification survives; only the stated reason was wrong. The sibling `topics/mathematical-structure-of-the-consciousness-physics-interface` L89/L156 says "outside-corridor" without a Born-breach claim and is left as is.
- **Inline ↔ References orphans (long-standing).** Torres Alegre (2025) was cited inline with no References entry. Chalmers & McQueen were named as a dated framework with no entry. **Fixed:** both entries were added and verified, and the list was re-alphabetised and renumbered. Atmanspacher, Stapp and Tonetto have no dated inline cite that lacks an entry (Tonetto is present). Those framework names are not new orphans; the 2026-06-04 review accepted them.

### Medium Issues Found

- **MGM (2019) premise list mis-stated.** It read "compositional neutrality, and measurement uniqueness". The Nature Communications abstract names "unitary quantum mechanics" plus "the assumption that ensembles on finite-dimensional Hilbert spaces are characterized by finitely many parameters", with partition-independence as the method. The finite-parameter premise is the one the Kent/MGM 2025 exchange is about, so leaving it out made the new reply sentence hard to follow. **Fixed** in §1, and the reply sentence was tightened to "pure-state and finite-dimensionality postulates", which matches the MGM 2025 abstract. **Sibling propagation queued (P2):** the same gloss appears at `topics/mathematical-structure-of-the-consciousness-physics-interface` L60 and `voids/interface-formalization-void` L65. The void also still says "demonstrated ... *unique*" without the Kent challenge.
