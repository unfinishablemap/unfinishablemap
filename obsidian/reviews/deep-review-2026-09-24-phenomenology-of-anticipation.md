## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

- **Misattribution (Laukkonen et al. 2025)**: The article said the authors "propose that consciousness arises precisely at the interface between continuous sensory perception and discrete, counterfactual policy selection." In the paper, that sentence describes "A complimentary view (cf. Whyte and Smith, 2021; Whyte et al., 2024)" that the authors cite. It is not their thesis. **Fixed**: the clause is removed.
- **Overstated position strength**: The article said "Three conditions are jointly sufficient for experience, they argue", and made "the recursion *is* the experience" their claim. The paper's abstract says "three conditions implying that [active inference can model consciousness]". Its introduction says "three conditions that seem necessary for consciousness". "Sufficient" appears nowhere as a claim about the three conditions; in fact the paper says the global model is "necessary but not sufficient" without epistemic depth. **Fixed**: the article now says the authors offer three conditions that "seem necessary", and gives the identity claim ("the recursion *is* the experience") to the functionalist reading the theory invites. The Map's reply is unchanged, because it answers that stronger reading.
- **Family resolution**: The same two defects appeared word for word in [[predictive-processing-and-dualism]] L82, which is where the refine-draft text came from. The same correction went there, and it is length-negative (4892 → 4861). The old wording also survives in `research/predictive-processing-consciousness-2026-01-14.md` L99, but that note quotes the sentence without attributing it to Laukkonen, so it was left alone.
- **Inline cite without a reference**: "Frischhut" was cited in the body but had no References entry. **Fixed**: added Frischhut, A. (2026), *Awareness without Time?*, Philosophical Quarterly 76(1):127–150, doi 10.1093/pq/pqae081, in the same form as [[non-temporal-consciousness]].
