## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)
The wing's most Chalmers-honest move is the **Relocation Objection** [[attention-as-interface]] runs against itself: the interface hypothesis "does not explain *why* phenomenal attention exists," and its value lies in "narrowing where the explanatory gap sits — from 'consciousness and the brain' to 'consciousness and attentional selection'." Stating plainly that your central hypothesis relocates rather than solves the hard problem, in the article that proposes it, is the explanatory gap taken seriously from the inside. The AST article keeps the same discipline pointed outward: "it explains access without explaining phenomenality" is exactly the phenomenal/access distinction doing its proper work.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)
[[attention-and-the-consciousness-interface]] gives Stapp his mechanism and then refuses to give him more than the evidence licenses. Its Tegmark paragraph is the corpus's best decoherence engagement: it grants that Tegmark's numbers exclude "any interface mechanism that requires brain-wide quantum coherence on cognitively-relevant timescales," states the surviving short-coherence integrationist response, and concedes that this response requires quantum-ordered ion-channel subsystems "neither of which has been demonstrated" — the mechanism's viability "presently rides on the short-coherence integrationist response rather than on any positive evidence." A quantum-interactionist wing that prices its own physics debt this precisely is worth more to the programme than one that cites Hagan et al. and moves on.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)
[[phenomenology-of-attention-to-absence]] extends what-is-it-like analysis to a domain physical description cannot even parametrise: the felt Pierre-lessness of the café, the silence that "does not subtract experience; it furnishes a positive experience whose content *is* the absence." And [[phenomenology-of-returning-attention]] finds first-person structure at the finest grain in the corpus — the unwilled surprise of noticing versus the authored effort of returning, two phenomenal characters inside one two-second event. Both articles treat phenomenal character as data with structure, not decoration.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)
The wing consistently treats attention as activity under varying constraint rather than a faculty that switches on and off: wandering and returning as mode transitions, habituation as the phenomenal field's ongoing contraction, willed and instructed attention as different processes rather than different intensities. [[attention-schema-theory]] gives Whitehead his own section and uses it correctly — the prehension alternative "preserves what AST gets right — attention's central role — while rejecting its eliminativist conclusion." This praise stays on the process axis; no evidential tier is at stake anywhere in the wing, and no article asks process-resonance to do evidential work.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)
[[attentional-economics]] is the wing's contribution to agency theory, and its central reframe is genuinely Kanean: "The decisive exercise of agency isn't the moment of choice but the *prior allocation of attention* that shapes what options are vivid" — self-forming action relocated from the dramatic decision to the quiet budget. The three-level structure (selection, witness mode, choosing the mode) gives ultimate-sourcehood a concrete architecture, and the evidence hub's attractor-seed analysis states with unusual candour exactly where libertarian freedom would have to live: in whether "the fluctuation tipping a balanced attractor contains a quantum-indeterminate component a conscious agent can bias."

### The Mysterian (McGinn)
The wing marks its own epistemic limits structurally, not rhetorically. The Epistemological Challenge section of the evidence hub notes that "reports about a disordered attention system are produced by that same disordered system"; the returning-attention article's transit analysis concedes real-time access to attentional capture is absent by construction; and the relevance-void discussion in [[attention-as-interface]] names a filter that "inherits the opacity it tries to dissolve." One sentence in that last discussion converts humility into over-claim — see the calibration concern below — but the structural mapping of what attention cannot see of itself is exemplary.

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)
**This persona's verdict is load-bearing for the review, and on five of seven articles it is enthusiastic.** No minimal-organism boundary case appears anywhere in the wing, so no five-tier placement is at stake; the discipline shows in the interface-evidence register, where the same possibility/probability distinction applies. Four passages deserve naming as corpus-wide models:

1. **The self-demoting test** in [[graduated-middle-path-valence-modulated-attention]]: the article specifies the abolition-by-clamp experiment that would confirm its stance, then states that "confirming the middle path is confirming the weaker thesis" — its own confirming outcome withholds the strong non-epiphenomenality claim. It then turns its own razor on its remaining daylight over the value-blind steelman: "a positive thesis that moves no measurable the design can reach is itself empirically inert. The daylight over the steelman is not bought by any evidential advantage; it rests, openly, on prior Map commitments." A framework claiming its preferred reading as framework-internal preference rather than clamp-earned discrimination is tenet-as-evidence-upgrade praise-worthily not done, executed twice in one article.
2. **The borrowed-risk admission** in [[attention-and-the-consciousness-interface]]: after constructing its first forward prediction (effort scaling with option-set entropy), the article demonstrates that active inference predicts the same scaling by a fully physicalist route, and concludes "Most of the model's falsification risk currently rides on this kind of borrowed risk rather than on specific quantitative claims of its own... the framework still owes a prediction that separates the interface reading from its strongest computational rival." Deriving your own best prediction and then downgrading it because the rival matches it is statistical honesty at the design stage, before any data exist.
3. **The Cai et al. (2024) scoping** in the same article: the mouse dopamine dissociation is explicitly confined to the two layers the paper tests — "their findings are equally consistent with Berridge incentive-salience without an added third layer... this proposal stands or falls with the broader interface argument, not with Cai et al."
4. **Underdetermination owned** in [[phenomenology-of-returning-attention]]: after laying out three accounts of the noticing, the article states that all three "predict similar neural and behavioural data," and that "the Map's preference is a stance taken within that underdetermination, not a result forced by the data." Its illusionism section ends "It remains pressure rather than proof."

On two articles the persona's verdict is instead the calibration concern below — and one of the flagged sentences (opacity-as-evidence) is the exact move this persona exists to catch: absence of introspective access to a mechanism presented as positive evidence for the mechanism's non-physicality.

## Content Strengths

### attention-and-the-consciousness-interface.md
- **Strongest point**: The determinism-horn analysis — granting that attractor dynamics plus seed noise constitute a complete physical symmetry-breaking story, then locating the Map's entire commitment in one falsifiable place: "That the tipping fluctuation contains a biasable quantum degree of freedom is the substantive, currently unproven commitment on which this horn of the interface reading rests."
- **Notable quote**: "If that seed is exhaustively classical and thermal, the interface has no purchase here and the attractor account is not merely compatible with the architecture but exhaustive of it."
- **Why it works**: The article's rivals are always presented at their strongest before the Map's reading is stated as a wager on a named unknown, and the clinical kaleidoscope converts four disorders into an architecture map without claiming any of them adjudicates the metaphysics.

### graduated-middle-path-valence-modulated-attention.md
- **Strongest point**: The four-row prediction table (strict value-blind / steelman / middle path / value-sensitive) that makes abolition-by-clamp the middle path's characteristic signature — and the demonstration that this signature demotes rather than vindicates the strong claim.
- **Notable quote**: "The whole point of specifying the coupling is that the same experiment which would confirm the middle path is the experiment that demotes it."
- **Why it works**: Every discipline the corpus has built — evidential-status, possibility/probability, distinction-without-empirical-difference — is applied to the article's own thesis, including against its own residual daylight over the steelman.

### attention-as-interface.md
- **Strongest point**: The phenomenal/computational distinction table, which blocks the "AI has attention, so AI might be conscious" inference in one move and gives the whole wing its working vocabulary.
- **Notable quote**: "Neither, by performing selection, thereby *experiences* selecting."
- **Why it works**: The distinction is put to work immediately — against AST's slide from modelling to experiencing, and in the Relocation Objection against the hypothesis itself. Three of its tenet-section sentences, however, lag the wing's register — below.

### attention-schema-theory.md
- **Strongest point**: Granting the rival its best reply before pressing. The bare regress objection is conceded as "not decisive," Graziano's map-is-not-mountainous response is stated fairly, and only then does the article relocate the objection to the step AST has not earned.
- **Notable quote**: "Relocating the demand from 'what observes the model' to 'what makes its operation felt' does not dissolve it."
- **Why it works**: The five-condition "What Would Challenge This View?" section specifies what would reverse the Map's own verdict — contemplative training confirming AST, the haecceity problem dissolving, fine-grained neural control of phenomenology — a falsifier list for a *critique*, which is rarer than a falsifier list for a thesis.

### attentional-economics.md
- **Strongest point**: The habituation boundary — the observation that the option-set for attentional allocation is not fixed but contracts as familiarity removes contents from the phenomenal field, so contemplative de-habituation *expands the economy itself* rather than optimising within it.
- **Notable quote**: "The meditator isn't just learning to focus more intensely; they're learning to *de-habituate*—to restore the familiar to the phenomenal field and thereby expand the attentional economy."
- **Why it works**: The economic framing generates non-obvious structure (liquidity, opportunity cost, capital constraints on deliberation via working memory) rather than relabelling familiar claims. Its evidential register on the bandwidth figure lags the wing — below.

### phenomenology-of-attention-to-absence.md
- **Strongest point**: The determinacy analysis — distinguishing experienced absence from mere ignorance by the expectation structure that makes only *this* absence phenomenally present.
- **Notable quote**: "Absence-perception is ignorance made phenomenally present by the structure of expectation."
- **Why it works**: The five-variety taxonomy (perceptual, auditory, temporal, social, cognitive) gives the phenomenon real grain, and the body grants the predictive-processing reply fairly ("This is fair as far as it goes") before locating the residue in phenomenal character. One tenet-section sentence forgets that concession — below.

### phenomenology-of-returning-attention.md
- **Strongest point**: The authorship asymmetry inside a single micro-event — noticing happens *to* you, returning is something *you do* — read as the bidirectional structure the tenets predict, with the underdetermination of the three accounts stated in full.
- **Notable quote**: "Richer confabulation is an awkward explanation for a capacity that becomes more precise under training."
- **Why it works**: The training gradient (gap shortens, noticing softens, effort releases) is deployed as pressure on illusionism at exactly the weight it can bear — "pressure rather than proof," with the framework boundary named.

### 1. attention-as-interface.md — three tenet-register sentences lag the wing (task generated)

1. **L222 (opacity as evidence)**: "That opacity is itself evidence for the structural-non-physical reading the Map prefers: a wholly-physical filter ought, in principle, to be inspectable; one whose operation cannot survive being looked at fits the dualist picture." The premise is one the corpus itself denies: the wing's own AST article documents the standard physicalist expectation that a control model "systematically omits the neural details, making its own basis incomprehensible to introspection" — physicalism predicts introspective opacity of mechanism; it does not predict first-person inspectability. Introspective opacity is compatible with both frameworks and evidence for neither; the article's own Relocation Objection register is the model for how this paragraph should end.
2. **L230 (Schwartz unhedged)**: "Schwartz's OCD neuroplasticity provides empirical grounding" — flat, where sibling [[attentional-economics]] carries the honest form: 18 participants, no independent replication, causal role of attentional effort versus behavioural change open.
3. **L232 (MWI differential-results)**: "Self-locating explains the *appearance* of efficacy but not the *differential results* of engaged versus passive attention" claims an in-framework victory: under MWI, engaged and passive attention have different physical correlates in every branch, so differential results have a within-branch story. The sibling standard is [[attention-and-the-consciousness-interface]]'s "the disagreement with MWI is a tenet commitment honestly noted, not a victory over MWI on its own terms."

Secondary: the frontmatter `description:` asserts declaratively ("Attention serves as the interface where consciousness influences the brain") what the article's own lead states conditionally.

### 2. attentional-economics.md — pre-discipline register on the bandwidth figure (task generated)

The article presents Zheng & Meister's 10 bits/s as "conscious bandwidth" throughout — the scarcity table (L43), "consciousness receives a tiny fraction—roughly 10 bits per second" (L48), "Conscious processing operates at ~10 bits/second" (L66), and the Minimal Quantum Interaction tenet section (L194). Both siblings have since installed the careful register: the figure "measures what conscious agents *do*, not what phenomenal attention processes internally" ([[attention-as-interface]]); it is "the throughput of the channel through which deliberate selection passes, not the bandwidth of the brain as a whole" ([[attention-and-the-consciousness-interface]]). The article was last deep-reviewed 2026-07-16, before those upgrades landed (07-25 through 08-08) — the same lags-its-own-wing pattern the 08-12 sleep review found. Additionally, L182 misstates the rival: "if consciousness were epiphenomenal, the correlation between felt effort and neural outcomes would be coincidental" — epiphenomenalism predicts exactly that correlation via a common neural cause; the honest sibling form is that the correlations "are compatible with weaker readings on which effort tracks underlying cognitive cost without itself doing causal work." The `description:` also asserts "attention training is metaphysically agency-enhancing" without the framework-conditional qualifier the body carries at every occurrence.

### 3. phenomenology-of-attention-to-absence.md — one tenet-section sentence refuted by its own body (task generated)

L91: "If consciousness were identical to physical processing, it would be difficult to explain how a physical system represents determinate absences, since there is no physical input corresponding to the absent object." The article's own expectation-violation section shows physical systems representing determinate absences — the MMN registers a missing sound "before consciousness does," and its Sartre section grants the functionalist reply that an unmatched prediction *is* an absence-representation ("This is fair as far as it goes"). The defensible claim, which the body already makes, concerns phenomenal character, not representation. A one-sentence re-register to the body's own formula; the article is also the wing's oldest-reviewed (deep review 2026-06-25), so the fix should not wait on the staleness cycle.

### High Priority

#### Hypnotic Suggestion: The Willed/Instructed Architecture's Missing Probe
- **Builds on**: The three-mode architecture (exogenous/instructed/willed) in [[attention-as-interface]] and [[structure-of-attention]]; the willed-attention neural signatures (frontal theta, bidirectional coherence).
- **Would address**: The corpus treats hypnosis only on the motor-agency side ([[volitional-control]]'s arm-levitation and Wegner discussion). The attention side is absent: posthypnotic suggestion can eliminate Stroop interference in highly hypnotisable subjects (Raz et al. 2005) — top-down attentional control with the willed phenomenology stripped. This is a natural dissection of the willed-attention bundle the wing treats as unitary: do the willed signatures track *internal initiation* or *felt authorship*? Suggestion-driven attention is instructed attention that outperforms willed attention on some tasks, which the three-mode table as written does not predict. Oakley & Halligan's programme frames hypnosis as precisely a cognitive-neuroscience instrument for attention and agency.
- **Estimated scope**: Section in [[attention-and-the-consciousness-interface]]'s clinical sequence (a fifth natural experiment) or in [[structure-of-attention]] — given the topics cap, prefer the section extension over a new page.
- **Tenet alignment**: Dualism and Bidirectional Interaction (which component of the willed bundle carries the mind-to-brain direction); Occam's Razor Has Limits (the "hypnosis is just compliance" simplicity claim versus the Stroop data).

- **Builds on**: The competition-selection architecture; the 3–4 selections/sec channel estimate in [[attention-and-the-consciousness-interface]]; [[attentional-economics]] names divided attention once, only to disclaim it.
