# Adversarial Referee Report — *The Post-Decoherence Selection Programme* (apex, rev. 2026-09-03)

## 1. TL;DR — Headline Verdict

The article is the most self-aware and least dishonest quantum-consciousness piece in the corpus, and it still fails as a *research programme* on its own terms: its five "components" are one framework wearing five hats (it says so), none of the recruited physics licenses outcome-selection by a mind (it mostly says so), and its own load-bearing claim — causally-robust, aggregate-undetectable, within-Born "selection" — has no worked toy model and no established amplification pathway (it says so). The dominant pathology is **confession-without-correction at industrial scale**: nearly every defect is named with precision and then banked as epistemic credit while every component and every optimistic section label is RETAINED in the same commit. The article's evidential labels (**Empirical / Open / Speculative**) are miscalibrated *upward* relative to the site's own better ladder (compatible / suggestive / discriminating) used in the `born-rule-and-the-consciousness-interface` neighbour: the borrowed physics is stamped **Empirical** and the dualist overlay silently inherits the glow. Net verdict: **REVISE-HARD**, with two components (weak-measurement-as-mechanism; contextuality-as-biological-mechanism) meriting **DEMOTE-TO-COHERENCE-ONLY** and the "selection is energetically free" claim meriting **DELETE** as an unargued free lunch that contradicts the article's own improper-mixture premise.

## 2. Dimensional Analysis

### 2.1 Physics-substance audit (the three costs)

**No-signalling — RELOCATED, not evaded.** Gisin's 1989 no-go theorem (N. Gisin, "Stochastic Quantum Dynamics and Relativity," *Helv. Phys. Acta* 62, 363–371, 1989) forces the conclusion that any nonlinear or outcome-biasing modification of quantum dynamics generically permits superluminal signalling; the recent re-analysis by Bielińska et al., "Superluminal signalling and chaos in nonlinear quantum dynamics," *New J. Phys.* (2025), doi:10.1088/1367-2630/adcae1 (arXiv:2412.20854), confirms it — verbatim, "In 1989 Nicolas Gisin proved a powerful no-go theorem, according to which nonlinear quantum dynamics would lead to superluminal signalling, violating Einstein's causality," and that under general conditions "two spacelike separated parties can effectuate statistical superluminal information transfer." The corridor's defence is that it preserves the *unconditioned* long-run marginal exactly, so the ensemble map stays linear and no signalling occurs. This is internally consistent — but it is exactly the relocation, not the evasion, of the cost: the single feature that saves no-signalling (Born-exact aggregate preservation) is the same feature that empties "selection" of operational content. Torres Alegre (2025, arXiv:2512.12636, v3 rev. 4 Feb 2026) sharpens the squeeze — *any* strictly convex/concave deviation enables steering-based signalling — so the corridor is pinned: a bias detectable in aggregate signals; a bias undetectable in aggregate is epiphenomenal. The article files this correctly as the bias-without-deviation dilemma but presents the no-signalling result as something the corridor "inherits as a structural rather than auxiliary commitment," which reads as a win when it is a constraint the proposal barely survives.

**Energy conservation — asserted free lunch, contradicts the article's own premise.** The article claims actualisation "requir[es] no energy injection, consistent with conservation law constraints." This engages *none* of the 2015–2025 collapse-model energy literature. Every physical spontaneous-localisation dynamics (GRW, CSL) generically produces a steady, unbounded increase in mean kinetic energy — a manifest violation of energy conservation — curable only by adding a dissipative finite-temperature noise field that physically exchanges energy with the system (Smirne & Bassi, "Dissipative Continuous Spontaneous Localization (CSL) model," *Sci. Rep.* 5:12518, 2015). "Mere selection among pre-existing decohered branches is energetically free" is only true if the branches are already-definite alternatives — i.e. a *proper* mixture. But the article insists (correctly, following d'Espagnat 1976, and in lockstep with its `improper-vs-proper-mixtures` and `born-rule` neighbours) that decoherence yields an *improper* mixture and that reading its elements as pre-existing outcomes is a "category error." The article cannot have it both ways: if actualisation is "an additional postulate beyond unitary quantum mechanics" [apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme/) (its words), it is a physical dynamical modification, and the collapse-model literature is unanimous that such modifications are not energetically free unless a physical noise field is posited — which reintroduces a coupling with energetic cost. The free-lunch claim is **DELETE**-grade until it engages this literature.

**Born-rule statistics — the deepest problem, correctly identified, wrongly labelled elsewhere.** If aggregate statistics are exactly preserved, "selection" is undetectable and arguably vacuous — the article concedes this as its "sharpest conceptual liability" (P-Q3, high confidence it is the strongest live challenge; P-Q10, high confidence no toy model exists). This is the honest core and it should govern the whole article. Yet the *menu* section stamps "the probabilities are fixed" as **[Empirical]** on the strength of Zurek's envariance (2005) — a derivation that is *contested*, not settled: Schlosshauer & Fine (2005, *Found. Phys.* 35:197–213) diagnose an assumed state-probability link, and Mertens & van Wezel (2023, *Entropy* 25(3):435) show envariance does not necessitate the Born rule for all measurements. The article's own `born-rule` sibling flags exactly this. Stamping it **[Empirical]** in the programme article is a calibration asymmetry.

### 2.2 Are einselection/contextuality/weak-values doing the assigned work?

- **Einselection selects a BASIS, not an OUTCOME.** The article now states this explicitly and correctly ("What einselection fixes is the *basis*: the mixture remains improper, and no element of the menu thereby obtains"). [apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme/) The basis/outcome conflation that would have been fatal is *avoided*. But it is replaced by confession-without-correction: the article admits "Zurek's framework does not contain a 'selector' role" and that Zurek "leans toward an Everettian reading," then still recruits redundancy/objectivity as the "heavy lifting" done for the selector. Zurek is the author of the "existential interpretation" (arXiv:0707.2832) — Everett-adjacent, explicitly not a dualist; his own writing (e.g. "Quantum Darwinism, Classical Reality, and the Randomness of Quantum Jumps") states that redundantly-recorded pointer states explain the *perception* of collapse and even remarks that a "full account of collapse involves 'consciousness', and may go beyond just mathematics or physics" — a speculative aside, not a selector role. The co-optation is disclosed but not firewalled.
- **Contextuality: the slide is flagged but the biological-scale claim is closed against them.** Kochen-Specker (1967) and Spekkens (2005) are correctly stated, and the article explicitly disowns the slide from "outcomes depend on context" to "a mind supplies the context" ("the Kochen-Specker result... mentions neither consciousness nor causation"). Good. But the load-bearing "practical" claim — that KS contextuality has operational grip at biological scale *after* decoherence — is effectively refuted, not open: einselection's whole job is to fix a pointer basis in which the relevant observables commute, and KS contextuality is a statement about *non-commuting* observables. Post-decoherence, the menu is a commuting classical register; KS bites on the pre-decoherence algebra, not the decohered mixture the programme says consciousness acts on. The section's **[Empirical]** header is therefore doubly wrong: the physics is a theorem (not empirical) and the consciousness-relevant claim is closer to refuted-at-biological-scale than open.
- **Weak measurement is a measurement technique on post-selected ensembles, not a mechanism.** The article recruits Aharonov-Albert-Vaidman weak values and the TSVF/two-time destiny vector as an "alternative mechanism." Two hard problems. First, it requires "the realist interpretation of weak values" and discloses this — but does not engage the strongest deflationary challenge: Ferrie & Combes, "How the Result of a Single Coin Toss Can Turn Out to be 100 Heads," *PRL* 113:120404 (2014), which argues anomalous weak values "are not inherently quantum, but rather a purely statistical feature of pre- and post-selection with disturbance," nor the vigorous counter-exchange (Hofmann, Iinuma & Shikano, arXiv:1410.7126, "the disturbance in properly performed weak measurements is always low enough to completely rule out the effect"; Vaidman; Dressel). Citing weak-value realism as settled while the "genuinely quantum vs statistical artefact" debate remains live is a staleness/evidential-independence gap. Second, Aharonov & Gruss's (2005) destiny vector is a *deterministic, physical cosmological boundary condition chosen to recover Born statistics* — the two-time interpretation is local and deterministic in a two-time sense. Repackaging it as a non-physical mind supplying an *open* boundary condition is an epistemic-to-metaphysical slide the article labels "packaging"; but post-selection is a data-analysis protocol (you keep a sub-ensemble after the fact), not a physical mechanism by which anything chooses. As a *mechanism*, this component is **DELETE**; as a coherence-level analogy, DEMOTE.
- **Stochastic amplification: individually real, jointly unproven, biologically unestablished.** Mainen & Sejnowski (1995) and Thura & Cisek (2014, *Neuron* 81(6):1401–1416: "PMd and M1 activity signals decision commitment about 280 ms before movement"; "The neural signature of commitment is specific to volitional decisions") are correctly cited and real. But no experiment demonstrates the twelve-order-of-magnitude chain end-to-end — the article concedes this. The Fisher Posner-molecule route (M.P.A. Fisher, "Quantum cognition: The possibility of processing with nuclear spins in the brain," *Annals of Physics* 362:593–602, 2015, identifying Ca₉(PO₄)₆ Posner clusters as the candidate protecting neural qubits; experimental testing funded via a Heising-Simons Foundation grant from 2019), the only concrete quantum-to-neural amplification proposal, remains experimentally unconfirmed as of 2026: the existence, abundance, and in vivo lifetime of Posner-like clusters are still undetermined. "Plausible causal pathway" overstates what "each stage is individually established" licenses.

### 2.3 The standing blocking gate: predictive processing / active inference

**The article does not engage predictive processing, active inference, or Laukkonen, Friston & Chandaria 2025 ("A beautiful loop: An active inference theory of consciousness," *Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews* 176:106296) at all** — the thread runs on a separate concepts page and is not bridged here. This is a co-optation-firewall and evidential-independence failure, because the Beautiful Loop offers a fully non-dualist account of precisely the article's explananda: "Bayesian binding" is an explicit *selection-for-consciousness* mechanism (inferential competition to enter the world model — "Only the inferences that coherently reduce long-term uncertainty win"), and its precision-control hyper-model handles agency/commitment without any quantum interface. The amplification section's drift-diffusion commitment point (Thura-Cisek) is exactly what active inference models as precision-weighted policy selection. A physicalist rival that dissolves "selection" and "commitment" with no quantum bias is left uncited by an article whose entire architecture is a quantum story about selection and commitment.
