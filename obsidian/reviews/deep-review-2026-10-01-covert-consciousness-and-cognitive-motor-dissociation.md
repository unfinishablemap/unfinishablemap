## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

All four are fidelity or calibration errors that can be fixed inside the Map's framework. None is a disagreement at the framework boundary.

- **Internal contradiction (Occam's paragraph).** "The size of the gap is measured rather than speculative" contradicted the lead and §How Detection Fails, which say the true prevalence is unknown and published rates "behave more like floors than estimates". **Fixed**: "the gap has a measured floor rather than a speculative one."
- **Over-reach, Bidirectional Interaction paragraph.** "CMD *shows* cognition … a task *selected* and maintained" turned the inference that §From Activation to Consciousness marks as an inference into a demonstration. It also asserted intentional selection, the very thing Klein (2017), cited in that section, withholds. A reviewer who accepts the tenets would still flag it. **Fixed**: "CMD is strong evidence of cognition proceeding without motor output: a command understood and a task maintained."
- **Dropped qualifier (Nachev & Husain 2007).** The quotation stopped at "…not sufficient evidence for the associated behaviour". That turned the authors' conditional into a categorical claim. The source sentence continues "unless one has also shown that the same activation cannot occur without it." **Fixed**: the conditional is restored as a second grep-verified span, with the splice word "unless" kept outside the quotation marks. It also ties the section to §What Would Discriminate, which asks for an independent criterion.
- **Attribution scope and strength (Laigaard et al. 2026).** The article said the authors read the whole gradient (aetiology plus MCS− over UWS) "as showing" that "preserved large-scale cortical connectivity is a key substrate…". In the full text (§4.1) that sentence is about the **aetiology** result only, and the verb is "supports the notion that". The authors tie MCS− to "partially preserved frontoparietal network function" instead. **Fixed**: "Laigaard et al. take the aetiology result to support the notion that…".
