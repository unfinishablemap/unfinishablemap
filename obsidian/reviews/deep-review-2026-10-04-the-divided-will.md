## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

1. **Internal inconsistency on O2** (§What a Unitary Selector Must Add). The text said O2's auxiliary, a low-bandwidth fallible selector, is "motivated independently". It said the same of O4–O5's auxiliaries, which it scored "roughly even". Yet it scored O1–O3 "against" the selector, O2 included. The missing premise is Ainslie's: generic fallibility does not predict lapses confined to *particular kinds* of choice. **Fixed** (+15) with the clause "though fallibility alone does not predict lapses confined to particular kinds of choice".
2. **Indexicality given as an independent ground for the posit** (L114, "keep the posit on its independent grounds of agent causation and indexicality"). This contradicts tenets.md L121: the indexical objection *presupposes* the non-deflationary "I", the Map endorses that "I" on independent grounds from the agency cluster, and "the indexical question is meaningful" is itself a posited claim. It also contradicts L123: the dependency is "logical interdependence, not mutual evidential support". The sentence said the Kant article "requires" this. **Fixed**: the posit is kept "on the independent ground the [[tenets]] page names, agent causation's need for a persisting subject, rather than on felt unity, which the Kant article rules out". The new wording holds both before and after the queued Kant P2 (pessimistic-2026-10-04 Priority 1b).
3. **"Divided control" asserted as a fact about akrasia** (L112, "Akrasia is the case of phenomenal unity with divided control"). That is the partitioned reading. The Map's own candidate-relative selector reads the case otherwise, and the argument needs only compatibility. **Fixed**: "Akrasia thus shows phenomenal unity coexisting with what partitioned models read as divided control". The same wording sits on a seam page (see §Seam verdicts) and is queued as a P3.
4. **Overclaim in the lead**: "the one discriminating result in the literature". The search behind this was bounded: WebSearch was unavailable to the research note and the writer. The body also calls Ainslie's scoring "advocacy rather than an independent test", but the lead called it a test without qualification. **Fixed**: "his own scoring backed by two small independent studies, is the one discriminating result this survey found".
