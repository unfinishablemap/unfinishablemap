The convergence rests on the ChatGPT and Claude legs.

## Method Notes

- **Three reviews, two pages, one clean leg each.** See the opening section. The next time a reuse leg cannot fetch the commissioned page, the collected file's `subject_articles` will again name a page the reviewer did not read; a synthesis that trusted that field would have counted three reviewers on one page here.
- **The strongest signal is at the level of the cluster.** ChatGPT's collect run concluded that "sibling pages are already better calibrated on most points, which is itself the finding". Claude's concluded that the concept page "lags concessions its siblings have already made". Two reviewers, reading two different pages, found the same thing about how the Map propagates its own corrections: a concession lands on the specialist page and the hub pages keep the older, stronger sentence. Both recommend a cross-page check (ChatGPT's methodology item 31; Claude's items 1 and 7). No task was minted, because the remedy is a pipeline decision for the operator.
- **Reference hygiene on two pages out of two.** Each clean reviewer found named authorities with no reference entry and entries with no use in the body, on the page it read. Both recommend an automated body-to-reference check (ChatGPT item 30; Claude item 4, which its collect run declined in its blocking form). Two pages is a small sample, and both were chosen for review, but the hit rate is worth an operator's look.
- **Disputed claims were removed before clustering.** That is what turned the quantum-precedent overlap from an apparent two-reviewer finding into a false one, and what reduced the selectability overlap to a part of finding 2.
- **No tasks were merged or deleted.** Every overlap in this cycle is between tasks that edit different files or different sentences. Convergence is expressed as cross-references and run order.
- **Run order after this pass.** Topic page: adaptation and detectability (P1), zombie burden (P1), illusionism (P1, was fifth), teleology (P2), IIT and Russellian monism (P2). Concept page: amplification (P1, the only task that frees words), zombies and illusionism (P1), quantum grading (P2), citations (P2, adds reference lines and must follow the amplification task), "untenable" (P2, length-neutral). Across pages the queue interleaves them by priority, so the concept-page zombie and illusionism task runs after the topic-page zombie task and before the topic-page illusionism task; the task notes are written for that order.
- **Length.** Topic page 2,957 words against a hard threshold of 4,000. Concept page 3,496 against 3,500, with the gate firing at 3,500: every concept-page task must net to zero or less except the citation task, which spends words the amplification task frees.
