## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

The wing takes the explanatory gap seriously in the only way that survives a competent opponent: by engaging the physicalist who grants it. Neural-correlates L152 sets the Type-A physicalist ("denies there was ever a real gap") apart from the Type-B physicalist ("grants it and predicts it will persist"). It then holds the Map's reading at "a posited stance, conceded by neither physicalist, that stays provisional until dualism is shown to explain the gap better than the Type-B account." The IBE page makes the same move the organising principle of its first reply (L64, L80). Chalmers would praise the refusal to count the gap's persistence as evidence: falsifier 4 says "persistence decides nothing, since Type-B physicalism predicts it too" (NCC L142). He would also note that the page using his own NCC definition (Chalmers 2000, L71) now leaves his master argument against phenomenal concepts on the page that owns it, unrestated.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

Stapp would praise neural-correlates' closing falsifier paragraph (L144). It names where the Map is exposed: "P-Q9's conditional channel, untested in the brain, where neural outcomes would track a preregistered intention beyond the measured physical state, something physical closure forbids". It also names the mechanism debt (P-Q10, P-Q3). The IBE page states the same test as a three-way disconfirmer: raised by intention-conditioned departures from the Born marginal, unchanged by unconditioned or external-RNG nulls, lowered by well-powered conditional nulls (L82). That is the testable interface Stapp spent a career asking for. The hub says the opposite (L54, L122; consistency verdict (e) below).

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

The covert-consciousness page refuses the third-person shortcut both ways. It is "decisive against inferring absent experience from absent behaviour" (L33), and it also "forbids asserting any prevalence of hidden awareness" (L62). Nagel would praise neural-correlates' contemplative section, which keeps "what is experienced (which NCCs track)" apart from "*that* it is experienced (which NCCs don't straightforwardly capture)" (L128). He would also praise its report-dependence ladder (L178–187), which turns "NCC science presupposes the first person" from rhetoric into an audit of which link in the validation chain trusts testimony.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

The material is thin for this persona, and that is correct for an evidence wing. Two passages earn its praise. The IBE page concedes that ranking dualism first "is itself an inference to the best explanation over a lot that omits Russellian monism and idealism" (L64). That keeps the non-substance options Whitehead cares about in the candidate set. The hub's "federation of domains ... connected by bridge laws" (L116) avoids crude substance dualism. **Constraint check**: neither passage is used to move any claim up the ladder. The IBE lot remark *lowers* the Map's own abductive confidence (L84), so this persona's praise lands on openness, not on tier. Tenet-coherent, not evidence-elevating.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

Kane wants the conscious posit to do work, and the wing tells him exactly what that would cost. IBE: "the non-idleness of the dualist posit is owed, not shown" (L74). The only reply that could upgrade the tier "is a demonstration that the dualist posit earns predictive success the physical model does not" (L94). Neural-correlates draws the access/phenomenal line Kane's opponents exploit: functional dissociations show "conscious access makes a causal difference ... whether phenomenal experience does is left open, since an accompaniment reading predicts the same dissociations" (L156). Kane would rather have the honest debt than a forged receipt.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

The wing touches no minimal-organism claim, but it does make one clinical consciousness attribution, and the restraint there is the review's strongest single finding. The covert page reaches "strong evidence of probable consciousness, reached through a neural marker" (L70). It builds the false-negative case from the clinicians' own figures (38% of bedside responders detected, kappa 0.02–0.04; L60). It also refuses the misdiagnosis-rate shortcut (L72–76). Three non-upgrades deserve naming:

- Neural-correlates holds covert consciousness at "lends Bidirectional Interaction no support" (L156), although it is the most quotable datum on the page.
- IBE names the discipline's rule in prose ("a tenet may remove a defeater but may not upgrade the evidence forbids pretending otherwise", L74) and concedes a likelihood advantage to the opponent (L58).

**Where this persona dissents** is the parity vocabulary in the covert lead and reply (L33 "explain them equally well", L88 "standing, and matched"). By IBE's own L58/L70, an interface reading that reaches the CMD pattern through an output-side lesion fitted to it *accommodates* the pattern. Production *explains* it from its core. Parity on consistency is not parity on likelihood. Under the diagnostic test, a tenet-accepting reviewer would flag it. The slip runs in the Map's favour, so it is a calibration finding (Priority item 3), not a praise.

## Consistency Verdict {#consistency-verdict}

**(b) Accommodates versus explains: covert slips.** IBE: physical models "unify ... evidence that a dualist ontology merely accommodates" (L32).
