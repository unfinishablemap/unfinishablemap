## Overall assessment

The article is substantially better calibrated than a straightforward advocacy piece. It explicitly distinguishes a methodological claim from a metaphysical one, describes the dualist interpretation as the least well-supported classification, and acknowledges that indirect methods can constrain the target. Those are important strengths. [voids/minimal-consciousness-void](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/minimal-consciousness-void/)

Its central conclusion nevertheless remains too strong. The evidence supports something like:

> Deliberate, concurrent, reflective introspection can perturb some candidate low-content experiences and cannot provide an unmediated record of their prior phenomenology.

It does **not** establish that:

- every act of attention, metacognition or memory necessarily adds phenomenal content;
- there is an actual conscious state below every observable one;
- that state leaves no report-accessible trace;
- consciousness has no lower threshold;
- the inaccessible state is a common lower bound across humans, animals and physical systems;
- or ordinary attentional modulation evidences nonphysical-to-physical causation.

The bibliography contains no clearly fabricated source, and the explicit source quotations I checked are accurate. The more serious problem is **source-to-claim fit**: several genuine sources are made to carry conclusions stronger than those they state. There are also two material factual or attribution errors:

1. Tononi 2004 does not support the article’s claim that the relevant photodiode qualifies as conscious.
2. The claim that cessation-EEG research as a whole is based on one practitioner is false or, at minimum, seriously overbroad.

My overall verdict is therefore:

- **Bibliographic existence and basic metadata:** strong, with minor errors.
- **Quotation accuracy:** strong.
- **Inferential use of sources:** mixed.
- **Falsifiability of the central thesis:** weak.
- **Currency against 2025–2026 literature:** inadequate.
- **Tenet discipline:** improved in disclaimers, but not maintained through the implications section.
- **Cross-site integration:** extensive linking, but inconsistent propagation of corrections and caveats.

## 1. The main argument does not establish a structural impossibility

The headline says introspection “**guarantees the measurement is too high**”. Later, the article says it “**earns the epistemic claim outright**”. Both formulations exceed the evidence. [voids/minimal-consciousness-void](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/minimal-consciousness-void/)

### 1.1 Cognitive enrichment is not automatically phenomenal enrichment

Attention, metacognition, memory encoding, report preparation and phenomenal content are different variables. Recruiting more neural or cognitive machinery does not by itself show that the phenomenal target becomes richer. Attention can:

- change processing without changing the attended phenomenal property;
- select an already present feature;
- alter later access or memory rather than the original experience;
- or operate partly independently of conscious visibility.

Experimental work has produced factorial dissociations between attention and consciousness, including cases in which they have opposing effects. That does not prove that introspection is nonreactive, but it defeats the article’s unqualified identification of attending with phenomenally enriching. [PubMed Central (PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2889341/)

The article needs a bridging premise:

> For every candidate minimal conscious episode, any process sufficient to make it introspectively available necessarily adds a phenomenally instantiated feature to that same episode.

No cited source establishes this universal.

### 1.2 It conflates the target experience with later representations of it

Suppose a minimal event causes a memory, neural trace or later report. The memory and report may be structurally elaborate without that elaboration having been part of the original experience. A footprint can contain more investigatory information than the event that made it without retroactively enriching the event.

The argument therefore needs to distinguish:

1. complexity intrinsic to the target episode;
2. simultaneous access machinery;
3. causal consequences of the episode;
4. later reconstructed representations.

The article presently moves from the obvious complexity of stages 2–4 to a claim about stage 1.

### 1.3 The regress is conditional, not demonstrated

The article’s regress works only if every higher-order act is itself incorporated into a single total phenomenal state and the relevant measure of minimality is the complexity of that total state. Neither assumption is defended.

A theorist could instead hold that attention targets a component without the higher-order machinery itself appearing phenomenally, or that the inquiry concerns minimal **first-order content**, not the minimum complexity of the entire subject’s cognitive state. Higher-order theories are especially relevant here because their ordinary higher-order representations are generally posited as unconscious. The Map’s own higher-order-theory article says introspection introduces a third level rather than being identical to the higher-order state that allegedly makes the first-order state conscious. [concepts/higher-order-theories](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/higher-order-theories/)

### 1.4 The conclusion is self-sealing

The argumentative structure is:

1. Any state observed is disqualified as enriched.
2. Any reportable state is disqualified as nonminimal.
3. A still simpler state is inferred beneath it.
4. Its nonobservability is then treated as confirmation of the inference.

That makes failure to find the state expected under the theory, while finding a candidate also disqualifies it. Unless the article supplies possible observations that would count against the enrichment thesis, the proposed void is protected against empirical correction.

The problem is particularly clear in the claim that the real minimum is always below the observable one. There may instead be:

- no unique minimum;
- several incomparable minima;
- a lowest state that remains weakly reportable;
- a constitutive access threshold;
- or no phenomenality below the observed state.

The article acknowledges some of these possibilities later but still awards its own epistemic conclusion unconditional status.

### 1.5 Better instruments could help

The claim that instrumental improvement cannot help is justified only for **concurrent reflective first-person inspection**, and perhaps not even universally there. The article itself concedes that no-report paradigms, retrospective methods and third-person measures can constrain the target. [voids/minimal-consciousness-void](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/minimal-consciousness-void/)

Recent work reviews emerging covert measures based on ocular, autonomic, respiratory and cardiac signals while emphasizing the unresolved problem of separating conscious from unconscious processing. These methods do not provide a consciousness meter, but their continuing improvement could substantially narrow the hypothesis space. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40410390/)

The defensible claim is therefore not that better instruments cannot help, but that no foreseeable instrument provides a direct, theory-neutral readout of phenomenal minimality.

## 2. “Minimal consciousness” changes meaning throughout the article

The article treats several distinct lower-bound problems as though they converged on one basement:

1. **Minimal phenomenal content:** the least content in one adult human experience.
2. **Minimal human global state:** the weakest or simplest condition in which a human remains conscious.
3. **Minimal reportable state:** the weakest state accessible to memory or report.
4. **Minimal conscious system:** the least complex organism or artefact capable of any experience.
5. **Minimal degree:** the smallest positive amount on a graded consciousness measure.
6. **Minimal psychometric profile:** the low-dimensional or factor structure of reports about contemplative experiences.
7. **Minimal physical substrate:** the least physical organization satisfying a theory such as IIT or GNWT.

Evidence about one does not automatically constrain the others. An adult meditator with a mature brain entering a content-poor state is not a model of a simple organism that never possessed the suppressed cognitive machinery. The linked *Minimal Consciousness* article correctly identifies that disanalogy, but the audited article continues to combine the human phenomenology, animal distribution and physical-substrate questions in its lower-bound argument. [concepts/minimal-consciousness](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/minimal-consciousness/)

Bayne, Hohwy and Owen argue against treating global states such as anaesthesia, sleep and disorders of consciousness as positions on one scale. Birch, Schnell and Clayton propose multidimensional profiles for comparing forms of animal consciousness. Neither result establishes a separate consciousness/nonconsciousness threshold on every dimension. A multidimensional space may have a complex boundary, a partially ordered set, or dimensions that apply only after consciousness is already present. [PubMed+1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27101880/)

The article’s conclusion that multidimensionality makes the void multiply is therefore a Map-generated inference, not a result supplied by either cited paper.

# 4. Specific substantive overclaims

## 4.1 The MPE study does not reveal a hidden state below MPE

The article reasons from the richness of contemplative reports to an inaccessible simpler state. That is not the only explanation. Alternatives include:

- the state really is multidimensionally rich despite being judged simple;
- the questionnaire decomposes differences among episodes or practitioners rather than concurrent features of one episode;
- its factors partly reflect interpretation, vocabulary, tradition or retrospective reconstruction;
- felt simplicity is one property among many;
- or “contentless” means absence of ordinary object-directed content, not absence of all differentiable phenomenology.

Metzinger himself says that empirical identification of the simplest human state remains open. [Thomas Metzinger](https://thomasmetzinger.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Metzinger_MIT_Press_2024-1.pdf)

The strongest result supported by the MPE work is that the contemplative category of pure awareness has a complex and variable report profile. That does not entail an unreported phenomenal atom underneath it.

## 4.2 Reportability is not a single threshold

The article sometimes treats report, silent noting, memory and attention as one access boundary. They can dissociate. There are differences between:

- immediate report and delayed report;
- report failure and memory failure;
- absence of confidence and absence of content;
- phenomenal discrimination and metacognitive discrimination;
- failure to act and failure to experience.

Covert-consciousness research in clinically unresponsive patients is a particularly important counterexample to any simple identification of outward reportability with consciousness. Current work finds some apparently unresponsive patients capable of willful modulation detectable through EEG or imaging, although that literature concerns relatively sophisticated command following rather than minimal phenomenality. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40679720/)

## 4.3 A continuum can still have a boundary

The inference from graded consciousness to no threshold is invalid. Many quantities are continuous within their positive domain while having a determinate zero boundary. A theory might hold:

- a system must first satisfy an eligibility condition;
- eligible systems then vary continuously in degree;
- systems below eligibility have no consciousness.

Indeed, the criterion Φ > 0 is itself a boundary condition, however difficult it is to operationalize. The article cannot infer thresholdlessness merely from continuity above zero.

## 4.4 Multidimensionality may dissolve rather than multiply the minimum

In a multidimensional space, there may be no unique least positive point. One state could have less perceptual richness but more temporal integration than another. The states would be incomparable rather than arranged above several independent basements.

That would undermine the singular lower-bound metaphor. It would not automatically create a separate void on every dimension.

## 4.5 Fundamental consciousness could still require a physical interface threshold

The article suggests that if consciousness is fundamental, its lower bound cannot be fixed by a physical threshold. That does not follow. Even a dualist theory may require:

- a sufficiently integrated neural controller;
- a particular architecture;
- a minimum causal bandwidth;
- suitable indeterminacy;
- or another physical coupling condition.

The Map’s companion article itself invokes appropriate interface conditions. [concepts/minimal-consciousness](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/minimal-consciousness/)

Fundamentality concerns ontological derivation. It does not settle which physical systems instantiate, host or couple to individual conscious subjects.

## 4.6 The cessation evidence does not locate the lower edge

The article correctly notes that a report of nothing after cessation is not an independent confirmation of zero consciousness. But it still describes cessation as suggesting an edge.

Several alternatives remain:

- genuine absence of experience;
- experience without memory formation;
- a state that disrupts temporal continuity;
- an ultrabrief experience missed by reconstruction;
- category-driven retrospective interpretation;
- or transition through multiple different states grouped under one contemplative label.

The distinction between brief nirodha and extended nirodha samāpatti is especially important and currently blurred. [Meditation Research Program](https://meditation.mgh.harvard.edu/files/Laukkonen_23_ProgressInBrainResearch.pdf)

The 2025 review of mind blanking is also directly relevant. It emphasizes definitional and phenomenological ambiguity and treats seemingly contentless episodes as a diverse class rather than one zero-content state. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40280833/)

## 4.7 The hard problem does not gain evidential support from the void

A phenomenal realist can reasonably say that explaining a very simple experience remains an explanatory challenge. But this is conditional on accepting the phenomenal explanandum and a nondeflationary conception of it.

The article’s lower-bound discussion does not independently show that physical explanation must fail. It merely carries the familiar explanatory-gap dispute into a putative minimal case. That is philosophically useful, but not new evidence for the gap.

## 4.8 Animal distribution does not imply arbitrarily simple consciousness

Nagel’s observation that consciousness is widespread does not entail that it extends continuously toward arbitrarily simple organisms or physical systems.

The contemporary evidence base is more discriminating. The 2024 New York Declaration reports strong support for consciousness in mammals and birds and at least a realistic possibility in all vertebrates and many invertebrate groups. It is deliberately precautionary and does not endorse universal consciousness or a thresholdless continuum. [Google Sites](https://sites.google.com/nyu.edu/nydeclaration/declaration)

Recent work describes animal-consciousness research as an emerging empirical field rather than a deduction from a single philosophical criterion. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39977511/)

A particularly important omitted counterposition appeared in 2026: Jablonka, Ginsburg and Bronfman argue that evolved minimal consciousness requires a complex cognitive-affective architecture grounded in specifically organized biological affordances. Whether or not that account succeeds, it is a direct contemporary rival to the article’s asymptotic or substrate-light framing. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42750508/)

## 4.9 Attentional modulation does not evidence interactionist causation

The article’s statement that “**consciousness is causally efficacious**” shifts between two meanings:

1. processes described in conscious or intentional vocabulary influence later experiences and behaviour;
2. a nonphysical conscious cause alters physical outcomes.

The enrichment argument may support the first in an ordinary psychological sense. It does not discriminate between the second and a physicalist account in which attention, intention and their effects are all neurally realized.

The Map’s own edge-states article states this correctly: meditation-induced attentional changes are compatible with intra-physical causation, and evidence for downward interaction would require an outcome underdetermined by its neural antecedents. [voids/minimal-consciousness-void](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/minimal-consciousness-void/)

## 4.10 The Occam inference is a category error

A questionnaire with 92 items and a tentative 12-factor model does not show that consciousness itself is irreducibly complex. It shows that reports across subjects are statistically and semantically heterogeneous.

Psychometric dimensionality may reflect:

- genuinely multidimensional phenomenology;
- multiple types of experience;
- individual differences;
- linguistic distinctions;
- questionnaire construction;
- cultural interpretation;
- or measurement noise.

It supplies no direct argument against ontological simplicity and therefore no particular support for the Map’s Occam tenet.

# 5. Counterarguments omitted or underweighted

## Attention without phenomenally richer content

The article should engage theories on which attention improves precision, selection or access while leaving the attended first-order quality unchanged. Even where attention alters phenomenology, the effect need not be monotonic enrichment: it can suppress context, narrow the field or simplify experience.

## Pre-reflective awareness

Phenomenological traditions distinguish reflective inspection from pre-reflective awareness. The Map’s observation-and-measurement article similarly distinguishes passive inner perception from active inner observation. The audited article nevertheless generalizes from deliberate introspection to any attention or memory. [voids/observation-and-measurement-void](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/observation-and-measurement-void/)

A minimal state might therefore be weakly self-present without becoming a reflective object.

## First-order and recurrent-processing theories

The alternatives section covers HOT, GNW and illusionism, but omits first-order representational and recurrent-processing views. Such theories can hold that local or recurrent sensory activity is sufficient for phenomenality before global report access. They therefore offer a direct response to the article’s assumption that sub-report states must either be unconscious or hidden behind a metaphysical void.

## Predictive-processing and active-inference accounts

The article mentions Metzinger’s tonic-alertness model but does not treat predictive processing as a full rival theory of minimal consciousness. A 2026 review by Whyte and colleagues explicitly extracts a minimal and testable theory of consciousness from shared active-inference commitments. That is unusually close to the article’s central question and should not be omitted. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41289944/)

## Constitutive-access views without standard HOT

One can reject inaccessible phenomenality without accepting Rosenthalian HOT. A theorist might hold that some form of availability, discrimination or self-organizing access constitutes the minimum. The article’s three-framework taxonomy is therefore not exhaustive.

## Null hypothesis: no unique natural minimum

There may be many minimal kinds relative to different architectures, or no least element in the relevant partial order. This is not the same as an asymptotic sequence of progressively smaller experiences. It may mean the question has been under-specified rather than that reality contains an inaccessible basement.

## Method-relative rather than metaphysical opacity

Different methods may reveal different aspects without any single method capturing the whole state. That is a familiar measurement problem, but it does not entail a permanently inaccessible object. Triangulation can improve knowledge even without a privileged direct measurement.

# 6. Stale or missing current literature

The bibliography is disproportionately anchored in 2004–2021 work despite major developments available before the article’s November 2026 revision.

### COGITATE 2025

The article presents GNW’s ignition threshold as a determinate physicalist alternative but omits the large adversarial test of GNWT and IIT published in *Nature* in 2025. The results supported some predictions while challenging others, including important prefrontal and temporal predictions. [Nature+1](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08888-1))

The omission is especially conspicuous because the Map’s own GWT article already discusses COGITATE and the proponents’ response. [concepts/global-workspace-theory](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/global-workspace-theory/)

### IIT 4.0 and post-2023 testing

Relying on Tononi 2004 while making a concrete claim about simple-system consciousness is not adequate in 2026. IIT 4.0 substantially refines the formal apparatus and substrate-selection conditions. The 2025 adversarial study should accompany both the theory and the 2023 pseudoscience controversy. [PubMed+1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37847724/)

### Covert measures of consciousness, 2025

Kronemer, Bandettini and Gonzalez-Castillo’s 2025 review directly updates the article’s discussion of indirect access. It documents emerging physiological measures but stresses the unresolved confound between conscious and unconscious processing. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40410390/)

### Mind blanking, 2025

Andrillon and colleagues’ review bears directly on purportedly contentless waking states, their heterogeneity and their relationship to sleep and meditation. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40280833/)

### Minimal active-inference theory, 2026

Whyte and colleagues explicitly frame their proposal as minimal and testable, making it a central contemporary rival rather than an optional addition. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41289944/)

### Contemporary animal-consciousness work

Nagel 1974 should remain for the conceptual criterion but cannot serve as the article’s principal source on distribution. At minimum the article should incorporate the New York Declaration, the 2025 overview by Andrews, Birch and Sebo, and biological-threshold positions such as Jablonka, Ginsburg and Bronfman 2026. [Google Sites+2](https://sites.google.com/nyu.edu/nydeclaration/declaration)

# 7. Tenet-protective bracketing

The article deserves credit for repeatedly saying that dualism is an interpretation rather than an empirical result. This is a genuine improvement over simply treating introspective limitation as evidence for the Map’s ontology. [voids/minimal-consciousness-void](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/minimal-consciousness-void/)

The protection returns, however, in three later moves.

### 7.1 Framework boundary replaces comparative assessment

The article says illusionism and the Map share too little ground for either to defeat the other. That may describe a deep disagreement, but it should not terminate analysis. Both frameworks make claims about:

- report production;
- introspective confidence;
- neural and computational mechanisms;
- the relation between discrimination and judgment;
- apparent ineffability;
- and the consequences of perturbing metacognitive systems.

Calling the dispute bedrock without comparing those shared explanatory obligations risks insulating the tenet rather than testing it.

### 7.2 Fundamentality is allowed to settle the threshold question

The reasoning that a fundamental consciousness must have a nonphysical lower bound assumes what it is meant to illuminate. A dualist still owes an account of individualization, coupling and physical eligibility. Without that account, the framework has not displaced the threshold; it has moved it to an unspecified interface.

### 7.3 Ordinary psychological causation is redescribed as bidirectional interaction

Attending to an experience changing subsequent experience is common ground among physicalists, functionalists and dualists. It cannot support interactionist dualism unless the article identifies an effect that physical neural dynamics leave underdetermined.

The linked edge-states article already makes precisely this correction, which means the present article is not merely under-argued but inconsistent with a nearby site-level methodological standard. [voids/edge-states-and-void-probes](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/edge-states-and-void-probes/)

The article has strong link density but weak **claim synchronization**. Several linked pages contain qualifications that should have altered the audited page’s wording.

# Concrete potential improvements

1. **Rewrite the opening thesis of *The Minimal Consciousness Void*.** Replace the universal necessity claim with a conditional one: deliberate concurrent reflective introspection may perturb some candidate minimal states, while indirect methods can constrain but do not yet identify a unique minimum.
2. **Remove the claim that the epistemic conclusion is fully earned.** State three live possibilities: a hidden sub-introspective state, a constitutive access boundary, and no unique natural minimum.
3. **Add a “Which minimum?” section.** Separate minimal phenomenal content, minimal human global state, minimal reportability, minimal conscious organism, minimal physical substrate and minimal psychometric profile.
4. **Define phenomenal enrichment operationally.** Specify what would count as increased phenomenal complexity independently of increased neural activity, confidence, memory or report capacity.
5. **Add disconfirmation criteria.** Examples should include stable reports under manipulated attention, convergent indirect measures locating a reproducible floor, evidence that higher-order processing changes access without changing phenomenology, and a predictive substrate threshold that generalizes across new cases.
6. **Correct the Tononi passage.** Remove the claim that Tononi 2004’s photodiode qualifies as conscious; explain the original contrast and update the discussion to IIT 4.0.
7. **Add COGITATE 2025 to both the IIT and GNW sections.** Present its mixed findings and the proponents’ responses rather than using either theory as a settled lower-bound model.
8. **Correct the MPE-92M description.** Refer to 92 questionnaire items, a tentative 12-factor model explaining 44% of variance, substantial cross-loading and incomplete model fit—not 92 established characteristics of a minimal state.
9. **Qualify the non-independence claim.** Explain that the survey uses a new sample but inherits theoretical framing, item construction and a shared contemplative report tradition.
10. **Import Metzinger’s own caution.** State prominently that felt simplicity does not prove objective minimality and that simpler human states may exist.
11. **Rewrite the Schooler paragraph.** Say that meta-conscious reports can diverge through detection, transformation and substitution, while also noting that the paper finds cases of apparent accuracy and proposes indirect validation.
12. **Separate attention, introspection, metacognition, memory and report.** Do not treat them as a single enrichment operation.
13. **Repair the higher-order-theory account.** Distinguish an unconscious higher-order representation allegedly constituting consciousness from conscious introspection of that representation.
14. **Expand the physicalist rival set.** Add first-order, recurrent-processing, predictive-processing and active-inference approaches, including Whyte et al. 2026.
15. **Relabel the illusionist mechanism as a reconstruction.** Cite Frankish for the broad programme and newer sources for specific distortion or self-monitoring mechanisms.
16. **Correct the cessation sample statement.** Distinguish the 2023 two-laboratory preliminary programme from the 2024 intensive n=1 EEG study.
17. **Separate nirodha from nirodha samāpatti.** Do not slide between brief events, extended attainment, canonical descriptions and laboratory observations.
18. **Add the memory-null alternative to cessation.** Treat absent retrospective content as compatible with absent experience, disrupted encoding, lost temporal integration and category-dependent reconstruction.
19. **Add the 2025 mind-blanking review.** Use it to present contentless-seeming states as heterogeneous and conceptually unsettled.
20. **Update the distribution section.** Replace Nagel as the principal empirical source with the New York Declaration, contemporary marker-based animal-consciousness research and live restrictive accounts such as Jablonka, Ginsburg and Bronfman 2026.
21. **Remove the inference from widespread animal consciousness to asymptotic consciousness.** Treat broad distribution, continuous degree and absence of an eligibility threshold as three independent propositions.
22. **Correct the multidimensional argument.** Explain that multidimensionality may eliminate a unique ordering rather than generate one separate existence threshold per dimension.
23. **Remove the Occam conclusion from the questionnaire results.** Psychometric complexity does not establish ontological complexity.
24. **Rewrite the bidirectional-interaction paragraph.** State that attentional modulation is neutral between physicalist and dualist causal descriptions; reserve support for interactionism for genuinely discriminating evidence.
25. **Revise *Minimal Consciousness*.** Correct its repeated photodiode error, break the circular dependence on the void article, and distinguish interface eligibility from consciousness’s alleged fundamentality.
26. **Synchronize *Observation and Measurement Void*.** Propagate its passive-inner-perception versus active-observation distinction into every enrichment argument.
27. **Synchronize *Higher-Order Theories*.** Add a reciprocal note to the void article explaining why ordinary HOTs do not themselves amount to introspection.
28. **Synchronize *Global Workspace Theory*.** Make COGITATE and its contested interpretation part of the shared GNW summary used across the site.
29. **Synchronize *Descriptive Experience Sampling*.** Require all articles citing DES to import its core error channels rather than leaving them in a companion page.
30. **Synchronize *Edge-States and the Probing of Voids*.** Use its neutrality about physicalist and dualist causal readings as a site-wide constraint.
31. **Create a recurring-claim register.** Track propositions such as the photodiode example, necessary introspective enrichment, multidimensional thresholds, cessation sample size and attentional causal efficacy, with one canonical formulation propagated across pages.
32. **Introduce a claim–source ledger.** For every citation, record the exact claim supported, source passage, bibliographic metadata, whether wording is quoted or paraphrased, and a support rating such as direct, partial, analogical or contrary.
33. **Separate citation verification from entailment review.** A real paper and accurate quotation should not count as a successful citation unless the paper supports the conclusion drawn from it.
34. **Require falsifiers for every structural void.** A claim of principled inaccessibility should list conceivable evidence that would weaken the structural premise, not merely evidence predicted to remain inconclusive.
35. **Use an evidence-first tenet firewall.** Complete the neutral evidence assessment and rival comparison before adding a clearly separated site-perspective section; do not allow tenets to supply missing empirical premises.
36. **Label Map-generated reconstructions.** Phrases such as “the Map’s reconstruction” or “one possible implementation” should accompany specific mechanisms not stated in the cited source.
37. **Add freshness triggers.** Reopen an article whenever a major adversarial experiment, systematic review or new direct rival appears, rather than relying only on elapsed-time review schedules.
38. **Distinguish narrow edits from deep reviews in page metadata.** A last-modified date caused by one cross-link or sentence revision should not imply that every citation and empirical claim was revalidated on that date.
