- **Tenet 2 mis-described in the two-widths paragraph.** The 09-08 addition said Minimal Quantum Interaction constrains the outbound coordinate "naming the selection-only channel as the narrowest outbound class the Map will accept". The tenet names no class, and the corpus's own account is that it is a class-level commitment: [[channel-class-taxonomy]] says "The Map does not commit to a single class but ranks them by minimality", with selection-only "strictest", a preferred band of selection-only / probability-bias / basis-choice, and energy-injection "explicitly ruled out"; [[dualism-channel-width-axis]] calls selection-only "the strictest reading". "Narrowest the Map will accept" also reads as a floor, where the tenet's operative constraint is a ceiling. *Fixed*: "with the selection-only channel as its strictest reading and energy-injection as what it rules out". A tenet-accepting reviewer would flag it. *Fixed*: reports "place enhanced experience in a period when the outbound leg was down".
