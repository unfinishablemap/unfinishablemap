## TL;DR

Both reviewers recommend **major revision**. They converge on one diagnosis. The article's concessions are honest, but it reads *anattā* as a denial of *permanence* only, and never faces the no-persisting-subject objection that bears on the Map's downstream persisting-subject commitment. - **ChatGPT vs Claude on how much Buddhist support to claim**: ChatGPT (§7.1, C8) says the article gets compatibility from mere logical room and should not put it in a support register. Claude (§4.3) says the article *under*-claims, because classical Indian schools "uniformly rejected physicalism in favor of either a dualist or an idealist ontology" (publisher description). **Adjudication: both can hold.** Classical anti-physicalism is real positive support for the Dualism tenet's *negative* claim. It is not support for property dualism, interaction or a persisting subject, and ChatGPT's point that a mental/material dharma taxonomy is not modern property dualism limits how it can be used. The refiner should add the anti-physicalism concession scoped that narrowly. The Yiannopoulos p. 187 quote remains an unverified lead (PhilArchive 403).
