## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found (8, all fixed)

1. **Huemer's four accounts misnamed.** The body listed "likelihoodist, curve-fitting, prior probability, and pragmatic". The paper's §2 headings (PhilArchive full text via Wayback) are 2.1 Empiricist, 2.2 Boundary Asymmetry, 2.3 Numerousness and 2.4 Likelihood. Huemer explicitly sets pragmatic accounts aside: "I shall focus on epistemic accounts." Fixed, and the dropped "naturally" was restored to the follow-on sentence.
2. **Sober quote cited to the wrong work.** "the legitimacy of parsimony stands or falls ... (and a posteriori) considerations" has 0 hits in Sober's "What Is the Problem of Simplicity?" (Sober's own PDF, 47k characters; control: "simplicity" ×109). It is verbatim in Sober 1990, "Let's Razor Ockham's Razor": "My local approach entails that the legitimacy of parsimony stands or falls...". SEP "Simplicity" also attributes it there (as Sober 1994, the reprint). Re-cited to 1990. The 2002 chapter's own hedged line ("It may turn out that simplicity has no global justification") was added, so its reference stays in use.
3. **Biogeography example inverted.** The body said extensionist biogeography (land bridges) "seemed more parsimonious than continental drift". Baker's SEP case study says the opposite: extensionist theories, culminating in Croizat's tectonic account, "are clearly less ontologically parsimonious than Dispersal Theories". The 2026-02-06 research note had it right ("seemed less parsimonious"), so the article compressed it the wrong way round. Rewritten from SEP, with a calibration that dispersal survives in the modern account.
4. **Smart claim unsourced and contradicted.** "attracted immediate and sustained criticism ... never mounted an adequate defence in his subsequent work" had no source after the 06-21 refine removed the unsupported SEP "severely criticized". Smart published "Ockham's Razor" in 1984 (Fetzer ed., pp. 118-28, listed in SEP "Simplicity"). His own SEP entry (rev. 2007, `citation_author` Smart) says Lewis's and Kirk's arguments mean "there is no need for explicit use of Ockham's Razor as in Smart (1959)". Rewritten to that verified record, plus Smart's own statement of the precondition (see Enhancements). The heading is unchanged, because it is now accurate in the narrower sense that Smart dropped the razor from the identity theory's case.
5. **Churchland quote unverified and mismatched** (open P3 at todo L2329, item 2). The 1988 rev. ed. p. 18 (Google Books `_7CBvggqOE4C` search-within; control query "Ockham" gave 2 hits, PA18) reads "This is not yet a decisive point against dualism, since neither dualism nor materialism can yet ...". "conclusive argument for materialism" has 0 hits. The 1984 edition has no searchable text anywhere (IA holds only the 1988 and 2013 editions). Re-cited to 1988 p. 18. Only the verified span is quoted; the rest is paraphrased.
6. **IEP quote mislocated and its qualifier dropped.** "unprecedented in our ontology" is in Josh Weisberg's IEP "Hard Problem of Consciousness" entry, in the weak-reductionism section, not in "its discussion of identity theory" (the IEP "Identity Theory" entry has 0 hits). The full clause is "seems unprecedented in our ontology given that consciousness is a macro-level phenomenon". The quote now carries "seems" and the macro-level qualifier, and a References entry was added (it was an inline↔References orphan that every prior ledger missed).
7. **Necessity claim contradicting the tenets page.** "no experiment can distinguish physicalism from property dualism" conflicts with `tenets/tenets.md` L75, where a deviation conditioned on intention "would test the corridor itself" and "no such test ... has yet run". Changed to "no experiment has yet distinguished".
8. **Zanotti misdescribed.** The body said Zanotti "challenges the 'modesty rationale'" and gave an unsourced "because ... background assumptions" clause. The abstract (IUSS repository OAI record, verbatim) shows the reverse: Zanotti argues modesty is "the most promising way to account for the epistemic value of parsimony", then that "the modesty rationale does not ultimately favour physicalism over dualism". The convergence paragraph's "Zanotti's targeted critique of modesty" and "three ... all reach the same conclusion ... about consciousness" were also corrected. Sober never addresses the mind-body case; he now "explains why it should be expected".

### Medium Issues Found (6, all fixed)

- **"This is not X. It is Y." cliché** at L40 ("The point is not that ... It is that ..."), recast. The same paragraph was recalibrated from "the precondition ... is unmet" to "cannot simply be assumed", because explanatory parity is the disputed point. A physicalist must grant that much, which makes the defensive argument robust. The lead got the matching "or at the least is the very point in dispute".
