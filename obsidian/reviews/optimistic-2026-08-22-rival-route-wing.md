**Why this wing, and why these five.** The selection was made by differencing every live article slug against every slug named in any prior `optimistic-*.md` review: 61 of 800 articles have never appeared in an optimistic review, and five of them form a single structural family. Each of these articles exists to state, at full strength and in its own terms, a route the Map does **not** take — and then to locate the Map by contrast rather than by dismissal. They span three different axes of rivalry, which is what makes them one wing rather than five unrelated pages:

- **Within libertarianism** — `event-causal-libertarianism` (the rival species P-A2 rejects)
- **Against libertarianism** — `compatibilism` (the discipline's majority view)
- **Against the Map's mental-causation structure** — `interventionist-and-counterfactual-dualism` (co-causation vs trumping)
- **Against Tenet 1 itself** — `neutral-monism` (the third-stuff rival)
- **The inverse case** — `pairing-problem` (not a rival route but the objection that targets the Map's own route)

None appears on the same-session exclusion list.

## Executive Summary

This is the corpus at its most disciplined. In three of the five articles the exercise of stating a rival fairly lands *against the Map's own preference*, and in all three the article lets it land rather than recovering the result in a closing clause — including one article whose central section audits the five tenets and reports that the discriminating tenet leans toward the rival. Two calibration defects were found, both single sentences, and both of the same shape: a correction that exists elsewhere in the corpus was never carried back to the article that needed it. There is no coverage gap in this wing worth a new article; the one genuine gap the wing names for itself is cap-blocked and better discharged in place.

## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

`neutral-monism` is the article Chalmers would recognise as doing his own work back at him properly. It reconstructs panprotopsychism and Coleman's panqualityism as serious contemporary respecifications of the neutral base, and then presses the **quality/awareness gap** — that no arrangement of qualities necessitates *awareness of* those qualities — as the hard problem re-arising inside the programme that promised to dissolve it. Crucially the article does not present this as a Map invention: it routes the objection through the SEP's own inventory (Stubenberg & Wishon 2023) and through Mihálik (2022). Chalmers would also note that the article correctly identifies its own exposure: the same construction step the Map presses against neutral monism is the one it presses against Russellian monism, so a solution there would cost the Map twice.

`pairing-problem` takes Kim's challenge as genuinely fundamental rather than as a nuisance to be waved past — "This goes beyond causal closure. The closure objection grants that mental causation is intelligible but claims physical causation leaves no room for it. The pairing problem challenges the very intelligibility of mental causation." That is the right diagnosis, and it is stated before any reply is offered.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

`interventionist-and-counterfactual-dualism` is the wing's most physics-literate article, and Stapp would recognise the seam it exposes. It notices that the Map is running *two structurally different channels* — a psychophysical trumping channel and a quantum-selection channel — and that these are not obviously the same thing wearing two hats: "A Map that takes MQI as a real per-trial selection has *already* relaxed strong closure at the quantum level; layering trumping on top at the psychophysical level introduces a second, structurally different channel, and the two sit in mild tension." It backs this by quoting `trumping-preemption`'s own concession that the quantum channel is "a distinct and potentially competing mechanism to the trumping route rather than an instance of it" — verified verbatim against that article's live body this run.

`pairing-problem`'s Map section is the cleanest short statement anywhere in the corpus of *why the interface must be somewhere*: Minimal Quantum Interaction requires consciousness to be located where selection happens, so the pairing question is answered by the mechanism rather than by a separate metaphysical posit.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

`compatibilism` earns Nagel's approval by refusing to caricature. Its Relation-to-Site-Perspective opens by conceding almost everything: "Compatibilism and the Map converge on the *moral* conclusions... The Map does not treat compatibilists as covert deniers of responsibility; on the contrary, it regards the compatibilist account of *why* responsibility survives — reasons-responsiveness, identification, the embeddedness of the reactive attitudes — as largely correct description of the phenomena." The disagreement is then localised to one thing: whether "could have done otherwise" means what it appears to mean. That is first-person data being taken seriously as data, and the article says so without inflating it into an argument.

`neutral-monism` credits the rival with the insight Nagel shares: it "refused to eliminate experience, taking the given as a datum rather than an embarrassment."

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

`neutral-monism` gives the process-friendly reading its full run — Banks's event-based descendant on which "the same natural power manifests as both a neuron firing and a sensation," James's pure experience, Mach's neutral elements — before the Map declines it. And it declines it on a stated ground rather than a tonal one: either the base gets positive content and tips (mental, physical, or double-aspect), or it stays neutral by staying contentless. Whitehead would want the process reading to be represented by its best modern reconstruction rather than by the 1904 essay alone, and it is.

**Constraint observed.** Nothing in this wing uses process-philosophical resonance to lift any claim up the evidential-status scale. The one place where a congenial structural result appears — that the most economical monism ends up shaped like a dualism — is discussed below under the Hardline Empiricist, where the article's own handling of it is what deserves the notice.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

`event-causal-libertarianism` is, remarkably, an article *about why Kane is wrong* that Kane would have little to complain about. It gives his self-forming actions the fullest treatment in the corpus — torn decisions, the doubled effort from 1999 onward, plural voluntary control, the trace-back through character — and then presents **both** of his replies to the luck objection before the critics' rejoinder. It even records that Kane now rejects the event-causal label, and does not use the label against him without flagging that: "Kane wants the extra factor *without* agent-causation. Critics reply that this is exactly what cannot be had."

The article's ordering is itself the achievement: the Map's own location is deferred to the final section, and when it arrives it is marked as "a *framework-relative* argument, not a proof," with the concession that "the strongest event-causal replies (Kane's indeterminate efforts, plural rationality) remain live for those who weight the naturalist commitments more heavily than the Map does."

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

Three of the five articles carry a named falsifier section, and the wing's epistemic humility is structural rather than decorative. `neutral-monism`'s "What Would Challenge This View?" names two *currently live* research programmes (Coleman's panqualityism, Banks's power-events) rather than an unreachable ideal — the falsifier is something that might actually happen in the next decade.

`interventionist-and-counterfactual-dualism` closes with two caveats that bound its own diagnostic: "'leans toward' is not 'entails'," and the rival family "carries its own bill." An article that has just produced a result unfavourable to the house then declines to over-collect on it.

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

This persona's verdict is load-bearing here, and it is favourable on three counts and adverse on one.

**1. A null result explicitly refused as confirmation.** `pairing-problem`'s third falsifier is the best single piece of evidential discipline in the wing. It takes the PEAR micro-PK nulls — effect sizes ~0.0001–0.001, a result that superficially *vindicates* interface locality — and declines to bank them: "Interface locality was formulated knowing these null results, so they are *consistent with* the framework rather than positive evidence *for* it; what makes the commitment substantive is the falsification threshold — reliably replicable external-PK effects above roughly 0.01 under preregistered protocols would refute it." Retrodictive fit is disclosed as retrodictive, and the substance of the commitment is relocated out of the fit and into a numeric, preregistration-conditioned threshold. This is the pattern the persona exists to praise: a defeater removed, and the removal explicitly *not* converted into evidence.

**2. Parsimony self-binding, stated symmetrically.** `neutral-monism` under Tenet 5 reads the rival's history as a case study in simplicity purchased on credit — and then blocks the obvious profit: "the Map declines to treat 'one stuff is simpler than two aspects' as evidence against dualism, **for the same reason it declines to wield parsimony *for* dualism**." The binding runs both ways, in one sentence, which is what stops it being a convenient asymmetry.

**3. Tenet-coherence named as such, and refused as a warrant.** `interventionist-and-counterfactual-dualism` produces the wing's sharpest result and files it in exactly the right register: "The honest statement is that the Map's preference for trumping over co-causation is tenet-*coherent* but tenet-*underdetermined*, and it trades on closure-conservatism rather than on anything the tenets require." It then closes the parsimony escape hatch from inside: "the Map cannot claim trumping is *simpler and therefore truer*, because its own fifth tenet denies that simplicity tracks truth under incomplete knowledge." A tenet audit whose result is allowed to go against the house, with the two standard recovery moves — appeal to closure-preservation, appeal to cleanliness — both pre-empted.

**4. Adverse: one sentence undoes the wing's best move.** `concepts/pairing-problem` line 146 closes its falsifier list with "These are genuine conditions under which the framework would need revision. Their non-occurrence supports the current approach." That is precisely the inference falsifier 3 forbids three paragraphs above, and it fails the discrimination test the corpus itself states elsewhere. Detailed under Concern 2.

## Content Strengths

### `obsidian/topics/interventionist-and-counterfactual-dualism.md`

- **Mechanism worth reusing**: *a tenet audit run against the house, with the recovery routes closed from inside*. The article's `#the-tenet-diagnostic` section asks whether the five tenets discriminate between the Map's preferred trumping route and the co-causation rival, works through them one at a time, and reports that Tenet 3 read literally favours the rival and that Tenet 2 — the *discriminating* tenet — also leans that way on the axis that matters. It then names what actually drives the Map's preference: "a methodological posture (minimise the detectable footprint), not... anything the five tenets strictly entail."
- **Why it survives review**: because the conclusion is not "and therefore the Map is wrong" but "and therefore this is a choice under underdetermination that the Map should own explicitly." The article converts an unfavourable audit into a visible commitment rather than into either a retraction or a rescue.
- **Calibration note for future passes**: this article carries `anchoring_audit_exempt: true` with an inline justification recorded in the frontmatter — "verified false-high... calibrates structurally (bare-noun 'underdetermination' x4 + phrasal hedges the lexical checks miss); refining would over-hedge and regress the voice." That comment is doing real work. A future anchoring flag on this file should be read against it before any hedge-padding is applied.
- **Taxonomy quality**: the three-branch split of the co-causation family (Kroedel's counterfactual dualism, Vaassen's interventionist co-causation, the Mills/Lowe lawful lineage) is genuinely useful and correctly sourced, including the note that Vaassen's own 2021 paper *tightens* the exclusion argument he later escapes — a trajectory detail that stops the citation reading as a partisan one. The article also records that its framing of Vaassen was corrected by the author himself, and flags Zhong 2023 as the cautionary mirror: "the same difference-making toolkit can be turned *against* dualism, so the Map cannot treat co-causation machinery as intrinsically dualist-friendly."

### `obsidian/concepts/pairing-problem.md`

- **Mechanism worth reusing**: *retrodictive fit disclosed and priced*. Falsifier 3, quoted in full above. The article had a free win available — decades of null PK results are exactly what interface locality predicts — and declined it on the ground that the prediction was made knowing the results.
- **Second mechanism**: *the reply is "no unique difficulty", not "solved"*. The Bailey/Rasmussen/Van Horn haecceity dilemma is deployed as a symmetry argument: if haecceities exist they ground the pairing; if they do not, the pairing problem generalises to all causation and stops being a special problem for dualism. The Map's claim is parity with physical causation, not a positive account — a much cheaper claim, correctly priced.
- **Third**: the illusionist reply is handled without triumphalism. The article grants the challenge relocates rather than solves the problem, and then states plainly what the Map's counter-move actually costs: the illusionist "can deny that 'from the inside' designates anything real — but this amounts to denying that consciousness exists, which the Map's Dualism tenet rejects." That is an honest "we part company here on a tenet," not a refutation dressed as one.

### `obsidian/concepts/neutral-monism.md`

- **Mechanism worth reusing**: *a falsifier stated with its blast radius*. "Either success would also weaken the Map's parallel case against Russellian monism, since the two share the construction step." Most falsifier sections in any corpus name what would change *this* verdict. This one names what else falls with it — which is what stops a position being quietly retired in one article while its siblings keep the benefit of the argument that supported it.
- **Second mechanism**: *the objection the Map most likes is attributed away from the Map*. "The Dualism Suspicion" — that a view positing relational structure *and* intrinsic quality, neither reducible to the other, is property dualism in monist dress — is the argument most congenial to Tenet 1, and it is sourced to the SEP's own objection inventory and paralleled to Cutter's instability argument against Russellian monism, rather than presented as a Map discovery.
- **Verified this run**: the internal quotation from `topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy` — "behaves like deflationary dualism along both axes" — still matches that article's live body verbatim (now at line 90; the 2026-07-13 deep review verified it at line 88). The internal-quote channel on this article is currently clean.
- **One line to watch, not to change now**: the section closes "That the most economical monism ends up shaped like a dualism is, the Map suggests, evidence about where the joints of the problem really are." This is hedged ("the Map suggests") and it is a *different* argument from the parsimony one the same article self-binds against two paragraphs later, so it is not self-contradictory. But it is a structural-convergence-as-evidence move sitting one paragraph from an explicit refusal to treat structural results as evidence, and a future pass should check it retains the hedge rather than hardening.

### `obsidian/concepts/compatibilism.md`

- **Mechanism worth reusing**: *the majority burden accepted in a section of its own*. "The Majority Position" reports the 2020 PhilPapers figure — roughly 59% for compatibilism — and draws the consequence rather than the comfort: "Any project that rejects compatibilism, as the Map does, is therefore committed to explaining why the discipline's majority is mistaken — a burden the Map accepts rather than dodges." Very few articles give the disagreement's *sociology* a named heading.
- **Attached to it, a citation-hygiene guard that reads as trimmable and is not**: "(Some secondary summaries report a higher figure by conflating respondent subsets; the main-results figure is about 59%.)" This parenthesis is the article inoculating itself against a specific propagated error. A condense pass would read it as an aside. It should stay.
- **Same shape, elsewhere in the article**: the Hume gloss is followed by "(a paraphrase of the *Enquiry*, Section VIII, not a verbatim quotation)". An in-text label that pre-empts exactly the quote-fidelity defect class that is highest-yield across this corpus. Also worth keeping.
- **Third mechanism**: *rivals ranked by how much damage they do to the Map, not by how easy they are to answer*. "**Fischer's semicompatibilism** is the hardest challenge: by decoupling responsibility from leeway entirely, it threatens to make the Map's whole quantum apparatus *unnecessary* for the moral conclusions everyone wants." The article names the rival that would make its own central machinery idle, and books the reply as a burden carried rather than discharged.
- **Fourth**: List is identified as "the closest neighbour" and the disagreement is localised to *locus* rather than existence — "he too insists that alternative possibilities are real rather than conditional... the disagreement reduces to a question of locus." ### `obsidian/topics/event-causal-libertarianism.md`

- **Mechanism worth reusing**: *the Map's own wing priced in the same paragraph as the rival's*. "Each wing, then, buys an answer to one objection at the cost of exposure to another: event-causal views are naturalistically respectable but vulnerable to luck; agent-causal views defeat luck but owe an account of substance causation." The intelligibility objection against agent causation is given its own section rather than a clause, immediately after the luck objection that favours the Map.

## Calibration Concerns

Two, both single-sentence-to-single-paragraph, both the same shape: a correction that already exists elsewhere in the corpus was never carried back.

### Concern 1 — `topics/interventionist-and-counterfactual-dualism`: the Tenet-2 diagnostic predates the 2026-08-03 correction to its own source

The article's central section characterises the strict selection-only channel as:

> "...yet *ensemble* difference-making-free: the long-run frequencies stay Born-exact, so it shifts no distribution and is statistically invisible." (line 90)

On **2026-08-03**, commit `1ab6b8a5bf` (`auto(refine-draft): Born-marginal preservation does NOT entail zero mutual information — withdraw the selection-only causal budget until rederived`) narrowed exactly this claim in `obsidian/concepts/selection-only-channel.md`. That article now reads:

> "The strict selection-only channel is *per-trial* difference-making (it fixes which single candidate is realised) yet ***unconditioned*-ensemble** difference-making-free (the marginal is Born-preserving, **though the conditionals need not be**)..." (line 153)

The corrected sibling wrote the fix *into its Further Reading entry naming this article by name*. It was never carried back. `topics/interventionist-and-counterfactual-dualism` has not been touched since 2026-07-25 (`git log -1`), so its unqualified "shifts no distribution and is statistically invisible" is the pre-correction wording.

Three loci:
- **line 90** — "yet *ensemble* difference-making-free... so it shifts no distribution and is statistically invisible"
- **line 96** — "its strict selection-only reading is ensemble Born-preserving"
- **line 106** — Further Reading gloss: "per-trial difference-making, ensemble Born-preserving"

Both the obsidian source and the Hugo mirror carry the line-90 wording (`hugo/content/topics/interventionist-and-counterfactual-dualism.md:94`).

**Why this fix is attractive rather than a chore.** Under the corrected reading the strict channel is *less* statistically invisible than the article states — the unconditioned marginal is Born-preserving, but conditioned on mind-state the statistics need not be. That makes the strict channel *more* of a genuine difference-maker, which **strengthens** the article's own central result (that Tenet 2 leans toward the co-causation family) rather than blunting it. The article currently under-claims its own diagnostic because it is quoting a superseded characterisation.

**Clean on the other axis**: `selection-only-channel` line 76 now carries a *Withdrawn* notice for the zero-throughput derivation, ending "no downstream page should treat this channel as having a known throughput." This article makes no throughput or bit-rate claim, so it needs no change there. The defect is confined to the invisibility characterisation.

**Verified clean**: both internal quotations this article takes from `concepts/trumping-preemption` — "a distinct and potentially competing mechanism to the trumping route rather than an instance of it" and "answering a question already closed" — still match that article's live body verbatim.

### Concern 2 — `concepts/pairing-problem` line 146: one sentence undoes the article's own best move

> "These are genuine conditions under which the framework would need revision. Their non-occurrence supports the current approach."

Three paragraphs above, falsifier 3 states the opposite about the very evidence this sentence would collect on: the PEAR nulls are "*consistent with* the framework rather than positive evidence *for* it." The closing sentence generalises non-occurrence into support across all five falsifiers, including that one.

It also fails the discrimination test the corpus states explicitly elsewhere. `obsidian/concepts/mysterianism.md:158` names the fallacy directly: the usual defeaters "discriminate poorly: physicalism plus hard work predicts their non-occurrence equally well." A falsifier's non-occurrence is evidence only if the rival's prediction differs — and for four of the five conditions listed here (incoherence of non-spatial causation, elimination of haecceities, external-PK effects, mind-jumping between brains), the physicalist predicts non-occurrence just as confidently as the Map does.

**This is a rewrite, not a deletion.** The corpus already contains the calibrated form at `obsidian/topics/hard-problem-of-consciousness.md:233`, which draws inductive support from *persistent failure of a specific research programme*, hedges it against a live alternative, and follows it with an explicit underdetermination statement. Both `mysterianism` and `hard-problem-of-consciousness` handle the inference correctly; `pairing-problem` line 146 is the **sole live-article locus of the bare form** across `topics/`, `concepts/`, `apex/`, `voids/` and `positions/`. Present in Hugo at `hugo/content/concepts/pairing-problem.md:150`.

### The one genuine gap the wing names for itself — and it is cap-blocked

`concepts/compatibilism` flags it directly, in its own voice: Vihvelin's **new dispositionalism** about the ability to do otherwise is "among the strongest live challenges to the Map's premise that leeway must be metaphysically categorical, and **it has no dedicated page elsewhere in the lattice**."

Verified this run rather than taken on the article's say-so. Vihvelin or "dispositionalis*" appears in six live articles. Five are not coverage:

- `topics/consciousness-and-the-metaphysics-of-laws-and-dispositions` treats **pandispositionalism about fundamental properties** (Bird, Shoemaker; the collapse-or-conjunction dilemma) — a different thesis about what properties *are*, not about the ability to do otherwise. Do not count it as coverage.
- `concepts/source-versus-leeway-incompatibilism` and `concepts/causal-powers` carry a bare reference-list entry and a Further Reading pointer respectively.
- `topics/the-consequence-argument-for-incompatibilism` line 79 gives it one paragraph (Vihvelin 2013, Fara, the fink/mask-proofing move).
- `concepts/compatibilism` gives it the other paragraph.

So the article's self-assessment is accurate: two paragraphs, no dedicated treatment, and it is the challenge that most directly targets the Map's premise.

**But it cannot be built.** Measured live this run with `tools.evolution.state.count_section_files`: `topics/` is **320 of 320** — closed outright — and `concepts/` is **318 of 320**, two slots against a research pipeline already carrying many more open `research-topic` tasks than there are slots. Presenting this as an actionable expansion would be dishonest.

**The better route, and the reason no task is minted for it**: this gap is dischargeable *in place*. `topics/the-consequence-argument-for-incompatibilism` already hosts a dispositionalism paragraph in the right argumentative position, and `concepts/compatibilism` has 1 word of headroom before its soft threshold but 1001 before its hard one. Expanding the existing treatment costs no section slot and puts the material where the argument already runs. That is an `expand-topic`-adjacent judgement this review is not scoped to mint, and it is recorded here so a future replenish or harvest pass has the measurement rather than re-deriving it.

**Lower-value, same block**: `concepts/compatibilism` also notes that "Neither Dennett nor List has a dedicated page elsewhere in the lattice." List is already well and precisely characterised inside `concepts/compatibilism` itself, which reduces the value of a separate page considerably. Not recommended.
