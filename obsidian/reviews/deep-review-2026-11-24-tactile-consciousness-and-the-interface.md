## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

- **Quotation cited to the wrong translation, with one word wrong.** The "double sensation" quote was cited to the Landes translation (1945/2012). The wording is Colin Smith's (Routledge & Kegan Paul, 1962, p. 93), reached through the IEP Merleau-Ponty entry, whose "PP" is the Smith edition. The IEP also prints "the role"; the 1962 text reads "the roles". Grepped in the archive.org scan of the 1962 edition: "an ambiguous set-up in which both hands can alternate the roles of 'touching' and being 'touched'". **Fixed**: quote corrected to "roles", inline cite and reference now Smith 1962 with page. The 2026-11-18b encyclopedia-quotation sweep had listed this line as primary-in-encyclopedia, unlocated; it is now located.
- **Dropped qualifier on McGlone et al. 2014.** The article said the C-tactile class "is" the "neurobiological substrate for the affective and rewarding properties of touch". The abstract reads "Here, we propose that a class of low-threshold mechanosensitive C fibers ... represent the neurobiological substrate ...". **Fixed**: "McGlone and colleagues propose it as ...".
- **Orphan inline citations.** "(review in Làdavas & Serino; Rizzolatti and colleagues)" carried no year and had no References entries; both earlier ledgers reported the inline/References cross-check as complete. **Fixed**: now (Rizzolatti et al., 1981; review in Làdavas & Serino, 2008), with the tool-extension claim cited to Iriki, Tanaka & Iwamura (1996). Three references added, each checked at PubMed and Crossref.
- **Dissociation claim uncited and partly unverifiable.** "People lacking the fast discriminative fibres ... fail to feel the same stimulus on the glabrous palm where those fibres are absent" had no citation. The hairy-skin half is Olausson et al. 2002 (abstract: "In a unique patient lacking large myelinated afferents ... activation of C tactile (CT) afferents produced a faint sensation of pleasant touch"). The palm half could not be confirmed in any source read this pass, and "absent" is a currency risk. **Fixed**: sentence rewritten to the abstract's content and cited; the palm clause removed.
