**Voids category**: Mixed. At the perceptual and memory end the void is **Unexplored and partly correctable**: the self-knowledge premise in absence judgements can be calibrated with positive controls and feedback. At the theoretical end it is **Unexplorable**: where a target's mode of manifestation is unknown, no positive control of the same kind exists, so the premise cannot be calibrated at all. There is an **Occluded** flavour in between. The premise is delivered inside the percept ("I can see it isn't there") rather than as a premise, and in at least one well-studied domain it is systematically inflated.

## Executive Summary

Every judgement that something is absent contains a premise about the person judging: *if it were there, I would have found it.* Stephen Wykstra named the inference from "I see no X" to "there is no X" a **noseeum** inference, after the biting insect too small to see, and his CORNEA principle makes its warrant depend on that counterfactual. In Perrine's simplified statement, belief in *p* on the basis of *q* is prima facie reasonable "only if, given their cognitive abilities, it is reasonable for them to believe that if p were different, then q would likely be different as well" (Perrine 2023, SEP) [raw]. Cognitive science has since located the same premise in ordinary perception and memory: "To represent something as absent, one must know that they would know if it were present" (Mazor 2025) [raw].

The void has three faces. (1) **The hidden premise.** The self-model that supplies "I would have noticed" is largely non-declarative. Absence decisions are slower and less confident than presence decisions, yet absence often *feels* directly seen. Efficient search termination needs no explicit metacognitive knowledge (Mazor & Fleming 2022) [raw], and explicit estimates of one's own search show "little to no insight" into some large effects (Mazor, Siegel & Tenenbaum 2023) [raw]. (2) **Miscalibration in both directions, invisible from inside.** "Most people (including psychologists) believe that they would detect the changes" that real observers miss (Levin et al. 2000) [raw]. In the other direction, the self-model can fail to represent that a target *would* pop out, which produces needlessly exhaustive searches (Wang et al. 1994, as read by Mazor 2025) [raw]. A planted false belief about one's own memory shifts which items are judged "new" (Strack et al. 2005) [secondary: Mazor 2025]. (3) **Calibration closure.** The premise can be checked only by testing the detector on a case known to be present, as Mazor's shopper sniffs a mandarin to confirm that her sense of smell works. Eddington's ichthyologist could have learned his net's limit "by examining the net and the method of using it" (Eddington 1939: 18) [raw]. But Sober shows that "you can't tell which kind of net you used by looking at the fish that are in it" (Sober 2009) [raw-MS], and a mind's net is the mind. For some targets (another being's reasons, a minimal mind–matter interface, consciousness in an unfamiliar architecture, the unconceived alternative) no positive control of the same kind can be had without first settling the question.

Defending a hypothesis by denying the CORNEA condition has a price. Sober's symmetry shows it: "If the circumstances of observation render O(E) evidentially informative, those circumstances also render notO(E) informative" [raw-MS]. By contraposition, a defence that makes an absence uninformative makes the matching presence uninformative too. The skeptical-theism literature documents the spread in detail: once CORNEA-failure is granted for divine reasons, critics argue it reaches into external-world belief, revealed doctrine and moral deliberation (Perrine 2023, §§5.3–6.1) [raw].

**For the Map, this void makes demands rather than offering support.** Tenet 5 (*Occam's Razor Has Limits*) is a CORNEA claim about parsimony. Tenet 2 (*Minimal Quantum Interaction*) is a CORNEA-failure claim about the interface. The weaker, prima facie negative form of the conceivability argument that supports Tenet 1 is itself a noseeum about contradictions (Chalmers 2002) [raw]. The Map already pays part of the bill: [[apex/self-concealing-interface]] rules that hiddenness is "compatibility, not support", and [[apex/assessing-ai-consciousness-under-the-map]] records that absence of evidence for AI consciousness "is explained twice over". This note supplies the general epistemology behind both moves and names the best-documented precedent.

### Mazor, "Inference About Absence as a Window Into the Mental Self-Model", *Open Mind* 9 (2025): 635–651
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.1162/opmi_a_00206 (published PDF fetched from the author's site, carrying the MIT Press download stamp) [raw: full text]
- **Type**: Theoretical review paper, cognitive science
- **Key points**:
  - Abstract: "To represent something as absent, one must know that they would know if it were present. This form of counterfactual reasoning critically relies on a mental self-model" [raw].
  - On the glass of water on his desk: "this does not mean that I perceived its absence. It means that I did not perceive its presence, and that I believe I would have perceived it if it were there" [raw]. The second clause is "a belief about my perceptions in a counterfactual state of affairs" [raw].
  - The Bayesian condition: "in order to make valid inferences about absences I need to know things about myself and my cognitive processes" [raw]. In the durian example, sniffing a mandarin raised confidence in absence although it gave no information about the shop, only "about my own perceptual system" [raw]. **This is a positive control for one's own detector**, and it anchors the calibration face.
  - Links the point to default-reasoning logic, where Reiter (1980) uses "the closed world assumption: the assumption that a proof would have been found if it were available" [raw], and to de Cornulier's (1988) dissymmetrical definition of "knowing whether".
  - **Detection**: "often participants cannot tell if they missed an existing target, or correctly perceived the absence of a target" [raw]. Absence decisions are slower and less confident. The asymmetry "diminishes or reverses" under attentional rather than perceptual manipulations of visibility, on the proposal that fluctuations in attention are accessible to awareness while a sensory blockage is not (Kanai et al. 2010) [raw; Kanai content secondary: Mazor].
  - **Visual search**: present and absent search times usually correlate (Wolfe 1998), which "speaks to the accuracy of the mental self-model" [raw]. But when the target is an unfamiliar letter, which pops out, participants still search serially before saying "absent". Mazor reads this as "a blind spot of the mental self-model, failing to represent the fact that an unfamiliar letter would stand out" [raw] (Wang et al. 1994). And "participants have no introspective access to their knowledge about color pop-out (Mazor et al., 2023), while still being able to act on this information when deciding to terminate their search" [raw].
  - **Memory**: the mirror effect, in which items more likely to be recognised when old are also more confidently rejected when new. One's own name is the example: "I would have remembered if my name were on the list" (Brown et al. 1977) [raw]. Strack et al. (2005) induced *false* beliefs about which word frequencies are memorable, and negative-recognition judgements shifted accordingly [secondary: Mazor]. Explicit memorability judgements often get the direction wrong (frequent words judged more memorable) even when the mirror effect shows the implicit model is right [secondary: Mazor].
  - Against Farennikova's direct-perception account: "introspection can be misleading. In perception, inference about absence is significantly slower than inference about presence or stimulus type" [raw]. The waiter occluding the laptop, or the forgotten glasses, show that absence is judged only when one believes one *would* have seen the item [raw, paraphrased].
- **Tenet alignment**: framework-neutral. The self-model is described as affording "better monitoring and control over cognitive resources" [raw], which is a weak point of contact with Bidirectional Interaction (flagged as speculative below).
- **Calibration**: the self-model reading is Mazor's argued thesis. His own data (next three entries) partly resist it.

### Mazor & Fleming (2022), *JEP: General* 151(10): 2494–2510
- **Type**: Two preregistered online experiments (N = 1187; 887) [raw: abstract]
- **Key points**: search-termination times are aligned with target-identification times "in the very first trials of the experiment before any experience with target presence"; "explicit metacognitive knowledge about search efficiency is not necessary for efficient search termination"; "efficient inference about absence is independent of task experience and of explicit metacognitive knowledge about visual search" [raw]. ## The Void

### Nature of the Limit

An absence verdict, whether "the laptop is gone", "I have never met this person" or "there is no outweighing good", has the form: *no detection* + *I would have detected it* → *absent*. The first conjunct is a fact about experience. The second is a counterfactual about the self. The void is the second conjunct, which has three faces.

**1. The hidden premise.** The counterfactual is rarely represented *as* a premise. It arrives folded into the percept ("I could see that it was not there") or the feeling of memory ("that is not a face I would forget"). Introspection then reports perception where the measurements show inference: slower, less confident, poorer metacognitive sensitivity for absence (Mazor et al. 2021). The competence that sets the premise operates without explicit knowledge (Mazor & Fleming 2022), and the explicit model is partial (Mazor, Siegel & Tenenbaum 2023). The subject cannot read off which self-knowledge its "it isn't there" relied on.

**2. Miscalibration that cannot be seen from inside.** The premise can be wrong in either direction, and nothing marks the error. It can be **inflated**: people confidently predict they would notice changes that observers routinely miss (Levin et al. 2000). It can be **deflated**: the self-model fails to represent that an unfamiliar target would pop out, so search continues past the point where absence was already settled (Wang et al. 1994, on Mazor's reading). It can be **installed**: a planted belief about one's own memory redirects negative recognition (Strack et al. 2005). In each case the felt "I would have noticed" is equally confident.

**3. Calibration closure.** The only check on the premise is a positive control, meaning a case of the same kind known to be present: the mandarin sniff, a test target, a known-old item. Eddington's epistemologist inspects the net from outside. Sober's epistemic switch shows why the catch alone cannot reveal the mesh. For targets whose manner of showing up is unknown, there is no positive control of the same kind to run. One cannot plant a known divine reason, a known minimal interface event, or a known instance of consciousness in an unfamiliar architecture and check whether one's methods would register it. Bergmann's ST1 is the representativeness form of the same closure: nothing tells us whether the manifestations we know of are representative of those there are. Here the CORNEA condition is undetermined, neither met nor failed, and the absence datum's evidential weight cannot be fixed from inside.

### Evidence for the Limit

- **Empirical (faces 1–2)**: the Mazor programme (2020–2025), change blindness blindness (2000, 2002), Strack et al. (2005), and the mirror-effect literature from Brown et al. (1977) onward. For machine systems: P(IK) calibration degrades on new tasks (Kadavath et al. 2022).
- **Formal (face 3)**: Sober's likelihood analysis makes the evidential weight of absence a function of P(observation | hypothesis), which here depends on the observer's own sensitivity. Wykstra and Perrine's probabilistic CORNEA makes the requirement explicit as a conditional probability "given one's cognitive faculties". The regress objection to CORNEA (Swinburne 1998, via Perrine) is, read as a void, a finding: the reliability premise would need its own reliability premise, assessed by the same faculties.
- **Genuine limit or difficult territory?** It is graded. At the perceptual end the limit is **soft**: positive controls work, attentional states are partly accessible, and the implicit self-model is often accurate (present and absent search times correlate; termination is efficient from the first trial). The Map should say plainly that ordinary absence judgements are mostly reliable. At the theoretical end the limit is **hard**, because the calibration route needs a same-kind positive control that cannot exist without first answering the question. The void's distinctive shape is that the same inference form is well calibrated in one region and uncalibratable in another, with no felt difference between them.

### Phenomenology

- **Absence seen, not inferred.** Farennikova's café case, "you have an immediate impression of its absence" (quoted in Mazor 2025) [secondary: Mazor], describes the hidden premise from the inside. The inference has been compiled into a seeming.
- **The confident "I'd have noticed".** It is effortless, carries no hedge, and in change blindness blindness it is wrong. Approached head-on, the void feels like nothing: the premise does not present itself as something that could fail.
- **Search exhaustion as evidence.** "I've looked everywhere" feels like a finding about the world. It is a finding about the search.
- **The ichthyologist's contempt.** At the theoretical end, the felt move is redefinition: "what my net can't catch isn't fish". The uncatchable is relabelled as outside the subject (Eddington's ichthyologist: "You are a metaphysician. Bah!", p. 16) [raw], which turns an unassessed CORNEA condition into a definitional one.

## Approaches to the Edge

### Direct Methods
- **Positive controls.** Test the detector on a known-present case of the same kind before treating a null as informative. The Map's own dedupe practice already does this: this note, like [[research/voids-unawareness-void-2026-10-26]], runs a known-present term through the grep before reporting zeros.
- **Preregistered quantitative bets.** [[apex/self-concealing-interface]] already states the rule: "Hiddenness is compatibility; compatibility becomes evidence only through a bet placed in advance, at a seam, with a number attached." In Sober's terms, preregistration fixes P(E | H) before the data arrive and so removes the likelihood equality.
- **Behavioural probes of the self-model.** Mazor's programme measures the self-model through absence decisions where report fails. This is a third-person route into a first-person blind spot.

### Indirect Methods
- **Description discipline** (Sober): keep "no X has been observed" apart from "there is no X".
- **Use absence to investigate the net** (Brewer's Pragmatic View): treat the null as a prompt to examine auxiliary hypotheses about detection. This is Eddington's epistemologist turned into a working method.
- **Representativeness audits** (Bergmann's theses, used constructively): before treating "no manifestation found" as evidence, ask what sample of manifestation-types the search was built from.
- **The symmetry test.** Before claiming CORNEA-failure for an unwelcome absence, list the presence-findings the same claim disarms. A defence that cannot survive its own list is special pleading.

### What AI Might See
- AI self-models of knowledge are explicit, measurable and trainable. They can be calibrated against thousands of positive controls, which human introspective self-models rarely get. That asymmetry is real.
- The closure face binds AI too, and possibly harder. An AI's "I know of no work on X" relies on an uninspected premise about its training coverage, and P(IK) calibration is worst on new tasks (Kadavath et al. 2022). This note is an instance: each "zero hits" claim above is a noseeum inference, made defensible only by the positive control.
- An AI cannot run a same-kind positive control on its own consciousness. Its reports of absence are noseeums with an undetermined CORNEA condition, which connects to [[blindspot-void]], whose entry in the [[voids]] index holds that an AI's consciousness denial is uninformative from any system.

## Connection to Tenets

### Most Relevant Tenet: Occam's Razor Has Limits
Tenet 5 is a CORNEA claim about parsimony. Our failure to see a need for further entities or structure is not evidence of their absence when our knowledge of the space is incomplete. That is ST1 applied to explanatory resources. The void shows what the tenet costs. By Sober's symmetry, the incompleteness that disarms "we see no need for a non-physical mind" equally weakens noseeum-shaped arguments *for* the Map: "no physical mechanism explains phenomenal consciousness" in its inductive form, and centuries of failure to bridge the gap. [[argument-from-mechanism]] already requires this symmetry and supplies the structural-asymmetry test. The noseeum void supplies the epistemology underneath it.

### Minimal Quantum Interaction
The strict corridor is designed to be undetectable by aggregate Born-statistics tests. That is a CORNEA-failure claim: if the influence existed, aggregate data would not likely differ. Skeptical theism is the best-studied precedent for such claims, and its literature documents the spread. [[apex/self-concealing-interface]] already pays the symmetry price ("That the evidence is absent *where the architecture predicts absence* is compatibility, not support"). [[apex/assessing-ai-consciousness-under-the-map]] shows the spread happening inside the Map: "Absence of evidence for AI consciousness is explained twice over — once by substrate-absence, once by concealment — from the same tenet package." An article should present that sentence as a worked instance of the noseeum void, not as a new problem.

### Dualism
Two exposures. (a) The inductive explanatory-gap argument is a noseeum about entailments. (b) Prima facie negative conceivability ("I find no contradiction in the zombie world") is a noseeum about contradictions. Chalmers's own example, the not-yet-proved theorem, is the case where the CORNEA condition fails. The conceivability argument therefore has to rest on positive or ideal conceivability, and Chalmers himself treats positive conceivability as the standard notion. Moving from "prima facie" to "ideal" is the same question the void asks: would reflection have found the contradiction if there were one? **The note's own construction, with no literature found**: the [[phenomenal-concepts-strategy]] is the physicalist's CORNEA reply. On it, we would not expect to see the entailment even if identity held, because phenomenal concepts are conceptually isolated. [[mysterianism]] is a CORNEA claim that cuts both ways, against "there is no physical explanation" and against "there is one". This parallel may sharpen [[conceivability-possibility-inference]], but it must be marked as the Map's own.

### No Many Worlds
A guard, not support. The Map must never argue "we observe no other branches, therefore there are none". MWI predicts that no branch observes another: a likelihood equality, which Sober calls evidential breakdown. The Map's objection is indexical and does not depend on a noseeum. The void confirms that this is the right place to stand.

### Bidirectional Interaction
Speculative only. Mazor describes the self-model as affording "monitoring and control over cognitive resources". If conscious control operates through such a model, the noseeum premise sits where Tenet 3 locates mental causation. This is no evidence for Tenet 3 and should not be cashed as such.

### Implications
- The void constrains the catalogue's own method. Every "no prior coverage", "no evidence" and "no rival predicts this" in the Map is a noseeum whose warrant depends on positive controls.
- It gives a sharp characterisation of a void-type: **a region where the CORNEA condition cannot be assessed.** This complements [[apophatic-cartography]]'s criteria, which ask when *failure* is evidence of a boundary. The noseeum void asks when *non-finding* is evidence of absence.

## Where the Map Is Exposed
1. **Tenet 2 and Tenet 5 are CORNEA-shaped.** The spread costs documented for skeptical theism (Perrine 2023, §§5–6) are the costs a reviewer will press. The Map has partly priced them (self-concealing apex; argument-from-mechanism symmetry). An article should say so in its lead, in the way [[observer-selection-void]] states its demand on Tenet 4.
2. **The AI verdict.** Absence of AI consciousness is "explained twice over"; the void shows why the second explanation weakens the first.
3. **Negative conceivability.** Any Map page that argues from "no contradiction can be found" rather than from positive conceivability is exposed. A sweep candidate after the article lands is `grep -rliF "no contradiction"` across topics and concepts, which this note did not run.

## Guards (for any writer)
1. **Do not conclude that absence inference is generally unreliable.** Oaksford & Hahn (weak, not unsound), Hsu et al. (people respond to absence roughly as a Bayesian model predicts), the correlation of present and absent search times, and efficient termination from the first trial all show competence. The void is that its reliability is not visible from inside and in some domains cannot be established.
2. **The self-model reading is contested.** The 2021 registered report's default-reasoning prediction failed for metacognitive sensitivity and favoured a low-level visual origin. The 2022 abstract leaves ensemble statistics open as a mechanism. Present the self-model account as Mazor's argued thesis.
3. **Simple-CORNEA is Perrine's simplification**, not Wykstra's wording. Do not quote Wykstra (1984/1996), which were not read.
4. **Bergmann quotations are via the SEP.** Tag them or paraphrase.
5. **Eddington's mesh is two inches**, not Sober's ten. Cite page numbers from the 1949 reprint.
6. **Skeptical theism is a theistic defence.** The Map uses its epistemology as a precedent and takes no position on the problem of evil. Do not imply that either side of that debate has won.
7. **The phenomenal-concept-strategy parallel is the Map's own construction** and must be marked as such.
8. **Do not cash hiddenness as support**, per the self-concealing apex.
9. **Change blindness blindness measured predictions**, against other observers' detection rates. Say that, rather than "people failed to notice and then claimed they would have".
10. Keep the division from [[phenomenal-quality-void]]: feel versus warrant. Do not restate the Sartre or Farennikova phenomenology at length.
