## Why no sixth candidate

The brief was assess-first: write a new note only if a candidate clearly stronger than the five appeared. None was sought by web search, for a reason the queue already recorded on 2026-10-05 (todo L1226): with two slots and five un-triaged notes, a sixth banked note cannot outrank them and LIFO makes minting strictly net-negative. No other empty cell of comparable clarity surfaced from the corpus scan.

## Method and its limits

Each note was read in full and scored by one hand against the four criteria, using the criterion text and the two failure modes the four-criteria page attaches to each. The scoring is calibration, not proof: passing does not make a void genuine, failing does not make it illusory. The four-criteria page's standing limit applies in full — one grader, no pre-registered rubric — so these grades are a comparison between five notes, not an independence score for any of them. The operational facts (counts, headroom, absorption greps) were measured this run and are reproducible; the grades are judgement.

Headroom below is `hard − 1 − body words` from `analyze_length` on 2026-10-06 (gate trips at `>=` 3000), and counts the reference list.
