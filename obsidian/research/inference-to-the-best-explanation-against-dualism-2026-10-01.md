**The analytic answer, in one paragraph.** IBE is the form under which the physicalist's closure and parsimony arguments are both run, so the reviewer is right that "both are compatible" is an answer to a *likelihood* challenge, not to an *explanatory-virtue* challenge. Against IBE the Map reaches the **compatible** tier on the reviewer's seven explananda and no higher — its own corridor reading ([[ensemble-level-epiphenomenalism]]) predicts the seventh explanandum, the absence of measurable non-physical inputs, so that premise discriminates nothing in either direction. The Map reaches **suggestive** only by adding explananda the physicalist's list omits (phenomenal character itself, the acquaintance asymmetry, content determinacy), and the status of those explananda as data is exactly what Type-B physicalism contests. The honest rejoinder costs three things: an explicit engagement with the phenomenal-concepts strategy rather than Type-A (the reviewer's §5.2 point); an admission that the dualist posit currently earns no predictive success; and acceptance that the Map's own PCT abduction is exposed to the same bad-lot objection it would raise against the physicalist.

## Major Positions

### The physicalist IBE (the reviewer's §5.1)
- **Proponents**: McLaughlin 2010 (explicitly, by title); Melnyk 2003 (as a posteriori scientific hypothesis); Papineau 2001/2002 (the closure induction); Stoljar's "Argument from Methodological Naturalism" (SEP) as the unlabelled form.
- **Core claim**: physical models unify neural dependence, lesion-selective deficits, stimulation and pharmacology effects, developmental and degenerative change, access/memory/report, inter-system causal interaction, and the absence of independently measurable non-physical inputs; identity (or realisation) is the best explanation of that unification; a dualist psychophysical ontology adds nothing explanatory and so is idle.
- **Form**: ABD1-style rival ranking on scope and simplicity (Lipton's loveliness), with parsimony as the tie-breaker when fit is equal (Melnyk's reported ordering).
- **Relation to site tenets**: directly opposed to [[tenets#^dualism|Tenet 1]]; the idleness premise is the exclusion form of the [[tenets#^bidirectional-interaction|Tenet 3]] challenge.

### The explanandum-dispute reply (Gertler; Levine; Chalmers)
- **Core claim**: IBE's ranking criteria apply only among theories that accommodate the same data; the dualist's arguments are precisely that physicalism does not accommodate the datum of phenomenal character. "We should be wary of taking the perceived threat to simplicity as grounds for skepticism about the data used to support dualism" (Gertler 2020).
- **Relation to site tenets**: this is the Map's existing position in [[philosophy-of-science-under-dualism]] (acquaintance asymmetry) and [[explanatory-gap]]. Its cost is that the Type-B physicalist accepts the datum and explains the gap conceptually (phenomenal concepts), so the dispute moves to whether the phenomenal-concepts strategy is a *lovelier* explanation of the gap than dualism — a second-order IBE the Map has not run. The reviewer's §5.2 makes the same point from the other side.

### The Bayesian deflation (Okasha, Lipton ch. 7, Henderson)
- **Core claim**: IBE's explanatory virtues are, or emerge from, priors and likelihoods. On this reading "both are compatible" is the full likelihood story — P(E | dualism) ≈ P(E | physicalism) for the seven explananda — and the residual disagreement is about priors.
- **Relation to site tenets**: documented positions in the IBE literature; the application to the dualism debate is a **Map reconstruction**. It fits the Map's own self-description — Tenet 1 is "a commitment the Map owns, not a result it reports" ([[tenets]] L55) — because a prior is exactly what a commitment is in this register. The cost: it concedes that the evidence does not move the dualist's credence, which is the compatible tier stated in Bayesian vocabulary.

### The bad-lot reply (van Fraassen 1989)
- **Core claim**: ranking the best of the candidates one has does not license belief unless one has reason to think the true theory is in the lot.
- **Relation to site tenets**: [[tenets#^occams-limits|Tenet 5]] is a special case ("simplicity is unreliable with incomplete knowledge"). Two-edged: the Map's PCT abduction ([[phenomenal-constitution-thesis#abductive-bet]]) is an ABD1 ranking over deflationism, weak liberalism and PCT, and inherits the same vulnerability.

## Key Debates

### Is IBE, rather than closure or parsimony, the master argument?
- **Sides**: Gertler and Stoljar both rank mental causation / closure as the most influential argument; Stoljar then says the methodological-naturalism (unification) argument "seems just as persuasive — in fact, rather more so". The reviewer treats IBE as the form that needs no closure premise and no NCC-to-identity entailment.
- **Map reconstruction**: the three are not rivals but levels. Parsimony is a ranking criterion inside IBE; the closure principle is, on Papineau's own presentation, an IBE conclusion from the empirical record; the reviewer's seven explananda are the unification case stated as the explanandum set. So the physicalist's master argument is IBE *with* closure as one of its outputs and parsimony as one of its criteria. A page that answers only parsimony (the three existing notes) or only closure (the closure survey) answers a part. Label: Map reconstruction; the "levels" claim is not found in any retrieved source.
- **Current state**: ongoing. No retrieved source disputes that the unification case is abductive.

### Does "both are compatible" answer IBE?
- **Sides**: the reviewer says no; the Map's NCC page ([[neural-correlates-of-consciousness]] L77 "NCC findings are compatible with all three") and the evidential-status discipline treat compatibility as a defensible floor.
- **Map reconstruction**: compatibility is a likelihood claim and is a complete answer to an argument of the form "E entails identity". It is not an answer to "identity is the loveliest explanation of E". The Map's register already has the vocabulary for the difference (compatible / suggestive / discriminating; *is compatible with* / *supports* / *can be recruited by*). What is missing is a page saying that against the IBE form the Map's claims sit at *compatible* and that the Map accepts this.
- **Current state**: the reviewer's charge stands as stated; the Map's reply is available but unwritten.

### Is the dualist's ontology explanatorily idle?
- **Sides**: Kim's exclusion (via Gertler: "no explanatory work remains for non-physical factors to perform"); the SEP's Lowe objection ("sacrificing simplicity for no gain in strength", attributed to Weir 2021b). Against: Gertler's point that exclusion assumes structure-and-dynamics description is "not only accurate but also exhaustive".
- **Map position**: Tenet 3 makes consciousness causally relevant, but [[tenets#^tenet-3-standing]] holds this at "available, not actual" and P-Q10 records no worked toy model. So the Map's current answer to idleness is a promissory note, and the corridor reading's defining feature — Born statistics preserved exactly (P-Q2) — means the Lowe objection applies with full force: the Map's interaction is, by design, "invisible to scientific observation". [[ensemble-level-epiphenomenalism]] already names this as "hidden idleness".
- **Current state**: unresolved inside the Map; the honest label is that the posit is *not yet* shown to be non-idle.

### Loveliness, likeliness and Tenet 5
- **Sides**: Lipton holds loveliness and likeliness "will tend to be coextensive" (per the review); van Fraassen holds "the converse most likely holds".
- **Map reconstruction**: the physicalist's IBE buys its force from loveliness (one framework, no extra laws — Gertler's "elegance"). Tenet 5 is the claim that in *this* domain the coextension fails because the lot is incomplete. That blunts the inference by denying the bridge from lovely to likely; it does not supply a lovelier dualist explanation. A rejoinder that leans on Tenet 5 therefore ends at "the physicalist's IBE is unlicensed here", not at "dualism is the better explanation" — which is a defensive, compatible-tier result.

## The Analytic Question: Tier Mapping

The question set by the task: which tier does the Map reach against IBE, and what would an IBE-based rejoinder cost? Taking the five candidate replies in the brief:

**(a) The explanandum is mis-specified.** *Documented position* (Levine 1983, Chalmers 1996 as standard references in the corpus; Gertler 2020 retrieved). IBE unifies the correlates; it does not explain phenomenal character; the dualist's extra ontology is not idle because it answers an explanandum the seven omit. Tier reached: **suggestive** — but only if phenomenal character is admitted as a datum, which the Type-B physicalist grants and then explains conceptually. Cost: the page must run the second-order IBE against the phenomenal-concepts strategy, which no Map page does as IBE; the corpus's [[phenomenal-concepts-strategy]] treatment would need a cross-reference. Note that [[parsimony-epistemology]] L90 already says Type-B's reply "does not restore parity" because parsimony is synchronic — that argument transfers to IBE only if loveliness is also synchronic, which Lipton's "if correct, provides the most understanding" formulation does not obviously grant.

**(b) The posit is not idle because Tenet 3 makes it causally relevant.** *Framework-conditional implication*. Tier reached: **compatible**, because the standing is "available, not actual" and no predictive success is claimed. Cost: the page must say in its own voice that the Map cannot currently answer the idleness premise with a result, only with a commitment; any sentence claiming the dualist ontology "does explanatory work" inherits the P-Q3/P-Q10 debt per [[tenets#^tenet-3-standing]].

**(c) Loveliness versus likeliness.** *Map reconstruction* from a documented distinction. Tier reached: **compatible** (defensive). Cost: Tenet 5 is a commitment; invoking it against IBE while running IBE for PCT requires stating why the PCT lot is complete enough and the physicalist's is not — a burden the Map has not discharged.

**(d) The absence-of-inputs premise is predicted by the corridor reading.** *Framework-conditional implication*, already in the corpus ([[ensemble-level-epiphenomenalism]] L39–45). This is a genuine reply: the seventh explanandum cannot favour physicalism over corridor dualism because both predict it. Tier reached: **compatible**, and the reply simultaneously concedes the point that makes the Lowe/Weir objection bite — a posit tuned to leave no trace is the paradigm of "no gain in strength". Cost: the page must present (d) and the idleness charge together, not (d) alone.

**(e) Bad lot.** *Documented position* (van Fraassen via SEP). Whether dualism is in the physicalist's lot depends on (a): if phenomenal character is not admitted as data, dualism is not a candidate explanation of anything in the set, and the physicalist's ranking never considers it; if it is admitted, the lot is widened and the ranking must be re-run. Tier reached: none directly; (e) is a constraint on the opponent's inference. Cost: symmetric exposure of the PCT abduction.

**Verdict on tier.** Against the IBE form the Map reaches *compatible* on the physicalist's explanandum set and *suggestive* on the widened set, with the widening itself contested at bedrock. It does not reach *discriminating*, and the register's own rule — a tenet may remove a defeater but may not upgrade the evidence level — forbids pretending otherwise. The correct page therefore says: the physicalist's IBE is the strongest form of the case; the Map's answer is that the explanandum set is incomplete and the lot is incomplete; both answers are commitments the Map owns; and the dualist posit's non-idleness is owed, not shown.

**What an IBE-based rejoinder would cost in commitments.** Three, in order of price. First, explicit engagement with Type-B physicalism as the live opponent, replacing the Type-A nod the reviewer's §5.2 criticises. Second, a stated disconfirmer for the idleness claim — what observation would show the posit is doing work — which per the "specify disconfirmers" rule in [[project/evidential-status-discipline]] the Map must be able to give and currently cannot beyond the P-Q10 toy-model debt. Third, a consistency statement about the Map's own abductions (PCT, [[cluster-integration-discipline]]'s "matches the shape of the cluster") so that the bad-lot and loveliness objections are not deployed asymmetrically.
