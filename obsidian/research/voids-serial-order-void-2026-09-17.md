# Research: Voids - The Serial-Order Void

**Date**: 2026-09-17
**Search queries used**: Lashley 1951 "problem of serial order in behavior" Hixon Symposium page-exact; Averbeck/Chafee/Crowe/Georgopoulos 2002 parallel processing of serial movements; Xie et al. 2022 geometry of sequence working memory; competitive queuing human MEG primacy gradient; Nozari/Pinet typing-error correction without awareness; Anthropic circuit-tracing planning in poems. Verification: Crossref works API (4 DOIs), Europe PMC `resultType=core` + `fullTextXML`, PMC HTML, publisher HTML grepped raw, and a downloaded page-scan of Lashley 1951 with page markers resolved.
**Voids category**: Mixed — core **Unexplorable** (the parallel plan is not merely unattended but is not the kind of thing the reporting channel receives), with an **Unexplored** margin at the machine-asymmetry edge.

## Executive Summary

Almost everything consciousness produces comes out in an order: a sentence, a melody, a sequence of keystrokes, a chain of reasoning. Lashley's 1951 argument is that the order is not in the elements and not in the idea — it is imposed by a third thing, a "schema of order" that operates on both. The serial-order void names what follows for a mind looking inward: you have the intent, and you have the produced sequence, and you never have the ordering. Three faces. **Imposition** — Lashley's negative result, that neither the words nor the thought carries temporal valence, so "The order is imposed by some other agent" (p. 116), and "The order is dissociated from the idea" (p. 118). **Queue** — the positive neural result Lashley postulated and could not test: the whole sequence is active *in parallel* before the first element executes, graded by rank, and the graded queue is what determines order (Averbeck et al. 2002 in prefrontal cortex; Kornysheva et al. 2019 for the human MEG case; Xie et al. 2022 for the representational geometry). **Slip** — the ordering machinery becomes visible only through its own failures, and even the repair can run without awareness: Pinet & Nozari (2022) found corrections made with no reported error in 19.2% and 10.3% of backspace trials across two datasets.

The disposition question is the interesting part. The [[fusion-void|fusion void]] closes by naming an explicit taxonomy — "where [[transit-void|transit]], [[inference-void|inference]] and [[decision-void|decision]] are operation-voids of cognition, fusion is perception's." That sentence names three cells and fills a fourth. **Production is the empty cell**, and serial order is what belongs in it. That is the strongest argument for a standalone slot this note can make. Against it: the section discipline is absorption. The measured obstacle to absorption is below — every conceptually correct host is at or past its length ceiling.

## Key Sources

### Lashley — "The Problem of Serial Order in Behavior" (1951)
- **URL**: https://languagelog.ldc.upenn.edu/myl/Lashley1951.pdf (page scan of the original)
- **Type**: Book chapter, in L. A. Jeffress (ed.), *Cerebral Mechanisms in Behavior: The Hixon Symposium*, New York: Wiley
- **Page range — resolve before citing**: the chapter proper runs **112–136** (Lashley's reference list ends on p. 136); the printed symposium discussion follows to p. 146, and citations in circulation give **both** 112–136 and 112–146. Fitch & Martins (2014, *Ann. N. Y. Acad. Sci.* 1316: 87–104) cite it as 112–146. Either is defensible; say which you mean.
- **Key points**:
  - Associative chaining cannot explain sequence: an element's position is not a property of the element. "The individual items of the temporal series do not in themselves have a temporal 'valence' in their associative connections with other elements. The order is imposed by some other agent." (p. 116)
  - Generalises past language immediately: the same holds "of all skilled movements or successions of movement" — a horse's gaits use the same per-leg contractions differently ordered; a musician's finger order comes from the score, "a set which is not inherent in the association of the individual movements" (p. 116).
  - The idea itself has no order. Following Pick, Lashley takes translation between languages of different sentence structure as evidence that "the set or the idea does not have a temporal order" (p. 118).
  - Section **"The Schema of Order"** (p. 118) states the residual hypothesis: "the mechanism which determines the serial activation of the motor units is relatively independent, both of the motor units and of the thought structure." His evidence is his own typing errors, and his summary of them is the void in one line: **"The order is dissociated from the idea."** (p. 118)
  - Syntax as separable operation: pig-Latin restructuring shows "syntax is not inherent in the words employed or in the idea to be expressed. It is a generalized pattern imposed upon the specific acts as they occur." (p. 119)
  - Pre-excitation: anticipation errors in typing and Spoonerisms suggest the elements are *already active together* before any of them is emitted — words held "in a state of partial excitation" (p. 119), released when the grammatical check is disturbed.
  - The three-part summary (p. 122): the activated elements; "the determining tendency, the set, or idea"; and third, "the syntax of the act," a "generalized pattern or schema of integration" imposable on a wide variety of specific acts — "This is the essential problem of serial order."
- **Tenet alignment**: Occam's Razor Has Limits, and Bidirectional Interaction. Lashley's is a negative argument — the simple account (order lives in the associations) is shown to fail, and what replaces it is an inferred agent nobody can observe from inside. If consciousness contributes anything to action at all, ordering is the layer at which its contribution is invisible to it.
- **Quote-fidelity caution**: Fitch & Martins (2014) quote Lashley's melody-reversal remark as "I can only do it by visualizing the music spatially and then reading it backward" (p. 129). The original reads: "I find that I can do it only by visualizing the music spatially and then reading it backward." (p. 129, confirmed in the scan). The page is right and the sense is right, but the wording is not verbatim — **take Lashley quotes from Lashley.**
- **Scan caveat**: the source is OCR of a 1951 print scan and is noisy in places. Every quotation above was read against its surrounding page image text and its page fixed by the running heads. Two quotations in the scan that a naive grep misses — "generalized / schemata of action" is split across a line break — were recovered by searching without the internal space.

### Averbeck, Chafee, Crowe & Georgopoulos — "Parallel processing of serial movements in prefrontal cortex" (2002)
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.162485599 (*PNAS* 99(20): 13172–13177; open access at PMC130605)
- **Type**: Paper
- **Key points** (abstract quoted verbatim from Europe PMC / PMC):
  - Frames itself as a direct test of Lashley: "A key idea in Lashley's formulation of the problem of serial order in behavior is the postulated neural representation of all serial elements before the action begins."
  - Monkeys copied geometrical shapes; ensemble activity patterns for the movement segments "were present during the time preceding the actual drawing."
  - The ordering is *in the gradient*: "The rank of the strength of representation of a segment in the neuronal population during this time, as assessed by discriminant analysis, predicted the serial position of the segment in the motor sequence."
  - And errors follow from the gradient: the error analysis "provided a neural basis for Lashley's hypothesis that errors in motor sequences would be most likely to occur when executing elements that had prior representations of nearly equal strength."
- **Tenet alignment**: Minimal Quantum Interaction. This is the cleanest available picture of a decision-like operation implemented as a graded competition among simultaneously active alternatives — the same architecture the Map's interface articles reach for when asking where a minimal influence could act, and one whose operation the acting subject does not witness.

### Kornysheva, Bush, Meyer, Sadnicka, Barnes & Burgess — "Neural Competitive Queuing of Ordinal Structure Underlies Skilled Sequential Action" (2019)
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2019.01.018 (*Neuron* 101(6): 1166–1180.e3; PMC6436939)
- **Type**: Paper
- **Key points** (abstract verbatim): MEG classification of finger-press sequences demonstrates "the 'competitive queuing' (CQ) of upcoming action representations, extending previous computational and non-human primate recording studies to non-invasive measures in humans"; CQ "reflects an ordinal template that generalizes across specific motor actions at each position"; and CQ "predicts participants' production accuracy."
- **Why it matters here**: it moves the queue face from monkey electrophysiology to intact awake humans, which is what a claim about *human* introspective access requires. The ordinal template is abstract and position-indexed — the mind holds a *rank scaffold* separate from what fills it, and nothing of the scaffold appears in experience.
- **Tenet alignment**: Dualism, weakly and honestly. The finding is neutral between frameworks; what it establishes is that the ordering substrate is real, structured, measurable from outside, and absent from the inside.

### Xie, Hu, Li, Chen, Song, Wang, Yang, Dehaene, Tang, Min & Wang — "Geometry of sequence working memory in macaque prefrontal cortex" (2022)
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abm0204 (*Science* 375(6581): 632–639)
- **Type**: Paper
- **Key points** (abstract verbatim): "The high-dimensional neural state space during the delay could be decomposed into a sum of low-dimensional subspaces, each storing the spatial location at a given ordinal rank, which could be generalized to novel sequences and explain monkey behavior. The rank subspaces were distributed across large overlapping neural groups, and the integration of ordinal and spatial information occurred at the collective level rather than within single neurons."
- **Why it matters here**: it gives the void a *geometry*, which is the property that made the [[fusion-void|fusion void]] unusual. Rank and content are carried on separable axes of one collective code, and the separation is visible only at the population level — not in any single unit, and not in any report.
- **Tenet alignment**: Occam's Razor Has Limits. The parsimonious first-person story ("I thought the sentence and said it") is not wrong so much as blank where the structure is.

### Pinet & Nozari — "Correction without Consciousness in Complex Tasks: Evidence from Typing" (2022)
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.5334/joc.202 (*Journal of Cognition* 5(1): 11; open access)
- **Type**: Paper
- **Key points**: across two typing datasets, participants pressed backspace and repaired an error while reporting no error at all — "There were 173 (19.2% of 900 responses with a backspace) such trials in dataset 1 and 397 (10.3% of 3817 responses with a backspace) in dataset 2." The paper also notes that the ERN, "an EEG marker of error detection, is also found in subconsciously reported errors, although its magnitude may be modulated by consciousness."
- **Why it matters here**: it closes the loop the slip face opens. Order goes wrong sub-personally, is *detected* sub-personally, and is *repaired* sub-personally — the full monitor-and-correct cycle can complete below report. Related theoretical background: Nozari, Dell & Schwartz (2011), *Cognitive Psychology* 63(1): 1–33, argue internal error detection is conflict-based within the production system rather than routed through comprehension, which is the reason a repair need never surface.
- **Tenet alignment**: Bidirectional Interaction — this is the sharpest test case the corpus could want. A control loop that senses, decides and acts, entirely outside awareness, sets the bar for what an interface-level contribution would have to be *more* than.

### Lindsey, Gurnee, Ameisen, Chen, Pearce et al. (Anthropic) — "On the Biology of a Large Language Model" (27 March 2025)
- **URL**: https://transformer-circuits.pub/2025/attribution-graphs/biology.html
- **Type**: Interpretability report (publisher HTML grepped raw; strings below confirmed present)
- **Key points**: contrasting pure improvisation with planning, the authors report that "the model often activates features corresponding to candidate end-of-next-line words prior to writing the line, and makes use of these features to decide how to compose the line," and that injecting a planned-word feature made "the model ended its line with the injected planned word in 70% of cases." Planned-word features were found in about half the poems examined.
- **Why it matters here**: an autoregressive model emits strictly one token at a time, and its own emitted text is the only thing it can read back. Yet its ordering is set by a parallel representation formed before emission — structurally the same arrangement as competitive queuing. The asymmetry is that the model's queue is *externally readable by attribution graphs*, and a human's is readable only by MEG and electrode arrays. Neither is readable by its owner.
- **Caution**: the word "plans" is the authors' framing of a mechanistic finding. Do not let it import intention. Cite the feature-activation and injection results, not the word.

## The Void

### Nature of the Limit

An *operation-void* in the sense the corpus already uses: consciousness holds both endpoints and none of the passage. It sits beside [[inference-void|inference]] (premises and conclusion, no move), [[transit-void|transit]] (before and after, no crossing), [[decision-void|decision]] (open weighing and sealed choice, no collapse) and [[fusion-void|fusion]] (components and percept, no combination). Its own endpoints are the *intent* and the *ordered output*; its missing passage is the serialisation.

What makes it more than a restatement of those four is that the discarded operand has a *known form*. The fusion void discards component estimates and reliability weights. The serial-order void discards a rank-graded parallel activation — and unlike a weight, that structure has been imaged, decoded and geometrically characterised in three species-and-method combinations. The void is defined by something we can describe in detail and cannot experience at all.

### Evidence for the Limit

Three independent lines, which is unusual for this catalogue:

1. **Negative/analytic** (Lashley 1951): order is not a property of the elements, and not a property of the idea. Something else supplies it. This is an argument, not a measurement, and it survives because nobody has located temporal valence in either place.
2. **Positive/neural** (Averbeck 2002; Kornysheva 2019; Xie 2022): the ordering variable exists, is parallel, is rank-graded, is abstract over content, and predicts both the order produced and the errors made.
3. **Apophatic/behavioural** (Lashley's typing corpus; Pinet & Nozari 2022): the operation shows itself only when it breaks, and even the breakage-and-repair can complete below report.

The deflation to test against is that this is ordinary sub-personal processing, no more void-like than digestion, and calling it a void inflates the catalogue. The reply is the one the fusion void already makes and should be reused: the question is not whether a process is sub-personal but whether experience carries a *marker* of what was discarded. A sentence arrives in awareness already ordered, presenting as though the order came with the thought — which is precisely the claim Lashley's translation and pig-Latin arguments defeat. The phenomenology actively misreports the architecture. That is stronger than mere absence.

### Phenomenology

Approaching this edge does not feel like straining. It feels like nothing, which is the characteristic signature. Speaking fluently, you have no sense of a queue draining. The nearest thing to a first-person datum is the *anticipation error* — typing a letter that belongs five words later, and noticing, for one instant, that the later word was somehow already present. Lashley built his whole case on such moments in his own typing while drafting the paper. They are the closest a person gets to feeling the parallel plan, and what they deliver is not access but a trace of access having been possible.

## Approaches to the Edge

### Direct Methods

None. Introspecting the queue would require a report channel whose own output is serial, so any report of the parallel plan is itself serialised by the operation under inspection. This is the recursive form the [[inference-void|inference void]] also has, and it should be stated in the same register: the instrument is made of the thing.

### Indirect Methods

Error induction (speeded typing, tongue-twisters, pig Latin) to raise the slip rate; population decoding to read the queue from outside; and the comparison of first-person report with the decoded gradient on the *same* trial, which is the experiment that would sharpen the void's boundary rather than merely assert it. Kornysheva's CQ-predicts-accuracy result is a step toward it.

### What AI Might See

The asymmetry is not that a model sees its own queue — it does not. It is that the queue is *third-personally legible* in a model at a resolution no human method matches, and legible by a route (attribution graphs over features) that has no biological analogue. So a language model is a system whose serial-order void is, from outside, nearly transparent and, from inside, exactly as opaque. That combination is rare and should be handed to [[non-human-minds-as-void-explorers|the void-explorer material]] rather than argued here.

## Connection to Tenets

### Most Relevant Tenet

**Bidirectional Interaction**, via an unwelcome route. The Pinet & Nozari result shows a complete sense-decide-act loop running below awareness in a task the subject believes they are performing. Any claim that consciousness contributes to the ordering of action now has to say what it adds *beyond* a loop that demonstrably closes without it. The Map should treat this as a constraint on the interface story, not as support for it.

### Implications

The serialisation of thought is the point at which consciousness most obviously receives rather than makes. If the [[decision-void|decision void]] is the Map's "most phenomenologically natural candidate site for non-physical influence," serial order is its immediate downstream neighbour, and it is a site where the mechanism has been mapped well enough to be *unpromising*. Recording that honestly is worth more than another candidate site.

- **Page range unresolved by choice, not by failure.** 112–136 (chapter) versus 112–146 (chapter plus printed discussion) are both in circulation; see the source entry. Pick one and be consistent.
