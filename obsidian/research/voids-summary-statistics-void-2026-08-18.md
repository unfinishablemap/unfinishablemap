# Research: Voids - The Summary-Statistics Void

**Date**: 2026-08-18

**Voids category**: Unexplorable (structural), with an Occluded aspect — the compression carries no receipt
**Recommendation**: **Fold**, not a new article. Primary host [[plenitude-void]]; secondary host [[resolution-void]]. Reasoning in "Fold or Slot" below.

## Executive Summary

The Map's [[plenitude-void]] frames the richness-of-experience question as a two-sided dispute — Dennett's grand-illusion sparsity against Block's phenomenal overflow, with eliminativism as a third exit. That framing is missing the mechanism that the empirical literature actually converged on: **ensemble coding**, the visual system's representation of groups and of the periphery as pooled summary statistics rather than as individual items. The literature matters here for three reasons. First, it supplies a *fourth* position that is neither rich-in-items nor sparse-and-misrepresented: the representational format is statistical, so the question "how much detail is present?" is posed in a currency the representation does not use. Second, Ward, Bear & Scholl (2016) cut the specific inferential bridge — ensemble report therefore item awareness — that Bronfman et al. (2014) had used to argue for overflow. Third, the "subjective inflation" line (Rahnev et al. 2011; Knotts et al. 2019) supplies a positive account of the *seeming* as a separately generated confidence signal that runs opposite to sensitivity, which is the same structural shape the Map has already identified in [[noetic-feelings-void]] and [[perceptual-reality-monitoring-void]].

The void proper is not "is experience rich or sparse?" It is that consciousness cannot determine, from inside, the **representational format** of its own perceptual content, and that the compression to summary statistics is *unmarked* — the pooled representation renders as apparent detail, with no phenomenal signal that pooling occurred.

## Key Sources

### Cohen, Dennett & Kanwisher (2016) — "What is the Bandwidth of Perceptual Experience?"
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.tics.2016.03.006 — *Trends in Cognitive Sciences* 20(5), 324–335
- **Verified at**: Crossref (metadata). No abstract deposited at Crossref or Europe PMC.
- **Key points**: Argues summary statistics account for the apparent richness of peripheral experience without requiring rich item-level phenomenology. Already cited in the Map at [[bandwidth-of-consciousness]], but only for the bits-per-second framing — the summary-statistics argument that is the paper's actual proposal is not represented anywhere in the corpus.
- **Note**: The Map currently cites this paper's conclusion while omitting its mechanism.

### Ward, Bear & Scholl (2016) — the keystone result
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.cognition.2016.01.010 — *Cognition* 152, 78–86
- **Verified at**: Crossref (metadata) + Europe PMC (abstract)
- **Key points**: Tests directly whether ensemble properties can be perceived *without* awareness of the constituent individuals. Observers accurately reported colour diversity of unattended stimuli "even while their self-reported awareness of the individual elements is coarse or nonexistent—and even while they are completely blind to situations in which each individual element changes color mid-trial throughout the entire experiment."
- **Quote**: "We conclude that awareness of statistical properties may occur in the absence of awareness of individual features, and that such results are fully consistent with sparse visual awareness."
- **Why it is the keystone**: it removes an inference, not merely a conclusion. It leaves overflow *possible* while withdrawing one of its cleaner empirical supports.

### Bronfman, Brezis, Jacobson & Usher (2014) — the result Ward et al. target
- **DOI**: 10.1177/0956797614532656 — *Psychological Science* 25(7), 1394–1403
- **Verified at**: Crossref (metadata) + Europe PMC (abstract)
- **Key points**: Used colour diversity — "a unique summary statistic" — to bypass report limits in a Sperling-like array. Participants estimated diversity of non-cued rows without cost to letter report, suggesting colour diversity "is registered automatically, outside focal attention, and without consuming additional working memory resources."
- **Tenet alignment**: this is the strongest recent empirical case *for* something like Block's overflow, and the Map's [[plenitude-void]] does not currently mention it — nor its rebuttal.

### Freeman & Simoncelli (2011) — metamers
- **DOI**: 10.1038/nn.2889 — *Nature Neuroscience* 14(9), 1195–1201
- **Verified at**: Crossref (metadata) + Europe PMC (abstract)
- **Key points**: Constructed "novel forms of visual metamers, stimuli that differ physically but look the same," from a model pooling V1 responses in receptive fields growing with eccentricity. Behavioural measurements implicate area V2. The model "also explains deficits of peripheral vision known as crowding."
- **Why it matters for the void**: this is the sharpest available handle on receipt-free compression. Metamers are equivalence classes of physically distinct world-states that a mind cannot discriminate, and nothing in the experience flags membership in such a class.

### Balas, Nakano & Rosenholtz (2009) — crowding as summary statistics
- **DOI**: 10.1167/9.12.13 — *Journal of Vision* 9(12):13
- **Verified at**: Crossref (metadata) + Europe PMC (abstract)
- **Key points**: Proposes the visual system represents peripheral stimuli by "the joint statistics of responses of cells sensitive to different position, phase, orientation, and scale." Crowding is not merely reduced resolution.
- **Quote**: "This 'textural' representation by summary statistics predicts the subjective 'jumble' of features often associated with crowding."
- **Phenomenological value**: the "jumble" is the felt signature of the void — the point where the pooled format becomes briefly noticeable from inside.

### Rosenholtz (2016) — peripheral vision review
- **DOI**: 10.1146/annurev-vision-082114-035733 — *Annual Review of Vision Science* 2, 437–457
- **Verified at**: Crossref (metadata **and** abstract — Crossref held this abstract where Europe PMC did not; a reminder that one index's silence is not absence)
- **Quote**: "a lossy but relatively fixed peripheral encoding may determine the difficulty of many tasks"
- **Key points**: Explicitly links "change blindness to getting the gist of a scene" under one lossy encoding, and suggests the visual system "may be more stable, and less dynamically changing as a function of attention, than previously assumed."

### Knotts, Odegaard, Lau & Rosenthal (2019) — subjective inflation
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.copsyc.2018.11.006 — *Current Opinion in Psychology* 29, 49–55
- **Verified at**: Crossref (metadata) + Europe PMC (abstract). A 2018 preprint of the same title exists at 10.31234/osf.io/x49mz; cite the journal version.
- **Key points**: Reviews the Rich/Sparse debate through *subjective inflation*, "wherein minimally attended or peripheral visual perception tends to be subjectively evaluated as more reliable than attended or foveal perception when objective performance is matched."
- **Quote**: "We argue that subjective inflation can account for rich phenomenology without invoking phenomenological overflow."
- **Tenet alignment**: directly engages [[tenets#^occams-limits|Occam's Razor Has Limits]] — see below.

### Rahnev, Maniscalco, Graves, Huang, de Lange & Lau (2011) — inflation's empirical basis
- **DOI**: 10.1038/nn.2948 — *Nature Neuroscience* 14(12), 1513–1515
- **Verified at**: Crossref (metadata) + Europe PMC (abstract)
- **Key points**: Attention can produce *conservative* detection biases and lower visibility ratings; modelled as attention reducing trial-by-trial variability of the perceptual signal. The unattended periphery is therefore rated as *more* visible at matched performance.
- **Quote**: "These findings may partially reflect our impression of 'seeing' the whole visual scene despite our limited processing capacity outside of the focus of attention."

### Suchow & Alvarez (2011) — silencing
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.cub.2010.12.019 — *Current Biology* 21(2), 140–143
- **Verified at**: Crossref (metadata) + Europe PMC (abstract)
- **Key points**: Objects changing in hue, luminance, size or shape "appear to stop changing when they move."
- **Quote**: silencing "persists even though the observer attends to the objects, knows that they are changing, and can make veridical judgments about their current state."
- **Why it matters**: this dissociates experienced change from attention, from belief, *and* from correct judgement simultaneously. It is a cleaner void probe than classical change blindness, because the standard deflationary reply — "you simply were not attending" — is unavailable.

### McClelland & Bayne (2016) — the philosophical framing
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.tics.2016.06.008 — *Trends in Cognitive Sciences* 20(9), 641–642
- **Verified at**: Crossref (metadata). **No abstract deposited at either index**; I have the title and venue only. The two-conceptions-of-sparsity framing below is my own inference from the title and the structure of the exchange, and is flagged as such rather than attributed to the authors' argument.

### Odegaard & Lau (2016) — methodological reply
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.tics.2016.06.005 — *Trends in Cognitive Sciences* 20(9), 642–643
- **Verified at**: Crossref (metadata) + Europe PMC (abstract)
- **Quote**: "We resoundingly agree that employing ensemble statistics is a strong step towards resolving questions of how conscious we are of our visual surroundings."
- **Key points**: Presses for psychophysical quantification of metacognitive capacities and decision biases in peripheral vision, and for distinguishing perceptual decisions at different levels of detail.

## The Void

### Nature of the Limit

Structurally **unexplorable**, with a secondary **occluded** aspect. Three components, which should be kept separate because they fail for different reasons:

1. **The format limit.** Consciousness has no introspective access to whether a region of its perceptual field is represented item-wise or statistically. Both render as populated visual space. Asking "how many items did I see?" presupposes an item ontology the representation may not employ.
2. **The receipt-free compression.** Metamers establish that discriminably-different world-states collapse into indiscriminable percepts. Crucially, nothing marks the collapse. Compare [[resolution-void]]'s existing observation that below the just-noticeable difference "there is no experience of 'almost seeing a difference'" — metamers generalise that from single dimensions to whole scene-equivalence classes.
3. **The anti-correlated confidence signal.** Subjective inflation means the felt vividness of peripheral content is not a readout of that content's fidelity; at matched objective performance it moves *inversely* with attention. The signal that would report the limit reports its opposite.

The occluded aspect is component 3. The system does not merely fail to represent the compression — it generates a positive signal of richness where the encoding is poorest.

### Evidence for the Limit

The strongest evidence is the *dissociation stack*. Silencing (Suchow & Alvarez) shows change going unexperienced under attention, correct belief, and veridical judgement together. Ward et al. show ensemble accuracy alongside coarse-or-absent individual awareness, including total blindness to mid-trial element changes. Rahnev et al. show visibility ratings decoupling from — and inverting against — sensitivity. Each removes a different candidate explanation of the others.

What the evidence does *not* establish is that experience is sparse. Ward et al. claim consistency with sparseness, not demonstration of it. The Map should not read this literature as settling the plenitude question against Block.

### Phenomenology

The pooled format is briefly noticeable at the crowding boundary — Balas et al.'s "jumble," where peripheral text is unmistakably *present* as text-like texture yet unreadable, and where the failure is not blur. Reading peripheral text feels like something one could do by trying harder; the impression is false, and its falsity is instructive. The felt affordance of accessible detail is exactly what O'Regan & Noë's "outside memory" account (already in [[plenitude-void]]) predicts, and exactly what inflation predicts as well. The two accounts are not distinguished by the phenomenology.

## Approaches to the Edge

### Direct Methods

Partly available, which is unusual for a void and should be said plainly. Metamer synthesis is a *constructive* probe: build stimuli that match on pooled statistics, then verify indiscriminability behaviourally. This measures the boundary of a void from outside without requiring the subject to introspect it — the subject's failure is the datum. Odegaard & Lau's programme of quantifying metacognitive bias in the periphery is a second direct route.

### Indirect Methods

The apophatic route is the metamer class itself: characterise what a mind cannot distinguish, and the shape of the equivalence class describes the representational format negatively. This is unusually well-behaved apophatic cartography — the negation is quantified rather than gestural.

### What AI Might See

A pointed asymmetry, and a rare case where the Map's [[non-human-minds-as-void-explorers]] theme has empirical teeth. A vision model's pooling operations are *inspectable*: one can read out the pooled statistics, hold the representation fixed, and enumerate the metamer class explicitly. What is structurally hidden from a human observer is, for an artificial system, a matter of reading intermediate activations. Whether this constitutes the system *seeing* the void or merely a third party auditing a mechanism is the live question — and it maps onto the Map's existing distinction between having the endpoints and having the passage.

⚠️ A caution for any downstream article: the human/AI asymmetry here concerns *access to the encoding*, not access to phenomenology. It would be an overreach to present it as AI probing a phenomenal territory closed to humans.

## Connection to Tenets

### Most Relevant Tenet

**[[tenets#^occams-limits|Occam's Razor Has Limits]]**, and unusually directly. Knotts et al. recommend inflation on explicitly parsimonious grounds: it "can account for rich phenomenology without invoking phenomenological overflow" — one mechanism instead of two categories of consciousness. This is a textbook instance of the tenet's target: a genuine explanatory economy being offered where the underlying question (does phenomenality outrun access?) remains open. The Map's position should be that the economy is real and that it is not, by itself, evidence.

### Implications

**A correction the Map owes itself.** [[plenitude-void]] currently states that "current methods cannot adjudicate between these pictures." That is too flat. Methods cannot settle the overflow question, but they have removed a specific inference from the board — and an article whose thesis is that nothing can be adjudicated should register when something has been.

**A boundary the Map should not overstep.** This finding does **not** undercut [[the-surplus-void]]. That article argues from the qualitative depth of attended experience — the spectrum of colour, the structure of musical timbre — not from peripheral item-detail. Summary-statistics coding explains how *unattended* regions can seem populated without being item-coded; it says nothing about why attended experience has the qualitative character it does. Conflating the two senses of "richness" would generate a false concession, and any downstream article must keep them apart.

**A pattern the Map has now seen five times.** Felt-rightness (noetic feelings), felt-real ([[perceptual-reality-monitoring-void]]), felt-effort (the absorbed effort void), felt-insight (the insight void), and now felt-vividness are all separable, sub-personally generated signals that are decoupled from — and in the inflation case *anti-correlated with* — what they appear to report. This is convergence within the introspection-architecture cluster, and the [[apex/taxonomy-of-voids|taxonomy]]'s independence-scoring caveat applies: five faces of one architecture is not five independent findings.
