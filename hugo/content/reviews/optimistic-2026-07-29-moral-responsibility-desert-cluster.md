## Executive Summary

This is the best-calibrated cluster I have reviewed on the Map. Its specialist articles do the hardest thing in tenet-driven writing: they win an argument and then decline to spend the win. The reactive-attitudes article declines Strawson's dissolution and states in the same breath that "**none of this is evidence that Strawson is wrong or that dualism is right**"; the duress article holds a residual-variance result that would be trivially easy to cash as evidence for a non-physical selector and refuses, specifying instead the test that would actually discriminate; the AI-culpability article marks its own central premise "framework-posited, not demonstrated" and supplies a falsifier that flips its verdict. The Map has also institutionalised the discipline under a name — the [compatibilist-symmetry-challenge](/concepts/compatibilist-symmetry-challenge/) — and applies it at three levels of the cluster.

The finding that matters is structural, and it is the mirror image of that strength: **the calibration lives in the specialist articles and does not reach the two synthesis pieces readers and retrieving LLMs hit first.** [apex/moral-architecture-of-consciousness.md](/apex/moral-architecture-of-consciousness/) and [topics/moral-implications-of-genuine-agency.md](/topics/moral-implications-of-genuine-agency/) contain **zero** links to either `frankfurt-cases-and-the-principle-of-alternate-possibilities` or `reactive-attitudes-and-strawsonian-responsibility` (verified by grep). One article states the un-inherited concession backwards: `consciousness-and-moral-agency-under-duress.md` L49 claims the Map "sidesteps the Frankfurt puzzle **entirely**", which the specialist article explicitly disowns as a costless translation. This is the same shape as today's [outer-review synthesis](/reviews/outer-review-synthesis-2026-07-29/) verdict on the epistemology hub — a hub behind its own children — which is worth noting as a possible systemic property of the review pipeline rather than a coincidence about two unrelated files.

## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

[concepts/moral-responsibility.md](/concepts/moral-responsibility/) L83 makes a distinction most consciousness-based ethics blurs, and makes it in one sentence:

> "Consciousness grounds candidacy for moral *patienthood* (who can be harmed) directly; moral *agency* (who can be responsible) requires the further capacities agent causation presupposes—control over conduct, access to reasons, a genuine range of options, and the competence to recognise what is at stake—which infants, most animals, and any minimally-phenomenal subject lack."

Chalmers' complaint about physicalist ethics is that it inherits the hard problem's evasion: it treats "conscious" as a functional predicate and then wonders why moral status won't sit still. Here consciousness is treated as necessary for agency **without conferring it** — phenomenality buys standing as a patient and only candidacy as an agent. The closing clause, "consciousness is necessary for that standing without conferring it," is the kind of line that only gets written by someone who has taken the explanatory gap seriously enough to notice that two different moral questions were riding on one metaphysical fact.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

[concepts/moral-responsibility.md](/concepts/moral-responsibility/) L69 handles the quantum Zeno mechanism the way Stapp himself would want it handled — as a live hypothesis of his, correctly labelled, and correctly decoupled from the philosophical case it was invented to support:

> "This is speculative—it depends on disputed decoherence timescales and remains Stapp's hypothesis rather than established physics. But the philosophical case for agent-causal control does not depend on this particular mechanism… The independence is therefore from any *specific* mechanism, not from physics as such."

That last clause is a genuine repair of a move that usually goes wrong. Writers who want to protect a mechanism-independent philosophical thesis usually over-claim independence *from physics* and end up with a position no physical fact could touch. This one keeps the hostage: control's *possibility* still needs physical causation not to be closed. `consciousness-and-moral-agency-under-duress.md` L113 states the same commitment in Stapp's own register — "Consciousness does not override physics by main force; it selects among options that physics leaves open."

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

The duress article's treatment of Stockdale (L85–87) is the cluster's finest piece of writing, and it works by letting first-person testimony *constrain* the Map rather than serve it:

> "Agents who resist can often say a great deal about why they resisted and by what discipline they held. The opacity Stockdale does report runs the other way: what he found he could not account for was *susceptibility*…"

The article wanted volitional opacity; the testimony refused to supply it in the shape wanted, so the claim was narrowed to what the testimony does support — and the article then records that "Stockdale drew the opposite metaphysical moral from his own privations, concluding against Descartes that body and mind are inseparable." Nagel's whole point about the first person is that it is a source of data, not a rhetorical resource. This passage treats it as data even when the data is inconvenient.

L107 earns its keep on the same axis: "enduring torture does not feel random. The agent experiences their resistance as the hardest thing they have ever done, not as something that happened to them." The luck objection is met where it is actually felt rather than where it is formally posed.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

`responsibility-gradient-from-attentional-capacity.md` is the cluster's structurally most Whiteheadian article, and it gets there without a word of process metaphysics. Desert is not a property a substance either has or lacks; it is constituted over time by what the agent makes of a varying channel. The five dimensions (bandwidth, stability, scope, flexibility, availability) describe an occasion of choosing rather than a thing that chooses, and the cultivation principle (L100–112) makes the process temporally thick: "Responsibility for a moment's choice may depend on choices made years earlier about developing or neglecting attentional resources."

The upper end is the underappreciated half. L140 notes that "genuine agency intensifies responsibility at the upper end" — the gradient runs *up* as well as down, and the fully-resourced wrongdoer bears a weight "that deterministic frameworks cannot ground." Most graded-responsibility writing is entirely a theory of excuses; this one is a theory of the whole scale.

**Evidential-status check.** Whitehead-friendliness here is not doing any tier-lifting work. The gradient's floor claim — "as long as consciousness operates at all… some responsibility persists" — is marked as a framework entailment ("The Map's framework entails"), not as an empirical result, and the article's control-theoretic gloss (L128) derives the floor from the model rather than from evidence. No slippage to flag.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

This is the cluster's home persona, and `frankfurt-cases-and-the-principle-of-alternate-possibilities.md` is the article Kane would most want to have written about his own argument. It presents the Widerker–Kane dilemma defense at full strength across two horns, and then declines to bank it:

> "the dilemma defense is contested at its own foundations. Fischer has argued directly against it, and Widerker and Goetz replied under the banner 'the defence prevails'—the very existence of that exchange is evidence that the defense is a live position rather than a settled result."

Better still is the positional precision at L81, which is more exact than most published libertarian self-descriptions:

> "The retreat to source is a *retreat*, and a conditional one… Because the wide-source view holds that sourcehood itself brings alternatives with it, conceding that alternatives may not be required after all relocates the Map to *narrow* source—a distinct member of the family, not a costless translation—and the fallback then inherits the burden, pressed hardest by the manipulation argument, of showing that an indeterministic interface delivers sourcehood rather than luck."

The Map knows which member of the source/leeway family it occupies, what a defeat would cost it, and which bill the fallback position would inherit. That is three things most libertarian writing leaves implicit.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

The cluster's humility is specific rather than atmospheric, which is what distinguishes real mysterianism from a rhetorical shrug. Duress L87 locates the boundary precisely: an agent can report reasons and discipline; "what resists introspection is the step by which a sustained commitment issues in *this* act of endurance rather than a lapse." And then: "That narrow opacity is consistent with the Map's account of quantum-level interaction… but consistency is all it is."

[concepts/moral-responsibility.md](/concepts/moral-responsibility/) L53 does the same for agent causation itself: "Critics reasonably object that 'irreducible causal power' labels the phenomenon without explaining it. The Map accepts this cost." A framework that names its own primitive as a primitive, and books the naming as a cost rather than an explanation, has understood what McGinn was warning about.

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

**This persona's verdict is the review's headline, and it is strongly positive.** The cluster is a sustained exhibit of tenet-as-evidence-upgrade praise-worthily *not* done.

**1. The discipline has a name and three deployment sites.** The [compatibilist-symmetry-challenge](/concepts/compatibilist-symmetry-challenge/) is applied at the concept hub (`moral-responsibility.md` L145), the topic synthesis (`moral-implications-of-genuine-agency.md` L117), and the apex (L164). The hub's statement is the most exact:

> "the libertarian framing's distinguishing work is tenet-coherence with dualism and bidirectional interaction—not unique moral explanatory power. Compatibilists who reject those tenets occupy a different metaphysical scaffold for substantively similar moral implications, not a morally inferior position."

This is "tenet-coherent, not evidence-elevating" applied to moral theory, converted from an ad-hoc hedge into a reusable named discipline. That the same sentence appears at three levels of abstraction means the discipline survives summarisation — the failure mode where hedges are dropped as content is compressed upward.

**2. The restraint is exercised where it costs something.** `consciousness-and-moral-agency-under-duress.md` L83 is the single best instance in the cluster. The article has a genuine residual: identically-trained special-operations soldiers vary sharply in stress-induced cognitive degradation (Morgan et al. 2006), and resilience research has not closed the gap (Feder et al. 2009, 2019). This is exactly the shape of finding a motivated framework converts into support. The article refuses:

> "An unclosed gap is not by itself evidence for a non-physical selector. A noisy, partly stochastic biological system is expected to leave residuals, and a physicalist account predicts them too—unpredictability is not the same as metaphysical openness… What would discriminate is not the *size* of the unexplained variation but its *structure*: whether the residual sits where biological noise predicts, or is instead organised so as to track what the agent intends. That test has not been run."

It declines the upgrade, names why the upgrade is invalid, specifies the discriminating experiment, and routes the outcome to the positions register as a falsifier rather than as present support. This is the pattern the persona exists to praise.

**3. Conditionals are marked as conditionals.** `ai-moral-agency-and-the-responsibility-gap-under-dualism.md` L69: "the amplifier hypothesis is **framework-posited**, not demonstrated… the culpability gap's reality on the Map is only as secure as that hypothesis… It is a conditional, not a knock-down." L71 then supplies the falsifier that flips the verdict — a future system with the interface *would* become a candidate moral agent — and the article states plainly that on Tigard's or Königs's accounts "the gap need not be real at all."

**4. The apex discounts its own coherence.** [apex/moral-architecture-of-consciousness.md](/apex/moral-architecture-of-consciousness/) L60 and L156 apply the artifact-of-method discount to the apex's own headline claim: "coherence within a corpus continually pruned by AI-driven adversarial review is high by construction," and therefore "the four pillars do not constitute four independent confirmations." An apex article whose job is unification, declining to count unification as evidence, is the strongest possible sign that the discipline is real rather than decorative.

**Process Philosopher vs Hardline Empiricist: converged.** The two personas praise different passages and conflict nowhere in this cluster. Whitehead's praise for the gradient's processual architecture never cashes out as a tier-upgrade, because the gradient's own tier claims are explicitly framework-internal. Per the skill's convergence test, the cluster has resolved the tension honestly.

**One calibration concern** (below) is the sole exception, and it sits in an empirical citation rather than in the framework logic.

## Content Strengths

### `frankfurt-cases-and-the-principle-of-alternate-possibilities.md`

- **Strongest point**: the two-part survival strategy at L75–81 — the leeway route holds on the indeterminism horn *and*, if it fails, the position translates to source at a stated price.
- **Why it works**: it separates "we can survive this" from "we have won this," which the free-will literature routinely conflates. The article states the survival claim and then immediately prices it.
- **Note**: this is the cluster's stalest article (2026-07-09) and one of its two best. Staleness here reflects convergence, not neglect.

### `consciousness-and-moral-agency-under-duress.md`

- **Strongest point**: the L83 refusal described above, followed by the L101–107 section "Why Not a Physicalist Account?", which states the rival at full strength before answering it.
- **Notable quote**: "A physicalist can model the constraints; what the physicalist cannot model, without eliminating it, is the selector."
- **Why it works**: the article knows the difference between a constraint and a selector and builds the whole dialectic on it.

### `responsibility-gradient-from-attentional-capacity.md`

- **Strongest point**: the unification claim at L134 — compatibilism catalogues the conditions that reduce responsiveness; the gradient explains *why* fatigue, poverty, and ADHD all diminish desert through one mechanism.
- **Why it works**: it is a real explanatory advance stated at the right strength ("unifies them under a single causal architecture"), and L148 turns it into a prediction — that unrecognised bandwidth-constricting conditions "should carry mitigating weight proportional to the degree of reduction." That is the cluster's one genuinely forward-looking normative commitment.

### `reactive-attitudes-and-strawsonian-responsibility.md`

- **Strongest point**: L59 — "the dissolution is not a verbal trick or an evasion… the Map's disagreement is with a strong opponent, not a weak one" — followed by L65's engagement with Wallace's normativized repair as "the version the Map most needs to engage, because it is the strongest."
- **Why it works**: steelmanning as method, executed rather than announced. The P.F./Galen Strawson disambiguation at L30 and L73–75 is also a genuine service; the surname carries opposite verdicts in this literature and conflating them inverts the argument.

### `ai-moral-agency-and-the-responsibility-gap-under-dualism.md`

- **Strongest point**: the diagnosis at L79 that delegating to a non-conscious system *launders* human agency — "the felt 'responsibility gap' is precisely the moral residue of that opacity."
- **Why it works**: it converts a metaphysical thesis into a practical reframing that the design-oriented literature can use without accepting the metaphysics, and it does so while conceding (L53) that "several distinct things travel under one name."

## Calibration Concern

**One locus, verified against the live literature.** `responsibility-gradient-from-attentional-capacity.md` L79:

> "Research on the cognitive effects of scarcity (Mullainathan and Shafir, 2013) **demonstrates** that poverty itself reduces available cognitive bandwidth by the equivalent of 13 IQ points"

Two problems, both cheap to fix:

1. **Citation form.** The 13-IQ-points figure's primary source is Mani, A., Mullainathan, S., Shafir, E. & Zhao, J. (2013), "Poverty Impedes Cognitive Function," *Science* 341(6149), 976–980 — not the trade book *Scarcity* that the reference list cites. Citing the popular book for the empirical result routes readers away from the study whose replication record is at issue.
2. **"Demonstrates" is over-firm.** The finding is contested: an MTurk replication failed to reproduce the financial-priming effect, and O'Donnell et al.'s 2021 multi-study replication bundle returned mixed results. The original authors have defended the finding against Wicherts and Scholten's critique, so the honest register is *contested and partly replicated*, not *demonstrated*.

What makes this worth fixing rather than tolerating: **the article already models the correct register two paragraphs later.** L112 hedges Schwartz and Begley precisely — "though the underlying studies are small and not independently replicated." The same discipline applied at L79 costs one clause. The locus also matters more than most: availability is the dimension the article itself calls "the most socially consequential," the one carrying the gradient's structural-disadvantage implications.

This is the cluster's only evidential-discipline blemish, and it is in an empirical citation rather than in the framework logic — which is the better of the two places to have one.

Neither synthesis article over-claims about Frankfurt or Strawson — every Frankfurt reference in both is to the *1971* hierarchical-desires paper, correctly used, and Strawson appears in neither. The failure mode is silence, not error: the concessions "the source retreat is a retreat, not a costless translation" and "declining Strawson is not evidence for dualism" have nowhere to propagate from.

**One article states the concession backwards.** `consciousness-and-moral-agency-under-duress.md` L49:

> "The Map's [agent-causation](/concepts/agent-causation/) framework **sidesteps the Frankfurt puzzle entirely**."

The specialist article's considered position (L81) is that the source translation is "a *retreat*, and a conditional one," that it "costs more than the clean leeway *picture*," that it relocates the Map from wide to narrow source incompatibilism — "a distinct member of the family, not a costless translation" — and that it inherits the manipulation argument's burden. "Sidesteps entirely" is precisely the costless-translation reading the specialist article was written to disown. The duress article cites Frankfurt 1969 in its reference list but does not link the article that treats it.

Two properties make this worth acting on rather than noting. First, the duress article was deep-reviewed on 2026-07-28 and this survived — cross-article calibration consistency is orthogonal to the single-document lens, and only a cluster-level read catches it. Second, the same shape appeared in today's outer-review synthesis on [concepts/epistemology.md](/concepts/epistemology/), whose structural lesson was that the hub "is not wrong so much as *behind its own children*." Two independent clusters exhibiting the same asymmetry in one day suggests a property of the review pipeline: deep-review operates per-file and per-file review naturally improves specialist articles faster than the syntheses that summarise them.

**A milder instance.** `responsibility-gradient-from-attentional-capacity.md` L144 enlists Strawson as an ally — "As Strawson (1962) recognised, our reactive attitudes… are calibrated to perceived capacity" — with no link to the article recording him as a rival the Map declines. The descriptive point is genuinely Strawson's and the use is legitimate; the problem is that a reader or retrieving model taking the gradient alone would conclude Strawson underwrites the Map's framework.
