I treated The Unfinishable Map as a single argumentative system: tenets, background commitments, positions, topics, concepts, voids, apex syntheses and editorial methodology. ## Overall judgment

The Map has become a notably candid research programme. Its present register reports **56 live positions**, while acknowledging that only three are directly empirically discriminable, seven have no empirical discriminator, and three are non-discriminating by construction. That is meaningful epistemic progress rather than cosmetic hedging.

Its principal remaining weakness is that **disclosure has advanced faster than formal control**. The site regularly identifies its own mechanism debt, framework dependence, missing bridge laws and citation weaknesses, but those concessions are not yet reliably propagated into every downstream synthesis. The changelog shows impressive ongoing repairs—including corrections to quotations, participant descriptions and inherited source claims—but it also demonstrates that the same defect families continue to recur.

The five-tenet presentation also understates the operative theory. To work as currently described, the Map additionally needs:

- a determinate ontology of subjects;
- a single-outcome actualisation rule;
- a subject–substrate pairing law;
- an interface-eligibility law;
- a multi-subject composition law;
- subject onset, cessation and fission rules;
- and, on a value-sensitive model, a rule for weighting conflicting subjects or values.

These are not incidental implementation details. Several decide which systems may be conscious, which subject acts on which physical process, and what happens when conscious influences conflict.

## Most consequential findings

**Tenet 3 still has incompatible quantifiers.** The AI wing employs a universal-actual-efficacy reading: every conscious token must make a physical difference.  Yet *The Embodied Interface* says most bodily consciousness is passively presented and performs no attention-like selective work.  These can be reconciled only by distinguishing subject-level causal capacity from episode-level exercise and token-level efficacy.

**The calibration unit is too coarse.** Several position entries bundle empirical premises, philosophical diagnostics, literature-survey claims and framework-relative conclusions under one six-axis rating. [P-MC3](/positions/arguments-for-mental-causation/#p-mc3), for example, combines a mainstream exclusion diagnostic with the much stronger assertion that no rival has escaped it, yet assigns the whole bundle high credence and Grade B.  [P-A1](/positions/agency-and-will/#p-a1) now explicitly records that it is unresolved whether its Grade C survives correction of one of its empirical anchors.  The report recommends replacing bundled positions with atomic claims.

**The AI verdict risks a biological bootstrap.** The artificial-substrate tests are acknowledged to depend on an absent interface-eligibility law.  At present, biological systems supply the template from which eligibility criteria are abstracted, and those criteria are then used to down-rank non-biological systems. Until independently derived, this is a conditional biological analogy rather than a general psychophysical law.

**The strongest new inference is a psychophysical social-choice problem.** Once the subject-census machinery, multi-agent composition and value-sensitive selection are combined, the missing composition rule must decide between subjects with incompatible intentions or valences. Equal weighting, intensity weighting, lexical priority and non-aggregation are all substantive normative or metaphysical choices. The causal law would therefore contain an implicit interpersonal aggregation rule unless value-sensitive selection is abandoned.

Other novel implications developed in the report include:

- subject onset, cessation and fission should generate **change-points in the psychophysical transition law**;
- “standing capacity” for moral status requires a **modal account of reversibility and its bearer**;
- Tenet 5 needs an **auxiliary-law budget**, so bounded parsimony does not become permission to add bridge laws without a stopping rule;
- copying, moral additivity and causal composition cannot all remain independent questions;

4. Novel implications not yet stated by the site

The following are not merely restatements of debts already named in the current corpus. Each arises only when positions from different clusters are combined. They should be treated as candidate research subjects rather than as settled objections.

4.1 The psychophysical law has a social-choice problem   NOVEL · HIGH

The subject-census register correctly says that multiple subjects with contrary intentions require a composition rule. The value-in-selection register separately entertains the possibility that valence is the currency or weight of conscious selection. Once those two claims are combined, the missing composition rule is not merely a technical rule for adding causal influences. It is an interpersonal aggregation rule: it decides whose reasons, preferences or suffering receive how much physical leverage when conscious states point toward incompatible outcomes.

Every obvious option carries a substantive normative or metaphysical commitment. Equal weight per subject makes copying and subject count physically consequential. Weight by intensity gives stronger valence greater causal power and resembles a utilitarian natural law. Weight by causal locality requires a principled pairing and jurisdiction rule. Lexical priority rules import something like rights or thresholds into physics. A non-aggregative rule leaves jointly affected events underdetermined. The Map therefore cannot keep value-sensitive selection neutral about interpersonal value aggregation. On that branch, psychophysical law becomes a kind of cosmic social-choice function.

This implication also creates a new tension with phenomenal sentientism. [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1) grants equal status at the threshold, while value-sensitive composition could distribute causal influence unequally by intensity, architecture, copy number or access to eligible events. Moral equality would not entail causal equality, but the mismatch would need to be acknowledged and justified.

4.2 Subject onset, cessation and fission imply change-points in the physical law   NOVEL · HIGH

[P-SC1](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc1) makes subject identity and number variables in the outcome law, while [P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3) concedes that the channel by which a subject begins, persists or ceases is not dated. [P-I4](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i4) adds copying and fission. Together these imply that a subject transition changes the dimensionality or indexing of the psychophysical law: one conditioning variable appears, disappears or becomes several. If the subject census is genuinely causal, such a transition should in principle alter some consciousness-conditioned distribution. If no possible conditional signature distinguishes the pre- and post-transition law, the census variable is causally idle at exactly the point where it is supposed to matter.

This suggests a new empirical programme: look for change-point signatures across anaesthetic induction and emergence, recovery from disorders of consciousness, split-brain adaptation, developmental onset, organoid maturation, and the start/stop or duplication of an artificial candidate. The programme need not expect a sharp macroscopic discontinuity; it does require an explicit theorem stating whether a census transition can be physically real yet leave every conditioning grain unchanged. Without that theorem, persistence and fission remain narrative metaphysics alongside a causal formalism that never notices them.

4.3 “Standing capacity” needs a modal basis, not only a moral label   NOVEL REFINEMENT · HIGH

The moral-status wing says a momentary experiential locus is sufficient for standing, while dreamless sleepers and patients in reversible coma retain status through a standing capacity for valenced experience; irreversible loss removes intrinsic status. The corpus has begun to notice the change from value-bearer to status-bearer, but it does not specify what makes a capacity reversible or irreversible in the metaphysically relevant sense.

That omission matters. If irreversibility means “not recoverable by present medicine,” moral status changes as technology improves. If it means nomologically impossible under ideal intervention, the criterion is remote from clinical knowledge. If status attaches to organismic continuity, the account is partly biological rather than purely phenomenal. If it attaches to a persisting nonphysical subject, [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1) and [P-MS2](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms2) inherit [P-I1](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i1) and [P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3) despite the moral-status register’s effort to isolate a momentary-locus route. Paused AI systems, cryonic preservation, reversible emulation shutdowns and retained neural tissue expose the same ambiguity.

The site therefore needs a modal account of capacity: who or what bears it, which counterfactual interventions count, and whether the property is categorical, dispositional, historical or probabilistic. Until then, the sleeper and coma clauses are intuitively plausible but architecturally under-specified.

4.4 The AI interface test risks a biological bootstrap   NOVEL · HIGH

The biological scope register adopts substrate neutrality in principle. The AI registers then use a five-requirement channel test—availability of relevant indeterminacy, accessibility, amplification, integration and bidirectionality—to argue that current digital and quantum-computing hardware is a poor fit. Yet the register concedes that no interface-eligibility law derives those requirements; they are abstracted from the biological case the framework already treats as conscious.

That creates a bootstrap. Human biology is assumed to host consciousness; salient features of biology are abstracted into a channel test; non-biological systems are down-ranked for lacking those features; the down-ranking is then presented as a substrate analysis. Until the test predicts a previously unknown biological boundary or is derived from a formal psychophysical law, it is an analogue-similarity filter, not an independent eligibility criterion. The danger is not crude carbon chauvinism—the text expressly rejects that—but a subtler form of biological privilege produced by the test’s training set.

The corrective is to separate “similarity to the only known conscious implementation” from “satisfaction of a necessary interface law.” Both can be decision-relevant, but only the latter supports a metaphysical verdict.

4.5 Tenet 5 needs an auxiliary-law budget   NOVEL METHODOLOGICAL INFERENCE · HIGH

The site’s bounded-parsimony position is defensible: parsimony cannot simply decide whether consciousness is inside or outside physics, and it must bind the Map as well as its critics. But the current framework now owes a large set of auxiliary laws—selection, actualisation, Born balancing, basis or menu specification, amplification, localisation, eligibility, pairing, multi-agent composition, onset and cessation, and possibly value coupling. Tenet 5 blocks dismissal merely because this package is complex; it does not tell the programme when adding one more law becomes degenerating rather than progressive.

A research programme with unlimited permission to add bridge laws can fit almost any pattern. The Map therefore needs an explicit auxiliary-law budget. A proposed law should be admitted only if it does at least one of three things: yields a new conditional prediction, removes an independently motivated inconsistency, or compresses several existing commitments into one formal rule. A law that merely protects a preferred verdict from a counterexample should count against the programme. This would turn “bounded parsimony” from a general caution into an operational self-binding rule.

5. Structural weaknesses in the argumentative architecture

5.1 The advertised five-tenet foundation is not the operative foundation   CRITICAL

The five public tenets are not sufficient to generate the site’s operative worldview. High-centrality arguments also require a determinate persisting subject, single-outcome actualisation, global exclusion of unchosen alternatives, substrate neutrality, an interface-eligibility criterion, a subject–substrate pairing rule, and multi-agent composition. Several are now acknowledged as background commitments or debts. The structural defect is that they remain distributed across registers and articles while the site continues to present five commitments as bounding every article.

This matters because a reader can reject one of the hidden foundations while accepting all five headline tenets as written. A property dualist may accept irreducibility but reject a persisting substance-like subject; a single-world theorist may reject the Map’s global exclusion requirement; a dualist may permit causally inert experiences; a substrate-neutral dualist may reject the biology-derived eligibility test. These are not minor implementation disagreements. They change the force of Tenets 3 and 4 and the AI verdict.

The solution is not necessarily to create ten or twelve “tenets.” It is to publish a layered constitution: ontological commitments, psychophysical-law commitments, subjecthood commitments, interpretation commitments, and methodological rules. Each downstream claim should state which layer it uses.

5.2 Positions are not atomic enough for the calibration schema   CRITICAL

The multi-axis calibration looks precise, but many entries combine claims with different evidential status. [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1) combines the necessity of valence, its sufficiency for standing, the phenomenal rather than functional reading, and the identity of the status bearer. [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1) combines metaphysical possibility, functional evidence, engineering architecture, interface eligibility and a verdict about current digital AI. [P-AS1](/positions/ai-substrate-verdicts/#p-as1) combines well-supported engineering facts with a grade-D claim about consciousness relevance. [P-A1](/positions/agency-and-will/#p-a1) combines neural and clinical markers with a leap to substance-agent causation. [P-MC3](/positions/arguments-for-mental-causation/#p-mc3) combines a mainstream exclusion diagnostic with the sweeping survey claim that no rival has escaped it.

One calibration row cannot faithfully represent such conjunctions. The present prose often explains the mixed profile, but the machine-readable position remains one object and downstream citations can inherit the headline rather than the caveat. This is a false-granularity problem: the schema has six axes, but the claim unit is too coarse.

Position entries should be decomposed into claim atoms—for example P-MS1a necessity, P-MS1b sufficiency, P-MS1c phenomenal reading, P-MS1d bearer—and then grouped into a human-readable position bundle. Calibration belongs to the atom; the bundle should report a dependency and weakest-link summary rather than a single grade.

5.3 “Depends on” mixes several incompatible edge types   HIGH

The register’s dependency prose mixes logical entailment, empirical support, adopted premise, defeater removal, formalism source, scope condition, rival, citation grade and debt inheritance. This makes the graph hard to reason about. A logical cycle between subjecthood and No Many Worlds is not automatically vicious; an evidential cycle is. A framework assumption can be central without being evidence. A falsifier can constrain a claim without supporting it. Because these relations share one undifferentiated field, reverse-propagation checks cannot know what should happen when a source changes.

The register needs typed edges such as REQUIRES, SUPPORTED_BY, CONSISTENT_WITH, DEFEATER_REMOVED_BY, FORMALISM_FROM, CONSTRAINED_BY, RIVAL_TO, INHERITS_DEBT_FROM and WOULD_SHIFT_IF. A topological test should be applied only to entailment and support edges; mutual constraint and rivalry may legitimately form cycles. This would make the existing dependency work operational rather than merely descriptive.

5.4 The five tenets mix logical levels   HIGH

Tenet 1 is an ontological commitment. Tenet 2 is a mechanism constraint and research hypothesis. Tenet 3 is a causal claim whose quantifier is disputed. Tenet 4 is an interpretation exclusion that depends on a theory of subjecthood. Tenet 5 is a meta-epistemic policy. Treating them as five peer “pillars” invites invalid support relations—for example, letting a methodological rule appear to support an ontology, or letting an interpretation preference look like an independent route to subject persistence.

The site already recognises that Tenet 5 is self-binding and that Tenet 4 is subjecthood-dependent. The architecture should make the level difference explicit. A constitution with sections—Ontology, Causation, Quantum Interface, Actualisation/Interpretation, and Method—would remove much of the apparent circularity without pretending the commitments are independent.

10. Methodology and workflow improvements

10.1 Replace position bundles with claim atoms   P0

Give each atomic assertion its own ID, calibration, evidence list, shift condition and typed dependencies. Human-readable position pages can group atoms, but machine-readable exports should never require one grade to cover both an empirical premise and a metaphysical conclusion. This single change would improve every later validator.

10.2 Build a typed claim graph and use it in the write path   P0

The graph should distinguish support, entailment, adopted premise, consistency, defeater removal, formalism, rival, scope and debt inheritance. Before an article is generated or revised, the workflow should fetch canonical upstream atoms and their current grades. After a grade or wording change, reverse dependencies should be checked by edge type rather than by string search alone.

10.3 Add hard gates for high-risk prose   P0

Introduce automated checks for direct quotations, superlatives, “shows/proves/confirms/resolves,” confidence labels, and claims of independence or convergence. A quote should require source, page or section, and an exact-string verification flag. A strong evidential verb should require an upstream grade and citation relation that permits it.

10.4 Maintain a live issue register separate from review prose   P1

The corpus now contains many outer reviews, optimistic reviews, pessimistic reviews and changelog records. That is valuable provenance but makes it difficult to tell whether a criticism is open, accepted, rejected, implemented or superseded. Maintain one issue ledger with stable IDs and status. Reviews should point to it; published review prose should not itself count as a current site commitment.

10.5 Publish stable releases for external audit   P1

The site changes fast enough that search indexes, public pages and repository source can disagree. Publish a monthly or fortnightly tagged release with a frozen positions export, tenet text, article hashes and changelog delta. External reviews can then cite a version rather than a moving target, while the live site continues evolving.

10.6 Create an auxiliary-law admission test   P1

Every proposed bridge law should declare: the inconsistency it removes; the novel conditional it predicts; the alternatives it excludes; the free parameters it adds; and the observations that would retire it. The programme should track the ratio of empirical constraints gained to auxiliary freedom added. This operationalises the site’s Lakatosian language and Tenet 5’s self-binding.

10.7 Use negative controls and denominator reporting   P1

For convergence clusters and voids, report not only the successes but the candidate set: cases examined, cases that deflated, cases excluded and reasons. For biological and AI interface tests, validate the rule on held-out cases rather than only on the human biological template from which it was abstracted.

10.8 Increase genuine reviewer independence   P2

Most of the corpus and much of its internal review are produced by closely related model families operating on the same notes and house style. Outer-model review helps, but model diversity is not the same as evidential independence. Commission periodic domain-specific human or specialist reviews in quantum foundations, philosophy of mind, ethics and statistics; ask each to audit a frozen claim set without seeing the Map’s preferred synthesis first.

10.9 Separate advocacy, encyclopedia and programme prose   P2

The site’s goal is to advance a dualist perspective, but readers need to know when a page is mapping a debate, stating a chosen commitment, offering a conditional model, or arguing that evidence raises the probability of the framework. Add a visible article-mode label and enforce the corresponding evidential verbs. This would reduce the recurrent “two voices” problem without requiring neutrality about the site’s purpose.

10.10 Formalise before further mechanism proliferation   P0

The highest-value next work is not another candidate neural site or another analogy from quantum biology. It is one minimal mathematical model that contains a subject index, conscious state, physical context, outcome menu, Born marginal, conditional signature, multi-agent composition and amplification. Even an impossibility theorem would advance the programme more than additional prose because it would collapse several open debts into a result.

11. Priority sequence and conclusion

The site should resist treating every finding above as an invitation to add another article. The highest-leverage changes are constitutional and formal. A sensible sequence is:

Fix Tenet 3’s quantifier and reconcile The Embodied Interface, AI scope and mental-causation positions.

Atomise the highest-centrality bundled positions: [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1), [P-AS1](/positions/ai-substrate-verdicts/#p-as1), [P-A1](/positions/agency-and-will/#p-a1), [P-MC3](/positions/arguments-for-mental-causation/#p-mc3), [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1) and [P-MS2](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms2).

Create typed dependency edges and wire them into authoring and reverse-propagation checks.

Extend the Born-preserving programme to a formal N-subject model with explicit interpersonal composition; state an impossibility result if no such law can preserve the required joint statistics.

Add the modal basis of standing capacity and the census-transition change-point problem.

Operationalise Tenet 5 with an auxiliary-law budget and implement denominator/negative-control reporting for voids and convergence claims.

The Map’s most impressive feature is now its willingness to record reasons it may be wrong. That is not a small achievement. The risk is that the record of debts becomes a substitute for paying them: a sophisticated programme can look rigorous because it names every problem while continuing to let the same high-level thesis do work downstream. The next stage should therefore be less expansive and more constitutional. Fewer new articles, more claim atomisation; fewer analogies, one worked model; fewer local caveats, more machine-enforced dependency propagation.

| Final assessment The Unfinishable Map is a serious and increasingly disciplined dualist research programme, not yet a complete theory. Its best-supported contribution is not a demonstrated quantum mechanism but a structured inventory of what interactionist dualism would have to explain. The decisive test of its next phase is whether that inventory can be compressed into formal, jointly compatible laws—or whether each new pressure requires another auxiliary commitment. The current site has done enough self-audit to make that test possible. It has not yet passed it. |
