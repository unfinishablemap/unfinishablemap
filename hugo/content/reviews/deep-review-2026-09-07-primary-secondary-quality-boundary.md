### Medium Issues Found

**2. Over-concession: "presses the Map's own epistemic structuralism just as hard."**
The article's whole statement of its own counter-argument, and the stalest version
of it in the corpus. The cluster has since moved past parity to a narrower and better
claim, stated in `ontic-structural-realism#newman` and echoed at `intrinsic-nature`
L85 and L95: what Newman's problem yields is *an asymmetry in available replies*, not
a refutation and not parity. The epistemic structural realist can locate the
individuator in the nature beneath the structure; the ontic reading, having denied any
such nature, must find one inside structure or do without.

This is the failure mode the corpus files as over-concession — a claim running
*against* the Map, which reviews ratify rather than catch, because conceding looks
like calibration discipline. "Just as hard" is not calibration; it is imprecision that
happens to point downward.

**Resolution**: the asymmetry installed, and installed *without* upgrading. The
self-exposure is kept explicit ("the objection was raised against *epistemic*
structuralism, which is the Map's own commitment, and the Map cannot press it as
though it were exempt") and the outstanding debt is now named rather than left vague
("it leaves the Map owing an account of its own individuator that it does not have").
The new text concedes something more specific than the old text did; it simply
concedes the true thing.

**3. The escape was tied to the strongest reading alone.** The paragraph's "on its
strongest reading" is used correctly and consistently with the cluster — it marks the
extension of OSR to the *mental*, not the strength of the escape. But the article
never noted that the escape survives *weakening*: Esfeld and Lam's moderate version
keeps objects as relata and denies them only intrinsic identity, absorbing the
relations-without-relata objection while still leaving no categorical natures for the
primary side to hold. Both `intrinsic-nature` L55 and the OSR article now carry this;
this article left the reader's first instinct — there cannot be relations without
relata — looking like a live reply when it is the one the rival is built to withstand.

**Resolution**: added, with the point of it stated ("the objection a reader reaches
for first is the one the rival is built to withstand"). Attribution held at the tier
the sourcing supports (see ledger).
