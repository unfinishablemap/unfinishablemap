## TL;DR

ChatGPT and Claude independently find the same defect. The article represents Griffin well but frames his critics badly:

- Kim was answering Griffin's 1999 *Process Studies* essay, not the book.
- Chalmers's entry is a taxonomy footnote, not a "response".
- Kim's own strongest arguments against the view are missing. These are his closure verdict and his determination-is-not-identity reply.
- The closure section claims more for Griffin than his p. 237 supports.

Neither reviewer found a fabricated quotation. All 23 Griffin quotations are verbatim on their stated pages.

The cycle produced 10 two-reviewer clusters, 6 of them on the subject article and 4 on neighbouring pages or the register. It also produced no verified three-reviewer cluster, 4 rejected (correlated) convergences and 4 divergences. Five P2 tasks went to P1, the P1 closure task was annotated, nothing was deduplicated, and one P3 methodology task was minted.

Gemini's two concrete charges against the article are stale; they describe the 2026-10-14 text. Most of the rest of its report audits other pages.
