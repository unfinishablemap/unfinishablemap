# Pessimistic Review — The Competency Cluster's Tenet 2 Paragraphs

**Date**: 2026-08-03
**Content reviewed**: `obsidian/topics/bacterial-chemotaxis-and-minimal-biogenic-cognition.md`, `obsidian/topics/neuron-less-animals-sponges-placozoans-and-the-lower-bound-of-cognition.md`, `obsidian/apex/competency-without-felt-experience.md`, `obsidian/topics/plant-cognition-and-the-plant-neurobiology-debate.md`, `obsidian/concepts/near-perfect-adaptation-and-control-theoretic-competency-without-experience.md`; comparison loci in `obsidian/topics/the-cognitive-immune-system-and-immunological-selfhood.md` and `obsidian/topics/brain-organoids-and-the-organoid-intelligence-question.md`

**Target rationale**: Selected by review-coverage scarcity. `near-perfect-adaptation-and-control-theoretic-competency-without-experience` (4 prior mentions across the whole `reviews/` corpus) and `neuron-less-animals-sponges-placozoans` (5) sit near the bottom of corpus-wide review coverage. ## Executive Summary

The cluster's philosophy is in good order — the framework-relativity is stated, the rivals (Lyon, Reber & Baluška, Bechtel & Bich, Man & Damasio, Taiz et al.) are engaged at full strength rather than caricatured, and the direct-refutation discipline passes cleanly with zero label leakage. The failure is confined, precisely and repeatedly, to **one paragraph type**: the "Tenet 2 (Minimal Quantum Interaction)" paragraph in the *Relation to Site Perspective* section.

In three articles that paragraph runs a truth-tracking parsimony argument for the *absence* of experience — "adding felt experience on top multiplies entities against a fully sufficient mechanistic account." That is the exact inference the immediately following Tenet 5 paragraph in the same articles declares blocked, that the cluster's own concept article names as forbidden, and that `tenets.md` explicitly disowns on Tenet 2's behalf in a passage written for this purpose. The corpus already contains the correct pattern twice over, in two sibling articles of the same cluster, so the fix is a rewrite against an in-corpus template rather than a research problem.

The sharpest point is that the Map has already adjudicated this against itself. `tenets.md` L69 states: *"Tenet 2's minimality is empirical-constraint minimality... The Map does not claim that within those constraints the smallest interaction is most likely true."* The three defective paragraphs claim precisely that, about experience rather than about interaction strength.

## Critical Issues

### Issue 1: Tenet 2's minimality is used as a truth-tracking parsimony argument against experience

- **Files**: `obsidian/topics/bacterial-chemotaxis-and-minimal-biogenic-cognition.md` L84; `obsidian/topics/neuron-less-animals-sponges-placozoans-and-the-lower-bound-of-cognition.md` L62; `obsidian/apex/competency-without-felt-experience.md` L120
- **Severity**: High (first two), Medium (apex)

The defective text, at the bacterial floor:

> The run-and-tumble loop is explained end to end by receptor binding, phosphotransfer, motor bias, and methylation feedback; **adding felt experience on top multiplies entities against a fully sufficient mechanistic account.** The principle is *no interface where no substrate*.

And at the sponge floor:

> The sponge sneeze and the placozoan feeding pause are explained end to end by paracrine chemistry and ciliary coupling. **To add felt experience on top would multiply entities against a fully sufficient mechanistic account.**

Four independent sources in the Map's own corpus forbid this move.

**(a) `tenets.md` L69 disowns it explicitly, on Tenet 2's behalf.** The paragraph headed *"Minimality as empirical constraint, not truth-tracking"* exists to prevent exactly this reading:

> Tenet 2's "minimal" is not the kind of minimality Tenet 5 declares unreliable. Tenet 5 binds parsimony as a *truth-tracking* heuristic... Tenet 2's minimality is *empirical-constraint* minimality, fixed by the Rules-out clause below: the interaction must respect the empirical record (no detectable energy injection, no Born-statistics violation, no conservation-law violation).

Tenet 2's minimality is scoped to the **magnitude of a physical interaction** — energy, Born statistics, conservation laws. It says nothing about how experience is distributed across organisms. The same passage adds that "Tenet 5's symmetric self-binding therefore applies to parsimony arguments *for or against* the framework," and a parsimony argument for the Map's own floor verdict is squarely within that scope.

**(b) The adjacent paragraph in the same article contradicts it.** In `bacterial-chemotaxis` the very next paragraph (L86) reads: *"It blocks the deflationist from wielding parsimony as a proof that nothing is felt... bacterial behaviour gives no purchase on the phenomenal question either way."* In `neuron-less-animals` (L66): *"their behaviour gives us no purchase on the question either way."* An argument that "adding experience multiplies entities against a sufficient account" is purchase, in one direction. Either the Tenet 5 paragraph is false or the Tenet 2 paragraph is inert; as written the reader gets both.

**(c) The cluster's own concept article names the move as forbidden.** `near-perfect-adaptation-and-control-theoretic-competency-without-experience` L83:

> The elegance of an integral-control explanation does not license inferring the *absence* of experience any more than the presence of regulation licenses inferring its *presence*: simplicity is not truth-tracking here.

**(d) `the-steelman-for-process-monism` L87 states it as a standing obligation:** Tenet 5, "applied symmetrically, forbids the Map from later invoking simplicity in its own favour."

**Recommendation**: Rewrite the three Tenet 2 paragraphs against the template the corpus already contains. `the-cognitive-immune-system-and-immunological-selfhood` L63 gets it right by restricting the claim to *interfaces*: *"declining to multiply interfaces beyond the machinery that could host them"* — a claim about causal footprint, which is Tenet 2's actual domain. `brain-organoids-and-the-organoid-intelligence-question` L76 gets it right by symmetry: *"Minimality... equally forbids confident denial."* Delete the "multiplies entities against a fully sufficient mechanistic account" clause at all three loci; keep the interface-allocation claim, which is legitimate.

### Issue 2: The slide from "no interface" to "no experience" needs a premise the Map rejects elsewhere

- **Files**: same three loci; the operative phrase is *"The principle is `no interface where no substrate`"*
- **Severity**: High

Tenet 2 and Tenet 3 can license *"no interface here"* — a claim about where consciousness exerts causal influence. They cannot by themselves license *"nothing is felt here."* Bridging the two requires the premise that experience requires an interface — i.e. that absent the neural substrate, there is no experience.

That premise is structurally identical to the one the Map firmly rejects when a physicalist deploys it. `plant-cognition-and-the-plant-neurobiology-debate` L78–82 rejects Taiz et al.'s argument in these terms:

> The middle premise is a **neural-necessity** claim... Under dualism, experience is not constituted by neural computation, so no inventory of absent neural structures settles the question by itself.

Swapping "neural computation" for "neural interface" does not change the shape of the inference. Note that the *body* of `bacterial-chemotaxis` handles this correctly and explicitly (L82: *"a framework-relative verdict, not a demonstration"*). It is only the Tenet 2 paragraph that drops the framework-relativity and states the principle flatly. The article is therefore inconsistent with itself between L82 and L84.

This is the epistemic/metaphysical equivocation check firing in a variant form: a *causal-footprint* reading of minimality (defensible, framework-internal) is being recruited to assert a *phenomenal-distribution* conclusion (not licensed).

**Recommendation**: Where "no interface where no substrate" is stated, mark it as what it is — a framework-relative allocation rule about causal influence, which yields a verdict on experience only via the Map's further interface commitment, and which the Map declines to press as a demonstration. The wording at `bacterial-chemotaxis` L82 is the model; the Tenet 2 paragraph should not read more confidently than the body paragraph two above it.

### Issue 3: "Complete mechanistic sufficiency" over-reaches the sponge sources it cites

- **File**: `obsidian/topics/neuron-less-animals-sponges-placozoans-and-the-lower-bound-of-cognition.md` L32
- **Severity**: High — this claim is the load-bearing premise of Issue 1 at this locus

> The picture that emerges is one of **complete mechanistic sufficiency**: purinergic and glutamatergic paracrine chemistry, ciliary mechanics, and epithelial conduction explain the behaviour **end to end, with no residue**...

The article's own citations do not support "complete" or "end to end":

- Leys & Anderson (2015) is titled *"Elements of a 'nervous system' in sponges"* and, as the article itself reports at L30, describes *coordinating mechanisms with some neuronal characteristics* — an inventory of elements, not a closed causal account.
- Ho, Goss & Leys (2025) is described in the article's own words as dissecting the contraction *"further,"* identifying ATP and glutamate as coordinating signals. "Further" concedes an open account.
- Kornder et al. (2022), also cited here, report mucus moved **against** the sponge's internal water flow and expelled over tens of minutes. Directed counter-flow transport is not explained by the paracrine chemistry and epithelial conduction the sentence enumerates.

Contrast the bacterial article, where the parallel claim is earned: the *E. coli* chemotaxis pathway genuinely is "one of the most completely characterised signal-transduction pathways in biology." The sponge case is where the sufficiency premise is weakest and where the parsimony conclusion drawn from it is strongest — the inverse of the correct pattern.

**Recommendation**: Downgrade to what the sources support — the coordinating chemistry is identified and no neural mechanism is implicated — and drop "complete," "end to end," and "no residue." The article's argument does not need the stronger claim: its thesis is that the behaviour gives no purchase either way, which holds whether or not the mechanism is fully closed.

### Issue 4: The over-generation reductio is run in only one direction

- **Files**: `obsidian/concepts/near-perfect-adaptation-and-control-theoretic-competency-without-experience.md` L55; and by omission across the cluster
- **Severity**: Medium

`near-perfect-adaptation` deploys a clean reductio against inflation:

> Any argument of the form "this system regulates robustly, therefore it experiences" over-generates: it licenses attributing experience to the op-amp. This is a standing reductio the Map can invoke against any future instance.

The mirror reductio is nowhere stated. "The mechanism is fully specified, therefore nothing is felt" over-generates in the other direction: on physicalist assumptions human behaviour is also mechanistically explicable without functional residue, so if mechanistic completeness licensed denying experience it would license denying it in humans. This is precisely why the explanatory gap is a gap — and `near-perfect-adaptation` L59 says so (*"the complete control story... untouched"*) without drawing the reductio.

The apex claims the discipline is bidirectional — *"a framework that only ever lowered credence in non-human minds would be a machine for denial"* — but the cluster supplies a standing reductio against inflation only. The deflationary reductio is the missing half of the symmetric instrument.

**Recommendation**: Add the mirror reductio to `near-perfect-adaptation` alongside the thermostat floor. It is one or two sentences, it costs the cluster nothing, and it is the sentence that would have prevented Issues 1–3 from being written.

### Issue 5: The competency cluster has no row in the tenet sub-reading matrix

- **File**: `obsidian/tenets/tenets.md` L155–166
- **Severity**: Medium

The matrix exists to catch articles importing a stronger tenet sub-reading than their argument needs — *"a downstream article that says 'Tenet 1 commits us to X' can easily import a stronger sub-reading than its local argument actually needs."* Issues 1 and 2 are textbook instances of exactly that failure, in a cluster of nine source articles plus an apex.

There is no row for the competency / marginal-organism cluster. The nearest analogue, **Animal consciousness**, marks *Minimal quantum interaction (corridor reading)* as **Not invoked** and *Anti-parsimony (Tenet 5 self-binding)* as **Optional** — which is itself evidence that the competency cluster's heavy, load-bearing Tenet 2 invocation is out of pattern for this kind of argument.

**Recommendation**: Not a task for this cycle's refine pass, but worth registering: a competency-cluster row would make the Tenet 2 dependency visible and gradeable. On the evidence here the honest grading is Tenet 2 *Optional at most* (the interface-allocation claim), with Tenet 5 self-binding **Required** rather than Defensive.

"'No functional residue that an experiencer is required to fill' is my sentence, not yours. If every term in the complete description names a dynamical feature, and nothing is left over, then whatever you think you are adding is doing no work — that is the whole point of the intentional-stance argument. ### The Quantum Skeptic (Tegmark)

Largely disarmed here — the cluster makes no coherence claims. But note the standing exposure: the cluster's operative principle is *"no interface where no substrate,"* and the Map has not specified what makes a substrate interface-capable beyond "neural." A sponge has excitable epithelia and calcium signalling; a bacterium has an excitable membrane. Reber and Baluška's continuity point is that the physics does not obviously supply a joint. The cluster asserts the joint rather than locating it.

### The Many-Worlds Defender (Deutsch)

Not engaged by this cluster and correctly so; no Tenet 4 dependency is claimed anywhere in the reviewed files.

### The Empiricist (Popper's Ghost)

The falsifiability challenge lands hardest, and — to the corpus's credit — `brain-organoids` L78 has already met it head-on: *"A position that no possible observation of the dish could disturb has stopped being cautious and started being immune."* That paragraph is the best writing in the cluster. Its absence from the bacterial and sponge floors is conspicuous: those two articles rule out every observation their subject affords (behaviour, coordination, memory, adaptive decision) and name none that would bear, with no corresponding acknowledgement of the debt. The organoid article shows the cluster knows how to pay this; two of its floor cases do not pay it.

### The Buddhist Philosopher (Nagarjuna)

"You look for the interface, find no substrate, and conclude the interface is elsewhere. But you have never found it anywhere — only ruled out places. A locus defined entirely by where it is not is not a locus." Fair, and the cluster is partly aware of it (`neuron-less-animals` L26: *"knowing where it is not is informative about where to look"*). The stronger form of the objection — that an interface never positively located is doing no explanatory work — is not engaged in these files, though `brain-organoids` gestures at it.

## Counterarguments to Address

### Steelman: is the Tenet 2 paragraph defensible as framework-internal?

**A defender would argue**: Tenet 2 is an intra-framework allocation rule. Given dualism plus an interface commitment, minimality says do not posit interfaces beyond need. That is not a general Occam argument about whether experience exists, so Tenet 5's self-binding does not reach it.

**Why this does not rescue the text as written**: the defence works for `the-cognitive-immune-system` L63, which says *interfaces* and stops. It does not work for the three defective loci, because they explicitly premise the conclusion on *mechanistic sufficiency for the behaviour* — "explained end to end... adding felt experience on top multiplies entities." Sufficiency-for-behaviour is a general explanatory consideration, not a framework-internal allocation fact, and the step from it to "therefore no experience" is the deflationist's step. A non-interacting experience in a bacterium would add zero non-physical influence on physical outcomes and so would be entirely untouched by Tenet 2. The paragraph rules it out anyway.
