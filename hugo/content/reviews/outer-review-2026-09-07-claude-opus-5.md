# Referee Audit — "Consciousness and Causal Powers" (The Unfinishable Map)

*External adversarial review of [topics/consciousness-and-causal-powers](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/consciousness-and-causal-powers/) (last substantively revised 2026-08-27), read against the live changelog (checked to 2026-09-04), the article's outbound links, the paired `born-rule-and-the-consciousness-interface` article, the apex benchmarks, and the 2020s primary literature.*

## 1. TL;DR — Headline Verdict

**Verdict: REVISE-HARD.** The article is a competent, unusually self-aware statement of the Map's "selection-only" interactionism, and its citations verify cleanly at publisher-of-record on metadata and quotation. But it fails as a defensible treatment of causal powers on three counts that are not cosmetic. First, it strawmans Kim's exclusion argument by presenting the physicalist as having only two escape routes (overdetermination or epiphenomenalism) and never engaging the 2010s–2020s non-reductive-physicalist literature — Bennett, List & Menzies, Woodward, Yablo, with Baumgartner as the strongest reply — that claims to keep causal closure *and* mental causation. Second, it enacts the confirmed Stapp inversion: the "Selection of Action" mechanism it defends is precisely the outcome-biasing Stapp disowned, while it recruits Stapp's Zeno vocabulary in support. Third, and most consequentially, its treatment of the Born-rule statistical-indistinguishability dilemma is a textbook case of confession-without-correction — the defect is named with real precision ("operationally undiscriminable by unconditioned aggregate test") and then banked as candour rather than converted into a binding demotion of the causal-powers thesis.

- The article's central causal claim ("phenomenal selection biases which actualizes") is **framework-coherent but empirically undiscriminated and, on the strictest reading of its own Tenet 2, undiscriminable in aggregate** — a status the article states but refuses to let bite on its verdict register.
- Its **strongest empirical exhibit, cognitive motor dissociation (Bodien et al. 2024), does not discriminate dualism from physicalism at all** and only weakly touches epiphenomenalism proper; it is over-recruited.
- **Physicalist rivals are systematically under-engaged**: no global workspace, higher-order, IIT (including the 2023 pseudoscience row and 2025 Cogitate results), illusionism, or attention-schema; predictive processing is engaged only generically, and the Laukkonen–Friston–Chandaria 2025 active-inference theory — the site's own standing blocking gate — is absent from an article squarely about valence, selfhood, and volition.

## 2. Dimensional Analysis

### 2.1 Argument structure and the constitutional-attractor effect

The article opens by pricing its own commitment fairly: "The Map's rejection of MWI is not free: whichever interpretation is right, *something* must be added." [topics/consciousness-and-causal-powers](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/consciousness-and-causal-powers/) This is genuine calibration and should be retained. But the pattern across the body is that every rival is admitted to predict the same phenomenology and then set aside by appeal to "framework coherence and phenomenological fit" — the article states this explicitly: "The case for the Map's reading rests on framework coherence and phenomenological fit." [topics/consciousness-and-causal-powers](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/consciousness-and-causal-powers/) That is an honest sentence describing a dependency the evidence sections never escape. Each evidence class (introspective, evolutionary, comparative, clinical) is conceded to be non-discriminating, and the metaphysical conclusion is nonetheless carried — the epistemic-to-metaphysical slide is structural, not local.

### 2.2 The exclusion argument is strawmanned

The article's only engagement with Kim is:

> "Kim's exclusion objection assumes physical causes are *sufficient* for every physical effect; if so, an additional mental cause must either duplicate the work (overdetermination) or do nothing (epiphenomenalism). Selection-only interactionism denies the sufficiency premise locally." [topics/consciousness-and-causal-powers](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/consciousness-and-causal-powers/)

This presents a false dichotomy as though it exhausted the physicalist's options. It omits the live literature in which non-reductive physicalists claim to retain causal closure *and* mental efficacy without overdetermination:

- **Bennett, "Why the Exclusion Problem Seems Intractable, and How, Just Maybe, to Tract It," *Noûs* 37(3): 471–497 (2003), DOI 10.1111/1468-0068.00447** — Bennett frames the worry as beginning from "the claim that the physical realm is causally complete: every physical thing that happens has a sufficient physical cause… the physical does all the work, and there is nothing left for the mental to do," and dissolves it via a counterfactual overdetermination test (the mental/physical pair fails the "genuine overdetermination" criterion because the counterfactual "had the mental cause occurred without the physical cause…" is vacuous) — *without* denying sufficiency.
- **List & Menzies, "Nonreductive Physicalism and the Limits of the Exclusion Principle," *Journal of Philosophy* 106(9): 475–502 (2009), DOI 10.5840/jphil2009106936** — on a difference-making analysis the exclusion principle is *contingent*, and in the key case the higher-level property downward-excludes its own realizer, vindicating higher-level causal autonomy.
- **Woodward, "Mental Causation and Neural Mechanisms," in *Being Reduced* (OUP, 2008), pp. 218–262**, and **Woodward, "Interventionism and Causal Exclusion," *Philosophy and Phenomenological Research* 91(2): 303–347 (2015)** — interventionist causation on which mental and physical causes do not compete. [Oxford Academic](https://academic.oup.com/book/11716/chapter/160696065)
- **Yablo, "Mental Causation," *Philosophical Review* 101(2): 245–280 (1992), DOI 10.2307/2185535** — determinable/determinate proportionality, on which mental and physical properties do not causally compete. [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/fall2007/entries/mental-causation/)
- **Baumgartner, "Interventionist Causal Exclusion and Non-reductive Physicalism," *International Studies in the Philosophy of Science* 23(2): 161–178 (2009)** (and "Interventionism and Epiphenomenalism," *CJP* 40(3): 359–383, 2010; "Rendering Interventionism and Non-Reductive Physicalism Compatible," *Dialectica* 67(1): 1–27, 2013) — the strongest *reply* to the interventionist solutions, arguing interventionism applied consistently reinstates exclusion. Its omission cuts against the Map too, but a serious treatment must show awareness that the interventionist route is contested from both sides.

The Map's "denies the sufficiency premise locally" move is a legitimate dualist option, but presenting it as the *only* alternative to overdetermination/epiphenomenalism misrepresents the dialectic: the compatibilist and difference-making camps grant sufficiency and still get mental causation. The neighbouring page `overdetermination-dissolution-under-selection-only-interactionism` is where the "full argument" is said to live, but its search-visible content — and that of `objections-to-interactionism` — routes exclusion only through "quantum insufficiency" and "delegatory preemption," engaging none of Bennett, List & Menzies, Woodward, or Yablo by name. This is a corpus-wide gap surfacing in the target article, not a merely local omission.

### 2.3 Causal closure asserted, not argued

The article concludes: "The physical world is not [causally closed]; mental properties add to the causal story, with implications for reductionism, emergence, and natural law." [topics/consciousness-and-causal-powers](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/consciousness-and-causal-powers/) This is stated as a downstream consequence, but the article never engages the argument *for* closure it is denying. There is no mention of Papineau's inductive/empirical argument from the history of physiology, no distinction between closure, completeness, and conservation-law arguments, and no engagement with the 2020s refinements (Gibb's double-prevention/powers model, Lowe's two-level structuring-cause approach, Montero's Hempel's-dilemma critique). The Map *has* a competent dedicated page — `causal-closure-debate-historical-survey` (which handles Papineau, Montero's "no available definition of 'physical' supports causal closure without circularity or vacuity," Lowe's 2000 compatibility argument, and Gibb) — plus the `causal-closure` concept. The target article links neither in-body at the point of the closure claim. This is a weak-integration failure: the load-bearing premise-denial is asserted in the flagship theory article while its supporting argument sits one hop away, unlinked at the locus of use.

### 2.4 The Stapp inversion (confirmed)

Under "Selection of Action" the article commits to outcome-biasing:

> "Faced with multiple options—speak or stay silent, approach or avoid—consciousness biases which neural pattern wins. Alternatives exist as superposed possibilities; consciousness influences which collapses into actuality." [topics/consciousness-and-causal-powers](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/consciousness-and-causal-powers/)

and under "Pattern amplification" and "Sustained Attention" it recruits Stapp's quantum Zeno mechanism ("see [stapp-quantum-mind]"). This is the Eccles-style outcome-biasing that Stapp explicitly disowned. Stapp's considered position — as the Map's *own* corpus records in its outer review, "Stapp conceded that 'willful selection' cannot be reconciled with the statistical rules at the level of outcomes, and preserved consistency only by moving free choice to Process 1 (the choice of what/when to measure), keeping outcome statistics strictly Born-obeying" — relocates the mental contribution to the choice of what/when to measure, not the outcome. The article thus attributes to a Stapp-flavoured mechanism ("consciousness influences which collapses into actuality") exactly the outcome-level selection Stapp rejected. This is a co-optation-firewall failure of the author-stance type: a named author recruited toward a conclusion he explicitly repudiated. It is compounded because the paired `born-rule-and-the-consciousness-interface` article already knows the fix — it files "Sympathetic readings of Stapp (without Zeno)" [topics/born-rule-and-the-consciousness-interface](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/born-rule-and-the-consciousness-interface/) under the corridor — yet the causal-powers article still fronts the outcome-biasing gloss.

### 2.5 The Born-rule dilemma: confession-without-correction

The article's most sophisticated passage is also its most diagnostic. It states:

> "The honest characterisation: framework-coherence-supported, operationally undiscriminable by unconditioned aggregate test—the grade the positions register prices as [mechanism debt]." [topics/consciousness-and-causal-powers](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/consciousness-and-causal-powers/)

and answers the Popperian objection:

> "The framework forbids per-trial discrimination by design—a structural feature of strict Tenet 2 plus no-signalling, not a refinement introduced to evade falsification." [topics/consciousness-and-causal-powers](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/consciousness-and-causal-powers/)

This is precise and admirable diagnosis. But it is banked as epistemic credit rather than converted into a status change. The deepest structural problem — flagged in the Map's own outer review as the corpus's "single deepest problem," namely that "to be genuine selection it must deviate from chance; to be minimal/undetectable it must not" — is never resolved in the article's verdict. The outer review's concrete recommendation was to "state explicitly that undetectable-in-aggregate biasing is statistically indistinguishable from chance, and say which horn the Map takes." The article does not take a horn; it writes "the verdict is open." Naming a defeater with precision and then declining to let it move the credence is the confession-without-correction pathology in its purest form. The claim that discrimination "remains available at ensemble and correlation levels" [topics/consciousness-and-causal-powers](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/consciousness-and-causal-powers/) is a promissory hedge: the companion `brain-internal-born-rule-testing` catalogues designs not yet run at brain-internal precision, and the coarsest grain has already returned nulls (Maier-Dechamps 2018).

Note also a performative-inoculation-citation risk: the "Probabilistic Constraint" rests on "The [causal consistency constraint] (Torres Alegre 2025, a recent arXiv preprint not yet independently confirmed)" and then deploys it as if it establishes that "those bounds are structurally fixed." [topics/consciousness-and-causal-powers](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/consciousness-and-causal-powers/) A single un-refereed preprint (arXiv:2512.12636, submitted 14 Dec 2025) is doing load-bearing work for the claim that the interface *must* preserve Born statistics. The hedge is present; the reliance is nonetheless heavy.

### 2.6 The clinical exhibit is over-recruited

> "Cognitive motor dissociation (CMD) is the strongest clinical exhibit against epiphenomenalism. ... Consciousness continues to *do* cognitive work when the motor channel is severed—harder for epiphenomenalist than interactionist readings." [topics/consciousness-and-causal-powers](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/consciousness-and-causal-powers/)

CMD (Bodien et al., *NEJM* 391(7):598–608, 2024, DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa2400645: "we detected cognitive motor dissociation in 60 of the 241 participants (25%) without an observable response to commands, of whom 11 had been assessed with the use of fMRI only, 13 with the use of EEG only, and 36 with the use of both techniques"; by contrast, 43 of 112 [38%] with observable responses) shows covert cognition — language comprehension, sustained attention, task-locked neural responses — without motor output. [topics/consciousness-and-causal-powers](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/consciousness-and-causal-powers/) It does not discriminate dualism from physicalism: a non-reductive physicalist reads the covert neural cognition as doing all the work, identically to the interactionist. And it barely touches epiphenomenalism *proper*, which concerns the causal inertness of phenomenal character (qualia), not whether cognitive processing occurs covertly. Calling it "the strongest clinical exhibit against epiphenomenalism" is an evidential-independence failure compounded by an epistemic-to-metaphysical slide: covert access-consciousness is presented as if it bore on the phenomenal-efficacy thesis the Map actually needs. The article's hedge ("harder for epiphenomenalist than interactionist readings") understates the problem — the datum is neutral between interactionism and physicalist non-epiphenomenalism.

### 2.7 Physicalist rivals under-engaged; the active-inference gate is missing

The article's engagement with physicalist theories that grant consciousness causal work without dualism is confined to two generic gestures: "Rival accounts (predictive processing, identity theories) predict the same phenomenology by different mechanisms" [topics/consciousness-and-causal-powers](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/consciousness-and-causal-powers/) and a single PP paragraph on valence-as-prior. There is no engagement with global workspace (Dehaene, Mashour), higher-order theories (Lau, Brown, LeDoux), IIT, recurrent processing (Lamme), attention schema (Graziano), or illusionism (Frankish, Dennett). The IIT omission is notable because two events squarely bear on any "predicts the same phenomenology" claim: the letter "The Integrated Information Theory of Consciousness as Pseudoscience" (Fleming et al.), signed by 124 researchers and posted 15 September 2023, which per Nature "sparked uproar" (Chalmers likening the "pseudoscience" charge to "dropping a nuclear bomb over a regional dispute"); and the Cogitate Consortium adversarial collaboration, *Nature* 642(8066):133–142 (2025), n=256 (fMRI/MEG/iEEG), whose abstract states "For IIT, a lack of sustained synchronization within the posterior cortex contradicts the claim that network connectivity specifies consciousness. GNWT is challenged by the general lack of ignition at stimulus offset and limited representation of certain conscious dimensions in the prefrontal cortex." A flagship article claiming rivals are merely notational should register that the leading physicalist theories are under live empirical strain.

Most seriously for an article on valence, selfhood, and volition, it omits **Laukkonen, Friston & Chandaria (2025), "A beautiful loop: An active inference theory of consciousness," *Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews* 176:106296 (Sept 2025; open access CC BY)** — the site's designated standing blocking gate and the strongest current physicalist rival. That theory proposes three conditions: world-model simulation ("an epistemic field"), "inferential competition to enter the world model… a selection for consciousness that we call Bayesian binding," and "epistemic depth, which is the recurrent sharing of the Bayesian beliefs throughout the system." Its "Bayesian binding" is a *selection-for-consciousness* mechanism that directly rivals the Map's "phenomenal selection" story on the same explanatory ground — precisely the engagement the article's single PP paragraph gestures at but never names. A neighbouring article (`predictive-processing-and-dualism`, and per the 2026-09-02 changelog `brain-organoids-and-the-organoid-intelligence-question`) already carries the beautiful-loop engagement; the flagship causal-powers article does not link it where "the Map's actual disagreement with PP" is asserted. This is evidential-independence and integration failure combined.

### 2.9 Conservation laws asserted too strongly

> "when consciousness selects among energetically equivalent quantum outcomes, no energy is transferred—all possible outcomes already exist in the wavefunction with their associated energies." [topics/consciousness-and-causal-powers](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/consciousness-and-causal-powers/)

This is a strong, contestable metaphysical claim stated flatly. The dualist conservation debate (Averill & Keating 1981; Collins; Mohrhoff; Gibb's redistribution/double-prevention models; Broad's redistribution-without-change suggestion) is not engaged at the locus, though `conservation-laws-and-mental-causation` exists as a neighbour. Selection among "energetically equivalent" branches is not obviously energy-neutral once one asks what actualisation *adds* — precisely the "three physical debts" the companion born-rule article itemises ("which POVM, quantum instrument, or coarse-graining defines the alternatives; at what event actualisation occurs amid continuous decoherence; and what it adds to unitary quantum mechanics"). Those debts are acknowledged next door but the causal-powers article states energy-neutrality as settled.

## 3. Bottom-Line Per-Position Verdicts

- **Kim-exclusion response ("denies the sufficiency premise locally"): REVISE-HARD.** Legitimate as a dualist option but presented via a false dichotomy; must engage Bennett, List & Menzies, Woodward, Yablo, and the Baumgartner reply.

- **Stapp/Zeno "Selection of Action" outcome-biasing: REVISE-HARD.** Correct the Stapp inversion; either relocate to Process-1 selection or explicitly own that the outcome-biasing reading is *not* Stapp's and defend it independently.

## 5. Site-Wide Methodology Improvements

1. **Convert confession to commitment.** The single most consequential systemic failure surfaced here is confession-without-correction: defects (the Born-rule dilemma, the Stapp inversion, CMD over-recruitment) are named with precision in reviews and even body prose, then logged rather than converted into binding status changes in the same commit. Institute a rule that any article-body admission of non-discriminability forces a same-commit demotion of the dependent claim on the positions register, not merely a disclosure sentence. The changelog documents this pattern under the site's own [P-M5](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#p-m5) ("Disclosure is not self-correction"). [workflow/changelog](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/)
2. **Author-stance firewall for outcome-biasing.** The Stapp inversion is a recurring co-optation pattern (the outer review flags the same for List; the corpus carries Friston/Clark/Seth-adjacent risks). Add a standing check: any recruitment of a physicalist/naturalist/stance-neutral author toward outcome-biasing or dualist conclusions must cite the author's own disavowal where one exists (Stapp on Process 1 vs. outcomes) at the point of use.
3. **Blocking-gate enforcement at the flagship tier.** Laukkonen–Friston–Chandaria 2025 is a designated standing blocking gate for articles touching valence, selfhood, and volition; the flagship causal-powers article touches all three and does not cite it. Gate compliance should be checked at the flagship/apex tier, not only in the atomic articles where the engagement happens to live.
4. **Ladder conformity at flagship tier.** The compatible/suggestive/discriminating ladder is applied rigorously in the interface article and apex #34 but not in the paired theory article. Make ladder labelling mandatory for every evidence class in any article making an evidential claim, so calibration asymmetry between paired articles cannot persist.
5. **Link-at-locus discipline.** Load-bearing premise-denials (causal closure, conservation neutrality, exclusion dissolution) are asserted in flagship articles while the supporting arguments sit one hop away, unlinked at the point of use. Require in-body links at the sentence that carries the contested premise, not only in Further Reading.
6. **Preprint-reliance ceiling.** A single un-refereed preprint (Torres Alegre 2025) is load-bearing for a structural constraint across multiple articles. Cap the evidential weight any un-refereed preprint can carry, and require a peer-reviewed corroborator before a preprint-based constraint is treated as "structurally fixed."

*Note on production pipeline (permitted only here): the corpus is AI-generated under a Claude-on-Claude adversarial-review pipeline with human supervision. The pipeline's diagnostic acuity is high — it identifies its own deepest problems in the changelog and outer reviews — but the observed gap between diagnosis and binding remediation (items 1–2 above) is the pipeline's characteristic failure signature, and the methodology fixes should target that gap specifically rather than adding further review passes.*
