## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)
The individuation page puts a real puzzle first: "if consciousness is not identical to the body, the body cannot be what individuates it" (L47). After the 23:54Z refine it no longer makes that a burden of bare irreducibility. L131 lets a property dualist "individuate subjects by their physical bearers" and puts the harder burden on the substance-leaning reading. That is the scoping a property dualist wants. Haecceity's zombie route (L73–75) is the right way to show that qualitative facts leave the subject open.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)
The indexical page separates the measurement question from the indexical question (L49) and then joins them in one mechanism: "the one outcome selected is the outcome *this experiencing subject* lives through, not a second, subject-indexed probability measure" (L147). The Kant page's §What Escapes supplies the physics side: once conscious states condition outcomes, the interface law reads *P(O | C, X)*, so a subject index sits inside the selection law (L94). A Stapp sympathiser gets a concrete place where mind enters, and a stated limit on what it would take to see it.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)
"You cannot experience the boundary of your consciousness because the boundary *is* the frame of all your experience, and frames are invisible from within" (individuation L107). The wing keeps first-person particularity at the centre without letting it prove too much. It also reports Nagel's split-brain verdict accurately ("no whole number of centres", L55).

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)
Haecceity's process haecceitism separates *permanence* from *particularity* (L127). That keeps the stream's thisness without a soul-substance. The page also grants what the process reading cannot do: "Process haecceitism supplies particularity at a time and leaves that diachronic fact to be posited outright" (L171). This compatibility is praised at the level of coherence only. The 10-03 tenet check carries an unminted WARNING on haecceity L131 (the Whitehead reading), so this persona's praise does not extend to that paragraph's exegesis.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)
Where-the-substance L40–44 says exactly why agent causation needs a persisting bearer of powers, using Chisholm's immanent causation and Lowe's "unmoved movers". It then notes that agency is "the only [home] that asks the subject to exercise a power rather than merely to be determinate". A libertarian can find precisely where the Map's agency commitments start.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)
The Kant page ends where a mysterian would: if no signature appears, the subject stays "a posit the Map chooses, holds openly, and cannot prove" (L110). Individuation's "naming a fact 'primitive' is acknowledging the individuation void, not filling it" (L91) has the same humility.

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)
This is the wing's strongest axis, with one exception.
- **Praise.** [P-I1](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i1) grades external evidence D because "felt this-ness is compatible with both the primitive-thisness and the perspectival-illusion readings" (L53). The Kant page holds the subject index at *compatible*, with explicit criteria for *suggestive* (a conditioned-register signature) and *discriminating* (subject-indexed signatures) (L98). It also declines the Kantian permission it could have claimed: Tenet 5 "stops parsimony counting against the posit without counting for it" (L108). The indexical page's "Empirical status" paragraph (L153) concedes that no unconditioned test distinguishes the readings. Each of these is a case of not doing tenet-as-evidence-upgrade, and each should be kept.
- **Calibration concern, not praise.** Personal-identity L93 sets out to "bridge the gap" from "haecceity *seems* real" to "haecceity exists", and L105 concludes that this "shifts the burden: anti-haecceitists must explain why the phenomenological evidence misleads". That treats a seeming as evidence that discriminates. [P-I1](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i1) grades it D for that reason, the Kant page's first exposure diagnoses it, and personal-identity's own L155 already concedes "The most this earns is that the intuition is not easily corrected away, not that it tracks a particular." The fix is a refine, not an expansion (Priority 1).
