## Critical Issues

### Issue 1: Two illusionists are cited as phenomenal-concept-strategy proponents, in six live articles including the tenets page

- **File**: `obsidian/positions/arguments-for-mental-causation.md` L56 ([P-MC1](/positions/arguments-for-mental-causation/#p-mc1)), and five sibling loci
- **Location**: "The phenomenal-concept strategy (Loar 1990, Papineau 2002, **Frankish 2016, late Dennett**) has a recognised answer, and the Map concedes it"
- **Severity**: **High**
- **Problem**: Frankish 2016 is *"Illusionism as a Theory of Consciousness"*. Illusionism denies that phenomenal properties exist; the phenomenal-concept strategy affirms they exist, are physical, and explains the epistemic gap through the structure of the concepts that pick them out. Frankish's cited paper argues *against* the conservative-realist family the strategy belongs to. Dennett is a quiner of qualia, not a phenomenal-concept theorist. Three independent checks confirm the misattribution:

  1. **The Map's own definitional article contradicts it.** [concepts/phenomenal-concepts-strategy.md](/concepts/phenomenal-concepts-strategy/) gives sections to **Loar** (L49), **Papineau** (L55) and **Balog** (L59); its reference list carries Loar 1990, Papineau 2002 and Balog 2012 and no Frankish or Dennett; and at **L103** it states: *"Some philosophers (Dennett, Frankish) suggest there are no phenomenal properties—only representations as if there were… This is illusionism—the most radical physicalist response."* The article names illusionism as a **distinct** response, not a variant of the strategy. Its L65 gloss of Zhong 2024 likewise names "Loar's recognitional route and Papineau's and Balog's constitutive route" as *the two ways* of cashing the strategy out.
  2. **The sibling register entry gets it right.** [P-D1](/positions/arguments-for-dualism/#p-d1) (`arguments-for-dualism.md` L48) cites the same strategy for the same job as **"(Loar 1990, Papineau 2002, Balog 2012)"**. Two register entries, one strategy, two rosters — and the correct third name (Balog) has been swapped out for two illusionists.
  3. **The reply put in their mouths is unavailable to them.** The conceded answer is that phenomenal concepts are "physically realised representational states **referring to physical states of the same brain**". That presupposes a phenomenal state to refer to. On illusionism introspective representations *misrepresent*; there is no such referent. The illusionist's answer to self-stultification is different in kind.

- **Why this is substantive, not bibliographic**: the corpus elsewhere handles illusionism carefully and reaches the **opposite** verdict — that the clash is a framework boundary the Map cannot cross, not a concession it makes:
  - [topics/self-stultification-as-master-argument.md](/topics/self-stultification-as-master-argument/) L77 — Frankish's functional-seeming defence "denies the proviso the charge needs rather than evading the charge, so the convergence **marks where the Map and the illusionist part company rather than refuting him within his framework**."
  - [concepts/illusionism.md](/concepts/illusionism/) L145 — "On the illusionist's own terms the convergence dissolves. **The Map presses past this only by holding the residue Frankish relabels is real**—which the convergence argument assumes rather than establishes."
  - [voids/minimal-consciousness-void.md](/voids/minimal-consciousness-void/) L94 — "The Map's disagreement here is a **framework boundary, not a refutation**, and is named as such… the clash is bedrock."
  - [concepts/psychophysical-laws.md](/concepts/psychophysical-laws/) L213 — "a foundational commitment, **not a refutation inside illusionism's framework**."

  Four articles take real care to mark the illusionist boundary honestly. The strategy parenthetical silently absorbs illusionism into an *in-framework concession* — the mirror image of the boundary-substitution the direct-refutation discipline forbids. Where boundary-substitution dresses a bedrock clash as a refutation, this dresses a bedrock clash as a **concession**. It makes the Map appear to have conceded more than its considered position concedes, and it erases a distinction four articles were written to preserve.

- **How it survived**: the changelog records an attribution check that performed exactly the manoeuvre [P-M3](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#p-m3) forbids. `obsidian/workflow/archive/changelog-2026-W20.md` L4371: *"**Attribution check**: PCS attributed to Frankish 2016, Papineau 2002, Loar 1990, late Dennett — **matches the standard literature attribution used in arguments/materialism-argument.md and concepts/phenomenal-concepts-strategy.md**."* [P-M3](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#p-m3) states that intra-corpus cross-checking "is explicitly *not* a substitute, because it **propagates rather than catches** metadata error, and AI-authored articles carry a real rate of fabricated or **mis-attributed** citation metadata that only live-literature verification detects." This is a worked instance of [P-M3](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#p-m3)'s named failure mode. It is also self-refuting on its own terms: one of the two files it cites as corroborating ([concepts/phenomenal-concepts-strategy.md](/concepts/phenomenal-concepts-strategy/)) is the file that contradicts it.

- **Recommendation**: **Do not simply delete the two names** — that discards substance. In each locus: (a) restore the roster to **Loar 1990, Papineau 2002, Balog 2012**, matching [P-D1](/positions/arguments-for-dualism/#p-d1) and the definitional article; (b) add one sentence marking the illusionist reply as a *separate* move met at the framework boundary, pointing to `topics/self-stultification-as-master-argument` L77 and `concepts/illusionism` L145 where the Map already does this work properly. ## Counterarguments to Address

### The strategy roster might be defended as a broad umbrella
- **Current content says**: Frankish and Dennett are phenomenal-concept theorists.
- **A critic would argue**: some authors use "phenomenal concept strategy" loosely for any account explaining the gap through concepts rather than ontology, and illusionism can be read as its limit case.
- **Suggested response**: this defence is unavailable *here*, on two counts. The Map's own definitional article draws the line the other way at L103, so the loose reading contradicts the article the parenthetical links to; and the specific reply the passages attribute — reference to physical states of the same brain — presupposes a referent illusionism denies. Either the definitional article is wrong or the six parentheticals are; they cannot both stand. The register should settle it in one direction and record which.

### [P-MC2](/positions/arguments-for-mental-causation/#p-mc2)'s category boundary needs a criterion it cannot supply
- **Current content says**: three categories keep the accounting honest — *causally coupled*, *report-grounded*, *inherited-discourse* — and "a person parroting consciousness vocabulary without checking it against their own states sits closer to the inherited-discourse pole than any human/machine line would place them."
- **A critic would argue**: sorting anyone into *report-grounded* rather than *inherited-discourse* requires knowing whether their consciousness-talk is *caused by* their experience. The only access to that is introspection — whose reliability is precisely what self-stultification is arguing for. The taxonomy uses the faculty whose standing is at issue to draw its own boundary. The claim about human parroters is also asserted with no citation and no argument, while doing real rhetorical work: it makes the three-way split look principled rather than fitted to the AI case it was built for.
- **Suggested response**: state the criterion, or state openly that the categories are *conceptual* — marking distinctions that matter if they obtain — rather than *operational*. The entry already models this kind of honesty elsewhere; the boundary claim should either earn support or be marked as illustrative.

### The Empiricist (Popper's ghost)
All four entries mark **empirical discriminability: n/a**. Taken at face value the domain is closed to evidence entirely — four positions at high or moderate credence, none answerable to any observation. The register is entitled to hold accounting claims about its own catalogue, and for [P-MC1](/positions/arguments-for-mental-causation/#p-mc1), [P-MC2](/positions/arguments-for-mental-causation/#p-mc2) and [P-MC4](/positions/arguments-for-mental-causation/#p-mc4) the n/a is honest. **[P-MC3](/positions/arguments-for-mental-causation/#p-mc3) is the exception, and it is inconsistent with itself** (see Unsupported Claims below): its third shift condition turns on whether quantum indeterminacy is ontological or epistemic, which is not a claim about the structure of a debate.

### The Quantum Skeptic (Tegmark)
[P-MC3](/positions/arguments-for-mental-causation/#p-mc3)'s escape from the exclusion argument is the whole quantum-interface programme, and the entry does the right thing by inheriting the debt explicitly rather than quietly — "no worked toy model, and the bias-without-deviation dilemma." A Tegmark-style critic would press that [P-MC3](/positions/arguments-for-mental-causation/#p-mc3) asserts "no rival *has* supplied the missing difference-making point" while the Map's own supplied point is an IOU. The entry is on the correct side of this — it says the diagnostic "is held with more confidence than the escape" — but the asymmetry deserves to reach the calibration line, not only the prose.

### The Many-Worlds Defender (Deutsch)
Absent from the domain, and defensibly so — Tenet 4 is argued elsewhere. Worth noting only that [P-MC3](/positions/arguments-for-mental-causation/#p-mc3)'s rival list (non-reductive physicalism, Russellian monism, functionalism, strong emergence, panpsychism) omits the Everettian, for whom the exclusion argument takes a different shape because no outcome is unrealised. No task; flagging as a possible expansion note for optimistic-review rather than a defect.

### The Buddhist Philosopher (Nagarjuna)
[P-MC2](/positions/arguments-for-mental-causation/#p-mc2)'s *report-grounded* category presupposes a subject whose experience grounds their talk. A Madhyamaka critic would say the category is empty at the limit: consciousness-talk arises dependently, and no strand of it is grounded in an independently-standing experiencer. [P-D2](/positions/arguments-for-dualism/#p-d2) already lists Madhyamaka emptiness among the irreducibility-respecting rivals the selection step must exclude, so the challenge is in-scope for the register and unaddressed in this domain. Low priority; noted, not tasked.
