## Critiques by Philosopher

### The Eliminative Materialist
Churchland reads the aphasia section as her own evidence. Differential lesion effects producing differential functional deficits with preserved non-linguistic cognition is textbook modular decomposition; no "user" survives the lesions — a coalition of undamaged subsystems does. The tool/user vocabulary is folk psychology doing the work the argument claims the data do: describe the same clinic in subsystem terms and the "different sort of event" disappears. The article's own Evidence and Dependency section half-concedes this ("aphasia as modular decomposition"); the body sections talk as if the concession had not been made.

### The Hard-Nosed Physicalist
Dennett targets the asymmetry claim — failure concentrating on the phenomenal side of one divide. That distribution is a selection effect, not a discovery: public language evolved for coordinating middle-sized public affairs, so of course it performs worst where triangulation is unavailable. The five failure modes are five styles of metacognitive report, and heterophenomenology catalogs them happily without a user behind them. The article anticipates the individual absorptions but treats the *pattern* as the residue rivals must explain; Dennett's reply is that the pattern is exactly what an evolved public-signalling system predicts, no dualist reading required.

### The Quantum Skeptic
Tegmark has little to attack — the Minimal Quantum Interaction paragraph is deliberately thin and says so. His remaining point: "the shared bandwidth ceiling is consonant with a minimal interface" is consonance purchased at zero risk, since the article admits a resource-constraint account predicts the same pattern. A tenet section that adds no discriminating prediction is decoration, and the honest flag ("framework-internal reading") does not change that.

### The Many-Worlds Defender
Deutsch finds no branch-indexed claims to dispute here — the article wisely stays off MWI terrain. His only note: "shaped limits are information" assumes the shape needs explaining by something about the *user*, when unremarkable computational-resource facts shape limits in systems nobody thinks have users.

### The Empiricist
Popper gives partial credit: the constitution-vs-tool contrast is run as a prediction contest (aphasia differentials, sampling data), which is more falsification-shaped than most metaphysics. But the article has no statement of what would count against the *tool* picture itself. The live falsifier exists and is even cited: the Franco-Martínez programme's next step. If a multisite study demonstrated unconscious WM *manipulation* — not maintenance — the directional-dependence face collapses. The article should name that exposure instead of leaving the tool picture unfalsified in practice.

### The Buddhist Philosopher
Nagarjuna turns the article's second failure mode against its thesis. Dissolution-on-articulation is cited as the tool failing against non-discursive content — but the contemplative traditions supplying that datum report that at the point of deepest insight the *subject* dissolves too. A fixed user wielding a chisel is precisely the reification those phenomenologies undermine; the article recruits their testimony for the tool claim while discarding their verdict on the user. If the failures "map the user," some of the mapped territory says there is no persisting user to map.

## Counterarguments to Address

### The asymmetry as selection effect
- **Current content says**: failure concentrating on the phenomenal side of the functional/phenomenal divide is the residue that survives the common-cause discount and that rivals must explain.
- **A critic would argue**: a public-coordination signalling system is *expected* to perform worst where public triangulation is unavailable; the asymmetry falls out of language's evolutionary function with no user exceeding any domain.
- **Suggested response**: concede the evolutionary account explains poor performance on private content, then press the remaining datum: it does not predict five *qualitatively distinct* failure phenomenologies, nor the directional dependence (recursion requiring access). The reply exists in fragments across the faces; one sentence naming the selection-effect counter would close the gap.

### Blanket universal about animal communication
- **Current content says**: recursion "separates human syntax from every known animal communication system."
- **A critic would argue**: Gentner et al. 2006 claimed starlings learn A^nB^n patterns; the finding is disputed (counting strategies), but "every known" invites the counterexample; and the source article carries the Everett/Pirahã hedge the apex drops.
- **Suggested response**: soften to the source's strength (wild communication systems; trained-ape sign strings) or add the disputed-lab-results hedge.
