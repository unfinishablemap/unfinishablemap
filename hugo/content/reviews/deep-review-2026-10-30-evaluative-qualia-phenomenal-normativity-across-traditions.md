## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found (fixed)

1. **Internal contradiction / tradition recruitment: all five traditions credited with finding irreducibility** (description, lead, L44), against the page's own L76/L112. Changed to "describe … as basic", which is the finding the page actually tallies (L56: "the narrower finding that evaluative character is basic rather than derived").
2. **Stale internal citation of [P-D3](/positions/arguments-for-dualism/#p-d3)** (L40, L54, L56). The page said the register does not settle the Confucian count. The register's 08-02 update does settle it, on this page's own ground.
3. ***Vedanā* recruited for normative authority** (L66). Under the twelve links, *vedanā* conditions craving, and practice aims to stop it governing action. So the tradition denies the normative authority that the L64 parallel imports. The repair keeps the parallel as descriptive only, and marks the separability of attention from the framework that trains it as assumed rather than shown. That matches the hub's 10-25 wording.
4. **Missing leading rival** (L114). If construction runs below introspective access, it predicts the same cross-traditional reports of feeling tone as basic, so the recurrence cannot tell basic from constructed. This is the shared-introspective-channel common cause that [P-M2](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#p-m2) requires the page to leave open. The old "the reply nevertheless concedes the point" also overclaimed. Physicalists concede nothing there, so it now reads "leave open". "Supplies no bridge" became "neither appeal yet supplies a bridge", matching the hub's "nothing argued here forecloses that".
5. **Flat assertion of the constitutive thesis** (L112). "Evaluation is basic, pre-reflective, and constitutive" was stated in the Map's own voice. It now reads "evaluation described as basic …", which matches the thermal-consciousness calibration fixed today.
6. **Unsupported fundamentality ranking** (L62). "the second of four foundations of mindfulness, more fundamental than mental states or conceptual content". The order of the four foundations (body, feeling, mind, *dhammas*) is not a ranking of fundamentality, and a gross-to-subtle reading would invert it. The clause is cut (−9).
7. **Anachronism** (L70). Mencius's four feelings were glossed as "innate manifestations of human nature's participation in cosmic pattern (*lǐ*)". *Lǐ* as cosmic pattern is Song Neo-Confucian (Cheng brothers, Zhu Xi), not Mencian. The gloss is now attributed to later Neo-Confucians. This matters for L56, which relies on the Mencian layer being securely pre-Buddhist and thin.
8. **Source/Map conflation, Kriegel** (L104). "making evaluative properties not just irreducible but absence-sensitive" attributed an irreducibility conclusion to Kriegel 2022, whose claim concerns epistemic value in a zombie world. It now reads "which on the Map's reading makes such values absence-sensitive".
9. **Author enlisted for dualism, Sosa** (L102). "identifies … normativity provides *independent* grounds for dualism". The Cambridge abstract describes two *motivations* for dualism that Sosa judges "relatively independent". The Map's verb "identifies" and the noun "grounds" enlisted him. It now uses his own terms.
10. **Tenet 5 used against the opponent** (L130). "Dismissing that as illusion demands an explanation at least as complex as accepting evaluative qualia as real" was an unsupported parsimony claim in the Map's favour, made under the tenet that says parsimony is unreliable. It now reads "A construction account may be simpler; the Map does not let simplicity decide while that account leaves unexplained why the construction is felt."
