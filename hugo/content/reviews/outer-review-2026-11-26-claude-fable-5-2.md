### 2.3 Depersonalisation

**What the article gets right.** Depersonalisation is the best available probe. Insight is preserved: under DSM-5 Criterion B, reality testing “remains intact”. [depersonalisation-2026-09-30](/research/depersonalisation-2026-09-30/) Reports carry an “as if” structure. The Knappik caveat is at least named.

**What it gets wrong.** “Depersonalisation shows that mine-ness can vanish” (L108, post-edit) still states as a result what is one side of a live dispute. Three published readings of the same clinical facts are available:

- **Billon (2016, 2023).** Mineness really is lacking, partially or globally, while experience continues. [philarchive](https://philarchive.org/archive/BILWII)[thought-insertion-2026-10-01](/research/thought-insertion-2026-10-01/)
- **The Zahavi camp.** For-me-ness is preserved. The patient can say “as if not mine” only because the episode is given to her, and Henriksen & Parnas (2019) argue the alienation “could simply not articulate itself” without it.
- **Guillot.** Depersonalisation “lacks both mineness and me-ness, but retains for-me-ness” (S, via the Map’s thought-insertion page). [thought-insertion](/concepts/thought-insertion/)[thought-insertion](/concepts/thought-insertion/)

The article’s own lead, glossing mine-ness as Zahavi’s for-me-ness, makes Billon’s reading and Zahavi’s reading incompatible. Its separability claim then contradicts self-and-self-consciousness (“no genuinely anonymous experiences exist”). [thought-insertion](/concepts/thought-insertion/) The thought-insertion page says this in so many words. [thought-insertion](/concepts/thought-insertion/) The neighbouring phenomenal-transparency-opacity-spectrum page says depersonalisation “makes it opaque without obliterating it”. [phenomenal-transparency-opacity-spectrum](/concepts/phenomenal-transparency-opacity-spectrum/) That is a third, incompatible statement of the same datum within the Map.

**Overreading reports.** The coherence argument, “If mine-ness were not separable, DPD would be impossible to describe”, is the Map’s reconstruction, not Billon’s. [depersonalisation-2026-09-30](/research/depersonalisation-2026-09-30/) It begs the question. The Zahavi camp reads describability as evidence *for* retained for-me-ness. Reports are clinically filtered, metaphor-laden (“behind glass”, which the Map’s own notes concede is unverified against the cited Sass et al. source) and frequently hedged as approximations: Billon’s footnote cites Radovic & Radovic 2002 on “as if” as a possible “mark of approximation”.

**Mechanistic literature under-engaged.** The affective and interoceptive accounts each predict selective loss of felt presence with intact cognition, without any separable phenomenal tag:

- Sierra & David (2011, *Consciousness and Cognition* 20(1): 99–108, DOI 10.1016/j.concog.2010.10.018), corticolimbic suppression: “[A] frontally driven suppression of activity in the amygdala and possibly other structures of the emotional system might lead, via the insula, to a state equivalent to a functional cortico-limbic disconnection that would impair the process by which perceptions and cognitions become coloured emotionally”, as quoted by Billon 2016. Billon’s “p. 163” locator falls outside the article’s 99–108 page range and needs checking.
- Seth, Suzuki & Critchley (2012, *Front. Psychol.* 2: 395, DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2011.00395), interoceptive predictive coding of presence: “In our model, DPD symptoms correspond to abnormal interoceptive predictive coding dynamics.”
- Gerrans (2019), mineness as the self-model’s explanation of absent predicted affect. [depersonalisation-2026-09-30](/research/depersonalisation-2026-09-30/)
- Ciaunica et al. (2022), “I overthink—therefore I am not”, failure to attenuate sensory precision. [depersonalisation-2026-09-30](/research/depersonalisation-2026-09-30/)

The Map’s clinical-dissociation topic page concedes the interface reading “now competes with a fully specified physicalist rival rather than filling a gap”. [depersonalisation-2026-09-30](/research/depersonalisation-2026-09-30/) The mine-ness article, as traceable, does not carry that concession. Two things are absent from the traceable text. One is the epidemiology: Hunter, Sierra & David (2004, *Soc Psychiatry Psychiatr Epidemiol* 39(1): 9–18) report that “Population-based surveys using diagnostic interviews yield prevalence rates of clinically significant DP/DR in the region of 1-2%” (1.2–1.7% one-month prevalence in a UK sample, 2.4% current prevalence in a Canadian sample), while transient symptoms have a lifetime prevalence of 26–74%. The other is the Cambridge Depersonalisation Scale’s factor structure. They matter, because the CDS shows depersonalisation is multi-factorial, which supports Knappik’s plurality over a single mineness feature.

### 2.5 Contemplative self-dissolution

The lead claims “certain contemplative states” pry mine-ness apart. The traceable lineage, the ownership-void research note now merged into this page, says meditative selflessness “is often described as peaceful or liberating rather than terrifying”. It lists “Buddhist anatta, Advaita witness-consciousness” together as “deliberate approaches to the ownership boundary”. [voids-ownership-void-2026-02-18](/research/voids-ownership-void-2026-02-18/) Both moves are defective:

- **Advaita is not a no-ownership view.** The *sākṣin* (witness) is precisely the retained, non-objectifiable subject. Grouping it with anattā erases the most important doctrinal fault line. Yogācāra *svasaṃvedana* (reflexive awareness) likewise retains a for-me-ness analogue without a self. This is exactly the Albahari/Dreyfus/Ganeri/Thompson/Zahavi dispute over whether selflessness keeps minimal subjectivity. Nothing in the traceable text adjudicates it.
- **Valence is cherry-picked.** Lindahl & Britton (2019, *JCS*) document meditation-related changes in sense of self that clinically overlap with depersonalisation. The Varieties of Contemplative Experience study (Lindahl et al. 2017, *PLoS ONE* 12(5): e0176239) places “sense of self” among its “59 meditation-related experiences across 7 domains”. It reports that “The associated valence ranged from very positive to very negative, and the associated level of distress and functional impairment ranged from minimal and transient to severe and enduring.” That undercuts the article’s tidy “pathology involuntary and terrifying / practice deliberate and peaceful” contrast.
- **Measurement literature absent.** The traceable apparatus contains none of: Millière 2017/2020, Millière et al. 2018, Nave et al. 2021, Dor-Ziderman et al. 2013/2016, Gamma & Metzinger 2021 (the MPE-92M, *PLoS ONE* 16(7): e0253694, DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0253694, whose analysis of 1,400 completed questionnaires found “A solution with 12 factors explaining 44% of the total variance was deemed optimal”), Laukkonen & Slagter 2021, or Chiarella et al. 2024. Testimony is treated as theory-neutral evidence. The Map’s own necessary-opacity page, deep-reviewed 2026-11-25, has already added a “Contemplative Counter-Case” using Deane, Miller & Wilkinson and Metzinger 2024. [changelog](/workflow/changelog/) Mine-ness has not caught up.

### 2.6 Fair hearing for deflationary and no-ownership accounts

**Fails.** Across the article’s traceable apparatus, the strongest deflationary positions are absent:

- The Lichtenberg/Anscombe/Wittgenstein no-ownership line.

### 2.7 Is the ownership void a genuine limit?

**What it claims.** The lineage gives “Occluded / Unexplorable (Mixed)”. Its core claims are that introspection “encounters ownership in every act of introspecting”, [voids-ownership-void-2026-02-18](/research/voids-ownership-void-2026-02-18/) that pathology yields “absence, never … mechanism”, and that ownership is “bedrock—presupposed by any analysis”. [voids-ownership-void-2026-02-18](/research/voids-ownership-void-2026-02-18/) The live §The Ownership Void states this conditionally (“If Zahavi is right … and Heidegger right …”). [changelog](/workflow/changelog/) That conditional is the article’s best feature, and it was correctly protected by the changelog’s L82 downgrade of “shows” to “suggests” on the vertiginous-question page.

**Verdict: overstatement, reducible to elusiveness.** The tests:

1. **Contrastive access exists.** Rubber-hand and full-body illusions, the CDS, psychedelic ego-dissolution scales and the MPE-92M manipulate and measure ownership. Contrastive method is the standard way to study it.
2. **An active research programme exists**, with candidate mechanisms: interoceptive precision, sensory attenuation, temporally deep self-models, hyper-modelling. “Absence, never mechanism” confuses what patients report with what science can infer.
3. **Grades are conflated.** “Currently unexplained” (true), “elusive to direct introspective thematisation” (Hume, Sartre, Shoemaker; defensible) and “in-principle unexplorable” (unargued) are run together. The conditional premise yields at most the middle grade.
4. **Self-insulation.** Any experimental success can be redescribed as explaining “functional role” only, so nothing could count against the void. The thought-insertion page concedes the analogous falsifier is unmeetable “by how the record is gathered”. [thought-insertion](/concepts/thought-insertion/)
5. **Internal contradiction.** If mine-ness can vanish while experience continues (L108), it is contrastively accessible and the void collapses to elusiveness. If it cannot, the pathology section misreads its cases. The article cannot have both.
6. **Guillot dissolves the paradox.** The “presupposed by any examination” argument applies, if at all, to *for-me-ness* (universal, transcendental), not to *mineness* (separable, measurable). Run on the separable feature, the void argument equivocates.

By the voids taxonomy’s own terms, this is “Unexplored” with an elusive core. It does not meet the “Unexplorable” bar. The 2026-08-06 merger of the void into a concept page was the right structural move. The title still advertises the void as a finding.

### 2.8 Standing blocking gate: Laukkonen, Friston & Chandaria 2025

**Engagement: none found.** No mine-ness material I could reach mentions Laukkonen, Friston, Chandaria, “beautiful loop”, epistemic depth or hyper-modelling. **The gate is failed.**

**What the beautiful-loop account says (P for metadata; S for content via the published abstract/highlights and secondary summaries).** Consciousness arises in an active-inference system with three components:

- a unified reality model (the “epistemic field”);
- inferential competition / Bayesian binding;
- epistemic depth: a recursive loop in which a global hyper-model forecasts precision across the hierarchy, so that the model “knows itself”. [closertotruth](https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/laukkonen-friston-and-chandaria-s-beautiful-loop-theory)

The theory states that consciousness “need not include an ego or represented first-person perspective”. [closertotruth](https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/laukkonen-friston-and-chandaria-s-beautiful-loop-theory) It proposes that minimal phenomenal experience occurs when “epistemic depth is maximally high, and the reality model is contentless”. It conjectures that psychedelics “can reliably increase epistemic depth” while relaxing abstract priors, including the self-model. [scu](https://researchportal.scu.edu.au/view/pdfCoverPage)

**How it explains mine-ness and its loss.** It maps cleanly onto Guillot’s split:

- *For-me-ness* corresponds to epistemic depth, the reflexive self-evidencing of the reality model. It is present whenever the system is conscious.
- *Mineness and me-ness* correspond to self-model content bound into that reality model. That content is a revisable prior.

The predictions follow:

- **Meditation / MPE.** Self-model priors are deconstructed while epistemic depth is maintained or raised. The prediction is luminous “selfless awareness” with reflexivity intact. This is the Albahari/Zahavi “minimal subjectivity survives” datum, derived without a separable non-physical tag.
- **Depersonalisation.** Precision on interoceptive and self-generated signals fails (Ciaunica et al. 2022; Seth et al. 2012; Deane et al. 2020). The self-model is retained but poorly evidenced, so experience feels unowned “as if” while the reflexive loop and insight persist. Valence differs because loss of allostatic control is aversive and voluntary prior-relaxation is not.
- **Thought insertion.** Precision-weighted context integration fails for specific inner-speech contents, consistent with Sterzer et al. 2016 and the corollary-discharge findings.

**Consequence for the article.** The beautiful loop predicts the very dissociation pattern the article calls “natural experiments that … expose [mine-ness] as a thing in its own right”. It also supplies a mechanism sketch where the article posits a void. Separability therefore does not discriminate. It is not even mildly suggestive for dualism over this rival. The beautiful loop also directly contests “absence, never mechanism”: it is a testable mechanism claim, with MPE-92M and psychedelic dose-response as handles. Under the gate, the article must engage it as the strongest rival.
