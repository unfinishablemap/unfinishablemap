## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

[samkhya-three-way-distinction](/concepts/samkhya-three-way-distinction/) gives Chalmers the sharpest possible statement of the gap by moving the line: "The explanatory-gap is not between mind and body but between awareness and everything else" [1 for `between awareness and everything else`]. If reasoning, ego and emotion are all *prakriti*, the residue that resists capture is narrower and harder to wave away than in any Western dualism. The page then declines the easy identification Chalmers's own vocabulary invites: O'Brien-Kop's contentless *purusha* "does not contain sensation, feeling, or experience" [1], so it "is precisely not phenomenal character in Chalmers's or Block's sense" [1]. A page that finds its best ally and then refuses to over-read him is doing Chalmers's work better than a page that annexes him.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

Stapp wants to know where mind gets purchase on physics, and the wing answers *not here*, each time in its own idiom. Kabbalah inverts its own analogy on quantity, modality and sequence, then adds that even the corrected temporal picture "does not carry the quantitative detail Tenet 2 needs" [1]. Sāṃkhya L117 says the reflection model "shares a structural concern with the Map's tenet" and in the next sentence that the Map "goes further than Samkhya" [1], a divergence stated at the exact point of resemblance. The Indigenous page says Minimal Quantum Interaction "has no counterpart here and would be foreign to the tradition's frame" [1]. The Chinese page's Tenets 2 and 4 do not appear in the body at all, which the 10-19 review already noted gives the quantum-sceptic "no purchase".

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

The Indigenous page is the wing's Nagelian page because it takes the *question* seriously enough to say the tradition may not ask it: Aboriginal thought "may not pose the hard problem at all" [1], and "That is not agreement with the Map's answer; it is a tradition that declines the Map's question." [1]. [mesoamerican-nahua-philosophy-of-mind](/topics/mesoamerican-nahua-philosophy-of-mind/) L39 says why, in the Map's own terms: the hard problem "needs two relata held apart" [1], and teotl supplies no pair, so the question "has nowhere to lodge" [1]. The Chinese page's L61 is the strongest single Nagelian sentence in the wing: after conceding that the medieval tradition did argue about what spirit is, "Nobody asked why a bodily process should be accompanied by anything it is like at all." [1]. That is the what-it-is-like question used as a test of the historical record, and the record is reported as failing it.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

Teotl is process metaphysics without qualification: "A thing *is* what it *does*" [1], with *olin*, *malinalli* and *nepantla* as the motion-patterns and "agonistic inamic unity" [1] as the generative tension. Chinese *qi* is "a single substance-process" [1] of which thought and tissue are phases. Kabbalah's sefirot are a descending emanation. *Constraint applied.* None of this moves anything on the evidential-status scale, and the pages are the ones that say so. Nahua: "Teotl-monism is a rival to Tenet 1 (Dualism), not an ally" [1]. Chinese: the organicist dissolution "is not, by itself, evidence that the distinction is unreal" [1]. Kabbalah: "One can borrow the shape without borrowing the substance—and must say so" [1]. Three process-friendly traditions, three verdicts of *rival*. Whitehead's praise and Birch's restraint land on the same sentences.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

The wing's one real sourcehood discussion is the negative one, and Kane should welcome that it is stated as a problem for the Map rather than smoothed over. Sāṃkhya L107: *purusha* "does not act, choose, or bias outcomes. This conflicts directly with the Map's Bidirectional Interaction tenet" [1 for `conflicts directly`], and the page presses the hard version: "can consciousness be both the kind of thing that witnesses and the kind of thing that selects among quantum superpositions?" [1]. The page's answer, that the passive witness is "motivated by soteriological concerns" [1] rather than by metaphysical evidence, is offered as "One response" [1], not as a refutation. The Indigenous page's rhyme between obligation-bearing agency and Bidirectional Interaction is held at "rhymes with" [1], the right register.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

Four pages refuse to adjudicate a dispute their own argument depends on, and each names the disputants. Kabbalah: Scholem's exile against Idel's restraint, and separately Mitnagdim against Hasidim, with the instruction that "even the choice between them is unsettled" [1].
