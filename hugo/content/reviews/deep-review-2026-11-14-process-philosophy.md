## Summary

This pass was not a no-op. Since 07-17 the page took seven refine-draft or cross-review edits (09-30, 10-10, 10-22, 10-24, 10-26, 11-09, 11-11). Together they added about 540 words, five References entries and about fifteen new external quotations (the regnant-occasion fold B1, Griffin pp. 181, 186 and 236, *The Concept of Nature* ch. There were two real defects, and both sit in the quantum and temporal sections that no recent edit touched:

1. **A stale Map-internal representation (L110).** The page said the [time-consciousness-growing-block](/apex/time-consciousness-growing-block/) apex "develops this connection" through a mental-pole analogy and a three-part mapping: prehension as inheritance, concrescence as collapse, and creativity as "the novel contribution consciousness makes to each moment". The apex dropped the mental-pole sentence on 2026-03-14 (`66ad2cd2ff`) and the three-part mapping on 2026-04-15 (`636482f6e0`). Its current L153 says only that concrescence "parallels quantum collapse" and that the precedent shows "the picture does not depend on quantum mechanics for its coherence". The creativity clause also contradicted this page's own L58, where creativity is Whitehead's universal "principle of novelty", and Whitehead's text: "consciousness presupposes experience, and not experience consciousness" (PR Part II ch. I). **Fixed.** The paragraph now reports what the apex says and labels the concrescence–collapse pairing as the Map's adaptation of Whitehead's vocabulary, not his claim. It adds the verified PR quotation.
2. **A Stapp attribution and a superlative (L134).** The text read "For Stapp, the 'Heisenberg choice' … and the 'Dirac choice' … parallel Whitehead's subjective aim and satisfaction", and called this "the most developed attempt". [deep-review-2026-02-17-subjective-aim](/reviews/deep-review-2026-02-17-subjective-aim/) had already found this exact one-to-one mapping overstated: there is no textual evidence that Stapp draws it. Its stability note says "Future reviews should not re-tighten the mapping without specific textual evidence". This page never got that correction. The raw text of Stapp's 1998 Whitehead paper (LBNL-42143) has zero hits for "satisfaction" and "subjective aim". It does draw the general actual-occasion/quantum-event correspondence, which the page keeps. **Fixed.** The parallel is now marked as structural, with Stapp's intent described as unclear, following the subjective-aim wording. "The most developed" became "among the most developed", and Epperson (2004) is named as the book-length comparator.
