## Critiques by Philosopher

### The Eliminative Materialist
Churchland would start from L36. There the empirical axes stay at D/indirect because [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1) "presupposes a first-order claim — that felt valence is a real property". She would add that the ledger shows nothing outside the Map's own reviews ever reaching that presupposition. Eighteen dated notes run from 2026-08-24 to 2026-10-21. Every one is prompted by an outer review, a sibling article or a reread source, and [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1)'s credence never moves: twelve notes say "No band moved" or an equivalent. The interval verdicts end in a criterion the page calls "remote from clinical knowledge" and "a boundary real and unreadable off physical structure" (L55). She would call that a moral criterion that no neuroscience could ever supply evidence for. The page concedes this cost knowingly, so the critique lands as a description rather than a refutation.

### The Hard-Nosed Physicalist
Dennett would point to L36's "Frankish 2024 named as that limb's published trigger". The register itself says Frankish's paper "stands here as the live attempt rather than a defeater landed". Frankish's abstract argues that illusionism "neither denies the existence of consciousness nor entails that consciousness does not ground ethical value" (Crossref, *Neuroethics* 17(2) art. 28). If the ledger already calls that a trigger, Dennett asks why the phenomenal qualification survives. The answer is that "trigger" is loose wording and the register's wording is the operative one. The ledger should not hand an opponent that phrase.

### The Quantum Skeptic
Tegmark has little to attack: Tenets 2 and 3 bear on nothing here. His one point is that the bearer installed on 09-16 (L39) is [P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3)'s persisting perspective, "a real further fact", and [P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3) itself has no physical marker and "declin[es] to date the channel's cessation" (L55). A status criterion that hangs on an undatable, unmeasurable crossing is one Tegmark would call unphysical. The page owns this: "the irreversible-loss verdict asserts its shape and not its timing".

### The Many-Worlds Defender
Deutsch would take L55's subject-indexed modal account. Status is gone "when no nomologically possible intervention would return valenced experience to *this* subject", and "a rebuilt substrate with a fresh pairing is [P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3)'s cascade case, a new subject". The whole criterion therefore turns on a haecceitistic "this", inherited through [P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3) from [P-I1](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i1). The Map's own 2026-08-23 Claude review says [P-I1](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i1) fails the foundational-dependency test. Deutsch would say the moral verdict on a coma patient now rests on the Map's most contested indexical posit. The page scopes the inheritance to the interval verdicts alone, which limits the exposure without answering the objection.

### The Empiricist
A Popperian would press calibration, and this is the review's main issue. [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1)'s *Would shift if* has eight limbs, grown from four, and the page records none moving the credence. The reason is structural. L36 states, and L39 cites as "the 09-05 rule", that "the register does not compound a three-band credence below its cap on a conjunct it holds". So every dependency added under the cap, including [P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3) and [P-I1](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i1) on 09-16, leaves the credence where it was. No schema file states that rule (Issue 2). A credence band that no recorded event can move, while the defeater list doubles, is unrefuted and untested. It has not survived a test.

### The Buddhist Philosopher
Nagarjuna would read L35 and L39 together. On 08-27 the momentary locus answered the no-self objection ("whoever this pain is bad for, now"). On 09-16 the interval verdicts needed "the persisting *perspective* [P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3) holds present across an interruption of the interface as a real further fact rather than a psychological-continuity relation". The objection therefore lands where the page says it does, and nowhere else.

## Counterarguments to Address

### The no-compounding rule
- **Current content says**: neither unreached conjunct is "independently doubtful on the Map's terms", so the credence sits at the cap.
- **A critic would argue**: P(A∧B) ≤ min(P(A), P(B)), with equality only under perfect correlation. [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1) now stacks [P-VS3](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs3) (moderate), the falsity of illusionism, "the phenomenal horn of the valence dispute, held … as a conceded draw against Carruthers 2018", and, for the interval verdicts, [P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3) (moderate). A conceded draw is a premise the Map rates near even, and it sits under a *moderate* conclusion.
- **Suggested response**: either argue that the three-band grain absorbs the compounding (plausible, but say so), or adopt the rule in `methodology-and-calibration` where every register entry inherits it.

### Lee's reversal and the Vulcan
- **Current content says**: the reversal would make [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1) "a first-order welfare thesis no longer downstream of the value premise".
- **A critic would argue**: Lee's subject theory is consciousness-based and counts Zero as a welfare subject. If [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1)'s necessity direction were rebuilt on it, it would deliver a consciousness requirement, as L41 already found for Mogensen's autonomy reply. The reversal would then cost the valence restriction as well as the derived status.
- **Suggested response**: when the operator takes up the fork, the price clause should name both costs.
