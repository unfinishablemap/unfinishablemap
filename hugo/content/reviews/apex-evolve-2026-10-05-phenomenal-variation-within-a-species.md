## Optimistic Review

**Connection Finder.** Three sources now meet at a point the synthesis had not marked. The contrast method's defensible form is within-subject; measurement theory says no axiom on one subject's comparisons fixes alignment between subjects; and tetrachromacy shows that even a certified functional difference licenses no cross-subject phenomenal ordering. Together they specify exactly which of the cluster's contrasts carry weight — the categorical ones — and why the vividness spectrum between the extremes does not.

**Synthesis Strengthener.** The strongest new point is that Schwitzgebel's unreliability is at once the dissolver against the cluster and the standing objection to the fading/dancing-qualia reductios, and that Chalmers' reply appeals to the same partial reliability the wedge needs. A functionalist can cap the cluster with organizational invariance or dissolve it with wholesale unreliability, but the second weakens the defence of the first. No source article states this; it is visible only when the cluster and the invariance article are read together.

**Human Reader Advocate.** The olfactory case gives the imagery class a concrete, slightly surprising illustration of why this variation went unnoticed for a century. Removing self-announcing sentences lets the classes read as argument.
