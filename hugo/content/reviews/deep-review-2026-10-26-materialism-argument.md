## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

1. **Internal contradiction: L68 Dennett dissolution test vs L70.** L68 argued: "if phenomenal concepts were confused functional surrogates, sustained contemplative training should dissolve the seeming ... Instead, training appears to *sharpen* phenomenal discrimination." L70 (installed 10-20) says that a persisting seeming "decides nothing, since a rich illusion and a veridical seeming predict it equally". The Map's own roster (`9afad1f283`, 09-05) files Dennett as an illusionist, so the L68 argument rests on the prediction the 10-20 fix said illusionism does not make. This string sibling was left live by a fix made by file. The residual "in-framework" clause also failed twice. "heterophenomenology still predicts that the *content* of trained reports should drift toward functional vocabulary" attributes to Dennett's method a prediction it does not make (source/Map conflation). "long-term practitioners' reports do not show that drift" is an unsourced, unscoped absence claim, and it sits against L135's own concession that the tradition's analysis "points toward *anattā*". The outer review's unadjudicated Churchland paragraph ([outer-review-2026-10-20-claude-opus-5-5](/reviews/outer-review-2026-10-20-claude-opus-5-5/) §6) makes the same point: the evidence "is therefore at best neutral". **Resolution**: the block now says contemplative evidence "cannot settle the matter". Sharper discrimination is "a functional gain an eliminativist also expects", a persisting seeming decides nothing ("as with Frankish, below"), and heterophenomenology's restriction stands. The disagreement is "methodological rather than settled". This does not re-flag "the Dennett reply is weak" (07-29 Stability Note). It goes further in the direction that note set, removing the last in-framework clause, which the 10-20 change had made contradictory.
2. **Stranded dependent of the 10-23 weak-Revelation fix (L74/L76).** "When we conceive of zombies, we are positively conceiving of a coherent scenario, not merely failing to see a necessity due to ignorance" asserts flat the step L66 now calls contested: Type-B physicalists say exactly that we fail to see an a posteriori necessity. L76 then drew the conclusion unconditionally ("Consciousness is something over and above the physics"). **Resolution**: L74 now reads "The Map holds that conceiving of zombies is positively conceiving a coherent scenario rather than failing, through ignorance, to see a necessity; Type-B physicalists contest exactly this (above)." L76 became conditional ("If that holds, consciousness is something over and above the physics: the zombie world has all the physical facts and none of the phenomenal ones"), and the duplicate zombie-world definition was absorbed.
3. **House ruling 9 / [P-Q8](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q8): magnetoreception read as support, and Luo 2025 framing wrong (L103).** The old text was: "Avian magnetoreception relies on quantum spin coherence in cryptochrome proteins persisting for microseconds, supported computationally by first-principles simulations of radical-pair stability in cryptochrome 4a (Luo et al., 2025). Evolution can harness coherence." The defects were (a) "relies on" against [P-Q8](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q8)'s "a leading model unsettled in vivo", (b) no mention that the coherence is unmeasured, (c) no mention of the strength gap, and (d) "Evolution can harness coherence" as licence. [P-Q8](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q8) says: "Citations of warm-quantum-biology that read as support rather than precedent are calibration drift and are corrected when caught." (e) Luo et al. 2025 does not report what it was cited for (see ledger). **Resolution**: the bullet now says the leading account is radical-pair spin chemistry in cryptochrome, that "its coherence, inferred at roughly 2–10 µs, has never been measured", and that "the current model cannot explain the strength of the behavioural effects (Hiscock et al., 2017)". It ends: "It is a [precedent, not a licence](/concepts/radical-pair-magnetoreception/): warm quantum effects can occur in living systems, at timescales far below neural ones." The 2–10 µs figure is linked to, not re-derived, since the concept page carries Kobylkov et al. 2019 and asks downstream pages to link it. Luo removed (now orphaned); Hiscock added to References.
4. **House ruling 5 (conservation, L106).** "consciousness might bias quantum indeterminacy in the brain without violating conservation laws or injecting energy" → "while keeping the [ensemble energy expectation](/concepts/conservation-laws-and-mental-causation/) intact, though per-trial and local accounting remains owed". This is the canonical wording of `apex/consciousness-and-agency` L129 and `topics/consciousness-and-causal-powers` L80.
5. **Dropped qualifier vs Tenet 2 (L141).** "Quantum indeterminacy provides this interface without requiring violations of physical law" → "Quantum indeterminacy is the candidate location for this interface." The tenet's own rationale says "provides a *candidate* location". The "without requiring violations" clause asserted flat what ruling 5 says is owed. −3 words.
6. **[P-D1](/positions/arguments-for-dualism/#p-d1) / [P-M2](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#p-m2): undiscounted convergence that also misdescribes the article (L139).** "The arguments here—from the hard problem, zombies, epiphenomenalism, and quantum indeterminacy—converge on the claim that materialism *cannot in principle* explain consciousness". There were two defects. (a) The hard problem, zombie and knowledge arguments are the premise-sharing cluster [P-D1](/positions/arguments-for-dualism/#p-d1) says "contribute little more than one strong argument". (b) The epiphenomenalism and quantum sections never argue in-principle inexplicability; they bear on interaction and closure. **Resolution**: "The hard-problem, zombie and knowledge arguments make the case that materialism *cannot in principle* explain consciousness ... They press one gap from shared premises, so they count as one strong case rather than three ([P-D1](/positions/arguments-for-dualism/#p-d1)); the epiphenomenalism and quantum sections bear on interaction rather than irreducibility." The sentence "The explanatory gap is a structural feature of materialist methodology rather than a temporary limitation" was dropped. It duplicated L51, and it was an unlabelled necessity claim under the method/history rule.
