## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)
The panpsychism article states the anti-emergence case cleanly ("Emergence can't be brute," Strawson) and correctly frames the hard problem as the *shared load-bearing premise* both panpsychism and the Map depend on — while flagging it as "a substantive commitment, not a settled fact" (panpsychism.md:49). Chalmers's own panpsychist/panprotopsychist distinction is rendered accurately, including his concession that panprotopsychism's answer to the zombie revenge is "less clear-cut than the original conceivability argument" (line 81). The combination-problem article quotes his real 2017 pessimism ("it is easy to be pessimistic about the prospects for a solution") without over-reading it as a concession that panpsychism lacks any advantage (combination-problem.md:128). This is Chalmers handled by someone who has read Chalmers.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)
The interaction alternative is presented with its mechanism (quantum-Zeno / orchestrated collapse) *and* its live objection (femtosecond decoherence vs millisecond neural processes) in the same breath (panpsychism.md:130). Stapp would recognize the physics-consciousness interface being engaged seriously rather than gestured at — and would appreciate that the article calls it "a research programme, not an established result" rather than overselling.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)
The grain problem is credited to Nagel and stated in his own terms — the categorical difference between discrete physical composition and the "smooth, unified character of experience" (combination-problem.md:80). First-person unity is treated as the explanandum that every proposed solution must answer to, not explained away.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)
The dedicated "Whitehead and the Process Tradition" section (panpsychism.md:63-69) is exactly the move this persona rewards: it reframes combination as "how do experiential *processes* flow into unified streams?" and makes phenomenal unity an *achievement* of creative synthesis rather than a static aggregation. Crucially — and this is what earns the praise rather than a flag — this process-friendly reframing is **never used to upgrade any organism's evidential status**. "Prehension" and "actual occasions" remove a defeater against the coherence of ubiquitous experience; they are not deployed as evidence that C. elegans or Hydra is conscious. The persona's constraint is honored.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)
Less central to this pair, but the Bidirectional Interaction tenet's insistence that consciousness "*influences* physics" rather than merely being its intrinsic aspect (panpsychism.md:126, 160) preserves the causal-efficacy Kane needs. The IIT critique makes exactly this point: if consciousness *is* integrated information, "it doesn't *do* anything separate from the physical" (line 160) — protecting genuine mental causation.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)
Both articles reserve a genuine seat for cognitive closure rather than name-checking it. The panpsychism article ends its testability section on the possibility that the combination problem is "permanently beyond human understanding" and therefore "potentially unanswerable by minds like ours — not meaningless, just potentially unanswerable" (line 172), which is McGinn's position stated precisely.

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)
**This persona's verdict is load-bearing for this cluster, and the verdict is strongly positive.** The articles repeatedly do the tenet-as-evidence-upgrade that they are structurally positioned to do — and decline it:

- **The boundary-case calibration is exemplary.** The 2024 New York Declaration is reported at its actual scope: "at least a realistic possibility" for cephalopods, decapods, and insects, but *explicitly not* for the minimal organisms (nematodes, Hydra, slime molds), so it is "weaker evidence for panpsychist continuity than for bees or octopuses," and "equally compatible with emergentist and functionalist frameworks that set a low threshold" (panpsychism.md:105). C. elegans, Hydra, and slime molds are held at "proto-experience / cannot unite micro-experiences into integrated subjectivity" — i.e. at the low rungs of the scale — never elevated to "realistic possibility" on tenet load.
- **The Φ evidence is quarantined precisely.** Both articles distinguish the autoregressive proxy Φ_AR (Antonopoulos, Fokas & Bountis 2015) from "the canonical IIT 4.0 Φ, which has never been computed for the worm," and conclude "this does not establish that C. elegans is conscious under IIT" (panpsychism.md:170; combination-problem.md:165). This is the single most common calibration error in this subject area, and it is handled correctly in two independent places.
- **Tenet-as-evidence-upgrade is praise-worthily *not* done.** The panpsychism article grants that "Parsimony favours panpsychism here … a cost the Map accepts … rather than understates" (line 130) — a rare instance of an article conceding a parsimony point *against its own framework* rather than tenet-spinning it away.
- **The "avoids the problem entirely" over-claim is self-corrected.** The combination-problem article's own lead/description says interactionism "avoids it entirely," and the "Unity of the Interface" section (combination-problem.md:151-157) then walks that back honestly: the Map "escapes *constitutive* combination without thereby dissolving every question of interface unity," and the residue "sits nearer bedrock than the 'avoids the problem entirely' framing implies." Naming your own strongest slogan as an over-claim is exactly the discipline this persona exists to reward.

**Convergence note:** because the Process Philosopher's praise (event-based reframing of unity) and the Hardline Empiricist's praise (no tier-upgrade on tenet load) both land without conflict, the cluster has resolved the designed tension honestly. No `refine-draft` calibration task is warranted.

## Content Strengths

### panpsychism.md
- **Strongest point**: The empirical-boundary-cases subsection (lines 97-105) — a model of how to discuss minimal-organism consciousness under a consciousness-friendly metaphysics without evidential inflation.
- **Notable quote**: "The verdict is consistent with panpsychism, but equally compatible with emergentist and functionalist frameworks that set a low threshold, and it concerns a far higher rung than the micro-experience panpsychism attributes to electrons."
- **Why it works**: It separates three registers that usually get collapsed — what panpsychism *predicts*, what the Declaration *affirms*, and what rung of the scale each concerns — and keeps the electron-level micro-experience claim distinct from the invertebrate-level realistic-possibility claim.

### combination-problem.md
- **Strongest point**: The "Unity of the Interface" section (151-157) distinguishing *constitutive* combination (building a subject from subjects — Coleman's incoherent summing) from *interface* unity (relating one already-unified subject to distributed matter), and refusing to pretend the latter fully vanishes.
- **Notable quote**: "constituting a subject from subjects and relating an already-unified subject to distributed matter are different burdens."
- **Why it works**: It answers the sharpest symmetry objection (that the Map "helps itself to the very thing panpsychism cannot earn") with a real disanalogy, then honestly locates the leftover residue near bedrock rather than declaring victory.
