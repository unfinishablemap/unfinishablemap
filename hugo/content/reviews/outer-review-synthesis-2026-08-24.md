## TL;DR

Three reviewers, three prompts, three houses — and one shared diagnosis. Each made **the correction that lands in one place and never binds anywhere else** the centre of its report, calling it *synchronisation failure*, *confession-without-correction*, and a register that *disguises contradictions as mechanism debts* respectively. That is the fourth independent diagnosis of this pattern and the first at 3-of-3.

The cycle's flagship defect is a textbook instance of it — `positions/subject-census` [P-SC1](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc1) infers census-invisibility from Born preservation, an inference `concepts/selection-only-channel` had withdrawn by counterexample. But the provenance carries a sting the reviewers missed: [P-SC1](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc1) was authored **57 minutes after** the withdrawal landed, not before it. **The dependent was created into an already-corrected corpus.** That falsifies the standing remedy — reverse-dependency propagation, which every reviewer has now proposed in some form, keys on a canonical claim *changing* and would never have fired here. The gap is a write-time read, not a change-time sweep.

Six convergent clusters (three actioned, three recorded), eight singletons, three genuine divergences. Three tasks upgraded P2 → P1; none deduplicated.

**Trust this cycle unevenly.** Verified-accuracy diverged sharply between legs — ChatGPT carried the cycle, Claude refuted eight of its own ten checkable claims including its self-declared most important finding, and none of Gemini's five numbered weaknesses survived as stated. Act on the adjudications in each review's Verification Notes, not on the reports.

## Convergent Findings

### C1. A correction or confession lands in one place and never binds anywhere else

- **Flagged by**: chatgpt, claude, gemini (3/3)
- **Verification**: Clean as a diagnosis; all three were adjudicated as restating the Map's own registered position **[P-M5](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#p-m5)** rather than as new charges. What is new is the instance, not the analysis.
- **Quotes**:

And §III.3: "the same propositions … are restated in each node rather than imported from a single canonical owner.   - **Claude Opus 5**: "Defects are named with unusual precision … and then converted into 'framework-boundary features the Map owns' rather than into binding status demotions of the articles that depend on them. Naming a defect is not discharging it." And: "The confession rate vastly exceeds the status-change rate."
  - **Gemini 2.5 Pro**: the framework "constructs a defensive perimeter of epistemological caveats — most notably encapsulated in its 'positions register' — that routinely function to disguise fatal theoretical contradictions as mere 'mechanism debts'."
- **Task action**: Recorded on the standing `NEEDS-HUMAN (methodology ratification) 2026-08-03` entry as its fourth addendum. No new task; no upgrade (the entry is operator-reserved by design).

**The finding that matters most is not the diagnosis — it is what the cycle's evidence does to the proposed cure.** That entry carries four remedies, (a)–(d), and this cycle's own flagship defect defeats the central one. `git log -S` puts the withdrawal at commit `1ab6b8a5bf`, 2026-08-03 05:36:01 UTC, and [P-SC1](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc1) — *including both defective spans* — at commit `8ca0143f5c`, 06:32:58 UTC the same morning. Fifty-seven minutes later, by a sibling `positions-evolve` run in the same automation window. Nothing went stale; a new node reproduced a refuted inference into a corpus that had already corrected it. Proposals (a)–(d) are all change-triggered or retrospective, as is ChatGPT's own fix 19. **None of them fires on this shape.** The enumeration needs a fifth item — an authoring-time canonical-read requirement, checking a new node's borrowed propositions against their owner at write time regardless of whether the owner recently moved.

And the second-order instance is worse than the first. The uncorrected [P-SC1](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc1) wording did not merely sit there: Gemini's audit **quotes it as a premise** and builds its central epiphenomenalism charge on it. A hostile external reviewer, explicitly instructed to consult the register before attacking, was misled by the unpropagated node into an argument the corpus had refuted three weeks earlier. An unpropagated correction does not stay a local defect; it becomes a claim about the Map made by third parties.

Two disputed claims would ordinarily be correlated error and count for nothing. The standing rule is right and is not being waived here. That is evidence the prose overrides the calibration for a careful adversarial reader — which is precisely the defect, and it is stronger evidence than a second reviewer simply agreeing would have been.

What this does **not** license: any change to [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1), which is correctly calibrated, or any adoption of either reviewer's framing. The task's DECLINE-IF clause stands unchanged. Convergence raises the priority of looking, not the obligation to find.
