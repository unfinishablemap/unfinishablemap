## Errors

Bald BEFORE shape: an authorship or difference-making claim asserted as though self-evident, with no grant to the Everettian and no marking of global exclusion as a posit.

### apex/altered-states-as-interface-evidence.md — L184
- **Tenet**: 4 (No Many Worlds) — **global-exclusion** calibration
- **Quote**: "The meditator who releases selection experiences its absence—a qualitative shift from choosing to witnessing that would be illusory under many-worlds, where branching occurs regardless. ... This variation in selection capacity is coherent only if selection is real; under many-worlds, it reduces to variation in the *illusion* of selection—explanatorily empty."
- **Issue**: The strongest overstatement found this run. The article's whole evidential structure — the convergence matrix mapping selection capacity across states — is declared unavailable to the Everettian. It is not. Branch-relative accounts grant variation in selection capacity as fully real *branch-locally*: the lucid dreamer's robust control and the meditator's release differ in-branch exactly as the matrix describes, and the phenomenological contrast is not illusory on any branch. "Explanatorily empty" is the claim the article has least earned. What branching denies is that the foreclosure is global — that not-flying is nonactual *anywhere*.
- **Recommendation**: Grant the Everettian the matrix entire (it costs the article nothing — the matrix is in-branch data), then locate the disagreement in whether the foreclosure the phenomenology presents is global. Mark global nonactuality as a `[[tenets/background-commitments|posit the Map adopts]]`. Drop "explanatorily empty".

### topics/consciousness-evolution-and-biology.md — L148
- **Tenet**: 4 — **global-exclusion** calibration
- **Quote**: "Under Many Worlds, every outcome is realised somewhere; consciousness biasing one branch over another changes nothing about the totality of outcomes."
- **Issue**: A pure difference-making claim, and the clearest unmarked instance of the exclusion posit in the corpus. "Changes nothing" is true only if a difference must be *global* to count as a difference. The Everettian denies exactly that: selection pressure operates branch-locally, evolutionary explanation is branch-local, and a bias that raises the measure of one branch is a real difference on any branch-relative reading. The sentence presents the exclusion condition as an observation about totality rather than as the framework's chosen starting point.
- **Recommendation**: The article-specific point is available and strong — an Everettian can grant consciousness-as-adaptation branch-locally, so the disagreement is not about whether the adaptation story runs but about whether "a single evolutionary narrative" is required for the *consciousness-evolution problem* to be well posed. State that, cite the posit, keep the conclusion.

### apex/testing-the-map-from-inside.md — L256
- **Tenet**: 4 — **global-exclusion** calibration
- **Quote**: "The settling moment when alternatives collapse, the felt rejection of unchosen options—these match collapse interpretations, not many-worlds branching where all outcomes occur."
- **Issue**: Compact and entirely uncalibrated. The felt rejection of unchosen options is available branch-locally in full; Everettian phenomenology includes the settling moment. This locus is doubly worth fixing because the article's whole subject is *what first-person evidence can and cannot establish*, and it here reads a phenomenological datum as discriminating between interpretations it does not discriminate between. That is the article's own thesis being violated in its tenet block.
- **Recommendation**: Note that the phenomenology is neutral between the readings, and that what makes the felt rejection *veridical as global foreclosure* is the posit, not the phenomenology. This turns a defect into an instance of the article's thesis.

## Cross-Calibration Errors

The two calibrations crossed — the finding class the driver specifically asked for.

### topics/evaluative-qualia-phenomenal-normativity-across-traditions.md — L108
- **Claim type**: moral weight / significance of suffering → should take **indexical** grounds
- **Grounds actually used**: single-branch reality — an exclusion-flavoured ground
- **Quote**: "The moral stakes that evaluative qualia generate—real suffering, real goodness—require a single-branch reality where outcomes matter. If all quantum branches are equally real, the felt badness that five traditions identify as irreducible carries no ultimate significance."
- **Issue**: Two defects in one sentence. First, wrong calibration: the significance of felt badness does not turn on whether alternatives are globally nonactual; it turns on whether *this* token badness is mine rather than a counterpart's. Measure-weighting says how much value a branch carries, not why this branch's badness is the one that counts for me — that is the indexical point, and the article never reaches it. Second, "carries no ultimate significance" is bald: an Everettian holds that branch-local badness is fully real badness, and a measure-weighted ethics is a live position, not an absence of significance.
- **Recommendation**: Re-ground on indexicality (the article's sibling `topics/emotion-and-dualism` L144 already has the correct wording and can be the model), and replace the significance-denial with the narrower claim the article needs.

### apex/contemplative-path.md — L199
- **Claim type**: authorship — "this moment's choices shape this practitioner's future" → should take **global-exclusion** grounds
- **Grounds actually used**: indexical identity
- **Quote**: "Many-worlds defenders argue that practice remains meaningful within each branch, but the Map holds that indexical identity—being *this* practitioner rather than a branching ensemble—matters for what contemplative traditions actually teach."
- **Issue**: Softer than the errors above, and partly calibrated already — it does raise the branch-local reply, which most loci do not. But it answers an *authorship* objection with an *indexical* premise, and the two come apart: granting a determinate "I" does not by itself deny that the unchosen path was taken by a counterpart. The claim that practice-choices author a future needs the exclusion posit; the indexical premise supports only the accompanying "felt singularity" clause.
- **Recommendation**: Keep the indexical premise where it belongs (felt singularity, *this* awareness) and add exclusion grounds for the authorship clause. Low-risk, one-clause fix.

## Warnings

Correct calibration direction, but closing on a flat assertion the Everettian denies. All four are the moral-weight family and share wording; treat as one fix.

### topics/phenomenal-value-realism.md — L245
- **Quote**: "If all branches are equally real, suffering is never truly reduced—only redistributed. ... Many-worlds makes moral urgency incoherent."
- **Issue**: The indexical grounding ("*this* suffering, in *this* branch") is correct and should be preserved. The closing "makes moral urgency incoherent" overshoots — Everettian decision theory reconstructs urgency as measure-reduction, which is a position to disagree with, not an incoherence.

### concepts/consciousness-value-connection.md — L166
- **Quote**: "Many-worlds dilutes value across infinite branches where it cannot be concentrated or redirected."
- **Issue**: The preceding sentence is correctly indexical ("presupposes that indexical facts matter"). The closing sentence then argues in measure language and asserts as fact something the branch-measure literature denies. The measure claim adds nothing the indexical claim has not already secured; the cheapest fix is deletion.

### topics/ethics-under-dualism.md — L202
- **Quote**: "If every choice actualised in some branch, suffering would never truly be prevented — only redistributed."
- **Issue**: Indexical-led and therefore correctly calibrated in direction; the closing conditional is the family's shared bald sentence. Same fix.

### topics/motor-control-quantum-zeno.md — L128
- **Quote**: "The Map's objection is not that branching eliminates the phenomenology of choice but that it eliminates its metaphysical significance: if every option is realised somewhere, the agent's contribution reduces to indexical perspective rather than genuine selection."
- **Issue**: The closest thing to the AFTER shape found outside the sweep — it already grants the Everettian the phenomenology and already names exclusion ("one outcome actualised, alternatives excluded"). What it does not do is mark the exclusion condition as a posit; "eliminates its metaphysical significance" is stated as a result. One clause short of correct.
- **Note**: This locus also uses the phrase "reduces to indexical perspective" pejoratively while the Map's own No-MWI case is *grounded* in indexical perspective. Worth a second look for internal consistency independently of the calibration.
