## Summary

Direct contradiction of the tenets is **absent**. Nothing in the corpus endorses eliminative materialism, illusionism, epiphenomenalism, quantum mysticism, or energy injection in the Map's own voice; every hit on those patterns is exposition-to-refute or a scoped concession. On the classic reading of this skill the corpus is clean.

Every finding below is instead an **uncalibrated-inheritance** failure: an article asserting a stronger tenet-derived result than the corpus has earned, where the calibrated wording *already exists elsewhere in the corpus* and simply has not propagated. This is the same failure mode the 07-28 Tenet 4 pass found, in three new places.

**Tenet 2 is the best-disciplined tenet in the corpus** and produced no errors at all. Roughly twenty files carry the "empirically indistinguishable from chance" caveat explicitly and correctly, [positions/quantum-interface.md](/positions/quantum-interface/) is exemplary across all ten of its positions, and no psychokinesis-endorsement, energy-injection or New-Age framing appears anywhere in Map voice — every psi mention is a disavowal or a firewall argument. Its four warnings are concentrated in two files.

Four families account for the findings; three produce all seven errors:

1. **The "epiphenomenalism is self-undermining" family (Tenet 3)** — the largest. `tenets.md` is unusually explicit that the Map holds Tenet 3 "*not* because the self-stultification argument refutes epiphenomenalism from inside the epiphenomenalist's framework", and that the phenomenal-concept strategy *survives* the self-undermining charge. Seven files nonetheless assert the refutation flat. The canonical calibrated statement sits at [concepts/bidirectional-interaction.md](/concepts/bidirectional-interaction/) L59 and is excellent; it has not reached its dependants.
2. **The parsimony-asymmetry family (Tenet 5)** — articles disarming parsimony when it is aimed at the Map, then wielding it in the Map's favour, sometimes in the same paragraph and usually under the Tenet 5 alignment heading itself. Tenet 5's Rules-out clause names this exact failure: "internally—any Map argument that leans on parsimony as if this tenet did not apply to it".
3. **Substance-lean mis-sourcing (Tenet 1)** — the smaller family. Not leakage into the wrong cluster so much as **wrong attribution**: articles saying the persisting-subject commitment "follows from the tenets" when `tenets.md` L56 and L180 place it downstream of *agent causation*, with Tenet 1 explicitly neutral.
4. **Corridor-vs-outside-corridor slippage (Tenet 2)** — warnings only. Articles treating a *minimum-outside-corridor* mechanism (one that predicts detectable deviation) as though it were what the tenet requires, or recasting the corridor's by-construction indistinguishability as a mere sensitivity limit that better instruments could overcome.

The structural observation worth carrying forward: in all three families the corpus contains a **model article that gets it right** and one or more dependants that do not. These are propagation failures, not thinking failures, which makes them cheap to fix and easy to verify.
