## Reply

# External referee report: Post-decoherence selection programme

**Recommendation: major revision before this article can support apex-level conclusions.**

## Executive judgment

The article identifies a legitimate **location** at which an interactionist theory might place consciousness: after environmentally induced decoherence has stabilised approximately classical alternatives, but before—or as part of—the production of a single actual outcome. That avoids requiring biologically useful macroscopic coherence to survive for hundreds of milliseconds.

It does not, however, provide the missing **law**. Moving selection after decoherence does not make the Born rule, no-signalling, conservation, or relativistic consistency disappear. It relocates those requirements from a pre-decoherence interaction Hamiltonian into an unspecified rule that chooses, actualises, or privileges one decohered alternative.

The article deserves credit for unusually explicit concessions. It acknowledges that its five components share a common selection-shaped architecture rather than independently triangulating the hypothesis; that decoherence leaves an improper rather than ignorance mixture; that no end-to-end model exists; and that the actualisation step remains unspecified. [apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme/) Those concessions prevent the article from making the crudest “five lines of evidence” mistake.

Nevertheless, much of the body quietly regains evidential confidence that the opening has surrendered. Quantum Darwinism, contextuality, weak measurement and stochastic amplification are treated as if they successively constrain or support the proposed selector. In fact, they mostly answer different questions:

- quantum Darwinism helps explain stable, redundant classical records;
- contextuality limits certain classes of ontological models;
- weak measurement defines an operationally successful measurement regime;
- stochastic amplification explains how small physical perturbations can sometimes influence large downstream trajectories.

None supplies a reason-responsive post-decoherence actualisation rule. Their conjunction is useful as a **research checklist**, but it is not yet positive evidence for consciousness-mediated selection.

The changelog makes the present article internally stale in an important respect. On 10 September 2026, the Map accepted that its Local Friendliness commitments place it on an absolute-outcome horn and require a departure from universal unitary evolution; it also clarified that “minimality” applies only at the proposed consciousness-selection point, not to the theory’s physical background. [workflow/changelog](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/) That concession has not been propagated into the 3 September apex article. The programme must now explain how consciousness-mediated selection relates to the background objective actualisation already needed to secure observer-independent facts.

My central findings are:

1. **No-signalling is relocated, not evaded.** Preserving the unconditioned Born marginal is far too weak. A viable model must preserve all remote marginals under interventions, steering, conscious-context conditioning and sequential composition.
2. **Energy conservation is not automatically violated, but neither is it secured.** “No energy is injected” is not an energy ledger. The answer depends on whether selection changes the quantum state, adds a hidden actual branch index, or leaves all branches in place.
3. **The residual menu-refresh problem is real but secondary.** It is a recurring symptom of the absence of a complete state-update and actualisation law, not the final small constraint after the major physics has been solved.
4. **The contextuality section contains a substantive definitional error.** It reverses Spekkens’ operational-equivalence condition and slides among Kochen–Specker contextuality, generalized contextuality and ordinary dependence on physical context.
5. **The weak-measurement component is considerably overstated.** Weak-value experiments verify conditional pointer statistics, not an ontic future boundary or a consciousness-selected global history.
6. **The amplification component establishes possible gain, not conscious control.** The Mainen–Sejnowski result is not a demonstration of stochastic resonance, and an energy-amplification ratio does not measure causal specificity.
7. **The article’s treatment of rival interpretations is partly criterion-loaded.** Everett, Bohm and objective-collapse theories are sometimes dismissed for declining to give consciousness the role the Map wants, rather than for failing on independently established physical criteria.

## 1. What post-decoherence placement does—and does not—buy

Environmental decoherence can suppress interference between certain robust states and make reduced density matrices approximately diagonal in a dynamically selected basis. Quantum Darwinism adds the possibility that information about those states becomes redundantly encoded across environmental fragments. Neither process, by itself, transforms an improper mixture into a unique actual event.

The article is therefore right that there is an interpretive or dynamical question left after decoherence. But its heading that presents this as a gap “physics cannot close” and labels it empirical is too strong. Physics has several candidate ways of closing or dissolving the gap:

- objective-collapse dynamics adds a physical stochastic actualisation law;
- Bohmian mechanics adds actual configuration variables;
- Everettian theories retain all branches and treat definiteness as branch-relative;
- modal and relational approaches assign actuality or fact-relativity through other formal structures.

One can reject those approaches, but their existence means that the inference

> decoherence does not produce a unique outcome  
> 
> therefore a nonphysical selector is required

is invalid. The empirical fact is that decoherence alone does not yield a unique outcome in the unitary formalism. The claim that the remainder must be filled by consciousness is an additional metaphysical and theoretical proposal. The neighbouring Quantum Darwinism page is more careful on exactly this point, noting that Zurek does not concede the interpretation the Map wishes to draw. [topics/quantum-darwinism-and-consciousness](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/quantum-darwinism-and-consciousness/)

### The missing mathematical object

A minimally explicit account would specify a quantum instrument {Mi​}:

p(i∣ρ,C)=Tr[MiC​(ρ)],ρi′​=Tr[MiC​(ρ)]MiC​(ρ)​,

where C represents whatever conscious condition, reason, intention or valence is supposed to influence selection.

The averaged map must normally satisfy

i∑​MiC​

being completely positive and trace-preserving—or the theory must state explicitly which part of that standard framework is being replaced.

At present the article does not say whether consciousness changes:

- the probabilities p(i);
- the post-outcome physical states ρi′​;
- only an additional “actual branch” variable;
- the system–environment factorisation defining the alternatives;
- the coarse-graining under which alternatives count as distinct;
- or the global boundary conditions of an entire history.

Those options have sharply different consequences. Until one is selected, the claims about no-signalling, conservation and Born compatibility are not assessable.

### An ontology trilemma

The programme appears to face three broad options.

**Physical collapse.** Consciousness eliminates all but one alternative or causes a nonlinear state update. The model then owes a collapse law, a relativistic composition rule, a conservation account and proofs of Born/no-signalling compatibility.

**Hidden actual branch index.** The quantum state remains uncollapsed, but one branch is additionally actual. The theory then resembles a hidden-variable or modal theory and owes an evolution/equilibrium law for that actual index. Contextuality becomes a constraint on that law, not evidence for it.

**All branches remain physically equivalent.** Consciousness merely occupies or identifies with one branch while all continue. That is close to an Everettian or self-locating account and no longer supplies the strong single-outcome actualisation the article advertises.

The article currently moves between the first two readings. Its language of converting an improper mixture into actuality suggests collapse, while its assurances that no energy is added and that the options already exist suggest selection of a pre-existing hidden alternative.

## 2. Born statistics and no-signalling

### Preserving a histogram is not preserving quantum statistics

The article proposes that conscious selection might redistribute individual outcomes while leaving the unconditioned Born marginal unchanged. [apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme/) That is not enough.

A physical theory must reproduce, or deliberately predict deviations in, at least:

1. single-measurement marginals;
2. correlations between separated measurements;
3. probabilities conditioned on preparation and measurement settings;
4. probabilities conditioned on conscious states or intentions;
5. sequential and multi-time probabilities;
6. postselected statistics;
7. outcomes under remote steering into different ensemble decompositions;
8. probabilities under interventions rather than merely passive observation;
9. memory-dependent frequencies across repeated trials.

A selector could preserve the global proportion of two outcomes while making one outcome more likely under conscious condition C1​ and the other more likely under C2​. The overall histogram would remain Born-distributed if the contexts balanced. Yet the conditioned statistics would differ from quantum mechanics and could permit communication or empirical detection.

This matters especially because the programme requires **reason-responsive efficacy**. If a conscious reason R affects which branch becomes actual, then ordinarily one expects

P(i∣ρ,R)=P(i∣ρ)

for at least some physically matched cases. That is a conditioned Born deviation. If no possible conditioning, intervention or downstream observation can reveal such a difference, the proposed causal role becomes operationally indistinguishable from a metaphysical relabelling.

### A Bell-pair diagnostic

Consider two separated laboratories sharing

∣Φ+⟩=2​∣00⟩+∣11⟩​.

Suppose decoherence produces two correlated alternatives and Alice’s conscious selector can favour the 0 or 1 branch. Averaging over Alice’s naturally occurring conscious states might leave Bob with the correct 50–50 marginal. But if Alice can intentionally set, influence or reliably correlate with the selector condition, Bob’s local outcome can reveal that condition.

The relevant requirement is therefore not merely

P(b∣y)=a∑​P(a,b∣x,y),

but something closer to

a∑​P(a,b∣x,y,do(C=c))

being independent of Alice’s setting x and of every locally controllable selector condition c.

If the programme says that conscious influence is never locally controllable, it must still address correlations between intention, choice and outcome. Otherwise it loses the agency it is intended to explain. If it says selection is strictly local and cannot determine a global branch, it must explain how separate local actualisations compose into one consistent set of macroscopic facts.

### What the no-signalling theorems establish

Simon, Bužek and Gisin showed that, under familiar assumptions concerning density matrices and remotely preparable ensemble decompositions, deterministic nonlinear evolution permits signalling and no-signalling drives the observable density-matrix dynamics toward linear completely positive maps. Later work has clarified that no-signalling by itself does not exclude every generalized nonlinear framework; carefully constructed alternatives can avoid signalling by changing other structural assumptions. [arXiv+1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0102125.pdf)

That nuance cuts both ways. The apex article should not claim that a bare appeal to no-signalling immediately derives the whole Born framework. But neither can it claim that preserving one marginal establishes compatibility. The burden is to identify the full framework being retained and then prove no-signalling within it.

The Torres preprint is relevant but currently used too broadly. Its reconstruction operates within a finite-dimensional generalized-probabilistic setting and relies on an interconnected set of assumptions concerning operational equivalence of ensembles, steering, local tomography, purification, continuous reversible dynamics and additional spectral/symmetry structure. Its own limitations leave context-dependent alternatives as an open issue rather than proving every context-sensitive selection rule impossible. [arXiv+4arXiv+4arXiv+4](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.12636v3) The article should present Torres as a constraint conditional on a framework, not as a general barrier that consciousness somehow slips through.

### The central Born-efficacy dilemma

The programme needs to state which horn it accepts:

- **Exact operational Born equivalence:** every observable joint, conditional, sequential and intervention distribution equals standard quantum theory. Conscious selection may then be ontologically real, but no experiment supports it over rival ontologies.
- **Context-conditioned deviations:** conscious variables alter some observable probabilities. The theory becomes empirical, but must specify the deviations, satisfy no-signalling and confront existing precision tests.
- **Fine-grained deviations with compensating averages:** deviations occur but cancel in coarse statistics. This still changes conditioned distributions and therefore does not avoid the preceding burden.

The neighbouring Born-efficacy page recognizes part of this dilemma and asks for a toy model plus an explicit no-signalling demonstration. [apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/) The apex article should inherit that requirement rather than presenting marginal preservation as substantial progress.

## 3. Energy conservation and thermodynamic accounting

The article’s claim that post-decoherence selection is minimal because it injects no energy is not established by locating selection after decoherence. [apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme/) Energy accounting depends on the state-update rule and ontology.

For projectors {Πi​}, an unconditioned projective update gives

ρ′=i∑​Πi​ρΠi​.

The mean energy change is

ΔE=Tr[H(i∑​Πi​ρΠi​−ρ)].

This vanishes automatically only under additional conditions—for example, when the relevant projectors commute appropriately with the total Hamiltonian or the initial state has a special form. Decoherence-selected pointer states are determined by robustness under the system–environment interaction; they are not generically exact eigenstates of the total energy.

For a selected outcome i,

Ei′​=Tr(Πi​ρ)Tr(HΠi​ρΠi​)​.

Even where the ensemble average is conserved, individual selected outcomes can have different energies. The Map’s own conservation article already acknowledges this distinction and concedes that ensemble preservation does not yet demonstrate thermodynamic innocence. [concepts/conservation-laws-and-mental-causation](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/conservation-laws-and-mental-causation/) The apex presentation is consequently stronger than the neighbouring analysis warrants.

Three questions must be separated:

1. **Eventwise conservation:** does each actual transition balance energy and momentum across the selected system, apparatus and environment?
2. **Ensemble conservation:** is mean energy preserved only after averaging over outcomes?
3. **Thermodynamic resource cost:** does implementing, recording or exploiting the selection consume work, entropy capacity or low-entropy memory?

Modern analyses of quantum measurement emphasize that cost depends on the complete measurement implementation—including apparatus, memory, bath, timing and measurement quality—not just on the abstract appearance of projectors. Conservation-constrained measurement is also subject to Wigner–Araki–Yanase-type limitations. [Quantum Journal+1](https://quantum-journal.org/papers/q-2025-01-28-1614/)

This does not prove that every consciousness selector must violate conservation. A hidden-variable actualisation that leaves the wavefunction unchanged may have a different energy status from a physical collapse. A total-system instrument could include compensating environmental changes. But the article may not infer conservation merely from the slogan that the chosen branch was “already present.”

### Amplification does not solve the energy question

A small control signal can direct a much larger energy flow supplied by an external power source. A transistor, enzyme, detonator or neural threshold crossing can therefore have a tiny initiating energy relative to its downstream consequences. This is ordinary gain, not free energy.

Consequently, the article’s multi-order-of-magnitude amplification chain does not show that consciousness can create a macroscopic effect without physical accounting. It shows only that, if a microscopic state difference exists and enters the right nonlinear dynamics, endogenous metabolic energy might amplify it. The theory still owes the origin, probability and conservation status of that state difference.

## 4. Is menu refresh the residual problem?

The article treats “menu refresh”—how a new set of alternatives is generated after one outcome is selected and further decoherence occurs—as an important remaining constraint. [apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme/) It is a genuine problem, but not the final modest piece left after a selector has otherwise been secured.

### There is no exact, unique menu yet

Einselection ordinarily yields an approximate robust basis relative to:

- a chosen system–environment division;
- a particular interaction Hamiltonian;
- a timescale;
- a tolerance for decoherence and record distinguishability;
- a coarse-graining of macroscopic alternatives.

Recent work continues to emphasize that Darwinistic objectivity depends on nontrivial Hamiltonian structure, sufficient environmental fragments and choices about subsystem factorisation. [arXiv+3Quantum Journal+3APS Journals+3](https://quantum-journal.org/papers/q-2021-11-08-571/) Thus the selector is not yet being handed a canonical finite list by settled physics.

Even where a pointer basis is highly stable for practical purposes, a consciousness theory must say at what grain alternatives become eligible. “Neuron fired” and “neuron did not fire” are coarse macroscopic descriptions containing immense microscopic multiplicity. A selector over exact microbranches is very different from a selector over functionally defined neural alternatives.

### Refresh requires the whole missing dynamics

After an outcome i, the theory must provide a new state ρi′​, evolve it, define a new factorisation and coarse-graining, identify the next eligible alternatives, and specify their probabilities. That is just a sequential stochastic theory:

P(i1​,…,in​∣C1​,…,Cn​)

together with state-update and composition rules.

The refresh problem therefore cannot be solved independently of:

- the actualisation ontology;
- the quantum instrument;
- temporal composition;
- memory effects;
- relativistic ordering of spacelike selections;
- conservation bookkeeping;
- the complete joint Born law.

It is better described as a **visible symptom of the missing process law**.

### Quantum Darwinism does not turn refresh into clerical bookkeeping

The article initially placed weight on redundant environmental records as though a selector might need to manage a multiplicity of independently agreeing fragments. The 10 September changelog correctly retracts that line of thought: Darwinistic fragments carry the same correlated branch index, so selecting one branch fixes a mutually consistent set of records rather than requiring independent record-by-record rewriting. [workflow/changelog](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/)

That correction removes one exaggerated obstacle, but it does not validate selection. It sharpens the real question: **what globally or locally makes one branch index actual?** If the answer is a global branch selector, relativistic nonlocal consistency becomes central. If the answer is local actualisation, the model must show how local events compose without contradictory records or signalling.

## 5. Audit of the five components

## 5.1 Post-decoherence selection

This is the programme’s strongest component as a **placement strategy**. It avoids reliance on long-lived neural superpositions and accepts the standard lesson that decoherence occurs rapidly in warm macroscopic systems.

Its weakness is that “after decoherence” is not a sharply defined fundamental time. Decoherence is continuous, approximate and scale-dependent. Environmental records emerge progressively, while neural dynamics contains nested open systems at molecular, membrane, cellular and network levels. The theory needs an eligibility criterion more precise than “once decoherence has happened.”

The article also slides from:

- decoherence does not by itself yield a unique outcome,

to:

- there is a selectable menu,

to:

- consciousness can actualise one member without otherwise changing physics.

Only the first claim follows from the standard measurement-problem discussion. The second and third require additional ontology.

## 5.2 Quantum Darwinism

Quantum Darwinism offers a serious account of why certain observables become effectively objective: multiple observers can infer the same pointer information from different environmental fragments. It can therefore help identify the sort of macroscopic records with which a consciousness-selection model must be consistent. [Quantum Journal](https://quantum-journal.org/papers/q-2021-11-08-571/)

But it does not provide:

- a unique actual outcome;
- a consciousness-sensitive probability rule;
- a canonical exact branch decomposition;
- evidence that environmental redundancy waits for consciousness to decide;
- an explanation of how local selection respects relativistic causal structure.

The article’s phrasing that the menu contains exactly the einselected states, “no more and no less,” is too exact. Darwinistic objectivity is conditional and approximate. Doucet and Deffner show that Darwinistic behaviour requires restrictive features of the interaction structure rather than arising generically from any open-system dynamics. Touil, Yan and Zurek emphasize that consensus depends on fragments containing sufficient information. More recent factorisation critiques raise questions about whether apparently classical subsystems and their pointer states are unique without additional physical structure. [APS Journals+2arXiv+2](https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevX.14.041064)

The article should also avoid treating Zurek’s envariance-based route to Born weights as an uncontested derivation. The relation among envariance, probability assumptions and the Born rule remains debated. [MDPI](https://www.mdpi.com/1099-4300/25/3/435) “Zurek argues” would be more defensible than “the weights are inherited” unless the programme explicitly states the extra assumptions.

**Verdict:** useful for record stability and basis approximation, but neutral concerning conscious actualisation.

## 5.3 Contextuality

This section requires substantial rewriting.

Spekkens’ generalized noncontextuality principle does not say that operationally equivalent preparations can yield different statistics. Operational equivalence means precisely that the preparations yield the same observable statistics in all allowed measurements. A preparation-noncontextual ontological model then represents those operationally equivalent procedures by the same ontic distribution. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0406166) The article’s present formulation reverses this relationship.

The discussion also conflates three notions:

- **Kochen–Specker contextuality:** the impossibility of a global, context-independent assignment of outcome values satisfying the relevant functional constraints;
- **generalized contextuality:** the failure of ontological representations to respect operational equivalences;
- **ordinary physical context dependence:** a system responding differently because its environment, apparatus or biological state differs.

Only the third is immediately useful to the article’s idea that selection might depend on the whole conscious situation. But ordinary context sensitivity does not acquire evidential support merely because quantum theory exhibits formal contextuality.

The claim that contextuality follows straightforwardly from noncommutativity is also misleading. Kochen–Specker contexts consist of mutually compatible—hence commuting—observables; the obstruction concerns embedding these overlapping compatible sets in one global noncontextual value assignment.

Most importantly, the post-decoherence regime can be effectively described by a diagonal, approximately commutative pointer algebra. The programme must identify a specific contextuality scenario that survives the relevant decoherence and is causally available to the selector. Recent results are nuanced: generalized contextuality can disappear after finite decoherence in some superconducting-qubit scenarios, while other constructions retain contextual behaviour even with strong dephasing. [arXiv+1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.13421) There is no generic inference from “quantum systems are contextual” to “a post-decoherence consciousness selector has contextual room to act.”

**Verdict:** contextuality is a constraint on candidate hidden-variable selection laws, not positive evidence for consciousness-mediated selection.

## 5.4 Weak measurement and time-symmetric structure

Weak measurement is experimentally well established as an operational protocol. Preselection, weak coupling and postselection produce conditional pointer shifts characterized by weak values. The neighbouring Map article correctly notes that these findings do not by themselves settle the ontology. [concepts/weak-measurement-and-post-selection](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/weak-measurement-and-post-selection/)

The apex article is less restrained. A two-state-vector or time-symmetric representation can encode dependence on both initial and final boundary conditions, but the successful statistics do not prove that a backward-evolving physical state exists or that a future conscious act selects the past history. The inference from formal usefulness to ontology is disputed, including in the contemporary exchange over whether weak traces identify where a particle “was.” [APS Journals+1](https://journals.aps.org/prresearch/pdf/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.5.023048)

There is also a tension with the programme’s post-decoherence location. Anomalous weak values generally depend on maintaining the relevant coherence and interference structure; decoherence can destroy the resource that gives weak values their distinctive character. [APS Journals](https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevA.108.L040202) The article needs to specify whether weak measurement is:

- a proposed biological mechanism;
- a formal analogy for conditioning;
- a possible experimental probe;
- or support for a global atemporal ontology.

Those are different claims.

An atemporal whole-history formulation does not automatically solve no-signalling or free-choice problems. It moves them into a global measure over histories. That measure must reproduce ordinary quantum correlations, avoid exploitable retrocausality, explain how settings and conscious boundary conditions are distributed, and say what distinguishes selection from ordinary postselection.

**Verdict:** legitimate mathematical inspiration, but presently no evidential bridge to consciousness.

## 5.5 Stochastic amplification

The general proposition is sound: nonlinear, metastable biological dynamics can amplify very small physical perturbations. Neural threshold events can influence subsequent population activity, motor commitments and environmental outcomes.

But that establishes only a possible **gain stage**. It does not establish:

- that quantum alternatives remain functionally distinct at the relevant location;
- that consciousness chooses among them;
- that the choice correlates with reasons or values;
- that the perturbation propagates reliably rather than being washed out;
- that downstream behaviour differs beyond ordinary neural variability.

The Mainen–Sejnowski experiment showed highly reproducible spike timing in neocortical neurons under fluctuating current injection. It is not, by itself, an experiment on stochastic resonance. [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/7770778) The Map’s neighbouring amplification article is actually more accurate, describing it as a related result outside the stochastic-resonance literature. [topics/amplification-mechanisms-consciousness-physics](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/amplification-mechanisms-consciousness-physics/) The apex article should use the same characterization.

Likewise, Thura and Cisek studied commitment-related dynamics in a particular reach-decision paradigm. Their timing does not establish a universal interval in which conscious selection acts. [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/24656257) Evidence concerning neural criticality also needs care: avalanche-like signatures and apparent critical dynamics can arise from latent-variable structure without fine-tuning to a true critical point. [eLife](https://elifesciences.org/articles/89337)

The article’s order-of-magnitude chain from microscopic energy to muscular work risks confusing energy gain with causal evidence. What is needed is a quantitative transmission model containing, for example:

P(spike∣i,C),I(i;network trajectory),P(behaviour∣i,C),

along with false-amplification rates, noise susceptibility and the metabolic work source.

**Verdict:** amplification makes microscopic efficacy dynamically conceivable, but contributes no evidence for the selector or its semantic control.

## 6. Rival interpretations and tenet-protective reasoning

The article’s comparison with alternatives is too compressed to bear the argumentative work assigned to it.

### Everett

Everettian approaches do not simply deny that definite records exist. They explain definiteness as branch-relative and retain all decohered outcomes. Their central debts concern probability, ontology, preferred structure and personal identity. The Map may reasonably reject branch-relative actuality, but it should engage those arguments rather than define “actualisation” so that Everett fails by stipulation.

### Bohmian mechanics

Bohmian mechanics does not regard the outcome gap as merely verbal. It supplies actual configurations whose effective wavefunctions occupy definite branches. Whether the model is satisfactory is a separate question involving nonlocality, relativistic extension, equilibrium and the status of configuration. Its relevance to this article is especially close because an added “actual branch” variable could turn the consciousness proposal into a branch-index analogue of hidden-variable theory.

### Objective collapse

Saying that objective-collapse models make consciousness a spectator identifies their incompatibility with the Map’s interactionist tenet; it does not identify a physical defect. A fair comparison must ask which account has the better empirical predictions, mathematical consistency, relativistic prospects and ontological economy—not which one assigns consciousness the preferred role.

### The gap-filling inference

The article eventually says that the residual gap lies precisely where a nonphysical principle operates. [apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme/) The evidence supports only:

1. unitary decoherence does not uniquely actualise an outcome;
2. a single-outcome interpretation therefore needs some additional account;
3. consciousness selection is one conceivable additional account.

It does not establish that the form of the gap independently matches consciousness. Without a selector law, “the fit” is generated by defining consciousness as that which fills the unfilled step.

### “Pre-Keplerian” status

Calling the programme pre-Keplerian is defensible if it means that the page is organizing questions before a mature model exists. It becomes tenet-protective if it excuses indefinite absence of:

- quantitative predictions;
- model comparison;
- failure conditions;
- operational definitions;
- or evidence capable of discriminating consciousness selection from ordinary quantum mechanics.

Tycho supplied precise observations that constrained later theories. The five components here are not analogous observations of conscious selection; they are bodies of theory and experiment that a future selector would have to coexist with.

## 7. The Local Friendliness update changes the architecture

The 10 September update is not a peripheral addition. The Map now acknowledges that retaining absolute observer-independent outcomes, locality and the relevant freedom/no-superdeterminism assumptions cannot all coexist with universal unitary treatment of conscious laboratories. It chooses absolute outcomes and accepts a nonunitary objective-reduction background. [workflow/changelog](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/) This is a defensible explicit choice, but it generates a new integration problem.

If objective reduction already creates one absolute outcome, when does consciousness select?

### Possible layering A: objective reduction first

If physical reduction has already selected an outcome, consciousness has no residual menu to actualise. It might respond to the result, but that is not post-decoherence selection.

### Possible layering B: consciousness participates in reduction

Consciousness biases or constitutes the reduction event. Then it changes outcome probabilities or the collapse instrument and inherits the full Born, no-signalling, energy and relativistic burdens.

### Possible layering C: consciousness selects before objective reduction

Consciousness determines which alternative the subsequent physical reduction confirms. This is effectively a hidden-variable or retrocausal theory unless a concrete coupling law is supplied.

### Possible layering D: background reduction applies only outside consciousness

That would require a principled boundary separating conscious and nonconscious actualisation and risks returning to observer-dependent collapse.

The updated Local Friendliness page itself notes that the theory’s background is not minimal in the sense of retaining universal unitarity. [concepts/post-decoherence-selection](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/post-decoherence-selection/) The apex article should therefore replace any global “minimal new physics” suggestion with a narrower claim: conditional on an independently motivated objective-actualisation background, consciousness might add a small bias or eligibility rule. But that narrower claim makes the need to specify the division of labour even more urgent.

## 9. Overall assessment

As a research-programme page, the article has genuine value. Its best contribution is organizational: it separates basis stability, outcome actualisation, probability constraints, temporal structure and downstream amplification. It is also more candid about mechanism debt than many consciousness-and-quantum proposals.

As a physics argument, however, it currently places too much weight on architecture and too little on dynamics. The five components do not compose automatically:

einselection+redundancy+contextuality+weak values+amplification⇒conscious actualisation.

A successful composition would require explicit interfaces:

conscious state⟶eligible quantum instrument⟶selected physical event⟶neural propagation⟶behaviour,

with equations and constraints at every arrow.

The programme is not refuted merely because such a model is absent. But neither do the cited literatures provide cumulative empirical support for it. At present, post-decoherence placement reduces one mechanism debt—the need for long-lived macroscopic coherence—while exposing a deeper one: the theory needs a complete, globally consistent actualisation law. Menu refresh is one manifestation of that debt, not the principal remaining obstacle.
