## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

- **Cross-realizer behaviour claim stronger than its hub (old L116).** The article said the Map's causal-trace reading of behaviour "holds only within a shared realization" and that Data's behaviour, "fully explained by his silicon, is no evidence that he has experience". That claim has three problems:
  - `concepts/problem-of-other-minds` L164 holds that biological similarity is "a strong abductive consideration, not a categorical gate" and that "neural dissimilarity weakens the inference to AI consciousness without licensing a confident denial of it".
  - The article's own C2 (p. 402) keeps superficial functional equivalence as a defeasible reason, and L57 says the problem "leaves reasons standing on both sides".
  - "Fully explained by his silicon" assumed that Data's substrate is causally closed. That prejudges the substrate question the next paragraph says is open.

  A reviewer who accepts the tenets would flag "holds only" and "no evidence" as stronger than the Map's own hub. **Fixed**: the passage now reads "draws its force from a shared realization … Across realizers, Tenet 3 supplies no inference from Data's behaviour to experience in him, idle or otherwise, and adds nothing to C2's defeasible reason, which [physical dissimilarity weakens without cancelling]". The bracketed phrase pipes to the hub's Substrate Chauvinism section. The guard still applies in both directions: Tenet 3 licenses no same-effects inference, and nothing is conceded to epiphenomenalism.
- **Page cite (old L43).** The quote "could arise for someone who has no conception of another person" was cited to p. 392. In the JSTOR text layer, "could arise for" ends p. 391 and "someone who …" opens p. 392. **Fixed**: now pp. 391–392. The research note's quote-status list carries the same slip.
- **Page cite (old L58).** The quote "is of a piece with the epistemic problem …" was cited to pp. 420–421. The whole quoted sentence is on p. 421; p. 420 ends at "the functional analog of human consciousness in Commander Data". **Fixed**: now p. 421.
