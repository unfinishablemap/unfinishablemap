## Warnings

### Warning 1 — `concepts/tenet-falsification-conditions` L40: a "decisive for every reading" condition the endorsed reading survives

**Tenet**: 2 (Minimal Quantum Interaction). Over-concession — surrenders the tenet on evidence it does not lose to.

> L40: "One condition is decisive for every reading—if quantum coherence were shown to be physically impossible in all biological neural systems, **no quantum indeterminacy would remain for consciousness to exploit** and the Minimal Quantum Interaction tenet would fail."

The sentence conflates *coherence* with *indeterminacy*. [tenets/tenets.md](/tenets/) denies the identification in two places:

- L73: "**Even once coherence has fully dissipated, the question of *which* outcome becomes actual remains physically open.** If collapse or actualisation is a real event, consciousness could bias selection at that point without requiring sustained coherence at neural timescales."
- L77: "This dispute matters only for candidate mechanisms that require *pre-decoherence* coherence at neural scales (Orch OR, Fröhlich-microtubule lines). **Post-decoherence-selection proposals do not depend on it.**"

Post-decoherence selection — the path the Map "endorses most strongly" per Tenet 3's Definition — operates on the improper reduced-state mixture left *after* decoherence has completed. A proof that coherence cannot survive in neural tissue removes nothing it uses. The condition is therefore decisive for the pre-decoherence readings only, not "for every reading."

The antecedent is also ambiguous between "coherence cannot *survive* at neural timescales" (which the corridor survives) and "no neural degree of freedom ever enters superposition at all" (which it arguably would not). The imported source disambiguates toward the first.

**New in this window**, at commit `682d7a1d5c` (2026-08-21) — an edit whose stated purpose was that the article "flattens Tenet 2 into 'decisive falsification'". The flattening was moved rather than removed.

**Propagation source, outside the window**: `topics/falsification-roadmap-for-the-interface-model` L79 — "*Decisive (for all readings)*: Demonstration that quantum coherence is physically impossible in all biological neural systems, under all conditions, at all timescales relevant to neural processing. **If no quantum indeterminacy exists in neural tissue for consciousness to exploit, every reading of the tenet fails.**" The "at all timescales relevant to neural processing" qualifier pins it squarely to the coherence-survival reading.

**Third instance, same file, pre-existing and unchanged in this window**: L68 — "What would count: **proof that quantum coherence cannot survive in any neural system**, or that classical noise fully accounts for neural stochasticity." Same slide; worth catching in the same pass.

**Recommendation**: scope the shared condition to the pre-decoherence readings and state that the corridor survives a coherence-survival proof, citing `tenets.md` L73/L77 — or, if the stronger "no superposition ever forms" antecedent is intended, say so explicitly and drop the "no quantum indeterminacy would remain" gloss. Fix all three loci; both trees are in sync, so one obsidian pass plus sync clears each.
