## Executive Summary

The page is careful, well sourced and honestly against the Map on its main point. The prior deep review's stability notes are respected here: the verdict that the trauma-selectivity point goes to the brain-only rival is not reopened. The two edits made since that review contain three defects.

1. **L92 asserts a parity its own source denies.** "The protective half of the datum is weaker for the rival and the interface reading alike" treats the rival's "protective" as momentary relief. On this page, though, the rival's protectiveness is "the passive end of an evolved defence cascade", which is a survival sense. The page it summarises says that sense "belongs to the rival's evolutionary account alone, and these designs cannot test it" (`topics/peritraumatic-dissociation` §Four Senses, item 4). So the sentence hands the Map a partial recovery the evidence does not give it. That softens the binding concession in the one direction the stability notes forbid.
2. **The Danböck 2024 result is understated and filed in the wrong place.** The page has "immobility and numbing did not appear", under the *autonomic* heading. The study found something stronger: acute dissociation "was linked to higher self-reported negative affect responses to trauma scripts". That is a state-level result against the model's defining claim that the subtype "is characterized by overmodulation of affect" (Lanius et al. 2010, abstract). The finding belongs beside the page's own L80 point that the literature "press[es] on its affect-first premise", and it is missing there.
3. **The lede was left behind by the 2026-11-09 heading change.** The ledger now reads "The autonomic signature only partly did", but the lede still says "there is no reliable autonomic signature".

None of these moves the comparative verdict toward the Map. Item 1 restores ground to the rival. Item 2 counts against the rival's affect premise and against the interface reading's felt-relief claim alike, as `peritraumatic-dissociation` already records.

## Critiques by Philosopher

### The Eliminative Materialist (Churchland)

She would welcome the page and press its remaining reservation. "Nothing in the model explains why inhibited limbic output should feel unreal" is the residue every neuroscience page leaves, and it shrinks as models improve. Laurin et al.'s prior-and-precision story already says why the system *judges* its surroundings unreal. The stability notes class this as a framework-boundary disagreement, and the page labels it so ("compatible with, not evidence for"). It is not a defect.

### The Hard-Nosed Physicalist (Dennett)

Dennett would go after L92. The page reads "protective" in both accounts as a claim about the moment, then scores both as weakened. But the rival never needed momentary relief. A defence cascade is protective in the way a startle reflex is: it was selected because it improved survival under inescapable threat. That claim is untouched by worse later PTSD course and by the absence of felt relief, and the source page says so. Putting the two readings in the same "alike" sentence reintroduces the agentive gloss ("protecting itself") as a peer of an etiological function claim. That is the inflation he would point to. This is the strongest critique, and it is Priority 1.

### The Quantum Skeptic (Tegmark)

He has nothing to say. The page marks Minimal Quantum Interaction "not engaged" and does not lean on it.

### The Many-Worlds Defender (Deutsch)

Not engaged either, and the page says so. No issue.

### The Empiricist (Popper's Ghost)

The Popperian would credit the page for letting a failed prediction cost the Map, then press on the rival's rescue. "The mirror image failed, and the dynamic version survived" rests on the 2010 paper's alternation clause and on Chiba's model, whose tested pole is avoidance rather than dissociation. That leaves no dissociation-specific evidence that the alternation exists. The one *state-level* test on the page is Danböck 2024, and it found more negative affect during acute dissociation, which an overmodulated pole should not produce. The deep review already settled that "survived" means "consistent with", so this review does not task it. Installing the Danböck finding at L80 (Priority 2) puts the relevant evidence on the page without rewording the heading.

### The Buddhist Philosopher (Nagarjuna)

Nagarjuna would question the residue's framing. Why should inhibited limbic output "feel unreal"? That question assumes "feeling real" is the default property of experience, the thing that needs no explanation. On his view the default sense of a real, owned world is the constructed overlay. Depersonalisation subtracts part of that overlay, aversively and without insight. On that view the Dualism residue asks the wrong question. The page wisely leaves the [mine-ness](/concepts/mine-ness/) standoff "untouched", so the point is low severity. It is noted for whoever next works on the Dualism clause.

- **Severity**: High. The page ends up overstating the Map's position, in the direction its own stability notes forbid.
