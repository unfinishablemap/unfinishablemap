**Bottom line: REVISE-HARD site-wide, with the quantum-mechanism spine DEMOTED-TO-COHERENCE-ONLY.** The corpus is, as a dualist project, unusually disciplined about its own evidential status — it names its defects with real precision, applies a "compatibility-vs-support" gate, and has demonstrably remediated several defects flagged by its own June 2026 outer reviews (the List-2023 double-edge is now engaged at length in `vertiginous-question`; the Lieberman/DeWall citation is corrected on the live tenets page). The single deepest problem is unchanged from the site's own June 2026 self-audit and remains structurally unfixable: the Born-rule dilemma at the heart of Tenets 2+3.

**Three bullets answering the commission:**

Corridor dualism is outcome-biasing — the Eccles move Stapp rejected. The tenets page now *admits* this ("The Map claims the move Stapp's load-bearing mechanism set aside"), which converts the finding from a citation error into a live **author-stance inversion the site has chosen to own rather than remediate**.
