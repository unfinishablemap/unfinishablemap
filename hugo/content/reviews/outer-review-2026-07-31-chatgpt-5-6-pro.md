# External audit: *Penrose Gravity-Induced Collapse and Empirical Prospects*

## Overall verdict

**Major revision required. The article is not reliable as a current empirical review.**

The article has several genuine strengths. It concedes that only part of the Diósi–Penrose landscape has been excluded; it correctly gives the original Donadi lower bound as R0>0.54×10−10 mR_0>0.54\times10^{-10}\,\mathrm mR0​>0.54×10−10m; its numerical description of the 2026 sodium-cluster interference experiment is approximately right; and its summary of the result reported by Trillo and Navascués is broadly faithful. It also usefully admits that freeing R0R_0R0​ weakens the naturalness claim associated with Penrose’s proposal. [topics/penrose-gravity-induced-collapse-empirical-prospects](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/penrose-gravity-induced-collapse-empirical-prospects/)

Those virtues are outweighed by a foundational error. The article’s narrative is that Penrose’s original proposal corresponds to R0=0R_0=0R0​=0, that this parameter-free zero-cutoff model was experimentally falsified, and that a nonzero fitted R0R_0R0​ subsequently rescued it. That is not what the cited experiment found. The point-particle R0=0R_0=0R0​=0 limit is divergent and gives instantaneous collapse; it is not a viable model point that an experiment later excluded. Donadi et al. instead tested a **finite**, Penrose-motivated nuclear mass-density prescription. This error infects the article’s empirical history, its naturalness argument, and its use of the result to illustrate the Map’s tenet about simplicity. [topics/penrose-gravity-induced-collapse-empirical-prospects](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/penrose-gravity-induced-collapse-empirical-prospects/)

The article also omits the strongest current direct bound, published by XENONnT in March 2026, despite being substantively reviewed four months later. It treats theoretical preprints as though they were experimental advances, misattributes the 2026 matter-wave paper, misstates what ordinary unitary quantum dynamics predicts in gravitational-entanglement experiments, and makes factually false claims about which sources of decoherence disappear in space. Its final evidential framing is asymmetrical: negative results are confined to ever-narrower variants, while a hypothetical positive collapse result is allowed to support the Map’s much broader dualist commitments.

## 1. Underground spontaneous-radiation bounds

### 1.1 The R0=0R_0=0R0​=0 claim is wrong

The article states that “Penrose’s original version effectively sets R0R_0R0​ to zero, treating mass distributions as point-like,” and twice identifies R0=0R_0=0R0​=0 as the parameter-free version that failed. [topics/penrose-gravity-induced-collapse-empirical-prospects](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/penrose-gravity-induced-collapse-empirical-prospects/)

Donadi et al. say the opposite. For a point mass, the gravitational self-energy integral diverges, producing instantaneous collapse, which they describe as clearly wrong. Both Diósi and Penrose therefore use a smeared mass density, although they motivate it differently. Diósi introduces a phenomenological spatial resolution; Penrose associates the mass distribution with a stationary Schrödinger–Newton wavefunction. For the germanium nuclei in the experiment, that Penrose prescription gave the finite estimate

R0=0.05×10−10 m.R_0=0.05\times10^{-10}\,\mathrm m.R0​=0.05×10−10m.

The experiment found

R0>0.54×10−10 mR_0>0.54\times10^{-10}\,\mathrm mR0​>0.54×10−10m

with probability 0.95, thereby excluding that **finite, theoretically fixed Penrose prescription**. [ar5iv+2ar5iv+2](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2111.13490)

The correct history is therefore:

1. R0=0R_0=0R0​=0 is a pathological point-particle limit, not an empirically falsified physical model.
2. Penrose supplied a finite effective mass distribution without treating its size as a freely fitted parameter.
3. Donadi excluded the resulting germanium-specific estimate within a particular dynamical realization.
4. Allowing R0R_0R0​ to vary freely preserves other DP models but sacrifices the original prediction of its value.

The article has a legitimate naturalness criticism available to it, but it states the wrong one. The cost is not that experiment forced Penrose to move from zero to nonzero smearing. The cost is that a finite quantity that was supposed to be physically determined has become a phenomenological free parameter.

This also invalidates the article’s assertion that “the physically simplest version (R0=0R_0=0R0​=0) failed.” The zero-cutoff limit is not a simple successful theory with one failed prediction; it is mathematically ill-defined.

### 1.2 The experiment does not test Penrose’s timescale formula alone

Penrose gives the heuristic decay timescale

τ∼ℏEΔ,\tau\sim\frac{\hbar}{E_\Delta},τ∼EΔ​ℏ​,

but does not supply a complete stochastic collapse dynamics. Donadi et al. explicitly note this and assume Poissonian collapse in order to derive a Lindblad master equation and its associated diffusion and radiation. The numerical radiation bound therefore constrains a Poissonian, Markovian DP completion with a specified mass-smearing rule—not the bare statement that gravitational self-energy determines a characteristic lifetime. [ar5iv+1](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2111.13490)

There is a broader theorem that spatial-collapse dynamics satisfying assumptions including translation covariance and no-signalling must have diffusive consequences. That supports non-interferometric testing in general. It does not make the exact emission rate, spectrum, regularization convention, or inferred R0R_0R0​ bound independent of the chosen dynamics. [APS Links+1](https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevLett.130.230202)

This distinction matters for the article’s treatment of Orch OR. McQueen’s objection is not merely an opportunistic relocation of the target. Orch OR was never supplied as the exact Poissonian, white-noise DP master equation used to calculate these radiation rates. That does not make Orch OR empirically successful; it exposes how underspecified its collapse dynamics is. Calling the distinction a “rescue” without first establishing that Orch OR committed to the tested dynamics is tendentious.

### 1.3 The radiation terminology is careless

The article describes the Donadi search as one for “bremsstrahlung X-rays.” Its region of interest was 1–3.8 MeV, with still higher-energy primary photons considered up to 100 MeV. That is predominantly gamma-ray rather than ordinary X-ray language. Majorana and XENONnT subsequently used lower-energy X-ray regimes. “Collapse-induced spontaneous electromagnetic radiation” would avoid conflating the experiments. [ar5iv+1](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2111.13490)

The word “bremsstrahlung” is also potentially misleading. The signature is generated by collapse-induced stochastic diffusion of charged particles, not simply by an ordinary externally accelerated charge in a classical detector environment.

Its DP result was an interpretation of the same null data, not the experiment’s primary model target. [arXiv+1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.01343)

More importantly, Majorana published an erratum in 2023 correcting a typographical error in its DP limit. The corrected 95% confidence lower bound is approximately

### 1.6 The atomic radiation model has changed

A 2024 *Physical Review Letters* paper showed that low-energy atomic emission can depart strongly from the earlier limiting pictures in which nuclear protons emit perfectly coherently while electrons emit independently. At X-ray energies, atomic charge distributions, electron coherence, and electron–proton cancellation become material. XENONnT’s improved analysis explicitly incorporated these effects. [APS Journals+1](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.132.250203)

The article presents the radiation prediction as a simple, settled bremsstrahlung consequence and does not alert readers that the inferred limits depend on the atomic-emission model and energy regime.

## 2. Matter-wave interferometry

The article is approximately right that the 2026 experiment observed interference from sodium clusters around 170,000170{,}000170,000 daltons and more than 7,000 atoms. The reported mass range was 143–197 kDa, with a mean of 172 kDa, and the branch separation was approximately 133 nm. The work increased the standard macroscopicity measure by more than an order of magnitude. [nature.com+1](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09917-9)

There are nevertheless four problems.

**First, the citation is misattributed.** The first author is Sebastian Pedalino, not Markus Arndt. “Arndt et al.” is not standard citation practice merely because Arndt was the corresponding author. The reference should be Pedalino et al. [nature.com](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09917-9)

**Second, “the largest particles yet shown to exhibit quantum behaviour” is too broad.** They are the most massive objects demonstrated in matter-wave interference. The Nature paper itself notes that a bulk acoustic resonator with an effective mass of 16 micrograms—about 101910^{19}1019 Da—had previously been placed in a quantum superposition, albeit over only 10−1810^{-18}10−18 m. Different macroscopicity experiments trade mass against separation, coherence time and control. [nature.com](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09917-9)

**Third, the article files the experiment under “Levitated Nanoparticle Interferometry.”** The sodium clusters were propagated in a matter-wave beam interferometer; this is not an optically levitated nanoparticle experiment.

**Fourth, the fourteen-order mass gap to the Planck mass is not a scientifically adequate DP sensitivity measure.** A DP rate depends on the mass distribution, branch separation, smearing length, geometry and duration, not on total mass alone. The Nature analysis constrains a class of generic minimal macrorealist modifications through an experiment-specific visibility calculation. It does not report a direct DP exclusion in terms of R0R_0R0​. [nature.com+2nature.com+2](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09917-9)

The Planck-mass comparison can remain as popular intuition, but it should not be presented as the experimental distance to a decisive DP test. A projected visibility-loss or parameter-exclusion calculation would be needed.

## 3. Levitated optomechanics

This is the least reliable section of the article.

### 3.1 The 2025 “blueprint” is not an experimental result

The unnamed source matching the article’s description appears to be Ridha Horchani’s December 2025 arXiv preprint. It is a single-author theoretical proposal for a levitated nanosphere, conditional displacement, environmental calibration and Bayesian model comparison. It reports no experiment and is centred primarily on CSL rather than on a direct DP test. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.02838v1)

The preprint also contains an obvious internal numerical contradiction. It lists

Γenv≈8.99×10−3 s−1,ΓCSL≈7.93×104 s−1,\Gamma_{\rm env}\approx8.99\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}},
\qquad

but immediately says environmental decoherence overwhelms CSL by nineteen orders of magnitude. The quoted numbers instead make the CSL rate approximately 8.8×1068.8\times10^68.8×106 times larger. The stated coherence time is likewise incompatible with those rates, and a later figure uses yet another pair of rates. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.02838v1)

A current empirical survey should not rely on this preprint without flagging and resolving those inconsistencies. The Map’s decision to leave the source unidentified makes independent checking unnecessarily difficult.

### 3.2 The 2026 QFI paper is estimation theory, not an experimental target result

The 2026 preprint develops quantum Fisher-information methods for an idealized optomechanical Gaussian system. It studies Diósi-type gravitational decoherence as an example and says that this model is equivalent in the relevant form to the classical-channel model introduced by Kafri and collaborators. It is a theoretical sensitivity analysis, not an experiment and not a direct new DP exclusion. [arXiv+1](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.14841v1)

More damaging to the article’s presentation, the preprint says that the gravitational and thermal contributions are formally indistinguishable in the mathematical structure of the master equation. It therefore does not by itself show that an experiment can identify an excess as gravitational collapse rather than imperfectly modelled thermal noise. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.14841v1)

“Providing concrete parameter targets for experimentalists” overstates what has been delivered. “Deriving idealized precision bounds and detection strategies” would be more accurate.

### 3.3 Actual levitated constraints concern narrower dissipative variants

A more relevant empirical result derives bounds on linear-friction dissipative DP and CSL models from existing ultralow-damping levitated-optomechanical data. It excludes particular combinations of collapse-field temperature and localization length—for example, low-temperature regions of the dissipative DP model. Those are genuine empirical bounds, but only on the specified **dDP** and **dCSL** extensions, not on standard Markovian DP as a whole. [arXiv+1](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.04665v2)

The article needs to distinguish:

- an enabling platform milestone;
- a theoretical experimental proposal;
- a sensitivity forecast;
- a bound inferred from existing data;
- a purpose-built test;
- and an observed anomaly.

At present it groups the first four together as an advancing “experimental frontier.”

## 4. Gravitationally induced entanglement

The account of Trillo and Navascués is largely accurate. Their DP dynamics can generate entanglement below a critical separation proportional to R0R_0R0​; alternative transverse-spreading configurations can extend the accessible distance; and under selected parameters the entanglement can survive for more than a day. Their result challenges the inference that observing gravitationally induced entanglement, by itself, proves that gravity is fundamentally quantum. [arXiv+1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.02287)

The article’s next sentence is nevertheless false:

> “Entanglement should exhibit distance-dependent behaviour that standard quantum mechanics without collapse does not predict.”

Ordinary unitary quantum dynamics with a gravitational interaction already predicts distance-dependent phases and entanglement. What is distinctive about DP is a **particular relation among separation, smearing length, entangling interaction and collapse-induced decoherence**, including the claimed critical distance. Distance dependence itself is not the discriminator.

The article also presents one side of an active dispute as settled:

- Aspelmeyer argues that, once realistic tabletop constraints are imposed, observing GIE would rule out current Diósi–Penrose collapse models, and that this is compatible with Trillo and Navascués once the relevant physical regime is specified. [Comptes Rendus+1](https://comptes-rendus.academie-sciences.fr/physique/item/10.5802/crphys.270.pdf)
- Feng, Vedral and Marletto argue that collapse-based models capable of generating the entanglement do so through nonlocal features, so they do not constitute a local classical counterexample to the entanglement witness. [ar5iv+1](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2503.19774)
- Di Biagio argues in the opposite direction that the locality assumptions behind the usual no-go inference are not natural in gravity and that classical gravity can entangle under broader assumptions. [arXiv+1](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.02683v2)
- Boulle and Franzmann emphasize that subsystem independence, required by the experimental inference, becomes nontrivial in gauge and gravitational settings. [arXiv+1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.17024v2)

None of these papers reports an observation of gravitationally induced entanglement. They are theoretical analyses of what a future result would imply. The Map should present this as a live dispute over auxiliary assumptions, not as an already established DP falsification protocol.

## 5. MAQRO

The article’s sole MAQRO citation is from 2012. That is inadequate for a section purporting to describe current prospects.

The current MAQRO-PF white paper describes a proposed low-Earth-orbit pathfinder submitted to the UK Space Agency’s Frontiers 2035 road-mapping process. It is not an approved ESA mission and it reports no new data. The current plan includes smaller hosted or rideshare concepts and a roughly 150 kg standalone option; a full ESA M-class MAQRO mission would be a subsequent ambition conditional on the pathfinder. [arXiv+2arXiv+2](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.01777v1)

The claim that space “eliminates” seismic vibration, thermal radiation and residual-gas collisions is false. Space improves free-fall time and removes direct terrestrial seismic coupling, but the current design still treats spacecraft vibration, atmospheric drag, solar-radiation pressure, thermal stability, ionizing radiation and residual gas as major problems. The white paper explicitly says the ambient vacuum of space is insufficient and proposes an internal chamber targeting pressures down to 10−1310^{-13}10−13 mbar. [arXiv+2arXiv+2](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.01777v1)

The accurate formulation is that space may reduce or transform some environmental limitations while creating others. It does not supply a noise-free environment.

The opening prediction that experiments may decide the issue “within the next decade” is therefore unsupported. The cited optomechanical studies are theoretical preprints, GIE remains unobserved, and MAQRO-PF remains at the proposal and technology-development stage.

## 7. Rival accounts and strawmen

### GRW and CSL

The article contrasts Penrose’s gravity-derived proposal with GRW and CSL parameters “chosen to match observation.” That is rhetorically loaded.

GRW and CSL are phenomenological, but they supply explicit stochastic dynamics, amplification mechanisms and calculable experimental signatures. Penrose supplies a physical motivation and a timescale heuristic, but not a complete collapse law. Turning that heuristic into testable dynamics introduces choices concerning mass smearing, temporal noise, stochastic process, regularization and normalization. The comparison should therefore be “physically motivated but dynamically incomplete” versus “phenomenological but mathematically explicit,” not “derived theory” versus “parameters chosen to fit.” [ar5iv+1](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2111.13490)

The article also omits a central theoretical cost shared by standard white-noise collapse models: continuing stochastic energy injection. Dissipative and colored variants were introduced partly to address that problem, at the cost of further parameters. This is relevant both to the charge of ad hoc rescue and to why exclusions of the white-noise model do not automatically transfer to its extensions. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.04665v2)

### Decoherence and unitary quantum mechanics

“Decoherence-only” should not be treated as one complete rival theory. Environmental decoherence explains the suppression of observable interference after environmental degrees of freedom are traced out, but by itself does not specify a unique ontology or explain why one outcome is experienced. Everettian, Bohmian, relational and other approaches combine unitary dynamics with different ontological or interpretive additions.

That measurement-problem distinction is a legitimate argument for studying collapse. It does not follow that every failure to observe collapse is neutral, or that every excess-decoherence observation would identify collapse. The 2026 QFI paper used by the Map acknowledges that thermal and gravitational contributions can be mathematically indistinguishable at the master-equation level. A positive excess would require independent noise calibration, alternative-new-physics tests and successful prediction across different masses, materials, geometries and temperatures. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.14841v1)

### Classical and hybrid gravity

The GIE literature shows that “classical gravity” is not one well-defined empirical alternative. Semiclassical mean-field models, measurement-and-feedback models, stochastic gravity, hybrid classical–quantum models and nonlocal classical mediators have different entanglement capacities. The Map cites the result most favourable to DP while omitting the dispute over whether its entanglement is genuinely compatible with a local classical mediator. [arXiv+2arXiv+2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19774)

A serious review should identify the auxiliary assumptions each proposed witness tests: locality, subsystem independence, mediator structure, Markovianity, spatial mass smearing and the availability of nonlocal classical interactions.

## 9. Tenet-protective bracketing

The article is unusually explicit that gravitational OR “provides what The Unfinishable Map requires”: observer-independent prebiotic collapse plus an opportunity for consciousness to influence outcome selection. This starts from a doctrinal need and searches for a compatible mechanism, rather than first asking which mechanism the evidence favours. [topics/penrose-gravity-induced-collapse-empirical-prospects](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/penrose-gravity-induced-collapse-empirical-prospects/)

Four forms of protective bracketing follow.

### 9.1 Negative evidence is narrow; hypothetical positive evidence is broad

A null result is confined to a particular R0R_0R0​, noise spectrum or collapse dynamics. That scoping is scientifically appropriate.

But a hypothetical confirmation of objective collapse is then said to provide empirical support for the Map’s “metaphysical commitments.” That is too broad. Evidence for a stochastic physical collapse law would support objective collapse and count against strictly unitary Everettian dynamics. It would not by itself support:

- substance or property dualism;
- nonphysical mental causation;
- consciousness-sensitive outcome selection;
- neural implementation of that selection;
- or the Map’s minimal-intervention principle.

A completely physicalist objective-collapse theory would predict the same baseline experiment. The article does not separate evidence for the shared physical mechanism from evidence for the Map-specific psychophysical addition.

### 9.2 The stated defeater is effectively unreachable

The article says the Map must reconsider only if “collapse models are entirely falsified.” [topics/penrose-gravity-induced-collapse-empirical-prospects](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/penrose-gravity-induced-collapse-empirical-prospects/)

No finite experiment can exclude every future colored, dissipative, non-Markovian, nonlocal or differently regularized collapse model. Setting “all collapse models” as the trigger permits the favoured tenet to survive an indefinite sequence of local exclusions. A legitimate falsification roadmap must name a finite family of models and parameter domains to which the Map currently assigns substantial credence, then specify how each null result changes those credences.

### 9.3 Consciousness bias is declared empirical without being modelled

The article says consciousness biases outcome probabilities subtly enough to evade present Born-rule constraints but strongly enough, cumulatively, to perform cognitive work. It then calls this “an empirical commitment, not a free parameter.” [topics/penrose-gravity-induced-collapse-empirical-prospects](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/penrose-gravity-induced-collapse-empirical-prospects/)

That is backwards. Until the site specifies a coupling law, the relevant neural variable, an effect-size scale, temporal integration, conservation constraints and predicted statistical deviations, the proposal contains multiple unconstrained degrees of freedom. “Small enough not to have been detected but large enough to matter” is a tuning condition, not a prediction.

There is also a missing no-signalling analysis. A consciously controllable alteration of local outcome probabilities in entangled systems requires proof that it cannot be converted into superluminal signalling. Collapse models are normally engineered carefully around this constraint; the Map cannot simply add outcome-directed agency downstream and assume consistency.

### 9.4 Speculative consequences are stated as historical facts

“Gravity collapsed superpositions in the early universe, forming definite stars and chemistry” is not an experimental result. It is what the Map’s preferred mechanism would say if it were true. Standard unitary accounts combined with environmental decoherence and an interpretation also describe the emergence of stable classical records. The sentence should be explicitly conditional.

The article similarly uses the failure of its supposed R0=0R_0=0R0​=0 model to vindicate the site’s tenet that simplicity is unreliable. Apart from being built on the erroneous zero-cutoff history, this move risks neutralizing the evidential cost of every added parameter. Rejecting crude appeals to simplicity does not eliminate Bayesian penalties for flexibility, post hoc accommodation or loss of prior predictive specificity.
