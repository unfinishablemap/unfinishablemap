## Critical Issues Found

1. **Schopenhauer presented as a nihilist who concludes meaninglessness makes life not worth living** — misstates both the ground of his pessimism and his position on value. Fixed in the live article **and** in `archive/concepts/nihilism.md`, which served a variant of the same error.
2. **Camus's revolt described as "continuing to create meaning"** — attributes to Camus precisely the move he spent *The Myth of Sisyphus* attacking as philosophical suicide, and enrols him in a label he rejected. Rewritten: he is now presented as a **limit case** for the section's thesis rather than an instance of it.

## Medium Issues Found

3. **Heideggerian authenticity collapsed into Sartrean freedom** — fixed with an explicit distinction.
4. **Four orphan references** (Dennett, Zahavi, Merleau-Ponty, Kierkegaard) — all anchored where they do genuine work rather than dropped.
