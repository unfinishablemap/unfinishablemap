## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

- **Eccles attribution error (locus/work conflation).** The article said *The Self and Its Brain* (1977) "locates the interaction at the synapse". The 1977 book places the self-conscious mind's liaison in cortical modules of the dominant hemisphere (Eccles's "liaison brain"); the synaptic locus and the probability-of-exocytosis mechanism are the later Beck & Eccles (1992) model. *Fixed*: the sentence now distinguishes the 1977 cortical-module locus from the 1992 synaptic one, and keeps the Beck & Eccles quotation where it belongs.
- **Position-strength misstatement about James and Bergson.** The lead and the neutral section said the transmission theorists were "silent about the other half rather than opposed to it" and "could hold it without committing themselves on mental causation". James argued in "Are We Automata?" (*Mind* 1879) that consciousness is efficacious, and Bergson's brain in *Matter and Memory* is an organ of action that introduces indetermination into the body's response. The silence is the thesis's, not the authors'. *Fixed* in both places; James 1879 added to References (Crossref-verified: *Mind* os-4(13), 1–22, doi 10.1093/mind/os-4.13.1). The correction strengthens the Map's reading: the people who stated the inbound leg affirmed the outbound one elsewhere.
- **Quote-fidelity: "risks unfalsifiability" attributed to two articles.** The verbatim phrase occurs only in [terminal-lucidity-and-filter-transmission-theory](/topics/terminal-lucidity-and-filter-transmission-theory/) (line 140). [near-death-experiences](/concepts/near-death-experiences/) says the move is "ad hoc" and "post hoc and difficult to test" and "risks becoming unfalsifiable", and runs it as "constraining mechanism" versus "interface channel" rather than "filtering" versus "expresses itself". *Fixed*: the lead now attributes each phrase to the article that contains it, and the diagnostic section notes the NDE article's variant wording.
- **Factual overreach: "The oldest objection to interactionism — Leibniz's".** Elisabeth of Bohemia's 1643 challenge predates the conservation objection; the corpus's own [history-of-the-interaction-problem](/topics/history-of-the-interaction-problem/) opens with it. *Fixed*: "the classic physical objection", with a cross-link.

### Medium Issues Found

- **Calibration of the diagnostic criterion (the Empiricist's point).** "Each is identifiable on evidence independent of the filter reading" and "that is independently observable" overstated the symmetry: an outbound failure is directly observable (behaviour absent, cortical intent signal present), but an inbound change is observable only through report or through the imaging proxies covert-consciousness research relies on. A tenet-accepting reviewer would still flag "independently observable" as too strong for the inbound leg. *Fixed*: the asymmetry is now stated, and the rescue claim reads "observable without assuming the filter reading". The implications summary was aligned to match.
- **"The only member of the cluster where 'loosening' is reported concurrently."** Terminal-lucidity episodes are also concurrent expression. What psychedelics uniquely offer is controlled dosing with a known-working outbound leg. *Fixed* to say that.
- Rouleau & Cimino's second quotation was longer than the point needed. *Trimmed* to the two verbatim fragments that carry it.
