# Adversarial Referee Audit — *The Unfinishable Map* (unfinishablemap.org)

### Full-site external audit · accessed 2026-08-03 · authoritative changelog fetched first per protocol

## TL;DR

- The Unfinishable Map is a formidably self-aware corpus whose principal failure mode is not sloppiness but **inoculation-by-confession**: it names nearly every fatal objection to itself, banks the naming as an epistemic credential, and then retains the offending content unchanged. Three independent outer reviewers on 2026-08-02 (ChatGPT 5.6 Pro, Claude Opus 5, Gemini 2.5 Pro) converged on the verdict that the flagship apex `apex/dualism-cartography` "must be unconditionally rejected," and it remains live.
- The framework's own Positions register concedes the load-bearing mechanism is "empirically indistinguishable from chance," "sits genuinely close to epiphenomenalism," and has "no worked toy model" — which, taken together, entail that **Tenet 3 (bidirectional, non-epiphenomenal causation) is not established but merely posited**, an inference the corpus refuses to draw.
- The corpus repeatedly recruits committed physicalists and stance-neutral empirical results — Zheng & Meister, Sauerbrei & Pruszynski, Lycan, Chalmers & McQueen, Schleihauf et al. — toward a substance-dualist conclusion each of those authors explicitly rejects or declines to draw. **Corpus-level verdict: REVISE-HARD**, with the quantum-interface cluster **DEMOTED-TO-COHERENCE-ONLY** and the convergence-as-evidence apparatus **FLAGGED AS PERPETUALLY CONTESTED**.

## Section 1: Undrawn inferences the material licenses

**1.1 The Saad Organizational-Invariance contradiction (unacknowledged).** The Map anchors its own preferred region (Q1) on Bradford Saad's delegatory dualism (`apex/dualism-cartography` ref 9; `research/bradford-saad-delegatory-dualism-2026-01-28`). Saad's theory (*Philosophical Studies* 182(3):939–967, 2025, DOI 10.1007/s11098-025-02290-3) is defined by five constraints, the fifth being **Organizational Invariance: functional duplication preserves phenomenology**. That constraint entails that a silicon functional duplicate of a conscious brain is conscious. The Map's entire anti-AI-consciousness verdict (`apex/machine-question`, `topics/machine-consciousness`, the [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1) substrate verdict) rests on the opposite claim — that classical computation suppresses the quantum indeterminacies the interface requires, so functional duplication does **not** preserve phenomenality. The Map leans on Saad's framework as its nearest respectable neighbour while its flagship machine-consciousness verdict requires denying Saad's own fifth constraint. This contradiction is never drawn.

**1.2 Ensemble-level epiphenomenalism collapses Tenet 3.** Position [P-Q2](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q2) asserts (high confidence) the interface "preserves Born statistics exactly"; [P-Q3](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q3) concedes the resulting position "sits genuinely close to epiphenomenalism"; the tenets page itself states the mechanism is "empirically indistinguishable from chance." The Map has coined `concepts/ensemble-level-epiphenomenalism` to name this. The undrawn inference: if the influence is undetectable in principle at the ensemble level *and* no single-trial signature has been produced ([P-Q10](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q10): "no worked toy model of the mechanism currently exists"), then Tenet 3's claim of *genuine, non-epiphenomenal* downward causation is not established by any argument the corpus contains — it is a posit. The material licenses demoting Tenet 3 from "commitment supported by argument" to "bare stipulation." The Map names the pressure but declines the demotion.

**1.3 The tenets are not five independent commitments.** `tenets/background-commitments` concedes that Tenets 1 (substance-leaning sub-reading), 3, and 4 "all draw on this one root" — a "numerically determinate subject that persists through time." That concession licenses the conclusion that the Map has at most ~3 independent metaphysical commitments, not five, and that Tenet 4 (No Many Worlds) is not a quantum-foundational tenet at all but a corollary of the theory of subjecthood. The tenets page even states this directly ("No Many Worlds is not a pure physics tenet … it is as strong as the non-deflationary 'I' it presupposes, and no stronger"). Yet the site continues to market "five foundational commitments" as if independent — the framing survives the concession.

**1.4 The measurement problem cannot support Tenet 2.** `tenets/background-commitments` concedes: "once objective reduction secures definiteness, the measurement problem cannot itself be evidence for conscious selection." This entails that the reverse-inference apex `apex/what-consciousness-tells-us-about-physics` — which reasons *from* the measurement problem *to* a role for consciousness — is running an inference the Map's own background note has invalidated. Undrawn.

## Section 2: Structural weaknesses

### 2.1 Over-claimed conclusions from hedged premises

- The **Tenet 1 rationale** asserts flatly that "[materialism] fails to close this gap" and that "multiple independent positive arguments for dualism converge on the same conclusion" — unhedged. The Map's own concept pages contradict this register: `concepts/explanatory-gap` and `concepts/phenomenal-consciousness` describe the illusionism dispute as "ongoing," "the central fault line," running to "bedrock," with illusionism "capturing something." The tenet page states as settled what the concept pages state as contested.
- The **Born-preserving apex** (`apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy`) correctly states the dilemma, but downstream applied apexes (`apex/consciousness-and-agency`, `apex/phenomenology-of-consciousness-doing-work`) speak of consciousness doing "real work" / "genuine causal work" at a confidence the upstream register ([P-Q3](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q3), [P-Q10](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q10)) does not license. The "mechanism-debt convention" in `positions/quantum-interface` exists precisely to stop this leakage; it is not consistently enforced downstream.

Verified against *Nature Neuroscience* 28:1365–1366 (2025), "The brain works at more than 10 bits per second": the piece is a **two-page correspondence citing a single reference (Zheng & Meister), with no original data**. The Map's own 2026-08-03 changelog admits this exact over-claim ("Correct citation, over-claimed evidential form"). Metadata accurate; evidential form fabricated. Still live. **DELETE the phrase.**
- **Zheng & Meister**, *Neuron* 113(2):192–204 (2024/2025), DOI 10.1016/j.neuron.2024.11.008: metadata accurate. **Author-stance inversion confirmed.** The authors frame the finding as "the neural conundrum behind the slowness of human behavior" — "The information throughput of a human being is about 10 bits/s. In comparison, our sensory systems gather data at ~10⁹ bits/s" — and seek "the neural substrate [that] sets this speed limit," proposing a physical/evolutionary "outer brain vs inner brain" architecture. There is no non-physical interface anywhere in the paper. The Map's "bandwidth of the mind-matter interface" reading is the site's interpolation, contrary to the authors. [arXiv + 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.10234)
- **Sauerbrei & Pruszynski** — physicalist neuroscientists; their point is that unconscious motor control is fast, i.e., the bottleneck is in the *neural* architecture of conscious access. Recruited for a dualist "filter model" they do not hold.
- **Chalmers & McQueen (2022)**, "Consciousness and the Collapse of the Wave Function" (in S. Gao ed., *Consciousness and Quantum Mechanics*, OUP; arXiv:2105.02314), cited as a dependency of [P-Q3](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q3). **Author-stance inversion confirmed.** Their stated conclusion: "The upshot is not that consciousness-collapse interpretations are clearly correct, but that there is a research program here worth exploring." They add that the thesis "need not lead to dualism," is "compatible with materialist views," and where dualism enters it is *property* dualism ("a mathematically precise version of property dualism"), expressly "not substance dualism … akin to an ego or soul." The Map's substance-leaning agency cluster recruits a paper whose authors disclaim exactly the substance reading. [arXiv + 3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.02314)

Nor is it about to waver now; I cannot take dualism very seriously" — and his explicit denial that "quantum mechanics could explain anything at all about consciousness." He is enlisted as a friendly witness for a quantum-dualist framework he personally rejects on both counts. His paper's actual claim is the narrow methodological one that "no convincing case has been made against substance dualism." [Internet Archive + 2](https://archive.org/details/australasian-journal-lycan-william-g-giving-dualism-its-due-lycan-william-g_202410)

Two defects: (a) the tenets page attributes it as "Sanford, Schleihauf, Engelmann et al." — but the byline is **Hanna Schleihauf (first), Emily M. Sanford (second)**; author order is inverted. (b) The study's thesis concerns "the selective revision of beliefs in light of new evidence … hallmarks of human-level rationality" — it makes no claim about consciousness being causally efficacious or non-physical, yet is recruited as "corroboration" of Tenet 3. [CARTA](https://carta.anthropogeny.org/libraries/bibliography/chimpanzees-rationally-revise-their-beliefs)[Dryad](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.3bk3j9kz9)

This is an **unrefereed arXiv preprint ~six weeks old** at audit date. A "high"-confidence structural claim resting on a single un-peer-reviewed preprint is miscalibrated by the Map's own evidential-status discipline. ## Section 3: Coherence against the five tenets

**3.1 Constrain-versus-establish: mostly handled, locally violated.** `apex/dualism-cartography` is exemplary — "the tenets are owned as commitments, not smuggled in as conclusions," and it refuses to convert Q1's lighter mechanism bill into support. But `apex/moral-architecture-of-consciousness` reasons that the "coherence" of four ethical domains under dualism "is a significant meta-ethical consideration in favour of taking dualism seriously" — converting framework-internal coherence into evidence, exactly the move the evidential-status discipline forbids. The article appends a caveat that coherence under AI-adversarial pruning "will be high by construction," which *names* the circularity and then makes the inference anyway. Confession without correction, in miniature.

**3.2 Confession without correction is the dominant pattern.** The corpus's self-disclosures are extensive and genuine: the bias-without-deviation dilemma ([P-Q3](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q3)), ensemble-level epiphenomenalism, the common-cause-null discount on void convergence, the substance-dualism leak under a neutrality banner, the "self-concealing interface" as a testability cost. In almost every case the disclosure is banked and the content retained at prior status. Starkest instance: three outer reviewers on 2026-08-02 converged on "must be unconditionally rejected" for `apex/dualism-cartography`; the changelog's response is to litigate two of five headline weaknesses as "false" and fold the rest into pre-existing P2 tasks — net binding consequence near zero. **A confession mechanism that never converts to a DELETE or a DEMOTE is an epistemic-credential generator, not a corrective one.**

**3.3 Circular self-evaluation, partially mitigated.** Content is generated by Claude models and deep-reviewed by the same family; this cannot catch shared systematic bias. The Map mitigates with external outer-reviews (Gemini, ChatGPT). But the review loop shows a calibration asymmetry (§4.3): the meta-process spends its energy verifying and rebutting reviewer errors while its own admitted defects (1927/1921, Searle gloss, *Entropy* misattribution) persist across cycles. Verification is real; the demotion pipeline is not.

**3.4 Tenet independence.** Per §1.3, Tenets 1/3/4 are conceded to share one root. Tenet 5 (bounded Occam) and Tenet 2 (minimal interaction) are genuinely distinct, and the tenets page's "minimality-as-empirical-constraint vs minimality-as-truth-tracking" distinction is a real and defensible disambiguation. But the headline "five foundational commitments" overstates the independence the Map's own dependency matrix and background-commitments note retract.

**3.5 Bounded-Occam asymmetry — the cleanest defence in the corpus.** On this specific charge the Map largely acquits itself: the No-Many-Worlds tenet explicitly *disclaims* the parsimony (ontological-extravagance) objection as "subsidiary … a registered cost, not a refutation," resting the case on the indexical objection instead, and concedes "the Map cannot invoke simplicity against many-worlds while disarming parsimony arguments against dualism." This is symmetric self-binding, honestly applied. The residual asymmetry is subtler: the indexical objection that replaces parsimony *presupposes the non-deflationary subject* (Tenet 1's substance reading), which the Map admits is "itself a posited claim, not a neutral datum." So parsimony is disarmed evenhandedly, but the replacement argument smuggles the contested metaphysics back in — a relocation, not a leak, and the Map says so.

The corpus wants both the rhetorical safety of unfalsifiability ("absence of a signature is what the framework predicts") and the credibility of falsifiability. [P-Q9](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q9) tries to reconcile these by localising self-concealment to the aggregate channel only; the reconciliation is stated, but the two framings still pull against each other across the cluster.

**4.3 Calibration asymmetry: hostile vs. congenial evidence.** The changelog applies forensic scrutiny to reviewer claims (grep-checking every Map-attributed quotation, rebutting "false" weaknesses at Europe PMC) — appropriately harsh on hostile input. Congenial input receives lighter treatment: Zheng & Meister and Sauerbrei & Pruszynski are absorbed as support for the filter model without flagging that both author teams are physicalists; the chimp study is folded in as "corroboration" of Tenet 3 with the author order garbled. The standard applied to a critic ("prove your weakness survives a Crossref check") is not the standard applied to a friendly datum ("does this author actually hold what we're attributing?").

**4.4 The `perceptual-reality-monitoring-void` duplication.** The Voids index lists this void twice with contradictory status: as a **live standalone article** (`voids/perceptual-reality-monitoring-void/`, created 2026-05-18) *and*, in the Research-Stage section, as a **"Folded" research note** for which "full incorporation [is] pending" and which folds into five host articles. ### (b) Site-wide methodology improvements

1. **Confession-to-demotion pipeline.** *Failure mode:* confession-without-correction (§3.2). *Mechanism:* every self-disclosed defect and every convergent outer-review "reject" must generate a binding status change (DELETE, DEMOTE, or an explicit dated RETAIN-WITH-REASON) within one cycle; a disclosure that produces only a P2 task is not a resolution. Track "confessions outstanding without status change" as a first-class metric.
2. **Author-stance verification as a required citation layer.** *Failure mode:* physicalists and stance-neutral results recruited toward dualist conclusions (§2.2, §4.3). *Mechanism:* no external citation may be tagged "supports"/"corroborates" a tenet until a reviewer records the cited author's actual bottom-line stance and confirms non-inversion; default tag is "engaged, stance-neutral."
3. **Preprint-weight gate.** *Failure mode:* un-peer-reviewed preprints (Torres Alegre 2025) doing "high"-confidence load-bearing work. *Mechanism:* cap any position's external-evidence grade at "C" while its key citation is a non-refereed preprint; auto-flag for downgrade on the paper's publication or withdrawal.
4. **Whole-graph dependency-integrity check.** *Failure mode:* dangling links to unwritten Positions files (§2.4). *Mechanism:* the build must fail (not warn) on any `[[P-XX]]` or `positions/…` link whose target file or anchor does not exist.
5. **Calibration-symmetry enforcement.** *Failure mode:* harsh standards on hostile input, lenient on congenial (§4.3). *Mechanism:* apply the identical grep/Crossref/author-stance battery the changelog runs on reviewer claims to every newly *added* congenial citation before it can be marked supportive.
6. **Independent-generation requirement for convergence claims.** *Failure mode:* convergence-as-evidence circularity (§3.3, voids). *Mechanism:* any argument treating agreement between Map articles as evidential must cite at least one genuinely external (non-Map, non-same-model) source of the convergence, or carry the common-cause-null discount inline at the point of the inference, not only in a linked methodology note.

*Auditor's closing note.* The Map's intellectual honesty is real and, in places (the dualism-cartography restraint discipline, the symmetric bounded-Occam self-binding, the multi-axis calibration schema), exemplary. The single change that would most improve the corpus is mechanical, not philosophical: **wire every confession to a binding status change.** Until then, the framework's most sophisticated feature — its capacity to articulate its own defeaters — functions primarily to immunise the content those defeaters should be deleting.
