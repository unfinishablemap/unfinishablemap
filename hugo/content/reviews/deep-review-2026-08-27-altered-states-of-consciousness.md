## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

1. **MQI tenet paragraph put the outbound coupling on a pharmacological (inbound) perturbation.** L150 read: "The Orch OR hypothesis proposes that psychedelics enhance quantum coherence in microtubules, providing a potential mechanism linking altered states to the quantum interface between mind and matter." MQI specifies the *outbound* leg (consciousness selecting among outcomes the physics leaves open); a drug that alters what the brain admits is an inbound datum and cannot by itself touch that leg. This is the same defect the psychedelics deep review corrected as critical, and it is a calibration error inside the Map's framework — a tenet-accepting reviewer would still flag "linking altered states to the quantum interface" as an outbound claim drawn from inbound evidence. **Resolution**: rewritten. The paragraph now states that the tenet specifies the outbound leg, that altered-state data are inbound and silent on it unless a selection-side effect is separately shown, that a microtubule mechanism would be a candidate substrate for that leg rather than an explanation of the filtering documented here, and that the Orch OR reading stays at the speculative tier with nothing on the page depending on it.

2. **The Orch OR sentence in §Psychedelics made the same move in miniature.** L86: "psychedelics might enhance this participation" — "participation" being conscious participation in collapse, i.e. the return channel. **Resolution**: now "might perturb the substrate that participation would run through—an inbound perturbation of a proposed outbound channel."

3. **No leg assignment anywhere in 47 uses of filter vocabulary.** The generic concept for the whole cluster could not tell a reader which half of the coupling any state's evidence bore on, while the psychedelics topic it links already could. **Resolution**: one paragraph added to §The Filter Theory Interpretation (immediately after the section's production-vs-configuration opener, where the page first leans on "filter"), stating the distinction, sorting the family, and cross-referencing the psychedelics article's body-by-body walk rather than repeating it.
