# Hostile Referee Report: “Pain, Consciousness, and Causal Power” (The Unfinishable Map, topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power, live version of 2026-11-03)

## 1. TL;DR

The article should be **DEMOTE-TO-COHERENCE-ONLY**, and it should be retained only after the REVISE-HARD edits in Part 4. None of its four central claims gives evidence that discriminates in favour of interactionist dualism. Two of them (C and D) are either stipulated or non sequiturs. Its headline thesis, that pain “binds the bare-correlation version, not the phenomenal-concept one”, appears only in the page’s meta-description and abstract. [topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power/) The body never states it or argues for it.

- **Claims A and B: asymbolia does not deliver what the article says it does.** The “bare-correlation vs phenomenal-concept epiphenomenalism” split is a coinage of the site’s own register ([P-MC1](/positions/arguments-for-mental-causation/#p-mc1)), not a distinction found in the literature. [positions/arguments-for-mental-causation](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/arguments-for-mental-causation/) It misfiles the phenomenal concept strategy, which is a physicalist position, as a kind of epiphenomenalism. It also leaves out the actual sophisticated epiphenomenalist replies: Chalmers’ 2003 acquaintance/constitution account and Robinson’s common-cause reply. [stanford](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/win2014/entries/epiphenomenalism/)[consc](https://consc.net/papers/belief.html) On asymbolia, the clinical base is tiny. Berthier et al. studied 6 patients with extensive lesions and comorbid deficits, and only 3 of them had pain thresholds tested. [cambridge](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/14D42EF12870DC9C6BA506F2C5F5AC5F/S0031824823001678a.pdf/pain-asymbolia-is-not-pain.pdf) Feinstein et al. (2016, *Brain Structure and Function*) state that “No studies have replicated the original finding of pain asymbolia following insula damage.” The best-developed reading of the syndrome (Klein 2015; Gerrans 2020) is a physicalist account in terms of lost care or self-attribution. [concepts/pain-asymbolia](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/pain-asymbolia/)[oup](https://academic.oup.com/mind/article-abstract/124/494/493/2606077) The site’s own concept page already concedes that the dissociation is a “shared explanandum”. [concepts/pain-asymbolia](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/pain-asymbolia/) The topic page still opens by calling it “the single most discriminating empirical test case in philosophy of mind.” [topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power/)
- **Claims C and D fail.** Claim C (character and causal power are inseparable) is either false or does not discriminate between dualism and its rivals. Grahek’s own reading of asymbolia is pain *felt* without motivational force. Grahek presents this as a counterexample to motivationalism, which is the opposite of inseparability. [oup](https://academic.oup.com/mind/article-abstract/124/494/493/2606077) If “awfulness” is redefined as the motivational component, the claim becomes near-analytic, and a functionalist or response-dependence theorist accepts it gladly. The article itself cites one such theorist: Noordhof 2021 says the missing aversive disposition *explains* the missing painfulness. [whiterose](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/178347/1/document.pdf)[topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power/) Claim D (dreaded pain motivates behaviour before any nociceptive signal) runs together “no peripheral signal” and “no physical cause.” Anticipation has dedicated neural substrates. Ploghaus et al. (*Science* 284:1979–1981, 1999) found anticipation activations in the medial frontal lobe, insula and cerebellum, at sites separate from the pain-evoked foci. Berns et al. (*Science* 312:754–758, 2006) found that extreme dreaders differed from mild dreaders in how fast activity rose in the posterior cortical pain matrix while waiting. Conditioned placebo and nocebo pain responses can be triggered by masked cues: Jensen et al. (*PNAS* 109:15959–15964, 2012) report that the mechanisms “can operate without conscious awareness of the triggering cues”. Their participants gave mean ratings of 25 for the low-pain face and 44 for the high-pain face at identical temperatures. Predictive processing absorbs the datum completely. One of the article’s own sources, Noordhof, argues that pain’s painfulness cannot be basically imagined in the first place. [whiterose](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/178347/1/document.pdf) Claim D should be deleted as evidence.
- **Calibration and the blocking gate both fail.** The page never engages the predictive-processing/active-inference rival: it cites no Laukkonen, Friston & Chandaria 2025, no Büchel et al. 2014, and no Gerrans. Even though the dependent concept page already contains the valence-prior rival, the topic page reduces it to two subordinate clauses, so the standing blocking gate is FAILED. [concepts/pain-asymbolia](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/pain-asymbolia/) The page has no compatible/suggestive/discriminating labels. Its lede, its Dualism paragraph and its Bidirectional Interaction paragraph contradict the body’s own hedged concessions, and its No-Many-Worlds paragraph contains a substantive physics error. Ten of its 21 references are never cited in the body. One of them (Kragel & LaBar 2015) cuts directly against the passage it would naturally support.

### 2.5 Misreadings and slides, by section

- **“Pain’s Resistance to Reduction”, phenomenal concept strategy.** The page says the strategy “concedes that something about first-person acquaintance with pain escapes third-person physical description — which is the dualist’s central point.” [topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power/) This is an epistemic-to-metaphysical slide. Loar, Papineau and Balog concede a *conceptual* gap precisely in order to deny an *ontological* one. The dualist’s central point is the ontological claim, and the strategy is built to block it.
- **“Theory by Theory”, IIT.** The page says IIT “predicts that *any* loss of integration reduces consciousness in a general, undifferentiated way — phi is a single scalar measure.” [topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power/) This is a strawman. In IIT 3.0/4.0, Φ gives the *quantity* of consciousness, while the *quality* is given by the cause–effect structure, so a local lesion can remove a specific quality. The objection does not touch IIT.
- **“Theory by Theory”, representationalism.** The page says a red square and tissue-damage-as-threat “should differ in what they are about but not in whether they hurt.” [topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power/) This ignores evaluativism (Bain; Cutter & Tye 2011) and imperativism (Klein; Martínez 2011), on which the *content* of pain is evaluative or imperative, so differing in whether it hurts *is* differing in content. The page’s own phrase “tissue-damage-as-threat” already concedes an evaluative content.
- **“Quality-Action Mapping.”** The page says qualitative character “arguably tracks the behavioural difference more transparently”. [topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power/) Transparency to introspection is an epistemic property. Inferring causal priority from it is a slide from the epistemic to the metaphysical. No citation is given for the sharp/burning/aching behavioural taxonomy.
- **“The Inseparability…”, “felt severity.”** The page says extended responses “track the *felt severity* of the pain, not merely the magnitude of tissue damage.” [topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power/) The physicalist agrees and points to central pain representations that dissociate from stimulus intensity, such as the anticipation and expectation effects in Ploghaus 1999, Koyama 2005 and Büchel 2014. Since felt severity covaries with a neural variable, the observation does not discriminate.
- **“No Many Worlds.”** The page says that under many-worlds “every supervenience mapping is realised somewhere”. [topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power/) This is an error. Everettian branches share one set of dynamical laws, and MWI does not vary psychophysical laws across branches. The paragraph is tenet leakage built on a false premise and should be deleted.
- **“Minimal Quantum Interaction.”** The suggestion that “pain’s motivational urgency and suffering’s sustained behavioural restructuring might represent two modes of that influence” has no content beyond the tenet. [topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power/) It inherits the Born-preservation debt that the site’s own apex calls its “strongest live challenge” ([P-Q3](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q3)) without mentioning it. This is evidential-independence failure: the pain data cannot support a mechanism that the apex says has “no worked formal model”. [apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/)

### 2.6 Where the epiphenomenalist pushes hardest (and whether the page replies)

1. **Robinson’s reward-system reply to James.** The SEP entry gives the epiphenomenalist’s alignment story. Neural events in sensory systems cause feelings. Separate neural events in a reward/aversion system cause continuance or avoidance. The pleasure/displeasure alignment then follows lawfully from the alignment of the neural events, so no “staggering accident” occurs ([https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/epiphenomenalism/](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/epiphenomenalism/); Robinson 2007, JCS). [stanford +2](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/epiphenomenalism/) The page’s question “why this particular supervenience mapping?” is answered by a lawful psychophysical correlation fixed once, and selection never had to “see” the qualia. [topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power/) **Page reply:** one clause conceding the “law itself is shared” and a link to the co-optimization reply, which it says the evidence constrains “without yet discriminating against.” [topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power/) The concession is accurate, but it empties the section’s title (“Hardest Case”) of content.
2. **Chalmers’ constitution reply to self-stultification.** On the epiphenomenalist’s own view, phenomenal beliefs are partly *constituted* by experience and justified by acquaintance. The causal theory of knowledge that self-stultification needs is therefore denied, not violated. [consc](https://consc.net/papers/belief.html)[wiley](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/nous.70002) **Page reply:** none. The “phenomenal-concept version” the abstract invokes is not this view. It is [P-MC1](/positions/arguments-for-mental-causation/#p-mc1)’s type-B physicalism, which is not epiphenomenalism at all, because if phenomenal properties are physical properties they inherit those properties’ causal powers. [positions/arguments-for-mental-causation](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/arguments-for-mental-causation/) **The real sophisticated epiphenomenalist is missing from the page.**
3. **The asymbolia lesion does the work.** Remove the insular disconnection and you remove both the affect and the behaviour. The common neural cause explains both. **Page reply:** concedes this (“constrains Kim’s reading rather than refuting it”). [topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power/) That reply works only as a concession, and the lede and Site-Perspective sections should match it.

### 2.7 Where the functionalist / predictive-processing theorist pushes hardest

1. **Decomposition is our result, not yours.** Asymbolia shows that “pain” is a cluster of separable functional components, which is exactly what Melzack & Casey (1968), Rainville et al. (1997) and Ploner et al. (1999, the converse case: “pain affect without pain sensation”) predicted from functional neuroanatomy. The page’s dilemma for functionalism (“Either way, functionalism must acknowledge…”) is a dilemma functionalists embrace: on the second horn the affective component just *is* the motivational role. [topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power/)
2. **Lost care / depersonalization.** Klein (2015) and Gerrans (2020; 2024) explain asymbolia as the loss of care for bodily integrity or the loss of self-attribution. That explains why the deficit extends to threats and verbal menaces, which a pure “awfulness quale excised” reading does not predict. [concepts/pain-asymbolia](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/pain-asymbolia/)[mq](https://researchers.mq.edu.au/en/publications/what-pain-asymbolia-really-shows/) **Page reply:** none on the topic page. The concept page engages the reading properly. [concepts/pain-asymbolia](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/pain-asymbolia/)
3. **Active inference absorbs anticipation, placebo and valence.** Büchel, Geuter, Sprenger & Eippert (“Placebo Analgesia: A Predictive Coding Perspective,” *Neuron* 81(6):1223–1239, 2014) explain placebo analgesia through priors whose effect depends on “the magnitude but also the precision of expectations”. Valence becomes a higher-order estimate of model fitness (Hesp et al. 2021). Laukkonen, Friston & Chandaria (2025, NBR 176:106296) give a general theory of consciousness built on active inference, with three conditions: a generative reality model, inferential competition, and recursive “epistemic depth”. [ovid](https://www.ovid.com/journals/nebior/abstract/10.1016/j.neubiorev.2025.106296~a-beautiful-loop-an-active-inference-theory-of-consciousness)[asteriskmag](https://asteriskmag.com/issues/10/consciousness-catching) Dread is expected free energy over predicted aversive states, computed by a model that needs no current nociceptive input. **Page reply:** the placebo paragraph’s single clause (“an access-level reading… predicts it equally”) and the dissociation paragraph’s “valence-prior account predicts it too”. [topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power/) **Verdict on the blocking gate: FAILED.** The rival is residualized into two subordinate clauses, none of the named literature is cited, and the Site-Perspective paragraph then reasserts the anticipation claim without the hedge.
