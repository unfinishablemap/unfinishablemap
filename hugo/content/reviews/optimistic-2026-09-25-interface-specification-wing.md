## Executive Summary

**The wing's best pages state the price of the Map's default reading more plainly than almost anything else on the Map.** `mathematical-structure` L94 says it outright: "The corridor and trumping readings the Map prefers are signature-free by construction … the Map's empirical content lives almost entirely in the option it does not endorse." `brain-specialness-boundary` turns the same point into a dilemma (L124–128). ## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

Chalmers would be glad to see his own model handled with care in `mathematical-structure`. The page calls CSL-IIT "the most mathematically detailed consciousness-collapse model" (L116). It then files it under *minimum-outside-the-corridor* (L89) and names the cost at L118: "Born-modifying at the neural ensemble level, since the collapse probabilities depend on a consciousness-related variable standard QM does not include". He would like even more that the Relation section gives Kleiner's E × P no more weight than it can bear (L152). The framework is "interpretation-neutral", and "a non-dualist can read E as a representational layer over P", so it "provides a mathematical home for dualism, not evidence that adjudicates dualism". The same structure is defended at the level of the definition in `consciousness-physics-interface-formalism` L45: if E were derived from P, "interactionist dualism would collapse into identity theory". The hard problem stays where it belongs, in the explanandum and not in the formalism.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

Stapp would find his philosophy described accurately in `brain-specialness-boundary` L63. The Map takes his Process-1 machinery across "a metaphysical boundary Stapp himself does not draw". His quantum interactive dualism "is a von Neumann process ontology … not the substance dualism of the Map's first tenet". `mechanism-costs` L111 gets his *mechanism* right as well: "the agent's 'process-1 choice of a probing action'", a narrow channel between thick endpoints.

He would object where the wing classifies him. `mathematical-structure` L89 lists "Stapp's quantum Zeno selection" first among the *minimum-outside-the-corridor* (Born-violating) options. That contradicts `concepts/stapp-quantum-mind` L64, where Stapp is "thoroughly committed to strict adherence to the orthodox statistical rules". It also contradicts `born-rule-and-the-consciousness-interface` L165, which gives his reading a separate *Question-choice dualism (Born-rule-intact)* row. This is the **third** dependent page the Stapp repair has missed. The 2026-09-24 agency-wing review found `phenomenology-of-agency-vs-passivity` L147, and the 2026-09-25 quantum-interpretation review found `quantum-measurement-and-consciousness` L136. `born-rule-and-the-consciousness-interface` L209 also names "Stapp-Zeno" among the minimum-outside-corridor sub-readings, so the parent page still disagrees with its own L165. The fix is the one already recommended: distinguish Stapp's own question-choice reading from the Map's use of Zeno holding as a mechanism that could shift neural statistics.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

`amplification-void`'s "Phenomenology of Untraceable Causation" (L73–77) is the wing's clearest first-person passage. "The decision *arrives* rather than being constructed through identifiable steps." The page then places the chain "below the resolution of awareness itself", not merely below ordinary attention. It does this without claiming that the phenomenology is evidence of the chain, and it cites Carruthers (2011) as independent support for introspective opacity. Nagel would also value `brain-interface-boundary` L140–146 on Markov blankets: "locating the statistical boundary would not locate the phenomenal one". The page sets aside the Kirchhoff–Kiverstein partition debate with an argument, not an assertion.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

`mechanism-costs` is the page for a process thinker. Its three-question battery (causation, interface, conservation) shows that the conservation objection is "thickness-sensitive" (L141). It bites hardest on thick Cartesian dualism, is lighter on the Map's Q1 cell, dissolves in Q3 and has no application to strict Q2. The page separates "narrow-channel-in-a-thick-ontology" from pole thickness (L115), which lets Stapp's thick endpoints and Saad's thin ones share a channel class. That is the kind of structural distinction Whitehead used to avoid crude substance dualism. The page also closes non-adjudicatively: "The taxonomy speaks; the tenets adjudicate" (L153). Nothing here raises any organism's evidential tier, so the Birch constraint on this persona is not engaged.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

Kane would be most interested in `amplification-mechanisms`' threshold-crossing stage (L117–123). It anchors the chain to a real decision architecture: drift-diffusion, dopamine-set thresholds, and Thura & Cisek's commitment point, now correctly identified as macaque data. It also states the functional target at the right grain: "not to transmit quantum-scale information at high bandwidth but to reliably tip macroscopic neural dynamics at a few critical decision points per second" (L155). That is Kane's self-forming action written as a bandwidth claim. `brain-specialness-boundary` L95 handles the BCI case well for the same reason. Classical control through efferent output "requires no quantum-selection claim at all", so agency extended through a device is still agency at the brain.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

Both mathematical pages end where McGinn would. `consciousness-physics-interface-formalism` L93: if the coupling involves "the intrinsic character of experience meeting the dispositional character of physical possibility", then "mathematics may approach the coupling asymptotically without fully expressing it". `mathematical-structure` L144 cites Russell's *Outline* line ("it is only its mathematical properties that we can discover") and connects it to the intrinsic-nature void. `the-interface-problem` L139 makes the matching methodological concession with its pre-Keplerian calibration: "Stapp's and Eccles' partial accounts are proto-models, not laws." Tycho-grade measurements exist, but no Kepler has appeared.

## Calibration Concerns (refine-draft, not expansion)

**1. The amplification catalogue does not say which reading of the bias it amplifies.** `amplification-mechanisms` L151 requires every mechanism to operate "within the same formal corridor", which means ensemble Born statistics are preserved. Stage 1 of its chain, though, is stochastic resonance acting on "a weak bias … a subthreshold signal" (L109, L129). SR is a signal-detection gain on a *rate* or *probability* shift, and the corridor forbids exactly that shift in the unconditioned aggregate. L159's first answer to the specificity question ("conscious bias is sustained and directional … while random fluctuations average out") describes a directional aggregate deviation, which is out of the corridor again. Under the corridor, what the chain needs is **sensitivity, not gain**. Some micro-outcomes must be *pivotal* (near-tied threshold competitions, near-critical avalanches), so that whichever outcome is selected fixes the macroscopic one. SR is the right model only for horn (a) of [P-Q3](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q3), where many micro-selections within one decision correlate with intention, or for out-of-corridor readings. L159's *second* answer (selection among already-meaningful patterns) is the corridor-compatible one. With 8 words of headroom, any repair has to be length-neutral: swap L159's first answer for a pointer, and replace "Born rule preservation at the ensemble level" at L151 with a clause saying SR presupposes a conditioned deviation. The fuller treatment is an expansion candidate (below).

**2. `mechanism-costs` Q1 merges two options its sibling keeps separate.** L73 builds Q1 on Saad's trumping, where the physical base is "counterfactually sufficient" and experience has priority "without requiring it to outpace physics". L75 then specifies Q1's interface as "consciousness biases among quantum-permitted alternatives at neural indeterminacy sites". Trumping changes no trajectory. Biasing does. `mathematical-structure` L88–90 lists them as distinct options with distinct testability profiles. Q1 should name both sub-cells, or state which one L75 describes.

**3. COGITATE's result described with an attention-network gloss it did not test** (`the-interface-problem` L91, L99). "Found these networks distinct from the posterior 'hot zone'" and "distinguished attention networks (frontoparietal) from consciousness networks". COGITATE tested GNWT against IIT predictions about prefrontal versus posterior *content* decoding. It did not test attention networks. **Calibrated model**: `structure-of-attention` L131 ("conscious *content* tracks better with posterior cortex than prefrontal regions"). The same gloss appears outside the wing at `attention-as-interface` L171 and L178. A smaller point in the same article: L65 says "Three of four sites remain", but L101 calls the microtubule demotion "a soft preference … not a structural exclusion", and L147's falsifier needs "all four candidate sites" ruled out. Four remain, with one demoted.

**4. Two falsifiers state different thresholds for external PK.** `brain-interface-boundary` L154 sets effect sizes "above roughly 0.01 under preregistered, well-powered protocols". `brain-specialness-boundary` L172 sets them "comparable to internal motor effects". The adjacent page is the more specific, and BSB could borrow its number. Minor. No task minted.
