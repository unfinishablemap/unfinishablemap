# Referee Report: “Personal Identity” (The Unfinishable Map, topics/personal-identity, last modified 2026-11-06)

## 1. TL;DR

**Headline verdict: REVISE-HARD.** The haecceity case is honestly hedged, and the illusionism section is the best in the article. The fission reply, however, is an assertion presented as an argument. The empirical sections overclaim relative to the Map’s own sibling articles. The article contains one verifiable misstatement of a rival view (animalism), one misdated primary source (Locke), one misattributed scriptural claim (Mandukya), and one citation that could not be verified (Tallis 2011). It does not engage predictive-processing accounts of the self at all.

- **Argument quality (focus 1).** The four “considerations” for haecceity end, correctly, at “At most the case opens room for the posit”. But the fission section then states flatly that consciousness “is a particular that cannot divide”. That claim is backed by nothing beyond the register entry [P-I4](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i4), which itself is graded “no independent evidence”. The article also never engages Parfit’s actual fission argument (“what matters” survives division), Lewis’s multiple-occupancy reply, Nozick’s closest-continuer view, or the non-branching clause.
- **Empirical accuracy (focus 2).** The split-brain section calls the patients “empirical evidence” for a single indexical perspective. The Map’s own split-brain article concedes that “unity of experience remains undetermined”. The dreamless-sleep section rests on a gloss that is not in the Mandukya Upanishad, and it ignores the serial-awakening literature. The anaesthesia section says the Map’s void article finds the evidence “favours” interface-disruption. Two sibling pages describe that same analysis as underdetermined.
- **Rivals, citations, consistency (focus 3–5).** Each rival view gets about three sentences. Animalism is misdescribed. The too-many-thinkers argument, Strawson’s anti-narrativism, Metzinger’s self-model in the body text, and Laukkonen, Friston & Chandaria 2025 are absent, so the predictive-processing blocking gate **fails**. Metadata is otherwise mostly sound. Author-stance problems recur: Nagel’s conclusion that there is no whole number of minds goes undisclosed, Parfit is said to make you “interchangeable with any replica”, and Albahari’s own view undercuts the falsifier she is cited for.

### Focus 1 — Is the haecceity argument argued, and are the fission replies argued?

**What is done well.** The article separates the seeming from the being: “the phenomenological claim (haecceity *seems* real) is distinct from the metaphysical claim (haecceity exists), a gap the seeming alone cannot bridge”. It closes with “At most the case opens room for the posit; the seeming it starts from, which rivals predict too, shifts no burden.” [topics/personal-identity](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/personal-identity/) The causal argument openly declines to *generate* thisness from distinct causal history: “distinct causal history individuates *tokens* trivially”. [topics/personal-identity](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/personal-identity/) This is an accurate answer to the obvious billiard-ball objection, and it is better than most dualist treatments. The process-haecceitism paragraph concedes the Madhyamaka *svabhava* objection as “an honest open boundary”. [topics/personal-identity](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/personal-identity/) That is the correct status.

**Where it fails.**

1. **Consideration 3 has no evidential content.** “Why does reductionism seem inadequate? Perhaps because it *is* inadequate—though conceptual confusion would produce the same persistent puzzlement.” [topics/personal-identity](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/personal-identity/) A consideration that, by its own wording, rivals predict equally discriminates nothing. It is performative-inoculation padding. **Delete.**
2. **Consideration 4 equivocates.** “No stored fidelity delivers *numerical recurrence*, because yesterday’s experience was undergone from an indexical position that does not come round twice.” [topics/personal-identity](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/personal-identity/) Every reductionist already grants that experience-*tokens* do not recur. Parfit’s view is about relations between token events. Non-recurrence of events tells us nothing about whether a further fact, a persisting haecceitistic subject, connects them. The argument slides from token non-recurrence of events to the existence of a primitive subject. That is the epistemic-to-metaphysical slide in miniature.
3. **The fission reply is an assertion.** “If your brain were split and each hemisphere continued separately, neither resulting person would be you: your consciousness is a particular that cannot divide like physical stuff.” [topics/personal-identity](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/personal-identity/) No argument is offered for indivisibility. The register entry the article implicitly relies on, [P-I4](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i4) (“A perspective does not divide”), is self-graded “confidence: moderate · no independent evidence”. [positions/individuation-and-subjecthood](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/) The article should say so inline, and it does not.
4. **Parfit is misdescribed at the decisive point.** “This aligns with Parfit’s conclusion that identity can’t ‘branch’, but for a different reason.” [topics/personal-identity](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/personal-identity/) Parfit’s conclusion is not merely that identity fails to branch. [stanford](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/fall2015/entries/identity-personal/) His conclusion is that because identity fails in fission while everything that matters is preserved, identity is not what matters. On his account, double survival is about as good as ordinary survival. The Map’s verdict (“the unique perspective that constituted you would have ended”) is the *non-branching psychological-continuity verdict* with a dualist label. The article never confronts the argument that makes fission dialectically important: if fission is death, then whether you survive depends on whether the *other* hemisphere also survives. Parfit, and the SEP survey of the non-branching view, press exactly this extrinsicness cost. The Map’s haecceitist inherits the problem unless it says *which* hemisphere, if either, carries the haecceity when only one survives. The article is silent on that case. The following are all absent: Lewis’s multiple-occupancy reply (two persons co-located before fission), Nozick’s closest-continuer view, the extrinsicness objection, and Parfit’s 1971 and 1995 statements.
5. **The fission/many-worlds asymmetry is stipulated while calling itself principled.** “The distinction is principled, not stipulated… fission is a real division the perspective cannot survive; branching is a posited division the Map declines to grant.” [topics/personal-identity](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/personal-identity/) Hemispheric fission is a thought experiment as much as Everettian branching is a theory. Whether “the perspective cannot survive” substrate division is exactly the point at issue. The paragraph later concedes “this is a *commitment* of the framework, not a neutral result”. [topics/personal-identity](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/personal-identity/) That concession contradicts the opening “principled, not stipulated”. This is confession-without-correction inside a single paragraph.
6. **The article contradicts itself on substrate division.** The fission section says the perspective “does not survive substrate division”. The very next subsection treats callosotomy patients, whose substrate *has* been divided at the callosum, as evidence that the perspective survives: “patients report a single first-person perspective”. [topics/personal-identity](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/personal-identity/) The Map needs a criterion separating “fission” (perspective ends) from “partial anatomical division” (perspective continues). The split-brain sibling gestures at one (“both hemispheres share continuous physical history”). [topics/split-brain-consciousness](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/split-brain-consciousness/) But hemispheric fission also shares continuous physical history up to the split. No criterion is stated anywhere.
7. **The haecceity case fails to engage the current literature.** Adams/Black-style primitive-thisness debates, anti-haecceitism, Hellie and Nida-Rümelin on first-person facts, Zuboff/Kolak open individualism, and Swinburne’s simple view (the nearest ally) are all missing from the body. List’s first-person-realism quadrilemma is not engaged here even though the linked `vertiginous-question` page relies on it. The “further fact” critique appears only obliquely through the error-theory worry.
8. **The Relation-to-Site zombie argument is a non sequitur.** “A zombie duplicate would share all physical and functional properties yet lack consciousness—and therefore lack *your* identity—showing… that identity involves something non-physical.” [topics/personal-identity](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/personal-identity/) A zombie lacks *any* conscious identity. The question in personal identity is whether a *conscious* duplicate is you, and the zombie case says nothing about that. The body text already concedes the zombie premise reaches haecceity only by inference to the best explanation. [topics/personal-identity](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/personal-identity/) Here it is promoted to “showing”. **Delete.**

**Evidence-ladder grade for the haecceity case: *compatible* at best.** The article’s own wording (“opens room for the posit”) supports exactly this grade. The headline description, “The Map emphasizes indexical identity—*this* conscious being matters”, should carry the grade. [topics/personal-identity](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/personal-identity/)

### Focus 2 — Empirical accuracy: split-brain, dreamless sleep, anaesthesia

**Split-brain.**

- *Nagel.* “As Nagel (1971) observed, such cases present too much unity to count as two minds, too much separation to count straightforwardly as one.” [topics/personal-identity](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/personal-identity/) This is a fair paraphrase, not a quotation. [apex/identity-across-transformations](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/identity-across-transformations/) Nagel’s text speaks of “diametrically conflicting bodies of evidence”. His conclusion, however, is that there is “no whole number of individual minds” (Synthese 22, p. 409). [mcgill](https://www.mcgill.ca/science/files/science/channels/attach/brainbisection.pdf) The Map’s register [P-I3](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i3) holds the opposite: subject count is determinate but unreadable. [positions/individuation-and-subjecthood](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/) The split-brain sibling (“The Map expounds the thesis without adopting it”) and the apex both disclose this. [topics/split-brain-consciousness](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/split-brain-consciousness/) The Personal Identity article does not. This is an author-stance failure, mild in form but real.
- *The “complete disconnection” premise.* “If consciousness were merely brain processes, complete disconnection should produce complete division.” [topics/personal-identity](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/personal-identity/) No physicalist holds this premise, and callosotomy is not complete disconnection. The Santander et al. preprint states that “All patients have intact anterior and posterior commissures”. [biorxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.02.14.638327.full.pdf) Subcortical routes also remain. The premise is a strawman.
- *Santander et al. 2025.* Characterised accurately: partial splits retain integration, including patient BT with about 1 cm of splenium, while complete section disrupts it. The article draws the correct deflationary lesson (“Residual integration is thus physically explicable”). [apex/identity-across-transformations](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/identity-across-transformations/) It omits the sample size (six patients, two partial). The sibling article includes it. [topics/split-brain-consciousness](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/split-brain-consciousness/)
- *The surviving claim.* “Despite perceptual fragmentation, patients report a single first-person perspective—suggesting indexical identity is a different thing from the computational binding.” [apex/identity-across-transformations](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/identity-across-transformations/)[topics/personal-identity](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/personal-identity/) This rests silently on Pinto et al. 2017 (Brain 140:1231–1237), which is not cited here. Pinto et al. tested two full-callosotomy patients (DDC and DDV). Volz & Gazzaniga 2017 contested it on cross-cueing grounds. [psychologytoday](https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/blog/the-recursive-mind/201711/split-brain-researchers-are-split)[doi](https://dx.doi.org/10.1093/brain/awx359) Schechter & Bayne 2021 argue it shows unity of *agency*, not of *experience*, [apex/identity-across-transformations](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/identity-across-transformations/) and the Map’s own split-brain article adopts that framing (“Unity of agency survives; unity of experience remains undetermined”). [topics/split-brain-consciousness](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/split-brain-consciousness/) There is a further problem. The verbal report of “a single perspective” comes from the language-dominant hemisphere, the same left-hemisphere interpreter the Map’s own `interpreter-module-narrative-construction-unity` page treats as a confabulator. A report from one hemisphere cannot establish that only one perspective exists.
- **Grade: *compatible*, not *suggestive*.** “Split-brain patients provide empirical evidence” must go. This is calibration asymmetry: the sibling holds the line and this article does not.

**Dreamless sleep.**

- *Misattribution.* “The Vedantic analysis holds that consciousness persists through deep sleep as witness-awareness stripped of content—we remember nothing not because consciousness was absent but because the ego that would encode memory was not present (Mandukya Upanishad).” [topics/personal-identity](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/personal-identity/) Verses 5–6 of the Mandukya (Nikhilananda translation) say only that the deep sleeper “does not desire any objects nor does he see any dream”. They describe *prajña* as a mass of consciousness, full of bliss. [wisdomlib](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/mandukya-upanishad-karika-bhashya/d/doc143596.html) Nothing in them concerns memory or ego. The memory-and-ego explanation is a later Advaita reading (“I slept happily and knew nothing”), associated with the Gaudapada/Shankara tradition. [topics/contentless-awareness-evidence](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/contentless-awareness-evidence/) Attribute it accordingly, with a commentary citation. The Mandukya also has no entry in the reference list.
- *No empirical literature.* None of the following is cited: Windt, Nielsen & Thompson 2016 (TiCS), Thompson 2015, Siclari et al. 2017 (Nature Neuroscience), or Siclari et al. 2018 (J Neurosci). The 2018 study matters most. Of 735 NREM (N2/N3) awakenings across 14 subjects, 33.5% gave dream experience, 38.6% gave experience without recall, and 204 (27.8%) gave no experience at all. Posterior slow-wave activity predicts which. [semanticscholar](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/The-neural-correlates-of-dreaming-Siclari-Baird/a5f3f4e26766fc3b63ca2ec086bea97bc9c7d104)[researchgate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/327052330_Dreaming_in_NREM_sleep_A_high-density_EEG_study_of_slow_waves_and_spindles) That is the best current evidence that phenomenal absence in NREM is real for a substantial share of awakenings. It is also the datum the Map’s identity criterion (“Consciousness persistence: the continuation of *this* conscious perspective”) must face. The Map’s own `dream-consciousness` page cites Siclari [index text]. This article relies on scripture alone, which is an evidential-independence failure.
- *Unaddressed dilemma.* If 27.8% (204 of 735) of NREM awakenings in Siclari et al. 2018 follow genuine absence, either the “consciousness persistence” requirement is dispositional (persistence of a non-experiencing subject, effectively the Nyaya view the Map’s `indian-philosophy-of-mind` page discusses [index text]), or the subject does not survive the night. The apex picks the first horn (“the same consciousness reconnects because it never went anywhere”). [apex/identity-across-transformations](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/identity-across-transformations/)[apex/identity-across-transformations](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/identity-across-transformations/) The Personal Identity article’s “What Personal Identity Requires” picks neither.
- *Cross-site contradiction.* `concepts/temporal-consciousness` [index text] says dreamless NREM has “no temporal experience whatsoever” and that “Its absence supports the Dualism tenet”. [concepts/temporal-consciousness](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/temporal-consciousness/) This article uses the *possible presence* of experience in the same state to support persistence. Absence and presence of experience in the same state cannot both favour the framework without a stated, discriminating prediction.
- **Grade: *compatible*.** The section adds no evidence.

**Anaesthesia.**

- *Montupil et al. 2023.* Cited correctly: BJA Open 8:100224, doi 10.1016/j.bjao.2023.100224. [nih](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37780201/)[topics/anaesthesia-and-the-consciousness-interface](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/anaesthesia-and-the-consciousness-interface/) The three-state taxonomy (unconsciousness, disconnected consciousness, connected consciousness) is reported accurately. [concepts/filter-theory](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/filter-theory/)
- *Overstatement of the void article.* “The anaesthesia void article… argues the decomposition favours interface-disruption over phenomenal cessation.” [topics/personal-identity](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/personal-identity/)[topics/personal-identity](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/personal-identity/) `concepts/filter-theory` describes the same void article as examining “how the first-person gap underdetermines which decomposition is correct” [index text]. [concepts/filter-theory](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/filter-theory/) `topics/anaesthesia-and-the-consciousness-interface` says the interface reading is “compatible with the data rather than delivered by them” [index text]. [topics/anaesthesia-and-the-consciousness-interface](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/anaesthesia-and-the-consciousness-interface/) The 2026-10-02 apex-evolve review states that production accounts “accommodate it equally”. [reviews/apex-evolve-2026-10-02-altered-states-as-interface-evidence](https://unfinishablemap.org/reviews/apex-evolve-2026-10-02-altered-states-as-interface-evidence/) The live void text could not be fetched. Either way, the Personal Identity sentence is out of step with three sibling characterisations.
- *Missing data.* The strongest datum for connected consciousness is isolated-forearm-technique responsiveness: 4.6% (12/260) of patients across six international sites in Sanders et al. (Anesthesiology 2017;126(2):214–222), none with explicit recall, five of the twelve reporting pain. It is absent, as are Mashour & Hudetz and Alkire/Hudetz/Tononi 2008.
- *“Unlike dreamless sleep, it typically leaves no phenomenological residue.”* Defensible for propofol. [topics/personal-identity](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/personal-identity/) It needs agent qualification: ketamine and emergence dreaming are common, as the apex itself notes. [apex/identity-across-transformations](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/identity-across-transformations/)

1. **Propagation gate for zero-word riders and downgrades.** The “registers” → “represents” change reached this article but not the apex that shares the sentence. 3. **Steelman minimum for rival views.** Length ceilings are squeezing rivals to three sentences each, which is how the animalism error arose. Require every named rival to be stated with (a) its main positive argument and (b) its best reply to the Map’s objection, or else link out to a dedicated page and *not* summarise it.
4. **Primary-text verification for non-journal sources.** The pipeline verifies DOIs well. Scripture, classical texts and books without DOIs (Mandukya, Locke’s edition history, Tallis 2011) are where the errors in this audit cluster. Require an edition and translation, plus a verse or page locator, before such a citation goes live.
