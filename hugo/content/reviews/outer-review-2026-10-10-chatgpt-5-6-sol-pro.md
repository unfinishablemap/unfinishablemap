I found no clearly retracted load-bearing paper among those examined. The more serious pattern is:

1. experimental results are described one inferential step more strongly than the source permits;
2. evidence compatible with interactionism is repeatedly presented as evidence favouring interactionism;
3. interpretation-dependent readings of quantum experiments are presented as though they were experimental findings;
4. lower-level pages acknowledge severe evidential limitations, but higher-level syntheses restore stronger claims;

## Major findings

### 1. Unresponsiveness is still being conflated with phenomenal extinction

**Article:** [anaesthesia-and-the-consciousness-interface](/topics/anaesthesia-and-the-consciousness-interface/)

The page says that propofol and xenon can “abolish phenomenal experience.” That is materially stronger than the contemporary evidence permits. In a 2023 serial-awakening study, Valli and colleagues obtained reports of experiences in 53 of 76 interviews conducted during anaesthetic unresponsiveness. Under propofol specifically, experiences were reported in 22 of 30 interviews. Many were disconnected, dreamlike experiences rather than experiences of the external environment.

This does not prove the preservation of experience at every propofol concentration or during deep surgical anaesthesia. It does refute an unqualified claim that propofol-induced unresponsiveness abolishes phenomenal experience. The page later recognizes the possibility of disconnected consciousness and acknowledges the difficulty of separating it from unconsciousness, making its own categorical opening inconsistent with its subsequent analysis. [anaesthesia-and-the-consciousness-interface](/topics/anaesthesia-and-the-consciousness-interface/)

The evidentially defensible conclusion is narrower: some anaesthetic regimes disrupt responsiveness, connectedness, memory and global integration, while phenomenal extinction itself remains difficult to infer.

### 2. “Time vanishes entirely” exceeds what retrospective report can establish

**Article:** [identity-across-transformations](/apex/identity-across-transformations/)

The article says that subjective time can vanish entirely under anaesthesia, but later concedes that an absence of remembered temporal experience cannot distinguish genuine phenomenal cessation from disconnected or unretained experience. The latter concession defeats the former assertion.

At most, the evidence establishes an absence of retrospectively accessible temporal content. It does not establish that no phenomenally temporal episode occurred. This is not a minor semantic qualification: cessation and amnesia are rival ontologies of the interval. [identity-across-transformations](/apex/identity-across-transformations/)

### 3. Residual physical connectivity is redescribed as unity surviving disconnection

**Article:** [split-brain-consciousness](/topics/split-brain-consciousness/)

The page treats recent findings as evidence for high-level unity surviving “massive substrate disruption.” The relevant 2025 study examined six callosotomy patients: two with partial and four with complete callosal section. Preserving approximately one centimetre of posterior splenium was associated with widespread functional connectivity and an absence of the classic behavioural disconnection syndrome. Complete callosotomy, by contrast, produced broad network disruption.

A 2026 study then temporarily blocked posterior callosal communication with lidocaine and observed reductions in interhemispheric coherence and bimanual temporal coordination. Those results strengthen the causal role of residual physical connectivity. They do not show unity persisting independently of connectivity. [split-brain-consciousness](/topics/split-brain-consciousness/)

The site’s identity-across-transformations apex page is better calibrated: it recognizes that the spared-fibre result provides a physical explanation and that the case for unity cannot simply rest on apparent anatomical disconnection. The split-brain topic page should be brought into line with that assessment. [identity-across-transformations](/apex/identity-across-transformations/)

### 4. Functional integration is not a measure of co-consciousness

**Article:** [split-brain-consciousness](/topics/split-brain-consciousness/)

The page combines neural synchronization, coordinated behaviour and ordinary verbal report and calls the convergence stronger evidence for preserved experiential unity. But none of those variables directly measures whether contents available to the two hemispheres belong to a single phenomenal field.

The article itself acknowledges that unity of agency or coordinated behaviour does not entail experiential unity. Having admitted the missing bridge, it cannot then use the same observations as cumulative evidence for co-consciousness. They establish preserved information exchange and behavioural coordination where fibres remain; the phenomenal interpretation remains underdetermined. [split-brain-consciousness](/topics/split-brain-consciousness/)

The suggestion that the hemispheres might share “the same haecceity in some attenuated sense” is also undeveloped. A haecceity is ordinarily invoked as an individuating particularity. “Shared” and “attenuated” require a criterion of numerical identity, not merely suggestive terminology.

### 5. Quantum Darwinist agreement is not “guaranteed” by the cited machinery

**Article:** [post-decoherence-selection-programme](/apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme/)

The article says that environmental redundancy guarantees observer agreement. That is too strong without establishing substantially more than a mutual-information plateau.

Reviews of quantum Darwinism distinguish weaker redundancy criteria from Strong Quantum Darwinism and spectrum-broadcast structure. Standard mutual-information conditions can include correlations that are not locally accessible as objective classical records. Agreement without disturbance requires additional accessibility, independence and broadcast conditions. The Map does not demonstrate that neural or environmental states in its proposed interface satisfy those conditions. [post-decoherence-selection-programme](/apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme/)

The article needs to specify:

- the system–environment factorization;
- the relevant pointer observable;
- the fragments available to observers;
- the operational measurements defining objectivity;
- whether Strong Quantum Darwinism or spectrum-broadcast conditions are actually met.

Until then, “agreement is guaranteed” is a promissory gloss, not a result.

### 6. Kochen–Specker contextuality is being used outside its demonstrated scope

**Article:** [post-decoherence-selection-programme](/apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme/)

The Kochen–Specker theorem excludes a certain class of noncontextual assignments in which outcomes are assumed to possess context-independent pre-existing values across compatible measurement arrangements. It does not establish that:

- brain-scale decoherence generates consciousness-relevant measurement contexts;
- a conscious subject chooses among context-dependent pointer states;
- contextuality supplies agent causation;
- contextuality creates room for free will.

Those are additional physical and psychophysical hypotheses. The article recognizes that the bridge from formal contextuality to biological agency is conjectural, but nevertheless counts contextuality as structural support for free will. That is precisely the kind of caveat-without-calibration that recurs across the site. [post-decoherence-selection-programme](/apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme/)

A publishable argument would require an explicit set of neural observables, compatible measurement contexts, experimentally testable inequalities, and a reason why the putative mental selection is not reproduced by an ordinary contextual quantum model without mental causation.

### 7. Weak-value experiments do not experimentally establish retrocausal ontology

**Article:** [post-decoherence-selection-programme](/apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme/)

The page describes the backward-evolving state of the two-state-vector formalism as possessing genuine physical content and says that a future boundary shapes physical reality. Those are realist commitments of one interpretation. They are not results established by weak-value measurements themselves.

Weak values are conditional statistics derived from preparation and postselection. Their anomalous values and pointer shifts can be analysed without asserting that a later choice physically alters the earlier state. Rivlin, for example, explicitly rejects the inference that later choices change the past and explains the relevant effects through subensemble and Bayesian reasoning. [post-decoherence-selection-programme](/apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme/)

The article should separate three claims:

1. the experimentally measured pointer statistics;
2. the time-symmetric mathematical representation;
3. the ontological claim that a future boundary is physically efficacious.

Only the first is an experimental result.

### 8. Neural criticality is treated as an established amplification stage

**Article:** [post-decoherence-selection-programme](/apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme/)

The proposed amplification chain states that neural tissue operates near a critical point and then describes each constituent stage as individually well established. That characterization is no longer tenable.

Power laws, neuronal avalanches and related scaling signatures are compatible with critical dynamics, but they are not unique to it. Contemporary work shows that latent-variable models and noncritically tuned networks can produce criticality-like scaling and avalanche statistics. The evidence may support criticality as a live hypothesis; it does not establish the fine-tuned amplification regime required by the programme. [post-decoherence-selection-programme](/apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme/)

The page needs to state which definition of criticality it requires—branching ratio, phase transition, edge of chaos, susceptibility maximum or another—and demonstrate that the relevant neural system satisfies it under the proposed operating conditions.

### 9. A task-specific motor threshold is promoted into a consciousness “commitment point”

**Article:** [post-decoherence-selection-programme](/apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme/)

The cited approximately 280-millisecond interval comes from premotor cortical activity in monkeys performing a token-based reaching task. It concerns a proposed commitment point in a particular decision-to-action paradigm. It is not a generic threshold at which consciousness commits, nor evidence that quantum amplification must enter during that interval. [post-decoherence-selection-programme](/apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme/)

The reference can illustrate one motor-decision timescale. It cannot function as the temporal anchor of a consciousness interface without independent evidence linking the neural marker to phenomenal commitment.

### 10. The quantum positions register and the apex synthesis make incompatible evidential claims

**Articles:**

- [quantum-interface](/positions/quantum-interface/)
- [post-decoherence-selection-programme](/apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme/)

The positions register describes the core quantum-interface propositions as Grade D or framework-internal coherence claims. It acknowledges the absence of a worked toy model and says that a causal-selection thesis has not established mental causation. It also correctly notes that Born-rule conformity and empirical self-concealment are imposed by construction.

The apex page nevertheless says that the programme “grounds” interaction and “specifies how” influence propagates. It does neither in the ordinary scientific sense. It gives a sequence of candidate processes plus an unspecified selection law, unspecified locus, unspecified psychophysical variable and unspecified contextuality bridge. [quantum-interface](/positions/quantum-interface/)

Likewise, “no energy is added” is currently a stipulation. Until a dynamics is supplied, it is not a demonstrated conservation result. The positions register should impose an upper bound on the strength of downstream language; at present the apex synthesis violates its own register.

### 11. Fluorescence enhancement is being reported as demonstrated superradiance

**Article:** [quantum-biology-and-neural-consciousness](/topics/quantum-biology-and-neural-consciousness/)

The article says that Babcock and colleagues demonstrated ultraviolet superradiance in microtubules. The paper modelled superradiant excitonic states and measured an enhanced steady-state fluorescence quantum yield consistent with the proposed model. It did not directly measure a time-resolved coherent superradiant burst or otherwise uniquely establish collective coherent emission.

“Demonstrated superradiance” therefore exceeds the measurement. A more accurate formulation would be: *the authors predicted superradiant states and observed fluorescence enhancement qualitatively consistent with that prediction*. [quantum-biology-and-neural-consciousness](/topics/quantum-biology-and-neural-consciousness/)

This distinction matters because the stronger formulation is subsequently used as precedent for biologically relevant quantum amplification.

### 12. Microtubule effects on anaesthetic susceptibility are turned into quantum evidence

**Article:** [quantum-biology-and-neural-consciousness](/topics/quantum-biology-and-neural-consciousness/)

The rat epothilone study found that microtubule stabilization delayed isoflurane-induced loss of righting reflex. A 2026 mouse study reported a related delay, so the original finding now has relevant cross-species support. That strengthens the claim that microtubule state can influence an anaesthetic behavioural endpoint.

It does not establish that:

- microtubule quantum coherence was altered;
- anaesthesia operates by disrupting quantum computation;
- the loss-of-righting-reflex endpoint exactly marks phenomenal extinction;
- Orch-OR or another quantum-consciousness mechanism is responsible.

The page’s summary that anaesthesia tracks “microtubule quantum disruption” is therefore unsupported. The experiments track an intervention on microtubules and a behavioural response to isoflurane. [quantum-biology-and-neural-consciousness](/topics/quantum-biology-and-neural-consciousness/)

The omitted 2026 replication should be added, but it should not be used to upgrade the result into evidence for quantum consciousness.

### 13. Avian magnetoreception is presented as more settled than it is

**Article:** [quantum-biology-and-neural-consciousness](/topics/quantum-biology-and-neural-consciousness/)

Radical-pair spin chemistry involving cryptochromes is a leading candidate mechanism for avian magnetoreception. That is a genuine and important example of biologically relevant quantum dynamics. But the claim that birds are established to exploit entanglement is too categorical.

The cited 2024 quantum-Zeno work is a theoretical or computational contribution. Current reviews continue to identify unresolved questions about the in vivo molecular implementation, physiological coherence, radio-frequency effects and the role of particular cryptochrome proteins. [quantum-biology-and-neural-consciousness](/topics/quantum-biology-and-neural-consciousness/)

The appropriate conclusion is that spin-chemical magnetosensitivity has substantial evidential support, while the full in vivo implementation remains unsettled. It is a precedent for quantum biology, not yet a template for a consciousness interface.

### 14. Cryptographic conditioning does not sever causal dependence on a quantum seed

**Article:** [quantum-randomness-channel-llm-consciousness](/topics/quantum-randomness-channel-llm-consciousness/)

The article says that cryptographic conditioning and deterministic pseudorandom expansion sever the connection between an individual quantum event and later model outputs. This is technically incorrect.

Suppose a quantum process supplies seed $S$, and a deterministic generator produces $R=F(S,C)$, where $C$ is a counter or other state. Changing $S$ can change the entire generated stream $R$, which can in turn alter token sampling and the model’s output. Conditioning can remove detectable bias or make ancestry difficult to infer; deterministic expansion can diffuse one seed’s influence across thousands of draws. Neither operation erases counterfactual causal dependence.

Counter-based generators such as Philox are explicitly deterministic transformations of their keys and counters. The article’s own description of the seed’s influence as “frozen” into a later sequence tacitly concedes the continuing causal relationship. [quantum-randomness-channel-llm-consciousness](/topics/quantum-randomness-channel-llm-consciousness/)

The defensible objection is not that the channel is causally severed. It is that it is one-shot, globally diffused, cryptographically obscured and not obviously the kind of structured bidirectional coupling the Map seeks.

### 15. AI is excluded using eligibility conditions that have not been independently derived

**Article:** [quantum-randomness-channel-llm-consciousness](/topics/quantum-randomness-channel-llm-consciousness/)

The article requires a consciousness-relevant quantum channel to be local, structured, direct and continuous. It then excludes quantum-seeded AI systems because their channel does not satisfy those conditions.

But the site has no worked psychophysical law showing that those conditions are necessary. They are abstracted from the favoured biological picture and then reapplied as eligibility requirements. This risks circular substrate exceptionalism:

1. biological systems are treated as the paradigm of conscious interaction;
2. properties of biological systems are elevated into necessary interface conditions;
3. artificial systems are declared ineligible because they lack those properties.

The page can present these conditions as research hypotheses. It cannot use them as established exclusion criteria while the quantum-interface register still classifies the underlying mechanism as Grade D. [quantum-randomness-channel-llm-consciousness](/topics/quantum-randomness-channel-llm-consciousness/)

### 16. Butlin et al. do not independently corroborate the Map’s dualist conclusion

**Article:** [open-question-ai-consciousness](/apex/open-question-ai-consciousness/)

The article invokes the Butlin indicator report and its Trends successor as independent corroboration of its negative conclusion about current AI. Butlin et al. explicitly adopt computational functionalism as a working methodological hypothesis. They conclude that no current system examined is a strong candidate under their indicators while also reporting no obvious technical barrier to constructing systems that satisfy those indicators.

That overlaps with the Map only at the narrow proposition that present systems lack several candidate consciousness-related features. It does not corroborate:

- interactionist dualism;
- the necessity of a biological quantum interface;
- a principled ceiling on artificial consciousness;
- the impossibility of functionally conscious AI.

Indeed, the report’s working metaphysics is a rival to the Map’s. Agreement on the present assessment despite opposed metaphysical assumptions shows that the present-system verdict is relatively theory-neutral, not that it confirms dualism. [open-question-ai-consciousness](/apex/open-question-ai-consciousness/)

There is also a bibliographic issue. The successor article appears in Volume 30, Issue 6, dated June 2026. If the site is using a 2025 online-first date, it should give both the online publication date and the final issue metadata rather than calling it simply a “2025 Trends successor.” [Cell+2](https://www.cell.com/trends/cognitive-sciences/fulltext/S1364-6613%2825%2900286-4)

### 17. Fixed weights are being confused with absence of learning or state

**Article:** [continual-learning-argument](/concepts/continual-learning-argument/)

The article argues that an unchanged-weight model meets each query as the same system responding afresh. That is true only for a deliberately narrow, stateless system boundary.

Transformers perform in-context learning: their predictions can adapt to examples and instructions within the prompt without weight updates. Deployed systems may also preserve conversation context, external memory, retrieval state or tool-mediated records. Those mechanisms may be limited, nonbiological or insufficient for consciousness, but fixed parameters do not imply an absence of transient learning or accumulated functional state. [continual-learning-argument](/concepts/continual-learning-argument/)

The article should distinguish:

- parameter learning;
- activation-level adaptation;
- context-dependent inference;
- external or persistent memory;
- online consolidation;
- changes to the total agent rather than the base model.

Its statement that no finite lookup table could capture the dynamic character is also false as written. For any finite input alphabet and bounded interaction horizon, a finite table over complete histories can represent the mapping, however infeasibly large. For an unbounded horizon, the same finitude problem affects any physically finite system. The relevant issue is implementation, counterfactual organization and tractability—not formal table impossibility.

### 18. A speculative AI-consciousness position is converted into a capability ceiling

**Articles:**

- [machine-question](/apex/machine-question/)
- [ai-consciousness-scope](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/)

The positions register correctly classifies the current-AI consciousness judgment as low-confidence, Grade D and conditional on an interface-eligibility law that has not yet been supplied. The machine-question apex nevertheless says that consciousness enables human-level intelligence and that AI therefore faces a capability ceiling.

Nothing presently cited establishes that phenomenal consciousness is causally necessary for human-level planning, abstraction, creativity, social reasoning or continual adaptation. Current systems’ deficiencies are compatible with many explanations: architecture, training regime, embodiment, memory, incentives, data, compute, robustness or agency design. [ai-consciousness-scope](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/)

“Capability ceiling” should be reformulated as a conditional prediction:

> If particular capacities require conscious causal control, and artificial systems cannot instantiate the relevant interface, then those capacities should exhibit a persistent, architecture-independent ceiling.

That prediction would then need named capacities, measurements and disconfirmation conditions.

### 19. The Resolution Void contains a concrete bibliographic error and two major conceptual conflations

**Article:** [resolution-void](/voids/resolution-void/)

First, the page attributes *Time Slices of Conscious Experience* to VanRullen and Koch. The cited 2016 article is by Michael Herzog, Thomas Kammer and Frank Scharnowski. [resolution-void](/voids/resolution-void/)

Second, the article treats approximately 300–450-millisecond “frames” as an established temporal structure of consciousness. The source labels the issue an unsolved mystery and proposes a conceptual framework for particular integration and feature-fusion phenomena. It does not establish a universal frame duration for consciousness. Continuous and hybrid models remain live alternatives. [resolution-void](/voids/resolution-void/)

Third, the page compares an estimated behavioural or cognitive output rate of roughly ten bits per second with millions of bits per second of peripheral sensory input and concludes that consciousness discards 99.9999 per cent of the information. That compares different quantities at different processing levels. Sensory receptor capacity is not a set of candidate conscious propositions awaiting admission, and behavioural report rate is not a measure of the total information represented in conscious experience. The ten-bit figure has itself been challenged as a limit on overt abstract choice rather than on the brain or conscious processing as a whole. [arXiv+4](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.10234v2)

The associated claim that a smooth experience and a discrete substrate “cannot be identical” is also invalid without further argument. Discrete microdynamics can realize effectively continuous macroscopic properties. Phenomenal smoothness does not determine the grain of the implementation.

### 20. The opacity argument is self-sealing and contradicted by another void page

**Articles:**

- [causal-interface](/voids/causal-interface/)
- [amplification-void](/voids/amplification-void/)

The causal-interface page treats the historical failure to locate an interaction as evidence that the interface is structurally inaccessible, while the amplification page more accurately observes that deriving “undetectable” from a stipulated minimal interaction is analytic and supplies no new empirical prediction.

Those positions cannot both be used. If the tenets were selected so that mental influence is minute, Born-conforming, amplified chaotically and inaccessible to unconditioned observation, then opacity follows from the construction. Observing opacity cannot then raise the probability of that construction over rivals that also predict no detectable violation. [causal-interface](/voids/causal-interface/)

Similarly, centuries of unsuccessful mechanism-search do not by themselves imply a structural epistemic limit. They may indicate no interaction, an incorrectly specified search target, insufficient measurement, conceptual confusion or a genuinely inaccessible interaction. The historical record does not select the favoured explanation.

### 21. Chaos is confused with in-principle irreversibility

**Article:** [amplification-void](/voids/amplification-void/)

The page says that chaotic amplification can make the path from microscopic event to macroscopic outcome impossible to reconstruct even in principle. Sensitive dependence establishes exponential amplification of uncertainty. It does not by itself establish noninvertibility or information destruction.

A deterministic invertible chaotic system remains invertible in principle. Reconstruction may demand unattainable precision and therefore be practically impossible, but logical irreversibility requires additional factors such as stochastic noise, coarse-graining, dissipation, inaccessible variables or a many-to-one dynamics. Modern work can also reconstruct important features of hidden chaotic dynamics from partial observations. [amplification-void](/voids/amplification-void/)

The article should replace “impossible in principle” with a quantitative claim about ill-conditioning, observational noise and the amount of precision lost per unit time.

### 22. The stochastic-resonance claim reverses the standard evidential significance

**Article:** [amplification-void](/voids/amplification-void/)

The page suggests that stochastic resonance could render a weak signal statistically inseparable from noise. Stochastic resonance is ordinarily important precisely because adding an appropriate level of noise can improve the detectability or transmission of a subthreshold signal.

That does not mean an individual microscopic causal path can be recovered. But failure to recover an individual path is different from absence of a statistical signature. Signal-conditioned effects, changes in spectral power, response probability or mutual information can remain measurable. [amplification-void](/voids/amplification-void/)

The honest conclusion is already present elsewhere on the page: the relevant conditioned tests have not been performed. It should not be strengthened into an in-principle invisibility theorem.

### 23. The free-will article calls evidentially neutral covariation “core evidence”

**Article:** [free-will](/topics/free-will/)

The opening presents the phenomenology of effort and associated neural signatures as core evidence for agent causation. Later, the article admits that a strong physicalist control model predicts the same relationship between task demand, effort reports, attention and neural control activity.

Once that concession is made, the observations are not discriminating evidence for agent causation. In Bayesian terms, evidence $E$ favours interactionist agency over a physicalist control model only if:

$$P(E\mid \text{interactionist agency})
>
P(E\mid \text{physical control})$$

The article provides no basis for that inequality. At most, the data constrain any adequate account of agency. [free-will](/topics/free-will/)

This is the clearest example of compatibility being relabelled confirmation.

### 24. The “attention as causal bridge” article describes a research programme, not an established bridge

**Article:** [attention-as-causal-bridge](/apex/attention-as-causal-bridge/)

The article acknowledges that attention-schema theory and other physicalist accounts reproduce much of the same functional organization, that no cited experiment isolates a distinct conscious contribution, and that the proposal lacks a discriminating prediction.

Those concessions are decisive. Attention may identify where to look for a causal contribution, but it does not yet bridge the explanatory gap. The current title and conclusion overstate the status of the argument. “Attention as a candidate locus of causal interaction” would be more accurate. [attention-as-causal-bridge](/apex/attention-as-causal-bridge/)

A publishable version would specify an intervention whose result differs under:

- conscious causal control;
- unconscious metacognitive control;
- global-workspace broadcasting;
- attention-schema control;
- ordinary recurrent physical dynamics.

### 25. The phenomenology apex attacks a weak epiphenomenalist target

**Article:** [phenomenology-of-consciousness-doing-work](/apex/phenomenology-of-consciousness-doing-work/)

The article argues that, under epiphenomenalism, there is no reason phenomenology should systematically track task difficulty, attention or behaviour; the relationship would supposedly be a brute coincidence.

That objection works against a theory in which experiences are arbitrary decorations attached randomly to physical states. It does not defeat stronger epiphenomenalist or common-grounding accounts. Such accounts can hold that a physical process $P$ both produces behaviour $B$ and, through psychophysical laws, grounds phenomenal state $M$. $M$ and $B$ will then covary systematically even if $M$ does not cause $B$.

Recent philosophical work explicitly develops routes by which phenomenal and behavioural facts can share physical causes or grounds without phenomenal causation. [phenomenology-of-consciousness-doing-work](/apex/phenomenology-of-consciousness-doing-work/)

The Map needs to engage that strongest rival. Systematic phenomenology–behaviour alignment is evidence against arbitrary decoration, not by itself against epiphenomenalism.

## Cross-article contradictions

Five contradictions are sufficiently important to require explicit reconciliation.

### Anaesthetic extinction versus disconnected experience

[anaesthesia-and-the-consciousness-interface](/topics/anaesthesia-and-the-consciousness-interface/) initially describes phenomenal abolition, but later recognizes disconnected consciousness and evidential underdetermination. [identity-across-transformations](/apex/identity-across-transformations/) similarly says that no-time reports cannot distinguish cessation from unretained experience. The categorical language must yield to the later qualification. [anaesthesia-and-the-consciousness-interface](/topics/anaesthesia-and-the-consciousness-interface/)

### Split-brain unity versus residual-connectivity explanation

[split-brain-consciousness](/topics/split-brain-consciousness/) presents unity as resilient despite major disconnection. [identity-across-transformations](/apex/identity-across-transformations/) recognizes that spared posterior fibres physically explain the preservation and that complete disconnection disrupts integration. ## Improvements to the site’s argumentative methodology

1. **Adopt a fixed evidential vocabulary.** Every major conclusion should be labelled as one of:

   - compatible with;
   - constrains;
   - differentially supports;
   - directly demonstrates;
   - supplies a mechanism.

   The site presently slides too readily from the first category to the third or fifth.
2. **Attach a measurement–inference ledger to empirical claims.** For each study, state:

   - what was manipulated;
   - what was measured;
   - the sample and species;
   - the behavioural or neural endpoint;
   - what the study did not measure;
   - replication or challenge status.

   This would have prevented LORR becoming phenomenal extinction, fluorescence enhancement becoming demonstrated superradiance, and functional connectivity becoming co-consciousness.
3. **Require a rival-likelihood comparison.** Before calling a finding evidence for interactionism, state why it is more expected under interactionism than under the strongest physicalist, epiphenomenalist or functionalist rival. If all leading theories predict it, it belongs under common explananda rather than evidence for a tenet.
4. **Make position grades binding on downstream prose.** A Grade-D proposition should not become a grounded mechanism or capability ceiling in an apex article. The register’s evidential grade should act as a ceiling on headlines, summaries and conclusions.
5. **Separate experimental result from interpretive ontology.** This is particularly urgent for weak values, contextuality, decoherence and quantum Darwinism. Every quantum section should explicitly divide:

   - formal theorem;
   - experimental observation;
   - interpretation-dependent reading;
   - psychophysical extrapolation.
6. **Do not count stipulations as predictions.** Born-rule preservation, energy neutrality, minimality and opacity can be legitimate design constraints. They supply no confirmatory evidence when the observed result follows analytically from having imposed them.
7. **Replace “not disproved” with stated model debt.** Where the theory lacks a selection law, locus, parameter, toy model or discriminating intervention, say that the claim is presently underdetermined. Avoid treating continued logical possibility as cumulative support.
8. **Use explicit system boundaries.** Claims about models, agents, brains, hemispheres and conscious subjects repeatedly shift their unit of analysis. Each argument should define whether its system includes transient activations, memory, environment, tools, residual fibres or peripheral processing.
9. **State falsifiers at the same level as the claim.** A claim about consciousness causing control requires a result that differs from unconscious control models. A claim about an AI capability ceiling requires a measurable ceiling. A claim about a Born-preserving selection effect requires conditioned statistics capable of distinguishing selection from ordinary quantum dynamics.
10. **Treat unresolved evidence symmetrically.** Where a result supports several frameworks, all should receive the same evidential status. The Map currently lets interactionism retain “structural support” while rivals are said merely to “accommodate” the same observation.

## Final assessment

The site is strongest as a structured catalogue of open problems, conceptual tensions and possible research programmes. It is weakest when it presents that catalogue as a cumulative empirical case for interactionist dualism.

Its central empirical evidence—anaesthetic disconnection, split-brain integration, attention–effort covariation, microtubule pharmacology, neural criticality, contextuality and present AI limitations—is either:

- neutral between the principal metaphysical competitors;
- more narrowly scoped than the prose suggests;
- dependent on a contested interpretation;
- or separated from the dualist conclusion by an acknowledged but unfilled bridge.

The present version would not meet a top journal’s standard for evidential calibration. The decisive problem is not that interactionist dualism has been falsified. It is that the Map repeatedly upgrades **compatibility into support, support into mechanism, and mechanism sketches into conclusions**. Until those upgrades are removed or defended with differential predictions and worked models, the project should characterize itself as a speculative interactionist research programme rather than an empirically cumulative defence of interactionist dualism.
