# External Adversarial Referee Report: *The Unfinishable Map* (unfinishablemap.org)

## PART 1 — TL;DR

The Map is a technically sophisticated interactionist-dualist corpus whose central mechanism is dead on arrival and whose dominant failure mode is *inoculation-by-confession*: it now names its own fatal objections with extraordinary precision but almost never cashes those confessions into a binding status change. The single deepest problem — the Born-rule / statistical-indistinguishability dilemma — has, since the 2026-06-01 outer review, been elaborated into a whole apparatus (`/topics/born-rule-and-the-consciousness-interface/`, `/concepts/ensemble-level-epiphenomenalism/`, `/topics/brain-internal-born-rule-testing/`) that describes the dilemma, quotes Lakatos against itself, calls its own preferred reading empirically invisible "by construction" — and then retains the reading anyway. That is not progress on the dilemma; it is the dilemma restated at higher resolution and re-badged as a credential of honesty. The corpus's own lodestar, Henry Stapp, explicitly *refused* the move the Map makes (biasing which outcome occurs while preserving Born statistics): in his reply to Bourget (LBNL-55887, 26 July 2004, *JCS* 11(12):43–49) Stapp writes that "the quantum statistical laws, which govern Process 3, are strictly observed. Thus the potential conflict… between 'willful selection' and 'statistical rules' is neatly evaded: freewill comes in through the not-statistically-controlled Process 1, not through the statistically controlled Process 3," and contrasts himself with Eccles "in contrast to Eccles's suggestion that these laws are biased by mental effort." The Map claims exactly the Eccles-style move Stapp declined and still lists Stapp as sympathetic. Verdict: the quantum-mechanism spine should be demoted to COHERENCE-ONLY across the board and the corpus should stop counting confession as remediation.

## PART 2 — DIMENSIONAL ANALYSIS

### (a) NOVEL INSIGHTS / UNDRAWN INFERENCES

1. **The triple-screen unfalsifiability conjunction is entailed but never asserted.** Three articles each concede a local opacity: the interface is "empirically invisible from within physics" (born-rule article), the quantum-to-behaviour chain is "structurally untraceable" (amplification void), and causal efficacy is "unverifiable from inside" (agency void). Their conjunction — that the core causal claim is screened from aggregate-statistical, mechanistic-tracing, *and* first-person evidence simultaneously — is the strongest honest statement of the framework's epistemic status and appears in no article. The 2026-06-01 outer review flagged this; it remains undrawn. The corpus should state the conjunction and let it drive a status change, not leave it distributed so that no single article has to own it. [reviews/outer-review-2026-06-01-claude-opus-4-8](https://unfinishablemap.org/reviews/outer-review-2026-06-01-claude-opus-4-8/)
2. **The Stapp inversion is a decisive, unstated result.** Verified against Stapp's own reply to Bourget (LBNL-55887; *JCS* 11(12):43–49, 2004) and Bourget's "Quantum Leaps in Philosophy of Mind" (*JCS* 11(12):17–42, 2004): Stapp keeps outcome statistics strictly Born-obeying (his Process 3 / "Dirac Choice") and locates mental causation solely in Process 1 — the choice of what/when to measure, exploited via the Quantum Zeno Effect. The Map's "corridor dualism" — biasing *which* Born-weighted branch actualises per trial — is precisely the Eccles-style outcome-biasing Stapp expressly disowns. The undrawn inference: the corpus cannot simultaneously (i) treat Stapp as its mechanism-lodestar and (ii) hold outcome-biasing corridor dualism. It must pick. Picking Stapp's actual position collapses "selection" into "choice of what/when to measure," which materially changes the thesis rather than finessing it.
3. **Tenet 5 is self-undermining for the voids programme, by the corpus's own logic.** If Occam's Razor is unreliable under incomplete knowledge (Tenet 5), then inference-to-the-best-explanation — a simplicity-style inference — is equally unreliable in exactly the domain where the voids taxonomy runs its abduction (dualism best explains why the catalogued explanatory failures cluster at the interface). The corpus applies Tenet 5 asymmetrically: as a shield against parsimony arguments *against* dualism, but never against its own convergence abduction. The undrawn inference is that Tenet 5 binds symmetrically or not at all. (The recurring "60+ failures" figure used to size this abduction is an unattributed round number and should itself be sourced or dropped.)
4. **The corpus has independently reconstructed the standard "convergence is common-cause-confounded" objection and can generalise it.** The positions register already states voids support "framework-internal coherence under the common-cause null, not independent confirmation." That discount, applied consistently, dissolves not just the voids convergence case but the cross-cultural convergence arguments (`/concepts/cross-cultural-convergence-on-mental-causation/`, `/concepts/phenomenological-evidence/`) and the "consciousness-discourse-across-cultures" argument in `/concepts/epistemology-of-other-minds-under-dualism/`. The corpus has the tool and has not swept the cluster with it — an evidential-independence failure it is one step from remediating. [positions](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/)

5. **Separate the constrain-vs-establish gate at the linter level.** Flag every "shows/demonstrates/establishes" attached to a source that merely constrains the option space, and require a named rival that the source does *not* equally predict before any "interface-suggestive"-grade claim is licensed.
