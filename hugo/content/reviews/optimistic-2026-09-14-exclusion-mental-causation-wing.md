### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

The wing keeps non-identity non-negotiable and pays for it in the open. [causal-exclusion-argument](/concepts/causal-exclusion-argument/) declines the two most attractive physicalist rescues — Shoemaker's subset account and Yablo's determinables — on a stated boundary rather than a found fault: "a proper subset of *physical* powers is available only to a property that has a physical realizer to inherit from," and the determinable route "is purchased by making the mental a less specific way of being physical — which is what premise 3, read as the dualist reads it, refuses." That is the dualist saying why the mainstream answers are not available *to it* while conceding they "may work perfectly well for the non-reductive physicalist." [quiddity-epiphenomenalism-and-the-contingency-thesis](/concepts/quiddity-epiphenomenalism-and-the-contingency-thesis/) does the same for the monist: it grants the quiddities and still shows the phenomenal aspect idle, then refuses to bank the result — "The contingency thesis tells us where one bridge is weak, not that any other bridge will bear weight."

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

The wing handles Stapp with more care than Stapp is usually handled. [causal-closure-debate-historical-survey](/topics/causal-closure-debate-historical-survey/) states Process 0 correctly (the free choice of *which* measurement, logically prior to von Neumann's Process 1 — the label is Stapp's own, "formerly referred to as process 4"), immediately says most physicists regard the framework as non-standard, and then shows that the closure gap survives without him: "orthodox quantum mechanics does not specify which outcome actualises, and this indeterminacy is a feature of the theory itself." [conservation-laws-and-mental-causation](/concepts/conservation-laws-and-mental-causation/) refuses to let the Zeno mechanism inherit conservation compliance from the framework: measurement back-action in a bath-coupled brain (Erez et al. 2008; Elouard et al. 2017) means "conservation compliance… must be audited mechanism by mechanism, and for the Zeno mechanism specifically that audit is open." Stapp would recognise the courtesy of being taken seriously enough to be audited.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

[pain-consciousness-and-causal-power](/topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power/) earns Nagel's approval on the page's phenomenology of suffering — grief that "involves no tissue damage yet can be among the most intense forms of suffering," dread and moral anguish "generated by conscious reflection," the temporal architecture that "reaches into the past through regret and grief" and "into the future through dread and anxiety." The pain–suffering distinction is the wing's best first-person work: it shows that the evaluative dimension has phenomenal character that "does not decompose into sensory dimensions," and then hedges exactly where a phenomenologist should — the unifying valence is "an interpretive reading the cases are compatible with rather than one they establish."

### Finding 1 — The "Heyes (2018)" reference is Dunstone & Caldwell's paper, and three deep reviews certified it
