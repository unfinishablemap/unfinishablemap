# Pessimistic Review

**Date**: 2026-09-02
**Content reviewed**: [positions/consciousness-scope.md](/positions/consciousness-scope/) (domain preamble + [P-CS1](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs1) through [P-CS5](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs5)). First review of this file — oldest unreviewed content in the corpus (modified 2026-06-22).

## Executive Summary

The register's individual entries are unusually disciplined — multi-axis calibration, honest grade-D self-assessments ("a permission, not a datum"), empirically live shift conditions. The genuine defects are *seams*: the June-written domain preamble was never harmonised with the entries that evolved under it. The preamble asserts "each follows from a tenet" where [P-CS1](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs1)'s own 2026-08-03 correction says its neutrality premise is *adopted, not derived*; it asserts every position survives a coherence-only demotion of the quantum programme where [P-CS5](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs5) explicitly depends on Tenet 3's interface reading; [P-CS1](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs1)'s centrality line omits [P-CS5](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs5) from its list of dependents; and [P-CS3](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs3)'s `Argued in` cites [infant-consciousness](/voids/infant-consciousness/), a void about the *loss and unreachability* of infant experience that nowhere argues the early-emergence claim. One citation-verification result: Passos-Ferreira 2024 *Neuron* is real (Crossref: "Can we detect consciousness in newborn infants?", doi:10.1016/j.neuron.2024.04.024) but is engaged by no corpus article — it exists only in this register and the outer review that recommended it.

## Critiques by Philosopher

### The Eliminative Materialist
Churchland would note that everything in this register that touches evidence is neuroscience she could endorse: marker convergence, developmental correlates, agent-specific anaesthetic pharmacology, single-cell chemotaxis mechanics. The dualist contribution is confined to re-labelling — "correlate-identification" rather than constitution, "interface disruption" rather than production. Strip the labels and the verdicts (graded animal consciousness, early infant onset, no prokaryotic experience) are unchanged. The "minimal-dualism spine" [P-CS1](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs1) is, on her reading, idle machinery: it does no sorting work any particular case turns on, which the register itself half-concedes by grading it D and calling it a permission. A framework whose spine "settles no particular case" is a spine the cases do not need.

### The Hard-Nosed Physicalist
Dennett's target is [P-CS4](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs4). "The interface is disrupted, not consciousness itself" is the theorist's free move: whatever the data show — graded anaesthetic transitions, severed hemispheres, dream reports — the interface reading redescribes it at zero cost, and the entry admits this ("compatible with the data, not forced by it"). He would press the concession harder than the register does: a reading that is *never* forced by any observation in the cluster is not a moderate-credence empirical position but a preferred idiom. And the subject-count leg — split-brain "does not disclose a subject-count [P-I1](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i1) holds determinate" — inflates an intuition of unity into a metaphysical fact that, by the register's own admission elsewhere, no possible measure reaches. That the determinacy "leaves no trace in the connectivity" is treated as a clue; Dennett would call it the definition of a fact not worth positing.

### The Quantum Skeptic
Tegmark gets unusual credit here: the register is architected precisely to survive his critique, quarantining the quantum apparatus in the companion registers. But he would then read [P-CS5](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs5)'s dependency line and find Tenet 3 back in the room — "the interface reading that locates no substrate site for proto-choice at the prokaryotic floor." The "no interface site" premise is a deliverance of the quantum-substrate analysis; if decoherence arguments demote that programme, the register loses its stated ground for declining to locate experience in bacteria, and the symmetric underdetermination of [P-CS5](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs5) loses one of its legs. The advertised quantum-independence of the domain is four-fifths true, and the preamble states it as five-fifths.

### The Many-Worlds Defender
Deutsch would aim at the [P-I1](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i1) import in [P-CS4](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs4). A "determinate subject-count" with empirical discriminability *none* is exactly the kind of question Everettians dissolve rather than answer: subject-counting is a bookkeeping convention over decohered branches, not a further fact. The register holds the count determinate because Tenet 4's indexical objection needs a determinate "I" — which, he would say, is reasoning from the conclusion. The honest entry-level hedging ([P-I1](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i1): "tenet-driven rather than empirically compelled") makes his point for him.

### The Empiricist
A Popperian audit divides the register cleanly. [P-CS2](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs2) and [P-CS3](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs3) pass with credit: their shift conditions name observations the marker literature could actually deliver (later onset convergence, systematic marker confounds, clade upgrades). [P-CS1](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs1) and [P-CS4](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs4)'s shift conditions are all philosophical re-evaluations — "a successful reduction," "illusionism vindicated," "the filter model independently undermined" — no observation statement among them. [P-CS5](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs5) declares its object-level question undecidable *by construction* and then assigns the meta-thesis moderate credence; Popper would ask what evidence could ever move that number, and the entry's honest answer ("the literature's failure to yield any discriminating observable") is an argument from absence that a single new formalism could void. The register's own `empirical discriminability` axis is doing the demarcation work — the criticism is that the domain preamble sells the whole set as one robust spine when the axis shows two evidential kinds.

### The Buddhist Philosopher
Nagarjuna would observe that the register's own evidence points at emptiness and its commitments point away. [P-CS2](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs2) establishes a gradient with no determinate cut-off; [P-CS5](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs5) finds a floor where the question of a subject "is one the world declines to answer"; anaesthesia yields graded, component-wise dissolution. Everywhere the Map looks, boundaries blur and the subject fails to appear as a findable thing — and the register's response is to hold, via [P-I1](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i1), that subject boundaries are nonetheless real, determinate, and unreadable. To assert a determinate essence precisely where all observation shows dependent, graded arising is clinging formalised as calibration. The register at least quarantines the move as framework-internal grade D, which is more honesty than most.

## Counterarguments to Address

### The weakest-link problem in [P-CS4](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs4)'s grade C
- **Current content says**: [P-CS4](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs4) (grade C, "compatible with, not forced by") asserts split-brain "severs the interhemispheric channel without disclosing a subject-count [P-I1](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i1) holds determinate."
- **A critic would argue**: The subject-count leg of a grade-C position is inherited from a grade-D, framework-internal position with empirical discriminability *none*. The calibration line advertises C; the weakest link in the Asserts is D. A reader citing [P-CS4](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs4) for the fragmentation reading silently takes on [P-I1](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i1)'s full framework-internal burden without the grade telling them so.
- **Suggested response**: The Depends-on line does record [P-I1](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i1), so the cascade is legible to a careful reader — but the register's whole design principle is that a downstream article "can cite exactly the burden it is taking on." One clause in the Asserts ("a determinacy the Map holds on framework-internal grounds — [P-I1](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i1), grade D") would make the grade honest at the point of use. This is a candidate refinement, not an error.

### The epistemic/metaphysical bridge in the fragmentation reading
- **Current content says**: The interface reading treats unreadability as expected — "the determinate answer leaves no trace in the connectivity" (source article's phrasing).
- **A critic would argue**: This runs the equivocation pattern in reverse: an epistemic premise (no physical measure discloses the subject-count) is offered as *consonant with* a metaphysical thesis (the count is determinate and the anatomy is only a channel). Consonance is cheap — the same unreadability is equally consonant with there being no fact to read (Nagel's actual position, and the illusionist's).
- **Suggested response**: The register survives this because it claims only compatibility and holds the determinacy on separately-registered tenet grounds. The vulnerable phrasing lives in the source articles ("That unreadability is the clue" — [split-brain-consciousness](/topics/split-brain-consciousness/) closing section), which edge from *consistent with* toward *evidence for*. Worth one lens-pass on that article's closing paragraph at its next review; the register entry itself is correctly hedged.
