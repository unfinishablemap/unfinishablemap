## Executive Summary

The wing is one continuous argument told in seven registers: the consciousness–brain interface does not switch off, it disassembles — channel by channel through hypnagogia, mode by mode through sleep, agent by agent under anaesthesia — and the two void articles mark where the first person loses the right to report on the process. Read in sequence, the seven articles form the corpus's most complete worked example of the *graded, multi-channel interface* thesis, and six of the seven hold the Map's calibration discipline at full strength, repeatedly declining to claim the production/filter question settled when the data underdetermine it. The one finding requiring action: [concepts/sleep-and-consciousness.md](/concepts/sleep-and-consciousness/) — the oldest-reviewed article in the wing (deep review 2026-07-11) — still carries three tenet-section over-claims its siblings have since scrubbed from their own registers, and one of them ("lucid dreaming proves the interface is trainable") sits in the frontmatter `description:` itself, the navigation surface a fetching model reads first. ## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)
The wing keeps the explanatory gap exactly where it belongs. [lucid-dreaming-and-dualist-rendering](/topics/lucid-dreaming-and-dualist-rendering/) states the dream-form of the hard problem in one line — the dream version asks "why there might be *more* experience when the system is doing less" — and [dream-consciousness](/topics/dream-consciousness/) closes its epiphenomenalism section by granting the type-B identity response its force before holding the line: if phenomenal properties were neural properties, dreaming should be deducible from a complete neural description of REM, and it is not. The gap is asserted where it is defensible and nowhere else.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)
[anaesthesia-and-the-consciousness-interface](/topics/anaesthesia-and-the-consciousness-interface/) is the model for how the Map should touch quantum mechanisms: Craddock's microtubule proposal and Wiest's delayed-luminescence work are cited as "contested but suggestive," and the stochastic-emergence finding — two orders of magnitude of variability in genetically identical mice — is explicitly *not* claimed for the quantum reading: "it is equally consistent with classical neuronal noise and does not by itself support the quantum reading." A quantum-friendly wing that refuses to cash cheap quantum cheques is worth more to the interactionist programme than one that claims every stochastic residue.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)
The two void articles centre first-person structure with unusual precision. [anesthesia-void](/voids/anesthesia-void/) has a section literally titled "Phenomenology of Having No Phenomenology" and extracts real structure from it: no duration-residue, no experience of the gap *as* a gap, an emergence phenomenology that belongs to the edge and never to the interior. [sleep-consciousness-void](/voids/sleep-consciousness-void/) ends on the strongest self-reflexive sentence in the wing: "not only is the territory too vast, but the mapper cannot access all of itself." That is the what-is-it-like question turned on the Map's own method, and the article does not flinch from it.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)
The wing's central move is anti-binary everywhere: "Consciousness doesn't switch off during sleep—it modulates through states" ([sleep-and-consciousness](/concepts/sleep-and-consciousness/)); "the consciousness-brain interface does not switch off. It disassembles" ([hypnagogic-phenomenology-and-interface-modulation](/topics/hypnagogic-phenomenology-and-interface-modulation/)); three states under stable anaesthesia, not one ([anesthesia-void](/voids/anesthesia-void/), after Montupil). Consciousness is treated as process under varying constraint, never as a substance that pops in and out of existence — crude substance dualism is structurally absent. This praise is for the process framing only; it neither needs nor licenses any evidential upgrade, and the wing never asks it to.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)
Lucid-dream control is the closest thing the corpus has to agency under laboratory verification — pre-agreed eye-signal tasks executed during confirmed REM — and the wing resists the temptation to over-read it. Control is "negotiated rather than dominated" ([dream-consciousness](/topics/dream-consciousness/)); many lucid dreamers achieve awareness without control; [hypnagogic-phenomenology-and-interface-modulation](/topics/hypnagogic-phenomenology-and-interface-modulation/) maps agency at finer grain still, as micro-moments of authorship alternating with passive reception during sleep onset. Imperfect, fluctuating control is precisely what a genuine but bounded selectional influence should look like, and the wing says so without claiming the bound's mechanism is proven.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)
The void articles institutionalise epistemic humility rather than gesturing at it. The dreamless-sleep inference problem — absence of memory does not discriminate absence of experience from absence of encoding — is stated, given its Vedantic counter-reading, and left honestly open because "both predict the identical waking report." [anesthesia-void](/voids/anesthesia-void/) concedes that third-person workarounds "translate rather than recover" phenomenal character. These are cognitive-closure claims of exactly the scope the evidence supports: closed from inside, partially probeable from outside, with the residue named.

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)
**This persona's verdict is load-bearing for the review, and on six of seven articles it is enthusiastic.** The wing's recurring sentence-form is the discipline itself: "The evidence does not adjudicate between these framings" ([dream-consciousness](/topics/dream-consciousness/)); "the case below argues only that the dualist horn carries less explanatory debt, not that the evidence forces it" ([lucid-dreaming-and-dualist-rendering](/topics/lucid-dreaming-and-dualist-rendering/)); "intellectual honesty requires acknowledging that the disinhibition account explains the same phenomenological pattern without invoking non-physical consciousness" ([hypnagogic-phenomenology-and-interface-modulation](/topics/hypnagogic-phenomenology-and-interface-modulation/)). Four passages deserve to be named as corpus-wide models:

1. **The Hu et al. (2023) treatment** in [anaesthesia-and-the-consciousness-interface](/topics/anaesthesia-and-the-consciousness-interface/) separates the authors' claims from the Map's naming ("*Active reboot* is the Map's name for the mechanism class, not theirs") and then *downgrades its own convergence count* — "three mechanism classes plus one within-class replication, a slightly weaker convergence claim than a four-class reading would make and the one the study supports." Voluntarily weakening a favourable convergence claim to what the study licenses is tenet-as-evidence-upgrade praise-worthily not done.
2. **The Konkoly (2026) caveats paragraph** in [dream-consciousness](/topics/dream-consciousness/) reports N=20, the p = .037 versus p = .057 model-dependence, the missing confidence intervals, and the absent replication — statistical honesty applied to the wing's own flagship result.
3. **The consensus-convergence hedge**: the anaesthesia article's strongest exhibit — mainstream anaesthesiology adopting the disconnection-not-extinction framing filter theory predicted — is immediately priced as "*suggestive* rather than *vindicating*," with the production-model reinterpretation conceded in the same paragraph.
4. **Intra-framework calibration** in [anesthesia-void](/voids/anesthesia-void/): the article adjudicates between two *dualist* readings (interface-disruption versus memory-failure), gives three reasons for its preference, and then concedes "None of this is decisive. A dualist who preferred memory-failure could redescribe the same evidence... the Map should not pretend otherwise." Calibration against rivals is common in the corpus; calibration between the Map's own live options is rarer and harder, and this is the cleanest instance I have seen.

The wing touches no minimal-organism boundary case, so no five-tier placement is at stake; the discipline shows instead in the production/filter register, where the same possibility/probability distinction applies. On one article it fails — see the calibration concern below.

## Content Strengths

### anaesthesia-and-the-consciousness-interface.md
- **Strongest point**: The four-component pharmacological dissection (phenomenal presence, access, temporal binding, memory encoding) with the xenon–ketamine contrast as its sharpest exhibit — same primary receptor, opposite phenomenological outcomes.
- **Notable quote**: "What it shows is that a framing filter theory committed to ahead of the evidence has become, over the last decade of clinical work, the framing the field uses to describe what it sees."
- **Why it works**: It converts a pharmacology literature into an interface map without once claiming the pharmacology decides the metaphysics, and its "What Anaesthesia Cannot Tell Us" section does the boundary-marking before any critic has to ask.

### anesthesia-void.md
- **Strongest point**: The asymmetric-gap analysis — clock-time full of events on the outside, "no time at all" on the inside — plus the demonstration that the first-person report of a gap does not entail a phenomenal gap (intraoperative awareness, disconnected consciousness).
- **Notable quote**: "The subject does not experience the gap *as* a gap."
- **Why it works**: It preempts the strawman ("naive 'one master switch' physicalism... is not the physicalism actually defended in the literature") and stakes the Map's reading on grounds it names as living *outside* the void — consilience across altered states — rather than on the void itself.

### dream-consciousness.md
- **Strongest point**: The phenomenal-mode-tracking table — cognitive outcome tracking a gradient of phenomenal richness across NREM, non-lucid REM, incorporating dreams, hypnagogia, and lucid REM — which turns scattered findings into a single testable pattern.
- **Notable quote**: "Cognitive outcomes track this phenomenal gradient, not simply whether the brain is 'processing.'"
- **Why it works**: The epiphenomenalist challenge is met with three named problems (specificity, mode, gradient) and the honest rider that "none is individually decisive" — pressure without over-claim.

### hypnagogic-phenomenology-and-interface-modulation.md
- **Strongest point**: The disengagement sequence (attention, then voluntary imagery, then agency, then reality-monitoring) offered explicitly as "a proposed and typical progression rather than an invariant law," with the retrospective-report circularity named against itself.
- **Notable quote**: "The consciousness-brain interface does not switch off. It disassembles."
- **Why it works**: A single nightly transition is made to do the work of a lesion study, and the article's own falsifier section lists the exact condition (simultaneous rather than sequential disengagement) that would kill its thesis.

### lucid-dreaming-and-dualist-rendering.md
- **Strongest point**: The rendering dilemma run through the physicalists' own concessions — Hobson's "innate virtual reality generator," Metzinger's "waking life is a form of online dreaming" — so the phenomenological premise arrives pre-granted by the opposition.
- **Notable quote**: "The dilemma itself is neutral between the two readings — it sharpens the question rather than settling it."
- **Why it works**: Every one of the four capability signatures carries its own production-model counter-reading in the same section; the article claims explanatory-debt asymmetry, never proof.

### sleep-consciousness-void.md
- **Strongest point**: Bidirectional inaccessibility as the void's structure — waking cannot re-enter dream acceptance, dreaming cannot access waking critique — which survives the physicalist's neural explanation because the neural story explains the difference, not the mutual unintelligibility.
- **Notable quote**: "Not only is the territory too vast, but the mapper cannot access all of itself."
- **Why it works**: The Vedantic four-state analysis is deployed as a genuine alternative hypothesis with its own predictions, not as decoration.

### sleep-and-consciousness.md
- **Strongest point**: Consolidation as the counter-dissociation — computationally rich, phenomenally silent processing that gives the wing its "computational binding without phenomenal binding" distinction.
- **Notable quote**: "Sleep closes a door; anesthesia breaks the hinges."
- **Why it works**: It is the wing's connective tissue, and its state table is the reference the other six articles implicitly rely on. Its tenet section, however, is the wing's one calibration lapse — below.

## Calibration Concern: sleep-and-consciousness.md Lags Its Own Wing

Per this skill's design, the Process Philosopher and the Hardline Empiricist should converge on an honestly calibrated article. On six articles they do. On [concepts/sleep-and-consciousness.md](/concepts/sleep-and-consciousness/) they part: the process framing is excellent, but three passages run the pre-discipline register that the wing's later-reviewed siblings (deep reviews 2026-07-14 through 2026-07-27) have already abandoned, and the sweep confirms all three are unique to this file (plus its Hugo mirror) — no wider family:

1. **L106**: "This proves the interface is trainable" — and the frontmatter **`description:`** (L3) repeats it: "lucid dreaming proves the interface is trainable." The sibling standard is [dream-consciousness](/topics/dream-consciousness/)'s "supporting the view that consciousness has trainable parameters." The description line is the worse locus: it is the navigation surface a fetching model reads before any of the body's hedges.
2. **L136** (Bidirectional Interaction tenet): "The trainability of lucidity... provides evidence: you cannot train an epiphenomenon." The slogan begs the question against the epiphenomenalist, who reads lucidity training as neural plasticity (MILD-style regimes altering circuits) with the phenomenal accompaniment changing in tow. [lucid-dreaming-and-dualist-rendering](/topics/lucid-dreaming-and-dualist-rendering/) handles the identical evidence correctly, giving the physicalist neuroplasticity reading first and then making the narrower initiation point.
3. **L138** (Minimal Quantum Interaction tenet): "This makes testable predictions: lucid dreams should show neural signatures closer to waking attention (which 2025 research confirms)." Attention-like signatures in lucid REM are predicted by virtually every theory in which metacognition returns; they are compatible with, not a discriminating confirmation of, quantum Zeno selection. The wing's own formula for this situation is "compatible with both interpretations and does not by itself adjudicate between them."

Milder, same pass: L128's illusionism contrast ends "Contemplative traditions report the latter" — prediction-confirmation framing sixteen lines after the article itself grants those reports "remain difficult to verify."

This is a calibration concern, not a praise, and the fix is small: re-register four sentences to the wing's own sibling formulas. A refine-draft task has been generated (see Tasks Generated).

#### Hypnopompia and Sleep Paralysis: The Ascent the Wing Never Maps
