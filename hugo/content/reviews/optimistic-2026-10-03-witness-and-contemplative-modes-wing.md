## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

Witness-consciousness §"The Witness Cannot Be the Brain" (L134–140) presses the right question: if the witness is one brain state representing another, why does one get to be the observer? It then concedes, in §The Illusionist Challenge (L146), that Tallis's regress "proves nothing" taken bare. Chalmers would welcome a dualist page that takes the explanatory gap seriously yet will not let its own favourite reply stand unexamined. The witnessing void's "background hum" passage (L77) uses Chalmers's own phenomenology in its proper register: something is present and nothing can be grasped.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

Meditation-modes L141 is the most honest Stapp-friendly paragraph in the corpus. In Stapp's model each Process 1 event is both awareness and probing. So "awareness without probing is unavailable inside his framework and fewer events would mean sparser experience, the opposite of what meditators report." The page names the hypothesis it needs, that awareness and probing can come apart, as "a further Map commitment the Zeno model does not supply". Stapp would recognise his own mechanism used carefully rather than recruited. The 12:40Z carry fits the same model: a witness that still issues some Process 1 events can still cause its reports.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

The observer-witness page's "Phenomenology of Witnessing" (L40–52) keeps four features distinct: effortlessness, spaciousness, non-reactivity, and presence without intervention. Choice-and-volition's four components of willing (L78–102) give the active pole an equally fine grain. Read together, the wing gives both poles a first-person anatomy. Nagel's question is answered twice, in the selecting register and in the witnessing register.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

Witness-consciousness L152 explains why the witness cannot become its own object: "the subjective aspect of an 'actual occasion' is never available as an object to that same occasion." That is a structural explanation, not a mystical one. Meditation-modes L167 maps the two modes onto the physical and mental poles, then declines to press the mapping ("the mapping is speculative and this article does not press it"). The wing never turns process-compatibility into an evidential upgrade. Whitehead's praise here is coherence praise, and the pages keep it that way.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

The choice-and-volition three-level structure (L137) is select, largely refrain, choose whether to enter selecting mode. It now carries the canonical sentence: "the witness must still steer a little, or it could not cause its own reports." Kane wants agency located at a genuine decision point. The veto section (L98–102), with Brass and Haggard's "whether" component, supplies one, and the witness passage no longer undercuts it by describing a causally idle mode.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

The witnessing void is the wing's mysterian centre: "The light that illuminates the room does not illuminate its own illuminating" (L41). It separates its limit from three neighbours (self-opacity, introspective opacity, the recursion void), so the closure claim is specific: first-order and operation-level, not a depth limit. Its "weaker challenge" paragraph (L111) concedes that the void may be "attenuable without being eliminable". McGinn would count that as humility doing analytic work.

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

This persona's verdict is the most important one for this wing, and it is mostly favourable.

- **Meditation-modes earns the strongest praise.** It declines three evidence upgrades that were available. Plasticity is "evidence the Map shares with its rivals" (L46). Trained effortlessness "is shared evidence rather than a mark in the Map's favour" (L110). The traditions "are not independent witnesses… the convergence carries the weight of one lineage's pattern in regional variants" (L124). Each time the page had a structural chance to slide shared evidence up the scale, and each time it declined.
- **The witnessing void applies [P-V1](/positions/voids-as-evidence/#p-v1)'s lineage discount to its own leg** (L99): the analytic leg "discounts to roughly one arrival", and "the tenets' capacity to explain that opacity removes a defeater without adding weight". This is the named pattern, *tenet-coherent, not evidence-elevating*, applied to a void's own evidential base.
- **Witness-consciousness and meditation-modes both rate the beautiful-loop rival correctly**: the witness reports are "consonant with the Map's reading rather than probative against the rival" (witness L119, L148; modes L143, L151).
- **The flag: three pages still run the upgrade the concept pages retracted.** Apex L160, contemplative-practice L157 and choice-and-volition L151 treat sharpened discrimination under training as evidence against illusionism.
