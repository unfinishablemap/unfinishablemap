These are six of the best-calibrated pages in the voids catalogue. Five of the six, written by different forks for different queue tasks, reach the same **exposure shape** ([The Convergence](#convergence)). The handedness void is its formal model:

1. **Invariance.** The subject's verdict is the same on both sides of the difference it would need to detect.
2. **Contrast-only exposure.** The difference shows only when two runs are compared, and a subject cannot always run both cleanly.
3. **Closure without uptake.** A comparison, once run, yields knowledge and leaves the verdict as it was.

No page names the shape. `three-kinds-of-void` already names the operation-void family in the same grammar and is the natural home (priority item 3). Clause 1 is the voids-side form of the 10-09 rule "presentation is not certification", minted as a P3 for `evidential-status-discipline`. ## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

The modality void's third deflation (L76) is the move Chalmers makes against eliminating the first-person question by third-person fiat. Grant that neurobiology individuates the senses. Then the first-person *count* loses its authority, but the *attribution* face still stands, "where the question is not which carving is correct but why a subject is handed an unauditable verdict about their own experience — a stake no third-person taxonomy can retire." The page concedes everything a physicalist owns and keeps exactly the residue that is first-personal. The numbing void's structural face does the same for value. "No subject undergoes the sum" (L62) is a claim about where experience is had. The page notes that a physicalist can say it too (L94: "a sum of brain states is not a brain state that hurts"), and it ends: "Dualism sharpens the point without owning it."

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

The wing's restraint about physics is what makes its few quantum remarks credible. Handedness L104 refuses the most tempting move it had: "reaching for parity violation as an interface mechanism would be overclaiming." Offloading L125–127 states the Tenet 2 speculation on the brain side, because the tenet locates the interaction there. It also discards the aid-side contrast as unworkable ("a pseudo-random generator or a temperature-sampled model is deterministic given its seed"). It separates the *occasion* of selection from the *capacity* for it: delegation removes occasions. Preference L116 lists the empirical constraints a selection among prepared orderings would have to respect (no energy injection, no Born-statistics violation, no conservation violation) and marks the question "untested here". A Stapp sympathiser gets a precise place to look and no claim that anything has been found there.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

Assent's transparency face is phenomenology done well: "the attitude is looked *through* and never *at*" (L79). The page then turns this into a fact about agency. Evaluative control "takes no handle on its object and produces no experience of pushing, so forming a belief at will feels like finding nothing to push on" (L91). The numbing void quotes the two first-person reports that matter most and declines to make them do more than they can. Szent-Györgyi's "I am unable to multiply one man's suffering by a hundred million" and Dillard's daughter's "Lots and lots of dots in blue water" (L80) together show that imagination turns the many into texture. Modality opens with the line Nagel would underline: "Seeing is given as seeing; flavour is given as taste" (L44).

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

Three pages put an *event* where a state was expected. Preference L116 says that if construction is right, "an event rather than a state fills that gap". Assent finds "one causally central, phenomenally absent event" (L41). Numbing's one open route to a felt sum is the [combination-problem](/concepts/combination-problem/) (L108: "whether subjects compose a further subject"), the question that concrescence answers in process terms. These resonances are real. **Evidential-status check:** none of them is used to move anything up the five-tier scale. No organism or system is assigned a tier anywhere in the wing, and the combination-problem pointer is a further-reading line. It is not offered as evidence that sums are undergone. The pages are tenet-coherent and nowhere evidence-elevating, so no slippage is flagged.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

Kane's self-forming actions happen in conflicted deliberation, and the offloading void notices what delegation does to them: "a delegated deliberation is an occasion that does not occur in the subject's brain at all" (L125). Assent's evaluative control is the kind of agency Kane needs and compatibilists often miss. It is genuine, it works through reasons, and it is not managerial ("one acts on one's beliefs by attending to the world", L91). Preference's first reading puts any conscious contribution at "which prepared member governs the act" (L109), which is a selection among options already prepared. In all three cases the page notes that the account is "fully compatible with physicalism" (assent L107) or "available, not actual" (preference L112). Kane would want more. The Map is right to give him exactly this much.

The handedness void is the mysterian's best friend and sternest critic at once. It shows a limit "provable *relative to a background physics*" that turned out false (L70), so every "in principle" claim must answer "relative to which theory?". **What the shape adds.** The 10-09 rule says a first-person presentation does not certify the fact it presents. This wing shows from the catalogue side what certification would require: a comparison between runs, which an experimenter can stage more easily than the subject. It also shows what certification delivers when it comes: testimony that leaves the presentation unchanged. The handedness void is the only case where the invariance was a theorem relative to a theory, and the only case where the invariance broke. That is why it serves as the model and should be cited first.

**Calibration fence.** The shape is a fact about how these limits are measured. The five instances may share one upstream, a single introspective channel reading its own outputs, so under [the common-cause null](/project/common-cause-null/) the shape counts once. ## Expansion Opportunities

### High Priority

#### Mirror invariance and its unlearning
- **Builds on**: `handedness-void` "What Survives in the Subject" (L76–86) and item 1's Tenejapan evidence; `categorical-perception-void` (perceptual learning reshaping discrimination).
- **Would address**: the handedness void treats Kant's left/right difference as felt and bodily. It does not mention that the *visual* system generalises across mirror images by default, and that learning a script with mirrored letters breaks this. Kolinsky et al. (2011) report that illiterate adults "displayed some sensitivity to enantiomorphic contrasts but performed far worse than all the other participant groups when the task required paying attention to such contrasts". They conclude that learning such a script "pushes the beginning reader to break the mirror invariance characteristic of the visual system". Danziger & Pederson (1998) report the same across ten communities, with a script-dependent Tamil exception. This would be a fourth subject-side face, a perceptual indifference to handedness that culture removes. It would also be the rival to item 1's spatial-frame reading of the Tenejapan data.
- **Leads (Crossref metadata and OpenAlex abstracts verified 09:40Z)**: Kolinsky, Verhaeghe, Fernandes, Mengarda, Grimm-Cabral & Morais (2011), *JEP: General* 140(2) 210–238, doi 10.1037/a0022168.
