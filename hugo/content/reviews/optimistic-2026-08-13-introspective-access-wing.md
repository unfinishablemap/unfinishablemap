## Executive Summary

This wing is where the Map turns its evidential discipline on its own instruments, and four of the five articles hold that discipline at full strength — including the two places it is hardest to hold: adjudicating a live 2024–2026 dispute (anendophasia) and discounting one's own five-way convergence (both apexes). The wing's distinctive achievement is *symmetric scepticism about first-person data*: introspective evidence is down-weighted even where it points the Map's way. The opportunities are almost entirely connective and cap-free — the inbound apex (introspection reconstructs) and the outbound apex (expression compresses) describe two halves of one bounded report-channel and neither cites the other, and the hub has not absorbed the anendophasia article's healthy-adult evidence. ## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

The wing takes the explanatory gap seriously at the meta-level, which is rarer than taking it seriously at the object level. The tool apex's finding that linguistic failure concentrates on one side of a divide — "Language handles the physical-functional domain because it belongs to that domain; it falters precisely where its subject matter stops being the kind of thing it is" — is the explanatory gap recovered from the first-person side, and the article says so: the semantic gap is the explanatory gap "wearing linguistic dress". Equally Chalmers-friendly is the hub's refusal to let a congenial architecture do evidential work it hasn't earned: "Compatibility-with-prediction is not entailment-of-evidence." The hard problem is honoured here not by asserting irreducibility but by keeping the registers separate while the structural picture accumulates.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

The wing's interface picture is disciplined in exactly the way an interactionist should want. The hub's Bidirectional Interaction section makes the prediction that matters: if the interface leaves no phenomenal markers, "A markerless interface is, from the subject's side, observationally indistinguishable from no interface" — so the evidential case for mental causation must run through statistical, behavioural, and instrumental traces, never through felt tags the confabulation face has already discredited. The perceptual reality-monitoring void locates its discriminator at the top-down/bottom-up interaction site the causal-interface void flags, and the tool apex keeps Minimal Quantum Interaction "deliberately thin" — a framework-internal reading, "never as a result the linguistic evidence delivers". This is how to hold an interface theory without over-drawing on it.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

First-person structure is treated as data throughout, with unusual resolution. The five modes of linguistic failure are qualitatively distinct phenomenological findings — "The diversity is the datum" — each diagnosing a different relation between conscious content and linguistic form. The edge-phenomenology signatures are precise enough to be diagnostic: *revealed unmarkedness* ("*I was sure, and I was wrong, and the surety carried no internal hedge*"), *felt vacancy* in derealization, *having-had-and-lost* for presence-type voids. And the anendophasia article ends on the most Nagelian sentence in the wing: nothing in the behavioural or sampling literature "tells us what it is like to be an anendophasic person, or whether anything is" — the what-it-is-like question held open precisely because the measures underdetermine it.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

Reconstruction-not-readout is a process-philosophical finding. "Consciousness encounters its own thoughts already finished." — verdicts as *arrivals*, experience as constructive synthesis rather than a substance inspecting its own states, is the picture a process metaphysics would draw, and the wing draws it from Nisbett-Wilson, Schnider, and Perky rather than from speculation. Note also what the tool apex's "user" is *not*: the user is characterised entirely by the structured pattern of the tool's failures — dissociation, directional dependence, shaped limits — never as a homuncular spectator, which keeps the view clear of the crude substance dualism the constitution theorists like to attack. Per this persona's standing constraint, none of this praise upgrades any evidential tier, and the one passage where saturated-presence resonance threatens to become evidence (`presence-type-and-absence-type-voids`, Dualism tenet section) is flagged below as a calibration concern, not praised.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

The wing contributes one of the Map's best ordinary-language exhibits of agency: the felt effort of the search for words. "An epiphenomenal consciousness could at most watch itself failing to speak; it could not try." What makes the exhibit strong is that it survives the wing's own introspection-scepticism — it does not rest on a feeling of authorship (which the confabulation face has discredited as evidence) but on the *structure of the activity*: candidate generation, evaluation against an unexternalisable standard, rejection, retry. The hub is equally careful in the other direction: it explicitly relocates the evidential case for mental causation away from phenomenal tags, so the agency claim is never built on the introspective signals the cluster has shown to be reconstructed.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

This wing is the most systematic map of cognitive closure the corpus holds, and it is disciplined about the claim's status. Bounded access to one's own substrate; a fifth-order metacognitive ceiling; an information-theoretic compression floor on report; a discriminator that "cannot be examined by the consciousness it constitutes". The presence/absence axis even gives closure a phenomenology: "absence-type limits feel like walls, presence-type limits feel like windows that close under inspection." Crucially, the closure claims stay empirical rather than dogmatic — the reality-monitoring void names the training-and-introspection findings that would "reduce the void to a contingent skill gap", and the hub names four operational falsifiers. Humility about what we can know, held falsifiably: the mysterian position at its best.

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

This persona's verdict is load-bearing for the wing, since every article trades in first-person evidence and two carry tenet-load-bearing claims. Four of five articles pass at the highest standard in the corpus:

- **`inner-speech-and-anendophasia` is the wing's exemplar and among the best-calibrated articles on the site.** It adjudicates a live dispute by adopting the weakest defensible register ("The case is open but unsettled, and the honest modal is suggestive rather than demonstrative."); it reports the coiners' concession and marks both follow-up replies as unrefereed preprints; it moves its argumentative weight onto the sturdier evidence (unsymbolized thinking) so nothing depends on the contested zero — "the honest position is that the argument does not need the contested claim"; it applies introspection-scepticism symmetrically ("the Map should apply its scepticism symmetrically rather than only where the answer is inconvenient"); and it reports the datum that cuts against its own tidy story: "That asymmetry is inconvenient for a tidy story about phenomenology floating free of function, and it is what the data say." It even names the limit of its own best evidence: sampling "has narrowed the question being asked rather than escaped the first-person predicament".
- **Both apexes discount their own convergence.** The hub: "Coherence is not treated as ampliative beyond the load-bearing patient-population evidence." The tool apex runs the common-cause null on its own five faces — they share machinery and were selected by the same lens, "so their agreement raises the explanatory bar for rivals without constituting independent confirmation" — and closes with an Evidence and Dependency section that names which component survives framework collapse. Tenet-as-evidence-upgrade is praise-worthily *not* done, twice, in the two places the temptation is strongest.
- **`presence-type-and-absence-type-voids` deflates its own cross-tradition roster** — Otto read James, so the numinous and the noetic quality are "genealogically linked rather than independent" — and concludes: "Two surviving features is a far smaller result than a roster of converging traditions would suggest — but it is a result, and the smaller claim is the one this method actually supports." Its Bidirectional Interaction paragraph is model calibration: labelled speculative, with named evidence that would tilt it. **But its Dualism and Occam tenet paragraphs run a register the body has not earned** — see the calibration concern below. One article, two paragraphs; the rest of the wing is clean.

## Calibration Concern: Tenet Sections Assert What the Body Brackets

**File**: `obsidian/concepts/presence-type-and-absence-type-voids.md`. This is a Process-Philosopher/Hardline-Empiricist conflict of exactly the kind the review method is designed to surface, so it is flagged as a concern, not a praise, and a refine-draft task is minted (the wing's only task).

The article's own §The Heterophenomenological Challenge (L116–118) states the deflationary rival fairly — presence-type reports as "confabulated retrospective claims about a transient emotional signal", no categorial excess needed — and stakes the classificatory claim on the weaker ground that survives either way: "*whatever* the eventual ontology, voids of these two shapes produce predictably different phenomenological reports". That is the right register, and the classification genuinely does survive either way.

Two tenet paragraphs then quietly convert the bracketed question into a settled datum:

1. **L124 (Dualism)**: "presence-type voids reflect excess over any functional description" and "The fact that music can deliver felt significance that exceeds every acoustic and neural account" — asserted flatly. On the deflationary reading the article itself grants as live, there is no such fact; the excess-over-any-account is precisely what is in dispute. "The Map interprets this as evidence…" is at least marked as interpretation, but its antecedent is not.
2. **L126 (Occam's Razor Has Limits)**: "Presence-type voids resist such readings because the excess is itself the datum. A simpler theory that predicts less felt significance would not thereby explain the felt significance that occurs; it would merely deny that the phenomenon exists." This mischaracterises the rival the article stated correctly ten lines earlier: the deflationist denies the *excess reading* of the felt significance, not that felt significance occurs.

The fix is small and the article contains its own template: re-key both paragraphs to the conditional register of the adjacent L128 Bidirectional Interaction paragraph (speculation labelled as speculation, tilting evidence named), or key them explicitly to the non-deflationary branch ("if the deflationary account is wrong…"), which §The Heterophenomenological Challenge already sets up. Nothing else in the article needs touching — the axis, the hybrid-case analysis, and the roster deflation are the article's strengths.
