## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

Block is the page Chalmers would recognise most quickly. It keeps phenomenal realism, Block's term, apart from the Map's stronger commitment. It separates the three grains of isomorph: superficial, psychofunctional, and his own fine-grained organisational one (L48). It refuses to run Data's conceivable blankness as a dualist argument except "through the contested modal-rationalist premise that conceivability guides possibility" (L127). The "Ground of rational belief" paragraph (L57) treats a technical epistemic term as technical. That is the discipline the hard-problem literature rarely gets from its allies.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

ER is the first Map page to look at the *worked empirical case* of the Schwartz, Stapp and Beauregard model, Ochsner et al.'s (2002) reappraisal experiment. It treats that case with respect but without partisanship. Stapp would value two things. One is the precise statement that in his model "the agent chooses and holds the question (von Neumann's Process 1) and leaves the outcome to nature" (L101). The other is that the example is placed under the clinical-neuroplasticity down-count rather than added as a new stream. Block's "A Ground on Paper Only" (L109) gives the same courtesy to a conditioned Born-statistics deviation: it states the deviation as a conception, then notes that the Map's own Born-preserving default "runs against" it.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

TI and Witness both keep the first-person record central while being plain about its reach. TI quotes survivors sparingly and without graphic detail ("I just became like a log of wood", L60). It also refuses to let the motor half's objective check stand in for the awareness half: "the closure is measured and the awareness beside it is not" (L78). Witness keeps every score in the reporting register, "Every questionnaire score is a score of *reported* attitude" (L35). It also holds weak Revelation only: a report that awareness presents as an observer "is a report of a presentation, real data that does not certify the observer's nature" (L106). Nagel would see both pages taking the question "what was it like then?" seriously enough to say what would answer it.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

ER's "Is There a Before?" section (L81–L85) gives the constructionist continuum its due. If generation and regulation are one process, "where does control enter?" becomes a question about "a goal-directed influence [bearing] on a continuous construction" (L85). Precommitment treats the agent as extended in time, an earlier settling suspending a later one (L98). Witness reports Bernstein et al.'s view that observing "creates a distinction" (L106). All three are process-friendly framings. **Constraint honoured:** none of them is used to raise anything's evidential standing. ER declines the move explicitly ("the location is fit, not evidence", L37), Precommitment declines it as "The fit discriminates nothing" (L100), and Witness files Bernstein's view as "closer to constructivism than to the Map's reading".

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

Precommitment is the Kane page in the wing. It separates the two questions that free-will writing often runs together: whether the bound person is the binder, and whose verdict should govern (L70–L78). It shows the bound will is live: "Externalising the binding does not show that the decision to bind was idle, and the bound will stays causally live: Ulysses' later will moves his voice and limbs, and the device blocks only its route to the outcome" (L96). Its reading of Steward, "an agent using its earlier settling to suspend its later settling on one matter" (L98), moves the luck objection's contrastive question to the earlier time without claiming to answer it.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

Block is honest about closure in both directions. It reports Block's denial, against McGinn, that the knowledge "can be known now to be beyond our ken" (L69), and his "may be temporary" (L40). The guard against a permanent-void claim is held. TI ends with the design that would decide the awareness half and the ethics that limit it (L106–L110). Witness says the stimulation test is "specifiable today with one limb measured ad hoc" (L76). That is humility about what is known now, not a verdict about what is knowable.

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

This persona's verdict carries the most weight here, because TI touches animal TI and Block touches simple animals.

- **TI is the model of restraint.** "The taxonomic breadth alone makes immobility a poor marker of experience, so TI raises no animal's standing on the Map's sentience scale" (L88). The section turns the criterion on itself, so this is a risk, not a defect. Priority 4 adds the fence at +14.
- **ER and Precommitment** pair each placement with its physical twin ("a physical valuation architecture predicts the same timing record", ER L93; "A physical planner precommits equally well", Precommitment L38). ## What the Wing Shares

**Not a wing convergence: "fit, not evidence".** All five use it, but so does nearly every Map page written since September. It is house calibration and does not distinguish this wing.

**(1) The timing of control (ER, Precommitment). Real.** Both pages place any conscious contribution at an *earlier policy moment*, through the same self-control scheme. ER puts it at the goal, the identification and selection stages, and "pre-episode plans", which "are the self-control article's 'moments of policy'" (L91). An if-then plan keeps the override (ER L91, Precommitment L64); a binding device removes or prices it.
- Each page also narrows "earlier is better" in its own domain. Sheppes and Gross replaced the generic timing hypothesis for emotion (ER L67). The precommitment field record shows a cost of the *earliest* intervention: "many binders mis-size their own bonds" (L38). ER leaves the self-control asymmetry "unresolved" (L71), and Precommitment holds the evidence that bears on it.
- Seneca's three *motus* (ER L95) and Ulysses at the mast (Precommitment L36) have the same shape: a voluntary moment before a stage "beyond our control".
- The pages share one research lineage (Gross → Duckworth, Gendler & Gross), yet link each other only through Precommitment's frontmatter.

**(2) Awareness known only through later report (TI, Witness). Real, and the strongest pairing.** The two pages describe one method problem in two fields:

|---|---|---|
| The field's main instrument does not ask the decisive question | the TIS "asks about detachment but not whether the event was perceived or remembered" (L36) | scales measure "reported relation to contents, reported reactivity and performance", not structure (L106); no validated session measure of non-reactivity was found (L72) |

**(3) The Map facing the predicament it diagnoses (Block). So the wing has a **gradient in how far a decisive test can reach**:
- Witness: an attitude profile, with one limb ad hoc.

Block also states the self-application most strongly ("The Map's sample, like the physicalist's, is us"). TI (L122) and Witness (L102) make the same move at a smaller scale.
