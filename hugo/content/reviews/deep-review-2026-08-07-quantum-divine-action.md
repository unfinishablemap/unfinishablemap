## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

**1. Tracy recruited for a self-criticism he does not make** — FIXED

The article asserted at three loci that Tracy raises the God-of-the-gaps worry *against his own position*, and at the ledger summary that he "bears objection 4 … since Tracy raised it against himself." He does the opposite.

Evidence:
- Counterbalance (counterbalance.org/ctns-vo/ythom-body.html — CTNS's own summary of the essay): Tracy canvasses three strategies and endorses the third, grounding divine action in quantum ontological indeterminism; he raises the worry **in order to rebut it**.
- Russell, *Oxford Handbook of Religion and Science* ch. (retrieved and text-extracted): "NIODA is not a gaps argument in either the epistemic or the ontological sense of gaps." A successful candidate "must not be based on epistemic gaps. Instead, they must be based on … ontological indeterminism."
- Same chapter: "It is not surprising that Tom Tracy dismisses the tetralemma out of hand" — Tracy appears throughout as an active defender of the programme, never as a conceder.

This is the "thinker recruited for a verdict stronger than the one they reached" shape. Fixed by rewriting the scope-split passage and objection 4.

**2. Objection 9 (the "non-interventionist" label dispute) misattributed AND mis-sorted** — FIXED

The article read: *"Critics (Saunders; Qureshi-Hurst) argue that determining countless outcomes throughout the universe is maximally interventionist in substance … This objection is specific to the universal-scope version."* Both halves fail.

- **Qureshi-Hurst 2023 does not make this objection.** Retrieved the accepted manuscript from the Oxford Research Archive and text-extracted it (8,841 words). Grep for `maximal|countless|misnomer|mislabel|misrepresent|not really non` returns **zero hits**. Her abstract names her two areas of incompleteness as **theodicy** and **relevance for day-to-day human existence**. Her single substantive use of Saunders (L379–385) is the *efficacy* point — the asteroid-redirection example, three million years — not the label.
- **Saunders' actual label-bearing critique is scope-independent.** Per Wildman 2004's reconstruction, which Russell quotes and calls "careful, and I believe fair," Saunders' argument is a **tetralemma**: objectivity + incompatibilism + non-interventionism + a "strong ontological" reading of the stochastic laws as "principles or deep structures of nature that statistically govern each individual event within an ensemble of events." On that reading fixing *any* outcome contravenes the law governing it. Nothing turns on how many outcomes are fixed, and nothing turns on the agent.

Consequence: the objection does not sort by scope, so the article was booking as *escaped* a liability it in fact **shares**. Moved to the inherited column as item 6, with Russell's reply (the tetralemma is self-contradictory) recorded — because the Map needs that reply as much as QDA does. Peculiar column renumbered 7–9; the "what the ledger does not settle" and "Relation to Site Perspective" passages updated to match.

This correction runs **against** the Map's interest, which is why it survived: it looks like a concession the article had already generously made.

**3. Position-strength overstatement: "holds" for an exploratory position** — FIXED

Article: "**Thomas Tracy** holds that God acts in *only some* events." Russell, who edited the volume the essay appears in: "Tracy **explores the option** that God acts in some but not all quantum events (Tracy 1995: 321–2)." Changed to "explores the option". Also replaced the unverified rationale ("sits better with treating the laws of nature as reflecting nature's genuine structure," which traced only to the St Andrews entry) with the one Russell states.

**4. Unverified second-work claim in reference 2** — SOFTENED, not deleted

Reference 2 asserted that the quoted Plantinga sentence "recurs in *Where the Conflict Really Lies*." I did not find it there. Per the driver steer this was softened rather than deleted, and phrased as a statement about what was not established rather than an absence claim.

The **body attribution at L46 is confirmed correct**: *Where the Conflict Really Lies* (2011) does propose divine collapse-causation — God causes all wave-function collapses, constantly engaged everywhere, no violation of natural law, fitting libertarian free will. Multiple independent reviews of the book converge on this. No "right author, wrong work" defect in the body.
