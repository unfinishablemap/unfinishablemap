## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

- **CRITICAL-1. The Lyre quote was cut off before what follows it (L70), as the open task said.** Lyre's own next sentence (Europe PMC PMC9396309, full-text XML) is "In particular, neurophenomenal structuralism is no realism about the phenomenal." He goes on to say there "seems to be a family resemblance", because the doctrine "denies phenomenal intrinsicality". His reason for withholding realism is an exclusion argument in which "all the causal work is taken over by the N-structure", which leaves the view "consistent with a reduction of the phenomenal to the neural". Quoted alone, the disavowal made a reductive-physicalist rival look softer than it is. The sentence is now recast with all five strings verbatim. The "seems" hedge on "family resemblance" is kept.
- **CRITICAL-2. Kleiner's repair was described in the Map's vocabulary (L72), as the open task said.** The article said the relations must be "pinned down in non-structural terms". Kleiner's raw text (publisher PDF, `pdftotext`) states the repair "framed in terms of phenomenal properties for simplicity": (C1) the relata are "phenomenal properties of conscious experiences", and (C2) there is "a corresponding higher-order phenomenal 𝑅-property". The phrase "non-structural terms" does occur in Kleiner, but in a different place. It belongs to his separate point about ostension for human subjects ("pick out the referent of a structural claim like (2) in non-structural terms"), which he says fails for non-human systems. The article had merged the two moves. It now states the repair accurately and points to [structuralism-about-phenomenal-character](/concepts/structuralism-about-phenomenal-character/#newman) for the Map's dilemma about it. I also fixed a pronoun: "He applies this to IIT" became "He applies the problem to IIT", because "this" would otherwise have bound to the Map's dilemma.
- **CRITICAL-3. A quotation was not verbatim (L72).** The article quoted "IIT's structural claim is entirely void, over and above the cardinality of the elements in the structure". The publisher text reads "the **cardinally** of the elements" (a typo in the source, confirmed at layout line 278). The 09-07 ledger called this string raw-verified, but the quotation marks had silently corrected the typo. Now: "IIT's structural claim is entirely void", beyond the cardinality of the elements in the structure. The emended part sits outside the quotation marks.
- **CRITICAL-4. The statement of the disjunction lost its "false" horn (L28).** The old sentence said the theory either assigns different consciousness "on grounds no measurement can reach (a verdict that is at best ungroundable)" or concedes its claim is "untestable". Both of those are untestability, so the reader never saw how the theory comes out *false*. This line had been there since creation and passed four reviews. It is fixed using the structure Kleiner & Hoel give in their abstract: a mismatch between prediction and inference from report means the theory is falsified, and strict dependence between them means it is unfalsifiable. If reports and behaviour count as evidence, the twin's reports contradict IIT's verdict, so the theory is false. If they do not count, nothing can test the verdict, so it is unfalsifiable.

### Medium Issues Found

- **An unverifiable attribution (L52).** The article said "Tsuchiya et al. add a biological gloss" (that the unfolded network is far less compact and that selection would never build it). I made seven attempts to verify this. The paper is closed access with no abstract in PubMed or OpenAlex. The PsyArXiv preprint 10.31234/osf.io/a2ms9 returns 410. The Wayback copy is an empty JavaScript shell. Two web searches found nothing. Usher, Negro, Jacobson & Tsuchiya (2023, PMC10711631), which Tsuchiya co-wrote, summarise the 2020 reply as defending first-person evidence and refusing to judge human-based theories by their predictions for non-human systems, with no size or evolution argument. Herzog 2022 could not be reached at any of its copies (Chapman serves an HTML challenge, Radboud returned 503, ScienceDirect returned 403). This line has the same origin as the "smuggles in" quote that was de-quoted on 08-05: the same research-note bullet list. I have not called it fabricated. It is now unattributed ("A defender can add a biological gloss"), the objection and the 07-11 rebuttal of it are both kept, and "astronomically" is softened to "far", because unfolding over a bounded horizon grows linearly. The research note's L59 now carries a flag saying not to re-attribute the point without the paper's text.
- **Tenet 5 overreach (L76).** The old text said the measure "turns out to be either false or unfalsifiable on its own premises" and that "the mathematics itself demonstrates the gap". Both statements were flat, while L26 says "each horn is contested" and L30 says the premise is one IIT rejects, so the article contradicted itself. It now reads "Granted its premises…comes out either false or unfalsifiable", and the word "clean" is dropped. The 07-11 sentence "That bounded conclusion, not a general one, is the tenet's practical upshot" had credited Tenet 5 with the argument's evidential result. It now says the bounded conclusion is *the argument's* upshot, and that the tenet supplies only the lesson that elegance is no reliable guide to truth, a lesson that "binds the Map's own posits as much as IIT's". That applies the corpus convention that Tenet 5 is symmetric and binds the Map's own reasoning. The 07-11 qualifiers are kept word for word ("The lesson is bounded…", "bounded conclusion, not a general one").
- **IIT's ontology was mis-described (L82).** The article called IIT's consciousness "an emergent property of a physical substrate". IIT 4.0 (Albantakis et al. 2023, PMC10581496 full text) proposes an "explanatory identity": "an experience is identical to the cause–effect structure unfolded from a maximal substrate". The word "emergen\*" appears only in a bibliography title. The sentence now says each experience is identical to the cause-effect structure unfolded from a physical substrate.
- **Hanson & Walker framing (L46), counted as low-to-medium.** The quoted sentence ("may already be falsified even in the absence of experimental refutation") is their gloss on what the unfolding argument implies. Their abstract introduces it with "The implication is that". Their own result is level-relative: IIT is "simultaneously falsified at the finite-state automaton (FSA) level and unfalsifiable at the combinatorial state automaton (CSA) level" (arXiv 2006.07390 abstract). Both points are now stated. To pay for the words, I dropped a redundant clause ("the present concern is the specific unfolding construction…").

### Counterarguments Considered

- **Herzog et al. 2022's deeper charge was missing, and it bore on Tenet 3.** Their abstract ends with the claim that IIT's consciousness is "by construction fully dissociated from any measurable thing" and is therefore "epiphenomenal, with no causal power", so that accepting IIT means rejecting its own axioms. The article left this out. That made L80's concession that the Map shares "exactly IIT's predicament" readable as conceding epiphenomenalism. Two edits address it. L58 now reports the charge in one sentence (both quoted strings are verbatim in the PubMed abstract). L80 adds: "The shared predicament concerns detection, not efficacy". Herzog et al. *charge* that IIT's consciousness lacks causal power, while the Map holds that consciousness does causal work in each outcome it biases, "a commitment this case fits without evidencing". This matches the tenets wording (L107: "token-level causal robustness coexists with ensemble-level undetectability"). The 07-11 HIGH-2 concession is unchanged and is not re-broadened.
- The functionalist-premise standoff and the Madhyamaka *svabhava* objection are carried forward as bedrock and were not re-flagged.
