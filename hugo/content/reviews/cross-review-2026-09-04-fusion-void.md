## Critical Issues Found

### 1. False corpus-wide superlative (four loci, plus the upstream research note)

The article asserted: *"the void's boundary has been measured from outside, **which no other void in the Map's catalogue can say**."* This is a corpus-wide negative and it is refuted by [the erasure void](/voids/erasure-void/), which measures its own boundary from outside at population scale:

- Croy et al. 2012 — congenitally anosmic adults are "mostly unaware of the olfactory deficit as children."
- Adams et al. 2017 — 74.2% of a US cohort with *measured* olfactory dysfunction did not recognise it.
- The article's own gloss: *"the third-person instrument is strictly superior to the first-person channel for a fact about one's own consciousness. Standardised psychophysical testing detects in minutes what a lifetime of introspection cannot."*
- And it draws a boundary the same way fusion does: sudden post-viral anosmia *is* noticed, because "the comparator survives the loss and does the announcing." Gradual/congenital loss is not. That is a boundary, measured from outside.

[mapping-mind-space](/voids/mapping-mind-space/) is a second, weaker counterexample: "hidden dimensions can still be probed indirectly through psychophysics, neural decoding, and comparative ethology, which is why the void is unexplorable rather than wholly sealed."

Note the article's *own body* already carried the correctly-calibrated form at the taxonomy paragraph — "what makes this void unusual" — so the defect was an internal inconsistency as well as a false claim. The lead and the navigation surfaces asserted a *first*; the body said *unusual*.

**Loci found and fixed** (the claim had propagated before any reviewer saw it):
1. `obsidian/voids/fusion-void.md` frontmatter `description` — "The catalogue's first void whose boundary has been psychophysically measured."
2. `obsidian/voids/fusion-void.md` lead paragraph — "which no other void in the Map's catalogue can say".
3. `obsidian/voids/voids.md:196` — catalogue Specific Voids entry.
4. `obsidian/voids/voids.md:272` — research-stage entry.
5. `obsidian/research/voids-fusion-void-2026-09-02.md` — *the upstream source of the claim*, in its Potential Article Angles section. Corrected in place with a note naming the counterexample, so a future expand/replenish pass reading the note cannot re-mint the superlative.

**Resolution**: replaced with the defensible and still-distinguishing claim — *"the void's boundary is itself a measured quantity, which is rare"*. What fusion genuinely owns is that a single experiment (Hillis et al. 2002, within- vs between-modality metamers) both establishes the limit and locates its edge. Erasure's psychophysics quantifies how often the void obtains; Hillis measures where it stops. The distinguishing feature that justified the standalone disposition survives, correctly scoped.

### 2. Claim attributed to the Map that the owning article disclaims

The Dualism paragraph asserted: *"they belong on the brain side of any capability division, **and the Map says so plainly**."*

[capability-division-problem](/voids/capability-division-problem/) — the article that owns this calibration — says the opposite in its own §"Why the Division Resists Specification":

- *"Every candidate for pure brain-side processing becomes disputable"* (Phillips 2021 on blindsight retaining a degraded conscious residue).
- Its candidate transition point is **perceptual organisation**: *"grouping, figure-ground segregation, and binding into coherent objects mark the transition to conscious seeing"* (Ludwig 2023), with *"Consciousness may enter vision at the level of unified percepts, not individual features."*

Cue combination produces unified percepts from component estimates. The fusion void planted a "firmly, plainly brain-side" flag in the exact neighbourhood its host article says resists assignment. Nothing in the Map says this plainly.

**Resolution**: "sits **firmly** on the brain side" → "sits on the brain side" (lead); "and the Map says so plainly" → "an assignment the Map holds open to dispute rather than settled" (Dualism section). The two pages now agree.

### 3. Over-concession running against the Map

*"quantitatively modelled with no remainder resisting computational treatment"* is a stronger concession than the article needs or than the adjacent sentences support — the very next lines name a remainder ("it does not touch why the combined content is *experienced* as one"). It also runs against [capability-division-problem](/voids/capability-division-problem/)'s "consciousness may transform the character of all visual processing it touches."

**Resolution**: → "with no *computational* remainder". Same concession, correctly scoped to the modelling, and it stops asserting there is no remainder at all.
