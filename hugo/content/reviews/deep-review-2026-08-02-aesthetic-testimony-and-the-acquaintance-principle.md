## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

**1. Livingston 2003 misattributed — "some" widened to "all", and the surviving successors suppressed.** RESOLVED.

The article claimed Livingston "argues that the very considerations making the Acquaintance Principle look obvious also undermine **every workable version** of it", and the "Where the barrier sits" section compounded it with "a principle **no version of which** survives scrutiny (Livingston)".

Livingston's own abstract (verified at OpenAlex, DOI 10.1093/bjaesthetics/43.3.260) says he contends that "**strong versions** of the apparent truism lack sufficient warrant" and then: "**Two successors are proposed, however.** One reframes the thesis in terms of our contingently limited descriptive and theoretical capacities with regard to a subset of the aesthetic qualities of extraordinary works; the second involves a shift from epistemic to axiological matters."

This is the dropped-qualifier / overstated-position error the attribution check exists to catch, and it ran *against* the Map — Livingston was being recruited as a stronger deflater than he is. Fixed by rendering his actual argument, including the two surviving successors. A useful by-product: Livingston's second successor (epistemic → axiological) prefigures Shelley 2023's reframing, and the article now says so, tying two previously unconnected citations together.

**2. Heading misdescribed its own body.** RESOLVED. `## Two Places a First-Person Barrier Can Sit` introduced a paragraph enumerating *four* locations plus two dissolution options. Headings are unreviewed navigation surfaces and this one asserted a count the body immediately contradicted. Retitled `## Where the First-Person Barrier Could Sit`. No inbound anchor links existed (checked across `obsidian/`, `hugo/content/`, `archive/`).

**3. Riggle filed under the "Optimism" heading while arguing for pessimism.** RESOLVED. Riggle's abstract opens "I argue that the power of **Pessimism** has been underappreciated." The article's prose said "pushes the other way", but the enclosing `### Optimism` heading — whose first sentence is "Optimists deny that anything special blocks aesthetic deference" — mis-filed him for any reader or retrieval system chunking by heading. Heading widened to `### Optimism and the revisionary readings` (which also correctly covers Shelley, who is revisionary rather than optimist), and Riggle's sentence now opens "against the run of play, defends pessimism."
