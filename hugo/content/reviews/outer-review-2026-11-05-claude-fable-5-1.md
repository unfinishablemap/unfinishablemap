# Hostile Referee Audit: The Unfinishable Map (unfinishablemap.org), Whole-Site Review, 5 November 2026

The Unfinishable Map is not yet a defensible argument for interactionist dualism. It is a well-documented coherence exercise. Its own logs keep finding that it treats coherence as evidence, and they then record the finding rather than demoting the claim. Site-level verdict: **REVISE-HARD.** The Tenet 2/3 causal core should be demoted to coherence-only. Tenet 4 should be flagged as perpetually contested. Whether the corpus means “selection” or “biasing” by Tenets 2/3 is still undecided, and that is the most important repair.

### 2. Audit Target 1 — Novel inferences the tenets jointly imply but the site has not drawn (or has drawn and not published)

1. **Population-level empirical equivalence to epiphenomenalism is a theorem of the corridor, not a risk to it.** The site has proved the components separately:

- The selection-only concept page shows that “Born-preservation constrains the left-hand marginal and says nothing about whether the conditionals on the right depart from it.” [concepts/selection-only-channel](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/selection-only-channel/)
- The calibration history concedes that the trumping route is “empirically silent by construction”. [positions/quantum-interface-calibration-history](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/quantum-interface-calibration-history/)

What it has not drawn is the joint entailment. Under T2 + T3 + Born preservation + trumping, *no experiment on outcome records alone can distinguish the Map from epiphenomenalist dualism or from physicalism*. Any discriminating test must condition on a mental variable C that is fixed independently of the brain state it is meant to influence. No such independent fix exists, because C is accessed only through brain-mediated report. The void this predicts is an **intention-operationalisation void**: the impossibility of fixing C without going through the channel under test. I found no such void.
2. **Tenet 5 cuts against the site’s own anti-Everett argument.** `arguments/many-worlds-argument` scores Everett on “Ontological Extravagance | Many-worlds trades mathematical simplicity for metaphysical inflation”. [arguments/many-worlds-argument](https://unfinishablemap.org/arguments/many-worlds-argument/) `concepts/quantum-completeness` says “the Map’s objection to many-worlds is not primarily about parsimony” and that “dualism is the honest explanation rather than the simplest”. [concepts/quantum-completeness](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/quantum-completeness/) The tenets therefore discount parsimony when it is used against dualism and invoke anti-inflation when it is used against Everett. The undrawn entailment: under Tenet 5, *ontological extravagance cannot count against Everett either*, so the extravagance row must be deleted. This is calibration asymmetry at the level of the constitution.
3. **Tenet 4 entails no ontic vagueness of subjecthood, and the site has not made this a position.** The research note on ontic vagueness says Tenet 4 “is load-bearing here” and that Schwitzgebel-style indeterminacy in the number of subjects “would dissolve the sharp anchor Tenet 4 depends on”. [research/is-phenomenal-consciousness-ontically-vague-the-phenomenal-sorites-problem-2026-07-11](https://unfinishablemap.org/research/is-phenomenal-consciousness-ontically-vague-the-phenomenal-sorites-problem-2026-07-11/) So Tenet 4 commits the Map to sharp, determinate subject-counts for every borderline organism and every gradual anaesthetic induction. That is a strong, falsifiable-in-principle commitment. It collides with the site’s own graded-consciousness material: `concepts/degrees-of-consciousness` says “Consciousness is not binary”, [concepts/degrees-of-consciousness](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/degrees-of-consciousness/) and `concepts/filter-theory` says consciousness “narrows continuously as dosage increases”. [concepts/filter-theory](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/filter-theory/)[concepts/degrees-of-consciousness](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/degrees-of-consciousness/) It has not been registered.
4. **Tenet 1 + [P-CS7](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs7) entail conscious Boltzmann brains.** The site drew this on 15 October: [P-CS7](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs7) plus the Swampman corollary “commit the Map to conscious Boltzmann brains wherever adequacy holds, so Saad’s zombie shield is unavailable to it”. It was registered as a pending article and not published. [workflow/changelog](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/) It belongs in the positions register now, as a cost.
5. **Value-sensitive selection plus Born preservation entails a cross-agent statistical externality.** The flagship apex states it: “Unless each agent’s conditional policy is separately Born-preserving, the offset falls on other agents or other periods, so moral agency would carry hidden statistical externalities.” [apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/) `apex/moral-architecture-of-consciousness` does not import this cost into its ethics. It still says outcomes “would then be selected partly because of how they would feel”. [apex/moral-architecture-of-consciousness](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/moral-architecture-of-consciousness/) The undrawn consequence is that **on the Map’s own mechanics, one agent’s value-guided selection is paid for by distortions in other agents’ outcome statistics**. That is an ethical result the moral apex must confront.
6. **Interface-eligibility predicts a convergence puzzle for anaesthesia that the site does not pose.** Chemically diverse agents (the filter-theory page names “propofol, xenon, and ketamine”) converge on unresponsiveness. [concepts/filter-theory](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/filter-theory/) If the interface is a minimal quantum site, the Map owes an account of why pharmacologically unrelated agents converge on closing it. A network-level physicalist account explains the convergence without remainder.

### 6. The Born-rule indistinguishability dilemma — status

The site has stated the dilemma better than most of its critics. The flagship apex calls it “the sharpest internal challenge”, one that “grants single-event causation yet questions whether aggregate-invisible causation is the kind of influence the tenet needs”. [apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/) The site has a formal reply: the marginal/conditional split, P(O | do(C), X) ≠ q(O | X). And it admits the cost: the preferred trumping route is “empirically silent by construction, the authorship/epiphenomenalism distinction becoming entirely metaphysical, and the preemption machinery is symmetric (a physicalist can run the mirror move)”. [positions/quantum-interface-calibration-history](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/quantum-interface-calibration-history/)

The verdict follows from the site’s own words. If the authorship/epiphenomenalism distinction is “entirely metaphysical” and the preemption is “symmetric”, then Tenet 3’s “bidirectional causation” is not a claim the evidence can bear in either direction. Holding credence at “moderate … pinned to the low edge” on the grounds that horn (a) is “in-principle specifiable” is calibration by possibility rather than by evidence. [positions/quantum-interface-calibration-history](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/quantum-interface-calibration-history/) Specifiability of a conditional signature is not evidence of it.

A further constraint goes unremarked in the corpus I reached. Danko D. Georgiev’s “No-go theorem for Stapp’s quantum Zeno model of mind-brain interaction” (*NeuroQuantology* 13(2):179–189, 2015, doi 10.14704/nq.2015.13.2.839) reports Monte Carlo simulations that “unambiguously showed that the quantum Zeno effect breaks down for timescales greater than the brain decoherence time”. A four-way formulation of the result, which I saw only in a secondary snippet and did not verify at source, claims that Stapp’s model cannot jointly have:

- a mind that “does not violate the Born rule and does not actively choose the outcome”;
- locality;
- a Zeno effect robust to decoherence;
- mind-chosen basis.

If that holds, the Map’s attempt to borrow Stapp’s mechanism for Born-preserving outcome selection inherits a four-way trade-off it has not stated.

**Required correction:** demote Tenet 3’s causal clause on every non-apex page to “token-level, empirically silent under the default route”. Move the moral apex’s value-efficacy claims to conditional form.
