## Executive Summary

This is the best-calibrated wing I have read on the Map. Its centre of gravity is [phenomenal-conservatism](/concepts/phenomenal-conservatism/)'s boundary sentence — PC "earns phenomenal reports a seat at the evidential table without settling what they ultimately show" — and the wing honours that boundary from four directions: presentation absorbs Sellars by refusing the epistemic reading of the given; functional seeming and PC are explicitly written as two sides of one collision and neither pretends to have won it; objectivity separates the thin claim (perspective cannot be eliminated) from idealism it does not need. The two persona counterweights (Whitehead / Birch) converge in praising the same passages, which is the review's own test for honest calibration.

The opportunities are structural rather than remedial. **Two concepts the wing leans on have no page**: *cognitive penetration* (named in six live articles, Siegel cited in 29, adjudicated nowhere — and the Map's own contemplative-training claim in [phenomenal-depth](/concepts/phenomenal-depth/) is a cognitive-penetration case the wing never tests as one) and *the nature of a seeming* (both PC and functional seeming say the other "joins this disagreement from the other side"; nothing joins it in the middle, and the only page that could, [phenomenal-authority-and-first-person-evidence](/topics/phenomenal-authority-and-first-person-evidence/), is 522 words over hard). **One live contradiction**: [objectivity-and-consciousness](/concepts/objectivity-and-consciousness/) L117 says the problem of other minds "is dissolved" by second-person primacy; [the-second-person-evidential-channel](/topics/the-second-person-evidential-channel/) (2026-09-14) explicitly "declines the dissolution", and objectivity's own L73 agrees with the newer article. **One near-orphan**: [phenomenal-depth](/concepts/phenomenal-depth/) has two body inbound links corpus-wide and does not reciprocate the sibling that defines itself against it.

## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

[phenomenal-presentation](/concepts/phenomenal-presentation/) states the datum in its most minimal form and then refuses to let the datum do more than it can: "One can accept that all *justification* is conceptually structured (Sellars's point) and still hold that the warmth is felt before any concept classifies it (presentation's point)." That is the explanatory gap kept clean of the Myth of the Given — the objection most often used to wave the gap away. [phenomenal-depth](/concepts/phenomenal-depth/) then widens the explanandum honestly: "there is more to explain than a binary presence-or-absence of consciousness." Chalmers would also approve the wing's refusal to let [functional-seeming](/concepts/functional-seeming/)'s regress reply stand as a refutation — the self-representation section isolates "a deeper problem about self-representation specifically" rather than re-running the regress Frankish has answered.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

[objectivity-and-consciousness](/concepts/objectivity-and-consciousness/) is the wing's only quantum contact and it is correctly modest: the measurement-problem section is labelled "a minority position in physics" and the article's own conclusion is limited to "the interface between consciousness and physics may be where objectivity reaches its limit." Stapp would want the von Neumann process-1 point made sharper, but he would prefer this hedge to an overclaim, because it keeps the objectivity argument from depending on any particular interpretation.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

The objectivity article is Nagel's own argument, and its best line is its own: "Consciousness *is* subjective viewpoint. Applying objective methods to consciousness eliminates the subject matter." The three-perspective section (first/second/third) is the most accessible statement on the Map of why no single method suffices — "First-person alone leads to solipsism ... Third-person alone misses phenomenal character ... Second-person ... depends on prior first-person access." Presentation's Husserlian *passive synthesis* framing gives the phenomenological tradition a positive role rather than a decorative one.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

[phenomenal-depth](/concepts/phenomenal-depth/)'s gradient — "depth is not an all-or-nothing property but something that varies with the complexity and integration of conscious states" — and presentation's "floor of consciousness" both read as process-friendly: experience with internal articulation that thins toward a minimal case rather than switching off. Whitehead would praise the wing for never treating the subject as a substance that *has* experiences; presentation's "experience as undergone rather than performed" is closer to prehension than to Cartesian inspection. **Constraint honoured**: depth's panpsychism sentence is conditional ("*if* even very simple organisms have some phenomenal depth, this lends partial support to panpsychist intuitions, though the Map does not commit to panpsychism") — process-resonance is offered as compatibility, not as a tier upgrade for any organism. Nothing here to flag.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

[phenomenal-conservatism](/concepts/phenomenal-conservatism/)'s agency paragraph is the wing's most disciplined moment and Kane would recognise it as protecting his own position from cheap support: a seeming "does not underwrite the further claim that this phenomenology accurately represents non-physical downward causation ... bidirectional interaction is a framework-level commitment, not a directly introspectible datum." The felt activity of choosing is admitted as evidence about the phenomenology of agency, never as evidence for agent-causation — which is exactly the separation a libertarian needs if the view is to survive the charge of introspective wishful thinking.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

Depth supplies McGinn with a concrete mechanism for closure that the [mysterianism](/concepts/mysterianism/) page states only abstractly: "consciousness may have more internal structure than any mind can fully articulate about itself." Objectivity reads McGinn correctly — "not because the connection is supernatural but because our cognitive architecture cannot form the relevant concepts" — and presentation's "the last thing that could be subtracted while leaving experience at all" is the right shape for a limit claim: it names a floor without claiming to see beneath it.

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

This persona's verdict is load-bearing for the review because the wing *is* the Map's evidential register, and the verdict is largely favourable.

**Praised restraint.** [phenomenal-conservatism](/concepts/phenomenal-conservatism/) borrows only the weak sufficiency claim ("should not be read as endorsing the stronger necessity thesis or its inferential extensions"), keeps propositional / doxastic / credal justification apart, and states the cost of the "pain hurts" paradigm without flinching: "The invulnerability of the thin seeming does not transfer to the thick one, and the argument must not let it silently do so." The Loar paragraph concedes that a physicalist "can accept PC in full" — the article gives away the point that a weaker page would have kept. [functional-seeming](/concepts/functional-seeming/)'s zombie-convergence section ends "Whether this counts as explanatory progress or explanatory bankruptcy depends on prior commitments about whether there is something to explain beyond the functional facts" — a bedrock disagreement labelled as bedrock. [phenomenal-overflow](/concepts/phenomenal-overflow/) (spine) already says overflow is "not ... established fact." No five-tier label is misused anywhere in the wing.

**Calibration concern 1 — [phenomenal-depth](/concepts/phenomenal-depth/) L239 (definition paragraph).** "Each layer was present in the original experience, not added by closer attention." This is the *contested* claim, stated as the datum. The sibling [phenomenal-overflow](/concepts/phenomenal-overflow/) L73 names the objection by name — the "**Inflation fallacy**: Subjects mistake the *ability* to access any row ... for *actually having accessed* all rows" — and depth has zero hits for *inflat*, *Dennett*, *Cohen* or *constructiv* (measured this run). The 2026-06-25 stability note asks that the Dennettian/constructivist objection "continue not to be re-flagged as critical", and I do not: the point is not that the objection refutes depth but that the definition should not pre-empt it. A one-clause hedge with a piped link to overflow's inflation section installs the acknowledgment at near-zero cost in an article 1186 words under soft. **Whitehead and Birch converge** here: both can praise the gradient once the definition stops asserting what the gradient argument is meant to earn. Refine, not expand.

**Calibration concern 2 — [objectivity-and-consciousness](/concepts/objectivity-and-consciousness/) L117.** "**Implication**: The problem of other minds is dissolved by recognizing that second-person experience (mutual recognition) is primary." Three things are wrong with the word *dissolved* and none is bedrock: (a) [the-second-person-evidential-channel](/topics/the-second-person-evidential-channel/) L45, written 2026-09-14, states the Map's position on precisely this — "Reddy takes engagement to *dissolve* the problem of other minds; the Map keeps her evidence and declines the dissolution" — and its description line reads "other minds stay open"; (b) objectivity's own L73 says the second-person perspective "depends on prior first-person access (you must have experience to recognize it in others)", which is the *opposite* of a dissolution; (c) [problem-of-other-minds](/concepts/problem-of-other-minds/) L170 uses "relocates rather than dissolves" as the Map's own template for this move. The two articles do not link each other (0 hits each way). The article is 53 words over soft, so the fix is a same-length rewrite of L117 carrying a piped link to the newer article. This is a `refine-draft`, minted below at P2 because it is a live contradiction with a position the Map "holds the line" on, not a matter of taste.

**Minor — [functional-seeming](/concepts/functional-seeming/) L331.** "If functional seeming were the whole story, Mary would learn nothing—she already knew every functional fact." The sibling PC page (L95) already concedes the Loar/phenomenal-concept reply (Mary gains a concept under a new mode of presentation, not a new fact); functional seeming states the Mary point as if that reply did not exist. One subordinate clause with a link to [phenomenal-concepts-strategy](/concepts/phenomenal-concepts-strategy/) would bring the two siblings to the same strength. Cross-link table, not a task.

## Content Strengths

### phenomenal-conservatism.md
- **Strongest point**: the three-way separation of what PC secures — evidential base — from what it does not — the metaphysical conclusion, and the causal-power claim.
- **Notable quote**: "the principle earns phenomenal reports a seat at the evidential table without settling what they ultimately show."
- **Why it works**: the article gives the opponent the strongest position available (a physicalist who accepts PC *in full*) and then locates the real disagreement downstream ("about what an admitted seeming shows"). Its objection map is genuinely two-sided — eight objection families each with a live reply — and its correction of common mislabellings (Tucker as defender, McCain/Moretti as the *source* of the Bayesian reconciliation) is the kind of secondary-literature hygiene an LLM reader will otherwise get wrong. Style: no cliché constructs, no "load-bearing" (0 hits).

### phenomenal-presentation.md
- **Strongest point**: the four "Versus" distinctions (acquaintance / depth / overflow / active character) — a small taxonomy that other pages can inherit.
- **Notable quote**: "Presentation is what overflow is overflow *of*."
- **Why it works**: it isolates the smallest claim that still resists reduction ("every functional story explains a doing, while presentation is an undergoing") and then defuses Sellars without conceding him: the given is phenomenal, not epistemic, and acquaintance carries the epistemology. The Bidirectional section is an abductive argument from report-tracking, explicitly hedged — the 2026-07-27 review already ruled it not slippage and I agree.

### functional-seeming.md
- **Strongest point**: the Vantage-Point and Self-Representation sections, which press illusionism where it is weakest without re-running the regress Frankish has already answered.
- **Notable quote**: "Unlike combustion—which is indifferent to being called 'phlogiston'—a mental state that represents itself as phenomenal is not clearly separable from a state that *is* phenomenal."
- **Why it works**: the Epistemic-Seeming Mirror section (added 2026-07-21) is the wing's hinge — it states the Huemer/Frankish collision as a dilemma ("if functional seeming genuinely carries no phenomenal force, it is not a seeming in Huemer's justificatory sense ... if instead the illusionist restores felt force ... the regress ... reopens") and leaves the dilemma to the sibling. That deferral is the gap named under Expansion Opportunities.

### objectivity-and-consciousness.md
- **Strongest point**: the three-perspective section and the Husserlian claim that objectivity is *grounded in* intersubjectivity rather than opposed to it.
- **Notable quote**: "not view from nowhere, but view from everywhere conscious."
- **Why it works**: it states the paradox as structural ("phenomenal properties are *defined* by how they appear from the first-person perspective") and then routes the reader to [consciousness-and-the-problem-of-measurement-standards](/topics/consciousness-and-the-problem-of-measurement-standards/) for the mechanism. The Why-Not-Idealism section is a model of the "modest realism" register: three short reasons, no overreach. Style residue from the Sonnet-4.5 era: two "This isn't X. It's Y." constructs (L43, L84) — fix only when the article is next opened for content.

### phenomenal-depth.md
- **Strongest point**: the breadth/depth distinction that gives [phenomenal-overflow](/concepts/phenomenal-overflow/) a partner concept, and the three-modality tour (perceptual / affective / cognitive) with the asymbolia dissociation as evidence that the affective layer is separable.
- **Notable quote**: "The gap is not merely between neural activity and the *existence* of experience but between neural activity and experience's *internal structure*."
- **Why it works**: it converts depth into an explanandum-multiplier for the explanatory gap and gives mysterianism a mechanism (inexhaustibility). Its Relation section correctly runs the epiphenomenalism dilemma in the Map's standard form. Weakness is integration, not content (see below).
