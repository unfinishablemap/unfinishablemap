## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

1. **Kant: the failure was assigned to the wrong faculty, and the quotation to the wrong subject.** The page said "the mind attempts totality and cannot" and "sublimity is defined by exceeding cognitive capacity", then "In both, the mind discovers what Kant calls 'a faculty of mind which surpasses every standard of Sense'". In Bernard's text (§26) the sentence is: "to be able only to think it as *a whole* indicates a faculty of mind which surpasses every standard of Sense", where "it" is the infinite. The faculty that surpasses sense is reason, which succeeds; what fails is imagination's comprehension in one intuition. The page had reversed Kant's moral (a void where Kant finds a vindication of reason), and "In both" extended a sentence about the mathematical sublime to the dynamical one, where Kant's wording is "a faculty of resistance of a quite different kind" (§28). **Fix**: failure assigned to imagination; quotation given its subject; Kant's own conclusion stated; the Map's use marked as taking the first half of his analysis and leaving the second. The quoted span is unchanged and verbatim. History: the archived `aesthetic-void` carried the §25 wording ("a faculty of the mind surpassing every standard of Sense") under "In both cases"; the 2026-11-11 repair swapped in the §26 string and left "In both" standing.
2. **McGinn presented as generalising Hume, with the Map's extension attributed to him.** "McGinn's cognitive closure generalises: the space of possible thoughts has structural holes no recombination reaches." McGinn (1989) defines closure by "concept-forming procedures", and of the recombination picture he writes: "Hume mistakenly thought that human minds were Humean". The "holes no recombination reaches" sentence is the research note's own extension. **Fix**: closure stated in McGinn's terms, his rejection of the Humean picture quoted, the extension labelled as the Map's.
3. **Nisbett and Wilson over-read.** "We actively confabulate origin stories for our insights ... papering over the gap with false explanations." The paper never uses "confabulate"; its abstract says reports "will sometimes" be accurate; and its section on creative workers says they describe themselves "as bystanders", which is ignorance reported, the opposite of a false story. The support for misreport is the Maier (1931) two-cord result. **Fix**: the universal replaced with that result (subjects shown a useless cue before the helpful one "reported that the useless cue had been helpful and denied that the critical cue had played any role").
4. **Map-internal quotation not in the page quoted.** `[[conceptual-impossibility]]'s "bouncing back"`: the live page has "bounces back" (L34) and "bounces off" (L76); "bouncing back" occurs 0 times. **Fix**: "where thought 'bounces back'".
5. **Poincaré's hypothesis reported as an observation.** "He noted that a 'special esthetic sensibility' acts as 'the delicate sieve'". Poincaré: "This is only a hypothesis". **Fix**: "He proposed, as a hypothesis, that".
6. **Tenet section against [P-V3](/positions/voids-as-evidence/#p-v3).** The section opened with "connects to the Map's foundational commitments" and then asserted, flat, that Schopenhauer's and Kant's states "involve consciousness adopting configurations beyond ordinary information-processing" and that "the aesthetic mode involves consciousness directing physical processing". Neither author held the Map's dualism (the research note's own alignment line says Schopenhauer's metaphysics "is itself an idealism ... not a substance dualism" and that functional theories of attention "can absorb" the observation; the article dropped both). **Fix**: opener now states that none of the tenets is supported by this void ([P-V3](/positions/voids-as-evidence/#p-v3)) in the wording sibling voids use; both sentences marked as the Map's reading, with the author-stance clause and the functional alternative restored.
