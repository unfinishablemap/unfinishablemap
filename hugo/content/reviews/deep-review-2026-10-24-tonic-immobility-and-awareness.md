## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

- **Subject splice: Schalinski's shutdown sentence describes the stages after TI, not TI.** The old L57 said that in Schauer and Elbert's model "the shutdown 'consists of functional sensory deafferentation, motor paralysis, alterations of the consciousness, and loss of speech perception and production', ending in 'shutdown of physiological arousal and memory functions'". It presented this as the counter-picture to Kozlowska's TI-specific "vivid memory" claim. In the raw Schalinski et al. 2015 text (PMC4431999), the passage reads: "'fright' or tonic immobility is common ... Further stages of the defense repertoire include 'flag–faint' with its dissociative properties. ... It consists of functional sensory deafferentation ...". So "It" is the flag–faint dissociative responding. Table 1 lists "shutdown of physiological arousal and memory functions" under **Flag/faint**. The **Fright** row (unresponsive immobility) has no consciousness or memory item: it lists only numbing of anger and suppressed vocal behaviour. **Fix**: the paragraph now opens "Other accounts describe dimming". It gives Lloyd et al.'s within-TI claim first (dissociating the body from conscious awareness "is inherent to TI", verbatim at PMC7219877) with the STOP amnesia item. It then says that in Schauer and Elbert's shutdown model "fright, the stage they equate with TI, gives way to flag and faint, whose dissociative responding 'consists of ...'", with the memory-shutdown phrase placed "at that end". The lede's "shutdown models predict that awareness dims as the shutdown deepens" was already accurate and stays.
- **Description dropped its subject.** It said "Tonic immobility ... is known only from later report". That contradicts the body, which says the motor closure is checked objectively (body sway, heart rate; Volchan 2011) and that "the closure is measured". **Fix**: "Awareness during tonic immobility ... is known only from later report; the cascade predicts the shutdown with no experiential term, so the Map's interface reading is a fit, not evidence."
- **Unscoped absence claim in the family table.** The TI duration cell read "not measured in humans", which is a literature-wide absence. The research note licenses only "no human TI duration data were found". **Fix**: "no human data found".

### Medium Issues Found

- **Lede against body on the TIS.** The lede said the scale "asks nothing about" awareness, but the body says its two detachment items "index *reduced* presence". **Fix**: "the field's first scale asks about detachment but not whether the event was perceived or remembered". "First" replaces "main" because Reichenheim et al. 2014 attests it ("The first scale developed for its assessment was the 10-item Tonic Immobility Scale"); "main" was unverified.
- **"TI shows" presupposed awareness during TI.** The lede ("TI shows that awareness is not sufficient for action ...") and the RSP ("That awareness persists while action is blocked shows only ...") both asserted as fact the awareness the article calls contested. **Fix**: made conditional ("Where awareness does persist, TI shows only that it is not sufficient ..."; "Where awareness persists while action is blocked, that shows only ..."). Epiphenomenalism is still not conceded, and the converse is still refused.
- **"The late, passive end of the defence cascade"** contradicted L49 and Lloyd's "final stages ... beyond TI". **Fix**: "a late, passive stage".
- **Theme label not verbatim.** "TI as Mental Paralysis" sat inside quotation marks, but the source spells out "Tonic Immobility as Mental Paralysis" (abstract; the section heading is lower-case). **Fix**: verbatim form.
- **Bovin et al. 2008 dropped qualifier.** The text said "176 undergraduates", but the abstract has "Female undergraduates (N = 176)" and the title "among sexual assault survivors". **Fix**: "among 176 female undergraduate survivors of sexual assault".
- **Was the proposed test feasible?** The test calls for a perceptual task "during reported TI" in a state defined by inability to move. **Fix**: one sentence saying responses would need a spared channel, such as the eye movements the STOP keeps (b9).
- **"Either result would settle"** overstated what a re-elicitation study could reach. **Fix**: "would bear on". The ethics sentence now comes first, so the re-elicited-only scope comes before the claim. "The shutdown models predict degraded perception" became "Accounts of dimming predict degraded perception", to match the corrected L57.
- **"from brain states alone"** (cascade paragraph) overstated: the triggers include restraint and inescapability, which are circumstances. **Fix**: "without one" (an experiential term).
- **Motor-check timing garbled.** "checked objectively minutes after a re-elicited state" placed the objective check after the state, but body sway is recorded during it. **Fix**: "The motor report, given minutes after a re-elicited state, is thus checked against an objective measure".
- **Family intro vs table.** The intro said every row was a case "while motor output is unavailable", but the freezing row is "a parasympathetic brake, not paralysis". **Fix**: "with freezing as the contrast". The TI timing cell now reads "minutes (re-elicited) to decades later", matching the lede and the sleep-paralysis host's "minutes to decades afterwards".
