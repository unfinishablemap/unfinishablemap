## Executive Summary

The page is honest in its headline: it concedes compatibility, refuses discrimination, and names its costs. But three of its mechanisms are weaker than the page thinks, and each weakness makes the Map's position worse than stated.

- **The Bayesian deflation is contradicted by the source it quotes.** Henderson's abstract, which the page cites, places "the extent to which the explanation a theory provides depends on its core elements rather than on auxiliary hypotheses" in the *likelihood*. So "both are compatible" is a claim about consistency, not about equal likelihoods. The physicalist's loveliness advantage then partly reappears as a likelihood advantage. The triggering outer review made the same point in plain words: "Generic interactionism does not predict 'exactly the correlations'".
- **The corridor reply counts accommodation as prediction.** `tenets.md` L69 says Tenet 2's minimality is "fixed by" the empirical record of no detectable input. So the seventh explanandum is a constraint the corridor reading was built to satisfy, not one it predicts.
- **The *suggestive* tier on the widened set does not survive Type-B.** The lead says the strongest rival contests the datum. L62 and L78 say it grants the datum. The phenomenal-concepts strategy predicts the gap, so the register's own discriminator rule leaves the widened set at compatible until the second-order comparison is run. A sentence importing a Type-C ("future progress will close it") description of Type-B should go.

The idleness disconfirmer's fall clause is decorative given the corpus's own agency-budget result. The page also understates the empirical exposure that [P-Q3](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q3) rates *indirect*. Four fidelity defects remain: the SEP "Dualism" 2025 revision is co-authored by Ralph Weir, so the unresolved "Weir 2021b" is a self-citation; "on its leading defender's own presentation" overclaims; the Melnyk ordering is unverified; and the Stoljar equivalence is unlabelled. All four priority fixes fit inside the headroom (≈ +185 words in total).

## Critiques by Philosopher

### (a) The Type-B Physicalist (Papineau, Loar, Balog)

"Your first reply says phenomenal character 'is absent from the list' (L62). It isn't. The list includes 'neural dependence of mental states' and 'access, memory and report', and on my view those states *are* the phenomenal states, picked out under physical concepts. What is absent is a *phenomenal concept* of them. You read a difference in concepts as a difference in facts. That is the move my position exists to block, and your lead commits it twice:

- It says the widened explananda's 'standing as data is exactly what the strongest physicalist rival contests' (L30). I contest nothing of the kind. Your own L62 and L78 concede that I 'accept phenomenal character as a datum'. So the lead and the body disagree about who your opponent is.
- What I contest is whether the datum is *unexplained*. On identity there is no further fact to explain, and the gap is explained now by how phenomenal concepts work.

"The strategy predicts the gap, and with Balog's constitutional concepts it also predicts your 'acquaintance asymmetry'. So by your register's own rule (support needs a discriminator the rival does not also predict), the widened set is *compatible* against me. Your L78 admits 'the first reply's suggestive tier is provisional', yet the lead (L30) and the Verdict (L72) award *suggestive* without that qualifier.

"Last, L62's closing sentence says my reply is 'promissory'. That describes Type-C (the gap is closable by future progress), not me. Your own triggering review says it correctly: Type-B physicalists 'may expect indefinitely persistent conceptual opacity alongside ontological identity'. You imported the mislabel from `parsimony-epistemology` L90, and the conclusion you build on it ('does not restore parity because parsimony is synchronic') misses me."

*Assessment*: the Type-B critic is right on all four counts. The lead/body contradiction and the Type-C mislabel are plain defects. The tier point follows from the register's own rule in `project/evidential-status-discipline` (the rival-model section). The widened set also contains content determinacy, whose status as a datum *and* whose dualist explanation are the Map's own PCT bet. Counting it as an explanandum that lifts dualism counts that bet twice.

### (b) The Bayesian (Henderson, Okasha, Lipton ch. 7)

"Your page says compatibility 'is a claim about likelihoods' (L40). Then, under our name, it says 'both are compatible' 'is the whole likelihood story and the residual disagreement is about priors' (L56). Neither follows. *Compatible* means P(E | H) > 0. It does not mean P(E | dualism) = P(E | physicalism).

"Read the rest of the abstract you quote. Henderson says one key explanatory consideration is 'the extent to which the explanation a theory provides depends on its core elements rather than on auxiliary hypotheses', and that 'this type of consideration is reflected in the Bayesian likelihood'. Physicalism reaches neural dependence, lesion deficits and pharmacological change from its core. Dualism reaches them only through bridge laws or an interface auxiliary chosen to fit them. On emergent compatibilism that difference shows up in the likelihood. Your sentence 'two hypotheses can make the evidence equally probable while one explains it and the other merely survives it' (L40) describes exactly the case we price.

"So the deflation does dissolve your central distinction, but in the opposite direction. Loveliness is not left stranded beside equal likelihoods. Much of it becomes likelihood. And L88's 'a prior the evidence does not move' is either false (the likelihoods differ) or dogmatic (a prior of one). Neither is what `tenets.md` L55 means by 'a commitment the Map owns'."

*Assessment*: decisive against the current wording. The page's own source contradicts its reconstruction, and the outer review that triggered the page made the same point. That review's L89 says generic interactionism needs "detailed psychophysical bridge laws" and that "if those laws are simply chosen to reproduce every physicalist prediction, the view becomes empirically idle". The honest tier on the seven explananda is still compatible, but only for *dualism plus an interface auxiliary*. The Bayesian prices that auxiliary, and the page should say so.

### (c) The Constructive Empiricist (van Fraassen)

"You use my bad-lot objection to blunt the physicalist (L70), and you promise symmetry (L82, L90). Your page does not deliver it. There are three gaps.

"First, your reply 1 is itself an inference to the best explanation. It ranks dualism above physicalism as the explanation of phenomenal character over a two-member lot. Russellian monism, panpsychism, idealism and neutral monism are absent. If the physicalist's lot is bad, so is yours, and your page never turns the objection on reply 1. It turns it only on PCT.

"Second, your consistency statement says the Map's abductions 'reach no higher tier than the one it concedes to the physicalist here' (L82). What tier is that? The page calls the physicalist's IBE 'unlicensed here' (L66) and also says it is blunted 'without defeating it' (L90). The referent is indeterminate, so the statement cannot be checked. And it does not hold where you point it. The PCT page (L84) and register entry [P-TU1](/positions/thought-and-understanding/#p-tu1) both still bank unification: PCT is 'the one account that unifies content determinacy, the understanding/processing distinction and the dissociation data where rivals handle them piecemeal'. That is the very loveliness your Tenet 5 reply says cannot be trusted here. PCT now accepts bad-lot exposure (L86, installed by the cross-review) but not the loveliness exposure.

"Third, you equate Stoljar's methodological naturalism with IBE: 'That is the IBE form without the label' (L42). That assumes the methods of natural science *are* inference to the best explanation, which is exactly what I deny. Stoljar's own premise speaks of 'the most likely view', which is likeliness, not loveliness. The equation is a Map reading, stated as fact."

*Assessment*: right on all three. The symmetry is applied to the Map's *other* abduction and skipped for the one on this page. A related point belongs here: Tenet 5 as written (`tenets.md` L131–151) binds *simplicity*. It does not bind scope or unification. The physicalist's seven-explananda argument is mainly a unification argument. So the claim that Tenet 5 "holds that in a domain this incompletely known the bridge fails" (L66) overreaches twice. The tenet says simplicity is "not a reliable guide", not that the bridge fails. And it reaches only the parsimony and elegance parts of loveliness, not the scope part.

### The Eliminative Materialist (Churchland)

Your widened explananda are introspective posits: "phenomenal character", "acquaintance", "content determinacy". A mature neuroscience will explain why brains generate these self-descriptions. Treating them as data the physicalist must explain gives folk psychology a veto over the science that will replace it. The page answers Type-B well enough to see Type-A as "an opponent the IBE argument does not need" (L78). But the Type-A denial is only redundant if Type-B fails. If Type-B *succeeds*, the eliminativist's tidier conclusion is the one the IBE argument licenses next. *Assessment*: bedrock disagreement. It is correctly marked as a commitment (L72) and is not a defect.

### The Hard-Nosed Physicalist (Dennett)

The heterophenomenological explanandum is the subject's reports and dispositions to report that experience is ineffable and directly known. "Access, memory and report" is already the fifth item on the physicalist's list. So the widened set double-counts: once as report (explained) and once as the "phenomenal character" behind the report (posited). *Assessment*: this converges with the Type-B charge that "absent from the list" is a claim about concepts presented as a claim about facts. The same fix covers both.

### The Quantum Skeptic (Tegmark)

Reply 4 assumes the corridor exists. `ensemble-level-epiphenomenalism` L36 states that it "assumes that precondition rather than defending it": a brain-scale superposition that survives long enough to be selected. If decoherence forecloses that, there is no corridor and the seventh explanandum favours physicalism without qualification. The page mentions decoherence nowhere. *Assessment*: low severity, but one clause ("granting the coherence precondition the ensemble page assumes") would make reply 4 honest about what it inherits.

### The Many-Worlds Defender (Deutsch)

IBE is how Everettians argue: the Schrödinger equation unifies, and collapse is an idle posit. The Map rejects MWI by widening the explananda to an indexical datum ("why this branch") that MWI allegedly leaves out. That is structurally the same move as reply 1, and it is exposed to the same reply: the indexical datum is as contested as the phenomenal one. *Assessment*: the page need not engage MWI. But the parallel shows the widening strategy is a general Map move, so its tier should be stated consistently wherever it is used. Not ranked.

### The Empiricist (Popper's Ghost)

"Consciousness causes, but leaves no trace in any aggregate" is not even wrong. The page's candour about this is a virtue. But its one falsifiable sentence (the rise clause) names no grain, no condition and no experiment. The corpus has all three in horn (a) and the page doesn't use them. *Assessment*: the same finding as (d)5, which is priority item 3.

### The Buddhist Philosopher (Nagarjuna)

Both rivals in your IBE reify "mental states" as things needing an ontological home: neurons for one, a non-physical relatum for the other. Your Bayesian move makes dualism a "prior the evidence does not move" (L88). Holding a view the evidence cannot move is clinging to a view, which the middle way warns against. *Assessment*: this converges with the Bayesian's dogmatism horn. Changing "move" to "raise" (item 1) removes the claim of immunity.

## Counterarguments to Address

### "Identity explains the correlations"
- **Current content says**: physical models unify the seven explananda, and "identity or realisation explains the unification" (L34).
- **A critic would argue**: physicalists themselves dispute that identities explain anything. The standard reference is Kim's objection that identities do not explain correlations, answered by Block & Stalnaker and by McLaughlin's line. *Lead only: Kim 2005, Block & Stalnaker 1999 and McLaughlin 2010's content are unverified here and must be read before use.*
- **Suggested response**: an in-framework reply may be available, one that turns on whether identity is an *explanatory* hypothesis at all, without appeal to Map commitments. Worth a sentence once McLaughlin 2010 is read. Not ranked, because the source is unread.

### "Acquaintance is explained by constitutional concepts"
- **Current content says**: the acquaintance asymmetry is one of the widened explananda (L30, L62).
- **A critic would argue**: Balog's constitutional account of phenomenal concepts explains acquaintance on physicalist terms. *Lead: Balog, "Acquaintance and the Mind-Body Problem", in Hill & Gozzano (eds.), New Perspectives on Type Identity, CUP 2012. Unverified here.* The Map's PCS page already names "Papineau's and Balog's constitutive route" (L65).
- **Suggested response**: covered by priority item 2's provisional tier. The second-order comparison the page calls owed should start here.

### "The disconfirmer is a promissory note"
- **Current content says**: confidence rises or falls only with the toy-model programme.
- **Suggested response**: priority item 3 replaces the model clauses with the horn-(a) conditional test the corpus already specifies.
