## Reply

# External referee report

**Article:** *Basal and Bioelectric Cognition: Levin’s Morphogenetic Agency and Xenobots*  

**Recommendation:** **Major revision**

## Executive judgment

The article has a defensible and useful **narrow thesis**:

> Agency-, memory-, and goal-language does not entail phenomenal consciousness and cannot settle a consciousness attribution by itself.

It also contains important scope protections. It explicitly says that neither planarian nor xenobot experience has been shown absent, that behavioural markers remain defeasible rather than worthless, and that the evidence does not decide dualism against every specific functionalist theory. Those qualifications defeat any charge that the article simply declares basal organisms unconscious or dismisses behavioural evidence wholesale. [topics/basal-and-bioelectric-cognition](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/basal-and-bioelectric-cognition/)

The article nevertheless advances a materially stronger thesis at several points: that agency is **evidentially neutral**, that it supplies “no evidence either way,” and that Levin’s own phenomenal restraint supports that conclusion. That stronger thesis is not established. It rests on:

1. a slide from **not sufficient** to **not evidence**;
2. a false account of Michael Levin’s own published position on minimal consciousness;
3. empirical overstatements concerning planarian memory, genome independence, neural independence, ectopic eyes and xenobot construction;
4. a stale and one-sided literature base;
5. inconsistency with the Map’s own consciousness-scope register and neighbouring articles.

The changelog does not pre-empt these criticisms. Its latest dependency assessment says that the September 2 change softened the article’s valence subclaim, “not the decoupling this article invokes.” [workflow/changelog](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/)

My verdict terminology is:

- **False:** contradicted by the relevant primary source or bibliographic record.
- **Unsupported by the cited source:** the source does not establish the claim, although the claim might conceivably be true.
- **Overstated:** a weaker directional claim is supported, but the article adds exclusivity, magnitude or certainty not earned by the evidence.
- **Supported with qualification:** substantially correct once material limitations are made explicit.

## 1. Scope and hedging: criticisms the article has already answered

Several tempting objections would be unfair.

The article separates Levin’s exposition from the Map’s own integration; says that the experiments show neither presence nor absence of experience; retains behavioural evidence as part of a graded practice; and acknowledges that the results are consistent with, rather than uniquely confirmatory of, dualism. Its phrase “without asserted experience” is generally more defensible than “without experience.” [topics/basal-and-bioelectric-cognition](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/basal-and-bioelectric-cognition/)

Accordingly, the following charges are **refuted by the text**:

- **“The article claims xenobots and planarians are certainly unconscious.”** It does not.
- **“The article says behaviour is wholly irrelevant to consciousness.”** Its better-calibrated passages say behavioural markers are defeasible and substrate-sensitive, and must contribute to a convergence assessment.
- **“The article claims these experiments prove dualism.”** It expressly allows specific-functionalist interpretations.
- **“The article treats functional and phenomenal cognition as logically incompatible.”** Its argument is instead that functional competence does not settle phenomenality.

The problem is internal inconsistency. Those good qualifications coexist with the stronger statement that agency “is no evidence either way.” The report therefore concerns what the article **does claim**, not a more extreme position it disavows.

## 2. The central inferential problem: insufficiency is not evidential neutrality

Let A be an observed form of adaptive or goal-directed competence and C the presence of consciousness.

Three different propositions must be distinguished:

1. A does not entail C.
2. A is insufficient on its own to justify attributing C.
3. A is evidentially neutral between C and ¬C.

The first is clearly true. The second is plausible and is substantially supported by the cases discussed. The third is much stronger. In probabilistic terms, evidential neutrality requires the likelihood ratio

P(A∣¬C)P(A∣C)​

to be approximately one. Nothing in the planarian or xenobot experiments estimates that ratio.

### 2.1 These systems are not known negative controls

The article uses systems whose phenomenal status is unknown to show that their competencies do not support phenomenality. But unless their non-consciousness is established independently, they cannot function as negative controls for consciousness markers.

The experiments establish:

- competence is present;
- no phenomenal measurement has been made;
- the relevant papers need not invoke phenomenality to explain the observed function.

They do **not** establish:

- competence occurs in a system known to be non-conscious;
- the same competence is equally likely in conscious and non-conscious systems;
- an observer should assign exactly the same credence in consciousness before and after observing it.

The article carefully denies having shown absence, but then uses these unknown cases as though they supplied the evidential work of known absences.

### 2.2 Mechanistic sufficiency does not imply evidential irrelevance

Showing that a behaviour can be explained through bioelectric, cellular or mechanical processes without invoking consciousness establishes that consciousness is not required in that particular functional explanation. It does not establish that the behaviour is no evidence of consciousness.

That distinction is familiar elsewhere. A complete neural explanation of a human withdrawal response would not automatically make withdrawal evidentially irrelevant to pain. Whether it remains evidence depends on correlations, common causes, homology, theory and comparative background—not on whether the physical mechanism can be described without inserting consciousness as an additional causal step.

The neighbouring *Engineered Decoupling* article repeats the same invalid move: because competency is “accounted for without appeal to experience,” it concludes that competency is no evidence for experience. Explanation without appeal establishes **non-necessity in the explanation**, not a likelihood ratio of one. [topics/synthetic-minimal-agents-and-the-engineered-decoupling](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/synthetic-minimal-agents-and-the-engineered-decoupling/)

### 2.3 Non-assertion by an investigator is not evidence of neutrality

The article treats Levin’s unwillingness to make a categorical sentience claim as evidence. That is not legitimate without an additional premise connecting his non-assertion to the probability of consciousness.

An investigator may decline to assert phenomenality because:

- the available method cannot establish it;
- the paper is restricted to third-person functional capacities;
- the investigator holds a positive but speculative view below the threshold for an empirical conclusion;
- the hard problem prevents definitive external verification.

Indeed, Levin’s publications instantiate precisely this combination: caution about empirical demonstration alongside positive hypotheses about minimal consciousness. His restraint is therefore evidence about the **limits of the experiment**, not direct evidence about the organisms’ phenomenal status.

### 2.4 Conflict with the Map’s own register

[P-CS2](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs2) says that behavioural and anatomical markers raise the probability of consciousness through convergence while delivering no certainty. It says behavioural sophistication transfers “no verdict on its own,” not that behavioural sophistication transfers no evidential weight whatsoever. [positions/consciousness-scope](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/consciousness-scope/)

That is the right distinction. The target article currently cites only [P-VS1](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs1) in its positions block, despite its central thesis being primarily about [P-CS2](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs2). [topics/basal-and-bioelectric-cognition](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/basal-and-bioelectric-cognition/)

### Defensible replacement for the central conclusion

A version that the evidence supports would be:

> Bioelectric and xenobot results show that sophisticated goal-directed regulation can occur without any independent demonstration of phenomenal consciousness. Agency-, memory-, and goal-language therefore does not entail consciousness and should not carry a fixed evidential weight across substrates. The underlying behaviours may nevertheless contribute weak, theory-dependent evidence when combined with substrate, homology, learning, integration and other markers.

That conclusion is stronger than mere verbal caution, compatible with [P-CS2](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs2), and does not require treating an unresolved case as a negative control.

## 3. The portrayal of Michael Levin’s position

This is the article’s most serious factual problem.

### 3.1 “The programme is explicitly agnostic” — **false as a programme-wide attribution**

Levin often brackets first-person experience when presenting functional or experimental work. His 2023 paper on bioelectric networks expressly says that it intentionally avoids first-person experiential consciousness and concentrates on observable capacities. That supports a **paper-specific bracketing** claim. Levin, *Animal Cognition* 2023, DOI **10.1007/s10071-023-01780-3**. [Springer](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s10071-023-01780-3.pdf)

It does not support the article’s programme-wide claim. In his 2019 paper, Levin says it is natural to hypothesise that there is something it is like to be a tissue or organ, suggests that a pancreas may possess proto-consciousness, and says that the model can be regarded as a form of panpsychism. Levin 2019, DOI **10.3389/fpsyg.2019.02688**. [Frontiers](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.02688/full)

TAME is even clearer. After noting that the framework principally addresses functional properties, Levin says that it makes commitments concerning consciousness, proposes that consciousness comes in degrees and kinds, suggests that systems operating in morphogenetic and transcriptional spaces may possess minimal consciousness, and entertains the possibility that there is no true zero on the consciousness scale. He remains cautious about definitive third-person proof, but that is not phenomenal neutrality. Levin 2022, DOI **10.3389/fnsys.2022.768201**. [Frontiers](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/systems-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnsys.2022.768201/full)

### 3.2 “Panpsychist readings … are not Levin’s own” — **false**

The sentence is directly contradicted by Levin’s 2019 discussion and by TAME. It is also difficult to reconcile with two co-authored publications:

- Fields, Glazebrook and Levin explicitly develop a scale-free treatment of cognition **and consciousness** applicable to basal and synthetic systems. DOI **10.1093/nc/niab013**. [OUP Academic](https://academic.oup.com/nc/article/2021/2/niab013/6334115)
- Rouleau and Levin argue for the multiple realizability of sentience and present unconventional and engineered embodiments as appropriate test cases. DOI **10.1523/ENEURO.0375-23.2023**. [NCBI](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10646883/)

Levin is not a simple constitutive panpsychist who attributes equal consciousness to all matter. He makes organisation, cybernetic structure and goal-directed processes relevant. But a graded, organisation-sensitive position that sometimes approaches panpsychism is very much part of his own published work.

### 3.3 What the article may accurately say

The following formulation would be accurate:

> The xenobot and planarian experiments do not empirically demonstrate sentience, and several of Levin’s experimental or functional presentations bracket first-person experience. Elsewhere, however, Levin has advanced positive, explicitly speculative hypotheses about minimal and scale-free consciousness. The Map rejects those hypotheses while accepting his functional results.

That preserves the Map’s disagreement while representing the opponent in his strongest form.

### 3.4 Internal contradiction already present on the site

*The Engineered Decoupling* correctly identifies Rouleau and Levin’s sentience paper as the strongest rival and states that nothing in the engineered cases discriminates between their reading and the Map’s. [topics/synthetic-minimal-agents-and-the-engineered-decoupling](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/synthetic-minimal-agents-and-the-engineered-decoupling/)

Thus one sibling says Levin co-authored a substantial positive sentience case, while the target article says panpsychist or continuity readings are not Levin’s own. Both cannot stand.

## 4. Durant et al. 2017: planarian pattern memory

Durant et al., *Biophysical Journal* 2017, DOI **10.1016/j.bpj.2017.04.011**, is a striking and relevant experiment. The article is right to treat it as evidence that transient physiological intervention can produce durable changes in regenerative outcomes. The paper reports persistent double-headed and cryptic regenerative states, correlated global voltage patterns and an intervention capable of resetting some altered worms to a stable wild-type regenerative state. [RCA Storage+1](https://rcastoragev2.blob.core.windows.net/4819410a566a14a4e1edbd77dea419fc/PMC5443973.pdf)

The Map’s presentation nevertheless exceeds the evidence in several places.

### 4.1 “Continued producing two-headed offspring” — **supported with wording correction**

The altered state persisted through subsequent rounds of amputation without repeat octanol treatment, and two-headed worms also maintained the phenotype. But “offspring” ordinarily implies sexual or asexual reproduction of new organisms. Much of the relevant protocol consists of serial cutting and regeneration.

Use:

> “subsequent regenerates in repeated rounds of amputation”

or, where spontaneous fission was specifically observed:

> “descendants produced by fission.”

The result itself is real; the objection is to biologically ambiguous wording.

### 4.2 “Stored in the bioelectric network, not the genome” — **overstated and unsupported in its exclusive form**

The study supports the following:

- the intervention did not consist of DNA-sequence editing;
- a stable global resting-potential pattern was associated with, predicted and causally influenced future regenerative outcomes;
- manipulating the bioelectric state could reset some outcomes.

It does not establish that the relevant information is stored **only** in a bioelectric network, nor does it exclude gene-regulatory, chromatin, neural, structural or other cellular contributions. The authors explicitly say that bioelectric signalling operates with downstream target genes and chromatin modifications and that other epigenetic mechanisms may be integrated with it. They also identify the cell types storing the state as a question for future work. [RCA Storage+1](https://rcastoragev2.blob.core.windows.net/4819410a566a14a4e1edbd77dea419fc/PMC5443973.pdf)

A defensible replacement is:

> “The result demonstrates durable, non-DNA-sequence editing of regenerative pattern through a causally instructive bioelectric and epigenetic state that interacts with gene-regulatory machinery.”

“Genome-default target morphology” in Durant et al. does not mean that the genome has no causal role.

### 4.3 “Reversible” — **overstated**

In the reported reset experiment, 34% of double-headed worms treated with SCH-28080 returned to a stable single-headed regenerative state; vehicle controls remained double-headed. The intervention is impressive, but “partially resettable under ion-channel modulation” is more accurate than an unqualified “reversible.” [RCA Storage](https://rcastoragev2.blob.core.windows.net/4819410a566a14a4e1edbd77dea419fc/PMC5443973.pdf)

### 4.4 “Stable attractor state” — **supported as a model, not as a uniquely demonstrated mechanism**

Persistent states and transition behaviour are consistent with multistability and attractor dynamics. Durant et al. explicitly offer a model, and Pezzulo et al. later develop the bistability and pattern-memory interpretation. But this is a theoretical characterisation of the dynamics, not a directly observed object or the only model compatible with the data. Pezzulo et al. 2021, DOI **10.1098/rstb.2019.0765**. [RCA Storage+1](https://rcastoragev2.blob.core.windows.net/4819410a566a14a4e1edbd77dea419fc/PMC5443973.pdf)

Write “consistent with a multistable attractor model” rather than “behaving like a stable attractor” without qualification.

### 4.5 “Non-neural memory” — **unsupported by the cited source**

Planarians are not neurally empty. They possess a central nervous system, and Levin’s own TAME paper discusses two-headed planarians whose brains are connected by a CNS. Later work explicitly models a bioelectric circuit and a neural polarity mechanism as competing contributors to regenerative outcome. Grodstein and Levin 2022, DOI **10.1089/bioe.2021.0036**. [Frontiers+1](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/systems-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnsys.2022.768201/full)

Bischof et al. also found that nervous-system and epithelial polarity dynamically adapt to the altered double-headed morphology. That does not refute bioelectric causation, but it makes exclusive “non-neural” language particularly unwarranted. Bischof et al. 2020, DOI **10.1016/j.ydbio.2020.08.009**. [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC10474925)

The article should distinguish:

- a bioelectric mechanism that is not confined to synaptic neural processing;
- a demonstration that neural structures make no contribution.

Only the first is supported.

### 4.6 “No one is tempted to call [it] an experience” — **false**

Levin himself entertains minimal experience in tissues and morphogenetic systems. Fields, Glazebrook and Levin explicitly discuss scale-free consciousness. Even readers who reject those views cannot accurately be described as nonexistent. [Frontiers+2Frontiers+2](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.02688/full)

This sentence should be deleted. The argument does not improve by turning a contested literature into a rhetorical universal.

## 5. Pai et al. 2012: bioelectric eye induction

The underlying experimental result is accurately recognised as important. Manipulating membrane potential in non-eye cells induced ectopic eye tissue, including some complete and organised eyes outside the normal anterior eye field. The paper also supported membrane potential itself—rather than a channel-specific side effect—as an instructive regulatory variable. Pai et al. 2012, DOI **10.1242/dev.073759**. [DOI+1](https://doi.org/10.1242/dev.073759)

### 5.1 The DOI is wrong — **false bibliographic attribution**

The article gives DOI **10.1242/dev.077917** for the research article. That DOI belongs to a corrigendum concerning equal-contribution author labelling. The original research article’s DOI is **10.1242/dev.073759**. The correction states that the print and final online versions were already correct. [topics/basal-and-bioelectric-cognition](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/basal-and-bioelectric-cognition/)

The bibliography should cite the original DOI. The corrigendum can be added separately only if there is a reason to record the authorship correction.

### 5.2 “Induces complete, well-formed ectopic eyes” — **supported but frequency-overstated**

Approximately 20% of injected embryos developed ectopic eye tissue, while 7.5% developed large, complete ectopic eyes containing retina, retinal pigmented epithelium and lens. Ectopic structures occurred in the gut, tail and other locations outside the normal anterior neural field. [DOI+1](https://doi.org/10.1242/dev.073759)

The sentence is not false as an existence claim. It becomes misleading when written as though complete-eye production were the general or deterministic outcome.

A better formulation is:

> “Vmem perturbation produced ectopic eye tissue in about 20% of embryos and complete ectopic eyes in 7.5%, including structures outside the normal anterior neural field.”

### 5.3 “Instructive master signal” — **partly supported, partly overstated**

“Instructive” and “crucial upstream signal” closely track the authors’ conclusion. “Master signal” adds an implication of singular control or causal sufficiency beyond what the experiment establishes. The voltage signal acted through calcium-dependent pathways and eye-field transcriptional machinery; it did not replace the downstream developmental programme. [DOI+1](https://doi.org/10.1242/dev.073759)

Use “instructive upstream signal” and remove “master.”

### 5.4 “Bioelectric state acts as a set-point the tissue works to satisfy” — **unsupported by Pai et al.**

Pai et al. establish local induction of eye fate and downstream developmental organisation. They do not demonstrate a represented target, comparison against an error signal, or compensatory action that restores a target after perturbation. Those additional properties would be needed to distinguish a cybernetic set-point from an instructive developmental trigger.

The statement may be retained as a **Levin/TAME interpretation**, but not presented as something directly established by Pai et al. It should be labelled:

> “On Levin’s later cybernetic interpretation, such states may function as set-points…”

## 6. Xenobot claims

The article combines results from three distinguishable experimental platforms:

1. manually shaped, cardiomyocyte-driven xenobots reported in 2020;
2. self-organising, cilia-driven xenobots reported in 2021;
3. kinematic replication experiments reported later in 2021.

Failing to separate them produces several incorrect attributions.

### 6.1 Kriegman et al. 2020

Kriegman et al. 2020, DOI **10.1073/pnas.1910837117**, used computational design followed by physical construction from Xenopus embryonic tissues. The constructs showed locomotion, collective debris aggregation and wound recovery. Some stages of the pipeline explicitly required manual intervention, including shaping and layering cells. [PubMed Central (PMC)+1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6994979/)

#### “Locomote” and “repair themselves” — **supported**

These are fair summaries, subject to ordinary qualification about laboratory conditions.

#### “Navigate” — **underspecified**

The 2020 constructs produced movement trajectories, but the stronger claim of diverse navigation is more directly supported by Blackiston et al.’s later ciliated xenobots. The article should either specify what “navigate” means in the 2020 experiment or cite the 2021 platform.

#### “Push payloads” — **unsupported by the cited experimental result**

The 2020 paper experimentally reports aggregation of loose particles or debris. That is not equivalent to a demonstrated payload-delivery task. Potential carrying or delivery applications appeared in design and application discussions, but the observed result should be called “debris aggregation” unless a specific payload experiment is cited.

#### “The cells spontaneously cooperate to build” — **false for the 2020 platform**

The 2020 xenobots were physically assembled and shaped. Their cells showed self-organisation and plasticity within the constructed form, but the organisms did not spontaneously build the designed anatomy without intervention.

Blackiston et al. 2021 is the correct source for xenobots that arise through cellular self-organisation without scaffolds or microprinting, move through cilia, navigate in diverse ways, heal, exhibit group behaviours and support a proof-of-principle molecular memory. DOI **10.1126/scirobotics.abf1571**. [PubMed+1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34043553/)

This important paper is absent from the target article’s bibliography even though it supports several claims the article currently assigns to the 2020 paper.

### 6.2 “None of this is behaviour the source cells exhibit” — **false as an absolute**

The source cells contribute native capacities:

- cardiomyocyte contraction;
- epithelial cohesion;
- ciliary propulsion in the later platform;
- wound closure and tissue repair;
- ordinary cellular signalling and rearrangement.

What is novel is the deployment and coordination of these capacities in a new collective morphology. The defensible claim is:

> “The source cells do not exhibit this integrated organism-scale behavioural repertoire in their normal anatomical arrangement.”

That preserves emergence without pretending the component capacities appeared ex nihilo.

### 6.3 Kinematic replication

Kriegman et al. 2021, DOI **10.1073/pnas.2112672118**, genuinely reported kinematic replication under an explicit operational definition: multicellular assemblies moved through a supplied population of dissociated cells, compressed those cells into new assemblies, and thereby generated functional copies. Computational search identified shapes that postponed loss of replicative ability and improved output. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34845026/)

The article’s basic claim is **supported with qualifications**:

- dissociated embryonic cell feedstock was externally supplied;
- the environment was carefully constructed;
- the effective parent morphologies were experimentally fabricated;
- the process is not autonomous reproduction involving internally generated biomass, metabolism and an independently sustained life cycle;
- “self-replication” is correct in the paper’s kinematic sense, not every stronger ordinary-language sense.

These qualifications do not debunk the result. They identify exactly what was demonstrated.

### 6.4 “Goal-directed behaviour” — **interpretive rather than directly observed**

Locomotion, aggregation, repair and replication are observations. Whether they instantiate goals, representations or agency is a theoretical classification. The article occasionally acknowledges this terminology dispute, but elsewhere writes “genuine goal-directed regulation” as though the observation–interpretation boundary had disappeared.

That boundary should remain visible throughout:

- **Observed:** restoration, movement, aggregation, morphological persistence.
- **Control-theoretic interpretation:** error correction, target state, attractor.
- **Agentive interpretation:** memory, preference, goal pursuit.
- **Phenomenal interpretation:** felt valence, experience, subjectivity.

The evidence becomes progressively less direct down that sequence.

## 7. Counterargument coverage

The current treatment is inadequate because it says critics largely converge with Levin on phenomenal silence. The literature contains at least four importantly different positions.

### 7.1 Positive continuity and multiple-realizability views

Fields, Glazebrook and Levin treat basal and synthetic systems as relevant to scale-free consciousness, while Rouleau and Levin argue that sentience may be multiply realised in living and engineered systems. These are not merely unnamed commentators adding a panpsychist gloss. They are explicit, technically developed positions involving Levin himself. DOI **10.1093/nc/niab013** and DOI **10.1523/ENEURO.0375-23.2023**. [OUP Academic+1](https://academic.oup.com/nc/article/2021/2/niab013/6334115)

A serious response would ask which proposed organisational properties allegedly connect basal cognition to sentience, and why the Map regards those bridge principles as insufficient. Merely observing that the experiments do not prove experience does not answer the positive case.

### 7.2 Deflationary criticism of biological agency

DiFrisco and Gawne argue that strong biological-agency programmes are theoretically unsupported and that the relevant phenomena can be explained through evolved multiscale feedback mechanisms. DOI **10.1093/jeb/voae153**. Their target is stronger than every modest operational use of “agency,” so the paper should not be recruited as refuting all control-theoretic language. But it decisively disproves the target article’s sociological claim that genuine agency is “not seriously disputed.” [OUP Academic](https://academic.oup.com/jeb/article/38/2/143/7920097)

This rival attacks an earlier premise than the Map does:

- the Map grants agency but denies that it settles consciousness;
- DiFrisco and Gawne question whether an autonomous agency concept adds anything over mechanism and selection.

The distinction would improve the article.

### 7.3 Cautious basal-cognition research

Fábregas-Tejeda and Sims defend basal cognition as a potentially productive evolutionary research programme while emphasising epistemic cautions and the contested status of its central concepts. DOI **10.1007/s40656-025-00660-y**. This is a useful middle position between wholesale deflation and unqualified cognitive continuity. [Cambridge Repository](https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/items/d2645be8-c1bc-4f2a-b17e-2568e5aeb149)

### 7.4 Xenobot-specific threshold scepticism

Joy evaluates whether xenobots meet proposed minimal-cognition thresholds and argues that further capacities would be needed to reach what she calls Stage 1 xenobotic cognition. DOI **10.1016/j.endeavour.2024.100927**. This matters because the Map currently assumes the strongest description—engineering-grade agency—and then asks whether it supports experience. A more careful referee question is whether the systems yet satisfy a non-inflated criterion of cognition or agency at all. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38679490/)

### 7.5 The missing homology argument

Behavioural markers are not transferred between systems solely because the same intentional vocabulary sounds natural. Evidence in comparative consciousness often depends on combinations of:

- behavioural flexibility;
- neural or physiological homology;
- evolutionary continuity;
- learning and generalisation;
- integration across sensory and action systems;
- pharmacological and lesion effects;
- convergent markers.

A goal-like pattern in a voltage circuit may therefore have less evidential weight than flexible behaviour in a vertebrate without having exactly zero weight. That is precisely the kind of substrate-sensitive grading [P-CS2](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs2) already endorses.

### 7.6 Specific functionalism

The article commendably concedes that the cases do not refute functional theories that require a particular architecture rather than generic goal-directedness. That concession should move earlier. Xenobots are not fine-grained functional duplicates of systems accepted as conscious; hence their existence does not test organisational invariance or architecture-specific functionalism directly.

The evidence primarily rebuts the crude inference:

> displays some goal-directed competence, therefore conscious.

It does not rebut:

> a particular form of integration, recurrence, global availability or self-modelling contributes evidence of consciousness.

## 8. Currency and reference quality

The bibliography is not merely old; it is **selectively incomplete**. Landmark experiments from 2012 and 2017 remain appropriate, but an article revised in 2026 should connect them to the major 2020–2025 developments and disputes.

## 9. Integration with neighbouring articles

The site has dense links, but substantive integration is uneven.

### 9.1 *Competency Without Felt Experience*

The apex article amplifies the target’s strongest error by saying that none of the listed competencies is evidence of feeling and by treating systems as hosting no experiencer “on the Map’s reading.” Later, however, it calls behavioural markers suggestive in fish, says no absence has been proved, and admits that much of the convergence is generated by applying the same framework repeatedly. [apex/competency-without-felt-experience](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/competency-without-felt-experience/)

The apex should replace “none of it is evidence” with “none of it settles phenomenality or carries a substrate-independent evidential weight.” It should also stop treating sympathetic experts’ non-assertion as evidence of absence or neutrality.

### 9.2 *The Engineered Decoupling*

This article improves on the target in two ways: it explicitly says construction proves no phenomenal absence, and it presents Rouleau and Levin’s sentience argument as the strongest rival. [topics/synthetic-minimal-agents-and-the-engineered-decoupling](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/synthetic-minimal-agents-and-the-engineered-decoupling/)

But it retains the inference from “accounted for without appeal to experience” to “no evidence for experience.” It also says the functional question is closed by construction, immediately before conceding that 149 genes in the minimal-cell example have unknown functions. “Better constrained by construction” would be more accurate than “closed.” [topics/synthetic-minimal-agents-and-the-engineered-decoupling](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/synthetic-minimal-agents-and-the-engineered-decoupling/)

### 9.3 *Consciousness in Simple Organisms*

Its phrase “engineering-grade competence without asserted experience” is relatively careful. “Without independently established experience” would be better, because Levin has in fact advanced positive experiential hypotheses even though the experiments do not establish them. [topics/consciousness-in-simple-organisms](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/consciousness-in-simple-organisms/)

### 9.4 *Single-Cell Proto-Agency and the Evidence Problem*

The link description calls the target “Levin’s agency-without-experience decoupling.” That wording converts an absence of evidence into an evidenced absence. Replace it with “agency without independently verified experience” or “agency with phenomenality unresolved.” [topics/single-cell-proto-agency-and-the-evidence-problem](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/single-cell-proto-agency-and-the-evidence-problem/)

### 9.5 Consciousness-scope positions

[P-CS2](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs2) already contains the needed correction: markers raise probability through convergence, while behavioural sophistication supplies no verdict on its own. The target, apex and engineered-decoupling articles should inherit that exact discipline. [positions/consciousness-scope](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/consciousness-scope/)

[P-CS1](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs1) also warns that substrate permissibility is a permission rather than positive evidence. Correspondingly, a preferred neural-interface commitment may license a framework-relative withholding policy, but it cannot be counted as independent empirical support generated by the xenobot cases. ## 10. Overall assessment

| Question | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| Do the bioelectric experiments demonstrate causally important physiological control of morphogenesis? | **Yes.** |
| Do they justify describing some processes through memory, target and agency language? | **Reasonably, if the terms are explicitly operational or model-relative.** |
| Do they prove exclusive storage outside genomic, neural or other cellular mechanisms? | **No.** |
| Do xenobots show novel collective capacities? | **Yes.** |
| Does the 2020 xenobot paper show spontaneous construction and payload delivery? | **No, not as currently stated.** |
| Does the evidence show goal-directed competence is insufficient by itself to establish consciousness? | **Yes.** |
| Does it show such competence is evidentially neutral? | **No.** |
| Is Levin programme-wide agnostic about minimal consciousness? | **No.** |
| Are the article’s stronger conclusions independent support for dualism? | **No; they remain framework-relative.** |

The article should not be rejected at the level of its basic project. Its empirical subject is well chosen, and its intended distinction between functional competence and phenomenal presence is important. But the current argument gains much of its apparent force from an equivocation between **not proving**, **not warranting on its own**, and **providing no evidence**. Once that equivocation and the misrepresentation of Levin are removed, the surviving conclusion is more modest but considerably stronger.

### Site methodology

21. **Require a claim–source matrix for every empirical article.** Each sentence should be tagged as direct observation, statistical result, causal inference, computational model, author interpretation or Map interpretation.
22. **Add a DOI-resolution and corrigendum check to the publication pipeline.** Confirm that every DOI resolves to the cited title and whether it identifies an original article, correction, preprint or commentary.
23. **Introduce a “false versus unsupported” field into reviews and task generation.** A source mismatch should not automatically be converted into a claim of factual falsity.
24. **Track experimental platform versions explicitly.** When successive papers use different cell types, propulsion mechanisms, construction methods or environments, the article should not transfer capacities across generations without a source.
25. **Label evidence provenance.** Mark same-laboratory extension, computational modelling, review, conceptual defence and independent empirical replication separately to prevent apparent evidence multiplication.
26. **Add an evidential-language lint rule.** Flag transitions from “does not entail,” “does not establish,” “can be explained without” or “no verdict on its own” to “no evidence,” “irrelevant” or “decoupled” unless a likelihood or comparative-marker argument is supplied.
27. **Add an author-position consistency graph.** A page stating that Levin is phenomenally agnostic should automatically be checked against pages citing his panpsychist, continuity or sentience work.
28. **Propagate dependency corrections across the cluster.** A correction in *The Engineered Decoupling* acknowledging Rouleau and Levin should trigger review of the target article, apex synthesis and link descriptions that still attribute categorical agnosticism to Levin.
29. **Require one strong source from each major side of a live controversy.** For basal cognition, that means at minimum one programme advocate, one mechanistic critic and one cautious intermediary—not merely several papers from the originating laboratory.
30. **Make the scope-and-hedge pre-check a formal referee step.** Record which obvious charges are defeated by the article’s own qualifications before generating criticism, as this audit has done.
31. **Separate framework application from external evidence in every synthesis.** “The Map’s neural-interface commitment licenses withholding” should be labelled framework-internal; it should not re-enter later as empirical support for neural specificity.
32. **Replace expert non-assertion as an evidence category.** Investigator caution can document an experiment’s epistemic limit, but should never be counted as evidence for phenomenal absence or exact evidential neutrality without a separately defended bridge premise.
