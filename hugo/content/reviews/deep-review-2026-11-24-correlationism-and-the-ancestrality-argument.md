## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found
- **Misattribution (Golumbia 2016)**: the article said "Zahavi and Golumbia both trace *Korrelativismus* to Beck in 1928". Golumbia's full text (25 pp. with notes, grepped for Beck, Korrelativ, correlativ, 1928) never mentions Beck or an earlier use of the term; what he says about the term is that Meillassoux "appears to admit that he introduced" it to sidestep the anti-idealist literature. The Beck point is Zahavi's alone (ms p. 4, n. 3: "'correlationism' was in fact used and defined much earlier. Here is Beck in 1928"). The error came from the research note, which read only Golumbia's abstract, and survived the 2026-09-29 review, which verified Beck in Zahavi only. Corrected to "Zahavi finds *Korrelativismus* used and defined by Beck in 1928". The research note's two lines (L158, L222) were corrected with a dated note. No other Map page cites Golumbia.
- **Inexact Map-internal quotation**: the article put "interfacing with" in quotation marks for [prebiotic-collapse](/concepts/prebiotic-collapse/); that page reads "interfaces with" (L90, L150). Changed to the verbatim form with the clause re-cast.
- **Inexact quotation (Meillassoux 2012, subjectalism definition)**: "antimaterialism" → "anti-materialism". The transcript breaks the word at a line end ("anti-" / "materialism") and writes "anti-materialisms" and "anti-materialist" unbroken elsewhere; the 2026-09-29 ledger had the hyphenated form.
- **Paraphrase drift (Meillassoux 2012, p. 15)**: "unless the emergence is accepted as reasonless" replaced with his own words, unless *ex nihilo* emergence is understood as "at once entirely rational and entirely immanent". He calls the emergence "rational, but not 'reasonable' i.e. submitted to the Principle of Sufficient Reason" (p. 14), so "reasonless" invited the wrong reading.
