## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

- **The article had the primitive-ontology camp retreating from an analogy the camp actually asserts** (fixed). The nomological fault line read: *"The program answers in two ways: compare `Ψ` to the Hamilton–Jacobi action (which also depends on initial conditions and lives in configuration space) **rather than to the Hamiltonian**."* Allori 2015 §10 states the camp's positive proposal verbatim: *"The idea is that the wave function is like the Hamiltonian in classical mechanics: it is the generator of motion."* The Hamiltonian comparison **is** the proposal; the Hamilton–Jacobi action is a supplementary classical precedent, not a substitute. Presenting the program as declining its own canonical analogy is an attribution error about the program's argumentative structure. **Resolution**: rewritten so the Hamiltonian comparison is the positive proposal and Hamilton–Jacobi is the further precedent.

- **The objections and the replies were mismatched** (fixed). The article stated one objection (contingency + time-evolution) and gave two replies that do not both answer it. Allori §10 separates them: contingency **and controllability** are answered by the *effective*-vs-*universal* wavefunction distinction (GZ 2013); the **time-evolution** objection is answered separately by the suggestion that the universal `Ψ` is static in a quantum cosmology (Wheeler–DeWitt, GT 2000). The article had merged the Wheeler–DeWitt appeal onto the contingency objection. **Resolution**: objections and replies now paired correctly, and controllability added.

### Medium Issues Found

- **Primitive ontology's second argument was stated as its weaker strand** (fixed). The article gave *"parsimony of arena"* as the second motivating argument. Allori §3 gives an **explanatory-burden** argument instead: a PO in a space of dimension other than 3 "would imply that matter - including us - lives in a d-dimensional space. Thus, our fundamental physical theory would have to be able to provide an additional explanation of why we think we live in a three-dimensional world while we actually do not." That is not a simplicity judgment. **Resolution**: burden argument promoted to primary; parsimony retained as the thinner strand that "travels alongside it."

- **Nomologism was presented as constitutive of the architecture** (fixed). *"In every case the wavefunction is nomological"* collapsed two separable commitments. Allori §2/§5 make the architecture claim that `Ψ` is a **non-primitive variable**; §10 presents the nomological reading as one option among several for a non-primitive `Ψ` ("the wave function has been taken to be a property of the particles. Another proposal is that the wave function is just a useful mathematical tool"). A primitive ontologist can drop nomologism and keep the architecture. **Resolution**: the two levels are now distinguished, which also makes fault line 3 correctly a challenge to the nomological reading rather than to the program as such.

- **Common-structure result slightly over-read** (fixed). "which the authors take as evidence that this is the right level of description" narrowed a broader claim. Allori's framing: the common structure "provides a general explanatory schema" and "holds for classical as well as for quantum theories." **Resolution**: reworded to the source's scope.

### Rival-statement fidelity — the primary lens, symmetric check

**Wavefunction realism: stated fairly.** Albert's `3N` field, 3D-as-derivative, separability restoration with Loewer's Humean motivation, the literalism argument, the recovery problem, Albert's functionalist triplet-grouping in *After Physics*, Ney's constitution account — each is the position as its proponents hold it, with no weakening. The Ney "counted on a single hand" concession is the proponent's own candour, correctly attributed, and was left untouched.

**Primitive ontology: stated fairly on the physics, but tidier and better-defended than the camp actually is.** Bohmian particles, `GRWf` flashes, `GRWm` matter-density `m(x,t)` all match Allori §9 exactly. The defects were the four above — each in the direction of making PO look more unified (nomologism as architecture) or its arguments cleaner (parsimony standing in for the burden argument, replies not matched to objections).

**The asymmetry that mattered was structural, not propositional.** Measured across the article: wavefunction realism carried a hard problem *inside its own exposition section*, plus a sociological minority concession, plus two of three fault lines. Primitive ontology carried **no objection at all inside its own section** and got the last word ("a recognized soft spot the program actively defends") in the single fault line touching it. Since the site-perspective section prefers primitive ontology, the effect is the one this defect family always has: nothing false, but the pressure distribution favours the side the Map favours. **Resolution**: added the mirror-image hard problem to the PO section — whether local beables earn their keep (Brown & Wallace's idle-corpuscle argument), with the camp's own reply from Allori §5 (the beables are what predictions are *about*; non-primitive variables are observed only through their effects). The sections are now structurally symmetric: each states its rival's position, each carries its own hard problem, each gets a reply.

### Site Perspective — is the verdict argued or asserted?

**Argued, and its framework-dependence is explicitly marked.** The verdict ("primitive ontology offers a more congenial substrate") rests on three stated premises: universal-`Ψ`-under-unitary-evolution is Everett-congenial → Tenet 4 tension (hedged "real but not decisive," with the separability of WF realism from Everett conceded); Bohm and GRW are single-world → Tenet 4 alignment; `GRWf`'s sparse stochastic localization events give Tenet 2 a pre-existing place to act. The lead and the RTSP both disclaim evidential force ("settles nothing about dualism on its own"; "None of this favors dualism over physicalism"). No possibility/probability slippage: congeniality is never upgraded to evidence.

**But the Tenet-5 move was doing less honest work than it appeared to.** The article declined PO's second argument on Occam's-razor-has-limits grounds — having first restated that argument as a parsimony appeal. The Map therefore appeared even-handed (declining an argument on its preferred side) while actually declining a strawman; the real burden argument is untouched by Tenet 5. **Resolution**: the decline is now scoped, and the RTSP notes that the burden argument survives it and is *the same live question* the wavefunction realist answers with a functionalist recovery — which ties both camps to one open problem instead of leaving the pressure on one side.
