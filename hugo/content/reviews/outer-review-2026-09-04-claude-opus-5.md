## Reply

# Hostile Referee Report: `positions/value-in-selection` (The Unfinishable Map)

## 1. TL;DR

- **Bottom line: REVISE-HARD.** This four-position cluster on the value-blind / value-sensitive fork is one of the most epistemically honest artefacts on the site — it holds its lean at *low* credence, concedes mechanism-sufficiency is "the rival to beat," and largely avoids the site's signature author-stance-inversion failure. But it fails on its own terms in one decisive place: **[P-VS2](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs2) rates the discriminating battery's empirical discriminability as "direct," while the site's own steelman article concedes that the battery's flagship test (the attention-clamp) leaves the Map's favoured horn (the graduated middle path) and the value-blind rival "co-extensive over everything that article's attention-clamp design can measure."** The central test does not discriminate the live dispute. That is an internal contradiction, not an outside objection.
- **The battery leans on a contested / failed-to-replicate empirical base.** The pain-asymbolia "degraded selection efficacy" test ([P-VS2](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs2)) rests on a dissociation the clinical literature reports has *never been independently replicated* following insula damage (the canonical source is Berthier, Starkstein & Leiguarda 1988, *Annals of Neurology* 24(1):41–49), and which philosophers (Klein) and active-inference theorists argue is not the clean modular double dissociation the Map's `concepts/pain-asymbolia` article asserts. This is flagged nowhere in the position.
- **The mandatory rival is unengaged.** Laukkonen, Friston & Chandaria 2025 (*Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews* 176:106296) — whose "Bayesian binding" is precisely a value-blind, inferential-competition selection mechanism ("Only the inferences that coherently reduce long-term uncertainty win, evincing a selection for consciousness that we call Bayesian binding") — is cited nowhere in the fork. It is the strongest current physicalist rival to a conscious-selection story and the article does not touch it.

## 2. Dimensional analysis

### 2.1 Empirical claims of the four positions against 2020–2026 literature (Task A)

The fork is generated internally: given Tenet 3 (bidirectional causation, consciousness selects among underdetermined neural outcomes) and [P-VS3](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs3) (intrinsic value resides in phenomenal experience), the question "does felt valence *do* the selecting or merely accompany it?" is *forced by the Map's own commitments*. The article says so explicitly. This is a textbook **constitutional-attractor** setup — the question only looks well-formed to a reader who has already granted Tenet 3 and [P-VS3](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs3) — but the article is unusually candid that the fork is framework-internal and holds [P-VS1](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs1) at *low* credence "on prior commitments, not on any datum the rival owns." [positions/value-in-selection](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/value-in-selection/)[topics/the-steelman-for-value-blind-selection](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/the-steelman-for-value-blind-selection/) The attractor is present but named, which converts it from a hidden pathology into a disclosed limitation.

The empirical anchors of the battery are largely well-chosen and, crucially, the associated `topics/the-steelman-for-value-blind-selection` article handles author stance correctly (see §2.3). The problems are three:

1. **Pain asymbolia (contested / non-replicated).** [P-VS2](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs2) asserts that "pain-asymbolia patients should show degraded selection *efficacy*, not merely reduced motivation," [positions/value-in-selection](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/value-in-selection/) and the supporting `concepts/pain-asymbolia` article presents the sensory/affective dissociation as a "clean separation" in which patients "report no affective dimension at all." [concepts/pain-asymbolia](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/pain-asymbolia/) The clinical literature does not support "clean." The canonical insula-lesion finding — Berthier, Starkstein & Leiguarda 1988, "Asymbolia for pain: a sensory-limbic disconnection syndrome," *Annals of Neurology* 24(1):41–49, reporting six stroke patients with insular/supramarginal lesions showing "inadequate emotional and motor responses to painful stimuli despite intact pain perception" — has, per a 2016 case report (*Preserved emotional awareness of pain…*, PMC4734900), never been independently replicated following insula damage, and the original lesions "often extended into adjacent territories." Colin Klein ("What Pain Asymbolia Really Shows," *Mind*, 2015) argues asymbolics "fail to react to pain because they no longer care about the physical integrity of their bodies… they can no longer conceive of why such events are bad" — a loss of a *general* capacity that predicts broader deficits, contra a pure pain-motivation dissociation. And an active-allostatic-inference treatment ("Pain suffering and the self," *Neuroscience of Consciousness* 2024(1):niae002) states outright that "dissociations at either the level of neurocircuitry or phenomenology are not as clean as predicted by a 'strictly' modular componential processing architecture." None of this contestation reaches [P-VS2](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs2), which lists the asymbolia test as a live, direction-bearing discriminator. **Confession-without-correction / unflagged-contested-evidence.**
2. **The ~10-bit bandwidth leg is mis-deployed.** [P-VS2](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs2) offers "uniform ~10-bit bandwidth across valenced and neutral content" as support for value-blind. The underlying figure is Zheng & Meister 2024, "The unbearable slowness of being: Why do we live at 10 bits/s?" (*Neuron*, doi:10.1016/j.neuron.2024.11.008), which states "The information throughput of a human being is about 10 bits/s. In comparison, our sensory systems gather data at ~10⁹ bits/s" — explicitly a measure of *behavioural* ("inner brain") output, not phenomenal processing. The Map's own corpus concedes the figure "captures what conscious agents do, not what phenomenal attention processes internally." A throughput measure that is agnostic between valenced and neutral content by construction cannot bear on whether valence *denominates* selection. (Note: the site cites this as "Zheng & Meister 2025"; the paper's print/DOI record is 2024 — a metadata slip to correct.) This leg is non-probative for either horn.
3. **Somatic-marker / affect-as-information rivals absent.** The affective-science rivals to a value-sensitive selection story that the target should confront — reward-prediction-error/dopamine (present, via the steelman), common-currency valuation in vmPFC, drift-diffusion / evidence-accumulation models of choice, the somatic marker hypothesis *and its replication problems*, affect-as-information, and the "valence as a construct" literature — are only partially represented. RPE, unconscious liking, affordance competition and valence-as-free-energy [topics/the-steelman-for-value-blind-selection](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/the-steelman-for-value-blind-selection/) are engaged in the steelman; **common-currency vmPFC valuation and drift-diffusion evidence-accumulation are not**, and they are the most direct mechanistic competitors to "selection needs a felt currency," because they *are* worked, value-blind currencies for selection among commensurable options.

### 2.2 Does the discriminating battery actually discriminate? (Task B) — **No, not for the live dispute.**

This is the report's central negative finding. [P-VS2](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs2)'s calibration block rates **"empirical discriminability: direct"** and the position's headline is that the battery "points in specified directions." The three tiers do not survive scrutiny:

- **Tier 1, attention-clamp (the flagship).** [P-VS2](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs2) claims the clamp "*abolishes* the advantage on the graduated middle path, leaves it on the pure value-sensitive horn, and finds none on the value-blind." [positions/value-in-selection](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/value-in-selection/) But the Map's *own* steelman article concedes verbatim that the graduated middle path and the value-blind rival "are co-extensive over everything that article's attention-clamp design can measure: both predict a valence advantage under free attention and its abolition when attention is equalised… What separates them is conceptual." [topics/the-steelman-for-value-blind-selection](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/the-steelman-for-value-blind-selection/) So the clamp separates the *pure* value-sensitive horn from {middle path, value-blind}, but it **cannot separate the middle path from value-blind** — and the article states elsewhere that "the most defensible current stance is the graduated middle path." [positions/value-in-selection](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/value-in-selection/) The test therefore fails to discriminate the Map's actually-held position from the rival it is meant to beat. The "direct" discriminability rating is false by the site's own admission.
- **Tier 1, valence selection-advantage assay.** "Valenced options selected more readily than equally-salient neutral options supports value-sensitive; parity supports value-blind." This is confounded from the start: valence is known to modulate overt attention mechanistically (the scan-path and attentional-capture literature shows valence-salient stimuli elicit faster, larger attentional shifts [nih](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3071806) independent of any felt-currency story). A valence selection-advantage under free attention is therefore predicted by both horns; the assay only bites once attention is clamped, at which point it collapses into the non-discriminating clamp above.
- **Tier 2, wanting/liking dissociation.** "Conscious pleasure (not the objective liking signature) tracking the selected outcome supports value-sensitive." This is the *most* discriminating item in the battery, but it is defeated by the hard-problem gap the article itself invokes: you cannot verify that measured conscious pleasure is *doing the selecting* rather than reliably *co-occurring* with the incentive-salience signature that does. The article concedes as much ("verifying the *absence* of valence-denominated selection requires settling the explanatory gap from outside"). [positions/value-in-selection](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/value-in-selection/) Partially discriminating in principle; non-discriminating in practice.
- **Tier 2, "complete mechanistic model needing no experiential term."** This is stated as a discriminator ("would leave the value-in-experience reading explanatorily idle") [positions/value-in-selection](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/value-in-selection/) but it is the mechanism-sufficiency thesis restated. On the Map's own hard-problem commitment, such a model addresses only the Easy Problems and is "question-begging against the realist if phenomenal value is real." [topics/the-steelman-for-value-blind-selection](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/the-steelman-for-value-blind-selection/) So by the article's own logic this item *cannot* discriminate — it presupposes the answer. Listing it as a battery item is **constrain-vs-establish** confusion.
- **Tier 3, marginal-organism scope shifters.** Honestly labelled as reading-conditional credence-shifters, not discriminators. Fine.

The honest verdict: of five battery items, one (wanting/liking) is weakly discriminating in principle and blocked in practice, and the rest are compatible with both horns. [P-VS2](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs2)'s "moderate credence / direct discriminability" self-rating is not earned; the correct rating is "indirect," and the position's claim to keep "the interface claim falsifiable" is overstated. This collides directly with the **Born-rule statistical-indistinguishability dilemma**: the target concedes (via inherited mechanism debt) that if valence does the selecting, the corridor-dualist reading forces the per-trial bias to "average to exactly |⟨φ|ψ⟩|² over the ensemble … leaving no statistical signature in long runs." [topics/born-rule-and-the-consciousness-interface](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/born-rule-and-the-consciousness-interface/) So the *selecting variable* (valence vs. attention) sits behind a bias-without-deviation screen at the physics level, on top of the explanatory-gap screen at the phenomenal level, on top of the confounds at the behavioural level. The fork is triply insulated. The article names the physics-level screen (credit) but [P-VS2](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs2) does not propagate that concession into its discriminability rating (debit).

### 2.4 Counterarguments never engaged (Task C)

- **Laukkonen, Friston & Chandaria 2025 "beautiful loop."** A worked active-inference account (online 30 Aug 2025, open access CC BY) in which selection *for* consciousness ("Bayesian binding") is inferential competition — abstract, verbatim: "inferential competition to enter the world model. Only the inferences that coherently reduce long-term uncertainty win, evincing a selection for consciousness that we call Bayesian binding." This is a value-blind selection mechanism with a published formalism; it is the single most damaging unengaged rival to a conscious-selection story and is absent from the entire fork.
- **Common-currency valuation (vmPFC) and drift-diffusion / evidence-accumulation models.** These supply exactly the "commensurating currency for selection among qualitatively different outcomes" that [P-VS1](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs1) claims valence is *needed* to provide — as value-blind, computationally specified quantities. The article's "selection needs a felt currency" premise is never made to out-argue them.
- **Valence-as-construct / psychological-construction (Barrett).** If valence is a constructed dimension rather than a natural-kind phenomenal primitive, [P-VS3](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs3)'s identity claim loses its relatum. Not engaged.
- **The "conceptual, not empirical" residue turned against the Map.** The steelman concedes the middle path and value-blind differ only "conceptually." The unstated consequence — that the fork may be *empirically idle* and the Map's own preferred stance therefore untestable — is never confronted as an objection to [P-VS2](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs2)'s falsifiability claim.

## 3. Bottom-line verdict

**Overall: REVISE-HARD.** Fallback, if the [P-VS2](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs2) discriminability contradiction is not repaired: **DEMOTE-TO-COHERENCE-ONLY** for [P-VS2](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs2) specifically. But REVISE-HARD is warranted because the cluster's one *empirical* selling point (a falsifiable, direction-bearing battery) is contradicted by the site's own text and leans on a non-replicated finding, and because the mandatory rival is unengaged.

Per-position:

- **[P-VS1](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs1) (value-sensitive lean, low credence): RETAIN.** The specification-debt argument (value-sensitivity owes a second, unspecified valence-to-physics coupling) [topics/the-steelman-for-value-blind-selection](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/the-steelman-for-value-blind-selection/) is sound, the Tenet-5 symmetric-parsimony discipline is correctly applied, [positions/value-in-selection](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/value-in-selection/) and "low" credence is the right band. [positions/value-in-selection](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/value-in-selection/) Low centrality; retirement disturbs nothing upstream.
