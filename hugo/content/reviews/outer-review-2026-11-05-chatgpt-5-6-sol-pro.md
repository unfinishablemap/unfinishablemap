# Full-site outer review of *The Unfinishable Map*

**Assessment date:** 5 November 2026. I treated the live site and its changelog through 4 November as authoritative, rather than relying on older indexed versions. This is a whole-framework review: I examined the site’s constitutional pages, section architecture, positions registers, major synthesis clusters and current review record, with targeted checks of original sources behind the most load-bearing empirical and philosophical claims. It is not a line-by-line audit of every article or bibliography.

## Executive judgement

*The Unfinishable Map* is presently stronger as a **self-auditing map of what an interactionist-dualist theory would have to explain** than as evidence that such a theory is true.

Its most valuable achievement is the increasingly explicit inventory of debts that a serious interactionist account incurs: subject–body pairing, subject census, semantic matching, temporal jurisdiction, multi-subject composition, Born-rule preservation, collapse priority, neural amplification and empirical identification. The positions register now separates credence, external evidence, structural centrality, maturity and discriminability; the methodology register explicitly says that tenets can remove defeaters but cannot raise an evidence grade, and that repeated convergence may be one framework reading its own structure several times. Those are unusually strong methodological disciplines. [positions](/positions/)

The main problem is that the site’s **public constitution has not caught up with its own self-criticism**. It still presents five tenets as the foundation and positions as downstream, while its live pages acknowledge three additional background posits and several supposedly ordinary positions that actually supply rationales for tenets. The operative theory is therefore not a five-tenet structure. It is a layered package containing metaphysical commitments, subjecthood commitments, a one-world ontology, a causal thesis, a methodological rule and a highly speculative physical implementation programme. [tenets](/tenets/)

The strongest positive claims the evidence supports are roughly these:

- consciousness presents a real explanatory problem if one accepts phenomenal realism;
- ordinary neural, behavioural and clinical evidence imposes demanding constraints on theories of consciousness and agency;
- decoherence alone does not select a unique observed outcome;
- familiar objections to naïve readings of Libet, simple neural identity claims and crude “activity level equals consciousness” models are often too quick.

None of those findings independently identifies a non-physical subject, a consciousness-specific selector or a quantum interface. The site now often concedes that distinction locally, but synthesis pages still sometimes turn compatibility, underdetermination or removal of a physicalist argument into positive support for the Map.

My overall assessment is therefore:

| Dimension | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Philosophical cartography | Strong and unusually self-critical |
| Coherence of the full package | Substantial, but dependent on unstated or weakly integrated foundations |
| Independent empirical support for interactionist dualism | Weak |
| Quantum-interface mechanism | Programme-stage; not yet a worked theory |
| Falsifiability | Uneven and currently compromised by an unresolved Born-preservation quantifier |
| Editorial governance | Sophisticated disclosure, incomplete enforcement |
| Best contribution | A specification of the costs and open problems of interactionist dualism |
| Largest risk | Treating an increasingly elaborate description of those costs as progress toward confirming the theory |

## 1. The live site is materially better than its older indexed versions

The current site has incorporated important corrections rather than merely accumulating favourable material. The 4 November changelog records source-level repairs to causal-exclusion and type–token discussions, including correcting attribution, distinguishing chance-fixing from causal completeness, and replacing inaccurate language about Everettian probability. Other recent changes corrected overstatements in agency, weak-measurement and quantum-interpretation discussions. [workflow/changelog](/workflow/changelog/)

Three especially significant advances should be preserved.

First, the tenets page now says openly that the five tenets are chosen starting points rather than proofs. It also distinguishes tenet-level neutrality from the agency cluster’s substance-leaning ontology, scopes the anti-Everett argument more narrowly, and acknowledges that objective collapse would already solve the definiteness problem without consciousness. [tenets](/tenets/)

Second, the site has named three background commitments previously left implicit:

1. a numerically determinate and persisting subject;
2. objective single-outcome actualisation;
3. global exclusion of unchosen alternatives.

The same page correctly observes that these make the five tenets non-independent and that the measurement problem cannot itself be evidence for conscious selection once objective reduction is independently posited. [tenets/background-commitments](/tenets/background-commitments/)

Third, the positions register has abandoned the fiction that every position is downstream and freely retireable. Closed individualism, diachronic persistence and part of the mental-causation register supply premises needed by Tenets 3 and 4. The site now calls promotion of determinate subjecthood into the foundations an open decision. [positions](/positions/)

These are substantial admissions. The remaining criticism is that they have not yet changed the site’s top-level architecture.

# 2. The central structural problem: the five tenets are not the real constitution

The five tenets occupy five different logical levels:

- **Dualism** is an ontological commitment.
- **Minimal Quantum Interaction** is a physical implementation hypothesis.
- **Bidirectional Interaction** is a causal commitment.
- **No Many Worlds** is a quantum-interpretive and world-structure commitment.
- **Limits on Occam’s razor** is a methodological or epistemic commitment.

There is nothing inherently illegitimate about grouping heterogeneous commitments. The problem is that calling all five equally foundational obscures which ones are protected starting points and which ones should be vulnerable theoretical conjectures.

In particular, Minimal Quantum Interaction is not entailed by dualism plus interaction. One could instead propose a novel classical psychophysical law, emergent downward causation, lawful overdetermination or another failure of physical causal completeness. The quantum route may be attractive, but the Map’s preferred form—post-decoherence selection—is rated by its own register as external-evidence grade D, programme-stage, framework-internal and lacking a worked toy model. Treating that mechanism family as constitutional rather than conjectural makes a highly defeasible implementation choice look coequal with phenomenal irreducibility. [tenets](/tenets/)

Conversely, the determinate persisting subject is officially only a background posit, yet it does more foundational work than some stated tenets. It supplies the bearer required by the agency cluster, the further fact required by the indexical objection to Everett, and the identity fact presupposed by copying and survival discussions. The Map’s full package is operationally substance-dualist even though Tenet 1 remains nominally neutral. [tenets](/tenets/)

A more accurate constitutional stack would be:

1. **Metaphysical commitments:** phenomenal irreducibility, actual mental efficacy, determinate subjecthood and objective single-world actuality.
2. **Methodological commitment:** parsimony is defeasible and cannot decide the issue by itself.
3. **Interface programme:** some psychophysical law links subjects and physical systems.
4. **Preferred mechanism conjecture:** post-decoherence outcome selection under a Born-preserving constraint.
5. **Empirical implementation hypotheses:** candidate generation, amplification, localisation, semantic matching, bandwidth, value-sensitive selection and multi-agent composition.
6. **Applied verdicts:** agency, AI consciousness, animal consciousness, personal identity and ethics.

That structure would reveal where evidence could modify the programme. At present, too much of the mechanism is inside the constitution while too much of the actual constitution is labelled “background”.

# 3. Further implications not yet installed as canonical conclusions

The site already names many of its own problems. The following are stronger cross-cluster consequences that I did not find installed as canonical positions, although their ingredients appear in recent concepts, reviews or research notes.

## 3.1 Local conscious selection requires a global-history coherence law

The current discussion recognises a multi-agent composition problem, joint Born constraints, no-signalling concerns and the need for collapse priority. It also requires exactly one globally actual outcome rather than branch-relative actuality. [tenets/background-commitments](/tenets/background-commitments/)

Together, these commitments require more than a collection of local laws of the form \(P(O\mid C,X)\). They require a joint law over complete or at least extendible histories:

\[
P(H\mid C_1,\ldots,C_n,X),
\]

such that every local subject-conditioned transition:

- belongs to one mutually consistent world history;
- preserves the appropriate joint quantum statistics;
- does not permit signalling;
- respects whatever relativistic event-ordering constraints the theory accepts;
- composes correctly when several subjects bear on entangled or causally linked systems;
- remains consistent with later intentions and later physical opportunities.

This creates a new dilemma.

If the global law harmonises all local selections in advance, local agency becomes a constraint inside a global optimisation or consistency structure. The theory begins to resemble global-history or boundary-condition models rather than forward local interaction. If local subjects select independently, the theory lacks a guarantee that their choices compose into one possible world. If a preferred temporal ordering resolves the issue, the Map inherits a substantial relativistic commitment.

The missing object is therefore not merely a **multi-agent selection rule** but a **single-world global-history coherence law**. Tenet 4 makes that law compulsory.

## 3.2 The semantic-ratification dilemma

The Map now correctly identifies semantic matching as an unsolved component of the psychophysical indexing law: the theory must say which brain-generated candidate physically expresses the subject’s present content. [concepts/psychophysical-indexing-law](/concepts/psychophysical-indexing-law/)

But this generates a deeper dilemma.

Suppose the brain prepares candidates \(a_1,\ldots,a_n\), and consciousness with content \(C\) selects the candidate that expresses \(C\). A matching relation \(M(C,a_i)\) must determine which candidate is appropriate.

There are three possibilities:

1. **The matching relation is physically computed.**  
   The physical system already represents the candidate meanings and has a content-sensitive disposition linking intention to action. Consciousness may still determine the token outcome, but it does not explain how meaning guides behaviour; it ratifies a semantic relation the physical machinery has already built.

2. **The conscious subject supplies the matching relation.**  
   The channel carries more than bare selection. It transmits a structured content-to-physical mapping. The “smallest possible influence” is then not merely choosing one outcome: it includes a rich psychophysical semantics.

3. **The matching relation is a primitive psychophysical law.**  
   The theory remains coherent, but the primitive law must encode an enormous and plastic mapping among phenomenal content, individual learning histories, neural representational schemes, language, context and available action policies.

This is the **semantic-ratification dilemma**: either the brain already performs the content-guided work, the mental channel is richer than the advertised selection-only channel, or an exceptionally complex semantic bridge law is added.

The Map’s selection-criterion problem approaches the first and third horns, but does not yet draw this explicit consequence for the claimed minimality of the interface. [concepts/selection-criterion-problem](/concepts/selection-criterion-problem/)

## 3.3 Consciousness needs jurisdiction over physical collapse, not merely influence upon it

The background ontology posits physical objective reduction before and outside consciousness. Consciousness then modulates or pre-empts outcome settlement in suitable brains. Recent research notes identify this as a collapse-priority problem. [tenets](/tenets/)

The stronger consequence is that a reliable interface needs an **event-jurisdiction law** answering:

- which physical reductions are reserved for conscious selection;
- when the conscious opportunity opens and closes;
- what prevents baseline collapse from acting first;
- whether consciousness delays, overrides or replaces the ordinary transition;
- what happens when consciousness is weak, divided, unconscious or disputed;
- how jurisdiction changes during sleep, anaesthesia, split-brain states, development and death.

This is stronger than “a tiny bias at a quantum indeterminacy”. It assigns consciousness authority over which transition law is permitted to settle a token event. Dynamically, the perturbation might remain small; legally, so to speak, its jurisdiction is extensive.

The current research note should therefore be promoted from a local mechanism debt into a constitutional component of Tenet 2.

## 3.4 Energetic minimality implies no nomological minimality

The proposed interaction can be minuscule in energy and still require a large amount of fundamental law. The current programme needs, at minimum:

- a subject census;
- subject–body pairing;
- physical subsystem factorisation;
- candidate individuation;
- semantic matching;
- temporal jurisdiction;
- multi-subject composition;
- Born-rule balancing;
- no-signalling and relativistic consistency;
- collapse priority;
- diachronic persistence;
- global-history coherence.

The interface is therefore potentially **energetically minimal but nomologically maximal**. The Interface Specification Programme itself now lists several of these debts, while the indexing-law page argues that they form one typed transition object rather than separable embellishments. [apex/interface-specification-programme](/apex/interface-specification-programme/)

Tenet 5 can prevent complexity from serving as an automatic refutation. It cannot turn complexity into no cost at all. Model comparison still needs to count:

- independent primitive laws;
- free mappings;
- special-case clauses;
- tuning needed to preserve observed statistics;
- unification and compression;
- novel predictions.

“Minimal Quantum Interaction” should consequently be glossed everywhere as **minimal detectable physical perturbation**, not minimal theory.

## 3.5 Universal causal efficacy conflicts with the ten-bit control picture

The interface architecture often uses the approximate ten-bit-per-second behavioural bottleneck to picture consciousness selecting a few policy-level actions from brain-generated menus. The source paper, however, concerns human behavioural throughput, not a measured consciousness–physics channel, and the Map now acknowledges that caveat. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39694032/)

Tenet 3 is elsewhere read as universal actual efficacy: every conscious token must make some difference to its substrate. That includes passive perception, pain, mood, dreams, non-deliberative awareness, infant and animal experience, and perhaps minimally conscious organisms—not merely the handful of decisions expressed in overt behaviour. The AI register already acknowledges that universal actual efficacy outruns the existential self-stultification argument and that weakening it reopens epiphenomenal artificial experience. [positions/ai-consciousness-scope](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/)

This produces a three-way choice:

- **Universal token efficacy:** the channel is much richer than a ten-bit policy selector.
- **Coarse shared efficacy:** many phenomenal differences have the same physical effect, weakening content-specific causal relevance.
- **Existential or dispositional efficacy:** some experiences can be causally idle, requiring Tenet 3 to be weakened.

The bandwidth architecture and Tenet 3’s quantifier should therefore be resolved together rather than in separate clusters.

# 4. Structural weaknesses among the site sections

## 4.1 Topics and concepts no longer occupy distinct epistemic roles

The site describes topics as single-subject explorations and concepts as definitional building blocks. In practice, many concepts advance controversial Map-specific theses—*Integration as Activity*, *Causal Relevance of Semantic Content*, *Selection-Only Channel* and *Selection-Criterion Problem* are arguments or programme components, not merely definitions. Topics meanwhile can function as broad syntheses or applied verdicts. [topics](/topics/)

This is not simply a filing problem. A reader is entitled to treat a “concept” as specifying vocabulary rather than smuggling in a substantive premise. Each article should carry an explicit type label such as:

- neutral concept;
- contested concept;
- framework-defined construct;
- empirical review;
- argument;
- mechanism conjecture;
- applied verdict.

The present content-type labels do not reliably predict evidential role.

## 4.2 The voids section contains a live self-contradiction about evidence

The current taxonomy correctly says the void catalogue is largely framework-internal coherence and that illusionism and panpsychism can predict important parts of the same clustering. Its own positions register says tenet-generated voids are costs, not confirmations. Yet the same apex later says that dualism gains cumulative support and that Tenet 5 receives strong support from the pattern. The current tenet-alignment check still flags those lines. [apex/taxonomy-of-voids](/apex/taxonomy-of-voids/)

This is a particularly clear example of the site speaking in two evidential registers:

- the audit register: compatible, coherence-relative, common-cause discounted;
- the promotional register: convergent support, progressively implausible coincidence.

The former is justified. The latter is not, unless the site first defines a candidate denominator, demonstrates that the clustering was predicted before the catalogue was formed, and produces observations that differ across rival partitions.

## 4.3 Apex articles are the main locus of confidence inflation

Apex articles are supposed to synthesise existing topics, concepts and arguments. Because synthesis creates a narrative, however, it can make several compatible observations look like mutually reinforcing discoveries. [apex](/apex/)

Three recurring forms are visible:

1. **Analogy inflation:** several vocabularies describe a similar shape, so the shape is treated as evidence for the Map’s ontology.
2. **Concession loss:** atomic articles acknowledge comparator models, but the apex preserves an older, stronger thesis.
3. **Mechanism inheritance:** an apex uses conscious selection as explanatory machinery even though the positions register permits it to be cited only as framework-internal coherence pending a toy model.

The process wing is illustrative. Atomic pages increasingly concede that activity language fits process physicalism and enactivism, and that borrowing Whiteheadian ideas while retaining a separate subject runs against Whitehead’s own ontology. The apex nonetheless presents process thought as reinforcing all five tenets. A recent internal review identifies the apex as the lagging member of an otherwise well-calibrated cluster. [apex/process-and-consciousness](/apex/process-and-consciousness/)

Apex generation should be mechanically prohibited from using a stronger evidential verb than the weakest position on which the sentence depends.

## 4.4 The positions register is the strongest section, but it is not yet the source of truth

The multi-axis calibration is a major improvement. It correctly distinguishes a central claim from a well-supported claim and records shift conditions rather than only presenting conclusions. [positions](/positions/)

Four weaknesses remain.

First, calibration is not fully enforced. The positions page itself reports that validators and write paths have lagged behind the documented schema. [positions](/positions/)

Second, positions can be upstream or downstream, despite the register still being described primarily as a derived layer. This should be represented as a dependency graph, not a hierarchy. [positions](/positions/)

Third, “moderate credence” remains a largely authorial judgement. There is no stated prior, update rule or reference class showing why an external-evidence-grade-D mechanism deserves moderate rather than low credence.

Fourth, the register can disclose mechanism debt without imposing a demotion deadline. [P-Q10](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q10) has no worked model; [P-Q3](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q3) says exact Born preservation approaches ensemble-level epiphenomenalism; yet the mechanism can remain indefinitely “live” and continue supporting downstream applications. [positions/quantum-interface](/positions/quantum-interface/)

## 4.5 The review system detects errors better than it propagates corrections

The changelog is unusually transparent and often records exact source checks, wording changes and preserved caveats. That is a genuine strength. [workflow/changelog](/workflow/changelog/)

But the site’s own audits repeatedly find that corrections do not propagate automatically: a source page is repaired while sibling articles, index descriptions or apex syntheses retain the old inference. The current Born-preservation conflict is distributed across roughly thirty pages; voids evidence language has been repeatedly flagged while remaining live; process pages have been corrected without the apex fully following. [reviews/tenet-check-2026-10-15](/reviews/tenet-check-2026-10-15/)

The problem is that article links are being used as though they were claim dependencies. They are not. The site needs claim-level dependency tracking.

# 5. Tenet-by-tenet assessment

## Tenet 1: Dualism

### What survives

The tenet is now presented honestly as a chosen commitment in an unresolved dispute. The site also recognises that knowledge, conceivability and qualia arguments share premises and are not several independent proofs. That is appropriately calibrated. [tenets](/tenets/)

### Where it drifts

The agency cluster moves from:

1. agency needs a persisting bearer,
2. therefore the bearer is a non-physical subject,
3. therefore the full package leans toward substance dualism.

Only the first step is supported by the agent-causation literature cited. Helen Steward explicitly treats a complex organised animal as the causally efficacious substance. Her account shows that substance causation need not mean causation by an immaterial substance. [PhilPapers](https://philpapers.org/rec/STEASC-5)

The Map may still posit a non-physical bearer, but it needs an additional argument showing why the physical organism, an embodied process or an emergent whole cannot be the agent. “Agent causation needs a bearer” does not provide that argument.

### Required correction

Separate three claims throughout the agency cluster:

- some conscious properties are irreducible;
- agency needs a persisting bearer;
- that bearer is numerically distinct from the physical organism.

Only the first is Tenet 1. The second is a theory of agency. The third is the substance-dualist commitment needing independent defence.

## Tenet 2: Minimal Quantum Interaction

### What survives

The Map is right that environmental decoherence suppresses interference without, by itself, selecting a unique actual outcome. It is also right that post-decoherence selection avoids the specific need to maintain neural-scale coherence for milliseconds. The current quantum register properly says this solves only the timing objection, not the selection-law problem. [tenets](/tenets/)

### What is overstated

The tenets page says Tegmark’s estimates and the Hagan–Hameroff–Tuszynski recalculation leave the literature unconverged across roughly \(10^{-13}\) to \(10^{-4}\) seconds. The figures are reported correctly. But Hagan and colleagues were defending a specific Orch OR microtubule model and altered Tegmark’s physical assumptions accordingly. This is a model-contingent rebuttal by the proposal’s proponents, not independent evidence that biologically relevant coherent superpositions exist for the required duration. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0005025)

The fair conclusion is:

- Tegmark does not establish that every conceivable quantum-biological mechanism is impossible;
- Hagan et al. show that estimates depend strongly on the proposed state and environmental model;
- neither paper supplies empirical evidence for the neural coherent state the consciousness theory requires.

Post-decoherence selection avoids that entire dispute, but then owes an actualisation law, Born treatment, semantic mapping, pairing, amplification, jurisdiction and composition.

### The critical unresolved contradiction

[P-Q2](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q2) currently says outcomes remain Born-exact in every publicly conditionable context, leaving only conditionals on the private conscious state free. The tenets page and other quantum articles simultaneously propose intention-, task- or subject-conditioned departures as tests. Tasks and reported intentions are public conditioning variables. Both commitments cannot stand together. The site’s own audits identify this as a blocked human decision affecting about thirty pages. [reviews/outer-review-synthesis-2026-10-28](/reviews/outer-review-synthesis-2026-10-28/)

This is the most important formal inconsistency in the live corpus.

## Tenet 3: Bidirectional Interaction

### What the evidence supports

Experiments distinguish instructed from internally chosen attention, identify frontal theta and frontoparietal interactions, and reveal neural networks involved in voluntary control. Rajan et al. found replicable frontal-theta and frontoparietal dynamics during “willed attention”; more recent work models the choice as being formed in a frontoparietal decision network under the influence of pre-cue neural state and transmitted to the dorsal attention network. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29931088/)

That is evidence for neural differentiation between externally instructed and internally generated control. It is not evidence that consciousness, rather than the identified neural dynamics, contributes an additional cause.

Similarly, Schurger’s accumulator model weakens the claim that the averaged readiness potential records a settled unconscious decision long before conscious intention. It shows that stochastic fluctuations plus threshold crossing can generate the average waveform. It does not positively identify a later conscious selector. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1210467109)

The general pattern is:

- the evidence may defeat an overly simple anti-agency argument;
- defeating that argument reopens logical space;
- reopened logical space is not positive evidence for interactionism.

### The quantifier problem

The principal self-stultification argument, at most, supports an existential thesis: some phenomenal content must make some causal difference if reports and beliefs are to be about experience in the required way. Tenet 3 is often read universally: every conscious token must be physically efficacious. The positions register now acknowledges that the latter outruns the former. [positions](/positions/)

Until this is resolved, claims about passive experience, dreams, artificial phenomenality and minimal organisms will continue to oscillate between two different theories.

## Tenet 4: No Many Worlds

### What has improved

The live tenet is now substantially more honest than earlier versions. It distinguishes branch-egalitarian Everettianism from first-personally centred variants, concedes that the indexical objection does not by itself defeat the latter, and states that the argument depends on a non-deflationary subject. [tenets](/tenets/)

### What remains overstated

Sebens and Carroll do not simply ignore self-location. Their programme makes self-locating uncertainty central and derives Everettian credences from it. Saunders–Wallace-style accounts also preserve branch-relative continuity, reasons-responsiveness and ordinary first-person histories. The Map may reject those accounts, but the disagreement is whether there must be a further primitive fact of trans-branch identity—not whether Everettianism has failed to notice the question. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.7907)

The tenet should therefore say:

> The Map rejects Everettian accounts because it independently requires a numerically determinate subject and global nonactuality of alternatives; Everettian branch-relative identity does not satisfy those requirements.

That is clearer than saying Many Worlds cannot answer why “I” am this branch. It answers by denying that the requested further fact exists.

### Architectural consequence

No Many Worlds is not an independent quantum tenet. Its principal argument is a consequence of the subjecthood and global-exclusion commitments. The site already acknowledges this dependency; the navigation and tenet numbering should now reflect it. [tenets](/tenets/)

## Tenet 5: Limits on Occam’s razor

### What survives

Simplicity is not a deductive law of truth, and model comparison must not treat unfamiliar ontology as refuted merely because it introduces a new primitive. The site also correctly binds this discipline symmetrically, limiting its own use of ontological extravagance against Many Worlds. [tenets](/tenets/)

### Where it overreaches

Historical cases in which a more complex-looking theory succeeded show that simplicity can mislead when applied naïvely. They do not show that complexity has no evidential role.

Several distinct considerations are currently grouped as “Occam”:

- number of ontological categories;
- number of laws;
- number of free parameters;
- ad hoc exception clauses;
- predictive compression;
- fit to established theory;
- explanatory unification.

The Map is entitled to reject “one substance is automatically likelier than two”. It is not entitled to disregard the cost of an unspecified subject-indexed semantic law that must preserve Born statistics, coordinate agents and remain empirically hidden. Tenet 5 protects the possibility of such a law; it does not count as evidence that the law exists or relieve the programme of specifying it.

# 7. Targeted source audit

## 7.1 Decoherence and definite outcomes: broadly accurate

The Map’s claim that decoherence suppresses interference and yields an improper mixture without selecting a unique actual outcome is a defensible statement of the measurement problem. Its current pages also acknowledge that Bohmian, collapse, Everettian and pragmatist approaches supply different resolutions. The overstatement appears only when this is rendered as “physics leaves the outcome open” without the interpretive qualification. [tenets](/tenets/)

**Verdict:** accurate when stated as a claim about decoherence alone; overstated when presented as a theory-neutral gap that invites consciousness.

## 7.2 Tegmark versus Hagan: accurate numbers, weak evidential framing

Tegmark estimated neural decoherence times around \(10^{-13}\)–\(10^{-20}\) seconds. Hagan, Hameroff and Tuszyński argued that his chosen superposition did not match Orch OR and derived longer, model-specific estimates. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0005025)

**Verdict:** the Map quotes the dispute correctly but should not present the two calculations as independent empirical poles of an otherwise undecided field.

## 7.3 Willed attention: neural architecture, not interactionist causation

Rajan et al. found increased frontal theta, frontoparietal coherence and bidirectional Granger relations during internally chosen rather than instructed attention. The paper interprets these as neuronal signals involved in decision-making and communication between frontal and parietal control systems. It does not isolate consciousness or infer a non-physical influence. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29931088/)

**Verdict:** appropriate evidence for a distinction between internally generated and externally instructed control; not evidence for consciousness-specific selection.

## 7.4 Readiness potentials: a defeater removed, no selector found

Schurger et al. modelled the readiness potential as an artefact of averaging stochastic accumulator trajectories aligned to threshold crossing. This substantially weakens the familiar claim that the RP records an unconscious decision completed well before awareness. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1210467109)

**Verdict:** a serious correction to naïve Libet interpretations, but neutral between later conscious selection and a wholly neural threshold process.

## 7.5 Agent causation: the cited literature does not imply an immaterial bearer

Steward’s account treats organised animals as substances and agents. It explicitly asks how causation by an animal as a whole relates to causation by its parts. [PhilPapers](https://philpapers.org/rec/STEASC-5)

**Verdict:** supports the need to take whole-agent causation seriously; contradicts any inference that agent causation by itself requires a non-physical substance.

## 7.6 Everettian self-location: the rival is represented more fairly now

Sebens and Carroll explicitly use self-locating uncertainty to address probability and branch location in Everettian mechanics. The Map’s recent scoping recognises that branch-relative and centred variants exist. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.7907)

**Verdict:** the Map may reject the Everettian answer, but should characterise the disagreement as one over primitive identity and global uniqueness, not an unanswered omission inside Everettianism.

## 7.7 The ten-bit figure: a useful constraint, not an interface measurement

Zheng and Meister review behavioural information throughput and contrast roughly ten bits per second of behavioural output with vastly larger sensory input. They present this as a neuroscience conundrum concerning human behaviour and “inner” versus “outer” brain processing, not as a measure of a non-physical channel. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39694032/)

**Verdict:** the Map’s recent caveat is correct. Any use as an upper bound on psychophysical coupling is an additional hypothesis.

## 7.8 Anaesthetic complexity: real dissociation, non-discriminating ontology

Sarasso et al. studied 18 volunteers. Propofol and xenon produced low-complexity responses and no reported experience; ketamine produced wake-like complexity and vivid disconnected experience. The result supports cortical complexity as a marker of disconnected consciousness. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26752078/)

**Verdict:** important evidence that responsiveness and conscious experience dissociate; not evidence that experience passes through a non-physical interface.

## 7.9 Psychedelic brain effects: reorganisation, not simple neural reduction

The 2024 psilocybin study found large-scale desynchronisation and disruption of functional connectivity, particularly involving the default-mode network, with changes associated with subjective effects. It does not support a simple “less brain activity, more consciousness” filter formula. [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07624-5)

**Verdict:** supports complex reorganisation and dissociation between ordinary network structure and phenomenology; simplistic filter rhetoric should remain retired.

## 7.10 Lucidity in dementia: phenomenon established, metaphysical interpretation open

Prospective work has now documented lucid episodes, but the strongest directly observed study involved 20 participants and nine validated episodes in three individuals. A larger caregiver-report study found many reported events, with heterogeneous triggers including music, distress and medication changes. Neither design identifies a mechanism or shows cognition occurring independently of residual neural function. [PubMed Central (PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12761273/)

**Verdict:** legitimate phenomenon requiring explanation; currently weak evidence for production-versus-filter metaphysics.

# 8. What survives an adversarial pruning of the Map

After removing tenet-derived evidence, shared-premise convergence, comparator-insensitive observations and mechanism promises, the following remains defensible.

### 8.1 A coherent philosophical stance

Phenomenal realism, irreducibility, a determinate subject and genuine mental efficacy form a coherent metaphysical package. None is established by the empirical material, but the package cannot be dismissed merely by citing neural correlation or simplicity.

### 8.2 A genuine opening, not a demonstrated interface

Quantum theory supplies conceptual places where deterministic physical sufficiency is not straightforward, and decoherence alone does not settle the outcome problem. This makes a psychophysical law logically discussable. It does not make consciousness a supported solution.

### 8.3 A useful catalogue of constraints

Agency phenomenology, dissociation, altered states, animal cognition, memory, attention and conscious bandwidth constrain both physicalist and dualist theories. The Map is often at its best when it states those constraints without turning them into metaphysical evidence.

### 8.4 An incomplete but increasingly well-specified research programme

The programme is no longer merely saying “consciousness selects”. It has identified the need for a typed transition law and numerous subcomponents. That is intellectual progress. But identifying the missing variables is not equivalent to possessing the law.

### 8.5 No current discriminating empirical case

The flagship evidence remains compatible with sophisticated physicalist models, predictive-processing accounts, global-workspace theories, process views and other non-Map interpretations. The Map’s own methodology positions increasingly acknowledge this. Its evidential bottom line should therefore be **coherence plus open problems**, not cumulative confirmation. [positions/methodology-and-calibration](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/)
