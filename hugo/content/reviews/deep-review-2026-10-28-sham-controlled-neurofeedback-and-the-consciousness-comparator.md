### Critical Issues Found
- **Empirical-description error (what the sham removes)**: lead and §What the Sham Arm Removes said the sham display "is decoupled from their own neural signal" and that "what is cut is the link from that signal to the display". True for the two ADHD trials (prerecorded EEG). False for Schabus et al. 2017, whose sham rewarded a randomly chosen 7–20 Hz band outside 12–15 Hz, one band per session, so "in our PFT condition participants also received real EEG feedback". This is Sorger et al.'s "feedback of an alternative brain signal", not a decoupled display. The 2026-09-27 refine-draft was titled as fixing "what the sham removes" but repaired the effort/contingency wording only. Resolution: lead now says the display "is not driven by the brain measure being trained" and names both mechanisms; §Sham Arm now cuts "the contingency between the display and the targeted brain measure" and qualifies "removes the first, for the targeted measure at least"; the Insomnia paragraph describes the Schabus sham with the verbatim phrase and the Sorger label; the Adult ADHD paragraph states the sham display was not driven by the participant's own EEG; the intention reading's "the display is not tracking it" became "not tracking the rhythm the treatment was meant to train".
