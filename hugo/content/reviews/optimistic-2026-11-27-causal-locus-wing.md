## Executive Summary

These four pages refuse to oversell themselves, and each one says so outright:

- overdetermination: the dissolution is "conditional on the framework, not a standalone result that favours the Map over its rivals";
- delegatory dualism: "addresses" is "the right verb here rather than 'solves'";
- type-token: "citable as a framework-internal coherence result, not as established mental causation";

**The wing barely cites itself.** Of 12 directed pairs, only three links exist: overdetermination → delegatory dualism (2) and type-token → delegatory dualism (1). Four of the six pairs have no link in either direction.

**Unifying insight.** The wing contains *two incompatible species* of what constitution-vs-causal-work calls the causal-work reading. That page has only one.

- **Substitution** (Saad): the experience does work the physical state would otherwise do, with no change in effect.
- **Supplementation** (selection-only): the mind settles what physics leaves open.

`type-token-causation` L96 already says the two routes "have incompatible closure profiles". The two species also owe complementary debts, which no page states (see the Wing's Unifying Insight below).

## The Wing's Unifying Insight (not yet stated anywhere)

Each page puts the mind's causal contribution at a different joint. Read together, they show that "the causal-work reading" covers two incompatible species:

| Page | Where the contribution sits | What physics keeps | What the mind adds to the *effect* | The debt it owes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| overdetermination | the sufficiency gap at a selection event (L67) | the candidate set and Born weights | which candidate is realised | ensemble-level idleness, "held open rather than discharged" (L125) |
| delegatory dualism | a preempted default profile (L86) | counterfactual sufficiency of the physical state | **nothing**: experiences "cause exactly what the physical states would have caused if delegation hadn't occurred" (L110, 1 hit) | the default profile is a postulate, "never directly observed" (L86–L88) |
| type-token | the token grain (L84) | "physics still fixing every chance" (L142) | which outcome, in this run | "whether settling which outcome is actual counts as causing" (L80) |
| constitution vs causal work | the bridge from role to felt quality (L35) | the role | "a distinct causal increment" (L45, 1 hit) | none named: the page adjudicates nothing |

- **Substitution.** Delegation changes the bearer of the work but not the work. On Saad's Subset Law*, the experience's profile is a subset of the physical state's default profile, so the effects are the default effects.
- **Supplementation.** Selection adds something physics does not fix: the realisation of one candidate. It adds nothing to the unconditioned statistics.

`constitution-vs-causal-work` defines the causal-work reading by the increment (L45). Yet L80 catalogues [delegatory-causation](/concepts/delegatory-causation/) on that side of the bridge, though delegation, by its own account, adds no increment.

This matters because the constitution theorist's strongest reply is aimed at substitution. A felt quality whose causal profile is a subset of its base's default profile behaves exactly like the role under a phenomenal description. So delegation's surplus is a claim about *who* does the work, and that is the claim the constitution reading denies is a separate item.

The two species owe complementary debts:

- Substitution must show that a change of bearer with no change of effect is a change at all.
- Supplementation must show that settling is causing.

That also explains the fork between Saad and the Map. Saad chose substitution so as to keep Observational Closure. He says the costs of quantum interactionism "give dualists reason to develop alternative forms of interactionism" (1 hit). The Map chose supplementation, and it answers only the first of Saad's two objections (`observational-closure` L74). That fork is why the delegation–selection fusion had to be withdrawn: "trumping needs a sufficient physical cause and selection an insufficient one" (`delegatory-dualism` L186, 1 hit).

The cheapest single repair that installs the insight is Priority 3.
