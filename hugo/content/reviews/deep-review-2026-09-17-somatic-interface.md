## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

- **Dropped qualifier in new IGT sentence (fixed).** The 09-14 text said Maia & McClelland "found reportable knowledge of the deck contingencies whenever behaviour changed." Their abstract (Europe PMC) says that when participants behave advantageously "their verbal reports nearly always reveal evidence of quantitative knowledge about the outcomes of the decks that would be sufficient to guide such advantageous behavior." Replaced with "participants who behaved advantageously nearly always reported knowledge of the deck outcomes sufficient to guide that behaviour."
- **Hub asserts what a spoke withdraws: alexithymia and interoceptive accuracy (fixed).** The Interoception section said "People with reduced interoceptive accuracy have difficulty identifying their own emotional states." [alexithymia](/concepts/alexithymia/) reports the Van Bael et al. 2024 meta-analysis: no reliable link between task-based accuracy and alexithymia; the link is with self-reported sensibility. [interoceptive-consciousness-and-the-interface](/topics/interoceptive-consciousness-and-the-interface/) also records doubts about how valid heartbeat counting is (Desmedt 2020, Legrand 2022). The sentence now ties difficulty identifying emotions to sensibility rather than accuracy, and the claims about emotional intensity and sensitivity to others' emotions are hedged as "reported … mostly on heartbeat-counting measures whose validity is now contested."
- **Internal contradiction in the Occam's Razor tenet paragraph (fixed).** It said materialism fails to capture "what the evidence shows" and "Understanding requires the complexity of an interface model". That contradicts the article's own governing paragraph (para 3), which says the somatic data are neutral between the two readings and that the materialist functional account is complete. A reviewer who accepts the tenets would still flag this as overstated. Rewritten so the tenet does less: parsimony cannot favour materialism just because it posits less, when what it leaves unexplained is exactly what is in dispute.
