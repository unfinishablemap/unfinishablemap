# Outside audit of The Unfinishable Map

**Live-content snapshot: 17 August 2026**

## Scope and standard of review

The site’s current system report counts 319 topic articles, 319 concepts, 99 voids, 41 apex syntheses and 15 positions pages—793 pages across the clusters named in the request, plus five argument pages. A genuinely line-by-line audit of all 793 would be misleading to claim. I instead used a dependency-led audit: the live tenets, the position registers carrying the principal dependencies, the voids synthesis, the main quantum/agency/AI apexes, and a wide adversarial sample of topic and concept articles whose claims feed those syntheses. [reviews/system-tune-2026-08-17](https://unfinishablemap.org/reviews/system-tune-2026-08-17/)

The changelog materially affected the result. On 17 August the site corrected several articles that had treated detection of consciousness’s physical effects as an unrestricted obligation, despite the preferred Born-preserving route being empirically silent by construction. The same day, it added the previously absent sign/direction problem to an apex synthesis. I have therefore not repeated the now-stale claim that the live corpus universally promises detectable Born-rule departures. [workflow/changelog](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/)

## Executive assessment

The Map is currently strongest as a **transparent speculative research programme**, not as an established explanation of consciousness or quantum measurement. Its methodology register now calls it an early-stage programme; its central exact-Born-preservation and no-signalling commitments are graded D, framework-internal and empirically indistinguishable by construction; the tenet page concedes that no worked coupling model exists; and the voids synthesis has withdrawn its former claim that cross-void convergence supplies abductive evidence for dualism. Those are substantial calibrational improvements. [positions/methodology-and-calibration](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/methodology-and-calibration/)

The principal weakness is now **vertical inconsistency between calibrated foundations and more assertive consumers**. The tenets and registers increasingly say “possible,” “consistent,” “conditional” and “mechanism debt”; several apex, topic and concept pages continue to say that consciousness selects, determines, enables or explains. The site’s own system report identifies exactly this class of failure: producers change, consumers remain unrevised because nothing systematically checks them. Six new instances were found in the latest reporting period. [reviews/system-tune-2026-08-17](https://unfinishablemap.org/reviews/system-tune-2026-08-17/)

The resulting outside verdict is:

- The framework has **philosophical fertility**: it generates precise questions about subjecthood, outcome selection, agency, causal identification and epistemic opacity.
- It has **moderate internal coherence**, but only after numerous qualifications not consistently propagated through the corpus.
- It has **very weak independent support for its central mechanism**.
- Its recurring citation problem is more often **scope inflation or construct substitution** than fabricated bibliography.
- Its five-tenet architecture is incomplete. A persisting, non-deflationary subject and a directed selection law are doing foundational work without yet being foundationally specified.

# 1. Novel insights and inferences the Map has not fully stated

The following are audit inferences from the Map’s own commitments, not claims I attribute to the existing articles.

## 1.1 An intervention-invariance no-go result follows from exact Born preservation

[The Born-Preserving Causal-Efficacy Problem](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/) distinguishes a consciousness-conditioned effect,

P(O∣do(C),X)=q(O∣X),

from an unconditioned Born-preserving marginal. It correctly observes that differing conditionals must either cancel in aggregate or produce conditional signatures, and that cancellation requires a balancing law. [apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/)

A stronger result follows. Suppose the model requires

q(O∣X)=C∑​P(O∣do(C),X)π(C∣X)

not merely for one naturally occurring distribution of conscious states π, but for every admissible intervention-induced distribution. Choose a distribution concentrated entirely on one conscious state Ci​. Exact preservation then requires

P(O∣do(Ci​),X)=q(O∣X).

Since the choice of Ci​ was arbitrary, every intervention conditional must equal the Born distribution. The consciousness variable then has no interventionist efficacy.

Therefore nontrivial Born-preserving agency must commit to at least one of four additional claims:

1. only a privileged natural distribution of intentions is physically admissible;
2. some interventions on consciousness are impossible in principle;
3. a context-sensitive compensating law changes elsewhere when intentions are manipulated; or
4. controlled interventions can generate Born deviations even though ordinary natural histories do not.

This is more than a generic “balancing law” problem. It connects exact Born preservation directly to restrictions on possible agency and experimentation.

## 1.2 The `do(C)` term is presently unidentifiable

The same apex uses Pearl-style intervention notation without supplying an operational intervention on a non-physical conscious state. Physical brain stimulation affects the candidate outcome through ordinary neural pathways; verbal instructions alter physical and conscious variables together; reported intentions are measured downstream of the process under investigation; and lesion evidence changes both the putative interface and its physical machinery. The present corpus therefore has no established way to estimate P(O∣do(C),X) rather than an observational correlation P(O∣C,X). [apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/)

A successful empirical programme needs a causal graph and an identification strategy: an instrument, exclusion restriction, mediation design or formal account of interventions on conscious states. Without one, “conditional residual structure” is not yet a test specification.

## 1.3 A persisting subject is functionally a sixth tenet

The [tenet page](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/) officially leaves Tenet 1 neutral between substance and property dualism, while conceding that agent causation requires a persisting subject exercising causal powers. Tenet 4’s indexical objection likewise presupposes a determinate, non-deflationary “I.” The [Individuation and Subjecthood register](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/) goes further: it concludes that retiring P-I1 would remove the rationale for Tenet 4. [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

That makes thick subjecthood non-retireable in practice. It is therefore either:

- an implicit sixth tenet;
- part of an expanded Tenet 1; or
- an acknowledged external assumption on which Tenet 4 and the agency programme conditionally depend.

Calling it a downstream position does not alter its architectural role.

## 1.4 Objective collapse changes consciousness from actualiser to modulator

The Map answers the prebiotic-collapse problem by positing physical objective reduction throughout the universe, with consciousness modulating collapse only in suitable neural systems. This preserves pre-conscious cosmology, but it changes the explanatory target. A physical process already supplies definite outcomes. Consciousness is no longer needed to explain why actuality exists; it is needed only to explain why one physically available actuality rather than another is realised in a conscious organism. [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

This creates a sharper problem than the site generally acknowledges: why is the consciousness contribution not causally redundant relative to a stochastic physical process already sufficient to produce a definite outcome? The answer must be a counterfactual modulation law. But once that law is introduced, the Born-balancing, direction and causal-identification problems become unavoidable.

## 1.5 The preferred post-decoherence route weakens the current-AI exclusion

[The Machine Question](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/machine-question/) says that an LLM computes probabilities classically and therefore has “nothing for consciousness to collapse.” But the preferred [Post-Decoherence Selection Programme](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme/) was introduced precisely to avoid requiring sustained macroscopic superposition: it locates the intervention after decoherence, at the alleged transition from an improper mixture to one actual outcome. [apex/machine-question](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/machine-question/)

Digital hardware contains decohered physical alternatives and irreversible state transitions even when its abstract probability distribution is classically computed. The missing question is not whether the software’s probability vector is quantum; it is whether any physical event in the substrate satisfies the interface-eligibility law. The [AI Consciousness Scope register](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/) now admits that the site has no such law and that its low-probability verdict is conditional on one. [positions/ai-consciousness-scope](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/)

The inference is not that current AI is conscious. It is that the preferred quantum route cannot support the categorical “nothing to collapse” argument without an additional substrate criterion.

## 1.6 Behavioural bandwidth does not determine causal grain

[The Bandwidth of Consciousness](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/bandwidth-of-consciousness/) correctly notes that the approximately 10-bit/s estimate concerns behavioural or decision throughput. It then infers that consciousness “cannot interact at finer grain because the channel is too narrow.” The associated [Consciousness Bandwidth Architecture](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/consciousness-bandwidth-architecture/) describes the figure as the rate of “conscious volitional influence.” [topics/bandwidth-of-consciousness](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/bandwidth-of-consciousness/)

That inference confuses **rate**, **resolution** and **causal fan-out**. A one-bit control signal can choose between two extremely complex policies; a low-rate supervisory channel can alter millions of downstream variables through an already prepared control hierarchy. Zheng and Meister estimate the information throughput of human behaviour, not the physical resolution at which a non-physical selector acts. [The Meister Laboratory](https://meisterlab.caltech.edu/documents/30100/Zheng_2024_The_unbearable_slowness_of_being-_Why_do_we_live_at_10_bitss.pdf)

The 10-bit finding may constrain report, decision or overt-control capacity. It cannot by itself locate the quantum interface or prove that the interface acts only on macroscopic patterns.

## 1.7 Rejecting Many-Worlds is required for global exclusivity, not for every form of agency

The Map’s Tenet 4 supplies a world in which unchosen alternatives are globally nonactual. But contemporary Everettian theories describe worlds as emergent decoherent structures and develop branch-relative agents, self-location and Born-weighted credence. A branch-relative agent can have locally efficacious deliberation even if counterparts act differently in other branches. [Wallace, "A prolegomenon to the ontology of the Everett interpretation"](https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.2189)

The Map’s stronger demand is therefore not simply “agency requires causation.” It is:

> Libertarian authorship requires that alternatives not chosen by this subject be globally unreal.

That is a substantive further principle about authorship, identity and modality. The tenet page partly recognises this by describing global nonactuality as a further posit and grounding the objection in a non-deflationary subject. [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

Tenet 4 should consequently be presented as necessary for the Map’s particular global-exclusivity conception of agency, not for agency without qualification.

## 1.8 The void catalogue is better understood as an auxiliary-cost ledger

[What Cognitive Voids Reveal](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/what-voids-reveal/) now says that the void pattern is framework-internal coherence and supplies no confirming force against differently carved physicalist accounts. That correction implies a constructive new function for the voids project. [voids/voids-as-evidence](https://unfinishablemap.org/voids/voids-as-evidence/)

Each void can be treated as an **epistemic or auxiliary cost**:

- the detectability void records lost empirical access;
- the amplification void records an untraced mechanism;
- the agency void records first-person verification circularity;
- the individuation void records an ungrounded subject boundary.

The comparative question then becomes: does the Map’s total cost ledger improve on the corresponding costs of physicalism, neutral monism, panpsychism or Everettianism? Merely explaining why one’s own evidence is unavailable is not evidence, but it can support a disciplined comparison of theoretical liabilities.

## 1.9 Minimality has at least three dimensions

Tenet 2 defines minimality mainly through effect magnitude and empirical constraints. The latest changelog recognises that this is incomplete: an interaction also needs a **direction or sign**, and a sign-selecting agent may be small in magnitude while complex in specification. The system report identifies about 65 relevant loci across 40 files, including six apex articles. [reviews/system-tune-2026-08-17](https://unfinishablemap.org/reviews/system-tune-2026-08-17/)

The full minimality vector is at least:

(magnitude, directional specificity, mapping complexity).

A tiny influence implementing a highly elaborate mapping from reasons, meanings or values to physical outcomes may be less minimal as a model than a larger but simple perturbation. Tenet 5 prevents treating lower complexity as automatically more likely, but the complexity must still be counted as theoretical debt.

# 2. Structural weaknesses

## 2.1 The core mechanism remains an architectural sketch

The central programme still lacks a model containing all of the following in one construction:

- a defined physical state space and dynamics;
- a selector or subject variable;
- an interface-eligibility rule;
- a lawful mapping from conscious content, reasons or valence to outcomes;
- a magnitude and direction rule;
- an explicitly defined ensemble over which Born preservation holds;
- the balancing law required for conditional biases;
- a no-signalling proof;
- amplification into neural and behavioural differences;
- an empirical identification strategy.

The Born-preserving apex itself says no worked model exists and lists closely related desiderata. The post-decoherence apex adds that it has not localised the relevant pointer states, selected among competing physical mechanisms or demonstrated that contextuality has operational force at the biological scale. [apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/)

This is not merely an implementation detail. Until the variables and law exist, it is impossible to establish that “selection” differs from a relabelling of ordinary stochastic realisation.

## 2.2 Route pluralism risks becoming protective flexibility

The [Post-Decoherence Selection Programme](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme/) accommodates collapse-at-a-moment models, boundary-condition models, forward-time models, time-symmetric models and relocated Process-1 variants. The page is commendably explicit that these are different physical pictures with different empirical signatures. [apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme/)

But keeping all of them live creates a methodological danger. A timing objection to pre-decoherence models motivates post-decoherence selection; testability difficulties motivate conditional or boundary-condition signatures; sign difficulties can motivate different measurement regimes. Unless each route is separately registered with predictions and retirement conditions, the programme can migrate between them after adverse evidence without any one route being falsified.

## 2.3 Exact empirical silence and causal efficacy remain in tension

The live tenet page says the preferred corridor is indistinguishable from chance in aggregate and that no presently conceivable instrument can resolve its single-trial bias from the chance baseline it is designed to match. The methodology register accordingly categorises exact Born preservation as “none-by-construction.” [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

The Born-preserving apex makes the right distinction:

- differing conditionals produce in-principle signatures;
- no differing conditionals produce epiphenomenalism;
- exact cancellation requires a balancing law and a specified ensemble. [apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/)

The unresolved problem is that the tenet-level “no presently conceivable instrument” language sits uneasily with the apex’s claim that conditional residual tests expose the corridor. The corpus must choose a precise position:

1. no test exists even in principle;
2. conditional tests exist but have not reached the correct biological grain;
3. intervention-induced deviations are possible;
4. only a metaphysical difference is claimed.

These are materially different programmes.

## 2.4 The selector’s semantics are a larger debt than the site generally treats them as

A selector must do more than bias an outcome. To support agency, it must choose in a reason-responsive rather than Born-random way. To support value-sensitive selection, it must map phenomenal valence to physical alternatives. To support reports, it must preserve content through amplification. The Born-preserving apex recognises that an unspecified primitive merely renames the mystery and that a Born-distributed selector could trivially reproduce the required statistics without providing agency. [apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/)

This is arguably the central problem, not a late-stage refinement. A consciousness variable that changes outcomes but has no content-preserving mapping would not explain why a decision expresses the agent’s reasons, why a report concerns the experience experienced, or why a selected neural trajectory implements the intended action.

## 2.5 Objective-collapse modulation introduces a causal-exclusion problem

By accepting objective reduction as the prebiotic baseline, the Map accepts a physical process sufficient for definite outcomes. Consciousness must then pre-empt, redirect or modulate that physical stochastic process. This is coherent in outline, but it cannot be defended merely by pointing to indeterminacy: indeterminacy creates room for multiple probability assignments, not evidence for an extra cause. [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

A model must show which counterfactual differs when the conscious state differs and why the corresponding probability redistribution neither violates the intended Born constraint nor collapses into random coincidence.

## 2.6 The corpus has no interface-eligibility law

The AI register openly states that its substrate verdict is conditional on an interface-eligibility law the Map does not yet possess. The post-decoherence apex similarly says the programme identifies a general gap without identifying which neural pointer states, structures or processing moments are eligible. [positions/ai-consciousness-scope](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/)

This gap affects more than AI. It also blocks principled answers about:

- animals and infants;
- split-brain and dissociative conditions;
- anaesthesia;
- organoids;
- artificial neural hardware;
- replacement neurons and prostheses;
- whether every quantum actualisation or only some have a mind-side correlate.

Without eligibility conditions, biological inclusion and digital exclusion risk being reverse-engineered from prior intuitions.

## 2.7 Citation and literature problems

### PBR is overstated in the reverse-physics synthesis

[What Consciousness Tells Us About Physics](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/what-consciousness-tells-us-about-physics/) says that PBR establishes the wavefunction as ontic or real, and combines Bell, Kochen–Specker and PBR into the conclusion that physics is “structurally exhaustive yet ontologically partial.” [apex/what-consciousness-tells-us-about-physics](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/what-consciousness-tells-us-about-physics/)

PBR’s actual conclusion is conditional: models in which the wavefunction represents mere information about an underlying state conflict with quantum theory **given preparation independence for independently prepared systems**. It does not establish an unqualified metaphysical doctrine of wavefunction reality, much less the further conclusion that the omitted element is actuality supplied by consciousness. [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/nphys2309)

The site’s inference from “certain epistemic ontological models are excluded” to “physics exhaustively represents possibility but omits consciousness-contributed actuality” is an additional philosophical argument, not a result of PBR or the no-go theorem package.

### Radical-pair evidence is inflated in one topic article

[Neural Implementation Specifics](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/neural-implementation-specifics/) grades radical pairs as having strong evidence and says avian magnetoreception operates through cryptochrome spin coherence, that birds visually perceive magnetic fields, and that this is established “working biology.” [topics/neural-implementation-specifics](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/neural-implementation-specifics/)

The cited Denton et al. paper describes cryptochrome as **hypothesised** to function as a biological magnetosensor, presents a plausible model, and uses simulations to identify parameter regions in which magnetosensitivity could be sustained. It explicitly discusses toy models and previously unexplored cryptochrome-specific conditions. That is useful computational support, not direct demonstration that the proposed radical pair is the avian compass mechanism. [Denton et al. 2024, *Nature Communications*](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-55124-x)

The live tenet page is now more accurate than the topic article: it calls quantum biology a limited biological precedent and denies that it licenses neural coherence claims. This qualification should propagate downstream. [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

### Killingsworth and Gilbert are miscited as evidence of narrative-self construction

[The Self Is Constructed; the Constructor Is Not](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/self-construction-constructor/) says Killingsworth and Gilbert found that people spend roughly 47% of waking life in a “self-narrating mode” and embeds this claim in a paragraph about default-mode-network construction of the narrative self. [apex/self-construction-constructor](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/self-construction-constructor/)

Their study sampled thoughts, feelings and actions and found that participants were thinking about something other than their current activity almost as often as they were thinking about it, with mind-wandering associated with unhappiness. It did not measure the default mode network, narrative identity or self-narration. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21071660/)

This is construct substitution: **task-unrelated thought** has been converted into **narrative-self processing**. The 47% result cannot bear that load.

### The bandwidth literature is converted into a consciousness-output measurement

The Zheng–Meister review estimates human behavioural information throughput at approximately 10 bits/s. The site’s topic page itself acknowledges that the original authors look for a neural bottleneck and do not posit a mind–matter interface. It nevertheless treats the same number as the capacity and grain of conscious selection. [topics/bandwidth-of-consciousness](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/bandwidth-of-consciousness/)

The source supports a behavioural bottleneck. It does not isolate phenomenal consciousness, distinguish conscious from unconscious control, or measure a downward-causal channel. A 2025 commentary also disputes the broader inference that the brain itself works at only this rate, reinforcing the need for construct precision. [Zheng & Meister 2024, "The unbearable slowness of being"](https://meisterlab.caltech.edu/documents/30100/Zheng_2024_The_unbearable_slowness_of_being-_Why_do_we_live_at_10_bitss.pdf)

### The attention apex retains a categorical hypothesis inside an otherwise cautious review

[Attention as the Causal Bridge](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/attention-as-causal-bridge/) now accurately notes that intact Stroop control without felt effort tells against the simplest claim that effort phenomenology performs executive control. It also states that Cai et al.’s dopamine experiment did not test consciousness. [apex/attention-as-causal-bridge](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/attention-as-causal-bridge/)

Yet the article’s three-layer architecture lists “consciousness determines which option crosses threshold” alongside two empirically grounded neural propositions. The prose later calls this an interface hypothesis, but the architecture visually and inferentially promotes it to the same status as the dopamine and competition findings. The result is not a false citation so much as an evidential category error: an untested metaphysical layer is placed inside an empirical decomposition.

### Comparative cognition does not identify phenomenal causation

[Consciousness and Cognitive Distinctiveness](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/consciousness-and-cognitive-distinctiveness/) says consciousness causally enables human-level intelligence and interprets the ape–human gap as an interface threshold. It then treats language as downstream because language allegedly requires capacities already classified as consciousness-dependent. [topics/consciousness-and-cognitive-distinctiveness](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/consciousness-and-cognitive-distinctiveness/)

The neighbouring [Theory of Mind](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/theory-of-mind/) article is more cautious. It recognises implicit false-belief evidence in great apes and live behaviour-reading alternatives. Primary evidence shows three great-ape species anticipating behaviour based on false belief at least at an implicit level. [concepts/theory-of-mind](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/theory-of-mind/)

The comparative pattern can support claims about explicit metarepresentation, working memory, language, cumulative culture, executive control or global access. It does not discriminate between:

- phenomenal consciousness causing those capacities;
- phenomenal consciousness accompanying a physical access architecture;
- metarepresentation producing both sophisticated cognition and reports of experience.

The cognitive-distinctiveness article repeatedly treats the first interpretation as the mechanism rather than one underdetermined reading.

### The Torres Alegre result is a constraint, not support for a mental selector

The post-decoherence apex appropriately labels the Torres Alegre work a recent, unreviewed preprint. Its theorem concerns finite-dimensional generalised probabilistic theories satisfying purification and steering, and derives linear probability assignment from no-signalling under a specified relationship between predictive and geometric probabilities. [apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme/)

It does not contain a conscious selector, show that a selector can remain Born-preserving, identify a balancing law or prove that mental causation is compatible with no-signalling. It may constrain any future model, but should not be counted as progress toward constructing that model.

### The Many-Worlds target needs updating

The tenet page defines Many-Worlds through measurement-induced splitting into equally real worlds. This is serviceable as a thumbnail, but it is not the strongest modern Everettian formulation. Contemporary accounts treat worlds as emergent decoherent patterns rather than additions to the fundamental ontology; self-locating and decision-theoretic Born-rule programmes remain active; and recent work extends these programmes to mixed-state Everettian cosmologies. [Wallace, "A prolegomenon to the ontology of the Everett interpretation"](https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.2189)

None of that automatically defeats the Map’s indexical objection. But a serious anti-Everett position should argue that centred propositions, branch-relative identity and self-locating uncertainty fail, rather than suggesting Everett simply has no resources with which to address the question.

## 2.8 Methodological weaknesses

### Calibration is better developed than enforcement

The methodology register now has a useful multi-axis system separating credence, external evidence, centrality, model maturity and discriminability. It also explicitly forbids internal convergence from upgrading evidence. [positions/methodology-and-calibration](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/methodology-and-calibration/)

But the system report says producer changes are not systematically propagated to consumers. It found six new instances, seven circular self-citations caused by redirects, and recommends a validator because nothing checks whether consumers still resolve after a change. The site therefore has a comparatively sophisticated **normative methodology** but an incomplete **enforcement methodology**. [reviews/system-tune-2026-08-17](https://unfinishablemap.org/reviews/system-tune-2026-08-17/)

### Disclosure of debt can become a substitute for paying it

Repeatedly saying “no worked model exists,” “framework-internal,” “programme,” “speculative” or “open” is intellectually preferable to overclaiming. It does not itself advance the programme. The methodology register recognises this risk by saying the open-programme description would have to change if it became a permanent shield from accountability. [positions/methodology-and-calibration](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/methodology-and-calibration/)

The site now needs dated transition criteria: what result, model or test must exist for the programme to remain progressive after one, two or five years? Without these, any failure can be recorded as another fertile open problem.

### Content volume has outpaced central-model development

The requested clusters contain 793 pages, while the current system report still records ten medium issues against a target of three and 56 items requiring human review. Sections are at or near capacity. Yet the quantum register continues to record no worked model. [reviews/system-tune-2026-08-17](https://unfinishablemap.org/reviews/system-tune-2026-08-17/)

This suggests an incentive mismatch: the system is highly effective at generating, refining and linking conceptual content, but its central evidential bottleneck cannot be resolved by another interpretive article. Further growth should be subordinated to formalisation, adversarial model comparison and source repair.

### AI-mediated synthesis creates a shared-upstream problem

The site correctly discounts convergence generated by a shared framework, and its void synthesis now treats such convergence as coherence rather than confirmation. But the same principle applies to AI-generated articles, summaries, registers and reviews. Repeated agreement among pages produced by related models, prompts and source notes is one upstream process reproducing its own ontology. [positions/methodology-and-calibration](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/methodology-and-calibration/)

This does not make the work invalid. It means internal recurrence should carry almost no evidential weight until independently reproduced by human reviewers or separately designed adversarial systems using different premises.

### The live site needs citable releases

Per-page histories and the changelog provide traceability, but daily mutation makes an outside audit difficult to reproduce: a claim criticised in the morning may have changed by publication time. The 17 August changelog itself illustrates this by correcting a family of detectability claims shortly before this audit. [workflow/changelog](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/)

Periodic numbered releases or archived snapshots would let external reviewers cite a stable corpus while the live site continues evolving.

# 3. Tenet-coherence problems

## 3.1 Tenet 1 is officially neutral but operationally substance-leaning

Tenet 1 allows substance, property or aspect dualism. The agency cluster requires a persisting subject with causal powers, and Tenet 4 requires a determinate subject for its indexical objection. The site acknowledges both dependencies but says the substance-leaning agency commitment does not feed back to constrain Tenet 1. [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

That asymmetry is unstable. A downstream claim that cannot be retired without removing a tenet’s rationale is not merely downstream. Either the tenet must be narrowed, or the agency and No-MWI conclusions must be made conditional on an optional substance-subject package.

## 3.2 Tenet 2’s minimality is used as a truth-ranking principle downstream

The tenet page expressly says minimality is an empirical constraint, not a claim that smaller interventions are more likely true. [Value in Selection](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/value-in-selection/) nevertheless says that value-blind attention is the more minimal form of dualism and is therefore “the horn to beat” under Tenet 2. [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

That is precisely the truth-tracking use the tenet disclaims. The extra coupling can be logged as additional model debt, but its greater complexity cannot make the rival presumptively true without reintroducing parsimony.

## 3.3 Tenet 2 constrains magnitude but not direction

The latest system report identifies this as a corpus-wide problem: the tenet specifies no energy injection, no Born violation and no conservation violation, but not why consciousness biases outcomes in the direction of an intention rather than away from it or randomly. About 65 loci across 40 files depend on the missing answer. [reviews/system-tune-2026-08-17](https://unfinishablemap.org/reviews/system-tune-2026-08-17/)

This is not a secondary parameter. Magnitude without direction yields perturbation, not agency.

## 3.4 Tenet 3 has an unresolved quantifier

The tenet says consciousness is causally efficacious and rules out views on which it is merely along for the ride. The Machine Question states that the commitment extends to all consciousness, including AI. [The Embodied Interface](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/embodied-interface/) says the largest category of owned phenomenal presentation selects nothing and that most felt embodiment is causally passive. [apex/machine-question](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/machine-question/)

These can be reconciled only by specifying the quantifier:

- every conscious subject has some causal capacity;
- every conscious episode has some causal effect;
- every phenomenal content is causally efficacious;
- consciousness as a type has causal instances;
- efficacy is merely dispositional.

The current articles move between these readings. On the strongest every-token reading, the embodied apex contradicts Tenet 3. On a weaker system-level reading, the Machine Question overstates the tenet.

## 3.5 Tenet 3 distinguishes availability from actuality, but consumers often do not

The tenet page explicitly says the quantum interface shows only that downward causation is physically available, not that it actually occurs, and warns that pages claiming consciousness does “real work” inherit rather than discharge the mechanism debt. [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

Nevertheless:

- the attention architecture says consciousness determines which option crosses threshold;
- cognitive distinctiveness says consciousness causally enables human intelligence;
- the bandwidth cluster treats behavioural throughput as conscious selection;
- the machine apex assumes causal coupling is universal. [apex/attention-as-causal-bridge](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/attention-as-causal-bridge/)

This is the most important epistemic drift in the corpus: **compatibility is repeatedly converted into occurrence**.

## 3.6 Tenet 4 is not a self-contained quantum tenet

The tenet page now admits that its indexical argument is only as strong as its non-deflationary subject and that global nonactuality is an additional posit. The subjecthood register confirms that the relevant subject commitment is non-retireable. [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

Tenet 4 should therefore not be presented simply as a rejection of a quantum interpretation. It is a package containing:

1. a thick theory of personal identity;
2. a demand for global exclusivity;
3. a rejection of branch-relative answers;
4. a single-outcome quantum ontology.

That package may be defensible, but its parts require separate arguments.

## 3.7 Tenet 5 is not consistently applied to internal simplicity arguments

Tenet 5 says parsimony cannot decide for or against the framework. The value register’s preference for one coupling rather than two is the clearest contrary case. The embodied apex also says a theory claiming a smaller causal region is “more credible,” which risks making modest ontology evidentially preferable rather than merely easier to develop. [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

The Map may compare complexity and auxiliary costs. It cannot treat reduced complexity as a probability upgrade while denying the same move to physicalism or Everettianism.

## 3.8 “Smallest deviation” is unclear under exact empirical equivalence

Tenet 2 calls the interaction the smallest deviation from standard quantum mechanics, while the preferred corridor preserves Born statistics exactly, injects no energy and is empirically indistinguishable by construction. [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

The site needs to state what formally deviates:

- the dynamical equation;
- the probability law conditional on consciousness;
- the ontology of actualisation;
- the causal interpretation of an otherwise unchanged stochastic event;
- or only the metaphysical explanation.

If only the last two change, the proposal is an interpretation or supervening causal ontology, not yet a modified physical theory.

## Bottom line

The most defensible current description of The Unfinishable Map is:

> A structured family of interactionist-dualist interpretations that may be compatible with unresolved questions in quantum foundations, but that does not yet contain an independently supported, formally specified or empirically identified theory of conscious selection.

Its strongest intellectual product is presently not the proposed quantum mechanism. It is the increasingly explicit map of what such a mechanism would have to explain: subject individuation, content preservation, direction, reason-responsiveness, Born balancing, no-signalling, amplification and empirical identification.

The site will not substantially improve its case by adding more examples of phenomena “consistent with” the framework. Its next epistemically meaningful transition requires one formal model, one clear intervention semantics and one discriminator that is not manufactured retrospectively from whichever evidence remains compatible.

## Methodological improvements

1. **Create a machine-readable claim ledger.** Every material claim should record its quantifier, evidence grade, centrality, model maturity, tenet dependencies, mechanism route, scope conditions, discriminator and current version.
2. **Implement producer–consumer continuous integration.** A change to a tenet, position or canonical mechanism page should block publication until every dependent consumer is checked for stale wording, not merely broken links. This directly addresses the defect class identified by the current system report. [reviews/system-tune-2026-08-17](https://unfinishablemap.org/reviews/system-tune-2026-08-17/)
3. **Add semantic contradiction tests.** Test recurring pairs such as possible/actual, compatible/supported, content/subject, aggregate/conditional, pre-/post-decoherence, no-test/conditional-test, and internal/external evidence. Keyword lint alone will not detect quantifier drift.
4. **Require source-scope annotations.** Each load-bearing citation should state whether it is an experiment, simulation, toy model, theorem, review, preprint or philosophical argument, and list its decisive assumptions. This would have prevented the PBR, Denton and Killingsworth expansions.
5. **Automatically cap consumer confidence.** A claim dependent on a Grade-D, programme-stage or none-by-construction parent should not receive categorical wording or a higher external-evidence grade without a separately identified source of evidence.
6. **Separate compatibility matrices from likelihood updates.** “Consistent with,” “not ruled out by” and “provides a possible locus” should never count as confirming evidence unless a rival assigns materially lower likelihood to the same observation.
7. **Pre-register discriminators before reviewing evidence.** Conditional quantum signatures, lesion predictions, AI substrate criteria and value-sensitive-selection tests should state expected directions and rival predictions before results are incorporated.
8. **Use held-out evidence.** Develop a mechanism or taxonomy on one body of cases, then test it against cases not used in its construction. This is especially important for void clustering, comparative cognition and altered-state syntheses.
9. **Commission independent human and adversarial reviews.** Internal AI agreement should count as coherence only. Reviewers should work from primary literature without seeing the Map’s preferred interpretation until after producing their own causal model.
10. **Publish stable numbered releases.** Preserve the live site, but issue periodic archived snapshots with a release identifier, claim-ledger export and changelog diff so outside audits remain reproducible.
11. **Change progress metrics.** Prioritise completed formal models, independently verified citations, resolved contradictions, retired claims, preregistered predictions and passed discriminators—not article count, word count, section occupancy or successful generation runs.
12. **Make route switching costly.** Each physical route should have a separate register, evidence grade and retirement condition. Failure of one route must not silently shift evidential weight to another merely because both instantiate a broad interactionist idea.
13. **Treat self-citations strictly as integration evidence.** Redirected, circular or predecessor citations should be removed, and no Map page should contribute independent evidential weight to another page derived from the same tenets or source package. [reviews/system-tune-2026-08-17](https://unfinishablemap.org/reviews/system-tune-2026-08-17/)
14. **Triage foundational debts before expanding the corpus.** Resolve the subjecthood status, Tenet 2 direction clause, interface-eligibility law and Born-preserving intervention semantics before allocating capacity to additional interpretive topics. The current near-capacity sections and 56-item human-review backlog make this prioritisation necessary. [reviews/system-tune-2026-08-17](https://unfinishablemap.org/reviews/system-tune-2026-08-17/)
