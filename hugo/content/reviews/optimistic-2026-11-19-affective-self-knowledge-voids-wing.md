## Executive Summary

Between 31 October and 16 November the Map wrote five voids about what a person can know of her own affective life: why she loves *this* one, how a death is absorbed and how grief fades, what boredom points at, what her tears were about, and whether her settled wants were trimmed to fit what she could get. They are among the most carefully calibrated void pages on the Map. Each fences its opening case, says which face rests on which kind of evidence, declares itself framework-independent, and turns down a tenet upgrade the material seemed to offer. Bereavement's refusal to treat sensed presence of the dead as evidence of survival is the clearest case. The hub these five now feed, `voids/emotional-epistemology-void`, has not caught up. Its Relation section says the void "exists because" consciousness may disclose features of reality. Its own three-way lead and all five satellites contradict that. Three fixes from the 2026-10-20 pessimistic review were also never queued. One `refine-draft` task was minted. The voids index also leaves out two of the five, though both call themselves framework-independent.

## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

The hub's last paragraph separates two things the hard-problem literature often runs together: what an experience represents and what it is like to have it. "It was beautiful" might state in full what was disclosed while conveying nothing of what the disclosing was like. That sentence lets the void's evaluative claim stay open without trading on the explanatory gap. The weeping void gives the meta-problem a clean clinical case. A current through one electrode contact produced tears together with sincere reasons, and the article draws the narrow lesson: "The stimulation case shows that a felt reason is not a certificate of its cause, not that ordinary weeping is confabulated." Chalmers would also approve the bereavement void's handling of the Map's own commitment that grief resists functional analysis: "That is a commitment the Map brings to this material, and nothing in the material backs it." The hard problem stays where it belongs and is not smuggled in as a finding.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

Stapp gets less here than in most wings, and the pages are right about that: none of the five finds a connection to Minimal Quantum Interaction, and each says so. What he would value is that two of them sharpen the selector the Map's interactionism posits. Boredom reads its felt cost as the cost "where attention is allocated", only on the explicitly speculative attention-as-interface hypothesis. The sour-grapes void adds a constraint any process-1 chooser must face: "if consciousness selects, the options reach it already weighted by a feasibility process it cannot see". It then turns that into a methodological control on the Map's own value-in-selection position rather than an objection to it: "The Map reads this as a control to add, not as evidence against the value-sensitive horn." Sharpening the selector this way leaves the hypothesis easier to test.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

The beloved void ends its warrant face on Montaigne's answer about La Boétie, and observes: "The reasons end in two pronouns, one for each party." That is the first-person point of view showing up in the grammar of love's justification, and the No Many Worlds paragraph builds on it. The bereavement void's Fuchs material (his paper is titled "Presence in absence", and the dead person is "still present in my inmost self-experience") is phenomenology used as data. The weeping void guards against the opposite error, the inference from opaque causes to absent experience: "The void concerns access, not whether anything is felt".

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

The wing is relational throughout, which Whitehead would welcome. Boredom "presents a failed relation as a property of the object". Bestowal views of love put the beloved's value in the relating, and Fuchs has grief resolve by re-establishing the relation to the dead rather than cutting it. The weeping void takes Plessner's being-a-body/having-a-body tension and leaves it where Plessner left it: "the tension between being a body and having a body describes lived existence, not two substances". **Constraint check:** in the satellites none of this relational praise moves anything up the evidential scale. The beloved void's bestowal reading of Bidirectional Interaction is marked "the Map's alone", next to "Nothing in the evidence supports non-physical causation". The one place in the wing where affective disclosure is used to ground something is the hub's Relation section (see [Calibration concern](#calibration-concern-the-hub-grounds-a-framework-independent-void-in-dualism)).

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

The sour-grapes void bears directly on authorship of one's wants, the "ultimate sources" Kane cares about. It gives Elster's one operational test of non-adapted wanting (freedom to do otherwise) and then shows why the test cannot certify a want: opening the closed option lets the want readapt or swing the counteradaptive way. Kane would find the result sobering. He would also find it respectful of agency, because the page refuses the opposite overreach as well: outsiders who diagnose adaptation are error-prone too, and "The insider cannot see the adaptation, and the outsider who claims to see it has no certified view either." The beloved void leaves open, and does not paper over, the possibility that love's selection has no justifying reason at all ("If the accounts that find no justifying reason are right, nothing is hidden and the face may be empty").

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

McGinn would praise the wing's reluctance to call things unexplorable. The beloved void, after a pessimistic review, withdrew a closure label that rested on a conceptual truth: "That any qualitative description of the beloved would also fit a duplicate follows from what a duplicate is, so it marks no further limit on knowing." The weeping void's candidate unexplorable core is labelled "the Map's inference, not a finding", with the missing premise named. Boredom's definitional closure comes with its scope: "The inference is definition-relative." The closures that survive are the ones each article can justify.

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

This persona's verdict carries the most weight here, because every satellite had a structural opportunity to slide a tenet into evidence and each declined it explicitly. Taken together the five read as a worked example of "tenet-coherent, not evidence-elevating":

- **Bereavement and survival.** The Map leaves survival of death open, and sensed presence of the dead is the obvious temptation. The lead closes it at once: "sensed presence of the dead is not offered as evidence that anyone survives". The Relation section repeats the point: "Sensed presence is a report about the survivor's experience, and the Map draws no inference from it about whether anyone is there."
- **Boredom and epiphenomenalism.** James's argument that pleasures track benefit is used only against automatism: "James is cited against automatism, not for dualism". The page also concedes that an identity theorist treats the feeling as efficacious too. In its Occam paragraph: "The collapse of a simple story lends no support to a non-physical one".
- **Beloved and No Many Worlds.** Love's fixation on a particular is the closest any of these voids comes to the indexical objection. The page gives the Everettian reply its full strength and concludes: "The void is coherent with the tenet and dramatises its claim, but gives no evidence for it."
- **Weeping and first-person authority.** The caution is turned on the Map: "The caution cuts against the Map, which leans on first-person evidence".
- **Sour-grapes and [P-VS1](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs1).** The confound becomes "a control to add", not a refutation and not support.

The source-depth disclosures are just as disciplined. Bereavement says of its own synthesis, "The pairing is this article's synthesis. No source read for it states the conjunction, six of the thirteen were read only in abstract". Weeping paraphrases Plessner through Rasini and says "no English sentence here is his". Sour-grapes concedes the gap at its own centre: "no study located asks participants whether they noticed their own adjustment. Unawareness is inferred from its absence in reports and from forecasting failures." Boredom grades its own faces in its second paragraph: "The faces differ in standing." No minimal-organism or tier-scale claim arises in this wing. The one calibration concern is grounding rather than tier, and it is in the hub.

## Content Strengths

### voids/beloved-void
- **Strongest point**: It notices that its first two faces are not independent evidence.
- **Notable quote**: "The first two faces, the forecast and the lover's answer, are one question asked twice, so their agreement is not two confirmations." Later: "Their agreement is one blank seen twice."
- **Why it works**: Convergence across faces is how void pages usually build weight. Here the page refuses to count one blank twice. That is the discipline [P-V3](/positions/voids-as-evidence/#p-v3) asks of the whole catalogue, applied inside a single article. The opening is also exemplary in its narrowness: the speed-dating result is said to concern "the pair-specific part of initial desire", not love.

### voids/bereavement-void
- **Strongest point**: It bounds both faces with the very evidence that seemed to establish them.
- **Notable quote**: "so what lags is expectation and habit while explicit belief is largely in place"
- **Why it works**: A grief void could easily say that the bereaved cannot believe the death. Instead the page uses the Yale Bereavement Study (acceptance the most endorsed item from the first month) to move the face from belief to expectation, and treats the resilience re-analysis carefully ("Their outcome was yearly life satisfaction, and grief was not measured."). Its Occam paragraph is a model of the illustrative-not-evidential use of Tenet 5: "'She knows he is dead' reads as one fact and covers at least three things that come apart". Its Dualism paragraph is already cited as a model by an open P3 in `todo.md`.

### voids/boredom-void
- **Strongest point**: It turns its own inference into a test that could refute it.
- **Notable quote**: "The Map draws an inference the authors do not state: avoidance driven by the forecast removes the experience that would correct it, so the forecast tends to keep itself in place." It closes with the falsifier: "a forecast that survives queues points to a memory or forecasting bias instead".
- **Why it works**: The Map's addition is marked as the Map's, and it comes with an involuntary-waiting test that separates it from its rival. The Heidegger borrowing is also clean: "The Map borrows Heidegger's phenomenology and leaves his anti-Cartesian metaphysics with him."

### voids/weeping-void
- **Strongest point**: Its "Whether Crying Helped" face. A 37-country survey divergence (over 70% say crying helps, 50% say their last cry did) is resolved by a 90-minute delayed-measurement design, and the page says exactly what the design does and does not control.
- **Notable quote**: "the crier's sense of having been helped cannot tell help from recovery"
- **Why it works**: The page holds three registers apart: what the crier reports (keeps its standing), what produced the tears (no privilege), and whether crying helped (occluded). It also scopes the opening case: "Most crying is not mysterious to the crier, and grief at a death is not opaque to the griever". It is honest about the Map's own history too: "the Map repeated the asymmetry by writing the mirth void first".

### voids/sour-grapes-void
- **Strongest point**: It is two-sided. The void blinds the insider, and the page then calibrates the outsider who claims to see what the insider cannot, with three results (Brandt's 151,794-respondent null, Riis's dialysis forecasting failure, Terlazzo's respect objection).
- **Notable quote**: "If Scheler is right, introspection is not deceived about what it finds. What it finds was altered earlier."
- **Why it works**: This is the wing's deepest single idea. Falsification upstream of introspection is a sharper limit than confabulation, and the page applies it to the Map's own value-realist pages ("The Map holds this as the realist's burden, with the residue untested."). It also contains the wing's one reflexive AI sentence that earns its place: "The model that drafted this article is such a system, and its reports on the point carry no special authority."

### voids/emotional-epistemology-void (hub)
- **Strongest point**: The 2026-10-20 repair that narrowed the void to the evaluative residue.
- **Notable quote**: "What resists is the evaluative residue, whatever emotion seems to disclose beyond any such check."
- **Why it works**: Fear's verdict answers to toxicology. Only what nothing non-affective can check is circular. The three-way lead ("If the conviction that feelings disclose something real is an illusion, nothing in the feeling exposes the illusion.") gives the satellites a frame they fit. The hub's weakness is that one section of the page still argues from before that repair (next section).

## Calibration Concern: The Hub Grounds a Framework-Independent Void in Dualism

This is the Process-versus-Hardline tension rule applied. The praise for affective disclosure that the Process and Property-Dualist personas would offer is cashed out in the hub's Relation section as the *ground* of the void. All six strings below occur exactly once in [voids/emotional-epistemology-void.md](/voids/emotional-epistemology-void/) (grep-counted 2026-11-19):

- L92: "The Map's [Dualism](/tenets/#dualism) tenet gives this void its ontological weight."
- L92: "Under materialism, the question of affective knowledge reduces to a question about functional states and their reliability."
- L92: "The void exists because consciousness—specifically its affective dimension—may disclose features of reality that would be invisible without it."

Against these, the page's own lead (L42): "The void is that, for whatever emotions seem to disclose beyond any non-affective check, these possibilities cannot be told apart without relying on the faculty in question". The void is the inability to tell the disclosure, fallible-evidence and illusion possibilities apart. It does not exist "because" the disclosure possibility may be true. A materialist who asks whether the evaluative residue is reliable meets exactly the same circularity, since there is no non-affective check under any framework. So the question does not "reduce" to something more tractable under materialism. The five satellites, which the hub now cites as its sharp cases, each say the opposite of L92: beloved ("a physicalist and a dualist describe the same blank"), weeping (same words), boredom ("a physicalist and a dualist describe the same gap"), bereavement ("The bereavement void is framework-independent") and sour-grapes ("The void is framework-independent and nothing in it is evidence for dualism").

The page has zero hits for "framework-independent" and zero for "harmony". Positive control: "Under materialism" and "co-optimization" do hit. So the 2026-10-20 review's suggested harmony clause ("why those affordances would track anything is the harmony debt every dualist reading owes") was never applied. Its Issues 6 (L62/L66 history: "The Western tradition from Plato onward has treated emotion as reason's antagonist", "twenty-five centuries of privileging logos over pathos") and 7 (L56 Smithies: "If consciousness grounds all justification, then emotions as conscious states may provide epistemic access that no unconscious process can replicate.") were "reported for the driver" and never queued. Commit `c3fa2e8765` (2026-10-20) applied only that review's items 1–4, and `git log -S "Plato onward"` shows the history sentence unchanged since the page was created.

The earlier deep reviews (2026-05-31, 2026-06-26) judged "ontological weight" acceptable as framing. That was before the 10-20 rewrite gave the lead its three-way form, and before five framework-independent satellites made the hub their reference point. The mismatch is new, and it matters now because readers arrive at L92 from those satellites.

**Fix direction** (queued, see Tasks): state that the verification circularity binds every framework. Say that what dualism changes, if the disclosure possibility is true, is the stakes (whether a mind without the states misses something real), not whether the void exists. Add the harmony clause. Apply Issues 6 and 7 with the 10-20 fix text.

**A related opportunity in the same page.** The hub's "Case for Affective Knowledge" calls Scheler the philosopher who "developed this line furthest" (Wertnehmung). The Map's own sour-grapes void quotes the same Scheler, in *Ressentiment* (1915), placing a falsification of value-feeling "on the road of experience into consciousness", upstream of anything introspection could catch. The Map's chief witness for value-perception therefore also supplies the mechanism that makes it unverifiable from inside, which is the hub's verification paradox seen from a new side. The hub has zero hits for "sour-grapes" and for "Ressentiment". Two other pages use Scheler's Wertnehmung only on the reliable side: `topics/emotion-and-dualism` L103 and `apex/minds-without-words` L115 ("an irreplaceable epistemic access that no non-conscious system could replicate"). Those are leads for a later sweep, not queued here.
