### Critical Issues Found

- **Chronology error in the Eccles sequence (fixed).** The paragraph read: "The later
  proposal developed with the physicist Friedrich Beck did reach the synapse … (Beck &
  Eccles 1992); Eccles *then* paired each cortical *dendron* with a mental unit, the
  *psychon* (Eccles 1994)." The dendron/psychon pairing was published in Eccles (1990),
  *Proc. R. Soc. Lond. B* 240:433-451 — **two years before** the Beck collaboration's
  quantum-tunnelling model, which the 1994 book consolidates rather than originates.
  Citing only the 1994 book and joining it with "then" asserted a false order of
  development. Fixed: the two later steps are now given in their actual order, with
  Eccles 1990 cited as the source of the dendron/psychon pairing and 1994 marked as the
  consolidation. New References entry added.
- **Empirical-claim fidelity — what Beck & Eccles modelled (fixed).** The article said the
  proposal "model[s] transmitter release as a quantum tunnelling process." The paper's
  own abstract locates the tunnelling in *the trigger mechanism* of the presynaptic
  vesicular grid, not in transmitter release itself: "we present a quantum mechanical
  model for it based on a tunneling process of the trigger mechanism." Corrected to
  "modelled the trigger for vesicular release as a quantum tunnelling process."
- **Dropped qualifier on the scale of the effect (fixed).** "raises the probability of
  vesicular emission across thousands of synapses" dropped the paper's anatomical
  anchor — the abstract says "in the thousands of synapses **on each pyramidal cell**."
  Restored; the phrase is no longer than what it replaced.

### Medium Issues Found

- **Under-specified 1977 locus (fixed).** "cortical modules" alone understated Eccles'

  hemisphere's liaison areas. Sharpened to "open cortical modules in the dominant
