## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)
[P-D1](/positions/arguments-for-dualism/#p-d1) states the cost of the Map's own case in the terms a physicalist would use: the anti-physicalist arguments are "premise-sharing, not evidentially independent", and the defensible reading is "substantially stronger than any single argument," never "overwhelming." Chalmers would recognise the honesty of [P-MC1](/positions/arguments-for-mental-causation/#p-mc1), which concedes the phenomenal-concept reply to self-stultification and says the dispute "relocates rather than ends".

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)
The quantum-interface domain ranks Stapp's own mechanism below post-decoherence selection behind two named gates and keeps it "in the live mechanism set" ([P-Q4](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q4)). [P-Q3](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q3) says of the default mechanism that it "sits genuinely close to epiphenomenalism" and treats that as the challenge to answer. A sympathetic physicist is given something to work on, with the debt stated ([P-Q10](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q10)).

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)
[P-MA1](/positions/memory-and-autonoesis/#p-ma1) and [P-TU1](/positions/thought-and-understanding/#p-tu1) take first-person character as the thing to be explained and then say exactly what it earns: texture "constrains rival accounts without establishing irreducibility or causal efficacy" ([P-TU1](/positions/thought-and-understanding/#p-tu1)). [P-A4](/positions/agency-and-will/#p-a4) holds that "no agent can certify its own causal efficacy by introspection" and notes the limit is symmetric.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)
[P-CS7](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs7)'s universal coupling avoids a threshold and a selection principle among adequate systems while keeping adequacy required, "which separates the position from panpsychism". The praise is for the structure. It licenses no tier upgrade, and the register does not use it for one: [P-CS2](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs2) keeps nematodes, *Hydra* and slime molds at "weak and speculative".

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)
[P-A1](/positions/agency-and-will/#p-a1) commits to agent causation and then lists the rival families it has not engaged. [P-A5](/positions/agency-and-will/#p-a5) concedes that the libertarian framing does not do "unique explanatory work over sophisticated compatibilism" at the level of moral practice. Kane would prefer a stronger claim and would respect this one.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)
[P-CS5](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs5) holds parsimony's default at the prokaryotic floor "undefeated but not established". [P-SC2](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc2) and [P-Q10](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q10) are registered debts: things the Map owes and does not have. [P-I3](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i3) books "a variable it takes to be real and cannot read off its own physics".

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)
This persona's verdict governs the review, and it is favourable. [P-M1](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#p-m1) is the rule ("A tenet removes a defeater but never upgrades the evidence level") and the register applies it to itself: [P-VS3](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs3) carries high centrality on grade D and says so ("Tenet 1 removes the physicalist defeater; defeater-removal is not evidence"); [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1) grades its own verdict D and prices "the derivation's weakness, not the claim's improbability"; [P-V3](/positions/voids-as-evidence/#p-v3) has suspended the one step that directed weight toward a tenet. The index's read-back section counts eleven empirically inert entries and calls the count "a disclosed liability, not a coherence result". Birch and Whitehead do not conflict anywhere in the register. One caution, developed under Drift below: [P-V3](/positions/voids-as-evidence/#p-v3)'s title still asserts what its Asserts suspends.
