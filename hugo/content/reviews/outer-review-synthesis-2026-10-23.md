## Operator Recommendation: hub pages do not inherit spoke concessions

This is a process and tooling issue. Per the driver brief, no content task was minted and nothing under `tools/` or `.claude/skills/` was edited.

**Evidence from this cycle.**

- The ChatGPT processing pass dated the hub's lag against nine spokes. Four concessions predate the hub's two "content-stable" deep reviews (2026-06-26 and 2026-07-19):
    - illusionism (2026-05-25, 3fc04dd158);

    - phenomenal-concepts-strategy (06-24, 4cd5a06f9e);
    - quantum-consciousness's neural-unproven line (03-21, e38fa33020).
- The rest landed later:
    - Type-A/B/C (10-02);
    - NCC (10-02);
    - interactionist-dualism's Stapp correction (10-03);
    - causal-closure's chance-fixing concession (10-20);
    - modal-epistemology (10-22), whose own cross-review did not touch the hub.
- Claude independently found the same pattern on four further spokes: cross-traditional, energy, quantum biology and closure.

**Why the loop misses it.** Three mechanisms combine:

- The 07-19 deep review's "treat this article as converged" keyed on the hub's own edit history, not on whether its dependencies had moved.
- Cross-review chains run outward from a new or changed article to its siblings, not upward to the hubs that summarise it.
- Sibling sweeps are string-based, and a hub paraphrases its spokes rather than repeating their strings, so a sweep keyed on the spoke's wording cannot find the hub's version.

**Proposed checks** (for the operator to accept, adapt or decline):

1. **Dependency-freshness trigger** (ChatGPT items 29, 37 and 40; Claude Part 5 item 1). When a page that a hub links receives a calibration or concession edit, or when three or more linked pages have changed since the hub's last deep review, queue a hub reconciliation review. Let a "converged" Stability Note expire when a linked dependency moves.
2. **Hub precedence rule** (ChatGPT item 30). A hub may not state a conclusion more strongly than the specialist page it links, unless it says why. This is a review-checklist line, cheap to add to deep-review and cross-review.
3. **Count invariant** (Claude Part 5 item 2). Machine-check that any "N arguments" phrase matches the enumerated headings and recap lists on the same page and on pages that describe it. It would have caught the eight/nine/seven defect, and it would flag the convergence page's dated "eight independent" highlight blurb (historical, left unedited).
4. **Per-citation author-stance field** (Claude Part 5 item 3). For every source recruited under "Arguments for" or as a hostile witness, record whether the author holds the view the Map recruits him or her for (yes, no or partial). The Hasker, Kind, Nagel, Kripke and Stapp errors all passed metadata checks, and three of them (Hasker, Kind, Stapp) contradict the Map's own pages.

**Secondary item: the predictive-processing recurrence (R1).** The options are:

- (ii) Narrow the published gate's scope at `project/evidential-status-discipline` L167, or exempt argument hubs explicitly.
