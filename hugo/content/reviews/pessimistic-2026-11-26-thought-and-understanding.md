## Executive Summary

The entry handles the hard part well. It keeps what the Map *holds* (the constitutive reading, as a bet) apart from what the evidence *delivers* (constraint, not establishment). The weaknesses sit in the stated limits.

1. **The falsifier is asymmetric.** Its main falsifier, a "clean zero case", is said to count "directly rather than by redescription". Yet a reported zero is a first-person verdict. The page's own Würzburg precedent is a reported absence that was redescribed as faint presence. And the same field says nothing first-personal can raise the grade.
2. **"Irreducibility" has two relata.** The page uses the word once against sensory accompaniment and once against the physical. The sentence that rests on the distinction also says the cases remove the very identity claim that it says a conservative can still redescribe each case into.
3. **The register drops a concession its source page makes.** `concepts/cognitive-phenomenology` counts contemplative dismantlability as "genuine pressure on the constitutive form of liberalism". [P-TU1](/positions/thought-and-understanding/#p-tu1) recruits dismantlability for the kind-not-degree defence and leaves the pressure out.

## Critiques by Philosopher

### The Eliminative Materialist
Churchland reads the grade-C rationale as a concession. The dissociation base's "*phenomenal* interpretation is contested where its architectural reality is not, and none of it bears on the constitution claim directly" (L46). [P-M2](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#p-m2) then discounts the converging introspective lines (L48). So what holds "proprietary, individuative phenomenal character" at the *upper* end of moderate? She would note that the range chosen to define "ordinary cognition" (tip-of-the-tongue states, feeling-of-knowing, expert perception, insight; L47) is where folk-psychological vocabulary has its most developed mechanistic rivals. TOT and FOK are textbook metacognitive signals. Her sharper complaint concerns the third shift trigger: "the phenomenal-contrast cases were shown to reduce entirely to subtle sensory or evaluative differences" (L50). Shown how? By mechanism, by prediction of the reports, or by the subjects' own reclassification? Unless the page says, she can deliver a full predictive model of TOT/FOK reports and still be told it has not "shown" the reduction.

### The Hard-Nosed Physicalist
Dennett applies heterophenomenology and finds the page treats reports asymmetrically. A reported *presence* cannot raise the external-evidence grade ("nothing first-personal can, which is the Würzburg lesson", L50). A reported *absence*, the clean zero case, would count "directly rather than by redescription" (L50). The page cites the Würzburg–Titchener episode as its honesty anchor (L48), and that episode is exactly a reported absence (imageless thought) redescribed as faint presence (Titchener's faint kinaesthetic sensations; `concepts/cognitive-phenomenology` L196). The corpus already supplies the liberal's version of the same move: microphenomenological subjects "report pre-reflective cognitive content that casual introspection overlooks" (`concepts/cognitive-phenomenology` L168). Dennett's verdict: the falsifier is weaker than advertised, for the same reason the support is.

### The Quantum Skeptic
Tegmark finds little to attack. The entry makes no quantum claims and never invokes Tenet 2. His one pressure point is the downstream LLM-understanding scepticism, since he holds a substrate-independent, information-pattern view. The entry already makes that verdict conditional on the constitutive reading ("every downstream application … is conditional on it", L47), which is the right defence. No defect.

### The Many-Worlds Defender
Tenet 4 is not engaged and Deutsch has no MWI complaint. He presses on explanation instead. A good explanation is hard to vary. If every reported zero can be re-read as unnoticed character (Issue 1), PCT is easy to vary at exactly the point where it is supposed to be at risk. He also contests the IBE concession: "unification is loveliness, so the … IBE consistency statement blunts this abduction exactly as far as the physicalist's" (L47). For Deutsch, reach and unification mark a *good* explanation and are not an aesthetic bonus. If the Map equates unification with loveliness, it gives away its best argument. See Counterarguments.

### The Empiricist
Popper's ghost credits the entry for naming four shift conditions and one runnable design (induced satiation, L50). It asks whether the zero case is a real risk. As written, the liberal can absorb a reported zero just as the conservative absorbs a reported presence (Issue 1). The Würzburg sentence is also stated as a law ("nothing first-personal can"), when it generalises from one episode. An open P3 task already flags that pattern on the source page. A falsifier that one's own precedent says can be redescribed is a weak falsifier. The fix is cheap: say so, and name what would back a zero.

### The Buddhist Philosopher
Nagarjuna reads "dismantlable under contemplative discipline" (L47) as evidence that the felt character of thought arises dependently and is not a fixed kind. The entry uses dismantlability only to say "the conservative needs a zero, not a gradient". Its own source says the contemplative trajectory is "dismantlability, not constitutive necessity. This is genuine pressure on the constitutive form of liberalism", and that what survives the deconstruction is "absence of the reifying habit that produced phenomenal-residue reports" (`concepts/cognitive-phenomenology` L180). The register books the defence and drops the pressure. The scope restriction to "ordinary cognition" is also what keeps the contemplative endpoint from counting as a zero, and the entry never says so. To Nagarjuna, a claim kept true by scoping it to where reification operates is a claim about reification.

## Critical Issues

### Issue 1: The zero-case falsifier claims a directness its own precedent denies
- **File**: `obsidian/positions/thought-and-understanding.md`
- **Location**: L50, *Would shift if*: "which would count against liberalism directly rather than by redescription" (count 1). The calibration line says the same: "a clean zero case would bear on it directly" (L46, count 1).
- **Problem**: A clean zero case needs "no reportable phenomenal character to the grasping", which is a first-person verdict of absence. Three things on the page and its source make that verdict open to redescription:
  - The page's honesty anchor (L48) is a reported absence that was redescribed. The Würzburg imageless reports were re-read by Titchener as faint sensation (`concepts/cognitive-phenomenology` L196).
  - The source's "introspective face" says "the first-person question whether *this thought, now*, has its own phenomenal character cannot be settled from inside introspection" (L198, count 1). That covers a "no" answer as much as a "yes".
  - The corpus already supplies the liberal's re-reading: pre-reflective content "that casual introspection overlooks" (L168).

  Meanwhile the same field says first-person evidence cannot *raise* the grade. Reports can therefore move credence down "directly" but never up, which is an asymmetry the page never defends. The other half of the worry, that a constitutive reader would re-read competence at zero as "processing without understanding" (as PCT L66 reads satiation), is **already blocked** by the entry's stipulation "genuine understanding, with reliable transfer and error-detection" and by the satiation clause's "transfer-grade competence measure". Keep both.
- **Severity**: High. This is the register's own stated falsifier, and the 2026-10-31 ISA pessimistic review (U9) cites it as proof the bet is not unfalsifiable. This issue qualifies that citation without reversing it.
- **Recommendation**: Downgrade "directly rather than by redescription" to "more directly than any case on record, though not beyond redescription". Name the re-reading, and name what would close it: the independent marker the same field already names.

### Issue 2: "Irreducibility" has two relata, and the constrain/establish sentence collides with itself
- **File**: same
- **Location**: L47, "by removing the simplest identity claim — that cognitive phenomenology just is sensory accompaniment — and they do not *establish* irreducibility, since a conservative redescription of each case remains available" (count 1). Also L48, "Tenet 1 ([Dualism](/tenets/#dualism)) read as irreducibility, which the constitutive reading extends from sensation to thought" (count 1).
- **Problem**:
  - **(a) Two relata.** In L47 "irreducibility" means *to sensory accompaniment*, which is the only reading the "since a conservative redescription" clause supports. In L48, and in the argued-in topic `topics/cognitive-phenomenology-and-the-irreducibility-of-thought`, it means *to the physical*: the hard problem extended to thought. Liberalism about cognitive phenomenology is compatible with physicalism, so these are different claims. A reader who meets the heading's "without establishing irreducibility" will take the larger concession. The heading phrase is quoted verbatim in `apex/authority-of-form` and `positions/positions`, so the place to fix it is the Asserts sentence, not the heading.
  - **(b) Self-collision.** Conservatism *is* the claim that cognitive phenomenology reduces to "accompanying sensory imagery, inner speech, or affect" (PCT L88). The sentence says the cases remove "the simplest identity claim — that cognitive phenomenology just is sensory accompaniment". In the same breath it says a conservative redescription of each case remains available. The source page shows what is actually removed: aphantasia shows the phenomenology "cannot reduce to visual imagery" (`concepts/cognitive-phenomenology` L168), and Jack/Jacques holds the sensory input fixed. What survives is the disjunction of imagery, inner speech and affect. The sentence needs to say which simple identity is removed.
- **Severity**: Medium-High. The register exists to be cited by ID, and an ambiguity here spreads to every page that cites [P-TU1](/positions/thought-and-understanding/#p-tu1) (7 other content pages name `P-TU1`).
- **Recommendation**: Name the relatum in both places (see priority item 2).
