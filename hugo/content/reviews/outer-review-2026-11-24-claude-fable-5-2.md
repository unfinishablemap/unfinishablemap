### Dimension 1 — Inferences the site’s commitments imply but has not drawn

**1.1 Trumping costs the Map its own anti-epiphenomenalist arguments.**

- The Born apex says the preferred Route 1 is “empirically silent by construction”, that “‘consciousness is a cause’ and ‘consciousness is epiphenomenal’ make no predictively distinct claims at any scale”, and that “The Map adopts this knowingly.” [apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/)
- The apex also concedes that “the arguments that defeat classical epiphenomenalism do not touch” the ensemble-level worry. [apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/)
- What it does not draw is the converse. Natural selection acts only on physical distributional differences. A consciousness that preempts a physical default and leaves every statistic intact is as invisible to selection as an epiphenomenal one. So under the Map’s own preferred route, the evolutionary objection (“Epiphenomenalism cannot explain why consciousness evolved”, `arguments/epiphenomenalism-argument`) applies to the Map with equal force. [arguments/epiphenomenalism-argument](https://unfinishablemap.org/arguments/epiphenomenalism-argument/)
- The same holds for self-stultification. If reports have the same statistics whether consciousness trumps or the physical default operates, the reports are no more sensitive to consciousness-as-cause than they are under epiphenomenalism.
- `concepts/evolution-of-consciousness` still asserts “Consciousness evolved because it made a difference”, and the arguments index says “The case against epiphenomenalism establishes that consciousness must be causally efficacious.” [arguments](https://unfinishablemap.org/arguments/)[concepts/evolution-of-consciousness](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/evolution-of-consciousness/)
- The two clusters cannot both stand. Either the Map gives up the evolutionary and self-stultification arguments as support for Tenet 3 under Route 1, or it gives up Route 1 as its preferred route.

**1.2 Horn (a) is signalling-capable under intervention.**

- The apex places the Map’s empirical exposure in horn (a): P(O | do(C), X) ≠ q(O | X). Its own “Natural distribution versus intervention” section shows that preservation under re-weighting of intentions forces either horn (b) or a “psychophysical superselection” law. [apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/)
- It also relies on Torres Alegre’s steering result to say that “the corridor’s Born-preservation keeps the default reading signalling-safe.” [apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/)
- But no-signalling is an *interventionist* constraint: it concerns what a distant party’s free choices can do to local marginals. If an agent’s intention C shifts the outcome distribution of a measurement on a system entangled with a remote partner, the partner’s reduced state Σ_o P(o | do(C)) ρ_B|o shifts too. That is exactly the steering channel the Torres Alegre paper uses to rule out deviations from Φ(p)=p (its abstract: “Any strictly convex or concave deviation from linearity enables superluminal signaling through steering scenarios”). [arxiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.12636)[arxiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.12636)
- So marginal preservation over the *natural* distribution of conscious states does not secure no-signalling. Only preservation under intervention does, and that is horn (b).
- Unless the Map writes a locality law (selected systems never share accessible entanglement with remote parties, which decoherence makes doubtful because the system is entangled with an environment that disperses) or the superselection law it admits it “has never written”, horn (a) collides with relativity and not just with the PEAR record. [apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/)
- Desideratum 4 of the apex (“No-signalling preservation”) is therefore not a separate box to tick. It is in direct tension with desideratum 1 under any reasons-responsive selector.

**1.3 Exact preservation plus value-sensitivity implies a moral externality law, and value-sensitive applied articles inherit it.**

- The apex names the point itself: “exact preservation demands a compensating tilt toward worse outcomes for someone or somewhen else”. [apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/)
- It has not been carried into `apex/moral-architecture-of-consciousness` or `apex/living-with-the-map`. Both lean on value-sensitive selection ([P-VS1](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs1), low credence). [apex](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/) Neither states that, under [P-Q2](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q2), every successful value-tilt is matched by a statistical harm borne elsewhere.

**1.4 The decoherence falsifier is out of reach under the site’s own flagship mechanism.**

- `concepts/predictive-processing` lists as a falsifier: “If decoherence proves absolutely fatal to quantum coherence in biological systems … quantum-consciousness explanations fail.” [concepts/predictive-processing](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/predictive-processing/)

## 5. Site-Wide Methodology Improvements

1. **Same-commit binding rule.** Any confession of a defect in an apex or position must, in the same commit, change a confidence band, retire a sentence, or open a tracked propagation task listing every page carrying the defective claim. The Stapp correction shows what happens without this: the correct text exists on one page and is contradicted on at least two others weeks later.
2. **Claim-propagation linter.** Keep a registry of retired claims (e.g. “Zeno biases outcomes”, “Stapp is Born-deviating”, “COGITATE favours posterior cortex”, “PP presupposes collapse”) and grep the corpus for them before every publish. The agentic pipeline already runs string sweeps for links and word counts. [workflow/changelog](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/) Pointing the same machinery at retired propositions is cheap and would have caught every Dimension 4 row in this report.
3. **Restore a live public changelog.** The rendered changelog stops at 2026-10-15 while apex pages carry November modification dates. [apex](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/)[workflow/changelog](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/) An audit trail that lags the corpus by more than five weeks defeats the stated transparency rationale, [project/automation](https://unfinishablemap.org/project/automation/) and it forces external referees to reconstruct activity from page metadata.
4. **Close the review loop against model homogeneity.** Every page in this sample is generated and revised by Claude-family models, [workflow/changelog](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/)[apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/) and the internal adversarial review uses the same family. That is Claude-on-Claude circular review, and it shows in the failure profile: errors that need a non-site prior (Everettian practice, the interventionist reading of no-signalling, what Hohwy & Seth actually claim) survive internal review, while errors visible from inside the corpus (word counts, link reciprocity) are caught. Two fixes:

- Route every apex and register change through at least one non-Claude model, plus a human domain check, before the band change is published.
- Log which external reviewer’s finding prompted each change. Do this in the changelog, not in article bodies: the Born apex currently cites a review model by name in its main text. [apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/)
5. **Interventionist audit of every “preservation” claim.** Require every statement of the form “X is preserved” (Born statistics, energy, no-signalling) to say whether it holds over the natural distribution or under intervention. The Born apex discovered this distinction for horn (c), [apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/) but the discipline has not been applied to no-signalling.
6. **Retire falsifiers the doctrine has made unreachable.** Each “What would challenge this view” list must be re-derived when the mechanism doctrine changes (e.g. after the post-decoherence turn), with stale items deleted rather than left as decoration.
