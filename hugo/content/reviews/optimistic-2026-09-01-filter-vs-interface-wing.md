## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

What Chalmers would value first is that the page keeps the metaphysics out of the metaphor. "Stated at that level the distinction belongs to no camp — it is a fact about the causal structure each metaphor commits to, and a physicalist can draw it as readily as a dualist" (L46); dualism "is supplied on the Map by Tenet 1, not by the metaphor" (L56). He would notice that the strongest rival the page names is inside dualism — "consciousness is independent of the brain and receives from it but never acts on it" — and that the page grants it everything: "The position is coherent, immune to the conservation objection, and free of the interface problem" (L92). That is the epiphenomenalist horn of naturalistic dualism, stated at full strength by a site that rejects it, with the rejection routed to Tenet 3 and to another page rather than won here. The channel-width article's refusal to place Kastrup at "zero width" belongs in the same register: "A channel-free position is off the axis, not at its bottom" (L70).

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

The wing registers Stapp's variant at two loci without adopting it, which is what he would ask of a site that does not share his mechanism. The new page: "Stapp's proposal locates the influence one step earlier, in the choice of which question is put to nature, leaving the outcome to nature's statistical rule … The distinction is neutral on which variant fills the outbound leg. It requires only that there be one" (L64). Channel width does more — it gives him three coordinates where the parent grid gave him one: "thick mind, thick physical, narrow channel … This is the axis earning its name: it converts a judgement call into a coordinate" (L78), with the honest rider that his basis-choice layer is "wider than strict outcome-level selection-only, so his channel is narrow but not at the absolute floor" (L66). Stapp would also note that the Beck & Eccles sentence the new page quotes is verbatim — checked this run against the PNAS abstract via Europe PMC: "The consequent voluntary actions become effective by momentary increases of the probability of vesicular emission in the thousands of synapses on each pyramidal cell by quantal selection."

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

Two passages are Nagel's kind. Penfield's ownership asymmetry is stated as a what-it-is-like datum — "the evoked action was experienced as something done *to* them, not *by* them" (L32) — and then treated as "a genuine explanandum the comparator model must also address, not a datum simplicity alone can dissolve" (L74). ### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

Direction-of-interface-change is the most process-friendly page in the wing: its whole point is that "the *direction* the system is moving — not the substrate state at a single moment — is the load-bearing variable" (L36), and it calls itself "the *temporal-derivative* face of the same architecture that the memory-system vulnerability hierarchy reads off the *substrate-state* face" (L56). Events over states; which-way-it-is-going over what-it-is. The new page's "Two legs, one coupling" (L54) is relation before relata. Per this persona's constraint, none of that moves anything: the direction-of-change page credits its family "at the framework-internal-weight register" and once (L64), and process resonance is not a reason to credit it twice. Where Whitehead might want the two legs read as mutual prehension of one process, the Map declines — it keeps two relata and a seam — and the channel-width article says why the Map can afford to: "a narrow channel firmly (MQI), a thin mind-pole only loosely (mind-medium, with room to expand)" (L136).

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

This persona's verdict is the review's headline, because the wing is where restraint is done under the most temptation.

**What is praiseworthily not done.** Penfield L62–66 had the structural opportunity of the year: a live physicalist theory (Bachmann, Suzuki & Aru 2020; Suzuki & Larkum 2020) associates consciousness with integration at the apical dendrites of layer-5 pyramidal cells — the same compartment Eccles bundled into the dendron in 1990. **Where the discipline lapses.** Two loci, both calibration concerns rather than strengths, both minor in wording and real in consequence:

- **`direction-of-interface-change` L80** (§Relation to Site Perspective, Tenet 3): "If consciousness merely received from the brain, recovery order would be fixed by substrate readiness alone and direction should not matter." The page refutes this twice before reaching it. L44's hysteresis member is direction-dependence produced by "mutually inhibitory wake-active and anaesthesia-active populations [forming] a bistable switch" — a substrate mechanism with nothing received or acting. So the L80 conditional is false on the page's own terms, and the only member that even prima facie wants an engagement story is recovery-order reversal (the autonoetic swing) — which L70 already concedes "a dying-process-specific neuromodulatory surge" absorbs. - **`filter-vs-interface-distinction` L76**: covert consciousness "is the same pattern with a less localised lesion. Neither is a filter-loosening case, and on this criterion neither could be mistaken for one: the *width* of experience has not changed." Two lines earlier (L74) the page says an inbound change "shows only through report or through the imaging proxies covert-consciousness research relies on". Bodien et al. 2024's cognitive-motor-dissociation cohort are severe-brain-injury patients; what command-following in imaging shows is that *some* experience persists, not that its width is unchanged. The claim the argument needs is "no widening is in evidence", and that is all the criterion licenses. One clause; folded into the task below.
