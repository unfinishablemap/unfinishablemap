**Two escape routes, neither of which closes the dilemma.**

- *The conditional-marginal move.* `concepts/causal-closure` argues that Born preservation holds of "the *unconditioned* long-run marginal" and leaves "distributions conditioned on subject, task, or intention formally unconstrained". [concepts/causal-closure](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/causal-closure/)[concepts/causal-closure](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/causal-closure/) But conditional deviations that average out still *are* deviations from Born statistics in the conditioned ensembles. They are exactly what intention-to-RNG and Maier-Dechamps-type preregistered designs test. [topics/born-rule-and-the-consciousness-interface](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/born-rule-and-the-consciousness-interface/) The page itself admits "the coarsest of those conditional grains have already returned nulls". [concepts/causal-closure](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/causal-closure/)[concepts/causal-closure](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/causal-closure/) So the move turns an unfalsifiable claim into a partly falsified one, and then retreats to grains "no current method reaches". That is an epistemic-to-metaphysical slide wearing empirical clothes.
- *The "selection" vocabulary.* The Born topic now admits that "selection" read as a pick from definite alternatives "would presuppose the proper mixture decoherence never delivers". It also admits actualisation "owes three physical debts" (POVM, event timing, dynamical addition), and that nothing "discharges them". [topics/born-rule-and-the-consciousness-interface](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/born-rule-and-the-consciousness-interface/)

**The dilemma stands.** Either the influence is statistically invisible at every accessible grain, in which case "selection" adds nothing a physicalist-plus-brute-chance model lacks. Or it is visible, in which case it is Born-bending and the existing nulls count against it.
