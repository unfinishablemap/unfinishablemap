## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

1. **The 09-30 propagation clause was unscoped (L75).** It said the indistinguishability-from-chance charge "holds, by construction, under any *unconditioned aggregate* test", leaving a conditioned deviation as "the interface's one live empirical register". That holds only on the corridor reading. Outside-corridor readings accept a sub-detection Born departure, which falsifier (c) bites on in the unconditioned register (tenets L81). The clause also hung off a third em-dash in one sentence. **Fix**: split it off, scoped to the corridor reading Tenet 2's minimality endorses (tenets L69/L71): only an intention-, task- or subject-conditioned deviation would test the corridor ([P-Q3](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q3)), and outside-corridor readings are named as fallbacks accepting a Born departure held below detection. Attribution checked: the [P-Q3](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q3) anchor resolves (`positions/quantum-interface` L79 `^mechanism-debt`), and tenets L75 cites the same anchor for the conditioned register. No editor residue. The "live structural fork" wording does not appear.

2. **Tenet 1 misstated (L85, L33; tenet-check "In kind").** L85 said "The Map's Dualism tenet holds that consciousness differs from matter in kind, not in degree." Tenets L53 requires irreducibility to physical processes, "whether substance, property, or aspect", and is neutral between substance and property dualism. Distinctness comes from pairing Tenet 1 with Bidirectional Interaction ("distinct yet causally connected", L53). **Fix**: L85 now says the tenet requires only irreducibility, that process monism grants this, and that the difference in kind is the Map's further claim drawn from that pairing. L33 "distinct in kind from matter" became "in its interactionist form: ... distinct from matter yet causally connected with it". The bedrock line now reads "gradation or irreducibility in kind", which says which irreducibility is at issue. The Stability-Note bedrock itself is unchanged.

3. **Opponent typing (brief item 2).** The page never called process monism physicalism; L89 already scoped the interface programme to separating dualism from *physicalism*. Two places mistyped it, though:
   - The Further Reading gloss on [reductionism](/concepts/reductionism/) read "graded or aspectual reduction of mind to process still counts, for the Map, as a reduction it declines". That types the rival as reductive. It is also a phantom claim: [concepts/reductionism.md](/concepts/reductionism/) mentions this page only in frontmatter and says nothing about graded or aspectual reduction. **Fix**: "The reduction of mind to the physical that the Dualism tenet rules out, and that process monism rejects too."
   - The typing was absent where the page states the Map's reply. Chalmers 2003 (verified on the consc.net author's text) lists "Griffin 1998" among the versions of type-F monism, and says the protophenomenal form "can be seen as a sort of neutral monism". **Fix**: L85 now places process monism with nonreductive type-F, not with physicalism, linking [russellian-monism](/concepts/russellian-monism/) and [type-a-type-b-and-type-c-physicalism](/concepts/type-a-type-b-and-type-c-physicalism/). Is the reply aimed at the right type? Yes. The Whitehead reply (panpsychism, with the combination problem as its price) is the cost Chalmers himself names: type-D "does have the significant advantage of avoiding the type-F view's 'combination problem'".
   - **Consequence fixed at L89.** Chalmers builds microphysical closure into type-F; it is his third nonreductive option. The type-A/B/C page L128 makes D-vs-F turn on closure. So L89's "a process monist can re-describe any interface datum" now adds "though only by giving up the microphysical closure Chalmers builds into type-F". Without this, typing the rival as type-F would have contradicted the type-A/B/C page.

4. **Subject splice plus unsourced quote (L59).** The page read: the soul is not a separate substance "but 'a lower, dynamic level' of one graded existence", credited to readings by Nasr and Kalin.
   - The phrase is absent from SEP (0 hits).
   - It is absent from Kalin 2010 (Google Books search-within: 0 hits, with controls "substantial motion" 9 and "intellect and the intelligible" 6).
   - It is absent from Shameli, the source the corpus credits for it (0 hits in the full 11,790-word chapter, Wayback 2025-06-20 capture; the live site is Cloudflare-blocked).
   - Every version of the claim makes the *body* the lower level. The corpus's own wording on the host page is "Body ... is 'a lower, dynamic level of the soul's existence'"; Kalin p. 162 has the physical as the "lower level, not the opposite of what is beyond the physical". This page had made the *soul* the subject.

   **Fix**: replaced with Kalin's verbatim rendering, "material in its origination and spiritual in its subsistence" (Kalin 2010, pp. 162–163; the sentence's subject is "the human soul (or the self)"; p. 163 continues "this establishes an ontological continuity between soul and body"). The Nasr co-attribution is kept as it stood (see ledger).

5. **Whitehead subject splice (L51).** "Creativity is, in Whitehead's terms, 'the Category of the Ultimate'". In PR, "'Creativity,' 'many,' 'one' ... These three notions complete the Category of the Ultimate", and creativity is "the universal of universals". The 06-18 review had ratified "= creativity". **Fix**: "Creativity, which with 'many' and 'one' makes up what Whitehead calls 'the Category of the Ultimate,' is the principle by which ..." That creativity is the principle by which the many become one is verbatim in PR.

6. **Tenet 4 misstated (L89; tenet-check N).** The page said the Map retains the persisting subject "because Tenet 4 treats indexical identity ... as a datum given in the having of experience". Tenets L121: the Map endorses that "I" "on independent grounds ... not from within this tenet", and the indexical question's meaningfulness is "a posited claim, not a neutral datum". The sentence contradicted its own closing clause. **Fix**: the Map posits the subject on grounds independent of Tenet 4 (agency / agent causation); Tenet 4's indexical objection presupposes that subject rather than supplying it; the Tenet 5 concession is kept verbatim.
