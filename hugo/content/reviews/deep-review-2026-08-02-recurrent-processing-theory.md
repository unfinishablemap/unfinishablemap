## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

**1. COGITATE findings misreported, in the Map's favour — empirical-claim fidelity plus source conflation.** The article claimed COGITATE's "headline findings are broadly consonant with RPT's local, posterior, first-order emphasis: posterior cortical areas were sufficient for conscious content, and neither prefrontal involvement nor global broadcasting proved necessary."

Europe PMC full text (PMC12137136) shows the phrases "posterior cortical areas are sufficient" and "neither the involvement of PFC nor global broadcasting are necessary" **do not occur in the paper's own text**. They come from the IIT proponents' supplementary interpretation — one adversary's reading inside an adversarial collaboration — and were being presented as the consortium's result.

The consortium's actual conclusions run the other way for RPT in two places:
- Content information was found in "visual, ventrotemporal **and inferior frontal cortex**", and content-selective connectivity ran from FFA to "V1/V2, inferior frontal gyrus and intraparietal sulcus" — cutting against RPT's *locality*.
- "A lack of sustained synchronization within the posterior cortex" was the study's most direct negative finding — cutting against *sustained posterior recurrence*, which is RPT's proposed mechanism.

So the passage credited RPT with support from a result that substantially challenges it. **Resolution**: paragraph rewritten to quote the consortium's own abstract ("align with some predictions of IIT and GNWT, while substantially challenging key tenets of both theories"), to state both directions of pressure explicitly, and to add a provenance caution flagging the proponent-supplement origin of the circulating quote. This was flagged as a suspected drift in the 2026-07-11 pessimistic review's Unsupported Claims table and left unaddressed; it is now confirmed at the publisher and corrected.
