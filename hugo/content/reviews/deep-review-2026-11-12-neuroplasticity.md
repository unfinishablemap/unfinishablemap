## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

1. **Kral 2022 presented as overturning Lazar 2005.** The page listed Lazar 2005 and Hölzel 2011 together as "early studies" and said Kral's null meant "the earlier positive findings likely reflected small samples, lack of active controls, and circular analysis", under an intro saying long-term meditators' structural claims "have been weakened by replication failures". Kral et al. test eight-week MBSR in meditation-naïve participants; their reference list does not contain Lazar 2005, and their text separates "cross-sectional research of long-term meditation practitioners" from MBSR. Their limitations sentence is about "Prior studies on MBSR-related changes in brain structure". The 2026-07-16 review ratified the conflation ("cited AS the earlier positive structural findings later overturned by Kral 2022"). Fixed: the two designs are separated, the limitations are attributed to Kral and scoped to MBSR studies, and the page says Kral's trial does not test the long-term-practitioner comparison. Kral's own caveat about pre-existing differences in cross-sectional designs is used for Lazar.
2. **Schwartz 1996 result misdescribed** (reduced activity in "these regions"). See ledger. Fixed.
3. **Coiner error on "self-directed neuroplasticity".** See quotation ledger. Fixed.
4. **Stapp's mechanism described as outcome selection, against today's tenets.** The Tenet 4 paragraph said "Stapp's quantum Zeno mechanism requires genuine collapse—consciousness selects *one* outcome from a superposition." `tenets.md` (Bidirectional Interaction, "Outcome-selection, not context-selection") says Stapp located mental influence in Process 1 and "left the outcome to nature"; the 2005 paper says effort does "nothing but control 'attention density'" and speaks of "Nature's statistical choice". Fixed: consciousness chooses the question and its rate, nature returns the single answer. The same paragraph now states that the Map's rejection of MWI rests on the indexical objection and that keeping the selection space open is a consequence, not the ground.
5. **Decoherence objection stated as if the model needed surviving coherence.** L119 said most physicists doubt "that quantum coherence survives long enough ... for this mechanism to operate". Schwartz, Stapp & Beauregard say "Strong decoherence effects are certainly present, but they are automatically taken into account in the von Neumann formulation employed here." Under check (c-vi) the respondent's answer has to be on the page. Fixed: objection and reply both stated, minority status kept. This matches the corrected wording on `clinical-neuroplasticity-evidence-for-bidirectional-causation` L108 and the open P3 task on the other pages.
6. **Lead and tenet section graded above the body (calibration).** The lead said neuroplasticity "demonstrates that mental states can reshape neural architecture" and "creates problems for epiphenomenalism"; L138 says the correlation "discriminates nothing" between interactionism and epiphenomenalism. The tenet section opened "provides empirical support for several of the Map's commitments" and its Dualism entry said the attention–region match "becomes coincidental" without mental causation, which contradicts L138 and the epiphenomenalist pathway the page itself sets out. A tenet-accepting reviewer would flag all three. Fixed: the lead reports the finding and says it is "often pressed against" epiphenomenalism; the tenet section says the evidence is compatible with the tenets and short of establishing them; the Dualism entry says the match does not favour the interactionist explanation over its rivals. L138 "mental effort has physical consequences" became "effortful mental practice is followed by physical change", and "changes in brain structure" became "changes in brain function" (the page's own Kral paragraph makes structural change the less secure claim, and Schwartz's finding is metabolic). The stability notes of earlier reviews exempt bedrock disagreements, not these empirical-support sentences.
7. **Stale gloss on `mental-effort`** at L97 and L138. See quotation ledger. Fixed.
