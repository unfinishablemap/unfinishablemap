## Executive Summary

The Penrose/Diósi-Penrose empirical-prospects article is, on the Hardline Empiricist lens, the strongest single piece of evidential-status discipline in the corpus: it reports the disconfirmation of its own preferred baseline mechanism at full strength, declines a rescue that would have helped it, and concedes that "no honest reading of [the experiments] favours collapse." Five refine passes since 2026-07-23 have converged it, and no further work is recommended there.

The finding is that this discipline **has not propagated to the article's own designated companion**. The falsification roadmap — the Map's flagship "what would change our mind" document — still reports the 2021 Gran Sasso result with its polarity inverted, states that "Penrose's original proposal" survived when it is precisely Penrose's original proposal that was excluded, omits both later and tighter bounds entirely, and upgrades an unrefereed preprint its source article explicitly downgrades. Every divergence runs in the Map's favour. One `refine-draft` task minted.

## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

The DP article keeps the explanatory gap and the collapse mechanism on separate books. Line 92 is the load-bearing move: gravitational OR "determines *when* collapse occurs … but not *which outcome* it selects." The Map's interface claim is thereby quarantined from the physics it borrows, so a verdict on Penrose does not silently become a verdict on dualism. Line 114 states the consequence in the direction that costs the Map something: confirming gravity-related collapse "would strengthen the case for objective collapse and against many-worlds **without touching the interface claim**." A framework helping itself to a friendly physics result would not volunteer that.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

Stapp would find the mechanism taken seriously rather than name-dropped. The article carries the actual parameter (R₀), the actual bounds (0.54, 2.54, 4.5 × 10⁻¹⁰ m), the convention on which they are commensurable (Diósi's rate convention, "which differs from Penrose's by a factor of 8π"), and the scope limit that convention imposes — dissipative and coloured generalisations are not covered by white-noise exclusions. That is engagement at the level where the physics can actually bite.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

Restraint rather than praise here: the article does not reach for first-person considerations to soften a physics result, and does not let the hard problem's difficulty license optimism about the mechanism. The roadmap does the corresponding work at line 71, where the honest assessment of Tenet 1 concedes there is "no path to decisive falsification … today" and that dualism "earns its keep not by being directly testable."

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

Whitehead would like line 88's handling of Penrose's proto-consciousness suggestion: the article notes it "implies a panpsychism the Map does not endorse" and routes the tension to a comparative article rather than absorbing a friendly metaphysics for free. Note per the skill's constraint that none of this praise is used anywhere in the article as a tier-upgrade — the Map's affinity with objective collapse never becomes evidence *for* objective collapse. Compare line 104: "no honest reading of them favours collapse."

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

The corridor/selection division of labour (line 94) preserves a genuine locus of agency without inflating what physics supplies: OR provides "the *occasion* for collapse while consciousness … influences the *selection* among Born-permitted outcomes." The roadmap then prices that commitment honestly, conceding at line 111 that establishing the causation is *non-physical* rather than merely complex "seems extremely difficult."

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

The DP article refuses to schedule a verdict. The lead says "no timetable for a verdict is defensible"; MAQRO "shows that decisive tests are technologically conceivable, not that one is scheduled"; matter-wave interferometry is fourteen orders of magnitude short, and the article says so with the arithmetic. The roadmap matches it at line 153: "the Map is partially testable, partially a framework choice."

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

**This is the persona's best case in the corpus, and it is load-bearing for the review.** Four things the article does that are praiseworthy specifically as things it declined to do:

1. **It reports its own disconfirmation at full strength, and identifies what was excluded correctly.** Line 44: the bound is "roughly one order of magnitude above the value Penrose's own prescription fixes — … What the experiment excludes is therefore Penrose's own physically determined value, **not a limiting idealisation**." That last clause is a deliberate correction of the softer framing still live elsewhere in the corpus (see below).

2. **It prices the survival.** Line 48: the surviving model buys its survival by demoting R₀ to a free parameter, which "costs the model its distinguishing claim: the *natural* macroscopic quantum-classical boundary … that the instability argument above advertises." The rescue is granted and then invoiced.

3. **It declines a rescue that would have helped it.** McQueen (2023) argues the DP falsification does not touch Orch OR at all. The article could have banked this. Instead, line 52: "This is structurally a rescue of the kind Donadi flagged: the falsifying conditions are relocated to a regime current experiments cannot reach." A framework protecting its own would not name its own escape hatch as an escape hatch.

4. **It runs the unearned-confirmation test against itself.** Line 98 is the strongest passage: "Every null result it reports … is exactly what standard quantum mechanics without collapse predicts… Treating collapse as the baseline and the null results as mere constraints on its parameters inverts the dialectical burden: **the record so far has cost the collapse programme and cost its rival nothing.**" This is precisely the inverse of the "predicts exactly what we observe" family repaired elsewhere today — here the article applies the discipline to its own preferred mechanism, unprompted. Line 104 closes it: "no honest reading of them favours collapse."

Also worth naming: line 102 concedes that Bohmian mechanics solves the prebiotic-collapse problem too, so "the prebiotic-collapse problem therefore does not select gravitational OR uniquely" — surrendering a uniqueness claim the Map had standing to assert; and line 114 applies the same discipline to the *defeater*, refusing to set an unreachable falsification condition ("Blanket falsification of collapse models is a condition no experiment could deliver").

On the roadmap, the Tenet-2 sub-reading split (lines 77–95, table 139–151) is the corresponding structural achievement: it exists specifically to stop the mechanism-committed readings' experimental traction "being silently exported to the strict corridor or the trumping reading, where the same traction is not available," and it states plainly that the strict corridor is "Not Popper-refutable" and the trumping reading "empirically indistinguishable from epiphenomenalism on Born-rule data alone." Line 93 names the Lakatosian protective-belt objection as one "the Lakatosian critic reasonably reads" — conceded, not rebutted — and anchors the whole thing to the pre-Keplerian framework-stage calibration the persona explicitly praises.

**Verdict**: no possibility→probability slippage found in either article. The Process Philosopher and the Hardline Empiricist converge rather than conflict here, which by the skill's own test means the tension has been resolved honestly.

## Calibration Concern (minted, not praised)

### The roadmap's collapse record is stale, and every divergence from its own source article flatters the Map

The roadmap lists `penrose-gravity-induced-collapse-empirical-prospects` in `related_articles` (L35) and the DP article reciprocates (L22). They disagree, and the roadmap is on the wrong side of the disagreement three times over.

**(a) Polarity inversion — the primary finding.** Roadmap L91: "The Gran Sasso experiment (Donadi et al., 2021) rules out the simplest form of the Diosi-Penrose collapse model **but not Penrose's original proposal**."

The parameter-free model *is* Penrose's original proposal — that is exactly what makes it parameter-free. Penrose fixes R₀ from the nuclear wavefunction spread (0.05 × 10⁻¹⁰ m for germanium); Donadi et al. set R₀ > 0.54 × 10⁻¹⁰ m; Penrose's value is an order of magnitude below the bound and is therefore the thing excluded. The DP article says this outright and appears to be correcting exactly this wording: "What the experiment excludes is therefore Penrose's own physically determined value, **not a limiting idealisation**" (L44), and in the lead, "The version in which Penrose fixes the model's one length parameter from physics rather than fitting it was falsified in 2021; versions that fit it survive."

So the roadmap has it backwards: it credits survival to the version that was excluded, and does not report that the surviving version survives only by giving up the parameter-free claim.

This is not a one-off. The same inverted clause is live in two further loci — [topics/brain-internal-born-rule-testing.md](/topics/brain-internal-born-rule-testing/) L102 and L134, both reading "the original Penrose proposal survives with reduced parameter space" — and traces to [research/tenet-falsification-conditions-2026-04-05.md](/research/tenet-falsification-conditions-2026-04-05/) L152. Note also that the deep review of 2026-06-02 **ratified** the roadmap's wording as "the correct stance," which is why five subsequent refine passes on the DP article never reached it.

The bare phrasing "the simplest Diósi-Penrose model is ruled out", used in `philosophical-stakes-of-spontaneous-collapse`, `sorkin-delta-brain-internal-analogues`, `comparing-quantum-consciousness-mechanisms` and the archive, is **fine** and should not be swept — the defect is specifically the "but not / survives" clause that attributes survival to Penrose's own version.

**(b) The record stops in 2021.** The roadmap contains zero occurrences of "Majorana" or "XENON". Its source article carries three bounds on a common footing (Gran Sasso 0.54, Majorana 2.54 with its 2023 erratum, XENONnT 4.5 × 10⁻¹⁰ m), plus the Figurato 2024 two-sided squeeze. The roadmap's Tenet-2 status paragraph and the Orch-OR table row (L145) both present a five-year-old single bound as current.

**(c) An unrefereed preprint doing more work than its source allows.** Roadmap L91 says arXiv:2512.02838 "offers a near-term path to testing whether collapse is real"; L123 says it "could distinguish decoherence … from genuine collapse"; L150 gives "Near-term experimental blueprints" as No-MWI's entire traction cell. The DP article at L62 calls the same item "Horchani's unrefereed 2025 preprint," notes it is "proposal rather than measurement," and says it is "aimed principally at CSL." This is a currency/framing gap rather than a factual error, but it runs the same direction as (a) and (b).

**Why this matters more than a routine currency fix**: the roadmap's entire claim to credibility is that it reports what would go against the Map. An article that exists to state disconfirmation conditions, under-reporting the single strongest disconfirmation in its own domain, is a failure at the exact point where the document's value lies.
