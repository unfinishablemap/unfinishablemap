### Critical Issues Found
- **Calibration slippage (rule-out vs count-against)**: the lead said the reversals "rule out" the destroyed-monitor reading and §What Reversal Constrains said they "rule out the first reading for the stroke syndrome", while the same article (correctly) said they "count against" it. - **Unmeasured "no tissue change" claim + permanence confound**: the lead said reversal happens "without any change to the damaged tissue" (never measured) and the destroyed-monitor paragraph leaned on the video restoring awareness "for good" — but Vocat shows most cases resolve within weeks spontaneously, so the permanence is confounded with natural recovery. Resolved: the lead now says "within minutes, too fast for the damaged tissue to have changed"; the argument now rests on the *instant* restoration, with a parenthesis marking the persistence as weaker evidence.
