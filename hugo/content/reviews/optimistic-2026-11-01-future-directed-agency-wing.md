## Executive Summary

These three pages form a natural wing. PM covers holding an intention across time, CF covers representing the alternatives a choice is made among, and AU covers whether the choice that settles among them is the agent's own. All three are unusually disciplined about evidence. PM states its own central qualia claim as a "*theoretical posit*", and it splits the episodic-future-thinking (EFT) behaviour data into two causal pictures. CF concedes the LLM case honestly. ## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

CF's illusionism reply puts the burden in the right place. It asks the illusionist to "either deny the phenomenology (eliminativism) or explain how unconscious processes generate it". PM's regress argument (L134), that the seeming-to-project-forward "must seem *to* something", is the cleanest one-paragraph version of that point in the cluster. Chalmers would also value something the pages *don't* do. AU's Dualism paragraph (L194) rests on the felt contrast between "this choice is mine" and scripted compliance. It does not rest on the neural signatures, which the article has already called compatible with both readings. The phenomenal datum is kept separate from the correlational one.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

PM's Minimal Quantum Interaction paragraph (L170) is the most honest statement of the decoherence problem in the agency cluster. It names the timescale gap ("millisecond-to-day"). It retires the photosynthesis analogy on Duan et al. (2017); that metadata was publisher-verified in earlier reviews, and I did not re-check it. It ends by calling the tenet "a programmatic commitment contingent on future mechanism-finding rather than as established physics". Stapp's own programme is best served by that kind of statement. AU's MQI paragraph (L198) claims less: deliberation "may correspond to the interval" of interface engagement. CF's agent-causation paragraph is flatter, but its hedging is settled in the Stability Notes ("Quantum mechanism speculation — appropriately hedged"), so I do not re-flag it.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

PM centres something Nagel would recognise at once: what it is like to imagine tomorrow, as distinct from remembering yesterday. Its §The Impossibility of Anticipatory Qualia (L90–L102) is a genuinely original argument. The pastness quale tracks causal contact; the future "having caused nothing yet", so pre-experience is structurally impossible. The argument explains a phenomenological asymmetry from the causal structure of time, with no appeal to neural architecture. AU's two five-feature lists (L37–L63) are the best phenomenological taxonomy in the agency cluster: resonance, felt alternatives, effort, ownership and temporal thickness, each set against an inauthentic counterpart.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

PM's §Process Philosophy Perspective (L150–L152) reads the "pop" of prospective retrieval as "the occasion's felt reception of newly relevant possibilities". It is fully conditional: "if futuricity is genuine, it marks this distinctive mode". Process-friendliness here supports nothing beyond itself, which is exactly what the persona constraint requires. AU's Bergsonian "temporal thickness" and "whole self" give authentic choice its *durée* without treating the choosing self as a crude substance. CF's image of "alternatives collapsing to actuality—one becoming real while others fade to mere counterfactuals" reads naturally as concrescence.

**Where this persona's praise must stop.** It must not reach PM's Dualism paragraph (L168). That paragraph says flatly that future simulation "involves irreducible qualia—futuricity". This is the one place in the wing where the two personas conflict, and the conflict goes to the Hardline Empiricist below. It is Priority 1.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

AU's authentic choice is structurally close to Kane's self-forming actions (SFAs): effortful, torn, with alternatives that are really live, and owned whichever way they go (`topics/event-causal-libertarianism#kanes-self-forming-actions`, L46–L48). Kane would add one thing AU lacks, which is his *tracing*: "Many free actions may flow deterministically from a character that earlier SFAs forged" (ECL L48). AU's "Automaticity" bullet (L59) treats running automatically as a mark of inauthenticity. It never says that a habit or plan an earlier authentic choice set up runs automatically too and is still the agent's own. PM already supplies the resource: "The libertarian needn't claim every proximal motor command involves conscious intervention—what matters is that conscious planning participates in shaping the trajectory of action" (PM L114). Joining these is Priority 3. CF's three-step deliberation schema (L84–L90: represent, evaluate, select) is the cleanest statement in the corpus of why libertarian choice needs counterfactual representation.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

PM's L102 is pure McGinn: "Minds can access what has touched them but cannot reach forward to what hasn't." CF's handling of the LLM case is a model of epistemic humility. It leaves "whether consciousness is merely a biological route to this capacity or a constitutive requirement" open. It also admits that the understanding-gap reply "risks circularity". The Map's `voids/counterfactual-void` argues that the counterfactual faculty is tethered to actuality's neighbourhood, and that we can assess the tether only with the tethered faculty. CF never links that void, and the void never links CF (0 each way). That is the most Mysterian connection in the wing, and it is missing (Priority 2 d/e).

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

This persona's verdict matters most for this wing, and it is mostly praise.

- **PM, two-picture decomposition (L120–L122).** The EFT data are "consistent with two pictures". The Map commits to the phenomenology-causes picture "on independent grounds", and "Readers who do not accept those commitments should read the EFT evidence as compatible with consciousness participating in decision-making rather than as showing it does." This is the *tenet-coherent, not evidence-elevating* pattern stated in so many words. - **CF, the working-memory paragraph (L72).** "The argument rests less on the raw numerical gap than on what the span permits", and the 2±1 figure is "held loosely here". The Stability Notes settle the loose holding, and I do not re-open it. The citations were publisher-verified on 07-29; I did not re-check them.

The 09-27 rescoping adds "a relative increase in a spatial-cueing task, not a signature confined to willed action", which is restraint added after the last deep review.
