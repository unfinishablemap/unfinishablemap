## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

1. **Hardin's stance mislabelled (attribution error). Fixed.** L76 called Hardin "an eliminativist about colour qualia". The SEP "Color" entry (raw text) lists Hardin 1988 [1993] among contemporary colour *Eliminativists*, where eliminativism is the denial that physical objects have colours. His position on colour experience is materialist, not eliminativist (cf. his 1987 "Qualia and Materialism: Closing the Explanatory Gap", cited by the SEP inverted-qualia entry). The label was installed on 2026-07-28 by the author-stance pass and has now been corrected to "an eliminativist about object colour and a materialist about colour experience". The sentence's point (he presses detectability to undermine the thought experiment) is unaffected.
2. **Cross-article contradiction on the zombie argument. Fixed.** L175 said "The zombie and knowledge arguments do similar work without this tension", the tension being that Bidirectional Interaction makes strict behavioural identity plus phenomenal difference physically unrealised. `concepts/philosophical-zombies` concedes exactly this tension for zombies (its interactionist-escape passage and its Bidirectional Interaction paragraph: adopting the tenet "makes physically identical zombies nomologically impossible"). Only the knowledge argument escapes it. Now: "The knowledge argument does similar work without this tension; the zombie argument shares it", with a piped link to the zombie page.
3. **SEP quotation mis-located. Fixed.** L98 said "The same passage names its dissenter" before the Shoemaker "notable dissenter" quote. In the raw SEP text both sentences sit in §3.1, but about 7,000 characters apart. The dissenter note is attached to the anti-*functionalist* point (a zombie scenario refutes functionalism more simply), and the "almost uncontroversially" sentence is the anti-*physicalist* one. "On this point" therefore referred to a different claim from the one the article had just quoted. Now: "The same section names a dissenter from the parallel anti-functionalist claim". Both quotations are verbatim.
4. **Unverified quoted span (Schwitzgebel). Reduced.** L126 quoted Schwitzgebel (2011) as saying we are "frequently and massively mistaken". The 2026-10-23 review of `concepts/discrimination-problem` found "frequently" absent from both raw author drafts and reduced the quote there, leaving this sibling live. Retrieval this pass: PhilArchive and Springer returned 403 or challenge pages, so no raw grep was possible. A search index reports the span quoted at p. 129 of the 2011 book by Peels (2016, *Philosophical Studies*) and Ramm (2016). That is a lead, not a verification. The article now carries the strength unquoted ("massively rather than occasionally mistaken"), matching the sibling.
