## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

The hub does not treat the explanatory gap as a problem to be managed and then set aside — it makes the gap generative. `topics/phenomenal-value-realism` extends it into an *evaluative* gap: "The [explanatory-gap](/concepts/explanatory-gap/) between physics and experience extends to an evaluative gap—a dimension of the [hard-problem-of-consciousness](/topics/hard-problem-of-consciousness/) that metaethics has been slow to recognise. No equation captures why pain is bad." That is the hard problem doing real work in a domain that usually ignores it, rather than being cited as credentials.

The apex's four-textures argument for normativity (demand character, resistance to preference, gradedness across moral/epistemic/aesthetic/prudential domains) is the kind of structural phenomenological argument Chalmers would recognise as taking the datum seriously rather than explaining it away.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

The wing does not hide behind the interface — it prices it. The apex's "Open Frontier: Value in the Mechanism" section puts the register's verdict *before* the arguments ("The register's verdict belongs before the arguments"), states that value-sensitivity "requires a second mental-to-physical coupling — valence to physics — beyond the attentional one, and neither coupling has a worked implementation," and inherits rather than discharges the debt. `positions/value-in-selection` P-VS1 goes further, naming Stapp's observation-rate model as the *rival* horn's worked specification. Engaging the physics-consciousness interface while conceding which horn has the worked model is the honest version of this programme.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

`topics/phenomenology-of-moral-life` is the wing's most Nagelian article and its quietest strength. The temporal arc — pre-reflective strike, phenomenal deepening under sustained attention, oscillatory deliberative attention — is first-person data organised as data, not as illustration. Its treatment of *moral remainder* (the doctor who triages correctly and still feels genuine guilt for the one lost) is the clearest case in the wing of phenomenology constraining theory rather than decorating it.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

The wing avoids crude substance dualism at the point where it would be easiest to reach for it. `topics/moral-implications-of-genuine-agency` makes the substance commitment *local and visible* rather than inheriting it silently: "The Dualism tenet is neutral between [substance and property dualism](/concepts/substance-property-dualism/); what the moral arguments above lean on is the further commitment, drawn from [agent-causation](/concepts/agent-causation/), that the agent is a persisting subject exercising causal powers in its own right." A reader who accepts the tenet but rejects substance-bearing agents is told exactly where to get off.

The same article's treatment of Madhyamika is better than the usual accommodation move: "The Map and Madhyamika therefore disagree symmetrically rather than asymmetrically… Neither position simply lacks a feature the other has." That refuses the temptation to read a rival tradition as the Map minus a part.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

Agency is the wing's most developed pillar and it is developed without triumphalism. The moral-luck section relocates rather than eliminates: "Genuine agency relocates rather than eliminates causal luck… Constitutive and circumstantial luck partially absorb territory previously assigned to causal luck." Earlier drafts asserted elimination; the current text says so and corrects itself in-line.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

The wing's falsifiability sections are unusually honest about *what kind* of conditions they are. Both `moral-implications-of-genuine-agency` and the apex refuse to name near-perfect neural prediction as a falsifier, and name the refusal: treating these as falsifying conditions "would risk falsifiability theatre—gesturing at testability without bearing empirical risk." Declining to manufacture a testable-looking condition is a harder discipline than supplying one.

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch) — load-bearing for this review

This wing touches animal-consciousness boundary cases, so this persona's verdict carries. **It passes, in both directions, and the restraint is explicit rather than incidental.**

**Tenet-as-evidence-upgrade is praise-worthily *not* done.** The apex, at the exact sentence where the Process Philosopher's praise would cash out as a tier-upgrade, runs the P-M1 discipline instead: "Tenet 1 removes a defeater (animals have no metaphysical disqualification) but does not by itself raise evidential status." And it holds the line downward: "for cephalopods, decapods, and insects it is a 'realistic possibility, contested' rather than established consciousness; boundary organisms (nematodes, Hydra, slime molds) sit weaker still." Nematodes, *Hydra* and slime molds are named and held *below* realistic possibility — the precise case where tenet-load would most easily have been used as an engine.

**The strongest single line in the wing on this axis is the hub's refusal to claim an ally.** `topics/ethics-under-dualism` adopts Birch's decision procedure and declines to convert the borrowing into support: "That framework is deliberately metaphysics-neutral, and Birch himself assigns non-materialist views low credence, so the Map borrows his decision procedure without claiming him as an ally on the grounding question." Borrowing an action layer from someone who assigns your metaphysics low credence, and *saying so in the same sentence*, is the cleanest instance of "tenet-coherent, not evidence-elevating" in the wing.

**Precaution is kept as a discipline, not a licence.** The hub's invertebrate paragraph routes tenet-load to *precaution* while leaving the tier alone: insects stay at "Low" confidence in the patienthood table, and the argument reads "if dualism places no anthropocentric barrier on consciousness, even 'Low' confidence assigned to insects warrants precautionary consideration." The tenet buys precautionary weight; it does not buy a row-change.

**No conflict with the Process Philosopher.** The two personas praise different passages of the same wing without pulling against each other, which is the signal that the tension has been resolved honestly rather than papered over. I found no possibility/probability slippage to flag in the spine.

*One item I did not adjudicate and am flagging as a question, not a defect:* the hub's patienthood table rates insects "Low / Some" while the apex places insects at "realistic possibility, contested." These are two different scales (a confidence/weight table vs the five-tier evidential-status scale), and I did not establish that they conflict — a mapping between the two would be needed first. Recording it so a later pass can check rather than asserting a discrepancy.

## The single most reusable formulation, and where it should propagate

**The formulation** — `apex/moral-architecture-of-consciousness`, the Unity Argument:

> "The unity-as-evidence move requires the artifact-of-method discount [the countermeasures] specify. The four pillars were assembled within a corpus that prunes for internal consistency, and 'fits together' is a property such a corpus produces by construction. The defensible reading is therefore weaker than triumphant unification: dualism *makes available* a single ground for four domains that materialism leaves fragmented — the integrative move is genuine — but the four pillars do not constitute four independent confirmations. Each pillar bears its own evidential weight … the unity is what those pillars *enable*, not a fifth independent line."

All three quoted spans grep-verified verbatim in the raw file. This is the best writing in the wing: it concedes the coherence-inflation artifact, keeps what the integrative move actually buys, and closes off the tempting reading that four converging pillars are four confirmations.

**Where it should propagate: the hub.** `topics/ethics-under-dualism`'s Unity Argument makes the same claim without the discount:

> "Individual arguments may be disputed; the unification is what the Map takes to remain even where individual pillars are contested — though that structural claim is itself defeasible, as the falsifiability conditions below make clear."

Read to both ends: the hub *is* hedged — the trailing clause concedes defeasibility. But that is a different hedge. Defeasibility says the unity claim could turn out false; the artifact-of-method discount says the *evidence* for it is partly manufactured by the method that produced it. The hub carries the first and not the second, and two nearby passages lean the other way: "having paid that cost, unifies four domains materialism must address separately" reads the unity as a straightforward payoff, and falsifier #2 — "A materialist account achieving comparable unification would dissolve the unity argument" — treats unification as an evidential asset a rival could match rather than as a coherence artifact needing a discount first.

The hub also never cites `project/coherence-inflation-countermeasures` (0 occurrences), which the apex does.

**This is a fold, not an expansion.** One clause plus one link would carry the apex's formulation into the hub, in the wing's own voice. The hub has 652 words of headroom to its hard ceiling (3348 of 4000), so there is no length obstacle. I have **not** minted this: it is a calibration finding on a hub that would be better served by a single considered pass than by a P3 in a queue that already holds two tasks on this wing's spine. Recording it here so the next deep-review or apex-evolve pass on `ethics-under-dualism` has the target and the wording ready.

**Runner-up formulation, also from the apex** — the patienthood/agency separation, which is restraint stated as a strength: "Declining to run a responsibility floor off consciousness as such costs the position nothing; on this cluster it strengthens it, because patienthood is what grounds obligations toward beings who cannot be held responsible for anything."
