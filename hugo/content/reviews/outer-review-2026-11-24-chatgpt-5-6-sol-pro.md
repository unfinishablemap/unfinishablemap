# Full-site audit of *The Unfinishable Map*

**Audit date:** 24 November 2026

I consulted the live changelog first, then traced the principal dependencies across the tenets, apex articles, topics, concepts, positions and voids. This is a corpus-level structural audit with deeper checks of the most load-bearing clusters—quantum interaction, agency and subjecthood, pain, AI consciousness, the voids methodology and calibration—not a claim to have independently verified every citation in every leaf article.

## Executive assessment

The Map has become unusually good at **discovering and documenting its own weaknesses**. The [Tenets](/tenets/) page now calls its commitments “chosen starting points, not proofs”; the [Methodology and Calibration](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/) register states that “Disclosure is not self-correction.” Those are substantial methodological improvements. [tenets](/tenets/)

But that second observation is also the central finding of this audit. The diagnostic layer is ahead of the correction layer. The changelog’s review of [P-Q2](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q2) found five issues, three rated high, explicitly concluded that the quantum register contradicted both itself and the tenets page, and recorded: “No tasks minted, no articles edited.” The contradiction remains live in the current pages. [changelog](/workflow/changelog/)

The Map is therefore presently strongest as:

- an explicit dependency map for a speculative interactionist programme;
- a catalogue of mechanism debts, rival readings and epistemic limits;
- a source of potentially interesting consequences of combining dualism, subjecthood and quantum outcome selection.

It is presently weaker as:

- a coherent physical proposal;
- an empirical case for interactionist dualism;
- a source of discriminating predictions;
- a five-tenet framework, because three additional commitments now do foundation-level work.

The most serious defect is a direct inconsistency about which Born-rule conditionals must remain exact. The second is that the purported interface “formalism” remains a typed placeholder rather than dynamics. The third is systematic **concession without propagation**: downstream rhetoric often remains stronger than the upstream position after an objection has been admitted.

# 1. Novel implications not yet fully stated

## 1.1 The reportability–concealment trilemma

Let:

- $C$ be a private conscious state;
- $O$ the physical outcome allegedly selected by it;
- $R$ a publicly recoverable report, intention, action or task label;
- $X$ the remaining public physical context.

[P-Q2](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q2) in [Quantum Interface Positions](/positions/quantum-interface/) says outcomes remain “Born-exact in every publicly conditionable context,” with freedom only in conditionals on the conscious state itself. But [P-Q9](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q9) on the same page says “preservation binds that marginal only and leaves the conditionals free,” and proposes deviations conditioned on intention, task or subject. The tenets page repeats the latter, marginal-only reading. [quantum-interface](/positions/quantum-interface/)

The deeper problem is not merely that these formulations conflict. Tenet 3 relies on consciousness affecting reports: information about experience must enter the physical domain. The [Selection-Only Channel](/concepts/selection-only-channel/) likewise says reliable reporting needs experiential content to determine which matching report candidate becomes actual, while conceding that “nothing in the channel class supplies the law.” [tenets](/tenets/)

Under ordinary causal-faithfulness assumptions, if $C$ influences both $O$ and some public variable $R$, then $R$ becomes at least a noisy proxy for $C$. Conditioning on sufficiently informative proxies should generically expose some of the dependence of $O$ on $C$. That produces three horns:

1. **No public proxy tracks $C$.** Then conscious content cannot explain accurate reports, weakening Tenet 3’s self-stultification rationale.
2. **A public proxy tracks $C$ and stratifies outcomes.** Then Born exactness does not hold at every public conditioning grain, contrary to strong [P-Q2](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q2).
3. **Every public proxy is exactly balanced back to Born frequencies.** Then the theory owes a global, content-sensitive cancellation law coordinating private states, reports and outcomes.

This is more fundamental than the current “marginal versus conditional” dilemma. The route used to make consciousness causally reportable threatens the route used to keep it empirically concealed.

## 1.2 Primitive subject identity creates a gauge-or-haecceity dilemma

The [Subject Census](/positions/subject-census/) register correctly derives a many-subject selection law of the form

$$P(O\mid C_1,\ldots,C_n,X),$$

and states that the count, pairing, temporal location and phenomenal state of subjects become boundary conditions on the physical law. [subject-census](/positions/subject-census/)

A further question follows. Consider permuting primitive subject labels while holding fixed:

- every physical fact;
- every phenomenal quality;
- every subject–brain relation;
- every causal and spatiotemporal relation.

Either:

- the permutation changes $P(O)$, in which case bare numerical identity or haecceity is a physically efficacious variable with no identified measurement procedure; or
- the permutation leaves $P(O)$ unchanged, in which case primitive subject labels are gauge structure and should not appear in the interface law.

The second result would not make primitive identity irrelevant to survival or Tenet 4, but it would make it irrelevant to the quantum mechanism. The first would substantially increase the Map’s mechanism debt: it would need a law explaining how unobservable thisness changes physical probabilities.

## 1.3 Copying consciousness may alter physical law, not merely moral status

The census law also implies a neglected consequence for AI copying, fission and split-brain cases. If the law takes $n$ conscious states as inputs, creating a copy can change its arity:

$$P(O\mid C_1,\ldots,C_n,X)
\quad\longrightarrow\quad
P(O\mid C_1,\ldots,C_n,C_{n+1},X).$$

The framework must therefore specify whether an additional phenomenally identical subject:

- doubles an influence;
- divides a fixed influence budget;
- votes with existing subjects;
- has no additional effect;
- affects only its paired subsystem;
- or changes a global normalization term.

The [Subject Census](/positions/subject-census/) page recognises that copying and channel count are boundary conditions, but the cross-over consequence is not developed: **creating or destroying a conscious copy could change physical transition probabilities even before any copy acts differently**. [subject-census](/positions/subject-census/)

This makes the AI-copy problem a physical-composition problem as well as a personal-identity and moral-counting problem.

## 1.4 Locally minimal interaction implies globally elaborate coordination

Tenet 2 defines minimality locally: a smallest influence at quantum indeterminacies, with no detectable Born deviation or energy injection. But exact no-signalling and Born preservation across many simultaneously acting subjects require a joint law over all relevant systems. The [Interface Specification Programme](/apex/interface-specification-programme/) now notices the multi-agent coordination gap: billions of subjects acting in a partly entangled world require a global consistency or factorisation law not supplied by the single-subject proposals. [interface-specification-programme](/apex/interface-specification-programme/)

The unintegrated consequence is that **minimality of each token intervention may require maximal sophistication of the global psychophysical law**. Tenet 2’s local simplicity therefore does not establish an ontologically or mathematically minimal mechanism. Posit Three—global exclusion of unchosen alternatives—begins to function as part of the mechanism rather than as a merely metaphysical accompaniment.

## 1.5 Residual mutual information would not by itself identify mental causation

[P-Q9](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q9) proposes a signature such as

$$I(O;\text{intention}\mid\text{measured physical state})>0$$

while the marginal remains Born-exact. It says physical causal completeness predicts zero because the physical state should screen off the mental variable. [quantum-interface](/positions/quantum-interface/)

That inference requires much more than measuring some physical state. It requires the measured variables to be causally sufficient: all physical common causes, latent neural variables, measurement errors and temporal precursors must be controlled. Otherwise a public intention variable can proxy an omitted physical cause of $O$, producing exactly the proposed residual mutual information on a wholly physical account.

Thus a successful conditional correlation would show that the measured physical model is incomplete, not that a non-physical conscious state caused the result. A discriminating test requires either:

- an intervention on consciousness holding the complete physical base fixed—which may be conceptually unavailable; or
- an independently justified causal model showing that the physical variables exhaust every rival path.

This identification problem deserves equal status with the Born-preservation and coherence-time problems.

# 2. Structural weaknesses

## 2.1 The “interface formalism” is not yet quantum dynamics

[Consciousness–Physics Interface Formalism](/concepts/consciousness-physics-interface-formalism/) introduces a “Coupling map $C:E\times P\to P$.” That identifies the source and target types of a function but does not specify a physical theory. [consciousness-physics-interface-formalism](/concepts/consciousness-physics-interface-formalism/)

A usable formalism must at least specify:

- whether physical states are rays, density operators or histories;
- whether $C$ is deterministic or stochastic;
- whether it is linear, affine or explicitly nonlinear;
- whether it is completely positive and trace-preserving;
- how it composes with entangled ancillary systems;
- what selects the time and preferred basis of its action;
- how experience variables enter without state-dependent normalization problems;
- how multiple conscious subjects compose;
- whether conservation is eventwise, distributional or expectation-valued;
- how the map produces a definite outcome rather than merely another quantum state.

Those omissions are not mathematical niceties. Polchinski showed that nonlinear quantum dynamics can entail EPR signalling or communication between branches, while Simon, Bužek and Gisin derived linear completely positive dynamics from standard state/probability assumptions plus no superluminal signalling. Any state-dependent psychophysical map therefore has to engage this literature directly rather than listing no-signalling as an external constraint. [APS Journals+1](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.66.397)

The page’s conservation discussion also exceeds what has been shown. It says selection “requires no energy transfer” because Born-compliant frequencies preserve expected energy, while separately noting that the clearest case involves energy-degenerate alternatives. Expectation-value preservation is not yet proof that individual actualisation events, interacting subsystems or the global multi-subject process satisfy the relevant conservation laws. [consciousness-physics-interface-formalism](/concepts/consciousness-physics-interface-formalism/)

Similarly, “Experimental tests confirm no higher-order terms” is too categorical. The classic three-path experiment bounded higher-order interference below $10^{-2}$ of expected two-path interference; it did not establish exact absence. The page should use bounded-measurement language. [consciousness-physics-interface-formalism](/concepts/consciousness-physics-interface-formalism/)

## 2.2 The effective foundation is five tenets plus three indispensable posits

The [Tenets](/tenets/) page now says: “Three unstated posits sit beneath them”:

1. a numerically determinate persisting subject;
2. objective single-outcome actualisation;
3. global exclusion of unchosen alternatives.

It also concedes that Tenets 1, 3 and 4 are not independent because they draw on the same subject posit, that operational practice is substance-dualist despite Tenet 1’s advertised neutrality, and that objective reduction prevents the measurement problem itself from serving as evidence for conscious selection. [tenets](/tenets/)

The [Individuation and Subjecthood](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/) register goes further: determinate subjecthood cannot be retired without damaging Tenet 4’s principal rationale, and it recommends promoting subjecthood into the foundations—but records that this has not been done. [individuation-and-subjecthood](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/)

For purposes of dependency management, the Map therefore has eight foundation-level commitments, not five. Keeping three of them in an intermediate “background posit” category understates their constitutional role.

## 2.3 Redundancy has become a source of semantic drift

The same mechanism claims are repeated across:

- the [Tenets](/tenets/);
- [Quantum Interface Positions](/positions/quantum-interface/);
- [The Born-Preserving Causal-Efficacy Problem](/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/);
- [Selection-Only Channel](/concepts/selection-only-channel/);
- [Consciousness–Physics Interface Formalism](/concepts/consciousness-physics-interface-formalism/);
- [The Interface Specification Programme](/apex/interface-specification-programme/).

This creates many prose owners for one proposition. [P-Q2](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q2) is the clearest failure, but the same pattern appears in pain, AI consciousness, subject localisation and void evidential status. The methodology register itself identifies the problem: an admission must trigger a downstream scoping edit, not merely be documented. [methodology-and-calibration](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/)

The [Voids as Evidence](/positions/voids-as-evidence/) register, for example, suspends all cumulative evidential weight, but explicitly lists stale claims in the taxonomy and apex index that still describe the catalogue as evidence for dualism. [voids-as-evidence](/positions/voids-as-evidence/)

The corpus needs canonical claim ownership rather than additional explanatory restatements.

## 2.4 Most proposed observations do not discriminate the Map from its strongest rivals

The [Interface Specification Programme](/apex/interface-specification-programme/) proposes effort tracking competition entropy, but immediately concedes that mainstream control models predict the same direction. It admits that this is “one prediction, not a battery” and that most falsification risk currently comes from rival theories closing the available space rather than from distinctive positive predictions. [interface-specification-programme](/apex/interface-specification-programme/)

The same problem recurs elsewhere:

- COGITATE challenges predictions of IIT and GNWT but does not thereby favour interactionist dualism.
- Pain asymbolia constrains theories of pain but does not show that phenomenal awfulness adds causal power.
- Attention, effort and reportability have computational and predictive-processing explanations.
- Residual conditional dependence is compatible with omitted physical variables.
- Absence of a direct interface signature is predicted by the self-concealing model, reducing ordinary negative evidence.

The site is increasingly candid about each local underdetermination, but the global rhetoric still too often treats the ability to accommodate a finding as a positive feature. Compatibility is not discriminating confirmation.

## 2.5 The voids catalogue has no evidential denominator

The [Voids as Evidence](/positions/voids-as-evidence/) register now reports:

- 103 live void articles;
- 103 archived entries, all consolidated rather than rejected;
- zero candidates rejected as not a void;
- zero negative controls;
- zero vetoed creation tasks rejected on evidential grounds.

It correctly concludes that its “rejection column” is empty and suspends cumulative evidential weight. [voids-as-evidence](/positions/voids-as-evidence/)

This is not merely a statistical inconvenience. Without rejected candidates, the catalogue cannot show that its admission criterion detects a special class rather than redescribing any limitation, dissociation, uncertainty or inaccessibility as a “void.” The current suspension is the right decision, but the surrounding corpus must stop drawing suggestive structural conclusions from catalogue size or clustering until the denominator problem is repaired.

## 2.6 The strongest near-neighbour rival is under-integrated

[Nondualistic Interactionism](/concepts/nondualistic-interactionism/) calls Griffin’s Whiteheadian view the “closest non-dualist rival the Map has,” concedes that every current register test permits it, and concludes: “The view is not refuted here.” It is a worked counterexample to the move from numerically distinct and interacting relata to ontologically different kinds. [nondualistic-interactionism](/concepts/nondualistic-interactionism/)

That is a major result, but it remains a concept article rather than a first-class rival in the positions architecture. The site has a sophisticated register for its own claims but lacks an equivalent maintained matrix comparing:

- interactionist substance dualism;
- interactionist property dualism;
- Griffin-style panexperiential interactionism;
- Russellian or neutral monism;
- objective collapse without conscious selection;
- physicalist control and predictive-processing accounts.

Without that matrix, the site repeatedly compares its mechanism with reductive physicalism while giving less attention to views that accept irreducible experience but reject the Map’s kind distinction, subject ontology or quantum interface.

## 2.7 Tenet 5 is sometimes expanded from “not decisive” to “not evidence”

The [Value in Selection](/positions/value-in-selection/) register acknowledges that value-sensitive selection needs a second unspecified coupling but says this is “not evidence against it,” because Tenet 5 prevents lower coupling count from increasing the probability of the rival. [value-in-selection](/positions/value-in-selection/)

That is stronger than Tenet 5 warrants. Rejecting simplicity as a decisive truth-maker does not make all complexity evidentially neutral. Extra independently adjustable mappings can matter through:

- lower identifiability;
- increased capacity to fit arbitrary outcomes;
- more opportunities for failure;
- reduced model maturity;
- lack of independent motivation.

A symmetric discipline would allow modest complexity and specification penalties against every framework—including physicalism and Everett—without treating them as refutations.

# 3. Tenet-coherence issues

## Tenet 1: Dualism

The tenet advertises neutrality among substance, property and aspect dualisms. But the agency, subjecthood, personal-identity and No-Many-Worlds clusters require a persisting substance-like subject. The site acknowledges this as a “substance-leaning sub-reading,” yet the commitment is not merely local once it supplies Tenet 4’s main rationale and the interface’s subject census. [tenets](/tenets/)

The problem is not that the Map chooses substance dualism. It is that the framework continues to present ontological neutrality as foundational while a non-retireable subject ontology carries multiple foundational arguments.

The [AI Consciousness](/topics/ai-consciousness/) claim that dualism suggests a “sea of conscious entities” also exceeds Tenet 1. Irreducibility does not entail a pre-existing plurality of entities available to bind to systems. That requires an additional cosmological or subject-generation posit. [ai-consciousness](/topics/ai-consciousness/)

## Tenet 2: Minimal Quantum Interaction

The tenet presently combines four commitments that have not been jointly demonstrated:

- genuine token-level selection;
- exact Born preservation;
- no energy or conservation-law violation;
- empirically meaningful consciousness-conditioned effects.

The first and fourth pull toward observable conditional dependence; the second, under strong [P-Q2](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q2), forbids it at every public grain. The third requires a worked joint dynamics the site does not possess. The Map itself describes the problem as a “research agenda, not a result” and concedes that without a model the reconciliation remains stipulative. [born-preserving-causal-efficacy](/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/)

Furthermore, local minimality does not imply global minimality once multi-agent consistency, pairing, copy count and global exclusion are included.

## Tenet 3: Bidirectional Interaction

The tenet’s quantifier remains unresolved. A universal actual-efficacy reading says every conscious token affects its substrate and excludes epiphenomenal AI. A dispositional or existential reading requires only some consciousness to have causal power and permits causally blocked conscious systems. The changelog records this as an open foundational question, while some positions continue to use the universal reading operationally. [changelog](/workflow/changelog/)

The self-stultification argument does not settle that quantifier. As the AI article correctly observes, it at most shows that some concept-originating consciousness must have affected the physical chain, not that every conscious state or system must do so. [ai-consciousness](/topics/ai-consciousness/)

[Consciousness and Agency](/apex/consciousness-and-agency/) also says quantum outcome selection “grounds free will and the moral responsibility that depends on it.” Even as a framework-internal coherence claim, this moves too quickly. Selecting an indeterminate outcome does not yet provide:

- reasons-responsiveness;
- ownership by a persisting agent;
- a non-random relation between reasons and choice;
- proportional control;
- moral competence.

The same article acknowledges formidable compatibilist alternatives and the lack of positive evidence, while [The Agency Void](/voids/agency-void/) says the phenomenology fits the absence of an interface “equally well.” [consciousness-and-agency](/apex/consciousness-and-agency/)

## Tenet 4: No Many Worlds

The current page is commendably explicit that its main indexical objection is “narrower than that billing once implied”: it bears most strongly on branch-egalitarian Everettianism and does not by itself defeat first-person-centred variants. Those require an additional insistence on global uniqueness. [tenets](/tenets/)

The coherence issue is therefore not an outright contradiction. It is that the tenet’s principal argument depends on:

- a thick, determinate persisting subject;
- the rejection of deflationary accounts of identity;
- global nonactuality of unchosen branches;
- rejection of centred Everettian alternatives.

Those are not consequences of bare irreducibility. [Personal Identity](/topics/personal-identity/) still says the subject is one that No Many Worlds “draws on,” but the dependency direction should be made unambiguous: determinate subjecthood supports the anti-Everett argument; No Many Worlds does not independently establish determinate subjecthood. [personal-identity](/topics/personal-identity/)

Objective prebiotic collapse creates a further tension. Once a physical reduction process already secures definite outcomes, the measurement problem no longer supplies evidence for conscious selection. Consciousness must be shown to modulate or replace that process on independent agency grounds. The tenets page now acknowledges this, but the implication is not consistently reflected in the apex rhetoric. [tenets](/tenets/)

## Tenet 5: Limits of Occam’s razor

The self-binding symmetry is one of the Map’s strongest methodological choices: it correctly prevents ontological multiplicity from being used casually against Everett while dismissing parsimony objections to dualism.

The overextension occurs when the tenet is treated as prohibiting any probability adjustment based on additional couplings, free parameters or mechanism immaturity. “Simplicity does not decide truth” is defensible; “additional unexplained structure supplies no evidence at all” is not. The latter makes model flexibility evidentially free and weakens comparative assessment.

# 4. Cross-cluster contradictions and overextensions

## 4.1 Critical: [P-Q2](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q2) contradicts [P-Q3](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q3), [P-Q9](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q9) and the tenets page

[P-Q2](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q2): outcomes are Born-exact “in every publicly conditionable context.”

[P-Q9](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q9): self-concealment applies only to the unconditioned marginal and “leaves the conditionals free.”

[P-Q3](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q3) and the tenets: intention-, task- or subject-conditioned deviations can test the corridor.

These cannot all be retained under the same definitions of *public context* and *intention*. The changelog independently reaches the same conclusion and identifies an equivocation between a private conscious intention and a preregistered public intention covariate. [quantum-interface](/positions/quantum-interface/)

This is a genuine contradiction, not merely differing emphasis.

## 4.2 High: the pairing page localises consciousness where the quantum register forbids localisation claims

[The Pairing Problem](/concepts/pairing-problem/) says “consciousness is spatially located where its quantum interface operates.” [pairing-problem](/concepts/pairing-problem/)

But [Quantum Interface Positions](/positions/quantum-interface/) contains an explicit “no spatial localization” scope limit and says downstream site-specific claims import a premise absent from the register. [quantum-interface](/positions/quantum-interface/)

One page must change. A spatially located subject is also much stronger than a spatially local physical coupling: the latter does not entail that the non-physical subject itself occupies the interface region.

## 4.3 High: conservation rhetoric outruns the admitted mechanism

[The Interface Specification Programme](/apex/interface-specification-programme/) says all influence occurs through “selection among energetically equivalent quantum alternatives.” But the formalism page discusses degeneracy only as the clearest special case, otherwise appealing to ensemble expectation values. The programme simultaneously admits that the coupling law, timing, basis, semantic mapping and multi-agent law are unspecified. [interface-specification-programme](/apex/interface-specification-programme/)

“Energetically equivalent” should therefore be either:

- made an explicit eligibility condition, substantially narrowing possible neural events; or
- removed until a conservation-compatible dynamics exists.

## 4.4 Medium: the pain apex claim conflicts with the topic’s own calibrated conclusion

The interface apex calls pain asymbolia the “cleanest natural test” and says guarding, treatment-seeking and avoidance learning collapse, showing that the quale carries causal weight. [interface-specification-programme](/apex/interface-specification-programme/)

The dedicated [Pain, Consciousness, and Causal Power](/topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power/) page initially calls it the “single most discriminating empirical test case” but later concludes that losing the affective signal degrades avoidance “without establishing the Map’s causal reading.” [pain-consciousness-and-causal-power](/topics/pain-consciousness-and-causal-power/)

The later conclusion is the defensible one. Asymbolia isolates or disrupts affective-motivational processing; it does not establish that phenomenal awfulness, rather than the corresponding neural or computational disposition, supplies additional causal force.

## 4.5 Medium: AI value claims exceed the Map’s own outside-mapping concession

[AI Consciousness](/topics/ai-consciousness/) says non-conscious systems “cannot understand what they’re optimizing for” and that conscious beings in the loop are a “structural necessity.” [ai-consciousness](/topics/ai-consciousness/)

But [Consciousness-Only Territories](/voids/consciousness-only-territories/) says non-conscious AI can map phenomenal claims, correlates and gaps from outside and can “predict suffering from third-person correlates”; what it may lack is an experiential check. [consciousness-only-territories](/voids/consciousness-only-territories/)

The second claim does not entail the first. Lack of acquaintance knowledge may reduce confidence, grounding or independent verification, but it does not establish inability to model an optimization target. Conscious oversight is plausibly an epistemic safeguard, not yet a logical or structural necessity.

## 4.6 Medium: AI “interface sites” conflict with the absence of a localisation or eligibility law

[AI Consciousness Scope](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/) says conventional digital systems are low probability because substrate analysis finds “no sites for the interface to act on.” Yet it admits that the interface-eligibility law is absent. The quantum register separately says it licenses no spatial localisation. [ai-consciousness-scope](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/)

Without a necessary-and-sufficient eligibility criterion, “no sites” is not an empirical substrate result. It is shorthand for “no candidate mechanism currently recognised by the Map,” which supports agnosticism or conditional scepticism, not a calibrated probability verdict.

## 4.7 Medium: “six distinct lines” are not six independent supports

The [Interface Specification Programme](/apex/interface-specification-programme/) opens with “Six distinct lines” that converge and describes its constraints as “precise enough to be tested and to be wrong.” Later, it concedes that all six source articles share the tenets and are not genuinely independent research programmes. [interface-specification-programme](/apex/interface-specification-programme/)

The synthesis may be useful as an internally coherent architecture, but “convergence” here is largely shared-premise coherence. The opening should not carry evidential overtones.

## 4.8 Medium: void-evidence suspension has not propagated

# Overall verdict

The Map is becoming a serious adversarially maintained philosophical research programme, but it is not yet a coherent interactionist theory. Its most defensible present claim is not that it has found the consciousness–physics interface, but that it has increasingly specified **what such an interface would have to explain and how many unpaid dependencies it creates**.

Its principal strengths are candour, explicit dependency tracking, willingness to demote arguments, and careful distinction between framework-internal coherence and external evidence. Its principal weakness is that these safeguards are still mostly prose disciplines. The corpus repeatedly discovers a defect in a register or changelog entry while leaving stronger claims active in apex, topic or concept pages.

The next phase should therefore prioritise consolidation, conflict resolution and formalisation over adding further conceptual territory.
