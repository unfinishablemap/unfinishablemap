## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

1. **The field-record summary was contradicted by two of its own cited abstracts.** The lead said "take-up is low, effects are modest", and §field-record said "Flagship field trials show low take-up and modest effects." But:
   - Carrera et al. (2022) report "high take-up of commitment contracts for greater gym attendance, resulting in significant increases in exercise".
   - John (2020) says "the average effect on bank savings is large".
   - Ashraf et al. (2006) report an 81-percentage-point balance gain and conclude it is "a lasting change in savings".

   This is the over-concession direction, and it failed the result-direction leg. **Fixed:**
   - The lead now reads "take-up is often low, average effects are real".
   - The section opens "Two flagship field trials found low take-up and real effects".
   - The John quote now includes its "While the average effect on bank savings is large" clause.
   - Carrera's high take-up and the exercise gain are now stated.
   - The verdict "mixed" stands.
2. **Spellecy's qualifier was dropped.** The article said "the paternalism charge against Ulysses contracts 'can be answered'". The abstract says "the charge of *strong* paternalism can be answered". That is why the same sentence goes on to "weak paternalism". **Fixed** to "the charge of strong paternalism".
3. **The Schelling scenario was conflated.** The article said Schelling posed the authenticity question "for someone who arranges in advance to die after a disabling stroke". In the PDF, the question is posed for the generic advance decision ("I ask you in advance to see that I die if certain conditions befall me"). The disabling stroke comes afterwards, as the case for an implanted automatic device, and that victim is "inarticulate", so cannot be the self who might "speak with my voice". **Fixed** to quote "if certain conditions befall me".

### Counterarguments Considered

- **"A physical planner precommits equally well."** This is conceded throughout ("fit, not evidence"; "The fit discriminates nothing").
- **"An externalised binding shows the decision to bind was idle."** This is rebutted: Ulysses' later will still moves voice and limbs. The converse ("deliberateness shows consciousness does it") is also blocked by the cost-signal reading and [P-A4](/positions/agency-and-will/#p-a4)'s verification limit. Epiphenomenalism is not conceded.
- **"Fewness of interventions satisfies Tenet 2."** This is declined, matching tenets L69 and `self-control` L106.
- **"Precommitment evidences persistence."** This is declined, because the economic models run on any account of identity.

## Stability Notes

- **The exclusivity claim is definitional.** It is scoped to Elster 1979's condition (iii) and to the strategies the Map discusses. Future reviews should not re-flag it as an empirical overclaim. Re-flag only if the scope list or the "1979" anchor is removed.
- **"Fit, not evidence".** The physical-planner parity is the article's settled calibration. Process-philosopher pressure to treat the located contribution as support should be declined. That would be tenet-as-evidence-upgrade.
- **Bedrock disagreements:** Parfitian reductionism, Madhyamaka deflation, and physicalist readings of the selector. These are framework-boundary disagreements, not defects.
- **The field record is "mixed", and that should not oscillate.**
  - This review moved the summary *up* from "modest" because the abstracts say so.
  - A future review should not move it back down without a meta-analysis. It should not move it further up either: CARES's 3 pp is small, and John's 55% default and Carrera's lowered surplus are real failure modes.
- **The Dretske tie is analogy only**, on the "structuring Dretske credits to reasons" wording. In Dretske's general design sense, a device might count as a literal structuring cause. The article deliberately does not claim that.
- **The Elster 2000 side-bets question is open.** It is unverified whether *Ulysses Unbound* drops the 1979 exclusion of private side bets. The article is scoped to 1979. A future review with access to the 2000 text could check this. If the exclusion was dropped, add a clause; do not rewrite the thesis.
