3. **Sherrington bridge echoed the 1947-foreword claim under the 1940 title (L110).** The bridge glossed *Man on His Nature* (1940) as "two ultimates, related by bare correlation, with no basis for a mechanism." The [sherrington-dualist-lineage](/topics/sherrington-dualist-lineage/) article's central correction is that the "devoid of a basis for its very beginning" / no-basis-for-a-mechanism claim belongs to the June 1947 foreword to *The Integrative Action of the Nervous System*, not the 1940 text; the lineage article itself dates it "he said in 1947 that no basis for a mechanism existed." The 2026-09-02 cross-review asserted the bridge quoted nothing from the foreword, which is true of quotation but not of paraphrase. Replaced with a gloss that tracks the verified 1940 sentence ("know nothing of any relation between thoughts and the brain, except as a gross correlation," ch. 9 p. 290): "two ultimates whose relation science knows only as gross correlation."
