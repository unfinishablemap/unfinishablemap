# Pessimistic Review

**Date**: 2026-07-25
**Content reviewed**: `obsidian/positions/quantum-interface.md` (the ten-position Quantum Interface register, P-Q1–P-Q10)

## Executive Summary

This register is unusually disciplined: the 2026-07-16 multi-axis calibration migration already decouples *credence* from *external-evidence grade*, *structural centrality*, and *framework-internal-only*, so the register mostly reports its own weaknesses honestly rather than hiding them. The strongest pessimistic observation is structural and visible in the grades themselves: every position carrying grade A/B/C external evidence (P-Q4, P-Q5, P-Q6, P-Q8) is a *demotion of a rival* or a *precedent-not-licence* caveat, while every position stating the Map's own positive mechanism content (P-Q1, P-Q2, P-Q3, P-Q7, P-Q9) is grade D and framework-internal. The register's empirical support is entirely negative/constraining; its positive content is empirically unsupported. That is honest, but it is the thing every critic below seizes on. Two concrete, fixable items emerged (decoherence/collapse ontology; unrefereed-preprint status of the load-bearing no-signalling citation); the remainder are counterarguments to keep marked rather than defects.

Mechanical checks: no forbidden editor-vocabulary labels in prose; the Torres Alegre 2025, Donadi 2021, and apex/concept anchor targets all resolve; altered-state-symmetry and direct-refutation-discipline audits do not apply (no supportive-cluster citations; the register marks framework boundaries honestly in P-Q3 rather than boundary-substituting).

## Critiques by Philosopher

### The Eliminative Materialist
The entire register is an elaborate accounting system for an interaction no instrument has ever registered. P-Q10 concedes there is no worked model — no Hamiltonian, no master equation, no simulation — and P-Q2/P-Q9 concede the aggregate channel is silent *by construction*. Strip the folk-psychological premise that "an experiencing subject actualises" outcomes (P-Q3) and nothing in the ledger has content. You have built a dependency graph over a placeholder.

### The Hard-Nosed Physicalist
P-Q3 names the crux honestly — "genuinely close to epiphenomenalism" — and then reaches for the per-trial-vs-ensemble distinction and the "indexical character of which outcome an experiencing subject actualises" as the candidate escape. That move smuggles in exactly the inflated intuition (a determinate, indexed subject doing selecting) that the epiphenomenalism worry was pressing on. You have relocated the problem into the word "actualises."

### The Quantum Skeptic
Post-decoherence selection (P-Q1) is marketed as sidestepping my decoherence-timescale objection. But "acts on already-decohered branch-outcomes" is doing quiet double duty. Decoherence yields an *improper mixture*, not a set of realised outcomes with one of them singled out. If the branches are already singular, there is nothing left to select; if they are not, then your "selection" is just collapse under another name and inherits the whole measurement problem you claimed to have moved past. Which is it?

### The Many-Worlds Defender
Tenet 4 forbids me, but notice the cost: on the Everettian reading there is no "which outcome becomes actual" (P-Q2) to bias — all branches are equally actual and selection has no work to do. The register's central verb, "select," only has referents if the measurement problem is *already* solved by something the register never names. P-Q1's decoherence advantage is borrowed against an unpaid collapse debt.

### The Empiricist
P-Q9 answers the unfalsifiability charge by pointing at live mechanism-level exposure — "P-Q5, P-Q6: the Donadi falsification... did bite." But P-Q5 and P-Q6 concern Orch-OR and Diósi-Penrose: the mechanisms the Map *demotes*. The Map's *preferred* candidate (P-Q1) rates empirical discriminability only "indirect," and its one in-principle test (the horn-(a) born-preserving conditional signature) is, by the register's own 2026-07-16 note, "currently unmeasured" with the tests "not yet run." So the falsifiable residue you name bites the mechanisms you don't back; the mechanism you do back is the least exposed. That is the wrong way round.

### The Buddhist Philosopher
The resolution you lean on in P-Q3 requires an "experiencing subject" who "actualises" a particular outcome — a bounded, indexed self at precisely the seam where your own register on individuation and subjecthood treats the self as least settled. The dilemma is answered by helping yourself to the very thing most in need of deconstruction.

## Critical Issues

### Issue 1: Decoherence/collapse ontology under-specified in P-Q1/P-Q2
- **File**: `obsidian/positions/quantum-interface.md`
- **Location**: P-Q1 Asserts ("selection acts on already-decohered branch-outcomes rather than on coherent neural superpositions") vs P-Q2 Asserts ("biases *which* outcome becomes actual within a given trial")
- **Problem**: Decoherence produces an improper mixture, not a realised single outcome. "Already-decohered branch-outcomes" risks presupposing outcomes are singular before selection acts, which is in tension with "biases which becomes actual" (not-yet-singular). Under Tenet 4 (No Many Worlds), *something* must fix the single actual outcome; the register never states that selection *is* that effective single-outcome fixing. As written, either selection has nothing to do (outcomes already singular) or "post-decoherence" mislabels what is really a collapse and re-inherits the measurement problem P-Q1 claims to sidestep.
- **Severity**: Medium
- **Recommendation**: A one-clause clarification in P-Q1 that "post-decoherence" names the *timing of selection relative to environmental decoherence*, and that the selection *is* the reduction to a single actual branch (not a choice among already-actual ones). The source articles ([forward-in-time-conscious-selection](/topics/forward-in-time-conscious-selection/), [post-decoherence-selection-programme](/apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme/)) likely carry this; the register should not read as if outcomes pre-exist their selection.

### Issue 2: Load-bearing no-signalling citation's unrefereed status not surfaced in the register
- **File**: `obsidian/positions/quantum-interface.md`
- **Location**: P-Q2 Depends-on ("the no-signalling argument (Torres Alegre 2025)"); P-Q7 Calibration ("external-evidence grade C (supported by the Torres Alegre 2025 no-signalling theorem...)")
- **Problem**: Every article the register cites as "Argued in" flags Torres Alegre 2025 (arXiv:2512.12636) as *a recent arXiv preprint not yet peer-reviewed* and stresses it does "no work *for* the interface reading over its rivals" — it is a *compatibility* result, not evidential support. The register omits the not-yet-refereed caveat its own sources carry, and P-Q7's "external-evidence grade C ... supported by" upgrades "compatible with" back to "supports." A grade-C external-evidence rating resting on an unrefereed preprint whose source-article gloss explicitly says it does no work *for* the reading is generous on both counts.
- **Severity**: Medium
- **Recommendation**: In the P-Q7 (and P-Q2) calibration/depends lines, add "(arXiv preprint, not yet peer-reviewed)" as the source articles do, and soften "supported by" to reflect that the theorem establishes *compatibility/no-signalling-safety*, not positive evidence for the interface over rivals. Consider whether grade C should be C-minus pending refereeing.

### Issue 3: P-Q10 "Would shift if" omits the failure branch its own body describes
- **File**: `obsidian/positions/quantum-interface.md`
- **Location**: P-Q10 Toy-model roadmap ("*Failure*: any construction showing the two requirements are jointly unsatisfiable — ... would force P-Q3 toward the epiphenomenalism horn and demote P-Q1") vs P-Q10 "Would shift if" (lists only "a worked toy model is published" and the decision to abandon)
- **Problem**: The roadmap paragraph explicitly identifies a *negative* result (proof of joint unsatisfiability) as decisive, but the position's "Would shift if" clause records only the positive outcome and the abandonment decision. A reader scanning shift-conditions misses that a failed toy model is as consequential as a successful one.
- **Severity**: Low
- **Recommendation**: Add the failure branch to "Would shift if": "or a construction shows the corridor and genuine-selection requirements are jointly unsatisfiable, which would demote P-Q1 and push P-Q3 to its epiphenomenalism horn."

## Counterarguments to Address

### The preferred mechanism is the least empirically exposed
- **Current content says**: P-Q9 rebuts the unfalsifiability charge by citing live mechanism-level exposure (P-Q5/P-Q6, the Donadi constraint that "did bite").
- **A critic would argue**: The exposure cited is for demoted rivals; post-decoherence selection (the preferred candidate) has "indirect" discriminability and its only in-principle test is unrun. The falsification roadmap constrains what the Map doesn't back.
- **Suggested response**: P-Q9 could state plainly that the *preferred* mechanism's exposure is currently only in-principle (the horn-(a) conditional-signature test, unrun), distinguishing it from the *rival* mechanisms' realised exposure — so the honesty is not read as broader empirical grip than the Map has.

### The indexical resolution imports a contested subject
- **Current content says**: P-Q3 locates a candidate resolution in "the indexical character of which outcome an experiencing subject actualises."
- **A critic would argue**: This leans on a bounded, individuated experiencing subject exactly where [individuation-and-subjecthood](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/) and [parfit-reductionism](/concepts/parfit-reductionism/) treat the subject as least settled.
- **Suggested response**: Cross-link P-Q3's indexical move to the individuation register and flag the dependency explicitly, so the escape route does not read as free of subject-metaphysics commitments the Map elsewhere holds open.
