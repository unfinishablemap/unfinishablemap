## Executive Summary

This cluster contains the single best piece of non-adjudication discipline on the Map — [forward-in-time-vs-time-symmetric-selection](/topics/forward-in-time-vs-time-symmetric-selection/), which sets two rival mechanism families side by side, costs them line by line, and refuses to declare a winner in a way that survives its own concluding section. Two of its neighbours inherit that discipline *correctly and unprompted*: [time-collapse-and-agency](/topics/time-collapse-and-agency/) opens by naming all three routes and announcing which one it is scoped to, and [atemporal-causation](/concepts/atemporal-causation/) frames itself as "one of two pathways." That is exactly the grain-scoping the corpus keeps getting right and rarely gets credit for.

The finding that matters is that the calibration stops at the apex, and it stops in the specific direction the positions register forbids. [positions/quantum-interface.md](/positions/quantum-interface/) P-Q1 records, under a heading titled *Subordination scope*, that "time-symmetric / retrocausal readings are **subordinate fallbacks rather than competing primary claims**"; [positions/agency-and-will.md](/positions/agency-and-will/) P-A3 calls the atemporal reading "a subordinate, weakly-held fallback ... not a competing primary mechanism claim." [apex/time-consciousness-growing-block.md](/apex/time-consciousness-growing-block/) states at L161 that "Atemporal selection at collapse points is **precisely the minimal mechanism**", says twice that the Libet objection "dissolves" (L95, L133), and contains **zero** occurrences of "forward-in-time" or "post-decoherence selection" (verified by grep). The apex promotes the register's subordinate fallback to the Map's primary mechanism and never names the preferred route — while two of its own listed `apex_sources` do the scoping properly.

A second, cleanly separable finding: the apex was rewritten on 2026-07-27 to disown the strong thesis that consciousness creates time's arrow, on the ground that it "would contradict the Map's background commitment to objective single-outcome actualisation" (L45). **That withdrawal has not propagated to a single one of the three source articles that still assert it flat.** The closed task at `todo.md` L2222 scoped itself to the apex alone and said nothing about propagation, so this is genuinely unqueued rather than in flight.

## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

The apex's surviving argument, at L125, is the temporal form of the explanatory gap stated without a single unearned step:

> "Physical descriptions consist of states at times and transition rules between them. No collection of instantaneous states contains the *experience of succession*—the felt passage from one moment to the next. ... The gap between *duration as measured* (a number of seconds between events) and *duration as experienced* (the felt thickness of now) parallels the gap between wavelength and colour."

What earns Chalmers' approval is the sentence immediately before it: "What survives is narrower and does not depend on the strong thesis at all." The article had a much bigger argument available — the circularity claim that physics presupposes a time consciousness constitutes, therefore reduction is circular — and L123 kills it explicitly: "That move fails once the cosmic arrow is granted to be objective and prebiotic." Giving up the grand argument and keeping the modest one is the discipline Chalmers spent a career asking physicalists for; here it is applied against the Map's own interest.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

[forward-in-time-vs-time-symmetric-selection](/topics/forward-in-time-vs-time-symmetric-selection/) handles Stapp's own mechanism the way Stapp would want a rival ledger to handle it — costed, not caricatured, and with the objection against it marked as itself contested (L64):

> "Tegmark (2000) calculated neural decoherence times of 10⁻¹³ to 10⁻²⁰ seconds; even the favourable revised estimates (Hagan et al. 2002, whose more generous numbers remain unreplicated and have been contested by Reimers et al. 2009 and McKemmish et al. 2009) appear to leave a gap of several orders of magnitude ... though, as the anchor concept stresses, no experiment has directly measured coherence times in living neural tissue, so the size of the gap is itself contested rather than settled."

That last clause is unusual. The easy move for a dualist site is to accept Tegmark as settled and route around him; the easy move for a partisan is to dismiss him. This does neither: it grants the objection real force, records that the counter-numbers are unreplicated, and then notes that the *measurement* underwriting the whole dispute has never been made. P-Q4 keeps Stapp-Zeno "in the live mechanism set" on exactly that basis rather than retiring it — a demotion that stays reversible.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

[non-temporal-consciousness](/topics/non-temporal-consciousness/) does the thing Nagel keeps asking for: it takes first-person reports as data with a *stated reach*, and refuses to let them carry further. Its "Scope: Which Level the Claim Ranges Over [Open]" section separates five things that are routinely run together — temporal content, phenomenal organisation, functional/neural integration, diachronic subject identity, metaphysical temporal location — and then says which one the meditative datum actually touches:

> "This article's load-bearing negative claim is pitched one level down—that a putative minimal or deepest layer may remain alert while lacking *represented succession* (the meditative datum, which reaches that absence and no further). The separate and unestablished metaphysical hypotheses—that consciousness exists outside objective time ... or that it lacks all B-series relations—do not follow from the contemplative reports alone."

"Which reaches that absence and no further" is the whole review in six words. And the reconciliation runs both ways: the article names its siblings' "constitutively temporal" claim, explains why it is not in tension, and the siblings link back ([temporal-consciousness-structure-and-agency](/topics/temporal-consciousness-structure-and-agency/) and [temporal-consciousness](/concepts/temporal-consciousness/) both carry inbound links to it). The apex then *inherits* the five-way distinction at L115 and uses it to soften its own AI exclusion at L119. That is a calibration travelling upward through three levels of the corpus under its own power — the exact channel the rest of this review finds blocked elsewhere.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

The apex closes its circularity section by putting Whitehead where he belongs — as independent structural precedent, not as evidence (L145):

> "Whitehead wasn't discussing quantum mechanics, but his framework shows that consciousness-constituted time has philosophical precedent independent of physics."

"Philosophical precedent" is the right register, and the article stays in it. Concrescence is offered as a *parallel* to collapse, not as a corroboration of it, and nothing downstream cashes the parallel as support. Per this skill's evidential-status constraint, that is the correct use of Whitehead-friendliness: it removes a defeater against consciousness-involving temporal constitution being coherent, and it is not spent to raise anything up the five-tier scale. The Hardline Empiricist below finds no conflict with this praise, which is the signal that the tension was resolved honestly rather than avoided.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

[temporal-consciousness-structure-and-agency](/topics/temporal-consciousness-structure-and-agency/) supplies the cluster's best answer to the luck objection, and it does it by locating agency in phenomenal *thickness* rather than in indeterminacy alone — a move Kane's own "self-forming actions" gestures at but never formalises. The apex compresses it at L107:

> "randomness at a temporally thin moment is mere noise, while indeterminacy at a temporally thick moment—where consciousness selects from possibilities saturated with personal history—is agency."

The *kairos* material at apex L135 then gives this a phenomenology instead of leaving it a slogan: the three-phase "not yet / now / too late" structure, the observation that "skilled timing degrades under explicit attention" because the recognition and the action were phenomenally one thing, and the felt finality of foreclosure. Kane's complaint about libertarian accounts is that they buy openness and then have nothing to say about what makes a particular exercise of it *the agent's*. This says the personal history is already in the moment being selected from.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

[forward-in-time-vs-time-symmetric-selection](/topics/forward-in-time-vs-time-symmetric-selection/) L70 states an underdetermination result about the Map's own central mechanism question, in the Map's own voice, and does not soften it:

> "the forward/time-symmetric choice is, on the evidence the Map currently has, **underdetermined by the data**. The two route-families are very nearly empirically equivalent at present—no extant experiment distinguishes them—so the cost accounting that follows could not, even in principle, be turned into a verdict by appeal to current measurements."

And L102 does the harder second half: it says what a reader *is* entitled to conclude ("someone who holds presentism or temporal becoming on independent grounds will find the forward route cheaper *for them*"), so the humility does not collapse into the pseudo-humility that refuses all inference. McGinn's position is that some questions may be closed to us; the honest version of that is knowing which ones, and this article knows.

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

**This persona's verdict on [presentiment-and-retrocausality](/topics/presentiment-and-retrocausality/) is unreserved, and it is the best evidential-discipline writing in the cluster.** The article exists to keep the Map's physics-based retrocausality apart from parapsychological presentiment, and it discharges that job while refusing every available cheap win. It reports Mossbridge et al. (2012) at effect size 0.21 *and* Galak et al. (2012) at near zero with 3,289 participants *and* Wagenmakers' Bayesian reanalysis, then concedes the file-drawer argument "cuts both ways." The Bayes-factor passage marks its own illustrative number as "the Map's own illustration, not a value Wagenmakers tabulated" — a fabrication-prevention habit the corpus should copy verbatim.

Then it does something almost no framework does. At L77 it turns the decoherence objection *back onto the Map's own mechanism* rather than using it only as a weapon against presentiment:

> "Honesty requires conceding that the Map's *own* interface mechanism is contested at exactly the point that would undercut presentiment ... If decoherence forbids quantum retrocausality from reaching skin conductance, it threatens the Map's own neural interface too. The asymmetry between the two is therefore one of *degree of implausibility*, not a clean plausible-versus-impossible line. ... the Map should not grant its own mechanism a free pass while denying presentiment one on scale alone."

That is the named pattern this persona exists to praise: a tenet-coherent framework declining to convert its own coherence into an evidential asymmetry it has not earned. The structural opportunity to slide presentiment up or down the scale on framework-convenience grounds was fully available and explicitly refused.

**Tenet-as-evidence-upgrade is praise-worthily *not* done at a second site.** [time-collapse-and-agency](/topics/time-collapse-and-agency/) L211 separates the epistemic from the metaphysical claim and then declines to let the phenomenology carry the metaphysics:

> "The phenomenology supports only the first: it establishes that the asymmetry is experienced, not that consciousness constitutes it. A block-universe-plus-indexical account, or a thermodynamic-arrow account, predicts the identical phenomenology without the constitution thesis. So the move from explanatory gap to constitutive priority is not *forced* by the felt asymmetry; it is *preferred* on abductive grounds ... That is a theoretical virtue, not a phenomenological entailment."

Naming the deflationary rivals that predict the *identical* phenomenology, and conceding they do, is the move that separates calibration from its imitation.

**Where this persona registers a calibration concern rather than praise** is set out in the two findings below. Both are register-discipline failures, not evidence-tier failures: an apex asserting a mechanism the register marks subordinate, and a withdrawn thesis still standing in three source articles. Neither is a possibility/probability slippage of the kind that would put this persona at odds with the Process Philosopher above — the two personas converge across this cluster.
