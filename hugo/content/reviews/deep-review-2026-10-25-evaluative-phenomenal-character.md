## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found (all resolved)

1. **Convergence counted as a passed test, and an internal contradiction (old L162).** "If moral perception were mere projection, sustained contemplative attention should eventually reveal the projection mechanism. Contemplatives across traditions report the opposite." This has three problems.
   - It counts cross-tradition reports as a discriminating test. Under [P-M2](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#p-m2) they are one observation read N times.
   - It contradicts the page's own L146: Buddhist attention to *vedanā* "loosen[s] its grip rather than certify[ing] what it presents".
   - The test cannot discriminate. A construction running below introspection, which is the PP rival's claim, predicts the same reports.
   The sibling spoke [phenomenology-of-moral-life](/topics/phenomenology-of-moral-life/) L125 already says such reports are "consonant with moral realism without testing it against projection". The archived `phenomenology-of-moral-experience` had this gap flagged and fixed on 2026-03-14, but the copy here stayed live. **Fix**: the passage now reads "On their accounts deeper attention refines moral vision rather than exposing projection. Contemplative reports fit the constitutive reading without evidencing it: construction running below introspection would leave them unchanged." (0 words)
2. **An overclaim in the contemplative section (old L160).** "*vipassanā* discloses" uses a success verb. "(normativity is pre-reflective)" put a Map gloss on a report that concerns feeling tone, not normativity. "The methods of sustained attention are separable from the interpretive frameworks" was asserted flatly, against the framework-shaping that the 10-24 Metzinger 2020 correction recorded. **Fix**: the duplicate vipassanā sentence is merged into L158. L160 now reads "accept the report that feeling tone is pre-reflective … if, as it assumes without showing, attention is separable from the framework that trains it." (net −3)
3. **The lead stated the thesis as a result (L69, description).** Both now attribute it to the Map: "Conscious experience, the Unfinishable Map holds, carries…", and the description begins "The Map holds that…". (+3; the description is not counted)
4. **Hub stronger than spoke: the knowledge argument (L81).** "The [knowledge-argument](/concepts/knowledge-argument/) establishes that…" became "argues". The spoke says the argument "remains actively debated despite its creator's later rejection". (0)
5. **Source/Map conflation: Mulla Sadra (L152).** The old text said Sadra "holds that evaluative qualia are not representations *of* value". That puts the Map's vocabulary in Sadra's mouth. The spoke marks the evaluative application as reconstruction ("on this account"). It now reads "suggests that knowing value is participating *in* it rather than representing it." (−1)
6. **Hub stronger than spoke: the Confucian count (L154).** The hub discounted only *vedanā*/*rasa*. That left the Confucian case reading as a counted independent lineage, but the spoke's own heading is "Confucian Moral Phenomenology: Illustrative, Not Counted". Added "The Confucian case is illustrative rather than counted", linked to the spoke, and dropped the duplicate "For detailed treatment, see" link, since Further Reading keeps it. (+3)
7. **An unsupported empirical claim, possibly reversed (L134).** "experienced clinicians perceive suffering that novices miss" was flagged unsourced on 2026-02-17 and survived. The pain-assessment literature is mixed: a RAND outpatient study found that underestimation rose with nursing staff's years of experience. The example is cut. "Three considerations favour perception…" became "Proponents cite three considerations favouring…", so the perceptualist case is attributed rather than voiced by the Map. (net −4)
8. **An empirical claim at odds with the data (L188, equanimity).** The old response said "equanimity practices don't eliminate pain's negative valence; they change meta-attitudes". Perlman et al. 2010 (*Emotion* 10(1), doi 10.1037/a0018440) found that long-term meditators had "a significant reduction of self-reported unpleasantness, but not intensity" under Open Monitoring. So the affective dimension itself falls, not only meta-attitudes. It now reads "change meta-attitudes and may lower pain's negative valence, but do not eliminate it." The first-/second-order distinction survives, since L120 already admits gradedness. (+5)
