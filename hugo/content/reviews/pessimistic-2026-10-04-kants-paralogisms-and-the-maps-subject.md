## Executive Summary

The thesis holds where it claims to: a posit commits no paralogism. Three defects and one cheap calibration fix remain.

1. **The central move.** The page never meets Kant's own doctrine for *assumed* objects of reason. In the Doctrine of Method, a transcendental hypothesis "would thus be no explanation at all" (A772/B800), and hypotheses are allowed "only as weapons of war" (A777/B805). The lead's "survives the critique" therefore claims more than the posit/inference distinction earns.
2. **The empirical escape.** It is stated for "the subject" as a whole. But a signature conditioned on conscious state passes along Kant's elastic-ball relay with the state, and the register itself grades persistence's empirical discriminability *none* ([P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3)). The route reaches individuation, not the diachronic clause.
3. **Today's L86 insertion.** It is verbatim at source: Butler's "Thirdly", and Parfit p. 223 with n. 17. But it presents Butler's non-transfer inference, the very inference A363 denies, as though it had settled the matter. It closes with "Quasi-memory shows…", which is stronger than Parfit's own "seems to show".
4. **Tenet 3.** L96 drops the verb from Tenet 3's registered standing, so the fragment reads as "downward causation … not actual".

## Critiques by Philosopher

### The Eliminative Materialist (Churchland)
"A posit held 'openly' that the Map 'cannot prove' is a placeholder for ignorance. The page concedes that felt mine-ness, felt unity and memory all fail as evidence (L82–L86). What is left holding the subject up?"
- The page's answer is honest: Tenet 5 stops parsimony counting against the posit (L108).
- What it never says is what the posit *is* answerable to. That is the agency ground the tenets page names (L76) and the empirical route of §What Escapes. Priority 1 says it.

### The Hard-Nosed Physicalist (Dennett)
"Strawson 1966 diagnoses the Cartesian illusion as criterionless self-reference (L82). Then the Map keeps the illusion's referent by fiat."
- Dennett's sharper point is about L76, "A posit infers nothing, so it cannot commit a defective inference".
- The sentence just before it says the tenets page endorses the subject "on independent grounds, because agent causation needs a persisting subject". That is an inference, an indispensability argument from agency.
- The escape holds for the inference from the "I think", and the sentence should say so (Priority 1a).

### The Quantum Skeptic (Tegmark)
"Your index sits in *P(O | C, X)*. Any signature you could detect is a function of *C*. In Kant's relay the successor inherits *C*. So the index cannot see the swap the third paralogism is about."
- This is correct, and the register already agrees: [P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3) grades persistence's "empirical discriminability none".
- The page still tells the reader two things that overreach to the diachronic clause (Priority 2):
  - the Map "could borrow" Lowe's anti-swap reply through the empirical route (L86);
  - a subject-indexed signature would move "the Map's subject" into empirical psychology (L110).
- The synchronic census is properly held at *compatible*. No Minimal Quantum Interaction claim on the page goes beyond [P-SC1](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc1)/[P-Q2](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q2).

### The Many-Worlds Defender (Deutsch)
The page concedes that Tenet 4 presupposes the posited subject (L106), so there is nothing new to press there. The live point is at L86.
- Parfit's relay is the reductionist's reason for saying there is no further fact about which successor is me.
- Parfit states the conclusion outright: "Unless we have other reasons to believe in their existence, we should reject this belief" (§81).
- The page cites the relay but not the conclusion. The Map's answer, that agency is its other reason, is available (Priority 3, optional b).

### The Empiricist (Popper's Ghost)
The three-grade ladder (compatible, suggestive, discriminating; L98) is the page's best falsifiability device, but its top grade is ambiguous.
- "Two subjects leaving two distinguishable signatures" does not say whether the signatures differ by **conscious state**, which travels with a relay, or by **bare identity**, which would not.
- Only the second could test persistence, and that is the haecceity posit written into a physical law.
- Popper: say that persistence is untestable on the current formalism. Do not let the ladder look as if it covers it (Priority 2).

### The Buddhist Philosopher (Nagarjuna)
"Butler's 'Thirdly', that consciousness of being the same person proves the same substance, is the clinging that Kant and the Madhyamaka both take apart. Your page says it had 'excluded' the relay."
- The L86 Butler clause carries no evaluation, so a reader can take it as support. Priority 3 marks it as the inference A363 denies.
- L106 keeps the Madhyamaka "aggregate of conditions" at the framework boundary, which is fine and unchanged.

## Critical Issues

### Issue 1: The posit escapes the fallacy but not Kant's rule for hypotheses
- **File**: [topics/kants-paralogisms-and-the-maps-subject.md](/topics/kants-paralogisms-and-the-maps-subject/), L35 (lead), L76, L78.
- **Location**:
  - L35: "so the question is whether that subject survives the critique. On the Map's own reconstruction it does, because the Map *posits* its subject rather than *inferring* it…"
  - L76: "A posit infers nothing, so it cannot commit a defective inference."
  - L78: "The immunity has a price: …"
- **Problem**:
  - (a) **Kant has a separate chapter for posits.** The Paralogisms are not Kant's only word on the soul. The Discipline of Pure Reason with regard to hypotheses (A769–782/B797–810) concerns objects of reason that are *assumed* rather than inferred, and its example is the soul. To "assume the soul as simple substance (a transcendent concept)" would be a proposition "hazarded entirely arbitrarily and blindly, since the simple cannot come forth in any experience at all" (A771–772/B799–800). Kant goes on:
    - "A transcendental hypothesis, in which a mere idea of reason would be used for the explanation of things in nature, would thus be no explanation at all" (A772/B800).
    - "Hypotheses are therefore allowed in the field of pure reason only as weapons of war, not for grounding a right but only for defending it" (A777/B805).
    
    A Kantian answers the page's central move with this text. Positing changes which chapter applies; it does not remove Kant's objection. The page is about Kant and never mentions it, so the lead's "survives the critique" claims an escape the distinction does not earn.
  - (b) **"A posit infers nothing" conflicts with the sentence before it.** That sentence gives the tenets page's ground for the subject: "on independent grounds, because agent causation needs a persisting subject". The subject is inferred from agency, just not from the "I think".
  - (c) **Answerability (brief item 1).** The page says what the posit is answerable to only implicitly, through §What Escapes. Kant's rule makes it explicit:
    - defensive use is allowed;
    - explanatory use, in agency and selection, needs a footing in experience, and until it has one the Map parts from Kant at the framework boundary.
- **Severity**: High. This is the page's thesis.
- **Why this is not a re-flag**:
  - Stability Note 1 protects the posit/inference thesis against the charge that "a posit is an evasion". This issue keeps the thesis and adds the Kantian text that bounds it.
  - Stability Note 2 says not to strengthen the page toward "Kant permits the posit". The fix adds a *limit* (defence only, no explanatory standing) and grants nothing.
  - It does not pre-empt the deferred practical-postulate item (open P3 research task, todo L46). That concerns Kant's *practical* route to positing. This concerns his *theoretical* rule against hypotheses, which is the complementary text.
- **Recommendation**: Priority 1.

### Issue 2: The empirical escape is claimed for the whole subject but cannot reach the diachronic clause
- **File**: L37, L84, L86, L98, L110.
- **Problem**:
  - **The signature travels with the state.** In Kant's footnote, each substance communicates its state "together with its consciousness" to the next (A363–364n). [P-SC1](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc1)'s signature is a dependence of outcomes on conscious state, *P(O | C, X)*, identified through "an independent handle on each candidate subject's conscious state". Any such signature is carried by the state, so it runs unbroken through the relay, exactly as memory does.
  - **Only a bare-identity law could expose a swap.** That would be a law under which two subjects in the same state leave different signatures, which is the haecceity posit written into physics. The census trilemma does not consider it.
  - **The register already says so.** [P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3) (persistence) grades empirical discriminability *none*, while [P-SC1](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc1) (the census) is *in-principle*.
  - **The page says more.** The subject posit has two clauses, individuation and persistence (L76), and the route reaches only the first. Yet the page claims three things for the whole subject:
    - "the Map's subject could borrow that reply only through the empirical route below" (L86);
    - "the Map's subject is a posit that could become an empirical determination" (L98);
    - a subject-indexed signature would move "the Map's subject" into empirical psychology (L110).
    
    The lead's "the subject would be determined through its effects" (L37) carries the same scope.
  - **L84 under-reports A353.** "Kant holds that none is available a priori" omits the next paragraph of the passage the page cites: "it is also impossible to derive this necessary unity of the subject, as a condition of the possibility of every thought, from experience … the concept of absolute unity is far above its sphere" (A353). As written, L84 suggests an empirical premise could settle simplicity, a route Kant closes in the same passage.
- **Severity**: High. The page is inconsistent with its own register, over the scope of its one escape.
- **Why this is not a re-flag**: Stability Note 3 keeps the index at *compatible*. This narrows the scope of the escape and leaves the grade alone.
- **Recommendation**: Priority 2.

  - (a) **Framing.** Butler's "Thirdly" infers sameness of substance from consciousness of being the same person. A363 says exactly that such consciousness "does not prove at all the numerical identity of my subject", and Kant's elastic balls treat as conceivable the transfer Butler's premise rules out. The page puts "Butler (1736) had excluded this transfer" straight after "stated rather than argued", where it reads as the argument the Map lacks.     - Epistemic/metaphysical check: "tell" is epistemic and the sentence asserts nothing metaphysical, so there is no equivocation.
  - (c) **Optional.** Parfit's conclusion is a counterargument the page leaves unanswered.

The IEM deep review passed the quotes at source (pp. 164–165, subject checked), and "a current experience" narrows "current or directly remembered" outside quotation marks, which is acceptable. ## Counterarguments to Address

### "A posit is still a hypothesis, and Kant has a chapter on those"
- **Current content says**: a posit infers nothing, so it commits no paralogism and the subject survives the critique.
- **A critic would argue**: the Discipline denies transcendental hypotheses any explanatory standing (A772/B800) and allows them only for defence (A777/B805). The Map uses its subject to explain agency and selection.
- **Suggested response**: concede the rule. The posit can answer a dogmatic denial. Its explanatory use parts from Kant at the framework boundary until the empirical route gives it a footing (P1).

### "Your empirical route cannot see a relay"
- **Current content says**: the Map could borrow Lowe's anti-swap reply through the empirical route, and a subject-indexed signature would make "the Map's subject" empirical.
- **A critic would argue**: a signature indexed by state travels with the state, and Kant's case transfers the state.
- **Suggested response**: scope the escape to individuation. Say that persistence has no empirical route on the current formalism, citing [P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3) (P2).

### "Parfit: without other reasons, reject the subject"
- **Current content says**: Parfit's relay cites Locke and Kant.
- **A critic would argue**: Parfit's conclusion is to reject belief in such entities "Unless we have other reasons to believe in their existence" (§81).
- **Suggested response**: the Map's other reason is agency, so its subject is posited, not remembered (P3, optional b; it also reinforces P1's answerability).

### "Kant faced interactionism and dissolved it"
- **Current content says**: "Kant never faced" the Map's commitment.
- **A critic would argue**: Kant weighs physical influence, harmony and assistance and traces all three to one subreption (A389–390).
- **Suggested response**: scope to the commitment's form (P4b, P4c), with an optional clause naming A389–390.
