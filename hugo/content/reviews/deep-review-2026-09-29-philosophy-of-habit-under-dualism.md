## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found — Fixed

1. **Graybiel 2008 paraphrase-as-quote (closes the 08-08 open item).** The article quoted habits as becoming "relatively automatic and unconscious". Retrieved the full text (Annual Reviews reprint via a Princeton mirror; page markers 359–387 present, reference list present, 16.5k words; control terms "chunk" ×14, "evaluative" ×17). The phrase does not occur. The definitional sentence is: "Third, fully acquired habits are performed almost automatically, virtually nonconsciously, allowing attention to be focused elsewhere." **Resolved** — the quotation now carries the verbatim sentence. Same defect class as the Bergson (08-08) and Merleau-Ponty (07-18) de-quotes.

2. **"Task-bracketing" attributed to the 2008 review.** The term does not occur in the 2008 text (zero hits for "brack"). The *concept* does — "accentuated beginning and end" activity "marking the boundaries" of action sequences, "boundary-marked representations" — so this is a term-level anachronism (the label is Graybiel's later vocabulary), not a misattributed finding. **Resolved** — reworded to "accentuated activity at a routine's beginning and end marking its boundaries", which is what the 2008 text says.

3. **Proust quote in an edition the article does not cite.** The article had "if habit is a second nature, it prevents us from knowing our first". Grep of the raw Moncrieff text (*Cities of the Plain*, 1927; control "Balbec" ×178) gives: "For if habit is a second nature, it prevents us from knowing our original nature, whose cruelties it lacks and also its enchantments." A control grep of Moncrieff's *Within a Budding Grove* (Balbec ×165) returns no "second nature" at all, confirming the passage is in *Sodom and Gomorrah*. "Our first" is the Kilmartin/Enright revision; with no edition named the quote was uncheckable in principle — the defect fixed for Bergson on 08-08. **Resolved** — quotation now matches Moncrieff 1927 verbatim and a References entry names the edition.

### Medium Issues Found — Fixed

4. **Inline-to-References orphans.** Graybiel & Grafton 2015 (inline at "Habit Is Not Skill") had no References entry; the quoted Hume and Proust lines had none either. **Resolved** — three entries added (Graybiel & Grafton 2015 tuple re-verified at Europe PMC: *Cold Spring Harb Perspect Biol* 7(8): a021691, DOI 10.1101/cshperspect.a021691, PMC4526748). 5. **Aristotle gloss conflated two terms.** "Aristotle grounds excellence in habit (*hexis*)" treats *hexis* as habit; in *Nicomachean Ethics* II virtue is a *hexis* (settled state) acquired through habituation (*ethos*). **Resolved** — "makes virtue a settled state (*hexis*) acquired through habituation (*ethos*)". Term-level precision, no argument change.
