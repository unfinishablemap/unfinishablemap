## The Wing's Question: Does the Map Answer the Luck Objection Once?

**Verdict: yes on substance, with two drifts of register and one omission, none of them a contradiction.** The canonical statement is on page 3 (L83): selection within the Born distribution, "with the aggregate statistics exactly preserved", escaping Schrödinger's first horn "since no odds are altered", with the second horn — a selection that leaves the statistics untouched must say what distinguishes it from chance — booked against `positions/quantum-interface#^mechanism-debt` "rather than conceding epiphenomenalism". Checked page by page:

- **(a) 1930s randomness objection — page 3.** Canonical, as above. The page also does the one thing the brief asked every page to do: it treats the objection's several staters as *one* objection with two datings. L49: "The objection the Map now calls the luck objection has two separate datings in this period"; the L67–75 table assigns Schlick 1935 and Schrödinger 1936 to the "Same page" of the Map's reply. Nobody counts heads.
- **(b) Rollback / *Mind* argument — page 2.** Consistent. L79: "conscious selection leaves the Born statistics intact", [P-Q2](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q2) quoted; L86: the luck reply's owed modal structure, the wild-coincidence cost and the companion-law debt "are one unwritten law"; L90: "None of this concedes epiphenomenalism." L45 groups "the '*Mind*', 'Rollback', or 'Luck' argument" as one family — convergence counted as one observation. The only gap is nominal: page 2 names the debt by the apex's label ("companion law", via `apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy`) and never writes the `^mechanism-debt` anchor (0 hits), so a reader cannot see from this page that the register's [P-Q3](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q3) + [P-Q10](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q10) debt and the companion-law debt are the same entry. One piped link would close it (Cross-Linking, below).
- **(c) Basic Argument and constitutive luck — page 1.** Consistent by *scope discipline*: the page says what the interface does and does not answer. L125: "The interface says where a settling touches physics. It answers neither constitutive luck nor present luck" — near-verbatim with page 2 L104 ("The interface says where a settling would touch physics; it does not answer the luck objection"). L115 "No epiphenomenalism" is explicit. No odds language appears because none should: the page is about desert, not efficacy, and says so (L36, L115).
- **(d) Kane, plural voluntary control, event-causal reply — page 4.** Consistent at L78 ("consciousness *selecting* the outcome rather than receiving it ... developed in full at [qifw]"), L82 ("the Map treats that settling as a primitive whose modal structure is owed rather than supplied") and L90 ("a conscious selection at the interface is the agent's own settling"). **One intra-page drift**: L66 says the agent-causal species "answers luck by making the agent, rather than a prior event, the settler of the outcome", while L82 on the same page, and page 5 L99 citing "the event-causal article's verdict", hold the 10-20 ledger's fence — agent-causal views answer the *disappearing-agent form*, not luck as such, and the rollback is pressed against them too. L66 is the pre-fence wording that survived the fence (minted below, length-neutral).
- **(e) Agent-causal "acting for reasons" reply — page 5.** The most precisely stated of the six. L85 quotes [P-Q2](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q2)'s "only conditionals on the conscious state itself are free"; L87 puts the reasons "through the weights" that *physics* fixes, so nothing consciousness does shifts a public frequency; L36 cites `^mechanism-debt` by anchor and grades the causal-selection thesis "only as a coherence result inside its own framework". This is the register's citation grade applied exactly.
- **(f) Trilemma Horn 2 and Horn 3 — page 6.** Consistent where it speaks (L74, added since the 07-15 ledger: reasons "do not explain why A rather than B ... That difference is the agent's settling, a primitive whose modal structure is owed rather than supplied"), but it is the one page of six that **never says the statistics are preserved and never books the mechanism debt** (`mechanism-debt` 0 hits; `Born` 1 hit, L127, which says only that "which outcome actualises remains open"). On a page that never says "Born-preserving", Horn 3's verbs read as odds-shifting: L70 "Consciousness biases which option crosses threshold", L127 "biasing which already-prepared option actualises", L95 the Zeno mechanism "stabilising desired neural patterns against decoherence into alternatives ... satisfies the Map's Minimal Quantum Interaction tenet". L76 compounds it in the result register: "The felt cost corresponds to genuine causal engagement—consciousness doing work to stabilise the selected option against competitors", which is exactly the "does causal work" assertion the register's mechanism-debt convention says must carry the coherence-only grade. Minted below as one length-neutral task together with the L140 label (next paragraph).

## Executive Summary

The wing is where the Map's calibration discipline shows most clearly, because the luck objection is the one place the Map's own reply *terminates in a primitive and says so*. Pages 1, 2 and 5 each contain a section headed as the Map's own analysis, each ends at the same stopping point in nearly the same words ("who settles, not why the settling went one way"), and each books the residue against the register rather than against an opponent. The two historical pages (3 and the taxonomy on 4) refuse every lineage temptation: no builder, no critic and no defender is enlisted for dualism, and page 3's finding is stated against the Map ("its selection posit and the strongest objection to it are both older than it is"). The opportunities are all reads of papers the pages already name as unread, plus one rival (Planck) the Map admits it has not answered. The two defects are register drift on the oldest page of the six and a survived pre-fence clause on page 4; both are length-neutral.

## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

Page 1 is the model of a dualist page that declines the dualist's exemption. L84: "The S reply applies word for word ... Dualism buys no exemption." L121: "Dualism gains nothing, because note 8 extends the demand to a noumenal self." Page 2 L106 repeats it for the rollback: "Dualism gains nothing here. The argument runs with agent causation granted." A property dualist wants the hard problem taken seriously and *not* used as a universal solvent; these two pages do exactly that, locating the Map's reply in origination rather than in the ontology.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

Page 5 L85–91 is the first place in the corpus where the Born-exactness constraint is run *through the theory of reasons* rather than stated beside it: "Whatever the reason-states contribute, they contribute through the weights: they make some options likelier, and none becomes actual without the selection. That is Clarke's architecture." That is a Stapp-grade observation — it says which published agent-causal architecture a Born-preserving selector *is*, by construction, and then (L91) says the other route "puts the reasons exactly where the mechanism debt is owed". Page 2 L79 does the same for the replay: "Physics fixes only the public statistics, the average of those conditionals over the conscious states that actually occur." A quantum-mind theorist would recognise the grain argument as the correct place to meet van Inwagen's God.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

Page 1 handles Nagel's own paper with care that Nagel would notice: the Williams priority is stated from Nagel's own note ("to which the original version of this essay was a reply", L55), the coinage of "constitutive luck" is left "unsettled" between SEP and IEP (L57), and L59 says "Neither Nagel nor Williams is cited here for dualism." Page 6 L109's chess-engine passage keeps the implementing/grasping distinction as the thing the compatibilist "must account for" rather than a proof (L111: "This does not prove that consciousness is required for grasping").

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

Little of this wing touches process metaphysics directly, which is itself a strength: the luck objection is answered by a theory of the *agent's settling*, not by a claim that experience goes all the way down. Page 4 L88 keeps the Dualism tenet "neutral between substance and property dualism", so a Whiteheadian reader can accept the tenet and "part company at the agent-causal step" without being told the tenets excluded them. Per this persona's constraint, nothing on these pages uses process resonance to move any claim up the evidential scale; there is no tier-upgrade anywhere in the wing to object to.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

Page 4 gives Kane what the luck literature rarely gives him: his *current* view. §A Hybrid at the Seam (L60–62) reports the 2024 AC/EC position, the dropped doubled effort, and Haji's and Palmer's replies, and L86 names "Kane's indeterminate efforts and plural rationality" among "the strongest rival replies" that "remain live".
