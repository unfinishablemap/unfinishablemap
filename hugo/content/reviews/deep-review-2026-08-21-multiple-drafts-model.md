## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

**1. Register-inconsistent over-concession in §Relation to Site Perspective — the article conceded a question the Map's own register holds open.** The falsifiability paragraph added by the 2026-07-26 review read:

> "the phenomenal residue the Map retains is likewise not empirically discriminable from its absence — no probe could register the difference."

That is an unconditional in-principle undetectability claim, and it does not match what the Map holds. [P-Q3](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q3) carries the bias-without-deviation dilemma as **open**, with horn (a) — a born-preserving conditional signature, P(O | do(C), X) ≠ q(O | X) for some specifiable context X — explicitly registered as *in-principle specifiable* though currently unmeasured. [P-Q1](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q1) goes further and makes the point binding: the Map's mechanism preference **falls to *low* credence** if it commits exclusively to the empirically-silent Route 1 and forecloses Route 2, "the live interface-discriminating alternative." The register states outright that "the moderate band is therefore held partly *because* the Map keeps Route 2 open."

So the article volunteered, as a piece of admirable honesty, precisely the concession the register prices as a one-band demotion — and did so while asserting in the next sentence that the Map "takes its empirical risk elsewhere, in the interface commitments of Tenets 2 and 3." Those two sentences do not sit together: if consciousness is causally efficacious at the quantum interface (Tenets 2–3), its absence is not probe-invisible in principle; that is what the falsification roadmap is *for*.

This passes the §2 diagnostic test cleanly. A reviewer who fully accepts the Map's tenets would still flag the claim as miscalibrated — indeed *only* such a reviewer would, since the defect is visible only against the register. It is a calibration error, not bedrock disagreement, and it is the over-concession-gets-ratified shape exactly: the 2026-07-26 review did not merely miss it, it praised it as "the 'tenet-coherent, not evidence-elevating' pattern done explicitly."

**Resolution applied.** The paragraph now scopes the concession to what is true — *no measurement currently discriminates* the residue from its absence, and the Map's warrant for it is non-empirical — then marks where the concession stops, linking [quantum-interface](/positions/quantum-interface/) and stating that the in-principle discriminability question is carried as an open dilemma with one horn still specifiable, whose foreclosure the register counts as a demotion. The honest calibration the previous review wanted is preserved in full: no empirical support is claimed for the residue, and the standoff is still framed as two metaphysical defaults. What is removed is the extra step that gave away a live option.

### Medium Issues Found

- **Priority error on the cerebral-celebrity reframing.** "In 'Are we explaining consciousness yet?' (2001) Dennett reframed the positive account in terms of influence rather than location" implied 2001 as the point of reframing. The influence-over-location gloss ("fame in the brain," "cerebral celebrity") runs through Dennett's later work well before 2001. **Fixed** by rewording to "Across his later work Dennett came to reframe … stating it compactly in 'Are we explaining consciousness yet?' (2001)" — which removes the priority claim without adding a citation this pass did not verify at the publisher.
- **Overstated universal in §The Objection Split.** "is common ground shared across theories of consciousness, materialist and dualist alike" asserts unanimity that does not hold: IIT locates consciousness in a maximally irreducible complex, which is a privileged locus even though it posits no audience. **Fixed** — softened to "widely shared … though not uniformly," with the IIT exception named and cross-linked. This tightens the article's own argument rather than weakening it: the objection split is more credible when the shared ground is stated at its true extent.
