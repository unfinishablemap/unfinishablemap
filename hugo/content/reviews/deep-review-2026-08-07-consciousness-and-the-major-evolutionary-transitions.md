## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

- **Quasi-quotation attributed to a source that does not contain it (Michod).** The article read: "The higher-level individual becomes, *in Michod's phrase*, a new focus of adaptation." The phrase does not appear in either cited work. Verified two ways: (a) Michod 2007 full text (NCBI Bookshelf NBK254316, complete) — "focus" occurs exactly twice, once in "We focus on the transition from unicellular to multicellular life" and once in "A central focus of Solari's hydrodynamic work"; neither is the attributed phrase. Positive control passed (Volvox ×4). (b) The published abstract of Michod & Nedelcu 2003 (via OpenAlex, reproducing the journal abstract) does not contain it either. The phrase traces to a *seminar abstract* on philinbiomed.cnrs.fr for a Michod talk, which reads "the stabilized group becomes a new **focus level of complex adaptation**" — a different formulation, in a source the article does not cite. The prior review had waved this through as "faithful paraphrase", but the article's own "in Michod's phrase" claims verbatim wording. **Resolution**: replaced with Michod's actual words from the cited 2007 paper, confirmed verbatim at two separate loci in that text: *"Once cells specialise in those fitness components they can no longer survive and reproduce on their own, and in Michod's words 'the group becomes indivisible and, hence, an individual.'"* Stronger for the argument and now genuinely quotable.

- **Quantifier inflation in the Ginsburg–Jablonka taxa claim.** The article read: consciousness distributed "across vertebrates, **many** arthropods, some cephalopods". Birch, Ginsburg & Jablonka 2020 say verbatim (PMC7116763): *"Ginsburg and Jablonka (2019) conjecture that UAL is present in **most vertebrates**, **some cephalopod molluscs** (the coleoid cephalopods: octopods, squid and cuttlefish) and **some arthropods** (including honey bees and fruit flies)."* The authors further hedge that they "credibly conjecture that [honey bees and fruit flies] possess the whole UAL package, but we do not have hard evidence of this." Two drifts: *some* → *many* on arthropods (Arthropoda is the largest animal phylum; the source names two genera), and a dropped *most* on vertebrates. This is the §2.5 dropped-qualifier check — and the inflation ran in exactly the direction of the sentence's rhetorical work ("distributes consciousness generously"). **Resolution**: corrected to "across most vertebrates, some arthropods (honey bees and fruit flies among them), and the coleoid cephalopods". Note the article's original "some cephalopods" was *correct* and was not disturbed — the source does say "some cephalopod molluscs".

- **Internal tension introduced by the 2026-08-04 refine-draft.** The individuality section asserted the pluralism "enumerates evolutionary, physiological, and immunological individuals **and stops**", then immediately said the question "is **nonetheless asked**" — a self-contradiction across adjacent sentences, and redundant with the preceding sentence's identical point. **Resolution**: rewritten to "That axis is absent from the enumerated list, though not from the discussion—and how it gets handled when it does come up matters more to the Map than a bare omission would have."

### Medium Issues Found

- **Premise-strength drift running *against* the opponent.** The article said Godfrey-Smith "**settles the matter** in a subordinate clause". He does not settle it: he writes the hedged "*it seems that* the special features of subjects are organizational", and describes the paper as "very much an exploratory one". The article was making its opponent more dogmatic than he is — the inverse of the usual over-concession failure, but the same fidelity defect. **Resolution**: "disposes of the dualist option in a bracketing clause", which is accurate to what the clause actually does without inflating his confidence. The article's substantive charge — that the bracket is stipulated rather than argued — survives untouched and is correct.

Source abstract (verified via OpenAlex, publisher 403): "It expands the **types**, targets and regimes of natural and **sexual-social** selection." **Resolution**: restored "types" and "sexual-social".
