## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

The [P-AC4](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac4) rewrite is the honest form of the access/phenomenal split, and Chalmers would say so even though it quotes his own 2023 paper against the Map. ### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

Stapp would read the research note's "propagation criterion" as the Map finally saying where process 1 could and could not get a grip: "an indeterminate event is interface-relevant iff a selection at it can reach the system's functional trajectory without being verified or corrected away."
