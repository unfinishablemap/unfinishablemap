### Critical Issues Found

1. **Empirical claim cited to the wrong work and overstated.** "a finding validated empirically through the ... (EASE) instrument, which confirms self-disorders as a core feature of the schizophrenia spectrum that predicts future onset in at-risk populations" had only Parnas et al. 2005 behind it. The prediction result is Nelson et al. 2012, one sample of 49. Rewritten to name the study, the sample, and what was predicted (transition to psychosis); Nelson added to References.
2. **Source overstated.** "Sass and Parnas show ... What is lost is ... the pre-reflective structure". The paper argues for a model of *diminished* self-affection. Now "argue" and "disturbed, on their model". The stronger wording also contradicted the article's own thesis: if the structure of ownership were lost, experience would go on unowned, which the article says does not happen.
3. **Calibration: "demonstrating".** Depersonalisation was said to be "demonstrating that ownership is a separable structural feature, not an artefact of cognitive processing", under the topic sentence "provides clinical evidence for ownership as primitive". Separability of felt mine-ness does not show that ownership is primitive, and "not an artefact of cognitive processing" had no support. A reviewer who accepts the tenets would still flag it. The paragraph now says the reports suggest felt mine-ness is separable and that the cases fit primitive ownership without establishing it.
4. **Stale Map-internal gloss.** The article said the thought-insertion page "argues that none of them describes a conscious thought stripped of first-personal givenness". The live page (reworked 2026-10-06 and after) says none describes a conscious thought "outside any first-person perspective" and that whether what remains is for-me-ness is disputed (Lane). Gloss corrected.
5. **Five readings mis-enumerated.** The paragraph listed agency, ownership-loss, Billon, Ratcliffe and Wilkinson, and Guillot, which made Guillot's vocabulary the fifth reading and dropped the live page's fifth (for-me-ness disturbed, not lost). Now: five that the Map's page sets out; added-element and disturbed-givenness readings named; Guillot presented as the organising split.
