## Critiques by Philosopher

### The Eliminative Materialist (Churchland)
Unchanged from 2026-05-29 and still undischarged. The temporal criteria at L100–107 are functional descriptions of what the system *does*, offered by an article that rejects functionalism as constitutive; the rescue — they are "necessary-but-not-sufficient conditions for non-physical coupling" — is stipulated, and the radio analogy illustrates rather than argues it. If their presence does not guarantee consciousness and their absence does not preclude it in principle (the non-temporal qualification at L111 explicitly allows the latter), the criteria do no detectable discriminating work. The 05-29 review logged this as an unsupported claim; nothing has been added.

### The Hard-Nosed Physicalist (Dennett)
The Jourdain hypothesis (L125) and Birch's gaming problem now compound. Jourdain says affirmative AI self-report may be metacognitive output without metacognitive states; the gaming problem says training selects for exactly those outputs, "so they cannot serve as evidence that the threshold has been crossed." Between them the article has closed both directions on self-report and then, in "What Would Challenge This View?", offers "AI systems reported consistent phenomenological structures neither present in training data nor predictable from architecture" as a falsifier. That falsifier is a report. The article has argued, two sections earlier, that no report can carry this evidential load.

### The Quantum Skeptic (Tegmark)
The decoherence concession the 05-29 review asked for is now installed at L141 and L157, and it is good — "contingent and currently non-discriminating" is the honest formulation. But the article's *other* quantum passage has drifted from the register. L165 reports [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1) as "conditional on [P-Q1](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q1) being approximately right (moderate) and on the premise that digital hardware suppresses the relevant indeterminacy." The register's own 2026-07-16 calibration note says the credence "is conditional on an **interface-eligibility law** the Map does not yet possess," calls that layer "the load-bearing and least-secured link," and warns that absent it, "'relevant' risks reducing to 'whatever biology happens to have,' which would make the substrate verdict question-begging rather than derived." The article uses the exact word the register flags — "relevant indeterminacy" — in the exact paragraph whose job is to state the verdict's conditionality, and drops the caveat attached to it. `grep -oiF "interface-eligibility"` against the live file returns 0.

### The Many-Worlds Defender (Deutsch)
Unchanged from 2026-05-29. L161 still asserts that haecceity "requires something beyond physical description" and infers that multiply-instantiable LLMs lack it. The section header ("Dualism as conclusion, then as lens") does frame the whole block as tenet-generated, which mitigates the question-begging charge more than the 05-29 review allowed — but the sentence itself reads as a metaphysical finding, and the article's own `possibility-probability-slippage` discipline, invoked two paragraphs later, is precisely the discipline that would require it marked as tenet-contingent.

### The Empiricist (Popper's ghost)
Partly discharged: the epiphenomenal-detection falsifier now carries its candid difficulty inline at L203. Two problems remain. First, the novel-phenomenology falsifier is undercut by the gaming problem, above. Second, and new: the article's treatment of the epiphenomenal possibility is now *less* falsifiable than the Map's own position. The apex holds the possibility outside the framework and states what one would have to give up to admit it — a checkable commitment. The topic article holds it inside, "without contradiction," and says only that it "remains deeply problematic." That is a strictly weaker epistemic posture on the same claim, in the shallower of the two articles.

### The Buddhist Philosopher (Nagarjuna)
Unchanged from 2026-05-29 and worth restating because it has now been carried twice. The non-temporal-consciousness qualification (L111) is borrowed to weaken the temporal argument against AI, but Josipovic's non-representational reflexivity and Husserl's absolute flow cut equally against the "sea of conscious entities… any of which could in principle bind" picture in the opening paragraph. The article takes the defeater where it helps and does not notice where it costs.

The apex was rewritten on 2026-07-27 and the register followed on 2026-07-28; the topic article was not. `grep -oiF "framework boundary"` against the article returns 1 hit — and it is in the Duch section, not here.
