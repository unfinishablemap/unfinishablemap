# Optimistic Review — Inversion and Convergence Cluster

**Date**: 2026-07-28
**Content reviewed**: `obsidian/concepts/inverted-qualia.md`, `obsidian/concepts/qualia.md`, `obsidian/concepts/dualism.md`, `obsidian/concepts/philosophical-zombies.md`, `obsidian/concepts/organizational-invariance.md`, `obsidian/concepts/argument-from-mechanism.md`, `obsidian/concepts/first-order-representationalism.md`, `obsidian/topics/the-convergence-argument-for-dualism.md`, and the eight files of `obsidian/positions/`.

## Executive Summary

Tonight's work is genuinely good, and its two best products are both currently stranded. The Map now states, in four articles and in near-identical language, that the anti-physicalist arguments are premise-sharing rather than evidentially independent — a first-order verdict on the Map's own flagship case that the positions register does not contain, even though the methodology register (P-M2) explicitly names "the convergence-for-dualism case" as one of the two cases the discipline must survive. Separately, `inverted-qualia.md` resolved the grain dilemma and produced a type/token distinction that is exactly the shape of the answer [positions/quantum-interface.md](/positions/quantum-interface/) P-Q3 lists under "Would shift if" — and it deposited that resolution in the article with two words of length headroom while routing readers to `organizational-invariance.md`, which has 1,100 words of room, states the same dilemma, and remains explicitly neutral between its horns.

## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

He is the objector here, and the article treats him as one. `inverted-qualia.md` L132–138 is the strongest thing in tonight's diff: it identifies the sharpest objection to inverted qualia as coming from a fellow property dualist, states that this "cannot be deflected as physicalist question-begging, which is what makes it the sharpest case the article faces," and then declines the two cheap exits — it is neither a refutation of the Map nor a refutation of Chalmers. Chalmers would recognise his own qualifier being used correctly: the reply turns on *at a fine enough grain*, which is his phrase and his concession.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

The type/token move at L138 is the most technically interesting thing here. An isomorph reproduces the causal *pattern*, statistical envelope included; MQI concerns which *token* outcome is drawn inside that envelope. That is a difference that is causally efficacious without being organizational — which is precisely the structure a Born-preserving interactionism needs, and precisely what P-Q3's open dilemma asks for. Stapp would notice that the article does not claim to have paid the debt: "The Map owes an account of that distinction rather than a free escape."

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

L171's prediction list is first-person-respecting in the right way: aesthetic preferences, emotional valences, reaction-time asymmetries, and "the fine structure of introspective reports." The Map is not proposing to detect qualia; it is proposing that if phenomenal character does causal work, its signature lives in the grain of what a subject prefers and how quickly, not in a discrimination task.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

Little to praise or worry about in this cluster — it is a philosophy-of-mind argument-structure night, not a scope-of-consciousness night. No tier-upgrade pressure appears, so no Process/Empiricist conflict arises.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

Indirect but real: the type/token distinction is the same structure agency needs. If selection among physically open outcomes is efficacious without being organizational, then an agent's choices are not reproduced by a functional duplicate — which is what a libertarian wants from a physical picture.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

`organizational-invariance.md` L77 is the model here: "the claim to leave introspection intact stands as conditional on that question ... not as a settled free lunch." The article names the condition its own reply rests on and declines to bank it. (That same honesty is now the problem — see Leverage 2.)

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

This is where tonight earns its highest marks, and the praise is for two things the Map declined to do.

**It declined to borrow an opponent's empirical bite as corroboration.** `inverted-qualia.md` L173: the behavioural-difference prediction "is the Map's own, made in the teeth of Hardin's detectability argument rather than in alignment with it: having answered Hardin above by insisting the inversion argument is modal rather than empirical, the article cannot now borrow his empirical bite as corroboration." Hardin is an eliminativist about colour qualia. Having neutralised his argument on modal grounds, the article had a standing invitation to cite his detectability point as independent support two sections later — a mis-framing of exactly the kind the citation-framing-accuracy lens exists to catch — and refused it, in writing, with the reason given. That is the register's P-M3 self-citation discipline applied to an *opponent's* argument, which is harder.

**It reported empirical evidence that cuts against its own case.** L82 states that "the empirical record has moved since Hardin, and not the inversion argument's way," and then supplies the specifics: Hofer et al. 2005 on 1.1:1 to 16.5:1 L:M cone ratios, Webster et al. 2000 on unique-hue settings "largely unconstrained" by those differences, Emery et al. 2023 on variation resolving into local rather than global factors, Horiuchi & Nagai 2024 on appearance moving with discrimination training. Large physiological variation coexisting with stable appearance is a structuralist result, and structuralism is the reading that *deflates* strong inversion. The article states it as a finding rather than a fragility to be managed.

**And a third, in `argument-from-mechanism.md`:** the separation at L68–70 of the Granqvist 2005 God-Helmet null result from the near-death case. Where the sufficiency premise was never established, "there is no sufficiency-to-necessity slide to diagnose" — so the God Helmet is removed from the Map's own catalogue of materialist over-reach and returned to ordinary empirical dispute, leaving Mobbs & Watt 2011 as "the genuine article." That is a fallacy article shrinking its own evidence base to keep the anatomy clean. Grade this as a strength, not a loss.

- **Strongest point**: L138 — the article states the dilemma, declares which horn it takes ("This article is not neutral between the horns"), gives the reason (the MQI discussion predicts subtle behavioural differences, which takes the detectable horn), supplies the type/token distinction that keeps the horn survivable, and then books the resulting debt.

- **Strongest point**: L147–149, the count-inflation self-catch. Three sources of support run in parallel — the epistemology of convergence, the arguments themselves, the framework-level commitment to dualism — and the article refuses to read them as three converging routes: "the framework-level commitment is not an independent third route but a *selection rule operating on the output* of the first two."
- **Notable quote**: "convergence earns irreducibility; bidirectional interaction selects dualism among the irreducibility-respecting alternatives; the two earnings do not compound into a single triple-supported case for substance dualism."
- **Why it works**: it is the Map catching itself doing the thing it accuses others of, in its own flagship argument, with the corrected catalogue language supplied inline.

### `obsidian/concepts/first-order-representationalism.md`
- **Strongest point**: the new "Four Replies to Inversion" section correctly separates *cost* from *refutation*. Shoemaker's appearance properties and Chalmers' Fregean contents both work — and both reinstate the explanandum inside the explanans, which is fatal to a *reductive* programme and harmless to Shoemaker, who is not running one. L110 states this as a structural point about what any inversion-answering theory must do, not as a gotcha.

The positions register contains **no first-order entry for any of this**. It contains the methodological parent — P-M2, "Convergence is discounted to framework-internal coherence until a distinguishing test passes" — and it contains the *void-catalogue* instance, P-V1 through P-V3. P-M2's own Asserts paragraph names the missing entry: the discipline "is held *against the Map's own most ambitious arguments*: it is the rule the convergence-for-dualism case and the void-catalogue case must survive." One of those two cases is registered. The other, which is the case for Tenet 1, is not.

This is the clearest actionable gap in the corpus tonight, and `positions/` is at **9 files of 80** — the only section with headroom. Three entries are already fully argued and merely need registering:

1. **The anti-physicalist arguments are premise-sharing, not evidentially independent** — cumulative force real but modest; arguments within a cluster contribute little more than one strong argument from it; genuine force comes from across clusters (phenomenal intuition, explanatory gap, unity prong). Credence: high. External-evidence grade: n/a. Framework-internal: no. This is a position held *against* the Map's interest, which is what makes it worth registering.
2. **Convergence earns irreducibility; Bidirectional Interaction selects dualism** — the two earnings do not compound. The framework-level commitment is a selection rule on the output, not a third route. Depends on P-M2 and on the coherence-vs-evidential-status discipline.
3. **Cross-traditional convergence is near-independence, not a third confirmation** — the Indian/Western lineages are "strong enough to count against a parochially-Western-methodology explanation, not strong enough to be cited as a third independent confirmation" (L123), with the early-Chinese candidate assessed and declined on the merits.

Placement: a new domain file `obsidian/positions/arguments-for-dualism.md`. These are first-order claims about what the argument catalogue supports, not commitments about the Map's editorial conduct, so they do not belong in `methodology-and-calibration.md` — but they should carry explicit `Depends on: P-M2` links, and P-M2 should gain a reciprocal pointer so the parent/instance structure matches the P-M2 → P-V1 pattern already in place.

### Leverage 2 — the grain-dilemma resolution is in the wrong article

`inverted-qualia.md` L138 closes with: "[organizational-invariance](/concepts/organizational-invariance/) states both of Chalmers' arguments at full strength and works through a sharper version of the same dilemma."

It does not. `organizational-invariance.md` L77 states the *same* dilemma, in nearly the same words — "If minimal quantum interaction makes a behaviourally *detectable* difference, the quantum grain is coarse organization Chalmers' isomorph already reproduces ... if it makes *no* detectable difference, Joe's part-silicon stages are behaviourally identical" — and then stops. It has no type/token distinction, no MQI behavioural prediction, and it takes no horn. Its stance is explicitly the opposite of `inverted-qualia`'s: "The horns cut both ways ... the claim to leave introspection intact stands as conditional on that question ... not as a settled free lunch."

So the corpus currently has two articles on one dilemma with **incompatible stances**, and a pointer running from the resolved one to the unresolved one advertising it as sharper. The reader who follows the link is sent from a resolution to an open question described as a deepening.

This matters more than usual today because the 2026-07-28 highlight — "The Sharpest Objection Comes From a Fellow Dualist," whose blurb ends "The Map's reply owns a real dilemma rather than dodging it" — points at `inverted-qualia`, whose own further-reading route hands the visitor the version that does dodge it.

The fix is length-favourable in both directions. `inverted-qualia.md` is at 3,440 authored words with ~60 to hard and **cannot absorb** the type/token account it owes. `organizational-invariance.md` is at **1,904 words** — roughly 1,100 of room — and is the article whose entire "Relation to Site Perspective" section is about this exact grain dispute. Moving the account there and reducing `inverted-qualia`'s L138 to a pointer relieves the length-blocked article and gives the roomy one its missing conclusion.

### Leverage 3 (smaller) — the register under-states the Map's own empirical exposure

[positions/quantum-interface.md](/positions/quantum-interface/) P-Q9 restricts self-concealment to the aggregate-statistics channel and names the positive falsifiable residue as *mechanism-level*: coherence-time results, microtubule signatures, the Diósi–Penrose falsification, the falsification roadmap. Tonight `inverted-qualia.md` L171 added a channel that is on neither list — a **psychophysical** one. If phenomenal character is causally efficacious, any *actual* qualia inversion should show up in aesthetic preference, emotional valence, reaction-time asymmetry, and the fine structure of introspective report, at finer measurement grain than coarse discrimination tasks.

And P-Q3's "Would shift if" clause reads: "a positive account of how the bias makes a phenomenological difference without making a statistical one is worked out and survives review." The type/token distinction — pattern-with-envelope versus token-drawn-within-envelope — is a candidate account of exactly that shape. It is not a discharge of the debt (the article says so), but P-Q3 currently records only the per-trial-vs-ensemble move as a candidate, and this is a sharper statement of it that arrived tonight.

Both are register updates, not new positions, and both belong in the same `positions-evolve` pass as Leverage 1.
