## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)
The wing locates the explanatory gap where it belongs, between correlation and character. Neuro's Tenet 5 paragraph has the best one-line statement of it: "We can predict *that* compassion meditation correlates with insula activation, but not *why* this pattern feels like warmth rather than something else entirely." Neuro L115 says the same about the 7T jhana case: "Even granting it, contemplative neuroscience does not explain *why* these patterns carry these particular experiential qualities." Chalmers would also approve of Altered's careful falsifier "Perfect phenomenal-neural mapping … the filter would become explanatorily idle" (L139). It names the one result that would dissolve the gap, rather than declaring the gap permanent. Priority item 2 extends that care to Neuro L193, which still declares the gap "structural, not a temporary limitation".

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)
Stapp would welcome how seriously Neuro's Minimal Quantum Interaction paragraph (L197) takes the Zeno mechanism: "*repeated* observations can hold quantum systems in specific states despite decoherence between observations". He would admire that it then brakes itself: "though matching one neural timescale to another settles nothing about decoherence times, shorter by many orders of magnitude." Altered L153 is the cleaner piece of physics-consciousness thinking. Altered-state data are inbound, the tenet specifies the outbound leg, so "they are silent on it unless a selection-side effect is separately shown". A Stapp-sympathetic reader can hold his mechanism and still agree that these pages do not evidence it. The zero-cost pipe N6 points Neuro's gamma passage at the Map's calibrated zero-lag-gamma page, which says the synchrony is "classically explained".

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)
All three pages put first-person structure at the centre without letting it pose as third-person proof. Void L95–L97 is the wing's most Nagelian passage. Numinous experience "dismantles" pre-reflective self-awareness, and "Memory of the numinous is not the numinous. Evaluating mind and numinous mind cannot occupy consciousness simultaneously". That is a claim about what it is like whose force does not depend on any neural datum. Neuro's microphenomenology section (L65–L67) shows the same respect for method: "re-enactment rather than abstraction, and focus on 'how' rather than 'why.'"

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)
Neuro L171 gives process philosophy its own reading of the khanika and cessation data: cessation "may involve occasions with radically diminished content rather than consciousness's absence". Neuro L167 refuses to let the data favour Cartesian substance: "equally compatible with process philosophy, neutral monism, or Buddhist anti-substantialism". Altered L103 does the same for Madhyamaka, calling it "a genuine bedrock disagreement … not one the Map can refute from inside". **Calibration note.** This persona's praise is for compatibility only. No passage here uses process-philosophical resonance to raise any organism's evidential tier, so the Hardline Empiricist below has nothing to counter.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)
Neuro L195 reads meditation as having "the structure of self-forming actions: the meditator sustains attention against competing impulses". Altered L95 carefully calls flow's release of control "an outbound-leg reading … so the fit is coherence rather than confirmation". Kane would praise the agency structure and the honesty together. Priority item 1 makes Neuro's opening of the same paragraph match Altered's register ("fit, though not evidence over physicalism"). The self-forming-action sentence stays.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)
Void is the wing's mysterian page. The numinous register is "a specific instance of cognitive closure … over the veridicality of an experience that dismantles the faculty of evaluation" (L99). Its humility is real: "Proudfoot stands largely unanswered against the ineffability reports themselves: that something cannot be said is compatible with there being nothing determinate to say" (L87). L89 says that constitutive closure is "the Map's commitment, carried by dualism", not a finding. McGinn's own view is that closure is not a victory for either side. Priority item 4 keeps the void's kind conditional (L65) and stops L130 from inferring a "genuine cognitive boundary" from a stalemate. That brings the page in line with McGinn.
