## Ranked Priority List

1. **High. The probe face's two capstone quotations are cut so that the void looks more closed than its sources say** (L69, L73).

   - The SEP's "scientific miracle" sentence is relabelled "Its verdict" and placed after the infant and chick evidence. In the SEP it comes *before* that evidence, and the next sentence says that infants and animals "may yield more reliable results". The SEP also describes the action version as "freed from verbal-based identification". The article recasts that as a deficit.
   - Task #1 (P2).
2. **High. §Relation to Site Perspective treats Held's "no" as settled, which contradicts the article's own probe face** (L97, L101).
   - "The newly sighted have conscious access to seen shape" is the very conjunct Schwenkler denies (L69).
   - "The human record suggests it is at least partly acquired, and quickly" draws from the record the conclusion that "Neither verdict is clean" says it cannot support.
   - "Without weakening them" is asserted, not argued.
   - Task #2 (P2).
3. **Medium-High. Neutrality is claimed correctly but warranted wrongly, and breached once.**
   - Green 2024 is placed beside the dualism-neutrality claim as its warrant (L45, L95). His sentence concerns *internal versus external* determination of phenomenal character, a dispute in which dualism is not a party.

   - The same section overclaims twice. The human join is "open from neither side", although Amedi et al. 2001 show third-person access to the mature structure. And the machine test has "neither human confound", although any alignment step brings back Clarke's confound or the contamination one.
   - Task #3 (P2).

   - Task #4 (P3).

## Executive Summary

The page is well researched. All 31 quoted strings are verbatim in raw sources or abstracts. The Held et al. subject count appears only with its sources. No age is given for Meltzoff & Borton. The Leibniz paraphrase matches Clarke ("differing axes of symmetry"). The tenet section declines to enlist dualism. The defects lie in context and calibration. Twice the page cuts a source's sentence just before that source's constructive next step, so the probe face reads as more closed than Clarke or the SEP think it is. The Relation to Site Perspective section then takes Held's "no" at face value, against the probe face's own argument. Neutrality is the right verdict, but it is credited to Green for a dispute he does not address. It is also quietly breached in the AI section, where a structure/experience gap is assumed and not marked.

## Critiques by Philosopher

### The Eliminative Materialist (Patricia Churchland)

"You say the human join is 'open from neither side' (L89). Neuroscience opened the structural side twenty-five years ago. Amedi and colleagues (2001) found that haptic object-selective voxels overlap the visual lateral occipital complex and proposed a multimodal object-related network. Whether seen and felt shape share a representation in the mature brain is an imaging question with imaging answers. Your 'one content or two' becomes empirical the moment you stop putting it in folk-psychological terms. What the purity paradox closes is the *developmental* question, and you have let it stand in for the structural one." The page's own apex sibling cites Sanchez et al.'s cross-modal decoding, so the Map already accepts that third-person route exists for the senses.

### The Hard-Nosed Physicalist (Daniel Dennett)

"Your seam face says one shared content would be seamless and two contents fused by habit would be seamless too, so introspection cannot tell (L59). Tell me what makes them *two* if every downstream use treats them as one. On a functional individuation of content, two contents 'fused' so as to be interchangeable just are one. You have built a distinction that no possible observation, inner or outer, could detect, and then called its undetectability a void. In the AI section you make the hidden premise explicit without marking it. 'Alignment is a third-person structural fact, and whether aligned encoders yield one shape-experience or two … is the seam face's question.' That is the hard problem restated, not a neutral observation. Your own research note called it 'a dualist interpretation, not a finding', and the article deleted the label." This is the sharpest charge against a page that declares itself neutral in its lead.

### The Quantum Skeptic (Max Tegmark)

The page rightly says Minimal Quantum Interaction has no bearing. The physicist's charge is the one he always makes: you have not done the calculation. "Your machine experiment, 'encoders trained separately … tested for zero-shot shape matching at once, with neither human confound' (L89), skips the step that does all the work. Two separately trained latent spaces share no coordinates. To test a match you must put them in correspondence, and there are three ways to do it. Paired training is touch teaching sight again. Unsupervised structural matching is Clarke's inference in silicon. A shared 3D output format is the rationalist 'one geometry' built in by the engineer. Every route reintroduces one of the confounds you said the machine escapes." The AI case moves the purity paradox to a new place but does not remove it.

### The Many-Worlds Defender (David Deutsch)

No Many Worlds is correctly marked as having no bearing. Deutsch's objection falls on Tenet 5 (L93). "You say both historical camps 'claimed economy' and that neither economy picked out an answerable question. Cite one passage where Berkeley or Leibniz argues from parsimony. Berkeley's heterogeneity thesis rests on the nature of ideas (§129: 'there is no idea common to both senses' follows from 'no other immediate object of sight besides light and colours'), not on simplicity. And what failed was the individuation of the question. An ill-posed question defeats every method, parsimony included. Nothing here shows that *simplicity* misled anyone, so the illustration is a label, not a lesson." The page's own "illustrated, not vindicated" hedge is good discipline. The illustration underneath it is thin.

### The Empiricist (Karl Popper's Ghost)

"Molyneux is 'the void where the crucial experiment was run … and each run answered a neighbouring question while the original stayed open' (L45). Combine that with the question face, 'theory rather than data decides which one an experiment answers' (L43), and no result could ever count against the void. Each datum is reclassified as answering a neighbour. That is the conventionalist stratagem." The page partly answers this: "Whether the limit is real" (L75) concedes the probe face may be deflatable. But it then hides the most concrete closure condition its own sources offer. Clarke says inference might be ruled out because perceptual recognition is "characteristically fast" and "judgement independent in a way that rational inference is not". The SEP says naïve, vision-primed subjects "may yield more reliable results". A void that leaves out the published routes to its own closure inflates its own permanence. (Issue 1.)

### The Buddhist Philosopher (Nagarjuna)

"You ask whether the cube is one or two, and you grant the question three faces. You have not asked whether 'one' and 'two' are the right alternatives. Cheselden's boy caught the cat, looked, and set her down. Seen-cat arose in dependence on felt-cat. The tetralemma has room for *neither one nor two*: the seen and the felt shape co-arise conditionally, and no inherently existing 'shape content' is either shared or doubled. Your question face splits the question but keeps the reified 'content' each fragment asks about." The constructive point for the Map is in L101. If the arena is "at least partly acquired", it is a dependently arisen achievement. The Map's mind-side reading of cross-modal unity (`topics/dualist-perception` L118) then owes an account of that acquisition. "Without weakening them" assumes the debt is already paid. (Issue 2.)

## Counterarguments to Address

### "The human join is open from neither side"
- **Current content says**: in a human, the join is closed to introspection by habit and to experiment by the purity paradox (L89).
- **A critic would argue**: imaging opens the mature structure (Amedi et al. 2001). The purity paradox blocks only the developmental question.
- **Suggested response**: concede the structural half. The developmental half is still closed, which is the half Molyneux asked about (Task #3).

### "The seam face is a pseudo-question"
- **Current content says**: both hypotheses predict seamlessness, so introspection cannot tell (L59).
- **A critic would argue**: a distinction that no observation could detect is empty. Functionally fused contents are one content.
- **Suggested response**: say outright that, on functional individuation, the seam face has no further fact, and that it is a void only on views on which structure can leave experience open. The research note's Gaps section already says "Whether it has a fact of the matter at all depends on the question face".

### "Machines escape the purity paradox"
- **Current content says**: separately trained encoders tested at once face "neither human confound" (L89).
- **A critic would argue**: the correspondence step brings back contamination, inference, or a yes built in by design.
- **Suggested response**: name the step, and present the machine case as relocating the paradox (Task #3).
