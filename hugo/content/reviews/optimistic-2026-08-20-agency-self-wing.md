## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

[self-construction-constructor](/apex/self-construction-constructor/) is the wing's — arguably the corpus's — most general statement of the explanatory gap: after granting constructionism its full case in four registers, the residue is stated in one line of analysis, "structure is the very thing the construction supplies and experience is the very thing it presupposes." [sleep-paralysis-and-interface-reassembly](/topics/sleep-paralysis-and-interface-reassembly/) earns this persona's respect differently: it never claims the transitional states touch the hard problem at all — "The argument concerns where explanatory debts fall, not anything physicalism cannot in principle absorb."

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

[philosophy-of-action-under-dualism](/topics/philosophy-of-action-under-dualism/) gives the interactionist programme its action-theoretic half: the volition is where mind meets matter, and the article's formulation — "the trying selects, it does not shove" — is the cleanest one-line statement of selection-only interactionism the corpus has. The exclusion worry is met the right way, through Lowe's attack on causal closure rather than by wishing it away. And [wholeheartedness-void](/voids/wholeheartedness-void/) adds something structurally new to the interactionist picture: its Bidirectional Interaction paragraph proposes the *standing-identification state* as a candidate site for the "persistent shape" of any non-physical influence — most interactionist theorising fixates on the momentary decision, and a standing-state locus is a genuine conceptual extension, correctly flagged as oblique.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

The wing centres first-person data without letting it certify itself. [wholeheartedness-void](/voids/wholeheartedness-void/) holds that the felt difference between acting on a desire one identifies with and acting on a desire that merely wins "survives every revealed-preference reconstruction" — a phenomenological datum defended against operationalist absorption — while simultaneously denying that the phenomenology is self-authenticating (felt unity can be the work of suppression). [sleep-paralysis-and-interface-reassembly](/topics/sleep-paralysis-and-interface-reassembly/) treats the experiencer's report as a *channel decomposition available from the inside*, and its Alcaraz-Sánchez engagement shows phenomenology and network neuroscience converging on multidimensionality from opposite sides.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

Nothing in the wing is crude substance dualism. The action article's layered reading of Anscombe — formal cause for the rational structure, efficient cause for the volitional contact, explicitly owned as "its own reconciling move, not hers" — is process-friendly integration done with attribution discipline. The apex reads the self as an ongoing achievement (the narrative self as "an ongoing neural achievement") with the constructor as the invariant *for-whom*, and sleep paralysis renders the interface as coupling dynamics rather than substance mechanics. Per this persona's standing constraint, I checked each resonance for tier-upgrade work: the Alcaraz-Sánchez convergence is claimed "only as that" (structural), the apex's quantum-coherence gloss is flagged unestablished, and no passage converts process-compatibility into evidence. Nothing to flag.
