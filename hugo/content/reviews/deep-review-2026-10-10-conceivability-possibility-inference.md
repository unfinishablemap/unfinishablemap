## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

1. **Chalmers' "primary possibility" was misdefined (factual error about a source's technical term).** The old L68 said primary possibility "concerns whether a scenario is ruled out by the fundamental nature of properties themselves, not merely by how those properties happen to be instantiated in our world". Its example, "water being conscious might be primarily impossible", did not make sense. Chalmers 2002 (consc.net raw text) reads: "S is primarily possible (or 1-possible) if its primary intension is true in some possible world (i.e. if S is true in some world considered as actual)", and "Primary possibility and necessity correspond much more closely to epistemic notions such as apriority." **Fix:** the paragraph was rewritten from that definition, with the water/XYZ example running in the correct direction (primary possibility without secondary possibility). The core-claim line now carries Chalmers' own wording and label: "WEAK MODAL RATIONALISM: (Ideal primary) positive conceivability entails (primary) possibility"; "(1) Ideal primary positive conceivability entails primary possibility". The old text had dropped "primary" from the conceivability side.
2. **Block & Stalnaker 1999 was misattributed.** The article said they argue "the primary intension of 'consciousness' may itself be physically constituted, making the framework question-begging". Chalmers & Jackson 2001 (consc.net raw text), the reply written to this paper, summarises their actual central strategy. They argue that "truths about water and about life ... are not entailed a priori by microphysical truths, but that this is no bar to the reductive explanation and physical constitution of water and of life", and their section 10 argues that the two-dimensional framework does not supply those entailments. "Question-begging" and phenomenal-concept primary intensions do not appear in that summary. **Fix:** the sentence was restated to match.
3. **False physics claim plus an internal mismatch (2026-02-15 rewrite).** The old L100 listed "that effects could precede causes at the quantum level" among cases once thought inconceivable that turned out actual. Retrocausation is a minority interpretation, not a result. The response paragraph meanwhile called the same case "indeterministic physics". **Fix:** it now reads "that fundamental physical events could be undetermined", and the response says physics "went on to accept" these scenarios, with "(indeterminism, on most interpretations)". Bohmian mechanics and MWI are deterministic.
4. **The closing argument offered a false dilemma that contradicted the article's own body.** The old L144 said dismissing the gap requires *either* showing pseudo-conceiving *or* rejecting modal rationalism entirely. Type-B physicalism does neither: it grants the conceiving, keeps modal rationalism in general, and denies the inference only for the phenomenal case. The article's own Two-Dimensional and Cognitive Closure sections set out exactly that local route. `concepts/type-a-type-b-and-type-c-physicalism` calls Type-B "the live opponent" and lists this page as "the link Type-B denies". **Fix:** the local route is now a third move. Its cost is named as strong necessities (Chalmers 2003, raw text: "the only hope for the type-B materialist is to deny the central thesis (ii)"). The paragraph is calibrated to the Map's own tier: these costs bear on Type-B's plausibility and do not make the evidence favour dualism over it. The Map's verdict against Type-B in the taxonomy page is *compatible*, and this article now agrees with it.
5. **The Bidirectional Interaction paragraph slipped from possibility to probability (calibration).** It said the inference "also supports" the tenet by creating "conceptual room for it to be causally efficacious rather than merely epiphenomenal". Conceivable separation establishes that consciousness is distinct, not what it does, so it leaves room for epiphenomenalism equally. The fully specified zombie is in tension with interactionism. `concepts/philosophical-zombies` says this itself: "interactionism is downstream of it ... not a result conceivability delivers". A reviewer who accepts every tenet would still flag "supports". **Fix:** the paragraph now says the bearing is indirect, links the zombie page's Interactionist Escape section, and places the case for efficacy in the self-stultification argument.
6. **"which is precisely what dualism claims" conflated two claims.** The old L138 said ideal conceivability "reveals that consciousness is not conceptually entailed by physics—which is precisely what dualism claims". Conceptual non-entailment is what Type-B grants while staying physicalist. Getting from that to metaphysical non-necessitation is the job of the inference itself. **Fix:** the two steps are now stated separately.
7. **"universality" of the conceivability intuition was empirically contradicted.** The old L130 spoke of "the very persistence and universality of the conceivability intuition". Díaz 2021 (abstract verified at the BORIS Bern repository: "(1) problem intuitions are not widespread") says otherwise, and the Map corrected its own report of Díaz on 2026-10-07 and 2026-10-10. **Fix:** the sentence now reads "persistence ... under scrutiny ... though not a universal one", cites Díaz (2021), adds a References entry, and links `metaproblem-of-consciousness-under-dualism`.
