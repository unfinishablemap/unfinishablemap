## Executive Summary

The page's evidential discipline on the veridical-perception question is good and its central distinction (self-model dislocation versus disembodied consciousness) is sound. Four of its arguments do not hold as written.

1. **Embodiment.** L48, L104, L110 and L118 say OBEs *refute* the strong embodiment thesis that experience is constitutively tied to the bodily self-model. A dislocated self-model is still a self-model. Blanke and Metzinger (2009), whose framework the page borrows, read the same illusions as *confirming* that embodied selfhood is a constructed model, and the Map's own `concepts/self-model-theory-of-subjectivity` L51 says exactly that. The page's own L62 has the correct, weaker form ("reconfigured rather than abolished"); L104 contradicts it. Since the Dualism paragraph's only argument is that this refutation "removes one materialist-friendly constraint", the tenet section inherits the defect.
2. **Filter versus production.** L112 says filter theory "predicts" coherent scenes from displaced vantages "because consciousness is not bound to a single bodily vantage in the way production models would require". Production models require no such thing; Blanke's programme produces the displaced vantage directly. Compatibility is dressed as a differential prediction.
3. **AWARE grading.** L48, L92 and L94 treat the AWARE nulls as a test that "has been tested". In AWARE I, 78% of arrests occurred in rooms with no target shelf and neither patient who reported visual awareness was in an equipped room, so the visual test was not run on the cases that mattered. The sibling `concepts/near-death-experiences` L108 carries the needed hedge; this page does not.
4. **Reynolds.** L90 conjoins the flat-EEG standstill with the verified perceptions and says critics place them at "induction or emergence" with timing that "cannot be independently verified". Sabom himself concedes the OBE began "when the saw started up", during the opening craniotomy and before cooling began; both sides agree on the timing and dispute only whether anaesthetised hearing explains it (Woerlee 2011).

Currency: a controlled visual-target test of OBEs *outside* the near-death context has now been published and returned a null (Weiler et al., 2026), which updates L84 and falsifier 3; the most visible new NDE model (Martial et al., 2025, *Nature Reviews Neurology*) excludes dualist readings by stipulation, which is a live specimen for the Occam's paragraph; self-location has been imaged to a hippocampal–posterior-cingulate network rather than the TPJ alone (Guterstam et al., 2015); and the page's unsourced vestibular-dysfunction claim now has a 210-patient prospective series behind it with a complicating finding (Lopez and Elzière, 2018).

## Critiques by Philosopher

### The Eliminative Materialist (Churchland)

The page concedes everything that matters and then keeps a vocabulary that has lost its referent. "Self-model dislocation" is a neural event in a multisensory integration network; "disembodied consciousness" is the folk residue the page admits is "undemonstrated under controlled conditions". The only reason to keep both terms in play is that the second has not been *formally* retired. Churchland would add that the page's one positive philosophical claim, that OBEs refute strong embodiment (L104), is false by the page's own account of the data: the self-model is relocated, not removed, and the brain that relocates it is doing exactly what an embodied-cognition theorist says it does.

### The Hard-Nosed Physicalist (Dennett)

The analogies at L100 are asymmetric. Ordinary vision is not hallucination because there is an independent, well-characterised causal channel from object to retina; pain is not an artefact because nociceptors exist. The page asks that stimulation-induced OBEs be shown *necessary* before spontaneous ones are classified with them, but offers no positive channel for the alternative. Where the only known mechanism is the TPJ–vestibular network and the only putative alternative has returned zero controlled hits, parity with the page's own analogies places the burden on the exit reading. This is the standoff the 07-14 review marked as bedrock; it is recorded here as the counterargument the section should be read against, not as a defect. Dennett would also note that L120's demand that epiphenomenalists "explain why the dislocated self-model continues to support the same kinds of integrated cognition" is no demand at all: on epiphenomenalism the cognition is neural throughout, so the self-model's new location changes nothing about what the brain computes.

### The Quantum Skeptic (Tegmark)

The page's only route from OBEs to the Map's physics is falsifier 1 (L130), where it correctly notes that a replicable veridical channel would *breach* the Map's Born-preserving interface. Tegmark would press the consequence: the Map's interface is designed so that it could never deliver the one result that would distinguish an OBE from a self-model illusion. The page is therefore right that the data "favour the self-model account", and should say so without the filter-theoretic consolation at L112, which has no quantum content and no differential prediction.

### The Many-Worlds Defender (Deutsch)

Nothing in the page bears on branching. The one place it could is the identity question hidden in "where I am" (L108): if self-location is a representation the brain can set to any value, the indexical "here" the Map leans on for No Many Worlds is itself a computed quantity. The page does not notice that its own self-model account hands the Everettian a tool. Not a defect of this page; noted as an unexploited vulnerability.

### The Empiricist (Popper)

Credit where due: §What Would Challenge This View? is a real falsifier list and the AWARE nulls are accepted as evidence against the exit reading. The Popperian complaint is about *grading*. "Testable, has been tested, and has so far not been confirmed" (L94) describes a test that, in AWARE I, was never run on the awareness cases (no shelf in their rooms), and in AWARE II was run on at most 28 interviewees of whom one reported anything resembling external perception. A zero from an untested population is not a null result; it is an absent result. The page should state the denominators it is grading on, as the sibling NDE page does.

### The Buddhist Philosopher (Nagarjuna)

The page's "philosophically robust claim" (L124), that "the bodily self-model is dislocatable while consciousness persists", is what the Madhyamika expects: the sense of being located is a dependent construction, and the OBE is one more demonstration that it has no *svabhāva*. What Nagarjuna would reject is the inference the page draws from this at L104 and L118, that the relocatability of the self-model "makes room" for consciousness as a separate thing filtered through the body. Showing a construct to be a construct is not evidence for a substance behind it. The page's own L60 ("Phenomenology is not the same as ontology") is the right principle and L118 fails to apply it to the Map's side.

## Critical Issues

### Issue 1: OBEs do not refute the strong embodiment thesis; the page's own source and the Map's own SMT page read them the other way

- **File**: `obsidian/topics/out-of-body-experiences.md`
- **Location**: L48 ("they refute strong claims that the bodily self-model is necessary for consciousness"), L104 ("What stimulation work *does* refute is the strong version of embodied consciousness which holds that the bodily self-model is constitutive of conscious experience"), L110 ("it dissolves some, including the strong embodiment thesis"), L118 ("tells against the strong embodiment thesis … removes one materialist-friendly constraint"). Grep counts in the page: `refute` ×3 (L48, L104, L100's non-sequitur sentence is a different use), `strong embodiment` ×4.
- **Problem**: In every OBE the page describes, a self-model is present: a vantage point, a visual perspective, often a second body (L58). What changes is the *content* of the self-location representation, not whether consciousness is accompanied by one. The embodiment theorist's constitutive claim concerns the self-modelling system, not the accuracy of its location estimate, and no embodiment theorist holds that experience requires *veridical* self-location. Blanke and Metzinger (2009), the source of the self-location / self-identification vocabulary the page uses at L74, treat full-body illusions and OBEs as evidence *for* their account of minimal phenomenal selfhood as a constructed embodied model. The Map agrees elsewhere: `concepts/self-model-theory-of-subjectivity` L51 reads "When self-location and self-identification can be shifted by manipulating multisensory input, Blanke and Metzinger read this as evidence that embodied selfhood is a constructed model rather than a fixed datum" (grep ×1). So L104 contradicts a sibling Map page and inverts its own authority. It also contradicts the page's own L62, which has the defensible form: strong embodiment theses "must accommodate cases in which the bodily self-model is reconfigured rather than abolished". The inference at L104, from "the self-model can be moved" to "consciousness is at least separable from the *located-in-this-body* representation", is the epistemic-to-metaphysical slide the skill names: the evidence shows the *felt* locus is movable (phenomenal/epistemic); the conclusion asserts a *separability* of consciousness from its representational base (metaphysical). The Dualism paragraph then spends the refutation ("removes one materialist-friendly constraint and makes room for filter-theoretic readings"), so the tenet section rests on it.
- **Severity**: High.
- **Recommendation**: Downgrade L48, L104, L110 and L118 to L62's register ("constrains without refuting"; what is ruled out is only binding to the self-model's *actual* bodily position), cite Blanke and Metzinger (2009) for the opposite reading, and change "removes" to "loosens" at L118. Exact strings in the Priority List, item 1.

- **Problem**: Production models do not require consciousness to be bound to a single bodily vantage; the whole of §TPJ Stimulation is a production account that generates displaced vantages on demand, and the page accepts it (L76). So the "prediction" is shared by both framings and discriminates nothing. The page's description promises to resist "the inference from mechanism to dismissal"; this sentence runs the mirror-image inference, from mechanism to filter-friendliness. ### Issue 3: The AWARE null is graded without its denominators

- **File**: `obsidian/topics/out-of-body-experiences.md`
- **Location**: L48 ("has so far returned zero hits on hidden targets"), L92 ("no patient identified a hidden visual target"; "none identified the hidden visual image"), L94 ("is testable, has been tested, and has so far not been confirmed. - **Problem**: The Psi Encyclopedia's AWARE account, drawn from Parnia et al. (2014), reports that "as many as 78% of the cardiac arrests recorded in the study happened in rooms that were not so equipped" and that for the two patients with explicit visual/auditory awareness "neither could have done so, since there was none" (no shelf in their rooms). The AWARE I visual test therefore produced no observation at all on the cases where a hit was possible. AWARE II's abstract (Europe PMC, PMID 37423492) gives "Nobody identified the visual image, 1/28(3.5%) identified the auditory stimulus"; the fraction of those 28 whose arrest had the tablet and headphones running is not in the abstract and I could not retrieve the full text (see Could Not Verify). The sibling `concepts/near-death-experiences` L108 already carries the hedge the page needs: "few patients survive with memories, and fewer would be positioned to see targets" (grep ×1 there). The miscalibration here runs *against* the Map's own side, which is to the page's credit on motive, but a page that announces "the Map reads this evidence as it stands" should state what the evidence is.
- **Severity**: High.
- **Recommendation**: Add the AWARE I denominator at L92, re-grade L94 and L48 (Priority List, item 3). The 78% figure should be confirmed against the Parnia 2014 full text before installation; the Psi Encyclopedia attributes it to the paper, and the 2014 abstract does not carry it.

### Issue 4: The Reynolds paragraph misstates the timeline and the critics' claim

- **File**: `obsidian/topics/out-of-body-experiences.md`
- **Location**: L90, "she reported observing surgical details during a procedure in which she was deeply hypothermic, her EEG was flat, and her brain was drained of blood (Sabom, 1998). Grep: `anaesthesia induction or emergence` ×1 here; the identical phrase (American spelling) is ×1 at `concepts/near-death-experiences` L106.
- **Problem**: Three things are wrong. (a) The first sentence conjoins the verified perceptions (saw, remark about her small vessels) with the standstill state; by Sabom's own account the saw started during the opening craniotomy, hours before the 10:50 start of the arrest procedure, under general anaesthesia with normal brain function. The live dispute is whether the click-emitting earphones excluded hearing, not when the perceptions happened. - **Severity**: Medium-High.
- **Recommendation**: Rewrite L90 to separate the standstill from the timed perceptions and to state the actual dispute, adding Woerlee (2011) to References (Priority List, item 4). The NDE page's L106 is the *origin* of the phrase (that page was created 2026-01-15, this one 2026-05-08), not a derived copy, so it is reported here for that page's next review rather than minted as a propagation target.

### Issue 6 (Medium, currency): L84 and falsifier 3 are now out of date

- **Problem**: Weiler, Abraham, Acunzo and Hermansson (2026), *EXPLORE* 22:103506 (Europe PMC PMID 42617381), ran a concealed-target test with 21 practitioners from an OBE-training institute; 10 reported an OBE, 13 supplied descriptions, and "No individual trial showed statistically significant above-chance correspondence, and a combined rank-based test across all 13 trials likewise showed no evidence of above-chance performance." ### Issue 7 (Medium, currency): the mechanism is a network, and the page's TPJ-only framing is 2005-era
