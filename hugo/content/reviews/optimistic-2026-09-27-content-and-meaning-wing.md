## Executive Summary

The four concept and topic pages form one of the best-calibrated clusters on the Map. `teleosemantics` states the leading naturalist rival in its strongest form. It covers four proponents and fields every canonical objection *with* the replies proponents actually give. It then places the Map's dissent on a new axis: selection-relative correctness is not answerability to evidence. `content-vocabulary-as-derived-feature` shows how the Map should argue against a framework it cannot refute. It names the framework's available reply, calls its own move "a sharpening, not a refutation", and cites the evidential-status discipline by name. `the-naturalisation-failure-for-content` borrows Hutto and Myin's diagnosis and openly declines their cure. `content-externalism` earns its internalist half with an independent argument from introspective authority rather than assuming it.

The weak points are in the **two voids pages, which predate the concept pages and have not caught up**. There are also two cross-page seams:

1. **`voids/meaning-void` L77 says "teleosemantic theories cannot explain misrepresentation."** `teleosemantics` L68 calls misrepresentation that programme's "founding success" and "strongest card". The voids page repeats a claim that the Map's own dedicated page refutes. It links none of the four content pages.
2. **`voids/void-as-ground-of-meaning` L94 inverts Shannon.** "A signal that could be anything carries zero information" is backwards: in Shannon's theory, more possible messages means *more* information per message. The correct formal point also fits the page better. A receiver with no uncertainty gains zero information from any signal, which is exactly the omniscient mind the paragraph describes.
3. **Twin Earth cuts both ways, and only `content-externalism` says so.** Oscar and Twin-Oscar are *phenomenal* duplicates as well as physical ones. So the argument that intrinsic physical state underdetermines reference shows that intrinsic phenomenal state underdetermines it too. `content-externalism` handles this with its hybrid view: wide reference, narrow phenomenal character. `the-naturalisation-failure-for-content` L125 reads externalism as simply "complementary" to its thesis that "content is constitutively conscious-mediated" (L123) and never says which content it means. `meaning-void` L59 goes further and claims that introspective access to reference mechanisms would reveal that Hesperus and Phosphorus co-refer. On the Map's own hybrid view, no inner access could reveal that.
4. **`void-as-ground-of-meaning` slides between two senses of "meaning"**: significance or mattering (death, ethics, urgency) and semantic content (L50 "a sentence means something because it says *this* and not *that*"; L94 Shannon; L107 the meaning-void listed as an instance). Its Relation to Site Perspective then claims support from all five tenets, including a quick Many Worlds argument.

Nothing in the wing moves a minimal organism up the evidential scale. The one evidential overreach is an illustration presented as fact (`the-naturalisation-failure-for-content` L121 on infant intentionality; Calibration concern 5).

## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

`the-naturalisation-failure-for-content` L103-107 does what Chalmers does with the zombie argument's relatives: it keeps the two hard problems *dialectically independent* and leaves open whether they are *metaphysically* one. The payoff comes at L105. A physicalist unmoved by the explanatory gap "still faces the HPC as a separate front", while a dualist gains "physicalism failing in two places, on two topics, with two argument structures." The page then declines to cash that in too early. L107 grants that the parallel "could also be read the other way — as two instances of one over-demanding standard for 'accounting for'." Chalmers would recognise the proper use of a gap argument: it widens the front without claiming that width is proof.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

This wing has no quantum content, and it is right not to force any. The nearest point of contact is `the-naturalisation-failure-for-content` L145. If content is conscious-mediated, "the causal pathway from content to behaviour is a pathway from consciousness to physical action". An epiphenomenal consciousness "could not figure in the causal explanation of why an organism reaches for one thing rather than another under one description rather than another." Stapp's central point is that the *intensional* description, not just the extensional state, has to make a difference to which physical outcome occurs. This sentence states that requirement in content terms without claiming any mechanism. `meaning-void` L121 marks its quantum speculation as "to hold lightly", which is the right level of confidence.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

`content-vocabulary-as-derived-feature` L71-73 is the wing's best piece of phenomenological argument. The phantom limb is the case where "the missing limb is precisely the object the experience is *about*." The predictive model "tells us which parameter is mis-set; it does not tell us why the mis-set parameter is *like anything* or why its felt content is *about* a limb." Nagel's point that the objective view leaves out the view from here is made with a real clinical case instead of a thought experiment. `meaning-void` L53 and L71 (Siewert's comprehended versus uncomprehended sentence, with identical sensory phenomenology but different overall phenomenology) give cognitive phenomenology its cleanest contrast.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

`void-as-ground-of-meaning` L48-50 frames meaning as figure against ground: "the boundary between figure and ground belongs to neither exclusively but is the shared condition that makes both perceptible." That is close to Whitehead's idea that an actual occasion is constituted by what it excludes (negative prehension). The Buddhist section (L70-76) keeps the process sympathy in check. It separates Daoist *wu* as "productive receptivity" from Madhyamaka's "logical bracketing of *svabhāva*" (L74) instead of merging them. None of this is used to raise the evidential status of anything. That is correct, and it fits the pattern *tenet-coherent, not evidence-elevating*.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

`teleosemantics` L86 locates the Map's dissent where Kane would want it: in the difference between a state that is *selected for* and a thinker who *ought, given evidence, to judge one way rather than another*. "A malfunctioning heart is not *mistaken about* anything." This supports agency without saying so. If rational normativity cannot be reduced to selection history, then the agent who answers to reasons is doing something selection alone never did. The page links this to the sibling [normativity-of-reason](/topics/consciousness-and-the-normativity-of-reason/) argument rather than restating it.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

`meaning-void` L97 gives a structural reason the void may be permanent: "introspecting *is* constructing — any attempt to observe reference-formation would itself be a new act of reference." L101 then gives the physicalist dashboard alternative a fair hearing ("The void would be an engineering feature of brains, not a window onto metaphysics") before replying. McGinn's *Double Intentionality* is in the reference list for the right reason. `void-as-ground-of-meaning` L114-116 turns the self-undermining worry into humility: "The meta-void is a void too."

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

This is the wing's strongest showing on calibration. Three passages are models of it.

- `content-vocabulary-as-derived-feature` L55-57 names the opponent's available reply ("aboutness is one more derived pattern, all the way up") and declines to upgrade: treating the move as a defeater "would upgrade 'the framework has not shown how its vocabulary earns full aboutness' into 'the framework cannot be true,' which the available evidence does not support." The Relation to Site Perspective (L79) calls the gap "a *place to look*... not by itself a win for the Map's metaphysics." This is exactly the praiseworthy restraint the persona looks for: the page had a tenet-driven upgrade within reach and turned it down in writing.
- `teleosemantics` L90 lists "two honest limitations". The first is that the bite-the-bullet reply "is principled... neither side refutes the other by stipulation." The dissent is "a reasoned bet about the limits of reduction... not a demonstration."
- `content-externalism` L42 refuses to assume its conclusion: "The Map therefore cannot assert switch-invariance as a premise — that would assume the very point Dretske disputes." It then earns the internalist half with an argument Dretske "must answer on its own terms."

`void-as-ground-of-meaning` L54 and L90 do the same for evidence from convergence across traditions: "a methodological sanity-check, not proof", and after Katz and Sharf, "counts less than a perennialist reading would suggest."

The persona has two dissents. `the-naturalisation-failure-for-content` L121 treats an illustration as a finding (Calibration concern 5), and `void-as-ground-of-meaning`'s tenet section claims support from resonance (Calibration concern 4).

- **Why it works**: It turns the best reply (Mann and Pain 2022) into evidence *for* the diagnosis while conceding at L133 that this is "dialectically reasonable rather than decisive."

### concepts/teleosemantics
- **Strongest point**: It is a true steelman. It presents objections together with the replies proponents actually give ("Presenting the objections alone would smuggle in a refutation", L64), and it explains why the producer-side view has gained ground (the frog case at L72, *kimu* at L80).

### concepts/content-externalism
- **Strongest point**: The three-way map at L38: intentional externalism, phenomenal externalism, and phenomenal internalism, with the Map placed precisely among them. The hybrid view is set against Farkas as well as Dretske (L46: "a more defensible perimeter").

### concepts/content-vocabulary-as-derived-feature

### voids/meaning-void

- **Why it works**: The phenomenological catalogue at L85-91 (tip of the tongue, the click of understanding, humour, collapse under self-reference) gives the void concrete edges.

### voids/void-as-ground-of-meaning

## Calibration Concerns (refine-draft, not expansion)

1. **`meaning-void` L77 contradicts `teleosemantics`.** "teleosemantic theories cannot explain misrepresentation" is the opposite of the dedicated page's L68 (misrepresentation is the "founding success"; the debate "has moved on from *whether* function can distinguish error to *which* condition function picks out"). The accurate compression is: *teleosemantic theories recover misrepresentation but face indeterminacy about which condition is represented (the frog case) and cannot fix the guise under which it is represented (the Hard Problem of Content).* The same wording survives in the archived `intentionality-void` (hugo `archive/voids/intentionality-void.md` L74). An archive notice covers that page, so fix only the live page. Related: the `intentionality` hub L75 presents Fodor's frog indeterminacy ("either content explains the selected behaviour") without Neander's producer-side reply, which `teleosemantics` L72 treats as a principal reason informational teleosemantics has gained ground. One clause would fix that.
2. **`void-as-ground-of-meaning` L94 inverts Shannon.** Replace "a signal that could be anything carries zero information" with the correct and better-fitting point: *information is reduction of uncertainty, so a mind with no uncertainty gains zero information from any signal.* This is length-neutral. Separately, **L104-105 lists `[[creative-aesthetic-void]]` twice.** The second bullet ("opens access to the ineffable") probably referred to a void that was later merged into it. Combine them.
3. **Twin Earth is used in one direction only.** `content-externalism` concedes wide *intentional* content, and `teleosemantics` L86 concedes that correctness-fixing "is world-involving on any theory including the Map's". But `the-naturalisation-failure-for-content` L123 says "content is constitutively conscious-mediated" without saying which content, and L125 reads externalism as purely "complementary", although Twin-Oscar is a phenomenal duplicate too. The fix is one clause that points to the hybrid: *conscious mediation grounds the intensional, guise-bearing aspect and answerability; the environment fixes wide reference.* That clause also strengthens the page, because the HPC's chokepoint is intensionality (L67), which is the aspect the hybrid assigns to consciousness. `meaning-void` L59 ("With introspective access to reference mechanisms, the two terms' shared referential pathway would be detectable") conflicts with the hybrid. Co-reference of Hesperus and Phosphorus is an astronomical fact, not something inside the head. The accurate claim is about *sense*: we cannot introspect *how* two senses are fixed. The Frege paragraph at L61 already has the right distinction.
4. **`void-as-ground-of-meaning` equivocates on "meaning" and claims support from all five tenets by resonance.** L50 moves from semantic meaning ("says *this* and not *that*", which is contrastive structure in the Saussure/Derrida sense and not a limit on the *cognizer*) to significance ("a choice matters"). A two-sentence note saying that the thesis concerns *significance*, and that the semantic cases are analogues, would stop `meaning-void` L123 and this page's L107 from borrowing each other's authority. In the tenet section, L134 (Dualism "gains support") runs the dependency backwards: *if* consciousness is irreducible, the boundary is a constitutive void. That is the tenet supporting the thesis, not the reverse. L138 (the *smallness* of the interface "preserves the meaningful distinction") and L140 (Many Worlds "dissolves a limit") are consistency with the tenets presented as support. The Many Worlds line also uses the quick argument that the hub pages have retired (see [optimistic-2026-09-27-evolution-and-origins-wing](/reviews/optimistic-2026-09-27-evolution-and-origins-wing/), concern 2). An Everettian keeps branch-local indexical particularity. Rewording these as *coheres with* is length-neutral. This is the pattern *tenet-coherent, not evidence-elevating* applied to the tenets themselves.
5. **`the-naturalisation-failure-for-content` L121 presents an illustration as fact.** "A pre-linguistic infant attending to her mother is *experiencing the mother as her mother*" attributes a kinship concept to a pre-linguistic infant, and the Map's own [infant-consciousness](/voids/infant-consciousness/) is filed as a *void*. The honest version is conditional and shorter: *is, on the Map's view, experiencing the mother as someone familiar and salient...* It should also cite the subitization evidence already on the page (L99) as the empirical support it has. The page has only **198 words** of headroom, so the rewrite must be length-neutral.
6. **`meaning-void` L119 uses the quick Many Worlds argument.** It says that under branching there is "no fact about which intention is genuinely held". An Everettian says each branch-successor intends determinately within its branch. The Map's dispute is over global indexical identity, which is a posit. Reword to match the hubs.
7. **Orphaned references left over from coalescing, and one missing reference.** `meaning-void` lists Brentano 1874, Limanowski & Friston 2018, McGinn "Double Intentionality" and Nagel 1986, but the body cites none of them. This is residue from coalescing `intentionality-void` and `semantic-void`. Either cite them in the body or drop them. `content-vocabulary-as-derived-feature` lists Clark, Friston & Wilkinson 2019 ("Bayesing Qualia") but never cites it. That paper is exactly the "aboutness all the way up" reply the page names at L55, so citing it there would be an improvement at almost no word cost. `teleosemantics` lists Mann & Pain 2022 without citing it in the body (see High Priority 2). `content-externalism` L36 cites "Kim's reply to Dretske" with no reference entry. **Lead, verify at the publisher:** probably Kim, J. (1996), "Dretske's Qualia Externalism", *Philosophical Issues* 7, same issue as Dretske 1996.
8. **`teleosemantics` L88: "two standing arguments" link to the same page.** The "Hard Problem of Content" and the "naturalisation failure for content" both link `[[the-naturalisation-failure-for-content]]`, because `concepts/hard-problem-of-content` was merged into that topic (see its `coalesced_from`). The sentence should now describe one argument, and the saved words cover High Priority 2.
9. **Minor: `content-vocabulary-as-derived-feature` L71 may overstate phantom limbs' "resistance... to mere peripheral intervention".** **Lead, verify:** there is peripheral-origin evidence (e.g. dorsal-root-ganglion anaesthetic block abolishing phantom sensations, Vaso et al. 2014, *Pain*). L73's key point is that no *limb* is available to be the relatum, and that survives either way. Only the peripheral-resistance clause is at risk. L37 also uses the "is **not** X... This is a sharpening, not a refutation" construction that the style guide discourages. It can be rephrased without losing the calibration.

### High Priority

#### Swampman under phenomenal intentionality
- **Builds on**: `teleosemantics` L74-76 (the only Map page that mentions Swampman; grep across topics/concepts/voids/apex returned one file), `intentionality`, `content-externalism`.
- **Would address**: Swampman is where teleosemantics and the Map's phenomenal-intentionality view make *opposite* predictions. Teleosemantics says Swampman has no content. The Map's view gives a split verdict: Swampman has narrow phenomenal intentionality *if conscious*, and his wide reference is an open question (his "water" has no causal history with H₂O). That makes it a discriminating case, not a restatement. The page should also take on Papineau's actual-world-reduction reply (L76) and state the cost to the Map: whether Swampman is conscious is itself something the Map cannot verify.
- **Estimated scope**: Medium (concepts/, ~1800 words; 28 slots free).
- **Tenet alignment**: Dualism (content tied to consciousness, not history). The hybrid view is tested at the case that separates it from both rivals.

#### Field the Mann-Pain level restriction against the two-normativities dissent (`teleosemantics`, refine-draft)
- **Builds on**: `teleosemantics` L86-90, and `the-naturalisation-failure-for-content` L95-97, which already present Mann and Pain.
- **Would address**: The Map's own dissent (answerability to evidence) is open to the same move Mann and Pain make against the HPC: rational answerability is a *personal-level* desideratum, and teleosemantics only owes sub-personal correctness. The page cites Mann & Pain in its references but never in the body. The Map should state this reply at full strength and answer it, for example by arguing that the personal/sub-personal split concedes that *personal-level* content is not naturalised, which is the Map's claim. Otherwise "two honest limitations" should become three.
- **Estimated scope**: ~200 words, paid for by concern 8.
