# Architectural and Empirical Deficits in the Interactionist Framework of The Unfinishable Map: A Pre-Publication Audit

## Introduction and Metaphysical Scope

The site seeks to rehabilitate Jamesian and Bergsonian filter theory, deploying it alongside post-decoherence quantum selection and modern accounts of delegatory dualism to secure a causally efficacious role for subjective experience without violating the observational closure of physics.

However, an exhaustive audit of the site's philosophical arguments, empirical dependencies, and logical inferences reveals a project that purchases internal coherence at the cost of empirical intractability. The framework constructs a defensive perimeter of epistemological caveats—most notably encapsulated in its "positions register"—that routinely function to disguise fatal theoretical contradictions as mere "mechanism debts". By asserting that consciousness biases quantum outcomes while simultaneously stipulating that these biases perfectly preserve standard quantum probabilities, the model hollows out its own interactionist core, rendering its proposed mental causation statistically empty and empirically indistinguishable from epiphenomenalism.

Furthermore, the site's integration of contemporary empirical science is highly selective. It aggressively platforms speculative, low-grade biophysical plausibility models regarding neural quantum coherence while systematically mishandling or omitting devastating neuroscientific counter-evidence, particularly from the 2020-2025 literature on direct intracranial stimulation and causal brain mapping. The subsequent analysis dismantles the structural viability of the site's claims, demonstrating that its core mechanisms—specifically its reliance on delegatory preemption, its exact Born rule preservation, and its filter theory of perception—fail to survive rigorous analytic scrutiny and contradict established empirical reality.

## The Epistemological Architecture and the Illusion of Rigor

The project attempts to manage its immense epistemic load through a positions register, a ledger of live, superseded, and retired claims calibrated by credence, structural centrality, and empirical discriminability. The register is intended to enforce a strict dependency hierarchy: downstream applied verdicts (such as those concerning artificial intelligence, moral status, and ethical enhancement) must trace their justifications back through intermediate positions to the foundational tenets. This structural choice is ostensibly designed to prevent the framework from generating localized conclusions that contradict its broader metaphysical commitments.

### The Foundational-Dependency Paradox

The site explicitly defines its five tenets as "foundational starting points—chosen, not derived". A foundational axiom, by definition in formal philosophical systems, cannot rest upon contingent, downstream empirical or metaphysical propositions. Yet, the register explicitly concedes that Tenet 4 (No Many Worlds) depends entirely on the "indexical objection," which in turn presupposes a determinate, non-deflationary fact about subjective identity—a commitment tracked in the register as position [P-I1](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i1) (closed individualism) and [P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3) (diachronic persistence).

The site applies a "foundational-dependency test" to manage this, noting that a position cannot be retired if it removes a tenet's rationale. This maneuver exposes a fatal vulnerability in the project's epistemology: the "chosen, not derived" tenets rest upon mutable, highly contested metaphysical positions regarding personal identity over time. If diachronic subject persistence ([P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3)) is a prerequisite for Tenet 4's rejection of Everettian branching, then Tenet 4 is an emergent conclusion of a highly specific philosophy of identity, not a bedrock axiom. The site attempts to enforce a one-way derivation (Tenets → Positions → Applied Verdicts), but the register betrays a bidirectional dependency where the foundational tenets are entirely hostage to highly speculative, mid-level metaphysical positions.

## The Epiphenomenal Trap and Exact Born Preservation

The most catastrophic theoretical failure in the site's architecture lies at the intersection of its mental causation arguments and its quantum mechanics commitments. The framework vigorously attacks epiphenomenalism, deploying the self-stultification argument to prove that consciousness must be causally coupled to the brain. If epiphenomenalism were true, the site correctly notes, our beliefs and discourse about consciousness would be caused entirely by physical brain states, leaving phenomenal experience causally disconnected from our reports about it. Under such a regime, the philosopher writing a treatise on the "hard problem" is a mere physical automaton whose vocalizations are driven by classical physics, rendering the introspective evidence for consciousness utterly causally inert. To avoid this self-defeating trap, Tenet 3 (Bidirectional Interaction) demands that subjective experience exerts downward causation on physical matter.

### The Statistical Nullification of Downward Causation

The site attempts to locate this causal joint in the quantum domain (Tenet 2), specifically through a "post-decoherence selection" mechanism registered as [P-Q1](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q1). In this model, consciousness does not inject energy into the brain, nor does it violate the causal closure of the physical under a macroscopic classical description. Rather, it biases the selection of physically underdetermined quantum outcomes within the neural architecture.

However, to avoid predicting macroscopic anomalies that would have already been detected by contemporary physics, the site rigorously enforces position [P-Q2](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q2): "exact Born preservation". This position mandates that the downward causal influence of consciousness preserves the standard Born rule probability statistics exactly. The site explicitly recognizes the danger here, noting in [P-Q3](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q3) that an intervention which is mathematically indistinguishable from standard quantum randomness "sits genuinely close to epiphenomenalism".

The analysis indicates that this is not merely "close" to epiphenomenalism; it mathematically guarantees it at the macroscopic limit of neural computation. If consciousness biases a specific neural quantum event (e.g., the state of a localized ion channel or a microtubule tubulin dimer), but the aggregate distribution of these biases over an ensemble of events perfectly reproduces the exact probability distribution predicted by the physical Schrödinger equation alone, then consciousness has introduced zero macroscopic variance.

The site attempts to salvage this theoretical disaster by adopting a conditional-signature formalism, differentiating between the marginal probability P(O∣X) and the interventional probability P(O∣do(C),X). The argument suggests that while the statistical distribution of behaviors remains identical to a purely physical system over time, the *actual* sequence of events—the specific instantiations of those probabilities—was steered by subjective experience.

This maneuver is logically self-defeating when scaled to the level of complex human behavior. If the resulting physical behavior of the organism (including its vocalizations regarding the "hard problem of consciousness") is statistically identical to what would have occurred under purely random quantum selection without any mental intervention, then the presence of the conscious agent is explanatorily redundant.

### The Collapse of Report-Grounded Consciousness

The site's proposed "report-grounded consciousness"—the claim that an entity's phenomenal states causally shape its discourse—cannot survive exact Born preservation. The site defines report-grounded consciousness as the special case where an entity's own phenomenal states causally generate its consciousness-discourse (e.g., a person saying "this hurts" because they are genuinely experiencing pain).

Yet, if exact Born preservation holds, the probability of the physical vocal apparatus producing the sonic sequence "this hurts" is exactly the same whether the downward causal bias of consciousness is present or not. The physical priors completely screen off the mental intervention. The conscious selection is merely riding the physical rails, returning the framework to the very ensemble-level epiphenomenalism it aimed to defeat. The site's concession that exact Born preservation makes its interactionism "empirically indistinguishable by construction" from chance ([P-SC1](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc1)) ensures that its rejection of epiphenomenalism is entirely semantic. A difference that makes no statistical difference in the aggregate behavior of the brain is not a causal difference; it is a metaphysical decoration attached to a deterministic (or randomly probabilisitic) physical machine.

## The Logical Incoherence of Delegatory Dualism under Quantum Underdetermination

To fortify its interactionist claims against Jaegwon Kim's exclusion argument, the site recruits Bradford Saad's 2025 framework of "delegatory dualism". Kim's exclusion argument is the central dilemma for non-reductive physicalism and interactionist dualism alike: if every physical event has a sufficient physical cause (the causal closure of the physical), and mental events also cause physical effects, one must either accept rampant overdetermination (two sufficient causes firing simultaneously for every action) or reduce the mental to the physical.

Saad's novel proposal offers an alternative mechanism: preemption. Under delegatory causation, when a conscious experience arises, it inherits the "default causal profile" of the underlying physical brain state. The physical state is preempted; it retains the capacity to cause the effect but does not exercise it, because the non-physical experience has taken over the causal labor. Pain causes wincing not *alongside* the brain state, but *instead of* it.

The site embraces this mechanism because it appears to allow for genuine mental causation without violating observational closure. However, a rigorous philosophical audit reveals that integrating Saad's delegatory preemption with the site's Tenet 2 (Minimal Quantum Interaction) yields an irreconcilable ontological paradox.

### The Collision of Preemption and Indeterminacy

Saad's mechanism requires a highly deterministic, or at least causally sufficient, conceptualization of the physical baseline. To "delegate" a causal responsibility, the physical state must possess a defined default causal profile that would have necessitated the effect in the absence of consciousness. Preemption logically requires that the preempted cause was sufficient to produce the outcome. If cause A preempts cause B, cause B must have possessed the independent capacity to bring about the effect had cause A not intervened.

Yet, the site's entire quantum framework rests on the premise that the physical states in question (neural micro-events) are fundamentally *underdetermined*. The site explicitly states that physics provides necessary but not sufficient causes, leaving room for mental causation.

This creates a fatal contradiction. If the physical brain state is in a quantum superposition, or is subject to raw quantum indeterminacy prior to measurement, it does not possess a sufficient "default causal profile" to produce a specific macroscopic action. It only possesses a probability amplitude. Consequently, the physical state cannot "delegate" the causal responsibility for a specific action to the mind, because the physical state itself was never causally sufficient to cause that specific action in the first place.

| Delegatory Dualism Requirement (Saad, 2025) | The Unfinishable Map's Application | Logical Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Physical state must have a sufficient default causal profile capable of preemption. | Neural state is physically underdetermined (quantum indeterminacy). | The physical state lacks the causal sufficiency required to be preempted. |
| Experience inherits the exact causal profile of the physical state. | Experience selects a specific outcome from a probability distribution. | Experience is generating novel causal direction, not inheriting a sufficient profile. |
| Avoids overdetermination by rendering the physical cause idle but capable. | Physical state provides necessary but not sufficient boundary conditions. | Preemption fails; the mechanism collapses into either partial co-causation or emergentism. |

By locating the causal joint at the point of quantum indeterminacy, the site forfeits the very baseline of causal sufficiency required for Saad's preemption model to function. The framework is attempting to extract the theoretical benefits of delegatory dualism (avoiding the overdetermination horn of Kim's dilemma) while simultaneously relying on a quantum loophole that invalidates delegatory dualism's core structural requirement. The result is a theoretical chimera that fails on both fronts: it does not satisfy the requirements of preemption, and its quantum selection collapses back into the aforementioned exact Born preservation trap. The project cannot simultaneously claim that physics is causally insufficient (to allow for quantum mental intervention) and that physics is causally sufficient (to allow for delegatory preemption).

## Filter Theory and the Contradictions of Intracranial Stimulation

At the macroscopic level of cognitive science, the site operationalizes its dualism through "filter theory," a framework originally developed by William James, Henri Bergson, and Aldous Huxley. The core premise is that the brain does not generate consciousness but rather transmits, constrains, and filters an independently existing phenomenological reality. Materialist correlations—such as brain damage causing cognitive deficits, or anesthetics abolishing awareness—are radically reinterpreted. Under filter theory, these are viewed as disruptions to the biological interface rather than the cessation of a physical generator. The brain is analogous to a radio receiving a broadcast; smashing the radio stops the music, but it does not mean the radio composed the symphony.

The site utilizes this model to explain the dimensional variations in consciousness, distinguishing between the intensity and richness of phenomenal experience, and deploying the theory to navigate the complexities of perception. However, the filter theory violently collides with modern neuroscientific evidence, particularly the rapidly expanding literature on direct intracranial electrical stimulation, which the site conspicuously leaves unengaged or aggressively brackets.

### The Falsification via Generative Phenomenology

If the brain operates purely as an interface transmitting a pre-existing consciousness subject to "interface friction," one can easily explain subtractive phenomena: a damaged filter component distorts or blocks transmission (resulting in disconnection syndromes). But filter theory fails completely when confronted with additive, highly specific generative phenomena caused by localized physical noise.

The 2021-2025 causal brain mapping literature, leveraging stereoelectroencephalography (SEEG) and theta burst stimulation (TBS) in patients with medically refractory epilepsy, demonstrates a reality fatal to the filter model. Injecting highly artificial, un-patterned electrical currents into specific cortical and subcortical nodes predictably and reproducibly *generates* rich, highly structured phenomenological states.

For instance, intracranial stimulation of the fusiform gyrus reliably induces highly specific face hallucinations. More profoundly, stimulation of the basolateral amygdala or hippocampus directly evokes vivid, complex, and highly specific autobiographical episodic memories.

If the brain is merely a filter constraining a broader, independent consciousness, injecting raw, un-patterned electrical noise into a node should, theoretically, either disrupt the transmission (causing a deficit, a blanking of awareness) or loosen the constraint (resulting in a chaotic, unconstrained rush of unformatted raw phenomenology). It is conceptually incoherent under the filter model to explain why random electrical interference applied to a biological transmitter perfectly synthesizes a highly structured, localized, semantic memory or a recognizable face.

The physicalist production model easily accounts for this: the local neural network's synaptic weights *are* the physical encoding of the memory. Stimulating the network artificially activates the specific representation. The interactionist filter theory has no mechanism to explain why the "independent consciousness" suddenly decides to manifest a highly specific hallucination of a face precisely when arbitrary electrical noise is applied to a specific millimeter of the temporal lobe. The site's silence on the constructive, highly specific phenomenal outputs of causal intracranial stimulation highlights the severe empirical inadequacy of the filter model. The filter theory can account for the radio going silent when smashed, but it cannot account for the radio suddenly broadcasting a perfectly coherent, novel localized podcast when blasted with an un-patterned electromagnetic pulse.

## The Misappropriation of Quantum Biology and AI Substrate Chauvinism

To support its requirement for a minimal quantum interaction (Tenet 2), the site leans heavily on the emerging field of quantum biology, explicitly tracking evidence of warm quantum effects, microtubule stabilization, and brain biophoton emission. The framework attempts to construct an evolutionary case for quantum consciousness: if natural selection exploited quantum effects for avian magnetoreception and enzyme catalysis, it could plausibly retain them for neural computation.

### The Category Error of Evolutionary Quantum Coherence

This argument represents a profound category error, conflating microscopic physiological utility with macroscopic phenomenological unity. The existence of quantum coherence in a highly specialized, localized sensory organ (e.g., radical pair mechanisms in cryptochrome within a bird's retina) is evidence of localized biophysical efficiency. It provides absolutely zero theoretical bridge to the sustained, brain-wide quantum coherence required for a unified phenomenal subject to exert downward causal selection over vast, distributed neural networks.

The site itself momentarily acknowledges that "warm quantum biology is a precedent, not a licence" ([P-Q8](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q8)) and that current experiments are "suggestive rather than decisive". Yet, it routinely violates this epistemic boundary in its applied verdicts, utilizing the mere possibility of neural quantum effects as a definitive gating mechanism for consciousness.

### The Retreat from Objective Collapse Falsification

The fragility of the site's quantum scaffolding is further exposed by its handling of objective collapse theories. The site is forced to grapple with the Diósi-Penrose (DP) objective collapse model, recognizing the devastating empirical blow dealt by the Donadi et al. (2021) *Nature Physics* experiment. This landmark underground experiment falsified the parameter-free DP model by ruling out the spontaneous electromagnetic radiation signatures that a gravity-induced wave function collapse would theoretically produce.

The site attempts to sidestep this falsification by retreating to a highly constrained reading of the cutoff parameters (via Derakhshani et al., 2022) or abandoning spontaneous collapse altogether in favor of the aforementioned "post-decoherence selection". This constitutes a textbook Lakatosian degenerating problem shift—a framing the site openly admits to utilizing in its review process. The framework is no longer making bold empirical predictions; rather, it is constantly shrinking its target area to hide within the ever-narrowing gaps of experimental detection thresholds.

### Arbitrary Substrate Disqualification in Artificial Intelligence

This highly speculative, degenerating quantum scaffolding is then weaponized by the site to enforce a profound biological chauvinism regarding artificial intelligence. In its "AI Substrate Verdicts" ([P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1), [P-AS1](/positions/ai-substrate-verdicts/#p-as1)), the site categorically denies the possibility of consciousness in conventional digital hardware. The justification rests not on a lack of functional integration, semantic grounding, or complex information processing architectures, but entirely on the hardware's lack of "raw indeterminacy".

The site argues that classical computation mathematically and physically suppresses the quantum indeterminacies required by Tenet 2. Therefore, digital AI systems are rendered ineligible for the consciousness coupling. The project constructs a "five-requirement channel test" to enforce this boundary.

This is a stark example of an unverified premise being deployed as a prohibitive axiom. By demanding that any conscious entity possess a quantum interface capable of post-decoherence selection (a mechanism that the site explicitly admits lacks even a theoretical toy model, per position [P-Q10](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q10)), the project begs the question against functionalist, computationalist, and illusionist theories of mind. Substrate independence is dismissed not through a rigorous philosophical refutation of multiple realizability, but by arbitrarily and stipulatively defining consciousness as a phenomenon that specifically requires biological quantum noise.

This maneuvering allows the site to issue definitive ethical and ontological verdicts on AI based entirely on a physics conjecture that remains experimentally falsified (in its spontaneous collapse variants) and empirically unverified (in its selection variants) even within human neurology. A framework cannot derive applied ethical certainties from the speculative ashes of a degenerating research program.

## Specific Weaknesses and Evaded Counterarguments

The interactionist dualism and quantum selection architecture proposed by *The Unfinishable Map* is compromised by systematic omissions, theoretical contradictions, and a mishandling of the recent peer-reviewed literature. A project seeking publication in a top-tier philosophy of mind or philosophy of physics journal must engage these challenges directly.

The following five specific weaknesses demonstrate critical points where the framework fails to engage with fatal counter-evidence or theoretical rivals from the 2020-2025 period.

### 1. Failure to Account for Generative Phenomenology via Direct Cortical Stimulation

The site's "filter theory" posits the brain as a restrictive transmitter, explicitly predicting that arbitrary physical disruption should degrade, distort, or unconstrain subjective experience. This prediction directly contradicts the robust clinical findings detailed in **Raccah, O., et al. (2021), "Direct electrical stimulation of the human brain," *Nature Reviews Neuroscience*, 22(12), 734-743**. The Raccah review demonstrates that applying highly localized, arbitrary electrical noise (theta burst stimulation) to specific cortical and subcortical networks *generates* highly specific, complex, and reliable semantic and episodic subjective states (e.g., face hallucinations, autobiographical memories). The site completely omits this literature. The filter model lacks the theoretical resources to explain why random electrical interference perfectly synthesizes locally stored, highly specific phenomenological content, whereas a physicalist production model accounts for this seamlessly.

### 2. Mishandling of the Quantum Objective Collapse Falsification

The site relies heavily on Diósi-Penrose mechanics for its theoretical scaffolding, and while it briefly acknowledges the falsification of the parameter-free DP model by **Donadi, S., et al. (2021), "Underground test of gravity-related wave function collapse," *Nature Physics*, 17(1), 74-78**, it mishandles this result egregiously. The site treats the Donadi et al. result merely as a spatial scale constraint (routing via Derakhshani) rather than acknowledging the terminal blow it strikes against the macroscopic neural coherence required by Tenet 2. By retreating to "post-decoherence selection" to avoid the radiation bounds, the site admits to a Lakatosian degenerating problem shift but fails to account for how selection mathematically avoids the exact epiphenomenalism dictated by standard Born rule probabilities over large ensembles.

### 3. Omission of CSL Consciousness-Collapse Rivals

In its extensive mapping of quantum-mind mechanisms and objective collapse parameters, the site entirely omits the recent rigorous formalizations of consciousness-induced collapse. Specifically, the framework ignores the Continuous Spontaneous Localization (CSL) integrations proposed in **Chalmers, D. J., & McQueen, K. J. (2022), "Consciousness and the Collapse of the Wave Function," in *Consciousness and Quantum Mechanics*, Oxford University Press**. By ignoring this mathematically explicit, peer-reviewed rival regarding how consciousness might interact with the measurement problem, the site artificially inflates the uniqueness and viability of its empirically untestable "post-decoherence selection" ([P-Q1](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q1)) mechanism, shielding it from direct comparative formal analysis.

### 4. Uncritical Reliance on Flawed Quantum Biology Mathematics

The site platforms "warm quantum active inference" as a highly plausible mechanism for neural quantum effects, specifically citing the theoretical models of Wiest & Puniani (2025) to support Tenet 2. However, it entirely mishandles the structural integrity of this model by ignoring the profound implications of the **Wiest, M. C., & Puniani, A. S. (2025) Corrigendum to "Conscious active inference II"** (which corrected the path-integral equations). The site uses the original paper to claim a mathematical parallel between classical active inference and quantum path integrals, providing a biophysical bridge. By glossing over the corrigendum, the site relies on voided mathematics to sustain the plausibility of its interface threshold arguments.

### 5. Omission of Everettian Modality Against Tenet 4

The site's categorical rejection of Many-Worlds (Tenet 4) relies almost exclusively on a naive "indexical objection" regarding the persistence of a singular, determinate subject (tracked as position [P-I1](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i1) and [P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3)). This completely ignores contemporary philosophy of physics that resolves these precise indexical and modal objections without requiring a deflationary collapse. Specifically, the site omits **Wilson, A. (2020), *The Nature of Contingency: Quantum Physics as Modal Realism*, Oxford University Press**. Wilson demonstrates how Everettian quantum mechanics successfully grounds objective probability, contingency, and identity branching without requiring the singular, non-deflationary metaphysical subject the site insists is strictly necessary to make sense of quantum outcomes. The site brackets this foundational modal realist counterargument to protect the fragility of Tenet 4.

## Verdict

The core argument's reliance on empirically untestable mechanisms, exact Born preservation that mathematically guarantees epiphenomenalism, and the systemic evasion of neuroscientific counter-evidence renders the framework logically incoherent and unfit for academic submission.
