### PEAR Framing Verdict

**Direction of use: exposition-to-refute / boundary marker — legitimate. Not cited as support. No Tenet 2 conflict.**

Verbatim sentence as it stood:

> **Why psychokinesis research yields null results**: Decades of PEAR lab micro-PK research found effect sizes of ~0.0001–0.001—effectively zero.

The numbers are the ones PEAR reports, and the surrounding paragraph is calibrated correctly ("null PK is *consistent with* interface locality rather than positive evidence *for* it"). Tenet 2 rules out *empirically detectable* mind-matter interaction, and the article uses PEAR's null record as a boundary marker plus a falsification threshold (>~0.01) — the tenet-compliant direction.

The defect was narrower: **the sentence attributed to the source a verdict the source rejects.** Jahn & Dunne's own abstract describes "consciousness-related physical anomalies", "the scale and structural character of the **anomalous effects**", and argues for "a proactive role [for consciousness] in the establishment of objective reality". They are not reporting a null; they claim the effects are real and small. Writing "found effect sizes ... effectively zero" made PEAR's authors appear to concede the Map's reading.

Fixed by attributing the numbers and the gloss separately, which also strengthens the argument — the proponents' own best figures sit two orders of magnitude below the Map's stated falsification threshold. Inline `(Jahn & Dunne, 2005)` added; the reference had no inline anchor before.
