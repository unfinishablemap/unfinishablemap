## Executive Summary

The wing's best feature is a self-correcting calibration. ST's "Convergent Evidence" paragraph, rewritten on 10-26, counts four traditions as *one observation* and names the single test that would upgrade it: "upgradable only if a self-rewriting system met the same regress". That is [P-M2](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#p-m2) applied as it should be. PH's Bidirectional paragraph ("removes one defeater … does not by itself supply positive evidence") and RV's construct-relativity sentence, added 08-20, belong to the same discipline.

Inspection turned up four defects inside passages that look like strengths:

1. ST's Wittgenstein ladder quotation changes four first-person pronouns to "one" inside quotation marks, and it cites only the *Tractatus*.
2. RV's lead says research "consistently" finds a fifth-order ceiling. O'Grady et al. (2015) report high accuracy up to seventh order once the task is framed implicitly.
3. RV's falsifier section answers two of its own tests with "current evidence" that its research note lists as unstudied. Its phenomenology of depth is cited circularly with PH.
4. PH's contemplative paragraph treats cross-tradition convergence as evidence. That is the pattern the 10-26 sweep removed from ST but never carried to this page.

## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

PH runs the explanatory gap one level up, which most treatments do not. It grants the functionalist that recursive processing "*is* computationally distinctive", then presses the residual question (PH L54): "computational distinctiveness explains *that* self-referential processing differs from first-order processing, but not *why* the difference has a specific phenomenal character". It shows the hard problem recurring at every level of the tower and does not treat recursion as an escape from it. RV's "Strange loops face limits" paragraph (L90) has the right instinct but the wrong inference; see the Hardline Empiricist below and Priority 2.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

RV's Bidirectional and Minimal Quantum Interaction paragraphs (L120, L124) treat each recursive level as "an act of attention". Attention is where Stapp locates mind's entry into physics, which makes this the closest the wing comes to his process-1 picture. Both paragraphs are written in the right register: the ceiling is "consistent with a minimal interface: enough bandwidth for five levels of recursive attention, not enough for fifty". Neither claims the ceiling as evidence for the interface. One consequence goes unnoticed: if task format moves the measured ceiling by two levels (O'Grady et al. 2015; see Priority 2), a bandwidth reading has to make the bandwidth task-relative. That is a sharper and more testable version of the claim, so the empirical correction helps this persona.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

The pain example in PH's Transformation Thesis section (L50) does in four sentences what a paragraph of theory could not: "Attending to the fact that you are in pain changes the pain … This gap enables evaluation … comparison … and choice". ST's Nagel section (L60) states the perspectival regress compactly: "Every attempt to transcend perspective generates a new perspective, which is itself subject to the same limits". It also correctly records Nagel's own warning that the aspiration to objectivity can become "a form of blindness". The wing centres the first person at every turn.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

PH makes recursion temporal: "recursive thought requires duration, giving it a characteristic temporal thickness". It also makes Husserlian retention-protention self-involving ("*as its own*" is the self-awareness component). Both are process-friendly, and neither reifies a substance-self. ST's Hegel paragraph (L88) reads *Aufhebung* as an endless movement rather than a closure, which is congenial to Whitehead. **Constraint applied**: this praise does not extend to RV L116, where Dualism-coherence is used to *select* the structural hypothesis. That is the slippage the Hardline Empiricist flags below.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

PH's reflexive-distance section supplies a phenomenological foundation for ultimate authorship: "The difference between 'I want this' and 'I notice that I want this' is the phenomenological foundation of deliberation" (L64). The Moral Responsibility paragraph (L117) separates the agency and mine-ness layers. After the 10-27 repair it scopes the depersonalisation case honestly: the dissociation holds "only on the Gallagher-style reading (… active-inference accounts find felt control disturbed too)". Taking agency seriously while declining to overstate the pathology evidence is what Kane would want.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

ST's McGinn section is now source-faithful where it used to be a fabricated quotation (repaired 09-11). It says plainly that "McGinn's own conclusion is naturalist, not dualist". The Map borrows the closure structure and does not conscript the man. RV's three hypotheses (resource, structural, collapse) are kept "not mutually exclusive" (L68), and its 07-19 stability note forbids resolving them. That is the right humility about a limit whose nature is unknown.

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

This persona's verdict matters most here, because two of the three pages lean on tenets to frame an empirical limit.

**Praise.**

**Slippage found (calibration concerns, not praise).**
- **RV L116**: "This supports the structural hypothesis: the limit is not resource exhaustion but architectural necessity. Consciousness has a shape, and that shape includes shallow recursive depth." The paragraph opens in a hedged register ("may", "might"). It then closes by using Dualism-coherence to pick one of three hypotheses, against the article's own resource-hypothesis evidence (L52–54) and its 07-19 stability note ("Three hypotheses remain non-exclusive — do not 'resolve.'"). The 07-19 review's verdict, that "the tenet sections frame the recursion limit as *consistent with* the Map's view", fits L120 and L124 but not L116. The same paragraph says "just as physical systems face Gödelian constraints"; Gödel's theorems concern formal systems.
- **RV L90**: "If consciousness were merely computation, we might expect arbitrary recursive depth (given sufficient resources). The shallow ceiling suggests something beyond computational resource constraints." Finite working memory, which is the article's own resource hypothesis, predicts a shallow ceiling. The second sentence does not follow from the first.
- **RV falsifiers #1–#2 (L134, L136)**: "Current evidence: no tradition reports breaking through the fifth-to-seventh order ceiling" and "Current evidence: the fifth-order ceiling appears cross-culturally stable where measured." The article's own research note ([research/voids-recursion-void-2026-02-05.md](/research/voids-recursion-void-2026-02-05/) L308) lists exactly these as open gaps: "Do different cultures or contemplative traditions show different recursive depths? Has this been studied empirically?" One search of mine found no cross-cultural study of nested-intentionality depth. The control query (O'Grady et al. 2015) found its target at once. The defensible statement is "untested". The claim of stability "where measured" appears to have been made without any measurement behind it.
- **RV circular phenomenology (L76, L140)**: RV says PH "documents this characteristic" (content dissolution beyond fourth order). PH L91 restates the claim and cites nothing, while pointing back to RV for the empirical boundary. Both trace to the research note's own unsourced synthesis (L230), and the note itself records that "Detailed first-person reports of the recursion limit are sparse" (L312). RV's falsifier #4 nonetheless cites "Current phenomenological reports". This is self-citation presented as corroboration (Countermeasure 5; [P-M2](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#p-m2)'s self-citation rule).
- **PH L137**: "The convergence across traditions, however, constitutes evidence that self-awareness is structural rather than an artefact of particular cultural frameworks." Under [P-M2](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#p-m2), convergence counts as one observation until a distinguishing test rules out "one lineage" or "one curating framework". The sentence names no test. The Map's own `contemplative-practice-as-philosophical-evidence` lists transmission as the defeater ("If apparently independent traditions share a common source (reducing convergence to transmission)"). This is the same family as the 10-26 sweep (commit 99bc57c3b4), which reached ST, `mattering-void` and `self-maintained-cognitive-limits` but not PH.

## Defects Found on Inspection (strengths that turned out to be overclaims)

1. **ST L84, Wittgenstein's ladder.** The article quotes: "If the place one wants to reach could only be climbed up to by a ladder, one would give up trying to get there. For the place to which one really has to go is one that one must actually be at already." The only source in its research note is Wikipedia's "Wittgenstein's ladder" ([research/voids-self-transcendence-void-2026-03-18.md](/research/voids-self-transcendence-void-2026-03-18/) L76/L81). Wikipedia (oldid 1361427460, fetched today) prints: "I might say: if the place I want to reach could only be climbed up to by a ladder, I would give up trying to get there. For the place to which I really have to go is one that I must actually be at already." It attributes this to *Culture and Value*, revised edition, translated by Peter Winch, pp. 10–10e. So the Map's text is Wikipedia's English with "I might say:" dropped and four first-person pronouns changed to "one", all inside quotation marks. ST's References list only *Tractatus* 6.54 (1921), so a reader would take a 1930 notebook remark for the *Tractatus*. The impersonal "one" also erases the remark's first-person stance ("does not interest *me*"), which is the very feature the paragraph's reading needs. **Not checked**: the printed Winch wording. Wikipedia is an aggregator, and its footnote dates the revised edition 1991, whereas the von Wright/Nyman/Pichler revised edition is usually catalogued as 1998; this needs checking at the publisher. → Priority 1.
2. **RV lead (L36) and L42, "Research consistently finds … an asymptotic limit around fifth-order intentionality"; "provides precise measurements".** O'Grady, Kliesch, Smith & Scott-Phillips (2015), "The ease and extent of recursive mindreading, across implicit and explicit tasks", *Evolution and Human Behavior* 36(4), 313–322, DOI 10.1016/j.evolhumbehav.2015.01.004, report "high accuracy up to seven levels of embedding". They also report that presenting the task implicitly rather than explicitly improves performance, and they offer that as the explanation for the gap with earlier results. **Checked at abstract level only**, via repository listings (Lancaster eprints 72914; Durham repository); I did not read the full text. The finding undercuts "consistently" and "precise". It also bears on the three hypotheses: a ceiling that moves with task framing is evidence for the resource/task-demand reading over a constitutive floor at five. → Priority 2 (lead fix) and Priority 4 (fold the evidence in).
3. **RV L134/L136/L140 and L76**: answers to its own falsifiers that its research note says are unstudied, plus circular phenomenology (see Birch). → Priority 2.
4. **PH L137**: convergence counted as evidence; this is a carry-forward of the 10-26 sweep. PH L145 also cites "List's (2023)" inline with no References entry. The 07-13 publisher pass predates the 09-07 edit that added it. → Priority 3.

## Expansion Opportunities

### High Priority

#### The wing's two ways a recursion ends
- **Builds on**: RV "Collapse and return" (L80: "you are back at second-order, with no memory of the transition") and PH L99 ("the observer-observed distinction collapses into a more fundamental awareness that encompasses both — [witness-consciousness](/concepts/witness-consciousness/) without an object").
- **Would address**: the wing describes two different ways a recursion ends without noticing that they differ. Falling back to a lower level with no memory fits the *collapse* hypothesis. Settling into a ground that cannot be made an object fits the *structural* hypothesis. Which one happens may depend on training. If it does, contemplative practice is a natural tool for telling the hypotheses apart, and RV currently lacks one.
- **Estimated scope**: about 60–90 words in RV's Three Hypotheses section (headroom 853 under hard). Do it after Priority 4 reports, so the empirical framing is settled first.
