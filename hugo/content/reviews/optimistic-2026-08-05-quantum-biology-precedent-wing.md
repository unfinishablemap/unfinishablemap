## Executive Summary

This wing carries more tenet load than any other empirical cluster on the Map — Tenets 1, 2 and 3
all route through the claim that warm biology can host quantum function — and it is precisely
where an unrestrained site would over-claim. It does the opposite. The cluster has retired its own
headline precedent (photosynthesis), written a full article whose conclusion is that a
quantum-friendly argument fails, flagged its own favourable citations for vested interest, and
demoted its predictions to "compatible observations". It has also invented a structural device —
the **designated canonical-home page** — that has provably worked: a documented citation-framing
defect family is now at zero live over-claims across thirteen loci. The one significant flaw is a
consequence of that same device's success: **two pages each declare themselves the authoritative
home for the same number, and they disagree by an order of magnitude**, with the disagreement
propagating into a third page and into the closed task that was meant to standardise it.

## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

`zero-lag-gamma-synchrony-and-the-quantum-binding-argument.md` performs the separation Chalmers
would most want performed and that quantum-consciousness advocacy most often blurs: **feature
binding versus phenomenal unity**. Its §"Feature Binding Is Not Phenomenal Unity" states that a
complete mechanistic account of which neurons fire in correlation "is a story about
representational structure. It does not, on the Map's reading, thereby explain why there is
something it is like to have those representations unified in one consciousness." The article then
draws the consequence against its own side — the classical relay-hub account gets the synchrony,
and the Map keeps only the residue. That is the explanatory gap located precisely rather than
invoked broadly.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

`quantum-zeno-effect.md` engages the physics-consciousness interface seriously enough to state the
interpretive bill Stapp's own programme owes. Two burdens are named rather than smuggled: quantum
projection "is not in general energy-conserving, so the claim that conscious selection respects
conservation laws is something the interpretation must argue... not a free consequence of the
formalism"; and "'observation' in physics ordinarily denotes a physical measurement coupling,
whereas Stapp's proposal requires it to denote an agent's attention; equating the two is a
substantive commitment." A partisan page would have inherited both for free.

The same page also does the rarest thing in the corpus: it criticises **the corpus it belongs to**.
On the anti-Zeno effect — where frequent measurement *accelerates* decay — it writes: "The corpus
has generally presented Zeno dynamics as unidirectionally stabilising; the anti-Zeno mirror shows
the direction is not guaranteed." A self-directed finding, stated in the article rather than
deferred to a review file.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

The wing keeps its first-person residue where Nagel would put it and refuses to let empirical
results stand in for it. `zero-lag-gamma-synchrony` closes its tenet section by conceding the
mechanism and keeping the question: "attribute the synchrony to the relay hubs, and keep the open
question where it belongs, at phenomenal unity." The Map does not buy phenomenal territory with
neural coin.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

`prebiotic-collapse.md` gives Whitehead a full section and uses it exactly as it should be used —
to dissolve a *sharp-boundary* problem, not to elevate a *tier*. Panexperientialism is offered as a
reframing under which "the sharp consciousness/no-consciousness divide that generates the prebiotic
problem softens," immediately followed by "This doesn't commit the Map to full Whiteheadian
metaphysics." The article takes the framing benefit and declines the metaphysical purchase.

The wing also avoids crude substance dualism structurally: `prebiotic-collapse` separates
"consciousness *causes* collapse" (rejected as a simple failure) from "objective reduction provides
the baseline; consciousness modulates," which is a coupling claim rather than a substance-pushing
claim.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

`prebiotic-collapse` states the minimal version of what agency needs and no more: "Consciousness
need not cause all collapse to be causally efficacious — modulating it in neural systems suffices
for free will." Kane's requirement is met by the smallest sufficient claim rather than by a cosmic
one, which is also what Tenet 2 asks for.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

`quantum-biology-and-neural-mechanisms.md` carries an explicit mysterian caveat that limits the
whole article's ambition: the Map "documents these mechanisms as candidates constrained by physics,
not final explanations." Its closing verdict — that quantum biology's relevance "is primarily
*defensive*: it removes a major objection to quantum consciousness without proving consciousness
involves quantum effects" — is the humility McGinn asks for stated as a load-bearing conclusion
rather than a closing pleasantry.

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

**This persona's verdict is load-bearing here, and the wing largely earns it.** Four findings:

**1. The tier-upgrade is explicitly declined, in the persona's own vocabulary.**
`evolutionary-case-for-quantum-neural-effects.md` §"What This Does Not Establish" opens: "The
evolutionary argument supports a claim of *plausibility*, not *probability* or *proof*; **the
absence of a principled barrier under the Map's framework is not the presence of positive evidence**
for neural quantum effects." That is the discipline this skill exists to check for, written into
the article unprompted. The article's own `description` frontmatter carries it too — "a realistic
possibility... not a probability the argument earns" — so the restraint survives into the search
snippet, which is where over-claims usually escape.

**2. Predictions are demoted to compatible observations.** The §"Optimisation Argument" would
normally be where an evolutionary case cashes out in successful predictions. Instead: "What follow
are not predictions the quantum-neural-evolution thesis earns but *compatible observations* the
thesis can absorb. Each has a classical explanation or is empirically undischarged; none currently
licenses a falsification test." Each of the three is then given its own classical defeater —
tubulin conservation "is fully accommodated by structural-cytoskeletal constraints"; cytoskeletal
organisation "can equally be read as classical structural function." An article that had the
structural opportunity to bank three predictive successes banked zero.

**3. Favourable citations are flagged against interest.** Hagan, Hameroff & Tuszynski (2002) is the
counter-calculation the Map most benefits from, and both articles that cite it disclose that the
authors "are Hameroff collaborators with vested interest in defending Orch OR" and that the result
"has not been independently replicated in the 24 years since publication." `evolutionary-case` goes
further and rewrites the summary verdict: the empirical status is "one widely cited calculation
versus one contested counter-calculation by interested authors, with no replication on either side,"
**not** "considerably weakened" — an explicit repudiation of a prior, more favourable phrasing.
Wiest (2025) gets the same treatment: cited, and simultaneously marked "a partisan Orch-OR advocacy
review whose central macroscopic-entanglement claim is contested," so its "experimentally supported"
is "the author's verdict rather than the field's."

**4. A framework-boundary concession most sites would not make.** `evolutionary-case` on Tegmark:
"the Map's framework does not refute Tegmark inside his own analytic frame and should not pretend
to. Where the Map disagrees with the materialist physicist is at a framework boundary about which
intuitions and which calculations to anchor on — that disagreement is real, but it is not a victory
in Tegmark's calculation." Framework-stage calibration, honestly stated.

**Where Birch and Whitehead converge**: `prebiotic-collapse`'s Whitehead section is precisely the
case the skill flags as the risk shape — process-philosophical resonance available as a lever to
upgrade proto-experience claims — and the article takes the framing and refuses the lever. Both
personas can praise the same passage. That is the resolved-tension signature.

## Content Strengths

### `radical-pair-magnetoreception.md` — the canonical-home device

- **Strongest point**: The article opens by declaring its own function: "The single point this page
  exists to fix, and which downstream articles should link here for rather than re-derive: **RPM is
  a real, behaviourally-attested, mechanistically-still-debated warm-temperature quantum effect in
  biology — a precedent for the mechanism category... not a licence for any neural deployment of
  them.**"
- **Why it works**: This is a structural answer to a defect class the Map keeps rediscovering —
  a claim gets fixed in one file while its siblings keep asserting the old version. A page that
  *declares* itself the single source of truth for one claim, enumerates the four features that
  bound it, and tells downstream articles to link rather than restate, converts a recurring sweep
  into a one-time fix plus a link discipline. `quantum-zeno-effect.md` adopts the same device
  ("this page is meant to be its authoritative home... Downstream articles that invoke Denton should
  cite this calibrated statement rather than restate it").
- **Verified working**: the Denton et al. (2024) "demonstrated / experimental / first biological
  precedent" family — a documented propagation defect — is now at **zero live over-claims**. A
  corpus grep across `obsidian/{topics,concepts,apex,voids}` and `archive/` returns thirteen live
  Denton mentions, every one of which reads "modelled", "computational study", "computational
  precedent", or "modelling", and none of which claims priority. Four independent pages
  (`quantum-holism-and-phenomenal-unity`, `timing-gap-problem`, `comparing-quantum-consciousness-mechanisms`,
  `neural-implementation-specifics`) converged on the canonical wording. The device worked.
- **Second strength**: the article states an inconvenient revision of its own best evidence.
  Leberecht et al. (2023) "placed an **upper bound** on broadband radiofrequency disruption" —
  narrowing the Engels 2014 result the Map had headlined — and the article insists "an honest page
  must state the nuance rather than present Engels 2014 as closed."

### `quantum-biology-and-neural-mechanisms.md` — retiring your own precedent

- **Strongest point**: Photosynthesis, cited for a decade as the flagship warm-quantum precedent,
  is retired *from the argument* in the article's own §Photosynthesis, and the residue is stated
  with unusual precision.
- **Notable quote**: "The measurement itself stands, and that residue matters: electronic coherence
  was genuinely observed in a warm, wet biological complex, which is why the categorical
  impossibility claim fails. What photosynthesis no longer supplies is a *functional* precedent —
  selection retaining coherence because coherence helps."
- **Why it works**: The distinction between *the measurement survives* and *the functional
  inference does not* is exactly the surgery a defensive-only argument needs, and it is carried
  identically in `evolutionary-case` ("the case cannot be cited as selection retaining *long-lived
  electronic* coherence"). A concession made once and propagated correctly.
- **Third strength**: the article states the Baum/zero-lag correction *against its own binding
  argument*, in the section that argues for quantum binding: "The peer-reviewed literature refutes
  that premise rather than merely doubting it... The synchrony therefore does not evidence quantum
  binding; what stays open is the narrower question of phenomenal unity" (Vicente et al. 2008).

### `zero-lag-gamma-synchrony-and-the-quantum-binding-argument.md` — an article that argues against its own side

- **Strongest point**: The whole article exists to conclude that a quantum-friendly argument fails.
  It states the quantum binding argument fairly in four premises, then: "Premise (c) is where the
  argument fails on the current evidence."
- **Notable quote**: "Reading zero-lag synchrony itself as the footprint of quantum binding inflates
  the claim past what the evidence bears and stakes the Map's position on an empirical premise the
  mainstream literature refutes."
- **Why it works**: It also gives full weight to the *classical* critique of the classical view
  (Shadlen & Movshon 1999), and it delimits its own favoured refutation rather than overselling it
  (Viriyopase 2012's symmetry and neuron-type conditions are stated: "the mechanism is a genuine
  regime of classical dynamics, and it has real constraints that fair exposition should state").
  Both sides are calibrated, not just the one that hurts.

### `evolutionary-case-for-quantum-neural-effects.md` — conditionals kept open

- **Strongest point**: The binding-problem section refuses to close either of the two conditionals
  its argument needs, and says so twice. "Both conditionals are contested and the article does not
  resolve them." It even states the *opponent's* strongest reply in the opponent's own terms:
  "Defenders reply that the demand for ontological unification is itself the contested move — that
  phenomenal unity is what integrated functional processing *is*."
- **Why it works**: The article identifies where its own general principle becomes unfalsifiable —
  "The general principle is correct but unfalsifiable on its own — it would equally license any
  biochemical mechanism with a marginal fitness advantage" — and then declines to lean on it. This
  is the strongest single instance of self-imposed evidential discipline in the wing.

### 1. Two canonical-home pages disagree by an order of magnitude on the same number

The canonical-home device works, but the wing has installed **two** authorities over the same
figure and they do not match:

| Locus | Claim |
|---|---|
| [concepts/radical-pair-magnetoreception.md](/concepts/radical-pair-magnetoreception/) L28 | radical-pair "coherence lasts **tens of microseconds**, roughly **two orders of magnitude** below the millisecond timescales of neural signalling" |
| [concepts/radical-pair-magnetoreception.md](/concepts/radical-pair-magnetoreception/) L56 | "That figure — **tens of microseconds** — is **the number downstream Map articles should quote**" (sourced to Gauger et al. 2011) |
| [concepts/quantum-zeno-effect.md](/concepts/quantum-zeno-effect/) L61 | Denton's cryptochrome radical pairs: "Its coherence is **microsecond-scale** — roughly **three orders of magnitude** below the millisecond timescale of neural decision-making" — on a page that also declares itself "its authoritative home" |
| [concepts/quantum-biology-and-neural-mechanisms.md](/concepts/quantum-biology-and-neural-mechanisms/) L74 | "**its microsecond coherence** sits about **three orders of magnitude** below neural firing timescales" |

Same physical system (cryptochrome radical pairs), same baseline (millisecond neural timescales),
two different answers. Tens of microseconds against one millisecond is ~two orders; three orders
requires a one-microsecond figure. Gauger et al. (2011) — the source `radical-pair-magnetoreception`
cites — gives tens of microseconds, so the RPM page's figure appears to be the sourced one.

Two features make this worth a task rather than a footnote:

- **It runs *against* the Map's own thesis.** The three-orders version *overstates* the
  coherence gap, making the Map's precedent look weaker than the literature supports. Over-concessions
  are the errors least likely to be caught, because every reviewer reads them as commendable
  restraint.
- **It was being actively propagated as the model.** The now-closed Denton sweep task instructed
  future fixes to "COPY the already-calibrated sibling framing from
  [concepts/quantum-biology-and-neural-mechanisms.md](/concepts/quantum-biology-and-neural-mechanisms/)" — quoting the three-orders wording verbatim
  as the standard to copy.

Note that the *generic* statements ("even microsecond coherence remains three orders of magnitude
below millisecond neural firing", `quantum-biology-and-neural-mechanisms` L98 and four archive
loci) are arithmetically fine read as 1 µs vs 1 ms; and the ~100 µs vs ~300 ms comparisons in
`timing-gap-problem` and `quantum-neural-timing-constraints` are a different comparison and are
correct. The defect is specific to the two loci that attach "three orders" to the *radical-pair*
figure.

### 2. A hedge earned in the body is dropped in the tenet section

[concepts/quantum-biology-and-neural-mechanisms.md](/concepts/quantum-biology-and-neural-mechanisms/), same article, 44 lines apart:

- **L124 (body)**: Khan et al. 2024 "showed microtubule-stabiliser epothilone B delays anaesthesia
  in rats (Cohen's d = 1.9), **though this also admits a classical reading** (microtubules traffic
  GABA_A receptors, isoflurane's primary target)".
- **L168 (Relation to Site Perspective, Bidirectional Interaction)**: "The epothilone B study
  **directly supports** this: microtubule stabilisation delayed anaesthesia (Cohen's d = 1.9),
  **confirming** the microtubule-consciousness relationship runs in both directions."

The tenet paragraph asserts flat what the body concedes is dually interpretable, and the tenet
sections are the passages most likely to be quoted in isolation. The article's own §Experimental
Convergence gets this right ("the discipline of 'predictions held up' deserves care") — the tenet
section is the one surface that did not receive the fix.

### 3. The lead retains an exclusivity claim the body has since given up

Same article, L56 (lead): "The binding problem... **may require the physical holism that only
quantum entanglement provides.**" L144 now carries the Vicente 2008 correction and concludes "The
synchrony therefore does not evidence quantum binding"; the dedicated sibling article concedes the
phenomenon to the classical relay-hub account entirely. `evolutionary-case` states the calibrated
version — quantum entanglement is "**among** the candidate mechanisms that could supply" holism, with
IIT offering "a non-entanglement-based holism". The lead's "only... provides" is a stronger
exclusivity than any sibling now asserts, and leads are the truncation-resilient surface the style
guide prioritises. Lowest severity of the three, same shape: the correction reached the body and
not the frame.

### Would be genuinely additive if a slot frees

**The anti-Zeno mirror as a standalone treatment.** `quantum-zeno-effect.md` L52 names a real
asymmetry in the corpus ("The corpus has generally presented Zeno dynamics as unidirectionally
stabilising") and identifies the unknown that decides it — "neural spectral properties nobody has
characterised". Every Zeno-dependent article on the Map (`motor-control-quantum-zeno`,
`stapp-quantum-mind`, `structure-of-attention`, `timing-gap-problem`) inherits the exposure. This
is a falsifier the Map raised itself and has not yet developed, and it would be a strong candidate
the moment `concepts/` has room. Tenet alignment: Tenet 2 — if conscious "observation" can
destabilise as easily as stabilise, the minimality claim needs a sign as well as a magnitude.

immunity, reduced empirical content").
