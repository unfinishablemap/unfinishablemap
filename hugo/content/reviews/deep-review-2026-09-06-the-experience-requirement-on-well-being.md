Verified at the publisher of record (open-access copy of *Phil Studies* 176(7) via d-nb.info, `pdftotext` + grep), van der Deijl's sentence reads:

> "While **Lin (2016) recently argued that some of the force of the experience machine argument withstands these recent rebuttals**, the force of the objection in light of these criticisms appears much less strong **than it has long been seen**."

The article had rendered this as "Van der Deijl judges the objection's force 'much less strong' after these results (2019)" — dropping both the concessive clause and the comparative — and then restated it in the Map's own voice at §Allies as "the machine objection has lost most of its force".

**C2. Source/Map conflation — an illustration imported into van der Deijl's dilemma. FIXED.**
