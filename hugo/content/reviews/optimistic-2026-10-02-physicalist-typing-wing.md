## Executive Summary

The wing now shares one grammar: a reply *reaches* a type when it engages a premise that type holds, and reaching is not succeeding. That grammar is used on the routing page (L81) and visibly applied at phil-sci L100/L118, explanatory-gap L135 and the zombie page's response map. Three things are worth keeping exactly as they are. The Type-C collapse is reported as Chalmers's argument in both places it appears. The Map admits that its own psychophysical laws are as brute as Type-B's identity (parsimony L83, Leibniz L61). And four pages independently make Tenet 5 bind the Map's own abductions.

The seams are where one page still argues past a verdict another page has reached. Explanatory-gap runs the persistence reply against Levine's reading two paragraphs above today's fix, and Chalmers files Levine as Type-B. Phil-sci L84 needs strong Revelation, which the revelation-thesis page says the Map denies. The zombie page runs Chalmers's 2007 dilemma unnamed and misses its one feature that matters to Type-B. And the routing table omits the relocation objection, the Map's most-used anti-reductive move, whose page already runs Type-B's "identities need no explanation" exchange. That exchange is the one the open P2 is about to source from the IEP.

## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

The wing now reads his taxonomy faithfully. The three definitions are quoted, not paraphrased (routing L44). Type-Q is restored as his coinage (zombie L92), and type-F is the two-dimensional argument's exit (zombie L86, "or type-F monism is true"). He would most like the zombie page's Type-F cost: "The exit concedes the step the Map most needs, that structural physics does not entail experience" (L88). That is his own verdict on type-F, used by a dualist without overclaiming it. He would ask for one thing: where the zombie page runs his 2007 dilemma against phenomenal concepts, name it and say it assumes nothing about step 2, because that is why he built it (Priority 3).

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

Routing L77 places the Map in Chalmers's type-D by his own definition. It notes that his fourth collapse route ("appealing to consciousness itself, in the way that some theorists hold that quantum mechanics does") is the physics Tenet 2 posits, and it ends: "The point is dialectical and earns no evidence: nothing of the kind has been found." Stapp would value that the single place the wing allows a discriminating result is the quantum channel: "an outcome deviation conditioned on intention" (routing L120). The IBE page also states its disconfirmer in testable terms (L82).

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

Phil-sci L82 gets the acquaintance asymmetry right: "differently grounded from the electron's rather than better grounded: certain for the subject through immediate access, it lacks the public error-correction that makes the electron case robust." Explanatory-gap L147 separates content access from process access, so confabulation findings do not reach the datum. Nagel would also notice that the routing page records Chalmers reclassifying *him* (type-B or F, L64) instead of conscripting him. That is the right handling of a named sympathiser.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

The zombie page's ladder-kicking passage (L122–128) protects causal efficacy. ### The Mysterian (McGinn)
