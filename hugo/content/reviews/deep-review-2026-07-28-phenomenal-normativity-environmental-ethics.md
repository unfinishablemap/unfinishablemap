## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

1. **Fabricated verbatim attribution — Abhinavagupta *rasa* quotation.** The article presented *"nothing but aesthetic enjoyment, and this enjoyment consists exclusively in a kind of knowledge or consciousness"* as a direct quotation from Abhinavagupta, c. 1000 CE.

   Traced to source: the sentence occurs in the IGNOU / eGyanKosh teaching unit *Unit 4: Abhinavagupta's Philosophy of Rasa* (`egyankosh.ac.in/bitstream/123456789/35511/1/Unit-4.pdf`, p. 8), where it is **the unit author's own expository prose** summarising Abhinavagupta's doctrine. It is not marked as a quotation and is not a translation. The contrast is visible on the same page: the unit *does* mark its actual translated passages with quotation marks ("Objects in the world of poetry and drama have no place in the everyday world of our space and time…", and the *Abhinavabhāratī* passage introduced with "In a passage from Abhinavaguptabharati he says:"). The sentence the Map quoted sits in running exposition between them.

   No other publisher-of-record instance of the string exists; exact-phrase searches return only that unit, aggregator pages that do not contain it, and the Map's own pages.

   **Resolution**: de-quoted, not deleted — the *substance* is a faithful characterisation of Abhinavagupta. The passage now states the doctrine in the Map's own voice and grounds it in what the primary tradition actually holds: rasa as *alaukika* (outside the ordinary categories of true knowledge, false knowledge, doubt and probability) and constituted in the relishing awareness of the *sahṛdaya* rather than in the object observed.

   **Family resolution (§2.4.6)**: the same fabricated verbatim propagated to two further files, both corrected in this pass —
   - `topics/evaluative-qualia-phenomenal-normativity-across-traditions.md:68` — de-quoted, reframed as "as the commentarial tradition renders it", *alaukika* grounding added.
   - `research/evaluative-qualia-phenomenal-normativity-2026-03-27.md:119` — the propagation vector. Left in place as a source-extraction record but annotated inline: the string is the encyclopedia author's exposition, not Abhinavagupta's words; do not quote it as his.

2. **Source-fidelity defect — New York Declaration on Animal Consciousness (2024).** The article read: *"extended realistic possibility to all vertebrates and many invertebrates including cephalopods and possibly insects."* The Declaration's own text reads: *"at least a realistic possibility of conscious experience in all vertebrates (including reptiles, amphibians, and fishes) and many invertebrates (including, at minimum, cephalopod mollusks, decapod crustaceans, and insects)."* Two errors: "possibly insects" **reverses** the Declaration's "at minimum … insects" (a qualifier-strength inversion of the kind §2.5 targets), and decapod crustaceans were dropped entirely. **Resolution**: rewritten to the Declaration's own framing, with the minimum list restored. Verified at `sites.google.com/nyu.edu/nydeclaration/declaration`.

### Medium Issues Found

3. **Cambridge Declaration paraphrase understated and truncated.** The article said the 2012 Declaration *"affirmed consciousness in mammals and birds."* The Declaration's claim is that non-human animals, *"including all mammals and birds, and many other creatures, including octopuses,"* possess *"the neurological substrates that generate consciousness."* The Map's shorthand dropped the octopus/invertebrate clause — which supports the article's own sentientist argument — and stated consciousness where the Declaration stated substrates. **Resolution**: restored to the Declaration's wording. Verified at `fcmconference.org/img/CambridgeDeclarationOnConsciousness.pdf`.

4. **Banned construct at the *rasa* passage.** "The beauty is not in the landscape—it is in the evaluative consciousness of the perceiver" is the proscribed "not X. It is Y" pattern in em-dash form. Rewritten as a positive claim in the same edit.
