## Executive Summary

The three pages answer one question at three grains: *what must a subject be for experience to be good or bad for it?* ER answers it in welfare theory: which experience requirement, and which theory of welfare subjects. NS answers it in metaphysics: a momentary experiential locus, not a persisting self. OS answers it as an exegetical-normative test case: does granting that locus falsify Śāntideva's ownerless premise? Each page is careful, calibrated and candid about its costs. Yet **none of the three body-links either of the others** in one direction (ER→NS, ER→OS, NS→ER and OS→ER all count 0; NS and OS link each other). The one place where the three grains meet is unstated on the page the register cites for it. That place is the dreamless sleeper: ER adopts Lee's capacity version ("a dreamless sleeper remains one", L44), but a momentary locus cannot carry a capacity across an interval with no experience. The register already says so ([P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1): "for its interval verdicts alone [P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3) as the capacity's bearer … the momentary locus carries the derivation without either"), and ER does not. NS's own "least-developed part" (L114, diachronic harm) has a research note dated 2026-09-06 that recommends an article and was never consumed. It is the wing's one new-article opportunity.

## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

Chalmers would recognise his own examples used fairly. ER cites the zombie as van der Deijl's illustration of overextensive goods (L50). It meets his affectless Vulcans head-on: the Map "bites the bullet" (L82, via `sentientism#^vulcans`), denying them status rather than redefining the case away. He would most admire ER's L38 triage: "Lin 2021 is therefore a threat to [P-VS3](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs3) and an ally to [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1), and the Map should say so rather than fight him on sentientism." That sentence locates exactly where a phenomenal-value realist is exposed and where it is not, which is what taking the explanatory gap seriously in ethics looks like.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

Stapp finds little here directly, and that is correct for a value-theory wing. What he would endorse is ER's Bidirectional Interaction paragraph (L92). It makes the felt attitude a cause rather than a passenger ("because consciousness influences physical outcomes, the affect that carries the intuition is part of the pleading's causal story") and then declines to cash the tenet as proof: "Non-epiphenomenal downward causation remains a posit … and this paragraph inherits that debt." He could ask no more of a page that has no reason to discuss process 1.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

NS is Nagel's page. Its core move is to centre *for-me-ness*, the first-personal givenness of a token experience, and to show that the no-self traditions deny something else: a persisting owner. The heading "A Locus, Not a Career" (L82) states the point in four words. Nagel would also praise ER's reading of the experience machine (L64): "the friendships are counterfeits with one experiencer where two were wanted". That reading puts the deficit in *someone else's* missing point of view, which is the Nagelian thought that other minds are part of what a world contains. His own "Death" (1970) is the classic deprivation account, and it is the natural anchor for the expansion article proposed below.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

Whitehead would see his actual occasions in NS's momentary locus: subjects that exist only while their experience does, "serially replaced without being unreal" (L86, Strawson's thin subject), with a career that is a society of such moments rather than a substance beneath them. The page links [subjective-aim](/concepts/subjective-aim/) (L64), which carries the Whiteheadian vocabulary (7 hits for "actual occasion" there), but it never names Whitehead. He would praise that restraint. NS borrows "the structure and not the metaphysics" (L86) and calls its characterisation of the locus "a commitment, not a discovery" (L92). **Constraint honoured**: nothing in the wing uses process-philosophical resonance to raise any entity's evidential tier. The locus is asserted on a commitment, as both NS (L120) and OS (L129) say.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

Kane would value two things. First, ER treats agency as a real rival to the necessity direction (L78–L82): Kagan's robot mother and Mogensen's autonomy each get their strongest form before the reply. Second, NS refuses to let the value chain lean on the agency cluster. Prudence, promises and desert "require a subject that persists", and the Map holds one "on grounds drawn from agency and from Tenet 4's indexical objection, not from value" (L94). For Kane, a persisting self-forming agent is the bearer of diachronic goods. NS keeps that agent available without making the badness of pain depend on it.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

McGinn would praise the wing's open verdicts. NS's strongest rival, experience for no one, "is open, and the objection's strongest form stands or falls with it" (L51). NS's final calibration is "unrefuted and contested, not tested" (L122). OS keeps the scope question open on purpose ("The dialectical setting favours the narrow reading, and the Map has no textual argument for the wide one", L79). Its 10-24 deep review protects this with a stability note: do not harden the finding without a new textual argument. The [ownership void](/concepts/mine-ness/#the-ownership-void) is cited at the right moment in both NS (L92) and OS (L129): how a moment becomes someone's cannot be introspected.

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

The wing does not touch minimal-organism tiers. Lee's houseplant, Microsoft and ChatGPT (ER L72) are welfare-usage tests, not consciousness claims. What Birch praises is that all three pages use the *tenet-coherent, not evidence-elevating* pattern by name:

- ER L90: "The tenet removes a defeater without supplying the identity thesis, on which the global requirement still rests."
- NS L120: "Tenet 1 supplies the locus's irreducibility, not its existence: an abhidharma inventory of irreducible, ownerless suffering-moments satisfies the tenet too."
- OS L129: "Tenet 1 is neutral on bearers … The Map asserts a bearer on that commitment rather than on a finding."

ER L38 caps every verdict at the identity thesis, which the register grades as "argued phenomenologically with no independent empirical support". ER L94 refuses parsimony as evidence: "that a census confined to conscious subjects is leaner is no evidence that it is right." OS L127 makes Tenet 5 bind only the Map.

**Two calibration notes, both seams between pages rather than defects within one:**

1. **ER's sleeper verdict has an unstated bearer.** ER L44 adopts the capacity version ("a dreamless sleeper remains one"), as does CVC L120 ("the Map answers by capacity: a dreamless sleeper remains a welfare subject"). Neither page says that this verdict rests on [P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3). The register says so ([P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1), L53), and so does the apex: "the coma verdict is exactly as secure as [P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3), no more" (`moral-status-of-edge-cases` L93). The credence of the sleeper verdict therefore differs from the credence of the necessity direction, and ER, the welfare-subject page, is where a reader would look for that. CVC is at 3,519 / 3,500, already over its limit, so ER is the host (Priority 1).
2. **OS's narrow-reading verdict is scoped to the isolated value chain.** On the narrow reading, OS holds that the locus "leaves the premise true" (L41), because "a momentary locus has no future toward which to be partial" (L79). That is right for the locus alone. On the Map's full commitments ([P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3), [P-I1](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i1); parfit-reductionism's "*You* are something over and above any pattern", L147), there are persisting owners who could be favoured. OS L131 brackets this deliberately ("No Many Worlds is deliberately not drawn on"), and its 10-24 deep review drafted a 20-word clause naming the price but did not install it. Priority 4 installs that clause.

Neither note is a tier-upgrade. In both, a sound inference was stated at one level of commitment and read at another. The Process Philosopher and the Hardline Empiricist converge on NS, which indicates the tension was resolved honestly there.
