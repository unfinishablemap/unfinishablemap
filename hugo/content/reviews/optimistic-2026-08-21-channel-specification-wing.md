## Executive Summary

This is the strongest wing this reviewer has read for **evidential-status discipline under maximum temptation**. Five of the seven articles explicitly decline a tier-upgrade their own structure hands them for free, and they do it in five different ways — the taxonomy refuses to read strictness-of-fit-with-Tenet-2 as evidence for the strictest class; the channel-width axis refuses to read a *structurally forbidden rival cell* as support for the Map's own cell; the firewall refuses to read the parapsychological null record as confirmation and then volunteers that its own headline falsifier "runs little risk"; the apex hub derives a brand-new debt for the Map by thinking harder about its own commitment. The wing also coheres: five distinct vocabularies (phenomenological, Shannon-channel, cartographic, formal-interventionist, predictive) are cross-mapped onto each other explicitly rather than running as parallel duplicates.

Three concerns, all narrow, all verified on disk. The strongest is a **falsifiability over-claim** in `tenet-falsification-conditions`: it assigns Tenet 2 wholesale to "decisive falsification" and calls it "the Map's most testable tenet," where the roadmap it defers to for detail split that tenet into reading-relative profiles on 2026-05-23 and found the Map's *own default* reading "not Popper-refutable." The error runs **against** the Map's interest, which is exactly why three subsequent passes over the file did not catch it.

## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

Chalmers would find `born-preserving-causal-efficacy` the most honest treatment of a hard problem's residue he has read from a committed framework. The article's core move — separating **token counterfactual dependence**, **physical distributional change**, and **metaphysical authorship** into three claims a framework might be making, then observing that "only (2) has a conditional signature attached, so the Map's empirical exposure lives entirely in whether it asserts (2) at some specifiable grain" — is the kind of distinction-drawing that makes a debate tractable. He would especially approve of the article's refusal to let the trumping route pass as a solution: the route is rated "interface-compatible" but "explicitly **not** interface-discriminating," and the concession is stated flatly — "'consciousness is a cause' and 'consciousness is epiphenomenal' make no predictively distinct claims at any scale, and the distinction becomes *entirely metaphysical*. The Map adopts this knowingly."

He would also note, with approval, that the article credits his own joint work correctly and *against* the Map's convenience: the register's P-Q3 entry records that Chalmers and McQueen hold consciousness-collapse "need not lead to dualism" and is "compatible with materialist views," and that where they do go dualist it is *property* dualism rather than the substance reading the Map leans toward. A framework citing a friendly source while flagging that the source is friendlier to a rival reading is doing citation honestly.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

Stapp gets an unusually careful hearing. The wing declines his position, but it declines it *precisely*, and it separates the two things his framework does rather than treating them as one.

`channel-class-taxonomy` places him at Class 3 (measurement-basis-choice) and quotes him at the outcome level — the agent "chooses only the question" while "the answer is picked by 'Nature', in accordance with a specified statistical law" — then does something better than a rebuttal: it distinguishes the *basis layer* from the *outcome layer* and observes that "what satisfies Tenet 3 in this class is the outcome layer it shares with selection-only, rather than the basis layer stacked above it." `dualism-channel-width-axis` then relocates him onto a coordinate where his position stops looking anomalous: "thick mind, thick physical, narrow channel" — three values that the two-axis grid had to collapse into one cell "by judgement rather than definition."

Stapp would be pleased that the Zeno mechanism's fate is left genuinely open rather than dismissed. `coupling-modes` records that the corridor taxonomy files quantum Zeno under minimum-outside-the-corridor on ensemble grounds, then says plainly: "Whether Zeno-style timing control clears the ensemble bar is, as `stapp-quantum-mind` puts it, a live question — one this article does not settle in either direction." And Route 3 in the apex hub — Process-1 relocation — is logged as "an alternative the Map could in principle adopt — a human editorial decision, flagged honestly — not a fix the mechanism programme can quietly apply." That is a rival kept on the table with its own escape hatch labelled, not a straw man.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

Nagel would single out `interface-heterogeneity` for taking seriously the possibility that the *structure* of experience, not merely its content, varies across lineages. The article's sharpest passage resists the easy content-based deflation: octopus consciousness may lack unified binding "not because different content reaches the interface, but because the interface itself may operate in a distributed rather than centralised mode," and "content heterogeneity alone cannot explain why experience would be unified in one architecture and fragmented in another if the coupling mechanism is identical." That is the "what is it like" question asked at the level of organisation rather than of sensory modality.

He would also approve the *kind* versus *grade* distinction the article draws: interfaces may differ in kind (heterogeneity proper) or in grade (same mechanism, different bandwidth or fidelity), "two lineages could share a coupling mechanism yet differ markedly in efficacy," and "differences in cognitive *output* alone do not discriminate between kind and grade." Nagel's standing complaint about consciousness science is that it measures outputs and infers inner structure; this article says outright that the inference does not go through.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

Whitehead finds the wing congenial and — importantly — finds nothing in it that converts his congeniality into a promotion. `interface-heterogeneity`'s picture of coupling as something that varies by architecture, admits multiple operational modes, and may take different physical forms in different lineages is process-friendly through and through. So is `channel-class-taxonomy`'s framing of the interface as a *relation* specified by what crosses it rather than as a substance-to-substance contact.

The article that would please him most is `dualism-channel-width-axis`, because it makes his central structural point available as a coordinate: channel width is "a property of the *interface*," logically independent of how much ontology either pole carries, so "a thick mind can touch a thick world through a hair-thin channel." That is exactly the move that lets a framework avoid crude substance dualism without giving up interaction — and the article states it as a locating instrument, "cartographic, not adjudicative."

Per this review's standing constraint, none of the above is offered as support for any tier-upgrade, and the wing does not invite one. `interface-heterogeneity` places both heterogeneity and efficacy-variation at the **speculative integration** tier — the *lowest* rung — and adds that it "remains a conceptual framework rather than an empirical hypothesis." Process-philosophical resonance is high here and evidential status is low here, simultaneously and by the article's own statement. That is the honest pattern.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

Kane would recognise, and probably wince at, the wing's most uncomfortable derivation — and then respect that it was published. `born-preserving-causal-efficacy` follows the corridor commitment into the ethics and states what it finds: if selection is *value-sensitive*, tilting toward better outcomes for a subject, "exact preservation demands a compensating tilt toward worse outcomes for someone or somewhen else — a conservation law on outcome-luck the Map would have to own explicitly." And then the sharper rider: "Unless each agent's conditional policy is *separately* Born-preserving, the offset falls on other agents or other periods, so moral agency would carry hidden statistical externalities."

Kane's own project needs indeterminacy to leave room for ultimate responsibility. This wing tells him the room exists but that the corridor reading charges rent for it, in a currency (other people's luck) he had not budgeted. He would also note that the framework does not resolve this in its own favour: the compensation consequence is left standing as a debt "value-sensitive applied articles inherit rather than discharge."

The related restraint he would praise: `coupling-modes` refuses to let basis control do agency's work by proxy. It rehearses the natural defence — that basis selection at least puts "raise arm" into the option set — and then declines it: "The Map does not find that response sufficient, which is why its own commitment sits at the outcome level rather than here."

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

McGinn's characteristic point — that we may be constitutionally unable to see the solution — is met here by a framework that maps the shape of what it cannot deliver rather than claiming the shape is empty. `born-preserving-causal-efficacy` specifies six toy-model desiderata, identifies the one existing candidate skeleton (the agency-budget coupling result), and then itemises precisely which desiderata that skeleton fails: "It supplies none of (5): a coupling is silent on *why* a conscious state maps to a given outcome. It does not by itself establish (1), a coupling being a distribution rather than a causal structure. And because the published theorems are classical, (4) would have to be argued rather than inherited."

He would especially like that the article treats a *negative* result as progress: an impossibility proof that desiderata (1) and (2) cannot be jointly satisfied is listed second among the five things that would count as advancing the programme, and is described as "less a failure than a forced choice." A framework that has pre-committed to what would refute it, and ranked that refutation as valuable, is not hiding behind cognitive closure.

`tenet-falsification-conditions` earns his approval for the same reason at the meta level: it separates decisive, cumulative, and structurally-unfalsifiable commitments and refuses to pretend the third kind is the first — "A philosophical framework that combines metaphysical commitments, mechanism hypotheses, and epistemological positions *should* have varied falsification conditions. The alternative — claiming uniform testability — would be dishonest." (This principle is right; §Concern 1 below is that the article does not fully apply it to Tenet 2.)

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

**This persona's verdict is the load-bearing one for this wing, and it is strongly favourable with one specific exception.**

The tenet-as-evidence-upgrade is praise-worthily *not* done, repeatedly, in places where doing it would have cost nothing and gone unnoticed:

- **`channel-class-taxonomy`** builds a five-class hierarchy in which the Map's preferred class is, by construction, the strictest expression of Tenet 2 — and then refuses the obvious payoff: "The fact that selection-only's three constraints are the strictest expression of Tenet 2 is a structural observation about the menu, not a claim that selection-only is the right answer. Treating coherence-with-the-tenets as evidence-for-the-class would be a textbook case of `possibility-probability-slippage`." It also calls the hierarchy "a *menu, not a verdict*."

- **`dualism-channel-width-axis`** contains the single best piece of inferential hygiene in the wing. Having argued that the wide-channel/thin-pole region of logical space is mostly structurally forbidden, it explicitly blocks the inference a less careful author would have drawn: "that the wide-channel/thin-pole cell is mostly forbidden is *not* evidence for the Map's narrow channel: **ruling out one region of logical space does not raise the probability of the Map's region**; it only sharpens what the alternatives are." Eliminative arguments are the standard way frameworks smuggle in support, and this one is disarmed by name. Two further cautions sit beside it: naming MQI's axis "does not upgrade it to a finding," and the axis "does not certify that the floor of the axis is physically occupiable."

- **`parapsychology-firewall`** runs the slippage check in *both* directions, which is rarer than running it once. It names the folk-dualist inference (dualism true → psi should be strong) as "a version of `possibility-probability-slippage` run in reverse," refuses to bank the null psi record as support — "The pattern is equally consistent with there being no effect at all... The firewall is a constraint the interface must live within if it exists, not a footprint proving that it does" — and states that "the slippage discipline forbids the upgrade in both directions."

- **`coupling-modes`** disarms the parsimony-as-truth move in the Map's own foundations: MQI "is an empirical corridor rather than a likelihood ordering... It does not add that the smallest surviving mode is therefore the most likely true one; that inference is the parsimony-as-truth move Occam's Razor Has Limits disarms." The consequence is a genuine self-imposed cost — the Map cannot use its minimality tenet to pick a winner, so the discriminating work has to fall to Tenet 3. The wing pays that cost consistently in three separate articles.

- **`born-preserving-causal-efficacy`** pre-commits against its own most available comfort: "What would *not* count as progress: any move that answers the worry by re-describing efficacy and then declares the matter settled against a determined physicalist... treating its framework-internal re-description as an empirical demonstration would be calibration failure."

Two further items Birch would rate highly:

**The firewall discounts its own headline claim, unprompted.** The article's thesis is that the Map has a real falsifier here. Its penultimate section then says: "A falsifier is worth as much as the risk it runs, and this one runs little... The parapsychology channel is therefore close to evidentially inert in both directions... the firewall earns the Map less epistemic credit than the phrase 'we are falsifiable here' suggests, and the credit has to be earned somewhere the risk is live." Arguing for a claim and then marking down its epistemic value in the same article, with no reviewer forcing it, is the behaviour this persona exists to reward.

**The apex hub creates a new debt for the Map by reasoning more carefully.** The intervention analysis shows that preservation of the Born marginal over the *natural* distribution of conscious states is much weaker than preservation *under intervention* on that distribution, and that the gap collapses horn (c): "a cancellation that balances over natural intending will not survive an ensemble stacked with a single intention." The conclusion is a liability, stated as one: "Exact Born-preservation cannot be stated as a law over physical outcomes alone; it needs a companion law over the distribution of conscious reasons, and the Map has never written one — a debt the register should carry alongside P-Q10." Frameworks under pressure normally reason toward relief. This one reasoned toward an additional bill and then entered it in the ledger.

**The exception**: `tenet-falsification-conditions` states the discipline correctly and then, on Tenet 2 specifically, does not apply it — in the direction of claiming *more* falsifiability than the Map has. Detailed at Concern 1. Birch's verdict on the wing is otherwise: calibrated, and calibrated at the points where miscalibration would have been invisible.

**Process Philosopher / Hardline Empiricist convergence**: the two personas praise the same passages here — `interface-heterogeneity`'s speculative-integration tier placement, `dualism-channel-width-axis`'s cartographic posture, `channel-class-taxonomy`'s menu-not-verdict framing. Per this skill's design note, convergence indicates the tension has been resolved honestly rather than papered over. It has.

## Content Strengths

### The wing coheres — five vocabularies, explicitly cross-mapped

The single most valuable structural property here is that the five treatments of the interface are not redundant. Each has a distinct job:

| Article | Vocabulary | What it answers |
|---|---|---|
| `coupling-modes` | Phenomenological | What does the influence feel like it does? (basis / timing / probability) |
| `channel-class-taxonomy` | Shannon-channel | Which structural elements is mind licensed to touch? (five classes) |
| `dualism-channel-width-axis` | Cartographic | Where does a position sit relative to rivals? (a locating coordinate) |
| `born-preserving-causal-efficacy` | Formal / interventionist | Where could a selection leave a trace, if anywhere? (the trilemma) |
| `parapsychology-firewall` | Predictive | What must the Map predict *against*? (the outer boundary) |

And the cross-mappings are stated rather than left for the reader: `channel-class-taxonomy` Class 3 says "`coupling-modes`'s 'basis control' mode is this class viewed from the phenomenological side"; `dualism-channel-width-axis` identifies its width ordering with "the five classes the `channel-class-taxonomy` specifies in Shannon terms, where probability-bias and basis-choice are siblings rather than strictly ordered." Wings usually accrete near-duplicates. This one accreted a translation table.

### `born-preserving-causal-efficacy` — the trilemma and the intervention argument

- **Strongest point**: The (a)/(b)/(c) trilemma over the conditional signature, followed by the demonstration that horn (c) is unstable. The formal statement — the constraint binds the *marginal* `q(O|X) = Σ_C P(O|C,X)·P(C|X)` while saying "nothing about whether the conditionals on the right depart from it" — is what licenses token efficacy and aggregate invisibility to coexist without contradiction, and it localises where a signature would have to appear.
- **Notable passage**: "**State the level.** The trilemma is unstatable until the Map says over *which* ensemble Born-preservation is asserted... That commitment is weaker than it first looks, and deliberately so: it leaves the per-subject and per-intention conditionals formally unconstrained, which keeps horn (a) live rather than foreclosed. Asserting preservation at *every* conditional grain would choose horn (b) by stipulation."
- **Why it works**: It converts a slogan ("Born-preserving") into a claim with a specified scope, then shows that the *choice* of scope is itself a substantive commitment with consequences the framework must own. It also declines to overstate the surviving empirical room: intention-to-RNG micro-psychokinesis "*is* a conditional test at the coarsest grain, and it has returned preregistered nulls (Maier et al. 2018)," leaving "a narrower survival than the register has so far been asked to fund."

### `dualism-channel-width-axis` — two vacancies with opposite explanatory structures

- **Strongest point**: The distinction between *sociological* and *structural* emptiness. The parent taxonomy's Q4 vacancy is coherent-but-unfashionable ("Nothing forbids Q4; the discipline avoids it"); this axis's wide-channel/thin-pole vacancy is "mostly structural." The payoff is a prediction difference: "The Q4 vacancy is a place the field could go and won't; the wide/thin vacancy is mostly a place there is nothing to go to."
- **Why it works**: The article then refuses a clean verdict where the argument does not support one. It partitions its own conclusion — candidate-generation is analytically forbidden ("'Mind contributes novelty beyond the brain-encoded set' and 'mind carries no content beyond the introspective-and-brain horizon' are contradictories"), energy-injection is structural only on conservation-respecting readings, and one contingent sliver survives: a conservation-denying thin Cartesianism nobody has bothered to defend. "The forcing argument does not deliver a clean universal impossibility; it delivers a structured verdict with one exception."
- **Second strength**: It pre-empts its own sharpest objection. "Channel width is itself a family, not a scalar. 'Narrow' can mean *few bits per event*, *Born-statistics-preserving*, *energy-inert*, or *candidate-set-fixed*, and these need not coincide." That concession is what keeps the Stapp placement honest against `coupling-modes`, which files quantum Zeno as Born-rule-bending — the two articles use "narrow" in different senses, and one of them says so.

### `channel-class-taxonomy` — the empty class kept on purpose

- **Strongest point**: Class 5 (energy-injection) is retained despite being unoccupied: "No major contemporary dualist theory occupies this class. It is preserved here for definitional contrast: the position the conservation-laws argument refutes, and the foil against which the previous four classes earn their explanatory point."
- **Why it works**: It disarms the standard objection by *locating* it rather than arguing with it. Carroll's "what particles is that soul made of?" is named as "correctly aimed" — at a class the Map explicitly forbids itself. A reader can see in one table row both what the objection hits and that the Map is not standing there.
- **Also notable**: The per-trial information-rate row is careful in a way that is easy to get wrong — selection-only's rate "is not fixed by Born preservation, which constrains the unconditioned marginal only and leaves the mind-conditioned throughput open up to the *log₂(N)* ceiling," while probability-bias's `ε²/(2 ln 2)` figure is flagged as "a divergence approximation for this explicitly declared bias, not a consequence of Born preservation."

### `parapsychology-firewall` — the counter-intuitive prediction, owned in both directions

- **Strongest point**: The thesis itself. "Spectacular, reliable, ensemble-detectable psi would disconfirm the Map's framework rather than confirm it." A dualist framework predicting *against* the phenomena folk dualism expects is a genuinely discriminating commitment, and the article derives it from three named constraints rather than asserting it.
- **Notable passage**: "The firewall is not a hedge bolted on to protect the framework from awkward data" — and then, rather than leaving that as a claim, the demarcation is grounded structurally: the line is drawn "not as a reputational defence ('we are not those people') but as a structural consequence ('the interface we posit cannot produce those effects')."
- **Why it works**: It states the converse exposure too. "A demonstration that consciousness can influence physical outcomes *only* in ways that leave no aggregate trace at all... might push the Map toward `ensemble-level-epiphenomenalism`... The Map owns both edges."

### `coupling-modes` — declining its own synthesis

- **Strongest point**: The article constructs an attractive three-mode synthesis — "basis control sets the alternatives; timing control stabilises preferred patterns; probability control, if present at all, operates at the margin" — and then declines to adopt it, on principled grounds: "A coupling that selects only the question has answered the efficacy worry by becoming a context-setting dualism: coherent, but a different thesis from the one the tenets fix, and it fails by **thesis-drift rather than by evidence**."
- **Why it works**: Naming the failure mode as thesis-drift rather than empirical defeat is the precise diagnosis, and it recurs identically in `born-preserving-causal-efficacy`'s Route 3 ("It does not die empirically; it dies by no longer being the Map's claim"). Two articles independently reaching the same verdict on the same rival, in the same vocabulary, is what a coherent wing looks like.

### `tenet-falsification-conditions` — the asymmetry principle

- **Strongest point**: "Why Honest Asymmetry Matters" is correct and well-argued, and the reciprocity clause is the right instinct: "If the Map cannot specify what would change its mind, it has no right to criticise physicalism for the same failure."
- **Also good**: The Kleiner (2021) falsification problem is presented without special pleading — "This is not a special embarrassment for dualism" — and supported with the 2025 Cogitate adversarial collaboration, correctly attributed to the Consortium rather than to an individual first author.

### Concern 1 (primary) — `tenet-falsification-conditions` over-claims Tenet 2's falsifiability

The article assigns Minimal Quantum Interaction to the **"Decisive falsification"** type and calls it "**the Map's most testable tenet**", unqualified.

`falsification-roadmap-for-the-interface-model` — the article `tenet-falsification-conditions` explicitly defers to in its own lead ("For the detailed experimental programme, see the falsification roadmap") — split Tenet 2 into reading-relative profiles in commit `962c27d06e` on **2026-05-23**, and opens that section with: "the readings have materially different falsifiability profiles and **should not be flattened into a single condition**." Its findings include:

- *Strict corridor reading* (the Map's own default, register position P-Q2): "**Not Popper-refutable** by Born-rule tests, because the strict corridor predicts no Born-statistical signature by construction. The corridor is vulnerable instead to *motivation-loss / theoretical supersession*."
- *Trumping / preemption reading*: "Empirically indistinguishable from epiphenomenalism on Born-rule data alone."
- The tractability claim carries an italicised qualifier the concept page lacks: "This is the Map's most empirically tractable tenet *in its mechanism-committed readings*."

So the concept page's characterisation is right for the mechanism-committed readings (Stapp/Zeno, Orch-OR, Chalmers–McQueen, objective-collapse modulation) and wrong for the reading the Map actually prefers. Corroborating measurements on `obsidian/concepts/tenet-falsification-conditions.md`: zero occurrences of `corridor`, `post-decoherence`, `positions/quantum-interface`, or `brain-internal-born-rule-testing`. The file was created 2026-04-15 — before both the roadmap split (2026-05-23) and the register's seeding (2026-06-04) — and has been touched twice since (`last_deep_review` 2026-07-17, `ai_modified` 2026-08-03) without the profile propagating.

**Why this matters and why it survived**: the error inflates the Map's apparent falsifiability. It is an over-concession running *against* the framework's interest, which is the class of defect reviews ratify rather than catch — a reviewer scanning for over-claiming finds a claim of vulnerability and reads it as virtue. The wing's own discipline is the standard being missed: `parapsychology-firewall` does exactly the right thing four sections later by discounting a falsifier that "runs little risk."

**Note the scope limit**: the roadmap's *"Decisive (for all readings)"* row — coherence physically impossible in all biological neural systems — is consistent between the two articles, and this review does **not** flag it. The defect is confined to the unqualified type-assignment and the unqualified "most testable tenet" phrase.

### Concern 2 — `interface-heterogeneity` over-reports a sibling and omits the wing's anti-slippage marker

Two small, related items in the same article:

1. **Internal citation-framing.** The article states: "The `universal-coupling-response` **establishes** that consciousness couples wherever adequate architecture exists." The source article says it is what "The Unfinishable Map **adopts** as its most coherent response" and calls it "the most principled answer available" — a coherence ranking, not an established result. The verb should be re-framed to match the source, not deleted.

2. **Marker gap.** Its "Occam's Razor Has Limits" paragraph runs the defeater-removal move — parsimony favours a single mechanism, Tenet 5 says parsimony is unreliable here, therefore heterogeneity is taken seriously — without the explicit "this removes a defeater but supplies no evidence" marker that its five wing siblings all carry. Measured: `possibility-probability-slippage` and `evidential-status-discipline` each appear 0 times in this file, against 3 and 4 respectively in `channel-class-taxonomy` and `dualism-channel-width-axis`, and 4 in `parapsychology-firewall`.

**Mitigating, and the reason this is graded a marker gap rather than a live tier error**: the article *does* place both heterogeneity and efficacy-variation at the **speculative integration** tier — the lowest rung — and states that heterogeneity "remains a conceptual framework rather than an empirical hypothesis." No upgrade has actually occurred. What is missing is the guard rail, in the one paragraph structured to invite one.
