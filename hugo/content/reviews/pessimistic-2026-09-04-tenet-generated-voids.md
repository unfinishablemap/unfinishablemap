## Critiques by Philosopher

### The Eliminative Materialist (Churchland)

"Five commitments, five voids, one apiece. That is not a discovery about consciousness; it is a partition of your own vocabulary. You chose the five headings, then found exactly one unanswerable question under each, and now the neatness is offered as evidence that 'the voids are not accidental but structural.' The structure is in the filing system.

And you no longer need me to press this. Your own [tenets/background-commitments.md](/tenets/background-commitments/) says: *'the three are not mutually independent: they share a common posit. "Five stated commitments" is precise; "five independent commitments" is not.'* If Tenets 1, 3 and 4 all rest on one unstated posit about a numerically determinate persisting subject, then the Nature Void, the Detection Void and the Selection Void are not three characteristic voids of three commitments. They are one posit's shadow, cast three times. The article's headline structure counts it thrice."

**This lands.** See Critical Issue 3.

### The Hard-Nosed Physicalist (Dennett)

"'Productive failure as data.' 'Where the Map cannot go is itself cartographic information.' You have built a machine for converting your debts into assets. And look at the second bullet under *Why This Matters* — you call it **'Inoculation against criticism'** and treat it as a benefit: *'Critics cannot accuse the Map of hiding its gaps when the gaps are documented.'*

That is the immunising move stated in its own voice. Your Epistemic Privilege Void, four hundred words earlier, worries about exactly this — whether the transparency is 'genuine intellectual honesty or sophisticated self-protection' — and then the article turns round and lists the self-protection as a virtue with an approving heading. You cannot both worry about the manoeuvre and bank it."

**This lands, at medium severity.** See Issue 6. The article's own `taxonomy-of-voids` sibling states the prohibition plainly (L181: "treating the Map's costs as confirmations would invert the ledger"); this article predates that sentence and does not carry its discipline.

- **File**: `obsidian/voids/tenet-generated-voids.md`
- **Location**: lines 71 and 73 (Detection Void)
- **Problem**: The article states, unqualified, that "the tenet requires effects simultaneously real and **undetectable**" and that the Map's consciousness is "causally efficacious but **empirically silent**." Both are the pre-calibration form. The corpus now scopes the silence two ways: (a) to the *unconditioned aggregate* register — `tenets.md` Tenet 2 reads "under any *unconditioned aggregate* test the mechanism is *empirically indistinguishable from chance*… Preservation binds only that unconditioned marginal, however: a deviation *conditioned* on intention, task or subject would test the corridor itself ([P-Q3](/positions/quantum-interface/#mechanism-debt))"; and (b) to the *corridor reading specifically*, not to the tenet — `apex/judging-the-map-as-science` L143: "The tenet commits the Map to outcome-selection influence rather than to its detectability: the effects are in principle detectable on minimum-outside-corridor readings." [P-Q3](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q3)'s empirical-discriminability band moved `none-by-construction` → `indirect` on 2026-08-24 on exactly this argument.
- **Provenance**: the phrasing traces verbatim to the January seed note ([research/voids-tenet-generated-voids-2026-01-25.md](/research/voids-tenet-generated-voids-2026-01-25/) L166) and has survived unrecalibrated through nine deep reviews. `tenets.md` was itself repaired for the same defect in commit `cb47816154` ("the canonical page is now its own stale dependent") — that commit touched `tenets.md` only. This article is the stranded dependent of that sweep.
- **Severity**: High
- **Recommendation**: `refine-draft`. Scope the Detection Void to the *unconditioned aggregate* register and attribute the silence to the endorsed corridor reading rather than to Tenet 3 as such. The void survives the fix — the conditioned signature is *unmeasured*, [P-Q10](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q10)'s toy-model debt is unpaid, and Maier et al. Cite `[[positions/quantum-interface#^mechanism-debt|P-Q3]]`, which is a stable deep-link address the register maintains for this purpose.

## Counterarguments to Address

### The Selection Void's presupposition (Issue 4 — Medium)

- **Current content says**: the Map's MWI rejection rests on "the stronger claim that indexicality is genuinely one-branch-only and that MWI's needed cross-branch personal-identity relation (for Born-rule recovery via decision theory) is unavailable."
- **A critic would argue** (Deutsch and Nagarjuna converging): that claim presupposes a non-deflationary "I", which is itself a posit.
- **Suggested response**: the Map has already made this concession upstream — `tenets.md` L121, *"What the indexical objection presupposes (honestly noted)"*, endorses the non-deflationary "I" on independent grounds (the agency cluster's need for a persisting subject) and names the Madhyamaka deflationary route as "a genuine bedrock disagreement, not an in-framework defect the Map can refute," conceding under Tenet 5 that "the indexical question is meaningful" is "a posited claim, not a neutral datum." An article about what the tenets cannot establish from within is the *natural* home for that concession, and it is missing. Add two sentences to the Selection Void; link `[[buddhism-and-dualism]]`. Do **not** re-open the 2026-04-28 reframe, which is correct.

### The Completeness Void is now partly answered by the Map's own work (Issue 5 — Medium)

- **Current content says**: "Are five tenets enough? Too many? The Map provides no meta-criterion for what foundational commitments are necessary… **The framework cannot specify its own boundaries.**"
- **A critic would argue**: nothing — but the claim is now *false in part*, which is worse than contested. [tenets/background-commitments.md](/tenets/background-commitments/) enumerates three *unstated* posits sitting beneath the five stated tenets, and `tenets.md` carries a Tenet-Dependency Matrix mapping clusters to the commitments they inherit. That is the framework specifying a real part of its own boundary, after the article declared it could not.
- **Suggested response**: downgrade "cannot specify its own boundaries" to something like *cannot certify the specification complete*. The void survives in its honest form — the matrix and the three-posit list are themselves framework-internal products with no external check, and nothing guarantees a fourth posit is not lurking — but the flat "cannot" is now contradicted by a page in the same section.

### Immunisation framed as a benefit (Issue 6 — Medium)

- **Current content says**: under *Why This Matters*, the bolded heading **"Inoculation against criticism"** and the claim "Critics cannot accuse the Map of hiding its gaps when the gaps are documented."
- **A critic would argue**: this is the unfalsifiability manoeuvre named approvingly in the Map's own voice, and "critics cannot accuse" is a stronger claim than the article can support — a critic can and should accuse a framework of documenting its gaps *in order to* neutralise them.
- **Suggested response**: the article's Epistemic Privilege Void already holds this question open ("genuine intellectual honesty or sophisticated self-protection"), so the fix is consistency, not new philosophy: rename the heading to something that does not advertise the immunising function (*"Gaps documented rather than defended"*), soften "cannot accuse" to "cannot fairly accuse… of *hiding* the gaps," and cross-reference the Epistemic Privilege Void so the two passages read as one position. `taxonomy-of-voids` L181 is the model: costs are "the price the framework pays, not evidence for the tenets."
