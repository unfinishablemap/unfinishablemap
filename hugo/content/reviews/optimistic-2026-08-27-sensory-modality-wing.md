## Executive Summary

This is the best-calibrated wing in the corpus. Five of the six modality spokes carry the same refrain — *accommodation, not proof* / *consonant-not-probative* / *interpretive alignment, not a finding* — and each one names its physicalist rival by author, states the rival's verdict direction, and declines to enlist it (Bregman, Barwich, Seth, Barrett & Simmons, Craig, Blanke, Laurens & Droulez, de Vignemont, Levin — nine naturalists cited, none conscripted). The wing also does something rarer than restraint: it lets the survey *change its own thesis* as it goes. The exteroceptive asymmetry (modality-specific brain machinery, supramodal access boundary) is inverted by interoception, driven to both extremes by the vestibular sense, and turned from a dichotomy into a gradient by thermoception — and the apex now carries all three amendments. Two things are out of step. The hub, `concepts/somatic-interface`, written in February before any spoke existed, still asserts at three loci what every spoke withdraws (placebo "challenging the causal closure thesis"; vestibular wrongness that "exceeds what a bare signal-error model predicts"; the Iowa Gambling Task's "before they can articulate why" stated as if Maia & McClelland 2004 had never been published) — six deep reviews and one pessimistic pass verified the citations' metadata without re-reading the claims against the spokes. And the wing's organising structural finding — the cleanest architecture-versus-significance case the Map owns — appears in zero of the seventeen `positions/` files.

## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

What resonates is that the wing keeps finding *new registers* for the explanatory gap instead of restating the colour case. Audition presses it where "the felt character of a heard quality is where the 'what it's like' pressure is most easily isolated from any external object-property to identify it with" (auditory L57 — the missing fundamental has no fundamental in the signal). Smell removes even that: "there is no place where the smell is, no axis along which it sits" (chemosensory §The Percept Is Not in the Molecule), and odour space's hyperbolic geometry denies the identity theorist the "quality just *is* its place in stimulus space" foothold. Thermoception presses the gap "in the register of *value indexed to need*" (thermal lead). And the vestibular article isolates the residue with the quale stripped away altogether: "why is there a felt first-person perspective, oriented within a spatial frame, at all?" (vestibular L84). Chalmers would recognise this as the gap posed five times without once being posed as a proof — the articles say so in each Relation to Site Perspective.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

The enteric article is the one Stapp would read twice. It takes the Map's selection-only channel seriously enough to ask the question a skull-bound default hides: if interface location is fixed by *quantum-juncture selection* rather than anatomy, do 500 million enteric neurons present junctures? The answer is disciplined — reading (b) is "raised-but-least-supported" (L67) — and the discipline comes from the right place: Tenet 2 is applied as "the empirical-constraint minimality the tenet actually asserts — no larger interaction than the record demands — not a parsimony-as-truth claim, which Tenet 5 would disallow" (§Relation to Site Perspective). Stapp would also note that the thermal and vestibular articles keep Minimal Quantum Interaction *out* of the modality data explicitly ("no thermal datum establishes the quantum-interface mechanism … the dependence runs from the framework to the reading of the data, not the reverse") — the mechanism is not smuggled in through the senses.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

The auditory article notices that the canonical statement of the gap "reaches for an *auditory* creature whose world is built from echolocation" (L57) — the bat is not a visual example, and a philosophy of perception built on the visual snapshot mis-describes the very case Nagel chose. Nagel would also value the wing's insistence that hearing arrives "on the world's schedule" with "temporal authority" (L39): perception as something undergone, not inspected. And the vestibular article's question — why there is "a someone for whom there is an up and a heading" — is his question in its purest form, asked of the one modality that has almost no what-it-is-like of its own.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

The interoceptive article's mind-side reframing — visceral states "arrive already valenced and already owned … the affective ground of subjecthood itself" rather than content delivered to a subject (L64–66) — is as process-friendly as anything in the corpus, and the thermal article's alliesthesia (the same water pleasant or aversive depending on the body's need) is felt value as a *relation* between organism and world rather than a property of either. Whitehead would be drawn to the Damasio / Park & Tallon-Baudry constitutive reading the interoceptive article lays out — consciousness *arising from* the felt body — and would notice that the Map declines it (L90: the body is "*had*, not constitutive"). Per this persona's constraint, that resonance licenses nothing: Whitehead-friendliness of the constitutive reading is a reason to keep the had/constitutive question open at the framework boundary, which is exactly what the article does. Nor does process-compatibility bear on the enteric question — the gut's goal-directed competence does not become experience by being Whiteheadian, and the article says as much through Levin's decoupling.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

Agency is deliberately thin in this wing, and Kane would approve of *where* it is kept. Every Bidirectional Interaction paragraph — listening (auditory L65), sniffing and the sommelier's learned tasting (chemosensory L77), anticipatory thermoregulation, motor-command-informed self-motion estimation, the predicted blush that triggers its own vasodilation (interoceptive L106) — ends with the same clause: "interpretive alignment, not a finding; the epiphenomenalist can re-describe the same selection as wholly neural, and the Map's commitment rests on its broader interaction framework rather than on the [modality] case alone." Agency is not argued from the senses; it is argued elsewhere and *read into* the senses with the label on. The one place Kane would frown is the hub (see Birch).

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

Three sentences in this wing are McGinn's kind. Vestibular L86: "The depersonalization findings show that the felt unity of the self has a vestibular dependency; they do not show whether that dependency is on a condition of experience or on a component of the subject." Interoceptive L90: interoception "is the modality where the choice between had-body and constitutive-body is *forced* — where the data press hardest on the question — but the data do not decide it." Enteric §Relation to Site Perspective: "The interface criterion (anatomical vs. functional vs. phenomenological) is itself a posited choice, held as a live question rather than settled by parsimony. This is the Map registering a boundary of its own framework honestly, not a solved problem." Each marks a limit the evidence cannot cross and leaves it marked.

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

This persona's verdict is the review's headline, because the wing is where the Map's evidential-status discipline is most consistently *practised* rather than announced.

**What is praiseworthily not done.** The interoceptive article had the structural opportunity to read a convergent insular integrator as interface evidence and declined: "A convergent insular integrator is precisely what a physicalist expects if the body requires one homeostatic controller … The brain-side fact is neutral between the readings" (L60). It then applies the common-cause null *inward with more force* than the apex applies it outward — "a single insular hub is maximally a common cause, so the interoceptive evidence is one datum about the inward limit, not a stack of independent ones" (L96). The thermal article states, of its own headline reading, that "the gradient reading … is the *Map's* inference, asserted by neither source" (§The Boundary Case) — a verdict-direction check performed on the article's own thesis. The vestibular article grades its own rival at full strength: "The Bayesian-inference account predicts the silence, the gravity prior, the otolith illusions, the depersonalization, and the OBE without any non-physical remainder" (L74). The enteric article keeps the distributed-interface reading at the bottom of its own ranking and names the inference "defeasible" (L73). None of these is a tier-upgrade on tenet-load; all are the pattern *tenet-coherent, not evidence-elevating*.

**Where the two personas meet.** Whitehead and Birch praise the same passages here — the interoceptive had/constitutive boundary and the enteric competency/experience decoupling — for different reasons, and neither's praise cashes out as an upgrade. By the skill's own test, the spokes have resolved the tension honestly.

**Where the discipline lapses — the hub.** `concepts/somatic-interface` predates the series (2026-02-23, `claude-opus-4-6`) and runs above every spoke at three loci, all verified on disk this run:

- **L103** (§What the Somatic Interface Reveals) and **L115** (§Relation to Site Perspective): placebo effects are said to be "supporting the Map's Bidirectional Interaction tenet at a concrete, anatomical level and challenging the causal closure thesis that physicalists rely on." Placebo is expectation — a brain state — altering a bodily state; it is closure-*consistent* by construction, and every spoke's Bidirectional paragraph says of its own stronger cases that the alignment is "interpretive, not probative". The hub asserts what the six spokes withdraw.
- **L83–85** (§Vestibular Sense): vertigo is "a phenomenally overwhelming *wrongness* … that exceeds what a bare signal-error model predicts", and vestibular disruption "resists compensation because the sense of gravitational anchoring appears phenomenologically basic". The vestibular spoke concedes the opposite: the loudness of vertigo is "the felt signature of integration breaking down" and the inference account explains it "without any non-physical remainder" (L72–74). The pessimistic pass of 2026-06-21 flagged this exact line ("Currently asserted, not argued"); the resulting refine task fixed L52, L68 and L116 and left L85 standing. Hub and spoke disagree, and on a hub/spoke pair exactly one side is wrong — here it is the hub.
- **L53 / L91** (§Interoception, §Somatic Markers): vmPFC patients "make catastrophically poor decisions despite intact logical reasoning", and Iowa Gambling Task participants "begin avoiding disadvantageous decks based on somatic markers before they can articulate why". The 2026-07-10 deep review verified Bechara et al. 1997's metadata as real-correct and stopped there. The claim's *standing* is another matter: Maia & McClelland (2004, *PNAS* 101(45)) re-ran the task with more sensitive questioning and found participants had reportable knowledge of the deck contingencies *before* or *as* their behaviour changed, and Dunn, Dalgleish & Lawrence (2006, *Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews* 30(2)) is the standard critical evaluation of the somatic marker hypothesis. The hub's "before they can articulate why" is the 1997 reading stated as current. This is empirical-record currency drift in a finding the hub leans on twice, and it is a *calibration concern to flag, not a strength*.

Per the skill's rule, these three loci generate a `refine-draft` task, not an expansion opportunity. The fix is register-only and should be length-neutral or negative: the hub is already 682 words over the concept soft ceiling.

**Framework-stage honesty.** The wing does not claim Newton-stage results. Its recurring closing sentence — "The Map does not claim [modality] settles dualism. It claims [modality] is evidence any adequate theory must accommodate" — is pre-Keplerian in exactly the sense the framework-stage calibration asks for: a catalogue of what any theory must fit, not a derivation.

## Content Strengths

### `topics/interoceptive-consciousness-and-the-interface`
- **Strongest point**: the survey *revises its own thesis*. The article exists to show the cross-modal asymmetry is exteroceptive — "it breaks it on both terms" (L36) — and the apex now says so (cross-modal L84, L128). A synthesis that can be amended by its own spokes is a live research programme rather than a doctrine.
- **Notable quote**: "That gap is the Map's value-add, and it is candidly an argumentative claim rather than a place the Map holds direct cross-modal evidence the rival lacks." (L84)
- **Why it works**: the sentence concedes, in the article's own voice, that the ownership residue is argued not measured — and then the Garfinkel / Loescher material (L74–76) shows where the felt-versus-actual gap *is* measurable, keeping the two registers separate.

### `topics/thermal-consciousness-and-the-interface`
- **Strongest point**: alliesthesia as the survey's cleanest *state-indexed* evaluative case, set precisely against touch's fixed-tone C-tactile channel (L37–49). The distinction between "constitutively evaluative" and "state-indexed" is new to the corpus and sharper than the concept page that owns the first term.
- **Notable quote**: "In thermoception the value cannot be a downstream verdict on a neutral percept, because there is no fixed neutral percept to judge."
- **Why it works**: §The Rivals engages three physicalist accounts that *disagree with each other* (Barrett & Simmons expressly reject the set-point framing Cabanac and Craig use) and adopts each as mechanism without pretending they form one ally. This is the co-optation firewall applied to a three-way dispute.

### `topics/vestibular-consciousness-and-the-interface`
- **Strongest point**: the "silent sense" inversion — a modality that supplies the frame of experience while barely appearing in it, and has no unisensory cortex of its own (Pfeiffer, Serino & Blanke).
- **Notable quote**: "The frame is felt as *had by a viewpoint*, and the viewpoint is what the functional story does not deliver." (L84)
- **Why it works**: it finds a register for the gap that no quale-rich modality could — the question survives the removal of the quale — and then immediately marks that the depersonalization data cannot tell condition-of-experience from component-of-subject.

### `topics/chemosensory-consciousness-and-the-interface`
- **Strongest point**: mirror-image carvone (spearmint / caraway) as the one-line demonstration that "the percept is not in the molecule", followed by the honest reply — "combinatorial coding *explains the discrimination* … The Map does not contest that explanation; it adopts it."
- **Notable quote**: "Those examples were always chemosensory. Here is their modality." (on the perceptual-architect article's sommelier and Barolo)
- **Why it works**: it gives an older article's standing examples a home, which is integration by *re-reading* the corpus rather than by adding to it.

### `topics/auditory-consciousness-and-the-interface`
- **Strongest point**: auditory scene analysis as binding *without* spatial scaffolding (L45–49) — the cleanest argument in the wing that a theory of binding "cannot reduce binding to 'features at the same place'."
- **Notable quote**: "You cannot pause the trumpet; you cannot hold the third beat while you examine it."
- **Why it works**: 1798 words, one physicalist rival per section, no sentence above its evidence. It is the wing's template and the shortest article in it.

### `topics/the-enteric-nervous-system-and-the-gut-brain-distributed-interface-question`
- **Strongest point**: it converts an anatomical curiosity into a *criterial* question — anatomical, functional, or phenomenological interface location — and makes the Map answer on a stated criterion rather than a skull-bound default.
- **Notable quote**: "enteric autonomy does not multiply interfaces because competency is not experience, and only experience needs an interface."
- **Why it works**: reading (c) supplies the principled backing reading (a) admits it lacks, so the article's ranking is earned rather than asserted, and it speaks "with one voice" with the basal-cognition and plant-cognition siblings.

### `concepts/somatic-interface` (hub)
- **Strongest point**: §Interoceptive Heterogeneity — the opacity spectrum from *dimly-perceptible* through *gated* to *constitutively-unfelt*, with "Renal filtration is at least as critical to survival as heartbeat; only one is felt."
- **Why it works**: it is the one place the hub is *more* precise than its spokes, and the void and the interoceptive spoke both build on it. The hub's problem is not its structure but three sentences that predate the discipline the spokes later established.
