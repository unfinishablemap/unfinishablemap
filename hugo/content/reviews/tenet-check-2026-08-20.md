But "decisive" in an apex thesis line is the article's most truncation-resilient claim, and it invites precisely the refutation-reading L40 warns against, about precisely the variant L54 concedes is not refuted on its own terms. This is the alignment-surface pattern the 08-18 report named, appearing on day one of a fresh flagship rather than by drift.

**Recommendation**: rescope the thesis wording to the body's calibration — e.g. "carry the Map's case against branch-egalitarian Everettianism, while against first-personally centred variants a separate rejection of modal realism bears the weight" — or qualify "decisive" as decisive-within-the-framework. One phrase in three places; the body needs no change.
