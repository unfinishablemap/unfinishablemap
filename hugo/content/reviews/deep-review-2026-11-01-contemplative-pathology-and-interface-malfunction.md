## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

- **L83: misstates its dependency on two points (not propagated from the 10-10 anaesthesia repair).** (a) "hysteresis in which identical neural states correspond to different conscious states". Neural inertia means the same *anaesthetic concentration* goes with different states depending on the direction of transition. The anaesthesia page explains the hysteresis with a bistable neural switch, so the neural states differ. Read literally, the old wording asserted a supervenience failure. (b) "Those are the features that strain a production account." The anaesthesia page now says the opposite. L109: "Hysteresis need not puzzle production models". L139: graded restriction "is exactly what dose-dependent receptor occupancy predicts". L145: ketamine keeps waking-level complexity, and the patterns are "compatible with this tenet without favouring it". **Fixed.** The bridge sentence now says a dualist bridge "would have to come from" the wider record, and states that page's own grading and its four physical explanations. "Rather than merely surviving" was dropped, since it said the same thing as "persists". "Not its load-bearing element" became "not its main support".
- **L91: stale Map-internal paraphrase.** "patterns the anaesthesia case grades as compatible with the tenet without establishing it" was the *pre*-10-10 wording ("compatible with, without establishing"). The anaesthesia page now reads "without favouring it", a stronger concession. **Fixed** by a one-word swap.
- **L95: calibration error, plus a premise the register does not carry.** (a) "biasing quantum indeterminacies at specific neural sites" brings in the localization premise that `positions/quantum-interface` §"Scope limit — no spatial localization" disclaims. (b) It listed "decoherence rates" as a constraint, but tenets.md L69 says the Map's preferred route, post-decoherence selection, "sidesteps the requirement that quantum coherence survive at neural timescales". (c) "It cannot transcend them, and attempting to do so produces malfunction" stated as fact what is only a speculative integration the tenet allows. A tenet-accepting reviewer would still flag it, so it is correctable. The sibling `concepts/altered-states-of-consciousness` L153 says altered-state data are "silent on" this tenet. **Fixed.** The paragraph now offers "a speculative reason the interface might have" parameters, drops the site and decoherence clauses, puts the bound on "the neural dynamics that present those openings", makes the conclusion conditional, and ends "The tenet permits this reading; the contemplative record does not test it."
- **L69: unattributed quotation set next to a different citation.** The quoted "disembodied mind" / "corporealized" polarity comes from Fuchs (2005), not Sass & Parnas (2003), whose citation closed the previous paragraph. The 07-19 review's ledger wrongly said the polarity "match[es]" Sass & Parnas. The sibling `clinical-phenomenology-and-altered-experience` L91–L93 credits Fuchs correctly. **Fixed.** The polarity is now attributed to "Thomas Fuchs's (2005)" and a References entry is added. A stance clause was added: "though Fuchs himself rejects the Cartesian picture of a mind set apart from the body" (Fuchs 2005, p. 95: consciousness "is not the final link of a chain of deanimated physical processes as Descartes thought it to be"). "maps directly onto" became "maps onto".

### Medium Issues Found

- **L75: one-sided inference.** "consistent with a genuine interface … *rather than* consciousness being simply what brain activity produces". A brain that produces consciousness would also break under strain rather than reconfigure smoothly. The page's own L79/L89 symmetry admits this. **Fixed**: "…, though a brain that produces consciousness would have limits of its own."
- **L81: unsupported frequency claim.** "Practitioners experiencing severe contemplative pathology frequently report that awareness itself continues" had no source. Lindahl has no such category; its nearest is cognitive-domain "Clarity (20%)". **Fixed**: "Some practitioners … report".
- **L79/L83: "advanced dementia" as an abolition case conflicted with the dependency.** `consciousness-and-neurodegenerative-disease` L95 describes a patient with advanced Alzheimer's as conscious ("Yet she is conscious. She experiences pleasure and distress"). **Fixed** to "end-stage dementia" in both places.
- **L67: dropped qualifier, and a citation broader than what could be checked.** "diminished self-affection (loss of the implicit sense …)" overstated the construct; Sass & Parnas define it as a "*weakened* sense of existing as a vital and self-coinciding source of awareness and action". The citation also covered "perplexity", which I could not find in the cited work (see ledger). **Fixed**: "a weakened implicit sense", with the citation now limited to "the two distortions at the core of Sass and Parnas's (2003) account" and perplexity left uncited. The same sentence's "the practitioner having deliberately destabilised" implied intent to destabilise, which Lindahl's "unexpected, difficult or distressing" frame contradicts. It now reads "deliberate practice destabilising".
- **L43: position strength.** "Britton (2019) observes that contemplative practice follows" overstated what the paper does. Britton argues that mindfulness "is unlikely to be an exception to the inverted U-shape curved principle", and that her model "would predict that negative effects … would be more likely at higher doses". **Fixed**: "argues", and "at extreme intensities" became "at higher ones".
- **L93: "measurable neuroplastic changes" brought back the structural reading that the Map's own altered-states page L105 retracts** (Kral et al. 2022, *Science Advances* 8(20) eabk3316, Crossref-verified: no structural change from MBSR). **Fixed**: "measurable changes in brain function".
