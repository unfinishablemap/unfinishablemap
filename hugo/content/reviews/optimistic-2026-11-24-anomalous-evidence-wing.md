## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

Chalmers would recognise the wing's best move as his own: keeping the hard problem separate from every empirical anomaly. NDE L114 says the materialist mechanisms "address the explanatory gap no better for NDEs than for ordinary consciousness", which concedes that NDEs add nothing to the gap and declines to pretend they do. NDE L162 then states the inference in its honest form, "NDEs don't *prove* dualism; they raise the cost of the materialist account and leave the enriched-production escape open." A dualism that rests on the conceivability and knowledge arguments does not need a ceiling camera, and these pages write as though they know it.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

PF L65 is careful with Stapp. It quotes his 2006 formulation, on which the agent chooses the question and "nature returns answers, subject to specified statistical requirements", and then says plainly that the Map "takes on outcome-selection itself", a *stronger* claim than his. Stapp is not made to underwrite it. PRE L81 extends the same honesty to the Map's own mechanism: "If decoherence forbids quantum retrocausality from reaching skin conductance, it threatens the Map's own neural interface too." A quantum-mind theorist rarely sees a sympathetic source decline a free pass this explicitly.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

OBE L60 states the rule a phenomenologist most wants stated: "Phenomenology is not the same as ontology... The OBE's first-person structure is part of what needs explaining, not part of the answer." L62 then keeps what the phenomenology *does* establish, that "the body does not disappear from experience during an OBE; it appears at a distance, as an object rather than a subject." NDE L132 does the equivalent for "more real than real": the reported clarity is "*phenomenal vividness*... not *measured cognitive performance*". The first-person datum is kept and its evidential reach is measured.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

There is something here for a process thinker to like: OBE treats the bodily self as "constructed from continuously integrated multisensory signals" (L74), an event-like achievement and no fixed substance, and nothing in OBE, TL, PRE or PF uses experience-all-the-way-down to move a claim up the evidential scale.

NDE L156 is the exception, and it is logged as a concern and given no praise (Calibration Concern 1c). The paragraph has "Whitehead's process philosophy" offer "a complementary framework" on which "the brain provides an interface that dissolves during dying, potentially freeing consciousness from its normal constraints". Whitehead is on the style guide's co-optation roster as a process *monist* for whom an interactionist dualism is a bifurcation theory, and the Map's own `concepts/process-philosophy` L126 quotes his refusal of "any theory of psychic additions". The paragraph enlists him for the Map's interface picture with no stance sentence.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

Kane's concern is that indeterminism be the agent's own, and PRE L75 draws exactly that line: presentiment "concerns involuntary physiological activity... This is precisely *not* the conscious selection" the tenet describes; unconscious anticipation "would be something different: automatic organismic response rather than agent-level selection". PF L79 then names the price of a perfectly statistics-preserving selector, that the position "sits genuinely close to epiphenomenalism", and says "The Map owns both edges." An agency theorist is better served by that sentence than by any bent spoon.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

TL L134 is disciplined humility: "The first six levels are, in principle, settleable by ordinary clinical neuroscience", and only what survives them is a philosophical residue. PF L81 applies the same restraint to the Map's own falsifiability: "the firewall earns the Map less epistemic credit than the phrase 'we are falsifiable here' suggests". McGinn appears by name only at NDE L122, as one sentence reporting what "a mysterian perspective suggests"; he is on the roster, the sentence does not enlist him for the filter reading, and I leave it.

This persona's verdict governs the wing, and it is mostly favourable.
