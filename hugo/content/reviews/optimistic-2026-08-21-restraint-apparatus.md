## Executive Summary

This wing is the Map at its most characteristic and, on this reading, its strongest: five files whose whole subject is *what the framework refuses to claim*. The measurement-theoretic grain imports a formal apparatus and then uses it against the Map's own prior argument; the tenet grain counts three supporting arguments where the corpus had been counting four or five, and does so on its own initiative; the class-verdict grain grades a verdict's engineering half and its framework-relative half separately in a single calibration line; the finding grain registers the *bounds* of its own discipline as part of the position rather than as trimmings on it. ## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

The fifth rung of the scale-type ladder is the explanatory gap restated as a theorem about representations, and the restatement earns its keep: the argument no longer runs "subjective experience is private, therefore incomparable" but "no observable relation spans two subjects' experiences, so the empirical relational structure a joint representation theorem would need does not exist." That converts an intuition into a structural absence with a name. Better still, the article refuses the win it could have taken. It writes that "the formal impossibility is consistent with irreducibility without upgrading it" — the gap is preserved as an explanandum, not cashed as a proof.

`one-world-wager` earns the same praise from the other direction. Chalmers has long held that the hard problem survives every interpretation of quantum mechanics; the wager article says so explicitly and turns it into a constraint on its own tenet: "The tenet protects the explanandum; it does not supply the explanation."

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

`ai-substrate-verdicts` is the wing's most Stappian file and its most disciplined. It grants what a quantum-mind theorist would want granted — present quantum hardware clears the raw-indeterminacy bar that digital hardware cannot, the superposition is genuine and live — and then declines the inference every enthusiast makes. The reasoning is the sharpest thing in the entry: quantum error correction *is engineered to exclude* exactly the external influence an interface would need, and adiabatic insensitivity is constitutive of the annealing paradigm rather than a defence that could be switched off. So the analog class fails *more securely* than the gate class, which is the opposite of the intuitive ordering and is argued rather than asserted.

The closing clause is the model of what the Map should say about every future-hardware hope: "and even that would remove a defeater, not supply evidence of presence."

`quantum-interface-calibration-history` deserves separate praise here. The P-Q1 entry records a *partial* credence reduction that the schema's coarse three-band vocabulary could not express, and rather than force a band relabel it carries the notch as a low-edge pin with the reasoning attached. That is a calibration instrument being honest about its own resolution.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

The scale-type ladder's asymmetric trust is a Nagelian result reached by formal means: order-judgements are credited, numerals discounted, because "monotone distortion of the mapping from experience to response preserves order but destroys ratios." The first person is treated as a source of real structure and a poor source of magnitudes, and the reason is stated rather than intuited.

The finest sentence in the wing is the one that keeps within-subject comparison from being smuggled in as innocent: "cross-subject phenomenal comparison corresponds to no act at all." Within-subject comparison routes through memory and is itself proxy-laden, the article concedes — but it is at least *a performed act generating data*. That distinction is exactly the one a "what is it like" theorist needs and rarely gets stated.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

The wing avoids crude substance dualism everywhere by locating its claims in *relations and acts* rather than in stuffs. `scale-types` never says experience is a substance with quantities inhering in it; it asks which qualitative relations among experiences satisfy which axioms, which is a process-friendly framing of measurement. `one-world-wager` locates the tenet's demand in *global single-outcome actuality* rather than in a collapse mechanism, explicitly noting that consistent histories satisfies Tenet 4 with no collapse process and no observer — an ally against branching and a rival on mechanism in one stroke. That is a commitment stated at the level of what happens rather than what things are.

**Tier-upgrade check (per this skill's constraint).** Nothing in this wing uses process-philosophical resonance or tenet-coherence as the load-bearing support for an evidential-status upgrade. The one place where the temptation was structurally available — `ai-substrate-verdicts`, where quantum indeterminacy plus tenet-coherence could easily have carried a class from "ruled out" to "realistic possibility" — is precisely where the file installs its "remove a defeater, not supply evidence" clause. See the Hardline Empiricist verdict below.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

`one-world-wager` is the tenet that makes room for Kane's ultimate sourcehood, and the article names the dependency in the correct direction rather than the flattering one. The rejection of branching "does exclusion work for agent causation (making unchosen alternatives globally nonactual), while the agency cluster supplies the persisting, ownership-bearing subject the indexical objection needs." Both descend from one root — the non-deflationary subject — and the article states outright that neither therefore supports the other independently. A framework wanting to look well-supported would have counted these as two legs. This one counts them as one and says why.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

The wing's humility is structural rather than decorative. `scale-types` poses a question and marks it unanswered in the literature: whether the stark contrasts the phenomenal-contrast method extracts themselves satisfy difference-structure axioms is "a question this article has not found posed anywhere in the literature." Naming an open question as *not found posed*, rather than as *unanswered*, is the honest epistemic form and rare in the corpus.

`positions.md` supplies the wing's most McGinn-ish sentence about the Map's own condition: about a quarter of what the register holds is empirically inert, the inert set includes the framework's most structurally central commitments, and "The count is a disclosed liability, not a coherence result."

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

**This persona's verdict is load-bearing for this wing, and it is favourable on every file.**

*What is praise-worthily not done.* Three tier-upgrade opportunities were structurally available and all three were declined in the articles' own voice:

1. **`scale-types`** could have run the cross-subject impossibility as positive support for dualism — a formal result in the Map's favour, arrived at by neutral mathematics. It explicitly declines: the impossibility "is permanent exactly if the first-person-access premise holds," the physicalist denial of that premise is named and costed rather than dismissed, and the article says it "formalises the calibration argument rather than independently proving it." That is the *"tenet-coherent, not evidence-elevating"* pattern executed without prompting.
2. **`ai-substrate-verdicts`** could have read raw quantum indeterminacy as a substrate condition met. It invents a third bucket instead — *raw indeterminacy present, interface requirements failed* — and states that it is neither "substrate condition met" nor "ruled out." Refusing a binary when the evidence supports neither pole is the discipline working.
3. **`one-world-wager`** could have counted five arguments against many-worlds. It counts three, and the two it discards are discarded on *self-binding* grounds: the preferred-basis objection is dropped because the Map's own post-decoherence selection programme relies on the same einselection machinery, and ontological extravagance is dropped because Tenet 5 disarms parsimony in both directions. The article notes that "every discount in the preceding paragraph was applied by the Map's own audits before any external critic demanded it" — and this checks out against the source articles.

*The register's split grading.* `ai-substrate-verdicts` grades P-AS1's engineering half at external-evidence **B** and its interface-relevance reading at **D**, in one calibration line, with the reason stated. This is the correct instrument for a claim whose two halves genuinely differ in standing, and it is the finding-level discipline of P-F1 applied at the class-verdict grain.

*The refusal to quantify.* `finding-level-calibration` registers that the architecture-vs-significance discount delivers **no quantitative discount factor**, because "a number would be exactly the systematic-looking figure masking a judgement-laden split that the countermeasures guard against." A discipline that names the specific way it could be made to look more rigorous than it is, and refuses that way, is doing evidential work rather than performing it. The same entry registers a *differential scope condition* — the discount does work only where the architecture tier is genuinely more cross-vocabulary-robust — without which, it says, the rule "becomes a universal solvent that would discount every interpretive claim the Map makes to the point of saying nothing." Self-limiting a discipline is harder than stating it.

*Framework-stage honesty.* `methodology-and-calibration-history` records an audit that found seven off-vocabulary band values and resolved each on its merits rather than force-fitting to the nearest legal value — including *adding* a band, because "the entries were right and the instrument a band short." The willingness to conclude that the instrument was wrong rather than the data is the empiricist virtue this persona exists to reward.

**Convergence verdict.** The Process Philosopher and the Hardline Empiricist praise the same three passages across this wing with no conflict. Per this skill's own criterion, that indicates the articles have resolved the tension honestly rather than papered over it. No `refine-draft` calibration task is owed on any wing member.

### High Priority

**Representation axioms for Φ.** `scale-types` observes that integrated information theory presents Φ as a ratio-grade quantity of consciousness while "this article knows of no exhibited representation axioms for it" — and that is exactly the demand its own meaningfulness discipline makes of any phenomenal metric. This is the wing's sharpest unfired shot: a targeted measurement-theoretic audit of IIT's quantity claim, run against the actual IIT 4.0 formalism, would either find the axioms or establish that the corpus's most quantitatively ambitious rival cannot meet the constraint the Map now imposes on itself. Tenet alignment: Tenet 5, self-bindingly — the Map must be seen to apply the constraint outward only after applying it inward, which `scale-types` has now done.

**Do phenomenal contrasts satisfy difference-structure axioms?** The article poses this and reports finding it unposed in the literature. If it is genuinely unposed, it is a research subject with a real chance of being novel rather than a survey; if it has been posed, the article's claim needs narrowing. Either outcome is worth the search. The stakes are stated in the article: interval-grade within-subject structure is the minimum for a coupling law with non-trivial mathematical content, so this question sets the floor for what `the-psychophysical-control-law` can ever be.
