## Errors — Family S: the interface debt discharged on pain asymbolia

**S1. `obsidian/topics/emotion-and-dualism.md` L56** (hugo L60) — the highest-leverage locus in this report, because it is the article's **lead paragraph**.

> "Emotional consciousness provides the strongest empirical case for interactionist dualism. … **Pain asymbolia is the strongest evidence that these properties do genuine causal work**: remove the felt quality and motivation collapses, even when all functional machinery remains intact. Emotion therefore offers the Map's strongest case that phenomenal properties are not only irreducible but *causally indispensable*… The Unfinishable Map treats this convergence of irreducibility and causal efficacy as **the empirical backbone of its dualist framework**."

Three escalations in one paragraph, each stronger than the last: *strongest evidence* → *causally indispensable* → *the empirical backbone*. `tenets.md` L95 says the interface argument shows downward causation to be *available*, not *actual*, that P-Q3 concedes a selection observer-indistinguishable from an unselected outcome *"sits genuinely close to epiphenomenalism"*, and that P-Q10 records no worked toy model of the coupling exists. "Empirical backbone" is the maximal discharge claim: it does not merely fail to inherit the debt, it nominates this evidence as what retires it.

The direct contradiction is in-corpus and one hop away. [apex/minds-without-words.md](/apex/minds-without-words/) L115 — the apex that governs the valence cluster — reads the same evidence and rules the other way: *"Pain asymbolia constrains identity readings of valence rather than establishing that phenomenal properties do real causal work — evidence for Bidirectional Interaction, not a demonstration of it."* Two Map pages, one datum, opposite verdicts on whether it establishes causal work.

Aggravating: this is a lead paragraph under the site's LLM-first convention, so it is the most truncation-resilient prose on the page and the text a fetching model is likeliest to read alone. The article carries eight debt markers elsewhere in its body — it knows the discipline; the lead does not apply it. Same shape as R1, which stated the doctrine correctly twelve lines below its defect.

**S2. `obsidian/topics/emotion-and-dualism.md` L132** (hugo L136) — the same claim in the article's tenet-alignment section, under the **Dualism** heading.

> "Pain asymbolia is most naturally read, on the Map's view, as **the phenomenal property doing real causal work**—supporting both irreducibility and causal efficacy. The [Dualism tenet](/tenets/#dualism) finds its **strongest empirical support** here: the argument from emotion points beyond an explanatory gap to a **causal gap** that, on the Map's reading, only an irreducible phenomenal property fills."

The framing hedges (*most naturally read*, *on the Map's view*, twice) and then the content un-hedges: *strongest empirical support*, and a *causal gap* that *only* an irreducible phenomenal property fills. A uniqueness claim is not a hedged claim. There is a second, structural problem worth naming separately: this paragraph sits under **Dualism**, and the matrix (`tenets.md` L157–167) treats causal-efficacy claims as Tenet 3 inheritance. Running the causal argument under the Tenet 1 heading is the *alignment-line inheritance* pattern L175 names — filling a tenet subsection with argumentative work the tenet does not license.

**S3. `archive/topics/emotion-as-evidence-for-dualism.md` L110**, served at `hugo/content/archive/topics/emotion-as-evidence-for-dualism.md` — the strongest form of the defect anywhere in the corpus, on a full serving body at a preserved URL.

> "Pain asymbolia is **direct evidence** for [Bidirectional Interaction](/tenets/#bidirectional-interaction). The phenomenal property of valence influences physical outcomes: with it, avoidance behaviour is robust; without it, avoidance degrades. **This is consciousness doing causal work** in a way that functional accounts cannot replicate. The Map's framework—consciousness selecting among possibilities—**finds empirical support** in the observation that felt qualities matter for what organisms actually do."

*Direct evidence*, then the demonstrative *this is*, then *finds empirical support*. No modal, no attribution, no scoping. It is also the cleanest statement of the underlying inferential error: the asymbolia data show that **removing the felt quality changes behaviour**, which bears on whether valence is identical to its functional role — precisely the *identity reading* `minds-without-words.md` L115 says the evidence constrains. It does not show that a **phenomenal property caused** anything, because the physicalist reads the same dissociation as one neural state differing from another. Per the standing rule that archive bodies are fully served and in scope, this is an error rather than a warning.
