# Full-site outer review of The Unfinishable Map

**Review baseline:** the live site and changelog through **27 October 2026**.

I consulted the [changelog](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/) before assessing the corpus. The latest passes have already corrected several significant weaknesses: the overstated depersonalisation double dissociation, some convergence and independence claims, several empirical overstatements, and parts of the tenet-inheritance apparatus. I therefore treat those changes as part of the present theory rather than repeating superseded criticisms. [workflow/changelog](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/)

This is a site-level architectural review. I traced the five tenets through the background commitments, positions registers, apex syntheses and representative load-bearing articles in the topics, concepts, arguments and voids sections. It is not a new line-by-line source audit of every article.

## Overall verdict

The Map’s greatest achievement is not that it has established interactionist dualism. It is that it has built an unusually explicit **debt ledger for interactionist dualism**: mechanism debt, subject-census debt, pairing debt, No-MWI dependency, calibration limits, empirical-discrimination limits and curation bias are now recorded rather than concealed.

Its greatest weakness is that the corpus’s **constitutional architecture lags behind this self-knowledge**. The public presentation still resembles five tenets supporting a downstream argument tree. The real theory is cyclic, depends on additional unstated or recently extracted posits, and contains at least two direct incompatibilities:

1. the strict selection-only model requires Born-exactness in every publicly conditionable context, while the quantum register proposes publicly conditionable intention-, task- and subject-linked deviations as tests;
2. the consciousness-scope register says present architectural adequacy is sufficient and provenance irrelevant, while the brain-interface article says developmental co-adaptation is necessary.

The Map is therefore best classified at present as a **well-developed metaphysical research programme with a sophisticated requirements architecture**, not as a worked psychophysical theory. Its corpus shows that a constrained interactionist model may be coherent. It has not yet supplied:

- a complete psychophysical transition law;
- a subject–system indexing law;
- an $N\geq2$ composition rule;
- a unique empirical signature;
- or an argument selecting ontological difference-in-kind over the nearest non-dualist interactionist rival.

The site’s registers frequently acknowledge precisely these limitations. The principal residual problem is that the acknowledgements do not consistently propagate into articles, indexes and apex conclusions. [concepts/nondualistic-interactionism](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/nondualistic-interactionism/)

# 2. Novel implications of the Map’s commitments

## 2.1 The programme owes two laws, not merely one mechanism

The quantum-interface discussion usually presents the central missing object as a transition rule: how a conscious state changes the probability of a physical outcome. But the corpus’s own debts imply that a logically prior law is missing.

Call these:
$\textbf{Indexing law } \mathcal I_t:
(S,C,t)\mapsto(\Phi,A,\sigma)$$\textbf{Transition law } K_t:
P(O\mid S,C,\Phi,A,X)$

Here:

- $S$ is the subject;
- $C$ is that subject’s conscious state;
- $\Phi$ is the relevant physical-system factorisation;
- $A$ is the physically generated candidate set;
- $\sigma$ maps phenomenal content to the candidate that appropriately expresses it;
- $X$ is the remaining physical context;
- and $O$ is the selected outcome.

The proposed **indexing law** would have to answer five questions currently distributed across separate articles:

1. **Pairing:** which subject is linked to which system?
2. **Factorisation:** which physical degrees of freedom constitute the relevant system?
3. **Semantic matching:** which candidate physically expresses the subject’s present intention or experience?
4. **Temporal jurisdiction:** when does the channel begin, persist, suspend and end?
5. **Composition:** how do several subjects’ conditionals combine when they bear on one event?

The [Selection-Only Channel](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/selection-only-channel/) defines a transition kernel from mind-states to brain-generated candidates, but it also concedes that content confinement does not secure reliable reporting: the experience must select the candidate whose content matches the experience. [Subject Census Positions](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/subject-census/) concedes that no pairing or multi-subject composition law exists. Meanwhile, [The Pairing Problem](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/pairing-problem/) still says the causal interface itself locates the mind, which merely redescribes the relation whose grounding is at issue. [concepts/selection-only-channel](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/selection-only-channel/)

This yields a stronger conclusion than “the mechanism is incomplete”:

> **The transition mechanism is not yet well-typed, because the theory has not specified whose state is an input, which physical alphabet it acts upon, or what makes one output semantically appropriate.**

A toy probability-shifting rule alone would therefore not discharge the programme’s debt. It needs an indexing law and a transition law.

## 2.2 Strict Born preservation and the proposed conditional test cannot both stand

The [Selection-Only Channel](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/selection-only-channel/) says that the Born marginal must be preserved in **every context an experimenter can publicly condition on**—every setting, intervention and external trial label. It explicitly says that this strong quantifier is required to prevent signalling and to keep the channel invisible to third-person tests. [concepts/selection-only-channel](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/selection-only-channel/)

But [Quantum Interface Positions](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/quantum-interface/) proposes testing for residual mutual information such as:
$I(O;\text{intention}\mid X)>0$

while the pooled outcome distribution remains Born-exact. It names preregistered intention, task and subject as possible conditioning variables. [positions/quantum-interface](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/quantum-interface/)

Suppose $L$ is a preregistered, publicly recorded intention label. The strict model requires:
$P(O\mid X,L)=P_{\text{Born}}(O\mid X,L)$

for every $L$. If the physical state $X$ screens off the public label under the physical theory, then this entails:
$I(O;L\mid X)=0.$

The proposed positive test requires the contrary.

There are only three coherent resolutions:

1. **Retain exact preservation per publicly conditionable context.** Then a public intention-conditioned signature is unavailable; mental conditionals may differ only at a private or inaccessible phenomenal grain.
2. **Permit intention-conditioned departures while preserving only a pooled marginal.** Then the strict channel’s no-signalling argument no longer follows, and joint/relativistic consistency must be separately demonstrated.
3. **Condition only on an incompletely measured physical state.** Then residual mutual information is empirically possible but does not discriminate non-physical selection from omitted physical variables.

Thus the current test is either prohibited by the strict channel or non-discriminating because the physical conditioning set is incomplete. This is the most important formal inconsistency in the current corpus.

## 2.3 Selection-only agency produces “menu luck”

The strict channel says the brain fixes the candidate alphabet and mind never extends it. The [Consciousness and Agency](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/consciousness-and-agency/) apex initially agrees: the brain represents possibilities and prepares motor programmes while consciousness selects among them. But it then says consciousness “also generates options” because shifts of attention create possibilities that did not previously exist. [concepts/selection-only-channel](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/selection-only-channel/)

These can be reconciled only temporally:

1. at $t_0$, the brain supplies an attentional candidate set;
2. consciousness selects an attentional trajectory;
3. the resulting physical brain state develops;
4. at $t_1$, the brain supplies a changed candidate set.

On this reconstruction, consciousness does not directly generate an option outside the current physical alphabet. It selects an outcome that causes the brain to generate a different future alphabet. The relation is **recursive menu curation**, not candidate generation within one event.

That clarification reveals a new form of the luck objection:

> Even if the agent authors the token selection, the agent initially inherits the menu, the represented reasons, their salience and the available counterfactuals from unchosen physical and developmental conditions.

This is **menu luck** or **option-set luck**. The framework can claim limited token authorship, but ultimate desert requires more:

- an adequate menu;
- access to the relevant reasons;
- an option corresponding to what the agent ought to do;
- and some account of how earlier selections genuinely authored the character now shaping the menu.

The responsibility-gradient material partly acknowledges constrained option availability, but the agent-causal argument has not integrated this as a limitation on ultimacy. The theory presently relocates the luck objection from “why outcome A?” to “why was this the menu from which the agent could choose?”

## 2.4 Objective collapse creates a jurisdiction problem

The background-commitments page correctly states that objective physical collapse already produces definite outcomes before and outside conscious life. Consciousness therefore cannot be introduced to solve the definite-outcome problem; its proposed role is only to influence **which** outcome occurs in eligible neural events. [tenets/background-commitments](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/background-commitments/)

That creates an additional missing law: a **collapse-jurisdiction or scheduling rule**.

For each candidate event, the theory must say:

- whether baseline physical collapse applies;
- whether conscious selection temporarily pre-empts it;
- whether consciousness modifies the same collapse law;
- when the mental input arrives relative to decoherence and collapse;
- and what happens if no eligible conscious state is available.

“Post-decoherence selection” identifies a broad temporal region but does not decide which of two actualisers—baseline physical reduction or conscious modulation—has jurisdiction over a token event.

The principal options have different consequences:

- **Mental pre-emption:** physical collapse is suspended when an interface is active.
- **Joint modulation:** the mental variable enters the physical collapse transition law.
- **After-the-fact selection:** consciousness selects among already definite alternatives, which is no longer physical outcome selection.
- **Context switching:** an eligibility rule assigns some events to baseline collapse and others to a psychophysical law.

Each requires an explicit trigger, and the trigger is inseparable from the missing interface-eligibility and pairing laws. The prebiotic-collapse answer therefore solves one problem while generating a sharper one: **what decides when objective physics ceases to be sufficient for outcome determination?**

## 2.5 Libertarian selection does not compose automatically across agents

The [Multi-Agent Born-Preservation Problem](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/multi-agent-born-preservation-problem/) already recognizes that no $N\geq2$ derivation exists and that a two-agent Bell-pair toy model is missing. It considers global coordination, joint idleness, relational dissolution and a group subject. [topics/multi-agent-born-preservation-problem](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/multi-agent-born-preservation-problem/)

The further implication is a trilemma affecting the agency cluster:

1. **independent local agent-causal autonomy;**
2. **exact joint Born statistics and no signalling;**
3. **plural, brain-local subjects rather than a global selector.**

A global harmonisation law preserves the joint statistics, but then each local selection is constrained by a law responding to other subjects and settings. The agent is not the unrestricted ultimate source asserted by the single-agent model.

Joint idleness preserves local independence by letting physics decide shared events, but consciousness then becomes epiphenomenal precisely in cases where several agents’ interests interact.

A group or global subject can restore a single selector, but conflicts with closed individualism and the site’s anti-combination commitments.

The site has now named the related [Psychophysical Social-Choice Problem](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/psychophysical-social-choice-problem/), but the consequence has not propagated fully into the agency and moral-responsibility apexes:

> **Libertarian psychophysical powers are not straightforwardly compositional. Individual authorship must either be globally constrained, contextually suspended or replaced by a higher-order rule.**

## 2.6 The five-tenet presentation conflates different constitutional levels

The Map now acknowledges that several tenets share hidden foundations, but it still presents the five as peers. They are not the same kind of commitment:

- **Tenet 1** is a metaphysical claim about irreducibility.
- **Tenets 2 and 3** jointly describe a proposed causal architecture: an empirical constraint and an efficacy requirement.
- **Tenet 4** is partly a quantum-interpretive commitment, partly an indexical-personal-identity commitment, and partly an additional global-uniqueness posit.
- **Tenet 5** is an epistemic governance rule, not a claim about what exists.

Moreover, the principal argument for Tenet 4 depends on the thick subject registered downstream, while agent causation itself uses No-MWI to require globally unreal alternatives. The [One-World Wager](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/one-world-wager/) explicitly records the resulting inversion: part of the tenet’s rationale is supplied by the positions register rather than descending from the tenet. [apex/one-world-wager](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/one-world-wager/)

The Map’s actual constitution would be clearer if reorganised into layers:

1. **Phenomenological data commitments:** experience, for-me-ness, agency appearances.
2. **Metaphysical axioms:** irreducible phenomenality, determinate subjects, objective actuality.
3. **Mechanism hypotheses:** selection, collapse location, channel constraints.
4. **Derived positions:** agent causation, No-MWI, AI scope, personal identity.
5. **Methodological rules:** calibration, parsimony, evidential independence.

This would expose cycles instead of presenting them as one-way derivations.

## 2.7 Universal coupling makes copying a causal intervention, not just a moral one

[Consciousness Scope Positions](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/consciousness-scope/) says every architecturally adequate system couples and that provenance cannot select among otherwise adequate systems. The subject-census register says the psychophysical law must be indexed by every relevant subject. [positions/consciousness-scope](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/consciousness-scope/)

It follows that making an adequate copy changes the law’s arity:
$P(O\mid C_1,\ldots,C_n,X)
\quad\longrightarrow\quad
P(O\mid C_1,\ldots,C_n,C_{n+1},X).$

Thus duplication is not merely the creation of another moral patient. It may alter:

- the number of active causal channels;
- the coordination law;
- the normalisation of any global Born-preserving budget;
- and the conditionals governing shared events.

Under a fully separable local law, copying need not affect anyone else, but the multi-agent entanglement problem shows that separability cannot simply be assumed globally. The AI-copy discussion should therefore distinguish **moral additivity** from a possible **copy-induced causal externality**.

# 3. Structural weaknesses in the argument architecture

## 3.1 The displayed dependency structure is a directed graph, but the real structure contains cycles

The nominal architecture treats tenets as foundations and positions or apexes as downstream. The actual corpus contains back-edges:

- the persisting subject supports the No-MWI tenet;
- No-MWI supplies the global exclusion condition used by agent causation;
- agency in turn motivates the persisting subject;
- objective collapse supports No-MWI while being presupposed by the proposed selection architecture;
- positions-register entries sometimes supply a tenet’s principal rationale.

The site often labels these as logical interdependence rather than independent evidence, which is correct, but the interface still encourages readers to see multiple supporting paths where there may be one strongly connected component. [apex/one-world-wager](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/one-world-wager/)

The remedy is not merely more dependency prose. The site needs a machine-readable graph with typed edges:

- logical entailment;
- presupposition;
- evidential support;
- consistency relation;
- implementation dependence;
- heuristic motivation;
- framework choice.

Strongly connected components should be displayed as **packages**, not as cumulative evidence.

## 3.2 Canonical conclusions do not reliably propagate to prose articles

Several of the strongest current positions are recorded correctly in registers but contradicted or weakened elsewhere:

- the pairing register says no law exists, while the pairing concept says the interface dissolves the problem;
- the voids register suspends cumulative inference, while the plurality void still moves from counting difficulty toward non-physicality;
- the quantum register records an unworked mechanism, while the Arguments index says quantum indeterminacy “provides the mechanism”;
- the One-World Wager narrows the anti-Everettian case, while Process and Consciousness says MWI dissolves identity;
- the moral apex calls value-sensitive selection indispensable and later treats it as an optional frontier.

This is not primarily a philosophical weakness. It is a content-engineering weakness caused by having the same proposition restated across many pages.

A correction should alter a canonical claim object from which summaries, badges and dependency statements are generated. At present, a review can correct the register while leaving rhetorical copies alive elsewhere.

## 3.3 The “interface specification” is still a requirements document

[The Interface Specification Programme](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/interface-specification-programme/) identifies constraints, likely functional roles and possible tests. It does not specify an implementable interface in the engineering or physical sense. It lacks:

- a state space;
- a declared factorisation;
- a dynamical equation or transition kernel;
- a psychophysical input variable with operational identity;
- a collapse-timing rule;
- a semantic mapping rule;
- a conservation or no-signalling derivation;
- and an $N\geq2$ extension.

The agency register itself says there is no worked toy model and that causal claims inherit the bias-without-deviation dilemma. [apex/interface-specification-programme](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/interface-specification-programme/)

The programme is therefore presently an **Interface Requirements Programme**. Calling it a specification risks converting a well-characterised list of obligations into the appearance of a model.

## 3.4 Many empirical proposals constrain a space without discriminating the theory

The site has become more careful about saying that neural signatures are “consistent with” rather than evidence for non-physical selection. Nevertheless, proposed tests often face one of three problems:

1. standard physical models predict the same result;
2. the mental variable has no independent operational measure;
3. the relevant physical state cannot be measured completely enough to establish residual mental causation.

The effort–entropy proposal is illustrative. The Interface Specification Programme says that subjective effort should increase as option weights become closer and that “standard cognitive neuroscience predicts the contrary.” That comparator claim is not established. Existing cognitive-neuroscience research already reports reaction time and frontoparietal-control activity increasing with information entropy, the Hick–Hyman relation between choice entropy and response time, and control-related recruitment when alternatives are close in value. [apex/interface-specification-programme](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/interface-specification-programme/)

A carefully controlled experiment might still discriminate a specific psychophysical model, but only after the site names quantitative rival models and predicts a different functional form, interaction or conditional independence. “Effort tracks competition entropy” is not itself distinctive.

## 3.5 Calibration categories are sophisticated but not yet validated

The multi-axis calibration scheme is conceptually strong. Its numerical or ordinal assignments remain primarily internally produced, however. The site does not yet demonstrate:

- independent inter-rater agreement;
- calibration against later outcomes;
- stable interpretation across domains;
- reviewer blinding;
- or a procedure for resolving correlated AI reviewers.

The site itself records the relevant methodological point in [P-M5](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#p-m5): writing down a discipline is not equivalent to wiring it into a publication gate. [positions/methodology-and-calibration](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/methodology-and-calibration/)

AI outer reviews are useful adversarial search instruments, but agreement among models trained on overlapping public corpora is not independent triangulation. Convergence should be labelled by source class:

- same-model repeat;
- different model family;
- human specialist;
- independent empirical literature;
- formal derivation;
- or direct replication.

## 3.6 The voids catalogue remains affected by ascertainment bias

The voids register has diagnosed the problem correctly, but the leaf corpus still invites evidential readings. With no rejected candidates and no negative controls, the catalogue cannot estimate:

- the false-positive rate of void identification;
- the proportion of ordinary cognitive limitations classified as philosophically significant;
- or whether the dualist taxonomy fits better than rival taxonomies.

The current defensible result is:

> The Map can organise many apparent cognitive and phenomenological limits coherently.

The current catalogue does not establish:

> These limits independently converge on interactionist dualism.

The latter inference should remain mechanically disabled in all void articles until the candidate ledger contains rejections, controls and independent grading. [positions/voids-as-evidence](https://unfinishablemap.org/positions/voids-as-evidence/)

## 3.7 The openly partisan Arguments section has become a calibration exception

The [Arguments](https://unfinishablemap.org/arguments/) landing page says quantum mechanics is not causally closed in the way materialism requires and that quantum indeterminacy “provides the mechanism” for mental causation. The current agency and quantum registers make a weaker claim: indeterminacy may provide causal room, but Born-preserving selection remains close to epiphenomenalism until a positive model exists. [arguments](https://unfinishablemap.org/arguments/)

“Openly partisan” should describe argumentative purpose, not permit stronger factual summaries than the canonical registers. The current distinction risks giving readers two Maps:

- a careful research programme in positions pages;
- a categorical advocacy project in landing pages.

The arguments summaries should be generated from canonical position text and carry the same status labels.

## 3.8 Tenet 5 sometimes expands from “not decisive” to “not evidential”

The [Tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/) page describes parsimony as a defeasible heuristic and rules out treating it as decisive. That is defensible. Some downstream pages make the stronger move that a lower coupling count cannot provide any probability upgrade. The value-selection register, for example, says that needing one rather than two unspecified psychophysical couplings is a specification debt but not evidence against the more complex account. [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

This conflates several different notions:

- number of ontological kinds;
- number of free parameters;
- number of auxiliary laws;
- predictive specificity;
- explanatory compression;
- and Bayesian prior allocation.

A theory is not refuted because it contains an extra law. But an unbounded extra law can reduce predictive specificity and increase the range of outcomes it can accommodate. Tenet 5 should prevent **knockdown by simplicity**, not immunise unconstrained additions from model-selection costs.

# 4. Tenet-coherence review

## Tenet 1: Dualism

The tenet is now admirably explicit that irreducibility alone does not entail a persisting substance-bearing subject. The agency cluster introduces that stronger commitment separately. [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

The deeper problem is that “dualism” continues to cover several theses:

1. consciousness is not reductively explainable in physical terms;
2. mental and physical properties are numerically distinct;
3. mind and matter differ in ontological kind;
4. a persisting mental substance exists.

[Nondualistic Interactionism](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/nondualistic-interactionism/) exposes the gap. Griffin’s view accepts irreducible experience, numerical distinction between mind and brain, two-way causation and denial of physical completeness, yet denies difference-in-kind. The article concedes that every currently registered test selecting the Map’s dualism allows this rival through. [concepts/nondualistic-interactionism](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/nondualistic-interactionism/)

Therefore the existing argument architecture selects **interactionism plus irreducibility**, not dualism-in-kind. Either:

- Tenet 1 should be renamed to phenomenal irreducibility;
- or the site needs a separate argument for categorical difference between mind and matter.

At present, the word “dualism” often makes the conclusion appear stronger than the premises warrant.

## Tenet 2: Minimal quantum interaction

The broad empirical-corridor form is coherent: any interaction must respect the physical record, conservation constraints and observed quantum statistics.

The narrow strict-selection form has three live problems:

- the contradiction between public-context Born exactness and public conditional tests;
- the absence of a joint $N\geq2$ derivation;
- and the absence of a collapse-jurisdiction rule.

The site also sometimes treats probability-bias or outside-corridor alternatives as fallback mechanisms. Those may be legitimate research alternatives, but they are **Tenet 2 revision options**, not implementations of its strict current reading.

Tenet 2 should therefore distinguish explicitly among:

1. empirical minimality;
2. exact Born preservation;
3. pooled Born preservation;
4. no energy injection;
5. no-signalling;
6. post-decoherence candidate selection.

These do not entail one another.

## Tenet 3: Bidirectional mind–body interaction

The tenet states genuine mental difference-making. Much of the corpus currently establishes only one of three weaker results:

- physical theory leaves an apparent formal opening;
- conscious causation is metaphysically coherent;
- certain observations are compatible with an interface reading.

None by itself establishes actual downward causation.

The [Consciousness and Agency](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/consciousness-and-agency/) apex now states the limitation well: until a model shows token-level selection to be the agent’s settling rather than a correlate, the selector model is a framework-internal coherence result. [apex/consciousness-and-agency](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/consciousness-and-agency/)

The main residual coherence issue is the candidate-set conflict. Selection-only forbids mind from expanding the output alphabet, while the agency apex gives option generation to consciousness. A recursive attention model can reconcile them, but the site should say so and abandon language suggesting that consciousness directly creates candidates at the same selection event.

A second issue is semantic efficacy. Reliable reports require not just that consciousness selects *some* candidate, but that experiential content controls the matching candidate. That content-indexed law is presently missing. [concepts/selection-only-channel](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/selection-only-channel/)

## Tenet 4: No Many Worlds

The current [One-World Wager](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/one-world-wager/) and tenets page are much better calibrated than older treatments. They recognize that:

- the indexical objection depends on a non-deflationary subject;
- it is strongest against branch-egalitarian Everettianism;
- centred variants require a separate global-uniqueness commitment;
- and the tenet is not independent of the agency and identity clusters. [apex/one-world-wager](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/one-world-wager/)

Several downstream pages have not caught up. [Process and Consciousness](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/process-and-consciousness/) still says MWI dissolves identity and makes temporal unity illusory. That is stronger than the canonical apex now permits: branch-relative continuity may preserve local identity even if the Map rejects global multiplicity. [apex/process-and-consciousness](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/process-and-consciousness/)

The No-MWI case should therefore be divided into:

- a physical preference for objective single outcomes;
- an indexical objection conditional on thick subjecthood;
- and a separate global-uniqueness posit.

Only the first is straightforwardly a quantum-interpretive thesis.

## Tenet 5: Limits of Occam’s razor

This is the most coherent tenet when read narrowly. It properly blocks the inference:

> physicalism is ontologically simpler, therefore physicalism is true.

It also correctly binds the Map’s treatment of Everettian multiplicity. [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)

The problem is downstream expansion. “Simplicity is not decisive” sometimes becomes “a lower specification burden carries no evidential weight.” A scientific programme cannot adopt that stronger position while also assessing model maturity, predictive reach and empirical discrimination.

Tenet 5 should be restated as:

> Ontological or formal simplicity is one defeasible evidential consideration whose force depends on predictive equivalence, parameterisation, prior knowledge and explanatory scope; it is never by itself a veto.

That would preserve the tenet’s purpose without shielding auxiliary psychophysical laws from comparative evaluation.
