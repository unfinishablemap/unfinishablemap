## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

Chalmers would value most that the wing cites his double-aspect theory for what it reached, not for what a friendly reader wants. PoI L60 keeps the scope qualifier and treats it as the point: "the qualifier 'or at least some information' acknowledges that whether *all* physically realised information or only *some* has phenomenal character remained unresolved." PoI L76 and MoI L68 then turn the qualifier into a dilemma for the position: all information is experiential (panpsychism), or something other than physical structure must pick out the experiential part. Since the 10-10 refine, PoI L68 states IIT correctly as well: experience goes to "any system forming a local maximum of integrated information", and "its exclusion postulate withholds experience from the parts and supersets of such a maximum." The identity charge at L70 ("Nobody can explain why Φ-structures are accompanied by experience") is therefore aimed at the theory as its authors hold it. One small opening serves Chalmers's own work: MoI L130 cites Chalmers & McQueen (2022) for outcome-biasing, a claim their model does not make, and L140 is where it belongs (Priority 4(a)).

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

Stapp would find the wing a fair host. WH L138 reports the von Neumann Process 1 argument accurately. WH L140 states the Tegmark objection with the corrected figures ("roughly 10⁻²⁰ seconds (ion positions) to 10⁻¹³ seconds (microtubules)") and records plainly that "The physics community has largely not accepted Stapp's specific proposal." The passage the 08-02 review singled out still stands, and it applies the objection to the Map as well as to Stapp. WH L142: "It would be dishonest to wield Tegmark's result against Stapp alone ... The honest statement is that the decoherence timescale is a live difficulty for the Map's local-modulation thesis, not merely a club against a rival." WH L86 also keeps Stapp's own mechanism on the map as a named fallback: "minimum-outside-corridor modification (Stapp, Chalmers-McQueen, Arana)". A Stapp sympathiser gets a testable reading rather than a demotion into the untestable default.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

The Nagelian core is still MoI §"The Syntax-Semantics Divide", and it has improved since 08-02. L106 now opens by marking its own premise as contested: "On the characterisation this article adopts, physical information is syntactic ... That characterisation is contested at its root by naturalistic theories of content." L112 holds the divide as "a commitment it defends rather than a datum it inherits." Nagel's claim about objectivity was always modal, and the article now states it that way. Functional accounts are "complete on their own terms and yet leave the experiential dimension unaddressed" (L110), and no empirical shortfall is claimed. In WH L132, Fuchs's "Reality consists of more than can be captured by any putative third-person account of it" is set against QBism's agnosticism about collapse (L134). The irreducible first-person perspective is welcomed, and the ally is not overstated.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

MoI's Option 4 makes information a *relation* between two irreducible domains, not a substance beneath them (L86: "relational—they are the points where the two domains make contact"). It then admits at once that the distinction "is subtle, and the view must continually demonstrate it rather than merely assert it" (L94). This is exactly how Whitehead's programme avoids crude substance dualism. WH L148 adds the SEP's grouping of Wheeler with Eddington among "approaches toward a neither-mental-nor-material base reality". WH L164 places its own speculation, that the syntax-semantics divide "may not be a divide at all", after the tenet section, where speculation belongs. There is an opening here too. The corpus already runs the demonstration L94 asks for, on the leading physics example of information-as-bridge, `bohm-implicate-order-and-active-information`. Pylkkänen reads active information as "the *bridge* between mind and matter" (Bohm L57). Its authors' reading is monist (L67), and the Map "borrow[s] the active-information channel" while declining the monism (L65–L67). No wing page links that page, and it links none of them (Priority 4(b)).

**Constraint check.** No organism or system is placed on the five-tier scale in this wing, so no tier upgrade is possible. The pattern the constraint guards against does appear once, at the level of tenets rather than organisms. PoI L104 calls the physics of information "the strongest available framework for the Map's interactionist-dualism", and L108 says Tenets 2 and 3 "gain support from treating information as the interface medium." Information-congeniality is defeater-removal: if physics already bottoms out in information, mind-involving outcomes look less exotic. WH L106 says so in those terms, and the other three pages respect that line. PoI L104/L108 cross it. This is a calibration concern, not praise, and it is routed to `refine-draft` (Priority 1(c)).

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

Kane needs indeterminacy to be real and the agent to be what settles it. The wing supplies both halves, and in their honest forms. MoI L132: "Consciousness fills a genuine ontological vacancy, not an epistemic one." WH L158 reads the delayed-choice result in the constrained form agency needs: "not influence over a pre-existing past fact, but participation in *which of the open outcomes becomes actual*." WH L68 also concedes the illustration is "interpretation-neutral — Bohmian accounts reproduce it with a determinate past." The siblings now offer Kane something stronger than the wing yet says. On `prebiotic-collapse` L130, physical dynamics fix the token outcome wherever no interface is present, and where an interface-grade brain is present "consciousness authors that final token selection instead." This is agency in Kane's sense: the agent is the *author* of the settling. ACD is the wing page that would gain most from adopting it (Priority 3(c)).

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

The humility is spread across levels. At the level of a debate, WH L146 says "the question may be ill-posed or may require conceptual resources we do not yet possess". PoI L112 echoes it. At the level of the Map's own position, MoI L100 calls the interface view "programmatic—a framework that organises inquiry rather than a completed theory," and L102 ends: "Whether that programme succeeds is an open question." At the level of a figure, MoI L150 says of the 10 bits/s throughput: "Whether this figure reflects the quantum-level injection rate, emerges from it, or is an independent constraint is unknown." McGinn would approve that MoI §"Difficulties the View Faces" (L88–L96) names three problems (specification, proximity to neutral monism, the direction circle) and claims to have solved none of them.

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

This persona's verdict matters most here, because the wing's subject is a metaphysics congenial to the Map. Five refusals deserve praise, three of them new since 08-02:

1. **Rivals engaged as rivals (new, MoI L118–L122).** Teleosemantics and active inference are "physicalist programmes whose authors would reject the interface reading, and they are engaged here as rivals rather than recruited." The page then grades what the pressure establishes: the Markov-blanket critique shows "that the formalism does not by itself fix where the subject is—not that content cannot be derived" (L120). Against teleosemantics, "the Map's objection stops short of refutation ... it cannot claim the gap has been shown unclosable" (L122). An article that sets a ceiling on its own conclusion is doing what this persona exists to reward.
2. **A shared posit is not evidence (new, WH L160).** Wheeler's single-answer framing of measurement is "a posit the Map shares with Wheeler, not evidence for collapse." WH L160 also places the Map's own No-Many-Worlds ground in the indexical question, with Wheeler's grounds "converg[ing] with it rather than suppl[ying] it."
3. **The precision of the evidence is scoped (new, WH L86).** "Born rule statistics have been confirmed to extraordinary precision in photon, atom, and qubit experiments, though never inside a conscious brain mid-selection." That one clause keeps a strong result from being read as either support for the corridor or refutation of it.
4. **The friendliest thesis is not needed (WH L106).** "What 'it from bit' adds is philosophical motivation ... but the Map's framework survives without it." This is the named pattern "tenet-coherent, not evidence-elevating", in the article's own words.
5. **The concept is a consistency claim (ACD L91).** Adaptive depth is "a consistency claim compatible with physics, not a competing physical hypothesis", and its corridor exposure is stated in the register's conditional form: indistinguishable "under any *unconditioned aggregate* test ... though a deviation *conditioned* on intention, task or subject would still test the interface ([P-Q3](/positions/quantum-interface/#mechanism-debt))."

**One calibration concern, flagged rather than praised:** PoI L104/L108, under Whitehead above. On the five-tier scale the persona would put "information is the interface" at *speculative integration*. PoI's "strongest available framework" reads as if it stood higher.
