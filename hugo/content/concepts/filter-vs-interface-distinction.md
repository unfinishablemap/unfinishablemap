The Unfinishable Map describes the brain in two vocabularies that are usually run together: as a **filter** and as an **interface**. They make different causal claims. A filter model says the brain admits, attenuates, or sifts a consciousness it does not produce, and says nothing more: the traffic runs one way, from the world through the brain into experience. An interface model says the coupling runs both ways: the brain shapes what consciousness manifests, *and* consciousness acts back on the brain, selecting among outcomes the physics leaves open. Stated at that level the distinction belongs to no camp — it is a fact about the causal structure each metaphor commits to, and a physicalist can draw it as readily as a dualist. The Map's own reading, developed in the [section on the Map's reading](#maps-reading), is that the filter is the *inbound leg* of the interface: every filter claim is half of an interface claim, and the transmission thesis says nothing about the other half rather than opposing it — James and Bergson affirmed the efficacy of consciousness in other work, but not as part of the thesis.

Two points of status should survive truncation. The distinction clarifies the Map's own commitments and carries no evidential weight for them: nothing here raises the probability that the brain filters or interfaces rather than produces. And it is a *partial* repair of the filter cluster's acknowledged weak joint: the [terminal-lucidity](/topics/terminal-lucidity-and-filter-transmission-theory/) article concedes that classifying brain damage as "filter loosening" or "interface degradation" "risks unfalsifiability", and the [near-death-experience](/concepts/near-death-experiences/) article calls its own version of the move ad hoc and "post hoc and difficult to test". Sorting cases by causal *leg* gives that classification an independent criterion ([diagnostic section](#diagnostic)); it does not supply a predictor of which inbound disruptions loosen and which constrict.

## The Distinction Stated Neutrally {#neutral}

The transmission theorists framed the brain's role in optical terms. [James](/topics/william-james-consciousness/)'s 1898 Ingersoll Lecture put the thesis in one sentence: when we think of thought as a function of the brain, "we are not required to think of productive function only; we are entitled also to consider permissive or transmissive function" (James 1898). His analogies are a coloured glass, a prism, a lens: light "is by the glass sifted and limited", and the glass produces none of it. Bergson's *Matter and Memory* (1896) makes the same move for perception, with the brain as an organ of selection oriented toward action, admitting from the field of images only what bears on what the body might do next. Huxley (1954) popularised the picture as a "reducing valve" narrowing "Mind at Large" to what serves survival. In every version the causal traffic is inbound. A prism is acted on by light; it does not act on the sun. Nothing in the transmission thesis, as its authors stated it, describes consciousness changing the physical state of the brain. The silence belongs to the thesis rather than to its authors: James had argued in "Are We Automata?" (1879) that consciousness is efficacious, and Bergson's brain is an organ of action that introduces indetermination into the body's response. Neither carried that commitment into the optical analogies, which is why the thesis could be held without settling anything about mental causation.

An interface model adds the return channel. The historical hinge is Eccles. *The Self and Its Brain* (Popper & Eccles 1977) has the self-conscious mind reading from and acting on cortical modules — Eccles's "liaison brain" — and the later quantum model developed with Beck brings the locus down to the synapse and makes the mind's contribution a change in probability: "voluntary actions become effective by momentary increases of the probability of vesicular emission in the thousands of synapses on each pyramidal cell by quantal selection" (Beck & Eccles 1992). Whatever one thinks of the mechanism, the causal *shape* is new. The brain still constrains what consciousness manifests (the inbound leg), and consciousness now biases which physically available outcome occurs (the outbound leg). Two legs, one coupling.

Three consequences follow before any metaphysics is added. The terms are not interchangeable: a filter claim is strictly weaker than an interface claim, since it can be true while the return channel is absent. Transmission vocabulary carries no dualist commitment: Rouleau and Cimino (2022) defend a "transmissive theory of brain function" in which the brain receives, filters, and emits signals, but the signals are electromagnetic and environmental, and the authors "do not reject the productive model", proposing only to "amend" it to include "extracerebral EM signals from environmental sources". Their brain is transmissive and entirely physical, so the Map cites the paper as a rival, not an ally: filter-vs-interface and transmission-vs-production are orthogonal, and dualism is supplied on the Map by [Tenet 1](/tenets/#dualism), not by the metaphor. And the energy-conservation objection is asymmetric, targeting the outbound leg only (the [safe-but-inert section](#energy)).

## The Map's Reading: The Filter Is the Inbound Leg {#maps-reading}

The Map treats the filter as the inbound projection of a two-way coupling. Every filter is an interface, but a filter model describes only half of what an interface does. This is the Map's proposal, motivated by the tenets, and it goes beyond anything James or Huxley asserted.

The reading is forced by what the Map wants from each metaphor. The filter secures [Dualism](/tenets/#dualism): it re-reads the brain–consciousness correlations that motivate materialism as transmission rather than production, as [filter-theory](/concepts/filter-theory/) does. It does nothing for [Bidirectional Interaction](/tenets/#bidirectional-interaction), because a purely receptive brain leaves agency unexplained: a radio does not choose its station. A dualism that stopped at the filter would hold consciousness independent of the brain and causally inert with respect to it — [epiphenomenalism](/concepts/epiphenomenalism/) with an exotic source. The Map rejects that combination on Tenet 3's grounds, held as a metaphysical commitment supported by self-stultification and indirect evidence rather than delivered by the filter evidence. The [agency void](/voids/agency-void/) records why the outbound leg cannot be verified from the inside, and the tenets page registers that the quantum-interface argument shows downward causation to be [available rather than actual](/tenets/#tenet-3-standing). The distinction inherits that standing: it clarifies what the Map is committed to without discharging the commitment.

The [two-layer architecture](/concepts/mind-matter-interface/) already builds on this division — Layer 1 is the filter, Layer 2 the return channel — and this page names the seam between them. On the content of the outbound leg the Map's default is outcome-selection at quantum indeterminacies, in one of three [coupling modes](/concepts/coupling-modes/). Stapp's proposal locates the influence one step earlier, in the choice of which question is put to nature, leaving the outcome to nature's statistical rule ([stapp-quantum-mind](/concepts/stapp-quantum-mind/)); the Map registers that context-selection variant without adopting it. The distinction is neutral on which variant fills the outbound leg. It requires only that there be one.

## Why Pure Filter Theory Is Safe but Inert {#energy}

The classic physical objection to interactionism — Leibniz's, revived by contemporary physicists; see [history-of-the-interaction-problem](/topics/history-of-the-interaction-problem/) — is that a mind acting on matter must add energy or momentum the physics does not account for. The objection bites the outbound leg and nothing else. A prism, a valve, a receiver violate no conservation law, because they are acted upon rather than acting; a filter-only dualism is immune by construction. Filter theory never claims the causal power that would expose it.

The Map declines that immunity on purpose. Its answer to the objection is the one [conservation-laws-and-mental-causation](/concepts/conservation-laws-and-mental-causation/) develops from Cucu and Pitts (2019) and Pitts (2020): conservation is derived from symmetries that an external influence would break, and it holds locally rather than as a universal sum, so assuming it holds in the brain before looking presupposes that interactionism is false. The interface then acts by selection rather than injection — biasing which of the outcomes physics already leaves open becomes actual, with the ensemble energy expectation preserved even where individual trials differ. That is [Minimal Quantum Interaction](/tenets/#minimal-quantum-interaction) read as the price of Tenet 3: the smallest outbound leg that is still a leg. The price includes a testability cost, since a selection that leaves aggregate Born statistics intact is empirically indistinguishable from chance under any unconditioned test. A filter-only theory pays neither cost, and buys nothing with the saving. Here the distinction does its clearest work: it says exactly which half of the Map's position the conservation objection is an objection to.

## Diagnostic Use: Reading the Filter Cluster by Leg {#diagnostic}

The cluster's weak joint is the selectivity problem. If the brain constrains consciousness, most damage should loosen the constraint and enhance experience; in fact stroke, trauma, and dementia overwhelmingly diminish it, and the enhancement cases (psychedelics, near-death experiences, terminal lucidity) are exceptions. The corpus's standing reply distinguishes the brain's *filtering* function from "the interface through which consciousness expresses itself", (the near-death article's version contrasts a "constraining mechanism" with an "interface channel"), and both articles flag the reply as post hoc. The wording already sorts by leg — "filtering" is inbound, "expressing itself" is outbound — and making that explicit yields a criterion independent of the outcome being explained. An outbound-leg lesion leaves experience intact and removes its expression; an inbound-leg change alters experience while expression survives. The evidence for the two is asymmetric — an outbound failure shows in behaviour and in cortical signals that never reach the body, whereas an inbound change shows only through report or through the imaging proxies covert-consciousness research relies on — but neither depends on the filter reading.

**Outbound leg cut, inbound intact.** [Locked-in syndrome](/topics/locked-in-syndrome-as-the-negative-case-where-filter-loosening-does-not-apply/) is the clean case: its article already describes the interface's "two dissociable arms", ascending and descending, with the descending arm severed at the pons, and speech neuroprostheses decoding an intact intent-to-act signal from cortex give the diagnosis an engineering test. [Covert consciousness](/topics/consciousness-disruption-and-the-mind-brain-interface/) — command-following visible in imaging but not in behaviour — is the same pattern with a less localised lesion. Neither is a filter-loosening case, and on this criterion neither could be mistaken for one: the *width* of experience has not changed.

**Inbound leg altered, outbound intact.** [Psychedelic states](/topics/psychedelics-and-the-filter-model/) change the content and reported richness of experience while the subject can still speak, so the inbound claim is checkable in real time against a working outbound leg, under dosing that can be controlled — which no other member of the cluster offers.

**Both legs down, separably.** [Anaesthesia](/topics/anaesthesia-and-the-consciousness-interface/) dissects at least four components — phenomenal presence, behavioural access, temporal binding, memory encoding — and they fall on different sides of the seam: presence and binding are inbound, access is outbound, and the ketamine profile (vivid experience, no access) is an outbound-leg failure with the inbound leg running.

**Both legs return together.** [Terminal lucidity](/topics/terminal-lucidity-and-filter-transmission-theory/) is a puzzle on this scheme for the reason it is a puzzle on every scheme: a patient with substantial cortical loss briefly recovers coherent experience *and* coherent expression at once. Neither leg alone accounts for it, which is why the cluster's own weighting treats it as the costliest anomaly rather than as confirmation.

**Inbound claims carried by a later-restored outbound leg.** [Near-death reports](/concepts/near-death-experiences/) describe enhanced experience during a period when the outbound leg was down. Evidence about the inbound leg arrives only through the outbound leg after resuscitation, via memory, so the retrospective character of that evidence is structural rather than a defect of study design.

What this rescues and what it does not should be stated at the same volume. Classification by leg is principled: whether a case is "interface degradation" (outbound) or a change in filtering (inbound) is settled by whether expression or experience failed, which is observable without assuming the filter reading, so the charge that *any* damage could be retrospectively assigned to either category no longer stands in its general form. But the distinction says nothing about the inbound leg's own selectivity — why dementia narrows experience and terminal lucidity briefly widens it, both inbound. That residue is the selectivity problem proper, and it remains open; the corpus's other instrument for it, [direction-of-interface-change signatures](/concepts/direction-of-interface-change/), constrains readings without deciding them. The weak joint has been narrowed to one leg, not discharged.

## Rival Readings {#rivals}

**Synonymy.** Popular presentations of transmission theory use "filter", "receiver", and "interface" interchangeably. The cost is that the return channel enters unnoticed: a writer who has argued only for a filter can appear to have agency for free. The Map separates the terms to keep that bill visible.

**Filter-only dualism.** The strongest rival inside dualism accepts the inbound leg and denies the outbound one — consciousness is independent of the brain and receives from it but never acts on it. The position is coherent, immune to the conservation objection, and free of the [interface problem](/topics/the-interface-problem/). The Map's dispute with it is the epiphenomenalism dispute, run at [bidirectional-interaction](/concepts/bidirectional-interaction/), and nothing here settles it; this page adds only that the position *is* epiphenomenalism about the brain, however exotic its source, and that a filter never secured mental causation.

**Production.** A physicalist will say both metaphors are metaphors and that sophisticated production accommodates every member of the cluster by parallel moves. The Map concedes this at [filter-theory](/concepts/filter-theory/) and repeats it here: the distinction is bookkeeping internal to transmission views, sorting the cluster more honestly without making it discriminate against production.

**Physicalist transmission.** Rouleau and Cimino's model has both legs — the brain "is both receptive to and actively emits EM signals" — and both are physical. It shows that a two-way *physical* interface is not the Map's interface, and that the Map's reading needs Tenet 1 as a separate premise.

## What Follows from the Distinction Alone, and What Only on the Map's Reading {#implications}

From the neutral distinction alone: a filter claim is weaker than an interface claim; the conservation objection targets the outbound leg only; clinical cases can be sorted by leg on evidence that does not assume the filter reading; and a dualism with no outbound leg is epiphenomenalist about the brain whatever it says about consciousness's source. Only on the Map's reading: the inbound leg is the projection of a coupling whose outbound leg is outcome-selection at quantum indeterminacies; Minimal Quantum Interaction is the price of that leg; and the filter cluster's evidence is evidence about an interface rather than a valve.

## Relation to Site Perspective

The distinction is a coherence check on the tenets rather than support for them. [Dualism](/tenets/#dualism) is carried by the inbound leg — the filter's re-reading of correlation as transmission — and by nothing on the outbound side. [Bidirectional Interaction](/tenets/#bidirectional-interaction) *is* the outbound leg, and the page inherits that tenet's standing: downward causation shown available, not actual. [Minimal Quantum Interaction](/tenets/#minimal-quantum-interaction) specifies the outbound leg's smallest admissible form and is the cost the Map accepts for having one. [No Many Worlds](/tenets/#no-many-worlds) is presupposed by the outbound leg, since selecting an outcome is empty if every outcome occurs. [Occam's Razor Has Limits](/tenets/#occams-limits) disarms the complaint that the two-legged model is less simple than filter-only dualism; it does not license preferring the more complex model, and the Map does not ask it to. The reason for the second leg is Tenet 3, not parsimony either way.
