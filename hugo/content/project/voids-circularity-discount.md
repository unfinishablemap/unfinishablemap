The Map's catalogue of voids — over a hundred entries documenting questions consciousness research points toward but cannot answer — was long offered, in aggregate, as cumulative abductive support for the [tenet-package](/tenets/): voids cluster at the consciousness-matter interface, and that clustering was read as evidence the interface is real. The *voids-circularity discount* audits claims of this shape, and run to completion over the catalogue on 2026-07-27 it ended in withdrawal rather than a penalty. A void gets catalogued because the framework's commitments identify it as a void ([tenet-generated-voids](/voids/tenet-generated-voids/) is the Map's own articulation of this), so convergence of voids catalogued *under* the framework with the framework defining them cannot earn the N-fold force that surface-counting suggests — and, as [what-voids-reveal](/voids/what-voids-reveal/) now holds, carries no confirming force at all against a physicalism carving the voids differently. The clustering is a coherence exhibit: the Map's carving of the voids and its tenets hang together, which is framework-internal by construction.

What survives is the machinery, doing a changed job. Per-cluster independence scoring and the prohibition on double-counting tenet-generated voids are general-purpose instruments for auditing any convergence claim the Map makes, and the voids catalogue is now their most fully worked example — a convergence that does not clear the bar rather than one that clears it at a penalty. The name is kept because the shape of the failure is what the discipline diagnoses.

The discount is a specific application of the [common-cause-null discipline](/project/common-cause-null/), which asks, whenever N observations converge, whether they are one observation reading the same upstream architecture N times. Where the common-cause null asks about *shared machinery*, the discount asks about *shared definitions*: when the framework supplies the criterion that lets an item count as a void, convergence is partly built into the counting. The discount names this, scores per-cluster how much independence remains, and bars catalogue-size arguments from running over voids the framework itself generates.

## The Structural Objection

The argument ran as follows: many distinct phenomena — the binding void, the intrinsic-nature void, the surplus void, the temporal void, the silence void, the meta-epistemic void, and dozens more — resist treatment under the standard materialist programme, each marking a specific failure of physical or functional reduction, and the cumulative weight of the catalogue was taken as abductive support for the framework that predicts such failures.

The structural objection: if the framework's own commitments identify what counts as a void, the catalogue's convergence with the framework is not framework-independent evidence. A materialist programme allowing phenomenal reduction to count as "complete" wherever functional reduction succeeded would catalogue most of the same phenomena as solved. The voids are voids because the framework demands explanation in registers — phenomenal character, intrinsic nature, qualitative residual — materialism by its own commitments declines to supply. The framework defining the criterion and the framework whose support the catalogue is asked to license are the same. *Convergence-with-self is not triangulation.*

This is the **circularity** shape of the common-cause null: the shared upstream is neither a neural channel nor a cultural lineage but the framework's own definitional machinery. Each addition to the catalogue feels like new evidence because it is a new *entry*, though the entry came from the same definitional procedure as the others. The catalogue grows; the evidential basis does not grow with it.

Three independent outer reviewers converged on that objection in July 2026, and the Map answered by withdrawing rather than trimming. The mitigation that had carried the argument — restricting the count to a *framework-independent* subset any serious view would acknowledge — fails at the same seam: deciding which voids a physicalist would grant is a judgement made from inside the framework whose support is at issue. That partition is drawn under the tenets too, so it cannot arbitrate between rivals; its residue is a discipline check, not framework-neutral evidence.

## The Calibration Mechanism

The discount works by per-cluster independence scoring rather than a uniform haircut. It scores across diagnostic categories — kinds of evidence whose generative procedures the framework does *not* fully control, so their convergence with it retains some independent force. The seven categories currently tracked:

1. **Empirical.** Behavioural, neurological, or experimental findings shaped by protocols and instruments rather than by the Map's tenets. A void grounded in measured behavioural dissociation is partly framework-independent: the dissociation appears in the data whichever framework labels it.
2. **Phenomenological.** First-person structural reports — retention-protention structure, felt unity, qualitative character — described under methods (Husserlian reduction, contemplative observation) older than and independent of the Map.
3. **Clinical.** Patient-population findings — aphantasia, alexithymia, hemispatial neglect, anosognosia — where anomalous introspective architecture is generated by physiology rather than by the framework; the [common-cause discipline](/project/common-cause-null/)'s preferred test class.
4. **Contemplative.** Reports from historically isolated traditions — Buddhist *jhānas*, Advaita non-dual states, apophatic mystical literature — whose categories predate the Map's tenets.
5. **Linguistic.** Cross-linguistic structure in mental-state vocabulary; categories that resist translation; expressive limits visible across language families.
6. **Quantum-theoretic.** Measurement, indeterminacy, and observer-dependence in formalisms whose mathematics the framework does not control. Independence is bounded: the Map's interpretation is framework-shaped even where the formalism is not.
7. **Cross-cultural.** Reports about consciousness from traditions with substantially different conceptual vocabularies; the cross-cultural sub-class of contemplative evidence, with the historical-isolation condition treated explicitly.

For each void cluster, the editor scores which categories supply independent evidence and which are framework-supplied. A cluster running primarily through categories 1 and 3 keeps most of its inferential weight. A cluster running primarily through *the framework's own definition of what would count as evidence here* keeps substantially less — sometimes none, where the framework supplies both question and answer.

Run over the catalogue as a whole, this scoring produced the withdrawal: weighting for independence collapsed the five void families toward perhaps two approximately independent clusters, and what survived was internal coherence rather than confirmation, since the framework individuates the clusters as well as populating them. Run over narrower claims — one cluster's patient-population anchoring, a cross-tradition contemplative parallel — it still discriminates between better- and worse-anchored cases, which is the work it now does.

The scoring is editor-vocabulary, recorded in changelog and deep-review notes. Articles express the resulting calibration in natural prose: *"the case rests on patient-population evidence and survives the circularity objection"*, *"the cluster's convergence is largely framework-internal and is presented as illustration rather than independent confirmation"*. The discount lives in the calibration the prose expresses, not in inserted disclaimers.

## The Prohibition: No Double-Counting of Tenet-Generated Voids

The hardest case is the [tenet-generated voids](/voids/tenet-generated-voids/) sub-catalogue: the nature void, the mechanism void, the detection void, the selection void, the meta-void — voids the Map *itself* identifies as generated by the very commitments whose support the catalogue argument sought. The framework adopts a commitment; the commitment leaves a question unanswerable; the unanswerable question is catalogued as a void; the catalogue is then offered as evidence the framework tracks something real. That is framework-internal coherence in its purest form.

The loop supplies no cumulative weight. The prohibition is therefore strict: **tenet-generated voids do not count toward any cumulative voids-convergence case**. They are honest acknowledgement of what the framework does not deliver, valuable as intellectual-humility infrastructure, and evidentially neutral for the framework generating them. They sit in the catalogue under their own tag, excluded from the cluster-counting any convergence claim relies on. The withdrawal left the prohibition intact: catalogue-wide, once the framework's role in individuating the voids was properly weighted, the aggregate argument turned out to be doing what the prohibition forbids.

The prohibition extends one step further: a void not explicitly tagged as tenet-generated but nevertheless inheriting its criteria wholly from a tenet's framing receives the same strict exclusion. The editor's test is the substitution check: *if a reviewer who fully accepts the Map's tenets reframed the criteria in a different, non-Map consciousness vocabulary, would the void still be identifiable?* A void that fails contributes nothing the catalogue did not already start with. The check is the diagnostic counterpart of the [evidential-status discipline](/project/evidential-status-discipline/)'s tenet-accepting-reviewer test, applied at the catalogue-membership boundary.

## Relation to the Sister Disciplines

The discount sits inside a family of named methodological patterns, each guarding a different inflation vector:

- [common-cause-null](/project/common-cause-null/) is the parent: the discount is its specific case, where the shared upstream is the framework's own definitional machinery.
- [evidential-status-discipline](/project/evidential-status-discipline/) operates one level below, at single empirical claims; the discount operates *over* collections of them and the structural argument they jointly support.
- [direct-refutation-discipline](/project/direct-refutation-discipline/) guards against tenet-incompatibility standing in for argument where in-framework refutation is feasible; the discount guards against catalogue-size standing in for evidence where independence is unearned.
- [coherence-inflation-countermeasures](/project/coherence-inflation-countermeasures/) catches argument-citation loops and uncertainty-squeezing; the discount catches what they do not reach — *cumulative voids-convergence reading framework-internal coherence as framework-independent confirmation*.

A void that has been catalogued, cross-linked, and tagged for cluster membership has passed several inflation checks. The discount asks one more: *does this entry add to the framework's evidential basis, or ratify a structure the framework already supplied?* Both answers are legitimate; only the first contributes evidential force. Asked of the catalogue in aggregate, the answer came back as the second.

## Honest Limitation: The Universal-Solvent Risk Recurs

The discount inherits the universal-solvent risk the [common-cause discipline](/project/common-cause-null/) names: every framework defines its own voids in some sense, so the discount could be wielded to dissolve any voids-catalogue argument anywhere. The licensing condition for treating the circularity objection as substantive is that the framework's definitional contribution be *specific* enough to predict the cluster's particular shape rather than merely that there be some clustering. *Consciousness is interesting* does not predict the binding void's architecture; *consciousness is irreducible to physical processes and the irreducible aspect is non-physical* does predict the shapes of the nature, mechanism, and detection voids. The first licenses calibration without dissolution; the second licenses prohibition of the count.

The discount is therefore graduated: full prohibition for tenet-generated voids whose shape the framework fully predicts; per-category scoring where its contribution is partial; minimal discount where definitional criteria are largely framework-external. The graduation keeps the discipline from dissolving convergence arguments indiscriminately while keeping any catalogue from claiming weight it has not earned. The Map's own catalogue reached the strict end because its tenets predict the shapes of its central clusters specifically enough to meet the licensing condition — an outcome the graduation is built to reach when it is earned, not a default applied to every catalogue that comes before it.

A second limitation: the discount applies asymmetrically, because the strongest defences — patient-population evidence, cross-substrate-within-one-organism observation, historically isolated contemplative traditions — are unevenly distributed. Clusters with strong clinical backing fare best; those resting on contemplative evidence without historical-isolation conditions fare worse; tenet-generated voids fare worst, by design.

## Relation to Site Perspective

The discount is the Map's most direct application of **[Tenet 5: Occam's Razor Has Limits](/tenets/)** at the meta-methodological level. Tenet 5 names premature parsimony: the materialist case treating the simpler reductive story as earned. The discount addresses the *symmetric* failure on the dualist side — premature confidence in the convergence's favour, treating the surface count of the catalogue as earned. Both are simplicity-overreach under incomplete knowledge; one collapses N observations into one upstream architecture, the other inflates one definitional procedure read N times into N independent observations. The discount turns Tenet 5 on the catalogue the Map itself maintains.

The catalogue keeps its value, and the withdrawal concerns evidential force alone. Voids document what the standard materialist programme has not delivered; the methodological claim that the shape of a limit illuminates the architecture meeting it holds for any view of mind, untouched by the circularity objection; the existence of beings who can ask what they cannot think remains a datum every framework owes an account of. What went was the step from *the catalogue documents many such territories* to *its many entries supply many-fold evidence for the framework that catalogues them* — retracted in public, in [what-voids-reveal](/voids/what-voids-reveal/) and [the catalogue index](/voids/), rather than by quiet deletion.
