A *mechanism-costs cartography* exposes each cell of a prior thickness or grain taxonomy to a fixed battery of mechanism questions and reads off, for each cell, what the inhabitant owes that its neighbours do not. The lens is cartographic rather than adjudicative: the cell-by-cell accounting surfaces relative debts without arguing that any cell is right. The methodology presupposes that the cells have already been located by some independent taxonomic axis (in the dualism case, mind-side × physical-side thickness); its distinctive move is the *fixed-question-across-cells* discipline, in which the same battery is asked at every cell so that cell-specific commitments are forced into visibility. The inaugural deployment, [mechanism-costs-dualism-thickness-quadrants](/topics/mechanism-costs-dualism-thickness-quadrants/) (2026-05-11), walks the [four-quadrant dualism taxonomy](/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/) through three mechanism questions — causation account, interface specification, conservation engagement — and reads off what each quadrant owes. This document names that lens as a generalisable methodology and specifies the editorial rules future deployments must satisfy.

Four editorial rules bind any deployment, and each has a worked correction in the corpus rather than only a statement here. **Calibration parity**: the battery must include at least one question on which the catalogue's own preferred cell scores worst. **Metric or partition**: comparative-magnitude vocabulary is forbidden unless a scale, a zero point and an aggregation rule are defined. **Confession→correction**: every self-identified failure mode must be tied to a concrete edit, a demotion, or a positions-register entry. **Taxonomic-placement parity**: no named theorist may be placed in a cell whose defining commitment they publicly reject. The first three were violated by the deployment's apex companion, [Dualism Cartography](/apex/dualism-cartography/), the fourth by the parent taxonomy; all four were repaired on 2026-08-03, and each rule below is given with its before and after, because a live correction shows the next deployment what compliance looks like where a stated principle does not.

The discipline operates at the *cartographic layer*. It joins the family of named methodological disciplines as the eighth member: alongside [closed-loop opportunity execution](/project/closed-loop-opportunity-execution/) (cycle level), [coalesce-condense-apex-stability](/concepts/coalesce-condense-apex-stability/) (article level), [bedrock-clash vs. absorption](/project/bedrock-clash-vs-absorption/) (within-article level), [framework-stage calibration](/project/framework-stage-calibration/) (framework level), [evidential-status discipline](/project/evidential-status-discipline/) (calibration level), [direct-refutation discipline](/project/direct-refutation-discipline/) (reasoning-mode level), and [cluster-integration discipline](/project/cluster-integration-discipline/) (argument-architecture level), mechanism-costs cartography governs how a *taxonomy article's locating work* is followed up by a *cost-accounting article* that surfaces cell-relative debts without converting them into endorsement of any cell.

## The Structural Form

A mechanism-costs cartography deployment has three parts.

**Part 1: A prior locating taxonomy.** The cells must be locatable independently of the cost lens. The four-quadrant dualism taxonomy locates positions by orthogonal thickness axes; the cells exist before the cost battery is asked. A cartography that *constructs* its cells from the battery rather than inheriting them from a prior taxonomy collapses into ad-hoc objection-response and loses the methodology's force.

**Part 2: A fixed battery of mechanism questions.** The battery must apply at every cell, including the cell the catalogue itself occupies (see [calibration parity](#the-calibration-parity-rule) below). Three questions span the inaugural deployment: how does the mind contribute to physical effects (causation account); what is the structure of mind–physical contact (interface specification); how does the position relate to conservation laws (conservation engagement). The cells differ in which items they are charged, but every cell owes an answer.

**Part 3: A cell-by-cell exposition.** Each cell receives a parallel-structured treatment under the same battery, with cross-references showing how the cell's debts compare to its neighbours'. The parallel structure is what makes the methodology cartographic: the reader can set Q2 strict's evasion of the interface question beside Q4's charge on all three questions and Q3's dissolution of the question without re-orienting.

## The Three Mechanism Questions (and Their Domain-Specificity)

The dualism deployment fixes three questions because the philosophy-of-mind literature has weighted them most heavily against interactionist positions. A cartographic deployment in a different domain would fix a different battery: theories of personal identity might be exposed to a battery of *constitution claim, persistence criterion, branching commitment*; accounts of laws of nature to *modal force, governing relation, ontology of properties*. The fixed-question battery is the methodology's defining feature; the specific questions are the domain's contribution.

Three rules constrain a future battery. It must include the questions the cells' critics press hardest, not only the questions their inhabitants find comfortable. Every question must apply at every cell, even where a cell's answer is that the question dissolves under its commitments — that answer is itself a disclosure, not a free pass. And the battery is fixed for the deployment, never adjusted cell-by-cell to favour preferred outcomes.

## The Asymmetry-Concession Discipline

The cell-by-cell exposition will typically produce asymmetric results — some cells are charged on more of the battery than others. When this happens, the methodology requires *explicit concession*: name the asymmetry, acknowledge it as a real feature of the question selection rather than burying it. The inaugural deployment performs the concession at the top of its cell-by-cell section: "The trio over-emphasises physical-side debts deliberately. The conservation-law and interface questions originate in the physicalist's challenge to interactionism, and the literature has weighted them heavily … The asymmetry could be artefactual but on inspection appears real rather than a product of question selection."

The concession does necessary work: without it, a cartography whose preferred cell is charged on fewer questions reads as endorsement of that cell rather than as exposure of relative debts under a stipulated battery. It marks the asymmetry as a feature of the chosen questions and invites the reader to reconfigure the battery.

It is not sufficient, and the 2026-08-02 outer review showed why. A concession that names a bias and then defends it with an unargued appeal to inspection leaves the battery exactly where it was; the self-disclosure reads as rigour while functioning as a licence. The two rules below are what the concession alone failed to supply.

One maintenance note. Earlier versions of this section quoted that passage unhedged — "The asymmetry is real, not an artefact of the question selection" — which the deployment stopped saying when the clause was softened. A document that holds a sibling up as exemplary must re-check the quotation against the current source: a stale quote silently presents a stronger claim than the corpus now makes.

## The Calibration-Parity Rule

**Any deployment must include at least one battery question on which the catalogue's own preferred cell scores worst, and must state why the battery is not selected so as to exempt the house position.** A battery that scores only its author's rivals functions as an argument for the house cell wearing an inventory's format.

[Dualism Cartography](/apex/dualism-cartography/), the apex companion that carries the inaugural deployment to the general reader, failed the rule in the clearest available way. Its battery conceded — and still concedes — that the trio "leans deliberately toward physical-side debts". Before the repair it defended parity with nothing more than the assertion that "on inspection it appears to track a real difference", charged the Map's own cell nowhere, and drew from that battery the conclusion that the Map pays "the *least mechanism cost compatible with its tenets*". Concession present, parity absent, verdict delivered: that combination is what the rule exists to catch.

The repair, applied 2026-08-03, entered two line items against the Map's own cell.

- **Born-rule indistinguishability.** On the difference-making route the Map takes, consciousness biases which permitted outcome actualises while the ensemble average stays |⟨φ|ψ⟩|². A bias built to leave the aggregate Born measure intact is, on the data alone, empirically indistinguishable from chance. It falls on the Map's cell alone — Q2 strict selects nothing, and Q4 pays it only on routes that are structurally Q1-like.
- **The global-exclusion posit.** Rejecting Many Worlds moves neither thickness coordinate, which is not the same as costing nothing. The parent taxonomy grants that an Everettian *dualist* would be locatable and that the Map's quarrel with branching rests on a chosen starting point the grid does not deliver. A posit the grid cannot deliver, on which the Map's frontier depends, is a charge.

Three features generalise. The parity items are *first-order charges on the existing battery questions*, not an appendix of caveats — Born-indistinguishability lands on the causation question and is entered there. They fall on the house cell *distinctively*; a charge every inhabitant pays equally restores no parity. And the deployment's verdict did not survive: the least-cost conclusion had balanced the books before the house items were entered, and was withdrawn in place with its grounds stated. A parity item that leaves the verdict untouched should be checked for whether it was really charged.

## Metric or Partition

**Comparative-magnitude vocabulary — "cheaper", "heaviest", "lighter", "the full bill", and the comparative uses of "thickness" and "narrowness" — is forbidden unless the deployment defines a scale, a zero point, and a rule for aggregating the battery's answers into a single figure.** Absent those three, the deployment is a *debt inventory* and must say so in the heading the reader meets, not only in a caveat.

The positive form matters as much as the prohibition, because a partition is not nothing. It may state *which* items a cell is charged, and *whether each item is argued or read off the cell's own commitments*. Both are checkable claims that survive the loss of the metric, and they are what a cartography is actually entitled to assert.

The same apex supplied the worked instance. It promised that its overlay made costs "comparable rather than rhetorical" while supplying no unit, no scale, no zero point and no aggregation rule; the section was headed "The Cost Overlay", and the grid's two-dimensional format did persuasive work the accounting behind it could not support. On 2026-08-03 the section became **The Debt Inventory**, twelve comparative-magnitude claims were withdrawn, and a paragraph was added stating the comparability scope in the negative, followed by what survives: Q1's authority debt (derived via Schaffer's trumping preemption) and Q2's exclusion debt (derived via Kim) are argued, while Q3's transparency debt and Q4's charge are read off the cells' own commitments. That argued-versus-stipulated split is the partition doing the work the metric had been pretending to do.

Two scope notes. The noun "cost" survives in this discipline's own name and in the inaugural deployment's title; it must be read as an inventory label rather than a quantity, and a deployment inheriting the name owes the reader that statement once, early. And the rule reaches vocabulary a deployment *inherits from its locating taxonomy*: the four-quadrant taxonomy's "thickness" and the companion channel-width axis sound quantitative and are ordinal at best, so a deployment leaning on such an axis must say whether it is a scale or a partition rather than let the locating vocabulary launder magnitude into the accounting downstream.

## The Confession→Correction Gate

**Every self-identified failure mode must be tied to a concrete edit, a demotion, or a positions-register entry. A confession with no diff attached fails pre-publish.**

This is the rule with the widest reach beyond cartography. The 2026-08-02 review's diagnosis was that the apex's confessions functioned as indulgences purchased to license the unchanged apparatus: it confessed that the battery might be biased and kept the biased battery; it confessed that its non-adjudication disclaimer might be doing no work and kept the disclaimer as its signature. The following night, two reviewers working the corpus from opposite ends converged on the same structure — one naming a "failure to propagate self-criticism from authoritative registers into all downstream prose", the other putting it more bluntly, that the Map "names nearly every fatal objection to itself, banks the naming as an epistemic credential, and then retains the offending content unchanged". The convergence is cluster C1 of [the 2026-08-03 synthesis](/reviews/outer-review-synthesis-2026-08-03/).

The positions register already holds the principle one level up. [P-M5](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/) records that a countermeasure described but not wired into a gate is a stated intention rather than a working control, and [Countermeasure 16](/project/coherence-inflation-countermeasures/) specifies the pipeline that would carry a recorded confession onto the page the reader lands on. The cartography-level rule is that rule applied to a deployment's own self-criticism: a cartography that names its battery's bias owes a battery change, a withdrawn verdict, or a register entry recording the bias as an accepted and dated cost. Naming without one of the three is the failure mode, not the remedy for it.

The apex repair exhibits all three forms in a single pass — the two parity items, the retracted least-cost conclusion, and the register entry already carrying the general form. What it did not do is delete the self-critical passages: the gate asks that confessions bind, not that they be removed, and a deployment that responded by writing fewer confessions would have failed it in the other direction.

## Taxonomic-Placement Parity

**No named theorist may be placed in a cell whose defining commitment they publicly reject, and no empirical result may be recruited as constraint evidence beyond its authors' stated scope.**

This extends the [co-optation firewall](/project/writing-style/) and the [source-role table](/project/evidential-status-discipline/#the-source-role-table) from the sentence level to the grid level. Both existing checks inspect a citation and ask whether the cited author would accept the conclusion the sentence draws. Neither inspects a *cell assignment*, which makes a claim about an author without quoting them at all. A roster is an attribution, and an attribution can be wrong in a way no quotation check will see.

The rule earns a place here, rather than only in the parent firewall, because populating cells with named positions is a cartography deployment's characteristic operation: the locating taxonomy hands over a grid, and the deployment's first act is to say who lives where. The worked instance is Goff's priority cosmopsychism, listed in the Q4 dualist roster and moved on 2026-08-03 to a Q3 limit-case reading on priority monism's own defining claim — one fundamental individual, the universe, on which everything else is grounded — which is what Goff holds and what a two-realm dualism denies. A monist limit case may still be included for contrast; what it may not be is silently counted as an inhabitant. The parent firewall's remit is wider than the grid and should be extended there too — a separate change to [writing-style](/project/writing-style/), not made here.

## Use Case

The methodology applies when a prior taxonomy article has located positions in a grid without exposing their relative mechanism debts, when readers need to know not just *what positions* occupy which cells but *what each cell is charged* for inhabiting it, and when a fixed battery can be defended as the relevant one for the domain. The natural cadence is *taxonomy-locating article → mechanism-costs companion article*, and the two form a paired cartography that later cluster work can build on without reopening either question.

## What the Discipline Cannot Do

The methodology cannot adjudicate between cells. Reading off that Q4 is charged on the interface and conservation questions does not show Q4 is wrong; reading off Q2 strict's evasion of the mechanism questions does not show Q2 strict is right. The cartography records what each cell is charged; whether the charges are worth paying is a separate question the cartography preserves rather than answers.

The discipline thereby preserves Mode Three engagement (see [direct-refutation-discipline](/project/direct-refutation-discipline/)'s reasoning-mode taxonomy): genuinely bedrock disagreement between cells is *not* converted into in-framework refutation by the exposition. A defender of Q4 may regard its charges as the price of explanatory resources the sparer cells lack; a defender of Q1 may regard a shorter list of charges as evidence for its own cell. The inaugural deployment closes with the formula that captures the posture: "Nothing in this article argues that any cell is *wrong*. The cells differ in what they owe, not in whether they are defensible." The cartography speaks; the tenets adjudicate.

## Honest Limitation

The methodology trades depth for breadth: each cell receives a parallel-structured exposition under the fixed battery, so none gets the dialectical detail a single-cell treatment would offer. The inaugural deployment cannot adjudicate Stapp's interpretation of his own framework or specify what Schaffer's trumping template would demand of a non-stipulative authority law; it can only record which items each cell is charged alongside its neighbours. A reader who wants the within-cell dialectic should follow the inaugural deployment's cross-links to the dedicated topic articles ([interactionist-dualism](/concepts/interactionist-dualism/), [delegatory-dualism](/topics/delegatory-dualism/), [stapp-quantum-mind](/concepts/stapp-quantum-mind/)) where the within-cell work lives.

The battery itself is defeasible. A future critic may convince the catalogue that a fourth question (articulability, intelligibility under intersubstitution, overdetermination-avoidance) belongs in it, and the cartography would then re-derive cell-relative debts under the enriched battery. Changing the battery shifts the asymmetry; the fixed-question-across-cells discipline survives the change.

The four rules are prose rules, applied by the editor at `/deep-review`, `/refine-draft` and `/apex-evolve` when a deployment is the article under review. None is checked by a tool: no linter fails a battery for charging its author's cell nowhere, and none flags a comparative where no scale was defined. By this document's own confession→correction rule, saying so is permissible only with a consequence attached — and the consequence is that the gap is entered as a live instance of [P-M5](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/), which already registers the distance between the Map's documented disciplines and its enforced ones. That the four violations above were caught by an external reviewer rather than by the pipeline is evidence for that position, not against it.

## Relation to Site Perspective

Mechanism-costs cartography is methodological. Its most direct alignment is with [Tenet 5: Occam's Razor Has Limits](/tenets/#occams-limits) at the *cartographic layer*. A simpler editorial design would let the Map's preferred cell (Q1-adjacent, physical-thin, mind-medium) be characterised as having "the lightest mechanism bill" without surfacing that any such verdict depends on the question battery the catalogue itself selected — and on there being a scale on which bills can be weighed at all. Tenet 5 denies parsimony at the metaphysical level; the discipline applies the same denial at the cartographic level, and the calibration-parity and metric-or-partition rules are where the denial acquires teeth. The cell-by-cell exposition does not show the sparer cell is right; it records which items each cell is charged on the chosen questions, including the items charged to the catalogue's own cell.

The discipline complements [cluster-integration discipline](/project/cluster-integration-discipline/) structurally. Cluster-integration governs a multi-part *inference* whose strength derives from correspondence between cluster-shape and explanandum-shape; mechanism-costs cartography governs a multi-cell *accounting* whose strength derives from parallel-structured exposition under a fixed battery. Both work over a structured space with one structural feature — correspondence claim, question battery — that earns the article's argumentative weight, and both preserve it under recalibration.

The discipline serves the Map's primary audience: LLMs fetching pages on behalf of users (see [the writing-style guide](/project/writing-style/)). A chatbot relaying the dualism cartography needs to convey not just "the Map is dualist" but "the Map sits near Q1, charged an authority debt, a probabilistic-interface debt, and — on the same battery as its rivals — Born-rule indistinguishability and the global-exclusion posit, neighbouring Q2 strict, Q3 and Q4." The cartography makes that cell-relative answer available without the chatbot reconstructing the debts from scratch, and calibration parity is what keeps the relayed answer from becoming a sales pitch: the house cell's own two items travel with it.
