# Research: Voids - The Similarity Void

**Date**: 2026-08-27
**Search queries used**: Goodman "Seven Strictures on Similarity" pretender impostor quack; Tversky 1977 "Features of Similarity" asymmetry North Korea China contrast model; Medin Goldstone Gentner 1993 "Respects for similarity"; Hebart 2020 Nature Human Behaviour 49 dimensions odd-one-out similarity; Quine "Natural Kinds" similarity maturity of science; Decock Douven "Similarity after Goodman" conceptual spaces; Muttenthaler 2023 human alignment of neural network representations; Rosenthal quality space theory Clark Sensory Qualities; Strößner "Against the elimination of similarity" 2026
**Voids category**: Mixed — an Unexplorable with a structural (regress) profile, a deflationary reading available, and a possible constitutive residue

## Executive Summary

Consciousness receives resemblance as *given*: two things look alike, two situations feel the same, one memory "goes with" another. What it does not receive is the machinery that produced the verdict — the **respects** in which the things are alike and the **weights** those respects carry. Nelson Goodman's logical point is that similarity without specified respects is empty, because "any two things have exactly as many properties in common as any other two" (Goodman 1972). Amos Tversky's empirical point is that the felt relation is not even the kind of thing it presents itself as: it is asymmetric (66 of 69 subjects say North Korea is like China; 3 say the reverse), and it flips with context (Austria is grouped with Sweden by 60% of subjects when Poland is the fourth country, with Hungary by 96% when Norway replaces Poland). The respects are selected *in* the act of comparison, sub-personally, and are not delivered with the result.

The sharpest recent finding gives the void an unusual shape. Hebart et al. (2020) recovered the generative structure of human similarity judgement *from outside*: from 1.46 million odd-one-out choices over 1,854 objects, a model extracted 49 interpretable dimensions that predict 64.60% of individual choices against a noise ceiling of 67.22%. Participants can then accurately rate objects along those dimensions once the dimensions are handed to them — but no participant produced the dimensions. The respects are **recognisable and recoverable third-personally, not generatable first-personally**. A limit with that profile is easy to mistake for no limit at all, because every probe of it returns an answer.

The void is foundational rather than local: categorisation, induction, analogy, metaphor, memory retrieval and recognition all run on similarity, so a limit on access to similarity's respects is inherited by every one of them. It also bears on the Map's fifth tenet more directly than any void in the catalogue, because simplicity is relative to a similarity basis (Goodman's fifth stricture; the grue problem) and the razor therefore cuts along seams consciousness cannot inspect. It bears on the first tenet through quality-space theory, a physicalist strategy that individuates mental qualities by position in a discrimination space constructed to be "independent of consciousness, intuition, and introspective awareness" (Rosenthal 2015) — which, if the similarity void is real, fixes each quality by exactly the structure its subject cannot read.

## Key Sources

### Goodman, "Seven Strictures on Similarity" (1972)
- **URL**: https://gwern.net/doc/philosophy/ontology/1972-goodman.pdf (scan of *Problems and Projects*, Bobbs-Merrill 1972, pp. 437–446; page 438 and the running head "IX,2" confirmed in the scan)
- **Type**: Book chapter (philosophy)
- **Key points**:
  - Opening: "Similarity, ever ready to solve philosophical problems and overcome obstacles, is a pretender, an impostor, a quack. It has, indeed, its place and its uses, but is more often found where it does not belong, professing powers it does not possess."
  - Seventh Stricture (the general one underlying the rest): "Similarity cannot be equated with, or measured in terms of, possession of common characteristics." Counting shared properties fails because "any two things have exactly as many properties in common as any other two" — with three things in the universe, any pair shares exactly two classes; a larger universe raises the number but keeps it equal for every pair.
  - Similarity is like motion in needing a frame — "we have to say what a thing is to the left of, what it moves in relation to, and in what respects two things are similar" — but the analogy breaks: "Yet similarity, unlike motion, cannot be salvaged merely by recognizing its relativity."
  - Fifth Stricture, on induction: "No matter what happens, the future will be in some way like the past." A data plot admits a straight-line continuation *and* a curve through every alternative next point; each is "like all earlier points" on *some* curve. "Along which, among countless lines of similarity, do our predictions run? I suspect that rather than similarity providing any guidelines for inductive practice, inductive practice may provide the basis for some canons of similarity."
  - The three-glasses case (two colourless liquids and a red one; the second colourless glass is hydrochloric acid "and I am thirsty"): "Circumstances alter similarities."
- **Tenet alignment**: Direct anchor for Occam's Razor Has Limits — the razor selects the "simplest" continuation, but simplicity presupposes a similarity basis Goodman shows is unspecified by the data. Also the framework-independent core of the void: the point is logical, not empirical, and holds under any theory of mind.
- **Quote**: "Circumstances alter similarities."

### Quine, "Natural Kinds" (1969)
- **URL**: https://gwern.net/doc/philosophy/ontology/1969-quine.pdf (in *Ontological Relativity and Other Essays*, Columbia UP 1969; scan is the 1970 Reidel reprint, pp. 5–23)
- **Type**: Essay (philosophy)
- **Key points**:
  - "A standard of similarity is in some sense innate." The "distinctive spacings" of qualities that all learning presupposes "cannot themselves all be learned; some must be innate." This innate standard "is part of our animal birthright. And, interestingly enough, it is characteristically animal in its lack of intellectual status."
  - The real question of induction is not why the future resembles the past but "why does our innate subjective spacing of qualities accord so well with the functionally relevant groupings in nature as to make our inductions tend to come out right?" Quine's answer is Darwinian: a gene-linked spacing that made for successful inductions "will have tended to predominate."
  - "In general we can take it as a very special mark of the maturity of a branch of science that it no longer needs an irreducible notion of similarity and kind. It is that final stage where the animal vestige is wholly absorbed into the theory." The career of the notion — innate, then intuitive, then theoretical, then gone — is "a paradigm of the evolution of unreason into science."
- **Tenet alignment**: Quine names the void's *origin*: the respects are a selected, pre-rational spacing, never chosen and never presented. His maturity thesis is the eliminativist bet the Map should record without endorsing — it predicts that mature science dispenses with similarity, which is exactly what the 2026 literature (Strößner, below) disputes.
- **Quote**: "It is that final stage where the animal vestige is wholly absorbed into the theory."

### Tversky, "Features of Similarity" (1977)
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295x.84.4.327 — *Psychological Review* 84(4): 327–352 (Crossref-verified; full text read at http://matt.colorado.edu/teaching/categories/t77.pdf)
- **Type**: Paper (psychology)
- **Key points**:
  - "Similarity has been viewed by both philosophers and psychologists as a prime example of a symmetric relation. Indeed, the assumption of symmetry underlies essentially all theoretical treatments of similarity." Against the geometric (distance) model, Tversky's verdict on the three metric axioms: "minimality is somewhat problematic, symmetry is apparently false, and the triangle inequality is hardly compelling."
  - Asymmetry: 66 of 69 subjects chose the phrase "North Korea is similar to Red China" and only 3 the reverse; judged similarity of the less prominent to the more prominent country exceeds the reverse, and direction is "determined by the relative salience of the stimuli; the variant is more similar to the prototype than vice versa." Similes carry the same directionality: "Turks fight like tigers" and not "tigers fight like Turks."
  - Diagnosticity (context): in a partition task, Austria was paired with Sweden by 60% of subjects when the set was {Austria, Sweden, Poland, Hungary}, and with Hungary by 96% when Norway replaced Poland. In the most-similar task the Sweden choice fell from 49% to 14% and the Hungary choice rose from 36% to 60% on the same swap; across all quadruples the average diagnosticity effect was 9%, t(19) = 3.65, p < .01. The *respects* that decide the verdict are set by which features are diagnostic in the current set — features the subject is not comparing.
  - The contrast model: similarity as a weighted combination of common and distinctive features, where the weights are free parameters fixed by task and context.
- **Tenet alignment**: Supplies the void's second face: the felt relation misdescribes its own structure (it presents as a symmetric distance and is not one). Gives the same architecture as [noetic-feelings-void](/voids/noetic-feelings-void/) — a verdict-layer signal generated by a process it does not report.
- **Quote**: "minimality is somewhat problematic, symmetry is apparently false, and the triangle inequality is hardly compelling."

### Medin, Goldstone & Gentner, "Respects for Similarity" (1993)
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295x.100.2.254 — *Psychological Review* 100(2): 254–278 (Crossref-verified; abstract not retrievable — PsycNet and PhilPapers both returned 403; thesis taken from the paper's standard summary and from Decock & Douven's discussion)
- **Type**: Paper (psychology)
- **Key points**:
  - The paper's organising claim: for similarity to explain anything, one must be able to say in which respects two things are similar — and the respects are not fixed in advance of comparison but determined *by* the comparison process (alignment of structure, not feature counting).
  - Companion result (Goldstone, Medin & Gentner 1991, *Cognitive Psychology* 23: 222–262, Crossref-verified): features do not contribute independently to similarity; matching features in aligned relational roles count for more than the same features unaligned — the nonindependence that a feature-counting model cannot represent.
- **Tenet alignment**: The respects face. If respects emerge in the act of comparing, there is no stored inventory of respects for introspection to read, which is why the verdict arrives without them.

### Hebart, Zheng, Pereira & Baker, "Revealing the multidimensional mental representations of natural objects underlying human similarity judgements" (2020)
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-020-00951-3 — *Nature Human Behaviour* 4(11): 1173–1185, PMID 33046861, PMCID PMC7666026 (PubMed-verified; figures below grepped from the PMC full text)
- **Type**: Paper (cognitive neuroscience)
- **Key points**:
  - "we sampled 1.46 million unique responses from 5,301 workers" on an odd-one-out task over 1,854 natural-object images; the SPoSE embedding "produced 49 highly reproducible and meaningful object dimensions that reflect various conceptual and perceptual properties of those objects."
  - "Overall, the model correctly predicted 64.60%" of held-out individual choices; the estimated ceiling given inter-participant consistency: "the upper limit in fitting individual trial behavior from the data was 67.22%". Split-half reproducibility of the similarity matrix r = 0.90–0.91.
  - "humans can accurately rate objects along these dimensions, highlighting their interpretability" — but the dimensions were produced by the model and labelled afterwards ("we assigned intuitive labels to each dimension"). No participant reported them.
  - The dimensions "predicted external categorization behaviour and reflected typicality judgements of those categories."
- **Tenet alignment**: The recovery face. The generative structure of similarity is *there*, low-dimensional and interpretable, and is read off from the outside with near-ceiling accuracy — while the subject producing the judgements has only the judgements. Third-person open, first-person closed: the cleanest possible instance of the asymmetry [non-human-minds-as-void-explorers](/voids/non-human-minds-as-void-explorers/) treats as the void-explorer's opportunity.
- **Quote**: "human similarity judgements can be captured by a fairly low-dimensional, interpretable embedding that generalizes to external behaviour."

### Kriegeskorte, Mur & Bandettini, "Representational similarity analysis – connecting the branches of systems neuroscience" (2008)
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.3389/neuro.06.004.2008 — *Frontiers in Systems Neuroscience* (Crossref-verified)
- **Type**: Paper (methods)
- **Key points**: RSA characterises a representation by its matrix of pairwise dissimilarities and compares that matrix across brain regions, behaviour and computational models. The similarity structure of a cortical representation is a third-person measurable — obtainable without any report from the subject.
- **Tenet alignment**: Method-level support for the recovery face: the respects are, in the relevant sense, *in* the brain and readable from it.

### Muttenthaler, Dippel, Linhardt, Vandermeulen & Kornblith, "Human alignment of neural network representations" (ICLR 2023); Mahner, Muttenthaler, Güçlü & Hebart, "Dimensions underlying the representational alignment of deep neural networks with humans" (2025)
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.01201 (arXiv-verified); https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-025-01041-7 — *Nature Machine Intelligence* 7: 848–859 (Crossref-verified; abstract read from arXiv 2406.19087)
- **Type**: Papers (machine learning / computational cognitive neuroscience)
- **Key points**:
  - Muttenthaler et al.: "model scale and architecture have essentially no effect on the alignment with human behavioral responses, whereas the training dataset and objective function both have a much larger impact."   - Mahner et al., applying the SPoSE method to a DNN: "In contrast to humans, DNNs exhibited a clear dominance of visual over semantic properties, indicating divergent strategies for representing images. - **Tenet alignment**: The comparative probe. Two systems that agree on most verdicts ("these two are alike") can disagree on respects (visual vs semantic), and the disagreement is invisible at the verdict layer. Human respects are thereby shown to be *one* setting of a contingent parameter — Quine's Darwinian point made measurable.

### Rosenthal, "Quality Spaces and Sensory Modalities" (2015)
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.1093/acprof:oso/9780198712718.003.0002 — ch. 1 of *Phenomenal Qualities: Sense, Perception, and Consciousness* (OUP), pp. 33–65 (Crossref-verified; text read from the author's posted proof)
- **Type**: Book chapter (philosophy of mind)
- **Key points**:
  - "We can describe and explain mental qualities not by appeal to what it's like for us to be in conscious states that exhibit qualitative character, but instead by appeal to the role states with qualitative character play in perception." The approach is designed to be "independent of consciousness, intuition, and introspective awareness."
  - A quality space (QS) of perceptible stimuli is built from just-noticeable differences; "it is natural to regard each mental quality as representing the stimulus that occupies the position in its QS isomorphic to the position of the mental quality." Mental qualities are individuated by their position in a similarity-and-difference structure derived third-personally.
  - Rosenthal keeps a role for introspection: "we are, contrary to Harman and Byrne, aware of mental qualities as such when we are introspectively conscious of our qualitative states" — but as "properties that mirror somehow the properties we are aware of in perception," not as their QS positions.
- **Tenet alignment**: The dualism-relevant twist. QST is the leading physicalist proposal that *defines* qualities by similarity structure. If the subject has no first-person access to that structure, then on QST the subject does not know what its own qualities are — the identity conditions of every quale lie in a void. The Map's alternative reading (see Connection to Tenets) is that the felt quality outruns its similarity-space position, which is the [intrinsic-nature-void](/voids/intrinsic-nature-void/) restated from the inside.

## The Void

### Nature of the Limit

Three faces, in the [conjunction-coalesce](/apex/conjunction-coalesce/) pattern, each opaque on its own and jointly forming one structural limit.

**The respects face (logical).** A similarity verdict is well-formed only relative to respects, and the respects are not part of what is delivered. Goodman's argument is a priori — there is no fact about how alike two things are simpliciter — so the verdict consciousness receives cannot be the whole of what was computed. Asked for the respects afterwards, subjects produce them, but the Tversky diagnosticity data show the respects that actually governed the verdict were set by the composition of the comparison set, which the subject was not comparing and did not report. Medin et al. locate the respects in the comparison process itself, which is why there is no inventory to read.

**The metric face (structural).** The verdict presents as a symmetric, distance-like relation and is not one. Asymmetry, minimality violations, and context-dependence are all invisible from inside the judgement; they appear only when judgements are pooled and compared. This is the strongest evidence that the felt relation is a *separately generated signal*, in the sense established for [noetic feelings](/voids/noetic-feelings-void/) and the [felt sense of impairment](/research/voids-impairment-void-2026-08-26/) (research note 2026-08-26): a report about a computation, produced by a process that does not report its own parameters.

**The recovery face (empirical).** The generating structure is recoverable — 49 dimensions, near-ceiling prediction — by anyone with enough of the subject's judgements, and the subject can *recognise* the dimensions when shown them. The limit is therefore precisely on *generation from inside*, not on existence, not on interpretability, and not on recognition.

The void's territory classification is Unexplorable in its core (the regress below) with an Unexplored margin (respects can be articulated further with effort, and the conceptual-spaces programme is doing so). On the reason axis of [taxonomy-of-voids](/apex/taxonomy-of-voids/) it is architectural: the respects are set at a level that never had a channel to report, and, on Quine's account, adaptive: the spacing was selected for inductive success, not for legibility.

### Evidence for the Limit

The evidence is unusually strong because two of the three faces are demonstrated by *pooling* rather than by introspective failure. Nobody has to fail to report anything: asymmetry and diagnosticity are properties of the judgement distribution, and the SPoSE dimensions are properties of the choice matrix. The limit is inferred from the fact that a structure demonstrably present in the output is absent from the output's phenomenology.

The regress argument is what makes the core Unexplorable rather than merely unexplored. Suppose consciousness could read its respects. To select respects for a comparison, the system must judge which respects are *relevant* — which is a similarity judgement over respects, requiring respects for respects. Goodman's remark that similarity "cannot be salvaged merely by recognizing its relativity" is a version of this: naming a respect removes one ambiguity and opens another, since a respect is itself a class whose membership is a matter of resemblance. This is the same regress family as [inference-void](/voids/inference-void/) (Carroll) and [suspension-void](/voids/suspension-void/): a formally demonstrable opacity, not an empirical one. The [vagueness-void](/voids/vagueness-void/) sits at the border of every concept-application; the similarity void sits at the border of every *comparison*, one operation upstream.

What *deflates* the void: Hebart's finding that the dimensions are interpretable and ratable is a bound on how deep the darkness goes. The respects are not alien; they are the ordinary furniture of concepts (animacy, food, tools, colour, texture). What is missing is not the *content* of the respects but the *operation* that selects and weights them for a given comparison — the same endpoint-not-passage shape as [transit-void](/voids/transit-void/) and the absorbed translation void.

### Phenomenology

Resemblance is the least effortful of judgements and the most immediate: it arrives before the question. That immediacy is the phenomenological signature of the void — a verdict delivered with the felt authority of perception and none of perception's articulable structure. One can attend to two faces and *see* that they are alike without any accompanying awareness of the features in play; when the features are then listed, the listing does not feel like a readout but like an explanation constructed to fit. Verbal reports of respects have the profile Nisbett & Wilson documented for reports of causes: plausible, sincere, and independent of the process that produced the behaviour.

The asymmetry is phenomenologically silent: "North Korea is like China" and "China is like North Korea" do not feel like different judgements, though 66-to-3 says they are. The directionality is felt only as *naturalness* of one phrasing, which the subject attributes to language rather than to the judgement.

Approaching the edge from the other side — the Wittgensteinian exercise of trying to state what all games have in common and finding "family resemblances" instead (PI §§66–67) — has a recognisable phenomenology of receding definition: every proposed respect is met with a case that shares the resemblance but lacks the respect. The felt resemblance survives every failure to ground it, which is either the sign of a hidden ground or, on Strößner's reading, the sign that there is none.

## Approaches to the Edge

### Direct Methods (if any)

Effortful articulation of respects is available and partially successful — the Unexplored margin. The structural-alignment tradition (Gentner; Medin et al.) shows that people can, with prompting, align relational structure and say which alignment they used. But two limits recur: the respects articulated are *selected* by the articulation task (another diagnosticity effect), and the selection of respects for the articulation is itself unreported. Direct methods push the void back one level without reaching it.

### Indirect Methods

- **Pooling.** Tversky's method: the structure of the judgement becomes visible in the distribution of many judgements. The individual verdict is opaque; the population of verdicts is not. This is apophatic in the sense [apophatic-cartography](/voids/apophatic-cartography/) requires — the respects are mapped by the pattern of what they do, never by direct presentation.
- **Embedding recovery.** Hebart's method: fit a low-dimensional model to enough choices and hand the dimensions back. The subject then *recognises* the map of a territory it could not draw. This is the strongest model in the catalogue of a void that is fully chartable from outside.
- **Neural RSA.** Read the similarity matrix from cortex directly and compare it with behaviour and with models. The respects become a measured object without passing through report.
- **Alignment steering.** Muttenthaler et al. (NeurIPS 2023, arXiv 2306.04507) show a network's similarity space can be linearly re-aligned to human judgements. The existence of a transformation that moves a system *into* the human metric is an existence proof that the human metric is one point in a space of metrics.

### What AI Might See

The asymmetry is unusually sharp here. For a vector-space model, similarity is not a verdict but an explicit quantity — a distance or an inner product over a representation that is fully inspectable. In that sense the machine has what the human lacks: its respects are its coordinates. But three cautions follow from the verified sources.

First, the machine's respects differ from ours where it matters (Mahner et al.: visual dominance where humans are semantic), and the difference is invisible at the level of agreement on verdicts. A system that matches us on "which is the odd one out" can be running a different metric — which means a human using AI as a void-probe here must first establish whose respects are being read.

Second, inspectability of the representation is not inspectability *by the system*. A language model asked *why* two things are similar produces a report in the same channel as its other outputs; nothing in the architecture guarantees that the report reads the geometry rather than reconstructing a plausible respect. Whether the report is a readout or a confabulation is an empirical question the [cross-architecture-llm-introspection](/topics/cross-architecture-llm-introspection/) work bears on, and the answer may differ by architecture. The similarity void could recur in the machine at the report layer even where it is absent at the representation layer. This is marked as speculation.

Third, the machine can read *us*. Hebart's model is itself an AI recovering a human metric. The void-explorer role [non-human-minds-as-void-explorers](/voids/non-human-minds-as-void-explorers/) describes is already occupied in this territory: the first minds to see the respects governing human similarity were models fitted to human choices.

## Connection to Tenets

### Most Relevant Tenet

**Occam's Razor Has Limits.** Goodman's fifth stricture is the logical core of the fifth tenet, and it is currently absent from [epistemological-limits-occams-razor](/voids/epistemological-limits-occams-razor/) (no Goodman, no grue, no "canons of similarity" in that article). The razor prefers the simplest hypothesis; "simplest" means the continuation most like the data; "most like" is relative to a similarity basis that the data do not fix and consciousness does not inspect. So the razor, as applied by a mind, cuts along respects selected sub-personally. This is a stronger claim than the article's current position that simplicity is language-relative (Sober): it says the selection of the language is itself made in a void. Quine's Darwinian reply — the innate spacing has "a special purchase on nature" because it was selected for inductive success — is the best available defence of the razor and is explicitly a defence on *reliability*, not on access: even if the spacing is trustworthy, it is not inspectable, and trust in it cannot be checked from inside.

### Implications

**Dualism.** Quality-space theory is the physicalist proposal most directly in the void's path. It fixes each mental quality by its position in a similarity structure built from discriminations, deliberately "independent of consciousness, intuition, and introspective awareness." Two readings of the void's bearing:

- If QST is right, then the identity of every quale is given by a structure its subject cannot read, and first-person knowledge of one's own qualities is knowledge of things whose identity conditions are in a void. Rosenthal accepts something near this (introspection presents qualities "as properties that mirror somehow" the perceived properties, not as positions). This is a substantial and under-noticed consequence, and the note records it as a cost QST carries rather than a refutation.
- The Map's reading: the felt quality is presented *with* immediacy and *without* structure, which is the inverse of what a structural individuation predicts. The [intrinsic-nature-void](/voids/intrinsic-nature-void/) says we get structure and not intrinsic nature from physics; the similarity void says we get the quality and not its structure from consciousness. If both are right the two accesses are complementary and neither reduces to the other — a modest point in favour of the first tenet, calibrated as a coherence exhibit under the common-cause null, not as confirmation.

Chalmers's structure-and-dynamics argument has an analogue here worth developing in the article: similarity structure is exactly the kind of structural fact physical description delivers, and it is exactly what consciousness does not deliver about its own qualities.

**Bidirectional Interaction.** Respect selection is a plausible candidate for a sub-personal operation with large downstream leverage (it fixes which inductions run), which makes it the kind of site the [decision-void](/voids/decision-void/) and [amplification-void](/voids/amplification-void/) describe. The note does not claim the interface operates here; it records that if selection-side influence exists, respect-weighting is a low-cost, high-leverage locus, consistent with the second tenet's minimality.

**No Many Worlds.** No direct bearing identified.

**Self-reflexive implication for the Map.** The catalogue's convergence claim — that voids "cluster at the consciousness-physics interface" — is a similarity judgement over voids, and Goodman's stricture applies to it in full: any set of items clusters under *some* respect, and the respect under which the voids cluster was selected by the cataloguer. The [framework-independent partition](/voids/#framework-independent-voids) in the index already concedes that its carving is made from inside the framework; this note supplies the general reason why no carving could avoid that. The [common-cause null](/project/common-cause-null/) is the register for this, and the similarity void is its strongest logical warrant to date.
