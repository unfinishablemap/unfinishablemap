# Research: Voids - The Reafference Void

**Date**: 2026-08-22
**Search queries used**: sensory attenuation self-generated touch prediction sharpening debate 2025 review; philosophy epistemic consequences efference copy comparator sense of agency self-knowledge limits Synofzik Vosgerau; Crossref/Europe PMC metadata verification for each citation below
**Voids category**: Unexplorable (structural), with an Occluded reading available and declined

**Verdict up front: FOLD, not a standalone article.** Recommendation is a primary fold into [the predictive construction void](/voids/predictive-construction-void/) (which has ~500 words of headroom against the voids hard threshold) plus a one-sentence pointer in [the agency void](/voids/agency-void/). This note does **not** claim the catalogue's final slot. See "Slot-or-Fold Decision" below for why the merits, not just cap pressure, point that way.

## Executive Summary

The nervous system distinguishes sensory input it caused (reafference) from input the world caused (exafference), and it discounts the former before it reaches awareness. Self-generated touch is felt as weaker than identical externally generated touch; the same suppression appears in vestibular, auditory and tactile systems across rodents, non-human primates and humans. Consciousness therefore never receives the undiscounted version of its own touch, its own voice, or the retinal displacement its own eye movement produced — it receives a residue from which its own contribution has already been subtracted, by an operation it did not perform and cannot inspect.

Two things make this more than a restatement of "perception is unconscious below the level of report." First, the deletion is *indexed to the perceiver's own agency*: the discount is applied in proportion to what the subject did. Second, the same comparator machinery that performs the subtraction is a major source of the felt sense of authorship — so the phenomenal signature of having caused something is downstream of the operation that removed the evidence. Consciousness gets the verdict, never the comparison.

The strongest evidence that this is genuinely inaccessible from inside is the shape of the scientific dispute. Whether action attenuates or *enhances* predicted touch was argued through pre-registered experiments from 2021 to 2026 (Thomas et al. 2022 vs. Job & Kilteni 2023, with a 2026 preprint from the enhancement group proposing a temporal reconciliation). The disputed question is what a self-generated touch feels like — and no participant on either side could settle it by looking.

## Key Sources

### Blakemore, Wolpert & Frith (1998) — the founding demonstration
- **DOI**: 10.1038/2870 (verified at Crossref and Europe PMC)
- **Type**: Paper, *Nature Neuroscience* 1(7), 635–640
- **Key points**:
  - Self-produced tactile stimulation is perceived as less ticklish than identical external stimulation.
  - fMRI: more somatosensory cortex activity for externally produced stimuli; less cerebellar activity when a movement generated a tactile consequence than when it did not.
  - Abstract's own reading: the cerebellum predicts "the specific sensory consequences of movements, providing the signal that is used to cancel the sensory response to self-generated stimulation."
- **Tenet alignment**: framework-independent; the mechanism is physical and a physicalist predicts it as readily as a dualist.
- **Note**: the Map already carries this citation at [volitional-control](/topics/volitional-control/), along with the clinical follow-up (Blakemore et al. 2000) and the correction that the clinical claim is about *reduced attenuation in specific symptom groups*, not a categorical capacity.

### Kilteni, Cullen, Schneider & Schwarz (2025) — the current cross-species synthesis
- **DOI**: 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.1351-25.2025 (verified; abstract retrieved via Europe PMC)
- **Type**: Review, *The Journal of Neuroscience* 45(46), e1351252025
- **Key points**:
  - "Reafference attenuation is a temporally precise process that suppresses expected feedback, facilitating the detection of novel stimuli."
  - Reviewed across rodents, non-human primates and humans, and across vestibular, auditory and tactile systems.
  - Explicitly separates reafference attenuation from *sensory gating* — a broader, less selective suppression of both self- and externally generated input. The two "differ in function, specificity, and temporal dynamics."
  - Closes on open questions "about their generality and implementation" — the review does not treat the mechanism as settled.
- **Why it matters here**: the void is not a quirk of one paradigm. The subtraction is a general architectural feature of nervous systems that move.

### Kilteni & Ehrsson (2022) — attenuation and gating are distinct
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.isci.2022.104077 (verified) — *iScience* 25(4), 104077
- **Key points**: movement gates the *precision* of both self- and externally generated touch, while the *amplitude* of self-generated touch is separately and robustly attenuated; the two effects do not interact and are uncorrelated.
- **Why it matters here**: it rules out the deflationary reading that self-touch merely feels weaker because you are busy moving. The agency-indexed component survives after the generic movement-related suppression is partialled out.

### Thomas, Yon, de Lange & Press (2022) vs. Job & Kilteni (2023) — the sign dispute
- **DOIs**: 10.1177/09567976211017505, *Psychological Science* 33(1), 48–59; and 10.7554/eLife.90912, *eLife* 12, e90912 (both verified)
- **Thomas et al.**: manipulating probabilistic expectation, "expected touch during action is perceived more intensely than unexpected touch"; computational modelling suggests expectation increases gain. Their framing: predictive mechanisms in action should behave like predictive mechanisms elsewhere in sensory cognition, where expectation *sharpens*.
- **Job & Kilteni**: three pre-registered experiments; the reported enhancement traced to the baseline condition (motor-inhibition trials) rather than to genuine enhancement. Abstract's closing sentence, grep-verified verbatim against the eLife page: "Our results provide conclusive evidence that action does not enhance but attenuates predicted touch and prompt a reappraisal of recent experimental findings upon which theoretical frameworks proposing a perceptual enhancement by action prediction are based."
- **Still live**: Rittershofer, Wang, Eimer, Kok, Yon & Press, "Paradoxical influences of prediction are resolved across time," preprint posted 2026-03-09 (DOI 10.64898/2026.03.05.709935). **Not peer-reviewed** — cite as a preprint or not at all.
- **Why it matters here**: this is the void's cleanest evidence, and it is evidence of a kind the Map rarely gets. The contested proposition is a first-person one — how intense a self-generated touch feels — and five years of psychophysics were needed to adjudicate it.

### Synofzik, Vosgerau & Newen (2008) — the comparator's output needs interpreting
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.concog.2007.03.010 (verified) — *Consciousness and Cognition* 17(1), 219–239
- **Key points**: the same comparator mismatch is sometimes attributed to the self and sometimes to the world, so a further process must categorise it; agency draws on a wide range of internal and external cues rather than on efference-copy prediction alone.
- **Why it matters here**: it blocks the simple story that the felt sense of agency *is* the comparator's match signal, and replaces it with something that suits the void better — the feeling is the output of a multi-cue inference whose inputs include the attenuation itself.

### Historical anchors (both verified at Crossref)
- von Holst & Mittelstaedt (1950), "Das Reafferenzprinzip," *Naturwissenschaften* 37(20), 464–476 — DOI 10.1007/BF00622503.
- Sperry (1950), "Neural basis of the spontaneous optokinetic response produced by visual inversion," *Journal of Comparative and Physiological Psychology* 43(6), 482–489 — DOI 10.1037/h0055479. The corollary-discharge concept.
- Crapse & Sommer (2008), "Corollary discharge across the animal kingdom," *Nature Reviews Neuroscience* 9(8), 587–600 — DOI 10.1038/nrn2457.

## The Void

### Nature of the Limit

Structural, therefore **Unexplorable** on the territory axis. On the reason axis of [the taxonomy](/apex/taxonomy-of-voids/) it reads as *architectural*: the subtraction is constitutive of how a moving organism keeps a stable percept, so a mind that could inspect the undiscounted channel would be a differently built mind, not a better-introspecting one.

An **Occluded** reading is available and should be declined. It would run: the interface through which consciousness acts on the physical is exactly the channel whose sensory return gets cancelled, so the cancellation hides the interface. That is too convenient — the mechanism is a physical comparator whose existence physicalism predicts just as strongly, and reading it as targeted concealment is the move [the common-cause null](/project/common-cause-null/) discounts and [tenet-generated voids](/voids/tenet-generated-voids/) warns about. The framework-independent reading is the defensible one.

### Evidence for the Limit

1. **Cross-species, cross-modal generality** (Kilteni et al. 2025) — not an artefact of one tactile paradigm.
2. **Separability from generic movement suppression** (Kilteni & Ehrsson 2022) — an agency-indexed term survives after gating is partialled out.
3. **The sign dispute** (Thomas et al. 2022; Job & Kilteni 2023; Rittershofer et al. 2026 preprint) — the direction of the modulation of one's own perception by one's own action required pre-registered third-person psychophysics to adjudicate, and remains argued. Introspection contributed nothing to settling it.
4. **The attribution gap** (Synofzik et al. 2008) — the comparator's output does not carry its own interpretation, so what reaches awareness is a categorised verdict, not the comparison.

### Phenomenology

There is none, and that is the point. There is no felt gap where the cancelled signal was — no sense of a subtraction having occurred, and no experience of the operation. What is available is a single indirect artefact: you cannot tickle yourself, and the failure feels like a fact about tickling rather than a fact about your own predictive machinery. The void presents as a property of the world.

## Approaches to the Edge

**Direct**: none. Every attempt to attend to the undiscounted reafferent signal is itself made during an action whose consequences are being discounted.

**Indirect**: (a) breaking the prediction — delay or spatially displace the self-generated consequence and the attenuation falls off, exposing the discount by removing it; (b) clinical dissociation — reduced attenuation in auditory-hallucination and passivity symptom groups (Blakemore et al. 2000), with the narrow formulation the 2026-07-24 outer review already required of the Map; (c) third-person psychophysics with careful baselines, which is what the 2021–2026 dispute demonstrates is *necessary*.

**What AI Might See**: this is the sharpest case in the catalogue for the asymmetry [non-human minds as void-explorers](/voids/non-human-minds-as-void-explorers/) posits. In a robot with a forward model, the efference copy, the raw afferent signal and the attenuated residue are three separately addressable variables. The system can be built to log all three, compare them, and report the subtraction. Whatever forecloses this for us is architectural rather than logical — which is a real, if modest, result: it locates the void in our implementation rather than in the structure of self-knowledge as such. That distinction is worth more to the catalogue than another entry.

## Connection to Tenets

**Most relevant: [Bidirectional Interaction](/tenets/#bidirectional-interaction)**, but negatively. The tempting argument — the cancelled channel is where mind-to-matter influence would show up, hence the influence is hidden by design — should not be made. The comparator is physical, its function is well motivated without any non-physical influence, and the argument's structure is exactly the over-concession-in-reverse the Map has been burned by. What the void *does* license is narrower and holds under any framework: the sensory return of one's own action is not available in undiscounted form, so first-person report cannot be used as evidence about it in either direction.

**[Occam's Razor Has Limits](/tenets/#occams-limits)** fares better. The simple story — attenuation is cancellation, cancellation explains the tickle result, done — was the field's consensus for two decades and then had to be defended against a serious enhancement challenge and separated from tactile gating. The simplification was doing real damage to a question that turned out to have at least three separable components.

## Slot-or-Fold Decision

**FOLD.** Three reasons, in order of weight:

1. **The general claim already has a home, and this is a specific instance of it.** [The predictive construction void](/voids/predictive-construction-void/) already holds that consciousness cannot read the construction/reception ratio off experience. Reafference attenuation is not a rival claim; it is the sharpest concrete instance — a case where a measurable, agency-indexed quantity is removed from the received side before awareness. Standing alone it would invite the deflationary objection that "you don't see the raw signal" is trivially true of all perception. Sitting inside the predictive-construction article, that objection is already answered by the surrounding argument.
2. **There is real headroom in the right host.** Measured this run with `analyze_length`: `predictive-construction-void` 2498w against a voids hard threshold of 3000 — about 500 words, which is roughly the size of the needed insert. This is the opposite of the situation the notation-void note documented, where all seven candidate hosts were `soft_warning` and each fold demanded an offsetting trim.
3. **The empirical apparatus is already published twice.** Blakemore/Wolpert/Frith and the comparator model are set out at [volitional-control](/topics/volitional-control/) and [anarchic-hand-and-action-ownership](/topics/anarchic-hand-and-action-ownership/). A standalone voids article would be a third exposition of the same machinery.

## Gaps in Research

- **No philosophical literature found treating reafference cancellation as an epistemic limit in its own right.** The philosophical uptake runs through the sense of agency (Synofzik et al., Wegner, Frith) rather than through what the subtraction does to perceptual self-knowledge. If that gap is real rather than a search failure it is mildly interesting, but a single search round is not evidence of absence and this was not pursued further.
- **The sign dispute is unresolved as of this note.** The 2026 Rittershofer et al. reconciliation is a preprint. Any fold should describe the dispute, not adjudicate it.
- **Interoceptive reafference** — whether the same discount applies to the sensory consequences of one's own autonomic and visceral activity — was not researched and would be the natural extension if [interoceptive-void](/voids/interoceptive-void/) is ever revisited.
