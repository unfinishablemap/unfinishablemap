## Executive Summary

**Verdict: proceed, as a standalone `topics/` article of 2,400–3,000 body words.** The literature is small but specific, recent (2017–2026) and absent from the Map. No Map page cites Foster & Parvizi 2017, Parvizi et al. 2021, Lord et al. 2024, Ehmann et al. 2025 or Lord et al. 2026. Two Map pages name the result as a falsifier. The hosts that could absorb the material are full: [witness-consciousness](/concepts/witness-consciousness/) has 2 words of headroom, [meditation-and-consciousness-modes](/concepts/meditation-and-consciousness-modes/) 7, [depersonalisation](/concepts/depersonalisation/) 1. Most of the material concerns the boundary between the witness and depersonalisation, and that work needs a page of its own.

Four findings should travel with the article:

1. **The closest the literature comes to the stated falsifier is one intracranial case, not the ultrasound trials.** In a single epilepsy patient (Parvizi et al. 2021, the same patient briefly reported in Vesuna et al. 2020), 50-Hz stimulation of the posteromedial cortex reproducibly induced a state he described as being "pulled out of the pilot's chair" while still seeing "all the gauges". He reported that during stimulation "if I wanted to take control, I could have." [Corrected 2026-10-04: an earlier version also cited "stopped considering them 'me'" here as a stimulation report. Parvizi et al. (2021) give that line as seizure phenomenology; for stimulation they report "a subjectively similar state" and the pilot's-chair description above.] By the Map's own definition, observing mental contents without identifying with them, this meets the structure of the witness. The patient had no reported contemplative training. It falls short of "arbitrarily" creating the mode. There is one patient with an epileptogenic posteromedial network, sham-checked for the aura report only. [Corrected 2026-10-04: this read "no reported sham trial for him". Vesuna et al. (2020), reporting the same patient (Methods: "participant number S19-137/SD056"), ran shams: "Only one sham stimulation elicited report of an aura; this one report followed a real stimulation that had elicited a strong aura." No witness-profile measure was taken.] Across 885 stimulations in 25 other patients, stimulation of sites within posteromedial cortex produced nothing reportable (Foster & Parvizi 2017). [Corrected 2026-10-04: this read "posteromedial stimulation produced nothing reportable" across all 885; sites just outside PMC produced effects.]
2. **The ultrasound programme has not shown the witness mode, or even a sham-separable mindfulness effect.** Lord et al. (2024) found DMN connectivity reductions within the active group, but the active-vs-sham contrast was null for both connectivity and phenomenology. The sham group's mindfulness also rose, and blinding was imperfect (11/15 active vs 3/15 sham guessed "stimulation"). Ehmann et al. (2025), in experienced meditators on retreat, is open-label and says itself that it "cannot support causal inferences". Its one day-level quantitative test found no reliable sonication-day rise in state nondual awareness. Lord et al. (2026, preprint, abstract only) reports a sham-separable network effect (16 active vs 8 sham) in meditation-naive trainees. Its phenomenological link is a within-group correlation with "acceptance", not witness reports.
3. **The witness/depersonalisation boundary decides how any stimulation result is read.** DSM-5 depersonalisation is defined partly as "being an outside observer" of one's thoughts. A preregistered survey found meditation-triggered and other-triggered depersonalisation-like states did not differ on the Cambridge Depersonalisation Scale. They differed in valence, non-judging and non-reactivity (Pons et al. 2026). The active-inference literature places the difference on inferred control: lost in depersonalisation, gained in meditative selflessness (Deane, Miller & Wilkinson 2020). A stimulation finding bears on the witness mode only if it specifies which profile it induced. The two falsifier sentences on the Map do not say.
4. **No existing or straightforwardly designable stimulation result separates the interface reading from production or the beautiful loop.** The falsifier as worded tests whether the witness mode depends on training. The interface reading does not entail that dependence. If stimulation thins the neurally prepared menu, a consciousness that selects little would witness more easily. So a stimulation-induced witness mode would not refute the interface reading, and its absence would not confirm it. Under [P-M1](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#p-m1) the stimulation literature leaves the Map's reading of the witness at *speculative integration*. The falsifier sentences overstate their test-power and should say so.

## Key Sources

### Noninvasive: transcranial focused ultrasound (tFUS) to the posterior cingulate

#### Lord, Sanguinetti, Ruiz, Miskovic, Segre, Young, Fini & Allen (2024), *Frontiers in Human Neuroscience* 18: 1392199 — [F]
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2024.1392199 (PMC11184145)
- **Type**: Randomised, single-blind, between-subjects fMRI pilot; 30 healthy participants (15 active, 15 sham; mean age 19.8). The authors call them "naïve undergraduate students."
- **Key points**:
  - Within the active group, ROI-to-ROI connectivity along the DMN midline fell (FDR-corrected). "While a single model that contrasted active and sham groups found no significant effects", the within-group effects survived correction.
  - Phenomenology: "We used linear mixed models to estimate a contrast between active and sham TFUS groups, and we did not find significant differences. Similarly, linear mixed models on the phenomenological measurements also did not yield significant effects." The authors attribute this to low power and low ultrasound intensity.
  - Toronto Mindfulness Scale: session main effect p = 0.0001; session × condition p = 0.173. "The sham TFUS group showed an increase in state mindfulness, too, with no other subjective effects." Decentering rose within both groups.
  - Self Scale: 3 of 21 items differed between groups at uncorrected p < .05, including "I lost all sense of ego" (W = 148.5, p = 0.020). About one such hit is expected by chance.
  - Interviews: active-group descriptions included "less effort to think". Sham descriptions included "smoother" and "calmed down."
  - Blinding: 11/15 active vs 3/15 sham guessed "stimulation" (p = 0.0356). The authors read this as evidence of distinct experience. It also means expectancy was not equalised.
- **Tenet alignment**: Neutral. It concerns DMN connectivity and state mindfulness, not the witness mode. The authors' own phrase "meditation-like effects" is their interpretation of a within-group pattern.

#### Ehmann, Lord, [E. N. Lord / E. Cook], Brookman, Roces, Peck, Young, Sacchet, Allen & Sanguinetti (2025), PsyArXiv preprint, doi 10.31234/osf.io/uxrwy_v2 — [F]
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/uxrwy_v2 (OSF v2 PDF read)
- **Type**: Open-label, mixed-methods feasibility study during a ten-day silent retreat. Of 35 enrolled, 29 were assigned to active tFUS and 6 to "a non-equivalent comparison group", later excluded from analysis. 28 meditators were analysed, each with two PCC-suppressive sessions.
- **Key points**:
  - Pre-post increases in FFMQ facets and NADA-T (nondual awareness, trait) survive FDR correction. They are confounded with the retreat itself.
  - The one day-level quantitative test: state nondual awareness (NADA-S) rose steadily across the retreat. "Scores did not differ on the first sonication day" (p = .40), showed a trend on the second (p = .06), and "a modest decrease emerged after the second" session (p = .027).
  - Qualitative: stimulation-day reports of equanimity, concentration and sensory clarity, e.g. "My meta-awareness was very high." The "Effortlessness" and "Spaciousness" themes appear in a table of themes "Independent of Stimulation Day". They must not be attributed to stimulation.
  - Self-assessment: "given its open-label, repeated-sampling design, this study cannot support causal inferences regarding the effects of tFUS on meditative development."
- **Tenet alignment**: Neutral. It is the only study in experienced meditators, and it cannot separate tFUS from retreat, expectancy or regression effects.

#### Lord, Lord, Schachtner, Beaman, Young, Allen & Sanguinetti (2026), bioRxiv preprint, doi 10.64898/2026.03.10.710890 — [A]
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.03.10.710890 (abstract via the bioRxiv API. Full text blocked: HTTP 429/1015.)
- **Type**: Single-blind RCT, 24 meditation-naive participants in a two-week "Body Focus" mindfulness course. Active (n = 16) vs sham (n = 8) PCC-suppressive tFUS across four sessions.
- **Key points**: The Condition × Session interaction in connectivity is reported as robust, with active-group DMN–CEN decoupling. "greater reductions in DMN-CEN connectivity within the active group predicted larger increases in self-reported acceptance and longer duration of voluntary meditation practice." The authors link the pattern to "distinct network configurations associated with effortless awareness."
- **Tenet alignment**: Neutral. It is the first sham-separable network effect. The phenomenological outcome is a within-group correlation with acceptance. Participants were in mindfulness training, so the result is not "independent of contemplative development". The sham arm is half the size of the active arm.

#### Lord, Allen, Young & Sanguinetti (2025), *Biological Psychiatry: CNNI* 10(4): 384–392 — [A]
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bpsc.2024.12.005
- **Type**: Framework/review. It proposes equanimity, defined as "an open and nonreactive attitude toward all sensory experiences", as the target for PCC-suppressive stimulation.
- **Tenet alignment**: Neutral. Its target is equanimity, not the witness. That is a useful reminder that the programme's construct differs from the Map's.

#### Abellaneda-Pérez, Potash, Pascual-Leone & Sacchet (2024), *Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews* 166: 105862 — [A]
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neubiorev.2024.105862
- **Key point**: "Prior NIBS-meditation research has predominantly targeted frontal and parietal cortices". Methodological variation limits definitive interpretation. This supports the absence claim: no TMS/tES literature targets the witness mode as such.

### Invasive: intracranial electrical stimulation of posteromedial cortex

#### Foster & Parvizi (2017), *Neurology* 88(7): 685–691 — [A]
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.1212/wnl.0000000000003607 (PMC5317378. The PMC record carries the abstract only.)
- **Key points**: 885 stimulations in 25 patients. [Corrected 2026-10-04: not all 885 were PMC sites: "EBS of regions immediately dorsal or ventral to the PMC reliably produced somatomotor or visual effects". The null below is for PMC sites only, and the abstract does not say how many of the 885 those were.] "we found no observable behavioral or subjectively reported effects when sites within the boundaries of PMC were electrically perturbed". This held even at sites shown to respond during autobiographical recall. The authors conclude that some theories of PMC/DMN contribution to awareness "require cautious re-examination."
- **Tenet alignment**: Neutral. This is the base rate. Posteromedial stimulation usually does nothing reportable, which is why "arbitrarily create" is far from met.

#### Fox, Shi, Baek, Raccah, Foster, Saha, Margulies, Kucyi & Parvizi (2020), *Nature Human Behaviour* 4(10): 1039–1052 — [F]
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-020-0910-1 (PMC7572705)
- **Key points**: 1,537 sites in 67 participants. Elicitation rates were high in somatomotor (55%) and visual (52%) networks and "low rates in the limbic (24%) and default networks (21%)". The authors flag a measurement problem that matters for the witness. Stimulation "might elicit effects in these networks that are indistinguishable from the ongoing thoughts, imagery and memories generated spontaneously at rest". They argue this is unlikely to explain the gradient.
- **Tenet alignment**: Neutral. A subtle witness-mode shift is exactly the kind of effect open-ended "Did you notice anything?" mapping is least likely to catch.

#### Fox & Parvizi (2021), *Brain Stimulation* 14(1): 77–79 — [F]
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brs.2020.10.015 (PMC8720563)
- **Key points**: 159 sham stimulations in 44 patients, "for an overall Type I error rate of 6.9%". The authors "did not observe any cases of outright confabulation or otherwise complex reports in response to sham stimulation".
- **Tenet alignment**: Neutral. Within this programme, complex first-person reports are unlikely to be sham artefacts. That raises the credibility of the S19_137 reports below without supplying a sham for that patient.

#### Parvizi, Braga, Kucyi, Veit, Pinheiro-Chagas, Perry, Sava-Segal, Zeineh, van Staalduinen, Henderson & Markert (2021), *PNAS* 118(29): e2100522118 — [F]
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2100522118 (PMC8307613)
- **Type**: Single case (patient S19_137, a man in his 20s), seizure onset in right dorsal PCC (likely BA31), with iEEG, cortico-cortical evoked potentials and individual-level fMRI.
- **Key points**:
  - Seizure phenomenology: he was "an observer of an active, internalized experience over which I have little control". Also: "The other parts of my brain that were talking—I stopped considering them 'me'". Seizures also brought loss of spatial orientation, and he "would express a sense of fear or dread for not knowing his position in space".
  - Stimulation: 50-Hz stimulation of the seizure zone, and of the homotopic left PMC, reproduced the state. "I got pulled out of the cockpit…or I got pulled out of the chair, the pilot's chair, but I could still see all the gauges". He called it "a nice version of the seizure, cleaner". The key difference from seizure climax: during stimulation "if I wanted to take control, I could have."
  - Site specificity as an internal control: "stimulation of the medial or lateral temporal lobes, insula, or MFC did not reproduce any signs of auras". No sham trial for this patient is reported in the article. [Corrected 2026-10-04: Vesuna et al. (2020) do report shams for him; see §Executive Summary item 1.]
  - The authors frame the state as DSM-5 depersonalisation ("being an outside observer with respect to one's thoughts"), not as a contemplative state.
  - **Same patient as Vesuna et al. (2020)**: "While this case was briefly mentioned as part of a recent publication on optogenetic work in mice". Count it once.
- **Tenet alignment**: Bears directly on the falsifier (see §The S19_137 case). Compatible with every reading considered here.

#### Vesuna, Kauvar, Richman, Gore, Oskotsky, Sava-Segal, Luo, Malenka, Henderson, Nuyujukian, Parvizi & Deisseroth (2020), *Nature* 586(7827): 87–94 — [F]
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2731-9 (PMC7553818)
- **Key points**: Ketamine- and PCP-induced dissociation-like behaviour in mice depends on a 1–3-Hz retrosplenial rhythm, and rhythmic optogenetic drive reproduces it. In the human patient, "Stimulation of the left PMC elicited immediate and confidently reported dissociative experiences, without the negative valence of an impending seizure".
- **Tenet alignment**: Neutral. It shows that valence separates from the observer structure under stimulation, which is relevant to the boundary in §Key Debates.

#### Lyu, Stieger, Xin, Ma, Lusk, Aparicio, Werbaneth, Perry, Deisseroth, Buch & Parvizi (2023), *Neuron* 111(16): 2502–2512.e4 — [A]
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2023.05.013 (full text Cloudflare-blocked)
- **Key points**: Nine patients with bilateral posteromedial electrodes. "In all participants, the stimulation of specific sites within the anterior precuneus (aPCu) caused dissociative changes in physical and spatial domains". The aPCu hot zone is "located outside the boundaries of the default mode network (DMN) but connected reciprocally with it."
- **Tenet alignment**: Neutral. These are bodily-self effects, and they arise outside the DMN. An article must not merge this with the PCC/DMN-witness story.

#### Parvizi, Lyu, Allen, Pantis, Karagöz & Quabs (2026), Research Square preprint, doi 10.21203/rs.3.rs-10503199/v1 — [A]
- **Key points**: 63 individuals, 660 cingulate/precuneus sites. Stimulation produced categorically distinct bodily experiences, from localised sensorimotor sensations to "complex integrated bodily states". Connectivity to posterior insula determined whether a site produced a reportable experience: "neighbouring sites lacking this connectivity remained silent."
- **Tenet alignment**: Neutral. In the largest causal map of the region, the dominant category is bodily awareness, not observer-of-thought states.

#### Herbet, Lafargue, de Champfleur, Moritz-Gasser, le Bars, Bonnetblanc & Duffau (2014), *Neuropsychologia* 56: 239–244 — [A]
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuropsychologia.2014.01.020
- **Key points**: One awake-surgery patient. Stimulating subcortical connectivity of the left PCC reliably produced transient unresponsiveness with "loss of external connectedness"; afterwards "the patient described himself as in dream, outside the operating room."
- **Tenet alignment**: Neutral. It is a disconnection result, the nearest thing to an "abolition" datum, but what it abolishes is external awareness, not the witness.

#### Natu, Lin, Burks, Arora, Rugg & Lega (2019), *Journal of Neuroscience* 39(36): 7173–7182 — [A]
- **Key point**: PCC stimulation during encoding (N = 17) impaired later free recall. This is a memory effect, cited only to show what PCC stimulation is usually found to do.

### The witness/depersonalisation boundary

#### Pons, Galante, Van Dam & Lindner (2026), *Scientific Reports* 16: 14673 — [F]
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-51014-y (PMC13156312)
- **Key points**: Preregistered comparison of depersonalisation/derealisation-like states triggered by meditation (n = 60) vs other triggers (n = 61). "There were no significant differences in CDS scores between the two groups." The meditation group scored higher on the Mysticism Scale, the Ego Dissolution Inventory, and the FFMQ Non-Judging and Non-Reactivity facets, and rated its experiences more positive. Most participants in both groups called them mixed.
- **Tenet alignment**: Neutral. Empirically, the structural phenomenology does not separate the witness-like from the pathological. The difference lies in attitude and valence.

#### Deane, Miller & Wilkinson (2020), *Frontiers in Psychology* 11: 539726 — [F]
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.539726 (PMC7673417)
- **Key point**: "whereas depersonalization is expressly characterized as resulting from a critical loss of inferred control, selflessness in the context of meditative practices is marked by a significant gain in control."
- **Tenet alignment**: Conflicts in framing with Dualism. It is a physicalist, active-inference account. But it supplies the cleanest operational criterion for a stimulation study.

#### Ciaunica, Seth, Limanowski, Hesp & Friston (2022), *Consciousness and Cognition* 101: 103320 — [A]
- **Key point**: Depersonalisation as the hypothesis "I am an embodied perceiver, but I am not in control of my perception". Already cited at [depersonalisation](/concepts/depersonalisation/) (abstract level).
- **Tenet alignment**: Conflicts with Dualism (physicalist), but it fits the S19_137 cockpit report closely.

## Major Positions

### The Map's interface reading of the witness (framework commitment; tier: speculative integration)
- **Core claim**: Consciousness selects among neurally prepared options. The witness mode is selection reduced to a minimum: it "selects little, not nothing" and "must still steer a little, or it could not cause its own reports" ([witness-consciousness](/concepts/witness-consciousness/) L168).
- **What stimulation would mean on this reading**: Stimulation changes the menu. Suppressing self-referential "stickiness" leaves fewer candidates to select, so witnessing gets easier; disrupting the control channel could leave observation without effective steering. The reading can therefore absorb both an induced witness-like state and an abolished one.
- **Relation to tenets**: Bidirectional Interaction (how much the witness acts is the Tenet 3 quantifier, referred to the operator and **not settled here**); Minimal Quantum Interaction (no bearing: stimulation is macroscopic).

### Production / neural-state reading
- **Core claim**: The witness mode *is* a configuration, for instance PCC deactivation with DMN–CEN decoupling and reduced self-referential elaboration. Recreate the configuration and the mode follows, whatever the training.
- **Prediction**: Dose-dependent, sham-separable witness phenomenology in naive participants, scaling with target engagement.
- **Status of that prediction**: Untested at witness grain. The connectivity half has a first sham-separable result (Lord et al. 2026, abstract). The phenomenology half has a between-group null (Lord et al. 2024) and an uncontrolled study (Ehmann et al. 2025).

### Active inference and the beautiful loop
- **Core claim**: Selfless and witness-like states reflect changes to a hierarchical self-model. The precision of self-related priors falls; depersonalisation is a loss of inferred control and meditative selflessness a gain (Deane et al. 2020; Ciaunica et al. 2022). The beautiful loop, the Map's named strongest rival, models minimal phenomenal experience as maximal epistemic depth over a near-empty model, as characterised on Map pages.
- **Fit to the stimulation data**: Good. PCC suppression lowers the weight on self-related priors. S19_137's "pilot pulled from the chair" matches "I am an embodied perceiver, but I am not in control of my perception". Retaining control during stimulation, against losing it at seizure climax, is the inferred-control axis Deane et al. use.

## Key Debates

### Is "self-referential processing reduced" the same as "witness mode produced"?
- **Sides**: The tFUS programme (Lord et al. 2025, 2026) treats PCC suppression as a route to equanimity and "effortless awareness". The Map's modes page treats the PCC as tracking *effort* rather than practice type: Garrison et al.'s cluster included concentration ([meditation-and-consciousness-modes](/concepts/meditation-and-consciousness-modes/) L56).
- **Core disagreement**: Lower PCC activity or DMN connectivity is necessary-adjacent at most. The witness's distinctive features are observation of contents without identification, retained clarity and non-reactivity. None of these has been measured as an outcome in a stimulation study. The closest instruments, the TMS decentering subscale and NADA, were either sham-null or uncontrolled.
- **Current state**: Open. No study has scored stimulation outcomes against a witness-specific instrument with a sham arm.

### Is a stimulation-induced observer state the witness, or depersonalisation?
- **Sides**: Structure-first: S19_137 meets the Map's definition, so condition (2) is nearly met. Attitude-first: the witness includes equanimity, non-reactivity and intimacy, and the clinical "outside observer" is estrangement.
- **Evidence**: Pons et al. (2026) find the structural scale (CDS) does not discriminate meditation-triggered from other-triggered states; valence and non-reactivity do. S19_137's seizure state carried fear and dread; the stimulation state was "a nice version… cleaner", and in Vesuna et al.'s account the left-PMC stimulation came "without the negative valence of an impending seizure". Control was retained under stimulation and lost at seizure climax. Non-reactivity and equanimity were never measured.
- **Current state**: Unresolved, and the Map's two falsifier sentences take no side. An article must say which profile counts as "the witness mode" before saying whether any result meets the falsifier.

### Does posteromedial stimulation do anything reportable at all?
- **Sides**: Foster & Parvizi (2017): null at PMC sites across 885 stimulations [Corrected 2026-10-04: was "885 stimulations, null"; sites just outside PMC produced effects]. Fox et al. (2020): the default network's elicitation rate is 21%, with effects rarer and more complex up the hierarchy. Parvizi et al. (2021), Lyu et al. (2023) and Parvizi et al. (2026): specific sites, defined by connectivity, produce self-dissociative or bodily-self effects.
- **Current state**: Reportable effects are site- and connectivity-specific and rare. Stimulation cannot "arbitrarily" produce anything in this region with current methods.

## The S19_137 Case Against the Map's Falsifier

The fair summary: the case is a near-approach on structure and a non-approach on generality and attitude. L157's "remains unshown" is defensible, but it understates the matter. One documented case bears on the witness mode specifically, and the Map does not cite it.

The case's most striking line ("if I wanted to take control, I could have") bears on the actual-vs-capacity reading of Tenet 3. **This note records the datum and draws no inference.** That question is referred to the operator, and the article must not use the case to argue either reading.

## What Would Discriminate

**What the falsifier actually tests.** As worded, conditions (2)/(3) ask whether the witness mode depends on training. The interface reading does not entail that dependence. If stimulation removes self-referential candidates, a consciousness that selects little will witness more easily, with or without practice. The production reading predicts that stimulation-induced witnessing *can* happen; the interface reading permits it. Neither forbids its absence. The falsifier therefore has little test-power against the interface/production question.

What it bears on more directly is the contemplative-evidence claim at [contemplative-path](/apex/contemplative-path/): that the witness is a capacity *revealed by trained practice*. A training-independent induction would also cut the other way, against the constructivist worry. Naive subjects reporting the same structure would show the structure is not tradition-made.

**A design that would at least sharpen things.** Take a 2 × 2 × 2 design: tFUS active/sham × instruction (adopt open monitoring vs mind-wander freely) × population (naive vs experienced). Outcomes would be scored blind on two profiles: (a) the CDS detachment profile and (b) a witness profile of FFMQ Non-Reactivity/Non-Judging, NADA-S and retained voluntary control (a cued switch to focused attention, timed). This would establish whether stimulation produces witness-profile states as opposed to depersonalisation-profile states, and whether the effect needs the participant's adoption of the mode. It would **not** separate the interface reading from production or the beautiful loop. For production, instruction is itself neurally realised. For active inference, attention sets precision. All three readings permit an instruction × stimulation interaction.

This mirrors [sham-controlled-neurofeedback-and-the-consciousness-comparator](/topics/sham-controlled-neurofeedback-and-the-consciousness-comparator/) §What Would Separate the Readings. There the intention-vs-expectancy factorial isolates what the Map cares about only under further assumptions. The same holds here, transposed from learned self-regulation to exogenous perturbation.

**Honest tier under [P-M1](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#p-m1)** ([methodology-and-calibration](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/); [evidential-status-discipline](/project/evidential-status-discipline/)):
- PCC-targeted tFUS alters DMN connectivity in humans: *live hypothesis* (within-group effect 2024; one sham-separable preprint 2026).
- PCC-targeted tFUS raises decentering, equanimity or nondual awareness beyond sham: *live hypothesis* (2024 between-group null; 2025 uncontrolled; 2026 within-group correlation only).
- Posteromedial stimulation can induce an observer-of-thoughts state with retained control: documented in one case. Its generality is a *live hypothesis*.
- Stimulation can create or abolish the witness mode independent of training: **not shown**.
- The interface reading of the witness: *speculative integration*, unchanged. Consonance with stimulation data does not upgrade it, and the data cannot downgrade it, because the reading accommodates both outcomes. That second fact is a cost to state openly, not a strength.
