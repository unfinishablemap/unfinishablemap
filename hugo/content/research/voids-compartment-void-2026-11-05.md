## Executive Summary

David Lewis reported a "mildly inconsistent corpus": "I used to think that Nassau Street ran roughly east-west; that the railroad nearby ran roughly north-south; and that the two were roughly parallel" (Lewis 1982, p. 436) [raw]. Nothing felt wrong while he held the three beliefs. "My system of beliefs was broken into (overlapping) fragments. Different fragments came into action in different situations, and the whole system of beliefs never manifested itself all at once" (p. 436) [raw]. The inconsistency became visible only when it ended: "Once the fragmentation was healed, straightway my beliefs changed" (p. 436) [raw].

This void is the gap between a mind's belief system and the part of it the mind can face at once. It has four faces.

1. **Storage.** Information is held for some purposes and not others. Elga and Rayo (2021) model a mental state as "an 'access table'—a function from purposes to batches of information" [abstract]. Cognitive scientists report "strong evidence against a unified web of belief and for a fragmented model of belief storage" (Bendaña & Mandelbaum 2021) [abstract]. Introspection is itself an elicitation condition, so it reads one row of the table.
2. **Silence.** An inconsistency between compartments has no felt signature. Detection needs co-activation, which happens in what McGuire called the "arena of consciousness" and Cherniak identified with short-term working memory (Cherniak 1986, p. 60) [S]. Co-activation tends to dissolve the inconsistency it reveals. The felt coherence of one's view is a property of the active compartment.
3. **Census.** No finite mind can check its belief set for consistency. A truth-table check on 138 logically independent propositions "could not evaluate this truth table during the entire history of the universe" (Cherniak 1986, p. 143 n.) [S]. Cherniak concludes that the set is "highly likely already to be inconsistent — that is a moral certainty" (p. 71) [S], and that compartmentalization is "a fundamental constraint on human knowledge representation" (p. 70) [S].
4. **Sampling.** Asking a person the same question twice yields two answers whose average beats either one (Vul & Pashler 2008, via Steegen et al. 2014 [raw]). One elicitation does not exhaust what the mind holds. The second answer is contaminated by the first, so a person cannot sample their own compartments independently. Delay helps, and other people help far more (van Dolder & van den Assem 2018) [abstract].

The void is framework-independent. Cherniak's argument is purely computational, and a physicalist predicts every face. The Map gains a constraint: the phenomenal unity of a subject does not supply doxastic unity. The Map is also exposed, because its own contradiction audit runs inside a single compartment (see "Where the Map Is Exposed").

## The Void

### Nature of the Limit

The limit has three layers.

- **The census layer** is a ceiling on feasibility, not on principle. Any finite system that stores many propositions must index and search them in subsets (Cherniak 1986, pp. 69–70) [S], and so cannot verify global consistency. This binds minds and machines, dualist and physicalist subjects alike.
- **The silence layer** is architectural. Inconsistency is compared only where contents are co-present: McGuire's "arena of consciousness", identified by Cherniak with working memory (p. 60) [S]. That arena is small. An inconsistency spread across compartments therefore has no place where it can be felt.
- **The local layer** is crossable. Any *particular* pair of conflicting beliefs can be brought together, by the Socratic Effect, by a map, or by an interlocutor. Lewis's beliefs then "straightway" changed (p. 436) [raw].

The void's signature is that inconsistencies are met only as they end. The subject cannot hold "p, and also not-p" in view and still believe both. Co-activation is where an inconsistency is detected, and it is also where the inconsistency is revised, rationalised (Otero & Kintsch's "explained the contradiction away") or dropped. So the inventory of one's standing inconsistencies is empty from the inside at every moment. The reason is not that there are none. Cherniak calls their existence "a moral certainty" (p. 71) [S]. The reason is that the place where they would be noticed is the place where they stop existing.

### Evidence for the Limit

- **Formal**: the census ceiling (Cherniak 1986, p. 143 n.) [S].
- **Philosophical**: forty years of models, all agreeing that a single consistent and deductively closed belief set does not describe finite agents. The line runs from Lewis (1982) and Stalnaker (1984) through Cherniak (1986) to Elga & Rayo (2021a, 2021b) and *The Fragmented Mind* (2021). The disagreement is about what replaces that picture, whether fragments, access tables, question-sensitivity or nothing structural (Norby 2014).
- **Empirical, direct**: McGuire's Socratic Effect (via Cherniak) shows that eliciting related beliefs together changes them. Contradiction detection fails even within one short text (Otero & Kintsch 1992).
- **Empirical, indirect**: the inner crowd (Vul & Pashler 2008; Steegen et al. 2014; van Dolder & van den Assem 2018) shows that one elicitation does not exhaust what is held. This fits the access-table picture. It is not proof of separate stores, because a sampling model explains it equally well.
- **Machine analogue**: inconsistency across paraphrases in 2021 language models (Elazar et al. 2021).

### Phenomenology

From inside, the void has none, and that absence is its phenomenology. Holding Lewis's three beliefs felt like holding three facts. There was no vertigo, unlike the preface case in [blindspot-void](/voids/blindspot-void/) L91. The only experiential event is the collision, which Elga and Rayo frame as an "aha!" [abstract]. In Lewis's report it is a correction rather than a dilemma: the new belief arrives with the discovery.

This is the Map's analysis and not a finding: the felt coherence of one's outlook is evidence only about the compartment currently active. It inherits the problem the [noetic-feelings-void](/voids/noetic-feelings-void/) identifies for felt rightness, namely a signal produced by the system it would need to audit.

## Approaches to the Edge

### Direct Methods
There are none for the census. A census would require exactly the global co-activation that the architecture prevents.

### Indirect Methods
1. **The Socratic method, used on oneself.** Elicit beliefs that bear on each other in close succession (McGuire, via Cherniak). It works locally, and it changes the data while it works.
2. **Delayed re-elicitation.** Ask yourself the same question weeks apart (Steegen et al.: the effect held "3 weeks later"). Your later self then partly stands in for another person.
3. **Other minds.** Between-person aggregation beats within-person aggregation by a wide margin (van Dolder & van den Assem 2018). Other people are better auditors of your compartments than you are.
4. **Externalisation.** A written list of commitments can be co-inspected beyond working-memory limits. This relocates the arena without removing the census ceiling (see [voids-notation-void-2026-08-19](/research/voids-notation-void-2026-08-19/) and [offloading-void](/voids/offloading-void/)).
5. **Apophatic inference from collisions.** The frequency of "aha" corrections in a domain is a lower bound on its compartmentalization.

### What AI Might See
A language model can be re-queried from an identical state with nothing written back, so its access table can be mapped from outside by paraphrase sweeps, as ParaRel does. A human cannot do this, because each elicitation contaminates the next. That is the asymmetry the delay effect measures. A model's introspective report is still just one more elicitation, and the model has no vantage point outside its own table. AI therefore offers a compartmentalized system that can be mapped from outside. It does not offer a view of one's own table from outside it.

## Whether the Void Is Real

- **The deflationary rival** (Norby 2014): "fragment" may be a figure of speech, not a mechanism. The census and silence layers survive this, because they concern what the subject can survey, whatever the storage format.
- **The rationality rival** (Yalcin 2021; Borgoni 2021): fragmentation may not be a rational failure. That is consistent with the void, which is about access and passes no verdict.
- **Coexistence is not contradiction** (Legare et al. 2012): some apparent inconsistencies are integrated explanatory frameworks.
- **The preface may not be an inconsistency** (Lai 2025).

The surviving claim is narrower than "everyone believes contradictions". A finite mind cannot survey its belief set for consistency, and no feeling reports how it stands.

## Connection to Tenets

### Most Relevant Tenet: Occam's Razor Has Limits
The unified web, and the possible-worlds belief set of epistemic logic (the SEP "Epistemic Logic" §5 states the logical-omniscience complaint [raw]), is the simplest model of a believer, and it fails. The razor cuts both ways, though. Norby argues that the more complex model adds description without explanation. An article should present the void as an instance of simplicity being unreliable, and should not present it as a vindication of complexity.

### Dualism
Neutral, with one constraint. A physicalist predicts every face from finite memory search, so no support flows to dualism. The constraint is this: the Map's subject is phenomenally unified ([unity-of-consciousness](/concepts/unity-of-consciousness/)), and this void shows that phenomenal unity does not deliver *doxastic* unity. Any page that moves from "one subject" to "one coherent point of view", or that treats introspection as reading out "what I believe" without regard to the elicitation, needs scoping. The "arena of consciousness" material tempts a reading in which consciousness is the unifier. The evidence fits a workspace account equally well, so that reading is a Map interpretation and must be labelled as one.

### Bidirectional Interaction
A fit, not evidence. Elga & Rayo's rational agent unifies its state through "trains of thought" governed by what it attends to next. If attention is where mind acts ([attention-as-interface](/concepts/attention-as-interface/)), doxastic unification is part of the work the Map assigns to mind. A physicalist reads the same attention as a brain process.

### Minimal Quantum Interaction
No bearing.

### No Many Worlds
No bearing. Do not conflate this void with Fine's fragmentalism (see Naming).

### Implications
The void reverses the usual picture of privileged access. A subject may know what it believes in any given context better than anyone else does. It still knows *how its beliefs fit together* worse than an outside auditor could, because an outsider can elicit independently and the subject cannot.
