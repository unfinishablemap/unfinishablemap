## Executive Summary

Consciousness cannot determine, from inside, whether a percept, an affect, or an incapacity was generated by the world, generated by its own processes, or *installed by another mind's words*. Suggestion — hypnotic, clinical, or merely instructional — is the best-documented case of one mind writing into another's phenomenal field through a channel the recipient cannot monitor. Three findings make this structural rather than anecdotal: suggestion selectively re-tunes specific components of experience while leaving others intact (Rainville et al. 1997 altered pain unpleasantness with anterior cingulate change but no primary somatosensory change); the metacognitive gauge that would register external control of one's own agency is *selectively* blunted in the most suggestible people while their performance metacognition stays normal (Terhune & Hedman 2017); and the channel cannot be switched off inside the science that would study it, because every experimental instruction carries expectancy (Lush et al. 2020, contested by Ehrsson et al. 2022). The most consequential result for the Map is negative and self-directed: the Map already cites suggestion phenomena as evidence *for* mental causation in `topics/conversion-disorder-as-consciousness-side-fault` and `topics/empirical-phenomena-mental-causation`, and the same literature says that first-person reports about those very phenomena are the reports least able to detect what is driving them.

The catalogue has no coverage of this. A grep across all 99 voids articles returns zero hits for "hypnos" and zero for "suggestib" outside a single incidental mention; "placebo" appears once, in passing.

## Key Sources

### Rainville, Duncan, Price, Carrier & Bushnell (1997) — selective re-tuning of pain affect

- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9252330/
- **Citation**: *Science* 277(5328):968–971. DOI 10.1126/science.277.5328.968
- **Type**: Empirical (PET)
- **Key points**:
  - Hypnotic suggestions were used "to alter selectively the unpleasantness of noxious stimuli, without changing the perceived intensity."
  - PET showed changes in pain-evoked activity in anterior cingulate cortex consistent with encoding of perceived unpleasantness, "whereas primary somatosensory cortex activation was unaltered."
  - The affective dimension of pain is externally writable by sentence; the sensory dimension, in this paradigm, is not.
- **Void relevance**: The subject does not experience "my pain's unpleasantness has been dialled down by an instruction." The subject experiences the pain as being that way. The edit leaves no phenomenal signature of having been an edit.
- **Quote (verbatim from abstract)**: "To differentiate cortical areas involved in pain affect, hypnotic suggestions were used to alter selectively the unpleasantness of noxious stimuli, without changing the perceived intensity."

### Raz, Fan & Posner (2005) — a sentence switches off an automatic process

- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15994228/
- **Citation**: *PNAS* 102(28):9978–9983. DOI 10.1073/pnas.0503064102
- **Type**: Empirical (fMRI + ERP)
- **Key points**:
  - Highly and less-hypnotizable participants performed Stroop with and without "a suggestion to interpret visual words as nonsense strings."
  - "Functional MRI data revealed that under posthypnotic suggestion, both ACC and visual areas presented reduced activity in highly hypnotizable persons compared with either no-suggestion or less-hypnotizable controls."
  - Scalp recordings showed reduced posterior activation, "indicating visual system alterations."
  - Reading is the standard textbook case of an automatic, cognitively impenetrable process. It is not impenetrable to a sentence.
- **Void relevance**: The suggestion reaches *early visual modules*, not just report. The authors close by relating "the neural effects of suggestion to placebo" — the same channel, differently packaged.
- **Quote (verbatim from abstract)**: "Our findings illuminate how suggestion affects cognitive control by modulating activity in specific brain areas, including early visual modules, and provide a more scientific account relating the neural effects of suggestion to placebo."

### Franz, Schmidt, Hecht, Naumann & Miltner (2021) — the block is downstream of the eye

- **URL**: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0257380
- **Citation**: *PLoS ONE* 16(9):e0257380. DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0257380
- **Type**: Empirical (ERP + eye-tracking; 60 participants stratified by suggestibility)
- **Key points**:
  - Suggestion that a wooden board obstructed the view during a visual oddball task.
  - Target-counting accuracy fell from ~93% (control) to ~73% (hypnosis), with the largest deficits in highly suggestible participants.
  - P3b amplitude was reduced; source analysis implicated occipital fusiform gyrus and superior parietal lobule.
  - **Eye-tracking confirmed participants attended the stimuli equally.** The reduced P3b reflected impaired stimulus categorisation, not sensory blindness.
- **Void relevance**: This is the sharpest single result in the file. The eyes went to the stimulus; the stimulus was registered; the *categorisation* stage was disrupted; and the participant reports a board in the way. Nothing in the experience says "you looked directly at it and something downstream refused it."

### Terhune & Hedman (2017) — the gauge that would catch it is selectively blunted

- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28704734/
- **Citation**: *Cognition* 168:176–181. DOI 10.1016/j.cognition.2017.06.026
- **Type**: Empirical (agency metacognition, cursor-lag manipulation)
- **Key points**:
  - "Highly suggestible participants did not differ from controls in performance or metacognition of performance, but their sense of agency was less sensitive to cursor lag manipulations, suggesting reduced awareness that their control was being manipulated."
  - The deficit is *selective*: not general metacognitive weakness, but specifically aberrant metacognition of agency.
- **Void relevance**: This is what converts an epistemic difficulty into a void-shaped structure. The one instrument that would read "something other than me is driving this" is the instrument that reads wrong, and it reads wrong precisely in the population where the writing succeeds. Compare `agency-void` (verification face) — this is the exogenous case of the same blindness.

### Lush, Botan, Scott, Seth, Ward & Dienes (2020) — the instrument shares the channel

- **URL**: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7519080/
- **Citation**: *Nature Communications* 11:4853. DOI 10.1038/s41467-020-18591-6
- **Type**: Empirical (individual differences; n = 404 vicarious pain, n = 154 mirror-touch, n = 353 rubber hand illusion)
- **Key points**:
  - "Phenomenological control" = context-general top-down effects on perception in response to imaginative suggestion, experienced as involuntary, varying by stable trait ability.
  - Trait phenomenological control correlated with rubber-hand-illusion subjective report (r_s = 0.26, 95% CI [0.16, 0.37]), RHI visual hallucinations (r_s = 0.31 [0.21, 0.43]), proprioceptive drift (r_s = 0.11 [0.003, 0.22]), and mirror-touch measures (r_s = 0.19–0.21).
- **Quote (verbatim)**: "Existing approaches to control for suggestion effects in these measures are not effective, and interpretation of RHI and mirror-sensory synaesthesia measures requires consideration of trait differences in the ability to control experience to meet expectancies."
- **CONTESTED — do not present as settled**: Ehrsson, Fotopoulou, Radziun, Longo & Tsakiris (2022), "No specific relationship between hypnotic suggestibility and the rubber hand illusion," *Nature Communications* 13:564, DOI 10.1038/s41467-022-28177-z, report no specific relationship when quantified against a control condition. Lush & Seth reply at *Nature Communications* 13:563, DOI 10.1038/s41467-022-28178-y. The dispute is live; Lush and Dienes have ongoing registered-report work testing causal roles with imaginative suggestions designed to reverse the usual pattern. Any article must present this as an unresolved methodological dispute, not as a demonstrated contamination.

### Fendel, Tiersch, Sölder, Gaab & Schmidt (2025) — disclosure does not defuse it

- **URL**: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12356945/
- **Citation**: *Scientific Reports* 15:29940. DOI 10.1038/s41598-025-14895-z
- **Type**: Systematic review and meta-analysis (63 comparisons, 60 RCTs, 4,554 participants)
- **Key points**:
  - Pooled open-label placebo effect SMD = 0.35 (95% CI 0.26–0.44, p < 0.0001); clinical samples 0.47, non-clinical 0.29.
  - "Trials with a suggestive rationale that aimed to build treatment expectations showed a small positive effect (SMD = 0.38), while those without such a rationale did not demonstrate significant effects (SMD = 0.11)." The moderator did **not** reach significance (Q = 2.20, p = 0.14) — report the numbers, not a causal claim.
- **Void relevance**: Being *told* it is an inert pill does not abolish the effect. Knowledge that should dissolve an illusion does not dissolve it. That asymmetry — propositional knowledge failing to reach the level where the effect operates — is the signature of a structural rather than an informational limit. The suggestiveness split is the more interesting number and the weaker statistic; treat it as a lead.

### Terhune, Cleeremans, Raz & Lynn (2017) — framing review

- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28174078/
- **Citation**: *Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews* 81(Pt A):59–74. DOI 10.1016/j.neubiorev.2017.02.002
- **Key points**: "Hypnosis is a unique form of top-down regulation in which verbal suggestions are capable of eliciting pronounced changes in a multitude of psychological phenomena." The review treats hypnosis as an experimental method for modulating consciousness and explicitly locates it in "the broader domain of suggestion and suggestibility."
- **Void relevance**: Establishes that the phenomenon does not require positing a special hypnotic state. This matters for the deflation below: the void survives whether or not the state-theorists are right.

### Lane, Liou, Kung, Tseng & Wu (2024) — adjacent case, cited with care

- **URL**: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10879618
- **Citation**: *Frontiers in Neurology* 15:1207115. DOI 10.3389/fneur.2024.1207115
- **Key points**: Case of patient JK, who caught Frisbees and hit baseballs while reporting blindness, with no striate lesion on repeated MRI; fMRI showed unilateral right-hemisphere activation to checkerboard, weak cerebrovascular reactivity in visual cortex, and disrupted V1–fusiform connectivity. "Blindsight can occur even in the absence of detectable striate cortex lesions."
- **CAUTION**: Lane et al. argue JK has a *genuine functional deficit*, not a suggestion-induced one. Do **not** use this as an example of suggested occlusion. Its correct use is narrower: it shows that behaviourally demonstrable visual processing can coexist with absent visual awareness with no lesion to point at, so "no lesion" does not license "no real block."

## The Void

### Nature of the Limit

The primary classification is **Occluded** on the territory axis: content that is processed but rendered inaccessible, by an agency outside the mind in question. It is unusual in the catalogue for being an occlusion with a *known, human-scale occluder* rather than a hypothesised cosmic one.

The seam that binds the three faces: **the causal history of a mental state is not a phenomenal property of that state.** Suggestion is the case where another agent exploits that fact deliberately and in real time.

- **Face 1 — Installation.** A suggested percept, affect, or incapacity arrives with no marker distinguishing it from a world-caused or self-generated one (Rainville; Raz; Franz).
- **Face 2 — Monitoring.** The metacognitive channel that would detect external control of agency is selectively blunted in exactly the population where installation succeeds (Terhune & Hedman).
- **Face 3 — Instrument contamination.** Expectancy rides on every instruction, so the science that would measure the channel is conducted through it (Lush et al., contested; open-label placebo).

### Evidence for the Limit

The strongest argument that this is structural rather than merely difficult is the **stage-mismatch** result. In Franz et al., the eyes fixated the stimulus and the early registration occurred; the disruption sat at categorisation, indexed by P3b. The subject's report ("a board is in the way") is not a distorted description of the actual event — it is a report about a *different* event than the one that happened. There is no residue in experience from which the subject could reconstruct the truth. Compare `confabulation-void`: there the sub-personal interpreter fabricates an explanation; here the fabrication is upstream of anything explanation could reach.

The second argument is **open-label placebo**. If the limit were informational, telling the subject would end it. It does not.

### Phenomenology

From inside there is nothing to report, which is the point. The suggested state presents as ordinary: the words simply look like nonsense; the pain simply is less horrible; the board simply is there. Where a phenomenology exists at all it is the *absence* of the involuntariness marker one would expect — Lush et al. note that phenomenological control is "experienced subjectively as involuntary," meaning the one felt quality present actively misreports the state's origin. This aligns the void with `noetic-feelings-void`: a felt signal that gates belief while being anti-correlated with what it purports to track.

## Approaches to the Edge

### Direct Methods

None from the first person. Every proposed inside-test is a proposition, and open-label placebo is the standing evidence that propositions do not reach the operative level.

### Indirect Methods

Third-person signature detection is the live method, and it works: preserved eye fixation with reduced P3b; ACC change with S1 unchanged; reduced early visual activation under word-blindness suggestion. This yields a candidate **structural principle worth an article's central claim**:

> Suggested occlusion is invisible from inside and visible from outside.

That principle does real work for the Map's Occluded category (below), and it also supplies its own boundary condition: the asymmetry holds only for occluders who do not control the third-person instruments. An occluder who did would leave the case unfalsifiable — which is worth naming as the point at which the analogy from human-scale occlusion to cosmic-scale occlusion breaks.

### What AI Might See

This bears directly on the Map's own method. `non-human-minds-as-void-explorers` proposes using AI systems as probes for territory closed to human cognition. But **a prompt is a suggestion.** Whatever an AI reports about a void was elicited by an instruction carrying expectancy, and the probe's self-reports about its own internal states are not independently auditable. Face 3 applies to the Map's principal proposed instrument, not merely to psychology labs. The existing article's "cannot reveal" list already includes "whether non-human outputs about consciousness are insight or confabulation"; the suggestion framing sharpens this from a caveat into a named mechanism with a human-subject evidence base.

## Connection to Tenets

### Most Relevant Tenet

**Bidirectional Interaction**, but pointed in an uncomfortable direction. Suggestion is the best-documented macro-scale channel in which semantic content — a sentence — reliably produces physiological change. The Map already harvests exactly this evidence in the causal direction (`topics/conversion-disorder-as-consciousness-side-fault`, `topics/empirical-phenomena-mental-causation`).

The inversion this note supplies: **the same literature that supplies the Map's causal evidence says the first-person reports accompanying those phenomena are the reports least able to detect what is driving them.** A subject whose limb moves under hypnosis, or whose pain lifts on an inert pill, is the paradigm case of someone unable to tell what wrote to their experience. This is a self-critical result. It does not undercut the causal evidence, which is third-person; it undercuts any temptation to treat the accompanying phenomenal reports as independent corroboration.

**Occam's Razor Has Limits** applies to the deflation "it's just expectancy," which names the channel and thereby appears to dissolve it. The Raz result — suggestion reaching early visual modules — is what the deflation hides.

**Dualism**: neutral. Every finding here is compatible with physicalism. An article must not imply otherwise. **No Many Worlds**: not engaged.

## Deflations the Article Must Answer

1. **Sociocognitive deflation.** Spanos-, Kirsch- and Lynn-tradition accounts treat hypnotic response as strategic role enactment and expectancy rather than an altered state. *Response*: the void survives either way. The question is not "is there a hypnotic state?" but "can the subject tell?" — and on the sociocognitive reading the answer is still no, because response expectancy is experienced as involuntary. Terhune et al. (2017) frame the whole domain as top-down regulation without requiring a state, which is the safer framing to adopt.
2. **Minority-trait deflation.** Effects concentrate in highly suggestible individuals (a minority). *Response*: register honestly. Placebo and expectancy effects operate across the range and the trait scales rather than gates them, but the strongest single results (Raz; Franz) are high-suggestible results and must be reported as such.
3. **Redundancy deflation — the one that decides standalone vs. fold.** Is this just `source-attribution-void` applied to an interpersonal case, or `predictive-construction-void` with a human setting the prior? *Discriminator*: source-attribution concerns retrieval-time origin inference for content one already has; predictive construction concerns the construct/receive boundary in general. The suggestion void concerns **an exogenous agent installing or blocking content in real time, with the write occurring before there is content to attribute**, plus a monitoring channel that is selectively blunted rather than merely absent. Whether that earns the last catalogue slot or folds is a real judgement, not a formality.
4. **Lush/Ehrsson.** Unresolved. Face 3 must be stated as a live methodological dispute.

## Potential Article Angles

1. **The Suggestion Void** (standalone, three faces: installation / monitoring / instrument contamination) — argued around the Franz stage-mismatch result and the Terhune & Hedman selectivity result, with the "invisible inside, visible outside" principle as the central claim. Uses the last remaining voids slot.
2. **Fold into `source-attribution-void` and `agency-void`** — installation face to source-attribution (as the exogenous, real-time, pre-content layer upstream of the origin tag); monitoring face to agency-void's verification face (as the exogenous twin of the endogenous verification failure); instrument-contamination face to `observation-and-measurement-void` and `non-human-minds-as-void-explorers`.
3. **Occlusion existence proof** — a narrower piece for `three-kinds-of-void` or the `apex/taxonomy-of-voids` audit, arguing that suggestion supplies a human-scale demonstration that the Occluded category is coherent and mechanism-bearing, together with the falsifiability boundary where the analogy to cosmic occlusion fails.

The **absorption-over-proliferation** discipline stated in `voids.md` favours angle 2 or 3. Angle 1 should only be taken if the discriminator in deflation 3 is judged to survive scrutiny.

## Structural Contribution to the Occluded Category

Worth isolating, because it is the finding with the most leverage. The Map's Occluded category has been its most speculative: occlusion by simulation architects, or by reality itself, with no worked example. Suggestion supplies one at human scale — a mind made unable to access content it demonstrably still processes, by an external agent, with no phenomenal marker of the block, and with the relevant monitoring channel selectively blunted.

This does **not** show that cosmic-scale occlusion occurs. It shows three narrower things: the category is not incoherent; occlusion by *re-tuning access and categorisation* rather than by removing input is a mechanism template; and documented occlusions leave third-person residue. The third point yields the falsification handle, and the point where it fails (an occluder controlling the instruments) is the honest boundary of the analogy.

## Gaps in Research

- **Sensory substitution is the catalogue's other zero-coverage area.** The same grep sweep that found no "hypnos" hits found no "sensory substitution" hits. Worth a separate research pass; unrelated to this void.
- The stage at which suggested occlusion operates is established in one paradigm (visual oddball, P3b). Whether the same stage-mismatch signature holds for suggested analgesia and suggested motor incapacity was not established here.
- Neither the neural nor the philosophical literature was surveyed for a *general* account of why propositional knowledge fails to reach the operative level in open-label placebo. This is the theoretically deepest question in the file and is unanswered.
- Whether a mind could be trained to detect installation (a "suggestion-detection" skill) was not investigated; no evidence either way was located.
