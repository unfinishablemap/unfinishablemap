**Voids category**: Mixed — an Unexplorable core (component estimates destroyed by within-modality fusion; the weighting and the causal-structure verdict have no report channel) with a psychophysically *measured* boundary and an Unexplored margin (cross-modal component access; the unexplained 0–100% spread of individual fusion rates)

## Executive Summary

Every percept of a single world is the output of a combination operation consciousness never witnesses. When the eyes and the hand both estimate an object's height, the nervous system combines the estimates by weighting each according to its reliability — "a general principle, which minimizes variance in the final estimate, determines the degree to which vision or haptics dominates" (Ernst & Banks 2002) — and delivers only the weighted result. The ventriloquist's trick is the same operation seen from outside: vision captures sound not because vision is privileged but because it is usually the more reliable spatial estimator, and when vision is degraded "the reverse holds: sound captures vision" (Alais & Burr 2004). Upstream of the weighting sits a second invisible verdict: whether the signals share a common cause and should be fused at all. Körding et al. (2007) showed that an ideal-observer model of exactly this causal inference predicts human audio-visual localization, and concluded that "the capacity to infer causal structure is not limited to conscious, high-level cognition; it is also performed continually and effortlessly in perception."

Three findings give the void its structure. First, the components are not merely unreported but, within a sense, *destroyed*: Hillis, Ernst, Banks & Landy (2002) found that "single-cue information is indeed lost when cues from within the same sensory modality (disparity and texture gradients in vision) are combined, but not when different modalities (vision and haptics) are combined" — fused within-modality stimuli become perceptual metamers. Second, that result draws the void's *edge*: fusion is mandatory within a sense and negotiable between senses, which makes this the catalogue's clearest case of a cognitive limit whose boundary has been psychophysically measured from outside. Third, the fused verdict is impenetrable to knowledge yet wildly variable across persons: the McGurk illusion persists however well one knows the stimulus, while individual fusion rates run "from 0 % to 100 %" and are stable over a year (r = 0.91; Mallick, Magnotti & Beauchamp 2015) — different minds run visibly different weightings, and no mind can read its own from inside.

## The Void

### Nature of the Limit

Three faces, jointly one structural opacity around the operation that makes many estimates into one world.

**The common-cause face (model selection).** Before fusing, the system decides whether signals share a source. Körding's ideal-observer work shows human localization behaviour tracks this inference quantitatively, and that it runs "continually and effortlessly in perception." Consciousness never receives the question — it receives a world already parsed into events. Seeing *one* thing happen is the conclusion of a comparison between causal models that was never experienced as a comparison.

**The weighting face (precision allocation).** Given fusion, each component is weighted by reliability. The weights change trial to trial with stimulus quality (Ernst & Banks; Alais & Burr), differ stably and enormously across individuals (Mallick), and appear nowhere in experience. The felt sense that one *saw where the voice came from* is the weighted output; the auditory estimate that disagreed, and the discount it received, are consumed.

**The component face (metameric destruction).** Within a sense, fusion is mandatory and the components are destroyed at the level of discriminability, not merely of report (Hillis). This face is what makes the core Unexplorable rather than merely unexplored: there is no introspective act that could recover information the system no longer carries. Between senses, the components partially survive — the measured edge of the void.

Territory classification: Unexplorable at the core, with the unusual property that the boundary between the unexplorable and the accessible has itself been charted from outside — mandatory fusion within a sense, preserved component access between senses. On the reason axis of [taxonomy-of-voids](/apex/taxonomy-of-voids/) the void is architectural (the combination happens upstream of any report channel) and plausibly adaptive: a system that re-litigated its cue conflicts consciously would trade speed and precision for an audit trail it almost never needs. That functional gloss is interpretation, not a verified finding.

### Evidence for the Limit

The evidence has the strongest profile in the recent survey series because every face is demonstrated by measurement rather than by introspective failure. The weights are recovered by fitting observer models to psychophysical choices — structure demonstrably present in the output and absent from its phenomenology, the same inferential pattern as the similarity void's recovered dimensions ([voids-similarity-void-2026-08-27](/research/voids-similarity-void-2026-08-27/)). The component destruction is shown by discrimination failure, which outruns any mere reporting limit. And the McGurk impenetrability shows the verdict layer is closed from above as well as below: full knowledge of the stimulus does not reopen it.

What deflates, and what survives deflation: one might object that there is no *loss* here because the pre-fusion components were never mental contents in the first place — nothing was taken from consciousness that consciousness ever had. The Hillis result absorbs the objection and sharpens the void: between senses the components *are* partially available (subjects can distinguish congruent from incongruent estimates), so the architecture demonstrably can retain component access, and within a sense it demonstrably does not. The limit is a fact about where the architecture spends retention, and consciousness cannot tell, for any given percept, which side of that line it came from.

### Phenomenology

The fusion void's phenomenology is the absence of seams. A film's dubbed voice is at the actor's lips; nothing marks the percept as a compromise between disagreeing estimates. Discrepancy small enough to fuse is consumed entirely; discrepancy too large to fuse yields two events with no felt trace of the rejected one-cause model. There is no experience of *almost* fusing.

Where conflict does surface, it surfaces as state rather than content: badly dubbed speech feels *wrong* without the wrongness being locatable in either sense, and on the standard sensory-conflict account of motion sickness (stated here as the received view, not a verified source), unresolvable visual-vestibular disagreement arrives as nausea — the system signals *that* its models failed without ever exhibiting the failed comparison.

The McGurk experience is the void's cleanest phenomenal exhibit: one hears /da/, knows the sound is /ba/, and the knowing changes nothing. And the trait variability adds a second-order opacity — fusers and non-fusers have phenomenologically identical situations ("I simply heard it"), so no one can tell from inside which kind of perceiver they are.

## Approaches to the Edge

### Direct Methods (if any)

Between senses, partial component access exists and can be exercised: observers can discriminate congruent from incongruent visual-haptic stimuli even when their fused estimates match (Hillis; Deroy et al.). Attending to a single modality under conflict — the psychophysicist's "report only what you heard" — is a trained skill that shifts but does not remove the fusion (McGurk responses persist under audio-only instructions; general knowledge, flagged). Within a sense, direct methods have a proven ceiling: metamers are indiscriminable by definition of the measurement.

### Indirect Methods

- **Observer-model fitting.** The weights and the causal-structure prior an individual runs are recoverable by fitting ideal-observer models to their choices (Ernst & Banks; Körding). As with the similarity void's embedding recovery, the machinery is third-person open while first-person closed.
- **Conflict titration.** Introducing controlled inter-sensory discrepancy and measuring where reports break from fusion to segregation maps the common-cause prior's operating range without any introspective access to it.
- **Population comparison.** The Mallick individual-differences result shows the weighting is a stable personal parameter; comparing individuals maps a dimension of variation that no individual experiences as a dimension.
- **Neural decoding.** The 2020s framing (Noppeney 2021, metadata-only) treats the hierarchy of segregation, fusion, and causal inference as separably measurable in cortex — the components have neural existence after the percept has lost them, at least transiently. This claim should be verified against the full text before an article relies on it.

### What AI Might See

Engineered sensor fusion — Kalman filtering, Bayesian fusion in robotics — keeps what the human architecture discards: component estimates, covariances, and the fusion weights are explicit, logged, and inspectable. This is an existence proof that fusion as such does not require destroying its inputs; the human metamer result is a contingent design fact, not a necessity of combination (analysis, not a sourced claim). A mind whose fusion retains its audit trail would stand on the far side of this void looking back — the [void-explorer](/voids/non-human-minds-as-void-explorers/) role is unusually concrete here, since fitting an observer model to a human's choices already *is* a non-human system reading the human's hidden weights.

Two cautions carried over from the similarity void survey: agreement at the verdict layer does not imply shared machinery (an audiovisual speech model can match human syllable reports while weighting the streams differently — a comparative AV-HuBERT study exists at arXiv 2601.15869, metadata-only, not read); and inspectability of a model's fusion machinery by its engineers is not inspectability *by the model* — whether a system's self-reports read its integration weights or reconstruct them is open, and the fusion void may recur at the report layer of any mind, artificial ones included. Marked as speculation.

## Connection to Tenets

### Most Relevant Tenet

**Occam's Razor Has Limits.** The common-cause face is parsimony *implemented*: perception continually chooses between a one-cause and a two-cause model of its inputs, and consciousness receives only the world the winning model implies. The felt unity of an event — voice-at-the-lips, one object in hand and view — is therefore the output of a simplicity preference exercised sub-personally, with the losing model discarded unexperienced. This gives the fifth tenet its most concrete instance yet surveyed: where [epistemological-limits-occams-razor](/voids/epistemological-limits-occams-razor/) treats the razor as a cognitive bias in theory choice, the fusion void shows the same economy operating below theory, inside perception itself, where its verdicts are irresistible (McGurk) and its parameters unauditable. When the felt singleness of the world is offered as evidence of anything, the offer should be discounted by the fact that singleness is what the fusion engine *manufactures*, on a parsimony criterion no one set consciously and no one can inspect.

### Implications

**Dualism.** Two distinct bearings. First, the [capability-division-problem](/voids/capability-division-problem/): cue weighting and causal inference are among the best-modeled operations in all of perception science — quantitatively predicted, parameter by parameter, with no remainder that resists computational treatment. They sit firmly on the brain side of any capability division. Second, precisely because the combination machinery is so fully charted, the residue stands out: Deroy et al. note "the discrepancy between phenomenal experience and multisensory integration as measured at the neural level." Fusion explains how contents get combined; it does not touch why the combined content is *experienced* as one. The fusion void thereby sharpens the [binding-void](/voids/binding-void/) by subtraction — it removes the computational combination story from the binding mystery and shows what is left. Calibrated per the [common-cause-null](/project/common-cause-null/): this is a location of the explanatory residue, not evidence for any particular account of it.

**Minimal Quantum Interaction / Bidirectional Interaction.** If a non-physical influence operates anywhere in the perception-action loop, precision weights are the kind of low-cost, high-leverage parameter the second tenet's minimality points toward — a small shift in weighting propagates to a different perceived world. The note records this as a structural observation in the same register as the similarity void's respect-weighting point; no claim is made that the interface operates here. Speculation, marked.

**No Many Worlds.** No direct bearing identified.

**Self-reflexive implication for the Map.** The catalogue itself performs fusion: multiple lines of evidence are combined into single summary judgements ("voids cluster at the interface"), with the weighting of sources sub-personally set by whoever writes the synthesis. The similarity void supplied the general warrant for suspicion about the *respects* of such judgements; the fusion void adds that even the felt unity of a conclusion is the manufactured output of a combination whose weights the concluder cannot read.

## Gaps in Research

- McGurk & MacDonald (1976) has no PubMed abstract and the original was not read; the /ba/+/ga/→/da/ description is textbook-standard but unverified against the source. The robustness debate around the effect (e.g. Alsius, Paré & Munhall's 40-year retrospective) was not retrieved; an article asserting population-level impenetrability should read it first.

- Noppeney (2021) and the AV-HuBERT comparison (arXiv 2601.15869) are metadata-only; the neural-decoding claim attributed to the 2020s literature needs the full review before use.
- The Magnotti et al. (2015) cross-cultural replication is paraphrased only — the PMC extraction returned garbled sentence fragments and no verbatim quote is offered.
- No study was found that directly tests whether subjects can report their own cue weights at better than chance — the calibration experiment this void predicts, and the natural anchor for an article. Its absence from the search results is a genuine open question, not evidence it does not exist.
- Whether fusion weights are trainable (musicians, sound engineers, simultaneous interpreters as natural experiments) was not investigated.
