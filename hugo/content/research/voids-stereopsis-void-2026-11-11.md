**Voids category**: Mixed. Seen from the stereoblind side, the quality of binocular depth is **Unexplorable** before acquisition and only partly describable after it. Seen from the binocular side, there is a second gap that is better classed as naturally **Occluded** (in the sense of [three-kinds-of-void](/voids/three-kinds-of-void/)): people with two working eyes disagree about what their own one-eyed view is like, and closing an eye does not settle it. ## Executive Summary

Stereopsis is the vivid impression of solid form and of space between things that most people get from two eyes. About 7% of adults under 60 lack it, on one evidence synthesis (abstract read). Susan Barry, a neurobiologist who had studied and taught the physiology of binocular vision, was stereoblind from infancy and gained stereopsis at 48; her own report is that she could not picture it beforehand and had "no idea" what she had lacked. That makes her the closest thing on record to a real occupant of Mary's room, although her case falls short of the thought experiment in ways that matter. The second finding runs the other way. Binocular observers split over whether closing one eye flattens the world or leaves it much the same, and Eric Schwitzgebel has argued that it is an open question whether that split is a difference in experience or only in report. Finally, Dhanraj Vishwanath's work separates the quality of stereopsis from the depth information it was thought to carry, since the same impression can be induced with one eye and a picture. The void is neutral between dualism and physicalism; no tenet is upgraded by it.

## The Void

### Nature of the Limit

Three separable gaps, which an article should keep apart.

1. **The forward gap (Unexplorable before acquisition).** A person who has never had stereopsis cannot construct it in imagination from the other depth cues, from testimony, or from the neuroscience. Barry had all three and reports that she could not.
2. **The backward gap (naturally Occluded).** A person who has always had stereopsis cannot find out by closing an eye what stereoblind vision is like. Barry's explanation is that memory fills in. Whether or not that is the mechanism, the reports of binocular observers diverge, and no agreed method decides between them.
3. **The description gap.** Those who cross over describe the new quality mostly by negation and by borrowed touch-words: space that is "palpable", things that "float", seeing "not just infer[ring]". The vocabulary names what is no longer absent. This is a mild case of the pattern in [acquaintance-void](/voids/acquaintance-void/).

### Evidence for the Limit

- **For the forward gap**: two vision scientists, Barry and Bridgeman, were each surprised. Bridgeman expected the 3D glasses to do nothing. The surprise of experts is better evidence than the surprise of lay subjects, because lack of theory cannot explain it.
- **Against over-reading it**: Barry's knowledge was nowhere near the complete physical knowledge that Jackson's Mary is stipulated to have. Her case shows that a great deal of textbook knowledge did not supply the quality. It does not show that all physical knowledge would fail to.
- **For the backward gap**: Schwitzgebel's informal survey, the opposed published reports (Sacks, Mach and O'Shaughnessy on one side; Schwitzgebel on the other), and the commenter's view that both extremes are misreports.
- **Against treating it as permanent**: Schwitzgebel's proposed test is concrete. If stereogram depth estimates and stereo thresholds sort people the way their reports do, the backward gap shrinks to an ordinary individual-differences finding.
- **A complication**: Vishwanath's dissociation means there may not be one thing, "stereopsis", whose presence or absence is at issue. If the impression can be had monocularly through an aperture, a stereoblind person may already have had a version of it without knowing what it was. Whether Barry would have recognised aperture-induced stereopsis before her therapy is not something the sources say.

### Phenomenology

Before: no sense of lack. Barry "never imagined that she saw differently". This is the [inventory-blindness](/concepts/inventory-blindness/) pattern: an absent quality leaves no gap-shaped mark. After: astonishment, a sense that ordinary objects are newly present, and the retrospective judgment "I had no idea what I had been missing." For the binocular observer closing an eye: for most, little apparent change, with the suspicion (Barry's, and the commenter's) that the lack of change is itself produced by filling-in or by a blunt introspective instrument.

## Approaches to the Edge

### Direct Methods (if any)

For the forward gap there is, unusually, a direct method that sometimes works: acquire the capacity. Therapy, perceptual learning and immersive displays have each been reported to restore some stereopsis in adults. This distinguishes the case from bat sonar or tetrachromacy. But the method crosses the gap without mapping it. Once across, the person can no longer check what the earlier state was like, which hands them the backward gap instead.

### Indirect Methods

- **Objective anchors.** Random-dot stereograms give a performance test of disparity processing that needs no report. This is more than [palette-extension-void](/voids/palette-extension-void/) has for novel colour. The limit is that the test certifies disparity processing, and Vishwanath's point is that the quality is not the same thing as disparity processing.
- **Aperture viewing.** If monocular aperture viewing induces the same impression, a binocular observer has a way to vary the quality while holding the scene fixed, and a stereoblind observer has a possible preview. Neither use appears to have been studied as a first-person method.
- **Schwitzgebel's correlation test**, described above.
- **Longitudinal report.** People in stereo-recovery programmes could be interviewed before, during and after, with the pre-recovery predictions recorded in advance. The search found retrospective accounts only.

### What AI Might See

A system with no visual phenomenology, or none it can vouch for, stands to stereopsis as Barry stood to it in college: it can hold the geometry of disparity and every published description. It can also do something neither group of humans can, which is to compare the reports of both groups without a default setting of its own that feels like the neutral case. That is a narrow advantage. It allows the reports to be tabulated without the tabulator's own two-eyed habit deciding which reports sound sensible. It does not supply the quality.

## Connection to Tenets

### Most Relevant Tenet

**Dualism**, by way of the [knowledge-argument](/concepts/knowledge-argument/), with **Occam's Razor Has Limits** second.

### Implications

- The Barry case is a natural illustration of the intuition behind the knowledge argument, and it is attested by the person best placed to deny it. That is worth recording.
- It is not evidence for dualism over physicalism. Every standard physicalist reply applies without strain. On the ability reading, Barry gained a capacity. On the [phenomenal-concepts-strategy](/concepts/phenomenal-concepts-strategy/), she gained a new concept of a state she could already describe. On the plain neuroscientific reading, her binocular neurons were not doing their job, so she lacked a brain state, and no one expects reading about a brain state to produce it. Vishwanath's functional account adds that the quality may carry information about distance precision that she lacked.
- The honest claim is therefore modest: descriptive and theoretical knowledge at the level a working neurobiologist has does not confer the quality, and its absence is not noticed from inside. Both frameworks predict that.
- The backward gap bears on method more than on metaphysics. It is one more case where first-person reports about a basic feature of everyday vision conflict and nobody can say who is right, which is a reason for caution wherever the Map leans on such reports.
- Occam's Razor Has Limits gets a small historical example: the critical-period rule for stereopsis was simple, well motivated by animal work, and applied clinically for decades before adult cases contradicted its strong form.
- Minimal Quantum Interaction, Bidirectional Interaction and No Many Worlds: nothing here bears on them.

## Potential Article Angles

Based on this research, a voids article could:
1. **"The neurobiologist in Mary's room."** Lead with the two-sided structure: the stereoblind expert who could not picture stereopsis, and the binocular majority who cannot agree on what one eye shows. State early that the void is neutral between dualism and physicalism, as [molyneux-void](/voids/molyneux-void/) does.
2. **"A quality without its information."** Lead with Vishwanath's dissociation and ask what, exactly, the stereoblind lack if depth can be had without stereopsis and stereopsis without disparity. This angle sits closer to [palette-extension-void](/voids/palette-extension-void/) and risks overlap.

Angle 1 is recommended. Whichever is chosen, the article should not say that Barry "knew everything" about stereopsis, should not put the Mary comparison in Barry's or Sacks's mouth, and should not quote "Stereo Sue" until the New Yorker text has been read.
