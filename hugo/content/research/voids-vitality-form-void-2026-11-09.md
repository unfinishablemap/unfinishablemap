## Executive Summary

Every action has a goal (*what*) and a reason (*why*), and it also has a manner (*how*): it is done gently or roughly, with a rush or a drag, surging or trailing off. Daniel Stern named this third dimension *vitality affects* in 1985 and *forms of vitality* in 2010, defining it as "a Gestalt that emerges from the theoretically separate experiences of movement, force, time, space and intention" (Stern 2010, p. 5, as quoted by Ammaniti & Ferrari 2013 [raw]). Three findings mark the void. **First**, the dimension is pervasive and perceived: fMRI since 2013 shows a specific sector, the dorso-central insula, active when people see, hear, touch, produce or imagine actions carrying different vitality forms, "regardless of the modality by which they are conveyed" (Di Cesare et al. 2019 [raw]), and a 2024 effective-connectivity study separates the *how* stream from the *what* stream at the level of information flow (Di Cesare et al. 2024 [raw]). **Second**, the dimension had no scientific home: "Despite the importance of vitality forms in social life, only recently have neurophysiological studies been devoted to this issue" (Di Cesare, Gerbella & Rizzolatti 2020 [raw]); in 2017 "only two studies" had looked at it in autism (Di Cesare et al. 2017 [raw]). **Third**, the one concept built to hold it does not hold its shape: the only critical review finds "vitality affects" "very important, but ... not precisely defined" and "extended to so many areas that it tends toward disintegration" (Køppe, Harder & Væver 2008 [abstract]). Stern himself wrote that these are "elusive qualities (which) are better captured by dynamic, kinetic terms" (1985, p. 54, via Ammaniti & Ferrari [raw]): the vocabulary is borrowed from movement and music. The void is the gap between a dimension that is lived in every waking moment and recognised in every other person, and a conceptual apparatus that sorts experience by content and so has almost nowhere to put its form.

## The Void

### Nature of the Limit

The limit is one of thematisation, not of perception. Vitality forms are perceived (recognition tasks), produced (kinematics), neurally distinct (the insular *how* stream beside the parieto-frontal *what* stream), and socially consequential (an autism recognition deficit with goal recognition intact). What is missing is a place in thought. Psychology's organising categories — perception, emotion, cognition, action — all sort by content; a vitality form is not a content but the temporal-dynamic shape any content arrives in. So a subject asked "how did that feel?" typically answers with a *what* (an emotion word), and a scientist asked to study feeling studies discrete emotions, which Di Cesare and colleagues take pains to distinguish from the "continuous internal states" they are after [raw]. Stern's own book opens by asking "just how can we study this phenomenon?" [raw] — the question of a man who has found a dimension and has no instrument.

Three registers apply. *Unexplored*: for all of the twentieth century this was unthought territory with a handful of visitors (Stern, Langer before him, the dance phenomenologists), and the first scan is 2013. *Occluded*: it is not merely unvisited; the standing categories actively absorb it. The *what*-task in the 2014 study yields no insular signature; only the explicit *how*-task does [abstract]. A mind running the default task never meets the dimension. *Unexplorable?*: Køppe and colleagues' finding that the concept, once built, "tends toward disintegration" [abstract] when extended, and Stern's admission that the qualities are "elusive" and "better captured by dynamic, kinetic terms" [via A&F], suggest that the *how* may not survive being made a *what*. A concept of a vitality form is a content; the form is not. Whether this is a permanent limit or merely a young literature is the open question (see Whether the Void Is Real).

### Evidence for the Limit

- **Pervasive yet unmeasured.** "A third aspect of any human action" [raw] with "very little ... known about their neurophysiological bases" until the 2010s [raw]; in autism, where the social cost is highest, "only two studies" by 2017 [raw]. This is the pattern of a dimension nobody's category pointed at, rather than a rare phenomenon.
- **Dissociation from content.** Autistic children "struggle to identify VFs when observing others' actions, while they do not differ from neurotypical children in discerning the goal of the action" [raw]. The *what* and the *how* come apart in a population, which is what a distinct dimension predicts and what a mere attribute of content does not.
- **Modality-general substrate.** Seen, heard, touched, spoken and imagined vitality forms converge on the dorso-central insula, "regardless of the modality" [raw]. A dimension that belongs to no sense is one that the inventory of senses cannot surface; this is the structural reason it is overlooked rather than a historical accident.
- **Conceptual failure under load.** The only critical review of the concept finds it "not precisely defined" and prone to "disintegration" when extended across the neuropsychological, bodily, conscious and interactional domains [abstract]. A dimension that cuts across the categories cannot be defined within any one and dissolves when defined across all.
- **Borrowed vocabulary.** Every word for a vitality form is a movement word or a music word: surging, fading, forceful, hesitant, crescendo, rubato. Stern conceded this ("dynamic, kinetic terms" [via A&F]); Sheets-Johnstone's analytic terms ("tensional, linear, amplitudinal, and projectional" [raw]) are likewise kinetic. No natural language has a native lexicon for the *how* of a thought or a feeling as such.

### Phenomenology

Approaching the edge feels like this: one notices that an experience — a memory arriving, an idea forming, a mood shifting — had a *shape*, a swell and a subsidence, a thrust or a drift, and one reaches for a word and finds only metaphors from the body or from music. Stern's "dynamic shifts or patterned changes within ourselves" [via A&F] names the experience without naming the shapes. Sheets-Johnstone's dancer reports attention "not on doing movements but on being-in-movement, i.e., on the felt dynamics of movement itself" [raw], a mode in which the form is foreground and content recedes; but that mode is rare, trained, and lost the moment one tries to say what the form was. The attempt to describe converts the form into a content, and the content is not the form. This is the inverse of the usual ineffability case ([language-thought-boundary](/voids/language-thought-boundary/)): the trouble is not that a quality has no word but that any word installs the wrong category.

### Distinctions from Neighbouring Voids

- **[The tempo void](/research/voids-tempo-void-2026-10-16/)** concerns the *rate* of experience and whether it can be read from inside. The vitality form is the *shape* — a form can be slow and explosive, or quick and gentle. Rate is one of its five components (time), not the whole.
- **[The resonance void](/research/voids-resonance-void-2026-03-24/)** (Gendlin's felt sense, Rosa's resonance) concerns moments that feel *meaningful* beyond articulation. Vitality forms are present in every action including the meaningless ones; they carry no meaning-signal.
- **[affective-void](/voids/affective-void/)** and **[mood-void](/voids/mood-void/)** concern emotions and moods that may be unreachable. Di Cesare and colleagues define vitality forms against emotion: continuous, non-discrete, without autonomic signature [raw]. The vitality form is the shape an emotion (or a non-emotion) takes in time, not a feeling-state.
- **[The cognitive-phenomenology void](/research/voids-cognitive-phenomenology-void-2026-05-01/)** asks whether thought has a phenomenal character at all. The vitality form is one candidate for what that character consists in — the dynamic contour of a thought's arrival — which, if right, explains why the cognitive-phenomenology debate is so hard to settle: both sides look for a *what*.
- **[modality-void](/voids/modality-void/)** concerns how many senses there are and which delivered a content. The vitality form's modality-generality [raw] is a case for that void's third face: a channel that no sense owns is invisible to the modality inventory.
- **[categorical-perception-void](/voids/categorical-perception-void/)** concerns continua reported as already carved. Vitality forms are the un-carved continuum: the current literature's own labels ("gentle", "rude", "energetic") are the first carvings and the research notes their coarseness.
- **[infant-consciousness](/voids/infant-consciousness/)** and **[pre-conceptual knowing](/research/voids-pre-conceptual-knowing-2026-01-27/)**: Stern's claim that vitality forms are "part of implicit relational knowing" [via A&F] and his developmental chapter place the dimension as the infant's primary mode. If adults lose thematic access to it, the vitality-form void is one mechanism of the infant-consciousness void.
- **[interoceptive-void](/voids/interoceptive-void/)** concerns the body's partial opacity. The dorso-central insula is interoceptive cortex; the vitality form may be where interoception meets kinematics, but the void here is about the *form* of action and experience, not the body's state.
- **[conceptual-scheme-void](/voids/conceptual-scheme-void/)** is the general case (categories exclude what they cannot represent). The vitality-form void is a worked instance with a measurable dimension on the far side of the exclusion.

## What a Physicalist Account Predicts (calibration)

A physicalist reading has an easy story and it should be stated at full strength. The *how* of action is kinematics plus affect-modulated motor control; it is encoded in the insula and premotor cortex; it was under-studied because twentieth-century emotion science inherited a discrete-category programme (Darwin, Ekman) and action science inherited a goal programme (mirror neurons), both for sociological reasons. On this reading the void is a research gap now closing, with no bearing on the mind-body question; the 2013–2025 series is exactly what closing looks like. Nothing in this note refutes that reading. What the note adds is that the gap has a *structure* — categorical displacement, modality-generality, conceptual disintegration under load, a borrowed lexicon — that a sociological account does not predict and that recurs across unrelated voids. Whether that structure is a fact about categories or a fact about consciousness is the question the Map's framework asks; the sources do not answer it.

## Approaches to the Edge

### Direct Methods

- **The *how*-task.** The 2014 design shows the dimension appears when the task asks for it [abstract]. The direct method is to ask for the form rather than the content, and to build response formats that are not category labels: continuous sliders, matched movement, or vocal contour rather than emotion words.
- **Kinematic measurement.** The 2024 production study [raw] measures vitality forms as velocity and acceleration profiles. The form becomes a curve, which is a *what*, but a *what* that preserves the time-structure.

### Indirect Methods

- **Notation.** Music is the practice that has always written the *how*: dynamics, articulation, tempo markings. Kim's observation that empirical music-and-emotion research spent three decades on content before asking how expressive forms are shaped [abstract] shows the notation existed while the science ignored it. Dance notation (Laban) is a second system; neither has been borrowed for mental life.
- **Deficit populations.** The ASD dissociation (goal intact, form impaired) [raw] is the apophatic instrument: the dimension is shown by its selective absence.
- **Cross-lineage triangulation.** Sheets-Johnstone's Husserlian terms and Stern's psychiatric ones were developed separately and converge on a five-component kinetic Gestalt (Stern: movement, force, time, space, intention [via A&F]; Sheets-Johnstone: "space–time–force dynamic" [raw]). Convergence across lineages that do not cite each other for this claim is the kind of evidence that survives the lineage-convergence worry.

### What AI Might See

A language model inherits text's bias: text encodes *what* and *why* richly and *how* thinly (an adverb, a stage direction, a tempo marking). An AI trained on text should therefore reproduce the void, and probing it for the vitality forms of its own processing would find the same borrowed lexicon. Two asymmetries run the other way. A system with access to its own continuous activation dynamics could in principle report temporal contour directly, without the conversion to category that human report forces; and a robot that must *produce* vitality forms (the Sciutti line of work, unverified) has to represent them as parameters, which is a form of thematisation humans lack. Whether either amounts to *experiencing* a vitality form is the hard-problem question in a new place; what they would supply is a non-human description of the dimension to set against the human one, per [non-human-minds-as-void-explorers](/voids/non-human-minds-as-void-explorers/).

## Whether the Void Is Real

The honest reading is that two of the three registers are secure and one is open. That the dimension was *unexplored* is documented in the sources' own gap statements across twelve years [raw]. That it is *occluded* by content categories is supported by the task-dependence of its neural signature [abstract] and by the conceptual disintegration finding [abstract], though both admit the deflationary reading above. That it is *unexplorable* — that the *how* cannot be thought without becoming a *what* — is a hypothesis, and the kinematic and notational methods are live attempts to refute it. The research programme since 2013 is young, concentrated in one group (Di Cesare and Rizzolatti appear on every neural paper cited), and its stimulus set is coarse (gentle/rude/neutral). A void whose evidence comes from one laboratory is not yet a void with cross-observer convergence; the independent phenomenological lineage (Sheets-Johnstone; Langer as an unverified lead) partly compensates.

## Four-Criteria Calibration ([apophatic-cartography-four-criteria](/concepts/apophatic-cartography-four-criteria/))

One grader and no pre-registered rubric; these grades are judgement.

- **Signature specificity, moderate-to-strong.** The signature is recognisable from the breakdown alone: a dimension present in every action, reported with borrowed kinetic words, absent from the default task, dissociating from goal recognition in a clinical population, and dissolving its own definition when extended. It is distinct from the tempo (rate), resonance (meaning) and affective (which feelings) voids.
- **Structured persistence, moderate.** The gap persisted from Langer (1940s–50s) and Stern (1985) to the first scan (2013) and is explicitly restated as still-open in 2020, 2021, 2024 and 2025 [raw]. It has not yet been tested by a programme that tried to dissolve it; the deflationary reading has not been run as an experiment.
- **Cross-observer convergence, weak-to-moderate.** Two lineages (infant psychiatry and the phenomenology of movement) describe the same Gestalt independently, and musicology supplies a third. The neural evidence is one laboratory. The clinical population adds a despite-commitments channel.
- **Framework independence: high on the finding, low on interpretive weight.** Nothing in the finding depends on the Map's tenets; the insula stream and the ASD dissociation are physiology. The dualist reading (that the *how* is where phenomenal character is delivered in time) is framework-shaped and earns the finding no upgrade.

## Connection to Tenets

### Most Relevant Tenet: Occam's Razor Has Limits

A taxonomy of mental life with three content categories is simpler than one with three content categories and a dynamic-form dimension cutting across them. The simpler taxonomy was held for a century, and it omitted "a third aspect of any human action" [raw] that every competent social perceiver was using all along. The omission did not look like an omission; it looked like parsimony. This is the tenet's claim in miniature: with incomplete knowledge, the simpler scheme is not more likely to be right, and the thing it leaves out may be the most pervasive thing there is.

### Dualism

If phenomenal character is irreducible to content, the vitality form is a candidate for *where* the irreducible part lives: the felt contour of an experience in time, which is present whether or not there is any object, emotion or proposition to carry it. Stern's own publisher abstract reaches for exactly this — vitality as "a manifestation of life, of being alive" that "we are very alert to ... in ourselves" [raw]. The note does not claim that the form is non-physical; it claims that the dimension the content-categories missed is the one that most resembles what dualists say is missed.

### Bidirectional Interaction

The 2024 and 2025 connectivity studies leave open two directions: the form set "cognitively (from PM to DCI)" or "affectively (from DCI to PM)" [raw], with the feeling task showing the insula modulating premotor cortex and the execution task showing the reverse [abstract]. The *how* of an action is a channel in which an internal state shapes motor output continuously. Whatever the metaphysics, it is the channel the interaction tenet needs to exist, and it is measured.

### Minimal Quantum Interaction

No bearing. Vitality forms are macroscopic kinematic and insular phenomena; nothing here speaks to the scale of any non-physical influence.

### No Many Worlds

Weak bearing. A vitality form is indexical in the ordinary sense (it is *this* surge, in *this* body, now), and Stern's claim that the forms are the infant's first mode of relating [via A&F] makes them part of what fixes a particular self. This is a thematic link, not an argument.

### Implications

The vitality-form void suggests a general lesson for the Map's taxonomy of voids: some voids are not regions beyond the categories but dimensions orthogonal to them, present inside every categorised experience and invisible for that reason. The erasure void and the conceptual-scheme void describe the structure abstractly; this one has a measurable dimension on the far side, a clinical dissociation, and a documented conceptual failure, which makes it one of the few voids where the deflationary and the structural readings can be tested against each other.

## Potential Article Angles

1. **The how of experience**: a voids article on the dynamic form of experience as a dimension psychology's categories displace — leading with the 2013–2025 measurement series, the autism dissociation, and the Køppe disintegration finding as the conceptual signature; register mixed (unexplored → occluded), with the unexplorable question left open.
2. **Borrowed words**: a shorter framing on the lexical fact that every language names the *how* of feeling with movement and music words, read as evidence that thought has no native representation for its own dynamic form, and that notation (music, dance) is the one place humans thematised it.

## Gaps in Research

- Stern's own text was not read; all Stern quotations are via Ammaniti & Ferrari 2013 with page numbers. A direct read of *Forms of Vitality* chapters 1–2 would settle whether Stern himself frames the neglect as categorical.
- Langer's "forms of feeling" lineage is unverified.
- No independent replication of the insular finding outside the Parma/Genoa group was located. A search for `"vitality forms" insula -Di Cesare -Rizzolatti` is the next step.
- Whether vitality-form recognition can be *reported* on continuous scales without category labels (the direct method) has not, to this search, been tested.
- Whether any natural language has a native (non-kinetic, non-musical) lexicon for dynamic form is a linguistic question not addressed in the sources.
