## About this domain

These positions record what the Map holds about **perception at the interface** — the structural findings the sensory-modality survey has produced, and the discount each carries when read as evidence for the interface. The wing is eight modality articles and one apex: [capability-division-in-vision](/concepts/capability-division-in-vision/) set the division (brain-side extraction and organisation of candidate content, mind-side phenomenal unity and ownership), seven sister articles carried it beyond vision and then inward, and [cross-modal-capability-division](/apex/cross-modal-capability-division/) stated the claim none of them could state alone. Until 2026-09-14 that claim was registered nowhere: no position file mentioned the capability division, cross-modality, interoception or the supramodal signature. The first entry records it once, in the two-tier form [P-F1](/positions/finding-level-calibration/#p-f1) prescribes, so the spokes and the [dualist-perception](/topics/dualist-perception/) front door have a registered discount to cite instead of each restating its own.

The domain is kept apart from [Finding-level calibration](/positions/finding-level-calibration/), where the entry was first proposed as P-F2, for a reason of kind. That domain's intro commits its positions to being *normative* — conduct rules about how a finding is split and cited, with the empirical axes legitimately n/a — and [P-F1](/positions/finding-level-calibration/#p-f1) lists its instances in the domains they belong to rather than housing them. [P-PI1](/positions/perception-and-the-interface/#p-pi1) is a first-order claim about perception with graded evidence axes, so it would have falsified that intro; the file also stood 152 words under its soft length threshold against an entry of twice that. It is registered here as [P-F1](/positions/finding-level-calibration/#p-f1)'s fourth instance and cross-referenced from there.

**The foundational-dependency test.** [P-PI1](/positions/perception-and-the-interface/#p-pi1) passes in the forward direction and is freely retireable. [Tenet 1](/tenets/#dualism)'s rationale is the explanatory gap and the argument catalogue, and the apex itself says the supramodal signature does not *establish* the distinction it is read as the operational shape of; [Tenet 3](/tenets/#bidirectional-interaction)'s rationale does not rest on the mind side's part in active haptics or attention, which the apex holds as interpretive alignment. Retiring the entry returns the wing to eight per-modality claims and disturbs nothing upstream.

Calibration follows the [multi-axis schema](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#calibration-schema) (credence · external-evidence grade · structural centrality · model maturity · empirical discriminability · framework-internal only).

## Positions

## P-PI1: The cross-modal capability division is a two-tier finding — an exteroceptive asymmetry carrying its own scope condition on the architecture tier, and a reading held consonant with the interface and probative of nothing on the significance tier

- **Status**: live <span id="p-pi1"></span>
- **Calibration** ([multi-axis](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#calibration-schema)): credence moderate (high for the architecture tier, whose negative half is textbook sensory neuroanatomy and whose scope condition is anatomy of the same standing; low-to-moderate for the significance-tier reading, which is the Map's wager and conditional on the interface reading assumed elsewhere) · external-evidence grade B (the architecture tier has independent support throughout, but its positive half rests on one MEG cross-decoding study, Sanchez et al. 2020, so the tier is B rather than A; the significance tier is D, since the Global Neuronal Workspace predicts the identical signature and the reading adds no independent support) · structural centrality moderate (no tenet's rationale rests on it; the eight-article modality wing, its apex and the dualist-perception front door are organised by it) · model maturity developed (a worked structural argument with external grounding; no model of the mind-side family) · empirical discriminability indirect (the architecture tier is directly measured and would move on replication or confound; the significance tier moves only through mechanism-level dissociations a broadcast account cannot redescribe) · framework-internal only: no (the architecture tier is independent neuroscience; the significance reading is framework-shaped and says so)
- **Asserts**: The Map holds the cross-modal capability division as two claims at two discounts. **Architecture tier** (cross-vocabulary-robust, the smaller discount): across vision, audition, chemosensation, touch and proprioception the brain-side machinery that extracts and organises candidate content is modality-specific — retinotopic maps, tonotopic decomposition, a combinatorial receptor code, mechanoreceptor populations with a parallel affective fibre class — while the transition into awareness carries a supramodal signature that decodes across senses. That asymmetry is an *exteroceptive* regularity whose scope condition is part of the finding: interoception inverts both terms, its heterogeneous channels converging partially within one shared allostatic-interoceptive system spanning insula, cingulate and brainstem (the insula a node, not the hub) and its mind-side term becoming valenced, owned bodily feeling rather than a presented object; the vestibular sense drives both terms to their extremes — no unisensory cortex and almost no felt object, yet the spatial frame of the self — which resolves the mind-side term into a family (object-unity, owned affect, viewpoint-frame) rather than one contribution; and thermoception reports world and body through a single lamina I pathway, so the outward/inward partition the scope condition rests on is a gradient with thermal on the line. Within each sense the boundary is graded rather than sharp and unconscious integration is window-bounded. **Significance tier** (the Map's reading, the larger discount): the Map reads the asymmetry as the operational shape of the brain/mind distinction, and the differently weighted mind-side family as what one subject contributes — the ownership term the broadcast under-describes. It holds that reading as consonant with the interface and probative of nothing, because the Global Neuronal Workspace predicts the very same supramodal signature and reads it as broadcast, so the signature is a shared explanandum; and because the recurrence is one pattern with shared upstream architecture, the common-cause null caps it at a single datum — biting harder inward, where one shared system is a common cause of what its channels share, and hardest on the vestibular case, which *is* multisensory integration and has nowhere independent to converge from. Nothing in the architecture tier's robustness transfers to the reading; citing the wing's convergence as support for Tenet 1 is the pattern [P-F1](/positions/finding-level-calibration/#p-f1) forbids.
- **Depends on**: [Tenet 1](/tenets/#dualism) as the distinction the reading takes the asymmetry to be the shape of, read with the apex's own caveat that the signature does not establish it; [P-F1](/positions/finding-level-calibration/#p-f1), the split this entry instantiates with both tiers stated; [P-M1](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#p-m1) (the reading never upgrades the architecture tier's grade) and [P-M2](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#p-m2) with the [common-cause null](/project/common-cause-null/) (one pattern, shared upstream architecture, one datum); the cross-vocabulary-translatability test in [architecture-vs-significance-two-tier-discount](/project/architecture-vs-significance-two-tier-discount/), which the architecture tier passes and the reading does not; the named strongest rival, [the Global Neuronal Workspace](/concepts/global-workspace-theory/), engaged in the apex; the within-modality isolation of the ownership term that [pain-asymbolia](/concepts/pain-asymbolia/) supplies, contested as to how cleanly it separates.
- **Argued in**: [cross-modal-capability-division](/apex/cross-modal-capability-division/) (the finding, its scope condition, its four disanalogies and its ceiling); the spokes [capability-division-in-vision](/concepts/capability-division-in-vision/), [auditory-consciousness-and-the-interface](/topics/auditory-consciousness-and-the-interface/), [chemosensory-consciousness-and-the-interface](/topics/chemosensory-consciousness-and-the-interface/), [tactile-consciousness-and-the-interface](/topics/tactile-consciousness-and-the-interface/) and [dual-domain-capabilities-in-proprioception-and-spatial-imagination](/topics/dual-domain-capabilities-in-proprioception-and-spatial-imagination/); the inversion in [interoceptive-consciousness-and-the-interface](/topics/interoceptive-consciousness-and-the-interface/), the extreme in [vestibular-consciousness-and-the-interface](/topics/vestibular-consciousness-and-the-interface/), the gradient in [thermal-consciousness-and-the-interface](/topics/thermal-consciousness-and-the-interface/); the front door [dualist-perception](/topics/dualist-perception/); the orthogonal cut in [embodied-interface](/apex/embodied-interface/).
- **Would shift if**: a distance sense were shown to share its brain-side extraction machinery with another, which would break the negative half of the architecture tier and retire the asymmetry as stated; or the cross-modal decoding result failed replication, or were shown to track report or task demands rather than the transition into awareness, which would remove the positive half and leave a brain-side-only claim; or interoception were shown not to converge — organ-specific cortices with no shared system — which would remove the scope condition and restore the universal form, a rewrite rather than a retirement. In the other direction, a within-modality dissociation that isolated a mind-side contribution — ownership, or the viewpoint-frame — in a form a broadcast account could not redescribe would move the significance tier off consonant-not-probative and raise its grade; the isolations now in hand (pain asymbolia, vestibular depersonalisation) are each redescribable and do not.
