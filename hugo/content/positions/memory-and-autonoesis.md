## About this domain

These positions record what the Map holds about **memory phenomenology** — whether the felt character of remembering is constitutive of the kind of memory a person has or an accompaniment to it, and what the standing of that commitment currently is. The memory wing states the commitment in three places at three grains: [episodic-memory](/concepts/episodic-memory/) draws the conclusion ("which makes consciousness constitutive of the *kind* of memory you have rather than an accompaniment to it"), [phenomenology-of-memory-and-the-self](/topics/phenomenology-of-memory-and-the-self/) states the two tests that survive the relocation reply's bound, and [semanticisation](/concepts/semanticisation/) reports where the evidence on the decisive one stands. Until this file existed the register recorded none of it — a sweep returning one near-miss, a *Scope-context* line on the infant-consciousness entry in [consciousness-scope](/positions/consciousness-scope/) citing autonoetic emergence as context for a different claim.

The domain is kept separate from [Arguments for dualism](/positions/arguments-for-dualism/) for the reason that moved [P-TU1](/positions/thought-and-understanding/#p-tu1) out of it. That domain's entries are counting rules *about* the anti-physicalist catalogue, and its intro commits every one of them to being a restraint whose retirement would *strengthen* Tenet 1's case. [P-MA1](/positions/memory-and-autonoesis/#p-ma1) is a first-order commitment whose retirement would weaken the wing resting on it, so placing it there would have falsified that intro — and the file stood five words under its hard length threshold besides.

**The foundational-dependency test.** [P-MA1](/positions/memory-and-autonoesis/#p-ma1) passes in the forward direction and is freely retireable. [Tenet 1](/tenets/#dualism)'s stated rationale is the explanatory gap for sensory consciousness, the judgement that materialism has not closed it, and the positive argument catalogue; none of those rests on remembering having a phenomenal character that individuates memory kinds. Retiring [P-MA1](/positions/memory-and-autonoesis/#p-ma1) narrows the gap's claimed domain back from the temporal case and leaves the tenet's rationale intact. What retirement would cost is downstream: the dual-domain reading of memory, the autonoetic half of the capability division, and the Map's resistance to the contingentist reclassification of re-experiencing are conditional on it, and the articles that use them say so.

**What this domain does not yet register.** The *mineness* limb — felt ownership as a separable phenomenal feature, and the step from synchronic for-me-ness to a diachronic identity-ground — is deliberately not booked here. The parent article holds it *below* the pastness limb, on the stated ground that the self-model programme has already delivered a decomposition for bodily self-location and self-identification (Blanke & Metzinger 2009) where nothing comparable has been attempted for felt pastness. Its identity-grounding payload is separately carried by the haecceity commitment registered at [P-I1](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i1) and [P-I2](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i2) in [individuation-and-subjecthood](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/), so a second band here risks two entries on one claim. It is recorded as a candidate P-MA2, for its own deliberation rather than folding into this one.

Calibration follows the [multi-axis schema](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#calibration-schema) (credence · external-evidence grade · structural centrality · model maturity · empirical discriminability · framework-internal only).

## Positions

## P-MA1: Autonoetic re-experiencing is constitutive of the kind of memory one has rather than an accompaniment to it — held at moderate credence on a decisive test that exists in design and has never been run

- **Status**: live <span id="p-ma1"></span>
- **Calibration** ([multi-axis](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#calibration-schema)): credence moderate (the ceiling the parent articles assign, and the register does not upgrade what the articles hold; the horn is held against a live countermodel on the kind question and against De Brigard's construct-validity challenge, to which the Map has no decisive answer) · external-evidence grade D (the decisive measurement is unmade; what exists is instrument work — Zaman, Setton, Catmur & Russell 2024 recovering a stable latent structure for autonoesis — and indirect signals that dissociate reported reliving from retained content, Talarico & Rubin 2003 the clearest, with access uncontrolled throughout) · structural centrality moderate (Tenet 1's rationale does not rest on it; the dual-domain reading of memory, the autonoetic half of the capability division and the Map's resistance to contingentism are conditional on it) · model maturity developed (worked case with a falsifier whose design is now specifiable component by component from published parts, and no formal model) · empirical discriminability direct (the falsifier is a runnable experiment on instruments that exist — longitudinal within-memory tracking of felt re-experiencing, graded rather than binary, crossed with reinstatement probes; unusually for this register the obstacle is that no one has run it, not that nothing could) · framework-internal only: no (the falsifier is a real measurement bearing on the claim; whether its eventual verdict carries *metaphysical* weight depends on the Map's framing, which the corpus states as its own rather than assuming)
- **Asserts**: The Map holds that autonoetic re-experiencing is constitutive of the kind of memory a person has rather than an accompaniment to it: what separates remembering from knowing is a mode of consciousness and not the information carried, so an episodic memory that semanticises loses a phenomenal property and thereby changes kind. The commitment is held at moderate credence and no higher, and the reason is a measurement that has not been made. The reading is staked on a specific falsifier — whether felt pastness fades by degrees *within a single memory* as that memory semanticises — and on that decisive phenomenological measurement nothing has come in. The constitutive reading is therefore **unrefuted and untested, the weakest of the falsifier states**, and the moderate credence recorded here is to be read against that rather than as a record of tests survived; unrefuted is not survived, and the register books the untested standing as part of the position rather than letting it pass as support. Two further verdicts are registered alongside, because the standing is not uniform across limbs. On the *kind* question a serious live countermodel stands: Gentry and Buckner (2024) argue from transitional gradation that episodic and semantic memory are not two kinds, assert partially autonoetic transitional forms, and explain the apparent sharpness of recollective experience away by an error theory — which is argument plus error theory rather than measurement, so it neither triggers the falsifier nor is answered by it, and the graded reading of the category is now mainstream. On the *instrument* question the evidence that exists is too thin and too miscalibrated across groups to decide. What the Map declines is conceding the phenomenal limb to an error theory in advance of measurement; what it accepts on the record is that the measurement, properly designed, could take the reading away.
- **Depends on**: Tenet 1 ([Dualism](/tenets/#dualism)) read as irreducibility, which the constitutive reading extends from sensation to the temporal case; [P-M1](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#p-m1) (a tenet removes a defeater but never upgrades the evidence — the Dualism tenet motivates treating re-experiencing as irreducible and supplies none of the evidence for it, which is what keeps the falsifier doing work rather than decorating the claim); hard-problem realism together with the *bounded* relocation reply, the bound being what disqualifies functionalist accounts of semanticisation and computational accounts of mental time travel as falsifiers, since they specify how the state is produced and not why its output is experienced — an unbounded version would shield the claim from memory science entirely and earn nothing distinctive from it; the licence to treat the pastness quale as a datum rather than an illusion awaiting debunking, from [phenomenal-authority-and-first-person-evidence](/topics/phenomenal-authority-and-first-person-evidence/); the manner/content separation (Klein 2016) that lets the same proposition be held autonoetically or noetically; [P-TU1](/positions/thought-and-understanding/#p-tu1), the same constitutive-versus-accompaniment structure registered for thought, likewise held abductively with downstream applications conditional on it; [P-D1](/positions/arguments-for-dualism/#p-d1) (the memory case shares the phenomenal-realist premise with the rest of the catalogue, so it sharpens the explanandum in a new domain without adding an independent confirmation)
- **Argued in**: [episodic-memory](/concepts/episodic-memory/), [semanticisation](/concepts/semanticisation/), [phenomenology-of-memory-and-the-self](/topics/phenomenology-of-memory-and-the-self/), [anoetic-noetic-autonoetic-consciousness](/concepts/anoetic-noetic-autonoetic-consciousness/) (§The Constitutive-Contingent Debate); applied in [consciousness-and-memory](/topics/consciousness-and-memory/) and [reconsolidation-as-selection-window](/topics/reconsolidation-as-selection-window/), and reached from the other direction in [phenomenal-output-causal-machinery-dissociation](/apex/phenomenal-output-causal-machinery-dissociation/)
- **Would shift if**: longitudinal phenomenological tracking showed felt pastness fading by degrees within a single memory as it semanticises, **with access effects controlled** — on which the constitutive reading loses on its own terms. The access control is a condition of the result counting, not a hedge added after the fact: the Map proposes an access reading on which a smoothly graded *report* profile is compatible with an ungraded phenomenal property, the grading living in retrieval accessibility rather than in the quality retrieved, with odour-cued memories of the most thoroughly semanticised decade arriving with the strongest sense of being brought back in time (Willander & Larsson 2006) as its evidence. So an adverse result needs a graded re-experiencing instrument rather than binary remember/know — whose format is categorical by construction, a graded fade and a discrete flip generating identical data — crossed with reinstatement probes, so a fading report sorts into transformation (fails under every route) or access loss (returns under the right probe). The position would shift equally if felt pastness decomposed into separately manipulable cues with no residual felt quality once they were controlled, leaving no unified item at the relocated site; or if the measures operationalising autonoesis were shown to converge on nothing, De Brigard's construct-validity limb made good, on which relocation would have nowhere to run. It would shift upward if the access reading were established — re-experiencing returning whole under reinstatement where reports grade under standard recall — which would convert the strongest present signal from indirect to direct support, or if a phenomenological instrument of settled reliability replaced the one whose reliability is under active dispute.
