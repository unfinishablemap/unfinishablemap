## [P-SC2](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc2)

The Map owes a subject-pairing law and does not have one.

- **Updated 2026-08-16**: the pairing gap linked to its external counterpart, the *boundary problem*, already argued next door in [boundary-and-projection](/voids/boundary-and-projection/) (full references there). Gómez-Emilsson & Percy 2023 and Fekete et al. 2016 added to *Asserts*; *Would shift if* amended to admit imported progress, which it had excluded. No calibration change on any axis: naming an external counterpart for a debt does not discharge it, and the entry stays a meta-claim about the Map's own state, so the n/a external-evidence grade holds. Model maturity had the one case for movement, but a sharper statement of an unpaid debt is not a more mature model.
- **Updated 2026-09-24**: **two named debts booked; no band moved.** Outer review (2026-09-24, ChatGPT 5.6 Pro §2.6) raised a new inference rather than a factual error: the Map infers subjecthood in animals, AI systems and disorders of consciousness from behaviour, reports and neural signatures, which are the very outcomes the census-indexed law is meant to govern, so a census fixed by outcomes and then used to condition the outcome law selects its latent causes by their effects. The register already conceded that no eligibility or pairing rule exists, but it had not named this circularity. It is now booked in *Asserts* as a fifth gap, **census circularity**, together with the requirement it creates: eligibility criteria fixed independently of the tested outcome. This is the membership-level form of the independent handle [P-SC1](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc1)'s discriminability field already demands for identifying a census hypothesis. The review's second point, the **copies question** (whether same-intention subjects accumulate, dilute, cancel or renormalise in *P(O | C₁…Cₙ, X)*), is booked under multi-agent composition, which had covered only the conflict case. It is cross-referenced to the N≥2 composition gap that [multi-agent-born-preservation-problem](/topics/multi-agent-born-preservation-problem/) records and routes to [P-Q10](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q10); that page treats joint-correlation preservation, not concordant intentions, so the copies question is new to the corpus rather than imported from it. [P-AC3](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac3)'s additive rule does not settle it either: it counts copies as distinct subjects for moral weight and does not say how their conditionals compose. *Would shift if* gains a discharge condition for each. Why this is not a new position: both are further missing pieces of the one pairing law [P-SC2](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc2) already records as owed, so a separate entry would split a single debt. Why no band moved: credence stays *high* because the debt is still a fact verifiable by inspection, and model maturity stays *developed* because a more precise statement of what is unpaid does not make the model more mature (the same judgement as the 2026-08-16 note). The register edit was net-negative in length (2,595 → 2,536 words) because this pass also relocated the file's dated notes here.
