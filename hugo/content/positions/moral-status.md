## About this domain

This domain covers **moral standing — who can be wronged** — as a register question distinct from the two neighbours it is easiest to collapse into. The scope domains ([Consciousness scope](/positions/consciousness-scope/), [AI consciousness scope](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/)) say *where consciousness is*; the value domain's upstream premise ([P-VS3](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs3)) says *what bears intrinsic value*. Neither says which entities have moral status — which systems present a perspective that can be wronged rather than a description to be reported. The direction of derivation is itself registered: the Map derives the *necessity* direction of the status criterion from the value premise — normative teleology requires an experiencer for whom the trajectory is good or bad ([consciousness-value-connection](/concepts/consciousness-value-connection/)) — so [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1) sits downstream of [P-VS3](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs3), extending the ordering that file calls "numbered last, logically first"; sufficiency is a further commitment, registered as such in the entry. The two must not be identified: [P-VS3](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs3) would survive a widening of the moral circle past sentience, and a collapse of the status criterion would not by itself move the value realism it rests on.

Seeded 2026-08-12, the criterion having been stated in live articles ([sentientism](/concepts/sentientism/), [ethics-under-dualism](/topics/ethics-under-dualism/)) with no register entry recording it. Dated update notes live in [Moral status — calibration history](/positions/moral-status-calibration-history/); each entry indexes them from a **Calibration history** line, and every band, dependency and shift condition stays here. Every entry here passes the [foundational-dependency test](/positions/): no tenet's rationale runs through moral standing, so retiring an entry leaves all five tenets argued as before — the dependency runs the other way, the phenomenal reading standing or falling with Tenet 1.

**Scope decision (2026-08-24): enhancement gets an entry here, not a domain of its own.** Two outer reviews of 2026-08-23 found the register silent on cognitive enhancement; one proposed an enhancement-ethics domain covering interface preservation, identity continuity, consent, capability justice and precaution. Declined on the register's own terms: identity continuity is carried by [P-I1](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i1) and the pairing debt at [P-SC2](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc2), interface preservation by the quantum-interface mechanism debt, and consent and capability justice the Map does not yet *hold*, [ethics-of-cognitive-enhancement-under-dualism](/topics/ethics-of-cognitive-enhancement-under-dualism/) declining the bridge principles at each point — a domain of open questions would invert what the register is for. The commitment in it is a moral-status question, registered below as [P-MS2](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms2); applied verdicts, when they arrive, belong to the applied-verdicts domain in [the domain list](/positions/).

## Positions

## P-MS1: Valenced experience is necessary and sufficient for moral status, read phenomenally (phenomenal sentientism)

- **Status**: live <span id="p-ms1"></span>
- **Calibration** ([multi-axis](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#calibration-schema)): credence moderate (capped by [P-VS3](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs3), the premise it extends, and held at the cap — the calibration history says why) · external-evidence grade D (reads on the entry's one first-order presupposition, that felt valence is a real property for the criterion to track, for which Tenet 1 is the sole support and which the encyclopedia literature declines to distinguish from the functional role; the normative conjuncts are *n/a* by the schema) · structural centrality moderate · model maturity developed (both directions carry a worked argument, from different sources; what is commitment-level is the bridge between them, carried in *Depends on*) · empirical discriminability indirect (nothing tests the criterion directly; what bears on it is downstream — the phenomenology and illusionism verdicts that would move [P-VS3](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs3) move this with it) · framework-internal only: yes
- **Asserts**: The Map holds **phenomenal sentientism**: the capacity for valenced experience — experience that feels good or bad to its subject — is both necessary and sufficient for moral status, where the criterion is the presence of *felt* experience rather than the functional role pain and pleasure play in an information-processing system. This is a claim about *who can be wronged*, registered separately from [P-VS3](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs3)'s claim about *what bears intrinsic value*, and the derivation from the second to the first reaches the **necessity** direction only: normative teleology requires an experiencer for whom the trajectory is good or bad, so a system with a biological good but no subject offers a description to be reported rather than a perspective that can be wronged. **Sufficiency** — that possessing the capacity is enough — is not delivered by that argument; [consciousness-value-connection](/concepts/consciousness-value-connection/) holds it as a further commitment rather than a corollary, held on the mainstream sentientist ground, the argument from arbitrariness [sentientism](/concepts/sentientism/) runs against anthropocentrism. Phenomenal sentientism is the conjunction, and this entry registers it as one. The experiencer the necessity derivation needs is a **momentary experiential locus** — whoever this pain is bad for, now — not a persisting self ([a locus, not a career](/concepts/no-self-objection-to-phenomenal-value/#maps-reply)): status sits at the base layer of value, and the persisting subject the agency cluster asserts is a further claim this entry does not rest on. That reply to the no-self objection is unrefuted and contested, not tested. The necessity direction is where the live opposition sits, and the rival to beat is **biocentrism** (Taylor 1981/1986, Attfield 1987; Varner 1998 is a hostile witness rather than a clean rival — *In Nature's Interests?* ranks biological interests least weighty, and *Personhood, Ethics, and Animal Cognition* (2012) abandons biocentrism for sentientism) rather than ecocentrism, because the biocentrist concedes that status attaches to individuals and disputes only the experience requirement; the Map's conation-requirement reply (Feinberg 1974) locates that disagreement rather than settles it. The phenomenal qualification is the Map's own, licensed by Tenet 1 ([Dualism](/tenets/#dualism)): phenomenal sentientism stands or falls with the tenet, and does not claim independent support from the applied literature that leaves the distinction unmade. Its named rival on the reading of valence itself is Carruthers' evaluativism (2018), engaged at [valence](/concepts/valence/), where the Map concedes that neither side refutes the other from inside its own commitments and takes the phenomenal horn on the undelivered why-felt step rather than on parsimony. What the phenomenal reading buys is not a different verdict in any actual case but a different account of hard-case uncertainty — ignorance about a real boundary rather than indecision about where to draw one. Centrality is *moderate* on exactly that ground: overturning the criterion would restructure the applied-ethics wing while moving no tenet, no interface claim, and no scope verdict.
- **Depends on**: Tenet 1 ([Dualism](/tenets/#dualism)) — the phenomenal rather than functional reading; [P-VS3](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs3) via the necessity derivation in [consciousness-value-connection](/concepts/consciousness-value-connection/), taken at the momentary grain [no-self-objection-to-phenomenal-value](/concepts/no-self-objection-to-phenomenal-value/) defends (this entry depends on the bare realism claim alone and does not inherit [P-VS4](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs4)'s pluralism, which grades *stakes*, not status); the sufficiency direction as a further normative commitment, resting on the argument from arbitrariness in [sentientism](/concepts/sentientism/) rather than on [P-VS3](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs3); the standing-capacity reading of "capacity" ([consciousness-value-connection](/concepts/consciousness-value-connection/) — a dreamless sleeper remains a welfare subject); the falsity of [illusionism](/concepts/illusionism/); the phenomenal horn of the valence dispute, held at [valence](/concepts/valence/) as a conceded draw against Carruthers 2018; the conation-requirement reply to biocentrism (Feinberg 1974). Passes the [foundational-dependency test](/positions/) — freely retireable.
- **Argued in**: [sentientism](/concepts/sentientism/) (the criterion, the phenomenal/functional distinction, the biocentrism reply, and which experiencer standing needs), [ethics-under-dualism](/topics/ethics-under-dualism/), [phenomenal-value-realism](/topics/phenomenal-value-realism/), [consciousness-value-connection](/concepts/consciousness-value-connection/), [valence](/concepts/valence/) (the phenomenal/functional draw and the evaluativist rival), [phenomenal-normativity-environmental-ethics](/topics/phenomenal-normativity-environmental-ethics/)
- **Would shift if**: illusionism were vindicated — then "capacity for valenced experience" names no property and the criterion picks out nothing, retiring this entry together with [P-VS3](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs3); or a functional reading of sentience were shown to survive the illusionist threat with its rationale intact — Frankish's "The Ethical Implications of Illusionism" (*Neuroethics*, 2024), arguing that illusionism leaves consciousness's value-grounding role in place, is the live attempt rather than a defeater landed — which would collapse the phenomenal qualification and revert the Map to mainstream functional sentientism (the entry's distinctive content, not the moral circle, is what would go); or the biocentrist supplied an account of why a subjectless good generates duties that does not covertly appeal to mattering-to-someone, which would break the necessity direction and widen the circle past sentience; or the overinclusiveness and marginal-cases pressure were shown unanswerable within the status/stakes separation ([sentientism](/concepts/sentientism/) accepts a residue there knowingly), which would force grading status itself rather than only stakes; or a verified report of a valenced experience that was genuinely for no one — a state with no locus, not merely no self-model, so selfless MPE states ([scoped out there](/concepts/no-self-objection-to-phenomenal-value/#rivals)) do not count — which would sever felt value from mattering-to and retire this derivation with [P-VS3](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs3)'s; or robust intuitions held up that an affectless conscious being can be wronged — Chalmers' Vulcan (forthcoming; Shepherd 2024 takes it up), on which the Map bites the bullet at [ethics-under-dualism](/topics/ethics-under-dualism/) and [sentientism](/concepts/sentientism/) — breaking the necessity direction from the conscious side as the biocentrist limb would from the living side, and widening the criterion to consciousness as such.
- **Calibration history**: [notes of 2026-08-24, 2026-08-27 and 2026-09-05](/positions/moral-status-calibration-history/#p-ms1) — the fourth shift-limb answered by [P-MS2](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms2); the momentary-locus cut; the 2026-09-05 pass that scoped the derivation to necessity, named Carruthers, Frankish and Varner, weighed and kept every band, and declined decomposition and the coherence-only demotion as operator schema questions; and the Vulcan limb added later that day.

## P-MS2: Moral status is threshold, not scalar — enhancement raises what is at stake in a life without raising its status

- **Status**: live <span id="p-ms2"></span>
- **Calibration** ([multi-axis](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#calibration-schema)): credence low (a conjunction — the threshold reading inherits [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1)'s ceiling, while the stakes-grading half routes through [P-VS4](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs4) and cannot exceed *its* band) · external-evidence grade n/a (normative — how status relates to the degree of the capacity conferring it; no first-order presupposition beyond [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1)'s, so the empirical axes have nothing to read on) · structural centrality low · model maturity developed (both directions, a verdict-changing case and the argument's general form are worked in [ethics-of-cognitive-enhancement-under-dualism](/topics/ethics-of-cognitive-enhancement-under-dualism/) and [moral-status-threshold-or-degrees](/topics/moral-status-threshold-or-degrees/); short of *formalised* because that case turns on an intuition Kagan does not share and the non-aggregative flank stays open) · empirical discriminability n/a (normative) · framework-internal only: yes
- **Asserts**: The Map reads "capacity for valenced experience" in **[P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1)** as a threshold predicate rather than a magnitude. An intervention that widens or enriches the valenced experience of a subject *already* possessing the capacity therefore raises what is at stake in that life without raising its moral status. Sufficiency is a conditional on possession: "P suffices for Q" does not license "more P, more Q". Status moves only at a threshold crossing, in either direction: conferring the capacity confers status; removing it takes status with it, whether by the affective-only disenhancement [sentientism](/concepts/sentientism/) leaves open or by a bypass that raises measured performance while extinguishing felt valence. Consistency fixes the reading: enhancement is the marginal-cases pressure arriving from above, so a scalar answer here forces one below, and the Map has already declined to let a human's status fall with a capacity that is diminished or in abeyance — status tracks the standing capacity, which is why a dreamless sleeper or a patient in reversible coma keeps it ([consciousness-value-connection](/concepts/consciousness-value-connection/)). Where the capacity is irreversibly gone the same rule runs the other way: [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1) as worded entails that intrinsic status goes with it, and what remains owed concerning that person — prior wishes, history, standing relationships — is owed derivatively, not on a second ground of status. The register adopts that reading rather than a relational ground; [sentientism](/concepts/sentientism/) carries the same account. The affirmative ground is equal consideration of equal interests — the wrongness of a given suffering does not vary with who suffers it — which [ethics-of-cognitive-enhancement-under-dualism](/topics/ethics-of-cognitive-enhancement-under-dualism/) tests on a fixed-stakes pair: the same pain, one patient enhanced, where a sliding scale licenses shifting it onto the unenhanced patient, a threshold forbids that, and no reassignment of stakes reproduces the divergence. The upward grading is real but belongs to *stakes*, through [P-VS4](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs4)'s dimensions, which this entry inherits for that half alone and at [P-VS4](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs4)'s cost. The separation **relocates** Nozick's utility monster instead of blocking it, since equal consideration of *equal* interests legitimately weights a subject whose intensity ceiling has been raised, and an unbounded ceiling is an unbounded weight — the Map's extension of an argument Nozick aimed at aggregation across persons. That case shows the relocation is not verdict-inert, not that it is costless. The rivals of record are the gradualists: Kagan's *limited hierarchy* (*How to Count Animals, more or less*, 2019) and DeGrazia's sliding scale (2008), joined on the second-threshold question by McMahan's two tiers (2008), whose respect tier the Map declines; Sebo's *The Moral Circle* (2025) weights by expected value and is read as a credence over a threshold rather than a rival on this point. Centrality is *low*: retiring this disturbs nothing upstream, resolving an ambiguity inside [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1) rather than supplying anything [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1) needs.
- **Depends on**: [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1) (the criterion this reads, and the source of its ceiling); [P-VS4](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs4) for the stakes half only, never for the status half; the status/stakes separation and the disenhancement treatment in [sentientism](/concepts/sentientism/) — the stakes half being the layer a no-self deflation of the persisting subject would reach, while status holds at the momentary locus [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1) names. Passes the [foundational-dependency test](/positions/) — no tenet's rationale runs through it.
- **Argued in**: [moral-status-threshold-or-degrees](/topics/moral-status-threshold-or-degrees/) (the equality argument in general form after Wendler 2023, the arbitrariness objection and the presence-of-a-locus reply, Buchanan answered, one threshold not two, the aggregation residue); [ethics-of-cognitive-enhancement-under-dualism](/topics/ethics-of-cognitive-enhancement-under-dualism/) (the upward direction, the fixed-stakes case, the argument against the roster); [sentientism](/concepts/sentientism/) (the status/stakes separation, the marginal-cases collision, disenhancement as the downward crossing, the coma account); [phenomenal-value-realism](/topics/phenomenal-value-realism/) (the stakes grading); [ethics-of-consciousness-invertebrate-question](/topics/ethics-of-consciousness-invertebrate-question/) (the threshold / stakes / desert triad applied to aggregation)
- **Would shift if**: a defensible account were supplied of degrees of the *capacity itself*, as distinct from degrees of what the capacity delivers — which would make the magnitude reading available and reopen the fork; or a gradualist reason for weighting were found to survive translation into stakes — a reason for counting one patient's interests for more that is not about what that patient has at stake — the translation argument being the standing test, the fixed-stakes case its instance, and Kagan's acceptance that superior beings count for more the live pressure on it, a disagreement over which intuition to keep; or that case were shown to fail — its fixed stakes reproduced by reassignment after all — leaving the separation recommending no act the scalar reading would not and the utility-monster residue growing until the two are practically indistinguishable, which would trigger [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1)'s fourth limb and force grading status; or enhancement were shown to change the *number* of subjects rather than the capacity of one, which is a census question ([moral-census-opacity](/concepts/moral-census-opacity/)) this entry does not answer; or [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1) or [P-VS3](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs3) fell, retiring this with them.
