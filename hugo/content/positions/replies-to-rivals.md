## About this domain

These positions record the settled **grade of the Map's reply to each named rival view**: the grounds the reply stands on, what it earns, and what it leaves standing. Each rival is stated at full strength, in its defenders' own terms, and is marked undefeated wherever the Map has not refuted it. The domain holds no list of refutations. An entry here is an accounting claim about a reply the Map has published, transcribed from the page that argues it, so that other pages and tasks can cite the grade by anchor instead of restating it from memory.

One entry per rival. Calibration follows the [multi-axis schema](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#calibration-schema); on every entry in this domain the credence band attaches to the accounting claim and never to the falsity of the rival.

## Positions

## P-RR1: The Map rejects illusionism on three stated grounds and, past them, at a framework boundary; the verdict is on a live programme, not a defeated one

- **Status**: live <span id="p-rr1"></span>
- **Calibration** ([multi-axis](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#calibration-schema)): credence high (attaches to the accounting claim, that this is the Map's reply and this is its grade, checkable by inspection of [illusionism](/concepts/illusionism/); it is no credence that illusionism is false) · external-evidence grade n/a (an accounting claim about the Map's own reply, not a first-order world-claim) · structural centrality high (six entries name illusionism's vindication as a retirement condition) · model maturity developed · empirical discriminability n/a (accounting) · framework-internal only: yes (the rejection stands at Tenet 1's standing, a starting point the Map chooses and states as its own)
- **Asserts**: The Map holds that it rejects illusionism, and that its reply has this grade and no higher. In the hub's words: "the mechanism the view owes by its own standard is not yet built, the illusionist declines to grant what the Map takes as a datum, that experience is what we know most directly, and functional roles underdetermine experience. Past these the disagreement is a boundary between frameworks, not a refutation." Three riders belong to the grade. Against the regress objection, Frankish "is right that the bare regress proves nothing". The programme is still being developed, so the Map's verdict is "a verdict on a live programme, not a defeated one". And the target is stated as the illusionist states it: illusionism denies phenomenal consciousness "as traditionally conceived", experience bearing qualitative properties "whether physical or non-physical", "not consciousness in every functional or everyday sense". Only the first ground presses the illusionist on a standard he accepts. The second and third are the Map's own starting points; the hub offers the third "from a commitment it chooses rather than derives". They supply what the illusionist declines and no evidence against him ([P-M1](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#p-m1)).
- **Does not claim**: that illusionism is incoherent, self-undermining or self-stultifying; that the regress, Tallis or Strawson settles the matter; that illusionism denies consciousness in every sense; that the denial is a symptom of cognitive closure; that the mechanism cannot be built. Nothing here loosens the rejection either: the Map holds it, and the entry concedes nothing to epiphenomenalism.
- **Depends on**: Tenet 1 ([Dualism](/tenets/#dualism)) as the source of the datum and of the underdetermination ground, a chosen starting point; [P-M1](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#p-m1) (a tenet removes a defeater and never upgrades the evidence); [P-D1](/positions/arguments-for-dualism/#p-d1) (the datum is the phenomenal-realist premise the argument catalogue shares, so the rejection is not premise-disjoint from it); [P-MC1](/positions/arguments-for-mental-causation/#p-mc1) (which already records that the Map meets illusionism "at a framework boundary"). *Leaned on by*, without edits here: [P-CS1](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs1), [P-AC4](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac4), [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1), [P-VS1](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs1), [P-VS3](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs3) and [P-VS4](/positions/value-in-selection/#p-vs4) each name illusionism's vindication as a retirement condition, and this entry states what the reply they rest on has earned.
- **Argued in**: [illusionism](/concepts/illusionism/) (the hub this entry transcribes), [functional-seeming](/concepts/functional-seeming/)
- **Would shift if**: the mechanism were built: in the hub's words, "a mechanism meeting Frankish's two-part explanandum where the represented and the representing coincide would remove its main in-framework objection, leaving only the boundary disagreement". The grade would move the other way if "each attempt to specify the mechanism turns out to need what it denies—shown case by case, which the bare regress does not do". As an accounting claim the entry also shifts whenever the hub's wording does, and must then be re-transcribed.
