## About this domain

These positions record what the Map holds about the *evidential structure of its own case for Tenet 1* — not whether dualism is true, but what the catalogue of anti-physicalist arguments, taken together, is entitled to claim. The domain is the first-order counterpart of [P-M2](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#p-m2), which registers the common-cause null as a standing methodological rule and names this case explicitly as one of the two the discipline is held against: "it is the rule the convergence-for-dualism case and the void-catalogue case must survive." [P-V1–P-V3](/positions/voids-as-evidence/) registered the void-catalogue instance in June 2026; this file registers the argument-catalogue instance, so both of the Map's most ambitious convergence claims now carry a register entry stating their discount rather than only their force.

The entries are transcriptions of settled corpus wording, not fresh derivations. Each was argued in article prose first — in [the convergence argument for dualism](/topics/the-convergence-argument-for-dualism/), and in the concessions [philosophical-zombies](/concepts/philosophical-zombies/), [qualia](/concepts/qualia/), and [dualism](/concepts/dualism/) inherit from it — and is registered here so a downstream apex or applied piece can cite the discount by ID instead of restating it. Dated update notes live in [the domain's calibration history](/positions/arguments-for-dualism-calibration-history/); each entry indexes them from a **Calibration history** line, and every band, dependency and shift condition stays here. The positions are deliberately *self-limiting*: each subtracts from a claim the Map would like to be able to make. Overturning any of them would strengthen rather than weaken the case for Tenet 1, which is why their structural centrality bands are low to moderate despite the high credence attaching to them.

**The foundational-dependency test (applied 2026-08-03).** All three pass, and this domain is the test's mirror image. The rule asks whether removing a position leaves every tenet with an intact rationale (see [positions](/positions/)). [Tenet 1](/tenets/#dualism)'s stated rationale is the explanatory gap, the judgement that materialism has not closed it, and the positive argument catalogue — and no entry here supplies any of those. Each is a *counting rule about* the catalogue: [P-D1](/positions/arguments-for-dualism/#p-d1) and [P-D3](/positions/arguments-for-dualism/#p-d3) discount its independence, [P-D2](/positions/arguments-for-dualism/#p-d2) forbids the framework commitment from being counted as a third route. Retiring any of them therefore leaves Tenet 1's rationale not merely intact but apparently *stronger*, since what a retirement removes is a restraint. One clarification the test forces, because the entry reads like Tenet 1's argumentative support and is not: [P-D2](/positions/arguments-for-dualism/#p-d2)'s "Bidirectional Interaction selects dualism" clause operates *downstream* of the tenet, which is stated as irreducibility and is neutral between substance and property readings. The selection step earns the Map's interactionist reading, not Tenet 1, so its removal costs no tenet its rationale.

Calibration follows the [multi-axis schema](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#calibration-schema) (credence · external-evidence grade · structural centrality · model maturity · empirical discriminability · framework-internal only).

## Positions

## P-D1: The anti-physicalist arguments are premise-sharing, not evidentially independent

- **Status**: live <span id="p-d1"></span>
- **Calibration** ([multi-axis](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#calibration-schema)): credence high · external-evidence grade B (the dependency is establishable by inspection of the published arguments, and the phenomenal concept strategy is the mainstream physicalist statement of exactly this shared premise) · structural centrality moderate · model maturity developed (worked cluster analysis, no formal independence metric) · empirical discriminability n/a (a claim about argument structure, not about the world) · framework-internal only: no
- **Asserts**: The Map holds that its catalogue of arguments against physicalism converges on irreducibility from *shared* rather than independent premises, so their cumulative force is real but modest. The arguments form three clusters: phenomenal intuition (Mary's Room, the qualia arguments, Nagel), the explanatory gap (the gap itself, the zombie argument, Kripke's modal argument), and structural features (unity, intentionality); the argument from reason is a separate self-defeat charge rather than a fourth cluster. The first two clusters each rest on one shared premise — that first-person phenomenal reports reveal genuine features of reality, and that the gap between physical description and phenomenal character is real rather than merely apparent, the gap cluster's modal members also sharing [the inference from conceivability to possibility](/concepts/modal-epistemology/) — so if a cluster's common starting point fails, as the phenomenal concept strategy holds for both by denying that phenomenal concepts track a genuine ontological category, its members fail together rather than confirming one another. Arguments within a single cluster therefore contribute little more than one strong argument from that cluster, and the genuine force comes from *across* clusters: phenomenal intuition, the explanatory gap, and at least the unity prong resist physical explanation by routes that do not reduce to one another. The defensible reading is "substantially stronger than any single argument," never "overwhelming." The Map holds this concession as a standing calibration on every article that cites the convergence, not as a retraction of the case.
- **Depends on**: [P-M2](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#p-m2) (the common-cause null as standing convergence discount, instantiated here); [P-M1](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#p-m1) (coherence cannot upgrade its own evidential tier); [the per-cluster independence scoring](/project/per-cluster-independence-scoring/); the phenomenal concept strategy (Loar 1990, Papineau 2002, Balog 2012) as one physicalist statement of the shared premise
- **Argued in**: [the-convergence-argument-for-dualism](/topics/the-convergence-argument-for-dualism/), [philosophical-zombies](/concepts/philosophical-zombies/), [qualia](/concepts/qualia/), [dualism](/concepts/dualism/), [functionalism-argument](/arguments/functionalism-argument/); and instantiated independently, in a domain this entry never scored, by [aesthetics-and-consciousness](/topics/aesthetics-and-consciousness/), whose audit collapses five aesthetic lines onto the unity prong and explanatory gap, finding one (aesthetic creation) separable but not independent — [P-MC4](/positions/arguments-for-mental-causation/#p-mc4)
- **Would shift if**: an independence-scoring instrument showed one or more clusters to be genuinely premise-disjoint from the conceivability route (raising the cumulative reading toward multiplication); or both physicalist accounts of *why* the arguments share a premise were shown to fail — the phenomenal concept strategy in all its variants, and Stoljar's [epistemic view](/concepts/ignorance-hypothesis/), which faults our knowledge of the physical rather than phenomenal concepts; or the third-personally-anchored prong of the unity argument were shown to collapse into report-dependence after all, tightening the discount further by removing the one genuinely cross-cluster contribution.
- **Calibration history**: [notes of 2026-09-18, 2026-10-07 and 2026-11-10](/positions/arguments-for-dualism-calibration-history/#p-d1) — *Argued in* gains the functionalism argument's cumulative-case table; the second shift condition also requires Stoljar's epistemic view to fail; the cluster map restated to match the convergence page and the hub, Mary's Room moving to the phenomenal-intuition cluster. No band moves.
- **Last reviewed**: 2026-11-10

## P-D2: Convergence earns irreducibility; Bidirectional Interaction selects dualism, and the two do not compound

- **Status**: live <span id="p-d2"></span>
- **Calibration** ([multi-axis](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#calibration-schema)): credence high · external-evidence grade n/a (a counting rule about the Map's own case, not a first-order world-claim) · structural centrality moderate · model maturity developed · empirical discriminability n/a (normative) · framework-internal only: no
- **Asserts**: The Map holds that its case runs three sources of support in parallel — the epistemology of convergence, the body of arguments themselves (with the cross-traditional and cross-cultural patterns), and the framework-level commitment to dualism — and that only the first two are independent. The framework-level commitment is not a third route but a *selection rule operating on the output* of the first two. Cross-traditional and philosophical convergence earn *irreducibility* across the rivals that respect it (panpsychism, neutral monism, idealism, Madhyamaka emptiness); [Bidirectional Interaction](/tenets/#bidirectional-interaction) then selects dualism from within that set, on the strength of a separately-supported argument (the self-stultification objection to epiphenomenalism and the evidence for mental causation) rather than a bare appeal to commitment. The honest catalogue language the Map commits to is: *convergence earns irreducibility; bidirectional interaction selects dualism among the irreducibility-respecting alternatives; the two earnings do not compound into a single triple-supported case for substance dualism.* Counting the selection as a converging line would convert framework-internal coherence into evidential-status elevation — the count-inflation the coherence-vs-evidential-status discipline forbids.
- **Depends on**: [P-M2](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#p-m2) (the standing convergence discount this instantiates); [P-D1](/positions/arguments-for-dualism/#p-d1) (the within-catalogue dependency the selection sits downstream of); [evidential-status-discipline](/project/evidential-status-discipline/) (the coherence-vs-evidential-status rule); Tenet 3 ([Bidirectional Interaction](/tenets/#bidirectional-interaction)) as the selector, and [bidirectional-interaction](/concepts/bidirectional-interaction/) for its independent positive case; [P-MC1–P-MC3](/positions/arguments-for-mental-causation/) (the register home of the "separately-supported argument" this entry names — what self-stultification earns, where Tenet 3's universal reading outruns it, and the completeness-denial that supplies the exclusion criterion the selection step operates with), and specifically [P-MC1](/positions/arguments-for-mental-causation/#p-mc1), whose registered dependency on [P-D1](/positions/arguments-for-dualism/#p-d1) means the selector's warrant inherits this domain's own premise-sharing discount rather than standing clear of it
- **Argued in**: [the-convergence-argument-for-dualism](/topics/the-convergence-argument-for-dualism/), [dualism](/concepts/dualism/), [evidential-status-discipline](/project/evidential-status-discipline/), [materialism-argument](/arguments/materialism-argument/)
- **Would shift if**: the positive case for Bidirectional Interaction were shown to be independently supported by evidence that also bears on irreducibility (which would make it partly a genuine third route rather than purely a selection rule); or the irreducibility-respecting rivals were shown to be excluded by the convergence itself, so that no selection step were needed; or the selection were shown to be doing covert argumentative work upstream of the convergence rather than downstream of it, which would make the separation a presentational device rather than a real structural distinction and force a harder discount.
- **Calibration history**: [notes of 2026-08-02, 2026-09-06 and 2026-09-18](/positions/arguments-for-dualism-calibration-history/#p-d2) — the reciprocal pointer to [P-MC1](/positions/arguments-for-mental-causation/#p-mc1)–[P-MC3](/positions/arguments-for-mental-causation/#p-mc3) and why the grounding clause stands at its scope; the selector's warrant shares the explanatory-gap premise, so "separately-supported" is not premise-disjointness; *Argued in* gains the materialism argument. Calibration unchanged throughout.

## P-D3: Cross-traditional convergence is near-independence, not a third confirmation

- **Status**: live <span id="p-d3"></span>
- **Calibration** ([multi-axis](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#calibration-schema)): credence moderate · external-evidence grade C (rests on the historical record of Indo-Greek exchange channels through Gandhara and on a textual assessment of the candidate third lineage; independence claims about traditions are historically contestable and no independence score has been graded) · structural centrality low · model maturity developed · empirical discriminability indirect (further historical and textual work on candidate lineages bears on it) · framework-internal only: no
- **Asserts**: The Map holds that convergence between the Indian and Western traditions on consciousness's irreducibility is *near-independence* — strong enough to count against a parochially-Western-methodology explanation, not strong enough to be cited as a third independent confirmation. Independence varies and the Map does not overstate it: Buddhism and Vedanta share an Indian philosophical milieu, and Indo-Greek exchange through Gandhara created possible influence channels between Western and Indian thought. The natural candidate for a historically independent third lineage — early Chinese philosophy — was assessed and declined on the merits: Zhuangzi's butterfly dream concerns the fluidity of selfhood and sits closer to the Madhyamaka emptiness reading than to recognition of irreducibility, and the Neo-Confucian *li* / *qi* debates are not cleanly irreducibility-recognising, since *qi* is a physicalist-friendly category on many readings. Establishing a genuinely independent third lineage remains open work rather than a completed step, and the two named candidates are no longer untouched: the West African material has been assessed and returns a self-limiting verdict — [african-philosophy-of-consciousness](/concepts/african-philosophy-of-consciousness/) reads it as suggestive context for dualism rather than independent evidence, since counting Bantu, Akan, Yoruba and Zulu as separate votes for irreducibility would perform the unanimist flattening Hountondji names as ethnophilosophy's founding error — and the Mesoamerican instance of the Indigenous American candidate has been assessed and declined as a basin edge ([mesoamerican-nahua-philosophy-of-mind](/topics/mesoamerican-nahua-philosophy-of-mind/) flows away from irreducibility, not toward it), leaving Indigenous American traditions beyond the Nahua case as the remaining unassessed candidates. Two further candidate lineages have been weighed without moving the verdict. Islamic *falsafa* is credited — it meets the depth criteria and finds irreducibility, which is why the evaluative-qualia tally reads *three* near-independent strands rather than two — but it is not the missing independent third: it shares the Greek inheritance the Western tradition draws on, and the Neoplatonist common-cause audit assigns moderate-to-substantial shared-source weight to the Ibn Sīnā strand. The Neo-Confucian strand is declined again on a ground the *li* / *qi* assessment does not reach: Wang Yangming's *liángzhī* is on-target phenomenology but postdates a millennium of Chan Buddhist presence — a channel running back toward the Indian lineage already counted once — while the securely pre-Buddhist Mencian layer is independent yet claims only that moral response is innate. A further limit holds even where independence does: the traditions converge on the *problem* while diverging on the *solution* — Buddhist analysis concludes with *anattā*, Advaita with monism — so what the pattern evidences is the reality of the explanatory gap, which supports but does not alone establish dualism.
- **Depends on**: [P-M2](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#p-m2) (a convergence is a single observation read N times until a distinguishing test passes); [P-D2](/positions/arguments-for-dualism/#p-d2) (this convergence earns irreducibility, not dualism); the historical record of Indo-Greek transmission; the textual assessment of the early-Chinese candidate; [neoplatonist-common-cause-weight-for-cross-tradition-introspection-architecture-parallels](/topics/neoplatonist-common-cause-weight-for-cross-tradition-introspection-architecture-parallels/) (the common-cause audit whose moderate-to-substantial weighting of the Ibn Sīnā strand grounds the Islamic verdict the Asserts records)
- **Argued in**: [the-convergence-argument-for-dualism](/topics/the-convergence-argument-for-dualism/), [cross-traditional-convergence-on-consciousness-irreducibility](/topics/cross-traditional-convergence-on-consciousness-irreducibility/), [evaluative-qualia-phenomenal-normativity-across-traditions](/topics/evaluative-qualia-phenomenal-normativity-across-traditions/), [evaluative-phenomenal-character](/concepts/evaluative-phenomenal-character/), [african-philosophy-of-consciousness](/concepts/african-philosophy-of-consciousness/)
- **Would shift if**: a genuinely independent third lineage were established on textual and historical grounds (which would raise this toward a real triangulation) — the corpus's worked instrument for adjudicating such a candidate is the *basin-edge* analysis, defined in [japanese-philosophy-of-mind-kyoto-school](/topics/japanese-philosophy-of-mind-kyoto-school/) and worked in [chinese-philosophy-of-mind](/topics/chinese-philosophy-of-mind/) and [mesoamerican-nahua-philosophy-of-mind](/topics/mesoamerican-nahua-philosophy-of-mind/), whose Chinese case [cross-traditional-convergence-on-consciousness-irreducibility](/topics/cross-traditional-convergence-on-consciousness-irreducibility/) already uses as a partial discharge of the common-cause objection; or the Indo-Greek transmission channel were shown to have carried the relevant doctrines, which would collapse the Indian and Western traditions toward a single lineage and downgrade the position further; or an independently-graded independence score were applied to the traditions, replacing the current qualitative "near-independence" verdict with a calibrated one.
- **Calibration history**: [notes of 2026-08-02 and 2026-08-20](/positions/arguments-for-dualism-calibration-history/#p-d3) — the Islamic-credited / Neo-Confucian-declined assessments and the five-tradition article added; the Neoplatonist audit linked, the West African candidate recorded as self-limiting, the basin-edge set corrected to Kyoto/Chinese/Nahua. No band move: credence *moderate*, grade *C*.
