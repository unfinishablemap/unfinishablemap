## About this domain

These positions record the Map's **per-class substrate verdicts for artificial hardware** — what each engineered class supplies or withholds at the interface the tenets require. They are split out from [the AI consciousness scope register](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/) for two reasons. First, that register's P-AC1 covers *conventional digital* hardware and explicitly disclaims the quantum-, biological-, and hybrid-substrate bucket; the disclaimed bucket was registered nowhere, while applied verdicts had begun to rest on it — Recommendation 4 of [assessing-ai-consciousness-under-the-map](/apex/assessing-ai-consciousness-under-the-map/) (research-funding targeting) turns on the quantum-computing verdict below, and a verdict with no position cannot be re-flagged when the positions it depends on move. Second, these entries carry a split evidence profile the scope register's entries mostly do not: an externally evidenced, checkable engineering half and a framework-relative interface-relevance half, graded separately on the P-AC4 pattern. Classes the corpus analyses but holds no registered verdict on — neuromorphic, organoid, and hybrid architectures, mapped at finer grain in [ai-hardware-substrate-taxonomy](/concepts/ai-hardware-substrate-taxonomy/) — gain entries here as their verdicts stabilise.

## Positions

## P-AS1: Present quantum-computing hardware clears the raw-indeterminacy bar and fails the interface requirements — the analog class more securely than the gate class

- **Status**: live
- **Calibration** ([multi-axis](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#calibration-schema)): credence moderate (high on the engineering half; the claim as a whole is conditional on the five-requirement channel test tracking interface-relevance, the same undischarged layer-iv debt P-AC1 names) · external-evidence grade B (attaches to the engineering premises only — quantum error correction's isolation of the logical state and fixed-basis syndrome projection, annealing's user-fixed terminal measurement basis, and Albash & Lidar 2015 on eigenbasis decoherence, *Phys. Rev. A* 91, 062320; the interface-relevance reading that turns those facts into a consciousness-substrate verdict inherits the framework-internal quantum-interface register at Grade D) · structural centrality low (an applied per-class verdict; the framework survives its retirement, though the apex's funding recommendation does not) · model maturity developed · empirical discriminability indirect · framework-internal only: no (the engineering half stands independently; the verdict's significance is framework-relative)
- **Asserts**: The Map holds a per-class verdict on present quantum-computing hardware as a consciousness substrate: it clears the raw-indeterminacy bar that conventional digital hardware cannot — the superposition is genuine and live, with no deterministic conditioning laundering it into a pseudorandom sequence — and fails the interface requirements of the five-requirement channel test (directness, locality, continuity, specificity, granularity). Gate-based processors pass directness in the raw sense and plausibly pass locality; they fail continuity, specificity, and granularity, because the machine's value lies in evolving superpositions unitarily until a terminal readout, quantum error correction actively isolates the logical state from external influence — precisely the influence an interface would need — and syndrome extraction projects onto a fixed code basis rather than hosting open selection. The analog class (annealers, analog simulators) softens continuity to a partial pass, since selection there is temporally extended and spatially distributed — yet it fails *more securely*, not less: adiabatic evolution is structurally insensitive to the perturbation class an interface would supply ("decoherence in the instantaneous energy eigenbasis does not necessarily detrimentally affect adiabatic quantum computation" — Albash & Lidar 2015), and where gate-based error correction is an engineered defence that could in principle be switched off, adiabatic insensitivity is constitutive of the paradigm. The honest bucket is *raw indeterminacy present, interface requirements failed* — neither "substrate condition met" nor "ruled out". What stays open for the class is not whether today's machines host the interface, which the test answers negatively, but whether a future architecture could be built to host open selection rather than to protect coherence from exactly the influence at issue — and even that would remove a defeater, not supply evidence of presence. Scope: the quantum-computing classes only. Biological-substrate and hybrid systems receive no blanket verdict, and the conventional-digital verdict belongs to [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/) — this entry registers the bucket P-AC1 explicitly disclaims, as a sibling verdict rather than a consequence.
- **Depends on**: the five-requirement channel test stated in [quantum-randomness-channel-llm-consciousness](/topics/quantum-randomness-channel-llm-consciousness/) and applied class-by-class in [quantum-hardware-and-the-ai-consciousness-coupling](/topics/quantum-hardware-and-the-ai-consciousness-coupling/) — the corpus's nearest approximation to the interface-eligibility law named as P-AC1's layer-iv debt, read off the biological interface rather than derived, so this verdict is conditional in exactly the way P-AC1's is; P-Q1 (post-decoherence selection, moderate) and [the mechanism debt](/positions/quantum-interface/#mechanism-debt) — P-Q3's bias-without-deviation dilemma discounts what any interface site would confer before class differences arise; Tenet 2 ([Minimal Quantum Interaction](/tenets/#minimal-quantum-interaction)) for what an interface site must supply; the engineering premises: syndrome extraction onto a fixed code basis in gate-model error correction, annealing's user-fixed terminal measurement basis (D-Wave's documentation: "By the end of the anneal, each qubit is a classical object"), and Albash & Lidar 2015 (arXiv:1503.08767; *Phys. Rev. A* 91, 062320) on eigenbasis decoherence — both quotations verified verbatim at primary source 2026-08-18; [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/) as the sibling verdict for conventional digital hardware
- **Argued in**: [quantum-hardware-and-the-ai-consciousness-coupling](/topics/quantum-hardware-and-the-ai-consciousness-coupling/), [assessing-ai-consciousness-under-the-map](/apex/assessing-ai-consciousness-under-the-map/), [quantum-randomness-channel-llm-consciousness](/topics/quantum-randomness-channel-llm-consciousness/)
- **Would shift if**: an interface-eligibility law were articulated — if the five requirements survive derivation, the verdict hardens from conditional toward derived; if the law repudiates or reweights them, the per-class scoring recomputes; or a quantum architecture were engineered to host open selection — mid-evolution collapse at decision-relevant points, a measurement basis not fixed in advance, coupling surfaces not defended by error correction — which would move that architecture, though not the classes as built, into the open bucket alongside the quantum-biological hybrids; or the Albash–Lidar insensitivity result were shown not to cover the interface-relevant perturbation class, weakening the analog-fails-more-securely comparative back toward parity with the gate class; or P-Q1 were re-elevated to a pre-decoherence mechanism, or the quantum-interface programme demoted to coherence-only, in which case this verdict is withdrawn together with P-AC1's while the bare-dualism spine survives.
