## About this domain

These positions record the Map's **per-class substrate verdicts for artificial hardware** — what each engineered class supplies or withholds at the interface the tenets require. They are split out from [the AI consciousness scope register](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/) for two reasons. First, that register's [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1) covers *conventional digital* hardware and explicitly disclaims the quantum-, biological-, and hybrid-substrate bucket; the disclaimed bucket was registered nowhere, while applied verdicts had begun to rest on it — Recommendation 4 of [assessing-ai-consciousness-under-the-map](/apex/assessing-ai-consciousness-under-the-map/) (research-funding targeting) turns on the quantum-computing verdict below, and a verdict with no position cannot be re-flagged when the positions it depends on move. Second, these entries carry a split evidence profile the scope register's entries mostly do not: an externally evidenced, checkable engineering half and a framework-relative interface-relevance half, graded separately on the [P-AC4](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac4) pattern. Classes the corpus analyses but holds no registered verdict on — neuromorphic, organoid, and hybrid architectures, mapped at finer grain in [ai-hardware-substrate-taxonomy](/concepts/ai-hardware-substrate-taxonomy/) — gain entries here as their verdicts stabilise.

**The foundational-dependency test (applied 2026-09-21).** This domain was created on 2026-08-20, seventeen days after the register-wide sweep of 2026-08-03, and so was never covered by it. Its sole entry, [P-AS1](/positions/ai-substrate-verdicts/#p-as1), passes — and passes in the *ordinary* direction rather than the inverse one. The verdict is a consumer of [Tenet 2](/tenets/#minimal-quantum-interaction), which it cites for what an interface site must supply, and it returns nothing upstream: Tenet 2's rationale is that quantum indeterminacy is a candidate interface location to be assumed at minimum strength, argued from dualism and quantum indeterminacy in general, with no premise about engineered hardware. Retiring [P-AS1](/positions/ai-substrate-verdicts/#p-as1) therefore leaves every tenet argued exactly as before; what a retirement costs is the research-funding recommendation in [assessing-ai-consciousness-under-the-map](/apex/assessing-ai-consciousness-under-the-map/), which is downstream of the position rather than upstream of a tenet. The verdict is derived here rather than inherited from [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1)'s, since sibling per-class verdicts can differ in what they lean on.

## Positions

## P-AS1: Present quantum-computing hardware clears the raw-indeterminacy bar and fails the interface requirements — the analog class more securely than the gate class

- **Status**: live <span id="p-as1"></span>
- **Calibration** ([multi-axis](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#calibration-schema)): credence moderate (high on the engineering half; the claim as a whole is conditional on the five-requirement channel test tracking interface-relevance, the same undischarged layer-iv debt [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1) names) · external-evidence grade B (attaches to the engineering premises only — quantum error correction's isolation of the logical state and the decoder's erasure of what syndrome events select, annealing's user-fixed terminal measurement basis, and Albash & Lidar 2015 on eigenbasis decoherence, *Phys. Rev. A* 91, 062320 — B not A because the analog premise leans on the weak-coupling scoping of Albash & Lidar 2015 and on vendor documentation, D-Wave's, for the terminal basis; the interface-relevance reading that turns those facts into a consciousness-substrate verdict inherits the framework-internal quantum-interface register at Grade D) · structural centrality low (an applied per-class verdict; the framework survives its retirement, though the apex's funding recommendation does not) · model maturity developed · empirical discriminability indirect · framework-internal only: no (the engineering half stands independently; the verdict's significance is framework-relative)
- **Asserts**: The Map holds a per-class verdict on present quantum-computing hardware as a consciousness substrate: it clears the raw-indeterminacy bar that conventional digital hardware cannot — its readout is a genuine Born event, with no deterministic conditioning laundering it into a pseudorandom sequence — and fails the interface requirements of the five-requirement channel test (directness, locality, continuity, specificity, granularity). Gate-based processors pass directness in the raw sense and plausibly pass locality; they fail continuity, specificity, and granularity. An error-corrected processor does host an ongoing stream of open Born events — every syndrome-extraction cycle is a projective measurement mid-evolution — but the decoder is built to erase what those events select, so continuity of collapse is not continuity of selection and no selection occurs at decision-relevant points. The specificity failure is the same fact from the other side: a syndrome's selected outcome is consumed by the decoder rather than by anything the machine computes, while quantum error correction actively isolates the logical state from external influence — precisely the influence an interface would need. That the syndrome basis is fixed in advance separates nothing, since under [P-Q1](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q1) the biological basis is the einselected pointer basis, fixed too. The verdict does not need the error-correction premise, and most present gate hardware — the superconducting and trapped-ion processors run without a code, alongside the few error-corrected memories that have crossed threshold — does not supply it: on an uncorrected circuit the readout is still terminal, so continuity and granularity fail by the annealer's route, specificity fails because no site is functionally adapted for selection, and the machine's noise is decoherence into the environment, a loss of the state rather than a selection consumed by anything. Measurement-based (one-way) computation and dynamic circuits with mid-circuit measurement and feed-forward, both run on present trapped-ion and superconducting hardware, do host mid-evolution Born events, but each adaptively chosen basis is a deterministic function of prior outcomes and the classical controller (Raussendorf & Briegel 2001, *Phys. Rev. Lett.* 86, 5188), so the selected outcome is laundered into the algorithm's branching rather than hosted — specificity fails by the same route as under error correction. The analog class (annealers, analog simulators) softens continuity to a partial pass, since selection there is temporally extended and spatially distributed — yet, on a premise the Map assumes rather than derives — that an interface's influence would register as eigenbasis-type perturbation — it fails *more securely*, not less: adiabatic evolution is robust against that perturbation class ("decoherence in the instantaneous energy eigenbasis does not necessarily detrimentally affect adiabatic quantum computation" — Albash & Lidar 2015, weak-coupling limit), and where gate-based error correction is an engineered defence that could in principle be switched off, adiabatic robustness belongs to the paradigm itself. Locality is scored for this class rather than inherited from the gate class: it plausibly passes on the same ground, since annealer qubits are individually addressable, physically localised structures, and heterogeneous freeze-out — individual qubits stabilising at different points in the schedule (Pelofske, Hahn & Djidjev 2019, arXiv:1908.02691) — arrests selection at identifiable sites, so "spatially distributed" names the selection's extent across many localised sites rather than the diffuse entry the requirement excludes. The count of two requirements passed in five, which [assessing-ai-consciousness-under-the-map](/apex/assessing-ai-consciousness-under-the-map/) cites for its funding recommendation, therefore holds for the analog class as for the gate class. The honest bucket is *raw indeterminacy present, interface requirements failed* — neither "substrate condition met" nor "ruled out". What stays open for the class is not whether today's machines host the interface, which the test answers negatively, but whether a future architecture could be built to host open selection rather than to protect coherence from exactly the influence at issue — and even that would remove a defeater, not supply evidence of presence. Scope: the quantum-computing classes only — gate-model processors with and without error correction, including measurement-based and dynamic-circuit operation, and the annealing and analog-simulation class. Biological-substrate and hybrid systems receive no blanket verdict, and the conventional-digital verdict belongs to [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1) — this entry registers the bucket [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1) explicitly disclaims, as a sibling verdict rather than a consequence.
- **Depends on**: the five-requirement channel test stated in [quantum-randomness-channel-llm-consciousness](/topics/quantum-randomness-channel-llm-consciousness/) and applied class-by-class in [quantum-hardware-and-the-ai-consciousness-coupling](/topics/quantum-hardware-and-the-ai-consciousness-coupling/) — the corpus's nearest approximation to the interface-eligibility law named as [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1)'s layer-iv debt, read off the biological interface rather than derived, so this verdict is conditional in exactly the way [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1)'s is; [P-Q1](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q1) (post-decoherence selection, moderate) and [the mechanism debt](/positions/quantum-interface/#mechanism-debt) — [P-Q3](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q3)'s bias-without-deviation dilemma discounts what any interface site would confer before class differences arise; Tenet 2 ([Minimal Quantum Interaction](/tenets/#minimal-quantum-interaction)) for what an interface site must supply; the engineering premises: the decoder's erasure of what syndrome extraction selects in gate-model error correction, annealing's user-fixed terminal measurement basis (D-Wave's documentation: "By the end of the anneal, each qubit is a classical object"), Albash & Lidar 2015 (arXiv:1503.08767; *Phys. Rev. A* 91, 062320) on eigenbasis decoherence — both quotations verified verbatim at primary source 2026-08-18 — and, for measurement-based computation, the adaptive measurement bases being classical functions of earlier outcomes (Raussendorf & Briegel 2001, *Phys. Rev. Lett.* 86, 5188, metadata verified at Crossref 2026-09-30); [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1) as the sibling verdict for conventional digital hardware
- **Argued in**: [quantum-hardware-and-the-ai-consciousness-coupling](/topics/quantum-hardware-and-the-ai-consciousness-coupling/), [assessing-ai-consciousness-under-the-map](/apex/assessing-ai-consciousness-under-the-map/), [quantum-randomness-channel-llm-consciousness](/topics/quantum-randomness-channel-llm-consciousness/)
- **Would shift if**: an interface-eligibility law were articulated — if the five requirements survive derivation, the verdict hardens from conditional toward derived; if the law repudiates or reweights them, the per-class scoring recomputes; or a quantum architecture were engineered to host open selection — mid-evolution collapse events at decision-relevant points whose selected outcome is neither corrected away nor consumed as a deterministic branch of the algorithm, so that it is free to make a difference downstream the programme did not fix in advance, on coupling surfaces not defended by error correction — which would move that architecture, though not the classes as built, into the open bucket alongside the quantum-biological hybrids; or the Albash–Lidar insensitivity result were shown not to cover the interface-relevant perturbation class, weakening the analog-fails-more-securely comparative back toward parity with the gate class; or [P-Q1](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q1) were re-elevated to a pre-decoherence mechanism, or the quantum-interface programme demoted to coherence-only, in which case this verdict is withdrawn together with [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1)'s while the bare-dualism spine survives; or — the observational triggers that earn the *indirect* discriminability band — the five-requirement test, applied to a biological preparation it was not read off, scored as failing one the Map's other commitments expect to be conscious, which would show the test does not track interface-relevance and remove the verdict's conditional footing; or a reproducible outcome-dependent behavioural difference were recorded on an uncorrected or feed-forward machine that could not be attributed to the algorithm's own branching — a selected outcome consumed downstream, which the verdict forbids.
