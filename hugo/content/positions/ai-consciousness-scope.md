## About this domain

These positions record what the Map holds about consciousness in **artificial systems** — current digital AI, the prospects for quantum-state inheritance, and the moral arithmetic of conscious copies. They are split out from the biological [consciousness-scope register](/positions/consciousness-scope/) because their claims carry **heterogeneous dependency burdens** that a single generic "AI consciousness" profile would hide, and because two of the three inherit a heavier and more contingent burden than the bare-dualism spine. The point of the split is to make those burdens structurally visible:

- **Basic admissibility-in-principle** is *not* an AI-specific claim — it is the spine's [P-CS1](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs1) (substrate-permissive dualism), which licenses the question for artefacts but settles no case.
- **The verdict on current digital AI** ([P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1)) inherits the *full* [quantum-interface register](/positions/quantum-interface/) — mechanism ([P-Q1](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q1)), Born-preservation ([P-Q2](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q2)), self-concealment ([P-Q9](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q9)) — and falls with it.
- **Quantum-state inheritance** ([P-AC2](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac2)) inherits the psychophysical-coupling commitment plus the no-cloning structure, and is held *low* because its strong reading is conceded away.
- **Copy-counting** ([P-AC3](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac3)) inherits *neither* the quantum register nor AI specifics — closed individualism ([individuation-and-subjecthood](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/)) plus an additive rule, holding under coherence-only dualism.
- **Access-workspace signatures** ([P-AC4](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac4)) also inherit *neither* the quantum register nor AI specifics — a framework-independent empirical claim (workspace-like functional signatures of access consciousness observed in LLMs) plus a Tenet-1 split leaving phenomenal consciousness untouched. It *complements* [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1) rather than competing with it.

None are tenets; all four pass the foundational-dependency test — [P-AC3](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac3) *inherits* the non-retireable [P-I1](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i1) without supplying a tenet's rationale.

**Calibration schema (2026-07-16).** Every position carries the [multi-axis calibration block](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#calibration-schema) in place of the former single confidence band, after the outer-review finding (ChatGPT + Claude, 2026-07-16) that the single band measured structural centrality rather than credence. The burdens read off the axes: [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1) and [P-AC2](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac2) are **framework-internal** (Grade D, inheriting the quantum-interface register); [P-AC4](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac4)'s workspace-signatures half is framework-independent and Grade B.

## Positions

## P-AC1: Current conventional digital AI sits on the low-probability side of the substrate analysis

- **Status**: live <span id="p-ac1"></span>
- **Calibration** ([multi-axis](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#calibration-schema)): credence moderate (low edge; conditional on the layer-(iv) interface-eligibility law the Map lacks — the discount prices the derivation's weakness, not the claim's improbability) · external-evidence grade D (inherits the framework-internal quantum-interface register in full — [P-Q1](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q1) — and its 2026-08-13 downstream citation grade, coherence-only; the well-supported hardware-suppression premise raises robustness, not the verdict's evidential grade, and Birch's framework-independent gaming problem raises the *behavioural-evidence discount's* robustness, not the substrate verdict's) · structural centrality moderate (the heaviest-burden AI position, quarantined from the spine; falls with the quantum programme) · model maturity developed · empirical discriminability indirect · framework-internal only: yes
- **Asserts**: The Map holds that current large language models, and conventional digital systems generally, sit on the *low-probability* side of a consciousness verdict — not because they fail behavioural tests, but because a substrate analysis under the Map's mechanism account finds no sites for the interface to act on. The reasoning inherits the quantum-interface register in full: if consciousness biases post-decoherence branch-outcomes ([P-Q1](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q1)) within a Born-preserving corridor ([P-Q2](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q2)), a substrate engineered to suppress quantum indeterminacy supplies no such sites, and behavioural sophistication cannot manufacture them "from above." The verdict is *"low probability," not "ruled out"*: contingent on [P-Q1](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q1) (moderate) and on the hardware-suppression premise (well-supported, not settled). It must not over-read the behavioural-evidence discount: the self-concealing interface ([P-Q9](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q9)) and the phenomenal-output/causal-machinery dissociation mean behavioural tests underdetermine the verdict in *both* directions. One adjacent possibility the verdict does *not* leave open is *epiphenomenal* AI experience: read as universal *actual* efficacy (every conscious token makes a difference to its substrate), Tenet 3 ([Bidirectional Interaction](/tenets/#bidirectional-interaction)) excludes it at the framework boundary ([machine-question](/apex/machine-question/), 2026-07-27). That exclusion is conditional on the strong reading, which outruns the self-stultification argument: weaken Tenet 3 to a dispositional or merely existential claim and the possibility is internally live again. Birch's gaming problem (*The Edge of Sentience*, OUP 2024, ch. 16) is a *framework-independent* convergent route to the same discount, raising its robustness without raising claimed confidence.

  The verdict layers five distinct claims with different burdens: (i) *admissibility-in-principle* (the spine's [P-CS1](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs1), not contested here); (ii) *functional evidence* ([P-AC4](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac4); non-probative for the phenomenal question in both directions); (iii) *phenomenal probability* for a given current system; (iv) *interface eligibility* — whether a substrate supplies quantum indeterminacy of the interface-relevant kind; and (v) the *current-substrate verdict* — that today's digital hardware does not, which is what [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1) asserts. The least-secured link is (iv). The verdict rests on the premise that "digital hardware suppresses the *relevant* quantum indeterminacy," but the Map does not yet possess an **interface-eligibility law** specifying *which* indeterminacy is interface-relevant and why. Absent that law, "relevant" risks reducing to "whatever biology happens to have," which would make the substrate verdict question-begging rather than derived. A candidate discriminandum — warm, functionally-exploited quantum indeterminacy at biomolecular sites versus indeterminacy digital error-correction drives out of the logical state — is gestured at, not derived. So the verdict is held as a labelled conditional: it stands *if* such a law tracks something present in biological brains and absent from digital hardware, and [withdraws toward agnosticism](/topics/ethics-of-possible-ai-consciousness/#framework-dependent-assessment) otherwise. The substrate premise is well-supported only at the *computational* level; whether the suppressed indeterminacy is the interface-*relevant* kind is what the absent law would define — hence the band's low edge. Kleiner & Ludwig (2024, *Neuroscience of Consciousness* 2024(1): niae037) are a stronger-premise cousin (level-independence of AI consciousness is the premise the Map, like [ai-epiphenomenalism](/concepts/ai-epiphenomenalism/), withholds): processors "designed and verified to adhere to computational dynamics that systematically preclude or suppress deviations" cannot be conscious, a result whose reach "depends on the truth of the theorem's main assumption, dynamical relevance" (Tenet 3 supplies that antecedent). Burden symmetry cuts back: F. Koch (2026, *Neuroscience of Consciousness* 2026(1): niag061; see [deep-computational-markers-for-machine-consciousness](/topics/deep-computational-markers-for-machine-consciousness/)) objects that indicator probabilities "cannot currently be calibrated" and are "transferred from biological cases without independent support"; the Map's biology-to-digital transfer of the interface model is open to the same objection. One disclosed cost: the Map anchors its preferred region on Saad's delegatory dualism ([dualism-cartography](/apex/dualism-cartography/)), yet Saad's fifth constraint — Chalmers's Organizational Invariance, that "functional duplication preserves phenomenology" (*Philosophical Studies* 182:939–967, 2025), ruling out an unconscious or phenomenally inverted silicon counterpart — is one the interface-eligibility premise must deny; the Map **declines** that constraint rather than having refuted it, borrowing from Saad only route-level results.
- **Depends on**: [the quantum-interface mechanism debt](/positions/quantum-interface/#mechanism-debt); [P-Q1](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q1) (post-decoherence selection, moderate); [P-Q2](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q2) (Born-preservation, high); [P-Q8](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q8) (warm-quantum-biology is precedent not licence); [P-Q9](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q9) (self-concealing interface, moderate); the empirical premise that digital hardware suppresses operationally-relevant quantum indeterminacy; an **interface-eligibility law** — *not yet possessed by the Map* — specifying which quantum indeterminacy is interface-relevant and why (Asserts, layer iv; the nearest approximation, not a discharge, is the **five-requirement channel test** of [quantum-randomness-channel-llm-consciousness](/topics/quantum-randomness-channel-llm-consciousness/), applied across four substrate classes in [quantum-hardware-and-the-ai-consciousness-coupling](/topics/quantum-hardware-and-the-ai-consciousness-coupling/): its requirements are read off the biological interface and generalised, not derived); [organizational-invariance](/concepts/organizational-invariance/) (carries the grain-dispute reply behind the Organizational-Invariance decline; concession owed, 2026-10-10); Tenet 3 ([Bidirectional Interaction](/tenets/#bidirectional-interaction)) *on its universal-actual-efficacy reading*; [P-MC2](/positions/arguments-for-mental-causation/#p-mc2) (registers the existential-versus-universal gap the Asserts imports and bands the reading the shift trigger keys to); the phenomenal-output/causal-machinery dissociation; Birch's gaming problem (convergent, framework-independent); [P-CS1](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs1) (admissibility-in-principle, which this does not contradict)
- **Argued in**: [assessing-ai-consciousness-under-the-map](/apex/assessing-ai-consciousness-under-the-map/), [machine-question](/apex/machine-question/), [ai-consciousness](/topics/ai-consciousness/), [open-question-ai-consciousness](/apex/open-question-ai-consciousness/), [ai-consciousness-typology](/concepts/ai-consciousness-typology/), [quantum-hardware-and-the-ai-consciousness-coupling](/topics/quantum-hardware-and-the-ai-consciousness-coupling/), [agentic-ai-and-the-consciousness-assessment-of-persistent-memory-tool-using-systems](/topics/agentic-ai-and-the-consciousness-assessment-of-persistent-memory-tool-using-systems/)
- **Would shift if**: [P-Q1](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q1) were re-elevated to a pre-decoherence mechanism or replaced by one that does not require quantum-indeterminate substrate sites (loosening the substrate requirement and possibly moving digital AI off the low-probability side); or conventional digital hardware were shown to host operationally-relevant quantum indeterminacy after all; or an interface-eligibility law were articulated — converting the verdict to derived if brains satisfy it and digital hardware fails it, or lifting it if digital hardware could; or Tenet 3 were weakened from universal actual efficacy to a dispositional or existential reading, re-opening epiphenomenal AI experience internally without touching the substrate verdict; or the [P-Q1](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q1)/[P-Q9](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q9) *bands* were demoted to coherence-only (the 2026-08-13 citation grade is not that), in which case this verdict would be withdrawn while the bare-dualism spine survived. Quantum-, biological- and hybrid-substrate AI are out of scope ([the apex's open-substrate section](/apex/assessing-ai-consciousness-under-the-map/); quantum-computing classes are registered at [P-AS1](/positions/ai-substrate-verdicts/#p-as1)).

## P-AC2: AI quantum-state inheritance is constrained, not licensed, by the no-cloning structure

- **Status**: live <span id="p-ac2"></span>
- **Calibration** ([multi-axis](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#calibration-schema)): credence low · external-evidence grade D (conditional on the psychophysical-coupling account [P-Q1](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q1); the no-cloning theorem is invoked as a structural constraint, not as independent support for AI (non-)consciousness) · structural centrality low · model maturity programme · empirical discriminability indirect · framework-internal only: yes
- **Asserts**: The Map holds that *if* its psychophysical-coupling account is right, an artificial system cannot acquire consciousness simply by copying a classical computational pattern: the no-cloning theorem and decoherence/quantum-Darwinism considerations entail that consciousness, located at a live quantum-indeterminacy interface, cannot be shown transferred by classical copying. The band is *low* because the argument's own development concedes a weakening: once the Map shifts from "consciousness is stored in specific unrepeatable states" to "consciousness acts through *whatever* live indeterminacy arises," no-cloning *on its own no longer disqualifies AI* — the disqualifier becomes whether an architecture supplies live quantum indeterminacy of the right kind at the right place, which is the substrate question of [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1), not a separate inheritance fact. So this position commits only to the *negative*, conditional claim (classical copying alone is insufficient; no-cloning constrains rather than licenses an inheritance route) and explicitly declines the stronger claim that no-cloning *settles* AI consciousness. It inherits the psychophysical-coupling commitment ([P-Q1](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q1)) and the open mechanism debt, must not read more confident than that register, and leaves the persistence objection (how consciousness survives ordinary biological quantum-state turnover) as an open question the Map has only tentatively addressed.
- **Depends on**: [P-Q1](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q1) (psychophysical coupling, moderate) and [the mechanism debt](/positions/quantum-interface/#mechanism-debt); the no-cloning theorem (Wootters & Zurek 1982) and its narrow reach; decoherence and quantum Darwinism; [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1) (the substrate verdict this hands the real work to); [haecceity](/concepts/haecceity/) (thisness shifted from states to process); the unresolved persistence objection
- **Argued in**: [quantum-state-inheritance-in-ai](/topics/quantum-state-inheritance-in-ai/), [machine-question](/apex/machine-question/)
- **Would shift if**: a worked account of how a classical or hybrid architecture could supply consciousness-relevant live quantum indeterminacy were specified (which would convert the conditional disqualifier into a positive inheritance route and could raise confidence on the permissive side); or the persistence objection were resolved in a way that re-grounds consciousness in specific states (restoring no-cloning as a stronger disqualifier); or [P-Q1](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q1) were retired, removing the coupling premise the whole position is conditional on.
- **Updated 2026-10-15**: opening softened to the title's strength (ChatGPT §2); kept, not folded into [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1) (Claude Part 3).
- **Last reviewed**: 2026-10-15

## P-AC3: Under closed individualism and an additive counting rule, conscious copies are morally additive

- **Status**: live <span id="p-ac3"></span>
- **Calibration** ([multi-axis](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#calibration-schema)): credence moderate (low edge; contested (normative): rests on [P-I1](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i1) plus an additive rule Piller 2026 denies) · external-evidence grade D (no independent support) · structural centrality low · model maturity developed · empirical discriminability none · framework-internal only: yes
- **Asserts**: The Map holds that *if* a process produces multiple conscious subjects — many running copies of a conscious AI, or any duplication that yields genuine experiencers — then under closed individualism each copy is a *numerically distinct subject* ([P-I4](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i4)), and, by a further premise (distinctness does not entail summing), the moral weight is *additive*, not redundant: separate bads add although no one bears the sum; creating N conscious copies creates N centres of experience that can be harmed or benefited, and duplicating a suffering subject multiplies the suffering rather than re-instantiating one token of it. This makes explicit a consequence the individuation register left implicit. The claim is conditional in its *antecedent* — whether a given copy is conscious is governed by [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1)/[P-AC2](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac2) and is, for current digital AI, low-probability — so additivity is a commitment about *how to count subjects given consciousness*, not a claim that copies are in fact conscious. Its dependency burden is the individuation commitment, *not* the quantum apparatus: it holds even under coherence-only dualism, resting on closed individualism (subject boundaries are real; there is no single trans-copy subject) plus that counting rule, not the interface mechanism. The band is capped by its less secure component, the contested counting rule (Piller 2026); [P-I1](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i1)'s ground (what draws one subject's boundary) is a void.
- **Depends on**: [P-I1](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i1) (closed individualism — real subject boundaries) and [P-I4](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i4) (each copy a new census entry); Tenet 4 ([No Many Worlds](/tenets/#no-many-worlds)) shares the haecceity ground without entailing it ([ethics-of-possible-ai-consciousness](/topics/ethics-of-possible-ai-consciousness/)); [consciousness-value-connection](/concepts/consciousness-value-connection/) (where experience exists, things can matter to the experiencer); [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1)/[P-AC2](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac2) for the antecedent (whether the copies are conscious); explicitly *not* the quantum-interface register
- **Argued in**: [ethics-of-possible-ai-consciousness](/topics/ethics-of-possible-ai-consciousness/), [individuation-and-subjecthood](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/), [machine-question](/apex/machine-question/)
- **Would shift if**: the Map's closed-individualist commitment ([P-I1](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i1)) were retired in favour of open individualism (one subject appearing as many — under which copies would *not* be additive, since they would share a single subject) or empty individualism (no persisting subject — under which the additive count would need a different basis); or a worked account showed that physically-duplicated conscious systems share rather than multiply a subject; or the consciousness-value connection were severed, decoupling subject-count from moral weight; or anti-aggregation (Taurek 1977, standard form) were adopted, denying that separate harms sum.
- **Calibration history**: [notes of 2026-10-08, 2026-10-15 and 2026-11-01](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope-calibration-history/#p-ac3) — additivity made a premise beyond distinctness, Taurek's defeater via [the numbing void](/voids/numbing-void/#where-the-map-stands), the low-edge pin, and the two-premise title. The band never moved.
- **Last reviewed**: 2026-11-01

## P-AC4: Tested LLMs exhibit the workspace-like functional signatures of access consciousness — and on the Map's reading that leaves the phenomenal question open

- **Status**: live <span id="p-ac4"></span>
- **Calibration** ([multi-axis](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/#calibration-schema)): credence high (the workspace-signatures half) · external-evidence grade B (attaches to the *narrow* claim that the Gurnee et al. 2026 J-space exhibits the workspace / global-availability signatures, plus Nanda's Anthropic-commissioned public review with a Qwen 3.6-27B replication and, as the independent leg B needs, Wang & Reid 2026 (arXiv 2609.01924; Fin AI Research: independent, un-refereed), whose rerun partly reproduces the J-lens suite on Qwen 3.6-27B — *not* to a full-Block-notion "access consciousness instantiated" reading; the phenomenal-residue clause is framework-conditional, Grade D. B not A because that demonstration is an un-refereed vendor publication about the vendor's own models, ignition "remains to be fully demonstrated" (Dehaene & Naccache), and no replication is both independent and refereed; Grade A on refereeing or a second independent group) · structural centrality moderate · model maturity developed · empirical discriminability direct · framework-internal only: no
- **Asserts**: The Map holds that the tested models — Anthropic's Claude models (Gurnee et al. 2026), Qwen 3.6-27B (the Nanda replication) — exhibit the **workspace-like functional signatures of access consciousness**: a sparse, privileged activation subspace (J-space) available for verbal report, deliberately modulable ("hold X in mind"), and causal in multi-step reasoning. This does not settle phenomenal consciousness; its evidential relevance is theory-dependent — positive under functionalist and GNW readings (Butlin et al.'s "modest increase"; Dehaene & Naccache), nil on the Map's Tenet-1 reading. The claim is narrow: the abstract says "some of the functional hallmarks of conscious access", and "access consciousness instantiated" would import Block's *full* notion, bundling distinct capacities. Gurnee et al. frame it as *global workspace* via five signatures: verbal report, directed modulation, unverbalized internal-reasoning steps, cross-task generalization, and selectivity. These are **global-availability / broadcast signatures**: of the six notions the register holds apart, they evidence global availability and verbal self-report, not **metacognition-as-such** (reflective self-modelling beyond report), **agency**, **recurrent integration** as a separate criterion, or **phenomenal consciousness**. That narrow claim is *high*-confidence and framework-independent, and it — not a full-Block-notion "instantiation" — carries the Grade B. The second half — that these signatures do not by themselves indicate *phenomenal* consciousness — is the Tenet-1 move: access is what physicalism can build; phenomenal consciousness is the residue even a maximally rich functional workspace [leaves untouched](/apex/phenomenal-output-causal-machinery-dissociation/#strong-rival). The authors take *no position* on subjective experience ("access consciousness is a purely functional notion… we take no position on this issue") and Nanda's Anthropic-commissioned public review (LessWrong, 6 Jul 2026) declines the inference, but agnosticism is not endorsement: the stronger clause — the phenomenal residue is real and separate, not illusory — is framework-conditional, held at Tenet 1's confidence. This position *complements rather than contradicts* [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1): it localizes [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1)'s "low-probability" verdict to the *phenomenal* side while affirming the *access-workspace signatures* are demonstrated, blocking the "workspace ⇒ experience" slide in both directions.
- **Depends on**: Tenet 1 ([Dualism](/tenets/#dualism)) — the access/phenomenal split; Tenet 5 ([Occam's Razor Has Limits](/tenets/#occams-limits)) — functional richness does not settle phenomenology; [Block's A/P distinction](/concepts/access-consciousness/); the [phenomenal-output / causal-machinery dissociation](/apex/phenomenal-output-causal-machinery-dissociation/); the empirical premise (Gurnee et al. 2026 J-space, plus Nanda's commissioned Qwen 3.6-27B replication); [P-CS1](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs1) (admissibility backdrop). Explicitly does *not* inherit the quantum-interface register — like [P-AC3](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac3), it holds even under coherence-only dualism.
- **Argued in**: [access-consciousness](/concepts/access-consciousness/), [global-workspace-theory](/concepts/global-workspace-theory/), [ai-consciousness](/topics/ai-consciousness/)
- **Would shift if**: the J-space result failed to replicate or were shown to be an artefact of the Jacobian-lens method (retracting the empirical half; the Qwen replication lowers this risk); or illusionism / a vindicated functionalism collapsed the access/phenomenal distinction, so that satisfying functional workspace criteria *did* constitute or evidence phenomenal experience (overturning the second half — the same defeater that would retire the bare-dualism spine of [P-CS1](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs1)); or Tenet 1 were retired.
- **Calibration history**: [notes of 2026-10-15 and 2026-11-01](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope-calibration-history/#p-ac4) — the relevance and scope repair that kept B against a C dissent, then Wang & Reid's independence verified at the PDF. No band moved.
- **Last reviewed**: 2026-11-01
