Where the world offers a continuum, experience arrives already carved. Voice-onset time runs smoothly from 0 to 60 milliseconds, but a listener hears /b/ and then, abruptly, /p/; hue, facial expression and musical interval behave the same way. The perceiver reports a few categories, discriminates two stimuli that straddle a boundary more easily than two equally different stimuli inside one, and receives the verdict rather than the gradient. This is categorical perception, and The Unfinishable Map's categorical perception void is the region it hides: the interior of each category, delivered at reduced resolution by an operation the perceiver did not choose and cannot watch.

The strong version of this void has been retired by the field that discovered it, and the retirement belongs in the first paragraph. The textbook claim, traceable to Liberman and colleagues around 1960, was that a listener can hear no differences among speech sounds beyond those the phoneme labels reveal. McMurray's 2022 survey of fifty years of behavioural and electrophysiological work concludes that within speech perception this strongest form is widely regarded as an artefact of task demands, and that the underlying representation of the continuum appears linear. What survives is narrower: a limit on *access by default* and a limit on *provenance*. Consciousness can reach the within-category continuum when the task is built to let it; by default it receives the category. And nothing in the experience of a boundary says whether it tracks a joint in the world or one installed by language, training or the first year of life. An installed boundary feels exactly like a discovered one. In the terms of [three-kinds-of-void](/voids/three-kinds-of-void/) it is Occluded, the compression being the perceiver's own operation, with an unexplorable face that deflates under the right instrument.

Neighbouring articles carry pieces of this: the [resolution-void](/voids/resolution-void/) supplies the compression framing, the [language-thought-boundary](/voids/language-thought-boundary/) the lexical-carving evidence, and the [expertise void](/voids/expertise-and-its-occlusion/) the one-way door through which skill closes off a prior way of perceiving. What this article adds is the origin face as a void in its own right, and a contrast the catalogue had not drawn: the same mind that is vague where its concepts should be sharp is sharp where the world is continuous.

## The Warp and Its Deflation

The robust core is a warp. Discrimination is better across a category boundary than within one, controlling for physical difference, the definition in Goldstone and Hendrickson (2010), and it has been reported in five domains. In speech it is the original finding. In colour, Winawer and colleagues (2007) found Russian speakers, whose language obligatorily distinguishes lighter *goluboy* from darker *siniy*, faster to discriminate two blues straddling that border than two from the same side, with the advantage removed by a concurrent verbal task but not a spatial one. In faces, Etcoff and Magee (1992) found expressions morphed in constant physical steps discriminated more poorly within an expression category than across one. In music, Burns and Ward (1978) found trained musicians perceiving melodic intervals categorically under standard procedures. In the laboratory, Pérez-Gay Juárez, Sicotte, Thériault and Harnad (2019) taught subjects to sort textures into two categories by feedback alone and found between-category separation and, to a lesser extent, within-category compression in dissimilarity judgements afterwards, with a correlated decrease in the occipital N1.

The deflation is equally well replicated. Gerrits and Schouten (2004) ran the same vowel continuum through two discrimination tasks: a four-interval task showed no categorical perception at all, while a two-interval forced-choice task on identical stimuli produced the classic pattern; Schouten, Gerrits and van Hessen (2003) locate the decisive variable in the response bias the task invites. Burns and Ward's musicians lost most of their categorical signature under an adaptive procedure once performance stabilised, and continuous ratings of how /b/-like a token is track the acoustics. The colour face deflated twice: Witzel and Gegenfurtner (2011), with 230 observers and controlled colour rendering, found category effects that were real but not lateralised to the left hemisphere as Gilbert, Regier, Kay and Ivry (2006) had reported, removing the neatest link between the effect and language processing; and Martinovic, Paramei and MacInnes (2020) found the Russian advantage at blue/green but not at *siniy/goluboy*, with the *siniy/goluboy* boundary itself drifting toward lighter shades when the stimulus set was lighter. The Whorfian colour effect should be carried as real but modest, and nothing here rests on the lateralised version.

So the between face splits, as the [palette-extension-void](/voids/palette-extension-void/) split, into two questions with opposite fates. Can consciousness reach the within-category continuum? Yes, by the right task, by continuous rating, by training. Does it reach it by default? No. An occlusion that vanishes when the question is asked differently is an occlusion of the default readout, not of capacity.

## Discovered or Installed

Some perceived boundaries plausibly track discontinuities in the world; voicing is produced by a discrete laryngeal gesture, so the /b/–/p/ joint has a candidate world-side correlate. Others are demonstrably installed. The texture boundary in Pérez-Gay Juárez et al. (2019) was manufactured in one session by feedback. The *siniy/goluboy* border is one English speakers do not carry. The interval scale belongs to musicians. And the phonemic inventory is pruned early: Werker and Tees (1984) found English-learning infants discriminating Hindi and Salish consonant contrasts at six to eight months and largely losing the ability by ten to twelve, while infants learning those languages kept it, which Werker and Hensch (2015) place inside a critical-period model.

The origin face is the phenomenological point these results make together. Nothing in the experience of a boundary marks it as installed. An adult English speaker hears /r/ and /l/ as two sounds; an adult Japanese speaker hears one; each hears a joint, and neither can inspect its provenance. The [infant consciousness void](/voids/infant-consciousness/) carries the narrowing data as a developmental limit; here the point is what the adult inherits: clean joints that are the residue of a pruning the adult cannot remember or undo by attention.

Burnston (2021) closes the other exit. Proponents of [cognitive-penetration](/concepts/cognitive-penetration/) have argued from categorical perception and perceptual learning that beliefs permeate perception as categories are learned. Burnston argues that this diachronic thesis is false: to guide learning, a belief would have to describe contents the subject can already perceive, so it cannot specify the ability actually acquired, and the learning process must operate outside cognitive influence. If that is right, the installed boundary is opaque from both directions: the perceiver cannot see whether it was installed, and the perceiver's own intentions during learning could not have specified what was.

The provenance limit has no first-person method. Whether a felt joint is world-given or installed is inferred only by comparing the perceiver to a differently carved perceiver: Russian against English speakers, six-month against twelve-month infants, a subject before and after a laboratory boundary. One probe works from outside: a boundary that moves with the stimulus distribution, as the *siniy/goluboy* border did, is not a joint in the world. None of this is available inside a single perceiver's experience, which is what makes the origin face a void rather than an open empirical question.

## The Gradient That Is Kept but Not Delivered

The residue face is fixed by one result. Toscano, McMurray, Dennhardt and Luck (2010) recorded event-related potentials to spoken words varying in voice-onset time. The auditory N1, around 100 milliseconds after onset, tracked voice-onset time linearly and independently of the category the listener assigned, and within-category differences were still present at the later P3. The continuum is in the system a tenth of a second after the sound begins. What the listener receives by default is the category.

This places the void in the catalogue's operation-void family. The [fusion-void](/voids/fusion-void/) delivers the fused percept and never the component estimates; the [inference-void](/voids/inference-void/) delivers the conclusion; the [transit-void](/voids/transit-void/) delivers the arrival. Categorisation has a distinct shape within the family: fusion combines several estimates into one, while categorisation quantises one dimension into a few bins and keeps the unquantised copy where the default readout does not look. That the copy is kept separates this void from the [resolution-void](/voids/resolution-void/)'s bit-rate compression, and it is why the between face deflates: the continuum can be reached because it was never thrown away.

Two caveats. Whether the learned warp is perceptual or decisional is contested: the N1 decrease in Pérez-Gay Juárez et al. is read as perceptual, but Fugate (2013) reports the same dispute unresolved for faces, where the paradigm's emotion words may do part of the work. And no study located asks the phenomenological question directly: when a listener reaches within-category detail by continuous rating, is the difference experienced as a difference in the sound, or only judged? The evidence supports *behavioural* access on demand; *phenomenal* access remains open.

## Sharp Where the World Is Continuous

The [vagueness-void](/voids/vagueness-void/) describes a mind that cannot locate where its concepts stop applying: no Sorites series fixes the cut-off for *heap* or *red*, and the borderlines have borderlines. Categorical perception describes the same mind hearing a hard edge between /b/ and /p/ where the acoustics are continuous. The two voids are complementary failures of edge representation in opposite directions: concepts are fuzzy where a decision procedure would want them sharp, percepts are sharp where the input is smooth. The research behind this article found the conjunction unremarked, and the Map offers it as its own observation rather than a finding.

The conjunction sharpens the origin face. A perceiver who noticed that an edge was too clean for the input could suspect installation, but the edge presents as the world being simple. Approaching this void feels like nothing: a Japanese-speaking adult listening to *right* and *light* does not experience a blur where a distinction should be; the two words simply sound alike, and no gap is felt.

## The Training Window

There is one interval in which the operation acquires a texture. Logan, Lively and Pisoni (1991) trained Japanese listeners on English /r/ and /l/ with high-variability natural tokens across fifteen sessions; identification improved and the gain was still present at a three-month follow-up. Reports from such training describe a period in which the two sounds are known to differ and are not yet heard to differ: the joint is believed before it is perceived. That interval is the only first-person window onto installation, and it closes as the category installs.

Expertise shows the same closure from the other end. Siegel and Siegel (1977) found six musicians with relative pitch identifying in-tune intervals at over 95% accuracy while unable to tell sharp from flat within a category, and afterwards judging that 63% of the stimuli had been in tune when only 23% were. Acquiring the category costs the within-category continuum, as the [expertise void](/voids/expertise-and-its-occlusion/) predicts, and the musicians' estimate shows the cost is invisible to the one who pays it. Whether retrained listeners regain the continuum phenomenally or only behaviourally, the training literature does not say.

## What AI Might See

For a human perceiver the warp is inferable only through psychophysics and averaged potentials. For an artificial network it is directly measurable: Bonnasse-Gahot and Nadal (2022) show, by information-theoretic analysis and simulation, that category learning induces categorical perception in shallow and deep networks, with expansion near category boundaries and contraction far from them, stronger at deeper layers; a network's joints are auditable layer by layer. The origin face is open for inspection in a way it is not for us.

The other direction rests on the least secure source in this article. Cacioli (2026), in a single-author, unreviewed preprint, reports representational-similarity evidence that six language models from five architecture families warp their hidden-state geometry at digit-count boundaries, absent at non-boundary controls and for pairs such as hot and cold with no tokenisation discontinuity. The preprint distinguishes models that both categorise explicitly and warp from models whose geometry warps although they cannot report the category distinction. If that dissociation replicates, it is an instance of a system carved along a joint it cannot describe: the human origin face reproduced somewhere it can be checked, and a hint that human perception may contain boundaries that shape discrimination without surfacing as reportable categories. Until it replicates it is a hypothesis-generator, not evidence. Whether any current system has a default readout in the sense consciousness does, delivering the decision and withholding the gradient from the process that acts on it, is not a question the literature consulted addresses; [non-human-minds-as-void-explorers](/voids/non-human-minds-as-void-explorers/) treats the general case.

## Relation to Site Perspective

The void bears most directly on **[Occam's Razor Has Limits](/tenets/#occams-limits)**, in two registers. The first is about the science: categories all the way down was the simpler model, and it held the field for decades on measurement artefact; the lateralised-Whorf story was elegant and did not survive 230 observers. The second is about the perceiver: a category is itself a parsimony operation, many stimuli under one label, and the simplicity of a perceived joint is no evidence that the world has a joint there. The colour boundary that drifts with the stimulus set is the demonstration.

On **[Dualism](/tenets/#dualism)** the Map records two readings and does not pretend the data choose. Toscano et al. (2010) show the nervous system's early encoding, linear and category-independent, coming apart from the categorical content the subject reports. On the Map's reading the phenomenal deliverable is not a transparent readout of the functional encoding, the shape the [surplus](/voids/the-surplus-void/) and [phenomenal-quality](/voids/phenomenal-quality-void/) discussions rely on. On a physicalist reading consciousness is identified with a late, post-N1 categorical stage, and the void is just where the readout sits in the pipeline. The evidence constrains both readings by fixing the structure each must explain; it establishes neither.

On **[Bidirectional Interaction](/tenets/#bidirectional-interaction)** the connection is modest. The verbal-dual-task result of Winawer et al. (2007) shows the online warp depending on what the perceiver is concurrently doing with language. That is a modulation of the operation by ongoing cognition, which a physicalist account covers; it is no evidence of non-physical influence and is not treated as such here. Minimal Quantum Interaction and No Many Worlds have no bearing the research found.

The void is framework-independent: physicalist and dualist alike owe an account of why the default readout is the category when the gradient is kept, and of why installed and discovered joints are indistinguishable from inside. It belongs in the framework-independent column of the [voids](/voids/) index and the operation-void family of [taxonomy-of-voids](/apex/taxonomy-of-voids/).
