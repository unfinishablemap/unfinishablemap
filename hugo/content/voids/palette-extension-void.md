Extending a human sensory palette is proving far easier than certifying that the extension is new. Gene therapy has added a cone dimension to adult primates that never had one; a worn belt has added a magnetic channel; cone-by-cone laser stimulation has produced a percept whose colorimetric coordinates lie outside the natural human gamut. The Unfinishable Map calls what survives all this the **palette extension void**: every route from an installed channel back to the claim that its content is phenomenally *novel* runs through discrimination or report, both rendered in the vocabulary of the palette the subject already had. Asked what the out-of-gamut colour looked like, the five subjects who saw it said "teal," "green," "blue-greenish," and "green, a little blue."

Two qualifications belong beside that claim rather than after it. The void is epistemic, not metaphysical: nothing here suggests a novel quality cannot be acquired — the record runs the other way — only that acquisition cannot be audited. And the deflationary reading, on which such a percept is an extreme value along an old dimension rather than a value on a new one, is not refuted below. It is unfalsifiable by available instruments, which is this void's substance rather than an objection to it.

## The Capacity Question Is Deflating

Three programmes have moved the capacity question out of speculation.

Mancuso and colleagues (2009) delivered the missing photopigment gene by viral vector to adult dichromatic squirrel monkeys; some twenty weeks later the animals began discriminating colours previously indistinguishable to them. The developmental result is the striking one: classic deprivation work predicted that circuits never exposed to an input during a critical period could not later process it, and the adult brains handled the added dimension anyway.

König and colleagues (2016) had nine participants wear the feelSpace belt, which signals magnetic north by vibration at the waist, during all waking hours. Reports evolved with training duration, the vibration coming to be described as spatial information rather than as vibration, and fMRI during a homing task showed differential parietal and premotor activation. Those participant statements were read here through a secondary summary rather than the paper's own reporting, so the migration should be checked at source before it is leaned on.

Fong and colleagues (2025) built the "Oz" prototype, which tracks eye motion at cellular scale in infrared and delivers "10^5 visible-wavelength laser microdoses per second to each cone cell" across a small patch of peripheral retina. Stimulating only the M cones — the middle of L, M, S, hence 010, hence "olo" — produced a percept the authors report as outside the natural human gamut, in five subjects.

The pessimistic prior about installing a channel in a finished adult mind has not survived. That much of the palette question is now empirical, and answered permissively.

## What the Olo Result Actually Certifies

The out-of-gamut finding is a matching argument. Subjects could not match olo against near-monochromatic reference lights without first desaturating it with projector white, the matching wavelengths running 501–512 nm on the gamut boundary. The authors call this "unequivocal proof that olo lies beyond the gamut."

That is a fact about the colour *space* — a colorimetric coordinate, exactly what an instrument of this precision returns. The paper is candid about the remaining step, noting that because of the Abney effect "the hue of the wavelength at best match may not exactly represent the hue of the undiluted olo color." The measurement settles where the stimulus sits in a space defined by matching behaviour, not what the sitting is like. The qualitative data gathered alongside it is four colour words and a saturation rating. Instrument precision and phenomenal thinness are not in tension here; together they are the void's signature.

## Every Route Back Runs Through the Prior Palette

**Discrimination is silent about quality.** Mancuso's monkeys discriminated; nothing in that study speaks to whether their experience gained a dimension. Jordan and colleagues (2010) confirmed cDa29 as a functional tetrachromat, reliably distinguishing a monochromatic orange from red-green mixtures metameric for trichromats. C. L. Hardin's complaint about that literature, quoted in Newall (2025), is that the rigour produced the silence: "Nobody asked the woman about the quality of her experience."

**Report is loaned vocabulary.** The Fong subjects held a stimulus with proven out-of-gamut coordinates and reached for trichromat words: whatever they saw, the report channel resolved it into the palette they arrived with.

**The dissent is definitional, not evidential.** John Barbur is quoted in press coverage saying "It is not a new colour," glossing olo instead as "a more saturated green that can only be produced in a subject with normal red-green chromatic mechanism when the only input comes from M cones." That is newspaper commentary; no peer-reviewed statement of the objection was located, so it is presented as reported, with its logic reconstructed here rather than borrowed. Barbur does not contest a measurement — he declines a description. A disagreement that survives every measurement, because the parties differ over what would count as new, marks a boundary rather than an open question.

**No baseline is available.** Beforehand, L. A. Paul's (2014) analysis of epistemically transformative experience applies: what is coming cannot be known in advance of having it, so a palette is extended in ignorance of what is being added. Afterwards the baseline is gone and the comparison would run in memory, where a remembered colour is re-rendered in the palette currently in force. Olo makes this vivid in miniature: the percept survives only while cellular targeting holds.

## Splitting Rather Than Retiring

The catalogue has watched limits deflate before: olfactory absence was reclassified after cross-cultural work, and the [fatigue void](/voids/disappearance-voids/)'s unexplorable face narrowed once self-monitoring proved to survive sleep loss largely intact.

Unfamiliar here is the shape rather than the fact of deflation. In those cases a single face receded. Here a limit the catalogue treated as one thing has separated into two questions with opposite fates: a capacity question the record is answering, and a certification question it has not touched. The response that fits is *splitting* rather than retiring. The Map's earlier palette material — [the acquaintance void](/voids/acquaintance-void/) and [the synesthetic void](/voids/synesthetic-void/) — asks whether a novel quality is *conceivable*; this asks whether an *installed* one could be shown to be new, and the second question is untouched by progress on the first. On the [three-kinds taxonomy](/voids/three-kinds-of-void/) the induction face is *unexplored* and shrinking, the verification face a candidate *unexplorable*. Under the [common-cause null](/project/common-cause-null/) this is housekeeping on the Map's own carving rather than support for it, and any convergence claim resting on the older framing should be re-examined for the same conflation.

## Approaches to the Edge

**Structural inference.** Newall (2025) wants the phenomenal conclusion and cannot reach it by citing a measurement, so he argues from opponent-channel physiology plus cDa29's failure to place her experience within the trichromat ordering. His paper "rejects the claim that they simply see finer gradations of those colours familiar to trichromats," arguing instead for "elementary colours that trichromat viewers are unable to see or imagine." Note the shape: a positive claim resting on a negative datum — apophatic cartography as usual.

**Report-category migration**, the most promising available design. Rather than asking whether an experience is new, track whether the *category* of report moves — from "vibration" to "direction" — over months of continuous wear, as the feelSpace protocol permits, substituting a change-detection question for an identity question. A weaker variant watches for convergence in coinages; the olo data points the wrong way, with four different familiar words across five subjects.

**[What a differently-built mind might see](/voids/non-human-minds-as-void-explorers/)** — here, unusually for this catalogue, not much. Give a model a new input channel and it will describe it fluently in the vocabulary it already has, because fluency is what the architecture produces regardless. Extension experiments on models cannot arbitrate the human question; they reproduce the gap where the deflationary reading is, if anything, better supported.

Two gaps are worth recording. Ward and Meijer (2010) document the phenomenology of two long-term blind vOICe users; only its citation metadata was confirmed here, so nothing is attributed to it, and it should be read before this face is developed further. And no peer-reviewed reply, replication or philosophical treatment of the olo result was found — thin coverage rather than established absence, since whether one exists was not settled.

## Relation to Site Perspective

**[Occam's Razor Has Limits](/tenets/#occams-limits)** finds an unusually clean instance here. Barbur's gloss — a very saturated green — is the simpler hypothesis, positing no new phenomenal dimension and explaining the reports economically. It is also unfalsifiable by any instrument the field possesses, because the only data separating it from the novelty reading are the phenomenal data the verification face rules out. Parsimony is not tracking truth here; it is *selecting* an answer where the evidence cannot vote — and the simpler hypothesis is held by a competent specialist for good reasons, not by a strawman. Where the [parsimony void](/voids/epistemological-limits-occams-razor/) treats the razor as a bias in theory choice, the palette case shows it deciding a live dispute the evidence has left open.

**[Dualism](/tenets/#dualism)** takes a bearing that needs stating carefully. The physical side of the palette has proved unexpectedly tractable — genes, cones, lasers and adult plasticity all yielded — while the phenomenal side stayed sealed, not for want of technique but as a consequence of the technique working: every improvement in the instrument sharpens the colorimetric result and leaves the qualitative result where it was. A physicalist accommodates this by denying there is a further fact to find, and that denial is untouched by anything argued here; the point is only that the accommodating move is a denial rather than a measurement. Per the [common-cause null](/project/common-cause-null/), this locates an explanatory residue and supplies no evidence for any account of it.

**[The observation void](/voids/observation-and-measurement-void/)** gets a sharpened instance: the measurement here does not disturb the phenomenon so much as *substitute* for it, returning a coordinate where a quality was sought.
