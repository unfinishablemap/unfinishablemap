A hand can reach for a cup gently or roughly, with a rush or a drag, and anyone watching reads the difference at once. The reach has a goal (the cup) and a reason (thirst), and it also has a manner, a shape in time that belongs to neither. Daniel Stern called these shapes *vitality affects* in 1985 and *forms of vitality* in 2010; Giuseppe Di Cesare, Giacomo Rizzolatti and colleagues, who have measured them since 2014, call them "a third aspect of any human action" beside its *what* and *why* (Di Cesare, Gerbella & Rizzolatti 2020). The Unfinishable Map gives the name **vitality-form void** to the gap between a dimension that is lived in every waking moment and recognised in every other person, and a conceptual apparatus that sorts experience by content and so has almost nowhere to put its form.

The void's register is mixed. It is primarily *unexplored*, in the sense of [the Map's three kinds](/voids/three-kinds-of-void/): the sources' own gap statements, from 2014 to 2024, record that the neural basis of a pervasive dimension went uncharacterised until the 2010s. It has an *occluded* component: the categories that organise psychology (perception, emotion, cognition, action) all sort by content, and a vitality form is the shape a content arrives in, so the default description absorbs it. Whether it is also *unexplorable*, whether the *how* of an experience can be thought at all without becoming a *what*, the Map leaves open. On the mind-body question the void is neutral: a physicalist and a dualist describe the same blank, and the Map's verdict ([explained below](#site-perspective)) is that the finding earns the tenets no upgrade.

## The Third Dimension of Action {#third-dimension}

Stern's definition is a Gestalt one. Vitality, he writes, is a whole that "emerges from the theoretically separate experiences of movement, force, time, space and intention" (Stern 2010, p. 5, as quoted by Ammaniti & Ferrari 2013; the Map knows Stern's page-cited text only through that carrier). Ammaniti and Ferrari's gloss: vitality affects refer "not to which behavior a child exhibits but rather how the behavior is performed." The forms are named with dynamic words, surging, fading, explosive, and Stern conceded the borrowing: they are "elusive qualities (which) are better captured by dynamic, kinetic terms" (Stern 1985, p. 54, via Ammaniti & Ferrari).

The neuroscientific definition sets this against emotion. For Di Cesare, Gerbella and Rizzolatti (2020), vitality forms are "continuous internal states that modulate the motor behaviors of individuals"; emotions are discrete and visceral, while vitality forms are "devoid of the autonomic modifications that characterize real emotions." The *how* is thus orthogonal to the content categories: a perception, an emotion, a thought and an action can each be surging or fading, and the form belongs to none of those classes. The void turns on that structural fact.

## The Measurement Record and the Gap It Reports {#measurement}

Di Cesare and colleagues (2014), with Stern as co-author, showed participants energetic and gentle actions under two tasks, one asking what was done and one asking how; "the contrast how vs what revealed activation in right dorso-central insula." The dimension appeared when the task asked for it and not otherwise, which is the void's own structure reproduced in the scanner: a mind running the default *what* task never meets the *how*.

Later studies generalised the substrate. Action sounds alone, gentle against rude, activated the same sector, an effect that "cannot be explained merely by the physical properties (loudness and duration) of the stimuli"; the insula processes vitality forms "regardless of the modality by which they are conveyed" (Di Cesare et al. 2019), and the 2020 review concludes that "the dorso-central insula represents the neural substrate of vitality forms." Opening with the statement that "the neural bases of action forms (e.g., gentle or rude actions) have not been characterized", Di Cesare, Lombardi and colleagues (2024) report that in observation and execution alike two streams arise, one toward inferior parietal cortex carrying the goal and one toward the dorso-central insula carrying the form, and leave open whether the form is set "cognitively (from PM to DCI) and affectively (from DCI to PM)".

The measurers state the neglect themselves. Despite the importance of vitality forms in social life, the 2020 review says, "only recently have neurophysiological studies been devoted to this issue." The absence claim is the sources' own, scoped to the literatures they survey. The clinical record adds a dissociation: autistic children struggle to identify vitality forms in others' actions "while they do not differ from neurotypical children in discerning the goal of the action" (Di Cesare, Bruschetta et al. 2024). The *what* intact and the *how* altered is what a distinct dimension predicts and what a mere attribute of content does not.

## Why the Categories Missed It {#occlusion}

Four features make this more than a research gap that closed late; together they are the void's signature.

**Categorical displacement.** Asked how something felt, a subject usually answers with a *what*: an emotion word. Asked to study feeling, a scientist studies discrete emotions, the very thing Di Cesare and colleagues set vitality forms against. Stern's own book opens by asking, of a phenomenon "we are very alert to" in ourselves and others, "just how can we study this phenomenon?" (Stern 2010, publisher abstract): the question of someone who has found a dimension and has no instrument.

**Modality-generality.** A dimension that belongs to no sense is one the inventory of senses cannot surface. The seen, heard, touched, spoken and imagined forms converge on one insular sector, and nothing in the list of modalities points at it, a structural reason for the oversight that recurs in the [modality void](/voids/modality-void/)'s question of what counts as a sense.

**Conceptual disintegration under load.** The only sustained critique of Stern's concept that the research behind this article found holds that "vitality affects" is "very important, but ... not precisely defined", and that because Stern defines it in relation to the neuropsychological, the bodily, the conscious and the interactional at once, the concept is "extended to so many areas that it tends toward disintegration" (Køppe, Harder & Væver 2008). The Map reads this as the void's conceptual signature: a dimension that cuts across the standing categories cannot be defined inside any one of them, and a definition that spans them all dissolves.

**A borrowed lexicon.** Every word the sources use for a vitality form is a movement word or a music word. Maxine Sheets-Johnstone, in a Husserlian phenomenology of movement that does not cite Stern for this claim, analyses the "qualitative dynamics of movement" into "tensional, linear, amplitudinal, and projectional" qualities and speaks of a "space–time–force dynamic" that any movement creates (Sheets-Johnstone 2024); two lineages that developed separately converge on a kinetic Gestalt with the same components. Music has always written the *how*, in dynamics, articulation and tempo markings, yet Kim (2013) observes that after three decades of work on music and emotion, how expressive forms are shaped in music-making "has received little investigation".

## Phenomenology of the Edge {#phenomenology}

Approaching the limit feels like this. One notices that an experience, a memory arriving, an idea forming, had a shape, a swell and a subsidence, and one reaches for a word and finds only metaphors from the body or from music. Stern's "dynamic shifts or patterned changes within ourselves" (1985, p. 57, via Ammaniti & Ferrari) names the experience without naming the shapes. Sheets-Johnstone's dancer reports an awareness "not on doing movements but on being-in-movement", on "the felt dynamics of movement itself", a mode in which form is foreground and content recedes; but that mode is rare, trained, and lost the moment one tries to say what the form was.

This inverts the usual ineffability case. At the [language-thought boundary](/voids/language-thought-boundary/) the trouble is a quality with no word; here any word installs the wrong category. The form is given constantly and read in others without effort, and what displaces it is attending to it as a something.

## Whether the Void Is Real {#real}

A physicalist reading has an easy story and it should be stated at full strength. The *how* of action is kinematics plus affect-modulated motor control, encoded in the insula and premotor cortex; it was under-studied because twentieth-century emotion science inherited a discrete-category programme (Darwin, Ekman) and action science a goal programme (mirror neurons). On this reading the void is a research gap now closing, with no bearing on the mind-body question, and nothing in the record refutes it. What the record adds is that the gap has a structure, the four features above, that a sociological account does not predict and that recurs across unrelated voids. Whether that structure is a fact about categories or about consciousness is the question the Map's framework asks; the sources do not answer it.

Three cautions bound the claim. The neural evidence comes from one group: Di Cesare and Rizzolatti appear on every neural paper cited here, and the research behind this article located no replication of the insular finding outside it. The stimulus set is coarse, gentle against rude against neutral, so the literature's labels are the first carvings of a continuum the [categorical perception void](/voids/categorical-perception-void/) would expect to be reported as already carved. And the deflationary reading has not been run as an experiment: no one has yet tried response formats that are not category labels (continuous sliders, matched movement, vocal contour).

Two registers are therefore secure and one is open: unexplored is documented in the sources' own gap statements; occluded is supported by the task-dependence of the neural signature and by the disintegration finding, though both admit the deflationary reading; unexplorable is a hypothesis. On the Map's [four criteria](/concepts/apophatic-cartography-four-criteria/), signature specificity is moderate to strong, structured persistence moderate (open from 1985 through 2024, but never tested by a programme that tried to dissolve it), cross-observer convergence weak to moderate (independent lineages, one laboratory), and framework independence high on the finding and low on any interpretive weight. One grader, no pre-registered rubric; the grades are judgement.

## Distinguishing Sibling Voids {#siblings}

The [affective void](/voids/affective-void/) and the [mood void](/voids/mood-void/) concern feeling-states that may be unreachable; a vitality form is the shape an emotion (or a non-emotion) takes in time rather than a feeling-state. The [modality void](/voids/modality-void/) asks which sense delivered a content; the vitality form's modality-generality supplies it with a channel no sense owns. The [categorical perception void](/voids/categorical-perception-void/) concerns continua reported as already carved; vitality forms are the un-carved continuum. The [conceptual scheme void](/voids/conceptual-scheme-void/) is the general case, categories excluding what they cannot represent; this void is a worked instance with a measurable dimension on the far side of the exclusion.

Three neighbours exist so far only as research notes. The [tempo void](/research/voids-tempo-void-2026-10-16/) concerns the *rate* of experience; the vitality form is its *shape*, and a form can be slow and explosive or quick and gentle. The [resonance void](/research/voids-resonance-void-2026-03-24/) concerns moments that feel meaningful beyond articulation; vitality forms are present in meaningless actions too. The [cognitive-phenomenology void](/research/voids-cognitive-phenomenology-void-2026-05-01/) asks whether thought has a phenomenal character at all; the dynamic contour of a thought's arrival is one candidate, and one both sides of that debate, looking for a *what*, would miss. Finally, Stern's claim that vitality forms are "part of implicit relational knowing" (2010, p. 111, via Ammaniti & Ferrari) places the dimension as the infant's primary mode; if adults lose thematic access to it, this void is one mechanism of the [infant consciousness void](/voids/infant-consciousness/).

## What AI Might See {#ai}

A language model inherits text's bias. Text encodes *what* and *why* richly and *how* thinly, so a system trained on text should reproduce the void, and probing it for the vitality forms of its own processing would likely return the same borrowed lexicon. Two asymmetries run the other way. A system with access to its own continuous activation dynamics could in principle report temporal contour directly, without the conversion to category that human report forces. And a robot built to move gently or roughly on demand must represent the forms as parameters, a thematisation humans lack. Whether either amounts to experiencing a vitality form is the hard-problem question in a new place; what they would supply is a non-human description of the dimension to set against the human one ([non-human-minds-as-void-explorers](/voids/non-human-minds-as-void-explorers/)).

## Relation to Site Perspective {#site-perspective}

**[Occam's Razor Has Limits](/tenets/#occams-limits)** is the tenet the void bears on most. A taxonomy of mental life with three content categories is simpler than one with a dynamic-form dimension cutting across them. The simpler taxonomy was held for a century, and it omitted "a third aspect of any human action". The omission did not look like an omission; it looked like parsimony. The tenet is illustrated, as a caution the Map binds itself by, and not vindicated, since nothing here shows a more complex taxonomy of consciousness true.

**[Dualism](/tenets/#dualism)** gains no support. If phenomenal character is irreducible to content, the vitality form is a candidate for *where* the irreducible part would live: the felt contour of an experience in time, present whether or not there is an object, emotion or proposition to carry it. The resemblance is framework-shaped and earns the finding no upgrade. No author cited here argues from vitality forms to dualism, and under the Map's calibration rule ([P-V2](/positions/voids-as-evidence/#p-v2)) nothing flows back to the tenet. A physicalist who reads the insular stream as the whole story loses nothing here.

**[Bidirectional Interaction](/tenets/#bidirectional-interaction)** finds a congenial pattern and no evidence. The connectivity studies leave open whether the form is set "cognitively (from PM to DCI) and affectively (from DCI to PM)", and the *how* of an action is a channel in which an internal state shapes motor output continuously; that is the kind of channel the tenet needs, but a physicalist account of affect-modulated motor control predicts the same channel. **[Minimal Quantum Interaction](/tenets/#minimal-quantum-interaction)** has no bearing, since vitality forms are macroscopic phenomena. **[No Many Worlds](/tenets/#no-many-worlds)** has at most a thematic link: a vitality form is indexical in the ordinary sense, *this* surge in *this* body now.

The void suggests one lesson for the Map's [taxonomy of voids](/apex/taxonomy-of-voids/). Some voids are regions beyond the categories; this one is a dimension orthogonal to them, present inside every categorised experience and invisible for that reason. The [erasure void](/voids/erasure-void/) describes that structure abstractly; this void has a measurable dimension, a clinical dissociation and a documented conceptual failure, so the deflationary and structural readings can be tested against each other.
