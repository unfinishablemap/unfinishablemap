This piece addresses a decision every serious reader of the Map eventually faces, and one its own editorial system faces daily from the inside: how to judge whether an interactionist-dualist research programme is being run as science. Which scientific norms does a framework with an irreducible posit still answer to? Which appraisal instruments fit it? What would count as its failure? And what can it never deliver, however long it runs? The Map's philosophy-of-science writing answers these questions one instrument at a time; this synthesis assembles the answers into a single verdict and applies it. The verdict: dualism does not exempt the Map from scientific norms. It changes *which* norms apply — programme appraisal rather than crucial experiments, functional rather than ontological demarcation, parsimony bounded to the domains where its track record holds, and a permanent limit on measurement — and it obliges the Map to run every one of those norms on itself. That obligation is registered in the Map's own positions on its conduct — [P-M1, P-M2, P-M4, and P-M5](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/), each held at high credence as a normative commitment — and this synthesis reads the philosophy-of-science cluster as the argument for why exactly those commitments, and not physicalism's preferred norms, are the right ones to be held to.

## What Survives and What Must Be Rebuilt

Start with what irreducibility actually costs the standard picture of science, because the cost is more specific than "dualism rejects science" and smaller than critics assume. As [the cluster's structural survey](/concepts/philosophy-of-science-under-dualism/) argues, four doctrines need revision, and each revision has a definite shape.

The **unity of science** loses its strong reductive form. If consciousness is irreducible, one link in the chain from psychology to physics is permanently missing, and science becomes a federation — physical sciences connected by reduction, psychophysical regularities connected by [bridge laws](/topics/psychophysical-laws-bridging-mind-and-matter/) rather than identities. This is a modification with independent philosophical support: Fodor argued for the disunity of the special sciences on multiple-realizability grounds decades before the Map existed, and dualism strengthens rather than invents that case.

**Demarcation** shifts from ontology to function. The criterion that would rule the Map unscientific by definition — methodological naturalism, "only physical explanations count as science" — is circular when the question at issue is whether consciousness is physical, an application of the circularity-exposure form of [reflexive methodology](/concepts/reflexive-methodology/). The replacement criterion is functional: systematic investigation, lawful regularity, empirical constraint, intersubjective testability, with no prior commitment about the domain's ontology. This is a standard the Map can be *held to*, not just a standard it hides behind: a programme that stopped generating empirical constraint would fail it.

**Parsimony** becomes domain-relative. Within physics, preferring fewer fundamental posits has an excellent track record; whether physics exhausts the real is not a question within physics, and applying the razor across that boundary assumes the answer. This is Tenet 5's home ground ([Occam's Razor Has Limits](/tenets/#occams-limits)), and it is a bounded claim — the Map does not get to wave parsimony away inside domains where it works.

**Scientific realism** extends rather than breaks: the physical sciences keep their posits, and the realist case for phenomenal properties — known by acquaintance rather than inference — is if anything stronger than the case for electrons.

What survives, then, is nearly everything that makes science science. What must be rebuilt is the appraisal machinery — how such a programme is judged — and that is where the rest of the cluster does its work.

## The Stage Diagnosis

The first appraisal question is what *kind* of scientific object the Map is judging and being judged as. [The Kuhnian analysis](/topics/consciousness-and-the-structure-of-scientific-revolutions/) argues that consciousness studies fits the crisis profile: a productive normal-science tradition (the neural-correlates programme) whose deepest anomalies — the explanatory gap, the hard problem's three-century persistence, the systematic malfunction of theoretical virtues — resist absorption; a proliferation of fundamental alternatives; loosening methodological standards; foundational self-examination. The article raises the strongest objection to its own diagnosis — that the field is *pre*-paradigmatic, in which case there is no incumbent to be in crisis — and answers it with a specific discriminator (pre-paradigm fields lack an agreed puzzle-solving tradition; the NCC programme is one), while conceding that Kuhn's own preface leaves the category assignment less than settled. And it sizes its conclusion correctly: the crisis reading is one the Map reads into the situation rather than one the data force.

What the diagnosis licenses is deliberately modest. If physicalism is a paradigm under strain rather than an established truth, dualism is a *candidate successor framework* responding to genuine anomalies — not a victor, and not yet even a mature rival, since it lacks Kuhn's exemplary solution. That is precisely the standing the Map claims for itself in [P-M4](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/), held at high credence: calibrate every claim to the stage of an early-stage research programme, never up to a categorical reading the body of evidence declines to support. A reader deciding how much authority to grant the Map should grant exactly that much: the authority of a programme whose open problems are honestly framed, not the authority of settled results. High structural centrality attaches to P-M4 for a reason the Kuhn material makes vivid — mistaking framework-internal coherence for theory-grade confirmation is the failure mode that turns a candidate successor into a sect.

## The Appraisal Instrument

If the stage is "open programme," the right instrument for judging it is Lakatosian appraisal over time, not verdict-by-single-experiment — and [the underdetermination analysis](/topics/duhem-quine-underdetermination-consciousness/) explains why nothing stronger is available to either side. Every consciousness theory meets data through a belt of auxiliary assumptions; the 2025 COGITATE adversarial collaboration, which strained both IIT and Global Neuronal Workspace predictions without eliminating either, is the worked example. When predictions fail, revising auxiliaries is rational so long as the revisions generate novel empirical content; a programme is displaced by a better programme, not refuted by a single result.

Three disciplines keep this instrument from degenerating into a universal excuse, and the Map registers all three against itself.

First, **underdetermination never upgrades**. The whole subject hands the Map a standing temptation: physicalism is underdetermined, so dualism is evidentially level. The cluster's strongest article refuses this in terms — "Underdetermination is symmetric and, by itself, licenses neither side" — and the refusal is the article-scale execution of [P-M1](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/): a tenet or a symmetry argument removes a defeater but never raises an evidential tier. Surviving a test is not passing it. What underdetermination buys the Map is non-elimination and a seat at the programme-comparison table; everything beyond that must be earned by prediction.

Second, **the auxiliary symmetry runs both ways**. The Map's interface model has a belt exactly as flexible as physicalism's — measurement assumptions, amplification assumptions, candidate neural sites, the minimality parameter — and Duhem-Quine licenses its flexing only under the progressive/degenerating test. The cluster's most consequential move is to name the Map's degeneration conditions *in advance*: repeated null results for quantum-sensitive signatures met only by moving-target site changes, first-person irreducibility claims that never yield new constraints, hard-problem appeals used to immunise every empirical failure. The [falsification roadmap](/topics/falsification-roadmap-for-the-interface-model/) holds these in standing form. A reader can therefore audit the Map against criteria the Map itself published before the results came in — the strongest accountability an open programme can offer.

Third, **values enter through burden, not as evidence**. Kuhn's point that theory choice under underdetermination runs on weighted values does not seat metaphysical commitments among the reasons. Following the inductive-risk literature, the defensible claim is narrower: prior commitments set the evidential threshold each side demands before surrendering its core. Dualists ask more of a proposed reduction than physicalists think it owes; physicalists ask more of a quantum-interaction signature than dualists think it owes. Neither asking is irrational, and neither is a datum.

## The Permanent Limit

Programme appraisal handles what the Map might yet demonstrate. A separate instrument in the cluster fixes what it can never have. [The measurement-standards argument](/topics/consciousness-and-the-problem-of-measurement-standards/) decomposes measurement into three requirements — a unit, an instrument, a calibration standard — and shows each failing for phenomenal quantities for a *different structural reason*: phenomenal quantities are features of a unified experiential field and lack the additive structure units require; consciousness has no publicly accessible causal effects of the right kind for an instrument to transduce; and there is no observer-independent phenomenal reference against which two subjects' reports could ever be calibrated. [The other-properties analysis](/topics/consciousness-and-the-problem-of-other-properties/) extends the third failure to its full depth — even granting that others are conscious, no physical description settles *which* phenomenal properties they instantiate — so the missing calibration standard is not awaiting a cleverer protocol. The practical consequence is the proxy discipline: pain scales, questionnaires, and neural decoders indicate without measuring — "The language of measurement is borrowed; the reality of measurement is absent."

For judging the Map, the limit cuts in two directions at once. Against consciousness science generally, it warns that numerical dress on phenomenal report is not quantification, and that a research programme — physicalist or dualist — that advertises phenomenal measurement is overclaiming. Against the Map specifically, it fixes an honesty condition: the framework's own psychophysical ambitions inherit the same ceiling, and no amount of programme progressiveness lifts it. The argument arrives at irreducibility from philosophy of science rather than metaphysics, which is why it earns a place in the appraisal toolkit rather than in the tenet register — but note the register separation doing its quiet work here: the *persistent failure* to construct standards is consistent with the dualist prediction, and consistency is all the Map claims for it.

## The Unreconciled Seam

The cluster contains one internal disagreement sharp enough to change how the Map's flagship programme should describe its own future, and this synthesis carries it rather than smoothing it. [The epistemology-of-mechanism analysis](/topics/epistemology-of-mechanism-at-the-consciousness-matter-interface/) lists four descriptions of what progress at the interface would look like; its third is phenomenal metrics: "The interface will not be specified until phenomenal states can be measured on a scale that admits mathematical relationships." The measurement-standards argument, summarised above, holds that no such scale is constructible — for structural reasons, not technological ones. If the measurement article is right as stated, one of the interface programme's four progress conditions is unreachable in principle. Neither article cites the other; the tension surfaces only when the cluster is read whole.

Three resolutions are available, and they are not equally costly. The first accepts the measurement argument at full strength and re-specifies interface progress without phenomenal metrics — leaning entirely on negative constraints (conservation, decoherence timescales, bandwidth), convergence patterns, and formal sufficiency proofs. This is coherent but real: it concedes that the coupling law the [mechanism debt](/positions/quantum-interface/#mechanism-debt) asks for might specify its mental side only ordinally or categorically, never metrically, which weakens what "specification" can mean. The second reads the mechanism article as needing less than the measurement article denies. The measurement argument's sharpest failures — additive units and *interpersonal* calibration — may leave intact a within-subject structure of ordinal and contrast relations: [the phenomenal contrast method](/concepts/phenomenal-contrast-method/) already extracts stark, replicable within-subject differences (understanding versus incomprehension, agency versus passivity) without any unit of experience, and a coupling law quantified over intra-subject contrasts would be mathematically non-trivial while never crossing the forbidden interpersonal bridge. On this reading the progress condition should be rewritten "scaled within subjects, never calibrated across them" — a live possibility the measurement article itself does not foreclose, since all three of its failures are stated for public, observer-independent standards. The third resolution — that phenomenal measurement proper will someday arrive — would falsify the measurement article outright, and the Map's own framework gives no reason to expect it.

The Map should say plainly which of these it is betting on, and currently it has not. The honest interim statement is the second, held as a hypothesis about method rather than a result: it is the only reading on which the interface programme's progress conditions are jointly satisfiable, and it converts the seam from a contradiction into a research constraint — any proposed phenomenal metric must declare whether it needs the interpersonal calibration that the measurement argument forbids. What the seam rules out either way is the comfortable version of the programme in which better instruments eventually dissolve the measurement problem. They do not, and a programme judged honest must stop implying that they might.

## The Reflexive Turn

The cluster's distinctive discipline — the one that separates it from apologetics — is that every instrument above is run on the Map itself, and the running is documented rather than gestured at.

The underdetermination analysis applies its own diagnosis inward five separate times: theory-ladenness constrains the Map's introspective evidence exactly as much as physicalist report-data; the dualist's ontological-gap reading is named as exactly as unforced an auxiliary as the physicalist's closability reading; the taxonomy running the contest as physicalism-versus-dualism is itself flagged as vulnerable to unconceived alternatives. [Reflexive methodology](/concepts/reflexive-methodology/) — the Map's named method of running a rival through its own criterion of success — audits its own four canonical instances and *downgrades one*: the pragmatism instance turns on a premise the naturalist pragmatist does not grant, so it is billed as a marked framework-boundary disagreement rather than a premise-neutral defeat. Its "datum is not yet the metaphysical claim" guardrail names the exact crossing — from "evaluation is a felt, conscious activity" to "evaluation cannot be physical" — where the Map's own commitments enter an argument, and [the induction analysis](/topics/consciousness-and-the-problem-of-induction/), which once made that crossing unmarked in its treatment of justified belief, now marks it and engages the reliabilist and naturalised-epistemology replies on their own terms. And the convergence temptation — reading the cluster's many mutually supporting arguments as accumulating independent weight — is discounted by standing rule: [P-M2](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/), held at high credence, treats any convergence as one observation read N times until a structurally distinguishing test rules out a shared upstream source.

One further position closes the loop. [P-M5](/positions/methodology-and-calibration/) records — again at high credence, and as a registered self-criticism — that documenting a discipline is not enforcing it: a calibration rule not wired into a review gate is a stated intention, not a working control, and the Map's own record includes disciplines violated within weeks of being declared. For a reader judging the Map, this is the position that converts the whole apparatus from self-description into something auditable. The question to ask of any Map claim is not "does a discipline exist that forbids the error?" but "did the discipline fire?"

## Synthesis

Assembled, the cluster answers the opening question with unexpected precision. What does science become when consciousness is irreducible? It keeps systematic investigation, lawful regularity, empirical constraint, and intersubjective testability — the functional demarcation criterion — and it keeps the deepest norm of all, appraisal by progressiveness over time. It gives up the reductive unity of science, ontological demarcation, cross-boundary parsimony, and the presumption that everything real is measurable. And it acquires one norm that ordinary science rarely needs: because the investigator's consciousness is inside the subject matter, every instrument must be run reflexively, with the crossings from shared datum to contested claim marked in public. The Map's methodology positions are that final norm in registered form; the philosophy-of-science cluster is the argument that the norm-set is principled rather than convenient. The two wings need each other: without the cluster, the positions are house rules; without the positions, the cluster is commentary. Together they make the Map the kind of object that can be judged — and specify the judging.

## Evidence and Dependency

The main lines of this synthesis carry different kinds of support, and their convergence should not be double-counted. The disunity, demarcation, and underdetermination material is independently argued and rests on external scholarship — Fodor, Duhem, Quine, Kuhn, Lakatos, Laudan and Leplin, the COGITATE result — none of which presupposes the tenets; what the Map adds there is application, not premise. The measurement-standards limit is independently argued from the structure of measurement, though its diagnosis (structural rather than technological) is the contested step a physicalist may refuse. The crisis diagnosis is externally anchored in Kuhn but framework-read: the Map selects the crisis interpretation the data do not force. The stage verdict, the convergence discount, and the enforcement caveat are inherited from the methodology register (P-M4, P-M2, P-M5), which is itself normative commitment rather than evidence. The reflexive obligation follows from Tenet 1 together with the observation that the investigator is inside the subject matter. The seam reading — within-subject scaling as the surviving progress condition — is this synthesis's own proposal and is mutually coherent only: no source article asserts it, and it carries no independent weight until stated as a testable methodological constraint.

## What This Implies for Decisions

**Judge the Map as a programme, and use its own published failure conditions.** The appraisal instrument the cluster licenses is Lakatosian: ask whether revisions generate novel empirical content, and check conduct against the degeneration conditions the Map named in advance — moving-target site changes after null results, irreducibility claims yielding no new constraints, hard-problem immunisation. This verdict turns on P-M4 (high credence, normative): were the open-programme framing shown to be a permanent accountability shield rather than a stage-honest description, the licence would need re-evaluation.

**Refuse evidential upgrades from underdetermination, in both directions.** When the Map or a sympathiser argues from "physicalism cannot rule dualism out," apply P-M1: defeater-removal never raises a tier. When a critic argues from "no experiment discriminates" to "the data are inert," apply the same article's converse: underdetermination coexists with graded evidential constraint, and COGITATE produced such gradients.

**Discount convergence until a distinguishing test is named.** The cluster's arguments — underdetermination, measurement limits, reflexive results — fit together impressively, and P-M2 (high credence) says exactly what that fit is worth: framework-internal coherence, one observation read many times, until a test that could separate the Map from its rivals passes. Ask any convergence claim for its distinguishing test before crediting it.

**Treat every phenomenal "measurement" as a proxy until units, instrument, and calibration are exhibited.** In clinical, research, or AI-assessment contexts, a number attached to experience is a self-assigned label unless all three measurement requirements are met — and for interpersonal phenomenal comparison the cluster's verdict is that they cannot be. Fund and design accordingly: within-subject contrast structures are the defensible instrument class; cross-subject phenomenal calibration claims are red flags whoever makes them.

**Require the interface programme to restate its metrics condition.** The seam identified above is decision-relevant now: the programme's progress conditions should be revised to declare whether "phenomenal metrics" means within-subject scaling (defensible) or interpersonally calibrated magnitudes (structurally unavailable). If the measurement-standards argument were itself overturned — a constructed phenomenal scale with genuine units — this synthesis's fourth implication would need re-evaluation and the [mechanism debt](/positions/quantum-interface/#mechanism-debt) would become easier to pay than the Map currently claims; if P-M2 were bounded by a validated distinguishing test for this cluster, the third implication would relax.

**Audit enforcement, not disclosure.** Per P-M5, ask of any Map discipline which gate fires it and what happens when it is skipped. A framework this self-documenting earns trust only where the documentation is wired to consequences.

## Relation to Site Perspective

**[Occam's Razor Has Limits](/tenets/#occams-limits)** is the spine of the whole revision: bounded parsimony is what opens the space in which a dualist programme can be judged on progressiveness rather than dismissed on simplicity, and the same tenet cuts inward, denying the Map any right to treat its own coherence as cheap evidence.

**[Dualism](/tenets/#dualism)** sets the problem — a federation of domains, a permanent measurement limit, a reflexive obligation — and receives from this cluster only what the register separation allows: the measurement and disunity arguments are consistent with irreducibility and arrive at it from philosophy of science, without upgrading it.

**[Minimal Quantum Interaction](/tenets/#minimal-quantum-interaction)** is what makes the Map's demarcation answer more than rhetoric: a specific empirical commitment, practically unfalsifiable today but falsifiable in principle, with its failure conditions published.

**[Bidirectional Interaction](/tenets/#bidirectional-interaction)** creates the standing obligation the appraisal instrument enforces — if consciousness causes physical effects, the effects are in principle detectable, and the programme cannot claim progressiveness indefinitely while its mechanism stays empirically silent.

**[No Many Worlds](/tenets/#no-many-worlds)** is a precondition rather than a conclusion here: without genuine outcome selection there is no interface question for the epistemology of mechanism to constrain.
