A recurring confusion runs through the dualism debate: the question "is dualism true?" gets asked as though "dualism" named a single position. It does not. It names a whole territory of positions that differ in how much ontological weight they place on mind, how much on matter, how they relate the two, and what each owes by way of mechanism. Treating the territory as a point makes the debate intractable—objections that defeat one inhabitant sail past another, and a defender and a critic can each be right about different regions while believing they disagree about the same thing.

A cluster of the Map's articles has been quietly building the alternative: not an argument that dualism wins, but a *map* of the territory. The map has a grid (the kinds of dualism, sorted by ontological thickness), a debt inventory, a worked region, and a marked frontier (the sharpest open problem the whole territory shares). The organising discipline is the same throughout: chart the positions and their costs *without* claiming to settle which position is correct. That restraint—cartographic rather than adjudicative—is the cluster's signature, and the reason the map's grid and debt inventory are usable by readers who reject the Map's tenets as well as those who accept them.

This piece draws the cluster's separate sheets into one atlas. It walks the [grid](/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/), reads the [debt inventory](/topics/mechanism-costs-dualism-thickness-quadrants/) cell by cell, makes the [parsimony reasoning](/topics/parsimony-case-for-interactionist-dualism/) that governs where the Map locates itself, fixes the Map's region against its neighbours, and ends at the [interface problem](/topics/the-interface-problem/) and the [formalisation void](/voids/interface-formalization-void/) as the frontier where the cartography hands off to open work.

## The Grid: Two Axes, Four Quadrants

Dualist positions are usually sorted by the *kind* of mind–matter relation they posit: substance versus property dualism; interactive versus epiphenomenal; Chalmers' Type-D, Type-E, Type-F. A second axis is rarely named—how much ontological weight each side carries—and naming it is the [four-quadrant taxonomy](/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/)'s first contribution. Crossing *mind-side thickness* with *physical-side thickness* gives four quadrants.

**Mind-side thickness** ranges from thin (only what is phenomenally introspectable—bare qualia, the felt redness of red) through medium (a unified subject that persists and can intervene minimally, but stays anchored to the introspective horizon) to thick (a subliminal self or cosmic consciousness whose waking ego is a narrow selection from a far larger mental reality).

**Physical-side thickness** ranges from thin (physics' own ontology taken as lean—what physics says and nothing more, often paired with closure *only* at the indeterminacy level) through medium (standard quantum mechanics plus decoherence plus uncontroversial structural facts) to thick (hidden variables, Russellian quiddities, Stoljar's ignorance-facts, a fuller phase space than measurement reaches). "Max-physical" expands the physical side *ontologically*, not spatially.

Crossing the extremes gives the four cells:

- **Q1 (min-mind / min-physical):** *"Only what we need, on both sides, and no more."* Bradford Saad's delegatory dualism, minimal interactionism confined to indeterminacy, neutral-monist limit cases.
- **Q2 (min-mind / max-physical):** *"A thin mental surface over a rich physical world."* Epiphenomenalism, textbook property dualism, Stoljar's epistemic physicalism, Bohmian physics with thin qualia.
- **Q3 (max-mind / min-physical):** *"A rich mental reality; physics is just how it looks from outside."* Berkeley, Kastrup's analytic idealism, Myers–James subliminal-mind views with lean physics—mostly monist limit cases that illuminate what an adjacent dualism would commit to.
- **Q4 (max-mind / max-physical):** *"Two rich realms, genuinely distinct, genuinely interacting."* Cartesian substance dualism, hylomorphism, Stapp's quantum interactive dualism, Goff's priority cosmopsychism.

The thickness axis is genuinely orthogonal to the kind-of-relation axis. Nida-Rümelin and Descartes both count as Type-D interactionists in Chalmers' scheme, yet they sit at different points on mind-thickness: Nida-Rümelin's unitary immaterial subject is mind-medium, Cartesian *res cogitans* is mind-thick. Saad's delegatory dualism is arguably closer to Type-D than Chalmers' own naturalistic dualism, yet mind-thin where Chalmers is mind-medium. Classification by relation type does not discriminate these cases; the thickness axis does. That is why it needs its own name.

One nuance the grid compresses: *channel width*—how narrow the interaction interface is—can come apart from pole thickness. Stapp's intervention channel looks minimal (quantum-Zeno biasing only) while his observer and his quantum ontology both look thick. A reader who wants to hold channel width separate can add it as a [third axis](/topics/dualism-channel-width-axis/), which locates Stapp by definition where the two-axis grid placed him by judgement. The atlas keeps it as an optional overlay rather than folding it in, trading a little nuance for tractability.

## The Debt Inventory: What Each Cell Owes

Locating positions is not yet saying what occupying a cell *costs*. Two positions that share a relation-kind classification can sit in different thickness cells and owe entirely different debts. The [mechanism-costs reading](/topics/mechanism-costs-dualism-thickness-quadrants/) supplies the inventory by exposing every cell to one fixed battery of three questions—the same three at each cell, so that cell-specific commitments are forced into visibility:

1. **Causation account.** How does mind contribute to producing physical effects—overdeterminingly, preemptively, selectively, by biasing, or not at all?
2. **Interface specification.** What is the mechanism of mind–physical contact, where does it happen, and how narrow is the channel?
3. **Conservation engagement.** How does the position relate to conservation laws—does it owe a violation, a workaround, or nothing?

**What the inventory delivers.** The answers are recorded per cell as an itemisation, not a measurement: no unit, no scale, no zero point, no rule for aggregating three answers into one figure. The inventory therefore licenses saying *which* items a cell is charged and *whether* each is argued or stipulated, and nothing about how heavy any item is; comparative verdicts of the "cheaper than" kind, which earlier versions of this atlas issued, are withdrawn for want of a scale. Warrant is uneven too. Q1's authority debt is derived (from a sufficient physical cause conjoined with a genuine mental contribution, routed through Schaffer's trumping preemption) and Q2's exclusion debt is derived (from Kim), while Q3's transparency debt and Q4's three-way charge are read off the cells' own commitments rather than argued independently.

The trio of questions leans deliberately toward physical-side debts: the conservation and interface questions originate in the physicalist's challenge to interactionism, and the literature has weighted them heavily. Left there, the battery would score rivals only, so two further items—entered after the cells below—charge the cell the Map itself occupies.

**Q1 owes the authority debt—by two routes, not one.** With no hidden variables on the physical side and no thick subliminal layers on the mental side, any genuine mental contribution must work as something other than an extra force on a complete physical story. Q1 holds two ways of meeting that requirement, parting company on whether the physical cause is *sufficient*.

*The delegatory route* keeps the physical cause fully sufficient and reassigns authorship. Saad's framework deploys [trumping-preemption](/concepts/trumping-preemption/)—Schaffer's species of redundant causation in which a higher-order rule selects one of two sufficient processes as authoritative—to give experience preemptive priority over a physical state's *default causal profile* without outpacing physics. The route is therefore difference-making-free and preserves strong causal closure, and its price of admission is an *authority law*: a non-stipulative account of what makes the mental rule authoritative, rather than the bare stipulation (Merlin's spell, military rank) that settles Schaffer's fictional cases. *The difference-making route* requires the opposite—that the physical cause be *insufficient*. At a genuine quantum indeterminacy the dynamics fix the probability distribution but leave the token open, and conscious selection settles which permitted outcome actualises; nothing is preempted, because there was no sufficient cause to preempt. It gives up strong closure at the indeterminacy point, as any genuine interactionism must, and owes a specification of where the indeterminacy sites sit and how phenomenal state maps to selection bias.

The two cannot be fused into a single architecture: at a physically undetermined token there is nothing sufficient for trumping to preempt. The Map's own [specification programme](/apex/interface-specification-programme/) once presented them as one mechanism and has since separated them, calling the fusion "a foundational move the programme has not earned." What the routes do share is how they answer the conservation question: selecting or reassigning among energetically equivalent outcomes injects nothing. Q1's items are an authority law on one route, an indeterminacy-site specification and a control law on the other, and no licence to bank both routes' advantages at once.

**Q2 owes the exclusion debt.** Under the strict reading—phenomenal properties supervene on a physically complete base—the mind owes no causation account because it does no causal work, and has no interface because supervenience replaces interaction: the cell pays nothing on mechanism precisely because the mind has been deflated to the point of doing nothing. Outside the Map's tenets, Kim's exclusion argument presses the cost from within physicalism: if the physical base is sufficient, mental properties either overdetermine effects or do nothing. Any Q2 reading that wants to keep mental causation drifts toward Q1 (by adding indeterminacy) or Q4 (by enriching the mind). The Map's [Bidirectional Interaction](/concepts/bidirectional-interaction/) tenet rules pure Q2 out directly; a reader who does not share the tenet is free to read Q2's escape from the mechanism question as a virtue rather than an evasion.

**Q3 owes the appearance debt.** If everything is mental at base, mind–physical causation becomes the intrinsic dynamics of a single kind, appearing from outside as physical-on-physical causation. The interaction problem evaporates—and is replaced by the *transparency problem*: explaining why the intrinsic mental reality looks, from the outside, exactly like impersonal lawful physical structure. Conservation laws become descriptions of mental regularities, and "why this regularity rather than another" is a Q3-specific debt the other cells do not face in that form. A genuine Q3 *dualism* (mind thick, physics thin, both real) is rare, because once the mind does this much work the physical pole tends to lose explanatory weight and the position slides toward idealism.

**Q4 is charged on all three questions.** With both sides thick, the mind does robust causal work in an ontologically rich physical world, so none of the three is answered by deflation. Cartesian energy-transfer is the classical cost; Stapp's quantum-Zeno account is the contemporary attempt to keep the channel narrow while letting both endpoints stay thick. Specifying non-arbitrarily where two rich realms touch is Q4's signature burden—Princess Elisabeth's challenge to Descartes in its sharpest form. On conservation, Q4 pays either by accepting that conservation is *local and symmetry-conditional* (so interactionist intervention breaks a symmetry at the interface) or by routing influence through a probabilistic channel that is structurally Q1-like even inside a Q4 ontology.

**Two items charge the Map's own cell.** A battery that scores only its author's rivals is not an inventory of what positions owe. Two liabilities the Map carries in Q1 belong on the same list.

*Born-rule indistinguishability.* On the difference-making route the Map takes, consciousness biases which permitted outcome actualises while the ensemble average stays |⟨φ|ψ⟩|². A bias built to leave the aggregate Born measure intact is, on the data alone, *empirically indistinguishable from chance*—[the tenets page](/tenets/#minimal-quantum-interaction) and [the post-decoherence apex](/apex/post-decoherence-selection-programme/) concede exactly that. It is a first-order charge on the causation question, and it falls on the Map's cell alone: Q2 strict selects nothing, and Q4 pays it only on the routes that are structurally Q1-like.

*The global-exclusion posit.* No Many Worlds moves neither thickness coordinate, which is not the same as costing nothing. The companion taxonomy grants that an Everettian *dualist* would be locatable and that the Map's quarrel with branching "rests on the global-exclusion posit… a chosen starting point, not a result this grid delivers." A posit the grid cannot deliver, on which the Map's frontier depends, is a charge, and it is entered here.

Three patterns fall out of the inventory. The conservation objection that has driven the analytic dualism debate since Leibniz is *cell-specific*—an energy-transfer charge in Q4, a selection-without-injection answer in Q1, a redescription of the laws as mental regularities in Q3, structurally inapplicable in Q2 strict—so a blanket dismissal of "dualism" on conservation grounds treats a non-uniform grid as uniform. The interface-formalisation burden varies by cell the same way. And a Q4 defender and a Q2-strict defender may both call themselves dualists while facing entirely non-overlapping mechanism bills, which is why naming the cell first lets the mechanism question be asked at the right grain.

## Parsimony Within the Tenets, Not Across All Axes

The inventory invites a tempting shortcut: read the itemisation as a ranking, let parsimony select the cell with the fewest entries, and declare the Map's position the winner on simplicity. The cluster refuses that shortcut twice over. It fails first on the inventory's own terms—there is no scale on which lists of unlike items could be totalled and compared—and it would fail on the substance even if a scale existed.

The [parsimony reasoning](/topics/parsimony-case-for-interactionist-dualism/) runs in two stages, neither of which converts simplicity into truth. First, *defensively*, parsimony's authority in the consciousness debate was never established. Huemer's survey of four justifications for preferring simpler theories found that none extends naturally to metaphysical disputes lacking differential predictions; the historical record (Mach denying atoms, geocentrism, caloric theory) shows the simpler theory was often the false one; and parsimony's own advocates have qualified the argument—Lycan (2009), a committed materialist of over forty years who concludes that dualism should nonetheless be rejected, admits of it that "there is no evidence against that view; it only offends parsimony." Second, *dialectically*, even granting the objector that simplicity matters, the verdict does not come out as advertised: physicalism's ontological economy is purchased with explanatory debts (brute emergence, error theory about experience, or in-principle inaccessibility), so once every dimension of simplicity is counted, the razor stops pointing cleanly at physicalism and arguably tilts the other way.

The Map does not endorse *simpler-therefore-truer* in either direction. Its fifth tenet—[Occam's Razor Has Limits](/tenets/#occams-limits)—holds that parsimony is not a reliable guide to truth where knowledge is this incomplete, and that the self-binding applies to parsimony arguments *for* dualism exactly as to those *against* it. Earlier versions of this atlas summarised the Map's self-location as paying "the *least mechanism cost compatible with its tenets*." That formulation is withdrawn. It was internal bookkeeping presented as a result: it totalled costs on a scale the inventory never supplied, and it balanced the books before the two items charging the Map's own cell had been entered. What survives is weaker and checkable—the tenets forbid Q2 strict and permit both Q1 and Q4, and within the permitted region the Map's cell is itemised on the same battery as every other, including on the Born-indistinguishability and global-exclusion items no rival cell is charged. Parsimony operates *within* the region the tenets carve out, not as a universal preference for thinness, and Q4 inhabitants (Descartes, Stapp, Goff) are not automatic losers—they lose only when a specific explanatory task fails to justify the extra weight.

## The Map's Region

The five tenets do not pick a single cell. They carve out a *region*. Dualism rules out the idealist limit of Q3; Bidirectional Interaction rules out pure Q2 epiphenomenalism; Minimal Quantum Interaction pushes the physical side toward thin (physics complete except at probabilistic indeterminacies) without directly constraining mind-side thickness; No Many Worlds moves neither coordinate, though it is charged its own item above; Occam's-Razor-Has-Limits explicitly refuses to let thinness count automatically as a virtue, keeping Q4 a live option. Net: the tenets forbid pure Q2 and the idealist limit of Q3, leaving Q1, Q4, and physical-thin / mind-variable hybrids between them as genuine options.

Within that region the Map's *current default reading* is physical-thin plus mind-medium—close to Q1, with room to expand along the mind-axis if Myersian or Jamesian evidence ever warrants. Of Q1's two routes the Map takes the difference-making one: selection among quantum-permitted outcomes where the physical antecedent leaves the token open, with strong causal closure surrendered exactly there and nowhere else. Saad's delegatory trumping stays logged as a distinct alternative rather than a component of the Map's mechanism. What the Map borrows from it is route-level results: ensemble-level observational closure, and the argument that experience is not epiphenomenal. Reading off the rest of the profile: the interface is a narrow probabilistic channel at neural indeterminacy sites, biasing the wavefunction's actualisation without injecting energy; conservation is answered on two *independent* grounds, not one cumulative one—selection-without-injection preserves it, whereas the Noether response grants that an intervention breaks the symmetry the law is conditional on, making local non-conservation consistent with interactionism (Cucu and Pitts). The second is an alternative to the first, not extra support for it, as [the conservation treatment](/concepts/conservation-laws-and-mental-causation/) sets out.

The region's neighbours sharpen what the default reading costs and buys. Drifting toward Q2 would relinquish Bidirectional Interaction and dissolve the interface problem trivially, at the price of epiphenomenalism's evasion of mental work. Drifting toward Q4 would inflate the mind (Myers–James subliminal selves, a Stappian thick observer) or the physics (Russellian quiddities, hidden variables), taking on interface and conservation items the lean reading avoids in exchange for explanatory resources it lacks. Drifting toward Q3 would expand the mental side until the physical becomes extrinsic appearance, putting the Map adjacent to analytic idealism at the cost of the Dualism tenet at the limit.

Two things about the Map's region need stating plainly, because an atlas of this kind is exactly where advocacy tends to creep in. First, the Map's region is a *posited commitment*, not a proven conclusion. Tenets 1 (Dualism) and 5 (Occam-has-limits) are doing load-bearing work in placing the Map where it sits; they are starting points the Map adopts and defends elsewhere, not results derived from the cartography. The map shows where the commitment lands and what it owes—it does not show that the commitment is correct. Second, the mechanism debts the Map's cell carries (the interface specification, the symmetry-breaking question, and the two items entered on the inventory above) are *costs to be paid*, not disproofs of the rival cells. A debt the Map owes in Q1 is not evidence against Q4; it is the price of the Map's own location. Per the [evidential-status discipline](/project/evidential-status-discipline/), reading a rival cell's longer list as a refutation of that rival, or the Map's own list as positive support for the Map, would both overstate what the cartography delivers. The grid speaks; the tenets adjudicate; and the tenets are owned as commitments, not smuggled in as conclusions.

## The Frontier: Interface and Formalisation

Every cell that keeps the interface real and lean inherits one debt the other cells can defer, and it is the debt the Map's own region carries: specifying the interface. This is where the cartography hands off to open work, and where the cluster is most careful not to overclaim.

The [interface problem](/topics/the-interface-problem/) has two faces. *Where* in the brain does the interface operate? And *how* do conscious states map quantitatively to physical selections? On location, the Map has moved from agnosticism among four candidate sites (microtubules, ion channels, cortical microcolumns, attention networks) to *constrained pluralism*: the interface operates through the attention-motor architecture, with molecular-level quantum effects providing the substrate—"at the functional level, through the molecular level." On specification, two partial accounts exist (Stapp's attention-observation coupling, Eccles' intention-probability coupling), but the psychophysical control law—the lawful mapping from phenomenal effort to physical selection bias—remains unspecified. The sharpest form of this gap is the [articulability of the Q1 authority law](/concepts/articulability-of-q1/): whether the non-stipulative authority-selecting rule the Map's region owes can be specified at all without inflating one of the two thickness axes. The honest calibration is *pre-Keplerian*: the framework has Tycho-analogue measurements (the ~10 bits/second bandwidth constraint, the Born-rule causal-consistency result, the theta-band willed-attention signatures of Rajan et al. 2019) and constraint structures of the kind that precede laws, but not the law itself. The first of those carries a caveat inherited from [the specification programme](/apex/interface-specification-programme/): the ~10 bits/second figure (Zheng & Meister 2025) measures *behavioural output* bandwidth—observed typing, speaking, choice rates—not the consciousness-physics interface. The Map treats it as an approximate upper bound on conscious selection bandwidth, and future psychophysical work may revise the estimate.

Beneath the specification gap sits a deeper question the [interface formalisation void](/voids/interface-formalization-void/) raises: can the answer even *be* mathematical? Mathematics describes structure—relations, symmetries, magnitudes. If the interface is where physical structure meets the non-structural actuality of experience, then any mathematical description produces more structure and stops exactly at the actualisation event. The Born rule illustrates the ceiling. Masanes, Galley and Müller (2019) derive it as the only consistent probability rule given basic quantum postulates, so consciousness acting at measurement would have to preserve it—the probability distribution is fixed, but which specific outcome actualises is not determined by the formalism. That derivation is under live published challenge: Kent (2023) exhibits non-quantum measurement and state-update rules satisfying all its assumptions, and Stacey (2022) argues it assumes state-update linearity at the step it purports to prove; the authors have replied, so the question is contested rather than closed, and the result presumes finite-dimensional ensembles. The ceiling holds either way, since no candidate rule fixes the token. Mathematics surrounds the interface perfectly and penetrates it not at all. Whether this is a gap awaiting a better formalism or a principled limit is itself open: the frameworks tried so far (Kleiner's experience space, the IIT+CSL collapse model, category-theoretic approaches) are active and incomplete rather than failed, but each stops at the same place—suggestive of the principled reading without settling it.

The frontier is the cluster's sharpest open question, and stating it as open is part of the discipline: by the Map's own assessment the interface problem is its most significant unresolved challenge, and the framework's long-term credibility depends on progress there. The cartography's job is to locate the frontier and show why it is the debt the Map's region cannot defer—not to pretend the debt has been paid.

## Synthesis: A Map, Not a Verdict

Laid end to end, the cluster turns "is dualism true?" from an unanswerable question about a point into a *navigable* question about a territory. The grid says what positions there are; the inventory says what each owes under one fixed battery—an enumeration, not a measurement, charging the Map's own cell as well as its rivals'; the parsimony reasoning says how to weigh those costs, within a region the tenets fix and never as simplicity-equals-truth; the Map's region says where one set of commitments lands and what it must pay; the frontier says where the territory's hardest unpaid debt sits.

What unifies these into a single methodology is the refusal that runs through all of them: *map, do not adjudicate*. The grid does not argue any quadrant is correct. The inventory records debts cell by cell "without converting per-cell lightness on the chosen battery into endorsement"—and, since the withdrawal of its comparative vocabulary, without ranking the cells at all. The parsimony case removes a barrier without installing dualism. The Map's region is marked as a posited commitment, its debts as costs rather than proofs. That refusal is what makes the atlas usable—and keeping it cost something: a disclaimer of adjudication earns nothing while the apparatus beneath it quietly ranks, so the ranking went rather than the disclaimer. A reader who rejects the tenets can still use the grid; a reader who accepts them gets in addition a precise account of which region they have committed to and what it will cost to inhabit. Cartography serves both because it claims less than advocacy would—and, in claiming less, charts more.

The atlas also names its own genre. The cell-by-cell reading instantiates a generalisable [mechanism-costs cartography](/project/mechanism-costs-cartography/): take a prior locating taxonomy, expose every cell to a fixed battery of the questions the cells' critics press hardest, and read off cell-relative debts without converting any cell's itemisation into endorsement. Dualism is the inaugural domain; the same discipline could chart theories of personal identity, or laws of nature. Naming the method, and binding future deployments to the same non-adjudicative rules, is what distinguishes this cartography from the hand-waving that has historically stood in for a map of the dualist terrain.

## Evidence and Dependency

Most of what this atlas delivers holds independently of the Map's commitments. The grid — two orthogonal thickness axes crossing the relation-kind axis — is a classificatory result a reader can accept while rejecting every tenet; its warrant is the demonstrable orthogonality, not the framework. The debt inventory is independently sourced but unevenly argued: the debts are pressed in the external literature — Kim's exclusion argument, Schaffer's trumping preemption, the conservation objection (Cucu and Pitts), Elisabeth's interface challenge — though only Q1's and Q2's items are derived from those arguments, the rest being read off each cell's own commitments. The parsimony reasoning rests on externally evidenced material (Huemer's survey, the historical record of false simpler theories, Lycan's concession), though the use the piece makes of it is inherited from Tenet 5. The frontier's constraints are externally evidenced (the ~10 bit/s figure — a behavioural-output measure treated as an upper bound, not an interface measurement — the contested Born-rule uniqueness derivation, theta-band willed-attention signatures — which Rajan et al. read through ordinary conflict and cognitive-control processes, and which therefore cannot carry interface-specific weight without a discriminating result) while their interpretation stays open.

The Map's *self-location* near Q1 is inherited, and it is not the only inheritance. This is a **tenet-conditioned cartography**: the tenets do more than place a final pin on an otherwise independent map. The dependency runs through five channels.

| Tenet | What it conditions |
|---|---|
| **Dualism** | *Scope.* Constitutes the territory: without irreducibility the grid degenerates to one occupied cell. |
| **Minimal Quantum Interaction** | *Physical axis, and the frontier.* Fixes the physical-side coordinate at thin, selects the narrow-probabilistic-channel reading, and generates the interface and formalisation debts. |
| **Bidirectional Interaction** | *Exclusions.* Rules pure Q2 out of the admissible region and forbids retreat to epiphenomenalism. |
| **No Many Worlds** | *Frontier, and a charge.* Makes outcome selection a real event requiring a law; entered on the inventory as the global-exclusion item. |
| **Occam's Razor Has Limits** | *Costs.* Blocks the inference from Q1's itemisation to Q1's truth, keeps Q4 live, and is why the inventory reports debts rather than a ranking. |

## Relation to Site Perspective

This synthesis serves the tenets by showing how they function as *constraints on a space* rather than as a derivation of a single position.

**[Dualism](/tenets/#dualism)** makes the territory a territory: only if consciousness is irreducible is there a grid of ways to weight mind against matter. The cartography treats it as the posited commitment that opens the map, not a conclusion the map proves.

**[Minimal Quantum Interaction](/tenets/#minimal-quantum-interaction)** sets the physical-side coordinate at thin and supplies the narrow-probabilistic-channel reading of the interface—and with it the frontier, since the interface and formalisation debts are what such a channel must eventually pay.

**[Bidirectional Interaction](/tenets/#bidirectional-interaction)** closes off pure Q2 and forbids retreat to epiphenomenalism when specification proves hard: it demands consciousness make a physical difference, which demands the difference be specifiable.

**[No Many Worlds](/tenets/#no-many-worlds)** underwrites the frontier—specification only makes sense if there is genuine selection among exclusive outcomes for a law to govern—and is itself charged on the inventory as the global-exclusion item, a posit the grid does not deliver.

**[Occam's Razor Has Limits](/tenets/#occams-limits)** is the tenet the cartography most directly embodies. It keeps the parsimony reasoning honest in both directions, refuses to let the Map's own Q1 itemisation count as proof, and authorises Q4 as a live option that thinness-as-virtue would wrongly exclude. The whole map is an exercise in this tenet: charting debts without letting any cell win by default.
