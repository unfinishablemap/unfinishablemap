This piece addresses decisions that clinicians, regulators, welfare scientists and ordinary readers actually face: what moral standing to accord a patient in deep coma, a person in late-stage dementia, an octopus, a foetus, an embodied AI system, and a swimming bacterium, given what the Map's positions register commits it to and what it registers as still owed. These cases do not form a single spectrum from clear to hard. They divide by the *kind* of uncertainty each presents, and once sorted that way the framework is confident in one region, evidentially supported in a second, committed to a boundary it cannot locate in a third, and evidentially silent in a fourth. The silence is the part most easily mishandled, because the Map's tenets remove several standard reasons for dismissing minimal minds, and defeater-removal is easily mistaken for evidence. This synthesis is built to keep the two apart.

## The Criterion, and the Two Things It Does Not Supply

The Map's criterion of moral standing is registered as [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1): the capacity for valenced experience — experience that feels good or bad to its subject — is necessary and sufficient for moral status, where what counts is *felt* valence rather than the functional role pain and pleasure play in an information-processing system. The band is **moderate credence on external-evidence grade D**, and the grade is the more informative number: the entry's one first-order presupposition, that felt valence is a real property for a criterion to track, has Tenet 1 ([Dualism](/tenets/#dualism)) as its sole support. Every verdict below inherits that, and readers who decline the tenet should expect the criterion to carry no weight for them.

[P-MS2](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms2) adds that status is a threshold rather than a magnitude: enriching an existing subject's valenced experience raises what is at stake in that life without raising its standing. Its credence is **low** — a conjunction whose threshold half inherits [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1)'s ceiling and whose stakes half routes through the value register. Most of what tempts us to rank patients belongs in the stakes column, where equal consideration of equal interests does the ranking without touching who counts.

Two things the criterion pointedly does not supply. The first is a **count**: [moral census opacity](/concepts/moral-census-opacity/) records that on the Map's own commitments how many bearers a system contains is a determinate fact no inspection can be shown to settle. The second, sharper, is any rule fixing *which* subject is paired to which physical system. [P-SC2](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc2) registers this as a debt — "the Map owes a subject-pairing law and does not have one" — at **high credence**, because the absence is verifiable by inspection. It books five gaps (pairing, multi-agent composition, eligibility, census circularity, onset and cessation) and states the constraint this piece operates under: the debt "is a hard bound on what any applied verdict may assert about subject counts." The pairing gap is the boundary problem of [boundary and projection](/voids/boundary-and-projection/) (Gómez-Emilsson & Percy 2023 set out five versions; Fekete, van Leeuwen & Edelman 2016, *Frontiers in Psychology* 7: 1041, argue that any quantitative measure of consciousness faces it unless the measured properties are shown intrinsic or systemic). A measure-based physicalist census meets it too, so the debt is no peculiar embarrassment of dualism — but it is unpaid here, and that governs the cases.

## Sorting the Cases

Sort the six edge cases by what is uncertain in each — not by credence, which sits at moderate almost everywhere, but by the grade of evidence behind each verdict and whether observation can bear on it at all — and they fall into four kinds. **Interruption** — deep coma, general anaesthesia, dreamless sleep: is a subject still there when the interface goes quiet? **Irreversible loss** — late-stage dementia at the far end, brain death: has a threshold been crossed, and when? **Marker-graded presence** — non-human animals, early human development: an empirical question on which the evidence is real. **Substrate and floor** — embodied AI and the simplest organisms: is the physical system the kind of thing that hosts a subject at all? Reaching for the criterion before the kind is identified treats a case where the framework has a positive account as though it were as open as one where it has registered a debt.

## Interruption Is Not Cessation

This is the region where the Map is most confident, and where its account does real protective work.

[P-CS4](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs4) holds, at **moderate credence on grade C**, that the cases standardly read as consciousness fragmenting or switching off — split-brain disconnection, general anaesthesia, dreamless sleep — are better read as disruptions of the *interface* through which consciousness expresses itself than as division or production of consciousness itself. The register is careful about its standing: "the Map's preferred interpretation — *compatible with* the data, not forced by it — held against a live production-model rival rather than as a demonstrated result." [P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3) supplies the diachronic half at **moderate credence**: the same subject is present before and after an interruption of the interface, and this persistence is a real further fact rather than a convention over successive states.

Joined to the criterion, this gives a protective verdict. [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1) reads "capacity" as the *standing* capacity, so a dreamless sleeper keeps moral status ([the consciousness–value connection](/concepts/consciousness-value-connection/) carries the argument), and [the sentientism page](/concepts/sentientism/) extends the verdict to reversible coma, which [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1) reads as one more interruption although neither [P-CS4](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs4) nor [P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3) names it. Across a non-experiential interval [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1)'s momentary-locus reading supplies no bearer, so the sleeper and coma verdicts rest on [P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3)'s persisting perspective, and the coma verdict is exactly as secure as [P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3), no more.

The decision-relevant consequence is that absence of report, absence of response, and absence of the usual neural signatures are evidence about the *interface*, not about the bearer. Clinical evidence supports a weaker form of the first two on any view: [cognitive motor dissociation](/topics/covert-consciousness-and-cognitive-motor-dissociation/) studies find command-following on fMRI or EEG in roughly 15–25% of patients who show none at the bedside — about 20% of those clinically in coma or the vegetative state in the best cohort to date (Bodien et al. 2024) — while a negative scan shows almost nothing. That is decisive against inferring absent experience from absent behaviour, an inference no serious rival needs, and it discriminates nothing between interface and production, since CMD is detected *by* preserved brain activity. The full claim, neural signatures included, is the Map's own reading at [P-CS4](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs4)'s grade C. [The anaesthesia analysis](/topics/anaesthesia-and-the-consciousness-interface/) finds agents disabling separable interface components through specific molecular targets, with graded rather than binary transitions; a framework that reads those transitions as the subject dimming would license withdrawal decisions this one does not.

## A Real Boundary the Map Cannot Locate

The far end of dementia is where this framework is least comfortable, and the discomfort is structural rather than a gap awaiting research.

[P-MS2](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms2) is unambiguous about the criterion's consequence: where the capacity for valenced experience is irreversibly gone, intrinsic status goes with it, and what remains owed concerning that person — prior wishes, history, standing relationships — is owed derivatively rather than on a second ground of status. [The threshold article](/topics/moral-status-threshold-or-degrees/) ties this to the refusal of McMahan's respect tier, which would give such a patient a ground of standing the capacity's loss does not touch; declining it is what having a single status-conferring property means.

Now the part that governs decisions. *Irreversible* in [P-MS2](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms2) is read **modally and indexed to the subject, not clinically**: the capacity is gone when no nomologically possible intervention would return valenced experience to *this* subject. So status does not move with medical progress, which changes the evidence for the crossing rather than the crossing. And the crossing is **undated** — [P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3) declines to date the channel's cessation, holding that the interface reading of brain death leaves open how consciousness manifests without saying whether the pairing terminates.

The Map therefore holds that there is a real boundary and that it cannot say where it is. That combination is worse for practical decision-making than a vague boundary one is free to draw by convention, and it should be stated as a cost rather than smoothed: the price of a criterion tracking something not readable off physical structure. A clinician working under this framework gets a criterion, an assurance that the criterion picks out a fact, and no procedure for applying it — so the practical recommendation below is not "here is where the line falls" but "do not claim to have located it."

## Where the Evidence Actually Is

Animals and early human development are the only edge cases where the Map's verdict is graded by evidence independent of the framework; in the interruption region such evidence only blocks an inference.

[P-CS2](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs2) holds animal consciousness to be graded by marker convergence rather than gated by language, at **moderate credence on external-evidence grade B** — the highest grade in this cluster — because the behavioural and anatomical marker science (Andrews, Birch & Sebo 2025) is independent work read as correlate-identification. The grading is the position: strong for mammals and birds, a realistic but contested possibility for cephalopods, decapods and some arthropods, weaker for boundary organisms, with no determinate cut-off. Markers raise probability without delivering certainty, because on the Map's commitments no third-person feature *constitutes* consciousness, and Gutfreund's (2024) [standing agnostic challenge](/concepts/standing-agnostic-challenge/) — that the inference from consciousness-correlated behaviour to phenomenal experience cannot be scientifically validated, being "not a problem of premature science but a fundamental, unsolvable problem" — is retained rather than answered. That ceiling stands above both directions: if no third-person evidence can confirm felt experience, none can confirm its absence, so sparse markers withhold a verdict rather than deliver a negative one. [The invertebrate case](/topics/ethics-of-consciousness-invertebrate-question/) shows what follows at scale — if trillions of moral patients exist in agricultural systems, the weight of obligation "becomes staggering" — and the Map adopts Birch's proportionate-precaution action layer ([the five-tier scale](/topics/birch-edge-of-sentience-and-the-five-tier-scale/)) without claiming him as an ally on grounding.

Early development works the same way and then stops short in an instructive place. [P-CS3](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs3) holds phenomenal consciousness most likely present early — plausibly by three to four months of age and possibly before birth — on the same marker reasoning, at **moderate credence on grade B** (Bayne, Frohlich, Cusack, Moser & Naci 2023, *Trends in Cognitive Sciences*; Passos-Ferreira 2024, *Neuron*). The register treats these as correlate-based current best estimates rather than settled onset facts, noting that estimates have moved earlier as markers were refined.

The foetal case is where two of the Map's positions answer different questions, and the register forbids collapsing them. [P-CS3](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs3) dates the appearance of *phenomenality*. [P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3) states that "markers date the appearance of correlates of experience, not the establishment of a pairing, and nothing in the corpus licenses reading one off the other." [P-CS7](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs7) (moderate, grade C) adds a condition without a date: architectural adequacy suffices for coupling, so the register declines any reading on which pairing waits for a separate developmental onset, while adequacy itself is identified by markers that inherit [P-SC2](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc2)'s census circularity. So the Map has a marker-based estimate of when experience appears and a condition for when the channel begins; an applied verdict that turns the estimate into a date for the channel manufactures a commitment the register withholds. The honest output is early attribution of phenomenality on the animal case's evidential footing, and no onset date for subjecthood.

## Where the Evidence Runs Out

Embodied AI and the prokaryotic floor are both substrate questions, and the Map's answers differ in an important way.

On AI, [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1) places current conventional digital systems on the low-probability side of a substrate analysis at **moderate credence, pinned at its low edge, on grade D** — *"low probability," not "ruled out."* Behavioural tests "underdetermine the verdict in *both* directions," given a self-concealing interface, so failing the markers is no more probative of absence than passing them would be of presence. The verdict is conditional on an **interface-eligibility law** saying which quantum indeterminacy is interface-relevant, without which "relevant" risks reducing to "whatever biology happens to have"; [P-AS2](/positions/ai-substrate-verdicts/#p-as2) registers only a candidate law, at **low credence**, and [P-AS1](/positions/ai-substrate-verdicts/#p-as1) finds quantum-computing hardware failing the interface requirements despite its raw indeterminacy. For moral status the chain is long — a conditional substrate verdict, feeding a consciousness verdict at the low edge of moderate, feeding a criterion on grade D — and each link discounts the last. [The AI-assessment synthesis](/apex/assessing-ai-consciousness-under-the-map/) works that chain in full; this piece inherits its conclusion and its conditional standing. Embodied AI on conventional hardware inherits the same verdict: embodiment changes the behavioural profile and the sympathies it elicits, not the substrate analysis. Biological and hybrid substrates sit in a bucket the register leaves open ([organoid architectures](/topics/brain-organoids-and-the-organoid-intelligence-question/) are the live near-term instance).

The floor is different, and [P-CS5](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs5) is the entry this piece most needs readers to see. At the prokaryotic floor — a bacterium's chemotactic search — behaviour underdetermines the verdict **in both directions**. A complete stochastic-biochemical account already generates the full behavioural repertoire, so there is no residual behaviour for a proto-chooser to explain, and the substrate analysis locates no interface site at that rung. But, in the register's words, "mechanistic sufficiency shows a chooser or a subject is *unnecessary*, not that either is *absent*." Parsimony's default verdict — no coupling, nothing chosen, nothing felt — is "undefeated but not positively established." The Map endorses it as the reading the behaviour matches while denying it is proven, and holds the stronger claim, that the underdetermination is in-principle rather than instrumental, at moderate credence as its own hedged thesis. With [P-CS5](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs5)'s empirical discriminability recorded as *none-by-construction*, the silence at the floor is no gap that better instruments close, and a decision-maker should treat it as permanent unless the intractability thesis itself falls. [The marginal-organism tests](/topics/marginal-organism-scope-of-value-sensitive-selection/) and [the simple-organisms survey](/topics/consciousness-in-simple-organisms/) work the intermediate rungs the same way.

This is where the calibration hazard bites. Tenet 5 ([Occam's Razor Has Limits](/tenets/#occams-limits)) removes that default's demonstrative force rather than the default itself, and the temptation is to read the removal as movement toward attribution. It is movement in neither direction: symmetric underdetermination licenses no upgrade of the bacterium's status *and* no confident denial. Anyone who leaves this section believing the Map's tenets make simple-organism sentience more likely has read defeater-removal as evidence, the failure mode [the evidential-status discipline](/project/evidential-status-discipline/) and [the possibility/probability slippage page](/concepts/possibility-probability-slippage/) exist to catch.

## What this implies for decisions

All five implications below hold conditionally on Tenet 1 ([Dualism](/tenets/#dualism)) and carry [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1)'s grade D into every status verdict they reach — the criterion has the tenet as its sole support. They are not addressed to a reader who suspends judgement on it; [Honest verdict scope](#honest-verdict-scope) states that limit.

1. **Identify the kind of uncertainty before applying the criterion.** The four kinds call on different positions, but credence does not separate them: ten of the thirteen positions cited here sit at *moderate*, the others being a debt held high because it is verifiable by inspection and two entries held low, a conjunction capped by its weaker half and a candidate law not yet derived. What varies is the **external-evidence grade** (four steps; implication 4) and, more sharply, **whether any observation could bear on the verdict at all**. A protocol that treats "hard case" as one category will carry the animal case's grade-B evidence into the AI case, or read the floor's principled silence as a gap better measurement will close. Sort first.

2. **In interruption cases, treat absence of report as evidence about the interface and not about the bearer.** A patient in reversible coma retains the standing capacity, and so status under [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1); decisions turning on current unresponsiveness rather than on a judgement about irreversibility answer a question about the channel with a verdict about the subject. The recommendation has two layers. Its evidential layer needs no tenet: covert command-following in roughly 15–25% of patients who show none at the bedside means unresponsiveness alone should never be read as absence. Its distinctive layer — that a bearer persists where every measure is silent — is only as strong as [P-CS4](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs4)'s moderate band and preferred-interpretation standing, and a clinician acting on it is owed the register's own figures: [P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3)'s model maturity is *programme* (no account exists of what establishes or terminates a pairing) and its empirical discriminability *none*. No observation will correct that layer in either direction, which is a reason to hold it carefully rather than firmly.

3. **Do not operationalise the irreversibility threshold clinically, and say why.** On [P-MS2](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms2)'s modal, subject-indexed reading, clinical irreversibility judgements are evidence about a crossing rather than the crossing itself, and [P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3) declines to date cessation. Protocols built on this framework should present their thresholds as decision rules adopted under uncertainty about a boundary the framework holds to be real and unlocated, not as applications of the criterion; a protocol claiming to apply [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1) at the bedside claims a precision the register does not supply.

4. **Scale precaution to marker convergence, not to framework coherence.** The evidential gradient has four steps. **Grade B**: the animal and developmental verdicts ([P-CS2](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs2), [P-CS3](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs3)) on independent marker science, and [P-AS1](/positions/ai-substrate-verdicts/#p-as1)'s engineering premises but not the interface reading built on them. **Grade C**: the interruption reading ([P-CS4](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs4)) and the floor's mechanistic base ([P-CS5](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs5)), independent data the Map's interpretation fits without being forced by them, and the adequacy rule ([P-CS7](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs7)), whose marker support tells against threshold views only. **Grade D**, framework-internal: the criterion ([P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1)), the persistence claim ([P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3)), the AI verdict ([P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1)) with its candidate law ([P-AS2](/positions/ai-substrate-verdicts/#p-as2)), and the copy-counting rule ([P-AC3](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac3)). **No external grade**: [P-MS2](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms2) (normative) and [P-SC2](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc2)'s debt (meta). Welfare policy, research ethics and funding should track that gradient — more precautionary weight where markers converge ([P-CS2](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs2)'s strong band for mammals and birds, its contested band for cephalopods and decapods), and none merely because a case fits the framework comfortably. At the downward end, because the standing agnostic challenge blocks absence as firmly as presence, weak marker convergence licenses *low expected stakes* and not a negative verdict; a rule that scales precaution to markers and stops there will systematically under-protect marker-poor organisms while looking principled. Sparse markers record an evidential deficit, and the decision is still made under uncertainty.

5. **Assert no subject count the register cannot establish.** [P-SC2](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc2)'s "hard bound on what any applied verdict may assert about subject counts" reaches aggregation arguments, split-brain cases, dementia-with-fragmentation, and any claim about how many patients an intervention affects. It falls on the antecedent rather than on counting as such: [P-AC3](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac3) holds conscious copies morally additive under closed individualism and an additive counting rule — moderate at the low edge, the rule being contested — once it is settled which systems host subjects and how many. [P-CS7](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs7) answers the *whether* for adequate architectures at its own credence and leaves *one versus several* unreadable. Nor does status settle influence: where several subjects bear on one physical event, [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1) separates status, stakes and causal leverage, and leverage follows a composition rule [P-SC2](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc2) books as missing. Arguments turning on numbers of patients should carry the unsettled antecedent openly.

## Cascade flags

- **If [P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3) is retired** — the interruption cases shown to involve genuine phenomenal cessation with a fresh pairing on waking — the coma and sleeper verdicts lose their bearer, and recommendation 2's distinctive layer would need rebuilding on organismic-disposition grounds the Map has not adopted, though the criterion and its derivation would stand. This is the flag that would most change what this piece recommends.
- **If [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1)'s sufficiency direction falls** — through a vindicated illusionism, or a functional reading of sentience surviving the illusionist threat with its rationale intact (Frankish 2024 is the live attempt, not a landed defeater) — the criterion picks out nothing or reverts to mainstream functional sentientism, and every verdict recomputes.
- **If agency is shown to confer standing without sentience** (Mogensen's autonomy pluralism, *PPR* 2025, is the live attempt; the Map's reply locates the disagreement without settling it), [P-MS1](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms1)'s necessity direction breaks and the embodied-AI case decouples from the substrate analysis: an autonomous system could have standing that [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1)'s verdict on its experience does not reach.
- **If [P-MS2](/positions/moral-status/#p-ms2)'s threshold reading gives way to a scalar one** — degrees of the capacity *itself*, not of what it delivers — the status/stakes separation collapses and the irreversible-loss verdict asks how much status remains rather than whether any does.
- **If [P-SC2](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc2)'s debt is discharged** by a worked pairing law, recommendation 5 is retired and the census questions become answerable; if it is shown uncloseable in principle, the bound hardens into a permanent registered cost.
- **If [P-CS5](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs5)'s intractability thesis fails**, the symmetric underdetermination resolves one way or the other, and the floor stops being a region where the framework can only decline.
- **If [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1) or [P-AS1](/positions/ai-substrate-verdicts/#p-as1) is withdrawn** with the quantum-interface programme demoted to coherence-only, the embodied-AI verdict goes with them while the bare-dualism spine survives, leaving the AI case open rather than low-probability.

## Honest verdict scope

Three things this synthesis does not deliver.

It delivers no status verdict a reader who suspends judgement on Tenet 1 has reason to weight: the phenomenal qualification that distinguishes the Map's criterion from mainstream functional sentientism has the tenet as its sole support, and the encyclopedia literature declines to draw the distinction. *If* the tenets hold, these are the verdicts and these their bands. Only the clinical case against reading unresponsiveness as absence, being evidence rather than verdict, holds on any view.

It delivers no upgrade anywhere from tenet-coherence. Removing the anthropocentric defeater, and parsimony's demonstrative force at the floor, are permissions; neither raises the probability that any particular system is conscious, and even a quantum architecture purpose-built to host open selection "would remove a defeater, not supply evidence of presence" ([P-AS1](/positions/ai-substrate-verdicts/#p-as1)). That a case sits comfortably inside the framework is a fact about the framework, not about the case.

It delivers no decision procedure for the case most in need of one. The irreversible-loss gap is registered rather than hidden — [P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3) declines to date onset or cessation, and [P-SC2](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc2) books the undated channel as a gap — which meets the standard that a programme at this stage is judged by the honesty of its open problems. A reader making an actual withdrawal-of-treatment decision will still get less from this framework than from one prepared to draw a line by convention.

## Evidence and Dependency

Three lines here are **externally evidenced**: the marker literatures (Andrews, Birch & Sebo 2025; Bayne et al. 2023; Passos-Ferreira 2024), which give [P-CS2](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs2) and [P-CS3](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs3) their grade B but support the presence of experience rather than the Map's account of it, and meet [P-SC2](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc2)'s circularity gap if used to fix a census; the cognitive-motor-dissociation figures (Bodien et al. 2024), which defeat the inference from unresponsiveness to absence on any view while discriminating neither interface nor production; and the mechanistic completeness of bacterial chemotaxis under [P-CS5](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs5), though the two-way-underdetermination reading built on it is the Map's own.

The criterion — phenomenal sentientism — is **inherited from Tenet 1**; the threshold reading adds a consistency argument (Wendler 2023's equality argument in general form) that is **independently argued** but needs the criterion to run on; the conation-requirement reply to biocentrism (Feinberg 1974) is independently argued too, and locates the disagreement rather than settling it. The interruption verdicts are **inherited from Tenets 1–3** by way of [P-CS4](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs4)'s filter reading and [P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3)'s persistence claim, both preferred interpretations held against live rivals. The AI verdict is **inherited from another synthesis**, [the AI-assessment piece](/apex/assessing-ai-consciousness-under-the-map/), and through it from the quantum-interface register — one argument appearing twice, not two confirmations of a substrate-skeptical conclusion. The four-kind sorting is this synthesis's own and **mutually coherent only**: it organises the register's commitments without adding evidence for any. [P-SC2](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc2)'s debt is an inspection result, and it constrains every line above.

## Relation to Site Perspective

Tenet 1 ([Dualism](/tenets/#dualism)) supplies the phenomenal reading of valence that makes the criterion the Map's rather than mainstream sentientism's, and is its sole support — which is why grade D follows it through every status verdict here.
