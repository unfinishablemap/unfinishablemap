Philosophy of mind usually asks whether physics *permits* consciousness to play a role. The Unfinishable Map's [tenets](/tenets/) answer yes and go further: consciousness is irreducible, causally efficacious, and interacts with physics at quantum indeterminacies. This article reverses the question—[the reverse inference](/topics/the-reverse-inference/). If the tenets are correct—if consciousness modulates which quantum possibilities actualise within an otherwise lawful collapse process—what does consciousness, taken as a datum, tell us about the structure of physics and about what lies behind the wavefunction?

Quantum mechanics is not incomplete in the way Einstein thought (missing hidden variables) but *partial*—it describes one aspect of a two-aspect reality. The wavefunction captures the constraint space exhaustively: every possibility, every probability, every interference pattern. What it omits is the *selection principle*—what makes one possibility actual rather than another. If the tenets are correct, consciousness contributes to that selection by biasing which outcome an objective-collapse process delivers at the neural interface, not by causing collapse from outside physics. The measurement problem is then not a deficiency in the theory but a *disclosure*: physics, viewed from within, cannot see its own boundary with the non-physical.

**Headline guard.** "Consciousness selects" below is shorthand for a weaker thesis. Collapse occurs whether or not anyone is conscious—objective-collapse mechanisms (e.g. GRW-style spontaneous reduction) supply the baseline—and consciousness biases which outcome some of those collapse events deliver, within [Minimal Quantum Interaction](/tenets/#minimal-quantum-interaction) limits. That departs from the [von Neumann–Wigner reading](/concepts/von-neumann-wigner-interpretation/#relation-to-site-perspective) on which consciousness causes collapse. A second guard concerns evidence: [once objective collapse secures definiteness](/tenets/background-commitments/), the measurement problem's openness supplies the *location* for this bias, not evidence that it occurs; that burden falls on the agency arguments under [Bidirectional Interaction](/tenets/#bidirectional-interaction). The disclosure framing below is a framework-internal reading conditional on the tenets, not support for them.

## The Usual Direction: Physics First

The standard approach treats physics as the fixed frame within which consciousness must find a home; physicalists and dualists alike take physics as given. This concedes too much. Quantum mechanics has spent a century demonstrating that we do *not* know what the physical world is. The [measurement problem](/concepts/measurement-problem/) remains unsolved and no interpretation commands consensus; a 2025 *Nature* survey found physicists scattered across half a dozen incompatible views of what their own theory means.

Consciousness, by contrast, is the least defeasible datum we have: Descartes was right that experience exists. (Illusionists deny even this, holding phenomenality to be a representational artefact; their denial is the one move that, if vindicated, would dissolve the premise, and the reverse inference proceeds on the Cartesian datum instead.) With one secure datum and one insecure framework, the rational move is to let the datum constrain the framework. That asymmetry licenses a direction of inquiry, not a conclusion: the unsettled state of quantum interpretation shows where a constraint could bite, not that consciousness supplies it.

## What the Tenets Require of Physics

Taken as constraints, the tenets say what physics *must* look like if they are correct. The reasoning is conditional rather than circular:

### 1. Collapse Must Be Real and Singular

From [No Many Worlds](/tenets/#no-many-worlds) and [Bidirectional Interaction](/tenets/#bidirectional-interaction): the wavefunction does not merely appear to collapse—it actually does, and one outcome is genuinely selected from among possibilities, the selection that makes consciousness causally relevant.

This rules out Many-Worlds, decoherence-only accounts, and any interpretation where collapse is merely apparent or perspectival—including the [consistent-histories](/concepts/consistent-histories-interpretation/) programme, which realises a single history by Born-rule chance with no collapse and no selector, holding that none is needed. A selector is what the reverse inference requires, so the disagreement is a framework boundary, not a test the interpretation fails.

### 2. Physics Must Be Genuinely Open at Measurement

From [Minimal Quantum Interaction](/tenets/#minimal-quantum-interaction): the Born rule gives probabilities, not outcomes. No hidden physical variable determines the result; the openness is real, not a reflection of ignorance. This rules out Bohmian mechanics, superdeterminism, and any interpretation where outcomes are predetermined by physical facts we happen not to know.

[Observational closure](/concepts/observational-closure/), as Saad (2025) names it, sharpens the point: the evidence supports only *observational* closure—no observable violation of physical law—not the metaphysical claim that every event has a sufficient physical cause, and Buhler's (2020) survey finds every extant argument for universal closure either question-begging or an extrapolation from observed regularities. Saad does not offer that gap to the quantum route, though. His [delegatory account](/topics/delegatory-dualism/) fills it by preemption, and he holds that quantum interactionist dualism "violates Observational Closure" because nomically possible tests could distinguish it (2025, n. 15)—the charge the Map's Born-preserving reading is built to answer. A stronger physicalist formulation survives regardless: *chance-fixing* closure, on which physical causes suffice to fix their effects' chances, is one exact Born preservation grants. The Map's claim is token-level, that consciousness settles which outcome is actual where the chances leave it open, and whether settling counts as causing marks a [framework boundary](/concepts/causal-closure/).

### 3. Something Non-Physical Modulates Outcome Selection

From [Dualism](/tenets/#dualism) and [Bidirectional Interaction](/tenets/#bidirectional-interaction): the selection is not brute randomness. Consciousness modulates which outcome is actualised—biasing selection within a collapse process physics already supplies, in a way that stays statistically consistent with the Born distribution over ensembles (preserving [conservation laws](/concepts/conservation-laws-and-mental-causation/)). Collapse does not wait on consciousness; what consciousness contributes is a within-Born nudge to *which* outcome is realised where its interface is engaged.

Quantum probabilities therefore describe the *constraint space* within which consciousness operates rather than the full causal story. Physics specifies what can happen, with what weight, and—via [objective collapse](/concepts/spontaneous-collapse-theories/)—the baseline rate at which possibilities resolve.

### 4. The Interaction Is Minimal

From [Minimal Quantum Interaction](/tenets/#minimal-quantum-interaction): consciousness does not override physics, inject energy, or produce detectable deviations from quantum-mechanical predictions. The Map's default corridor reading goes further than the tenet requires and leaves the Born distribution exactly intact, so consciousness stays invisible to any *unconditioned* aggregate test—a consequence taken up under [empirical equivalence](#the-empirical-equivalence-problem) below.

This constraint is weaker than the Map has generally read it. Every clause above governs *magnitude*; none governs which way the influence points. [The sign problem](/concepts/sign-problem-for-conscious-observation/) makes the omission concrete for the Zeno-type mechanism the corpus has leaned on: whether repeated observation suppresses a system's evolution or accelerates it is fixed by a relation between the observation interval and the environment's correlation time—a property of the bath rather than of the observer. Kofman and Kurizki (2000) report that inhibitory Zeno behaviour "may be feasible in a limited class of systems" while accelerated decay "appears to be much more ubiquitous." Post-decoherence selection, which the Map endorses more strongly, involves no repeated observation, but the general point outlives the mechanism: an interface proposal owes a magnitude argument *and* a direction argument, and minimality supplies only the first.

## The Wavefunction as Partial Description

On this account the wavefunction is a *complete description of the structural aspect of reality*: it encodes every possibility, assigns every amplitude, determines every interference pattern. The sense of that completeness matters. As [recent disambiguation](/concepts/quantum-probability-consciousness/) clarifies, quantum mechanics is ψ-complete and Schrödinger-complete (dynamics fully specified), but *Born-incomplete*—the Born rule supplies no mechanism for which outcome actualises. [No-go theorems](/concepts/physical-completeness/) constrain the completions: Bell eliminates local hidden variables, Kochen–Specker non-contextual value assignments, and PBR ontological models on which the wavefunction is mere information about an underlying state—given preparation independence, an assumption ψ-epistemic models can reject. Determinism is the exception the theorems leave standing: de Broglie–Bohm restores it nonlocally and contextually, and the Map declines that route on tenet grounds, not no-go grounds. Reading the package as structurally exhaustive yet ontologically partial is this article's own contested inference, not a no-go result.

Einstein was half right. EPR argued that quantum mechanics, if local, must be incomplete; Bell later showed that no local hidden-variable theory reproduces its predictions. What the theory omits, on this reading, is not *physical* information but *actuality*—which possibility is realised. If the tenets are correct, that omission marks a boundary *of* the theory rather than a gap within it. Unlike Copenhagen instrumentalism, the account treats the wavefunction as ontologically real—correctly describing the structure of possibilities—but not ontologically *exhaustive*: reality has two aspects, structure (which the wavefunction captures) and actuality (which consciousness supplies at neural interfaces). A map of all routes through a city is complete as a map, yet it does not contain the fact of which route you take; that fact is contributed by the driver.

## The Born Rule as Interface Specification

If the wavefunction describes the constraint space and consciousness provides selection, then the [Born rule](/concepts/quantum-probability-consciousness/)—squared amplitude gives probability—describes the *interface* between them, for the alternatives within the brain's own dynamics. It tells consciousness what it has to work with: the relative weights of different possibilities. These weights are real: consciousness cannot select a zero-amplitude outcome, and higher-amplitude outcomes are actualised more often across ensembles. But *which* outcome is actualised in a single case the rule does not say. It specifies the *menu*, not the *order*—and the menu's entries are improper-mixture components awaiting actualisation, not already-definite alternatives.

How much ordering that leaves is a computable question. [The agency budget](/concepts/agency-budget/) maps the corridor onto perfectly secure steganography: a selector that must leave a public distribution exactly intact is a perfectly secure channel, and such channels are exactly the *couplings*—joint distributions that marginalise to the fixed distribution while correlating freely with the hidden variable. Elementary information theory then caps the channel at min(H(conscious source), H(Born distribution)) bits of reasons-correlated selection per event, at exactly zero statistical signature. The cap holds for a fixed distribution of conscious states. Outsiders can re-weight that distribution by instruction or recruitment without reading anyone's conscious state, and an ensemble stacked with one intention forces every conditional back to the Born value and the budget to zero—unless a further law, which the Map has not written (see below), blocks such re-weightings. The interface thus gets a capacity as well as a shape, on a condition still owed; the mapping onto mental causation is the Map's own construction, not a published result, and carries the coherence-only citation grade.

This reinterpretation has consequences:

**The Born rule is not fully explained by physics alone.** Gleason's theorem shows the Born rule is the *unique* probability measure consistent with Hilbert space structure; Deutsch-Wallace decision theory, Zurek's envariance, and Torres Alegre's relativistic-causality derivation (2025, an arXiv preprint not yet peer-reviewed) supply further internal derivations. These succeed as proofs that the rule is the only coherent assignment *given* the formalism, so the squared-amplitude form is not where an interface could show. But as the [analysis of derivation failures](/topics/born-rule-and-the-consciousness-interface/) shows, every strategy either postulates the Born rule, assumes it in disguised form, or relocates it to another assumption. What resists derivation is that one outcome actualises at all—a gap the interface reading fits, and several non-Map readings fit equally.

**Collapse interpretations have a structural advantage here.** Collapse theories postulate the Born rule outright. Many-Worlds must *derive* probability from a deterministic theory, and critics argue every derivation smuggles in what it claims to derive—a charge contested rather than settled, since Everettians regard the decision-theoretic and self-locating programmes as live. The asymmetry is therefore conditional: *given* the tenets' demand for genuine selection, an honestly postulated rule beats a recovery whose premises the Map independently rejects, which is a reading of the Map's commitments, not a neutral refutation of MWI.

**Intersubjective agreement needs no special explanation.** Agents measuring the same external system find the same distribution because the apparatus outcome is objectively reduced before any brain processes it; the interface reading applies only to alternatives within an agent's own neural dynamics. Either way the Born probabilities are objective chances, not subjective beliefs (pace QBism).

## The Measurement Problem as Disclosure

On this view, the [measurement problem](/concepts/measurement-problem/) reads as a *disclosure*—an indication that [physics, viewed from within, reaches a boundary it cannot cross](/concepts/physics-as-disclosure/), rather than a defect awaiting a within-physics solution.

The Schrödinger equation governs evolution perfectly until measurement, at which point the theory requires an additional rule with no physical mechanism. The search for a solution has produced interpretations that each eliminate the boundary by modifying what counts as real. The reverse inference reads it differently: if the tenets are correct, the boundary is real, and the theory reaches its limit because reality extends beyond what physics describes—the measurement problem marks where the structural aspect of reality meets the actualising one. Gödel's incompleteness theorems offer a suggestive rather than rigorous parallel, a system complete on its own terms that gestures beyond itself, and the pattern recurs in Lawvere's fixed-point theorem and Cubitt et al.'s (2015) spectral-gap undecidability. Throughout, support runs from the tenets to the reading: the disclosure framing is what the measurement problem looks like *if* the tenets hold, not a datum arguing for them.

## What Must Lie Behind the Wavefunction

If quantum mechanics describes one aspect of a two-aspect reality, what is the other aspect? The tenets constrain the answer:

**Not hidden variables.** Hidden-variable theories add more physics, and constraint 2 has already excluded them for eliminating the indeterminacy that makes consciousness relevant.

**Not more mathematics.** Adding equations produces more structure, not actuality; the gap is ontological rather than mathematical.

**Something with indexical character.** If the structural-actualising distinction holds, whatever settles actuality at the interface must have the first-person character that third-person description lacks—which is what consciousness is: the domain where things are *like something for someone*. The [indexical dimension](/topics/indexical-identity-quantum-measurement/) of the measurement problem—why does *this* consciousness experience *this* outcome?—is the question of what makes one possibility actual, viewed from the inside, and the [vertiginous-question](/topics/vertiginous-question/) formalises how it resists answer. The position requires a *metaphysical* indexical thesis: not merely that *this* subject lacks information about which branch they occupy (the self-locating and decision-theoretic recoveries arguably presuppose the very subject in question, a charge contested rather than settled), but that there is a fact about which outcome is actual *for* this subject. Even that does not exclude every many-worlds reading. A [diverging-worlds](/concepts/diverging-worlds-everettianism/) Everettian can grant each subject an outcome actual from within; what branching cannot supply is that the unexperienced outcomes are *globally* nonactual, realised nowhere—a [posit the Map adopts](/tenets/background-commitments/) rather than a conclusion the indexical argument delivers.

Consciousness need not *cause* collapse universally (the route to Wigner's solipsism trap). It grounds the *indexical fact* of which outcome this subject experiences at its own neural interface: physical mechanisms fix the basis of possibilities, and consciousness settles which is actual there. The [measurement problem](/concepts/measurement-problem/#the-indexical-dimension) and the [hard problem](/topics/hard-problem-of-consciousness/) then mark the same boundary—where third-person description encounters first-person fact—seen from different sides.

**The interface is the ontological joint.** If reality has two aspects, the interface between them is the fundamental ontological joint, and the Born rule describes its structure. [decoherence](/concepts/decoherence/) fixes the basis at which it operates without selecting the outcome. [Objective collapse](/concepts/spontaneous-collapse-theories/) supplies the baseline for pre-conscious physics, with consciousness modulating that process in neural systems.

The interface resists direct observation from either side. The [causal interface void](/voids/causal-interface/) explains why: the joint lies between two ontological domains and resists description in terms of either alone. The [amplification void](/voids/amplification-void/) adds that the *consequences* of selection are untraceable across the scales they would have to cross—an opacity ordinary neurodynamics supplies for any small perturbation, whatever its source, rather than one minimality has to earn. The [triple screen](#the-triple-screen) below prices both.

## Constraints on a Deeper Theory

If this picture is correct, a theory of everything would need to be *bi-aspectual*, describing structure, actuality and the interface between them. It would need to:

**Explain [prebiotic collapse](/concepts/prebiotic-collapse/).** For 13.8 billion years before consciousness evolved, quantum outcomes resolved somehow. Objective collapse provides the baseline; a complete theory would show how consciousness *modulates* it rather than replacing it, and which of the two transition processes—physical reduction or conscious selection—acts first in an interface-grade brain, a [collapse-priority debt](/concepts/prebiotic-collapse/#the-completeness-tension) no combined law yet discharges.

**Specify the coupling.** The Map's leading candidate, [post-decoherence selection](/concepts/post-decoherence-selection/), and the [coupling modes](/concepts/coupling-modes/) describe *how* consciousness might meet quantum events; a deeper theory would show *why* those modes are possible. Stapp's [quantum Zeno model](/concepts/stapp-quantum-mind/), the best-developed alternative, is the tenets' nearest neighbour rather than an implementation of them: it supplies Process 1—which question is put, and when—leaves the answer to the orthodox statistical rule, and so declines the outcome-selection the Map asserts, while owing its own [specification debt](/concepts/process-1-specification-problem/) over what fixes the basis chosen. Its one warm-biology precedent is narrow. Denton et al. (2024) *modelled*, rather than measured, Zeno-enabled magnetosensitivity in cryptochrome radical pairs, on a sub-microsecond coherence budget some three orders of magnitude short of neural timescales, and the measurement doing the Zeno work there is a spin-selective recombination reaction—a physical decay channel with no observer involved. Stapp's model needs an observation that is not merely another physical coupling, since that would decohere the state rather than protect it, so the precedent leaves the observer-involving variant unexemplified.

For Zeno-type mechanisms, two results meet inside one calculation. The agency budget allows about one bit per event for a yes/no attending; the sign problem says landing on the suppressing side of a bath-set crossover takes calibrated access to a spectral property of the agent's own neural bath. At coherence-only strength, locating one such crossover within a 300 ms window costs some forty bits against the three the Map's ~10 bits per second allows; the per-placement reading, tens of bits over some 10¹³ placements, is an upper bound that holds only where the crossover drifts. Both figures rest on a femtosecond bath scale computed for the Map rather than measured, so this is an order-of-magnitude argument, and it falls on the horn the agency reading needs. A deeper theory must show the price wrong, or the direction set without the agent paying it.

**Write the companion law.** Exact Born preservation cannot be stated as a law over physical outcomes alone. If the distribution of conscious states can be re-weighted at will, either no conditional ever departs from the Born value—the epiphenomenal horn—or a [companion law](/apex/born-preserving-causal-efficacy/#where-a-selection-could-show-if-anywhere-the-conditional-signature) constrains which populations of intention are physically admissible (psychophysical superselection), or a compensation law re-tunes the conditionals to each context's mix of intentions. A deeper theory must state and motivate one, and say at which level preservation holds: the register's default binds only the unconditioned marginal, keeping conditioned tests live, while the agency budget's law binds every publicly assemblable ensemble, which zeroes its budget under re-weighting.

**Connect to spacetime geometry.** Penrose's objective reduction links collapse to gravitational self-energy, suggesting the interface has geometric structure. The [nomic void](/voids/nomic-void/) raises a further possibility, after Wheeler's participatory ontology: that the laws of physics are mutually constituted with consciousness rather than prior to it.

## The Rival Programme: A Physics-of-Mind That Goes the Other Way

The most rigorous *programmatic competitor* runs the inference the other way. Włodzisław Duch's "computational physics of the mind" (Duch 1996, 2019) holds cognitive science to the modelling discipline physics applies elsewhere: mind objects as quasi-stable attractor states of brain dynamics, mental events as trajectories through psychological space, mind as the "shadow" of neurodynamics. That [formal tooling](/concepts/geometric-model-of-mind/) is methodologically appropriable; the rivalry lives in the metaphysical reading, on which physics is the secure starting point and mind the *expressed approximation*. Duch's anti-quantum-mind position ([the research dossier](/research/wlodzislaw-duch-consciousness-2026-05-02/)) deepens it: quantum proposals, he argues, use exotic physics to fill gaps a classical-computational model already fills. His is the strongest competent denial of *both* moves made here—that consciousness is not exhausted by neurodynamics, and that quantum indeterminacies are where the interface sits.

Duch reads the measurement problem as a within-physics problem settled by spontaneous collapse—which the reverse inference accepts as baseline—with consciousness contributing nothing. The programmes *converge* against Penrose–Hameroff-scale macroscopic coherence while *diverging* on whether anything below that scale is required. Per [framework-stage-calibration](/project/framework-stage-calibration/), the reverse inference sits *pre-Keplerian*, still gathering measurements toward specification laws Duch's model claims are unnecessary; until the falsification roadmap delivers, the choice between classical-shadow and bi-aspectual readings runs along philosophical rather than experimental lines.

## The Empirical Equivalence Problem

The reverse inference has a structural vulnerability. On the corridor reading consciousness operates strictly within the Born probabilities, so under any *unconditioned* aggregate test the framework predicts nothing distinguishable from standard quantum mechanics with random collapse—by construction, not for want of sensitivity. The minimality that keeps consciousness statistically invisible makes the position physically respectable and equally makes it hard to test. Preservation binds only the unconditioned marginal, though: a deviation *conditioned* on intention, task or subject would test the corridor itself ([P-Q3](/positions/quantum-interface/#mechanism-debt)), and no such test of the brain-internal corridor has yet run. Until one does, the reverse inference is a reinterpretation of what quantum mechanics means rather than a competing empirical theory.

Two considerations keep this from being fatal. The same charge applies to most interpretations—Many-Worlds, Copenhagen and QBism make identical practical predictions and are judged on coherence, explanatory power and consequences, terms on which the reverse inference competes. And the framework names where differential predictions could emerge, all untested: conditioned residual structure first, then regimes where modulated collapse meets gravitational self-energy.

The claim is therefore conditional: *if* the tenets hold, quantum mechanics is structurally partial in a specific way, the measurement problem marks that partiality's boundary, and the Born rule reads naturally as interface specification. The position would be undermined by a physical collapse law shown to be outcome-complete inside the brain as well, leaving consciousness nothing to select; by vindication of hidden variables; or by an account that denies consciousness any physical role while still explaining why it exists. None has occurred—the measurement problem remains unsolved, hidden-variable theories face severe constraints, and [the self-stultification argument](/concepts/self-stultification/) still presses on epiphenomenal accounts, as the Map's deepest objection to them rather than a refutation—but any would weaken the foundation.

### The Triple Screen

Empirical equivalence is one face of a larger conjunction the Map accepts piecewise but should state outright. The core causal claim—that consciousness biases neural outcomes—is screened from the three channels through which such a claim could ordinarily be evidenced, and the screens have separate origins.

The first is *minimality* on the corridor reading: consciousness leaves no deviation an unconditioned test could find, closing the **aggregate-statistical** channel. Under the agency budget's mapping that screen is a theorem—perfect statistical concealment and zero third-person evidence are one condition under two descriptions, so a proof that the corridor admits a nonzero channel is simultaneously a proof that no test an outsider can assemble witnesses it, on the budget's own law, which binds every publicly assemblable ensemble. That firms up the cost the framework carries rather than the case for the mechanism. The second is the [amplification void](/voids/amplification-void/)'s untraceability: even granting the bias, the chain from quantum nudge to macroscopic effect crosses four regimes no single framework spans, closing the **mechanistic-tracing** channel; ordinary neurodynamics supplies that closure for any small perturbation, so it does not depend on the first screen. The third is the [agency void](/voids/agency-void/)'s verification circularity: every first-person check on "did I cause that?" is run by the faculty whose causal status is in question, closing the **first-person** channel from inside.

The central causal claim is therefore not established by direct evidence. The channels that could supply it are blocked—the statistical one at least in its unconditioned form—and whether conditioned tests stay open depends on the level at which preservation holds, with none yet run at any brain-internal grain. The Map holds the claim on the strength of its tenets, and says so. Naming this is an admission rather than a defence: insulation from evidence is a cost the position carries, and triple screening removes potential defeaters without supplying support, since [removing a defeater is not evidence](/project/evidential-status-discipline/).

## Evidence and Dependency

A line of reasoning this convergent can mistake coherence for confirmation. The reverse inference is *independently argued* from the Cartesian datum that experience is not a representational illusion; grant illusionism and it lapses. The four constraints it reads off physics are *inherited from Tenets 1–4*, and the invisibility under constraint 4 rests on the corridor reading, a default the tenet permits rather than entails. The no-go results—Bell, Kochen–Specker, PBR, Gleason—and the closure scholarship (Saad 2025, Buhler 2020) are *externally evidenced*, but they show only that physics is Born-incomplete, not that consciousness rather than objective collapse supplies the missing actuality, and Saad himself counts quantum interactionism against observational closure. The triple screen is *inherited* from the corridor reading, ordinary neurodynamics and the agency void, and removes defeaters without adding support. The formal results sit lower than their mathematics suggests: the coupling theorems and the Kofman–Kurizki physics are externally evidenced but concern neither consciousness nor the Map, so the capacity ceiling and the direction shortfall placed on them are *mutually coherent only*—they price the framework's debts, the unwritten companion law among them, without moving the causal claim closer to evidence. The sign problem is a falsifier the Map raised against itself, so its absence from the critical literature is not agreement. Most of these lines are one premise package, the tenets, producing compatible readings rather than independent confirmations.

## Relation to Site Perspective

**[Dualism](/tenets/#dualism)**: The reverse inference makes dualism bear on physics. If the wavefunction is structurally partial and consciousness provides the missing actuality at neural interfaces, dualism is a claim about the architecture of reality rather than a philosophical add-on—one physics leaves room for without supporting.

**[Minimal Quantum Interaction](/tenets/#minimal-quantum-interaction)**: On the corridor reading, which the tenet permits rather than entails, the interface's invisibility to unconditioned aggregate tests follows by construction and is equally the source of its most serious limitation. The tenet also constrains the influence's magnitude while leaving its direction unfixed, the two-parameter obligation constraint 4 records.

**[Bidirectional Interaction](/tenets/#bidirectional-interaction)**: The reverse inference proposes *where* bidirectional interaction would occur (at the structural-actualising interface) and *why* aggregate statistics would not show it. If this picture is correct, causal closure stated as token sufficiency fails at measurement, as any indeterministic physics implies, while closure stated as chance-fixing survives; what the Map adds is something that settles the outcomes the chances leave open.

**[No Many Worlds](/tenets/#no-many-worlds)**: MWI dissolves the interface by making all possibilities actual. The reverse inference requires genuine actualisation—one outcome selected and the rest globally nonactual, a posit the Map adopts rather than derives. See [The Map's Case for One-World Actuality](/arguments/many-worlds-argument/) for the cumulative case.

**[Occam's Razor Has Limits](/tenets/#occams-limits)**: Denying consciousness any role in physics looks simpler, but purchases that simplicity by leaving the measurement problem and the hard problem unsolved where the reverse inference addresses them as one. That the two-aspect ontology buys explanatory economy is not thereby evidence it is true—the tenet binds parsimony arguments for the framework as tightly as those against it.

### Thickness commitments {#thickness}

The [four-quadrant taxonomy](/topics/four-quadrant-dualism-taxonomy/) sorts dualist positions by mind-side thickness crossed with physical-side thickness. The reverse inference commits cleanly on only one axis. The *physical side is thin*: the wavefunction's mathematics is structurally exhaustive, and no hidden variables, Russellian quiddities or ignorance-facts are invoked—physics is silent about actuality, not deficient about structure. The *mind side is underdetermined*: the argument needs consciousness only rich enough to ground indexical actuality, which a thin mind (bare first-person character plus selection) or a thicker one (a Myers–James subliminal self, a unitary Nida-Rümelin-style subject) could supply. The argument is therefore not hostage to a particular dualist settlement, living in Q1 as naturally as in Q4. What it rules out is physical-thick dualism (hidden variables, non-experiential quiddities) and the idealist limit where the physical side vanishes; later work choosing a mind-side thickness refines within the region it fixes.
