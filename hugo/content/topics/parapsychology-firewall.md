A naive reading of dualism predicts that mind should be able to push matter around: if consciousness is non-physical and causally efficacious, then strong telepathy, reliable psychokinesis, and decisive precognition look like exactly the evidence a dualist should want. The Map holds the opposite. **Spectacular, reliable, ensemble-detectable psi would disconfirm the Map's framework rather than confirm it.** The interface the Map's tenets actually require is *self-concealing by construction*—Born-statistics-preserving, no-signalling-respecting, and indistinguishable from chance under any unconditioned aggregate test. That last constraint is scoped rather than blanket: the indistinguishability holds for the unconditioned aggregate, while each token outcome is still selected rather than left to chance, and a deviation *conditioned* on intention, task or subject would test the corridor itself (the [mechanism-debt convention](/positions/quantum-interface/#mechanism-debt)). A large, lawlike psi effect would violate precisely the constraints (explained below under [The Three Firewall Constraints](#the-three-firewall-constraints)) that make the Map's interface compatible with physics. This article names that structural barrier the **parapsychology firewall** and explains why the Map sits on the *unfavourable* side of any sensational parapsychological result.

The firewall is not a hedge bolted on to protect the framework from awkward data. It falls out of [Minimal Quantum Interaction](/tenets/#minimal-quantum-interaction) and the no-signalling structure of quantum probability the way the corridor reading does in [selection-only-mind-influence](/topics/selection-only-mind-influence/). The Map's relationship to parapsychology is therefore the reverse of what folk dualism expects: the Map needs psi to be *small or absent*, not large.

## What the Firewall Is

The parapsychology firewall is the claim that the Map's tenets entail an upper bound on detectable psi (mind acting on systems outside its own brain, or acquiring content from outside it) so low that any effect crossing it would falsify the framework. Three distinct kinds of "spectacular psi" sit on the wrong side of that bound:

- **Macroscopic psychokinesis** — minds reliably biasing classical-scale physical systems (bending objects, deflecting dice, moving needles) by an amount large enough to survive replication.
- **High-bandwidth telepathy or precognition** — information transfer between minds, or from future to present, at rates that carry meaningful content reliably.
- **Lawlike ensemble effects** — any psi signature that persists in aggregate statistics under accumulating, well-controlled measurement, rather than regressing toward the chance baseline.

The Map predicts that none of these exists at detectable strength. Not because they are metaphysically impossible—the Map's whole point is that mind is causally efficacious—but because the *specific kind* of efficacy the tenets license is structurally incapable of producing them. The firewall is the boundary between the interface the Map endorses and the parapsychological phenomena it must predict against. A small deviation *conditioned* on intention inside the brain's own quantum processes is a different observable, taken up under [What Would Actually Bear on the Map](#what-would-actually-bear-on-the-map).

## The Three Firewall Constraints

Three commitments, each load-bearing elsewhere in the corpus, jointly erect the firewall.

### Constraint 1: Born-Statistics Preservation

The Map's default mechanism is selection within a corridor that leaves aggregate Born statistics exactly intact (position [P-Q2](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q2) in [quantum-interface](/positions/quantum-interface/)). Conscious selection biases *which* outcome becomes actual on a given trial, but the long-run frequencies still converge to |⟨φ|ψ⟩|². As [selection-only-mind-influence](/topics/selection-only-mind-influence/) derives, this drives the *signed* per-trial information rate toward zero across many trials. A reliable macroscopic PK effect is, by definition, a *persistent* departure from the unbiased distribution—a systematic deviation that does not wash out. That is the one thing the corridor forbids. So large, lawlike PK is not weak evidence against the Map; it directly contradicts the corridor reading the Map endorses most strongly. [The agency budget](/concepts/agency-budget/) gives this constraint a closed form: the selection the corridor permits is bounded above by the smaller of two entropies—the conscious source's and the Born distribution's—and delivered at exactly zero *unconditioned aggregate* signature. That arithmetic establishes less than it may appear to. The zero signature is the corridor's own stipulation rather than a derived result, and it binds that unconditioned marginal only—a deviation *conditioned* on intention, task or subject would still test the corridor, though none has yet run in the work the Map has reviewed: the intention-to-RNG nulls of Maier, Dechamps & Pflitsch (2018) concern an external RNG outside the Map's [brain-locality](/concepts/brain-interface-boundary/) scope, so they were predicted either way. What the budget adds is that a *nonzero* selection allowance survives the stipulation, so the firewall's demand for null aggregates is satisfiable rather than empty. The mapping of those information-theoretic results onto mental causation is the Map's own construction and not a published finding, and per the [mechanism-debt convention](/positions/quantum-interface/#mechanism-debt) it is citable as framework-internal coherence arithmetic, never as established mental causation.

### Constraint 2: No-Signalling

The Born rule can be derived from relativistic-causality / no-signalling considerations (Han & Choi 2016), and—as the [causal-consistency-constraint](/concepts/causal-consistency-constraint/) develops—non-affine deviations from the Born map enable superluminal signalling in finite-dimensional generalised probabilistic theories that also satisfy purification (Torres Alegre 2025). Two scope limits belong with that result rather than after it: it is not yet refereed, and whether its structural conditions reach a consciousness-physics interface is a Map-side inference rather than a source-side claim. The firewall does not rest on it. Born-preservation is already stipulated by the corridor ([P-Q2](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q2)), so the no-signalling result works as a bound on the *escape routes*: a variant seeking its minimum outside the corridor would have to show that its deviation is signalling-safe (position [P-Q7](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q7)). High-bandwidth telepathy or decisive precognition would be a channel carrying reliable information; a channel carrying reliable information that outruns light-cone structure breaks no-signalling. The Map buys single-system no-signalling safety (joint and Lorentz-covariant safety stay open under [P-Q7](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q7)) at the price of accepting that *no large effect can ever be visible at the ensemble level*. Spectacular telepathy would cash a cheque the Map has explicitly refused to write.

### Constraint 3: Content-Confinement

Under the strict selection-only reading, mind can only choose among candidates the brain has already generated; it cannot register, choose, or report content no candidate encodes. This is the content-confinement limit of [selection-only-mind-influence](/topics/selection-only-mind-influence/). Veridical precognition of arbitrary external facts—or telepathic reception of content with no local neural pre-image—would require mind to acquire information the brain never generated as a candidate. That is exactly what the read-and-select interface cannot do. The interface is read-and-select, not read-and-create, and a fortiori not receive-from-elsewhere.

Together these three constraints fix the firewall's height: any psi effect that is *large*, *lawlike*, or *content-importing* lands on the falsifying side.

## Why the Folk Intuition Runs Backwards

The intuition that psi would vindicate dualism rests on a picture the Map rejects: mind as a *force*, an extra causal push injected into the physical world alongside the four fundamental interactions. On that picture, more mind means more push, and a strong enough push shows up as bent spoons. But the Map's [Minimal Quantum Interaction](/tenets/#minimal-quantum-interaction) tenet expressly rules out "uncontrolled psychokinesis, energy injection by mind, and any interaction that would be empirically detectable under current experimental precision." Mind does not inject energy or force. It *selects* among outcomes physics already leaves open, within a corridor that preserves the statistics. That is a stronger claim than Stapp's (2006) interactive dualism, on which the agent chooses which question is put to nature and "nature returns answers, subject to specified statistical requirements"; the Map takes on outcome-selection itself, and the corridor is what keeps that stronger claim inside the firewall. An interface of that kind is invisible to exactly the experiments that would detect a force.

The folk inference also commits a version of [possibility-probability-slippage](/concepts/possibility-probability-slippage/) run in reverse. The slippage warned against elsewhere is treating *the tenets allow X* as *the evidence supports X*. The folk-dualist version treats *dualism is true* as *psi should be strong*—as though the metaphysical thesis carried an empirical prediction of large effects. It carries the opposite prediction. The Map's dualism is compatible with, and indeed seems to predict, a world in which the most rigorous parapsychology finds vanishing or null effects.

## The Demarcation Already in the Tenets

The Map has always drawn a sharp line between its constrained interface hypothesis and "quantum woo." Tenet 2 names the boundary explicitly: the tenet is "not to be confused with" quantum mysticism, including its use to justify parapsychology or magical thinking, and it "rules out" detectable energy injection by mind. The parapsychology firewall is the systematic statement of that demarcation. It explains *why* the line is where it is: not as a reputational defence ("we are not those people") but as a structural consequence ("the interface we posit cannot produce those effects").

This matters for how the Map reads parapsychological data. The decline effect, the inverse relation between sample size and effect size in RNG-PK meta-analyses (Bösch, Steinkamp & Boller 2006), and the strong Bayesian evidence for the null in a 12,571-participant sequential study (Maier, Dechamps & Pflitsch 2018) and in a pre-registered replication of an earlier positive result (Maier & Dechamps 2018, Study 2) are all consistent with a Born-preserving, self-concealing interface, as [selection-only-mind-influence](/topics/selection-only-mind-influence/) sets out. The micro-PK studies all target an external random-number generator, so [brain-locality](/concepts/brain-interface-boundary/) predicts no genuine effect in any of them whether or not the interface exists. But the Map does **not** read this corridor as evidence *for* the interface. The pattern is equally consistent with there being no effect at all; the decline-effect data are compatible with both readings and do not adjudicate between them. Reading "the data look the way a self-limiting channel would" as support for the channel would be textbook possibility/probability slippage. The firewall is a constraint the interface must live within if it exists, not a footprint proving that it does.

## What Would Actually Bear on the Map

If spectacular psi disconfirms the Map and a null result does not confirm it, what evidence *would* bear? The firewall sharpens the answer by ruling out the headline-grabbing channel and pointing at the quiet one. The discriminating observable is not effect *size* but the **content-confinement signature**: whether the dimensionality of reportable conscious content is bounded above by the dimensionality of the brain-generated candidate space (the Holevo-style ceiling of [selection-only-mind-influence](/topics/selection-only-mind-influence/)). That is a test the Map can lose—it predicts a specific ceiling—without being a test that any amount of dramatic psi could win. Beyond that test, the framework's empirical exposure appears to lie along the [falsification roadmap](/topics/falsification-roadmap-for-the-interface-model/) rather than in the parapsychology laboratory, and it is unevenly spread. Coherence-time and microtubule-signature results bear on the coherence-dependent candidate mechanisms, and collapse-model bounds such as the underground exclusion of the parameter-free Diósi-Penrose model (Donadi et al. 2021) bear on the physics those candidates have been paired with. None of them touches the endorsed post-decoherence path, which acts after coherence is gone. That path is exposed instead to a deviation *conditioned* on intention, task or subject in a brain-internal quantum process, a test not yet run in the work the Map has reviewed, and to supersession, should a decoherence-only account settle which outcome occurs.

There is also a converse worth stating plainly. The self-concealment that keeps the interface on the right side of the firewall has a price the register records at full strength: if aggregate statistics are exactly preserved and a selected outcome is observer-indistinguishable from an unselected one, the position "sits genuinely close to epiphenomenalism" ([P-Q3](/positions/quantum-interface/#mechanism-debt)). That is [ensemble-level-epiphenomenalism](/concepts/ensemble-level-epiphenomenalism/), the worry that a perfectly Born-preserving interface does no causal work the physical statistics need. The Map neither concedes the worry nor treats it as dissolved. Its candidate answer, the per-trial-versus-ensemble distinction together with the indexical fact of which outcome a subject actualises, is still open. A showing that the selection leaves no trace under any test, conditioned or not, and no content-confinement signature either, would leave that answer no empirical foothold and push the Map further toward epiphenomenalism. The firewall appears to protect the Map from the *force* picture's failure modes while exposing it to the *idleness* picture's. The Map owns both edges.

One cost of the firewall should be stated plainly rather than left for a critic to find. A falsifier is worth as much as the risk it runs, and this one runs little. The Map reads the existing evidence as showing that spectacular psi almost certainly does not occur, so the condition that would disconfirm the framework is one the framework already expects never to be met—while null results are, by the Map's own discipline, barred from counting as confirmation. Nor is that expectation independent of the record: Tenet 2's minimality is [empirical-constraint minimality](/tenets/#minimal-quantum-interaction), fixed by the requirement that the interaction respect the empirical record, so the firewall's height was set partly by the very record it is now said to predict. The parapsychology channel is therefore close to evidentially inert in both directions. That does not make the firewall dishonest: a framework that entails a prediction should say so, and this prediction is a real one that a sufficiently dramatic replicated result would break. What it means is that the firewall earns the Map less epistemic credit than the phrase "we are falsifiable here" suggests, and that the credit has to be earned somewhere the risk is live. For the endorsed corridor reading that place is not near-term: the coherence-time and collapse-model results that could arrive next year bear on rival candidate mechanisms, and the conditioned brain-internal test that would bear on the corridor itself is well beyond current reach at the neural grain.

## Relation to Site Perspective

The parapsychology firewall is the Map's [Minimal Quantum Interaction](/tenets/#minimal-quantum-interaction) tenet read as a prediction about parapsychology rather than as a mechanism for neural causation. The Map interprets the absence of replicable spectacular psi as consonant with its framework, while refusing to count that consonance as positive support—the [slippage discipline](/concepts/possibility-probability-slippage/) forbids the upgrade in both directions.

The firewall aligns with [Bidirectional Interaction](/tenets/#bidirectional-interaction) by locating mental efficacy in selection rather than force: consciousness makes the smallest difference the empirical record leaves room for, which is why it makes no *detectable bulk* difference. It aligns with [Dualism](/tenets/#dualism) by keeping the non-physical selector real while denying it the gross causal powers folk dualism imagines. And it inherits the framework's honest cost: the same self-concealment that keeps the Map consistent with the null record, and exposed to any spectacular result, is what generates the [ensemble-level epiphenomenalism](/concepts/ensemble-level-epiphenomenalism/) challenge logged as the strongest live open question in [the quantum-interface register](/positions/quantum-interface/) ([P-Q3](/positions/quantum-interface/#p-q3)).

The Map's position, stated as a wager: if a future result were to yield replicated psi that is large, lawlike or content-importing, the Map should treat that as disconfirmation and revise—because such an effect would mean mind influences matter in a way that injects signal, breaks Born statistics, or imports unmediated content, none of which the Map's interface permits. The Map is on record that it would rather be falsified than redraw the firewall to absorb the result.
