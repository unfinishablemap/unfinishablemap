In de Broglie–Bohm pilot-wave theory, the Born rule — the prescription that measurement outcomes occur with probability |ψ|² — enters not as an axiom but as an equilibrium condition: particle configurations happen to be distributed as |ψ|², much as a gas happens to be thermalised. The conditional this article examines is therefore sharp. **If** pilot-wave dynamics is the true mechanics, **and** non-equilibrium distributions (ρ ≠ |ψ|²) are physically possible, **then** the Born statistics observed in every experiment to date are a contingent fact about our cosmological epoch rather than a law of nature. Antony Valentini's research programme defends both conjuncts; the rival typicality school accepts the dynamics while resisting the contingency. The Unfinishable Map endorses neither side's framework — pilot-wave determinism removes the very indeterminacy the Map's tenets require, as the [Relation to Site Perspective](#relation-to-site-perspective) section below states plainly — and everything reported here holds inside that rival framework, at the level of theorems-with-premises and numerical simulations. No quantum non-equilibrium ensemble has ever been observed or produced.

Two further calibrations belong in this opening. First, nothing in this literature is about consciousness: none of the sources this article draws on connects quantum non-equilibrium to mind or free will, and every tenet-relevant inference drawn later is the Map's own, argued as such. Second, the programme's value to the Map is structural rather than evidential. It supplies a worked demonstration that "Born statistics hold" can name an emergent, in-principle-violable regularity rather than a fundamental law — and a price list showing what statistical-level deviation would cost, which is the Map's structural reason for claiming that its own proposed interface preserves Born statistics exactly rather than approximately ([the agency budget](/concepts/agency-budget/)).

## The Born Rule as an Equilibrium Condition {#quantum-equilibrium}

Pilot-wave theory gives particles definite positions at all times, guided by the wave function through the guiding equation; there is no collapse, and measurements have outcomes because configurations are always definite. Empirical equivalence with orthodox quantum mechanics then requires one further assumption, the *quantum equilibrium hypothesis*: that configurations are distributed according to |ψ|².

What makes |ψ|² special is *equivariance*, a property that follows immediately from the guiding equation together with the quantum continuity equation: if the configuration distribution matches |ψ|² at one time, the dynamics preserves that match at all later times. The Born distribution is dynamically self-sustaining — once in equilibrium, always in equilibrium. Sheldon Goldstein's Stanford Encyclopedia treatment draws the thermodynamic moral: Born probabilities "emerge naturally from an analysis of 'equilibrium' for the deterministic dynamical system that Bohmian mechanics defines."

The reframing cuts both ways, and the second direction is this article's subject. A condition, unlike an axiom, can fail. In frameworks where the Born rule is postulated, a "Born violation" would simply falsify the theory; in pilot-wave theory it would mean the world — or some subsystem of it — is *out of equilibrium*, with the same dynamics running underneath. Quantum non-equilibrium, ρ ≠ |ψ|², is a coherent physical regime of the theory. That coherence is the contingency thesis: the Born rule holds the way thermal equilibrium holds, as a state our region of the universe is in, not a rule reality is bound by.

## Relaxation or Typicality: Why We See Born Statistics {#relaxation-vs-typicality}

If equilibrium is a condition, something must explain why we find ourselves in it. Two schools answer differently, and the difference is where the contingency lives. Valentini's 2020 chapter frames the debate explicitly — a philosophical argument over the foundations of the theory's statistical mechanics, not a disagreement about any prediction accessible today.

**Dynamical relaxation (Valentini).** In two 1991 papers, Valentini constructed a *subquantum H-theorem* in deliberate analogy with Boltzmann's: a coarse-grained function measuring the departure of ρ from |ψ|² decreases under the dynamics, so generic initial distributions relax toward equilibrium on a coarse-grained scale. On this picture the early universe underwent a violent relaxation, and what experiments now sample is the relaxed aftermath — a subquantum analogue of Boltzmann's heat death, as Valentini puts it, in which the deeper structure of the theory has become invisible. Born statistics are an attractor the world reached, and departures from them are as physical as a temperature gradient.

**Typicality (Dürr, Goldstein and Zanghì).** The 1992 typicality analysis holds that no dynamical story is needed. With respect to the natural equivariant measure |Ψ|² on initial configurations of the universe, the overwhelming majority of initial conditions yield Born statistics for subsystems — just as the overwhelming majority of gas microstates yield Maxwellian velocities. Quantum equilibrium is quasi-necessary: a feature of typical universes, not a state that had to be reached and might have been missed.

The dispute is unresolved and largely philosophical, since the two schools agree about everything measurable today. Valentini's chapter presses that the typicality approach is "inherently circular and physically misguided," because the measure that defines typicality is the very equilibrium measure at issue; the typicality school replies that equivariance makes |Ψ|² the uniquely natural choice. The Stanford Encyclopedia entry — written by Goldstein, a principal of the typicality school — concedes that "the status of, and justification for, equilibrium distributions remains controversial." What turns on the outcome is the modal status of the Born rule inside the theory: on typicality, non-equilibrium is unphysical in practice; on relaxation, it is a real regime of a wider physics that cosmological history happened to erase.

## What Non-Equilibrium Would License {#signal-nonlocality}

The contingency thesis becomes more than bookkeeping when one asks what the non-equilibrium regime would permit. Valentini's answer is that two pillars of quantum phenomenology — no superluminal signalling and the uncertainty principle — are properties of the *equilibrium state*, not of the underlying theory.

The signal-locality theorem (2002) makes the first claim precise, and its scope conditions matter: any *deterministic hidden-variables theory* reproducing quantum statistics in equilibrium "must predict the existence of instantaneous signals at the statistical level for hypothetical 'nonequilibrium ensembles'." The underlying pilot-wave dynamics is already nonlocal, as Bell showed any such theory must be; equilibrium noise exactly screens that nonlocality from statistical view. Out of equilibrium the screen fails, and entanglement becomes a controllable channel — in the paper's words, "signal-locality is true only in equilibrium." The companion claim is *subquantum measurement*: an apparatus prepared in a distribution narrower than |ψ|² could resolve details of trajectories that equilibrium systems cannot, beating the uncertainty principle's limits, which on this reading are statistical facts about equilibrium rather than fundamental bounds.

Standard quantum mechanics then sits inside a wider physics roughly as thermodynamics sits inside statistical mechanics: exceptionless for equilibrium ensembles, silent about the larger space of states. The price is owned rather than hidden. Statistical-level superluminal signalling collides with relativistic causality as usually understood; Valentini treats the conflict as confined to a regime that no longer exists outside possible cosmological relics, while critics read the same implication as a point against the whole approach. The Map does not adjudicate that dispute. It reads the theorem adversarially, as pricing: within this class of theories there is nothing innocent between exact equilibrium, where the statistical-level budget for any influence is zero, and signalling.

## Relaxation Timescales, Residues, and the Cosmological Window {#cosmological-window}

Why has no laboratory seen non-equilibrium? The programme's own numerics supply the deflationary answer. Towler, Russell and Valentini (2012) estimated relaxation timescales numerically and found that, for typical wave functions with many superposed modes, coarse-grained relaxation is rapid — any primordial non-equilibrium in ordinary matter would have relaxed away long before laboratory physics began. The equilibrium we measure is what fast relaxation predicts.

Relaxation is not always complete, however. Long-time numerical studies (Abraham, Colin and Valentini 2014; extended to coupled oscillators by Lustosa, Pinto-Neto and Valentini 2023) found that the coarse-grained H-function can plateau at a nonzero residue whose size depends on the initial phases, and that systems with few superposed modes relax incompletely. Long-wavelength cosmological modes are exactly such few-mode systems — the physical basis for conjecturing that primordial non-equilibrium could survive at the largest scales.

That conjecture yields the programme's main proposed observational window. Colin and Valentini (2015) argued that incomplete relaxation at long wavelengths during a pre-inflationary era would suppress large-scale primordial power with an approximate inverse-tangent dependence on wavenumber, a candidate explanation for the low-ℓ anomalies in the cosmic microwave background; the companion analysis (2016) found that inverse-tangent form robust under varied initial conditions, “likely to be a fairly general and robust signature of quantum relaxation in the early universe” — conditional on stated assumptions, including a sub-quantum initial width and a radiation-dominated pre-inflationary expansion. Underwood and Valentini (2020) added a second channel: relic non-equilibrium surviving in decoupled exotic species could show up as anomalous spectral-line profiles.

The evidential status of all of this needs stating in one place. These results are theorems-with-premises and numerical simulations feeding derived predictions; none is an observation of non-equilibrium. Whether post-Planck data analyses support, disfavour, or merely fail to constrain the inverse-tangent deficit is a question this article has not surveyed, and the predictions are cited here as dated proposals rather than as claims about current empirical standing; laboratory proposals for producing non-equilibrium after 2016 are likewise unsurveyed. On the falsifier ledger the state is that nothing has come in: the non-equilibrium hypothesis is unrefuted and untested, which is weaker than having survived a test. What the cosmological window nonetheless demonstrates is methodological: a beyond-quantum regime can be lawful, bounded, and in-principle detectable, rather than an unfalsifiable addition.

## Relation to Site Perspective {#relation-to-site-perspective}

The mismatch comes first. Bohmian determinism leaves no indeterminacy for consciousness to resolve: [the Map's interpretations survey](/topics/qm-interpretations-beyond-many-worlds/) rates pilot-wave theory the interpretation most hostile to its framework, and nothing in this article revises that verdict or the [positions register's](/positions/quantum-interface/) preference for post-decoherence selection. What follows are structural lessons the Map takes from a rival's mathematics, not an adoption of the rival.

**[Minimal Quantum Interaction](/tenets/#minimal-quantum-interaction)** takes the price structure. The Map's default reading preserves Born statistics *exactly* (position P-Q2), and Valentini's signal-locality theorem is the structural reason for claiming exactness rather than smallness: within deterministic hidden-variables theories, any nonzero statistical-level budget is bought at the cost of superluminal signalling, breaching the no-signalling commitment position P-Q7 records. Non-equilibrium therefore enters the Map's ledger as the *priced fall-back* — [the agency budget](/concepts/agency-budget/) develops this adversarial reading — rather than as the Map's mechanism.

**[Bidirectional Interaction](/tenets/#bidirectional-interaction)** gains a modal lesson. At least one worked framework makes "Born statistics could have been otherwise" a physical statement rather than a category error. The Map speculates — this is the Map's own inference, found nowhere in the non-equilibrium literature — that if the true theory likewise demotes the Born rule from law to condition, the space of lawlike, minimal, mind-associated influence is wider than the axiom reading suggests. Two honesty notes bound the speculation: Valentini's programme locates non-equilibrium in the early universe and in exotic relics, never in brains; and the Map's default corridor reading does not use this door, since it preserves Born statistics exactly by construction.

The **typicality tension** is owned rather than smoothed. If Dürr, Goldstein and Zanghì are right, equilibrium is quasi-necessary and the wider physics is idle — the reading most in tension with the Map's interest in lawlike deviation, and it may be correct. One unexplored seam remains even then: [the Map's Bohm article](/concepts/bohm-implicate-order-and-active-information/) records Landsman's argument that reproducing Born statistics requires a contingent sampling of the initial configuration, which would relocate the contingency from dynamics to initial conditions. Whether that seam could host anything mind-associated is an open question the Map flags rather than answers.

**[No Many Worlds](/tenets/#no-many-worlds)** finds a structural ally. Pilot-wave theory is a single-world theory with definite configurations throughout, and Valentini's 2010 chapter defends that single-outcome reading against the Everettian charge that the theory's wave function already contains many worlds (Brown's reply in the same volume presses the charge). The Map's Tenet 4 shares the single-world commitment while rejecting the determinism that accompanies it here.

**[Occam's Razor Has Limits](/tenets/#occams-limits)** binds in both directions. Against the axiom reading: that the Born rule has held in every tested regime does not establish it as fundamental law, and the relaxation school provides a concrete model of how a universally observed rule can be an equilibrium artefact of our epoch — exactly the extrapolation-from-incomplete-knowledge Tenet 5 distrusts. Symmetrically against the Map: contingency is not established either. The non-equilibrium regime is unobserved, the typicality school stands as a live countermodel, and the Map must not quietly convert "coherently violable" into "probably violated."
