Memory systems do not fail together when consciousness is disrupted. Tulving (2002, p. 5) called episodic memory "more vulnerable than other memory systems to neuronal dysfunction"; the Map conjectures a fuller order across anaesthesia, sleep, hemispherectomy recovery, terminal lucidity, and dissociative states — [episodic / autonoetic](/concepts/episodic-memory/) most vulnerable, [semantic / noetic](/concepts/semantic-memory/) intermediate, [procedural / anoetic](/concepts/implicit-memory/) most preserved — a hypothesis with exceptions, not an established cross-condition finding. The disruption has a *second* signature: the order in which a system degrades is often *not* the mirror image of the order in which it recovers. Autonoetic consciousness is the last channel to return when a deeply anaesthetised brain emerges, yet is reported among the first to return in terminal lucidity. The Unfinishable Map reads both patterns as among the catalogue's stronger pieces of interface evidence: they appear to be the *shape* of evidence a multi-channel interface would predict, and a shape production theories seem to struggle to produce without ad-hoc fitting. The reading is offered as a serious interpretation the data make available, not one the data force.

The two patterns are two faces of one architecture: the vulnerability hierarchy is the *substrate-state* face, recovery-order asymmetry the *direction-of-travel* face. In the resulting accommodation contest, production theories can absorb both patterns, but each absorption costs an extra mechanism or a pattern explained after the fact; the [filter theory](/concepts/filter-theory/) reads the ordering off its core architecture and accommodates the asymmetry, at the price of the interface itself. The disagreement is over where each framework pays that mechanism cost ([mechanism-costs cartography](/topics/mechanism-costs-dualism-thickness-quadrants/)), not over the evidence: the patterns *constrain* the production-vs-filter comparison rather than *establishing* the filter reading — accommodation evidence under [the evidential-status discipline](/project/evidential-status-discipline/), not predictive success at single-event resolution.

## The Tulving Hierarchy as Diagnostic Framework

Endel Tulving's 1985 framework pairs three memory systems with three modes of consciousness ([developed at the concept tier](/concepts/anoetic-noetic-autonoetic-consciousness/)): **anoetic** with [procedural and implicit memory](/concepts/implicit-memory/) (fluency without self-representation), **noetic** with [semantic memory](/concepts/semantic-memory/) (knowing without re-experiencing), and **autonoetic** with [episodic memory](/concepts/episodic-memory/) and its future-directed counterpart (*re-inhabiting* a perspective, the self appearing twice — present rememberer and past protagonist). Wheeler, Stuss, and Tulving (1997) defined autonoetic consciousness as "the capacity that allows adult humans to mentally represent and to become aware of their protracted existence across subjective time."

The three systems are doubly dissociable. Patient K.C., after extensive bilateral brain damage, retained semantic and noetic awareness while losing episodic memory and autonoetic consciousness entirely (Tulving 2002); semantic dementia produces the inverse early in its course; skilled procedural performance can survive both. Klein (2014) separated *content* from *manner of experiencing*: the same proposition ("I visited Rome in 2019") can come with autonoetic re-experiencing or bare noetic recognition. That separability lets degradation work *channel-by-channel* rather than shifting one substrate uniformly, and it appears to do so in a specific order.

## The Cross-State Vulnerability Pattern

The table summarises the clinical-state cases; citations for each row, and their differing evidential grades, follow in the sections below.

| State | Anoetic / Implicit | Noetic / Semantic | Autonoetic / Episodic |
|-------|--------------------|-------------------|----------------------|
| Light anaesthesia | Preserved | Partially impaired | Severed (anterograde amnesia) |
| Deep anaesthesia (propofol) | Largely preserved | Severed | Severed |
| Ketamine "disconnected" state | Preserved | Variable | Preserved but content-decoupled |
| NREM Stage 3 | Preserved | Suppressed | Severed (no episodic encoding) |
| REM sleep | Preserved | Partial | Partial (lacks autonoetic self-coherence) |
| Hemispherectomy (adult-onset) | Preserved | Largely preserved | Initially impaired, often recovers |
| Terminal lucidity (case reports) | Not reported | Returns mid-episode | Returns earliest in the window |
| Dissociative amnesia | Preserved | Preserved | Selectively severed (autobiographical) |
| DID (between alters) | Preserved | Preserved | Reported severed; objectively transferred |
| Depersonalisation | Altered (bodily unreality) | Preserved | Present but felt as not-mine |

Two patterns stand out. The **ordering recurs** in most rows where the substrate itself degrades: autonoetic goes first, and under recovery returns earliest in some states (terminal lucidity) and latest in others (deep anaesthesia emergence), but in none of them arbitrarily. The ketamine row inverts it: autonoetic experience is preserved and vividly reported while its coupling to the environment and to noetic access gives way, a coupling failure rather than a channel failure. The **selectivity is sharp**: each state produces a specific cross-channel signature, not a uniform dim-out, and the dissociative rows (below) produce it with the substrate intact.

### Anaesthesia: The Anchor Case

[Anaesthesia](/topics/anaesthesia-and-the-consciousness-interface/) is the cleanest exhibit: the perturbation is reversible, dose-dependent, pharmacologically selective, and measurable in *both directions* within one subject. Adequate surgical anaesthesia produces anterograde amnesia — no episodic encoding — while implicit learning survives only inconsistently: words played under propofol can prime later performance without explicit recall (Deeprose et al. 2005), an effect a 61-study meta-analysis found in about a third of cohorts (Linassi et al. 2021). Sharpest is the xenon-ketamine contrast: Sarasso et al. (2015) found, in eighteen volunteers, low cortical-response complexity with no experience reported under either propofol or xenon, but wakefulness-like complexity under ketamine, whose subjects emerged describing long vivid dreams unrelated to the environment — two NMDA antagonists with opposite phenomenological outcomes, and channels therefore separable within a single receptor system. The hierarchy is the joint output of several pharmacologically distinct mechanisms converging on one ordering (surveyed in Mashour 2024), and that convergence carries the argument: cross-agent uniformity goes unexplained under any single mechanism, and a bandwidth/reconstruction-cost architecture is one thing that could explain it. The ketamine split — behavioural unresponsiveness, later vivid autonoetic recall — is one row of the dissociation pattern behind [the self-stultification master argument](/topics/self-stultification-as-master-argument/), and recurs outside pharmacology as cognitive motor dissociation, volitional brain responses without behavioural ones (Bodien et al. 2024).

### Sleep Stages: The Within-Subject Replication

Sleep tests the hierarchy within one subject across one night. **NREM Stage 3** suppresses autonoetic consciousness most strongly — episodic encoding is essentially absent and the sleep-mentation literature characterises such awakenings as more "thought-like" than re-experienced (Siclari et al. 2017 mapped the regional correlates) — while procedural consolidation proceeds at full strength. **REM sleep** reactivates the interface unevenly: dreams have propositional structure and vivid sensory presence, but the *autonoetic self-coherence* of waking episodic memory is conspicuously degraded (the dreamer rarely notices contradictions or projects a temporal self). Lucid dreaming, where some autonoetic self-awareness returns under training, is the exception to accommodate: the channel reading takes the REM blockade to be partial and reopenable under trained engagement, which the frontal re-engagement reported in lucid REM ([developed separately](/topics/lucid-dreaming-and-dualist-rendering/)) fits as readily as a production account does. **Hypnagogic transitions** seem to loosen in the same order as anaesthesia induction, though sleep-onset work has not resolved the sequence as finely (see [sleep-and-consciousness](/concepts/sleep-and-consciousness/)).

### Hemispherectomy: The Substrate-Loss Case

[Hemispherectomy](/topics/hemispherectomy-and-the-resilience-of-unified-consciousness/) provides a substrate-loss test the other cases cannot. In adults it produces severe initial impairments but typically substantial recovery of consciousness, language, and personality. The hierarchy-relevant claim is the *order* of recovery — procedural earliest, semantic more gradually, autonoetic new encoding last — a clinical impression, not a measured tier-by-tier outcome. Child-onset cases add a calibration constraint: early hemispherectomy produces near-complete recovery including autonoetic capacity, because the developing brain can reassign the architecture. On the interface reading these are real, partially substrate-anchored channels with plastic tissue assignment (the contested interpretive step, not a datum the pattern hands over); the production reading owes an explanation only if that order recurs across very different lesion topographies.

### Terminal Lucidity: The Recovery-Order Test

[Terminal lucidity](/topics/terminal-lucidity-and-filter-transmission-theory/) is the catalogue's strongest exhibit of selective channel-restoration. Patients with advanced dementia or other severe decline sometimes regain, shortly before death, a striking lucidity — recognising relatives, recalling episodes, reassembling an autonoetic narrative. The phenomenon is rare but repeatedly documented: Nahm and Greyson (2009) coined the term; their 2012 review with Kelly and Haraldsson collected 83 cases spanning 250 years, and Batthyány and Greyson's (2021) survey produced 124 detailed reports, over 80% showing return of memory, orientation, and verbal ability with most dying within hours to days.

Those surveys establish that the episodes occur; the *within-episode ordering*, which matters more here, rests on much less. Case narratives describe autonoetic recollection returning sharply at the start of the window, semantic content stabilising mid-episode, and the window closing as autonoetic access destabilises first — but no episode has been recorded prospectively, and relatives and carers writing afterwards are poorly placed to time a channel sequence. The ordering is what the case literature suggests, not a measured result.

Throughout, the substrate keeps degrading; the brain is not repairing itself in days. On the filter reading the interface may have briefly reopened channels that were blocked rather than destroyed; on the production reading a substrate insufficient yesterday becomes sufficient today before becoming insufficient tomorrow, through substrate-state mechanisms rival readings may propose (paradoxical "decompression" of inhibition, a reactive noradrenergic surge) that need no interface. The interface reading is a live hypothesis the evidence does not decide; because the reported return occurs under continuing degradation, terminal lucidity tests it most directly, and is the pivotal case for the direction-asymmetry argument below and its weakest link.

### Dissociative States: Selectivity Without Substrate Damage

Dissociative states decouple the channels without structural substrate damage. **Dissociative amnesia** selectively severs autobiographical (autonoetic) access while semantic knowledge, procedural skill, and working memory remain intact. **Dissociative identity disorder** is reported to [compartmentalise autonoetic access](/concepts/metacognitive-access-versus-retention/) *between* alter states, each with its own autobiographical timeline, while semantic and procedural memory transfer freely; Reinders et al. (2003) imaged the difference, reporting distinct regional cerebral blood-flow patterns for two senses of self in one brain. **Depersonalisation-derealisation** is unusually diagnostic: autonoetic content is present but the felt *ownership* is missing, suggesting autonoetic experience has internal structure (content plus mine-ness plus pastness) that can dissociate — the multi-channel reading's prediction one level deeper than the three-tier hierarchy itself.

The dissociative rows are the hierarchy's hardest test for production theories: with no substrate damage, the selectivity must be explained by *connectivity between intact regions* — channel architecture on the filter reading, a dependence a sophisticated production reading must concede — so the disagreement is over what kind of relationship that connectivity *is*, not over the evidence. These geometries, with the DID sociocognitive caveat, are developed in [the clinical-dissociation article](/topics/clinical-dissociation-as-systematic-evidence/).

## The Structural Argument: Graded-with-Separable-Channels

The hierarchy makes a claim single-state evidence cannot: *the order is robust across mechanisms*. Anaesthesia, sleep, hemispherectomy, terminal lucidity, and dissociation share no common molecular mechanism, yet the Map reads them as converging on one ordering of channel vulnerability. That [cross-mechanism convergence](/concepts/cross-mechanism-convergence/) is the explanandum, though not as five independent confirmations: cortical-response complexity, the variable Sarasso et al. (2015) track above, also describes the integration breakdown of NREM Stage 3, so the anaesthesia and sleep rows share one explanatory axis. The dissociative rows have no degradation variable to appeal to, but what they separate is [access, not storage](/concepts/metacognitive-access-versus-retention/), where intra-cerebral accounts compete and the interface reading adds no prediction yet.

A binary on/off production theory predicts uniform loss and cannot explain the ordering. A sophisticated production reading concedes that consciousness has separable *parts* and explains the ordering by differential cortical-area vulnerability: autonoetic depends on more cortical regions than noetic, which depends on more than anoetic, so any perturbation hits autonoetic first. The mechanism cost is the *additional empirical commitment* that the regional vulnerability ordering matches the channel hierarchy across *all* the mechanism-distinct perturbations. It is testable in principle: a narrow perturbation that hits an autonoetic-supporting region while sparing its noetic-supporting subregions should, on the production reading, produce a *different* ordering, while the filter reading still predicts the standard one. The catalogue has no clean case yet; [the targeted-lesion article](/topics/targeted-lesion-discriminating-tests-between-production-and-filter-readings-of-the-memory-hierarchy/) develops the design space, and naming the test rather than treating current convergence as already separating is what [the direct-refutation discipline](/project/direct-refutation-discipline/) requires of a [discrimination-problem](/concepts/discrimination-problem/)-class underdetermination. A predictive-processing reading instead absorbs the hierarchy through hierarchical-prior reweighting — arguably stronger, since it derives the ordering from independent commitments, but still owing *why* the higher-level prior produces autonoetic phenomenology, which may land it on the [hard problem](/topics/hard-problem-of-consciousness/) one level up.

The filter reading derives the ordering more directly, though not for free. Channels of higher bandwidth and higher reconstruction cost should be more vulnerable to partial blockade and slower to restabilise; autonoetic carries the highest bandwidth (temporal self-projection, double presence of self) and the highest reconstruction cost (recall that reopens the trace requires reconsolidation under conscious direction), noetic less, anoetic least. Given that assignment, the ordering and the cross-mechanism convergence fall out of the architecture rather than being added to it — but the assignment is not independently measured. Bandwidth and reconstruction cost are ranked partly by the very ordering they are then invoked to explain, a milder form of the after-the-fact fitting this argument charges against rivals, so the advantage is narrower than "predicted versus accommodated" suggests. The interface commitment is itself the mechanism cost, which leaves the filter theory's accommodation comparatively direct and its rivals' comparatively additional.

## Recovery-Order Asymmetry: The Direction-of-Travel Face

Production accounts that explain degradation and recovery from one substrate-vulnerability profile leave the *direction* of travel no explanatory role; the cross-state data give it one, in three cases.

**Anaesthesia: induction is not the reverse of emergence.** The asymmetry shows first in the *hysteresis* of state transition: Sepúlveda et al. (2019) characterise *neural inertia* as the resistance of circuits to changing state — inducing unconsciousness requires higher concentrations than restoring it, so the loss- and recovery-of-consciousness thresholds do not coincide, and the brain at a given concentration is in a *different* state depending on direction of travel. Channel order, by contrast, roughly mirrors induction's — autonoetic encoding is the first casualty and the last to stabilise — so here the asymmetry lies in thresholds and timing. Stone et al. (2025) found genetically identical mice under identical protocols emerging at times varying by at least two orders of magnitude — a stochastic neuronal-dynamics model fit where a deterministic pharmacokinetic one failed. Emergence is also *active* rather than passive drug washout: Hu et al. (2023), again in mice, traced it to ubiquitin-mediated degradation of the KCC2 cotransporter in one thalamic nucleus, a route that runs independently of which anaesthetic was given. Induction tracks drug concentration; emergence is separately mechanised and stochastically timed, its timing *not* fixed by macroscopic substrate state. Its *interpretation* is held at a live-hypothesis tier — equally compatible with classical noise and interface indeterminacy ([stochastic-emergence article](/topics/stochastic-emergence-as-quantum-interface-evidence/)); the robust point is narrower: emergence is not the deterministic reverse of induction.

**Terminal lucidity: recovery under continuing degradation.** The most striking case, because the *direction of substrate change* and the *direction of conscious change* point opposite ways. In deep-anaesthesia emergence the autonoetic channel returns *last*; in terminal lucidity autonoetic recollection is reported to return *first and most prominently*. If the reports have that sequence right — and the caveat above is the strongest reason to doubt it — two recoveries of the same channel proceed in opposite order, one on a rising substrate trajectory, the other on a *falling* one.

**Dissociation: onset is not the reverse of recovery.** Dissociative amnesia's *onset* can be gradual (a narrowing under stress); its reported *recovery*, when the barrier lifts, can be abrupt — on the interface reading, a bridge reconnected rather than re-grown. With nothing to repair, a production account cannot appeal to differential regional recovery rates.

These signatures form a family, catalogued at [direction-of-interface-change signatures](/concepts/direction-of-interface-change/). None is captured by a quasi-static substrate-state description; each requires explanation sensitive to *which way the system is moving*, which puts them in the seam-class register [the self-concealing-interface synthesis](/apex/self-concealing-interface/) names as where the Map's evidence can live.

A production account *can* recover the asymmetry by adding two direction-specific mechanisms, not one per signature: a dying-process neuromodulatory surge for terminal lucidity, a connectivity-gating story for dissociation. The hysteresis and stochastic-emergence terms are one commitment rather than two, since hysteresis is generic to bistable nonlinear systems and variable timing at threshold is what such a system does under noise. Both remaining additions are independently reasonable; the point is their *additivity* — neither follows from the other or from bistability. The filter reading is *consistent with* a direction-sensitive signature rather than deriving one: if channels differ in bandwidth and reconstruction cost, then *which* components block or reopen, and in *which order*, may depend on the direction and cause of change. That clause forbids no ordering, the mirror-symmetric one included: the unmeasured-assignment caveat sharpened, since here nothing is assigned at all. What survives is a parsimony point at the mechanism-cost layer, not positive evidence the filter reading is true, because a production account willing to pay the per-case cost absorbs every signature. What would *discriminate* the readings is the work of the [targeted-lesion (substrate-state)](/topics/targeted-lesion-discriminating-tests-between-production-and-filter-readings-of-the-memory-hierarchy/) and [direction-dependent (direction-axis)](/topics/direction-dependent-discriminating-test-design/) test-design articles — a reversible perturbation whose recovery ordering fails to mirror its loss ordering, which a static-threshold production model forbids and the filter reading permits.

## Evidential Calibration

First, both patterns are *accommodation evidence*. The filter theory did not predict the ordering in advance: it inherits Tulving's independently developed taxonomy and episodic-fragility claim; the fuller order is the Map's extrapolation. The architecture is what the cross-state data suggest; the interface reading is what they make available without yet settling.

Second, the patterns do not rule out production theories; they locate the *mechanism cost* their accommodations must pay. Recovery-order asymmetry removes the simplest substrate-symmetric production account but leaves intact every account willing to add direction-specific mechanisms. The cumulative cost across the hierarchy, the dissociation architecture, terminal lucidity, the propofol/ketamine divergence, and the two direction-asymmetry mechanisms is where the comparison comes due, not at any one case.

Third, the strongest single case (terminal lucidity) rests on the weakest evidence base; a dying-process substrate mechanism remains a live rival to the interface reading of it. The dissociative row is the cleanest accommodation case and the most contested. Lynn et al. (2014), replying to a defence of the trauma model of dissociation, hold that the notion that people can encode traumatic experiences without being able to recall them lacks strong empirical support — which puts in question not merely the *mechanism* behind compartmentalised autonoetic access but whether an objective memory barrier is there at all. What they do concede, as common ground with their opponents, is that DID is a disorder of *self-understanding*: compartmentalisation at the level of self-representation, which is the level the channel reading needs, but a weaker datum than a demonstrated inter-identity amnesia would be. Assembling several direction-dependent signatures earns *framework-internal weight*, not a tier-upgrade: the convergence makes the Map's reading more articulated; it does not discriminate it from rivals absent an independent test only one reading predicts.

## Relation to Site Perspective

Three of the Map's tenets do work here.

**[Dualism](/tenets/#dualism)** (Tenet 1) is engaged because both the vulnerability ordering and its direction-asymmetry fit a multi-channel interface architecture more directly than a substrate-emergent reading. The argument is comparative: the filter theory derives the ordering from architecture and accommodates the direction-sensitivity; rival readings absorb them by adding commitments. The dualist reading is not more parsimonious — interface architecture is not simple — but it pays its mechanism cost upfront, at the tenet level, rather than per-case at the accommodation level.

**[Minimal Quantum Interaction](/tenets/#minimal-quantum-interaction)** (Tenet 2) is engaged only through the filter reading's bandwidth picture, which the tenet does not supply: its minimality bounds detectability, not channel width. The autonoetic channel is reconstruction-heavy — episodic retrieval involves the selectional work the Map locates at the quantum-indeterminacy layer ([reconsolidation selection window](/topics/reconsolidation-as-selection-window/)) — and its recovery-order asymmetry is read as tracking the *direction* of interface change, a reading the terminal-lucidity evidence cannot yet separate from substrate state (below).

**[Bidirectional Interaction](/tenets/#bidirectional-interaction)** (Tenet 3) is engaged because the autonoetic channel, which the Map reads as failing first and swinging most in recovery order, is the one involving the most active subject-participation: re-experiencing, reconsolidation-under-direction, lucid-dream metacognitive control, autobiographical assembly. If consciousness merely received from the brain, recovery order would be fixed by substrate readiness alone — direction entering only through the substrate's own hysteresis, which shifts thresholds without reversing a channel order. Should the autonoetic channel prove to return first under falling substrate function and last under rising, that would favour a reading on which it is high-engagement in both directions — a conditional the terminal-lucidity evidence is not yet strong enough to discharge.

No single case carries the comparative argument; the convergence across [consciousness-and-memory](/topics/consciousness-and-memory/), [consciousness-disruption-and-the-mind-brain-interface](/topics/consciousness-disruption-and-the-mind-brain-interface/), [clinical-dissociation-as-systematic-evidence](/topics/clinical-dissociation-as-systematic-evidence/), and [neurological-dissociations-as-interface-architecture](/topics/neurological-dissociations-as-interface-architecture/), discounted where rows share an explanatory variable, does — channels that fail independently, recover independently, degrade in a recurring order, and recover in a direction-dependent one.
